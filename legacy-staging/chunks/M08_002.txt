## 8. Status and provenance rules

### Accepted status does not flow backward

When an audit accepts a corrected parent, it does not revive an earlier overbroad version. The manifest and claim catalog preserve both sources; the correction ledger controls use.

### Acceptance does flow through exact unchanged scope

Later handoffs often say an earlier theorem remains accepted and should not be re-audited. This carries the exact statement, not strategic commentary or exploratory conjectures surrounding it.

### Audit-pending results remain separate

At this checkpoint, Puzzler 27's four new parent/descendant statements are proved but audit pending. Explorer 27's three results are accepted after Puzzler 27's audit.

### Duplicate files do not create independent evidence

Exact byte duplicates count as one mathematical source with several aliases. Near-duplicates and revised versions remain separate because a correction may occur in only one.

## 9. How to challenge a compression decision

For any theorem omitted from the current bank:

1. locate its document capsule in `11_DOCUMENT_AND_CLAIM_CATALOG.jsonl`;
2. open its exact raw source by hash;
3. write its hypotheses, conclusion, and retained-information ledger in modern language;
4. identify the alleged parent;
5. test all three dominance conditions;
6. if the parent loses a consumed resource, restore the old result as a retained specialization;
7. if only the proof method is unique, add it to the technique atlas rather than duplicating the theorem node.

This makes the consolidation reversible without restoring chronological clutter.
