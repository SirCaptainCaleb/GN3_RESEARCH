## Policy changes incorporated

This generation incorporates the latest conversation-level architecture decisions, superseding earlier versions:

- no global handoff number;
- one mathematical producer stream per research role plus one Auditor disposition stream;
- mathematical dependencies form an explicit DAG;
- audit is a distinct-worker transfer boundary, not an automatic post-result step;
- Puzzler / Archivist owns the full historical theorem/lemma archive and recombination duty;
- Visionary strategy is a separate role-addressed control plane;
- every worker checks its own strategic inbox on `Continue` regardless of proof-chain relevance;
- guidance uses replacement semantics and can be explicitly cleared;
- Visionary communication has a high threshold and should raise targets rather than micromanage;
- different roles receive deliberately different information budgets to preserve separation of minds;
- the human may simply tell every worker `Continue`; routine synchronization is worker-owned.
