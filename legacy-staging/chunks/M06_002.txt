## 16. Reusable finite-order techniques

### Use forbidden overlong paths as local clauses

At order ten, any six-vertex tight path contradicts the longest-five normal form. A missing turn can therefore be forced by showing that its opposite sign would concatenate into a six-path.

### Build alternate longest paths and reuse their reverse stars

One forced turn often creates another five-path. Because every five-path is global-longest in the order-ten normal form, its reverse stars provide new forced turns. This was the engine of both historical mate-fork proofs and modern folds.

### Preserve labelled quotient signatures

When a four-vertex residual is transitive, keep the matching blocks and orientation labels. Signature agreement between two endpoint views can close a branch that an unlabelled classification cannot.

### Exploit exact profile equality

The modern order-ten work gains strength from exact sizes. Equality turns local longestness into universal reverse stars, makes pair shadows four plus four, and makes every consecutive trimer shadow exact.

### Separate root-common from pivot-specific structure

Whenever a pivot proof yields an exact complement, test whether deletion exactness already gives it at the original root. This separated the universal triple-shadow/anchor package from the richer dual-longest fold ancestry.
