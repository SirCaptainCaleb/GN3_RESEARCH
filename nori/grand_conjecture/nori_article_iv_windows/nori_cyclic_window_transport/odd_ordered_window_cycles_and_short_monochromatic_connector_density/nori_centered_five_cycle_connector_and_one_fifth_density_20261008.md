# Vertex-centered ordered-three-face odd five-cycle forces monochromatic four-edge connectors at every root

THEOREM (CENTERED FIVE-CYCLE; UNRESTRICTED ORDERED-THREE-FACE COLORING). Let n>=5. Fix ANY cube vertex z and ANY five distinct coordinate directions W={a,b,c,d,e}. Each directed shift w_i -> w_(i+1) is realized by the four-edge geodesic centered at z, with directions (p_i,p_(i+1),p_(i+2),p_(i+3)) and start root r=z xor e_(p_i) xor e_(p_(i+1)). Indeed after the first two edges this geodesic passes through z; its first and second ordered three-face windows are precisely w_i and w_(i+1). The five states w_i form a directed ODD FIVE-CYCLE in the honest ordered-window shift graph. For ANY binary coloring c of ordered three-faces (without the NORI antipodal oddness restriction), the five cyclic colors cannot alternate at every edge, so at least one adjacent pair w_i,w_(i+1) has the same color. Hence every vertex z and every five-coordinate set W possesses an actual four-edge geodesic centered at z with two equal-color consecutive three-face windows. This is a root-prescribed monochromatic two-window CONNECTOR local to a five-face.
QUANTITATIVE STRENGTHENING. Fix z and W, let ordered (a,b,c,d) range uniformly through all 5*4*3*2=120 distinct four-tuples in W, giving the corresponding centered four-step geodesic and two ordered-three-face window colors. At least 1/5 of these tuples yield equal colors. Proof: there are 5!=120 cyclic lists of W; each has five directed consecutive four-tuples and at least one monochromatic adjacency. Among all 120*5 incidences, each ordered four-tuple appears exactly five times (it determines the fifth direction and a cyclic rotation of the list). Therefore five times the number M of same-color four-tuples is at least 120, so M>=24 of 120, i.e. density >=1/5.
