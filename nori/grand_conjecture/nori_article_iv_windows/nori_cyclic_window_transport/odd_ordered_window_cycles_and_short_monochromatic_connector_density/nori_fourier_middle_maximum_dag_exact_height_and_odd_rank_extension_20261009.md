# Exact linear DAG height after deleting the antipodally symmetric Fourier transversal

Let n>=4 and let H^+ denote the full DIRECTED physical ordered-three-face window shift graph of Q_n. Its vertices are genuine ordered physical 3-faces (F,(a,b,c)) with a,b,c distinct, and directed edges from (a,b,c) to (b,c,d) whenever a,b,c,d are FOUR DISTINCT coordinate directions and the actual physical faces form two consecutive windows on a real 4-edge geodesic. Let T_mid be the subset of windows whose MIDDLE free coordinate is the MAXIMUM of its three directions. As proved in Item nori_fourier_debruijn_peak_transversal_20261009, T_mid is tau-invariant and meets every directed cycle.

**THEOREM (SHARP ACYCLIC HEIGHT).** The LONGEST directed path in the induced physical graph H^+\T_mid has exactly
   2n-6
directed window-shift edges. Equivalently, every directed walk avoiding T_mid has at most 2n-3 underlying direction occurrences; these bounds are attained by actual physical windows. In particular the graph is directed acyclic for a simple quantitative reason, not merely by a cycle-transversal argument.

**Proof.** Any directed window walk with k arcs projects to a length-(k+3) word p_1,...,p_(k+3) over alphabet [n], where every four consecutive letters are pairwise distinct. Absence of T_mid says that for every consecutive triple, p_(t+1) is NOT greater than both its two neighbors. Since all adjacent entries are distinct, the sequence of adjacent comparison signs can never contain a (+,-) pair. Therefore all '-' comparisons precede all '+' comparisons and the entire direction sequence is STRICTLY DECREASING followed by STRICTLY INCREASING (possibly one side empty). Call the minimum pivot m. Let q<=n be the number of DISTINCT alphabet symbols used anywhere in this word. On either monotone side a symbol occurs at most once. If we include the pivot in both sides, the total possible word length is 2q-1, minus one for each *missing occurrence* of a non-pivot symbol on one of the two sides.

There must be AT LEAST TWO missing occurrences: let s_2 and s_3 be the second- and third-smallest among the q actually used symbols. If no symbol is omitted from either side, s_2 occurs immediately before AND after m, repeating after two positions; this violates distinctness of each consecutive four-letter window. If there is precisely one omission, it must omit s_2 from one side, since otherwise s_2 still repeats at distance two. Then s_3 appears on BOTH sides, at distance exactly THREE: around m the word looks (...,s_3,s_2,m,s_3,...) or (...,s_3,m,s_2,s_3,...), again violating pairwise distinctness of four consecutive symbols. Hence at least two omissions, so length k+3<=2q-3<=2n-3, i.e. k<=2n-6.

For equality use the literal direction word
  (n,n-1,...,4,3,2,1,4,5,...,n)
of length 2n-3. It decreases then increases, so no triple has its middle letter maximal. Every four consecutive coordinates are pairwise distinct: within a monotone segment this is evident; across the minimum, the first repeated coordinate 4 is four positions away from its preceding occurrence, hence every four-consecutive block is injective. Follow these directions starting at ANY physical cube vertex; the resultant successive 3-face windows are genuine, and each four-edge segment has distinct directions, so the resulting window sequence is a directed path with 2n-6 arcs in H^+\T_mid. Since no directed cycle exists, all its window vertices are distinct. QED.

**GENERAL ODD-WINDOW EXTENSION (antipodal feedback transversal).** For ordered r-face windows with distinct direction labels and physical r+1-step overlap arcs (r+1 distinct directions), define T_j to comprise windows whose LARGEST free coordinate occurs in position j. A directed cyclic window walk projects to a cyclic letter sequence. Choose an occurrence of its largest symbol; that occurrence appears once in EACH of the r window positions in successive windows. Thus every T_j is a directed feedback vertex set of precisely 1/r of all physical ordered-r-face windows. Physical antipodal reversal maps T_j to T_(r+1-j). When r is ODD, the central-position T_((r+1)/2) is individually antipodally invariant. No claim that 1/r is minimum for the restricted (r+1)-distinct-coordinate graph; rotation r-cycles of the ordinary de Bruijn graph are prohibited by injectivity.

**Research implication.** For r=3, the complement of the middle-maximum transversal has precisely understood symbolic path geometry (valley sequences) and exact linear directed height. However physical path certificates with terminal memory and root-coupled transport are not automatic from symbolic DAG paths. In particular, the companion explicit n>=5 active NORI coloring shows this ENTIRE full-valley-geodesic class can be unusable for one-switch closure. A topological use of the DAG must therefore attach peak windows rather than discard them permanently.
