# NORI ordered-window shift graph is connected, but its antipodal topological index is only one

THEOREM (connectivity and index barrier). For every n>=5, the ordered-three-face window-shift graph H_n^(3), with vertices (F,(a,b,c)) and edges given by actual consecutive windows along four-edge cube geodesics, is connected. The involution tau(F,pi)=(bar F,reverse pi) acts freely on its geometric realization. Consequently its quotient double cover is connected and nontrivial, so the associated sign line bundle has nonzero first Stiefel-Whitney class; yet the cohomological index is precisely ONE (its square vanishes), and there exists a continuous tau-odd nowhere-zero map |H_n^(3)|->R^2. Therefore a bare Borsuk-Ulam argument on this 1-dimensional window graph cannot provide the higher-dimensional intersection forcing needed for the grand conjecture.
PROOF OF CONNECTIVITY. First forget exterior bits and retain only the ordered three distinct free directions. The shift relation (a,b,c)->(b,c,d) is legal whenever d is distinct from a,b,c. For any five distinct a,b,c,d,e, the directed shift walks (a,b,c)->(b,c,d)->(c,d,e)->(d,e,b)->(e,b,c)->(b,c,a) and (a,b,c)->(b,c,d)->(c,d,e)->(d,e,a)->(e,a,c)->(a,c,b) realize respectively cyclic rotation of a triple and interchange of its last two entries. These moves generate S_3, so all six orders of a fixed three-element set are connected. A shift replaces one free coordinate by an exterior coordinate, and the Johnson graph on three-element subsets of [n] is connected, giving connectivity of the direction-word projection. To connect the exterior-bit fibers, fix w=(F,(a,b,c)) and any exterior direction d. The two states w and w^d (same ordered triple, exterior d-bit toggled) share a predecessor window of ordering (d,a,b): the predecessor three-face has d free and exterior c-bit suitably fixed, and the first d-step may start at either d-corner, yielding the two successor faces. Thus all 2^(n-3) exterior-bit choices for a fixed ordered triple are connected. This lifts connectivity of the direction-word graph to H_n^(3).
FREE ACTION AND INDEX. No vertex equals tau of itself, and no shift edge joins w to tau w because the suffix (b,c) of (a,b,c) differs from the prefix (c,b) of (c,b,a). Hence tau has no fixed point in the 1-dimensional realization. Since the total cover is connected, its double-cover class w1 in H^1(|H|/tau;F2) is nonzero. The quotient is a graph, so w1^2=0. For an explicit zero-free odd R^2-map, trivialize the double cover over a spanning tree of the quotient, send the two sheets to opposite points of S^1, and extend equivariantly over each remaining edge (using a constant arc for sheet-preserving edges and a semicircle joining antipodes for sheet-exchanging edges). This produces an equivariant map to S^1 subset R^2. QED.
This sharpens the previous signed-window zero-crossing formulation: the natural NORI color field is genuinely topologically twisted, but its 1D graph has insufficient index. Any successful high-index construction must thicken this carrier into root-coupled simplicial cells carrying actual joint window/path reachability and valid two-window bridge extraction.
