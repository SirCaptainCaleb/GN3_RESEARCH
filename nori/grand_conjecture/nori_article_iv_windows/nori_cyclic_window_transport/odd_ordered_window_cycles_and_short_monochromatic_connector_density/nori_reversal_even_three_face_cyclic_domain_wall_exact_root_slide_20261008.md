# Reversal-even NORI faces: antiperiodic belts and exact three-seam one-switch extraction

THEOREM (NORI reversal-even subclass and k=3 cyclic seams). Let n>=4 and c(F,pi) be an ordered-three-face binary coloring satisfying both the active NORI antipodal reversal axiom c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi) and the additional reversal-even hypothesis c(F,reverse pi)=c(F,pi) for every F,pi. Then c(bar F,pi)=1-c(F,pi). Take any root x, permutation p=(p1,...,pn), and follow the 2n-edge closed geodesic belt with cyclic direction sequence p repeated twice, from x to bar x and back to x. For each of its 2n directed three-edge windows let a_j be the actual ordered-three-face color, numbered modulo 2n. The second half is antipodal to the first with the SAME ordered triple, so a_(j+n)=1-a_j. Put d_j=a_j XOR a_(j+1). Then d_(j+n)=d_j and m=sum_(j=1)^n d_j is a positive odd integer. A root slide along the belt cyclically shifts (d_1,...,d_n), preserving m.
For any root slide producing a full antipodal n-edge geodesic, its n-2 ordered-three-face windows have exactly n-3 internal transitions, a contiguous block of n-3 of these n cyclic seam bits. Thus if M_3 = max_j (d_j+d_(j+1)+d_(j+2)) with indices mod n, the MINIMUM number of color changes over all 2n root positions along this belt is EXACTLY D_min=m-M_3. In particular this belt supplies a NORI geodesic with at most one color change iff m-M_3<=1. If m=1, some root slide is monochromatic. If m=3, some root slide is one-switch iff at least two of its three cyclic domain walls occur within three consecutive seam positions. Therefore in a hypothetical NORI counterexample satisfying the reversal-even hypothesis, every m=3 belt must have its three walls cyclically separated by at least three seam positions; hence n>=9 is necessary for an m=3 belt to occur in such a counterexample. For n<=8 a reversal-even belt with m<=3 automatically yields closure; any bad belt must have m>=5.
PROOF. Reversal-even plus the active antipodal-reversal axiom gives antipodal oddness for unchanged triple order. The direction sequence in the second n edges repeats the first, so each directed triple window is on the antipodal physical three-face with identical order. This gives antiperiodicity. Consequently its n transitions satisfy XOR(d1,...,dn)=a_1 XOR a_(n+1)=1, proving m odd. Root sliding moves the start edge by one along the SAME geometric 2n belt; windows rotate accordingly. A full n-edge half has n-2 windows and therefore n-3 internal boundaries. Its omitted transitions among the n cyclic transitions form the complementary contiguous three-seam block. Maximizing omitted walls proves D_min=m-M_3. For m=3, M_3>=2 exactly when two walls have cyclic index distance at most two. If all three cyclic gaps are at least three, they sum to n>=9. QED.
SCOPE. This is a genuine face-dependent, ordered-three-face NORI subclass, strictly beyond edge coloring. In general NORI the reversal-even hypothesis fails; the two-channel monodromy (a_(j+n),b_(j+n))=(1-b_j,1-a_j) gives a periodic antisymmetric channel and the scalar seam parity is unavailable. This theorem does not assert the full NORI conjecture.
