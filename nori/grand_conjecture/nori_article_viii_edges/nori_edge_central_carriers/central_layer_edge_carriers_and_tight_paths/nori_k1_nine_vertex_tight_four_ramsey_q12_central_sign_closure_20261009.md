# Computer-certified 4-uniform tight-path Ramsey number and Q12 central-sign edge closure

Let X be any 9-element set. In every two-coloring of the unordered four-subsets of X, there is an ordered sequence of EIGHT DISTINCT elements (v1,...,v8) for which the FIVE consecutive 4-subsets {v_i,v_(i+1),v_(i+2),v_(i+3)} (1<=i<=5) have one common color.

**Exact SAT proof certificate.** Number the C(9,4)=126 variables by lexicographic ordering of four-subsets of {0,...,8}. For each ordered 8-permutation p of {0,...,8}, let t_i be the variable of the unordered set p_i,...,p_(i+3) for i=1,...,5. To encode failure, add two clauses (t1 OR ... OR t5) and (NOT t1 OR ... OR NOT t5), forbidding a monochromatic tight path. Reversal of p duplicates the same two clauses. There are 9!/2=181440 reversal classes, giving exactly 362880 distinct clauses, all width five. Z3 returned UNSAT on this full explicitly generated CNF (script nori_r4_tightpath_sat.py with R=4). The proof is computational conditional on the correctness of this exact exhaustive encoding and SAT solver; a small independent unsatisfiability certificate or conceptual proof remains to be extracted.

**Sharpness.** On a ground set of eight elements choose one vertex a; color four-subsets by their indicator of containing a. For ANY ordered 8-path, the first and last consecutive four-subsets partition the eight elements, so exactly one contains a, and the path cannot be monochromatic. Thus the 9-vertex threshold is sharp for this theorem.

**Q12 application (original antipodally odd physical edge conjecture, central vertex-sign subclass).** Let U have 12 coordinate directions. Suppose λ assigns bits to the 6-subsets with λ(U\\S)=1−λ(S), and every physical cube edge incident to rank 6 among ranks 5,6,7 has color λ(S) where S is its unique rank-6 endpoint (edge colors elsewhere unrestricted). Choose distinct h1,h2∈U, and choose nine of the other ten directions. Apply the proved tight four-subset theorem to t(T)=λ(T∪{h1,h2}). Obtain eight distinct a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h with five equal-colored 4-windows. Insert h1,h2 between the first four and last four to obtain the TEN-direction word
  (a,b,c,d,h1,h2,e,f,g,h).
Its FIVE consecutive 6-windows are precisely the five previous 4-windows plus {h1,h2}; all have common color q. There remain two unused directions u,v. Prepending u produces 6-set {u,a,b,c,d,h1}; appending v produces 6-set {h2,e,f,g,h,v}. These two 6-sets are complementary in U and therefore have opposite λ-colors. Exactly one has color q, yielding a MONOCHROMATIC tight 6-path on ELEVEN distinct directions. Applying the established exact physical Johnson middle-three-layer path correspondence gives a fully monochromatic 12-edge antipodal cube geodesic. Every edge of that geodesic lies in ranks 5-7 and therefore has color q.
