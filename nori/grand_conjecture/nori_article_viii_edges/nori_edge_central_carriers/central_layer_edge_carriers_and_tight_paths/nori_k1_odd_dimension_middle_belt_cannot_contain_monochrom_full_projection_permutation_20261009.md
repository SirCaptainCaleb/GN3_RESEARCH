# Every odd cube has valid antipodally odd edge colorings forbidding all middle-belt monochromatic antipodal geodesics

# All-dimensional sharp no-go for odd middle-level-only closure

Let n=2k+1>=3 and choose ANY fixed-point-free permutation sigma of the coordinate directions [n]. Color the ACTUAL undirected direction-i cube edge through vertex x by c_i(x)=x_(sigma(i)). This is physical because sigma(i)!=i, and antipodally odd because c_i(bar x)=1-c_i(x).

THEOREM. Every full antipodal n-geodesic whose vertices all lie in the two central Hamming-weight layers k and k+1 contains BOTH edge colors. Thus NO such two-middle-layer path can be monochromatic, although this same coloring always has many full monochromatic antipodal geodesics by the proved functional-dependency theorem.

PROOF. Along a length-n path staying within weights k and k+1, the vertex weights alternate. As n is odd, the FIRST and LAST traversed directions p_1,p_n have the SAME traversal orientation: both are 0-to-1 if the path begins at weight k, or both are 1-to-0 if it begins at weight k+1. Let s be the COMMON INITIAL ROOT BIT of directions p_1 and p_n (s=0 in the first case and s=1 in the second). Because sigma is a permutation and has no fixed point, there is a unique OTHER coordinate i with sigma(i)=p_1 and a unique OTHER coordinate j with sigma(j)=p_n; i and j are different. The direction-i edge is traversed AFTER p_1, since p_1 is the first direction and i!=p_1. Therefore its actual color is 1-s, as the p_1 bit was toggled already. The direction-j edge is traversed BEFORE p_n, since p_n is last and j!=p_n. Its actual color is s, as the p_n bit has not yet been toggled. Thus this genuine full antipodal path contains a color-s edge and a color-(1-s) edge and cannot be monochromatic. QED.

EXPLICIT FULL GOOD PATH for the cyclic driver sigma(i)=i+1 modulo n: starting root x=e_1 (only coordinate1 initially equals1), traverse directions (1,2,...,n). For each i<n, its color is current bit x_(i+1)=0 since i+1 has not yet been traversed; for i=n, its color is current bit x_1=0 since direction1 was traversed first from initial bit1. Hence every edge is color0 and the path is a genuine full antipodal n-geodesic. It necessarily leaves the central two layers for n>=5. Thus a full globally valid coloring may REQUIRE leaving the middle belt to find a monochromatic antipodal geodesic.

GENERAL LESSON. Any attempted proof of the ORIGINAL odd-edge conjecture that shows the presence of a full MONOCHROMATIC antipodal geodesic exclusively within the two middle Hamming-weight layers in EVERY odd-dimensional antipodally odd coloring is FALSE, for every odd n>=3. This is a rigorous physical counterexample to the restricted carrier strategy, not a counterexample to the edge conjecture itself. It complements the recently proved even/odd middle-rank sufficient conditions, whose hypotheses exclude this projection coloring in the relevant layers, and guards against treating central-band certificates as universal.
