# Even-cube middle-weight-root monochromatic belt geodesics can be excluded by antipodally odd central vertex labels

THEOREM (even-dimensional center-root carrier no-go). For each even n=2k>=2, choose ANY antipodally odd Boolean label lambda on the middle-rank k cube vertices, satisfying lambda(complement S)=1+lambda(S). Such labels exist, for example lambda(S)=1 if coordinate1 belongs to S and0 otherwise. Color every physical cube edge connecting a rank-k vertex S with a rank-(k+1) or rank-(k−1) vertex by the bit lambda(S). This is consistent because each such edge has EXACTLY ONE rank-k endpoint. The antipodal image of a rank k↔k+1 edge is a rank k↔k−1 edge with rank-k endpoint bar S, and its color is lambda(bar S)=1+lambda(S); hence the central three-level belt coloring satisfies the active odd-edge condition. Extend colors to ALL other physical edge antipodal orbits arbitrarily (possible because an actual physical edge is not its own antipode for n>=2).

Under this valid antipodally odd edge coloring, EVERY full n-edge antipodal geodesic that STARTS at a Hamming-rank-k vertex S and remains entirely inside the three middle vertex levels k−1,k,k+1 has AT LEAST ONE edge-color change. Indeed its FIRST edge touches middle vertex S and is colored lambda(S), and its LAST edge touches the complementary middle vertex bar S and is colored lambda(bar S)=1+lambda(S). These two colors differ. No assumption about intermediate edges is needed.

This directly refutes a tempting root-normalization lemma: one cannot insist that a universally forced center-belt monochromatic antipodal geodesic START or END at exact middle Hamming weight, even though the global edge conjecture may still hold. For n=4, exhaustive search of the central 3-level belt indicates paths starting from an OUTER belt layer can rescue monochromatic closure; the theorem does NOT forbid those. This is a proved dimension-independent restricted-method obstruction, not a counterexample to edge-geodesic closure.

Together with Item nori_k1_odd_dimension_middle_belt_cannot_contain_monochrom_full_projection_permutation_20261009, this shows why middle-rank global topology must retain path/root freedom: the odd two-middle-layer carrier can fail entirely, and the even three-middle-layer carrier cannot restrict its root to the middle rank.
