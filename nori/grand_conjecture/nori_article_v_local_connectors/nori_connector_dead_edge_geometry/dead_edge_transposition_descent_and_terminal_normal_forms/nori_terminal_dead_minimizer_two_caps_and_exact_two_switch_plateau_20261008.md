# Terminal-dead NORI switch minimizers have two forced constant caps; two-switch plateau block lengths are at least two

THEOREM (SHARP BOUNDARY DESCENT AT A TERMINAL DEAD EDGE). Let n>=7 and c be ACTIVE NORI, binary ordered physical 3-face colors satisfying c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi). Let P be a full antipodal geodesic with LAST cube edge e_i dead, and let t be the dead-edge local rigidity bit at its penultimate vertex z. Write its length-(n-2) ordered-three-face window word w=(w1,...,w_m), m=n-2. Dead-edge local rigidity plus antipodal oddness force w1=1-t, w_(m-1)=t, w_m=1-t. Let D(w) denote its total number of binary color transitions.
(1) If w_(m-2)=1-t, exchanging the final two coordinate directions produces a full antipodal geodesic P' with D(P')=D(P)-1, regardless of the new unconstrained face color. Proof: the final THREE old word entries are (1-t,t,1-t), with TWO changes, whereas the same transposition replaces them by (1-t,X,t), with EXACTLY ONE change for either X=0 or1; all earlier window colors are unchanged. Here the new last triple has dead direction i in its middle and is centered on the original dead edge, hence has color t.
(2) If w2=t, perform one BACKWARD root slide (move the last coordinate i to the FIRST position and move the root across the antipodal i-edge). The new first three word colors are (t,1-t,t), because the new FIRST dead edge is the antipodal partner of e_i and old w1,w2 are copied afterward. Then exchange the first two directions: the first three colors become (1-t,Y,t), with EXACTLY ONE transition, again independent of Y. All later window colors remain unchanged. Therefore this two-move operation produces P' with D(P')=D(P)-1.
COROLLARY (RIGID DEAD-END MINIMIZER CAPS). If P is globally switch-minimizing among ALL full antipodal geodesics (roots and orders allowed), neither strict descent can occur. Thus w1=w2=1-t and w_(m-2)=w_(m-1)=t, while w_m=1-t.
COROLLARY (TWO-SWITCH PLATEAU). Suppose D(P)=2 and P ends in a dead edge and is globally minimizing. Since the first and last word colors agree and the next-to-last is different, the two-switch word is NECESSARILY
  w=(1-t)^a t^b (1-t), with a>=2 and b>=2.
Hence m=a+b+1>=5, n>=7. Conversely, this necessary pattern does not by itself make the dead-edge endpoint removable; further compatible square certificates are needed. The two adjacent-transposition moves above operate on ACTUAL physical ordered face windows and require the genuine active antipodal-reversal rule only for the backward root-slide antipodal dead-edge bit. This is an exact local descent theorem, NOT a proof of grand closure.
