# Dead-hub chains force full monochromatic facets; every hypothetical counterexample has a three-live run on every projected antipodal geodesic

THEOREM (ALL-DIMENSION DEAD-HUB GEODESIC CHAIN EXTRACTION). Let n>=5, and let c be ANY binary coloring of ordered physical three-faces of Q_n (active NORI oddness NOT needed). Fix coordinate i and identify the parallel i-edges with Q_m, where m=n-1. A projected position z in Q_m is DEAD if its physical i-edge is traversed by no monochromatic directed 4-edge geodesic, and LIVE otherwise. The exact dead-edge rigidity gives each dead projected position z a bit t(z) such that EVERY ordered three-face through either endpoint of that i-edge with all three free directions distinct from i has color t(z). If two dead projected positions differ in at most THREE coordinates, then their bits agree: choose an ordered physical three-face whose free directions contain the differing directions and omit i; the two hubs lie on that same face.
Let P=(v_0,...,v_m) be ANY projected FULL ANTIPODAL geodesic of Q_m. Suppose P contains NO THREE consecutive LIVE positions. Then the first and last dead positions lie within the first/last two indices, and the gap between successive dead positions is at most THREE, since a larger gap would leave three consecutive live intermediate vertices. All dead hub bits along P therefore agree, say t. Every consecutive triple of projected edge directions of P spans a physical three-face that contains at least one of its four vertices and hence at least one dead hub: if all four were live, they would include a forbidden run of three live positions. Its color is therefore t. Thus ALL m-2 ordered 3-face windows of P within its fixed i-facet are MONOCHROMATIC t. Append the sole unused direction i at the end to give a full n-edge antipodal cube geodesic with exactly one additional ordered-three-face window and AT MOST ONE COLOR CHANGE. QED.
CONTRAPOSITIVE FOR ACTIVE NORI: If the grand one-switch conjecture FAILS, then for EVERY coordinate i and EVERY rooted antipodal geodesic of its projected Q_(n-1), there exist THREE CONSECUTIVE projected positions whose physical i-parallel edges are ALL LIVE (covered by genuine monochromatic 4-geodesic witnesses). This is a stronger dual HEX-type necessary condition than the previously proved fact that every such projected geodesic meets one live position. In particular, with L_i the set of live i-edges, each projected geodesic contains >=3 live positions, so |L_i|>=ceil(3*2^(n-1)/n) by averaging random rooted antipodal projected geodesics, each of whose n vertices is uniform.
TWO-STEP DENSITY: Each projected full geodesic has at least one three-vertex subpath v->v xor a->v xor a xor b whose three vertices are in L_i. There are exactly 2^m*m*(m-1) directed two-step geodesics (root and ordered distinct directions), and each fixed index among the m-1 possible length-two subpaths of a random rooted full geodesic is uniformly distributed over these. Thus the count of FULLY LIVE directed length-two geodesics in projected Q_m satisfies >= 2^m*m = 2^(n-1)*(n-1). This applies conditionally to any hypothetical no-closure coloring. Equivalently, if for SOME direction the fully-live two-step projected path count is <2^(n-1)*(n-1), grand closure follows. The proof produces an explicit physical full one-switch geodesic whenever the run-three obligation fails.
No claim is made that the required three-live runs alone force full closure; they capture a necessary obstruction on the matching of genuinely uncertified local four-window edges.

TWO-FACET MONOCHROMATIC CYLINDER (STRENGTHENING). The extracted projected antipodal (n-1)-edge geodesic P has not just one monochromatic lift but TWO SIMULTANEOUS monochromatic lifts into the two parallel i-facets of Q_n, with the SAME coordinate order and SAME color t. Indeed every ordered 3-face window of P contains a dead projected hub v_r. Both endpoints of the DEAD i-edge at v_r have all ordered-three-faces avoiding i identically colored t; translating the entire projected path by e_i leaves the relevant window triple free directions outside i and includes the translated dead hub, so its color remains t. Thus the two rails P^(i=0),P^(i=1) of the combinatorial cylinder P×K2 are monochromatic t with all their corresponding windows in the two physical facets certified by the SAME sequence of dead hub positions. Either rail can be extended to a full one-switch antipodal n-geodesic by crossing the unused i-direction at one endpoint. The cross-rung windows themselves need not have color t, so the result does NOT assert a monochromatic full n-geodesic.
