# Dead-edge rigidity creates sixteen-root, rank-four monochromatic terminal reachability packets

Let n>=8 and c be any ACTIVE NORI coloring. Suppose e_i={z,z xor e_i} is a dead physical i-edge. Its antipodal partner {bar z,bar z xor e_i} is dead. The dead-edge local rigidity forces every ordered 3-face through either endpoint of e_i with three free directions outside i to have a common color t, and every ordered 3-face through the antipodal pair with three free directions outside i to have color 1-t.
Fix ANY six-direction set W subset [n] minus {i}, any ordered terminal two-direction memory J=(a,b) with a,b in W, and its rank-four complementary support U=W minus {a,b}. For any 3-element subset S of U, there exists a MONOCHROMATIC SIX-edge geodesic with ordered directions (permutation of S, unique element of U minus S, a,b) starting at x=z xor S and ending at x xor W. Indeed after the first three flips it passes through z, and ALL four ordered-three-face windows contain z, are free of i, and hence have the common color t. The same holds after translating the hub and start by i, producing starting root z xor e_i xor S, since the identical local rigidity holds at the opposite endpoint z xor i. Similarly at the antipodal dead pair bar z,bar z xor i, the same terminal label is witnessed in color 1-t from starts bar z xor S and bar z xor i xor S. Because n>=8 and W omits i and at least one other direction, these four families of four roots are pairwise disjoint (the antipodal hub families disagree in at least one exterior coordinate outside W and i). Therefore a single FORMAL reachability label (J,U), |U|=4, belongs to the exact color-free profile L_x for AT LEAST SIXTEEN DISTINCT physical roots x:
  x in { z xor S, z xor i xor S, bar z xor S, bar z xor i xor S : S subset U, |S|=3 }.
Consequently the signed-root nerve contains a genuine 15-simplex on these 16 positive root types, witnessed by one and the same nontrivial rank-4 label, and its negative tau-reflection. The labels come from ACTUAL simultaneously valid monochromatic 6-geodesics and retain the complete ordered terminal two-coordinate memory. At n=7 the same construction holds but the near-hub and antipodal hub packets overlap, so we state the clean 16-root conclusion for n>=8.
This is an unconditional high-rank root-profile incidence theorem CONDITIONAL ONLY ON THE EXISTENCE OF A DEAD EDGE, NOT on grand failure. It supplies long reachability in the precise signed-root nerve that classical Tucker would need; however its tau-dual label has terminal order reversed and complementary support D_J minus U, which includes i. These sixteen direct mono six-geodesics ALL avoid i, so they do NOT automatically furnish an off-diagonal mixed-sign edge or an exact same-root reversed-tail collision. Resolving that tail-complement transport remains the missing grand closure mechanism.
