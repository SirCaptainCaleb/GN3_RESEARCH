# Rejected Q10 dead-edge diameter-two bound: missing terminal cap color control

REJECTED PROOF STEP — OPEN, NOT A THEOREM. A proposed Q10 claim that all dead edges in a hypothetical counterexample lie in two antipodal diameter-two projected sets (and number at most20) was NOT established. The flawed implication was: two same-direction dead hubs with projected distance four produce a 9-edge one-switch geodesic inside their common 9-facet, and one may append their shared dead direction i without adding a further change. Appending i adds an ordered-three-face window and can introduce a SECOND switch. Thus the argument does not force a full ten-edge one-switch witness when the two hub bits differ. A corrected proof needs a genuine i-cap color-control, another choice of cut/root, or an additional simultaneous physical witness. The special SAME-COLOR d=4 case can yield a MONOCHROMATIC 9-facet path (then appending i adds at most one switch), but this does not bound all t=0 positions against their t=1 antipodal mates at projected distance4. No diameter-two compression or <=20 cardinality bound has been proved. DO NOT elevate this open note as a theorem.
