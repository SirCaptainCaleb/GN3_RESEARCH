# Valid NORI has a connected global complex of monochromatic four-geodesic witnesses

Let n>=5 and let c be a valid active NORI binary ordered-three-face coloring, c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi). Let G(c) be the physical cube graph formed by the edges occurring in SOME genuine monochromatic directed four-edge geodesic. The proved dead-edge rigidity lemma says the absent cube edges M form a MATCHING. THEOREM: G(c)=Q_n minus M is CONNECTED, and has minimum degree >=n-1.
LEMMA (cube cut versus matching): if Q_n minus a matching M is disconnected, M contains ALL parallel cube edges in some direction i. Choose a component S and a crossing i-edge {z,z xor i} in M. Because M is a matching, the j-edges at its endpoints for every j!=i are not in M, so their endpoints stay on the same respective sides of S. The opposite i-edge in that (i,j)-square must therefore also cross S and belong to M. Iterating j-flips reaches every i-parallel edge; all lie in M.
IMPOSSIBILITY OF A FULL PARALLEL DEAD MATCHING UNDER NORI ODDNESS. Suppose all i-edges dead. At every vertex z the dead-edge local rigidity pattern gives a bit t(z): every ordered three-face through z with i absent has color t(z), with i middle has color t(z), and with i first/last has color 1-t(z). Faces with i free are shared by z,z xor i, yielding t(z)=t(z xor i). For j!=i choose an ordered triple omitting i but including j; its physical face contains both z and z xor j, yielding t(z)=t(z xor j). Since these edges generate Q_n, t(z) is globally constant. But the antipodal reversal of a triple containing i first has i last and would ALSO have color 1-t, contrary to active oddness. Thus no full i-parallel dead matching exists. The cube-cut lemma proves G(c) connected.
Let X(c) denote the physical simplicial union of the genuine monochromatic initial- and terminal-memory Freudenthal path cones. Every four-edge mono witness is in X(c), so X(c) contains G(c) in its 1-skeleton and is connected. Under hypothetical grand failure, all monochromatic paths have <=n-2 edges, each certified cone has dimension <=n-4, and X(c) has no antipodal-edge fixed point. It is therefore a connected FREE antipodal finite simplicial complex of dimension <=n-4. Its quotient double-cover has nonzero w1, equivalently cohomological Z2 index at least one: a connected nontrivial two-sheeted cover cannot have w1=0. No higher-index claim or grand closure follows. This is a topology-ready, genuinely path-certified global connectivity theorem.
