# Every centered NORI pentagon either has one forced connector or produces a fully certified monochromatic-witness square

Let n>=5. Fix a cube vertex z and cyclically ordered five distinct free directions p1,...,p5. Let P_i be the 4-edge geodesic with direction word (p_i,p_(i+1),p_(i+2),p_(i+3)) and center z, indices modulo five, starting at z xor {p_i,p_(i+1)} and ending at z xor {p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}. Let h_i be 1 exactly when its two ordered-three-face windows have the same color. The centered pentagon parity identity gives sum_i h_i=1 mod2, so the number of certified mono four-edge paths among P_1,...,P_5 is exactly 1,3 or5.
DICHOTOMY: Either exactly one h_i=1, in which case the five successive ordered-window colors around the pentagon alternate except for precisely the one equal-color seam (a rigid binary pattern, up to cyclic rotation and bit-complement), OR at least three h_i=1 and the union X(c) of HONEST initial/terminal monochromatic Freudenthal path-cones contains the COMPLETE physical two-dimensional cube square based at z on two of these five directions.
PROOF OF FILLED SQUARE: In any 3-element subset of five cyclic positions, two indices differ by 2 modulo 5 (because the graph whose edges join cyclic neighbors has maximum clique size two). Choose marked indices i and i+2. From P_i, the initial-memory cone contains the triangle with vertices {z, z xor e_(p_(i+2)), z xor {p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}}. From P_(i+2), the terminal-memory cone contains the triangle {z, z xor e_(p_(i+3)), z xor {p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}}. The two triangles share the diagonal {z,z xor {p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}} and together triangulate the entire physical square F(z;{p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}). Both are genuine monochromatic path-witness triangles (their witness COLORS need not agree), so their union belongs to X(c) as a true embedded PL 2-disk. QED.
For active NORI antipodal-reversal oddness, the dichotomy is carried equivariantly under antipodal reversal: color complementation preserves marked status and sends the certified square at z to its antipodal counterpart. This is a dimension-independent local route from a one-dimensional pentagon parity to actual two-dimensional path-compatible cells. The sharp one-marker alternative is genuinely attainable locally, including in valid NORI colorings, so the dichotomy is NOT grand closure. A high-dimensional proof might exclude the one-marker pattern from a globally compatible all-hub system, or use the resulting filled-square cells for an equivariant cup-product/KKM argument.
