# Vertices with all n incident edges monochromatic-four-geodesic-covered form an antipodal path separator

THEOREM (GLOBAL ANTIPODAL HEX SEPARATOR FROM ORDERED-WINDOW RIGIDITY). Let n>=5 and c be ANY ACTIVE NORI binary ordered-three-face coloring, with c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi). Let M be the matching of physical cube edges not traversed by any genuinely monochromatic directed four-edge geodesic. Let U consist of FULLY COVERED cube vertices, namely vertices z incident to NO dead edge. Then U is antipodally invariant and meets EVERY CUBE GRAPH PATH from x to bar x for EVERY vertex x, not merely monotone geodesics. Equivalently, every connected component of Q_n minus U contains no antipodal pair. Quantitatively,
  |U| >=ceil(2^n/(n+1)).
Since U is antipodally invariant, |U| is even and in fact |U|>=2ceil(2^(n-1)/(n+1)). In particular, every full antipodal cube geodesic passes through at least one vertex at which ALL n physical incident edges are certified by actual monochromatic four-geodesic witnesses.
PROOF. At a vertex z outside U exactly ONE dead edge e_i is incident, because M is a matching. Give z the 2-part SIGNED DEAD-LABEL lambda(z)=(i,t_i(z)), where t_i is the unique local rigidity bit for the dead i-edge: triples through z with i missing/middle have color t_i, and i first/last color 1-t_i. The bit is shared by both endpoints of e_i. If z,w are ADJACENT and both outside U, the dead directions MUST be equal. Otherwise their two dead edges have different directions and endpoint-set distance <=1, contradicting the proved distinct-direction dead-edge separation bound >=3. If that common dead direction is i, then t_i(z)=t_i(w): if w=z xor i, they share the same dead edge; if w=z xor j with j!=i, the two dead i-edges are parallel and neighboring in the projected (n-1)-cube, and their rigidity bits agree because the identical physical ordered three-face with free {i,j,k} (i in the middle, k distinct) contains both hubs. Thus lambda is CONSTANT along every ordinary cube edge inside the induced graph Q_n minus U.
But physical antipodality sends any dead i-edge to another dead i-edge and, by the active NORI oddness on antipodally reversed ordered triples, sends its rigidity bit t to 1-t. Hence if z outside U then bar z outside U and lambda(bar z)=(i,1-t_i(z)) !=lambda(z). If a cube path from z to bar z avoided U, its consecutive vertices would all be outside U, where lambda is constant along edges, giving equal endpoint labels. Contradiction. If z belongs to U, any path starting there already meets U. Therefore U meets every antipodal path.
For the quantitative estimate choose x uniformly from Q_n and a permutation p uniformly. The resulting full antipodal geodesic has n+1 vertices, each uniformly distributed on Q_n. Since every such path visits U, its expected number of visits to U is at least one, but it equals (n+1)|U|/2^n. Rearranging proves |U|>=2^n/(n+1), and antipodal pairing gives the even integer refinement.
TOPOLOGICAL CONTENT. The dead-direction/local-color potential lambda is a locally constant multi-sheet label on the complement of U and flips polarity under antipodality. Hence U is a genuine path-separating defect locus for any putative equivariant continuous extension, with a dimension-independent quantitative density. It is a concrete Hex/Borsuk-Ulam-style obstruction built out of ACTUAL monochromatic four-window geodesic certificates. However the separator does not yet supply a single long monochromatic geodesic or the exact complementary reversed-terminal-memory witnesses needed for NORI grand closure.
