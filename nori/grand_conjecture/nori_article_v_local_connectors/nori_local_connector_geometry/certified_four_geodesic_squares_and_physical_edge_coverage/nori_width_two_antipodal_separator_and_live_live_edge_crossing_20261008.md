# The fully covered NORI vertex separator has width two between antipodal dead-edge regions

Let n>=5 and c satisfy active antipodal-reversal-odd NORI coloring. Let M be the matching of physical cube edges that belong to NO monochromatic ordered-three-face four-edge geodesic. Let U be the set of vertices incident to NO edge in M. For each z outside U define its signed dead label lambda(z)=(i,t) where i is its unique incident dead-edge direction and t is its forced local two-color pattern bit.
THEOREM (TWO-STEP LOCAL CONSTANCY). Whenever z,w are both outside U and d_Qn(z,w)<=2, then lambda(z)=lambda(w). If their dead directions differ, this contradicts the previously proved >=3 physical endpoint-set separation for dead edges of different directions. If they agree and equal i, then the corresponding parallel dead i-edges have projected positions at Hamming distance <=2, and the proven common-physical-face rigidity gives equal t. If z,w are two endpoints of the SAME dead edge this also holds because they share t. Thus lambda is constant on every distance-at-most-two pair of vertices of the dead-incident set.
THEOREM (WIDTH-TWO ANTIPODAL SEPARATION). If z is incident to a dead edge, EVERY physical cube-graph path from z to its antipode bar z contains TWO CONSECUTIVE vertices in U, hence an entire physical cube EDGE whose BOTH endpoints have all n incident edges certified by genuine monochromatic four-geodesic witnesses. More generally, every path between dead-incident vertices with DIFFERENT lambda labels traverses an edge inside the induced subgraph Q_n[U]. Proof: suppose a path has NO pair of consecutive vertices in U. Moving along it, any consecutive dead-incident vertices are distance1, and any two dead-incident vertices separated by exactly one vertex of U are distance2. In either case the signed lambda values agree by local constancy; induction across all isolated U occurrences shows the labels of its first and last dead-incident vertices coincide. But NORI antipodal oddness sends lambda(bar z)=(i,1-t)!=lambda(z). Contradiction.
QUANTITATIVE COROLLARY. Let D=Q_n without U, the dead-incident vertices. For a uniformly random cube root x and coordinate permutation p, P(x,p) is a uniform rooted full antipodal geodesic. If x lies in D, the path contains at least one physical edge entirely in Q_n[U]. Thus the EXPECTED number of U-U path edges is at least |D|/2^n. On the other hand every physical cube edge occurs on a random rooted full antipodal geodesic with probability 1/2^(n-1), so E[# U-U path edges]=|E(Q_n[U])|/2^(n-1). Therefore
  |E(Q_n[U])| >= |D|/2 = (2^n-|U|)/2.
The class of fully locally covered vertices is hence not only an antipodal hitting set but a THICK antipodal separator: it supplies a full edge crossing in every antipodal path from a dead-incident root. The edge's endpoints have individually certified local four-window witnesses, but no single compatible full one-switch NORI path is obtained without an additional two-memory connector gluing theorem.
