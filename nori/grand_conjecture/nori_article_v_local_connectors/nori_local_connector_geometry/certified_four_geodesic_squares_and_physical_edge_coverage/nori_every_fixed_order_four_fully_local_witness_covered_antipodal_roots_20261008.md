# Every coordinate order has at least four antipodal roots whose entire path is covered by monochromatic four-geodesic witnesses

For active NORI binary ordered-three-face coloring on Q_n, n>=5, let M be the set of DEAD cube edges, traversed by no monochromatic directed four-edge geodesic. Dead edges form a matching, and the active antipodal reversal law makes M antipodally invariant. Moreover M CANNOT be a perfect matching: if every vertex were incident to one dead edge, the dead-edge direction function d(v) would be constant across every physical cube edge. Indeed adjacent vertices either share their one dead edge (then its direction agrees) or have two different dead edges whose endpoint sets are distance one; the proven interdirection separation theorem forbids different dead directions at distance <=2. Connectivity of Q_n makes d(v)=i constant, so every i-parallel edge is dead, contradicting the active oddness/local-rigidity proof. Hence |M|<2^(n-1). Since active antipodality pairs each dead edge with a distinct antipodal dead edge, |M| is even. Thus |M|<=2^(n-1)-2.
THEOREM (EVERY PERMUTATION, MANY ROOTS). Fix ANY permutation p of [n]. For each cube root x let P(x,p) be the directed full n-edge antipodal geodesic traversing the dimensions in order p. Say x is locally covered if EVERY one of the n physical edges of P(x,p) belongs to at least one ACTUAL monochromatic four-edge geodesic somewhere in Q_n (the witnessing four-path may be different for different edges). The number of such roots satisfies
  #{x: every edge of P(x,p) is live} >= 2^n-2|M| >=4.
PROOF. At the unique step when direction i appears, the physical i-edge traversed by P(x,p) depends 2-to-1 on root x, because the root bit in direction i can be varied without changing the undirected physical edge at that step. Consequently every dead physical edge occurs at EXACTLY two of the 2^n rooted p-geodesics. There are 2|M| total incidences of dead path edges across all roots. The number of bad roots with at least one such incidence is at most 2|M| by the union bound. So at least 2^n-2|M|>=4 roots are fully live-edge covered. Since M is antipodally invariant, the good-root set is antipodally invariant as well.
This theorem produces an actual full cube GEODESIC with complete local monochromatic four-edge witness COVERAGE FOR ANY PRESCRIBED DIRECTION ORDER. It does not assert coherence between the separate local monochromatic witnesses, nor <=1 changes along the chosen full path; achieving compatible witness gluing is still the grand-closure obstacle. The result suggests an exact repair-complex program where absence of a certified local edge is no longer the obstruction once one may move the root while keeping the coordinate order fixed.
