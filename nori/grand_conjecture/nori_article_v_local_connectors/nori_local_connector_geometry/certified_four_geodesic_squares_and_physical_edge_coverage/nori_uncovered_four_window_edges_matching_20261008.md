# Dead edges for monochromatic four-geodesics form a matching, sharp under NORI

Let n>=5 and color all physical ordered-three-faces of Q_n arbitrarily with bits. Call a cube edge DEAD if no directed four-edge geodesic traversing it has its two ordered-three-face windows equal. THEOREM: dead edges form a matching. Hence the physical union of actual monochromatic four-geodesics contains the whole cube graph except a matching: minimum covered-edge degree >=n-1. For active NORI antipodal-reversal-odd colorings the dead matching is antipodally invariant. This is sharp: a valid NORI coloring can have a prescribed dead edge.
PROOF AND COMPLETE LOCAL RIGIDITY. Fix dead e={z,z xor e_i}. For pairwise distinct outside directions a,b,c, put A(a,b)=c(F(z;{a,b,i}),(a,b,i)), B(a,b)=c(F(z;{a,i,b}),(a,i,b)), C(a,b)=c(F(z;{i,a,b}),(i,a,b)). A 4-geodesic crossing e as its third edge has window colors A(a,b), B(b,c). As its second edge, the two faces containing i are physically unchanged between z and z xor i, and their colors are B(a,b),C(b,c). Deadness forces A(a,b)=1-B(b,c), B(a,b)=1-C(b,c) for all distinct a,b,c. The first relation makes B(b,c) independent of c, say beta_b, and the second makes beta_a identical for all a outside a chosen pair {b,c}. Since n-1>=4, beta_a is the SAME bit t for all a. Thus for all outside a,b: ordered triples (a,i,b) have color t, while (i,a,b) and (a,b,i) both have color 1-t at z. From four-geodesics crossing e as their first or fourth edge, all ordered triples omitting i at BOTH z and z xor i have color t. These conditions are necessary, and also sufficient, for e to be dead.
Suppose two dead edges i,j meet at z. Their forced pattern bits are t_i,t_j. Choose a third coordinate k. The triples (i,j,k) and (i,k,j) both have color 1-t_i by the i-dead condition. By the j-dead condition the first has color t_j (j is middle) and the second 1-t_j (j is last). This requires t_j=1-t_j, contradiction. So dead edges never meet.
SHARPNESS: Choose one edge {z,z xor i}, a bit t, and prescribe at both endpoint hubs the ordered-three-face color t if i is absent or is the MIDDLE of its triple, and 1-t if i is first or last. All physical faces containing i at those hubs coincide and receive consistent assignments. Every four-geodesic traversing that edge crosses between opposite-colored windows. For n>=5 no prescribed ordered face is an antipodal-reversal mate of another prescribed face (exterior dimension n-3>=2, while the two hubs differ only in i), so extend the partial assignment by setting each antipodal-reversal mate to the complementary color and all remaining orbits arbitrarily. This yields an active valid NORI coloring with e dead.
A strong new geometric input to future Hex/Brouwer/Tucker methods: the certified monochromatic 4-path union has a nearly complete ONE-SKELETON, but it does not imply compatible long monochromatic geodesics. No grand closure is claimed.
