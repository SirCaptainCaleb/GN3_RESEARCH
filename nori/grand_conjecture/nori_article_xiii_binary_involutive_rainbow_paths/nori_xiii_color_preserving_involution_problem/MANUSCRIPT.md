## Color-preserving involutions and one-switch rainbow paths

Let \(G=(V,E)\) be a finite connected simple graph with an arbitrary edge coloring \(\lambda:E\to\mathcal C\) and a binary edge labeling \(\chi:E\to\{0,1\}\). Suppose an involutive vertex automorphism \(\tau:V\to V\) satisfies, for every \(uv\in E\),
\[
\lambda(\tau(u)\tau(v))=\lambda(uv),\qquad
\chi(\tau(u)\tau(v))=1-\chi(uv).
\]
Let \(k=\hat\delta(G,\lambda)=\min_{v\in V}|\{\lambda(e):e\ni v\}|\).

**Conjecture XIII.1.** There is a vertex-simple path \(P\) with at least \(k\) edges, all of distinct \(\lambda\)-colors, whose sequence of \(\chi\)-labels changes at most once.

Investigate proofs, counterexamples, and sharp bounds.
