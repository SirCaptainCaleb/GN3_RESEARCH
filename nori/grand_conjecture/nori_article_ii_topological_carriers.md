# Article II - Equivariant topology

# Equivariant topology of genuine antipodal geodesics

Let Q_n be the binary cube. An ordered physical three-face comprises a three-dimensional coordinate face F and an order (a,b,c) of its free directions. We consider c(F,(a,b,c)) in {0,1}, with c(bar F,(c,b,a))=1-c(F,(a,b,c)). A full geodesic consists of n distinct coordinate steps and is good when its n-2 consecutive ordered-window colors change at most once.

## The all-root geodesic pseudomanifold

Define K_n as the simplicial complex on cube vertices generated by the vertex sets of all full antipodal geodesics. Every facet has n+1 vertices, and its unique antipodal pair is its two endpoints. Fixing endpoints {x,bar x}, the internal vertices correspond to the nonempty proper initial supports of permutations of [n]. Hence their simplices form sd(boundary Delta^(n-1)), and the endpoint-pair block is its join with the endpoint edge. There are 2^(n-1)n! geodesic facets. A ridge obtained by deleting an interior path vertex has exactly two completions, exchanging the consecutive unused directions around that vertex; deleting an endpoint allows its corresponding root-slide completion. Therefore the facet sum is a mod-two cycle and K_n is a closed pseudomanifold. Bitwise complementation acts freely and preserves genuine path incidence.

This construction supplies an equivariant domain in which adjacent facet moves are real cube moves, not formal exchanges of direction labels. For an adjacent transposition at positions i and i+1, only ordered three-windows beginning in [i-2,i+1] may change: the vertices before the exchange and after both directions are traversed coincide. Every other ordered physical face has identical free directions and exterior bits.

## Physically witnessed color carriers

Let W_c have as vertices actual ordered physical windows and as simplices sets jointly occurring on one genuine good geodesic. Antipodal reversal sends an admissible witness to another admissible witness, yielding a free involution on these window states. Joint witnessing is a stronger condition than pairwise compatibility. It allows simplex membership to represent actual paths, but it can reduce the available topological index relative to the uncolored K_n. Thus an equivariant zero in K_n alone cannot certify good color windows.

Root motion is required even for legitimate odd colorings. Fix a root a in even dimension n and color each physical three-face by the parity of its fixed exterior bits differing from a, independent of free-direction order. Since n-3 is odd, antipodal reversal complements this parity and gives a valid coloring. From root a, consecutive windows have alternating colors, so every full rooted geodesic has n-3 changes. For n>=6 none is good. A forcing construction may therefore vary the root while retaining the physical positions of windows.

## Topological obstruction and extraction

The antipodal window-shift carrier supports a nontrivial degree-one quotient-cover class; physical root slides and temporal seam behavior can yield distinct cohomological information. A higher-dimensional Tucker or Borsuk–Ulam contradiction would require actual cells in which these classes have a nontrivial product, or an equivariant filling constructed from simultaneously witnessed paths. A suspended index-raising map is available conditionally: if a sphere maps equivariantly into the overlap of exchanged subcomplexes and bounds within one side, its two translated fillings glue into an equivariant map from the next sphere. The binding hypothesis is a genuine physical filling.

The established topology therefore identifies the appropriate moving-root domain, valid local exchanges, and explicit low-degree obstruction classes. The remaining theorem must establish high enough index on a *color-dependent physically witnessed* subcarrier, or derive an improving path exchange from the obstruction. No such global extraction theorem is claimed here.
