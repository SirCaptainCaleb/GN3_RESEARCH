# Aligned good facet words have interval exterior derivative

Theorem (paired-facet interval rigidity). Let n>=6 and fix a direction g, a root x on the remaining n-1 directions, and a permutation p of those directions. Let w0,w1 be the length L=n-3 ordered-three-face color words of the two parallel paths with the same projected root and order. Suppose the entire n-cube has no good full antipodal geodesic, and both facet words have at most one switch. Then each word has exactly one switch and their initial and final colors agree. Hence w_e=q^s_e (1+q)^(L-s_e), 1<=s_e<L, and the g-exterior derivative w0 XOR w1 equals 0^min(s0,s1) 1^abs(s0-s1) 0^(L-max(s0,s1)). In particular this derivative is zero at both endpoints and has at most one interior block of ones. Proof. A monochromatic facet path extends by appending g, so it is excluded. The physical prepending cap (g,p1,p2) at projected root x and appending cap (p_(n-2),p_(n-1),g) at projected endpoint x+1 are independent of the facet g-bit because g is free in both caps. Let their colors be A and B. For each one-switch facet word, failure of the prepended full path forces its initial color to differ from A; failure of the appended full path forces its terminal color to differ from B. Thus both first colors equal 1+A=B and both last colors equal 1+B=A. Each has a unique switch; their XOR is exactly the stated interval. The proof uses no antipodal symmetry. Under NORI antipodal reversal, the corresponding derivative for the reversed direction order is the reversal of the displayed derivative. Corollary: an aligned pair of good facet paths whose exterior derivative has a nonzero endpoint or disconnected support proves full n-dimensional closure. This provides a dimension-independent physical-root/exterior-derivative certificate, conditional on finding paired good facet paths.
