# Global reversal-even ordered-face families have exponentially many exterior color-changing edges

Fix an ordered triple pi of distinct cube directions in active NORI, and let m=n-3. Suppose on EVERY physical 3-face F with these free coordinates the same-face reversal parity c(F,pi) XOR c(F,reverse pi) is ZERO. NORI oddness then implies f(bar z)=1-f(z) for the induced exterior-cube color function f(z)=c(F_z,pi). Writing sign F(z)=(-1)^f(z), F is antipodally odd; Parseval and Fourier total influence show bichromatic exterior edges >=2^(m-1)=2^(n-4). Equality forces all nonzero Fourier coefficients to have degree1; Boolean degree-one functions are precisely signed dictators, so the coloring on those physical faces depends on exactly one exterior bit (or its complement). CAUTION: if parity zero only at one particular terminal cap, global oddness does NOT follow; do not apply exponential bound in that case. The single cap still has opposite antipodal color, yielding at least m exterior cut edges by m edge-disjoint antipodal cube paths. No web or literature search.
