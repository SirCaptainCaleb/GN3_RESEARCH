# Antipodal sensitivity forces sparsity on complementary triple

Statement:
Assume n=6 and c is an antipodally odd ordered-three-face coloring with no good antipodal geodesic. For each unordered triple T and middle axis b in T, define I(T,b) as the set of exterior coordinates d outside T on which the color of an ordered face with free triple T and middle b is sensitive for some assignment of the remaining exterior bits. Antipodal reversal makes I(T,b) well-defined for the two opposite orders on T. Write U=V\T. If d belongs to I(T,b), then for each e in U\{d}, I(U,e) is contained in {b}. Consequently, if |I(T,b)|>=2, then I(U,e) is contained in {b} for every e in U. Thus any dimension-six counterexample with two or more sensitivities in one orientation class forces all three opposite orientation classes to depend on at most one fixed coordinate.

Proof:
First, oddness gives f_{rev(pi)}(y)=1+f_pi(1+y) for exterior bit vectors y, with addition in F_2; hence a fixed-bit coordinate is influential for pi exactly when it is influential for rev(pi). Now choose T={a,b,c} and U={d,e,f}, with d influential for the order (a,b,c). A change in first-window color A under toggling x_d can be witnessed after fixing x_e,x_f. If the last-window color D for order (d,e,f) were sensitive to x_c, its sensitivity could be witnessed after independently fixing x_a,x_b. In the full order (a,b,c,d,e,f), the two middle window colors B,C are independent of both x_c and x_d, whereas A depends on the controllable x_d and D depends on the independently controllable x_c. Choose x_d to make A=B and x_c to make D=C. The resulting word (B,B,C,C) has at most one change, a contradiction. Thus c on U with order (d,e,f) is insensitive to c. Reversing the order on T to (c,b,a) preserves sensitivity in d, and repeating the same argument forces insensitivity of the U order (d,e,f) to a. Therefore I(U,e) subset {b}. Replacing (d,e,f) by (d,f,e) gives I(U,f) subset {b}. If there are two distinct influential d,d' in U, each middle coordinate e in U differs from at least one of them, so the preceding containment applies for every e in U.
