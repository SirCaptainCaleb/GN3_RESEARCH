# Quantitative seven-cycle incidence and robust Q10 NORI closure with exceptional face types

**Lemma (uniform one-seventh density).** Let n≥7 and let a color all ORDERED triples of distinct elements of [n] with two colors, without any reversal constraint. If A,B are chosen uniformly among ordered pairs of disjoint ordered triples (six distinct letters), then
Pr[a(A)=a(B)]≥1/7.

**Proof.** Choose a uniformly random ordered seven-tuple u_0,...,u_6 of distinct directions. Put T_i=(u_i,u_(i+1),u_(i+2)) with subscripts modulo 7. For every assignment of the seven labels, the disjointness cycle T_i--T_(i+3) has odd length seven, so at least one of its seven edges joins equal-colored ordered triples. By symmetry of random labels, each comparison (T_i,T_(i+3)) has exactly the uniform distribution of (A,B). Taking expectations yields 7Pr[a(A)=a(B)]≥1. QED.

**Theorem (Q10 closure tolerant of six exceptional reversal orbits of direction triples).** Let c be any legal antipodal-reversal-odd coloring of ordered PHYSICAL three-faces of Q_10. Suppose there exist a reversal-invariant ordered-triple table a(π)=a(reverse π) and a reversal-closed exceptional set E of at most 12 ordered direction triples such that, whenever π∉E, the actual face color c(F,π) is a(π) for exterior-one weight 3 and 1+a(π) for exterior-one weight 4. Every face of every exceptional ordered direction type π∈E may be colored ARBITRARILY, compatibly with NORI oddness. The colors on all other exterior-Hamming layers may also be arbitrary. Then Q_10 contains a full antipodal geodesic with at most one ordered-three-face color change.

**Proof.** Sample a uniformly random full direction order p. By the lemma, its first two consecutive ordered three-blocks satisfy a(p_1,p_2,p_3)=a(p_4,p_5,p_6) with probability at least 1/7. For a specific ordered triple π, the probability it occurs as ANY consecutive triple of p is (10−2)!/10!=1/(10·9)=1/90, because treating π as one ordered block leaves eight objects to permute. Hence by the union bound the probability some consecutive triple of p belongs to E is at most |E|/90≤12/90<1/7. Consequently an order p exists with BOTH equal-color first disjoint triple-blocks and no window whose ordered free triple lies in E. For this order δ_1=0, and the exact paired-root triangle-frustration theorem gives a root with ≤1 change under the ideal central law. Every actual paired-root window has exterior weight 3 or 4 and uses an ordered direction triple π∉E, so its ACTUAL face color agrees with the ideal law regardless of exterior fixed bits. Thus the same literal physical full geodesic has ≤1 change under c. QED.

**Quantitative refinement.** At least (1/7−|E|/90)·10! full direction orders satisfy the two simultaneous order conditions (in the obvious integer rounding sense), and each admits at least two antipodal actual good paired starting roots. Thus the robustness result provides a positive, explicitly counted family of full geometric witnesses.

**Scope and frontier.** This transports a coordinate-triple parity theorem to genuinely face-dependent exceptions concentrated in up to SIX reversal-orbits of ordered direction triples. The exception set is a set of ordered free-direction types; all physical fixed-bit instances of those types can be arbitrary. Quantifying exceptional individual physical faces, or extending the one-switch conclusion to unrestricted exterior dependence distributed among all types, requires additional root-sensitive incidence arguments.

**Corollary (Q8 robustness under 27 exceptional reversal-orbits).** Let c be a legal NORI coloring of physical ordered three-faces of Q_8, and suppose it agrees on the two central exterior-one layers with the reversal-even two-layer law a(π),1+a(π), except for at most 54 oriented direction-triple types closed under reversal (equivalently, at most 27 reversal-orbits). Every physical face with an exceptional ordered direction type may be arbitrary subject to oddness; all other exterior layers may be arbitrary. Then c has a full antipodal geodesic with at most one change, indeed two paired antipodal starting-root witnesses for some direction order.

**Proof.** For uniformly random full p of eight coordinates, a particular ordered triple π occurs as one of its six consecutive length-three windows with probability 1/(8·7)=1/56. Thus the chance that p encounters any of at most 54 exceptional ordered triples is ≤54/56<1. Choose p avoiding them. For n=8 the central paired-root syndrome δ has length m−2=2. Every binary δ of length two is empty, a singleton, or an adjacent pair, so the exact paired-root formula gives at most one change. All actual windows on the two central layers use permitted direction triples and hence agree with the ideal law. QED.
