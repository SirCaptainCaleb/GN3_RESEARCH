# Odd disjoint-triple cycle and elementary Q10 two-layer closure

**Seven-coordinate lemma (no coloring symmetry required).** For ANY binary coloring a of ordered triples of distinct directions and ANY selected seven distinct directions, there are two ordered triples on DISJOINT three-element supports with the SAME color.

**Proof.** Label the seven directions u_0,...,u_6 cyclically, with subscripts modulo 7, and put T_i=(u_i,u_(i+1),u_(i+2)). The supports of T_i and T_(i+3) are disjoint. Addition of 3 modulo 7 cycles through all seven indices, so the seven comparisons (T_i,T_(i+3)) form an ODD cycle. If every disjoint pair in this explicit cycle had opposite colors, the bits a(T_i) would alternate around a cycle of length 7, a contradiction. Therefore at least one comparison is a same-color pair of disjoint ordered triples. This is a concrete seven-vertex odd cycle in the disjointness (Kneser) graph KG(7,3), with fixed ordered representatives. QED.

**Corollary (explicit Q10 physical full-geodesic closure for central two-layer intercepts).** Let n=10. Suppose c is a legal antipodal-reversal-odd coloring of ordered physical three-faces, with c(F,π)=a(π) when the exterior-one weight equals 3, and c(F,π)=1+a(π) when it equals 4. Hence a(reverse π)=a(π). Other exterior weights are arbitrary. Then c has an ACTUAL full antipodal ten-edge geodesic with at most ONE ordered-three-face color change, together with an antipodal starting-root companion for the SAME direction order.

**Proof.** Select any seven directions. By the lemma choose same-color disjoint ordered triples A=(p_1,p_2,p_3) and B=(p_4,p_5,p_6); append the four remaining distinct directions as p_7,...,p_10 in any order. For the resulting full order, the paired-root curvature vector of length m−2=3 satisfies δ_1=a(p_1,p_2,p_3)+a(p_4,p_5,p_6)=0. Thus δ=(0,δ_2,δ_3), whose ones form at most a consecutive run of length two. The exact frustration minimum is μ(δ)≤1. Its constructive proof supplies two antipodal physical paired roots yielding full one-switch-or-monochromatic ten-geodesics. QED.

**Significance and remaining obstruction.** The seven-cycle gives a universal SAME-COLOR pair using only seven coordinate names, improving the basic three-disjoint-triples pigeonhole argument that uses nine. Coupled to the exact curvature formula it yields a short Q10 closure proof with unrestricted physical colors away from the two central layers. Higher-dimensional closure would follow from arranging the entire defect syndrome into one run of length ≤2; forcing an isolated zero defect alone becomes insufficient once m−2≥4. The fully face-dependent grand conjecture remains open.
