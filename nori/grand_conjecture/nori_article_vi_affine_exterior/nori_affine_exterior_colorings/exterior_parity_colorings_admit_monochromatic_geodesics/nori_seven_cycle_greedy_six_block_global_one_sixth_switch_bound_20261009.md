# Greedy seven-cycle block construction gives floor(n/6) switches in even-dimensional central NORI

# Universal one-sixth switch bound for central-two-layer physical NORI colorings

**Theorem (constructive all-even-dimension global-order optimization).** Let n≥6 be EVEN, and let c be any legal NORI ordered-three-face coloring whose values on exterior-one layers k=(n−4)/2 and k+1 are respectively a(π) and 1+a(π), with a(reverse π)=a(π). Colors on ALL other physical exterior layers are arbitrary subject to the NORI antipodal-reversal rule. There exists an ACTUAL full antipodal n-geodesic with at most floor(n/6) ordered-three-face color changes. Both a qualifying full direction order and two antipodal successful starting roots are obtainable by a deterministic O(n)-query, O(n)-time construction from the ordered-triple table a. The bound specializes to the FULL one-switch conjecture in dimensions n=6,8,10 for this class.

**Proof.** For n=6, the curvature vector δ has length one, so the exact paired-root minimum is at most one; choose any full direction order and decode its paired root. Now assume n≥8. Let n=6q+r, choosing r∈{2,4,6}, so q=floor((n−2)/6); equivalently q=floor((n−1)/6). We form q consecutive six-direction blocks. While at least seven unused directions remain, choose ANY seven; cyclically label them u_0,...,u_6. Among the seven ordered triple pairs
((u_i,u_(i+1),u_(i+2)), (u_(i+3),u_(i+4),u_(i+5))) (mod 7)
there is one same-color pair, since a proper alternating binary coloring around the odd 7-cycle of disjoint ordered triples is impossible. Append that pair of ordered triples consecutively as six directions of the full order. Exactly six previously unused directions are consumed. Because n−6(q−1)=6+r≥8 at the beginning of the final block, all q choices are feasible. Finally append the remaining r unused directions in any order.

Write m=n/2, L=m−2 for the paired-root triangle-curvature syndrome δ_j=a_(2j−1)+a_(2j+2), 1≤j≤L, of the resulting order. Each constructed six-block occupies positions 6s+1,...,6s+6, and its two ordered triples have equal a-colors. Therefore
δ_(3s+1)=0 for every 0≤s<q.
Between successive forced zeros there are exactly two uncontrolled defect positions; after the last forced zero there are exactly r/2 uncontrolled positions because L=3q+r/2−2. The proven exact paired-root minimum is μ(δ)=Σ_{maximal 1-runs}ceil(length/2). Each of the q−1 intervening two-position strips contributes at most one, and the final strip contributes at most ceil(r/4). Thus
μ(δ)≤(q−1)+ceil(r/4).
For r=2 or 4 the right-hand side is q; for r=6 it is q+1. In either case it equals floor(n/6). The paired-root construction obtains two antipodal full cube geodesics with exactly μ(δ) changes. Only seven ordered-triple evaluations per block, plus linear syndrome decoding, are required. QED.

**Slight extension to arbitrary reversal-asymmetric central tables.** For a general legal middle-table pair c_k(π)=a(π), c_(k+1)(π)=1+a(reverse π), the SAME six-block greedy order still forces δ_(3s+1)=0 (the seven-cycle applies to arbitrary a). Let R_p be the window indices whose ordered triple a(π_i)≠a(reverse π_i). The signed path-square perturbation theorem then constructs two actual antipodal full paths with at most min(n−3, floor(n/6)+2|R_p|) changes. In particular, the same O(n) proof applies whenever reversal-asymmetric windows along the constructed order are sparse.

**Open frontier.** The improvement from an arbitrary fixed-order ceil((n−4)/4) switch bound to floor(n/6) exploits ADAPTIVE choice of disjoint equal-color triple blocks. To reach one switch uniformly in n by this route, synchronize the overlapping curvature equations across blocks, forcing all nonzero δ into one run of length at most two. Greedy odd-cycle matching alone leaves approximately one independent unit of unavoidable curvature per six-direction block. The unrestricted NORI conjecture also requires controlling physical exterior-face sensitivity.
