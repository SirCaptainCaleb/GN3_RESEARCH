# Equivariant root transport and physical obstruction carriers

Let c color physical ordered three-faces of Q_n with c(bar F,rev pi)=1-c(F,pi). A full geodesic has n-2 ordered windows; a good geodesic has at most one change. The antipodal reversal tau maps each actual ordered window to its opposite physical face in reversed direction order, complementing its color.

## Witness complexes and physical involutions

Define W_c with vertices the actual ordered windows, and take as simplices exactly sets jointly witnessed along a single genuine good geodesic. The reversal tau preserves the good condition and acts freely on these window states. The distinction between a jointly witnessed simplex and a clique of separately witnessed window pairs matters: only the former has a path-extraction meaning. Actual window-shift edges and root-slide cells can be added to a carrier after checking their physical incidences.

The quotient-cover of an antipodal window-shift carrier has a nonzero degree-one obstruction. Root movement and temporal seam parity can define distinct degree-one classes. Higher-index forcing demands an actual cup product or compatible equivariant filling. A topological coincidence outside the genuine witness subcomplex supplies a location for an obstruction, not a physical good geodesic.

## Root mobility is essential

In even dimension n, fix a reference root a. Color a physical ordered three-face by the parity of its fixed exterior bits differing from a, independently of direction order. Antipodal passage complements n-3 fixed bits, an odd number, so this is a valid reversal-odd coloring. Along any full geodesic rooted at a, the exterior parity of the successive length-three windows alternates, since exactly one used coordinate enters the exterior set at each shift. Thus every such rooted full geodesic has n-3 color changes. For even n at least six it is not good. Consequently an equivariant proof of the existential grand conjecture must permit genuine root mobility.

## Two-cap packets and seam squares

A permutohedral antipodal position map may force packets of full geodesics sharing a central face or a pair of endpoint caps. The topological conclusion refers to a collection of paths, rather than one path with compatible seam colors. If a candidate extraction fixes two physical seam-square directions, the required root/order carrier can lose its equivariant index. The operative objects are therefore root-variable packets and genuine seam-square cells.

An equivariant suspension construction can increase index when a map of a sphere into the overlap of two exchanged witness subcomplexes admits a null-homotopy within one side: the two hemispherical fillings glue to an equivariant map of the next sphere. This is a valid conditional topological mechanism. Its application requires constructing those fillings from actual window-compatible paths. The current obstruction carriers establish nontrivial incidence and precise extraction obligations; their known topology alone does not force the grand conjecture.
