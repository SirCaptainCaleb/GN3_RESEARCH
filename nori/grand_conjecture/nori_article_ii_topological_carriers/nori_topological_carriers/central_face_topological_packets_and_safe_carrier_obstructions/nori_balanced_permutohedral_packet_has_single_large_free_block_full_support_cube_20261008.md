THEOREM (LARGE FREE BLOCK BEHIND BORSUK TWO-CAP NEUTRALITY). Let P_n be the standard permutohedron, whose proper faces correspond to ORDERED SET PARTITIONS [n]=B_1|...|B_r with r>=2. Vertices of a face H are exactly the permutations respecting the block order while allowing every internal permutation of each block. Fix a coordinate i and a set T⊆[n] minus{i} of k≥1 distinct coordinates. Assume H contains some finite ACTUAL permutation vertices π_s with positive weights summing1 and exactly balanced before-i bits E[1_{j precedes i}]=1/2 for EVERY j∈T. Then ALL directions of T lie in the SAME ORDERED PARTITION BLOCK B as i. Indeed, if j belonged to a different block than i, every vertex permutation in H would put j before i or after i with certainty, contradicting the 1/2 average.
Consequently |B|>=k+1 and dim H=sum_h(|B_h|−1)=n−r>=k. Moreover, for EVERY subset A⊆T, H contains some original permutation π whose directions from T preceding i are EXACTLY A: within B put the elements of A, then i, then the elements of T minus A, ordering the other elements of B arbitrarily. Thus the full {0,1}^T cube of relative positions (including each complementary pair) is realized by ACTUAL original permutation vertices of the SAME face H. The balance packet's selected geodesic witnesses need NOT cover that entire cube, and the paths filling the remaining positions need not preserve endpoint opposition or monochromatic central-window colors.
