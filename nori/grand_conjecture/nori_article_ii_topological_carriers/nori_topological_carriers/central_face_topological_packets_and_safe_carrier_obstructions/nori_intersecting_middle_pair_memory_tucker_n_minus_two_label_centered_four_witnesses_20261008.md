# Cubical Tucker plus a pairwise-intersecting direction-pair palette forces opposite-color centered mono-four witnesses sharing a middle direction

THEOREM (ACTUAL INTERSECTING TWO-DIRECTION MEMORY VIA HIGH-DIMENSIONAL CUBICAL TUCKER). Let n>=5 and c be any active NORI ordered-three-face binary coloring. At EVERY physical cube vertex z choose one genuinely monochromatic directed four-edge geodesic P_z CENTERED at z, guaranteed by the odd five-cycle. Choose the paths antipodally equivariantly: P_(bar z)=ΘP_z, the physical antipodal complement and reversal, so their mono window color q(z) flips while the UNORDERED MIDDLE DIRECTION PAIR T(z)={p2(z),p3(z)} is unchanged by antipodality.
Number directions 1,...,n. Define an (n−2)-coloring h of all unordered coordinate pairs T={a,b} by: if min(a,b)<=n−3, set h(T)=min(a,b); otherwise T is one of the three pairs within {n−2,n−1,n} and set h(T)=n−2. Every color class h^(-1)(j) is PAIRWISE INTERSECTING: for j<=n−3 all pairs share j, and the final three pairs form the triangle on {n−2,n−1,n}, any two of which intersect.
Give z the genuinely ODD signed Tucker label λ(z)=(-1)^{q(z)} h(T(z)) in {±1,...,±(n−2)}. Because q flips and T remains invariant under z↦bar z, λ(bar z)=−λ(z). Let X be the τ-equivariant Freudenthal triangulation of the (n−2)-dimensional cubical skeleton of the geometric n-cube boundary, with τ the vertex-complement map. Its quotient is the (n−2)-skeleton of RP^(n−1), so its covering class satisfies w^(n−2)!=0. If NO simplex of X contained two opposite signed λ-labels, the signed basis-vector affine map X→R^(n−2) would avoid zero and normalize to an equivariant map X→S^(n−3), contradicting nonzero w^(n−2). Hence two ACTUAL physical cube vertices z,w lie in ONE common coordinate face of dimension at most n−2 (so d_H(z,w)<=n−2) and have opposite λ-labels.
The equality of label magnitude gives h(T(z))=h(T(w)), and the pairwise-intersecting color-class rule gives T(z)∩T(w)≠emptyset. The opposite signs give q(z)≠q(w). Therefore two genuine, oppositely colored, centered monochromatic four-geodesic paths have physical middle squares with at least ONE COMMON FREE COORDINATE DIRECTION, and their centers occupy one physical codimension-at-least-two coordinate face. This holds for EVERY equivariant selection of one certified centered four-geodesic per hub, not only favorable choices.
