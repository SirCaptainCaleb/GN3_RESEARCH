# Static NORI path nerve splits into index-three incomplete part and isolated completed-witness simplices

THEOREM (exact static path-nerve component split). Let n>=8, and let c be any active NORI ordered-three-face coloring. E is the exact equivariant flag nerve of the two-sided physical boxes B(P)=S(P)×S(Theta P), over all <=1-switch partial and full rooted directed paths of length at least 3. Write E_short for the full subcomplex on INCOMPLETE paths (length<n), and for each root x admitting at least one good FULL path let C_x be the simplex on all such full paths rooted at x.

Then E is the DISJOINT UNION of E_short and the C_x. In particular C_x are isolated Theta-invariant connected components with fixed barycenters; E_short has a free Theta-action.

PROOF. For any full path from x, the common free coordinates of its ordered-three-face windows are empty, so B(P) is exactly the diagonal POINT (x,x). For an incomplete Q with nonempty unused support D, at each coordinate i in D, all points of B(Q) have source bit fixed to root(Q)_i and second-factor bit fixed to its complement, so B(Q) cannot meet ANY diagonal point. Distinct full roots x≠y yield different diagonal points, whereas all full paths at x have identical box. Thus there are no nerve edges between incomplete and full states, nor between distinct full roots; full-root cliques form C_x. Theta preserves every C_x and swaps its path vertices, fixing its barycenter. The incomplete union avoids the factor-swap diagonal, so its induced action is free. QED.
