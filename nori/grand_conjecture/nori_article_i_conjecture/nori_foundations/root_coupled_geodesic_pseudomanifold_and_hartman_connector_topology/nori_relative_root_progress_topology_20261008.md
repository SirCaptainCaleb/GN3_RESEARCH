# Relative high-dimensional homology of root-progress cube

Let C=[0,1]^n_root times [0,1]^n_progress, with involution J(r,s)=(r,1-s). Remove the fixed midsection F=[0,1]^n_root times {(1/2,...,1/2)} and call the result X. Let A be the part of X on the boundary of the root cube. For n>=2, radial projection in the progress coordinates gives an equivariant deformation retraction of the pair (X,A) onto (I^n times S^(n-1), boundary I^n times S^(n-1)). Relative Kunneth over F2 gives H_(2n-1)(X,A;F2)=F2, generated by [I^n,boundary I^n] cross [S^(n-1)]. Its boundary is the product of the root and progress boundary sphere fundamental classes. By contrast, X alone is equivariantly homotopy equivalent to the single progress antipodal sphere S^(n-1), since the root cube contracts. Thus dimension doubling provides a higher relative topological obstruction only with genuine root-boundary conditions. Deriving those conditions from coherent one-switch carrier reachability is open.
