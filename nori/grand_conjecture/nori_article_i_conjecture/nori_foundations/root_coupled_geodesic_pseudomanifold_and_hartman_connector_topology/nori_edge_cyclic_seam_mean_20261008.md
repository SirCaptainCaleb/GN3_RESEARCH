# Exact expected odd domain-wall count from monochromatic degrees

Let c be any antipodally odd binary edge coloring of Q_n (n>=2). At vertex v put k(v)=number of incident color-0 edges. Choose x uniformly from Q_n and p uniformly from permutations of [n]. Define the odd cyclic seam count m(x,p) as in the antiperiodic 2n-cycle item. Then E[m]=(2/(n-1)) E_v[k(v)(n-k(v))]. Proof: the n seams of the color cycle correspond to a two-direction turn at n cube vertices. For seams 1,...,n-1 the turn compares the edges of dimensions p_j and p_(j+1) at the intermediate vertex x XOR {p_1,...,p_j}. At the closing seam antipodal oddness changes the comparison into the two edges of dimensions p_n and p_1 at the root x. In every case the vertex is uniform on Q_n and the ordered pair of distinct directions uniform; its bichromatic probability is 2 k(v)(n-k(v))/(n(n-1)). Sum n terms and average. Equivalently E[m]=n^2/(2(n-1))-(2/(n-1)) Var_v(k(v)), because k(bar v)=n-k(v). This gives a genuine dimension-independent global accounting identity for the domain-wall defect. Since every m is positive odd, an average below 3 forces a cycle with m=1 and therefore a monochromatic antipodal geodesic. In particular n<=4 follows from k(n-k)<=floor(n^2/4) (n=2,3,4 respectively). For larger n, the mean identity alone does not force m=1. This result is specific to antipodally odd EDGE coloring and leaves the NORI ordered-three-face target open.
