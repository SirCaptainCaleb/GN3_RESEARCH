# Joint support reachability is necessary for Hartman labeling

An initial one-switch prefix can traverse any one coordinate before producing a colored three-face window, so all coordinate boundary targets are separately reachable for every coloring. In the proved rooted Q5 NORI obstruction there is a root with no good full geodesic. Nevertheless every strict support is reachable by a one-switch prefix because any prefix of at most four edges has at most two completed windows and at most one color change. In particular two different four-coordinate supports are reachable but their union is not. This proves that individual boundary-target reachability and even reachability of every proper support do not imply full one-switch completion. Hartman least-unreachable labels must encode joint feasible absorptions; a union-closure or compatible joint-realization theorem is genuinely necessary.
