# Strengthened global ordered-three-face switch bound from centered odd 5-cycles

STRENGTHENED THEOREM FOR ACTIVE NORI WINDOW SIZE THREE. For every integer n>=5 and every binary coloring c of the ordered three-dimensional faces of Q_n (without needing antipodal-reversal oddness), there is an antipodal n-edge geodesic whose (n-2)-symbol ordered-three-face word changes color at most FLOOR(4(n-3)/5) times. Equivalently there exists a full geodesic with at least CEIL((n-3)/5) equal-color adjacent ordered-three-face window pairs.
PROOF. The centered FIVE-CYCLE theorem shows that for every vertex z and any five-element coordinate set W, at least 24 of the 120 ordered quadruples of distinct directions (a,b,c,d) in W have c(F(z;{a,b,c}),(a,b,c))=c(F(z;{b,c,d}),(b,c,d)). This equality is realized by the genuine four-step geodesic with directions (a,b,c,d) centered at z (two edges before z and two after z).
Now choose a uniformly random root x and coordinate permutation p of [n], giving a full antipodal geodesic. Fix any of its n-3 adjacent ordered-three-face transition positions j. The common middle vertex z of the two 3-window segments is uniform on Q_n, and their four directions form a uniformly random ordered quadruple of distinct coordinates. Choose an extra fifth direction uniformly outside these four. Conditioned on z and the resulting five-coordinate set W, the quadruple is uniform among all 120 ordered quadruples of W, so the probability of equal colors at transition j is at least 1/5. Summing over the n-3 transitions gives expected number of color changes <=4(n-3)/5. Some full antipodal geodesic therefore has at most floor(4(n-3)/5) changes. QED.
This strictly strengthens the earlier 8(n-3)/9 bound obtained from odd 9-cycles; the earlier theorem remains valid as a weaker corollary. The stronger result uses odd five-cycles of REAL centered physical faces and is independent of the NORI antipodal coloring axiom. It is a dimension-independent quantitative partial closure theorem; the target <=1 switch for all dimensions remains OPEN.
