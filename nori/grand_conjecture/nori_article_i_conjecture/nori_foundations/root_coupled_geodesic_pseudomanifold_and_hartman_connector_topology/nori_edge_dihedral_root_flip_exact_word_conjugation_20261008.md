# Dihedral root flip preserves the cyclic seam index and conjugates the color suffix

For any antipodally odd binary EDGE coloring of Q_n (n>=2), take a full antipodal geodesic rooted at x with permutation p=(a,q_1,...,q_(n-1)) and edge-color word w=(A,B_1,...,B_(n-1)). Define the involution T(x,(a,q))=(x xor e_a,(a,reverse(q))). Then its edge-color word is EXACTLY w(TP)=(A,1-B_(n-1),...,1-B_1). Proof: root-slide along a first maps P to the root x xor e_a with permutation (q,a) and color word (B_1,...,B_(n-1),1-A), since the new closing edge is antipodal to the old first edge. Reversing the entire order at the new root complements and reverses the color word under edge-antipodal oddness, giving (A,1-reverse B). Applying T twice returns the initial root and permutation. Both operations preserve the underlying unoriented 2n-cycle, so m(TP)=m(P). This is a color-coherent root change by exactly ONE chosen coordinate a, preserving the first edge color while complementing and reversing every subsequent edge color. For fixed coordinate a, the map q->reverse q is an m-preserving bijection between all full geodesics rooted at x beginning with a and those rooted at x xor e_a beginning with a. Thus the complete multiset of cyclic seam counts conditioned on first direction a is identical at the two endpoints of the a-edge. In particular the minimum conditional seam field h_a(x)=min_{q}m(x,(a,q)) satisfies h_a(x)=h_a(x xor e_a), and the analogous equality holds for every moment/distribution function of m. This provides an exact detailed-balance root transport for topology/descent. It is EDGE-specific: with ordered-three-face NORI windows, a root slide shifts the ordered triple word but the new final window is a distinct triple (p_(n-1),p_n,a), so there is no first-window-preserving color conjugation without additional two-direction memory.

CORRECTION (geometric carrier). The root slide itself rotates the same physical 2n-cycle, and therefore preserves m. Reversing the full direction order at a fixed root preserves D and hence m, but GENERALLY changes the underlying 2n-cycle: the reversed-order geodesic is internally disjoint from the forward-order one, and its antipodal doubling is a different cycle. Thus the composite T preserves m by two successive algebraic invariances, not by acting on one unchanged physical belt. All exact word and conditional-multiset transport identities above remain correct.
