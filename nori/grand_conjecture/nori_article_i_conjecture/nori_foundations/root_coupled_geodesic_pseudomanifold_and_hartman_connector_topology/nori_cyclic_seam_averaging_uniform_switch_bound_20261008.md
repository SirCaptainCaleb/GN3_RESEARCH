# Universal half-switch bound from odd cyclic seams, all dimensions

THEOREM. Every antipodally odd binary EDGE coloring of Q_n (n>=2) admits a full antipodal geodesic with at most 2 floor((n-1)/4) color changes. In particular the bound is zero for n<=4, two for 5<=n<=8, four for 9<=n<=12, and grows by two per four dimensions thereafter. This is a uniform dimension-independent bound; it does NOT close the edge conjecture, which requests zero (equivalently one).
PROOF. For root x and permutation p let m(x,p) be the positive odd number of domain-wall pairs in the 2n-cycle obtained by traversing p twice. An exact cyclic identity gives m=2 floor(D(P)/2)+1. Root sliding rotates the 2n cycle, preserving m; choosing the starting vertex immediately after a domain wall yields an n-edge geodesic with D=m-1 changes. With x,p uniform, each of the n first-half turn positions samples a uniform cube vertex v and an ordered uniformly random pair of distinct directions. If k(v) is the number of incident edges of color zero, the probability the two turn edges have different colors is 2k(v)(n-k(v))/(n(n-1)). Hence E[m]=2/(n-1) E_v[k(v)(n-k(v))] <= 2 floor(n^2/4)/(n-1). Some chamber has odd m no greater than this bound. If n=2t+1 then its RHS equals t+1, so the largest permitted odd m is t+1 for t even or t for t odd. If n=2t>=4 the RHS equals t+t/(2t-1), strictly between t and t+1, so the largest odd m is t (t odd) or t-1 (t even). The n=2 case similarly gives m=1. In all cases m-1 <=2 floor((n-1)/4). Select a root slide omitting one wall to get a full geodesic with D=m-1. QED. The stated improved upper bound applies only to the binary antipodally odd edge case; the NORI three-face involution has distinct cyclic monodromy.
