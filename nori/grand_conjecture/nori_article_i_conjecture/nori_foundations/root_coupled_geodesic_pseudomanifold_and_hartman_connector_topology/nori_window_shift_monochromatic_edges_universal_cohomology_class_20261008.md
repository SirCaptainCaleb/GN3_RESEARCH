# Monochromatic window shifts define a coloring-independent nonzero cohomology class

Let H be NORI's graph of actual ordered-three-face windows, adjacent when consecutive along a genuine four-edge cube geodesic (n>=5). Give vertices any binary coloring c. Let h_c(e)=1 if the endpoints of edge e have EQUAL color and 0 otherwise. Over F2, the graph cochain identity is h_c=1_E+delta(c), where 1_E is the all-one edge cochain. Hence [h_c]=[1_E] in H^1(H;F2), independent of c. The centered five-coordinate window-shift PENTAGON is a genuine closed 5-walk in H, so it evaluates [1_E] to 1. Thus [h_c] is nonzero and every centered pentagon contains an ODD number of genuinely monochromatic four-edge connectors. More generally every odd closed walk has an odd number of monochromatic shift edges.
For the active antipodal-reversal-odd NORI coloring, tau(F,pi)=(bar F,reverse pi) acts freely on H and complements the colors. Therefore h_c is tau-invariant and descends to a 1-cochain hbar on the quotient B=H/tau. The class [hbar] is nonzero because its pullback is [h_c]. Choose one lift s(v) of each quotient vertex; let t(uv)=1 iff the chosen lift of the edge starting at s(u) ends at tau s(v), otherwise t=0. The voltage t represents the double-cover class w in H^1(B;F2). Since c(tau s(v))=1-c(s(v)), we have hbar(uv)=1+t(uv)+c(s(u))+c(s(v)). Thus [hbar]=[1_B]+w, independent of the coloring and of the chosen lifts. This establishes a canonical nonzero mod-two cohomology class represented by the real same-color-window connector edges. A full good NORI geodesic would require gluing these connectors into a compatible long path or obtaining reversed-tail terminal basin intersection. Graph H^1 alone cannot force higher cup products, so the remaining topological step is to build a higher-dimensional root-coupled witness complex that preserves this class and provides extraction.

OBSTRUCTION TO NAIVE FILLING OF PENTAGON CELLS. If a purported higher-dimensional topological carrier attaches an ordinary 2-disk along any centered five-cycle of window-shift edges and attempts to extend the SAME monochromatic-edge indicator h_c as a 1-cocycle, it fails: evaluating h_c on that pentagon boundary gives 1 mod 2. A cocycle on a 2-complex must evaluate 0 on every 2-cell boundary. Adding a coboundary cannot repair the parity, since coboundaries sum to zero around closed walks. Therefore any higher-dimensional thickening that kills these pentagon loops must either introduce further connector edges/windows that compensate the odd parity, keep pentagon loops unfilled, or encode the obstruction as a nonzero 2-cocycle curvature of a richer state complex. This explains the precise chain-level issue in transporting the canonical graph class toward a high-index Tucker/KKM construction.

TWO INDEPENDENT MOD-2 CLASSES. The tau-cover class w is nonzero because the ordered-window graph H is connected while tau is free. Its quotient B has another nonzero class alpha=[hbar]. These two H^1(B;F2) classes are LINEARLY INDEPENDENT: take any centered five-cycle in H; its projection to B lifts as a closed loop, hence w evaluates 0 on that projected cycle, while alpha evaluates 1 because five is odd and hbar marks an odd number of monochromatic window shifts. Therefore dim H^1(B;F2)>=2. This is a genuine distinction between the color-flipping antipodal twisting (w) and the unavoidable monochromatic-connector parity (alpha). Since B remains one-dimensional, alpha cup w vanishes in H^2(B); a higher-dimensional path-compatible carrier is required to exploit any mixed cup product.
