# A sensitive ordered triple forces constant complementary endpoint orientations

Statement:
Let c be an antipodally odd ordered-three-face coloring of Q_6 for which every antipodal geodesic has at least two window-color changes. Let (a,b,c,d,e,f) list the six coordinates. If the color of ordered face (a,b,c) is sensitive to the fixed exterior bit d for any choice of its other exterior bits e,f, then there is κ∈{0,1} such that, on every face with free set {d,e,f}, the ordered-face colors of (d,e,f) and (d,f,e) are both κ, whereas those of (f,e,d) and (e,f,d) are both 1−κ. In particular the four indicated ordered faces are each independent of all three exterior bits.

Proof:
Choose values e=e₀,f=f₀ witnessing sensitivity of A=c(abc) to the outside d-bit. For the six-direction order abcdef, the window word has shape (A(x_d),B(x_a),C(x_a,x_b),D(x_a,x_b,x_c)) with e₀,f₀ held fixed; toggling of previously traversed axes is absorbed into the arguments. Given arbitrary x_a,x_b,x_c, choose x_d such that A=B. Every path is assumed bad, hence the word (B,B,C,D) must have its last two adjacent transitions both nonzero: B=D=1−C. Consequently D as a function of its three exterior bits a,b,c is independent of b,c; it may initially depend on a. Antipodal oddness implies sensitivity of c(cba) in d at the complementary fixed (e,f)-assignment. Apply the identical argument to the order cbadef. The same last ordered triple def now has a face-color function independent of b,a. Combining the two restrictions makes c(def) constant on every exterior assignment; call its value κ. Return to the order abcdef: it forces B=κ at e₀,f₀ for both values of its exterior a-bit. Repeat the initial argument with order abc d f e, whose first and second windows are unchanged while its last window is dfe. The last face-color therefore equals the same κ for all a,b,c; hence c(dfe)=κ. Finally the antipodal-reversal law sends each constant orientation to its reverse with complemented value, giving c(fed)=c(efd)=1−κ for every face. No claim is made for the two orientations with d in the middle.
