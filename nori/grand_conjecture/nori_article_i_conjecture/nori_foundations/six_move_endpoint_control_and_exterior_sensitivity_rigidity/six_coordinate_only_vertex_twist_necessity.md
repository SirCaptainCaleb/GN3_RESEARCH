# No-good six-coordinate ternary labels are endpoint twists of alternating triangle orientations

## Development

Statement:
Let h(a,b,c)∈F₂ be defined for all ordered triples of distinct elements of a six-element set V and satisfy h(c,b,a)=h(a,b,c)⊕1. Suppose every permutation π of V has at least two color changes in its four consecutive h-values. Then there exist vertex bits s_v and an alternating triangle orientation α, satisfying α(abc)=α(bca)=α(cab) and α(cba)=α(abc)⊕1, such that h(a,b,c)=α(a,b,c)⊕s_a⊕s_c for every triple. Equivalently, the sensitivity h(a,b,c)⊕h(a,c,b) of swapping adjacent final directions is independent of a and equals 1⊕s_b⊕s_c.

Proof:
Define Δ(a,b,c,d)=h(a,b,c)⊕h(b,c,d) on ordered quadruples. On any six-element cyclic order (a,b,c,d,e,f), let Δ₀,…,Δ₅ be the cyclic successive differences of its six ordered-triple colors. Every cyclic rotation is a permutation with four consecutive triple windows; by hypothesis, each three consecutive Δ positions contain at least two 1s. Thus the zeros among six Δ values are separated cyclically by at least three positions. Also ⊕_{i=0}⁵ Δ_i=0 (telescoping around the closed word), so there are an even number of 1s and of 0s. Hence either all Δ_i=1 or exactly two zeros occur at opposite positions. In particular Δ(a,b,c,d)=Δ(d,e,f,a). Applying this to cyclic order (d,e,f,a,c,b) also yields Δ(d,e,f,a)=Δ(a,c,b,d). Therefore Δ(a,b,c,d)=Δ(a,c,b,d) for every four distinct a,b,c,d. Expanding gives h(a,b,c)⊕h(a,c,b)=h(b,c,d)⊕h(c,b,d). By reversal oddness the right side equals h(d,b,c)⊕h(d,c,b). Thus for each unordered pair {b,c}, the value q_bc:=h(a,b,c)⊕h(a,c,b) is independent of the exterior a (there are four possible a; the equality applies to all distinct pairs of choices), and q_bc=q_cb. Reversal oddness along the three adjacent transpositions abc→bac→bca→cba gives q_ab⊕q_ac⊕q_bc=1 for every triangle. Fix r∈V. Put s_r=1, s_v=q_rv for v≠r. The triangle identity implies q_uv=1⊕s_u⊕s_v. Define α(a,b,c)=h(a,b,c)⊕s_a⊕s_c. Swapping either neighboring pair in (a,b,c) changes α by q_uv⊕s_u⊕s_v=1. Hence α is alternating under all permutations of each triple, in particular cyclically invariant and reversal odd. The claimed representation follows. This is a necessary-form theorem; no sufficiency or global closure is asserted.
