# Universal-flipper reduction and five-coordinate closure

## Development

Let V=A⊔B. Say that an ordered-three-face coloring c has A as universal-flipper coordinates if, whenever a∈A is fixed on F, changing its fixed bit complements c(F,π), for every order π of F's free axes. Equivalently c(F,π)=(⊕_{a∈A\T}x_a)⊕g(π,x_{B\T}), where T is the free triple and g is independent of all fixed A bits. This is an intrinsic derivative property; it does not require antipodal oddness.
