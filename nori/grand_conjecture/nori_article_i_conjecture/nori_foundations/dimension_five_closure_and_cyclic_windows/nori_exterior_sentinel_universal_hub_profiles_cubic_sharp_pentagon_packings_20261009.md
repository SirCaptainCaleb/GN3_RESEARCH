# Arbitrary proper-support hub profiles and cubic-sized simultaneous sharp pentagon packings

**Theorem 1 (exterior-sentinel realization of ANY finite ordered-triple hub profile).** Fix n>=4, a proper coordinate set S⊊[n] of size m>=3, and a sentinel direction t∉S. Let h be an ARBITRARY Boolean function on ordered triples of distinct directions in S, without any reversal or antipodal condition. Then some fully legal ACTIVE NORI coloring of all ACTUAL ordered physical three-faces of Q_n has the following property at EVERY physical cube vertex z:

   c(F_z({a,b,c});(a,b,c)) = h(a,b,c)       if z_t=0,
                            1-h(c,b,a)     if z_t=1,

for all distinct a,b,c∈S. In particular all hubs in each t-layer induce exactly the SAME direction-only local ordered-triple profile on S, and the two layers are related by the exact complement-reversal involution.

**Proof.** For every physical three-face F whose free-coordinate support T is contained in S, its exterior t-coordinate is constant, say s(F)∈{0,1}. Define its color for any ordered orientation pi=(a,b,c) of T by h(pi) if s(F)=0, and by 1-h(rev pi) if s(F)=1. The antipodal physical face bar F has t-bit 1-s(F), so
 c(bar F,rev pi)=1-c(F,pi)
holds in BOTH cases, without imposing any condition on h. It is independent of starting corner inside F because s(F) is fixed, and every ordered actual physical face in this support family is covered exactly once. The antipodal-reversal involution preserves the unordered free-coordinate support T, hence the remaining faces with T not contained in S form disjoint reversal orbits; choose one arbitrary bit per orbit and the complementary bit on its mate. Thus the entire coloring exists and is legal. For each z and T⊂S, s(F_z(T))=z_t, proving the displayed formula. QED.

**Theorem 3 (simultaneously Ω(n³) fully sharp six-support chambers).** In Q_n, n>=7, there is a family B of pairwise distinct six-coordinate supports of cardinality at least
    ceil[ binom(n-1,3)/400 ]
such that ONE common legal active NORI coloring has the sharp 144-pentagon property of Theorem2 on EVERY support S∈B at EVERY physical hub. The six-supports pairwise intersect in at most TWO coordinates, but can overlap in one/two directions, so this is stronger than simply taking a disjoint partition.

Proof. Reserve one sentinel t and put N=n-1. Greedily pack six-subsets of the remaining N directions, deleting all candidate six-subsets that share >=3 directions with each selected block. Fix a selected block S. Every conflicting six-subset T contains at least one of the binom(6,3)=20 triples of S, so there are at most
    20·binom(N-3,3)
conflicting six-subsets, counting S itself and allowing overcount. Greedy maximality therefore gives
 |B| >= ceil[ binom(N,6)/(20·binom(N-3,3)) ]
       = ceil[ binom(N,3)/400 ].
Since any two B-blocks share at most TWO coordinates, no unordered triple T of coordinate directions belongs to two selected six-blocks.
