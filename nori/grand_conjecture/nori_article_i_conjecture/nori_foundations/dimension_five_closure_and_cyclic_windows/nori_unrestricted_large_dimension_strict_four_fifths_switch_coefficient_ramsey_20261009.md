# All physical ordered-face colorings have strictly sub-4/5 expected switch coefficient in sufficiently high dimension

# Universal strict improvement over 4/5 by cross-support hub Ramsey, without NORI oddness

**Theorem.** Let N0=2^2500 and epsilon = 1/(60*binom(N0,5))>0. For EVERY n>=N0 and EVERY arbitrary binary coloring c of ACTUAL PHYSICAL ORDERED THREE-FACES of Q_n (no antipodal oddness, face-independence, or reversal conditions assumed), a uniformly random physical ROOTED full antipodal n-edge geodesic has
  E[# of adjacent ordered-three-window color switches] <= (4/5-epsilon)*(n-3).
Therefore some genuine full antipodal n-geodesic has at most floor((4/5-epsilon)*(n-3)) switches. Moreover a uniformly random physical rooted five-edge direction-distinct geodesic has probability at least 2/5+epsilon of at most one three-window switch. N0 is deliberately enormous and explicit: the point is a positive improvement of the all-dimensional ASYMPTOTIC 4/5 constant for unrestricted physical colorings.

**Lemma 1 (elementary 3-uniform five-set Ramsey bound).** Every binary coloring H of unordered three-subsets of a set of size N0=2^2500 has a homogeneous five-set.

Proof without any literature lookup. Choose successively 71 distinct vertices v1,...,v71, maintaining a pool of future vertices. When v_t is chosen, for each i<t restrict the future pool to a majority color of H({v_i,v_t,w}) as w ranges over the current pool; at most t-1 consecutive halvings are needed. The total loss through the first71 stages is at most 2^(0+1+...+70)=2^2485, apart from 71 selected vertices; starting with 2^2500 leaves abundant room. Consequently, for every i<j<k among the chosen vertices, the color H({v_i,v_j,v_k}) depends only on i,j, not k. Define a two-color complete graph on v1,...,v70 by g(v_i,v_j)=H({v_i,v_j,v_71}) for i<j<=70; the preceding uniformity gives H({v_i,v_j,v_k})=g(v_i,v_j) whenever i<j<k<=70. The elementary two-color graph Ramsey recurrence R(s,t)<=R(s-1,t)+R(s,t-1), R(1,t)=R(s,1)=1, gives R(5,5)<=binom(8,4)=70. Hence the first70 vertices contain five with all graph pairs of one color q; all increasing ordered triples on these five have H-color q. QED.

**Lemma 2 (local hub rich-support count).** Fix ANY physical cube vertex z. For every ordered triple abc of distinct direction labels, put
 h_z(abc)=c(F_z({a,b,c}),(a,b,c)).
This is a well-defined arbitrary binary function on ordered triples; all six orientation colors on one actual physical face may vary independently. For any N0-subset T of directions, apply Lemma1 to the 2-coloring of unordered triples {a<b<c} inside T defined by h_z(a,b,c). It supplies a FIVE-subset S of T such that every increasing triple from S has the same hub color q. Therefore the increasing five-direction order on S has all three consecutive physical 3-face window colors q; this path's three windows all share the true cube hub z, as seen by rooting the five-edge path at z xor its first two directions. Call such S rich at z.

Let M_z be number of rich five-supports among all binom(n,5). Counting pairs (S,T), |S|=5, |T|=N0, S⊂T, gives
 M_z*binom(n-5,N0-5)>=binom(n,N0),
so M_z>=binom(n,5)/binom(N0,5).
This is an all-hub constant positive fraction, valid for every z and every physical face coloring.

**Lemma 3 (cyclic pentagon surplus, no reversal axiom).** Fix z and a five-set S. There are 24 cyclic direction orders, each with 5 rotations, hence 120 linear orders. For any cyclic order, write its five ACTUAL hub window colors w_j=h_z(p_j,p_(j+1),p_(j+2)), indices mod5. Let E be number of cyclic pairs w_j=w_(j+1). Since the cycle has length5, E is ODD, hence E>=1. If E=1, exactly2 rotations have <=1 switch among their three consecutive window colors. If E=3, at most1 rotation has two switches, hence at least4 are good; if E=5, all5 are good. Thus every five-support has >=48/120 good linear orderings at this hub, and >=24/120 equal pairs among all cyclic neighboring comparisons.

For a RICH support S at z, a MONOCHROMATIC five-letter order has three consecutive equal physical window colors. Its cyclic class has TWO adjacent equal comparison edges, forcing E>=3. Thus that ONE cyclic class gives >=4 good rotations instead of2, and >=3 equal pairs instead of1. The rich S hence contributes >=50/120 good linear orders and >=26/120 equal four-letter comparisons. This uses NO reversal symmetry. (If same-face direction-only reversal oddness holds, the reversed cyclic class is also rich and the 50/120,26/120 strengthen to 52/120,28/120, respectively.)

**Proof of the theorem: exact physical double-count.** At each ACTUAL z, average Lemma3 over a uniformly random five-support S and its 120 orders. The fraction of rich supports from Lemma2 is at least delta=1/binom(N0,5), hence
 Pr(<=1 switch on a five-path at hub z) >= 48/120 +delta*(2/120) =2/5+epsilon,
 Pr(h_z(abc)=h_z(bcd) for random ordered distinct a,b,c,d) >=24/120 +delta*(2/120) =1/5+epsilon.
The latter four-direction averaging identity holds exactly because a uniform cyclic five-order together with a uniform cyclic start is a uniform ordered four-tuple of DISTINCT coordinate directions (after sampling S uniformly): the 5th auxiliary direction is simply unused in the actual four-edge comparison.

Now choose uniform cube root x and uniform full direction permutation p. At each comparison position t=1,...,n-3, the four consecutive directions (a,b,c,d) are uniformly distributed ordered distinct coordinates; the physical hub z reached after the first two of these four directions is uniform in Q_n, independently of the four names because x is uniform. The two ACTUAL ordered face windows of the true path are precisely F_z({a,b,c}) with order (a,b,c), and F_z({b,c,d}) with order (b,c,d). Thus each switch indicator has probability <=4/5-epsilon. Summing n-3 indicators by linearity proves the full expectation bound. The five-path density is obtained by choosing a random five-support, its order, and a uniform root; its central hub after two steps is again uniform. All paths are literal direction-distinct geodesics with physical roots; NO independent local connectors have been concatenated.

**Limits / relation to grand closure.** This establishes a STRICT UNIFORM ASYMPTOTIC improvement on the previous 4/5 coefficient for ALL physical ordered-three-face colorings in very high dimension, without active NORI oddness. The numerical epsilon is far too small for one-switch closure, and the argument does not synchronize the rich supports into a common full path. It demonstrates that saturating every physical five-window pentagon is impossible at sufficiently high dimension because local monotone-hub Ramsey structure forces excess monochromatic comparisons. The true grand task remains exact same-root complementary reversed-two-tail reachability, perhaps using the excess pentagons as defect curvature in a global root-coupled topological carrier. This theorem assumes n>=N0 and makes no statement of a strict improvement for n<N0.
