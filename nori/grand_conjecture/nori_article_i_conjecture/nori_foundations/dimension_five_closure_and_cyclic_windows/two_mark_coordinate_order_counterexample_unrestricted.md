# Sharpness of the unrestricted five-dimensional theorem: two marked directions

## Development

For any n≥6, the 4+2 partition when n=6 gives the particularly small obstruction. The oddness hypothesis of the full NORI conjecture is essential already in dimension six; this family deliberately violates oddness, and no claim against NORI follows. It also cautions against trying to extend the unconditional Q_5 theorem by induction. The construction is coordinate-only and uses no dependence on fixed exterior face bits.
