# Exact realization of antipodal-separated downsets as geodesic reachability

Let Q_n be identified with subsets of [n], let bar S=[n]\S, and let c be a binary edge coloring with c(bar e)=1-c(e). Write R(x) for the vertices reachable from x by a monochromatic geodesic of either color, including the empty path.

THEOREM (exact realization at a monochromatic root). Let A be a downset in 2^[n] containing the empty set and every singleton, and assume A intersects bar A trivially. There exists an antipodally odd coloring with all edges incident with the empty root colored 0 and R(empty)=A if and only if the Hamming distance between A and bar A is at least two.

Necessity. Since all first edges have color 0, every nonempty monochromatic geodesic from the empty root has color 0. Every vertex of A is therefore 0-geodesically reachable. If an edge goes from U in A upward to V outside A, its color must be 1; otherwise a 0-geodesic to U extends to V. Suppose A and bar A have adjacent vertices U,V. Since A is a downset and the two sets are disjoint, their edge must be oriented upward from U in A to V in bar A. Its antipodal edge goes upward from bar V in A to bar U in bar A. Both edges have color 1 by the preceding boundary rule, contrary to oddness.

Sufficiency. Color every edge internal to A by 0 and every edge leaving A upward by 1. Impose the opposite colors on antipodal edges: edges internal to bar A have color 1, and edges entering bar A upward have color 0. These prescriptions are consistent. A and bar A are disjoint; a conflict between their boundary prescriptions would require an edge joining A and bar A, which is excluded. Complete all remaining antipodal edge orbits arbitrarily with opposite colors. Every vertex of A has an increasing path from the empty set wholly inside A, hence a 0-geodesic. No increasing 0-path can leave A, since every outward edge has color 1. All first edges have color 0, so color 1 reaches only the empty root. Consequently R(empty)=A exactly.

This is an existential classification under the displayed downset, singleton, and antipodal-freeness hypotheses; it does not classify unrestricted reachability sets. The proof constructs honest geodesic witnesses and an actual physical-edge coloring, not an abstract set system.

ROLE IN REACHABILITY TOPOLOGY. A principal label constructed solely from one R(x) must accommodate every downset A of the stated type. Even these downward-closed, contractible rooted reachability carriers have substantial freedom. A forcing theorem must use compatibility across roots or another hypothesis stronger than one-root accessibility and antipodal symmetry. This theorem concerns the edge proving ground; the ordered-three-face problem still requires directed memory and seam compatibility.
