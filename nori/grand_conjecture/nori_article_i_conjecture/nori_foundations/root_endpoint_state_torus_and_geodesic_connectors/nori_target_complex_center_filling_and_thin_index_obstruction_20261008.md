# Monochromatic antipodal geodesic iff center filled by honest target carrier

# Center-filling criterion and equivariant dimension obstruction for the honest target complex

Assume n>=3 and an antipodally odd two-coloring of the UNDIRECTED edges of Q_n. Let F_z be the affine n-cube of root/support states with physical target z=r XOR S at Boolean points. Let K_q(z) be the union of the standard Freudenthal simplices coming from complete prefix chains of all monochromatic q-geodesics starting at physical target z (equivalently ending at z after reversal). Write K=union_(z,q) K_q(z) as a subpolyhedron of the diagonal-fiber cross X_n=union_z F_z, with central point o=(r_i=s_i=1/2 for all i).

**Theorem 1 (exact central witness).** For any z and q, o belongs to K_q(z) if and only if a q-monochromatic antipodal geodesic from z to bar z exists. Hence o belongs to K iff the original antipodally odd EDGE geodesic conjecture holds for the given coloring.

Proof. Identify F_z with the progress cube s in [0,1]^n. Each simplex is a Boolean chain of nested supports S_0 subset ... subset S_k visited by a q-geodesic. If o lies in its convex hull, every coordinate must take averaged value 1/2. If S_0 is nonempty or S_k is not full, one coordinate is constantly 1 or constantly 0, contradiction. Thus the simplex contains both empty and full supports, so its witnessing geodesic has used all n distinct directions monochromatically. Conversely the full prefix chain simplex contains the long diagonal from empty to full, including center o.

**Theorem 2 (counterexample would be an equivariantly thin carrier).** If no monochromatic antipodal geodesic exists, then dim K_q(z) <= n-2 for every q,z, and hence dim K<=n-2. Proof. A length-(n-1) monochromatic geodesic has endpoints agreeing on exactly one unused coordinate. The two possible extension edges at its ends are antipodal, hence of opposite colors; one continues the path monochromatically to length n. So in a counterexample, every monochromatic geodesic has at most n-2 edges. Its prefix simplex has dimension equal to its length. A finite union of such simplices has dimension at most n-2.

Let alpha(r,s)=(1-r,s) be root complementation. It carries F_z to F_bar z and K_q(z) to K_(1-q)(bar z) by oddness, so alpha preserves K. The fixed set of alpha on X_n is exactly {o}: if all r_i=1/2 and (r,s) lies on the pair of diagonals for each coordinate, every s_i=1/2. Thus in a hypothetical counterexample, K is an alpha-free Z2-complex of dimension <=n-2.

**Theorem 3 (canonical equivariant physical cube graph).** The complex K contains an alpha-equivariant subdivision of the entire physical cube one-skeleton. Embed each physical vertex z as the empty-support apex a_z=(r=z,s=0). For an edge zz'=z XOR e_i of color q, K_q(z) contains the prefix edge in F_z from a_z to b=(r=z',s=e_i), and K_q(z') contains the prefix edge from a_z' to b'=(r=z,s=e_i). These two straight edges cross at the unique point m=(r_i=1/2,s_i=1/2, all other r_j=z_j,s_j=0). Their union contains an embedded arc a_z--m--a_z'; arcs of distinct physical edges have disjoint interiors, since their supports vary in distinct coordinate axes or at different fixed coordinates. This gives an embedded subdivided Q_n graph G subset K. Root antipodality alpha carries the embedded edge zz' to its antipodal edge bar z bar z', while swapping the colors, hence G is alpha-invariant.

**Index test.** In a counterexample the free alpha-space K has dimension <=n-2, so its mod-2 cohomological Z2-index is at most n-2 (if nonempty). On the other hand G is connected and its alpha double cover nontrivial, so the index of K is at least one. For n>=3 a proof that the actual colored-edge carrier data force index(K)>=n-1 would contradict its dimension bound and settle the edge geodesic conjecture. Such a high-index theorem is NOT proved here: the embedded physical graph alone has dimension one and supplies index only one. This isolates the missing color-dependent topological content and makes a testable target, rather than assuming ordinary BU on the full state cube will extract a path. The same framework remains only an outer support carrier for the full ordered-three-face NORI, whose path memory and seam compatibility still require separate treatment.
