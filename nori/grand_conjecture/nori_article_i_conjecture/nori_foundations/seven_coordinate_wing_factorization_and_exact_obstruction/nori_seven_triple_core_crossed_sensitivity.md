# Seven-coordinate crossed sensitivities force a one-change antipodal geodesic

This strengthens the seven-coordinate wing factorization by coupling the two wings through the shared pivot d. It also strengthens the contemporaneous seven-coordinate central-pivot endpoint criterion (item seven_coordinate_shared_pivot_endpoint_criterion). It is a local sufficient condition for full ordered-three-face NORI closure in dimension seven: two crossed sensitivities in the immediately adjacent windows bcd, def, together with central-coordinate sensitivity at one extreme window under the matching assignments, force a good path. A hypothetical counterexample must exhibit constant-opposite endpoint rigidity for every seven-direction order and every compatible four-bit environment admitting both middle sensitivities. Distinguish simultaneous sensitivity after matching the central color from unrestricted sensitivity at unrelated exterior assignments.

**Statement**

Fix seven distinct directions (a,b,c,d,e,f,g) in Q_7 and fix the initial bits at a,b,f,g. Write u=x_e, v=x_c, t=x_d. The five-window color word has the form (P(u,t), L(u), M, R(v), S(v,t)). Suppose both L and R are nonconstant. Let u_* and v_* be the unique values satisfying L(u_*)=M=R(v_*). If either P(u_*,t) or S(v_*,t) is nonconstant as t varies, a full antipodal geodesic with direction order (a,b,c,d,e,f,g) has at most one color change. More sharply, all eight choices (u,v,t) fail only if P(u_*,0)=P(u_*,1)=S(v_*,0)=S(v_*,1)=1-M.

**Proof**

The ordered-three-face window directions are abc,bcd,cde,def,efg. Coordinate e is exterior only to the first two windows, c is exterior only to the last two, and d is exterior only to the first and last. Thus varying u,v,t gives precisely the stated variable dependence, with M independent of all three variables. The two nonconstant binary maps L and R each take both values; choose their unique preimages u_*,v_* of M. For these choices the word is (P(u_*,t),M,M,M,S(v_*,t)). It has at most one change whenever either endpoint equals M. Failure for both t values therefore forces both endpoint functions to be identically 1-M. In particular either endpoint sensitivity to t contradicts failure, proving the lemma. This uses no antipodal-coloring hypothesis.
