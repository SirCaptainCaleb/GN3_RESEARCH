# Fourier shift-run transversal specialized to active NORI ordered physical triples

Let the alphabet be the q cube-coordinate names with an arbitrarily fixed numerical order, q>=4. Let H+ have all physical ordered three-face windows (F,(a,b,c)), a,b,c distinct, and a directed arc from (F,(a,b,c)) to (G,(b,c,d)) iff a,b,c,d are pairwise distinct and the pair occurs as consecutive windows of a genuine directed four-edge cube geodesic. This is the genuine NORI physical shift graph, not a graph with the root bits discarded.

**Fourier identity.** For omega=exp(2pi*i/3), Im(a*omega+b*omega^2+c*omega^3)=sqrt(3)(a-b)/2. Thus the user's A/B/I rule gives A iff a>b, B iff a<b, and never I on distinct-symbol triples. Its cyclic-shift predecessor of (a,b,c) is (c,a,b), which is A iff c>a. Hence a first A-run vertex on a rotation 3-cycle is exactly a>max(b,c). The original Fourier cycle transversal is the set T_first of actual windows whose first free coordinate is the greatest of their three.

**THEOREM.** Let T_first, T_middle, T_last be the three sets of physical ordered windows whose maximum free coordinate occurs respectively first, middle, last in their free-direction order. Each set meets EVERY directed cycle of H+. Each has cardinal 2^(q-3)*(q)_3/3, precisely one-third of the physical windows, and the three sets partition the vertex set. The physical antipodal reversal tau maps T_first to T_last, while T_middle is individually tau-invariant.

**Proof.** A directed closed walk in H+ projects onto a cyclic coordinate string (p_t) with every four consecutive symbols distinct, as each shift arc has a genuine four-edge witness. Choose any occurrence of the largest coordinate M in the cyclic string. The three consecutive directed-window vertices whose windows begin at t,t-1,t-2 contain M respectively as first, middle, last coordinate; their other coordinates differ from M. Thus these three vertices belong respectively to T_first,T_middle,T_last. Counting is by the three equiprobable positions of the largest among three distinct ordered coordinates, independently for each of the 2^(q-3) physical face assignments. Antipodal reversal reverses the free-direction order without changing their unordered triple, fixing the middle position and exchanging the outer positions. QED.

**Ordinary de Bruijn benchmark.** In the usual symbolic B(q,3) in which repeats ARE allowed, the user's Fourier selected set consists of triples a>b, a>=c, plus all q constant triples. Its cardinal is (q^3-q)/3+q=(q^3+2q)/3. This is optimal: ordinary B(q,3) has disjoint directed rotation cycles partitioning its q^3 vertices, specifically q singleton constant loops and (q^3-q)/3 length-three cycles. Every feedback set requires one vertex per cycle and the Fourier set meets that bound. For general de Bruijn word length r, the number of rotation cycles is the necklace count (1/r) sum_{d|r} phi(d) q^(r/d), generally larger than q^r/r due to shorter periodic orbits.

**NORI qualification.** Rotation three-cycles are NOT genuine four-distinct-direction shift cycles; the proposed direction word (a,b,c,a) repeats a and is illegal. Thus the 1/3 feedback set is not automatically a MINIMUM for the genuine graph. For example, with q=4, the projected distinct-four-direction shift graph consists of six disjoint directed four-cycles on 24 triples, so its minimum feedback set has size 6 rather than the Fourier set size 8.

More importantly, a directed-cycle vertex transversal does not meet every UNDIRECTED cycle of H+, nor each backward-forward sector H_i antipodal root-bit transport loop. The centered physical pentagons are directed and are indeed hit. However binary odd-cycle parity merely forces SOME monochromatic edge of each pentagon, not necessarily one incident to its selected transversal vertex. Therefore this produces a clean tau-invariant acyclic DIRECTED chart, not a proof of universal same-root two-tail overlap or of a beta*w mixed cup product. The precise next task is to compare these directed charts with the physical sector phase-cut gluing while retaining compatible actual good-geodesic certificates.

**Run-start quantifier correction.** In a directed cycle it is NOT true that EVERY beginning of an A-run is also the beginning of an A-run on its own rotation 3-cycle. E.g. take cyclic coordinate word (1,4,2,5,3). The vertex (4,2,5) has A because 4>2 and follows the B-vertex (1,4,2), so is an A-run start in the directed cycle; yet the rotation predecessor (5,4,2) is A, so (4,2,5) is not in the Fourier-selected T_first. The transversal proof above uses the occurrence of the GLOBAL maximum 5 to choose a suitable run start; it does not claim all run starts are selected.
