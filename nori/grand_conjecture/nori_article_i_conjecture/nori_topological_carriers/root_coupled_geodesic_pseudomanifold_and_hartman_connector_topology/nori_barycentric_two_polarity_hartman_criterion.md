# Two-polarity least-unreachable Sperner criterion

Suppose m>=3. For each nonempty U subset [m], choose an actual reversible repair state q_U with binary polarity beta_U and a proper set R_U of target indices reachable in its undirected repair component. Suppose [m] outside U is contained in R_U. Suppose R is constant on a repair component. Suppose nested U subset V with beta_U=beta_V imply q_U and q_V lie in the same component. Label U by the least index absent from R_U. This is a Sperner labeling of sd(Delta^(m-1)), hence some maximal nested chain has m distinct labels. Two members share the same binary polarity. They are nested, so their components, reachability sets, and labels agree, contradiction. Thus the data cannot coexist. Applying this criterion to NORI requires genuine component-coherent, jointly realizable targets. Mere coordinatewise progress reachability fails, as shown by the rooted Q5 obstruction.
