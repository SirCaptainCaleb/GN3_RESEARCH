# NORI one-switch flag carrier fills 4-skeleton but physical antipodality fails

# Ordered-three-face NORI flag carrier: automatic four-skeleton and a genuine symmetry obstruction

Let n>=5 and let c(F,pi) be a binary ordered-three-face coloring with c(bar F,rev pi)=1-c(F,pi). Define B_NORI(c) subset sd(Q_n) by taking all rooted barycentric flag simplices associated with geodesic prefixes of length k whose (k-2) ordered-three-face window colors have at most one change (for k<=2 there are no windows, declared admissible), and all their simplicial faces.

**Theorem 1 (four-skeleton).** For ANY ordered-three-face coloring, B_NORI(c) contains the entire barycentric subdivision of every physical face of dimension at most four. Indeed an ordered-geodesic prefix of length k has max(k-2,0) windows, hence at most max(k-3,0) changes. For k<=4 this is <=1. Every flag in a face of dimension <=4 extends to a rooted complete flag in that face. The same physical face-barycenter semantics as the edge case remain exact: a maximal flag ending at a face H is an actual geodesic realizing a one-change ordered-three-face word.

**Theorem 2 (physical antipodality can FAIL to preserve this flag carrier).** There exists a valid NORI coloring on Q_5 such that physical cube complementation alpha(z)=bar z fails to preserve B_NORI(c).

Proof. Consider the full geodesic with root 00000 and direction order (1,2,3,4,5). Assign its three ordered-window colors (123),(234),(345) to 0,1,0 (two changes, not admissible). On the full geodesic with ROOT 11111 and the SAME direction order, assign its three ordered-window colors to 0,0,0 (admissible). These six ordered-face objects occupy independent orbits of the involution (F,pi)->(bar F,rev pi): their direction triples are the three consecutive increasing ordered triples and never any of their reversals. Thus the partial assignment extends arbitrarily by choosing a color for each other orbit, then assigning its involution partner the complementary color. In the ordinary barycentric subdivision, alpha sends the first maximal 5-simplex to the second (same order, complemented root). The first is absent from B_NORI and the second present, proving failure of alpha-invariance. The argument embeds in every n>=5 by using a fixed physical 5-face and extending colors consistently.

**Correct involution.** NORI oddness sends a directed path P=(v_0,...,v_k) to Theta(P)=(bar v_k,...,bar v_0), complementing and REVERSING its window word. On the physical barycentric rooted flag, alpha instead sends the root v_0 to bar v_0 and retains the direction order, so it generally differs from Theta. On a full antipodal path, Theta starts again at v_0 but reverses the direction order. Therefore the ordinary barycentric subcomplex, though it fills every physical 4-face, is not automatically an equivariant carrier for the actual NORI color axiom.

**Conclusion.** The original physical n-cube gives an exact face-label geometry and automatic 4-skeleton for the GRAND problem. To exploit Z2-index/Tucker topology one must lift the carrier to retain order orientation and give Theta a simplicial action (e.g. the proved finite two-ended memory automaton). Any claim that the physical cube 4-skeleton itself yields index four from the NORI oddness axiom is unjustified without such an equivariance repair. This is a structural guardrail, not a counterexample to NORI.
