# High-index all-root complex forbids equivariant continuous root–endpoint selection

Let n>=3. Let K_n be the all-root cube-geodesic simplicial pseudomanifold under cube antipodality, X_n its realization with antipodal edge midpoints removed, and T_n=(boundary[0,1]^2)^n the root–endpoint torus under simultaneous endpoint complementation. There is no continuous equivariant map F:X_n->T_n. Proof: near each removed midpoint, X_n contains an invariant linking sphere S^(n-1) with standard free antipodal action, by the established local-link theorem. The torus has an equivariant nowhere-zero map T_n->R^2 obtained by projecting to the first square factor and centering it. Any equivariant F would compose to a nowhere-zero odd map S^(n-1)->R^2, contradicting Borsuk–Ulam since n-1>=2. Equivalently, X_n has cohomological index >=n-1 while T_n has index 1. In particular, a collection of root-dependent mono-geodesic certificates that locally selects one pair of endpoints cannot glue to an equivariant continuous torus-valued selection over all of X_n. This is a rigorous obstruction to a color-free root choice, not grand closure: to turn it into a proof one must show that under a hypothetical no-good edge coloring, the actual reachable path-state certificates force precisely such a continuous equivariant global selection. The obstruction also shows that merely choosing a consistent endpoint/root on each rooted geodesic chamber without checking shared-root-slide ridges is topologically impossible. The statement relies only on the established geometry of K_n and T_n, and makes no inference for the ordered-three-face conjecture.
