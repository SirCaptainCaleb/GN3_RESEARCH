# Root-slide obstruction classes and antipodal selector topology

Fix a physical cube root and consider full antipodal orders from that root. Root-slide squares, reversal involutions and odd path labels can carry strong antipodal topological information, but such information must still preserve the physical faces of the candidate geodesic. The following arguments separate those two obligations and establish rooted parity obstructions before extracting genuine topological packets.

FIXED-ROOT OBSTRUCTION INSIDE VALID FACE-LOCAL NORI; NECESSITY OF ROOT MOBILITY.

Let n>=6 be EVEN, and fix an arbitrary reference cube vertex a in F2^n. For any ordered three-face (F,pi), let W be its three free coordinates and let y_j (j outside W) be its fixed exterior bits. Define
 c_a(F,pi)=sum_(j outside W)(y_j XOR a_j) mod 2.
This is independent of the choice of starting corner within the face, and independent of the order pi of its three free directions.

THEOREM.
(1) c_a satisfies the ACTIVE NORI oddness condition c_a(bar F,rev pi)=1 XOR c_a(F,pi).
(2) Every full antipodal geodesic starting at the specific root a has window color word
 (0,1,0,1,...) of length n-2, hence exactly n-3 changes. Every full geodesic starting at bar a has complementary alternating word and the same maximal number of changes. Thus NO geodesic from EITHER endpoint of the antipodal root pair {a,bar a} has at most one change.
(3) Nevertheless, for EVERY direction order p, there exist exactly eight starting roots yielding a MONOCHROMATIC full antipodal geodesic under c_a. Hence c_a is an explicit valid NORI coloring with abundant global closure but complete failure of the two prescribed antipodal endpoint-root charts.

PROOF. Every ordered three-face has m=n-3 exterior coordinates, and m is odd. Replacing F by bar F flips each exterior y_j and hence changes the parity of the sum by m mod2=1. Reversing the three free directions does not affect the formula. This proves (1).

For a full path starting at a, at its i-th consecutive three-face window precisely the i-1 previously traversed exterior coordinates have been toggled relative to a, while all later exterior coordinates agree with a. Thus the displayed parity is i-1 mod2, independent of the direction permutation. For a path starting at bar a, the i-1 previously traversed exterior coordinates have been toggled into agreement with a, while the remaining n-(i+2) exterior coordinates still disagree with a, so the window color is (m-(i-1)) mod2=1 XOR (i-1 mod2). Both color words alternate throughout, giving n-3 changes. This proves (2).

For any direction order p, write the differences between the proposed starting root y and a as d_i=y_(p_i) XOR a_(p_i). The exterior disagreement count J_i satisfies J_(i+1)-J_i=1-d_i-d_(i+3). Impose d_(i+3)=1-d_i for i=1,...,n-3. The three freely chosen initial bits d1,d2,d3 determine exactly eight roots, and make all J_i identical. Therefore the parity of J_i, which is exactly c_a at the i-th window, is constant. This proves (3). QED.

STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE. This is a SHARP COUNTEREXAMPLE to any purported theorem guaranteeing a one-change geodesic with a prescribed antipodal endpoint pair, even under genuine ordered-face locality and full NORI oddness. It shows that root mobility is logically necessary for a uniform NORI proof. The root-progress 2n-cube, endpoint root slides, and the topological gluing of distinct Freudenthal root charts cannot be replaced by a fixed-root argument. It is not a counterexample to the grand conjecture; indeed it has eight monochromatic starts for every coordinate order.

## High antipodal index of the punctured all-root geodesic complex

Let \(K_n\) be the \(n\)-dimensional simplicial complex whose facets are the vertex sets of antipodal cube geodesics, with involution \(\tau(v)=\bar v\). Write \(m_x\) for the midpoint of the antipodal edge \(e_x=\{x,\bar x\}\), and \(X_n=|K_n|\setminus\{m_x:x\in Q_n\}\). The preceding all-root-complex theorem proves that these midpoints are exactly the fixed points.

**Theorem.** For \(n\ge2\), the free involution on \(X_n\) has mod-two cohomological index at least \(n-1\): if \(w\in H^1(X_n/\tau;\mathbb F_2)\) classifies the double covering, then \(w^{n-1}\ne0\). Consequently every continuous odd map \(f:X_n\to\mathbb R^{n-1}\) vanishes; more strongly, its restriction to an arbitrarily small invariant linking sphere about *each* \(m_x\) vanishes.

**Proof.** For a fixed edge \(e_x\), its simplicial link is \(L_x=\operatorname{sd}(\partial\Delta^{n-1})\cong S^{n-2}\). The involution acts there by complementing nonempty proper coordinate subsets, which is a free PL antipodal involution. The geometric link of the *interior point* \(m_x\) is the suspension \(\Sigma L_x=S^0*L_x\): its two suspension poles correspond to the directions toward \(x\) and \(\bar x\), and \(\tau\) interchanges these poles while acting by complement on the equator. This is equivariantly the standard antipodal \((n-1)\)-sphere. Choose a sufficiently small invariant geometric linking sphere \(S_x\subset X_n\). The quotient \(S_x/\tau\) is \(\mathbb RP^{n-1}\), and the restriction of \(w\) to it is its generator. Hence the restriction of \(w^{n-1}\) is nonzero, proving the global lower bound. The Borsuk–Ulam theorem applied to the restriction \(f|_{S_x}\) gives a zero for each x. \(\square\)

**Comparison and exact extraction gap.** The root–endpoint torus \(T_n=(S^1)^n\) with simultaneous-endpoint complementation admits a nowhere-zero equivariant \(\mathbb R^2\) map and has index one, whereas the punctured all-root geodesic complex has index at least \(n-1\). Thus \(K_n\) is a viable high-index arena for dimension-dependent topology. A forced zero on its linking sphere is only a balanced *combination* of local full-geodesic chambers. Since fixed-root good-geodesic claims fail in legitimate NORI colorings, the unresolved step is to define a coloring-dependent odd map whose local zero admits an extraction using root-slide chambers, actual ordered-face windows, and a bound of one color change. The theorem gives the topological forcing half of this program, not grand closure.

**Root-localization caveat (essential).** Every simplex meeting a sufficiently small neighborhood of \(m_x\) belongs to the closed star of \(e_x\). All top-dimensional simplices in this star encode full geodesics *with endpoints \(\{x,\bar x\}\)*. Thus a forced zero on \(S_x\) is root-local at the level of its geometric carrier. Valid NORI colorings can make every geodesic with these prescribed endpoints bad. Accordingly there cannot be an all-colorings theorem that extracts a good geodesic **with the same rooted endpoints solely from the chambers supporting each local zero**. To obtain grand closure, a labeling near \(m_x\) must convey nonlocal repair/reachability data, permitting a zero to certify a good path with a different root, or a global theorem must select a carrier extending across root-slide ridges. This local-versus-nonlocal distinction is the precise geometric obligation.

Let n>=3. Let K_n be the all-root cube-geodesic simplicial pseudomanifold under cube antipodality, X_n its realization with antipodal edge midpoints removed, and T_n=(boundary[0,1]^2)^n the root–endpoint torus under simultaneous endpoint complementation. There is no continuous equivariant map F:X_n->T_n. Proof: near each removed midpoint, X_n contains an invariant linking sphere S^(n-1) with standard free antipodal action, by the established local-link theorem. The torus has an equivariant nowhere-zero map T_n->R^2 obtained by projecting to the first square factor and centering it. Any equivariant F would compose to a nowhere-zero odd map S^(n-1)->R^2, contradicting Borsuk–Ulam since n-1>=2. Equivalently, X_n has cohomological index >=n-1 while T_n has index 1. In particular, a collection of root-dependent mono-geodesic certificates that locally selects one pair of endpoints cannot glue to an equivariant continuous torus-valued selection over all of X_n. This is a rigorous obstruction to a color-free root choice, not grand closure: to turn it into a proof one must show that under a hypothetical no-good edge coloring, the actual reachable path-state certificates force precisely such a continuous equivariant global selection. The obstruction also shows that merely choosing a consistent endpoint/root on each rooted geodesic chamber without checking shared-root-slide ridges is topologically impossible. The statement relies only on the established geometry of K_n and T_n, and makes no inference for the ordered-three-face conjecture.

The resulting index and odd-degree statements apply to honest root/order parameter spaces; they do not authorize a single equivariant choice of monochromatic terminal branch. The obstruction to a prescribed root must be retained when subsequently using these packets.
