# Full-sphere Borsuk–Ulam synchronizes a genuine endpoint-opposed path with central two-color witnesses in one physical three-direction cap face

THEOREM (SIMULTANEOUS FIRST-LAST ENDPOINT-BALANCE AND CENTRAL PHYSICAL COLOR: THREE-CAP JOINT PACKET). Let n>=7, fix any active NORI ordered-three-face coloring, fixed full path root x, distinguished direction i, and any THREE OTHER pairwise distinct directions a,b,c. Set T=[n] minus{i,a,b,c}, so k=|T|=n−4. Write actual full x-rooted order π and its ordered-three-face window word w_1,...,w_(n−2). Define the integer endpoint-imbalance
  e(π)=w_1+w_(n−2)−1 ∈{−1,0,+1}.
This obeys e(rev π)=−e(π), and for n>=7 obeys |e(π)−e(π')|<=1 whenever π,π' differ by one adjacent coordinate swap (since the first and last triple windows cannot both be altered by one adjacent swap). Thus an ACTUAL e=0 path is a full antipodal geodesic whose first and last ordered-3-face colors are opposite.
Define the genuine central polarization scalar h(π) as follows:
- for ODD n=2m+1, h(π)=2w_m−1∈{−1,+1}, the signed color of the unique central ordered triple window;
- for EVEN n=2m, h(π)=w_(m−1)+w_m−1∈{−1,0,+1}, the imbalance of the two middle ordered triple windows.
In BOTH cases h(rev π)=−h(π) under the active NORI same-root full-order reversal rule w(rev π)=1−reverse(w(π)). For j∈T put b_j(π)=1 iff j occurs before i. Then b_j(rev π)=1−b_j(π).
The full permutohedron boundary ∂P_n is a free antipodal (n−2)-sphere. Its ORIGINAL vertices are actual full direction orders. Prescribe the odd R^(n−2)-valued vertex map

whose dimension is (n−4)+2=n−2. Extend by face-barycentric averaging and linear interpolation on the equivariant barycentric subdivision. Borsuk–Ulam gives a zero z in the proper permutohedron face H. By convexity of face vertex values and Caratheodory there are at most n−1 ACTUAL full x-rooted geodesics π_r among ORIGINAL vertices of ONE COMMON PROPER FACE H, together with positive weights α_r summing1 and

Each common proper face lies inside at least one facet fixing a nonempty proper prefix direction support S. Since all j∈T are balanced relative to i, if i is not in S then S⊆{a,b,c}; if i∈S then [n] minus S⊆{a,b,c}. Hence the entire packet lives in a genuine EARLY or LATE at-most-THREE-direction cap chart, all paths pass through the same actual physical cube vertex x XOR S, and the remaining all-but-three directions have synchronized before/after-i marginal balance.
Most importantly H also contains at least ONE ACTUAL ENDPOINT-OPPOSED PATH π* with e(π*)=0. To prove this, if the chosen weighted packet contains an e=0 vertex, done. Otherwise its e values are only ±1, and E[e]=0 ensures the face H contains true permutation vertices with both signs. These central-color witnesses and the genuine endpoint-opposed path are all original full x-rooted geodesics in the SAME PROPER FACE H, although they need not be one and the same path.
This unifies odd/even parity and gains simultaneous physical endpoint opposition at a cost of exactly ONE additional free cap direction relative to the earlier n−3 coordinate plus central-color packet.
