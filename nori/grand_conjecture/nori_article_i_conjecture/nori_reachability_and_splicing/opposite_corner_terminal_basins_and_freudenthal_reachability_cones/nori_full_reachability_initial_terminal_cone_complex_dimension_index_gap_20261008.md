# NORI no-closure gives a free antipodal low-dimensional path-cone complex and a Borsuk–Ulam index gap

THEOREM (DIMENSION GAP FOR ACTUAL MONOCHROMATIC MEMORY CONES). Fix n>=5 and any active NORI ordered-three-face coloring c. Define the physical-vertex simplicial complex X(c) as the UNION of all Freudenthal witness-suffix cones K_T(y,J) for every terminal y and ordered terminal pair J=(a,b), and all witness-prefix cones K_I(s,J) for every initial s and initial ordered pair J. The vertices are actual vertices of Q_n. Each simplex records comparable physical cube vertices lying on a SINGLE directed monochromatic geodesic whose last two directions (for K_T) or first two directions (for K_I) are the prescribed J. These simplices are genuine faces of the all-root full-geodesic complex K_n, since every partial geodesic extends to a full cube geodesic. Under antipodal reversal Theta, terminal cones transform into initial cones with reversed memory and complemented witness color, so X(c) is a tau-invariant subcomplex of K_n.
Suppose the grand NORI one-switch conjecture FAILS for c. Then no directed monochromatic (n-1)-edge geodesic exists: appending its sole unused dimension to such a path adds at most one ordered-three-face window and gives a full antipodal geodesic with at most one color change. A fortiori no monochromatic n-edge geodesic exists. Consequently every mono path has <=n-2 edges. A terminal or initial length-m witness gives a root-chain Freudenthal simplex with m-1 vertices (after retaining only the physical starting points of suffix paths from terminal J, respectively endpoints of prefixes from initial J), and hence dimension m-2<=n-4. Thus dim X(c)<=n-4.
The antipodal action on X(c) is FREE: the fixed points of tau on |K_n| are exactly midpoints of antipodal cube-vertex edges, whereas no simplex of X(c) can contain such an edge, because its physical path diameter is at most n-4 (when counting only the root/endpoint chain after deleting the fixed 2-direction memory). The quotient X(c)/tau therefore has cohomological dimension <=n-4, so for its cover-class w, w^(n-3)=0. Conversely the punctured all-root complex Y_n=|K_n| with its antipodal-edge midpoints removed contains a small tau-invariant linking sphere S^(n-1) around each removed midpoint, whose quotient is RP^(n-1) with w^(n-1)!=0. Therefore no continuous tau-equivariant map Y_n -> |X(c)| exists under the no-closure hypothesis: restricting to a linking sphere would send the nonzero (n-1)st power of the sign-cover class to zero, a contradiction.
This gives a PRECISE CONDITIONAL TOPOLOGICAL CLOSURE SCHEME. If one can show that absence of a full NORI one-switch geodesic supplies a continuous tau-equivariant map from the high-index all-root complex (or any linking S^(n-1)) into the honest initial/terminal monochromatic witness-cone complex X(c), then grand closure follows immediately by index. The map must arise from coherent root/window exchange or a multivalued carrier/selection theorem; local five-cycle existence alone does not construct it. X(c) itself is a low-dimensional TARGET in this scheme, not a valid standalone high-index Borsuk–Ulam DOMAIN. This explicitly separates the established index obstruction from the still-unproved coherence/selection obligation.
