# Recursive monochromatic antipodal-corner labels on the ordinary cube face poset

Fix any binary undirected edge coloring of Q_n. For each nonempty physical face H with free coordinate set S(H), define its relative antipode on corners by a_H(z)=z XOR 1_(S(H)). For q in {0,1}, let
Lambda_q(H)={z in vertices(H): there is a q-monochromatic shortest path IN H from z to a_H(z)}.
Interpret the length-zero path as belonging to both colors when H is a vertex.

**Theorem 1 (face-label recurrence).** For any positive-dimensional H and corner z of H,
z in Lambda_q(H)
iff there exists i in S(H) with
c({z,z XOR e_i})=q and z XOR e_i in Lambda_q(H_i(z)),
where H_i(z) is the opposite (dim(H)-1)-facet of H with coordinate i fixed to 1-z_i (all other free coordinates of H retained). This is an exact induction on face dimension and retains a genuine shortest-path witness at every step.

Proof. The first edge of any q-geodesic spanning H must flip a free i and be q-colored. All remaining directions are free coordinates of the opposite facet H_i(z), traversed exactly once from z XOR e_i to a_H(z). Conversely prepend the q-edge to a q-geodesic spanning H_i(z). QED.

**Theorem 2 (face antipodes and global oddness).** For ANY undirected edge coloring, Lambda_q(H) is invariant under a_H, by reversing a monochromatic spanning geodesic. If the coloring is antipodally odd on Q_n, with global complement tau(z)=bar z, then
tau(Lambda_q(H))=Lambda_(1-q)(tau H).
For H=Q_n, face antipodality a_H equals tau and tau H=H, so
Lambda_0(Q_n)=Lambda_1(Q_n).
Thus the grand EDGE conjecture reduces to showing that this common full-face corner label set is nonempty.

**Theorem 3 (barycentric-face realization).** In the centrally symmetric monochromatic flag subcomplex A of sd(Q_n), the face-barycenter b_H belongs to a q-witnessed ROOTED FLAG whose root is z exactly when z belongs to Lambda_q(H). More precisely: the flag simplex from {z} to H generated by a q-monochromatic path spanning H contains b_H, and conversely any q-flag containing b_H and rooted at z gives such a path. Thus these corner sets Lambda_q(H) are the exact topological labeling of all barycentric face vertices, with no false convex coincidences. A contains the full-cube barycenter exactly when Lambda_0(Q_n)=Lambda_1(Q_n) is nonempty.

**Counterexample obligations.** In any hypothetical counterexample, Lambda_q(H)=empty on every (n-1)-dimensional facet H and for both colors. Indeed a monochromatic geodesic spanning such a facet has length n-1, and the two possible extensions in its sole missing direction occur at antipodal edges with complementary colors; one necessarily extends the monochromatic geodesic to length n. Hence the facet centers are excluded from A as well as the whole-cube center, even though all physical edge midpoints belong to A.

**Topological proof target.** Show that no antipodally odd coloring can induce face labels Lambda_q satisfying the exact recurrence and involution rules with both full-face labels empty. This is a dimension-independent Sperner/Hex formulation on the ORDINARY cube face lattice. The recurrence, involutions, geometric realization and facet exclusion are proved; the forcing theorem asserting a nonempty full-face label remains open.
