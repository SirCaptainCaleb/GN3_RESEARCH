# Full NORI: exact 2n-belt wall-gap criterion and reversal-twist transport

THEOREM (dimension-independent, unrestricted NORI ordered-three-face coloring). Let n>=4. Given any root x and permutation p of [n], follow the simple 2n-edge cube cycle obtained by running p from x to bar x and then running p again from bar x to x. Its cyclic 2n successive length-three directed windows are actual ordered three-faces: at edge start j put a_j=c(F_j,(p_j,p_(j+1),p_(j+2))) with p repeated and indices modulo 2n. Define b_j=c(F_j,(p_(j+2),p_(j+1),p_j)), reversal-asymmetry t_j=a_j XOR b_j, and transition bits d_j=a_j XOR a_(j+1), all indices modulo 2n. The active NORI axiom c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi) gives a_(j+n)=1-b_j and b_(j+n)=1-a_j. Therefore t_(j+n)=t_j and d_(j+n)=d_j XOR t_j XOR t_(j+1). In particular, for the signed total wall count W=sum_(j=1)^(2n) d_j, let k=# {1<=j<=n:t_j!=t_(j+1)} (with t_(n+1)=t_1) and u=# {1<=j<=n:t_j=t_(j+1),d_j=1}; then EXACTLY W=k+2u, with k even. Thus local reversibility-type boundaries create single unmatched antipodal wall pairs, and matching-type seams contribute zero or two walls. The root slide along the belt rotates all arrays without changing W, k, or u (up to redefining the first n entries; the counts are stable since type has period n and equality-type d pairs match).
ROOTED EXTRACTION. A full n-edge antipodal geodesic based at any of the 2n cycle vertices has n-2 directed three-face windows and n-3 consecutive transition seams. If it starts at belt edge j, its exact NORI change count is D_j=sum_(s=0)^(n-4) d_(j+s). Therefore this belt has a good at-most-one-change full geodesic iff at least one cyclic interval of length L=n-3 in the 2n-bit transition word contains at most one wall. Equivalently, suppose W>0, list wall positions cyclically w_1,...,w_W and positive gaps g_i=w_(i+1)-w_i mod 2n, with sum g_i=2n. Then EVERY full geodesic on this belt is bad (>=2 changes) iff g_i+g_(i+1)<=n-3 for every i (indices modulo W). Indeed the longest seam interval starting immediately after wall w_i and containing at most the next wall has length g_i+g_(i+1)-1; it can reach L precisely when the consecutive-gap sum is >=L+1. The W=0 case immediately gives a monochromatic geodesic.
QUANTITATIVE COROLLARY. In a hypothetical NORI grand counterexample, on EVERY antipodal 2n-belt, the total cyclic triple-window wall count is at least six (and in fact W>=2 ceil(2n/(n-3))). Proof: the 2n intervals of length L each must contain >=2 walls, so (n-3)W>=4n. W is even because it is the total transitions of a cyclic binary word. In particular a belt with W<=4 is an immediate full one-switch NORI certificate, WITHOUT the reversal-even assumption. In terms of reversal twist, a bad belt must obey k+2u>=2 ceil(2n/(n-3)), plus every consecutive wall-gap sum <=n-3. If t identically 0 on a belt, d is n-periodic and the n-seam half-count m is odd; if t identically 1, d is n-periodic with m even. For t mixed, root slide changes the n-seam half-count by 0 or +/-1 only when crossing a reversal-type boundary: m_(j+1)-m_j=d_(j+n)-d_j, so it is zero if t_j=t_(j+1), and +/-1 otherwise.
This gives an exact NORI-wide root-slide defect and type-wall conservation law. It supplies no existence theorem forcing W<=4 or a forbidden wall gap; that is the remaining grand-closure obligation.
