# Exact signed window-shift graph and equivariant zero-crossing formulation of the NORI grand conjecture

For n>=4 define the undirected WINDOW-SHIFT GRAPH H_n^(3). Its vertices are all ordered three-faces (F,pi) of Q_n, where pi=(a,b,c) lists the three distinct free directions in order. Two such vertices are adjacent precisely when they occur as consecutive ordered-three-face windows along some four-step geodesic of Q_n, with direction word (a,b,c,d) and subsequent triple (b,c,d), for four distinct directions. Let tau(F,pi)=(bar F,reverse pi). Antipodally reverse any witnessing four-step geodesic: its ordered windows are exactly tau(w') then tau(w); thus tau is an involutive graph automorphism. It is free on graph vertices, since reversal of three distinct coordinates cannot fix pi. Moreover no vertex w is adjacent to tau(w), because a shift from (a,b,c) to (c,b,a) would require its suffix pair (b,c) to equal the reverse-prefix pair (c,b), impossible for b!=c. Hence tau acts freely on the full geometric realization |H_n^(3)|. For every valid NORI coloring, c(tau w)=1-c(w); its signs s(w)=(-1)^c(w) define an odd PL function on |H_n^(3)|, equivalently a continuous section of the real sign line bundle over |H_n^(3)|/tau. That section has exactly one zero at the midpoint of each window-shift edge joining opposite-colored windows and none on monochromatic shift edges. A full antipodal geodesic with n edges determines its n-2 consecutive ordered three-face windows w_1,...,w_(n-2), giving a path of n-3 edges in this shift graph; the number of its color changes is exactly the number of zeros crossed by this path. Consequently the NORI grand conjecture is EXACTLY the existence, for every tau-odd coloring, of one distinguished full-geodesic window path crossing the zero set at most once. The conjugate geodesic rooted at the same x with reversed coordinate order induces the path tau(w_(n-2)),...,tau(w_1), whose colors are complementary reversed. Unlike in the edge-colored case, these two paths need not concatenate via legal window-shift edges, and the natural 2n cyclic belt has half-period operator alpha(F,pi)=(bar F,pi), different from tau: the bundle's forced nontrivial holonomy does not automatically apply to that belt. Therefore an honest topological closure proof must add root/order-reversing connectors compatible with actual window shifts, or use the two-window splice criteria to extract a good full path. This proves the correct equivariant combinatorial carrier for the actual NORI conjecture; it does not force a path with <=1 zero yet.
