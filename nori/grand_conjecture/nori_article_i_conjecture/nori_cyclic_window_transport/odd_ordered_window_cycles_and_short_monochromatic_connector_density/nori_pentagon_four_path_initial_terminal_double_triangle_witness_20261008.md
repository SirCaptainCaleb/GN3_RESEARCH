# Every monochromatic four-edge NORI connector supplies two compatible root/endpoint simplices meeting at its center

Let c be a binary coloring of ordered three-faces of Q_n, n>=5. For a genuinely monochromatic directed four-edge geodesic P with vertices v0 -> v1 -> v2=z -> v3 -> v4 and four successive distinct coordinate directions (a,b,c,d), its two ordered-three-face window colors coincide in some q. Let K_T(y,J) denote the honestly witnessed Freudenthal TERMINAL-memory cone of monochromatic geodesics ending at y with final pair J, and K_I(s,K) the INITIAL-memory cone of monochromatic geodesics starting s with first pair K.
THEOREM (CERTIFIED TWO-TRIANGLE CONNECTOR). The physical geodesic triangle sigma_T={v0,v1,v2} is a simplex of K_T(v4,(c,d)), certified by the successive suffixes of the one path P after removing its first two directions. The triangle sigma_I={v2,v3,v4} is a simplex of K_I(v0,(a,b)), certified by prefixes of P after removing its final two directions. Both are color-q witnesses of the SAME physical four-edge path and share the actual center vertex v2=z. Thus every monochromatic shift edge between ordered-three-face windows is an honest two-cone, two-triangle connector with a common physical vertex and shared complete witness, rather than merely two agreeing abstract vertex colors.
For any central vertex z and five-element coordinate set W, cyclically arrange W=(p1,...,p5). The five successive centered four-edge paths P_i with directions (p_i,p_(i+1),p_(i+2),p_(i+3)) have roots r_i=z xor {p_i,p_(i+1)}, centers z, and true endpoints s_i=z xor {p_(i+2),p_(i+3)}. Their adjacent ordered-window labels form the genuine centered pentagon. The number of P_i producing these certified two-triangle monochromatic connectors is ALWAYS ODD (1,3,5). In the full active NORI coloring, antipodal reversal Theta(P) reverses the coordinate word and complements all window colors, so the entire connector system is equivariant: a terminal memory cone at one endpoint is sent to an initial memory cone at the antipode of that endpoint, and each certified pair of triangles is sent to its mirrored pair.
LIMITATION: Intersecting two cone REALIZATIONS at one vertex alone does not imply they are both witnessed by one and the same monochromatic four-edge path, or even that their colors agree. The connector must retain the path-witness incidence P. These certified local witnesses now make the pentagon parity available on a root/endpoint cone-incidence complex, potentially supplying the higher-dimensional carrier missing from purely one-dimensional window-shift cohomology. For grand closure, one must propagate/compose these incidences until reversed-tail terminal cones meet at a common physical root with complementary supports. None of the local incidence or parity alone establishes this global forcing.
