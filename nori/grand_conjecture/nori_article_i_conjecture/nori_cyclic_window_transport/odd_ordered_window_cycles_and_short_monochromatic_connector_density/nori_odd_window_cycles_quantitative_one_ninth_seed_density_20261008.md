# Odd-window cycles force a 1/(2k+3) density of monochromatic seeds and good local antipodal geodesics

QUANTITATIVE ODD-CYCLE SEED THEOREM. For any integer k>=1, in every binary coloring of ordered k-dimensional cube faces of a fixed (k+2)-dimensional cube Q_(k+2), at least a fraction 1/(2k+3) of ALL directed geodesics of length k+1 have their TWO consecutive ordered k-face windows the SAME color. Consequently at least a fraction 1/(2k+3) of ALL directed full antipodal (k+2)-edge geodesics have at most one color change among their three ordered k-face windows. No antipodal/reversal axiom is required. In particular every five-dimensional face of ANY NORI ordered-three-face coloring contains at least 1/9 of its directed length-four geodesics as monochromatic two-window seeds, and at least 1/9 of its directed FULL five-edge antipodal geodesics are one-switch good.
PROOF. Let m=k+2 and L=2k+3. There are N=2^m*m! directed geodesics of length k+1 in Q_m, because their root is arbitrary and their k+1 directions form an injection of [m] missing one coordinate. There are also N choices of a root and ordered full list (a1,...,a_m) defining the canonical odd L-step closed window walk, direction word (a1,...,a_m,a2,...,a_m). Each such walk has L consecutive directed length-(k+1) geodesic subpaths, and its odd length forces at least one of those subpaths to have same-colored consecutive ordered-k-face windows. Therefore among all N*L incidences (walk,position), at least N are monochromatic two-window occurrences. The hypercube automorphism group generated by translations and coordinate permutations acts transitively on directed length-(k+1) geodesics, and preserves the family of all N canonical odd walks and their L positions. Consequently every directed length-(k+1) geodesic appears with the SAME multiplicity N*L/N=L among these incidences. If M counts monochromatic two-window geodesics, L*M>=N, giving M/N>=1/L. Each such seed can be completed to a full antipodal geodesic by inserting the sole unused dimension before OR after the path, producing TWO distinct directed full geodesics that remain good because the original two consecutive windows agree and the full word has only three windows. Conversely each directed full geodesic has exactly TWO directed length-(k+1) consecutive subpaths (prefix/suffix). Hence 2M counted completion incidences are borne by at most two incidences per good full geodesic, so at least M good full geodesics exist, of the total N directed full geodesics. This proves the same 1/L density. QED.
This provides a robust, dimension-independent, face-local supply of one-switch (k+2)-dimensional paths. It is NOT a global NORI result in n>k+2: the remaining directions must be absorbed with compatible seam windows, a genuine extraction obligation.
