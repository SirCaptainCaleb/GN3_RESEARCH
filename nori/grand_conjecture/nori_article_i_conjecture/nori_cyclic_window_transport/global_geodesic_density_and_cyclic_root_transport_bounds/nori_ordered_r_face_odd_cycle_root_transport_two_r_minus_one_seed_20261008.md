THEOREM (universal ordered-r odd-cycle root transport). Fix r>=2, n>=2r-1, any binary coloring of actual PHYSICAL ordered r-faces of Q_n, any vertex z, and any 2r-1 distinct directions B. For an ordered r-tuple T let H_z(T) be the literal color of its physical free-r-face through z. Then among all (2r-1)! permutations pi of B, at least (2r-2)! give genuine (2r-1)-edge paths starting at x=z XOR first(pi), along pi, with at most r-2 changes among their r ordered-r-face windows. NO antipodal NORI axiom is used. Proof: partition the permutations into (2r-2)! directed cyclic classes, each of length q=2r-1. For a cycle p_0,...,p_(q-1) consider the q genuine through-z colors h_i=H_z(p_i,...,p_(i+r-1)), indices modulo q. Since q is ODD, not all successive colors can differ, because sum_i(h_i+h_(i+1))=0 in F2 whereas q=1. Choose a cyclic rotation with h_0=h_1. The actual q-edge geodesic rooted at x=z XOR p_0 and following that rotation has its first free-r face through z (p_0 is free), and after its first edge reaches z so its second face is also through z. The first two ordered window colors therefore agree; there are only r-2 remaining adjacency positions, proving the claimed switch bound. The number of equal-color cyclic adjacencies is ODD, since sum_i(1+h_i+h_(i+1))=q=1 mod2. Every cyclic class yields >=1 distinct successful permutation.
