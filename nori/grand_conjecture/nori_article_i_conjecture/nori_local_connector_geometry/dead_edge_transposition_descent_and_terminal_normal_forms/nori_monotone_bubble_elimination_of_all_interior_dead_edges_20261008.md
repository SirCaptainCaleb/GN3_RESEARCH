# Every NORI geodesic has a non-increasing-switch coordinate-swap normal form with no dead edges in its interior

THEOREM (MONOTONE DEAD-EDGE BUBBLE ELIMINATION; ALL DIMENSIONS). Let n>=7 and color the physical ordered-three-faces of Q_n with arbitrary binary colors (no antipodal oddness required). Let M be the set of DEAD cube edges, which form a matching by the previously proved exact rigidity theorem: a cube edge is dead iff no directed monochromatic four-edge geodesic traverses it. Fix any root x and full n-edge directed antipodal geodesic P(x,p), and let D(P) be the number of changes between its consecutive ordered 3-face window colors. Then there exists a FULL directed antipodal geodesic P'(x,p') FROM THE SAME ROOT x such that
 (i) D(P')<=D(P),
 (ii) NO physical path edge in positions 4,5,...,n-3 is dead.
The new permutation p' is obtained constructively by adjacent swaps and preserves the set of used coordinate directions. Thus every starting root has a switch-minimal geodesic, relative to that root, whose dead physical edges all occur within the first THREE or last THREE positions. This yields a genuine variational normal form for the active NORI conjecture, stronger than merely finding a dead-edge-free geodesic from every root with no guarantee on switch number.
PROOF. Suppose the current geodesic has a dead edge in an interior position k with 4<=k<=n-3, and denote its coordinate by i and the subsequent different coordinate by j. Swap these two consecutive directions in the permutation, without changing the root. The proved DEAD-EDGE SWAP THEOREM gives D(new)<=D(old). The two physical edges affected are the old dead i-edge e={z,z xor i}, and the old following j-edge from z xor i to z xor i xor j; the latter cannot be dead because it shares an endpoint with e. In the swapped path, the new j-edge runs from z to z xor j, which ALSO shares z with e and hence cannot be dead. The new i-edge runs from z xor j to z xor j xor i. It may be dead, but it now sits at position k+1 rather than k. All other path edges are physically unchanged because swapping commuting cube flips leaves the vertices before and after the two-edge square unchanged.
Define the NONNEGATIVE integer potential Phi(P)=sum_(k=4)^(n-3) (n-2-k)*1{the edge at position k is dead}. Every above right swap strictly decreases Phi: it removes the dead position k, and either replaces it by a dead position k+1 with weight exactly one smaller (if k+1 remains interior), or by no new interior dead position. The new j-edge is live and no other edge's dead status changes. Thus repeated swaps terminate when Phi=0, which is exactly condition (ii). At each move the global change count D never increases, proving (i).
The result does NOT force <=1 switch: dead edges may still occupy boundary positions, and even a path with NO dead edges can have several color switches because its separate local four-geodesic witnesses may be incompatible. The next forcing target is to strengthen the normal form into a compatibility-preserving elimination of SWITCH BOUNDARIES or a reversed-tail common-reachability collision.
