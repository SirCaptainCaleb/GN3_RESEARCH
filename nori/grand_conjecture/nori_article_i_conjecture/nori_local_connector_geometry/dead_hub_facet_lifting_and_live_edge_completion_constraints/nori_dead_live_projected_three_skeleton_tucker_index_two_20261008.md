# For every direction, the genuine live-edge projected three-skeleton carrier has antipodal cohomological index at least two

THEOREM (UNCONDITIONAL HIGHER-DIMENSIONAL TOPOLOGY OF GENUINE MONOCHROMATIC FOUR-PATH COVERAGE). Let n>=5 and c be an ACTIVE NORI coloring of physical ordered 3-faces of Q_n, c(bar F,rev pi)=1-c(F,pi). Fix ANY coordinate direction i, let m=n-1>=4 and identify the 2^m physical i-parallel cube edges with projected cube vertices v∈Q_m. Let D⊆Q_m be the projected DEAD positions: their actual i-edges are not traversed by ANY monochromatic directed 4-edge geodesic. Let L=Q_m\D be LIVE positions: their physical i-edges have GENUINE monochromatic 4-edge witnesses.
For each dead v∈D, the exact dead-edge rigidity lemma supplies one binary bit t(v). Its two key properties are (A) if v,w∈D have Hamming distance <=3, then t(v)=t(w); (B) t(bar v)=1-t(v) from active NORI physical antipodal reversal. Property (A) follows because a 3-dimensional face using the differing exterior coordinates and avoiding i contains hubs of BOTH dead edges (and the remaining free coords may be chosen as necessary); its color equals each dead rigid template bit. No two opposite t dead vertices can occur in one projected 3-cube.
Let C_m be the THREE-DIMENSIONAL CUBICAL SKELETON of the boundary ∂[0,1]^m (for m=4, its entire boundary S^3). Endow each 3-cube and its lower-dimensional subfaces with the standard staircase/Freudenthal triangulation, using the same global order of the m coordinates. This triangulation is globally consistent and equivariant under central inversion τ(v)=bar v: coordinatewise complementation sends each increasing chain simplex to an increasing chain simplex with the vertex order reversed. Write X for the resulting free finite antipodal simplicial complex of dimension3. Its quotient X/τ is the 3-skeleton of the cubical CW decomposition of ∂[0,1]^m/τ ≅ RP^(m-1). The covering class w∈H^1(X/τ;F2) satisfies w^3≠0: the standard nonzero third power in RP^(m-1) restricts nontrivially to its 3-skeleton, since restriction of cellular cohomology in degree3 to the 3-skeleton is injective.
Assign to each projected cube vertex v the REAL scalar f(v)=+1 when v dead and t(v)=0, f(v)=−1 when v dead and t(v)=1, and f(v)=0 when v live. Then f(bar v)=−f(v). Extend f affinely over every simplex of X to obtain a continuous τ-ODD PL function F:|X|→R. Every simplex of X lies in a projected cube face of dimension <=3, so all its dead vertices have the SAME nonzero sign, by local rigidity property(A). Consequently F vanishes on a simplex PRECISELY on its face spanned by the live vertices. Thus its FULL ZERO LOCUS is the honest induced/live-vertex SUBCOMPLEX

where X[L] consists of all Freudenthal simplices with every vertex in L. There are NO spurious barycentric cancellations between oppositely signed dead hubs: those dead vertices cannot cohabit a 3-face.
By the standard odd-real-function ZERO-LOCUS INDEX theorem (also used in NORI's permutohedral endpoint-zero theorem), w1(Z/τ)^(3-1)=w1(Z/τ)^2≠0 whenever w1(X/τ)^3≠0. Hence
  BOXED: ind_Z2(X[L])>=2
FOR EVERY direction i in EVERY active NORI coloring and all n>=5.
GEOMETRIC EXTRACTION. Since w1^2≠0, some connected component of X[L] is invariant under τ: if every component were exchanged in pairs, choosing a sign on one from each pair would define an equivariant map X[L]→S^0, making w1=0. Take a live projected vertex v in such a component. Its antipode bar v lies in the same component, joined by a path in the simplicial 1-skeleton of X[L]. Every simplicial edge is contained in a physical projected 3-dimensional cube face, hence connects TWO ACTUAL live positions at Hamming distance at most 3. Thus for each direction i there exists an ANTIPODAL CHAIN v=v0,v1,...,vr=bar v of real parallel i-edges, each with a genuine monochromatic four-edge witness and with d_H(vj,vj+1)<=3. This holds even though the ordinary live graph Q_m[L] can be disconnected, as witnessed by existing explicit NORI colorings.
SHARPNESS OF AVAILABLE LOCAL TOPOLOGY. The method uses facewise rigidity up to THREE exterior-coordinate differences; it does NOT extend automatically to the 4-skeleton because dead vertices at projected distance4 can legitimately carry opposite rigid colors. The produced chain moves by up to three cube directions per step and does NOT automatically preserve one ordered-two-direction terminal memory, witness color, or a single directed geodesic. Consequently it is a rigorous *topological global connector carrier*, not full one-switch NORI closure. The remaining combinatorial step is to extract compatible witness/path transport from this nonzero 2-dimensional antipodal index.
