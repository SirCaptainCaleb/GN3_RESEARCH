# In Q9 a hypothetical NORI counterexample with a uniform seven-face hub must possess a centered same-color monochromatic near-spanning core

THEOREM (Q9 HUB-COLOR BRANCH FORCED BY THE ACTUAL NORI CAP PIVOT). Fix an ACTIVE NORI ordered-three-face coloring of Q9, c(bar F,reverse pi)=1-c(F,pi). Let U be ANY seven-coordinate set, with its two missing directions {a,b}, and let z be a physical cube vertex such that ALL physical ordered three-faces containing z with free triple in U have one uniform bit t. Then at least one of the following holds: (I) the NORI grand conclusion already holds, namely a full 9-edge antipodal geodesic with <=1 ordered-window color switch; or (II) there is a monochromatic 7-edge U-geodesic P with color EXACTLY t whose first THREE directions are the three coordinates of S={u in U:x_u !=z_u} for its starting root x (so |S|=3), and whose first three steps arrive at the uniform hub z. In particular the root projected onto U has Hamming rank3 about z.
PROOF. Apply the proved uniform ordered-k-face hub root-layer dichotomy for k=3 in the physical U-facet through z. If branch A holds, its centered same-color mono7 path gives II. Otherwise, branch B says that for EVERY U-root x with four U-coordinate differences S relative to z, and for EVERY distinct f,l∈S, there exists a monochromatic seven-edge path from x to x xor U of color 1-t, with FIRST direction f and LAST direction l. Indeed the dichotomy supplies all orders (S minus{l}, U minus S, l), and freely permuting the three first directions realizes any f!=l in S. Choose two distinct f,l in S: the paths with first/last f->l and l->f are BOTH monochromatic of the SAME color 1-t, with identical projected root and endpoint. They occur in the SAME physical U-facet. The previously proved first-last pivot theorem for active NORI says such matching first/last memories with SAME monochromatic color force a FULL one-switch 9-edge antipodal geodesic. Thus branch B implies I. In the absence of I, A must hold, proving the theorem.
ADDITIONAL CAP RESTRICTION IN NO-CLOSURE CASE. The centered t-mono path P uses an initial set S of three U-directions and a complement R=U minus S of four directions. Permuting just the first three directions of P preserves all its physical ordered three-face window colors: its first four windows contain z and are uniformly t, while its fifth and last ordered window begins after the fourth direction and is unchanged. Thus for EVERY f∈S there exists a mono7 U-geodesic rooted at the SAME x, colored t, with FIRST direction f, while the LAST direction l∈R is fixed. Applying the four-facet cap theorem under hypothetical failure yields c(F(x;{a,b,f}),(a,b,f))=t for ALL f∈S, and c(F(x;{a,b,l}),(a,b,l))=1-t. Both cap colors are independent of the omitted a,b bits. This is a genuine root-coupled three-versus-one cap polarity imposed near an otherwise uniform hub.
APPLICATION TO DEAD EDGES. In Q9 any dead physical edge i at z forces every ordered three-face through z using directions other than i to be uniform t. Thus for each j!=i, choosing U=[9] minus{i,j}, the present theorem says grand closure holds OR a centered uniform-t monochromatic seven-edge core exists in the physical U-facet through z, rooted at projected Hamming distance3 from z. Its antipodal mate carries the reversed complementary-color core in the opposite full cube facet. This is a strong new necessary configuration for a hypothetical Q9 counterexample with a dead edge, not a proof that such a configuration is impossible.
