# Universal (2k+1)-cube monochromatic hub-extension theorem for ordered k-face colorings

THEOREM (TWO-SHELL MONOCHROMATIC HUB EXTENSION, ALL WINDOW ORDERS k>=1). Let k>=1 and d=2k+1. Color all physical ORDERED k-dimensional faces of Q_d arbitrarily with two colors, with NO antipodal condition. Suppose there is a cube vertex z and a bit t such that EVERY ordered k-face containing z has color t, independently of its distinct free-direction order. Then Q_d contains a FULL MONOCHROMATIC antipodal geodesic of length d=2k+1.
PROOF by contradiction. Assume there is no mono full d-geodesic. Index the d coordinate directions by a set D. First derive a FORCED ONE-SHELL RULE: for every s in D and every ordered list (a1,...,ak) of k distinct directions from D minus{s}, the physical ordered k-face through z xor s with free directions (a1,...,ak) has color 1-t. To see this, write (u1,...,uk) for the remaining k directions of D minus ({s} union {a1,...,ak}). Consider the full d-edge geodesic with ordered direction word (u1,...,uk,s,a1,...,ak), rooted at z xor {u1,...,uk}. After k edges the path visits z. Its first 2k edges have k+1 consecutive ordered k-face windows, all containing z and therefore t: their window start indices 1,...,k+1 have their vertex-index intervals including the central time k. The FINAL kth-window, with ordered free directions (a1,...,ak), is the window beginning immediately after the additional s-step, so its physical face contains z xor s. Since the entire path is not monochromatic by hypothesis, this last window has color 1-t. This proves the universal one-shell rule.
Choose any r in D and put y=z xor r. Every ordered k-face through y using only directions from D minus {r} now has color 1-t by the one-shell rule. Choose an arbitrary permutation (p1,...,p_(2k)) of D minus {r}, start at y xor {p1,...,pk}, and traverse all 2k directions in that order. The path meets y after k edges, and each of its k+1 ordered k-windows contains y, so all are 1-t. Append r as the final (2k+1)th edge. The new last k-face window has ordered free directions (p_(k+2),...,p_(2k),r) and contains z xor p_(k+1): it begins at y xor p_(k+1), and r is free, so flipping r enters z xor p_(k+1). Its ordered directions avoid p_(k+1), hence the one-shell rule at s=p_(k+1) makes its color 1-t. Thus the FULL antipodal (2k+1)-edge path has ALL k+2 windows colored 1-t, contradiction. QED.
GENERAL EMBEDDING. In an ambient ordered k-face colored Q_n with n>=2k+1, if a vertex z has uniform color t on every ordered k-face whose free directions lie in a chosen (2k+1)-element set D, then the physical D-coordinate face through z contains a monochromatic full D-geodesic, with no restrictions on the other fixed exterior bits. This is a fully dimension-independent hub-to-spanning lemma; it extends the previously known simple hub construction of monochromatic 2k-edge geodesics by ONE EXTRA edge via a nontrivial shared-face shell argument. In NORI k=3 it upgrades a constant-color seven-direction physical hub to an actual monochromatic SEVEN-edge geodesic.
