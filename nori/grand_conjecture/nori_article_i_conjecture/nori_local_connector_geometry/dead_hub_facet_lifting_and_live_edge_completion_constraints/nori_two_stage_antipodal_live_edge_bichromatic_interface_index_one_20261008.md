# Two-stage Borsuk–Ulam dichotomy: a doubly monochromatic i-edge or an antipodally connected mixed-color four-path interface

THEOREM (TWO-STAGE EQUIVARIANT MONOCHROMATIC CONNECTOR). Let n>=5 and c be any active NORI antipodally reversal-odd coloring of physical ordered 3-faces. Fix any cube coordinate i and let projected m=n-1 cube positions index physical i-parallel edges. Let L_i be those projected positions whose i-edges are traversed by at least one GENUINE monochromatic directed 4-edge geodesic. At each v∈L_i let K_i(v)⊆{0,1} be the set of monochromatic window colors of these ACTUAL path certificates. It is nonempty and satisfies K_i(bar v)=1−K_i(v) by physical antipodal reversal. Let X_i be the genuine live-vertex Freudenthal subcomplex of the equivariant triangulated 3-skeleton of ∂[0,1]^m. The preceding proved 3-skeleton theorem gives ind_Z2(X_i)>=2.
Then one of TWO genuinely physical alternatives holds:
(A) There exists a physical i-parallel cube edge traversed by TWO (possibly differently rooted and ordered) genuinely monochromatic 4-edge geodesics of OPPOSITE window colors; equivalently K_i(v)={0,1} for some live v.
(B) All K_i(v) are singleton colors {q_i(v)}. Then q_i:V(X_i)→{0,1} satisfies q_i(bar v)=1−q_i(v). Interpolate g_i(v)=(-1)^{q_i(v)} affinely on the Freudenthal simplices of X_i, giving an ODD continuous PL real function G_i:|X_i|→R. Its zero locus Y_i=G_i^{-1}(0) has equivariant cohomological index ind_Z2(Y_i)>=1 (since ind_Z2(X_i)>=2 by the standard real-odd-map zero-index drop theorem).
Therefore Y_i has a connected component invariant under projected antipodality. Every simplex meeting Y_i has ACTUAL LIVE i-edge positions among its vertices with BOTH monochromatic certificate colors 0 and1, for otherwise G_i would have constant sign there. Each such simplex lies inside a physical projected 3-dimensional cube face, so its differently colored genuine i-edge witnesses are at Hamming distance at most3. A chain of adjacent zero-locus simplices joining a point of Y_i to its antipode yields an antipodally coherent continuous interface comprised of literal opposite-color mono4 path certificates on nearby physical i-edges. This is stronger than existence of a single color boundary.
PROOF. If (A) fails, every live i-edge has a unique mono4 witness color. Applying Θ to its witnessing 4-geodesic gives a 4-geodesic traversing the antipodal i-edge, with ALL ordered-face window colors complemented, proving q_i(bar v)=1−q_i(v). The unique-color scalar extends continuously and oddly over the antipodal simplicial complex X_i. Apply the same relative cup-product zero-locus theorem previously used in the permutohedron and dead/live three-skeleton: w1(X_i/τ)^2≠0 forces w1(Y_i/τ)≠0. A nonzero first cover class implies at least one invariant connected component, as otherwise all connected components would come in switched pairs and permit an equivariant map Y_i→S^0. On each zero simplex its live vertices include both signs, and every vertex has real witness color, proving (B).
LIMITATION. The geometric interface points of Y_i are interpolation points, not actual cube vertices or monochromatic paths. What is CERTIFIED is the proximity and colors of actual witness edge pairs in each supporting 3-face and their antipodally coherent incidence. The interfaces DO NOT automatically yield a single root-monochromatic connector or an at-most-one-switch full n-geodesic; that physical memory-compatibility step is the remaining grand NORI frontier. In particular the alternative (A) here refers to opposite-color 4-geodesics traversing the same physical i-edge, which is slightly broader than opposite-color CENTER-SQUARE certificates of the edge-shadow alternative A.
