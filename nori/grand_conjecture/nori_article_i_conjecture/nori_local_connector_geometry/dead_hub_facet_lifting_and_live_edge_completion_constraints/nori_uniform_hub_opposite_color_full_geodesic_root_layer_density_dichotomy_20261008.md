# Uniform ordered k-face hubs either give a centered mono spanning geodesic or certify an entire opposite-color root layer

THEOREM (UNIFORM HUB ROOT-LAYER DICHOTOMY AND QUANTITATIVE CERTIFICATION). Let k>=1, d=2k+1, and color ordered physical k-faces of Q_d with bits. Assume EVERY ordered k-face through vertex z has a common color t. Exactly one OR BOTH of the following assertions hold:
(A) There exists a full d-edge monochromatic geodesic of color t rooted at SOME vertex x of distance k from z and using, as its first k directions, precisely the k coordinates where x differs from z. Thus its first k edges enter z and its first k+1 windows contain z.
(B) For EVERY root x with Hamming distance d(x,z)=k+1, there are AT LEAST (k+1)(k!)^2 DISTINCT full d-edge monochromatic antipodal geodesics rooted at x, ALL of color 1-t.
PROOF. If (A) holds, stop. Suppose (A) fails. Fix a coordinate s and any ordered k-tuple T=(a1,...,ak) of distinct directions avoiding s. Let U consist of the other k directions. The full geodesic with direction order (U,s,T), rooted at z xor U, enters z after k edges, and its first k+1 ordered k-face windows have color t. Since (A) fails, the final window, which is the ordered physical face through z xor s of order T, has color 1-t. Therefore the ENTIRE rank-one shell satisfies: at every neighbor z xor s, every ordered k-face whose free directions avoid s is colored 1-t.
Now fix a rank-(k+1) root x=z xor S, |S|=k+1. Choose ANY r∈S, ANY ordering of the k coordinates of S minus{r}, and ANY ordering of the k coordinates of D minus S. The resulting d-coordinate order (S minus{r} in chosen order, D minus S in chosen order, r) defines a full antipodal d-edge geodesic from x. After the first k steps the path reaches y=z xor r. Its first 2k edges use directions other than r and are centered at y, so all first k+1 ordered k-face windows contain y and have color 1-t by the shell rule. Its last ordered k-face starts after the next direction s (the first of D minus S), has free directions from the last k-1 unused non-r coordinates followed by r, and contains z xor s because r is free. By the shell rule at z xor s, that final window is ALSO 1-t. Thus the full geodesic is monochromatic 1-t. Distinct choices of r, of the k! first-block orders, and of the k! second-block orders give distinct ordered direction permutations, hence at least (k+1)(k!)^2 full monochromatic geodesics at EACH of the binom(2k+1,k+1) roots x on the complete rank-(k+1) sphere around z. QED.
NORI APPLICATION. With k=3, d=7, a hub uniform on all 3-faces of a chosen 7-dimensional physical coordinate face yields either a full mono 7-path centered at z with root distance3 and color t, or at least 4*(3!)^2=144 mono 7-paths of the opposite color from EACH of binom(7,4)=35 distinct rank-four roots. In total the latter case certifies at least 5040 distinct full ordered 7-face geodesics. Every globally DEAD cube edge induces such a uniform star at both of its endpoint hubs on every 7-coordinate subsystem avoiding its dead direction, giving an explicit rank-five monochromatic terminal-memory reachability layer in the second branch.
This strengthens a mere one-path witness to an all-roots-on-a-Hamming-sphere alternative, retaining real root identities and ordered paths for a future topological nerve construction. It does NOT assert the (A) and (B) cases are mutually exclusive, and it does not alone supply full-n complement support collisions when n>2k+1.
