# Two-neighbor covering theorem: each live parallel-edge position has at least two live projected neighbors

THEOREM (SHARP LOCAL HYPERCUBE HELLY FILLING FOR DEAD EDGE HUBS). Let n>=5 and c be ANY binary coloring of actual physical ordered 3-faces of Q_n, with no antipodal constraint. Fix coordinate i and identify its 2^(n-1) parallel physical cube edges with vertices of a projected Q_m, m=n-1. Let D_i be projected positions of DEAD i-edges (not traversed by any monochromatic directed 4-edge geodesic), L_i its complement. Then the induced projected graph Q_m[L_i] has MINIMUM DEGREE AT LEAST TWO, if nonempty. Equivalently, if at least m-1 of the m neighbors of a projected position v lie in D_i, then v itself is DEAD.
PROOF. Each projected dead position u has an exact rigid local color bit t(u): at either endpoint hub of its i-edge, every ordered physical 3-face omitting i or having i in the MIDDLE has color t(u), while every ordered face with i in an END position has color 1-t(u). Any two distinct dead neighbors u=v xor e_j and w=v xor e_k of v lie at projected Hamming distance2, so they necessarily have the SAME rigidity bit t, since an ordered 3-face through both hubs with free coordinates {i,j,k} and i middle forces equality. Suppose all except at most ONE of the projected neighbors of v are dead. Consider either physical hub endpoint z on the i-edge at v and ANY ordered 3-face through z. Its free triple includes at least TWO directions outside i. Since there is at most one missing neighbor direction, at least one free exterior direction j has v xor e_j DEAD. The physical ordered face through z ALSO contains the corresponding endpoint of that dead i-edge, and therefore has color t if i is absent or middle, and 1-t if i is at an end. This rigid color template holds for EVERY ordered 3-face through BOTH physical endpoints of the central i-edge. The established converse of the dead-edge local-rigidity lemma says this is SUFFICIENT for the center edge itself to be dead: every four-step geodesic containing i must cross between two opposite forced colors. Therefore v∈D_i, contradiction to its being live.
Thus each live projected vertex has at least two live projected neighbors. For active NORI the sets D_i,L_i are antipodally invariant. The threshold is OPTIMAL: the separate explicit live-square/dead-boundary construction for n>=9 gives live i-edges with exactly two live projected neighbors, so the conclusion cannot be universally improved to min-degree3 using even active NORI oddness. This is a real rank-two local filling theorem for physical ordered-face labels, not a connectivity or global one-switch closure theorem.
