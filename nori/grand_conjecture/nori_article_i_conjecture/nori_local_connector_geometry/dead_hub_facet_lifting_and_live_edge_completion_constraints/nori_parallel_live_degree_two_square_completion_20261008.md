# Every degree-two live parallel-edge position forces the fourth vertex of its projected square to be live

THEOREM (FORCED SQUARE FILLING OF MINIMAL LIVE DEGREE). Let n>=5 and color all physical ORDERED 3-faces of Q_n with bits, no oddness required. Fix direction i and identify its physical parallel i-edges with vertices of projected Q_m, m=n-1. Let D_i be projected positions of DEAD i-edges (none lies on a genuinely monochromatic directed 4-edge cube geodesic); let L_i be their complement. The dead-edge local-rigidity theorem gives each u in D_i a bit t(u) such that at both physical endpoints of its i-edge, EVERY ordered triple color is t(u) if i is ABSENT or MIDDLE, and 1-t(u) if i is FIRST or LAST. For any dead u,v at projected Hamming distance <=3, t(u)=t(v), by a common physical 3-face through both hubs avoiding i if the distance is3, or containing i with middle i if distance<=2.
Suppose v∈L_i has exactly TWO live projected neighbors v xor a, v xor b, in two distinct directions a,b outside i; all its other m-2 neighbors v xor j are dead. Then its diagonal w=v xor a xor b MUST ALSO BE LIVE.
PROOF. Assume w dead. All dead neighbors v xor j (j notin{a,b}) have common t because any two differ in at most2 exterior coordinates. The diagonal w is at projected distance3 from any v xor j, and is also dead; hence its rigid bit is likewise t. Consider either hub endpoint z of the central i-edge at v, and any genuine ordered physical 3-face F through z with free ordered coordinate triple pi.
- If i is absent, the 3 free directions contain some j notin{a,b}; the physical face F also contains the corresponding endpoint of dead i-edge at v xor j, so c(F,pi)=t.
- If i is present, the two other free directions include some j notin{a,b}, unless they are exactly {a,b}. In the former case F contains the dead hub at v xor j, and the dead template gives c(F,pi)=t when i is MIDDLE and 1-t when i is an END.
- If its free directions are exactly {i,a,b}, then F contains the diagonal dead hub w, whose template uses the SAME bit t, giving the identical positional formula.
Therefore EVERY actual ordered 3-face through either endpoint of the central i-edge satisfies the dead-edge local template with bit t. The established converse of the local rigidity theorem says these conditions imply the central i-edge is DEAD, contradicting v∈L_i. Hence w live. QED.
COROLLARY. Every vertex of degree exactly2 in the induced projected graph Q_m[L_i] lies on a complete physical 4-cycle (projected coordinate square) contained in L_i. Thus a connected component isomorphic to a chordless cycle of length6 or greater, with all vertices degree2, is impossible as a live-carrier component: at each vertex its two neighbors in the cycle would force a square whose fourth vertex cannot belong to an induced chordless longer cycle. A connected component where every vertex has degree2 must be an ordinary projected 4-cycle. In the active NORI case the square-filling rule holds simultaneously in each direction and is stable under antipodal reversal. It is not sufficient for long-range connectivity or grand one-switch closure: explicit valid NORI colorings have isolated live-square components in n>=9.
