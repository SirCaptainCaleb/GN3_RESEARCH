# Two-bit cubical Tucker forces two physical-square-neighbor hubs with actual opposite-color, opposite-middle-orientation monochromatic four-paths

THEOREM (EXACT TWO-BIT TUCKER PAIR FROM ACTUAL CENTERED MONOCHROMATIC FOUR-GEODESICS). Let n>=5 and c satisfy active NORI c(bar F,rev pi)=1-c(F,pi). Fix any total order of coordinate directions. For EVERY physical cube vertex z, the universal centered odd-five-cycle theorem guarantees the existence of at least one genuinely monochromatic directed 4-edge geodesic P_z with CENTER vertex v_2=z, using four distinct ordered directions p1(z),p2(z),p3(z),p4(z) and a common color q(z) on its two ordered 3-face windows. Choose one such actual centered path for one vertex from each antipodal pair {z,bar z}, and at the partner choose P_(bar z)=ΘP_z, its physical antipodal complement with REVERSED ordered direction list. This is always possible and guarantees q(bar z)=1-q(z), and (p2(bar z),p3(bar z))=(p3(z),p2(z)).
Give each cube vertex a TWO-BIT signed label
  L(z)=(epsilon(z),omega(z))∈{−1,+1}²,
where epsilon(z)=(-1)^{q(z)}, and omega(z)=+1 if p2(z)<p3(z) in the fixed total coordinate order and omega(z)=−1 otherwise. Both components reverse sign under physical antipodality: L(bar z)=−L(z).
THEOREM CONCLUSION. There exist two distinct ACTUAL physical cube vertices z,w lying in the SAME physical two-dimensional cube face (so d_H(z,w)∈{1,2}) such that
  q(z)=1−q(w)
AND simultaneously the ordered central middle pairs have OPPOSITE total-order orientation:
  [p2(z)<p3(z)] != [p2(w)<p3(w)].
This is guaranteed for EVERY antipodally equivariant selection P_z of actual monochromatic centered 4-paths, not just some favorable selection. The selected P_z,P_w are real rooted physical path witnesses with opposite color and opposite orientation bits.
PROOF VIA THE TWO-SKELETON. Let X be the Freudenthal/staircase triangulation of the TWO-dimensional cubical skeleton of ∂[0,1]^n, n>=5, with its natural free vertex complement antipodal involution. The quotient is a 2-dimensional CW skeleton of RP^(n−1), so the classifying double-cover cohomology class u obeys u²!=0 in H²(X/τ;F2). Extend the two-coordinate vertex function L linearly over simplices to obtain continuous τ-ODD F:|X|→R². If F were nowhere zero, its normalization would be an equivariant map X→S¹, forcing u²=0, contradiction. Hence F vanishes on a simplex σ. Every vertex vector belongs to the FOUR corners {±1}² of a square. A set of at most three such corners whose convex hull contains the ORIGIN MUST contain two ANTIPODAL corners: without an antipodal pair at most two adjacent corners can be chosen, whose convex hull avoids0. Thus two ACTUAL simplex vertices z,w have L(w)=−L(z). A Freudenthal simplex of the 2-skeleton lies inside one true physical coordinate square, giving d_H(z,w)<=2. The label formula gives the two physical conclusions. QED.
CRUCIAL GENERALIZATION/BOUNDARY. The exact extraction of a literal complementary ACTUAL label pair from an affine zero is special to TWO sign coordinates on a two-dimensional simplex. With THREE or more sign bits, the origin can lie in a simplex whose hypercube-corner labels contain NO pair of complete complementary strings (e.g. +++,+−−,−+−,−−+). This explains why naive multidimensional bit-string Tucker may produce only a neutral simplex, whereas the present two-bit result yields a real pair of witnessed NORI paths.
LIMITATION. Opposite orientation here means the SIGN of the ordered middle pair relative to a fixed global coordinate-name order. It does NOT assert the two middle pairs use the SAME two coordinate directions, nor that their physical middle squares meet or their starting roots agree. It is a rigorous low-dimensional, root-coupled local connector theorem, not a full antipodal one-switch geodesic.
