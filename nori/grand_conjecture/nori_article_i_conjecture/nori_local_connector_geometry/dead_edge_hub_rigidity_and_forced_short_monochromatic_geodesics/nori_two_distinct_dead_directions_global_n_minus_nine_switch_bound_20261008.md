# Two dead-edge directions yield a full antipodal NORI geodesic with at most n−9 changes in every dimension n≥10

THEOREM (DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT TWO-HUB QUANTITATIVE BOUND). Let n>=10 and let c satisfy active NORI antipodal-reversal oddness. Suppose there are DEAD cube edges in two DISTINCT coordinate directions i,j, i.e. neither is traversed by any monochromatic four-edge ordered-three-face geodesic. Then there exists a FULL antipodal n-edge cube geodesic whose ordered-three-face color word has at most n-9 color changes. For n=10 this is the FULL grand one-switch conclusion. The n=8,9 mixed-direction situation was separately resolved (indeed monochromatic for n=9; n=8 mixed dead directions are impossible).
PROOF. Pick a dead i-edge and a dead j-edge, replacing the latter by its antipodal partner if necessary to minimize their endpoint distance. Because the edges' directions differ, we can match their i,j bits by choosing endpoints z,w, leaving difference set T of size d among the n-2 other coordinates. The proven dead-direction separation plus its antipodal counterpart gives 3<=d<=floor((n-2)/2). In particular n-d>=6 for n>=10. Let R=[n] without T. Choose any three distinct directions A=(j,a,b) within R not containing i (possible because |R|>=6). Let B be the ordered list of all other R directions, ending wherever convenient, so i in B and |B|=ell=n-d-3>=3. Build a full n-edge geodesic starting at x=z xor {j,a,b}, then run three directions A to z, then all d directions of T to w, then ell directions of B to bar x. The first four ordered triple windows all contain z and avoid i, and therefore ALL have one color t_i forced by dead-edge rigidity. Exactly four consecutive later windows centered at w, with window starts d,d+1,d+2,d+3 (index origin at initial vertex), avoid j and have a single color t_j. These two four-window blocks are separated by max(d-4,0) intervening windows; if d=3, they overlap in one physical window, forcing t_i=t_j. There remain ell-3 terminal windows after the four-window block around w, of arbitrary colors. Hence for d>=4 the total change count is at most (d-4+1)+(ell-3)=d+ell-6=n-9. For d=3 the common hub color makes the anchored blocks a single constant-color run and the only changes can occur in the ell-3 arbitrary terminal windows, totaling ell-3=n-9. Therefore the desired full geodesic exists.
This bound uses genuinely physical ordered-face labels, not edge colors. It is conditional on dead edges in two directions, and for n>10 is not the grand <=1 conclusion. It provides a new dimension-independent way to amplify TWO monochromatic local singularities into a near-spanning, globally rooted path with a quantified small defect. For dimensions 10<=n<=28, n-9 is strictly better than the earlier unconditional floor(4(n-3)/5) bound on the switch number; it can tie or become weaker at larger n.
