# Unique-color NORI square certificates force complete incoming/outgoing saturation except for one aligned outer direction

THEOREM (EXACT CERTIFIED MIDDLE-PAIR SATURATION). Let n>=5 and let c be ANY binary coloring of physical ordered three-faces. Fix cube vertex z and ordered distinct middle directions (b,c). Let O=[n] minus {b,c}. For every outer direction a in O define the INCOMING ordered-window bit A_a=c(F(z;{a,b,c}),(a,b,c)); for every outer d in O define the OUTGOING bit B_d=c(F(z;{b,c,d}),(b,c,d)). A monochromatic four-geodesic centered at z with direction word (a,b,c,d) exists exactly when a!=d and A_a=B_d; its color is the common bit. Suppose there exists a certified connector of color q, but there is NO connector of opposite color 1-q at this same middle pair and hub (in particular this applies whenever the physical square has a singleton edge-shadow certificate in NORI's case B).
Then EXACTLY ONE OR MORE of these three saturation descriptions holds: (I) every incoming A_a equals q; (II) every outgoing B_d equals q; (III) there is a UNIQUE common exceptional outer direction t such that A_t=B_t=1-q, while A_a=B_d=q for every a,d different from t. Equivalently, at least one of the two windows' outer-entry families is identically q, unless their only 1-q entries are the SAME single outer direction. PROOF: Let X={a:A_a=1-q} and Y={d:B_d=1-q}. Any two DISTINCT indices a in X,d in Y would give an actual opposite-color centered four-edge certificate, forbidden by hypothesis. Consequently X or Y is empty, or X=Y={t}; this is the complete classification. There is no other possibility. In case X=Y={t}, all other incoming/outgoing bits necessarily equal q, giving III. The existence of one q-connector excludes a degenerate all-(1-q) pattern.

Thus in the absence of a shared physical edge carrying opposite-color square certificates, the certified middle-pair states form a genuine globally antipodal-reversing INCOMING/OUTGOING SATURATION FIELD, with only a sharply classified one-coordinate fault at any state.
