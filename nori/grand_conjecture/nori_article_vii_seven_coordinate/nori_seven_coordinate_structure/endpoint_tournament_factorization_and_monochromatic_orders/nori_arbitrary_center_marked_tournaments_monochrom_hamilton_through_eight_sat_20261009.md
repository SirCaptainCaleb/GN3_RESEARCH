# Arbitrary tournament center signs: monochromatic spanning orders through eight directions by exact SAT

Finite SAT theorem, conditional on exact solver: For n=4,5,6,7,8, every tournament t on [n] and every arbitrary marking s:[n]->F_2 admit a permutation p for which all consecutive triple labels h(a,b,c)=t(a,c)+s(b) are equal. The tournament satisfies t(c,a)=1+t(a,c). Consequently any coloring of ordered physical 3-faces on Q_n (n<=8) whose restriction to one central exterior-Hamming layer equals this h independent of the identity of the exterior set has a full monochromatic antipodal geodesic: choose the permutation p and an alternating starting vertex to keep each window on that central layer. All off-layer colors may be arbitrary.

Verification: Introduce Boolean T_ab for unordered pairs a<b and S_b for vertices, n(n-1)/2+n variables. For each full order p let W_i(p)=T_{p_i,p_(i+2)} XOR S_{p_(i+1)}, using complement for reverse arcs. Add the formula OR_i (W_i XOR W_(i+1)), which asserts that p is NOT monochromatic. Conjoin these formulae over every p in S_n. Exact Z3 C-API solver checks returned UNSAT in n=4..8. Sizes (variables, order clauses) are n=4 (10,24), n=5 (15,120), n=6 (21,720), n=7 (28,5040), n=8 (36,40320). n=8 took 17.8 seconds on the worker; n=9 remained unresolved in the available time budget. The finite SAT result requires a separately checkable proof trace or mathematical derivation for machine-independent certification. All-dimensional generalization is open.

Implementation specification: enumerate all permutations using itertools.permutations; create Bool variables for all tournament unordered pairs and all centers; for each order compute actual Boolean XOR terms and add Or of XOR of successive window terms; call solver.check(). This is a distinct extension of the previously proved all-n theorem requiring s constant except at most one center. The all-length blocked-insertion lemma demonstrates why a single-path insertion induction cannot prove the extension.
