# Signed-root reachability nerve has two universal poles, with every cross-bridge requiring a half-dimensional monochromatic path

Let n>=5, d=n-2, and let L_x be the exact color-free terminal-two-tail root profiles (J,U), nonempty proper U subset D_J, from the signed-root nerve theorem. Let S_(x,+)=L_x and S_(x,-)=tau L_x, tau(J,U)=(reverse J,D_J\U). The nerve N has 2^(n+1) signed-root vertices (x,+),(x,-), and a simplex of signed roots whenever their profile sets share one formal label.
THEOREM. (1) The full simplex on ALL positive signed roots is present in N, and so is the full simplex on ALL negative signed roots. Indeed for every x, every J and every singleton U={i} subset D_J, the three-edge terminal path (i,a,b) is automatically monochromatic, so the fixed nonempty collection E of ALL singleton labels is contained in every L_x. The collection tau E is contained in every tau L_x. Thus the positive and negative root sets are universal contractible poles.
(2) A mixed-sign edge (x,+)--(y,-) exists precisely when there are J and nonempty proper U subset D_J such that U belongs to R_J(x), while D_J\U belongs to R_reverseJ(y). Such an edge forces at least ONE mono directed ordered-three-face-window geodesic of length >=2+ceil(d/2), because at least one of |U|,d-|U| is >=ceil(d/2). For y=x, this is exactly the NORI grand-extraction witness. For x!=y, it is an off-diagonal complementary-tail coincidence without yet sufficient endpoint compatibility.
(3) If every monochromatic directed geodesic has length at most 1+ceil(d/2) (so every terminal reachability support |U|<ceil(d/2), provided d odd; for d even a safer strict condition is |U|<d/2), then N has NO mixed-sign simplex and is exactly the disjoint union of the two full sign simplices. More generally the exact sufficient condition is: ALL reachable proper U in every R_J(x) have |U|<d/2. Then any label in a positive profile has support size <d/2 and any label in a negative profile has support size >d/2; no overlaps. N equivariantly retracts to S^0, has trivial first cover class on the quotient, and there is no nontrivial Tucker index to exploit. Thus a classical-Tucker proof via this nerve must use genuine longer monochromatic reaches OR a richer carrier than independent signed-root profiles.
(4) Under NO grand closure, N has a FREE involution and contains no edge (x,+)--(x,-). If there exists a mixed edge (x,+)--(y,-), necessarily x!=y. The four vertices x+,y-,x-,y+ support an invariant four-cycle, with the other two sides inside the universal positive/negative simplices and the opposite cross edge supplied by tau. The restriction of the nerve's sign-cover class w1 to the quotient of this antipodally invariant four-cycle is nonzero; hence index(N)>=1. Conversely, without any mixed edge the two universal sign simplices are disjoint and index(N)=0. Thus for a counterexample, the first cohomological-index jump from 0 to >=1 is exactly the existence of at least one OFF-DIAGONAL complementary-tail profile coincidence. Higher index sufficient for Tucker closure remains an open forcing problem.
PROOF: Statements (1),(2) are direct definitions and automatic length-three one-window paths. (3) follows from strict half-support separation. For (4), tau sends x+ to x-, y+ to y-. The mixed edge and its tau image, plus positive and negative clique edges, form a four-cycle with antipodal half-turn; its quotient is a circle carrying the nontrivial connected double cover. Hence pullback of w1 is nonzero, proving w1 on the nerve quotient nonzero. In the absent-cross case N is two disjoint simplices swapped by tau, whose quotient is a simplex and has w1=0. QED.
This is a structural theorem about the exact NORI reachability nerve, not grand closure.
