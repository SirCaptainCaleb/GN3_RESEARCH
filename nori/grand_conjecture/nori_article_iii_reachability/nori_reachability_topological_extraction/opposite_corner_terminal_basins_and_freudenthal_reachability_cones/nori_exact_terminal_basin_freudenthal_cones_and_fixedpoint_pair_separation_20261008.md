# Terminal basins admit honest contractible Freudenthal cone carriers; grand closure is a cross-cone intersection

THEOREM (HONEST FREUDENTHAL CARRIERS OF TERMINAL BASINS). Fix active NORI ordered-three-face coloring, an ordered terminal pair J=(a,b), terminal y, D=[n] minus {a,b}, and the physical d=n-2 cube H={x:x_a!=y_a, x_b!=y_b}. Put b0=y xor {a,b} and b1=b0 xor D. The two COLOR-FREE monochromatic terminal reachability basins are A=T_J(y) and B=T_revJ(y xor D), both subsets of H, rooted respectively at b0 and b1. Use the standard Freudenthal triangulation F of H with Boolean-support coordinate order based at b0.
For each actual directed monochromatic geodesic P:x->y ending J, consider the sequence of physical roots obtained by successively deleting the FIRST direction from P, down to the terminal two-edge path beginning at b0. This is a chain of comparable H-cube vertices, with support sizes descending by exactly one; it spans a simplex of F. Let K_A be the subcomplex of F generated by ALL such actual witness-root chains. Analogously define K_B using genuine mono geodesics x->y xor D ending revJ, whose trimming chains terminate at b1. Then:
(i) K_A and K_B are NONEMPTY simplicial CONES with respective apex vertices b0 and b1. Indeed every generating witness chain contains the base apex, and adjoining the apex to a face of any generating simplex stays inside that simplex. Therefore both path-certified carriers are contractible, and their vertex sets are EXACTLY A and B. This remains true though A,B need not be Boolean-lattice downsets: a witness reaches the corner along SOME shortest suffix chain, not along every subset deletion.
(ii) Because K_A,K_B are subcomplexes of the SAME Freudenthal simplicial complex F, their geometric realizations intersect iff they share an actual physical cube vertex. Therefore |K_A| intersect |K_B| nonempty iff A intersect B nonempty. By the exact opposite-corner terminal-basin equivalence, existence of SOME y,J for which these honest cones intersect is precisely the grand NORI one-switch conclusion.
(iii) The individual cone carriers have no higher reduced homology and can be disjoint. Even for valid antipodally reversal-odd NORI colorings in all even n>=8, the explicit exterior-Hamming-parity coloring gives one pair of such genuinely certified disjoint cones: A is the radius-one star about b0 while B is the radius-three star about b1, separated in the facet cube. Thus fixed-point, KKM or Hex theorems applied to ONE pair of cones cannot force a universal intersection from their separate contractibility and rooted accessibility.
PROOF. The trimmed directed path remains monochromatic since its window sequence is a suffix of the original window sequence; each trimming removes one coordinate that is used in the original path exactly once. Viewed from b0, the physical root supports along trimming are nested, so every such chain is a Freudenthal simplex. By construction any reachable root occurs in a generating chain, proving vertex coverage. Every chain contains b0 and every face extends by b0 inside its generating simplex, giving the cone. For B, the same argument applies from b1; the Boolean complements of nested coordinate supports remain nested, so all B chains also lie in the common standard Freudenthal triangulation rooted at b0. In any abstract simplicial complex, the geometric realizations of subcomplexes intersect precisely along the geometric realization of their common subcomplex, hence intersection implies shared vertex. The exact NORI splice theorem gives the last equivalence. QED.
TOPOLOGICAL RESEARCH TARGET. Seek an equivariant, higher-dimensional NERVE OR DIAGRAM formed by the full family of these path-certified cones over all y,J. Apply colorful KKM, multicolored Hex or a chain-compatible Tucker theorem to that diagram. The extension must produce an ACTUAL intersection between cones related by the reversed terminal-memory involution; an abstract convex-hull coincidence across unrelated paths is insufficient.
