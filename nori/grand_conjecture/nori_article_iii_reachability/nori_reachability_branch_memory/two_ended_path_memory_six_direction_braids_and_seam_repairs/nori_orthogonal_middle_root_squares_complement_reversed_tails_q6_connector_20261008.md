The two direction supports are disjoint outside their reversed two-direction tails: first outside support U={u,v}, second outside support V={k,l}, with terminal orders (a,b) and (b,a).

**LEMMA 1 (complete two-middle-coordinate root invariance).** For ANY genuine monochromatic length-four ordered-three-face geodesic with direction order (d1,d2,d3,d4) rooted at x, and ANY subset H⊆{d2,d3}, the SAME direction word rooted at x xor H has EXACTLY THE SAME TWO ordered physical three-face windows (same face and ordered directions), and therefore the same monochromatic color.

Proof. The first physical 3-face is free in (d1,d2,d3), while the second is free in (d2,d3,d4). Both have d2,d3 as FREE coordinates. Changing the starting root bits in those two coordinates also changes the corresponding intermediate physical vertices only in coordinates that are free within BOTH faces. Thus their exterior-bit assignments, physical faces and direction orders remain literally unchanged. This is independent of antipodal symmetry and coloring complexity. QED.

**THEOREM 2 (two orthogonal root squares meet in exactly one root per transverse fiber).** Assume the two given roots x,y agree outside

Then there is a UNIQUE root z in the intersection

Proof.
