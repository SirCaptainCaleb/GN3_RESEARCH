# NORI: the grand conjecture is refuted in every dimension \(n\ge9\)

**New dimension-free strengthening.** The explicit \(Q_9\) physical ordered-three-face counterexample extends to every \(Q_n\), \(n\ge9\). Keep three directions of class 0, \(n-4\) directions of class 1, and one sentinel; retain exactly the \(Q_9\) face-coloring rule. The new *hereditary sentinel-word contraction* lemma shows that deleting any ordinary direction occurrence never increases the number of adjacent window-color switches. All comparisons affected by a deletion fit inside seven consecutive letters; a complete 22,272-case finite-local check proves the lemma. Repeatedly deleting class-1 letters reduces any allegedly good geodesic to the verified 2,016-case \(Q_9\) obstruction. The complete theorem, proof and verifier are in foundational Subsection 7, *Hereditary sentinel-word contraction and NORI counterexamples in every dimension at least nine*. Article I and the Foundations Section incorporate the result. Positive \(Q_5,Q_6\) theorems remain true; dimensions seven and eight remain open.



## Current mathematical status

A legal binary coloring assigns \(c(F,\pi)\in\{0,1\}\) to each **physical ordered three-face** \(F,\pi=(a,b,c)\) of \(Q_n\), independently of traversal corner, and satisfies \(c(\bar F,\operatorname{rev}\pi)=1-c(F,\pi)\). A full antipodal geodesic is good when its \(n-2\) consecutive ordered-three-face colors change at most once.

**The universal NORI grand conjecture is FALSE.** An explicit legal physical-face coloring of \(Q_9\) forces at least two changes on **every** antipodal geodesic, for every root and coordinate ordering. See foundational Subsection 6, *Dimension-nine counterexample to the physical ordered-three-face NORI conjecture*, and Article I composition v17. Its concise, fully reproducible finite certificate checks only 2,016 cases: 56 binary class words (three zeros, five ones), nine positions for a special sentinel direction, two sentinel starting bits, and both possible middle-sentinel colors. The minima by sentinel position are \(3,2,2,2,2,2,2,2,3\). A prior independently certified \(Q_{15}\) counterexample remains in foundational Subsection 5; its dimension bound is superseded.

**Positive theorems remain:** unconditional \(Q_5\) closure for every physical-face coloring and complete reversal-odd \(Q_6\) closure. No claim of minimum counterexample dimension is made: dimensions seven and eight remain unclassified by these results. The earlier broader class of traversal-corner-dependent directed length-three walks is separately refuted by rank parity.

## Why the refutation is genuinely physical

Partition directions into classes \(A,B,\{s\}\) of sizes \(3,5,1\). Give \(A\)-directions type \(0\) and \(B\)-directions type \(1\). On a free ordered triple avoiding \(s\), color by its fixed \(s\)-coordinate XOR the reversal-even rule \(h\): \(h=1\) exactly for triple \(000\) or for a middle \(1\) adjacent to \(0\). On triples containing \(s\), color \(1\) when \(s\) is first, \(0\) when last, and a fixed strict-order comparison of the two other directions when \(s\) is middle. Antipodal reversal complements all four cases. Along any full geodesic only the binary class sequence, \(s\)-position, \(s\)-start bit, and the middle-order comparison affect the seven window colors. The exhaustive certificate establishes that none of these words is good.

The full rigorous proof and executable verifier belong to the Subsection manuscript, not this overview.

## Article map after the refutation

- **Article I — Foundations.** Unconditional \(Q_5\), legal \(Q_6\), the decisive \(Q_9\) counterexample, earlier \(Q_{15}\) independent certificate, and quantitative rooted thin-shell obstructions. This is the primary article resolving the original question.
- **Article II — Equivariant topology.** Physical geodesic pseudomanifold, real exchanges, and limitations of abstract carriers. A theorem forcing universal good-path extraction would contradict the \(Q_9\) coloring. Restricted-class and low-dimensional carrier questions remain meaningful.
- **Article III — Complementary reachability.** Same-root, complementary-support and reversed-terminal criteria remain exact equivalences. They cannot be universally guaranteed, as the \(Q_9\) witness forbids their extracting a full path.
- **Article IV — Window transport.** Optimal physical antipodal window-shift distance and forced local monochromatic connectors remain true. The \(Q_9\) witness demonstrates that local connector abundance does not ensure globally compatible one-change extraction.
- **Article V — Local connectors and exchanges.** Dead-edge, maximal-good-path and genuine seam-window lemmas remain valid, but no universal terminating exchange theorem producing a good full path can hold.
- **Article VI — Exterior-bit methods.** Affine and restricted nonlinear closure theorems survive. The \(Q_9\) witness exploits one special exterior bit together with a reversal-even direction-word obstruction, showing that global oddness alone does not force an affine-style root solution.
- **Article VII — Seven-coordinate methods.** Seven-direction wing and pivot lemmas, restricted \(Q_7/Q_8\) positive results, and prescribed-root/slot obstructions remain important for determining the smallest possible counterexample dimension.
- **Article VIII — Edge coloring.** The physical antipodally odd edge-coloring problem and its affine/Johnson/tight-path results are distinct. The \(Q_9\) face counterexample does not settle the edge version.
