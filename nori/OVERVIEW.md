# NORI: conjecture and mathematical landscape

A legal NORI3 coloring assigns a bit to each **physical ordered three-face** `(F,(a,b,c))` of the Boolean cube, independently of the traversing corner, and satisfies `c(bar F,(c,b,a))=1-c(F,(a,b,c))`. The original conjecture requested a full antipodal geodesic with at most one change among consecutive three-face colors. **That unrestricted conjecture is refuted**, already in `Q9`; this is not an open objective. Colorings with unbounded compulsory switches exist in every fixed ordered-face dimension `k >= 3`.

**Current strongest NORI3 construction (October 10, 2026).** A physical one-sentinel lift of the self-dual Devine–Milans `(3,3)`-tournament gives every `n >= 5` a legal NORI3 coloring in which **every monochromatic geodesic has at most `2 ceil(log_2(n-1))+6` moves**. Hence `S_3(n) >= ceil((n-2)/(2 ceil(log_2(n-1))+4))-1 = Omega(n/log n)`. This supersedes the multilevel square-root switch lower bound asymptotically and refutes a universal `Omega(sqrt n)` monochromatic-geodesic guarantee. The universal **lower** bound on longest monochromatic geodesic length, including the proposed `Omega(log n)`, is open. The Devine–Milans lower bound for abstract `(3,3)`-tournaments **does not transfer automatically** to genuine physical NORI3 colorings.

**Current research directions.** Investigate sharp unrestricted monochromatic-path scale `Omega_k(log n)` and corresponding extremal switches; separate same-face reversal oddness and antipodal invariance to preserve boundary 3-tournaments; edge-ordered realizability with global line-graph acyclicity and **original-vertex-simple** paths; and unrestricted NORI2 / NORI1. The logarithmic construction violates same-face reversal-oddness on **every triple**; at least two of six ordered values per triple must change to make it boundary-compatible. The old unrestricted fixed-switch hierarchy, proofs of the refuted one-switch conjecture, and a universal square-root long-path target must not direct new workers.

**Historical landscape below.** The following Article summaries describe meaningful proved tools, finite obstructions, and the earlier proof program. Statements about unfinished attempts to force a one-switch full path are **historical**, not assertions that the unrestricted NORI3 conjecture remains open. Several version numbers and old Item references may be stale; consult live Article/Subsection compositions and `nori.changes(...)`. A coloring of arbitrary directed length-three walks is not equivalent to a coloring of physical cube faces.

**Article I — finite forcing and root mobility.** Q5 closure follows because hypothetical failure makes every three-window color word alternate; independence of exterior bits then makes the color depend only on direction order, and a binary alternating labeling around a five-cycle is impossible. Q6 closure first obtains a monochromatic four-edge connector inside a five-face, then uses **six fully specified rooted geodesics**: equality of *actual physical faces* and antipodal-reversed faces forces inconsistent four-window bad words. This proves Q6 even for nonlinear exterior dependence. The six-path gadget cannot be embedded naively in arbitrary higher dimensions: additional fixed exterior coordinates can obey contradictory signed equality/complement cycles. Full-dimensional replacement certificates, new root-moving forcing families, and induction mechanisms remain open. Stronger restricted Q7/Q8 and Q6 robustness statements test mechanisms; they do not alone provide a transition to all dimensions. See `nori_article_i_conjecture` composition v15; `nori_q6_922_good_geodesics_pentagon_certificate_20261009`; `nori_q6_six_oddness_defect_and_single_support_robustness_20261009`.

**Article II — equivariant topology.** Full geodesics form an all-root pseudomanifold with authentic adjacent exchanges; quotient classes detect some global symmetry. The color-dependent carrier must consist of *jointly witnessed* genuine good paths. Ambient equivariant zeros and formal square diagonals do not certify such paths, and some contiguous-path complexes collapse to essentially window-graph topology. A concrete high-index product or filling with physical witnesses remains to be established. See Article II and `nori_good_contiguous_path_flag_complex_equivariantly_collapses_to_window_graph_20261009`.

**Article III — complementary terminal reachability.** A same-root pair of monochromatic witnesses on complementary supports with reversed two-direction tails yields a full good path. The criterion is **exactly equivalent** to NORI; no strictly easier intersection theorem is yet demonstrated. Equivariant root-profile coincidences at different roots do not force the required diagonal. Legal Q7 examples have a root with good paths on every proper support yet no good full path at that root, ruling out support coverage as a sufficient lifting rule. A useful advance would supply a new invariant compelling a *same-root, terminal-compatible* intersection. See Article III; `nori_exact_color_free_reversed_two_tail_complement_reachability_grand_equivalence_20261008`; `nori_q7_all_proper_supports_good_one_root_no_full_rooted_geodesic_explicit_1680bit_certificate_20261009`.

**Article IV — window transport.** Actual physical window-shift paths connect a window to its antipodal reversal in optimal length `max(6,2 ceil((n-1)/2))`; opposite endpoint colors force plentiful monochromatic four-edge connectors. This does not select compatible connectors along one full direction-distinct geodesic. Earlier transport constructions retain useful proofs, while improvements to distance/counts have not yet supplied an extraction mechanism. See Article IV; `nori_equivariant_window_shift_short_antipodal_path_many_middle_connectors_20261009` (sharp result) and `nori_batched_buffer_exterior_root_shuttle_antipodal_h_i_length_n_connector_density_20261009` (different construction).

**Article V — dead edges and exchanges.** Dead-edge rigidity gives monochromatic hubs, and a longest good partial geodesic imposes two-sided cap-color constraints. Adjacent swaps affect at most four physical windows, and splicing brings two genuinely new seam windows. Colors at candidate caps need not align. A well-founded improving root-slide/exchange, or a different globally certified splice, remains open. See Article V; `nori_global_one_terminal_dead_edge_variational_normal_form_20261008`.

**Article VI — exterior-bit algebra.** Affine color classes admit root-equation solutions and structured nonlinear families have forcing theorems. General nonlinear colorings need additional control. Equality/complement parity around cycles of fixed exterior bits can obstruct attempted face identifications. A general root-control principle or an entirely different construction could make these methods apply beyond special families. See Article VI and the Q6 robustness and Q8 one-sentinel Items.

**Article VII — seven-coordinate tests.** The exact shared-pivot window form `(A(t),M1,M2,M3,E(t))` explains when one root bit can repair endpoints. Crossed-sensitivity and restricted exterior families yield special closure results. The endpoint choices remain correlated, and legal colorings refute prescribed-root/slot and coverage-only generalizations. A reusable multi-pivot repair lemma remains unproved. See Article VII; `nori_q7_fixed_sentinel_third_and_fifth_slot_2sat_counterexample_20261009` and the Q7 proper-support certificate.

**Article VIII — related edge coloring.** Affine syndromes, block scheduling, and central Johnson tight-path arguments prove special *edge-coloring* results, including a Q12 middle-belt theorem derived from a nine-vertex tight-four SAT result. The two nine-vertex publications overlap in theorem and computational mechanism; keep their distinct presentation/provenance. Edge-color words do not determine the ordered physical three-window colors. A faithful transfer is still required. See Article VIII and `nori_k1_nine_vertex_tight_four_ramsey_exact_sat_q12_central_edge_closure_20261009`.

**Other possible ideas.** A genuinely global root-averaged switch-count identity or signed/Walsh noncancellation mechanism might bypass both the terminal-profile and local-connector reductions; no general identity has been established. A full-dimensional forcing certificate inspired by Article I is likewise a proposal, with the signed exterior obstruction requiring attention. New approaches may be developed independently of existing Articles.

**Interpretation.** The various “global compatibility” gaps need not be one theorem: Article II needs true joint witness cells, III needs same-root complementary terminal data, IV needs compatible connector selection, V needs terminating exchanges, and VI–VII need coherent physical exterior assignments. These could share a deeper principle, or expose information lost by different abstractions. The volume of prior work describes history and effort, not mathematical promise.
