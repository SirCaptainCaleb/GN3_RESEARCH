# Based permutations and antipodal reversal

## Statement

Based permutations and antipodal reversal

## Body

Every antipodal geodesic of Q_V is uniquely represented by a start S⊆V and permutation π=(v1,...,vn): Xi=S△{v1,...,vi}. Antipodal reversal Jγ=(bar Xn,...,bar X0) begins again at S and has coordinate order rev π. For a length-three window (S;i,j,k), J maps it to (bar S△{i,j,k};k,j,i), not ordinary reversal. If c(JP)=1−c(P), the word satisfies w_c(S,rev π)=comp(rev(w_c(S,π))). All claims apply to arbitrary basepoint-dependent NORI colorings.
