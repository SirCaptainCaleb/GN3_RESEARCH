# Discrete ham sandwich theorem

**Summary:** Any d finite point sets in R^d admit one affine hyperplane simultaneously bisecting all d sets.

## Statement

Let P_1,...,P_d be finite subsets of R^d. There is an affine hyperplane H such that each open halfspace bounded by H contains at most floor(|P_i|/2) points of P_i for every i.
