# Directed roots: convex zero iff a directed cycle

## Statement

Directed roots: convex zero iff a directed cycle

## Body

Let D be a finite directed graph and assign to each arc i→j the vector e_i−e_j. Then 0 belongs to the convex hull of these vectors if and only if D has a directed cycle. A directed cycle gives a zero sum of its vectors with equal positive coefficients. Conversely a convex representation of zero gives nonnegative arc weights with total sum one and balanced inflow and outflow at every vertex. Positive support of this circulation contains a directed cycle. This lemma is independent of the NORI coloring and may aid extraction from topological zeros only after genuine root labels and a legitimate extraction map have been constructed.
