# Ordered three-face galleries under an explicit orientation-blindness hypothesis

## Statement

Ordered three-face galleries under an explicit orientation-blindness hypothesis

## Body

Let c be a coloring of based length-three geodesics (X;i,j,k). Suppose additionally that c(X△T;i,j,k)=c(X;i,j,k) for every T⊆{i,j,k}; this is orientation blindness within the traversed three-dimensional face, not a consequence of antipodal-reversal oddness. Then c is a function of the underlying three-dimensional cube face with free axes {i,j,k} together with their ordered list (i,j,k). Every antipodal geodesic produces an ordered-face gallery of overlapping consecutive three-faces, whose shared face has the two middle axes. Antipodal reversal takes a face to the antipodal face and reverses its axis order. This is a distinct intermediate subclass between general based-window colorings and translation-invariant coordinate-only colorings. The rank-parity counterexample is excluded by the additional within-face orientation-blindness. Adapted from NOR orientation_blind_windows_as_ordered_cube_face_galleries; no reduction from unrestricted NORI is implied.
