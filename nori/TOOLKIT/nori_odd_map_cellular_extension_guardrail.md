# Odd cellular interpolation and the missing extraction step

## Statement

Odd cellular interpolation and the missing extraction step

## Body

Let K be a finite simplicial complex with a simplicial fixed-point-free involution τ, and let chamber or vertex labels L satisfy L(τv)=−L(v) in a real vector space. When labels are defined on chambers, assign to each face barycenter the mean of labels of all incident chambers and interpolate affinely on the barycentric subdivision. The resulting map is continuous and odd, provided the involution bijects the incident chambers of paired faces. If the interpolated map vanishes, the zero lies in a convex hull of the labels appearing on its supporting face flag. This statement is a general topological construction, not a NORI existence proof. A proof of the NORI conjecture needs a valid extraction from such a balanced configuration to one genuinely colored antipodal cube path; the ordinary permutation-chamber involution used in restricted NOR must be checked against NORI's actual antipodal reversal.
