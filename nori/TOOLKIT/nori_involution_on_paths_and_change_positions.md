# Antipodal reversal preserves change counts and basepoint

## Statement

Antipodal reversal preserves change counts and basepoint

## Body

For an antipodal path γ with word (a0,...,a(n−3)), the antipodally reversed path Jγ=(bar Xn,...,bar X0) has word (1−a(n−3),...,1−a0). This follows by pairing each window of Jγ with the antipodal reversal of the corresponding window of γ. Its change positions are reflected, its number of color changes is unchanged, and Jγ begins at the same starting cube vertex as γ. No relation between the color of a window and the color of its ordinary reversal is asserted. This symmetry is an exact consequence of the NORI oddness hypothesis.
