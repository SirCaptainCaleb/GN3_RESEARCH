# One-change word versus a weaker width-one seam

## Statement

One-change word versus a weaker width-one seam

## Body

For a binary word a1...am, having at most one color change is equivalent to 0^p1^q or 1^p0^q. A common directional relaxation defines p as first position of zero and q as last position of one and asks q≤p+1. This width-one condition is weaker: the word 0101 violates the intended one-change property, while some short mixed seams can survive weakened separation tests. Any proof delivering only a bounded or width-one mixed seam requires a further elimination argument before it proves NORI. The observation is purely combinatorial and applies to actual basepoint-dependent NORI color words. Adapted from NOR port, one_change_and_the_width_one_inversion_relaxation.
