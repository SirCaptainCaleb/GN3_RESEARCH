# Windows, disjoint direction supports, and a caveat

## Statement

Windows, disjoint direction supports, and a caveat

## Body

On a geodesic with distinct successive flip directions v1,...,vn, the ith length-three window traverses vi+1,vi+2,vi+3. Windows indexed at distance at least 3 have disjoint direction supports, and at distance at least 4 have disjoint vertex-index intervals. This does not imply their colors are independent when c depends on the window's actual base vertex. Reordering earlier flips can alter later window basepoints. Consequently independent-coordinate product arguments from NOR need an additional invariance or coherence lemma. Adapted from NOR disjoint_status_windows_form_boolean_cubes.
