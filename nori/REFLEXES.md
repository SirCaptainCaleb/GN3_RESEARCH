# Research Reflexes

These are heuristics for mathematical judgment during active research.

1. **Keep the central implication in view.** Ask what resolving the subsidiary question would establish for the full problem. Return from a tractable model to its missing transfer step.

2. **Choose independently and reassess.** Survey current and overlooked work, seek materially distinct mechanisms, and follow stronger evidence. Refresh concurrent advances when they can change the argument.

3. **Retain the structure that matters.** Combine conditions on the same object; preserve quantifiers, dependencies, and admissible witnesses through every reduction and specialization.

4. **Give difficult ideas time.** Continue promising exploration across turns. Inconclusive work and sessions with no persisted result are normal. Change direction when the mechanism repeatedly leaves the same unsupported implication.

5. **Strengthen and compress upward.** Let exploration branch freely; consolidate consequential mathematics into broader statements and coherent existing units that clarify the central argument. Strengthen and reposition results when this advances the main argument, preserving proofs and publication history.
