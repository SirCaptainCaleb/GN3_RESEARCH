## Research note

THEOREM (odd co-singleton turn Fourier covariance). Let n>=5 be odd, and define d_ij([x])=[c_i(x) != c_j(x)] for any legal antipodally odd coloring of Q_n. For even S, let b_ij(S)=E_[x] chi_S([x])d_ij([x]), chi_S(x)=(-1)^(sum_{k in S}x_k). Put a_ij=b_ij(empty). For each direction z let S_z=[n] minus {z}, and on K_{n-1} with vertex set D_z=[n] minus {z} define R^z_ij=b_ij(S_z)-b_zi(S_z)-b_zj(S_z). Let P_W be orthogonal projection of pair weights on K_n onto the zero-vertex-degree-sum subspace, and P_z the analogous projection on K_{n-1}. If G(O)=T_c(O)/2 and mu=E G=2 sum_{i<j}a_ij/(n-1), then
Var G >= L=(2/(n-1))||P_W a||² + sum_z [2/((n-1)(n-2))||R^z||² + 2(n-3)/((n-1)(n-4))||P_z R^z||²].
For p=Pr(G=1), p >= max(0,[L-(mu-3)(n-mu)]/[2(n-1)]). Hence L>(mu-3)(n-mu) forces a monochromatic full antipodal geodesic, for every legal coloring.

PROOF. For an even number N>=4 of labels, let u be a random permutation, and define A_R(u)=sum_{j=1}^{N-1}(-1)^j R_{u_j,u_{j+1}}. Let Z_e denote the signed indicator that e occurs as a consecutive pair in u. The Gram entries E Z_e Z_f are 2/N if e=f, -2/[N(N-1)] when e and f share one label, and 4/[N(N-1)(N-3)] when disjoint. The last follows from permutation symmetry and the deterministic sum_e Z_e=-1. The Gram operator has eigenvalue 2/[N(N-1)] on pair-additive edge weights v_i+v_j (its alternating sum telescopes to -v_{u_1}-v_{u_N}); its eigenvalue on zero-degree-sum edge weights is 2(N²-2N-1)/[N(N-1)(N-3)], using adjacent and disjoint sums -2R_e and R_e. Therefore
E A_R²=2/[N(N-1)]||R||²+2(N-2)/[N(N-3)]||P_W R||².

For the full cube, sample folded root [x] and direction order pi uniformly. Fourier expansion over even characters chi_S of the folded vertex group gives Var G=Var_pi F_empty(pi)+sum_{even nonempty S}E_pi F_S(pi)². The first term equals 2||P_W a||²/(n-1), by the Hamiltonian direction-cycle Gram. At S=S_z, rotate pi to begin at z; this changes F_S by at most a sign and preserves its square. Write the remainder as u_1,...,u_N with N=n-1 even. Since chi_S flips at all directions except z, its cyclic turn sum is b_{z,u_1}+b_{z,u_N}+sum_{j=1}^{N-1}(-1)^j b_{u_j,u_{j+1}}. Pair-additive terms b_zi+b_zj cancel the two boundary entries, giving F_{S_z}=A_{R^z}(u). Apply the preceding Gram formula. The S_z are distinct and nonempty, so their contributions add and give the claimed variance bound. Since G ranges over odd values from 1 to n, pointwise (G-3)(n-G)>=-2(n-1)[G=1]. Averaging and rearranging yields Var G <=(mu-3)(n-mu)+2(n-1)p, proving the quantitative forcing condition.

SEVEN-CUBE CERTIFICATE. Over F2, index directions 0..6 and set:
c0=1+x1+x5+x6; c1=1+x0+x3+x4; c2=1+x4; c3=1+x1+x5+x6; c4=x1+x2+x3+x5+x6; c5=1+x4; c6=x0+x2+x4.
