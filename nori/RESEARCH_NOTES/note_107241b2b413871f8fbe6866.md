## Research note

Take an edge ordering of K_n, flip each reversal-class triple comparison independently with probability eta, 8 log n/n <= eta <= 1/2. With high probability every comparison-faithful spanning subgraph has at most 4 n log n/eta edges. For a fixed q-edge graph G, the number of adjacent edge pairs is h=sum_v choose(deg(v),2) >= 2q^2/n-q. Its probability of being faithful is <= exp(-eta h). Put q=ceil(4n log n/eta), then union bound over at most (e n^2)^q graphs gives probability at most exp(-q(6log n-O(1)))=o(1). Chernoff concentration gives error count asymptotic to eta n choose(n-1,2). The Caro-Wei cleaning theorem is therefore optimal to within a logarithmic factor for dense-subgraph purification. This does not bound paths which bypass erroneous comparisons without globally deleting every conflict.
