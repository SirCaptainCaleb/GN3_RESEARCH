## Research note

For n>=6 take the folded cube F=Q_n/tau, adjoin an odd five-cycle via two long vertex-disjoint paths to obtain a 2-connected graph G, and equip it with a double-cover monodromy extending the cube cover and odd on the five-cycle. Let H=L(G), and D be the pointed dual of [B_H,0;a,1]. Every port cycle has a two-point port-cocircuit intersection: restrict its turn signature to H_cube and apply Wu-Yang. Yet the unique shortest odd-monodromy turn cycle is the added C5, so the shortest port cocircuit has size six. Alternating the lifted C10 edge colors makes all five turns on C5 change, yielding intersection six and violating P4. Consequently faithful line-graph double covers, 2-connectivity, cographic deletion and universal PI2 are insufficient. Full proof in the research conversation; it uses the line-graph cycle-to-base cycle-space map and connector lengths greater than n+5.

**Set-up and connectedness proof.** Let \(\tau\) denote complementation of cube vertices; \(F_n=Q_n/\tau\). Every vertex orbit removed from \(F_n\) corresponds to removing two vertices from \(Q_n\), which remains connected because \(Q_n\) is \(n\)-vertex-connected. Hence \(F_n\) has no articulation vertex. Attach \(C_5\) by two internally disjoint paths with distinct endpoints in both blocks; removing any one vertex still leaves a connection and connected remnants, so the resulting \(G\) is 2-vertex-connected. The two-cover of \(G\) is defined by any \(\mathbb F_2\) voltage cochain restricting to the antipodal class on \(F_n\) and giving odd sum on \(C_5\); the cover is connected because the first restriction is nontrivial.

For \(H=L(G)\), every admissible coloring cycle of \(D=M([B_H,0;a,1])^*\) comes from \(d=a+\delta f\). The induced turn subgraph on original folded-cube edges is \(L(F_n)\) with the canonical cube monodromy. Restrict \(d\) there. Wu--Yang's theorem on nongeodesic monochromatic antipodal paths, together with Article XI's odd-girth/zero-turn quotient theorem, gives an odd-monodromy simple turn cycle \(C\) with \(w_d(C)=1\). Then \(C\cup\{p\}\) is a cocircuit of \(D\) intersecting \(X_d=\operatorname{supp}(d)\cup\{p\}\) in two elements. Thus \(\mathrm{PI}_2(D,p)\) holds for every port-containing cycle, irrespective of how the coloring is extended outside the cube block.


**Minimum port cocircuit.** The line-graph turn map \(\varphi:Z_1(L(G);\mathbb F_2)\to Z_1(G;\mathbb F_2)\) preserves covering monodromy and does not increase support size. The image of an odd-monodromy turn cycle contains an odd-monodromy base simple cycle of no greater length. Inside \(F_n\), such base cycles have length at least \(n\), because their lifts join antipodal cube vertices. Within \(C_5\), the nontrivial simple cycle has length five. Any cycle crossing both long connectors has length more than \(2L>5\). Thus odd-monodromy turn cycles have length at least five. Equality forces their five turn vertices to represent the five edges of the added \(C_5\), inducing its unique line-graph five-cycle. Therefore there is one minimum port cocircuit, of size six.

**Failure of four-intersection.** The cover restricted to \(C_5\) is a ten-cycle with deck shift advancing five edges. Color its consecutive edges alternately zero and one; the deck shift complements colors, and all five projected turns disagree. Extend this to an admissible coloring on the remaining deck-paired cover edges. For its port cycle \(X_d\), the unique minimum port cocircuit \(Y_5\) has \(|X_d\cap Y_5|=1+5=6\). Thus even 2-connected line-graph covering geometry plus universal unrestricted two-intersection cannot imply the minimum-port four-intersection property.

**Limit of this obstruction.** A one-vertex-sum construction already suffices, but the two-connector construction excludes articulation points. The local balanced-generator and homogeneity properties of the canonical resource-gallery systems do not hold here. Another balanced-shortcut example in Article XI already refutes a stronger unrestricted abstract matroid formulation; this note isolates failure within ordinary line-graph double covers without asserting any counterexample to the cube or marked-gallery conjectures.
