## Research note

Design requirement for the replacement NORI grand conjecture: whatever common mathematical structure is proposed should naturally produce monochromatic geodesics of a uniform prescribed length x (the same x across relevant instances/roots), in the relevant higher-uniformity coloring model. This should make antipodal co-reachability and concatenation usable: two such geodesics meeting at a vertex, with antipodal endpoints, concatenate to an antipodal geodesic, and splitting once at their common vertex leaves two monochromatic subpaths regardless of the colors of higher-face windows straddling the split. The target should encode meaningful long uniformly colored geodesic witnesses rather than merely arbitrary local monochromatic fragments. Treat this as a desideratum for selecting the shared structure and conjecture, not as an established theorem.
