Reproducible certificate for the published projective two-turn incidence and Fourier kernel manuscript (composition version 3). Script: https://github.com/SirCaptainCaleb/GN3_RESEARCH/blob/172cee3a81e9338c392e02b95688161d76841345/scripts/nori_projective_two_turn_verify.py (Git commit 172cee3a81e9338c392e02b95688161d76841345). Run: python scripts/nori_projective_two_turn_verify.py ; requires Python 3.10+ and SymPy. Independent complete pair-incidence enumerations agree with N(t,u) in Q3,Q4,Q5,Q6 on 78,1176,12880,115440 unordered pairs respectively; each of three sampled legal colorings per dimension independently satisfies the global first and second moment formulas. The Q5 10x10 Fourier Gram blocks indexed by even S have spectra |S|=0: 0(4),6(5),30(1); |S|=2: 0(2),6(2),10+-4sqrt(5)(1 each),7+-sqrt(17)(2 each); |S|=4: 0(4),6(3),14(3). Therefore lambda_5=10-4sqrt(5)>1. With the degree-explicit orthogonal residual D_5(2)=D_5(3)=2 at each of the 16 projective vertices, the paper proves every fully balanced legal Q5 coloring has >=5 coherent supports with T=2; if b projective stars are unbalanced, the first-moment bound instead gives >=6b such supports. These certificates supplement dimension-uniform proofs in the manuscript.
