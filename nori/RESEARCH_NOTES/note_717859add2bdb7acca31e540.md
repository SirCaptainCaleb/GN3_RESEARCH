## Research note

Under a hypothetical legal NORI1 counterexample, fix root x and let C_x^<=k be the union of both monochromatic rooted Freudenthal prefix complexes generated by prefixes of length at most k, 0<=k<=n-1. Every C_x^<=k is a simplicial cone at x, and its antipodal partner is C_barx^<=k. The two cones are disjoint: intersection would give a full antipodal geodesic with at most one color switch, and root-slide would turn it monochromatic. Both cones lie in the cubical boundary S^(n-1). Being collapsible PL subpolyhedra, they admit disjoint ball regular neighborhoods. Therefore each complement Z_x^k=S^(n-1) minus (C_x^<=k union C_barx^<=k) has homotopy type S^(n-2). For k increasing, natural inclusions Z_x^(k+1) -> Z_x^k induce isomorphisms in reduced integral homology by naturality of Alexander duality applied to two connected contractible components. Since both sides have sphere homotopy type, the inclusions are ordinary homotopy equivalences. Thus homotopy persistence of the filtered complement at a single root is CONSTANT under the counterexample hypothesis. Any successful proof needs cross-root incidence or stronger certificate-sensitive chain structure; ordinary individual-root rank filtration cannot force NORI1. This refines the already known Article XIV fact that each prefix cone is contractible and the unfiltered root complements have sphere type.
