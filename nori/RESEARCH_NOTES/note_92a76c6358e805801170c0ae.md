## Research note

The 2026 Wu-Yang monochromatic antipodal path theorem ensures that a shortest monochromatic antipodal walk P exists for every legal odd coloring. Let its length be L=n+2e. For each direction i appearing 2k_i+1 times, consider the 2k_i intervals between successive occurrences. Every interval contains an active square derivative delta_i c_j(v)=c_j(v) xor c_j(v^i)=1 on one of its traversed j edges. Indeed, write P=A i B i C and remove the two displayed i steps while translating B in bit i; this yields a shorter antipodal walk. If all i-derivatives along B vanish, the shorter walk remains monochromatic, contradicting shortest length. Intervals for each i are disjoint, hence summing derivative incidences over each path-edge occurrence and each direction i gives at least sum_i 2k_i = L-n=2e. This is a uniform walk-erasure obstruction, not proof of e=0. Example Q4 coloring c0=x1, c1=x0+x2+x3, c2=x0+x1+x3, c3=x0+x1+x2 has all-zero antipodal path rooted at vertex 1 of direction word 0,2,0,3,0,1 but no zero geodesic at that root. Each of the two successive direction-zero intervals contains a nonzero square derivative. The repeated-direction budget complements the Minty-style fold potential in Article XV.
