## Research note

Formal countermodel to a root-seam-only NORI1 closure argument. For any n and any legal antipodally odd edge-color assignment, prescribe the same abstract rooted tropical kernel K_x=[[2,3],[3,2]] at every root x. At every root edge e set its shared oriented seam energy E_e=3 and both endpoint forcing gaps g_e^x=g_e^y=0. Define h_x=1, t_x=3, s_x=0 everywhere. These data obey the parity and boundary Lipschitz constraints of HL24; the antipodal conjugation and orientation identities HL25; both neighboring-root seam gap identities HL29-HL30; the scalar root-seam curl and projective coboundary constraints HL40-HL43; and the forcing-weighted continuity inequality HL45. Independent products of this formal configuration also preserve the constant kernel under the max-tropical product law HL23. Yet every root has positive grade h_x=1. The model is NOT asserted to be realizable by an undirected edge coloring and does not challenge NORI1. It establishes the logical insufficiency of the listed rootwise parity, antipodal symmetry, scalar seam forcing, square curl, and tropical product properties alone: any proof must additionally exploit path realizability, for example coupled facet kernel matrices rather than only their single oriented entries, consistency of different seam orders across squares, or incidence constraints between optimizing full geodesics.
