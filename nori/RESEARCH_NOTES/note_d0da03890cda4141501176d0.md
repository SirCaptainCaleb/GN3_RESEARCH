## Research note

A red midpoint collision follows if the safe-order permutation difference span exceeds (N-1)(N-2)/2, where N=2^m. Proof: center the permutation matrices by subtracting the all-ones matrix divided by N. On doubly centered matrices the twisted Frobenius form B(A,B)=tr(A^T B J) has positive index (N-1)(N/2-1) and negative index (N-1)N/2. Under zero collision counts, every centered pair has B-value -1. Their differences lie in an isotropic subspace orthogonal to a negative vector, giving the rank bound.
Critical square: fix pair i and exterior pair parity u. For starting parity p, a pair order pi is safe if the parity before i, namely p_-i plus the indicator of labels preceding i, differs from u. Safe orders have unchanged full red endpoint maps across this switch. If the switched coloring is bad, their permutation span must obey the rank ceiling in every p. The number of safe orders is m!-k!(m-1-k)!, where k is the required predecessor-set size; this is at least (m-1)(m-1)!.
Reverse sweeps: The map for reverse(pi) from parity p+1 is the inverse of the map for pi from p. Furthermore, reverse(pi) is safe from p+1 if and only if pi is safe from p. Thus, at a critical switch, the union of graphs of all safe half-sweep endpoint maps has no pair of antipodal sources sharing one endpoint, and no pair of antipodal endpoints sharing one source.
