## Research note

The proposed strengthening of the adjacent-root complementary half-path property, requiring the root-joining direction to occur first in the half-path containing it, is false even for balanced legal Q4. Put c1=x4, c2=1+x3, c3=1+x4, c4=x3. This is undirected, antipodally odd and vertex-balanced. Writing p=x3, q=x4, the monochromatic two-step direction words are: pq=00: color0 {14,43}, color1 {23,34}; pq=01: color0 {32,41}, color1 {12,21}; pq=10: color0 {12,21}, color1 {32,41}; pq=11: color0 {23,34}, color1 {14,43}. A direct table check shows that every same-color complementary-support pairing across a colored root edge uses direction 3 or 4 and that direction appears second, never first, in the half-geodesic containing it. Yet monochromatic full geodesics exist, e.g. root 0000 order 1432 of color0. Exhaustive enumeration of all 65,536 legal Q4 colorings independently found the weaker adjacent-root pairing always present, while 304 colorings lack the first-direction refinement. The intermediate universal n+2 bound from the weaker pairing remains conjectural.
