## Research note

Let k≥3 and n≥k satisfy n+k≡0 (mod 2). Color every oriented (k−1)-edge geodesic P=(v₀,…,vₖ₋₁) of Qₙ by c(P)=|v₀| mod 2. Its antipodal reversal P*=(v̄ₖ₋₁,…,v̄₀) satisfies c(P*)=n−|vₖ₋₁|≡n−|v₀|−(k−1)≡1−c(P) (mod 2). Along every geodesic, successive (k−1)-edge windows alternate colors, since consecutive initial vertices have opposite weight parity. Hence no geodesic of length at least k is monochromatic in these windows.
