## Research note

The free reversal carrier \(M_n\) in the new Subsection has covering index \(n-1\), but this obstruction is already present on each individual boundary sphere \(\partial_xM_n\cong S^{n-1}\). Thus an odd map from that boundary to \(S^{n-2}\) is impossible regardless of the cube coloring. An argument cannot infer from the absence of a favorable geodesic that a dimension-lowering map exists on every rooted sphere by a formal operation: rooted cube colorings can lack monochromatic complete geodesics.

The equatorial \(S^{n-2}\) inside each link is the type-\(A_{n-1}\) Coxeter sphere of dimension orders for one root, with reversal acting antipodally. For a boundary tournament on \(n\) vertices the Hamilton order carrier is also type-\(A_{n-1}\). The unrestricted boundary triple colors have not been embedded into cube-edge color fields. A faithful common color representation remains open.

**Possible mechanism, unproved:** construct a color-dependent map of pairs or equivariant chain assignment, with boundary values in high-dimensional type-A color carriers and interior exchanges in the endpoint-symmetric affine complex. Under the absence of a low-switch witness, seek a relative filling into a target whose covering index vanishes. The mathematical obstacle is coherent coloring across all triangle/square residues and root fibers. The high index alone yields no existence theorem.
