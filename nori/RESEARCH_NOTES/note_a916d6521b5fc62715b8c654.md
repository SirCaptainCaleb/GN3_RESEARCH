## Research note

Reachability volume fails as a dimension-uniform NORI1 forcing strategy under Cartesian products.

Define F_c(x) as the set of subsets S of the n directions for which a red dimension-simple path from x to x xor 1_S exists. Let c on Q_n and d on Q_m both satisfy the antipodal oddness and undirected-edge conditions. Their block product e=c boxplus d on Q_(n+m), where directions in each block use the rule from that block alone, is again legal. Every red dimension-simple path in the product projects to a red dimension-simple path in each block; conversely two such paths may be concatenated or interleaved. Therefore for every pair of roots (x,y),

F_e(x,y)=F_c(x) x F_d(y),

and |F_e(x,y)|=|F_c(x)||F_d(y)|. In particular the maximum relative red-reachability volume is multiplicative. Monochromatic full antipodal geodesic witnesses also tensorize: combine full witnesses from the blocks.

Explicit four-cube seed, with all sums in F2 and bit indices 0..3:

c0=1+x3, c1=1+x2, c2=x3, c3=x2.

Every rule ignores its own bit and changes under global complementation, so c is legal. Its red rooted reachability cardinality depends only on (x2,x3). For (0,0), it is 5; for (1,0) or (0,1), it is 8; for (1,1), it is 4. To verify, directions 0,2 are controlled by x3 and are red in complementary x3 states; directions 1,3 are similarly controlled by x2. In state (0,0), one may choose direction 2 then 1, or direction 3 then 0, yielding the five sets empty,{2},{2,1},{3},{3,0}. For state (1,0), red reachability permits precisely empty,{1},{2},{1,2},{2,3},{1,2,3},{2,3,0},{1,2,3,0}; (0,1) is analogous. For state (1,1), one may independently use directions 0,1, yielding four sets. A red full antipodal geodesic exists from every root with (x2,x3)=(1,0) or (0,1).

Take k independent copies of this coloring. In dimension 4k the maximum size of a red rooted reachable set is 8^k, hence its fraction of all vertices is at most (1/2)^k. Nonetheless the product coloring has red full antipodal geodesics. This disproves any proposed universal lower bound bounded away from zero on the maximum rooted red-geodesic reachability density, even restricted to legal colorings known to have witnesses.

Epistemic separation: this is a rigorous, all-dimensional obstruction to volumetric cut intersection. It does not refute a topological or algebraic cut compatibility theorem retaining the order of edges. Moreover, no monochromatic full geodesic does not imply red graph components are antipode-free: red paths of nonminimal length may exist. The Wu-Yang (2026) theorem assures a monochromatic antipodal graph path for every legal coloring, while shortening its excess to zero remains open. Thus future global cut arguments need to use geodesic-relative path order, not ordinary connected components or the sizes of rooted reachability families.
