## Research note

Universal two-way balanced double-escape lift. In Q6 use paired direction bits a_i,b_i for i modulo 3. Let g_(a_i)=a_(i+1)+a_(i+2)+b_(i+1) modulo 2 and g_(b_i)=1-g_(a_i). Each pair is oppositely colored at every vertex; the rules ignore their own bits and depend on three other bits, so g is legal antipodally odd and vertex balanced. Starting at zero and traversing (a0,b1,b0,a2,a1,b2) gives a zero full antipodal geodesic. The gadget has no monochromatic three-face: a hypothetical such face must have one free direction in each a_i,b_i pair; changing either free bit of pair i toggles g_(a_(i-1)), hence changes the color of the selected free edge of pair i-1, contradiction. For any original legal odd coloring c of Q_n and integer k>=0, take the already-proved two-way vertex-balanced bounded-Fourier lift ctilde_k on Q_(2n+2k) and form independent-block product C_k=ctilde_k box g on Q_(2n+2k+6). A zero full geodesic in the product projects to one in c. Conversely a zero full geodesic in c lifts to one in ctilde_k, and concatenating with the exhibited zero geodesic in g gives one in the product. Thus C_k has a zero full geodesic if and only if c does. The product is vertex-balanced and odd. Its monochromatic maximal half-face would need all n+k free directions of the corresponding color in the first block and all three in the gadget block, forcing a monochromatic three-face of g; impossible. By the previously proved singleton halfpath support and backward extension theorems, at every vertex both colors admit multiple halfpath direction sets and geodesics of length N/2+1 with that vertex second. Every direction rule has Fourier degree <=max(n-1,3), independent of k; balance kills all top permissible Walsh coefficients. Consequently unrestricted NORI1 is equivalent to its restriction to balanced even-dimensional colorings having no monochromatic half-dimensional face, equivalently double escape at every vertex. The reduction preserves badness in arbitrarily high dimensions and is not a proof of NORI1.
