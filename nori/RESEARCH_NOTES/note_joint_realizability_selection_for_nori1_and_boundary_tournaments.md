## Research note

## Decisive research question

Can a simplicial complex of **jointly realizable** physical path certificates be constructed for both mandatory families: (i) every legal antipodally odd NORI1 cube-edge coloring, and (ii) every ordinary boundary 3-tournament, without assuming a global order on the edges of K_n? Can a topological Hall/Sperner conclusion in this complex force a full monochromatic antipodal edge geodesic or a long vertex-simple monochromatic tight path, respectively?

## Status and evidence

**Open approach, not a theorem.** Aharoni–Haxell's economically hierarchic triangulation amplifies extension against at most |I|−1 boundary constraints into a rainbow collection of *pairwise* compatible labels. Aharoni–Chudnovsky–Kotlov's bounded-neighbor sphere fillings and Holmsen–Martínez-Sandoval–Montejano's q-star connectivity theorem give genuinely wider versions. These published mechanisms are recorded in the Toolkit. They do **not** supply any NORI extension condition or legal full-path extraction.

Known Article II carrier results show that pairwise compatibility, abstract equal-support diagonals, or ambient parity index can lose simultaneous witnessing by actual physical ordered faces. This is a **proved limitation of proposed carriers**, not a proof that every jointly witnessed construction fails. The Q9 legal NORI3 counterexample rules out a theorem applying blindly to all reversal-odd physical three-face colorings, but does not rule out NORI1 or reversal-compatible boundary tournaments.

## Discriminating next investigation

For a small well-specified class of NORI1 partial paths or boundary-tournament tight paths, define a *joint* witness relation: a set of labels is a simplex **iff** a single cube root and coordinate order (for NORI1) or a single vertex-simple ordinary-graph tight path (for tournaments) realizes all labels. Check the relation under forgetting labels and its restriction to index subfamilies. Identify an extension statement quantified over **all** simultaneously realizable boundary constraints, not merely pairwise comparisons; determine whether it yields the required index-face connectivity or an economical triangulation labeling. A small explicit obstruction to this exact extension implication would be valuable and should be recorded with the support class and certificate.

### Exact implication required before investment

A rainbow simplex must decode to ONE FULL desired object. Neither a clique of pairwise consistent windows nor several independently successful proper supports is enough; known root-conditioned Q7 coverage counterexamples and topology warn against this inference. Any proposed axioms should be tested separately on unrestricted NORI1 and on all ordinary boundary tournaments, with the edge-ordered subfamily only a fallback. Avoid claiming success merely from topology or adding a condition that excludes the mandatory families.
