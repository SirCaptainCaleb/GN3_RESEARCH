## Research note

Let n>=5. Ordinary reversal-odd boundary tournaments are precisely orientations of the edges of J(n,2), yielding 2^(n(n-1)(n-2)/2) independent colorings. Rank-two oriented matroids on n labeled elements are realizable by planar signed vector directions and hence have at most (2n)! possibilities, from circular orders of 2n signed directions. For n>=5, 2^(n(n-1)(n-2)/2)>(2n)!. Thus orientation data of the underlying U_{2,n}, with no additional independent exchange-edge choices, cannot encode every boundary tournament. Proof: an exchange edge between {a,v} and {v,c} corresponds to one reversal-pair of triples (a,v,c),(c,v,a); the color of every such pair is independent. The standard rank-two oriented-matroid circular-order classification gives the factorial upper bound. This rules out a source-oriented-matroid-only unification and strengthens the equivariant-base-polarity obstruction. It does not rule out matroids with extra flag or cover elements.
