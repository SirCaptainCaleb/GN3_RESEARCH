## Research note

Let two cube-edge colorings f,g be cyclic-local. For every direction i let Di be neighboring-bit pairs where fi=0 and gi=1. If each Di contains at least three of four inputs, then for ANY fixed permutation of directions some starting root traverses the full geodesic with f word all0 and g word all1. Proof: fixing the order converts each Di into a binary relation between the starting bits of directions i-1,i+1, still with at least three allowed pairs. These relations form one binary constraint cycle when n is odd, two when n even (with parallel edges for n=4). Every relation with at most one forbidden pair is closed under relational composition and contains some diagonal pair. Therefore each cycle admits a consistent assignment, recovered by backtracking in O(n). This improves the local-overlap theorem and requires neither selecting the order nor a topological root argument.
