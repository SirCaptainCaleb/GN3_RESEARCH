## Research note

For any antipodally odd vertex-balanced edge coloring c of Q4, the projective Petrie cycles have only odd grades 1 and 3. Their total is M=2^(4-2)*(4-1)!=24. The universal mean switch identity gives E(S)=4/2=2, and E(S)=(n-1)T1/(nM), where T1=sum_C grade(C). Thus T1=(4/3)*2*24=64. Solving N1+N3=24 and N1+3N3=64 gives N1=4 and N3=20 for every such balanced coloring. Let R be zero-edge adjacency and P antipodal permutation, so tr(P R^4)=2N1=8. For the antipodally symmetrized weighted adjacency Rbar=A/2, one has tr(P Rbar^4)=4!=24, since tr(P A^4)=2^4*4!. Hence the inequality tr(P R^n)>=tr(P(A/2)^n), even when restricted to legal vertex-balanced R satisfying PRP=A-R, is false in Q4 by factor three. This rules out the naive operator Jensen/pinching lower bound for the first odd matching-trace coefficient; the fixed pinching endpoint values do not force its slope at zero. Independent full enumeration of all 65,536 legal Q4 colorings found 48 balanced cases, all with N1=4, and agrees with the moment proof.
