## Research note

Let d>=3 be odd and let N=2^(d-1). In the folded cube Cayley model use generators g_i having the unique relation sum_i g_i=0. Over F2 let T_i be translation by g_i, and for nonempty label set I define A_I=sum_{i in I}T_i. THEOREM: if |I| is odd, A_I^2=I and rank A_I=N. If |I| is even, then I is proper, A_I^2=0, rank A_I=N/2, and ker A_I=im A_I. A contracting homotopy is explicit: choose i in I and a linear character ell on the vertex group with ell(g_i)=1 and ell(g_j)=0 for all j in I different from i; possible because every proper generator subset is independent. Let M_ell be diagonal multiplication by ell(x) and set H=M_ell T_i. Each translation satisfies T_j M_ell+M_ell T_j=ell(g_j)T_j. Therefore A_I H+H A_I=I, and when A_I^2=0 this yields ker A_I=im A_I and rank N/2. COROLLARY: for any orientation D_I of the subgraph using directions I, D_I+D_I^T=A_I in F2, hence rank D_I>=N/2 for odd |I| and rank D_I>=N/4 for nonempty even |I|, also over R. LIMITATION: all proper even-direction undirected binary complexes are contractible independently of legal coloring; ordinary mod-two homology cannot distinguish a NORI1 counterexample from a successful coloring without orientation-sensitive structure.
