## Research note

Let \(n=2m\ge4\), and fix \(o=0^n\in Q_n\). Color an undirected edge joining the Hamming layers \(j\) and \(j+1\) about \(o\) by \(c(e)=j\bmod2\).

**Theorem (root transport is indispensable).** This is an antipodally odd coloring. Every full geodesic from \(o\) has the alternating color word \(0,1,0,1,\dots,1\), independently of its coordinate permutation. Hence none has at most one change. On the other hand, a full monochromatic antipodal geodesic exists precisely from starting vertices in the middle Hamming layer \(\{x:|x|=m\}\), and from each such \(x\) there are \(2(m!)^2\) directed monochromatic antipodal geodesics. The total directed count is \(2(2m)!\), a fraction \(2^{1-n}\) of all directed full cube geodesics.

**Proof.** Under cube complementation an edge joining layers \(j,j+1\) is carried to one joining layers \(n-j-1,n-j\). Since \(n\) is even, \(n-j-1\equiv j+1\pmod2\), proving antipodal oddness. Every geodesic from \(o\) increases its Hamming weight by one at each step; its successive edge colors are \(0,1,\ldots,n-1\bmod2\).

Any monochromatic walk under this coloring has its Hamming-layer coordinate oscillating between two consecutive levels: after crossing from level \(j\) to \(j+1\), the next edge with the same color must cross back, because the forward crossing to \(j+2\) has the other color. The same holds for a downward step. Hence a monochromatic full antipodal geodesic has equal initial and terminal Hamming weights, forcing \(|x|=n-|x|=m\). Conversely, for any \(x\) of weight \(m\), choose arbitrary orders of its \(m\) zero coordinates and \(m\) one coordinates. Alternately flip one zero then one one, or one one then one zero. The Hamming weight alternates between \(m,m+1\) or \(m,m-1\), so all \(2m\) edges have the same color. There are \((m!)^2\) choices for either alternation type, and every monochromatic full geodesic must alternate up/down and belongs to one of these families. With \(\binom{2m}{m}\) middle-layer roots the total is \(2\binom{2m}{m}(m!)^2=2(2m)!\). All directed full geodesics number \(2^n n!\), giving fraction \(2^{1-n}\).

**Rooted-gallery reversal identity.** More generally, for any antipodally odd cube coloring, fixed root \(x\), and direction-permutation \(\pi\in S_n\), write \(C_x(\pi)\) for the \(n\)-bit color word of the associated full geodesic. Then
\[
\boxed{C_x(\operatorname{rev}\pi)=\mathbf1-\operatorname{rev} C_x(\pi).}
\]
Indeed reverse the original path and apply the cube antipode: it begins at \(x\), follows directions in the reversed order, and every edge color is complemented. This action on the permutation vertices is free, but cannot force a rooted low-switch witness: the even-dimensional layer coloring above realizes the alternating word on every permutation from \(o\). Its reversal-complement symmetry follows directly for even \(n\).

**Methodological implication.** A proof using solely adjacent token transpositions within the rooted permutohedron and the rooted reversal-complement identity cannot ensure a full one-switch path at its prescribed root. The variable root and its transport between gallery systems must enter. The result is an obstruction to *fixed-root extraction*, not a counterexample to NORI1: monochromatic full geodesics occur at the middle-layer roots.
