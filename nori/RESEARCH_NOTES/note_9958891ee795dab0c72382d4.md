## Research note

For a legal antipodally odd cube edge coloring, let U_i flip bit i on a zero-colored i-edge, otherwise fix the vertex. For distinct i,j, U_i and U_j commute if and only if c_i is invariant under flipping bit j and c_j is invariant under flipping bit i. Proof: if U_i,U_j commute, compare bit i in U_i U_j(x) and U_j U_i(x) along a red j-edge to obtain c_i(x)=c_i(x xor e_j). For a blue j-edge, the antipodal edge is red and oddness transports this equality back. Interchange i,j. The converse is immediate. Therefore the U-commutator graph equals the undirected Boolean dependency graph. Since every c_i is odd under all-bit complementation and ignores bit i, some other bit influences it: no vertex of the commutator graph is isolated. Its components are independent Cartesian coloring factors. Verified on random legal cubes of dimensions 2 through 6 (540 total instances).
