## Research note

An uncolored edge-star at every cube vertex already has disjoint antipodal cones, one-dimension-lower equivariant sphere complements, and homotopy-equivalent root transports across all edges. These topological properties alone cannot imply NORI1; higher monochromatic prefix simplices carrying used-dimension information are indispensable.\n\nTheorem. For n>=3, D_x is the union of the n closed cube edges incident to x. It is a collapsible tree, with D_barx = A D_x. The two are disjoint since x and barx are graph-distance n apart. For adjacent x,y the common edge e=xy is contained in D_x intersection D_y. Write Z_x = S^(n-1) minus (D_x union A D_x) and Z_e = S^(n-1) minus (e union A e). Then Z_x and Z_e have equivariant homotopy type S^(n-2), and the equivariant inclusions Z_x -> Z_e <- Z_y are homotopy equivalences. This holds independent of all colorings. Proof. An edge-star contracts to its apex, and its antipodal pair is disjoint. Their complement in the cube boundary has equivariant homotopy sphere type by the projective regular-neighborhood argument. Inclusion of the edge pair e union Ae into the star pair D_x union AD_x identifies the two collapsible components. Naturality of integral Alexander duality gives an integral homology isomorphism on complements. Both complements have the homotopy type of S^(n-2), so this is a homotopy equivalence; free equivariant Whitehead upgrades it to an equivariant homotopy equivalence. Similarly at y. Consequence: any argument using solely the cone and complement sphere data, adjacent common bridges, and their fundamental-class transports cannot distinguish monochromatic prefix complexes from these uncolored edge-stars. The omitted higher support-graded simplices must enter the proof.
