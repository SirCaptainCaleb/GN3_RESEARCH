## Research note

For arbitrary edge coloring f and root r not admitting a nondecreasing full geodesic, orient the cube from r by subsets. Write Z for zero-only reachable states, V for nondecreasing-prefix reachable states, U for states permitting nondecreasing completion to the top, and O for states permitting all-one completion. Then Z intersect U and V intersect O are both empty. Changing a one edge u-v to zero restores r precisely when u lies in Z and v lies in U. Changing a zero edge u-v to one restores r precisely when u lies in V and v lies in O. Necessity follows by splitting a newly favorable path at the recolored edge, and sufficiency by concatenating its specified prefix and suffix.
