## Research note

Let c_i(x) in F2 be the binary color of an undirected edge in direction i of Q_n, with c_i(x+e_i)=c_i(x) and c_i(bar x)=1+c_i(x). Let w(x,p) be the full antipodal geodesic profile indexed by direction, W_x its set over all orders p. For distinct i,j define v_ij(u)= (c_i(u)+c_i(u+e_j))e_i +(c_j(u)+c_j(u+e_i))e_j, and let V be the linear span of all these vectors.

THEOREM 1: For every root x, aff(W_x)=x+a+V, where a may be chosen as w(0,p0) for any order p0. PROOF: Two orders differing by an adjacent swap i,j after prefix A have profile difference v_ij(x+e_A). These swaps connect all direction orders. Each v_ij is independent of bits i,j because ci ignores i and cj ignores j, so every such v_ij(u) is realized by a swap at any fixed root x by choosing the prefix A to realize the other n-2 bits. Hence span(W_x-W_x)=V independently of x. For y orthogonal to V, the weighted edge cochain omega_i(u)=y_i*c_i(u) has zero circulation around all squares. Thus omega is a vertex gradient phi(u)+phi(u+e_i). Along every full geodesic from x, y dot w(x,p)=phi(x)+phi(bar x)=psi(x). Oddness gives psi(x)+psi(x+e_i)=omega_i(x)+omega_i(bar x)=y_i. Hence psi(x)=psi(0)+y dot x. Since psi(0)=y dot a, every w(x,p)+x+a is in V. Together with the difference-span result this proves the affine-hull formula.

THEOREM 2: dim V>=ceil(n/2), sharp for every n>=2. PROOF: Each ci is nonconstant on the other bits because it reverses under the antipodal map. Thus for every direction i there are j != i and u with partial_j ci(u)=1. Its v_ij(u) is a generator of V supported on at most two indices and containing i. A basis selected from the two-sparse generators must cover every one of the n indices, so n<=2 dim V. Sharpness: for n=2m, divide directions into pairs a,b with c_a=x_b and c_b=x_a. Then V is spanned by the m vectors e_a+e_b. For n=2m+3, append a three-direction factor with c_0=x_2,c_1=x_2,c_2=x_1, whose V is spanned by e_0 and e_1+e_2. The total dimension is m+2=ceil(n/2).

COROLLARY: For uniform root X, any chosen order P (including an order depending on X) satisfies w(X,P)+V=X+a+V. Thus the profile quotient F2^n/V is uniformly distributed. The result concerns affine hulls; it does not claim all words in a root's coset are attained. Exhaustive enumeration in Q4 gives rooted ranks 2,3,4 with frequencies 768,100096,947712 among 65536 colorings times 16 roots. Random Q2-Q6 checks confirm the affine coset identity.
