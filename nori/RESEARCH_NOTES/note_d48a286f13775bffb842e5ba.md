## Research note

Conjecture: for every even n and every legal coloring q obtained by changing arbitrary antipodal edge pairs in one direction i of a stationary parity coloring, its M=n!/2 monochromatic-zero antipodal paths contain K=binomial(n-1,n/2) members pairwise disjoint in all edge-pair orbits of directions other than i. Equivalently, the minimum exterior edge-orbit transversal has size at least K. This would improve the proved two-scale robustness threshold from K/(3/2-1/n) to K. The conjecture is unproved. A joint mixed-integer search chose both an arbitrary i-bridge selector and a minimum exterior transversal, considering all possible selector choices without enumerating them: optimum is 3 in n=4 and 10 in n=6, both equal K. Separate path-packing optimizations in n=6 found at least K disjoint paths for every sampled selector; a stronger packetwise simultaneous-choice problem remained unresolved. The proof target is a dimension-uniform multicolor path-packing or symmetric-chain construction, without specializing hypothetical NORI1 counterexamples.
