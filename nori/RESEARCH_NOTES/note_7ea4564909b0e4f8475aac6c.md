## Research note

Article IX research direction: treat the ordinary edge coloring lambda and binary reversal-odd coloring chi as independent constraints on ONE path, and seek a topological selection theorem guaranteeing a witness satisfying both. Existing Toolkit literature_fixed_point_theorems includes Gale colorful KKM, and literature_holmsen_martinez_montejano_general_position_complex_q_star_2016 includes the Kalai–Meshulam colorful-simplex criterion. Three more directly compatible theorem families have now been added: (1) literature_meunier_su_multilabeled_sperner_fan_simultaneous_simplex: two or more separate Sperner labelings have one simplex with prescribed simultaneous diversity quotas, and independent Fan labelings have alternating faces on one simplex with quotas summing to the Z2-index; (2) literature_mcginnis_matroid_colored_kkomiya_kkm_simultaneous_selection: a matroid-basis selection together with one common intersection under Komiya/KKM covering hypotheses; (3) literature_frick_wellner_colorful_borsuk_ulam_simultaneous_cover_selectors: antipodal covering families or face-respecting continuous response maps allow common-point selections with prescribed signs or designated maximal responses. A common mathematical program is to find an intrinsic space of ordered partial path states for both NORI1 and boundary 3-tournaments, prove the relevant topological face-covering or equivariant-labeling hypothesis from the vertex involution and ordinary color incidence, and establish that the resulting simplex/common point assembles into ONE vertex-simple rainbow path whose binary word has at most one switch. The topology supplies a simultaneous selector, not the assembly lemma, and no ordinary-color preservation by tau is assumed. Do not add an artificial state complex merely to invoke a theorem without checking its simplices correspond to realizable paths.
