## Research note

THEOREM. Let the fifteen nonzero points of F2^4 have lifts v_t=(epsilon_t,t) in F2^5. There are 105 four-element sets Q with xor of their quotient points zero; at least 45 of them also have xor of the full lifted columns zero. Proof. Let f(0)=0 and f(t)=(-1)^epsilon_t elsewhere. The 16 unnormalized Walsh transforms F(u) are odd integers, with sum_u F(u)^2=240. For any odd integer F, writing q=F^2 gives q^2>=34q-225, because q belongs to 1,9,25,49,..., and equality holds at 9,25. Consequently sum F(u)^4>=4560. The additive quadruple energy is sum F^4/16>=285. The 645 ordered zero-sum quadruples with repeated entries contribute +645, and the others contribute 24 times the signed sum chi over the 105 distinct quartets. Therefore chi>=-15. Since the number N of zero-lift-sum quartets equals (105+chi)/2, one has N>=45. Independent enumeration of 16384 odd-parity lift patterns confirms sharpness, attained by 448 patterns. Full proof and checking script: nori_rank5_projective_reduction.md and nori_rank5_projective_certificates.py. This counts simultaneous dependencies of one matrix; it does not by itself prove that a compatible quartet permits a legal peeling.
