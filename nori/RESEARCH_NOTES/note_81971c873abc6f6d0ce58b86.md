## Research note

Under orientation reversal J on the oriented 2-plane Grassmannian, the positive-prefix transversal U_T is invariant, its canonical frame (e1,e2) becomes (e1,-e2), and its deformation retraction to S(T-perp) is Z2-equivariant. Therefore U_T/J has homotopy type RP^(n-3) and its covering class has zero (n-2)-th power. In particular, no equivariant map S^(n-2) -> U_T exists from the antipodal type-A Coxeter sphere, even though each proper ordered-parabolic cell has all its extreme-switch plane vertices in some U_T associated with its first block. This locates the root-selection obstruction in transitions between positive-prefix transversals rather than in any one local chart.
