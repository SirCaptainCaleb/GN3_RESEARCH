## Research note

Let A_c(x) be the maximum length of a direction-simple path beginning at x with color word 0*1*. For independent cube colorings c and d, A_(c box d)(x,y)=A_c(x)+A_d(y). Projection proves <=; to obtain >=, interleave the zero runs from factor paths and then interleave their one runs. Reversing a one-change path changes word 1*0* to 0*1*, so the unrestricted maximum L obeys L(c box d)=L(c)+L(d). Now let k(c) be the smallest switch count of any full antipodal geodesic. For a starting color a, set k_a(c)=min_P (switches(P)+1_[firstcolor(P)!=a]). Then k(c box d)=min_a max(k_a(c),k_a(d)): projection bounds each factor, and merging their alternating monochromatic runs in aligned phases attains the bound. If c and d are antipodally odd, antipodal translation of a full path complements its color word, so k_a(c)=k(c) for either a. Hence k(c box d)=max(k(c),k(d)). In particular for every arbitrary coloring c and h>=1, k(c to the h-fold Cartesian power)=k(c). Thus product powers amplify omitted directions in one-change paths, but do not amplify full-geodesic minimum switch count.
