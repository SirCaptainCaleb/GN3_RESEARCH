## Research note

**Proposition (canonical oriented matroid basis covers).** For a loopless rank-r matroid M, take two orientation choices of every basis B, and connect two oriented bases whose underlying bases differ by one exchange when the orientations induced on their common (r-1)-element set are opposite. The resulting graph is a two-sheet covering of the ordinary basis-exchange graph. For M=(direct sum over i=1..d of U_{1,2}), it has two connected components each isomorphic to Q_d: write an orientation sign eps relative to the block-ordered basis; exchanging the element in one block changes eps, so eps times (-1)^(number of selected 1-elements) is invariant. For M=U_{2,n}, the oriented bases are pairs (a,b), and opposite induced orientation is precisely the ordered-pair overlap adjacency (a,b)~(b,c), with a,b,c distinct. Its involution swaps (a,b) and (b,a). The projection to J(n,2) has trivial monodromy on top triangles ({a,b},{b,c},{c,a}) and nontrivial monodromy on star triangles ({a,b},{a,c},{a,d}), as follows by lifting the ordered pairs. This distinguishes the canonical cover from parity lifts which turn top triangles into six-cycles.

Give each oriented-basis adjacency the ordinary color of the inserted element, modulo parallel classes, when traversed from the endpoint inducing the positive reference orientation on the shared facet toward its negative endpoint. This reproduces cubical coordinate colors for the partition matroid, and the third-vertex coloring c for (a,b)~(b,c) in U_{2,n}. The reference orientation is the increasing ground-set order.

**Theorem (Hamming positive baseline).** Let M be the direct sum of d copies of U_{1,q}, q>=2, and let G be any connected component of its canonical oriented-basis graph, equipped with this parallel-class coloring. Then, for every binary edge labeling (without an involutive assumption), G has a zero-switch rainbow simple path of length at least d/8. Indeed each coordinate-color subgraph of the full oriented graph is a union of regular bipartite K_{q,q}-matching graphs (for q=2 a union of isolated edges). Choose a perfect matching from each coordinate-color subgraph. Their union is a spanning d-regular properly d-edge-colored subgraph. At least half its edges have one binary label; in that one-label subgraph the average degree is at least d/2, and a nonempty subgraph of minimum degree at least d/4 exists by iterative low-degree deletion. A maximal rainbow simple path in this core has length at least d/8, since proper edge colors bound the number of used-color outgoing edges at its endpoint by its length and at most that many endpoint neighbors already belong to the path. In the q=2 disconnected case use one cube component; each coordinate-color subgraph there is itself a perfect matching. This proves the assertion.

**Structural conjecture candidate.** Require (i) canonical oriented-basis exchange geometry and its intrinsic entered-element resource coloring; (ii) all matroid circuits have at most three elements; (iii) the induced matroid automorphism action on nontrivial parallel-class resources is 2-transitive; and (iv) the canonical reversal polarity (orientation deck involution for a connected rank-two model, antipodal parallel-pair complement for the cubical model), with arbitrary binary labels subject only to chi(tau e)=1-chi(e). Conjecture that L_1>=k/8 for ordinary minimum color degree k. Its rank-one homogeneous Hamming part follows from the theorem. A connected rank-two matroid with p>=3 equal-size parallel classes contains the reversal-invariant induced B_p on any choice of one representative per class; the restricted binary labeling is an arbitrary reversal-odd boundary coloring, so the boundary Hamiltonian one-change conjecture would imply a path of p-2 edges, linear in the ordinary color degree p (or p-1 when class size one). This is a candidate condition and reduction, with the rank-two assertion open. No claim is made that 2-transitivity is necessary or that this is the weakest possible condition.

**Known obstruction.** The graph from M=U_{2,m} direct-sum U_{1,2} has the canonical oriented-basis exchange geometry yet admits a color-preserving involution and reversal-odd binary labels with one-switch paths O(log m), as recorded in note_12c735386a943993b3aac032. This matroid's resource palette has two distinguished kinds, so it violates condition (iii).
