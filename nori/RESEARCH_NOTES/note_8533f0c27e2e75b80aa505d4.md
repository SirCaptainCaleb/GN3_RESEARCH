## Research note

Let d=(d_k)_(k=1)^m be an arbitrary undirected binary edge coloring of Q_m: d_k(z)=d_k(z+e_k). Add bits s,t and define C on Q_(m+2) by C_s(z,s,t)=1+t and C_t(z,s,t)=1+s. For each original direction k, set C_k(z,0,0)=0; C_k(z,1,0)=d_k(z); C_k(z,0,1)=1+d_k(bar z); C_k(z,1,1)=1, with binary color arithmetic.

THEOREM (two-bit suspension and witness-count identity). C is legal and antipodally odd. If N_R(C) counts oriented rooted red full antipodal geodesics, and N_BR(d) counts oriented rooted full antipodal geodesics of d whose colors have the form 1^r 0^(m-r) (0<=r<=m), then N_R(C)=2 N_BR(d). Consequently C has a monochromatic full antipodal geodesic if and only if d has a full antipodal geodesic with at most one color change.

PROOF. Edge consistency is inherited from d. Antipodal oddness pairs the faces st=00 and 11 and the faces 10 and 01; the helper rules 1+t,1+s are odd. Face 00 is isolated in the red graph: its original-direction edges are red and its helper edges are blue. All original-direction edges in face 11 are blue. The helper-red connections form 10--11--01. Thus a red full geodesic traverses these two helper edges consecutively, starts in 10 and ends in 01, or reverses that arrangement.

Orient the red lifted path from 10 to 01. Its original-direction prefix inside 10 is a red d-path R from v to bar u. After the two helper edges, its original-direction suffix inside 01 runs from bar u to bar v. Antipodally transporting this suffix yields a blue d-path B from u to v. The concatenation B R is a full d-geodesic from u to bar u with a blue prefix and red suffix. Conversely, given such a decomposition B:u->v and R:v->bar u, the lifted red path starts at (v,1,0), follows R, crosses t then s to face 01, and follows the antipodal image of B to (bar v,0,1). This is a red full geodesic. Reversal bijects the lifted red geodesics starting in 01 with those starting in 10, giving the factor two. A blue full geodesic in C has a red antipodal image.

A one-switch geodesic in any odd coloring can be made monochromatic by starting at its switch vertex, following its red segment and then the antipodal image of its blue segment. Therefore the universal NORI1 conjecture and the universal arbitrary-coloring full-geodesic one-switch conjecture are equivalent, with this explicit dimension-(m+2) transfer proving the reverse implication.

UNIFORM ROOTED OBSTRUCTION. For n>=4, set C_0=1+x_1, C_1=1+x_0, C_2=x_0, C_3=x_1, and C_k=x_0 for k>=4. This is legal and odd. Its red component through zero is the induced (n-2)-cube x_0=x_1=0; every red path rooted there is confined to those n-2 directions. It contains a red geodesic using all of them. The three other starting corners e_0,e_1,e_0+e_1 of the omitted-direction square admit no red path using all directions 2,...,n-1, because at least one of directions 2,3 is persistently blue. Nonetheless (3,1,0,2,4,...,n-1) is a full red geodesic from e_0. Hence both root-preserving near-path augmentation and fixed omitted-square rerouting fail uniformly in this affine family.

This theorem identifies the unrestricted one-switch problem inside legal odd colorings; it does not settle that problem.

CANONICAL CLASSIFICATION AND SHARP CODIMENSION. Let c be a legal odd coloring on Q_n, and suppose F is an all-red face of codimension two which is a connected component of the red subgraph. After relabeling and complementing the fixed bits, assume F has helper bits s=t=0. All original-direction edges in F are red, and all helper edges leaving F are blue. Edge consistency makes c_s(z,s,0)=1 independently of s, and c_t(z,0,t)=1 independently of t. Antipodal oddness forces c_s=1+t and c_t=1+s globally. It forces the original-direction edges in the opposite face 11 to be blue, and identifies those in face 01 as the complement of the colors in face 10 after antipodally complementing z. Thus c coincides with the suspension of the arbitrary coloring d=c|_(face 10). Conversely every suspension has this isolated red face. Therefore arbitrary binary edge colorings of Q_(n-2) are in bijection with legal odd colorings of Q_n carrying a designated isolated all-red codimension-two face.

There is no isolated all-red face of codimension one in any legal odd cube coloring. If F is the face s=0, each transverse s-edge incident with F would be blue. Edge consistency makes the s-edges incident with the opposite face s=1 blue as well, contradicting the demand that antipodal s-edges have complementary colors. Codimension two is consequently the first possible codimension of an isolated all-red face, and its classification realizes the full unrestricted one-switch difficulty.


COUNTEREXAMPLE NORMAL FORM. If an odd coloring c of Q_n has no monochromatic full geodesic, then it has no one-switch full geodesic either: a blue-then-red geodesic could be rooted at its switch, followed by its red suffix and the antipodal translate of its blue prefix, producing a red full geodesic. Regard c as an ordinary coloring d and apply the suspension. The resulting odd coloring on Q_(n+2) has no monochromatic full geodesic, yet it possesses an isolated all-red Q_n face containing 2^n n! oriented rooted red geodesics of length n. Thus a counterexample, if one exists, can be taken in the canonical isolated-face class. In this suspended counterexample, for the two helper labels s,t, the only roots of red direction-simple paths using all other n labels lie in the isolated 00 face: face 11 has no red original-direction edges, while the existence of such a path within face 10 or 01 would yield a monochromatic full geodesic in d, hence a one-switch witness. Therefore R_(s,t)=2^n, saturating the source threshold 2^((n+2)-2).

More generally, for any ordinary d that has neither red nor blue full geodesics, its suspension has R_(s,t)=2^m even though it may have a full monochromatic geodesic. For example, take d to be constant in each direction with at least one permanently red direction and at least one permanently blue direction; it has a blue-then-red full geodesic by ordering the directions, while no monochromatic full geodesic exists in d. The suspension then simultaneously has a successful full red geodesic and saturates the one-quarter source bound for the helper pair. This demonstrates sharpness of the strict source threshold independently of the existence of any NORI1 counterexample.
