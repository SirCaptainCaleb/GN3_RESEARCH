## Research note

For every n>=4, an antipodally odd binary edge-coloring of Q_n admits monochromatic full antipodal geodesics but can have a prescribed sweep order pi with U_pi^2(x) != bar x for every root x. Moreover, in dimension n=4r, there are good antipodally odd colorings and prescribed orders with d_H(U_pi^2(x),bar x)>=r for every root x. Here U_i swaps a color-zero i-edge and fixes a vertex if that i-edge has color one; U_pi composes the U_i once in order pi.

Proof. In Q_4 index bits 0,1,2,3 and put c_0=x_1, c_1=x_0, c_2=x_0, c_3=x_1. The rules ignore their own bits and reverse under bitwise complement. For pi=(0,1,2,3), the first two gates U_1 U_0 induce the permutation (00 10 01) on (x_0,x_1), fixing 11; U_2,U_3 leave those bits unchanged. Its square sends the first two bits to their complement only when initially 01. For that initial pair, the first sweep sends 01 to 00, then flips bits 2 and 3. The second sweep sends 00 to 10, leaves bit 2 unchanged, and flips bit 3 again. Thus bit 3 returns to its original value, so d_H(U_pi^2(x),bar x)>=1 for every x. The coloring nevertheless admits a zero full geodesic from integer root x=1 in order (0,2,3,1).

For n>4 retain these rules and put c_i(x)=x_0 for i>=4. The old bits evolve independently of the added bits under the order (0,1,...,n-1), so antipodal two-sweep reachability still fails at every root. The extension has a zero full geodesic from integer root x=1 in order (0,4,5,...,n-1,2,3,1).

For n=4r, take r Cartesian copies of the Q_4 coloring, each traversed in local order (0,1,2,3), and let pi concatenate those r orders. The gates for different factors commute, so U_pi^2 acts blockwise. The Hamming deficiencies add and each factor contributes at least one, proving the lower bound r. Every factor possesses a zero full geodesic, so their product does also, by concatenating the factor paths.

Therefore a fixed-order two-sweep argument cannot ensure a witness, or even a sublinear antipodal Hamming deficit. Any unrestricted proof using this sufficient criterion must vary the order or combine orders; the existential two-sweep conjecture remains open.
