## Research note

**Theorem (strict coherent transport over monochromatic cubical faces).** Under a hypothetical counterexample to the NORI1 monochromatic-geodesic conjecture, let \(F\) be any nonempty proper cubical face of \(Q_n\), all of whose cube edges have the same color \(a\). Put
\[
Y_F=\partial[0,1]^n\setminus(F\cup AF).
\]
For every vertex \(x\) of \(F\), the entire geometric face \(F\) is contained in the color-\(a\) geodesic-prefix cone \(K_x^a\), and hence there is a canonical equivariant inclusion
\[
Y_x^a=\partial[0,1]^n\setminus(K_x^a\cup AK_x^a)
\hookrightarrow Y_F.
\]
Every such inclusion is an equivariant homotopy equivalence. Moreover, whenever \(F\subseteq G\) are monochromatic cubical faces of color \(a\), the inclusions \(Y_G\hookrightarrow Y_F\) give a strictly commuting diagram of equivariant homotopy equivalences. Every path used in constructing these inclusions traverses each free dimension of its face at most once.

*Proof.* From any corner \(x\in F\), a monotone geodesic to any other corner of \(F\) uses only the dimensions varying in \(F\), without repetition. Since all edges of \(F\) have color \(a\), every rooted Freudenthal simplex in its canonical triangulation is an \(a\)-monochromatic geodesic-prefix simplex. Their union is the whole face \(F\), proving \(F\subseteq K_x^a\). Being proper, \(F\) has some fixed dimension, so \(F\cap AF=\varnothing\). It is a convex cubical ball and hence collapsible. The complement \(Y_F\) has the equivariant homotopy type of \(S^{n-2}\) by the same regular-neighborhood argument as the preceding dimension-drop theorem.

The inclusion \(F\cup AF\hookrightarrow K_x^a\cup AK_x^a\) bijects their two contractible components, so Alexander duality makes the complement inclusion \(Y_x^a\hookrightarrow Y_F\) an integral homology equivalence, hence an equivariant homotopy equivalence between antipodal homotopy spheres. If \(F\subseteq G\), then \(Y_G\subseteq Y_F\), and these inclusions commute on the nose. The same duality argument proves every map is an equivariant homotopy equivalence. \(\square\)

**Interpretation.** The colored-geodesic carrier problem already admits coherent higher-dimensional transport on the entire subposet of monochromatic cubical faces, of every rank. The missing chains must deal with **mixed-color** cubical faces. This matches the local color-filtered Freudenthal exchange-defect formula: its unmatched terms arise when the two monotone routes through a square do not have the same monochromatic eligibility. No extra coloring restriction is introduced, and this theorem alone does not fill the mixed faces or establish the full NORI1 conjecture.
