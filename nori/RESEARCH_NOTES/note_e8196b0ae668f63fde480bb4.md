## Research note

Fix an antipodally odd coloring c of Q_n, with U_i the involution that swaps a zero i-edge and fixes a vertex whose i-edge is one. A red direction-simple path A can be realized as the nonstationary steps of a sweep if and only if each direction i absent from A has a color-one edge at some vertex of A. Indeed the omitted directions must be stationary updates; conversely insert all of them at suitable visited vertices, where they leave the path unchanged.

**Theorem (paired-sweep interval certificate).** Let P=(v_0,...,v_n) be a zero full antipodal geodesic, with direction i traversed in step j_i (1<=j_i<=n). Set L_i=min{t:c_i(v_t)=1}, R_i=max{t:c_i(v_t)=1}. Both are defined by antipodal oddness, which gives c_i(v_n)=1-c_i(v_0). Let
\[
I_i=[\min(L_i,j_i),\max(R_i,j_i-1)]\cap\mathbb Z.
\]
Then the split positions k at which the prefix from v_0 and the reversed suffix from v_n both admit completion into full red sweeps are precisely
\[
\boxed{\bigcap_{i=1}^n I_i.}
\]
Failure for a specified path is certified by a pair of directions with disjoint intervals (the interval Helly theorem).

*Proof.* A direction i used after split k is missing from the prefix and must be idly offered at a prefix vertex, requiring L_i<=k<j_i. A direction i used before or at split k is missing from the reversed suffix, and must be idly offered in that suffix, requiring j_i<=k<=R_i. Because c_i(v_(j_i-1))=c_i(v_(j_i))=0, the union of these ranges (discarding empty ranges) is one interval I_i. The preliminary blue-idle observation then proves the claim.

**Endpoint-color corollary.** If c_i(v_0)=1 then L_i=0 and I_i starts at 0. If c_i(v_0)=0 then R_i=n and I_i ends at n. Therefore the permissible splits form [alpha,beta] with alpha=max_(i:c_i(v0)=0) min(L_i,j_i) and beta=min_(i:c_i(v0)=1) max(R_i,j_i-1), taking empty maximum 0 and empty minimum n. If alpha>beta then an initially one direction must occur earlier in P than an initially zero direction. In particular a zero full geodesic that traverses all its initially zero directions before all its initially one directions is paired-sweep-completable at the boundary.

**Audit.** Independent exhaustive rootwise sweep comparisons agree with this criterion in 40 random legal Q3, 50 Q4, and 50 Q5 colorings, and in the published legal Q5 rootwise obstruction (tables 7331,9966,ACCA,10F7,3B23), excluding roots 14 and 17. Verification file: nori_sweep_intervals_audit.py.

This gives a dimension-uniform completion criterion for an already supplied full red geodesic. It does not establish its unrestricted existence, so NORI1 remains open.
