# Nonlinear square-switch input cocycles

Let Q_(2m) have legal paired-flat red gates f_i(p_-i,t_-i), where the red pair matching T_i toggles pair parity p_i and toggles fiber bit t_i by 1+f_i. A legal antipodally paired alternating-square switch in pair i toggles its gate at a prescribed exterior parity u and two complementary exterior fiber assignments z,z+1.

THEOREM 1. On a fixed intermediate parity fiber, the old and new red pair-i matchings differ by a fiber permutation L_i consisting of two disjoint transpositions, or by the identity if the exterior parity is not u. The two transpositions swap the two values of t_i over exterior fiber assignment z and over exterior assignment z+1. Consequently rank(P_L-I)=2 when affected, the changed source set has size four, and L commutes with fiber antipode J:t->t+1.

Proof. The selected red direction in pair i reverses at the two switched exterior assignments and nowhere else. Because its gate ignores t_i, both values of t_i are exchanged at each exterior assignment. These swaps are disjoint, with permutation matrix differences of rank one each. Complementation interchanges the two swaps, so J and L commute.

THEOREM 2 (common-prefix simultaneous transport). Fix a starting parity p, a pair label i, a prefix A of distinct other pair labels, and any suffix B ordering all remaining labels. Let R_A be the old red fiber permutation of A, and L the local transposition pair of Theorem 1 at parity after A (or identity). Define tau_A=R_A^(-1) L R_A. Then F'_(A,i,B)=F_(A,i,B) tau_A for EVERY suffix B. Thus every changed half-sweep permutation matrix differs from the old by rank two; all endpoint matrices with prefix (A,i) are right-multiplied by the SAME permutation matrix P_tau. Their affine span dimensions, linear relations and the singular values of the matrix sum over all suffixes are unchanged by this switch.

Proof. The prefix and suffix contain no pair i, so their red matchings are unchanged. Factor the full sweep as F=R_B T_i R_A and F'=R_B T_i L R_A=F R_A^(-1)L R_A. The remaining conclusions follow from rank and similarity invariance of permutation matrices.

MIDPOINT GRAM LAW. For complete pair orders pi,rho sharing prefix (A,i), define W_(pi,rho)=tr(P_pi^T P_rho J). The switched collision count is W'_(pi,rho)=tr(P_pi^T P_rho tau_A J tau_A^(-1)). The replacement antipodal matching tau_A J tau_A^(-1) differs from J on at most eight source states, and its permutation matrix differs from J by rank at most four. Thus the entire common-prefix collision Gram family changes through ONE shared low-rank antipodal pairing perturbation, not independent changes for each order pair.

Proof. Substitute P'_pi=P_pi P_tau and P'_rho=P_rho P_tau into the trace and apply cyclicity. The permutations tau and J tau J have supports of size at most four; their product alters at most eight states. Expand tau J tau^(-1)-J=(tau-I)J tau^(-1)+J(tau^(-1)-I), with each term of rank at most two.

THEOREM 3 (prefix-order conjugacy). If A and B order the same subset D of pair labels preceding switched label i, and both read the switched exterior parity, then tau_B=H tau_A H^(-1), with H=(R_B)^(-1)R_A. Proof: tau_A=(R_A)^(-1)L R_A and tau_B=(R_B)^(-1)L R_B; substitute and cancel. Thus perturbations for different predecessor orders are linked by the original nonlinear order-exchange permutations. This is the shared-gate compatibility missing from arbitrary independent permutation perturbations.

COROLLARY. If tau_A commutes with antipode J, then every midpoint collision count W_(pi,rho) among orders with prefix (A,i) is unchanged by switching the square. When A is empty, tau_A=L commutes J, so this applies unconditionally, recovering a quantitative form of the two-hit word transport. For nonempty A the commutation hypothesis may fail. One cannot deduce general preservation from this corollary alone.
