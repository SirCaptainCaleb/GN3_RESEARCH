## Research note

There exists an antipodally odd coloring of Q4 with a projective Petrie cycle of grade three such that EVERY cyclic adjacent transposition of its four dimension directions, allowing arbitrary root slides and orientation reversal, produces another grade-three cycle, although the coloring has ten grade-one cycles among its twenty-four projective cycles. Explicit certificate: index antipodal edge-pair orbit bits in direction order i=0,1,2,3, with canonical lower endpoints respectively (0,2,4,6), (0,1,4,5), (0,1,2,3), (0,1,2,3); color the canonical endpoints by the 16-bit mask 0x5a88 and their antipodal mates by complementary values. The cycle represented by root 2 and direction order (0,2,1,3) has word 0110, grade three. The cycle represented by root 3 and order (2,1,0,3) has word 1101, grade three. For each cycle all cyclic adjacent-swap neighbors have grade three. Enumeration of the 24 projective cycles (4n rooted descriptions per cycle) confirms ten grade-one cycles and two exchange-stable grade-three cycles. Thus minimizing projective switched-turn count by single adjacent exchanges may get stuck even in a good coloring; the negative-chirality grade-three class is not sufficient for local exchange descent. Reproducible script /mnt/data/nori_exchange_local_test.py and independently reproduced orbit-mask enumeration. This is an obstruction to a dimension-uniform proof via arbitrary single-adjacent-swap strict descent, not a counterexample to NORI1.
