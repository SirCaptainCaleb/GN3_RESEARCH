## Research note

In a bad odd cube coloring, any antipodal edge pair whose reversal makes the coloring good gives a rooted edge repair in every facet transverse to its direction. To see this, take a monochromatic zero antipodal geodesic after reversal. Its bridge in each other direction remains zero, so the bridge correspondence identifies a favorable root in the repaired lower facet. The same root was not favorable before reversal, or the original cube coloring would have been good. All these root repairs are synchronized by one geodesic.
