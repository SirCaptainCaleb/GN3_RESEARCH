# Prescribed-orbit avoidance: a direct forcing target and Q5 computation

Let \(\mathcal O_n\) denote the coherent supports of antipodal full geodesics and \(G(c)=\{O\in\mathcal O_n:T_c(O)=2\}\). For an antipodal edge orbit \(e\) consider
\[
\mathcal P(n,e):\quad \text{every legal }c\text{ admits }O\in G(c)\text{ with }e\notin O.
\]
This strengthens NORI1. It fails in dimension three: listing orbits lexicographically as \((0,0),(0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,1)\), the bitmask \(0x1\) has two good supports and both contain \((0,2)\).

**Finite result.** Exhaustive enumeration of all 65,536 legal \(Q_4\) colorings confirms \(\mathcal P(4,e)\). For \(Q_5\), the SciPy/HiGHS integer feasibility model described below reported **infeasible** after 224 branch-and-bound nodes (1.66 seconds), supporting \(\mathcal P(5,e)\). Since cube automorphisms act transitively on antipodal edge orbits, one representative orbit suffices. No independently checkable formal infeasibility certificate has been extracted: the five-dimensional conclusion is therefore reported here as a reproduced solver computation, pending a mathematical proof.

**The Boolean feasibility formulation.** Each antipodal edge orbit \(a\) has one bit \(x_a\), determining a legal coloring. For each projective turn \(t\) with incident orbits \(a,b\), introduce bits \(y_t,h_t\) and impose
\[
y_t-x_a-x_b+2h_t=s_t,
\]
where \(s_t\in\{0,1\}\) is determined by the chosen representatives of the antipodal edges at the projective vertex. Then \(y_t=I_t(c)\). For each coherent support avoiding the fixed orbit \(e\), impose \(\sum_{t\in O}y_t\ge3\). These sums are odd, so this condition excludes precisely the two-turn supports avoiding \(e\). Supports through \(e\) are left unconstrained. In dimension five this uses 40 edge-orbit bits, 160 turn bits, 160 parity auxiliaries, 160 turn equations and 168 avoided-support inequalities. The formulation is satisfiable if and only if \(\mathcal P(5,e)\) fails.

**Sparse-witness certificate.** With the 40 orbit colors specified by bitmask \(0xf6234de0ff\) in lexicographic order, a legal \(Q_5\) coloring has 23 good supports among 192. Every orbit lies on 24 supports, so orbit avoidance cannot be deduced from an unconditional lower bound of 25 or more good supports. The 23 good supports have empty common edge-orbit intersection. For fixed orbit \(e=(0,0)\), the avoiding-support energy histogram is \(20\) at \(T=2\), \(127\) at \(T=6\), and \(21\) at \(T=10\), yielding
\[
\mathbb E(T\mid e\notin O)=253/42>6.
\]
The existing anchored mean criterion fails to detect these 20 avoiding witnesses, demonstrating that the stronger orbit-avoidance claim requires information beyond a scalar conditioned first moment.

**All-dimensional status.** If \(\mathcal P(n,e)\) held for all \(n\ge4\), connectivity of legal colorings under orbit flips would imply unrestricted NORI1. A direct \(Q_6\) feasibility run stopped at 30 seconds without resolving satisfiability. The all-dimensional strengthening remains unproved and could fail.
