## Research note

THEOREM (type-A alternating-rook realization of reversal-odd boundary 3-tournaments). Let V be a finite n-set, n>=3, and Ω={(a,b):a,b in V,a≠b}, with vertex involution τ(a,b)=(b,a). The rook graph on Ω joins ordered pairs differing in precisely one entry. Regard each rook edge in both orientations. A boundary coloring b(a,h,c) satisfying b(c,h,a)=1-b(a,h,c) corresponds bijectively to a directed rook-arc bit β satisfying β(v,u)=1-β(u,v) and β(τu,τv)=β(u,v): on (a,h)->(c,h) and on (h,a)->(h,c), prescribe β=b(a,h,c). These two arcs are exchanged by τ; their reverse arcs correspond to the reversal of (a,h,c). Conversely every row rook arc determines b, proving the bijection.

For a vertex word v1,...,vn with consecutive triples distinct, put q_i=(v_i,v_(i+1)) for odd i and q_i=(v_(i+1),v_i) for even i, i=1,...,n-1. This is a rook gallery alternating changes to first and second entries. Its arc-bit word satisfies β(q_i,q_(i+1))=b(v_i,v_(i+1),v_(i+2)) for all i. Conversely a rook gallery beginning with a change to the first entry, then alternating entries, reconstructs a unique such vertex word. The root assignment ρ(a,b)=e_a-e_b belongs to W={z in R^V:∑z_v=0}, of dimension n-1, and obeys ρ(τq)=-ρ(q). Its n-1 roots are independent if and only if all original vertices v_i are distinct: they are signed incidence columns of the successive unordered edges {v_i,v_(i+1)}, and independence is equivalent to those edges forming a forest; connectedness of the walk and n-1 edges then give the Hamilton ordering. Equivalently, for rook galleries with distinct consecutive triples, the roots of all nonconsecutive states must be orthogonal, since e_a-e_b and e_c-e_d have zero inner product precisely when their two unordered symbol pairs are disjoint. Consequently the boundary one-switch Hamilton assertion is equivalent to the existence of an alternating rook gallery with n-1 independent type-A roots and at most one bit switch.

Under reversal of a full vertex ordering, its associated rook gallery becomes the reversed gallery for odd n and τ applied to the reversed gallery for even n. In either case the bit word is complemented and reversed, since β is reversed on reversing a directed arc and is preserved under τ. The inversion of a boundary triple therefore comes from reversing gallery direction, NOT from β(τ directed-arc)=1-β(directed-arc). This preserves tight-path coherence but differs from the undirected ordered-pair graph model previously recorded in note_ordered_pair_antipodal_graph_bridge_and_decoding_obstruction_20261011.

TOPOLOGICAL BRIDGE AND ITS LIMIT. Wu and Yang, A Chain-Level Borsuk--Ulam Obstruction Proof of Norine's Antipodal-Coloring Conjecture, arXiv:2607.19276 (2026), use a rook labeling of cube vertices into the same Ω, whose involution is τ and whose antipodal root embedding is the very same e_a-e_b in W. Their Freudenthal-gallery chain map uses positive root cones and subdivision-invariant chains. This gives a native geometric meeting point for boundary tournament galleries and the cubical antipodal component method. But the Wu--Yang theorem guarantees a monochromatic antipodal path not necessarily a geodesic. Failure of a MONOCHROMATIC ANTIPODAL GEODESIC does not prohibit monochromatic antipodal connectivity, and hence cannot directly produce Wu--Yang's off-diagonal component-pair map. Likewise their rook galleries are not automatically alternating or root-independent; a basis-and-switch extraction theorem is missing. The positive cones of simple type-A roots generally overlap, whereas Coxeter chambers are the DUAL inequality cones, so topological use of these root galleries must not assume the positive cones give a fan. These facts are precise limitations on the proposed transfer, not a refutation of it.
