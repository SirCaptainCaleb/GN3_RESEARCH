## Research note

Under the hypothetical absence of a one-switch full geodesic, let \(C_x=K_x^0\cup K_x^1\) be the two-color geodesic-prefix cone and \(Z_x=S^{n-1}\setminus(C_x\cup C_{\bar x})\), with \(C_{\bar x}=AC_x\). Alexander duality over the integers gives
\[
\widetilde H_{n-2}(Z_x;\mathbb Z)
\cong\widetilde H^0(C_x\sqcup C_{\bar x};\mathbb Z)
\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
Choose its generator \(\eta_x\) corresponding to the function equal to \(1\) on \(C_x\) and \(0\) on \(C_{\bar x}\), modulo constant functions.

**Theorem (signed root transport).** For every cube edge \(xy\), the canonical bridge complement \(Z_{xy}\) identifies the generators \(\eta_x,\eta_y\) with the same signed generator of \(\widetilde H_{n-2}(Z_{xy};\mathbb Z)\). On the other hand
\[
Z_{\bar x}=Z_x,\qquad \eta_{\bar x}=-\eta_x
\]
under the set-theoretic identification of these complement spaces. Therefore the integral top-homology transport along the whole cube graph has the sign monodromy of the antipodal root cover when the roots \(x,\bar x\) are identified.

*Proof.* For an edge of color \(a\), the bridge \(B_{xy}^a\subset C_x\cap C_y\) sits in the positive component of each opposed pair of cones. Restricting the class \(1\) on the positive component and \(0\) on its antipode to \(B_{xy}^a\sqcup AB_{xy}^a\) gives the same class at both endpoints. Naturality of Alexander duality proves the first statement. Replacing \(x\) by \(\bar x\) exchanges the two components. In reduced \(H^0\), exchanging the characteristic functions sends \(1_{C_x}\) to \(1_{C_{\bar x}}=-1_{C_x}\) modulo constants. This gives the sign reversal. \(\square\)

**Limit of top homology.** These generator identifications are independent of the sets of dimensions used by the witnessing paths. They therefore retain only the ordinary antipodal orientation local system and cannot by themselves force a monochromatic full geodesic. To adapt Wu–Yang, the target chains must carry the support filtration and the higher coherence of colored cubical squares; otherwise the argument collapses back to ordinary connected-component transport.
