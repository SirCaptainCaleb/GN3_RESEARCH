## Research note

Literature correction and research priority (14 October 2026). Lawrence Hollom, 'Hypercube geodesics with few colour changes', arXiv:2605.20184v3 (28 May 2026), Theorem 1.3: for every two-edge-coloring of Q_n there exists a full antipodal geodesic with at most (pi/2+o(1))*sqrt(n) color changes. No antipodal oddness assumption. Leader and Long, 'Long geodesics in subgraphs of the cube', Discrete Mathematics 326 (2014), guarantee a monochromatic geodesic of length ceil(n/2) and hence a linear bound on switches. Therefore Article XV Subsection 26's mean-projective-switch result 2*floor((n-1)/4), its improvement 2k-2 for n=4k+1, and its polynomial density result are mathematically potentially valid but asymptotically superseded as existence bounds. The local exchange-flatness theorem (constant rainbow projective switch count implies count one) retains independent structural content and may be worth investigating, but future closure work should not invest in linear quantitative switch estimates. Hollom's optimality refers to minimum switch count averaged over uniformly random starting roots, NOT the globally best root; the layered coloring has good central roots. Sources https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20184 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20184.
