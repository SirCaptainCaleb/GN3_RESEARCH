## Research note

For a legal antipodally odd coloring without a monochromatic full geodesic, let K=K0 union K1, I=K0 intersect K1, and R=AJ. The actions of R on K and I are free. The covering class alpha_I in H^1(I/R;F2) is nonzero if and only if at least one connected component of I is setwise R-invariant: paired components give a split double cover, while an invariant connected component gives a connected, nonsplit double cover. The seam transgression identity alpha_K^2=delta(alpha_I) therefore implies that alpha_K^2 nonzero forces an R-invariant connected component of I. This strengthens the necessary fixed-incidence-edge condition: an R-fixed edge in the coarse red-blue component graph may hide two distinct seam components exchanged by R. Such an edge alone need not support nonzero alpha_K squared. A higher index must arise from a connected red-blue intersection component with its own nontrivial free reversal action. This is a conditional localization theorem, not an existence proof.
