## Research note

If a hypothetical legal NORI1 counterexample has a full geodesic of word 0^a 1 0^b with a,b>=1, write u,v for the adjacent vertices immediately before and after the exceptional edge i. Root-rotating the word to u gives 1 0^b 1^a; rotating once more to v gives 0^b 1^a 0. Both have two switches and no legal counterexample can have zero or one. By the rooted antipodal kernel classification HL24, both roots have K=[[2,3],[3,2]]. The shared (n-1)-face geodesic v->bar(u) between the two rotations has word 0^b 1^a; its augmented K_10 cost is 1+1+1=3. Since the unrestricted rooted K_10 at each endpoint root is already 3, the constrained shared facet K_10 is also 3, and both nonnegative endpoint-forcing gaps HL29 are zero. Thus every normalized hypothetical counterexample includes a pair of adjacent unpolarized grade-one roots joined by a zero-forcing-gap seam. This does not prove NORI1: a zero-gap seam can coexist with a positive diagonal root obstruction.
