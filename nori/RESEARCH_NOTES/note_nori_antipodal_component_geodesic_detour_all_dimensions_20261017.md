## Research note

THEOREM. For every n=7m+k>=7 with 0<=k<7, there is a legal antipodally odd coloring of Q_n having a red component C with an antipodal pair but no red full geodesic. Its least red antipodal path length is 11m+k=n+4m; a red full geodesic exists in a different component.

BASE Q7. Vertices are integers, with antipode 127 XOR x. Put S={0,1,2,4,7,16,23,32,33,55,63,69,71,97,101,119,127}. Color every edge induced on S red and every edge leaving S blue. Each antipodal edge orbit receives compatible prescriptions; give unconstrained orbits complementary colors, red on the smaller canonical edge. The induced graph on S has 18 edges, is connected and has only the antipodal pair {0,127}. BFS levels from 0 are {0}; {1,2,4,16,32}; {33}; {97}; {101}; {69}; {71}; {7}; {23}; {55}; {63,119}; {127}. The distance from 0 to127 is 11. A red full geodesic elsewhere starts at 3, order (3,0,1,4,6,5,2), and visits 3,11,10,8,24,88,120,124.

LIFT. Partition n into m blocks of seven directions and k extra directions. Color each seven-block by the fixed Q7 coloring independently. In every extra direction color its edge by the first bit of the first seven-block. These are undirected colors and antipodal complementation reverses every color. The red component of the all-zero vertex is C=S^m times F2^k: the red copies of S are connected within each extra-bit layer, and the extra-direction edges at the all-zero seven-block tuple connect the layers. No red edge leaves C.

Every antipodal pair in C projects to {0,127} in each seven-block. Thus each antipodal red path inside C uses at least 11 steps in each block and at least k extra-direction steps. Equality is attained by first flipping the k extra directions at the all-zero root, then traversing the eleven-edge red path 0,1,33,97,101,69,71,7,23,55,63,127 in each block. For a red full geodesic elsewhere, start at 3 in each seven-block, follow the base red direction order in each block, then flip all extra directions: the first block ends at 124, whose first bit is zero, so all extra steps are red. Antipodality maps C to a blue component with the same distance.

VERIFICATION. Standalone file nori_geodesic_stretch_verifier.py independently checks the base orbit colors, induced graph and breadth-first levels, and tests legality, component equality, distance witnesses and red full geodesics for n=7,...,14. The formula proves every n>=7. This obstructs componentwise conversion of antipodal monochromatic connectivity into a monochromatic full geodesic, while NORI1 still holds in the examples.

**Universal antipodal component distance-order theorem and sharp two-step detours.** Fix n>=3 and a legal antipodally odd red/blue coloring. If a red component C contains x and bar x, let L=d_C(x,bar x). Then |C|>=L+n-1. Proof: take a shortest red path P of L edges. For each direction i, precisely one of the antipodal edges at x and bar x in direction i is red. Their n other endpoints are pairwise distinct for n>=3 and lie in C. Any such red neighbor of x on P must be the first internal path vertex, for otherwise using its red incident edge shortens P; analogously a red neighbor of bar x on P must be the last internal vertex. Therefore at least n-2 of the n neighbors lie outside the L+1 path vertices. In particular any component containing antipodes but no full red antipodal geodesic has size at least 2n+1, because L>=n+2 by parity. More generally, L=n+2k implies |C|>=2n+2k-1. The earlier 17-vertex Q7 component with L=11 attains the bound for (n,k)=(7,2), and the minimum 2n-1 red components containing full geodesics attain k=0.

**Sharpness at the first positive excess in every n>=8.** In Q8 take the rooted red path P8=(0,1,3,7,15,14,30,62,63,127,255), direction sequence (0,1,2,3,0,4,5,0,6,7), and vertex set S8={0,1,2,3,4,7,8,14,15,16,30,32,62,63,127,191,255}. Its 17 vertices comprise the eleven path vertices plus the extra root leaves 2,4,8,16,32 and opposite endpoint leaf 191. The induced graph is connected and has intrinsic distance d(0,255)=10: its compulsory central chain 3,7,15,14,30,62,63 uses direction 0 twice, and its only two optional end-square chords are 2--3 and 63--191. The pair {0,255} is the only antipodal pair. The nonendpoint vertices have Hamming diameter six, and for every i, at least one of 2^i and 255 xor 2^i lies in S8. Consequently every edge internal to S8 may be red and every edge leaving S8 blue, without conflict under antipodal color reversal; the remaining edge orbits can be completed freely. The full red geodesic outside S8 with root 5 and direction order (3,0,4,2,5,6,7,1), visiting 5,13,12,28,24,56,120,248,250, can be imposed simultaneously. Each of its first six edge orbits is unrestricted by S8; the last two edges are antipodes of blue boundary edges and therefore forced red.

For n>=8 write M_n=2^n-1 and let P_n be the rooted path starting at zero with direction list (n-1,n-2,...,8,0,1,2,3,0,4,5,0,6,7). Set R_n={0,1,2,3,4,5} union {8,...,n-1}, and define S_n=V(P_n) union {2^i:i in R_n} union {M_n xor 2^6,M_n xor 2^7}. The path has n+2 edges and n+3 vertices; its only overlaps with selected endpoint neighbors are first vertex 2^(n-1) after the root (for n8 this is 2^0) and penultimate vertex M_n xor 2^7. Thus |S_n|=2n+1.

**Induction for the construction.** In Q_(n+1), the new path starts from lower-layer zero, crosses the new direction, then follows P_n in the upper layer. The other lower vertices are the root-side singleton leaves. Upper vertices project to S_n after excluding the off-path root-side singleton leaves. Hence S_(n+1) is connected, has one antipodal pair, and its projected vertices all lie in S_n. Let T_n=S_n\{0,M_n}. The base anticode bound diam(T8)=6 holds. Under this lift, all upper nonendpoint vertices project to T_n or to the old root, and all lower nonendpoint vertices are root-side singletons. Two upper T vertices have distance <=n-2; an upper old-root and old T vertex have distance <=n-1; two lower singleton vertices distance <=2; an upper old T vertex and lower singleton have distance <=diam(T_n)+1<=n-1; an upper old-root and a lower singleton have distance 2. Thus diam(T_(n+1))<=n-1. Endpoint-neighbor coverage is preserved, including the new first direction. The component-coloring completion criterion from the four-sector theorem therefore holds in every dimension: induced S_n edges may all be red, boundary edges all blue, and the remaining edge orbits colored complementarily.

No full antipodal geodesic can lie in S_(n+1), because deleting its step in the new direction would project it to a full antipodal geodesic in S_n, forbidden by induction. The given P_(n+1) has length n+3 and parity prohibits an intervening path length; hence its antipodal distance is (n+1)+2. For a simultaneous red geodesic outside S_n, take root with base-eight-bit mask 5 and all higher bits one, use the base-eight direction order (3,0,4,2,5,6,7,1), and then directions 8,...,n-1. Under each lift this path follows the prior outside path in the upper layer then crosses the new direction at its terminal vertex. Its old-direction edges remain compatible with forced component colors, and the new-direction closing edge cannot be antipodal to an induced component edge since its old-bit projection lies outside S_n. Thus prescribe all its edges red and finish the coloring with the antipodal rule. This gives a legal coloring with a size-(2n+1) red component whose sole antipodal pair has red distance n+2, and a red full antipodal geodesic in a different component, for every n>=8.

**Verification.** The standard-library Python script nori_sharp_component_lift.py checks antipodal orbit compatibility of all internal-red, boundary-blue and exterior-geodesic prescriptions; then BFS computes the resulting component and its antipodal distance, and checks the exterior geodesic in dimensions n=8,...,15. Cases all pass. The induction proves all n>=8.

**Minimum antipodal component obstruction (proved for all n>=8).** The explicit legal coloring and sharp-order component S_n constructed above have precisely two red connected components containing antipodal pairs. The smaller is S_n, size 2n+1, with sole antipodal pair at red distance n+2 and no red full geodesic. The other, D_n, contains the explicit exterior red full geodesic and has at least 113 vertices for n=8 and at least 2^(n-1)-(n-2) vertices for n>=9. Both bounds exceed 2n+1. The antipodal image gives corresponding blue components. Therefore selecting the smallest monochromatic component with antipodal vertices does not force a geodesic.

**Connectivity proof.** Put L_n={0,...,2^(n-1)-1} and W_n=L_n minus S_n and bar S_n. W_n is red connected: base cases n=8 and 9 follow from BFS on respectively 112 and 238 vertices with the stated edge-orbit completion. For n>=9, W_(n+1) splits along bit p=n-1 into a p=0 slice equal to W_n with unchanged colors and a p=1 slice equal to a p-cube with its zero and all-one corners removed, all edges red. Both are connected and a red edge from 3 to 2^p+3 joins them.

Every lower-half barred vertex outside S_n has a red edge into W_n, since its antipode lies on a blue S_n boundary. For n>=9, the barred lower-half set consists of 0,64,128,192,193,225,240,241,248,252,254,255 and the masks 2^r-1 for 9<=r<=n-1; only 0 lies in S_n. At Hamming weight at least four, there are at least four downward neighbors and at most two barred vertices at the lower weight, while S_n has only zero and singleton vertices in the lower half, so a W_n neighbor exists. The masks 64,128,192,193 have W_n neighbors 68,132,196,129. For n=8 the sole barred lower-half vertex outside S_8 is 64, with W_8 neighbor 68. Thus all lower-half vertices outside S_n lie in one red component D_n. Every antipodal pair has one lower-half endpoint; hence any red component with an antipodal pair is S_n or D_n. The exterior full geodesic proves D_n contains such a pair. Counting lower-half vertices gives the claimed size bounds.

**Verification.** Scripts nori_min_antipodal_component_verifier.py and nori_min_ap_component_induction_audit.py check component connectivity, the recursive slice identities and all antipodal components in dimensions 8 through 15. The original conjecture remains open.

**Theorem (universal forced exterior red geodesic for odd sharp detour components).** Fix any odd n>=9 and the size-(2n+1) sharp-detour component vertex set S_n defined above. In EVERY legal antipodally odd coloring having S_n as a red connected component, some red full antipodal geodesic avoids S_n, independent of the coloring of all external unprescribed edge orbits. Thus prescribing an additional red full exterior witness, as done in the original example, is unnecessary for odd dimensions.

**Explicit proof.** Use bit-mask vertices, M=2^n-1. Start at r=48, with direction order
(6,7,2,3,0,1) followed by the ordered pairs (9,8),(11,10),...,(n-2,n-3), and finally (4,n-1,5).
The first edge 48--112 is unprescribed. All subsequent n-1 edges join bar S_n to W=V(Q_n)\(S_n union bar S_n), and therefore are forced red as antipodes of blue edges leaving the red component. To verify membership, the successive bar S_n anchors along this path are 240,252,255; then 2^j-1 for every even j=10,12,...,n-1; then M xor16. The first three are complements of translated base-path vertices, the intermediate initial-segment masks are complements of the high-direction prefix path vertices, and M xor16 is the complement of the singleton endpoint leaf {4}. Between these anchors, the path visits the masks 112,244,253; then (2^j-1) xor 2^(j+1) for j=8,10,...,n-3; then (2^(n-1)-1) xor16; and finally M xor48. Together with the root 48, all these belong to W: none occurs in the component's high-prefix plus translated base-path core or the singleton/complement-singleton leaves, nor in the antipodes of those vertices. Consequently every edge after the first is bar S_n--W, forced red. The direction list is a permutation of all n directions and terminates at M xor48.

Rotate the first step to the end, changing the root to 112. Its first n-1 edges are the same forced-red edges; the final edge is the antipode of 48--112, and therefore has the opposite color. One of the two full geodesics is red, and both avoid S_n. This proves the result with only one free antipodal edge orbit.

**Verification.** Standard-library verifier nori_odd_connector_uniform.py reconstructs S_n, checks every path vertex and edge against the full component/boundary prescriptions, confirms one unspecified first edge and all other forced-red edges, and checks the antipodal cyclic rotation for all odd n from 9 to 79. This is a dimension-uniform fixed-coloring theorem only for the specified sharp-detour component shape, not for arbitrary legal colorings.
