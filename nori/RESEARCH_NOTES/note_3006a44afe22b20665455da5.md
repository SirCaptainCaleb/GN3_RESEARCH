## Research note

For n=2m, if an a-colored cube edge xy in direction i joins roots supporting a-colored half-geodesics with complementary direction sets S,S^c, then their endpoints u,v satisfy v=bar(u)^i. Reversing the first, crossing xy and following the second produces an a-colored (n+1)-walk between u,v. The antipodal i-edges at u,v have opposite colors; adjoining the a-colored one produces an a-colored antipodal (n+2)-walk. If direction i is first in one half-geodesic, the concatenation contains an immediate backtrack, and erasing it gives a monochromatic full n-geodesic. Universal existence of such a root-edge pairing is open.
