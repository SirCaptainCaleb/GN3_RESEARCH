## Research note

Let n=2m, xy=x(x^i) have color a, and suppose color-a m-prefixes from x and y use complementary supports. Suppose the witness from x contains i in second position, with first direction j. Its initial two edges in order (j,i) are color a and the root edge in direction i is color a. If the opposite j-edge in that original (i,j)-square also has color a, swapping those first two directions yields another a-colored midpoint prefix beginning in i, and the adjacent-root endpoint cancellation theorem produces a monochromatic full antipodal geodesic. Thus, under hypothetical failure of NORI1, the opposite j-edge must have color 1-a, and the original square has three a-colored edges and one opposite-colored edge, i.e. square curvature one. The same argument applies with the other half-prefix rooted at y. In particular, if n=6 and all square curvatures vanish, any adjacent-root complementary midpoint pair forces NORI1: its joining direction is first, second, or last in a three-step half-prefix; endpoint cases are immediate and the middle case cannot be blocked by flatness. The universal existence of the pairing remains open.
