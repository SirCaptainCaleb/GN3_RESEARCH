## Research note

**Theorem: exponential obstruction to sparse root-Walsh Gram certificates.** Let n>=9 and take the legal cyclic affine reader coloring c_i(x)=x_{i+1 mod n}. Fix any direction order pi. Let K_pi(A) be the root-Walsh matrix from the current Fourier forcing manuscript, indexed by q>=2 distinct characters on the folded-root group, with (S,R)-entry equal to the Fourier coefficient of G_pi-3 at S triangle R. For every pi and every A with
sqrt(2e(n-1)ceil(ln q)) <= n-7,
the matrix K_pi(A) is positive semidefinite, despite the coloring having monochromatic full geodesics. Consequently, for any alpha<1/(2e), all principal root-Walsh matrices with q<=exp(alpha(n-1)) are positive semidefinite for sufficiently large n. Every subexponential-size family of such tests therefore fails uniformly over pi for this coloring.

PROOF. For any pi, the n traversed edge-color bits are an invertible affine permutation of the root bits. Writing s_t=(-1)^(w_t) for the bits of this word gives
G_pi=n/2 -(1/2)sum_{t=1}^{n-1}s_t s_{t+1}+(1/2)s_n s_1.
Thus the nonconstant Walsh spectrum consists of precisely n distinct two-bit characters, each of coefficient magnitude 1/2, and the constant coefficient is n/2. These n characters span the even-subset character group and satisfy the unique binary relation that their symmetric difference is empty. Choose n-1 as a basis to identify the Fourier-label group with F_2^(n-1). A diagonal sign conjugation makes their contributions equal to -1/2 times the standard (n-1)-cube adjacency matrix. The remaining generator contributes a signed perfect matching, of operator norm at most 1/2 after restriction to any q labels. Therefore, with d=n-1, if M is the adjacency matrix of the q-vertex induced subgraph of Q_d, then
lambda_min K_pi(A)>=(n/2-3)-(rho(M)+1)/2.

For every k>=1, trace(M^(2k)) counts closed walks of length 2k inside the induced cube. A closed cube walk traverses every direction an even number of times. Pair its 2k step positions into k equal-direction pairs, and assign a direction to each pair. This bounds the number of closed walks per starting vertex by (2k-1)!!d^k and gives
rho(M)^(2k)<=tr(M^(2k))<=q(2k-1)!!d^k<=q(2kd)^k.
Set k=ceil(ln q), so q^(1/(2k))<=sqrt(e). It follows that
rho(M)<=sqrt(2e(n-1)ceil(ln q))
and lambda_min K_pi(A)>=(n-7-sqrt(2e(n-1)ceil(ln q)))/2.
The stated condition makes the right side nonnegative. If q<=exp(alpha(n-1)) and alpha<1/(2e), the square-root term grows with leading coefficient at most sqrt(2e alpha)<1 times n-1; the same conclusion holds for sufficiently large n. QED.

SCOPE: This is a limitation of negative-eigenvalue certificates based on principal root-Walsh submatrices. It does not rule out full-matrix certificates or inequalities coupling multiple geodesic orders and nonlinear cube-star identities. It is distinct from the previously archived fixed-moment histogram obstruction.
