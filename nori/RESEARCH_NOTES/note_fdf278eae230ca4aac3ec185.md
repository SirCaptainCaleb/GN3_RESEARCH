## Research note

THEOREM. For any involutive matching cover of Q_m, choose a base fiber and let F_pi be the maps induced by red matchings for every permutation pi of all m labels. The subgroup generated by F_rho^(-1)F_pi acts transitively on the intersection of every connected component with that base fiber. Proof: for any subset S, the image of a group orbit under a partial label-order sweep is independent of that order, since both prefixes extend by a common suffix and differ by a full-sweep comparison generator. These transported orbit sets are closed under every matching move (choose the matching label last and use involution), hence form the entire connected component. COROLLARY. For a nontrivial red component with k>1 sheets, any root has at least two distinct full-sweep endpoints. If a paired-flat component containing antipodes has k<=3, two antipodal roots must have overlapping endpoint sets in the same opposite fiber, yielding a red antipodal full geodesic. The transfer matrix B of sweep counts yields a symmetric stochastic collision channel BB^T/(m!)^2 with connected support and positive diagonal on every red component fiber. The all-dimensional proof and Q4/Q6/Q8 checker are in nori_sweep_holonomy_generation.md and nori_cover_sweep_holonomy_verify.py.


NEW ALL-DIMENSIONAL THEOREM (alternating sweep holonomy). In paired-flat Q_(2m), m>=2, write pair parity p_i=x_(2i) xor x_(2i+1) and fiber bit t_i=x_(2i). The unique red pair move U_i is the shear (p,t)->(p+e_i,t+h_i(p_not_i,t_not_i)e_i). Antipodal oddness implies h_i(p_not_i,t_not_i+allones)=1+h_i(p_not_i,t_not_i). For fixed p, h_i is therefore balanced in its 2^(m-1) exterior fiber inputs. The shear U_i:F_p->F_(p+e_i), identified with permutations of 2^m fiber states, is a product of 2^(m-2) disjoint transpositions. Hence its sign is (-1)^(2^(m-2)), and the sign of every complete pair-order sweep Phi_pi is (-1)^(m 2^(m-2))=+1. All comparison holonomies Phi_rho^(-1)Phi_pi consequently belong to the alternating group A_(2^m), for every base fiber and arbitrary nonlinear gate selectors.

COROLLARY. A red covering cannot have a single two-sheet connected component and all other components single-sheeted. The previous complete-sweep generation theorem ensures that some holonomy transposes the two states of the nontrivial component. If all other sheet components are singletons, this transposition is odd on the entire parity fiber, contradicting alternating holonomy. This conclusion does not require the two-sheet component to contain antipodes. It does not prohibit coupled nontrivial components whose holonomy signs compensate.

NEW TENSOR TRANSFER THEOREM. For legal paired-flat c on 2m directions and d on 2k directions, define B_p^c(t,s) to count pair-order sweeps from fiber root (p,t) to target (bar p,s). For the independent block-product coloring, B_(p,q)^(c boxplus d)=binom(m+k,m) B_p^c tensor B_q^d. Proof: each global order projects to unique factor orders, and each specified pair of factor orders has binom(m+k,m) interleavings; cross-block red matchings commute. Thus the normalized transfer P=B/m! and the collision kernel K=P P^T factor as P_prod=P_c tensor P_d and K_prod=K_c tensor K_d. The complete-sweep comparison group on a product fiber is the direct product of the two factor holonomy groups, by choosing identical permutations in one factor. Accordingly the complete spectral multiset of K_prod consists of products of factor eigenvalues; on connected component fibers the spectral gap is the minimum of the two factor gaps. The previously known scalar midpoint-count product law is the trace of this stronger operator factorization.

These are structural same-coloring constraints, not a proof of existence of a one-step antipodal collision. A future forcing argument must still use the paired-flat one-bit shear identities beyond alternating parity and component connectivity. Independent analytic proof is stored at /mnt/data/nori_even_holonomy_tensor_theorem.md and executable checks at /mnt/data/nori_even_sweep_holonomy_verify.py: gate and sweep permutation parities checked for Q4/Q6/Q8 samples, full product transfer distribution Q4 boxplus Q4.

NEW UNIVERSAL PATH-LEVEL INTERTWINING LAW. Let J complement all fiber bits and T_i flip only fiber bit i. Each red pair gate obeys U_i J=T_i J U_i and U_i J U_i^(-1)=T_i J, with source and target parity fibers correctly understood. For a full red order pi=(i_1,...,i_m), the two output fiber words Phi_pi(t) and Phi_pi(t+allones) agree in bit i_1. The first gate's control values are complementary at antipodal input states, so their updated i_1 bits agree, and later pair gates leave that bit fixed. Dually, the two outputs of the inverse complete sweep Phi_pi^(-1) on complementary target inputs agree in its last-order bit i_m. Equivalently the conjugated antipodal involution Phi_pi J Phi_pi^(-1) preserves both hyperplanes of constant t_(i_1), while Phi_pi^(-1)J Phi_pi preserves hyperplanes of constant t_(i_m). These are path-realizable endpoint compatibility laws for every nonlinear legal paired-flat coloring, not averaged moments. They do not imply same-order endpoint collision (Phi_pi is bijective); in the existence problem one must vary the two halfpath orders and possibly the root. The updated verifier checks both laws for all complete orders and parity fibers of sampled Q4,Q6,Q8 colorings.
