## Research note

On V={1,2,3,4}, define b(a,b,c) as the parity of the full permutation (a,b,c,d), where d is the omitted vertex. Reversing the triple reverses this parity, so b is a boundary 3-tournament. For every ordering (a,b,c,d), the consecutive triple labels b(a,b,c) and b(b,c,d) differ: the underlying four-permutations differ by an odd four-cycle. Hence no monochromatic four-vertex tight path exists. A full-resource selector conjecture for all boundary tournaments is false, even though reversal antisymmetry and the pointed line-cover matroid representation hold.
