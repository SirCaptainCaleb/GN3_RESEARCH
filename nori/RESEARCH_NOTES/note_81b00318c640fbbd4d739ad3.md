## Research note

For a legal cube coloring, let U_i be the red edge-swap permutation matrix in direction i and K the sum of all n! ordered products of the U_i. Since each U_i is symmetric and reversing an order transposes its product, K is symmetric and nonnegative, with row sums n!. A one-pass sweep ending at the antipode must swap in every direction, so K_(bar x,x)=(R^n)_(bar x,x). Thus tr(PK)=tr(PR^n). Further, tr(PK^2)=sum_(x,v)K_(v,x)K_(v,bar x) is the count of different-order paired-sweep midpoint coincidences. To interpolate, replace U_i by t I+(1-t)U_i, obtaining the symmetrized K(t). Then tr(PK(t))=(1-t)^n tr(PR^n). For every 0<t<1, tr(PK(t)^2)>0 if and only if a red full antipodal geodesic exists: any pair of meeting lazy sweeps must flip every direction once between them, and any red full geodesic can be split and padded with stationary offers. At t=0 the trace tests the stronger deterministic paired-sweep conjecture. The order of vanishing in t of tr(PK(t)^2) records the fewest forced idle offers on red edges among all such two-arm constructions. This is a nonnegative matrix-polynomial deformation preserving the cube's antipodal target.
