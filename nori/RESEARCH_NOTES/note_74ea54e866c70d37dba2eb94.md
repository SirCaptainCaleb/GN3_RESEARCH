## Research note

For m>=2, choose a direction permutation pi and subset A containing pi's last entry but not its first, and write B=[m] minus A. Let W(pi,A)=pi|A,pi,pi|B, where restrictions preserve order. Direct word reversal gives rev(W(pi,A))=W(rev pi,B). Since every red edge-swap U_i is an involution, the return permutation associated with the reversed word equals the inverse: H_(rev pi,B)=H_(pi,A)^(-1). Consequently if H_(pi,A)(x)=bar x then H_(rev pi,B)(bar x)=x. The map (pi,A,x) to (rev pi,B,bar x) is a fixed-point-free involution on all antipodal bigon holonomy witnesses, because the base root x differs from bar x and pi differs from its reversal. Therefore the TOTAL number of rooted oriented antipodal-return witnesses across the full bigon parameter family is even for every legal coloring. In particular a direct odd-parity count on all such witnesses cannot establish unrestricted NORI1. A successful equivariant topological method must assign further signed weights, restrict to an oriented fundamental domain with controlled boundary, or use more refined incidence compatibility. This extends the known reversal pairing of identical two-sweep witnesses to the complete simple-bigon family.
