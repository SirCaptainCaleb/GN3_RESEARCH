## Research note

Proposed global quantitative strengthening of NORI1 (OPEN, not established): For any arbitrary binary edge coloring f of Q_m, let W_f(x) count full x-to-antipode geodesics whose color words are nondecreasing 0-star 1-star. Conjecture that summing over antipodal pairs gives SUM min(W_f(x),W_f(bar x)) >= 2^(m-1). The already-proved weighted bridge identity says the left side is the smallest possible number of monochromatic-zero antipodal geodesics in an odd coloring of Q_(m+1) extending f as its lower facet. Thus proving this single unrestricted inequality would imply NORI1 and a stronger exponential multiplicity statement N0(c)>=2^(n-2). Exhaustive enumeration verified this for m=1,2,3; computations with local optimization in dimensions four and five found no violation, but are not proofs. This path-counting inequality is not known to follow from the triangle-compatible tournament count, since most compatible tournaments may be cyclic.
