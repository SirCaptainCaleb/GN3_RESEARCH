## Research note

For every \(k\ge2\), let
\[
M_k=\left(\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}U_{1,2}\right)\oplus U_{1,3}.
\]
Its bases are \((x_1,\ldots,x_k,c)\in\mathbb F_2^k\times\{0,1,2\}\), and its basis graph is \(Q_k\square K_3\), of diameter \(k+1\). The canonical free involution \(\tau\) complements all \(k\) binary coordinates and fixes \(c\).

**Proposition (failure of full-resource positive galleries under deck-odd orientation).** There is a coloring \(\chi\) of the oriented basis-graph edges with
\[
\chi(v,u)=1-\chi(u,v),\qquad
\chi(\tau u,\tau v)=1-\chi(u,v),\tag{1}
\]
such that no injective sliding-window gallery with \(k+1\) successive basis exchanges, each changing a different matroid component, has all its arcs colored one. In particular, even a canonical deck-odd reversal-odd orientation of the basis graph of a partition matroid need not admit a positive gallery whose exchange count equals the basis-graph diameter.

*Construction.* Write \(a=x_1,b=x_2\). On the component-one and component-two edges oriented from bit \(x_i\) to \(1-x_i\), define
\[
\chi_A=a\oplus\mathbf 1_{\{c=1\}},\qquad
\chi_B=b\oplus\mathbf 1_{\{c=2\}}.\tag{2}
\]
On each additional binary component \(i\ge3\), set \(\chi_i=x_i\), the departing bit. For the \(U_{1,3}\) exchange \(c\to d\), let \(\chi_C=1\) precisely when \(c\) precedes \(d\) in the following total order:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
(a,b)& \text{order for positive exchanges}\\\hline
(0,0)&2\prec1\prec0\\
(0,1)&1\prec0\prec2\\
(1,0)&2\prec0\prec1\\
(1,1)&0\prec1\prec2.
\end{array}\tag{3}
\]
Every binary-edge orientation reverses its color when traversed backward. Each row in (3) is a linear order, so reversing a \(C\)-exchange also complements its color. Complementing \(a,b\) interchanges the rows \(00\leftrightarrow11\) and \(01\leftrightarrow10\), whose orders are reverses; the other binary bits do not occur in (2)--(3). Hence (1) holds for every edge.

*Proof of obstruction.* In a gallery using each component once, erase exchanges in components \(3,\ldots,k\). Their colors and effects on \(a,b,c\) do not influence (2)--(3). It therefore suffices to rule out three positive exchanges using each of \(A,B,C\) once. Write the \(C\)-exchange as \(c\to d\), \(c\ne d\), and denote the binary bits immediately before \(C\) by \(a_C,b_C\).

If the \(A\)-exchange occurs before \(C\), positivity in (2) gives \(a_C=\mathbf1_{\{c=1\}}\). If it occurs afterward, positivity gives \(a_C=\mathbf1_{\{d\ne1\}}\). Similarly,
\[
b_C=\begin{cases}\mathbf1_{\{c=2\}},&B\text{ before }C,\\
\mathbf1_{\{d\ne2\}},&B\text{ after }C.
\end{cases}\tag{4}
\]
These necessary conditions restrict the \(C\)-exchange as follows:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
c\to d&\text{necessary state at }C&\chi_C\\\hline
0\to1&a_C=0,\ b_C\text{ arbitrary}&0\\
1\to0&a_C=1,\ b_C\text{ arbitrary}&0\\
0\to2&b_C=0,\ a_C\text{ arbitrary}&0\\
2\to0&b_C=1,\ a_C\text{ arbitrary}&0\\
1\to2&(a_C,b_C)=(1,0)&0\\
2\to1&(a_C,b_C)=(0,1)&0.
\end{array}\tag{5}
\]
The final column follows directly from the four linear orders (3). Thus if the \(A\) and \(B\) exchanges are positive, the \(C\) exchange is negative, regardless of their temporal order. Consequently no positive gallery uses all \(k+1\) components. \(\square\)

**Implication.** The naive unified conjecture asserting a positive reversal-odd sliding-window gallery of at least \(\operatorname{diam}\Gamma(M)\) exchanges for every partition matroid with canonical deck-odd exchange orientation is false in all dimensions. The obstruction occurs in a legitimate partition matroid, with local \(U_{1,3}\)-exchange tournaments transitive and with the polarization induced by a canonical basis involution. It does not refute NORI1 (which has no \(U_{1,3}\) component), the boundary-tournament square-root bound, or the seam-controlled marked-gallery conjectures of Article XI. It shows that combining the cubical diameter term with the terminal-set square-root term requires an additional compatibility condition that excludes mixed-factor phase frustration.
