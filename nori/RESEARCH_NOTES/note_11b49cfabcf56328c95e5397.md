## Research note

For m at least three, prescribed paired sweeps have 2^(m-2) collisions per parity fiber.

Set p_i=x_(a_i)+x_(b_i), t_i=x_(a_i) in F2. For i=0 use arbitrary Boolean g_u(t_1,...,t_(m-1)), indexed by exterior parity u=p_-0, with g_u(v+1)=1+g_u(v). For 1<=i<=m-2 let f_i=h_(i,p)(t_(i+1),...,t_(m-1)), where each h is odd under simultaneous complementation of its arguments and is independent of p_0. Let f_(m-1)=t_0+eta_p(t_1,...,t_(m-2)), where eta is even under complementation and independent of p_0. All gates ignore their own pair and obey antipodal oddness.
