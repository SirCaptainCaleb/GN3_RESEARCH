## Research note

In Q6, color edge direction i by x_{sigma(i)} for sigma=(01)(23)(45). This is antipodally odd and invariant under Klein four-group relabelings on A={0,1,2,3}. At root zero, the 24 A-first dimension orders with suffix 4,5 have color words (0,1,0,1,0,1) or (0,0,1,1,0,1). Their switch-rank sets are {1,2,3,4,5} and {2,4,5}. Put T=Z4 and L=T wedge (e4-e5). From Zp=(p/4)T+up for p<=4 and Z5=T/2+(e4-e5)/2, the full quadratic bivector projects onto L as (5/4)L or (3/4)L, respectively. Thus all 24 tensors lie in a strict positive halfspace, in sharp contrast to the boundary tournament parity tetrahedron, whose tensor barycenter vanishes on each Klein orbit. Legal cube edge coloring can retain the entire Klein symmetry and supply a nonzero external bivector. This distinction blocks a naive identification of the two coloring consistency constraints based solely on local permutation symmetries.
