## Research note

A legal affine Q4 counterexample to adjacent-root continuity of the tropical orientation field.

Number directions 0,1,2,3 and use binary arithmetic:
c_0(x)=x_1,
c_1(x)=x_0+x_2+x_3,
c_2(x)=x_0+x_1+x_3,
c_3(x)=x_0+x_1+x_2.
Every direction rule ignores its own bit and depends on an odd number of other bits. Thus the coloring is well-defined on undirected edges and reverses under antipodal translation.

For the rooted two-ended tropical switch kernel K_x(a,b) (Article VIII, equations HL23--HL25), direct classification of the 24 full direction orders gives these temporal-word multiplicities:

root x=0: word 0101 occurs 16 times, word 0111 occurs 4 times, word 0001 occurs 4 times.

root x=1 (bit zero set): word 0010 occurs 6 times, word 1110 occurs 2 times, word 1010 occurs 8 times, word 1011 occurs 6 times, word 1000 occurs 2 times.

For each path word w, its augmented (a,b) cost is [a != first(w)] + (# successive color changes in w) + [last(w) != b]. Minimizing over the displayed word lists yields
K_0=[[2,1],[3,2]], K_1=[[2,3],[1,2]].
Consequently the antipodally odd scalar root polarization s_x=(K_x(0,1)-K_x(1,0))/2 satisfies s_0=-1 and s_1=+1, although vertices 0 and 1 are adjacent. Thus the candidate adjacent-root Lipschitz property |s_x-s_{x+e_i}|<=1 is FALSE, even for affine legal colorings with four directions.

For the oriented seam x=1, y=0, i=0, its color is b=c_0(1)=0. The common (n-1)-face energy is E=K_01(y=0,z=bar x=14)=3, whereas rooted energies K_01(x=1,bar x=14)=3 and K_01(y=0,bar y=15)=1. Thus the nonnegative even endpoint forcing gaps of (HL29) are 0 and 2. The quantized seam law (HL30) explains the sign jump without contradicting antipodal oddness.

The coloring itself is successful (NORI1 holds in Q4), and no counterexample to NORI1 is claimed. The theorem refutes a naive scalar discrete intermediate-value proof. Any higher global obstruction must control joint root values and quantized seam penalties rather than merely antisymmetry of s under antipodal translation.
