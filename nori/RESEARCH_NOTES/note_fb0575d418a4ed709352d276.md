## Research note

**Explicit obstruction to transporting rank-two convex fillers through an arbitrary critical-dimensional contraction.** Take six symbols 0,1,2,3,4,5 and the terminal braid cell with orders (a,b,c,3,4,5), where (a,b,c) permutes 0,1,2. Prescribe ordered triple bits by b(012)=b(021)=b(102)=0 and their reverses b(210)=b(120)=b(201)=1; prescribe b(y,z,3)=1 only for (y,z)=(1,2), zero for the other five ordered distinct pairs in {0,1,2}; prescribe b(t,3,4)=1 for t=0,1,2 and b(3,4,5)=0. Reversal-conjugate triples receive complementary bits; all other triples are arbitrary on reversal pairs. This defines a legal unrestricted reversal-odd coloring.

The six color words and corresponding switch-prefix ranks are:
012: (0,1,1,0), S={2,4};
021: (0,0,1,0), S={3,4};
102: (0,0,1,0), S={3,4};
120: (1,0,1,0), S={2,3,4};
201: (1,0,1,0), S={2,3,4};
210: (1,0,1,0), S={2,3,4}.
Thus all six flags are bad. Let B_abc be the full quadratic switch bivector sum over every pair of switch ranks for the flag abc345. Their convex hull avoids zero: the decomposable covector ((e_0^*+e_1^*+e_2^*)/3) wedge e_5^* is strictly negative on every B_abc, by the rank-two full-bivector separator theorem.

Nonetheless, set u=(-17,-5,43,-19,-13,11) in W, and use contraction F_abc = B_abc u (matrix form, which differs from interior contraction by a global negative). Direct rational calculation gives the **positive convex relation**
23 F_012 + 14 F_021 + 2 F_120 + 2 F_201 = 0.
The coefficients sum to 41; no selected F is zero. The four selected projected vectors are affinely independent in their three-dimensional image subspace {x in W: x_4=x_5, <x,u>=0}. Hence the origin lies in the relative interior of their simplex, and this cancellation persists under all sufficiently small perturbations of u; the exceptional contraction vectors that fail the universal rational-faithfulness property form a meager algebraic subset, so the phenomenon also occurs for faithful contractions arbitrarily close to this u.

**Conclusion.** A fixed rationally faithful contraction preserves individual nonzero flag labels and their distinct rays, but need not preserve the zero-free convex hull of a rank-two Coxeter residue. Therefore a color-sensitive convex filling in Lambda^2 W cannot simply be pushed linearly into the critical (n-2)-dimensional target. This does not rule out other faithful contraction choices or nonlinear equivariant fillings.
