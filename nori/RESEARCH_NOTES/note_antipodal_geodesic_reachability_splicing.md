## Research note

Let R(a) be the vertices reachable from a by a monochromatic geodesic of either color, including the empty path. For antipodal a and ā, R(a)∩R(ā)≠∅ if and only if there exists a 2-cp antipodal geodesic: geodesics a→x and x→ā concatenate because d(a,x)+d(x,ā)=n. Their colors may differ. If a specified color is essential, write R(a;i) for the vertices reachable by a color-i geodesic; then R(a;i)∩R(ā;i)≠∅ characterizes a monochromatic antipodal geodesic of color i.
