## Research note

A legal paired-flat Q6 coloring with eight-bit gate masks C6,8F,01 has a root 7 whose four red full geodesics all cross edge 31->29 internally, while none of the eight vertices of its paired-square orbit starts a red full geodesic. Extending with added pair selectors x1 preserves the same root-local orbit crossing for every even dimension; other roots retain avoiding red full geodesics. Proof in nori_selector_locked_root_cut.md.
THEOREM (selector-locked spectator pairs). Let c be any legal paired-flat coloring on Q_(2m), x a root, and e one red edge in original direction d that every red full geodesic rooted at x traverses. Add k pairs with gates f_j(y)=y_d while retaining all old gates independent of the new bits. For a fixed lifted root X=(x,s), every red full geodesic projects to a red full x-geodesic, hence crosses e. Each new pair must take one red step before direction d and its other red step after it: the selector y_d flips at direction d and otherwise stays constant. Thus the added bits at e are independent of all step interleavings, and the lifted paths all cross one lifted edge. If e is in paired-square orbit S, all rooted lifted paths meet the same orbit S_k. If no vertex of S roots a red full geodesic, the same holds for S_k by projection. A red full path avoiding S can be extended by placing one new-pair red step before direction d and one after, so the lifted coloring remains globally successful from other roots.
EXPLICIT CERTIFICATE. For Q6 pairs (0,1),(2,3),(4,5), pack each gate's four outside bits into z in increasing original-direction order and put f_i=bit_(min(z,15-z))(M_i) xor [z>=8], with eight-bit masks M=(C6,8F,01). From root7 the only four red full direction words are (3,4,1,2,5,0), (3,4,1,5,2,0), (4,3,1,2,5,0), (4,3,1,5,2,0). They all traverse edge 31->29 in direction1 as their third step. Its paired-square orbit has old exterior bits 7 and 8 and vertices {28,29,30,31,32,33,34,35}; none starts a red full geodesic. There are 200 rooted red full geodesics avoiding that orbit from other roots. For k new pairs with gate y1, lifted root 7 with new bits zero has 4(k!)^2 binom(k+2,2)binom(k+3,3) red full geodesics, all hitting the same lifted square once internally. Direct direction-subset checks give 4,48,960 rooted paths for Q6,Q8,Q10, zero rooted avoiding paths and zero successful roots in the respective square orbits. See independent checker nori_single_root_all_dim_cut_verifier.py.
ROOTED PHASE CUT. In the monotone direction-subset red DAG rooted at x, retain the live vertices on some red full path from x to its antipode. If every such path uses precisely one edge of selected red set S, then each live vertex v has a uniquely determined number phi_x(v) in {0,1}: count S-edges on any live red prefix x->v. Two prefixes with different counts could be extended by the same red suffix v->bar x, giving full red paths with different S-hit counts, impossible. Each live oriented edge u->v satisfies phi_x(v)-phi_x(u)=1_(uv in S). This makes S a root-local directed cut, but does not generate a global cut across all roots. Moreover any red full paths, possibly from different roots, can exchange u->v segments when their ordered endpoints u,v agree: each segment uses the same set of original directions u xor v. Arbitrary exchanges of prefixes or suffixes between unrelated roots remain invalid. The selector-locked family shows that a root-local cut with one common red edge and no S-rooted geodesics can persist in every even dimension while other roots give avoiding witnesses. Hence NORI1 forcing must combine different successful roots or produce an independent repair. Full details nori_selector_locked_root_cut.md.
