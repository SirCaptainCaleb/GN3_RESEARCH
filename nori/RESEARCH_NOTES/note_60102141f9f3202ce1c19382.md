## Research note

Let \(n=2m\ge4\). Under the antipodally odd Hamming-layer coloring \(c(e)=j\bmod2\) for edges joining the \(j\)-th and \((j+1)\)-st layers about \(0^n\), the directed monochromatic full geodesics start precisely at vertices \(x\) of weight \(m\). Their token orders alternate between initially zero and initially one coordinate directions, with either type permitted first.

**Proposition (vanishing after monochromatic selection).** Fix any such \(x\), an ordering of its \(n\) coordinate resources, and let \(\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)\) be the determinant sign of the full-rank resource gallery with direction order \(\pi\). Then
\[
\sum_{\pi:\,P(x,\pi)\text{ monochromatic}}\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)=0.
\]
The same holds after summing over all roots, although this coloring has \(2n!\) directed monochromatic antipodal geodesics.

**Proof.** The initial zero-coordinate set \(Z=\{i:x_i=0\}\) has cardinality \(m\ge2\). Fix distinct \(a,b\in Z\). Every monochromatic full geodesic alternates an upward step flipping an element of \(Z\) with a downward step flipping an element of its complement. Exchanging the positions occupied by directions \(a\) and \(b\) preserves this alternation, preserves the color word and endpoint, and reverses the parity of the direction permutation. The operation is a fixed-point-free involution on the monochromatic gallery set, so the signed sum vanishes. Other roots have no monochromatic full gallery. \(\square\)

**Interpretation.** The bare determinant-sign degree already cancels on most Hamming endpoint fibers (see the full-rank permutation-gallery theorem). Here, selecting all successful monochromatic full cube geodesics still produces zero signed count. A successful degree-based proof must employ more refined local coefficients or quotient by these sign-reversing symmetries; the chirotope sign and zero-switch predicate alone are insufficient. This restricts a proposed method and does not refute NORI1.
