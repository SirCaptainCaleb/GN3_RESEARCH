## Research note

For arbitrary edge coloring, a root initially lacking nondecreasing geodesics becomes favorable after changing one edge from one to zero precisely when the edge tail is reachable from the root through zero edges and its head admits a nondecreasing completion to the antipode. This follows by splitting the repaired path at its changed zero edge. The two endpoint sets are disjoint before repair.
