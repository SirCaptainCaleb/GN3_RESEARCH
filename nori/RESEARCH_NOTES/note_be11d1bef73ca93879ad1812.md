## Research note

Number directions 0,1,2,3. Define c_0=x_1, c_1=1+x_3, c_2=x_0+x_1+x_3, c_3=1+x_2 over F_2. Each rule ignores its own bit and has odd dependence parity, so this is a legal antipodally odd coloring of Q_4.

The full geodesic rooted at integer 0 in order (0,1,2,3) has temporal word 0100, with one minority edge. The three adjacent-swap orders (1,0,2,3), (0,2,1,3), (0,1,3,2) have respective words 1100, 0110, 0111. Forward cyclic root slide to integer root 1 and order (1,2,3,0) gives word 1001. Inverse cyclic root slide to integer root 8 and order (3,0,1,2) gives 1010. Each elementary neighbor has at least two minority edges.

Nevertheless the geodesic rooted at integer 9, order (0,1,2,3), has word 0000. Thus monotone nonincreasing minority-defect repair by adjacent swaps and cyclic root slides fails for an arbitrary one-defect witness, even for a legal affine coloring.
