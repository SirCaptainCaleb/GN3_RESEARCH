## Research note

Suppose an antipodally odd edge coloring admits a monochromatic path P from x to its antipode of length n+2. Its coordinate word traverses one coordinate s three times and all others once. Let r be the smaller number of intervening coordinates between consecutive occurrences of s. Replace the segment s,W,s with the r-edge W path on the other s-layer. The resulting path is an antipodal geodesic, and all its edges outside the replacement retain the original monochromatic color. Therefore it has at most r+1 switches. Rotating a geodesic root and omitting a positive cyclic turn makes the minimum number of switches even; hence mu(c) <= 2 floor((r+1)/2). In particular, mu(c) <= 2 whenever r<=2. Thus in a hypothetical coloring with mu(c)>=4, every monochromatic antipodal path of length n+2 must have at least three other directions between the first and second s and between the second and third s; necessarily n>=7. This is a local certificate for shortening a port cocircuit of size n+3 and intersection two into a minimum-size port cocircuit of intersection at most four.
