## Research note

Counterexample to universal connectivity of favorable roots. In Q4 order direction-edge bits by direction i=0,1,2,3 and then lower endpoints x from 0 to 15 with bit i=0. Set the 32 edge colors to the concatenated binary string 10001100111100100101010100110011. Direct enumeration of all 16 starting roots and all 24 direction orders gives A0={0,3,4,5,8,10,12,13,14,15}, interpreting integers as binary cube vertices. Root 3 has no neighboring vertex in A0, so A0 is disconnected. Yet 3 and 12 are antipodal and both favorable, as expected: this is not a NORI1 counterexample. The result rules out a proposed universal proof based merely on graph connectivity of A0.
