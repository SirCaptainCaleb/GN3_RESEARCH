## Research note

Assume an antipodally odd Q_n edge coloring has no full monochromatic antipodal geodesic. Every monochromatic (n-2)-edge geodesic P from x to y with unused directions i,j has both unused-direction edges at x and y of the opposite color: otherwise it extends to a monochromatic (n-1)-edge geodesic, which already implies NORI1 by the near-full extension theorem. As tau(y)=x XOR e_i XOR e_j, the opposite corner of the {i,j}-square at x is tau(y), so its two incident unused-direction edges have the original path color, by deck oddness. The analogous square at y has opposite corner tau(x) and the same complementary edge pattern. This yields a pair of forced bichromatic terminal squares for any such maximal near-full path, suggesting simultaneous codimension-two completion as a promising next argument. Not a closure proof.
