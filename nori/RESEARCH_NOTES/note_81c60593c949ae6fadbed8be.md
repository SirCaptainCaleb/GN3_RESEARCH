## Research note

For exterior ordered symbols E=(e1,...,er), let s_e be each nonzero signed tournament score in W_A, v=e_e1-e_er and T=1_A-(|A|/n)1. The formula L(lambda)=[sum_e lambda_e s_e+(1-sum_e lambda_e^2)T] wedge v is nonzero for every simplex probability vector lambda: away from simplex vertices its T component is positive, at each vertex the score is nonzero. Face restriction is compatible; reversing E negates L. All L(lambda) lie outside and orthogonal to the local tetrahedral parity bivector module, so cone F((1-t)o+ty,lambda)=(1-t)L(lambda)+t B_boundary(y) is a nonzero family of compatible rank-three fillings even for opposite score channels. This repairs interpolation among exterior channels, not gluing between different moving blocks or arbitrary residues.
