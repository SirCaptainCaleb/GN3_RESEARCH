## Research note

**Proposition.** Fix distinct coordinates a,b of Q_{m+2}, and identify the four codimension-two faces indexed by (a,b) with Q_m. For any two arbitrary undirected-edge colorings d,e of Q_m, there exists an antipodally odd edge coloring c of Q_{m+2} whose restrictions to the adjacent faces F_{00},F_{10} are d,e. Moreover all edges in exterior directions a,b can be chosen freely on one representative of each antipodal edge pair.

**Proof.** The antipodal map sends an edge of F_{00} to an edge of F_{11}, and an edge of F_{10} to one of F_{01}; no antipodal edge pair is contained in F_{00} union F_{10}. Assign d and e on these two faces. Assign the complementary colors to the respective antipodal edges in F_{11} and F_{01}. Every remaining edge is in direction a or b and thus belongs to an antipodal pair disjoint from all already colored edges. Assign one color arbitrarily to each such pair and its complement to the opposite edge. This gives a globally antipodally odd coloring. QED.

**Consequence.** The pair of restrictions used in Article X endpoint-coincidence induction has no intrinsic coupling whatsoever, beyond its eventual completion by the exterior edges. Hence any assertion that EVERY ambient antipodally odd coloring admits corresponding at-most-two-switch face-spanning endpoints in a FIXED adjacent face pair is equivalent to the universal common-terminal assertion E_2(d) intersection E_2(e) nonempty for all pairs of arbitrary edge colorings of Q_m. A statement asserting existence of SOME adjacent pair among the many choices can still exploit their global coupling. Restricting oddness separately to either face is invalid.
