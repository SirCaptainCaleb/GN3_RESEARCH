## Research note

Strengthening the prior adjacent-root complement bridge: if two color-a midpoint geodesics from adjacent color-a roots x,y in direction i use complementary direction sets, then a full monochromatic antipodal n-geodesic exists whenever the half-path containing i has i at EITHER END. First i: the root-joining edge cancels a reverse first step; add the favorable antipodal terminal i-edge. Last i: remove that last edge; reverse the remaining path if needed, cross the root edge and follow the other half-path. The resulting n steps use each direction once and have antipodal endpoints. Thus a hypothetical no-geodesic coloring must keep i strictly internal in every such pair of witnesses; n=4 cannot have an internal i in any half-path.
