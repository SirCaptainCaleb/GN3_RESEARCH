## Research note

THEOREM (simple geodesic-bigon normal form). Let c be any antipodally odd legal coloring of Q_m and form paired balanced Q_(2m), projecting (x,y) to z=x XOR y. NORI1 for c is equivalent to existence of a red lift of one of these SIMPLE base cycles with fiber endpoint bar x: choose a direction permutation pi, nonempty proper subset A containing pi's last label but not its first, and B=[m] minus A. Let W be the concatenation pi restricted to A, then all pi, then pi restricted to B (each restriction retains relative pi order). Every direction occurs twice.

SIMPLE CYCLE PROOF. Start at base zero. The first segment visits supports contained in A, ending at A. In the middle segment, after using prefix T of pi, the base state has support (A minus T) union (B intersect T). Every strictly internal middle state has a nonempty A part (pi ends in A) and B part (pi begins in B); those states are all distinct and cannot meet first/last segment states. The final segment visits supports contained in B, ending at zero. Hence W traces a simple 2m-cycle, decomposable into two internally disjoint antipodal geodesics between the base corners A and B.

FORWARD. Given a red full geodesic in c starting at x, with direction order pi, let A={i:c_i(x)=blue}, B={i:c_i(x)=red}. The first pi label lies in B, and the last lies in A, by red color at the final antipodal vertex. In the paired lift take dummy steps for A at the starting old vertex, then the original red path pi, then dummy steps for B at the final old vertex, with dummy orders inherited from pi. Every step is red, and the projected base word is W.

CONVERSE. Any red lift of W from fiber x to bar x uses each base label twice. Since the original x bit i changes parity once between x and bar x, among the two lifted occurrences of base direction i precisely one uses the original direction and one the paired dummy direction. Hence the lifted red walk uses all 2m distinct directions once. After deleting dummy steps it becomes a red full antipodal geodesic in original c.

HOLONOMY. Red lifting of base direction i acts on fiber x by U_i(x)=x XOR e_i if c_i(x)=red, otherwise identity. Let H_W be their composition for word W. Unrestricted NORI1 is equivalent to some pi,A,x satisfying H_W(x)=bar x. There are m!*2^(m-2) choices of parameters, possibly including repeated representations of some cycles. The theorem gives a complete short embedded base-cycle target for future topology or exchange, not an unconditional existence theorem.
