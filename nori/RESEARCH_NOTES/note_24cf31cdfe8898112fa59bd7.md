## Research note

Let c be any legal edge coloring of Q_n and suppose a root r and tournament T on [n] determine an all-red triangle-compatible edge-row selection w_i=r xor (sum of e_j over j preceding i in T), with the i-th bit ignored, and c_i(w_i)=0 for all i. Suppose S is a set of n-k directions whose induced tournament is transitive, ordered s_1,...,s_(n-k). Suppose each j outside S has a threshold cut q_j along S: s_t precedes j in T if and only if t<=q_j. Insert each exceptional direction j at its threshold cut, choosing any tie order. This gives a single full antipodal geodesic rooted at r on which every edge in a direction of S is red; hence at most k edges are blue. Proof: for i in S, every direction preceding i in the new total order agrees with its predecessor status in T, including all exceptional directions, so the traversed direction-i edge has the same exterior row w_i and is red. The path visits each direction once. No independent witness gluing occurs. With two exceptional directions p,q, if their cuts coincide, order them according to their T arc to obtain an all-red full geodesic. If their cuts differ and their mutual T arc agrees with cut order, T is transitive and the same conclusion holds. Otherwise they form one backward exceptional arc and the only row discrepancies under the inserted order are w_p xor e_q and w_q xor e_p. The two exceptional edge colors on the resulting geodesic are the original Boolean derivatives D_q c_p(w_p) and D_p c_q(w_q) (color 0 at w_p,w_q). If both derivatives vanish, the completed full geodesic is monochromatic. In any hypothetical bad coloring, every such shared-root near-transitive all-red row selection with two exceptional directions must therefore have a nonzero derivative in at least one of these two sites. A reproducible randomized original-color audit of n=4,5,6,7 checks the insertion and derivative identities at https://github.com/SirCaptainCaleb/GN3_RESEARCH/blob/eaed36651c026cd8efe619ba45046d6acbf38b36/nori/certificates/nori_article_xvi_spine_completion_verify.py . Limitation: the theorem is conditional on a near-transitive row selection. It does not establish that such a selection exists in every legal coloring; proving sufficiently small exceptional sets across arbitrary colorings remains the missing forcing mechanism.
