## Research note

**Proposition (nonzero winding on the INITIAL dihedral residue).** In \(Q_4\), for the standard second-switch label \(\ell(x,\pi)=(-1)^{w_1(x,\pi)}e_{s_2(x,\pi)}\in\{\pm e_2,\pm e_3\}\), there is an antipodally odd undirected-edge coloring such that the eight full-geodesic flags in an initial rank-two parabolic \(r_0,r_1\) residue each have at least two switches, but the induced canonical short-edge boundary map of the dual eight-gon has winding number \(+1\) around the target circle \(S^1\). It therefore admits no filling of that dual two-cell with its specified boundary edge paths.

*Certificate.* Index cube vertices by binary integers \(0,\ldots,15\), with dimension \(j\in\{0,1,2,3\}\) flipping integer bit \(2^j\). Give the following twelve undirected edges the indicated colors, listed in order:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrrrr}
e &(0,1)&(0,2)&(0,4)&(1,3)&(1,5)&(2,3)&(2,6)&(3,7)&(4,12)&(5,13)&(6,14)&(7,15)\\
c(e)&0&1&0&1&1&0&1&1&1&0&0&0
\end{array}
\]
All twelve belong to distinct antipodal edge orbits, so complement their antipodal mates and assign any remaining orbits arbitrarily.

Consider the initial square face with dimensions \(0,1\), leaving suffix dimensions \(2,3\) in that order. The eight flags in order around its \(r_0,r_1\) residue, with root written as an integer and order as a dimension word, have the following full-geodesic words and second-switch labels:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{root}&\pi&\text{word}&\ell\\ \hline
0&0123&0110&+e_3\\
1&0123&0110&+e_3\\
1&1023&1010&-e_2\\
3&1023&1001&-e_3\\
3&0123&0101&+e_2\\
2&0123&0110&+e_3\\
2&1023&1010&-e_2\\
0&1023&1010&-e_2
\end{array}
\]
(The symbol \(e_2\) denotes the first target-circle axis, and \(e_3\) the second; they are indexed by switch locations, not cube dimensions.) The successive target vertices, including return to the start, are
\[
+e_3,\ +e_3,\ -e_2,\ -e_3,\ +e_2,\ +e_3,\ -e_2,\ -e_2,\ +e_3.
\]
Every consecutive pair is equal or adjacent in the four-cycle \((+e_2,+e_3,-e_2,-e_3)\). Their signed quarter-turn increments sum to \(+4\), giving winding \(+1\). Thus the boundary is not nullhomotopic. \(\square\)

**Implication.** The two initial flag exchanges \(r_0,r_1\) can already exhibit nontrivial winding. Hence the proposed strategy of filling *each rank-two dihedral residue independently using the canonical shortest target edges* is false even for the original cube geometry. This does not disprove NORI1: the coloring above has favorable complete paths elsewhere. In higher dimensions the loop lies inside a higher-dimensional simply connected target sphere, so this is a method obstruction in dimension four, not an obstruction to individual dihedral fillings in all dimensions. Compatibility of color-sensitive corrections across neighboring cells remains the issue.
