## Research note

# A balanced shortcut refutes short-port amplification

This note refutes both forms of Conjecture A in Article XI: even binary representability, cographic port deletion, triangle/square generation of the balanced cycle space, connected shortest-cocircuit exchanges of symmetric difference at most six, and universal unrestricted two-intersection do not imply P_4.

## Construction

Use the established cube turn graph H=H_6=L(Q_6)/tau and its monodromy cochain a. Write cube vertices as integers 0,...,63, with tau(x)=63 xor x. The three consecutive cube edges
e_0={0,1}, e_1={1,3}, e_2={3,7}
give a two-turn path u_0,u_1,u_2 in H, where u_i=[e_i]. The vertices u_0,u_2 are nonadjacent. Add one edge z=u_0u_2, obtaining H^+. Extend the cochain by
a^+(z)=a(u_0u_1)+a(u_1u_2).
Thus T={u_0u_1,u_1u_2,z} is a balanced triangle. Set
D^+=M([B_{H^+} 0; a^+ 1])^*,
with port p. This is a binary pointed matroid on 482 elements. Its deletion at p is M(H^+)^*, hence cographic. Also D^+/z=D_6.

## All the proposed hypotheses hold

1. Universal two-intersection. For every port-containing binary cycle X of D^+, restriction X_0=X\{z} is a port-containing binary cycle of D_6. The established six-dimensional monochromatic antipodal-geodesic theorem, already used in Articles VIII and XI, gives a port cocircuit Y of D_6 with |X_0 intersection Y|=2. Cocircuits of D^+/z are precisely the cocircuits of D^+ avoiding z, so Y is also a cocircuit of D^+, with |X intersection Y|=2. This uses only the known finite-dimensional cube theorem, not the all-dimensional geodesic conjecture or the 2026 nongeodesic theorem.

2. Triangle/square generation. The kernel of monodromy on the graph cycle space of H is generated by its star triangles and square turn cycles, as proved in Article XI. If a balanced binary graph cycle vector of H^+ uses z, add the balanced triangle T to eliminate z. The remainder is balanced and supported in H. Hence the same generators together with T generate Z((D^+)^*\p), and each is a circuit of size three or four.

3. Port cogirth and complete classification of shortest witnesses. Every odd-monodromy cycle avoiding z has length at least six. Replacing z in an odd-monodromy cycle by u_0u_1u_2 gives an odd closed walk of length one greater in H. Any odd closed walk contains an odd simple cycle, so the original cycle has length at least five. Equality is attained. If its length is five, the replacement is a length-six odd closed walk; minimality of the odd girth implies it is simple. By the cube contact-excess theorem it is the turn cycle of a full antipodal geodesic.

Choose its orientation and lift so that its consecutive edge states include e_0,e_1,e_2. This forces the initial three cube directions to be 0,1,2, starting at vertex 0. The remaining directions are 3,4,5 in an arbitrary order pi. Conversely every such order gives a shortest cycle:
C_pi=(u_0,u_2,[f_1],[f_2],[f_3],u_0),
where f_1,f_2,f_3 are the successive edges from vertex 7 to vertex 63 in directions pi, and the closing contact uses tau(e_0). There are precisely 3!=6 such cycles. Therefore g_p(D^+)=6, and its shortest port cocircuits are Y_pi=C_pi union {p}.

4. Connected bounded exchanges. Adjacent transpositions of pi change two successive cube edges f_i,f_{i+1}. Only three turns change, so |Y_pi symmetric-difference Y_pi'| is at most six. The adjacent-transposition graph on S_3 is connected. In fact the complete threshold-six exchange graph here is a six-cycle.

## A port cycle meeting every shortest cocircuit in six elements

Prescribe an antipodally odd cube-edge coloring c by c(e_0)=1 and c(e_2)=0. On the three-dimensional subcube with bits 0,1,2 all equal to 1, color an edge in direction j in {3,4,5} by
c({x,x xor 2^j})=1+(popcount(x>>3) mod 2) mod 2,
where x_j=0. Prescribe complementary colors on its antipodal subcube. These assignments and those on e_0,e_2 have disjoint antipodal edge orbits, so extend arbitrarily to the other orbits.

For every pi, the lifted edge-state list
e_0,e_2,f_1,f_2,f_3,tau(e_0)
has colors 1,0,1,0,1,0. Let d be the resulting admissible turn cochain on H and set d^+(z)=c(e_0)+c(e_2)=1. Since d=a+delta f, the same switching function gives d^+=a^++delta f. Thus X=supp(d^+) union {p} is a binary cycle of D^+ containing p.

All five turns of every C_pi have d^+-value one. Consequently
|X intersection Y_pi|=6
for all six shortest port cocircuits. In particular P_4(D^+,p) fails, although all four hypotheses of the proposed amplification conjecture hold.

## Independent finite audit

Direct construction gives |V(H)|=96, |E(H)|=480, and |E(H^+)|=481. Binary elimination of all old star-triangle and square generators plus T gives rank 385=481-96, the full dimension of the balanced cycle space of H^+. Enumeration of all four-step paths in L(Q_6) from e_2 to tau(e_0) gives precisely six. Adding z gives six length-five odd cycles; the constructed coloring gives port-intersection list [6,6,6,6,6,6]. Their threshold-six exchange graph has degree list [2,2,2,2,2,2] and is connected. The symbolic classification above proves completeness; these computations are supplementary checks.

## What the obstruction isolates

The coextension is faithful: {z,p} is independent in D^+, and the port-avoiding cocircuit T reconstructs z from two old turns. Nevertheless contraction raises port cogirth from six to seven. Universal two-intersection is inherited from the surviving cube, while the shortcut creates a new, uniformly expensive shortest witness family. Thus bounded reconstruction, bounded balanced generators, and exchange connectivity do not preserve the alignment between unrestricted low-intersection witnesses and minimum cardinality.

This does not refute NORI1, the canonical cube short-port assertions, or the resource-marked boundary conjectures. The added edge joins nonincident cube-edge states, so it is not an allowed cube turn. A replacement theorem must retain geometric/resource legality or impose a condition preventing balanced shortcuts from lowering the relevant witness length; the existing cogirth-preserving contraction theorem survives unchanged.
