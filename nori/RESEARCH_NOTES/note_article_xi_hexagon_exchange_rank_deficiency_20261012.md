## Research note

# Binary rank of global geodesic-exchange hexagons

**Status: all-dimensional dimension formula now proved for n>=5 in Article XI Subsection 20, with an explicit affine charge and direct-sum normal form. Finite computations below remain independent checks.** Let \(H_n=L(Q_n/\tau)\). Let \(\mathcal G_n\subseteq Z_1(H_n;\mathbb F_2)\) be the set of length-\(n\), monodromy-one turn cycles associated with full antipodal cube geodesics. Put
\[
\mathscr B_n=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{C\triangle C':C,C'\in\mathcal G_n\}.
\]
By the connected adjacent-coordinate-exchange theorem and the balanced-hexagon relation already established in Article XI, this is also the binary span of all six-turn cycles produced by a single adjacent transposition in the cyclic direction order. In particular
\[
\mathscr B_n\subseteq\ker\alpha\cap\ker\kappa.
\]

**Finite GF(2) rank computations.**
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrr}
n&|\mathcal G_n|&|E(H_n)|&\dim\mathscr B_n&
\dim(\ker\alpha\cap\ker\kappa)-\dim\mathscr B_n\\
3&4&12&3&2\\
4&24&48&22&9\\
5&192&160&115&4\\
6&1920&480&378&5\\
7&23040&1344&1113&6
\end{array}
\]
Here \(|V(H_n)|=n2^{n-2}\), \(|E(H_n)|=\binom n2 2^{n-1}\), so
\[
\dim(\ker\alpha\cap\ker\kappa)=|E(H_n)|-|V(H_n)|-1.
\]

**Theorem (hexagon-span codimension; proved in Article XI Subsection 20).** For \(n\ge5\),
\[
\boxed{\dim\mathscr B_n=|E(H_n)|-|V(H_n)|-n
=n(n-2)2^{n-2}-n.}
\]
Equivalently the space of balanced turn cycles modulo global adjacent-swap hexagons has dimension \(n-1\):
\[
\boxed{\dim\bigl((\ker\alpha\cap\ker\kappa)/\mathscr B_n\bigr)=n-1.}
\]
The formulas also happen to hold at \(n=3\), but **fail** at \(n=4\), where the deficiency equals nine rather than three. The exception must not be omitted from any eventual theorem.

**Equivalent local star-triangle form.** Let \(\varphi:Z_1(H_n)\to Z_1(F_n)\) be the line-graph turn projection, where \(F_n=Q_n/\tau\). The image of \(\mathscr B_n\) is generated by all coordinate squares of the folded cube, which span \(\ker\beta\) for the covering monodromy \(\beta\). Therefore
\[
\dim\varphi(\mathscr B_n)=(n-2)2^{n-2}.
\]
The kernel of \(\varphi\) is the direct sum of the star-clique cycle spaces and has dimension
\[
(n-1)(n-2)2^{n-2}.
\]
The proved rank formula is equivalent to
\[
\boxed{\dim\bigl(\ker\varphi/(\mathscr B_n\cap\ker\varphi)\bigr)=n.}
\]
Thus the problem can be reformulated as finding \(n\) intrinsic residual star-triangle classes after quotienting by all balanced hexagon exchanges. These may encode a new cohomological invariant of the polarized matroid-gallery complex.

**Reproducibility algorithm.** For each \(n\), label turn edges by a quotient cube-vertex orbit \([x]\) and an unordered direction pair \(i<j\). For each root \(x\in\mathbb F_2^n\) and permutation \(\pi=(i_1,\ldots,i_n)\), traverse coordinates in order, and take the XOR of the \(n\) turn-edge basis bits at the successive contact vertices, including the seam joining \(i_n\) back to \(i_1\). Deduplicate the resulting cycles \(C\); fix one \(C_0\), and use standard GF(2) Gaussian elimination on integer bitsets to compute the rank of \(\{C\triangle C_0:C\in\mathcal G_n\}\). The number of distinct cycles agrees in all tested dimensions with \(2^{n-2}(n-1)!\).

**Scope and relevance.** This computation is not a proof of the two-switch or monochromatic-geodesic conjecture. Even if the rank conjecture holds, the linear span alone cannot constrain *integer* intersection costs, because every color cochain differs from the covering monodromy by a cut. It instead detects a precise residual obstruction to generating all balanced turn cycles by legal geodesic exchanges. A useful next step is to identify an intrinsic basis of the \(n\)-dimensional residual star-triangle space and determine whether its nonlinear refinements obstruct high-cost geodesic gallery configurations.

**Correction and new cross-check, October 12.** In the displayed conjecture the expansion of \(|E(H_n)|-|V(H_n)|-n\) must be
\[
\boxed{n(n-2)2^{n-2}-n,}
\]
not \(n(n-3)2^{n-2}-n\). The unsimplified expression and all tabulated dimensions \(n=3,\ldots,7\) were correct.

For \(n=8\), a deterministic pseudorandom sample of 125,000 rooted full geodesics (Python seed 12345) yielded GF(2) difference-span rank **3,064**, stabilizing by sample 10,000. This agrees with the corrected predicted value
\[
|E(H_8)|-|V(H_8)|-8=3584-512-8=3064.
\]
Unlike the fully enumerated calculations through \(n=7\), the \(n=8\) sample is only a **proved lower bound** on the full span's rank, not an exhaustive upper bound. The conjectural codimension is seven in that dimension.

**Resolution.** Article XI Subsection 20 proves this dimension formula for every n>=5 and constructs a complete charge pair (parity, vertex vector modulo the all-ones vector) whose common kernel with monodromy is the global hexagon-exchange span. Stationary-free antipodal walks have vertex charge equal to the half-excess vector of their direction multiplicities modulo four. The same Subsection proves a direct-sum cycle-space decomposition into exchange hexagons, n fixed star triangles, and one antipodal-geodesic cycle.
