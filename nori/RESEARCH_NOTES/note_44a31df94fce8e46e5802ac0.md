## Research note

ALL-DIMENSION SHERALI-ADAMS AND SOS BARRIERS FOR COHERENT-OBSTACLE PATH BLOCKERS.

Consider any legal cube dimension n>=3 and any family F of full antipodal geodesics, including the family avoiding a prescribed coherent obstacle. Every path Q traverses n distinct antipodal edge orbits J_1,...,J_n. Assign independent orbit signs z_J in {-1,+1}; the color of Q's edge in orbit J_i is its orbit sign multiplied by a fixed phase epsilon_i. The linear blocker for the event Q is all red is
H_Q(z)=sum_{i=1}^n(1-epsilon_i*z_{J_i})/2 - 1 >= 0.
For an integral coloring, imposing H_Q>=0 for every Q excludes all-red paths in F.

THEOREM 1 (linear Sherali-Adams rank barrier). The fair independent product distribution on all edge-orbit signs supplies feasible lifted Boolean moments for EVERY rank d<=n-2 Sherali-Adams relaxation of this blocker system, no matter how many path clauses overlap. Proof: conditioning at most d orbit bits fixes at most d of the n path-edge variables. If r of the path variables are fixed, the expected number of blue edges is at least (n-r)/2. Thus the expected blocker is >=(n-r)/2-1>=0. Multiply by the nonnegative indicator of each conditioning event to obtain every lifted constraint. The product distribution satisfies all Boolean and moment-consistency axioms. Hence an infeasible blocker system cannot be refuted by Sherali-Adams below rank n-1.

THEOREM 2 (SOS multipliers require growing degree). Define D(n)=floor((n+1-sqrt(6n-3))/2). For all 0<=d<=D(n), every full path Q, and every real multilinear polynomial p(z) of Walsh degree at most d in all orbit signs, the fair product distribution obeys
E[H_Q*p(z)^2] >= [n/2-1-sqrt(d*(n-d+1))]*E[p(z)^2] >=0.
Consequently the same fair Boolean moments are feasible for SOS/moment localizations by squared polynomial multipliers of degree <=d, even if the full Boolean blocker system is UNSAT. The bound D(n)=n/2-O(sqrt n) forces degree proportional to dimension before such localizations can refute the system.

PROOF. Gauge each epsilon_i into its independent orbit variable. Then H_Q=n/2-1-(1/2)*sum_{i=1}^n z_i. Expand p in the Walsh basis indexed by subsets of all orbit variables. Fix the Walsh subset outside Q; multiplication by sum_i z_i preserves that exterior label, and within it acts on subsets of the n Q-variables of size <=r<=d as adjacency of the n-dimensional Hamming cube induced on Hamming weight levels 0,...,r. By Perron-Frobenius, its maximal eigenvector is nonnegative and invariant under all n-label permutations, because the induced ball graph is connected. In the orthonormal basis of uniform level vectors the adjacency operator is tridiagonal with off-diagonal weights sqrt((k+1)*(n-k)) for k=0,...,r-1. For r<=d<=n/2, all these are at most sqrt(d*(n-d+1)), so its spectral norm is at most 2*sqrt(d*(n-d+1)) by the maximal tridiagonal row-sum bound. Sum the orthogonal exterior-label blocks to obtain |E[p²*sum_i z_i]| <=2*sqrt(d*(n-d+1))*E[p²]. Substitution gives the stated inequality. The condition d*(n-d+1)<=(n-2)^2/4 is equivalent, in the relevant range, to d<=(n+1-sqrt(6n-3))/2.

INTERPRETATION. This is not a NORI1 proof. It is a universal lower bound on degree for a specific direct blocker-relaxation proof programme. It applies to the path families in Article XVI whether or not they are mutually geometrically coherent, and strengthens the archived finite level-two computational relaxation limitation. Thus raising a bounded number of moment levels on the individual path blockers cannot establish a dimension-uniform coherent-obstacle avoidance theorem. A successful forcing proof must exploit order transport or overlap among path constraints through additional nonlinear identities, or employ degree growing with n.
