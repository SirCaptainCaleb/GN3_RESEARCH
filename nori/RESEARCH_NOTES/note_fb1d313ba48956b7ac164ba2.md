## Research note

For legal odd colorings c on Q_n and d on Q_m, let N(c) count ordered rooted red antipodal geodesics. Then N(c square d)=binom(n+m,n) N(c) N(d). Proof: each product geodesic uniquely restricts to one full geodesic in each factor, and each pair has binom(n+m,n) interleavings. Consequently Z(c)=N(c)/n! is multiplicative. Independently verified exhaustive enumeration of all 65536 legal Q4 colorings found N in {8,12,16,20,24,28,32,36,40,44,48}, with frequencies {48,2464,11472,13440,15840,9408,7584,2688,1776,672,144}; min N=8, mean N=24, max N=48. To certify: index the 16 antipodal edge-orbit variables; for each of 384 rooted direction orders precompute the four variables and the required bits for red; evaluate each of 65536 assignments and count matching paths. An independent vectorized bit-mask implementation reproduces the table. Taking h Cartesian copies of an N=8 coloring yields N=(4h)!/3^h, hence normalized witness count Z=3^{-h}. Thus there is no universal constant positive lower bound for N/n! even among colorings satisfying NORI1. All numerical claims are finite exhaustive verified certificates; the product formula and its consequence are analytic theorems.
