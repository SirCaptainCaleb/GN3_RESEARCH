## Research note

For an antipodally odd binary edge coloring of Q_n let X count one-colored edges, S count adjacent color switches along a uniform full geodesic, and D(x) be signed incident-edge color imbalance. Write Delta=n/2-E(S)=E_x[D(x)^2]/(2n). Then Var(X)>=n/6+Delta/2 when n is odd; Var(X)>=n/6+n*Delta/(2*(n+1)) when n is even. Proof: combine the proved geodesic color-count Walsh variance with Parseval and the mean switch identity. With a_i(T) the Fourier coefficient of edge color i, b(T)=sum_{i notin T}a_i(T)=-Dhat(T)/2, and k_s=1/(s+2) for odd s, Var(X)-n/6=(1/4)sum_{odd T}k_s*Dhat(T)^2+sum_{odd |T|>=3}(1/3-k_s)sum_i a_i(T)^2. Both sums are nonnegative. For odd n, relevant s<=n-2 implies k_s>=1/n; for even n, s<=n-1 implies k_s>=1/(n+1). Apply Parseval. Independently verified by rational full-geodesic enumeration through Q6: nori_variance_switch_tradeoff_audit.py.
