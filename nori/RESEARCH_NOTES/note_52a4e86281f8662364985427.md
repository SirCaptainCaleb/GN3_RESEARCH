## Research note

Proposed path to NORI1 closure by strongly connected dependency reduction (14 Oct 2026). The proven paired-root seam lemma says a sink dimension block B can be removed whenever for every two outside assignments x,y the B-fiber favorable-root sets A(g_x) and antipodal(A(g_y)) intersect. A sufficient, but not necessary, condition is strict majority of favorable roots in every fiber. Proven families: arbitrary terminal components of size at most three; directed cycle components with arbitrary nonlinear outside dependence; fan-cycle graph (directed cyclic spine with arbitrary extra incoming arrows to one hub, predecessor derivatives allowed to deactivate in fibers); star graph (hub-to-leaf arrows with nonlinear leaf-to-hub feedback, derivatives allowed to deactivate in fibers), with a sharp favorable-root fraction at least three quarters; partial-matching/square-factorable fibers with varying matchings via a distinct cross-intersection theorem. All these results are in the mutable Article XV subsection cartesian_factorization_of_paired_roots_and_interaction_prime_reduction and companion cross-orientation subsection. Next SPECULATIVE structural claim: For any rooted tree on directions {0,...,m}, with root hub 0, if each nonhub edge color is a_i + lambda_i*x_parent(i) (lambda_i in F2) and the hub edge color is an arbitrary Boolean function of the other bits, then |A(f)|>2^m, potentially >=3*2^(m-1). Prove or refute by analyzing the possible seam positions of the hub in the two opposite tree orientations of the all-zero and all-one nonhub constraints; a bad root would force the hub Boolean function to take opposite constant values on two collections of tree order ideals/filters. The strict-majority conjecture is already proved at the two extreme tree shapes: directed path (fan-cycle result) and star (three-quarter theorem). A proof for arbitrary out-arborescence would remove all terminal SCCs with a single nonlinear feedback target and functional incoming dependencies, without tracking global geodesic families. Ultimate NORI1 would follow from establishing the needed cross-favorability for every possible terminal SCC fiber class. Do not treat the rooted-tree claim as established.
