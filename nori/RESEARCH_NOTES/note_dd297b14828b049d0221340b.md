## Research note

For a paired-flat legal coloring c on Q_(2m), let T_i be the red perfect-matching involution in direction pair i, and let T_pi denote any sweep using each pair involution once in order pi. Define W(c) as the number of triples (x,pi,rho) with T_pi(x)=T_rho(bar x), where x runs over all 2^(2m) roots and pi,rho over the m! orders. Every such collision gives a red full antipodal geodesic by reversing the second halfpath. These midpoint collisions form a strict, currently unproved all-dimensional sufficient forcing proposal within the already-established paired-flat class equivalent to unrestricted NORI1.

THEOREM 1 (paired-square Lipschitz bound). A paired-flat antipodal alternating-square-pair switch c->c' changes W by at most 16(m!)^2. Proof: it modifies only one pair involution T_i, at the eight vertices of the two switched squares. Every m-letter full pair permutation uses T_i once; its prefix and suffix maps are bijective, so at most eight starting roots change their endpoint in a prescribed order. There are m! orders. For a triple to change its collision status, at least one halfpath endpoint must change. Counting at most 8m! affected root/order pairs on either half and at most m! arbitrary orders on the other gives 16(m!)^2.

THEOREM 2 (strict critical scarcity). If c has a red full geodesic and c' has no monochromatic full geodesic, then W(c)<=16(1-1/m)(m!)^2. The archived temporal-word two-hit involution ensures that every red full geodesic in c hits precisely one switched red edge, at an internal step. Four undirected edges in the switched squares are red under c. For one such red edge and one fixed internal step, a midpoint collision triple is determined by an orientation of that edge (two choices), an order of the other m-1 pairs in the half containing the step ((m-1)! choices), and an order of the m pairs in the opposite half (m! choices); backward application of the pair involutions fixes the root. Thus the number through that edge is at most 2(2m-2)(m-1)!m!=4(1-1/m)(m!)^2. Summing over the four originally red switched edges yields the theorem. A hypothetical critical good neighbor therefore has midpoint collision probability at most 16(1-1/m)/2^(2m), an exponentially small fraction of all root and order triples.

THEOREM 3 (joint fiber-order matrix). Split each vertex into pair parities p_i=x_(2i) xor x_(2i+1) and fiber bits t_i=x_(2i). Let B_p(t,s) count pair permutations pi mapping (p,t) to (bar p,s). The B_p are sums of m! permutation matrices. With J the fiber complementation map t->t+allones, W(c)=sum_p tr(J B_p B_p^T). Each B_p has all row and column sums m!, whence tr(J B_p B_p^T)>=2(m!)^2-||B_p||_F^2. A positive right side on any fiber is sufficient for a red full geodesic; this is a nonuniversal spectral dispersion criterion that preserves the full same-coloring root and direction-order interaction.

FINITE TESTS. The paired-flat Q6 two-order-midpoint avoidance SAT encoding has 24 odd-selector variables and 6144 blocker clauses; system Z3 returns UNSAT, without an independently checked proof log. An adversarial paired-flat Q8 with four 64-bit selector tables 55a2aa5f05aaba55, 66ff66ff00990099, 60606069f9f9f9f, 6f6f6f6f09090909 has no red geodesic with repeated pair word pi pi from any root or order, but its independent pair-order halfpaths (2,0,1,3) from root zero and (3,2,0,1) from the antipode meet at vertex 165. The corresponding red original-direction word is (5,0,2,7,3,1,4,6). A sample paired-square switch changes W from 6192 to 6336, consistent with Theorem 1.
