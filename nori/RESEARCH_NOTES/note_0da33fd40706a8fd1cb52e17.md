## Research note

**Proposition (no bounded-window decoding of NORI1 through signed type-A orders).** Let \(n\ge4\), let \(\pi=(i_1,\ldots,i_n)\), and let \(w(x,\pi)=((-1)^{x_{i_1}}i_1,\ldots,(-1)^{x_{i_n}}i_n)\) encode a full antipodal geodesic. Fix a position \(j\) and a subset \(J\subseteq[n]\) of observed signed-letter positions such that \(j\in J\) and \(J\ne[n]\). There exists an antipodally odd coloring of the undirected edges of \(Q_n\) for which the color of the \(j\)-th edge of the geodesic cannot be determined from the signed letters \(w_\ell\), \(\ell\in J\), even with \(\pi\) and \(j\) fixed.

**Proof.** Choose \(k\notin J\), so \(k\ne j\). Define the color of a dimension-\(i_j\) edge with endpoint \(y\) by \(c_{i_j}(y)=y_{i_k}\). This is independent of \(y_{i_j}\), and \(c_{i_j}(\bar y)=1-c_{i_j}(y)\). For every remaining dimension \(h\), choose some \(h'\ne h\) and set \(c_h(y)=y_{h'}\), giving a complete legal NORI1 coloring. At step \(j\) of \(P(x,\pi)\), the color is \(x_{i_k}\oplus\mathbf1_{\{k<j\}}\). Roots \(x,x'\) differing only in bit \(i_k\) induce identical signed letters on \(J\), while the \(j\)-th edge colors differ. \(\square\)

**Consequence.** Every universal decoder for the edge at position \(j\), with the order held fixed, must retain all \(n-1\) root signs outside \(i_j\). In particular, assigning edge colors using a reversal-odd boundary-tournament label of a fixed number of consecutive signed symbols in \(H(x,\pi)=(w_1,\ldots,w_n,-w_n,\ldots,-w_1)\) cannot represent unrestricted NORI1 when \(n\) is larger than that window. The paired-symbol type-A embedding remains faithful as a path representation, but its color data requires global signed-root information or a nonlocal incidence mechanism.
