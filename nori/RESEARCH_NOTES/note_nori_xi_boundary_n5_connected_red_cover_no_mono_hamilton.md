## Research note

On n=5 original vertices, index the 30 reversal-orbits of ordered triples by increasing middle vertex and then increasing outer pair a<c, and give reversed triples complementary colors. Take red representative mask 0x3ed25058. The undirected red covering graph S(K5) on all 20 ordered-pair states is CONNECTED and contains 30 red covering edges, graphic rank 19 and nullity 11. Thus its red component partition has one part and its component flat is ALL covering edges. The zero-turn component-contact multigraph has one vertex and 88 loops (sum over ten original unordered pairs of red degrees a_x*a_reversed_x). Accordingly its pointed contraction has at least 88 parallel mates of the port. Nevertheless all 120 spanning original-vertex words have one or two internal color changes: 48 with one, 72 with two, and none with zero; so there is no monochromatic Hamilton tight path. This mask is the existing Article XI five-cycle sharpness certificate. The red forward directed transition digraph (a,b)->(b,c) has no directed cycles, although its undirected red graph is connected; its longest forward directed walk revisits original vertices. Hence covering connectivity, maximal flat F=E and abundant port parallelism do not force a forward resource-independent spanning path. The spanning one-turn conjecture remains satisfied on this example.
