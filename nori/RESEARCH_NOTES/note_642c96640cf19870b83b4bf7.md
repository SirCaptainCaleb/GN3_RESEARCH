## Research note

Define D_x^k as vertices reached from x by red walks with at most k steps decreasing Hamming distance from x. Let U_x be red-increasing closure and B_x the one-red-decreasing-edge operator. Then D_x^0=U_x({x}) and D_x^(k+1)=U_x(D_x^k union B_x(D_x^k)). For legal block products, D_(x,y)^k(c boxplus d)=union over a+b<=k of D_x^a(c) cross D_y^b(d). Proof: project any red product walk onto its factors, splitting descending steps; conversely concatenate factor walks. The least k such that bar x lies in D_x^k equals shortest red antipodal path excess. If no red full geodesic exists from x, red-geodesic reachable regions from x and bar x are disjoint; a red length n+2 path exists if and only if a red edge descends from the x-reachable region to the bar-x-reachable region. Such a path has precisely one decreasing step, producing the red bridge certificate.
