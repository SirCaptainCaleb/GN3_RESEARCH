## Research note

In Q_n with f_i constant or signed reader x_{p(i)}+eps_i, the paired roots are precisely those whose reader-cycle initial colors alpha_i are nonuniform on each cycle. Thus |P|=2^(n-sum ell_j) product(2^ell_j-2) >= 2^(n-tau), equality only for two-cycles. A uniform alpha cycle excludes one monotone orientation by comparing its first and last direction steps; nonuniform alpha permits monochromatic cycle paths for either color, and directed reader-tree insertions preserve each monotone phase. Exhaustive mixed Q2-Q4 cases confirm the formula.
