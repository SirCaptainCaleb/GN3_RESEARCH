## Research note

Let M(u,v,w)=uv+uw+vw over F2. On Q4 set c0=1+x2, c1=M(x0,x2,x3), c2=1+x0, c3=M(x0,x1,x2). These are legal antipodally odd rules, with no pair of complementary or identical direction functions. For every root (a,b,c,d), the full-geodesic color word of order (0,1,2,3) is (1+c,M(1+a,c,d),a,1+M(a,b,c)). If a differs from c, the first three colors equal (a,1+a,a), forcing two switches. If a=c, the first, third and fourth colors equal (1+a,a,1+a), also forcing two switches. Yet at root x=6, order (0,2,1,3) is fully red. For h independent copies on Q_(4h), every root with the concatenated reference order has at least 2h switches. Every cyclic rotation has at least 2(h-1) switches. If the order is modified by k arbitrary contiguous interval reversals, at least h-2k original four-letter blocks remain contiguous and either forward or reversed; reversal of a full geodesic preserves its switch count. Each intact block forces two switches, so every root has at least 2 max(0,h-2k) switches, and any one-switch order requires at least ceiling(h/2) block reversals. All these colorings nevertheless admit a red full geodesic by concatenating the factorwise witnesses. Universal rigidity lemma: in an arbitrary binary edge coloring, two consecutive directions i,j in a full order yield opposite colors for every root iff their direction rules are pointwise complementary c_i(z)=1-c_j(z); both rules then ignore bits i and j. Proof: root variation gives c_i(z)=1-c_j(z xor e_i); evaluate at z xor e_i and use edge consistency. Therefore in the majority gadget every single adjacent switch is separately removable by some root, although no root removes enough switches simultaneously. Results concern unrestricted forcing lemmas, not closure of NORI1.

ROOT-SPARSITY THEOREM AND PATH-COUNT FORMULA. Retain the nonlinear legal Q4 coloring c0=1+x2, c1=M(x0,x2,x3), c2=1+x0, c3=M(x0,x1,x2), with M(a,b,c)=ab+ac+bc over F2.

Define R(c) to be the set of vertices from which a red full antipodal geodesic begins. Then
R(c)={x in F2^4: x0+x2=1 and x1+x3=1}.
Every x in R(c) supports precisely three red rooted full direction orders, whereas every x outside R(c) supports zero. Hence |R(c)|=4 and N_R(c)=12. Reversing any full direction order at the SAME starting vertex complements every direction's edge color (by antipodal oddness and edge consistency). Therefore the blue full-geodesic root set equals R(c), and every eligible root supports three blue full direction orders as well.

PROOF. If directions 0 and 2 have red edges on a full geodesic, their relative order forces x0+x2=1. More precisely, when (x0,x2)=(1,0), direction 2 precedes direction 0, while when (x0,x2)=(0,1), direction 0 precedes direction 2. In either case, after the first of directions 0,2 and before the second, the currently held bits at positions 0 and 2 are both one. Therefore no red edge of direction 1 or 3 can be traversed strictly between those two directions, because M(1,1,s)=1 for either remaining bit s.

Outside that interval the current bits at positions 0,2 are different, and M(0,1,s)=s. Thus direction 1 has color equal to the current bit 3, and direction 3 has color equal to the current bit 1. For both edges to be red, the bits initially at positions 1,3 must differ. If direction 1 precedes direction 3, necessarily (x1,x3)=(1,0); if direction 3 precedes direction 1, necessarily (x1,x3)=(0,1). Hence x1+x3=1. Conversely, given both parity conditions, use the forced relative order of directions 0,2 and the forced relative order of directions 1,3, keeping the members of the 0,2 pair consecutive. Every edge is red. The ordered 0,2 pair can be positioned before both 1,3, between them, or after both, and all three choices are red. No other arrangement can be red by the preceding necessity argument. Hence precisely three rooted red full orders exist at each eligible root.

UNRESTRICTED CARTESIAN ROOT-FIBER PRODUCT LAW. Let c,d be arbitrary binary edge colorings on independent cubes and let R_u(c) denote roots admitting a full path with direction-indexed profile u. Then for every pair of requested profiles (u,v),
R_(u,v)(c square d)=R_u(c) x R_v(d).
Indeed, restriction of any product geodesic to a factor is a full factor geodesic with the corresponding profile, and any two such factor geodesics admit all interleavings as product geodesics. Thus root-set cardinalities multiply, whether or not the factors are legal odd colorings. In addition, if each successful factor root supports k_c(u),k_d(v) full orders, its successful product root supports binom(n+m,n) k_c(u)k_d(v) full orders.

For h copies of the nonlinear majority gadget on Q_(4h), the full red (and blue) root set is the affine codimension-2h set defined blockwise by x_(4r)+x_(4r+2)=1 and x_(4r+1)+x_(4r+3)=1 (0<=r<h). Therefore
|R(c^{square h})|=4^h=2^(n/2), and |R|/2^n=2^(-n/2).
Every successful root supports (4h)!/(8^h) red direction orders, and the total number of red ordered rooted full geodesics is (4h)!/(2^h), with equal blue count. No two direction-color functions in this Cartesian family are pointwise identical or complementary: the four local rules are nonconstant and pairwise distinct up to complement, and rules on disjoint factors depend nontrivially on disjoint input bit sets.

This is an unbounded-dimensional counterexample to any universal favorable-root density lower bound larger than 2^(-n/2), even if one restricts to successful legal odd colorings with no two identical or complementary direction rules. It does not contradict NORI1: both colors have many full witnesses, but the starting roots are exponentially sparse. The same nonlinear Q4 gadget had previously been used for switch barriers; the root-fiber classification, three-order certificate and product counting identity are new properties of that construction.

ALL-DIMENSION REFINEMENT. For every n>=4 there exists a legal odd coloring of Q_n with all direction rules nonconstant and pairwise distinct up to complement, such that precisely 2^ceil(n/2) roots support a red (equivalently blue) full geodesic. In particular the successful-root density is 2^(-floor(n/2)).

PROOF. Write n=4h+r with 0<=r<=3 and h>=1. Use h disjoint copies of the nonlinear majority Q4 gadget from the preceding theorem. If r=2 or r=3, add a separate two-direction reciprocal-reader block c_a=x_b, c_b=x_a. Its red full-geodesic roots are precisely the two roots satisfying x_a+x_b=1: the orders ab and ba give red edges just when the two root bits differ. If r=1 or r=3, add one further direction s, with edge color c_s=x_1, where direction 1 lies in the first majority block. The new edge rule ignores its own bit and is odd under antipodal complementation. It differs from each old edge rule and from every old rule's complement; all old rules on disjoint factors are nonconstant.

The new direction imposes no restriction on eligible roots: given any red full geodesic in the preceding blocks, insert s immediately before direction 1 when the initial bit x_1 is zero, or immediately after direction 1 when the initial bit x_1 is one. Then c_s is zero, and its traversal leaves the edge colors of the older directions unchanged. Conversely, deleting s from any red full geodesic gives a red full geodesic in the older product; hence no previously ineligible root is newly admitted. The bit x_s of the new root is arbitrary. For a reciprocal two-direction block, full-geodesic root sets multiply by the previously proved product law.

The resulting favorable-root count is
4^h times (1,2,2,4) for r=(0,1,2,3), respectively, which equals 2^ceil(n/2). There are 2^n roots, giving fraction 2^(-floor(n/2)). No two direction rules on distinct product blocks are identical or complementary, because they depend nontrivially on disjoint vertex-bit sets. In the majority block no two rules are identical or complementary, and the singleton reader x_1 differs from every majority or reciprocal-block rule. Reversing a full order at its original root complements all edge colors, so the blue-root set is the same as the red-root set. This proves the claim.
