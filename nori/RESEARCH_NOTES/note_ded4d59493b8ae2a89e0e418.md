## Research note

**Theorem (full profile support with linear Shannon-entropy deficit).** For every n>=3, consider the antipodally odd undirected-edge coloring of Q_n with direction indices 0,...,n-1 given by c_0(x)=x_1 and c_i(x)=x_0 for 1<=i<n. Every rule ignores its own bit and reverses under complementing all bits.

Select a uniform starting root X and a uniform permutation Pi of [n], independently, and let W be the direction-indexed color word of the corresponding full geodesic. For every w in F_2^n, with r=|w_{1,...,n-1}|,
\[
\Pr(W=w)=\frac1{2n\binom{n-1}{r}}.
\]
Consequently every binary profile occurs, but its Shannon entropy satisfies
\[
H(W)=1+\log_2n+\frac1n\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}\log_2\binom{n-1}{r}
=\frac{n}{2\ln2}+O(\log n).
\]
In particular n-H(W)=(1-1/(2\ln2))n+O(\log n), with coefficient about 0.27865.

**Proof.** The position of direction 0 among all n directions is uniform. Let S be the set of other directions preceding 0. Its size K is uniform on {0,...,n-1}, and conditional on K it is a uniform K-subset. For i>0, the edge color when direction i is traversed is X_0 if i precedes direction 0 and 1-X_0 otherwise. Since X_0 is an independent fair bit, the number R of ones among W_1,...,W_{n-1} is uniform on {0,...,n-1}, and conditional on R=r the word is uniform on the binom(n-1,r) binary words of that weight. Direction 0 is traversed with color X_1 plus the indicator that direction 1 precedes 0. Since X_1 is independent of X_0 and Pi, W_0 is independent fair, proving the displayed law. The entropy decomposition gives the sum. Uniform Stirling estimates show log_2 binom(N,r)=N h_2(r/N)+O(log N), and the Riemann sum tends to integral_0^1 h_2(t)dt=1/(2 ln 2), yielding the asymptotic.

**Research consequence.** The manuscript's sublinear logarithmic *support* deficit criterion is closure-equivalent by Cartesian tensorization. Replacing support entropy log_2|W| by the Shannon entropy of W sampled using uniform root and order produces a false conclusion even for legal colorings with full profile support. This example blocks a near-uniform Shannon-entropy proof route unless a different, adaptively chosen distribution on the attained profiles is used.

## Balanced strengthening: full support with linear random-geodesic entropy deficit

For every d>=2 define on Q_(2d), with input bits (x_0,...,x_(d-1),y_0,...,y_(d-1)),
\[
c_0(x,y)=x_1,\quad c_i(x,y)=x_0\ (1\le i<d),\quad
c_{d+i}(x,y)=1-c_i(x,y)\ (0\le i<d).
\]
Each paired direction (i,d+i) contributes one incident edge of each color, so this coloring is balanced. Every rule disregards its own bit and is antipodally odd.

**Theorem.** Every binary direction-indexed profile on Q_(2d) is attainable, yet for uniformly sampled root and direction permutation the Shannon entropy of the profile W satisfies
\[
\boxed{\lim_{d\to\infty}H(W)/(2d)=1/(2\ln2)\approx0.72134752.}
\]
Thus even among balanced legal colorings with maximal profile support, uniform-path sampling may have linear entropy deficit, with asymptotic deficit ratio \(1-1/(2\ln2)\approx0.27865248\).

*Full-support proof.* Set initial x_0=0. For i in {1,...,d-1}, put old direction i before 0 when its target bit w_i=0, and after 0 when w_i=1. Put dummy direction d+i before 0 when w_(d+i)=1, and after 0 when w_(d+i)=0. All 2d-2 targeted bits outside {0,d} are realized, because only direction 0 changes x_0. Choose the initial x_1 to realize the old direction-0 color w_0, whose time-dependent value depends on whether direction 1 was placed before 0. Insert dummy direction d before or after direction 1 to realize either prescribed value w_d, leaving the previously fixed order relations and colors unchanged. All 2d directions are traversed once.

*Entropy proof.* Let R={1,...,d-1,d+1,...,2d-1}, with q=2d-2. Strip from the observed R-bits the fixed additive offsets of 1 in dummy directions. Each resulting bit is initial x_0 toggled if direction 0 preceded its step. Relative to the uniformly random order on R union {0}, the number of R steps before 0 is uniform on {0,...,q}, and their index set conditional on that number is uniform. Because initial x_0 is an independent fair bit, for every R-word v of weight r,
\[
\Pr(V=v)=\frac1{(q+1)\binom qr}.
\]
The weight of V is uniform on {0,...,q}. Consequently
\[
H(V)=\log_2(q+1)+\frac1{q+1}\sum_{r=0}^q\log_2\binom qr
=\frac{q}{2\ln2}+O(\log q).
\]
The two omitted color bits {0,d} contribute between zero and two further bits conditional on V. Therefore H(W)=H(V)+O(1), proving the limit. The asymptotic uses the uniform Stirling approximation to binomial coefficients and integral_0^1 h_2(t)dt=1/(2 ln 2). Direct independent enumerations for d=2 and d=3 confirmed all 16 and 64 profiles, respectively, and the R-marginal law.

This strengthens the preceding star example to the very balanced class sufficient for unrestricted NORI1. It rules out a proof based solely on near-maximal Shannon entropy under uniform roots and permutations, even when every full profile is realizable.


## Tensor-product strengthening: local pseudorandomness with linear profile-entropy deficit

For integers d>=2 and h>=1, let B_d be the balanced 2d-direction coloring of the preceding theorem, and set C_(d,h)=B_d^{boxtimes h} on Q_N, N=2dh. This is balanced and antipodally odd and has full direction-profile support. Let W denote its direction-profile word when both root and direction order are sampled uniformly.

**Theorem (local marginal independence does not enforce near-maximal profile Shannon entropy).** We have
\[
H(W)=hH(W(B_d)),\qquad
H(W)/N=1/(2\ln2)+O((\log d)/d).
\tag{EP1}
\]
For k uniformly sampled distinct direction indices, the joint distribution of their k incident colors at a uniform vertex, and separately the joint distribution of their k sampled full-geodesic profile bits, each have total-variation distance at most
\[
\boxed{\binom{k}{2}\frac{2d-1}{2dh-1}\le\frac{\binom{k}{2}}h}
\tag{EP2}
\]
from k independent fair bits. Moreover at least a fraction \(1-(2d-1)/(2dh-1)\) of direction pairs have vanishing two-direction square derivatives throughout the cube.

*Proof.* Each 2d-direction factor is balanced and odd, and its full-profile support is the binary 2d-cube. Independent Cartesian products preserve balance and oddness. Their full geodesic profiles concatenate independent factor profiles; the product-profile theorem (PW4) gives full support and additivity of entropy under uniform root and order. The previous balanced-entropy formula gives (EP1).

Partition the N direction indices into h blocks of size 2d. For k distinct uniform sampled directions, the probability of a collision in block membership is at most the union bound \(\binom{k}{2}(2d-1)/(N-1)\). Conditional on no collision, each selected direction depends only on an independent vertex-and-permutation block. Antipodal oddness makes each incident-color bit fair; antipodal translation of the root also makes each full-geodesic profile bit fair. Their joint laws are therefore products of independent fair bits conditional on the selected block indices. Coupling to this event proves (EP2). For directions from different factors, neither edge rule reads the other factor's vertex bits, so both square derivatives vanish. This gives the flat-pair fraction. QED.

Taking d=h=r -> infinity gives balanced legal colorings on Q_(2r²) with full profile support, asymptotically independent fair colors on every fixed number of randomly sampled directions, and asymptotically vanishing square curvature frequency, while their uniformly sampled full-geodesic profiles satisfy
\[
\lim_{r\to\infty}H(W)/(2r^2)=1/(2\ln2)<1.
\]
Thus even simultaneous local distributional regularity and full profile realizability do not yield a near-maximal Shannon-entropy estimate for uniform full-geodesic sampling. This is a rigorous negative scope theorem for entropy-based routes to the support-deficit closure criterion, rather than a NORI1 counterexample.


## Balanced full-support coloring with a linear profile/order rigidity barrier

**Theorem (all dimensions).** In the balanced antipodally odd \(2d\)-cube coloring \(B_d\) defined above, let
\[
S=\{1,\ldots,d-1,d+1,\ldots,2d-1\},\qquad |S|=2d-2.
\]
Given the direction-indexed profile \(w\) of a full geodesic, set \(q_i=w_i\) for \(1\le i<d\) and \(q_{d+i}=1-w_{d+i}\) for \(1\le i<d\), and put \(r=\sum_{i\in S}q_i\). Every path realizing \(w\) traverses direction \(0\) at a one-based position from
\[
\boxed{\{r+1,r+2,2d-1-r,2d-r\}.}\tag{OL1}
\]
Consequently, whenever \(r=d-1\), the designated direction \(0\) necessarily occurs at position \(d\) or \(d+1\), the two central positions. All such target profiles are realized, since the preceding theorem establishes full profile support.

**Quantitative rigidity.** For any target \(w\) with \(r=d-1\), each full geodesic \(P'\) that traverses direction \(0\) first or last obeys
\[
\boxed{d_H(w(P'),w)\ge d-1.}\tag{OL2}
\]
Thus even perfect vertex balance and full profile support cannot guarantee a profile-preserving root/square exchange that places a chosen direction at the seam. Moving direction \(0\) to the beginning or end requires changing a linear number of direction-color entries, with ambient dimension \(N=2d\).

**Proof.** In \(B_d\), among all dimensions only \(0\) flips the bit \(x_0\), while every adjusted color \(q_i\), \(i\in S\), equals the value of \(x_0\) immediately before that dimension is traversed. With starting bit \(x_0=a\), all members of \(S\) before \(0\) have \(q_i=a\), and those after \(0\) have \(q_i=1-a\). Hence the number of \(S\)-steps before \(0\) is \(r\) if \(a=1\), and \(2d-2-r\) if \(a=0\). The remaining dimension \(d\) can lie before or after \(0\), furnishing (OL1). When \(r=d-1\), precisely \(d-1\) members of \(S\) precede \(0\), so its position is \(d\) or \(d+1\).

If direction \(0\) is first or last, all adjusted colors on \(S\) are constant. Such a vector differs on at least \(d-1\) entries from a target with \(d-1\) ones and \(d-1\) zeros on \(S\); fixed dummy-bit complements preserve this Hamming distance. This proves (OL2). \(\square\)

**Independent finite audit:** Complete enumeration for \(d=2\) and \(d=3\) confirmed all \(2^{2d}\) profiles and the central-position restriction for every realization of each balanced-adjusted profile.

**Scope:** The construction is a dimension-uniform negative theorem for order-controlled profile-preserving exchange methods. These colorings themselves satisfy NORI1.
