## Research note

The involution must be an automorphism tau of the vertex set; its action on an edge uv is forced to be tau(u)tau(v). Parity reversal chi(tau(e))=1-chi(e) implies no edge is fixed setwise. A free vertex action makes the quotient a two-sheeted graph cover. At a fixed vertex, incident edges are paired with opposite parity, so the counts of both parity labels agree. A stand-alone involution of the edge set is too weak. The boundary ordered-pair involution tau(a,b)=(b,a) is on vertices; for third-vertex coloring lambda((a,b)(b,c))=c its image has color a, so ordinary color invariance must remain optional. Recent parity-cover and mixed-matroid obstruction examples explicitly define vertex maps and meet this requirement.
