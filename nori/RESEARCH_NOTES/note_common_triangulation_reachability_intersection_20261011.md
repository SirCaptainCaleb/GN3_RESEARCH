## Research note

Let T be a fixed cube-vertex triangulation of Q_n (e.g. staircase), R(a) the endpoints of monochromatic geodesics rooted at a, and K(a)=T[R(a)]. Then |K(a)| intersects |K(bar a)| if and only if R(a) intersects R(bar a): subcomplexes of one geometric simplicial complex intersect in the realization of their common subcomplex and hence share a vertex. Two monochromatic geodesics from antipodal roots to a common vertex use complementary coordinate sets and concatenate to a 1-splice antipodal geodesic. NORI1 rotation then produces a monochromatic antipodal geodesic. The triangulation imposes no requirement that simplices share a single path witness. The missing step is to force an intersection for some root; antipodal symmetry alone does not. No common intrinsic axioms for ordinary boundary tournaments and their vertex-simple tight-path witnesses were obtained.
