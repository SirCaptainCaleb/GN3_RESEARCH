## Research note

For arbitrary antipodally odd c on Q_n (n>=3), a universal relation W_sigma(x+h)=W_pi(x)+v with fixed h,v holding for all c,x exists if and only if sigma belongs to the dihedral orbit of pi. Let p_i,q_i be direction-prefix masks. Equality for every coloring requires h+p_i+q_i in {0,e_i,1,1+e_i} for each i. The symmetric indicators J_ij of reversal of the i,j relative order then obey J_ij=h_i+h_j+t for a constant t. If t=0, the two h-classes must each be contiguous in pi and sigma swaps them. If t=1, each class must be contiguous and sigma reverses each class. Thus sigma is cyclic or reverse-cyclic; the converse is the archived root-slide symmetry. There are precisely two mask pairs, related by antipodal complement. For a cyclic shift moving prefix A to the end, W_rot(x+A)=W_pi(x)+A. Combined with the archived covariance of fixed-order random-coloring profile multiplicities, red witness counts X_k for the n cyclic rotations satisfy E X_k=1, Var X_k=1-2/2^n, Cov(X_k,X_l)=-2/2^n for k!=l. Hence the sum Y has E Y=n, Var Y=n-2n^2/2^n and Pr(Y>0)>=n/(n+1-2n/2^n). The claim is probabilistic, not unrestricted NORI1. Exhaustive Q4: 528 of 65536 colorings have Y=0. Verifier: nori_dihedral_profile_transport.py; full proof manuscript: nori_dihedral_order_transport_research.md. ADDITIONAL THEOREM (sharp two-column gap). Let D(pi,sigma)=min_h #{i:h+p_i+q_i not in span(e_i,1)}. For n>=4, D=0 precisely for dihedrally related orders; otherwise D>=2, and an adjacent transposition attains 2. Proof: if all but k columns align for a shift h, on aligned directions i write h+p_i+q_i=a_i e_i+b_i 1. The symmetric order-disagreement indicators give b_i+h_i=t across all aligned i. For the remaining k, every outside bit j of h+p_k+q_k equals h_j+J_kj=h_k+t, so the last column also aligns. Therefore a single broken column is impossible.
