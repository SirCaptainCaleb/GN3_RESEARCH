## Research note

For a fixed root a and color i in a binary edge-colored Q_n, identify each reachable vertex with the subset S of coordinates in which it differs from a. The family F_i(a) of subsets admitting an all-i geodesic from a is accessible: every nonempty S in F_i(a) has at least one j in S with S\\{j} in F_i(a), by deleting the final step of a witnessing geodesic. It need not be downward closed, even for legal antipodally odd underlying edge colors. On Q_2 assign color 0 to edges 00--10 and 10--11 and color 1 to 00--01 and 01--11. Opposite edges have opposite colors, so the coloring is antipodally odd. At a=00, F_0(a)={empty,{1},{1,2}}, whereas {2} is absent. Consequently, the naive complex with faces given by monochromatically reachable supports is not necessarily simplicial. Any Tucker/Sperner carrier using these supports must introduce a legitimate simplicial replacement that preserves common-witness path reconstruction; downward closure cannot be assumed. This is a method-level caution, not a counterexample to NORI1 or to a prospective unified conjecture.
