## Research note

Literature integration, scope: Hehui Wu and Ningyuan Yang, A Chain-Level Borsuk-Ulam Obstruction Proof of Norine's Antipodal-Coloring Conjecture, arXiv:2607.19276v1, 21 July 2026, prove existence of a monochromatic antipodal path in every legal odd cube-edge coloring, but explicitly leave the geodesic strengthening open. They use vertex labels recording zero-connected components of x and its antipode; rook labels are ruled out by equivariant chain obstruction. Consequently the minimal monochromatic antipodal walk length L0(c) is finite and equals n+2e(c) with nonnegative integer excess e(c). NORI1 is equivalent to asserting e(c)=0 always. The already proved balanced walk-frontier lift preserves this finite excess. The rook reduction does not directly adapt to the geodesic-only hypothesis: monochromatic geodesic reachability lacks transitivity. Example legal odd Q3: c0=x2, c1=1+x0, c2=1+x1 mod 2. Zero geodesics join 000 to 110 through 100, and 110 to 011 through 010, but no zero geodesic joins 000 to 011, because both first steps on its shortest paths have color one. This is an explicit obstruction to a geodesic component-label argument.
