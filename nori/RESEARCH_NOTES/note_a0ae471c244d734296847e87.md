## Research note

Let c be an odd undirected Q_n edge coloring, n>=2. For k>=max(2,n-1), on Q_(2n+2k) with bits (x,y,a,b), set C_i=c_i(x), C_(y_i)=1-c_i(x), C_(a_j)=a_(j+1)+x_1+x_(r(j)), C_(b_j)=1+C_(a_j) mod2, where cyclic j and r(j)=2+((j-1) mod(n-1)). This is antipodally odd and pointwise degree-balanced; projecting any monochromatic full geodesic to the first n directions gives one in c. The undirected influence graph is connected: gadget a_j form a cycle, each b_j attaches to it, every x_i occurs in some gadget rule, and each y_i reads a nonconstant odd c_i. Conditional on x, the gadget source bits a_(j+1) are independent and uniform; hence 2^t binom(k,t)/binom(2n+2k,t) of all t-subsets of directions have independent fair incident colors. At most binom(2n,2)+6k direction pairs have nonzero square curvature: at most 4k gadget-old interactions, 2k adjacent-gadget interactions, and all old-old pairs. Thus this exceptional fraction approaches zero as k grows. If a bad coloring exists, it has connected perfectly balanced bad lifts with arbitrary fixed-order local color independence and locally flat squares on all but a vanishing proportion of direction sets. Hence proving NORI1 in this class implies NORI1 unrestricted.
