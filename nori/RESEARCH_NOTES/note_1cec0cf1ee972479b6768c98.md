## Research note

Under antipodally even lower-facet coloring, favorable-root weights agree at antipodal roots. Hence the bridge sum for monochromatic full paths is unchanged by every legal bridge flip. For a stationary parity coloring in even dimension, recoloring any number of edge pairs in one direction preserves its full zero path count n factorial divided by two.
