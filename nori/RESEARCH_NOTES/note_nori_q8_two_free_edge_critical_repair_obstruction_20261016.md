## Research note

**Q8 finite certificate: two-free-orbit repair fails around a minimal component.** Use directions 0,...,7 and integer bit masks. Let P_j=2^j-1, j=0,...,8. Take the minimum red component with vertex set S={P_j:0<=j<=8} union {2^k:1<=k<=5} union {255 xor 2^6}. Prescribe the backbone, all six endpoint pendant edges, and both optional internal end-square chords red. Prescribe every remaining edge touching S blue. Complementary edges are colored oppositely. The prescriptions are compatible and form a minimum-order component of 15 vertices.

Consider U_3={7,248}. Let F be the unprescribed antipodal edge orbits, outside all edges touching S or bar S. Enumerate all full red-geodesic candidates avoiding U_3 that have no prescribed-blue edges and use at most two edges from F. There are 352 distinct two-variable red-color requirements involving 66 unknown orbit variables. Every one of these candidate paths can be blocked simultaneously by a legal full coloring.

For an edge in direction i, represent it by its endpoint with bit i cleared together with direction i. Normalize its antipodal edge orbit to the lexicographically smaller of the two representatives, with the color sign toggled on antipodal exchange. Sort the 66 previously unprescribed orbit keys appearing in these 352 candidates. Give them the 66-bit integer color assignment
\[
\boxed{\texttt{0x3c031800000000000},}
\]
where the least significant bit is assigned to the first key. Give all other unprescribed orbit representatives color zero. Check the 352 candidate paths: every one has at least one blue edge. Therefore no red full geodesic avoiding U_3 uses at most two formerly unprescribed orbit incidences in this legal coloring. Nonetheless a red full geodesic does avoid U_3: it starts at integer vertex 5 and follows directions (3,0,1,4,5,6,7,2), using six formerly unprescribed orbit incidences.

Thus the proposed dimension-uniform claim that a red geodesic in a minimum-order component admits a monochromatic avoiding replacement using at most two formerly free orbit edges is false. This is a restriction on a repair method, and is consistent with strong antipodal-pair avoidance and NORI1.

**Reproducibility.** The complete finite verification is implemented in nori_two_free_sat.py and nori_twofree_q8_countermodel.py. The scripts enumerate all red-possible rooted full paths with at most two formerly free edges and forbidden pair U_3; form the resulting 352 two-variable exclusion clauses; solve the implication graph; verify the displayed canonical orbit assignment against every exclusion; and construct the indicated full red avoiding path. Related finite checks find the two-free-edge systems inconsistent in dimensions 5,6,7, for all prescribed minimum-component leaf/chord choices. In dimension 8, the model above is an explicit obstruction to that bounded repair strategy.

**Sharp four-free-edge repair threshold (finite theorem).** For the same Q8 minimum red component, with both chords red and forbidden interior pair U_3={7,248}, every legal antipodally odd completion contains a red full geodesic avoiding U_3 with at most FOUR edges from the previously unrestricted edge orbits. The earlier 66-bit assignment in this note shows that two do not always suffice. Any red geodesic avoiding U_3 must use an even number of those edges: it lies outside the red component S, has both endpoints in W=Q8\(S union bar S), and every red edge not inside W joins W to bar S (all edges within bar S are blue). Therefore the possible costs are 0,2,4,... and the lower and upper bounds give the worst-case threshold FOUR.

**Structural parity lemma (proved in all dimensions).** Let S be a red connected component of an antipodally odd coloring such that all cube edges induced on S are red, S∩bar S consists of one antipodal pair U_0, and no cube edge joins S\U_0 to bar S\U_0. Set W=Q_n\(S union bar S). Let P be a red full antipodal geodesic lying outside S. Both endpoints of P belong to W. Every red edge of P either joins W to bar S or has both endpoints in W; no red edge lies within bar S. Consequently the number f(P) of W-W edges satisfies f(P) ≡ n (mod 2). This holds without restrictions on the coloring of W-W edges. For a minimum-order red component of the preceding type with both square-closing chords red, any red full geodesic avoiding one of the interior backbone pairs U_j, 2≤j≤n-2, lies outside S because every red full geodesic inside S visits U_j. Hence the parity law applies to all such avoiding geodesics. The Q8 sharp threshold is an instance of this lemma.

**Scope.** This is a finite theorem about a fixed Q8 minimum-component configuration and its arbitrary full-coloring completions. The all-dimensional parity lemma explains the repair-cost parity, but the 35-path Boolean cover does not yet yield a uniform cover in higher even dimensions.

**Irredundancy of the 35-path forcing family (finite theorem).** Each of the 35 paths listed above is indispensable *within that chosen family*: for each candidate indexed i=0,...,34 there exists an assignment to the 21 free antipodal edge-orbit bits for which candidate i is red while the remaining 34 candidates all contain a blue edge. Therefore removing any one selected candidate destroys the covering property of this 35-path collection. This says nothing about the possible existence of a different, smaller path collection.

**Consequence for research strategy.** This cover's irredundancy and the absence of a dimension-uniform construction from the 35-path search argue against repeatedly expanding its finite Boolean constraints as a strategy for unrestricted NORI1. The dimensional obstruction and parity law remain useful; further work should seek a distinct fixed-coloring mechanism.

**Q10 central-pair obstruction to two-exterior-edge repairs (finite certificate).** Let n=10, P_j={0,...,j-1} and M=1023. Take the minimum-size red component S consisting of the prefix geodesic P_0,...,P_10 and the pendant leaves {k} for odd k=1,3,5,7 and D\{k} for even k=2,4,6,8. Color every edge in the induced graph Q_10[S] red, including both optional square-closing chords, and every boundary edge leaving S blue. These prescriptions extend to legal antipodally odd colorings. Let U_5={31,992}. There is such a completion with **no red full geodesic avoiding U_5 and using at most two edges whose antipodal edge orbits are outside the prescribed component/boundary colors**.

**Certificate.** Enumerating all geodesics avoiding U_5 and with at most two unspecified edge-orbit incidences produces 425 distinct clauses, each of length two, on 52 free antipodal edge-orbit bits. A clause records the two free orbit values required for the full geodesic to be red; forbid each clause by requiring at least one mismatch. The resulting 2-SAT instance is satisfiable. The constructive program nori_q10_center_two_free_countermodel.py obtains a satisfying 52-bit assignment via the implication graph, checks all 425 clauses against it, supplies arbitrary legal colors to the remaining orbits, and independently verifies that the red connected component containing the root is S of size 19. All 425 candidate constraints are falsified. This is a complete finite witness, separate from the red-geodesic existence question.

**Implication.** The Q8 four-path branching mechanism under alternating leaves repairs the near-end pair U_3, but the same alternating leaf geometry in Q10 admits a coloring defeating all candidate repairs for its central pair U_5 that use at most two unspecified edges. The number of exterior edge-orbit incidences needed by a successful repair can depend on the location of the critical pair relative to the backbone ends. This is a method obstruction, not a NORI1 counterexample: geodesics using four or more free edges remain possible.

**Avoiding geodesic in the Q10 two-free-edge countermodel.** The 52-bit 2-SAT completion produced by nori_q10_center_two_free_countermodel.py admits a red full antipodal geodesic avoiding U_5, rooted at 5, with direction order (0,1,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,2) and vertex sequence (5,4,6,14,30,62,126,254,510,1022,1018). All ten edges are red, none of these vertices is in U_5={31,992}, and six edges use orbits outside the initial component prescriptions. The verifier checks all these claims. The even-dimensional parity law with both end-square chords red makes exterior W--W usage even; the previous 425 two-term clauses exclude zero or two such edges. Thus the least number of exterior free edges on a red U_5-avoiding geodesic in this example is four or six. This confirms the obstruction is specific to low-exterior-edge repair and preserves a valid NORI1 witness.

**Sharp Q10 exterior repair threshold.** In the same 52-bit completion, there is a red full U_5-avoiding geodesic with four exterior W--W edges: root 5, direction order (0,4,2,3,5,6,7,8,9,1), vertices (5,4,20,16,24,56,120,248,504,1016,1018). The executable verifier checks redness of all ten edges, endpoint antipodality, avoidance of U_5={31,992}, and that precisely four traversed edges have orbits not fixed by the component. Its 425-clause 2-SAT certificate excludes zero or two such edges, and the component parity law excludes one or three. Therefore the minimum number of exterior W--W edges on any red geodesic avoiding U_5 in this completion is **four**. This establishes a Q10 sharpness phenomenon even with alternating pendant assignments, contrasting with the four-path two-free repair for near-end U_3 in the same component geometry.
