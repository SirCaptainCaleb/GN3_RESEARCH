## Research note

**Theorem (interface-color gluing law).** Let \(P,Q\) be partial paths of complementary dimension or vertex supports, with compatible joining interfaces and at least one colored local incidence each. Put \(r=\operatorname{sw}(P)\), \(s=\operatorname{sw}(Q)\), and let \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F_2\) be their final color bits. Let \(\varepsilon\) be the bit by which the reversal operation applied to \(Q\) changes its local colors. Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{sw}(\operatorname{glue}(P,Q))
=r+s+(\alpha\oplus\beta\oplus\varepsilon),}
\]
where the last bit is interpreted as the integer \(0\) or \(1\).

*Proof for NORI1 (\(\varepsilon=0\)).* Let \(P:x\to y\) and \(Q:\bar x\to y\) be cube geodesics. Their dimension supports are complementary: the dimensions where \(y\) differs from \(x\), and where it agrees. Reversing \(Q\) preserves its edge colors. The concatenation \(P\cdot Q^{\rm rev}\) traverses every cube dimension once, and its first color after the join is \(\beta\). Its switch count is \(r+s+\alpha\oplus\beta\), with the XOR interpreted as one integer bit.

*Proof for boundary tournaments (\(\varepsilon=1\)).* Let \(P\) end in the ordered pair \((h,c)\), let \(Q\) end in \((c,h)\), and suppose their vertex supports intersect only in \(\{h,c\}\) and exhaust \(V\). Reversing \(Q\) reverses and complements its triple-color word, so the first terminal-part color after the shared pair is \(1-\beta\). The concatenated Hamilton path has switch count \(r+s+\alpha\oplus\beta\oplus1\). Vertex-simplicity follows from the assumed complementary supports. \(\square\)

If either path has no colored incidences, omit the interface-comparison term.

**Corollary (filtration-one corrections can be absorbed).** A complementary path pair with switch budgets \((1,0)\) yields a one-switch full path whenever \(\alpha\oplus\beta\oplus\varepsilon=0\). The criterion requires matching incoming colors in NORI1 and differing incoming colors in boundary tournaments. Thus allowing a local one-switch correction need not introduce a second switch at the final join.

**Chain-level implication.** The bare switch filtration of rooted prefix complexes does not remember the last color, so it cannot distinguish whether a gluing incurs another change. An interface-sensitive Wu–Yang chain repair needs a terminal-color bit and a formal empty-path state. These are intrinsic to the original coloring, not new structural hypotheses. Families selected by terminal color need not be closed under simplicial faces; the appropriate carrier is a path-flag or semi-simplicial resolution retaining the endpoint color and used dimensions. This leaves the higher equivariant chain cancellation unproved.
