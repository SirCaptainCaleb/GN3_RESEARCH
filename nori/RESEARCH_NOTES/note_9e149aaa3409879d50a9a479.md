## Research note

Partition six vertices into A of size four and B of size two. Color (a,h,c) by zero when h belongs to A and at least one outer vertex belongs to B; color it one otherwise. The fifteen possible A/B patterns for a Hamilton ordering, grouped up to reversal, give four-bit words 1101, 1010, 0100, 1010, 0110, 1011, 0101, 1001, 0110. Every such word has at least two switches. The coloring is symmetric under exchanging a and c, showing that removing local reversal oddness invalidates the one-switch assertion.
