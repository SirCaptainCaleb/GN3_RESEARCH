## Research note

For the rank-three Hamming-layer coloring in Article XIV, let U be the union of all monochromatic Freudenthal prefix complexes. Every boundary square is contained in U and has zero curvature, yet U misses interior points near 000 and 111.

**Strengthening: two independent relative third-homology classes.** Let H be the central hexagon t1+t2+t3=3/2 inside [0,1]^3. For any t in H choose distinct indices j,k with t_j>=1/2 and t_k<=1/2 and let i be the remaining index. The monochromatic three-simplex with successive path vertices e_i, e_i+e_j, e_j, e_j+e_k has barycentric weights
(1-t_j, 1/2-t_k, t_j-1/2, t_k), all nonnegative and summing to one; their weighted sum is t. Thus H is contained in U.

Since both H and the full cubical boundary lie in U, the complement S^3\U has at least three connected components: outside the cube, the lower polar cap, and the upper polar cap. Alexander duality gives H_2(U;Z) of rank at least two. Contractibility of the cube then yields rank H_3([0,1]^3,U;Z)>=2. This is a pair of independent rank-three filling obstructions despite complete rank-two fillings.
