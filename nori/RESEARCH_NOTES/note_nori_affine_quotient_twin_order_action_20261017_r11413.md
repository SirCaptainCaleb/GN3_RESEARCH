# Order-compatible quotient-twin transpositions for legal affine NORI1

Let A in F2^(n x n) satisfy A_ii=0 and A*1=1. Let b_i(pi)=sum_(j preceding i in pi)A_ij for a full order pi, and put delta_ij=A_ij e_i+A_ji e_j. Write Q=F2^n/im A and [v] for a quotient class. The full geodesic profile at root x for affine coloring c_i(x)=a_i+(Ax)_i is a+Ax+b(pi).

**Lemma (arbitrary label-swap identity).** If tau_pq exchanges the positions occupied by direction labels p,q in an order pi, then
b(tau_pq pi)+b(pi)=delta_pq+sum_(w strictly between p,q in pi)(delta_pw+delta_qw).
Proof: pair p,q reverses order, and for every intermediate w both pairs p,w and q,w reverse order; all other pairwise order relations remain unchanged. Each pairwise reversal contributes its displayed delta-vector.

Call p,q QUOTIENT TWINS when delta_pw+delta_qw lies in im A for every w other than p,q. By the identity this holds iff the quotient change caused by swapping labels p,q is independent of the entire order. For a quotient-twin pair the change is always [delta_pq]. Necessity follows by comparing orders where p,q are consecutive with orders where p,w,q are consecutive, all other labels outside that interval.

**Theorem (order-compatible affine profile forcing).** Let T consist of any collection of quotient-twin pairs and let L be the linear span in Q of [delta_pq], pq in T. Then the random variable [b(pi)] under uniform pi in S_n has equal probabilities on all members of each L-coset. If L=Q, each quotient syndrome occurs on n!/|Q| full orders, all complete profiles occur somewhere in the legal affine coloring for every a, and the root-profile incidence graph admits a perfect matching.

*Proof.* The group H generated by the label transpositions tau_pq, pq in T, acts freely on full orders by relabeling. Each generator translates the quotient by its fixed vector [delta_pq], so composition induces a well-defined homomorphism chi:H->Q. It is well-defined because any identity word on permutations induces zero profile difference. Its image is L. In each orbit of the free H-action, the fiber over each member of its L-coset has the common size |ker chi|, giving the claimed distribution. When L=Q all quotient words occur equally often. Given any desired complete profile w, choose pi with [b(pi)]=[w-a], then choose a root x satisfying Ax=w-a-b(pi), realizing w. The established affine quotient-transversal matching construction matches roots to profiles bijectively.

*Fourier interpretation.* For lambda in ker A^T, the auxiliary inversion graph has edge indicator h_ij=lambda_i A_ij+lambda_j A_ji. Quotient twins p,q have identical adjacency to every third vertex in every such auxiliary graph. The character of the profile changes by h_pq under exchange of p,q; when h_pq=1 the transposition gives a fixed-point-free sign-reversing involution. The theorem constructs these compatible exchanges simultaneously in the quotient, rather than merely establishing their linear span.

**Theorem (connected examples with unbounded cokernel).** For every r>=1, n=4r, partition directions into r blocks of size four. Choose upper block-bidiagonal matrix A with diagonal blocks D having row masks [14,4,8,2] (least significant local direction zero), superdiagonal blocks J4 the four-by-four all-ones matrix, and all other blocks zero. Then A is legal, its undirected interaction graph is connected, rank(A)=3r, and the cokernel has dimension r. For each k, swapping labels 4k and 4k+1 is quotient-twin and changes only the k-th quotient syndrome bit. These r label exchanges commute, giving a free action of (Z/2)^r on the full direction orders. Therefore every one of the 2^r quotient syndromes occurs on (4r)!/2^r orders, and every affine coloring built from A has a full monochromatic antipodal geodesic and a perfect root-profile incidence matching.

*Proof.* Each row of D has odd weight. Adding a superdiagonal J4 block changes each corresponding row sum by four, preserving parity, and the diagonal of A vanishes. J4 couples successive blocks, making the undirected interaction graph connected. Both D and J4 have even column sums, so every vector in im A has even bit parity within each four-direction block. Hence rank(A)<=3r. Restrict each block's input to its last three bit positions: D maps those three bits bijectively onto the even-parity output subspace; the resulting block upper-triangular restriction has rank3r. Thus rank(A)=3r, and ker A^T is spanned by the indicators of the r blocks.

For any quotient character lambda constant on each four-direction block, the auxiliary graph contains K4 on each block whose lambda-value is one: each internal unordered pair has one directed dependency. Its cross-block adjacency is constant across all four vertices of each block, since external blocks of A are J4 or zero. Thus any two labels within one block are quotient twins. For p=4k,q=4k+1, delta_pq=e_p, which pairs to one with the k-th block indicator and zero with the others. These increments generate Q. The forcing theorem gives uniform syndrome coverage; the disjoint transpositions commute and exhibit the quotient action.

**Independent audits and limitations.** A subset-and-syndrome dynamic program verified uniform multiplicities for r=1,2,3,4: n=4 with 12 orders per syndrome; n=8 with 10080; n=12 with 59875200; n=16 with 1307674368000. An independent direct-order audit checked every order for n=4,8 and 100000 random orders for n=12, confirming that each selected label swap toggles only its block quotient bit.

The criterion is sufficient but far from universal. In exhaustive legal affine matrix censuses it certifies 20/140 corank-one matrices in n=4 and 3280/18960 corank-one matrices in n=5, while none of the matrices of corank at least two in these dimensions pass. These failures do NOT imply counterexamples to affine NORI1. The theorem gives a constructive order-compatible forcing mechanism of unbounded corank and connected interaction geometry, but does not resolve the unrestricted NORI1 conjecture.

**Theorem (classification of order-independent relabelings).** Give the complete graph on the n direction labels the F2-linear quotient edge labels
q_ij=[delta_ij] in Q=F2^n/im A. Let Aut(q) be its color-preserving permutation group. Then for any relabeling sigma in S_n, the difference
[b(sigma pi)]+[b(pi)] in Q
is independent of the full direction order pi if and only if sigma is in Aut(q). For sigma in Aut(q), the common difference chi(sigma) defines a homomorphism chi:Aut(q)->Q. The quotient-syndrome distribution of uniformly random full orders is constant on cosets of im chi. If chi is onto Q, every quotient class receives n!/|Q| direction orders and the legal affine coloring has a perfect root-profile matching.

*Proof.* Every adjacent interchange of two consecutive direction labels i,j changes the rooted affine permutation word b by delta_ij. Hence with id=(0,1,...,n-1),
[b(pi)]=[b(id)]+sum_(i<j with j appearing before i in pi)q_ij.
For any relabeling sigma and any order pi, consider a neighboring adjacent interchange of labels i,j producing pi'. In the relabeled orders sigma pi and sigma pi' the interchanged labels are sigma(i),sigma(j). Therefore the difference of the two putative relabeling increments is q_ij+q_(sigma(i),sigma(j)). Since every unordered pair can occur consecutively, the increment is independent of pi precisely when sigma preserves all quotient edge labels. In that case, composition of relabelings adds their increments, defining the homomorphism chi. The group acts freely on full label orders. Each group orbit maps onto one coset of im chi, with equal-size fibers, proving the uniformity assertion. The matching consequence follows from the affine quotient-transversal theorem.

For a transposition sigma=(p q), membership in Aut(q) is equivalent to q_pw=q_qw for every third vertex w, which is the quotient-twin condition. Its increment is q_pq. Thus the preceding twin-forcing theorem is the transposition-generated instance of this more general relabeling-group theorem.

*Finite scope audit.* Exhaustive enumeration over all 256 legal affine Q4 matrices and all 32768 legal affine Q5 matrices compared forcing by the full color-preserving relabeling group with forcing by quotient-twin transpositions alone. They certify the same matrices in these dimensions: at corank one, 20 of 140 in Q4 and 3280 of 18960 in Q5, and none of the higher-corank examples. This does not establish equivalence in arbitrary dimension. More sophisticated full-order operations may be necessary for the remaining affine matrices.
