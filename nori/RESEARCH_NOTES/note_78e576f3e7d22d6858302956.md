## Research note

Let c be an antipodally odd edge coloring of Q_n, with red edge-swap involutions U_i and full sweeps U_pi. Define G(c)={x: there is a color-zero full antipodal geodesic rooted at x} and H(c)={x: {U_pi(x):pi in S_n} intersects {U_sigma(bar x):sigma in S_n}}. The paired-sweep concatenation criterion gives H(c) subseteq G(c). The two sweep orders pi and sigma are allowed to differ.

**Theorem (rootwise paired-sweep incompleteness).** There is a legal coloring c of Q_6 with |G(c)|=62 and |H(c)|=60. In particular x=10 has a zero full antipodal geodesic, but the sets of red sweep endpoints from 10 and its antipode 53 are disjoint. The missing root pair is {10,53}.

**Certificate.** Index cube bits by 0,...,5, identify vertices with integers 0,...,63, and let rho_i(x) delete bit i. Let c_i(x) be bit rho_i(x) of 32-bit table T_i. The unsigned tables are
T=(0x2d4aad4b,0xfaf330a0,0x486aa9ed,0x9e68e986,0x0f77110f,0x362e8b93).
Each table is antipodally odd on the remaining five bits, and by definition ignores its own bit. For each root x, use the rank-subset recurrence: reachable[0]=true; whenever reachable[S] and c_i(x xor S)=0, declare reachable[S xor 2^i]=true for i outside S. Then x belongs to G(c) precisely when reachable[63]. This yields |G(c)|=62. Directly enumerate U_pi(x) for all 720 orders pi to form S_c(x); the test S_c(x) intersect S_c(x xor 63) yields |H(c)|=60, with the discrepancy G(c)\H(c)={10,53}. A zero full path from 10 uses direction order (1,2,5,4,0,3). Independent table-bit evaluation confirms the result.

**Theorem (exponentially incomplete rootwise sweep certificates in balanced good cubes).** For Cartesian products of independent edge colorings, G(c box d)=G(c) times G(d), S_(c box d)(x,y)=S_c(x) times S_d(y), and therefore H(c box d)=H(c) times H(d). Hence in Q_(6r), the r-fold Cartesian power of the example has |G|=62^r and |H|=60^r. Of the roots supporting red full geodesics, only the fraction (30/31)^r admits a paired-sweep midpoint witness.

Apply the standard balanced paired lift L to the six-bit example, yielding a vertex-balanced legal coloring on Q12. Successful geodesic roots lift as G(Lc)=G(c) times Q6. Every pair of lifted sweep endpoints meeting above antipodal lifted roots projects to a pair of original sweep endpoints meeting above antipodal roots, so H(Lc) is contained in H(c) times Q6. For the r-fold Cartesian product of Lc in dimension 12r, the proportion of successful roots supporting paired sweeps is at most (30/31)^r, which tends to zero. Each coloring is balanced and admits full monochromatic antipodal geodesics. This refutes rootwise completeness of even different-order paired sweeps, including balancedness and all-dimensional product amplification. It does not refute the existential paired-sweep conjecture, since every displayed product still has many paired-sweep roots.
