## Research note

Sharp unrestricted Fourier lower bound on monochromatic path multiplicity. Let n even, K=binomial(n-1,n/2), and c any legal odd coloring. For each direction i let alpha_i be the Walsh coefficient of its signed edge-color function on the full set of other n-1 directions. For stationary parity sign vector epsilon in plus-minus-one to the n, its antipodal edge-pair distance D(epsilon) from c equals 2^(n-3) times (n minus sum_i epsilon_i alpha_i). Each stationary coloring has n!/2 zero full paths, and each supported edge orbit belongs to n!/(2K) of them. The elementary union bound leaves at least n!/2 times positive-part(1-D(epsilon)/K) stationary zero paths surviving all discrepancies. The parity foliation theorem makes these surviving paths disjoint as epsilon varies. Thus N0(c) is at least n!/2 times the sum over all epsilon of positive-part(1-D(epsilon)/K). Equality holds at every stationary parity coloring: its own term is one, all other terms vanish since changing a parity sign differs on 2^(n-2)>K edge-pair orbits. This quantitative lower bound applies to every legal coloring and is sharper than using only the nearest stationary parity reference.
