## Research note

Given a legal odd coloring, take the directed influence graph j->i when rule c_i depends on bit j. Let K be vertices with a directed path to a directed cycle, and let S be its complement. Then S induces an acyclic graph, and there are no arcs from S into K. The K-direction rules therefore define a smaller legal odd coloring cK independent of S. For each root (u,v), the set of color profiles of all full direction-simple geodesics is W_(u,v)(c)=W_u(cK) times F2^S. Necessity follows by projecting any full geodesic onto K. For sufficiency, choose a K-geodesic of the desired profile and append the S-directions in topological influence order. Each new direction rule is odd on all directions included earlier, ignores future S bits, and has opposite colors at the current geodesic endpoints. Insert the new direction at the beginning or end to realize its chosen color. Earlier direction rules ignore the new bit and retain their colors. Thus the root fiber of every target full profile is R_t(c)=R_(tK)(cK) times F2^S. In particular the fraction of successful roots is unchanged, and NORI1 holds for c iff it holds for cK. K is the unique minimal direction set with acyclic complement and no incoming arcs from the complement: any such set must contain every cycle and all its ancestors. Equivalently it is the residual graph after repeatedly peeling singleton sink strongly connected components. This reduction is valid for unrestricted nonlinear direction rules. Independent tests on 12 random legal odd three-cube cores extended by two random odd feed-forward rules checked all 384 root-fiber comparisons.
