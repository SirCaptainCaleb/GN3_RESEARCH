## Research note

# Uncolored obstacle avoidance in Q5 and Q6 (solver-checked finite theorem)

Define an antipodal orbit of an undirected cube edge e as {e,bar e}; the orbit support O(P) of a full geodesic P is the set of its n traversed edge orbits.

**Finite computational claim (trusted SAT results).** For n=5 and n=6, every legal antipodally odd edge coloring of Q_n and every full antipodal geodesic P, regardless of the edge colors on P, admit a monochromatic color-zero full geodesic R satisfying O(R) cap O(P)=empty. The antipodal image bar R is a color-one full geodesic with the same orbit support, so it avoids O(P) as well. Hence every attained indexed color profile has an orbit-disjoint witness from any chosen full geodesic P, by independently complementing the color rules in target directions. The stronger arbitrary-profile conclusion holds for all w, even if w was not first known attained.

The claim is **finite and solver checked**, not yet accompanied by a self-contained mathematical proof or independently verified SAT proof log. It is compatible with universal full-profile saturation in Q5 and Q6 but strictly strengthens that finite result.

**Q4 obstruction.** In Q4, the same property fails. Under the canonical orbit enumeration, mask 0xA824 yields a legal coloring with no red full geodesic avoiding the four orbits of the canonical path rooted at zero with direction order 0,1,2,3. Independent direct enumeration verifies that no avoiding red full path exists. (The preceding mask 0x92AA is another example whose canonical P is red.)

**SAT formulation.** Automorphism invariance permits fixing P to x=0 and direction order (0,...,n-1). There are n*2^(n-2) antipodal undirected edge orbits. Give each orbit a Boolean variable t_o, recording the color on the lexicographically smaller undirected edge. The other edge has color 1+t_o. Every rooted full direction-simple geodesic R with O(R) disjoint from O(P) is represented by the clause
\[
\bigvee_{e\in R} \bigl(t_{o(e)}\mathbin\oplus s_e\bigr)=1,
\]
where s_e is 0 if e is the chosen orbit representative and 1 otherwise. This clause says that R contains a color-one edge. A satisfying assignment therefore is a legal coloring with no red full geodesic avoiding O(P). These clauses use **no color constraint on P**.

Rooted path reversal gives duplicate clauses and these are deduplicated. For n=4,5,6 respectively the SAT instances have orbit-variable counts (16,40,96), and avoiding-path clause counts (56,1010,16152). A ctypes implementation invoking the native libz3 SAT engine reports SAT at n=4 and UNSAT at n=5,6; the latter two results took around 0.01 and 1.45 seconds respectively. An independent SciPy/HiGHS mixed-integer encoding reports SAT at n=4 and infeasible at n=5, with no colors fixed on P. Direct path enumeration verifies the n=4 witness. Tracked Z3 UNSAT cores for the stronger original setup have length 678 in Q5 and 8016 in Q6 and use zero assumptions specifying red edges of P, corroborating the same strengthening. 

For n=7, the analogous SAT instance has 224 variables and 264138 avoiding-path clauses. Z3 returned UNKNOWN under a 25-second timeout. No conclusion is asserted for n>=7.

**Conditional implication.** If the uncolored obstacle avoidance property holds for every legal coloring in every sufficiently large dimension, it implies unrestricted NORI1, since it supplies a zero full geodesic in every such dimension. A hypothetical low-dimensional counterexample could be lifted by the known counterexample-preserving paired lift into arbitrarily large even dimensions, contradicting the property.

**Caution.** The older path-repair assertion that an arbitrary prescribed red geodesic can be replaced by a red path disjoint from its orbits fails at n=4. The new Q5/Q6 evidence concerns *all reference paths, independently of their colors*, and is stronger. The n7 SAT status and the absence of a dimension-uniform geometric forcing argument are the central open limitations.

**Adversarial falsification of unrestricted orbit-set avoidance.** The hypothesis that an arbitrary selection of one orbit per direction can always be avoided by a red full geodesic is false in Q5. With orbit indices assigned by scanning smaller endpoints and directions, select forbidden orbits (28,31,6,14,19), one in each direction. Choose the legal coloring with the 40-bit orbit mask 0x666ae4b58c (a representative edge has the bit at its orbit index; its antipodal edge has the opposite color). Enumerating all 32 roots and 120 direction orders gives 50 red rooted full paths, of which zero avoid the forbidden five orbits. The orbit representatives have smaller vertex endpoint values 8,9,1,3,4 in directions 0,1,2,3,4, respectively. The selection fails the three-direction coherence identity on directions 0,1,2: q_01=0, q_12=1, q_02=0 with q_ij=1+s_ij+s_ji. Thus the negative example cannot be the orbit set of any full geodesic. The dimension-uniform four-local coherence and 4n-fiber theorems are published in the existing full-profile Subsection at composition version 70.

**Updated scope.** The Q5/Q6 SAT result concerns forbidden orbit configurations coming from a single full antipodal geodesic. It cannot be extended to arbitrary independent orbit choices even in Q5. The n=7 coherent-obstacle SAT instance remained UNKNOWN at a 25-second Z3 cutoff.

### Half-root reachability-cut SAT compression (new formulation)

For n>=3 and a coherent obstacle O, the existence of a legal coloring with no red full antipodal geodesic avoiding O is equivalent to the following Boolean system. Introduce orbit color bits z_o, plus reachability bits R_(x,S) for every root x having x_0=0 and every direction subset S. Require R_(x,empty)=1, R_(x,all)=0. Whenever i is outside S and the direction-i edge from x+S avoids O, impose (not R_(x,S)) OR (edge color is blue) OR R_(x,S+i). This system is satisfiable if and only if a corresponding legal coloring has no red avoiding path: red paths force reachability by induction, while red-reachable subset indicators satisfy all clauses when no full red path exists. Considering only roots with x_0=0 suffices because reversal of a red full path interchanges antipodal roots without changing its support.

For canonical O with root zero and order 0,...,n-1, the system uses n*2^(n-2)+2^(2*n-1) variables and n*2^(2*n-2)-(n-1)*2^n clauses, including source/sink clauses. This replaces factorially many path exclusions with an exponentially sized monotone reachability encoding.

Independent Z3 checks: Q5 has 552 variables and 1152 clauses, UNSAT; Q6 has 2144 variables and 5824 clauses, UNSAT; Q7 has 8416 variables and 27904 clauses, unresolved under a 90-second cutoff. These finite findings are solver-checked, without independent UNSAT proof logs. Saved supporting files: nori_reachability_cut_obstruction_theorem.md and nori_avoidance_reachability_sat.py. This is a dimension-uniform encoding equivalence, not a proof of all-dimensional avoidance or NORI1.
