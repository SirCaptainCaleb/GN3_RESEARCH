## Research note

**Antipodal root-pair sparsity under Cartesian products.** On Q_2 with bits x_0,x_1 take the odd legal rules f_0=1+x_1 and f_1=1+x_0 modulo two. The favorable-root set for full geodesics with nondecreasing color word 0^*1^* is A_0(f)={01,10,11}: root 00 only produces the decreasing word (1,0), roots 01 and 10 produce (0,0), and root 11 produces (0,1). Its antipodal pair set P_0(f)=A_0(f) intersect bar(A_0(f)) is {01,10}. The existing product theorem A_0(f box g)=A_0(f) x A_0(g), P_0(f box g)=P_0(f) x P_0(g) yields |A_0(f^box h)|=3^h and |P_0(f^box h)|=2^h on Q_(2h). Thus the fractions of vertices that are favorable and antipodally paired favorable are respectively (3/4)^h and (1/2)^h. Both go to zero, even though each factor and product is itself a legal antipodally odd coloring admitting monochromatic antipodal full geodesics. This rules out a uniform positive density theorem for monotone-geodesic roots or their antipodal intersection, while the grand equivalent nonemptiness criterion remains open.
