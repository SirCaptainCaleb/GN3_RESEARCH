## Research note

For every m>=3, define f_1(x)=1-x_2, f_2(x)=1-x_1 and f_i(x)=0 for i>=3. At root 0^m the first among directions 1,2 has color one, and the second color zero, hence no nondecreasing full geodesic exists. Yet changing either of the two one-colored edges incident to the root to zero gives an all-zero full geodesic: traverse the changed direction first and the other second. Two distinct repair pivots may therefore share one root and one transverse bridge without a favorable path. This obstruction persists in all dimensions and refutes the proposed two-pivot shared-bridge exchange unless additional global information is used.
