## Research note

For n=2m, define R^m(a)={x∈Q_n:d(a,x)=m and some monochromatic geodesic joins a to x}, with the witnessing color unrestricted. If R^m(a)∩R^m(ā) contains x, monochromatic geodesics a→x and x→ā concatenate to an antipodal 1-splice geodesic: d(a,x)+d(x,ā)=n. Candidate: identify an intrinsic antipodally symmetric class of colored geodesic systems in which suitable midpoint co-reachability is forced, with rigorous encodings of both unrestricted NORI1 and ordinary boundary 3-tournaments. A 1-splice witness alone does not establish NORI1's 0-splice target.
