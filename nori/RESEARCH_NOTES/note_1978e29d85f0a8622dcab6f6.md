## Research note

For two cyclic-local colorings f,g, let D_i be the two-bit neighbor inputs where f_i=0 and g_i=1. If each D_i has size at least 2, a common full mono-0 f / mono-1 g path exists except when all D_i are antipodal pairs and sum_i(1+parity(D_i))=1 mod 2. Proof: cycle incidence parity and noncyclic orientation repaired by distinct doubled/flexible input sites, or by three flexible sites; all doubled parity obstruction is necessary. If instead f_i=0 on at least 3 of 4 inputs and g_i=1 on at least 3 of 4 inputs at all i, then A0(f) intersection A1(g) is nonempty without exception. The exceptional sets force g(edge)=1-f(antipodal(edge)); nonlinear-cycle theorem gives all-zero f full geodesic, whose reversed dimension order from same root is all-one g. Exhaustive check local 11^3/11^4 sets and 15625/390625 majority pairs dimensions 3/4 agrees. Details in ongoing manuscript.
