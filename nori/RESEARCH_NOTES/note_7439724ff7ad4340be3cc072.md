## Research note

**Theorem (all parabolic cells avoiding the first-order exchange admit canonical simplex fillings).** Work with the signed second-switch labeling \(\ell(x,\pi)=(-1)^{w_1}e_{s_2(w)}\) from Article XIV, defined on complete-geodesic flags with at least two color changes. Let \(B_n\) have simple chamber moves \(s_0\) (flip the sign of the first dimension) and \(s_i\) (exchange positions \(i,i+1\), \(1\le i<n\)). Suppose a dual Coxeter cell \(D\) corresponds to a parabolic coset \(gW_J\) with \(1\notin J\). Then every chamber in \(gW_J\) traverses the same undirected cube edge at its first step. Consequently all labels \(\ell(h)\), \(h\in gW_J\), have the same sign. Their vertices belong to one simplex of the boundary of the crosspolytope on the axes \(e_2,\ldots,e_{n-1}\). Any convex interpolation among these labels remains in that simplex, so the dual cell admits a canonical extension into the indicated face, consistent with affine interpolation on its edges.

*Proof.* The generators \(s_i\), \(i\ge2\), preserve the first dimension of the order and the starting cube vertex. The generator \(s_0\) flips only the bit of the first dimension at the starting vertex, so the first edge is unchanged as an undirected edge. Any word in these generators therefore preserves the first edge; its color, which determines the sign of \(\ell\), is constant throughout \(gW_J\). The positive (or negative) basis vertices form a simplex in the crosspolytope boundary, and convex interpolation within it avoids the origin. \(\square\)

This localizes the nontrivial cellwise extension obstruction in the proposed Wu–Yang adaptation to **parabolic cells involving the exchange \(s_1\) of the first two dimensions**. It is a structural restriction valid at every rank and for every antipodally odd cube-edge coloring, rather than a finite-order verification.

**Scope.** This theorem does not provide compatible fillings of cells whose parabolic subset contains \(s_1\). Such cells can include opposing second-switch labels, as shown by the explicit codimension-two witness in the published subsection. Nor does the statement alone construct a global map on the entire dual complex. It identifies where a support-sensitive analogue of Wu–Yang's degenerate-radial cancellation must operate.

**Research priority.** Investigate induced label chains on the \(s_1\)-containing parabolic cells and seek a rank/cancellation identity analogous to Wu–Yang Lemmas 5.1 and 5.4. An extension respecting their boundaries would make the second-switch labeling into an odd map from the full type-B sphere \(S^{n-1}\) to \(S^{n-3}\) and contradict Borsuk–Ulam. Avoid replacing this by restricted coloring-family classifications or small-order searches.
