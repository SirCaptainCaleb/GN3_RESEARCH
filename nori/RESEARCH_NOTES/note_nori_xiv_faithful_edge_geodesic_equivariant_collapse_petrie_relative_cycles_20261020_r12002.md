# Faithful edge-geodesic carrier and its relative root-slide cycles

Let n>=3. Let L_n be the simplicial complex whose vertices are the edges of Q_n and whose facets are the edge sets E(P) of all unoriented full antipodal geodesics P. Every simplex contains at most one edge of each dimension, and distinct full paths have distinct edge sets. Cube antipodality tau acts freely on |L_n|: tau takes an edge to a distinct edge of the same dimension, so a simplex cannot be tau-invariant.

**Theorem 1 (equivariant collapse).** L_n admits a tau-equivariant simplicial collapse onto a subcomplex of dimension at most n-2; hence w_1(L_n/tau)^(n-1)=0.

*Proof.* Let P=(v_0,...,v_n), with edge e_k=v_(k-1)v_k. For every 2<=k<=n-1 the ridge R_k(P)=E(P) minus {e_k} is contained in just one facet, E(P). Indeed, the remaining edges form two disjoint nonempty path components using n-1 different dimensions. Any full geodesic containing them must join their endpoints by an edge in the one missing dimension. The only adjacent endpoint pair is (v_(k-1),v_k); each other pair has Hamming distance at least two. Thus the completion is unique. Choose k so that e_k has least dimension index among the internal edges. This rule is invariant under reversing P and commutes with tau. All chosen ridges are distinct and free, and their tau-pairs are distinct. Collapse every facet along its chosen ridge, in tau-pairs. Every top simplex disappears; equivariant homotopy invariance yields the claimed vanishing. QED.

**Theorem 2 (relative Petrie cycles).** Let B_n be the subcomplex generated by all internal-deletion ridges R_k(P), 2<=k<=n-1. Over F_2 the degree-(n-1) relative boundary of E(P) consists of its two endpoint-deletion ridges. Every endpoint-deletion ridge lies in just two facets related by a forward or backward root slide. Hence the relative top boundary matrix is the vertex-edge incidence matrix of disjoint root-slide cycles, each comprising 2n facets, and
H_(n-1)(L_n,B_n;F_2) is isomorphic to F_2 raised to the rank 2^(n-2)(n-1)!.

*Proof.* An (n-1)-edge connected geodesic has two extensions by its missing dimension, at its two ends. They differ by a cyclic root slide. Thus endpoint-deletion ridges have two incident facets, and internal-deletion ridges have one. In the relative boundary only endpoint-deletion ridges survive. A root slide cyclically rotates the n distinct dimensions; its nth iterate is tau and its 2nth iterate is identity, with minimal period 2n. The number of unoriented facets is 2^(n-1)n!, giving 2^(n-2)(n-1)! relative cycles. Each contributes one independent F_2 top cycle, and no higher-dimensional cells exist. QED.

**Faithful colored coincidence.** For an antipodally odd coloring c, map each edge e of dimension i to (-1)^c(e)u_i, where u_i is the ith standard basis vector. Affine extension defines an equivariant simplicial map Phi_c:L_n->boundary(crosspolytope_n), whose restriction to each facet is a homeomorphism onto a signed orthant facet. A monochromatic full geodesic exists if and only if Phi_c(|L_n|) meets the pair of antipodal all-equal-sign facet barycenters. If no such path exists, projection orthogonal to (1,...,1), followed by normalization, gives an equivariant map L_n->S^(n-2). Theorem 1 shows that ordinary top-index theory alone cannot exclude this map. An improved topological argument must exploit the direction-preserving form of Phi_c or a color-sensitive relative structure, possibly coupling the cycles in Theorem 2 through adjacent-square exchanges. No such coupling theorem has been proved here.

## Profile transport and the limitation of individual Petrie classes

Write w(P) in F_2^n for the direction-indexed color profile of a full geodesic P. A forward root slide drops the first edge of direction a and appends its antipodal edge. Antipodal oddness gives

w(P^+) = w(P) + e_a.

The n successive slides replace all edges once and therefore take w(P) to w(P)+1. After 2n slides the initial profile returns. Thus every root-slide orbit traces a 2n-edge Petrie cycle in the profile hypercube; its first n steps are an antipodal geodesic. NORI1 asks whether some such cycle visits either constant profile.

For two rooted full geodesics differing by one adjacent exchange of successive directions a,b, all their other edges coincide. Their profiles consequently differ only in entries a,b. Let Y_n be the boundary of the profile cube [0,1]^n with the two constant-profile vertices removed. A proper face F of [0,1]^n meets these two omitted vertices in at most one of them. Whenever F intersect Y_n is nonempty, that intersection is contractible: it is the full face or a cube with one corner deleted. Faces carrying the two exchanged mixed profiles satisfy this nonemptiness condition. The two exchanged mixed profiles can therefore always be connected inside this punctured proper face. More generally any family of profiles agreeing outside a nonempty fixed set of directions is carried by such a contractible proper face.

Topologically, Y_n is equivariantly homotopy equivalent to S^(n-2), with central inversion acting antipodally. For n>=4, pi_1(Y_n)=0, so every individual pole-avoiding profile Petrie cycle is nullhomotopic. For n=3, the profile cube graph after removing the two poles is a hexagon. Every pole-avoiding Petrie cycle traverses that hexagon and represents a generator of pi_1(Y_3)=Z. Consequently any dimension-uniform forcing argument through Y_n must organize higher-dimensional compatibility among root-slide cycles; winding of one profile loop does not furnish an obstruction when n>=4.

The relative cycles from Theorem 2 have a useful boundary interpretation. For a root-slide orbit O set Z_O=sum_{P in O}[E(P)] in C_(n-1)(L_n;F_2). Its boundary lies in B_n, comprising precisely the internal-deletion ridges of its facets. Every such ridge belongs to a unique facet, and B_n has dimension n-2. It follows that the classes [partial Z_O] in H_(n-2)(B_n;F_2) are nonzero and independent for distinct O. In particular, the connecting homomorphism H_(n-1)(L_n,B_n)->H_(n-2)(B_n) is injective, mapping [Z_O] to [partial Z_O].

Nevertheless, if c has no monochromatic full geodesic, the signed orthant map Phi_c sends all of |L_n| into the punctured crosspolytope sphere Y'_n. Therefore

(Phi_c)_* [partial Z_O] = [partial (Phi_c)_# Z_O] = 0 in H_(n-2)(Y'_n;F_2).

This Stokes identity rules out direct homological detection of the puncture by the total internal-ridge boundary of one relative Petrie class, even though that source class is nonzero. A possible future obstruction must distinguish compatible portions of these boundaries or use higher exchange cells. The existence of the required global class remains open.
