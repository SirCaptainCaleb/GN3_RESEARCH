# R865 — Minimum five-cell inward-anchor recompletion splits by Q-X transition count, with the one-cross branch already dimer-localized

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten five-cell packet R862, with D={q_2,q_{m-2}}, W=H-D, and cells G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], G_2=Q[m-1,m], G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}. Let T be an exact two-cover of W in the minimum case sigma_G(T)=0, so each G_i occurs as one contiguous T-block and T has exactly three intercell selected transitions. Then: (1) T has no selected transition directly between G_3 and G_4. Indeed such an adjacency would make the four consecutive vertices of the two dimer blocks a tight Hamilton P4 on X={a,b,c,z}, contradicting the P4-free transitive frame of R594. (2) Let c_X(T) be the number of selected transitions with one endpoint in a Q-cell G_0,G_1,G_2 and the other in an X-dimer cell G_3,G_4. Then c_X(T) is one of 1,2,3. It is at least one because accepted R508 applied with deletion set D, surviving carrier S=V(Q)-D, and absorber Q forces every exact H-D two-cover to cross S|X; it is at most three because T has exactly three intercell transitions. Since G_3G_4 is impossible, the remaining 3-c_X(T) transitions are Q-cell to Q-cell. (3) If c_X(T)=1, then the three Q-cells are joined by the other two transitions into one Q-cell path, exactly one X-dimer is attached to that path, and the other X-dimer is an isolated T-rail. Equivalently the contracted five-cell forest is P4 disjoint union P1. Relative to the R508 partition S=V(Q)-D and C=X, T has tau(T)=1 and exactly two maximal C-blocks, namely the two physical dimers G_3 and G_4. Hence T is exactly an accepted R511 unique-transition recompletion packet with one pure C rail equal to one X-dimer and the other X-dimer the C-block of the mixed rail. After the accepted R512/R513 seam reduction, any surviving both-bad pure-seam residue has dimer pure-C rail and therefore lies directly in accepted R550 in the CS orientation or accepted R568 in the SC orientation. Thus the c_X=1 minimum branch is not a new five-cell species: it is a source-labelled specialization of the existing dimer unique-transition cell. The only genuinely new minimum five-cell transition patterns have c_X=2 or c_X=3.

When analyzing a minimum R862 recompletion, first count Q-X transitions. Discard c_X=1 as an already localized R511/R550/R568 branch and focus new work on c_X=2 or 3, where at least two source-labelled Q-X incidences coexist in the same exact pair-deletion frame.

Retain D; all five physical cells; the exact cover T; its three selected intercell transitions; the contracted two-path forest; the Q-X transition count c_X. In c_X=1 retain the identity and orientation of the pure X dimer, the mixed X dimer, the unique Q-X crossing and its Q cut index, and the full R511/R512/R513/R550-or-R568 seam certificates.

Does not assert the three source transitions yield simultaneous R176 descendants. Does not use the R664 witness families yet.

## Proof
Let D={q_2,q_{m-2}} and retain an exact two-cover T of W=H-D with sigma_G(T)=0 in the R862 partition. By R862, each of G_0,...,G_4 occurs as exactly one contiguous T-block and the total number of intercell selected transitions is t_G(T)=3. Contract each block to its cell label. Since T consists of two vertex-simple path rails, the contracted transition graph F_T is a two-component path forest on the five vertices G_0,...,G_4 with exactly three edges.

First exclude the edge G_3G_4. If a selected T-state joins the G_3 block to the G_4 block, then because sigma_G(T)=0 both blocks are complete contiguous dimers. The four consecutive vertices obtained by traversing the first dimer block, the selected interblock state, and the second dimer block form a tight P4 using exactly X={a,b,c,z}. This contradicts the P4-free conclusion of accepted R594. Hence no G_3-G_4 transition occurs.

Now apply accepted R508 with the same deletion set D and with S=V(Q)-D=G_0 union G_1 union G_2. The original path Q is a tight path on exactly D union S. Its complement in W is C=X=G_3 union G_4. Therefore every exact two-cover T of H-D selects at least one state crossing S|C. In the sigma=0 cell language, this is exactly a Q-cell to X-cell transition. Let c_X(T) denote their number. Since T has exactly three intercell transitions and none is G_3-G_4, 1<=c_X(T)<=3 and every remaining intercell edge is between two Q-cells.

Assume c_X(T)=1. Then the other two edges of F_T are Q-Q edges. There are three Q-cell vertices G_0,G_1,G_2. Because F_T is a forest, two Q-Q edges on these three vertices must form the unique three-vertex path, so all three Q cells lie in one connected component. The unique Q-X edge attaches exactly one of G_3,G_4 to that component; the other X-cell has no incident edge, because G_3G_4 is forbidden and there is only one Q-X edge. Thus F_T has shape P_4 disjoint union P_1. In particular one rail of T is exactly one complete X-dimer block and the other rail contains all three Q blocks and the other complete X-dimer block.

Return to the R508 partition S=V(Q)-D and C=X. The number tau(T) of selected S|C states is exactly c_X(T)=1. Because sigma_G(T)=0 and G_3G_4 is absent, the two maximal C-blocks in the literal T rails are precisely the two full physical dimers G_3 and G_4: one is the pure rail and the other is the unique C-block of the mixed rail. Hence the hypotheses and exact block word of accepted R511 are satisfied, with the pure C-block K having order two. Accepted R512/R513 give the complete unique-transition seam reduction. If an easy R512 output occurs, retain it. Otherwise the only hard residue is the R513 both-bad pure C/Q seam cell. Since the pure C rail K is a dimer, its parameter is s=1. If the mixed rail has C after S (CS), accepted R550 is exactly the dimer pure-C refinement of that R513 residue; if it has S after C (SC), accepted R568 is the exact dual refinement. Thus every c_X=1 sigma=0 recompletion is already governed by the accepted unique-transition/dimer seam machinery.

Consequently the only sigma=0 R862 transition counts not already reduced to that old cell are c_X=2 and c_X=3. No payment descendant or representative transfer is used anywhere in the classification.
