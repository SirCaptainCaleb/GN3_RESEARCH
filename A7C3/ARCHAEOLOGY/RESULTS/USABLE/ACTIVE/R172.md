# R172 — Fixed-Turn Quiet-Lineage Lex Descent

## Statement
Fix a retained proper tight turn J=(a,b,c) and enter its pair-bearing lineage. Let A_J subseteq {a,c} be the turn endpoints already captured as historical signed anchors by selected-incidence actualizations; set epsilon_J=2-|A_J|. Let M be the current balanced-pair mass. Define delta_J=0 when M>2. When M=2, let S be the current floor pair and set delta_J=2-|S intersect {a,c}|. Define Psi_J=(epsilon_J,M-2,delta_J) in ordinary lexicographic order on nonnegative integer triples. Along the chosen quiet, nonclosing, non-exit continuation that retains the fixed turn J, every macro step strictly decreases Psi_J. Hence no infinite quiet recurrence is possible in one fixed-turn lineage.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) smallest-counterexample setting; J=(a,b,c) is a fixed retained proper tight turn; the pair-bearing fixed-turn continuation uses the accepted retained-turn pair-mass tax above the floor, Complete Floor Pair Steering at M=2, Pair-Deletion Rigidity at the target-aligned floor, selected predecessor/successor incidence at b, endpoint-anchored signed-dimer actualization, Historical Anchor Protection / General Tight-Path Contact, and Cross-Generation Two-Pair Collision. Quiet means the chosen continuation has not taken a spanning two-cover or one of the accepted explicit productive historical pair/contact exits.

## Scope
History-relative / lineage-relative Strong theorem. It is a rank on one chosen proof lineage retaining the same graph-intrinsic turn J and its ancestry ledger. Representative changes are allowed only through the designated accepted fixed-turn macros that preserve that lineage data. It is not a graph invariant and not a monotone across arbitrary proof operations or switches to an unrelated turn.

## Applicability
Use to rule out free remint / recurrence after choosing one proper turn as a retained lineage coordinate. At M>2 the second coordinate pays; at the mass-two floor steering pays delta_J; at the aligned floor a fresh endpoint capture pays epsilon_J even if pair mass is subsequently reminted.

## Persistence
Retain throughout the lineage: J=(a,b,c); the active historical pair and mass M; floor-steering certificates when at M=2; the target pair {a,c}; captured endpoint set A_J; selected-incidence certificate at b; and all historical pair/contact exits. Witness names may change under accepted signed-support rebirth, but the fixed turn and endpoint-capture history are retained. A later remint may increase M only after epsilon_J has already strictly decreased.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Not a global well-founded rank for the Strong project. Does not control arbitrary operations after an explicit exit, deliberate abandonment of J, or switching to another turn lineage. Does not assert that every explicit pair/contact exit closes. Does not require or use CW-freeness, state-DAG height, scalarization, or finite repetition as contradiction. Proper cycles remain constructive outputs. The fixed-turn clock does not count historical capture events that occurred before entry unless they are part of A_J by the theorem's selected-incidence lineage definition.

## Proof
Fix the retained proper tight turn J=(a,b,c) and one chosen pair-bearing lineage satisfying the R172 quiet/nonclosing/non-exit fences. At the macro boundaries retain the historical captured-endpoint set A_J subseteq {a,c}, epsilon_J=2-|A_J|, the active balanced-pair mass M, and when M=2 the current singleton floor pair S and delta_J=2-|S intersect {a,c}|; set delta_J=0 above the floor. We choose the accepted macro granularity exactly as follows.

(1) Above-floor regime M>2. Apply the fully reconstructible certificate-retaining descent R415 to the active ancestry-bearing pair, retaining J as a fixed graph-intrinsic lineage coordinate and not abandoning its history. If a spanning two-cover occurs, this is a closing output and is outside the quiet continuation. Otherwise R415 reaches mass two through finitely many certified strict mass decreases. End the present macro at the first such decrease. Historical capture data cannot be lost, so epsilon_J cannot increase. If epsilon_J has already decreased during the segment, Psi_J decreases in its first coordinate. Otherwise epsilon_J is unchanged and M strictly decreases, so the second coordinate M-2 strictly decreases. If the new mass is two, delta_J may become positive, but lexicographic order has already decreased in the second coordinate. Thus every nonclosing above-floor macro strictly lowers Psi_J.

(2) Misaligned mass-two regime M=2 and S != {a,c}. Apply one nonclosing singleton-replacement macro from fully reconstructible Complete Floor Pair Steering R432 with target T={a,c}. R432 retains the incoming ancestry ledger and keeps pair mass two, while every nonclosing replacement lowers delta_T(S)=2-|S intersect T| by exactly one. Historical captured endpoints are retained, so epsilon_J does not increase. Hence either epsilon_J decreases first, or epsilon_J and M-2 remain unchanged and delta_J strictly decreases. Therefore Psi_J strictly decreases.

(3) Aligned-floor regime M=2 and S={a,c}, so the second and third coordinates are both zero. Work in the aligned pair-deletion frame and apply fully reconstructible R434. A selected predecessor or successor incidence at b yields an endpoint-anchored signed dimer at one outer endpoint e in {a,c}. If e has already completed a previous quiet selected-incidence episode, R434 gives an explicit anchor-contact/strict-growth event, hence a nonquiet exit. Therefore in a quiet nonclosing episode the endpoint e is fresh. Actualization either closes/exits or captures e as a new historical signed anchor. Immediately designate the protected return continuation as in R434. If a first contact is exposed, that is again an explicit nonquiet exit. Otherwise the continuation returns to a both-singleton floor retaining e and restores the opposite target endpoint, reaching the next aligned checkpoint with e recorded in the completed endpoint budget. Thus at the next quiet macro boundary A_J has strictly enlarged and epsilon_J has dropped by one. This remains a strict lexicographic decrease even if the episode temporarily or subsequently remints the active pair above mass two, because epsilon_J is the first coordinate.

These three regimes exhaust the chosen macro boundaries. The small difference between R172's historical wording 'already captured as historical signed anchors' and R434's stricter completed-return budget causes no gap: on a quiet lineage, a capture that has not yet completed its protected return is still inside the same macro; failure to complete quietly is an explicit contact/exit. Hence at successive quiet aligned macro boundaries every credited capture is exactly a completed retained endpoint episode. Therefore every quiet, nonclosing, non-exit macro retaining J strictly decreases the nonnegative lexicographic triple Psi_J. Ordinary lexicographic order on N^3 is well founded, so an infinite quiet recurrence in one fixed-turn lineage is impossible. The argument is lineage-relative and makes no claim across explicit exits, abandonment of J, or unrelated remints.
