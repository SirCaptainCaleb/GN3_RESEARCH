# R899 — The aligned long phase has four canonical exact covers of one common pair-deletion residue

## Statement
Retain the accepted R594/R619 frame in the ALIGNED phase and the surviving long range m>=6. Write L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, with X={a,b,c,z} and common aligned complementary root pair {b,z}. Then the single pair-deletion residue H-{b,z} has four simultaneous literal exact two-covers: T_L^a=(u,L,a,c) sqcup Q[2,m], T_L^c=(u,L,c,a) sqcup Q[2,m], T_R^c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,c,R,s), and T_R^a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,a,R,s). The two left representatives have the same rail-support partition and differ only by the order of {a,c}; the two right representatives likewise. Comparing a left representative with a right representative gives a fixed same-residue four-cross-state rectangle. Relative to the right support partition, T_L^a has exactly the two selected cross states L a and d s, while T_L^c has exactly L c and d s. Relative to the left support partition, T_R^c has exactly u v and c R, while T_R^a has exactly u v and a R. Thus the ALIGNED long phase can be entered through one common exact H-{b,z} fiber carrying four representatives and the simultaneous physical cross-state set {L a,L c,d s,u v,c R,a R}, with the variable end contacts forming the 2-by-2 choices {L a,L c} and {c R,a R}. No exactly-two-bad seam hypothesis is needed.

## Proof
Retain accepted R619 in the ALIGNED phase. Its four enlarged singleton-deletion covers are: H-z=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m] and H-z=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (b,a,c,R,s); H-b=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m] and H-b=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (z,c,a,R,s).\n\nIn the first H-z cover, b is the terminal vertex of the short rail. Deleting b therefore leaves the tight P4 (u,L,a,c), while Q[2,m] is unchanged; together they cover exactly H-{b,z}. This is T_L^a. In the first H-b cover, z is terminal, giving T_L^c=(u,L,c,a) sqcup Q[2,m]. In the second H-z cover, b is initial on the short rail, so deleting b leaves (a,c,R,s), giving T_R^c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,c,R,s). In the second H-b cover, deleting initial z leaves (c,a,R,s), giving T_R^a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,a,R,s). Thus all four are literal exact two-covers of the same residue H-{b,z}.\n\nNow compare support partitions. Every right representative has support partition Q[0,m-2] | {a,c,R,s}. In T_L^a=(u,L,a,c) | Q[2,m], the selected states on the short rail are uL,La,ac. Here u,L lie in Q[0,m-2] while a,c lie in the right short support, so La is the unique short-rail cross state. On Q[2,m], all adjacent Q states through d=q_{m-2} remain inside Q[0,m-2] until the state ds, after which s,R lie in the right short support; hence ds is the unique Q-rail cross state. Therefore the complete selected cross-state set of T_L^a relative to the right support partition is {La,ds}. The same argument for T_L^c gives {Lc,ds}.\n\nDually, every left representative has support partition {u,L,a,c} | Q[2,m]. In T_R^c=Q[0,m-2] | (a,c,R,s), the Q-prefix crosses this partition exactly at uv=(u,q_2), and the right short rail crosses it exactly at cR; thus its selected cross-state set is {uv,cR}. For T_R^a the corresponding set is {uv,aR}. These facts are simultaneous because all four covers are graph-intrinsic literal representatives supplied by the same R619 aligned frame. This proves the stated common-pair four-cover interface.
