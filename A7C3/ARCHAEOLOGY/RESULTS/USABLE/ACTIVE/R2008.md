# R2008 — Exceptional first-open blockers are already old-F successor states

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789413354.661259`

## Statement

Each of the two exceptional no-P4 occupied-successor blockers from R2006 is itself an old-F successor state.

- In the star-`00` residue the selected blocker is the nonconsecutive edge `b_{t+1}->b_{t-1}`.
- In the hook-`11` residue the selected blocker is the reverse spectator edge `b_2->b_1`.

Neither edge can belong to canonical `J[B]`. Since it is selected in the normalized pre-loss prefix, it lies in `F\J`. Consequently the first-open OUT copy is either already behind the `C_*` frontier, hence in its F-state on the augmenter, or lies on an off-`C_*` component already normalized to F.

Thus the two R516 residues are not new endpoint-only local obstructions: their blocking edge itself certifies the previously isolated old-F successor topology.

## Proof

A normalized prefix selects only edges from `F∪J`: on `C_*` the passed prefix is in its F-state and the untouched suffix in its J-state, while every off-`C_*` symmetric-difference component is normalized to its F-state.

In the star-`00` residue, the replacement successor is

`b_{t+1}->b_{t-1}`.

R1049 proves that every selected B-B edge of J is a forward consecutive spectator edge. The displayed backward two-step edge is therefore not in J. Since it is selected in the normalized prefix, it must lie in `F\J`. If its split copies belong to `C_*`, selection of this F-edge means the frontier has already passed that position; otherwise the edge lies on an off-`C_*` component in its normalized F-state.

In the hook-`11` residue, the replacement successor is

`b_2->b_1`.

Again R1049 allows only the displayed forward spectator edge `b_1->b_2` in J[B], possibly with that endpoint adjacency omitted altogether. The reverse directed edge `b_2->b_1` is never a selected J-edge. Its presence in the normalized prefix therefore makes it an `F\J` edge, with the same behind-frontier versus off-component dichotomy.
