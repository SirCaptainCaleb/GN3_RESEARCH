# R21 — Puncture Crossing Parent

## Statement

If a retained path is punctured and its surviving part is nonempty, every exact two-cover of the punctured graph selects a state crossing from the surviving carrier to the exterior. More strongly, the number of such crossings is at least the intrinsic path-cover number of the surviving carrier. A non-Hamiltonian survivor therefore forces at least two simultaneous crossings of the same cut.

## Scope
For any proper prescribed path removed from a historical cover, every exact two-cover of its complement selects at least the number of surviving historical runs minus two run-crossings. Restoring the prescribed path currentizes the whole packet and all these crossings in one minimum three-cover.

## Proof
Work in the intended smallest-counterexample puncture setting. Let K be the retained proper graph-intrinsic tight carrier before puncture, let D be the puncture set, assume the surviving carrier S=V(K)\\D is nonempty, and put E=V(H)\\V(K), the original exterior. Let T be any exact two-cover of the punctured graph H-D. The physical partition of V(H-D) is S sqcup E. Because K is a proper tight path in the hypothetical counterexample H, E cannot have path-cover number one: if E admitted a Hamilton tight path Q, then K sqcup Q would be a spanning two-cover of H. Hence pc(H[E])>=2. Apply accepted fully reconstructible intrinsic partition-cover deficit identity R7 to the partition {S,E} and the displayed two-rail cover T. If t is the number of selected states of T crossing S|E, then

t = pc(H[S]) + pc(H[E]) - 2 + sigma

for some representative-relative sigma>=0. Therefore t>=pc(H[S]). Since S is nonempty, pc(H[S])>=1, so every exact two-cover of H-D selects at least one carrier/exterior crossing. If S is non-Hamiltonian, pc(H[S])>=2 and hence at least two selected states cross the same physical cut S|E. This proves the first and stronger clauses of R21. The crossings are current selected states of this exact cover T; no orientation or endpoint role beyond the cut is inferred.

For the historical-run formulation, let a proper prescribed tight path P be removed from a historical spanning cover and split the surviving historical material into its maximal nonempty historical runs. Let T be any exact two-cover of H-V(P). Accepted reconstructed fragmentation identity R20, applied to this historical run coloring and the two current rails of T, says that the number of selected states joining distinct historical runs is at least r-2, where r is the number of surviving historical runs. Finally restore P beside the two exact complement rails. The literal union P sqcup T is a spanning three-cover of H; because H is a counterexample with no spanning two-cover, this displayed three-cover is minimum. Every selected crossing counted in T remains selected in this same restored representative, so the prescribed path and the full crossing packet are current together. This is exactly the second R21 clause. The two arguments determine only crossing multiplicity/currentness, not orientations, endpoint roles, or additional ancestry.
