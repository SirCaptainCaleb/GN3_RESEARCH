# R541 — Two-sided middle-gate polarity compresses the transitive R24 frame to two global cases

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let X be a transitive matching-height four-cell with M_R>M_S>M_L and let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=3, be a tight path with V(H)=X disjoint union V(Q). Assume X union {q_0} and X union {q_m} are P5-free and retain the endpoint reverse stars q_1 q_0 x and x q_m q_{m-1} for every x in X. By R534, at q_0 exactly one M_S-edge e_L is fully outgoing and its complementary M_S-edge f_L fully incoming; at q_m exactly one M_S-edge e_R is fully incoming and its complement f_R fully outgoing. The left gate e_L gives two tight prefix P5s, one absorbing each vertex of f_L, so for every w in f_L the induced subgraph on {w} union V(Q[2,m]) is non-Hamiltonian. Dually the right incoming gate e_R gives two tight suffix P5s, one absorbing each vertex of f_R, so for every w in f_R the induced subgraph on {w} union V(Q[0,m-2]) is non-Hamiltonian. Since M_S has exactly two edges, either e_L=e_R and f_L=f_R, or e_L and e_R are complementary and f_L and f_R are complementary. Thus the two-ended transitive frame has exactly an aligned or crossed middle-gate polarity.

## Hypotheses
H finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; V(H)=X disjoint union V(Q); X transitive matching-height; Q=(q_0,...,q_m) tight with m>=3; both endpoint five-sets are P5-free; the displayed universal endpoint reverse stars hold; R534 and R538.

## Scope
Explicit graph-intrinsic two-ended compression; the historical phrase hard R24 frame has been replaced by its literal required data.

## Applicability
Use before any inward propagation or pair-deletion case split. It replaces independent endpoint fan choices by one binary global polarity.

## Persistence
Retain the physical M_S matching, e_L,e_R, their complementary witness edges, the four explicit absorber P5s, and the induced one-witness non-Hamiltonicity statements.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim either polarity is impossible and does not synchronize exact covers of the one-witness subgraphs.

## Proof
For the left endpoint, let e_L={a,b} be the fully outgoing M_S-edge at q_0, so q_0ab and q_0ba are tight. Write f_L={c,d} for the complementary M_S-edge. Relabel c,d if necessary so that bd and ac are M_L-edges. Then the matching-height rule gives abd and bac tight because M_S>M_L. Together with q_1q_0a,q_1q_0b, the two orders (q_1,q_0,a,b,d) and (q_1,q_0,b,a,c) are tight P5s. The first has complement {c} union V(Q[2,m]) and the second has complement {d} union V(Q[2,m]); R538 therefore makes both one-witness tails non-Hamiltonian. For the right endpoint, let e_R={u,v} be the fully incoming M_S-edge at q_m and f_R={r,s} its complement. Relabel r,s if necessary so that ru and sv are M_R-edges. Then ruv and svu are tight because M_R>M_S. Since uvq_m and vuq_m are tight and every xq_mq_{m-1} is tight, the orders (r,u,v,q_m,q_{m-1}) and (s,v,u,q_m,q_{m-1}) are tight P5s. Their complements are respectively {s} union V(Q[0,m-2]) and {r} union V(Q[0,m-2]), so R538 gives the two right-prefix non-Hamiltonicity conclusions. Finally M_S consists of exactly two complementary edges. Hence the selected left and right gate edges are either the same edge, in which case their complementary witness edges are the same, or they are the two distinct M_S-edges, in which case the witness edges are complementary. This is precisely the aligned/crossed dichotomy.
