# R1006 — A dimer rail in a pair-deletion complement lifts to a trimer singleton fiber

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888 and let x,y be distinct vertices. Suppose an exact two-cover of G-{x,y} has a dimer rail {a,b} and opposite rail B. Then G-x has an exact two-cover T|B in which T is an increasing trimer on {y,a,b}. Consequently |B|>=6. The same conclusion holds with x and y interchanged.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order R888 counterexample; G-{x,y} has an exact two-cover with one rail of order two.

## Scope
Pure R888 short pair-deletion bridge into the accepted singleton trimer-fiber theory.

## Applicability
Use after R1005 or any pair-deletion argument reaches a dimer minimum rail. It converts the residue into the already-developed trimer singleton-deletion branch.

## Persistence
Retain the deleted pair x,y, the pair-deletion dimer {a,b}, an explicit increasing order T on {y,a,b}, and the same opposite rail B.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not eliminate dimer pair-deletion rails when the opposite rail has order at least six. Does not choose a canonical trimer orientation.

## Proof
Let G-{x,y}={a,b}|B with {a,b} a dimer rail. On the three vertices {y,a,b}, choose the two smallest of their three ordinary edge labels. Those two edges share a vertex, so orient the three vertices through their common endpoint in the order of the two labels; this gives an increasing trimer T on {y,a,b}. Hence G-x=T|B is an exact two-path cover: T covers y,a,b and B is inherited unchanged. Accepted R959 applies to every exact singleton-deletion two-cover in a minimum R888 counterexample, so |B|>=3. If |B|=3, this is a trimer/trimer singleton-deletion cover, excluded by accepted R978. If |B| is 4 or 5, it is excluded by accepted R975. Therefore |B|>=6. Interchanging x and y gives the symmetric assertion.
