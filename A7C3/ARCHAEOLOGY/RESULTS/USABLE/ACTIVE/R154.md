# R154 — Physical-star exact rank-two currentization

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let x be a vertex, and let F be a family of distinct physical two-vertex supports incident with x. Then F is simultaneously currentizable as selected supports in a path-forest/minimum-three-cover representative if and only if |F|<=2. For |F|=2, say F={{p,x},{x,y}} with p,x,y distinct, exactly one of the two composable mirror packets {px,xy} and {yx,xp} is compatible; it is the two-state packet of the unique tight trimer orientation (p,x,y) or (y,x,p), and that trimer can be currentized as one component of a minimum three-cover. For |F|>=3 simultaneous currentization is impossible.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; F consists of distinct physical pairs all incident with the same vertex x. Currentizable means there exists one path-forest/minimum-three-cover representative selecting one orientation of every support in F.

## Scope
Exact local synchronization ceiling for physical supports incident with one vertex. This is the stronger parent of the older Incident-Pair Mirror Currentization for bare physical-support coexistence; ancestry-rich captured-crossing specializations remain separate when their historical labels are consumed.

## Applicability
Use to decide whether a requested local incident-support synchronization target is feasible at all. For two supports it supplies the unique forced mirror trimer and a minimum-cover currentization. For three or more it deletes the target as mathematically impossible rather than merely technically unresolved.

## Persistence
For |F|=2 the physical support pair and the graph-intrinsic tight mirror trimer are stable facts; the selected orientations and complement cover are representative-relative. Different pairs around x may require different mirror orientations and different complement covers. No simultaneous currentization of an entire larger star is licensed.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not preserve historical selected orientations when the forced mirror orientation is opposite; does not preserve old carrier/component identity, residual rails, capture protection, concentration, endpoint roles, or ancestry. It does not synchronize separate pairwise currentizations. The impossibility for |F|>=3 concerns simultaneous selection in a path forest, not graph-intrinsic existence of the physical supports.
