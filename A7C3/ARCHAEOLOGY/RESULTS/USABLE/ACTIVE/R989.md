# R989 — A singleton-rich neutral-recombination family can be permanently trapped inside its selected-edge union

## Statement
There exists an edge ordering of K9 and nine increasing 4+4+singleton three-covers F_z, one isolating each physical vertex z, such that if U is the union of all directed selected edges appearing in the F_z, then: (i) U has ordinary bipartite matching number at least 7=n-2; (ii) every order-compatible directed matching contained in U has size at most 6=n-3; hence no finite sequence of R980-style neutral recombinations among covers whose selected edges remain in U can ever expose a positive recombination; but (iii) the ambient edge-ordered K9 is Hamiltonian by an explicit increasing Hamilton path using fresh directed edges outside U. Concretely the edge order is 37<15<58<17<67<07<12<24<36<27<48<28<68<56<01<18<47<08<25<34<04<57<14<06<26<35<02<38<03<23<16<05<46<13<78<45. The seed covers are F0=(4,8,6,1)|(7,2,5,3)|{0}; F1=(7,3,5,0)|(4,8,6,2)|{1}; F2=(7,3,5,0)|(4,8,6,1)|{2}; F3=(7,2,5,0)|(4,8,6,1)|{3}; F4=(5,8,0,3)|(7,2,6,1)|{4}; F5=(4,8,0,3)|(7,2,6,1)|{5}; F6=(7,1,8,0)|(4,2,5,3)|{6}; F7=(5,8,0,3)|(4,2,6,1)|{7}; F8=(7,3,5,0)|(4,2,6,1)|{8}. Their selected-edge union is U={0->3,1->8,2->5,2->6,3->5,4->2,4->8,5->0,5->3,5->8,6->1,6->2,7->1,7->2,7->3,8->0,8->6}. An ordinary size-seven matching in U is {6->1,1->8,0->3,4->2,5->0,2->6,3->5}. Exhaustive inspection of all C(17,7)=19448 seven-edge subsets shows that none is simultaneously a bipartite matching and locally increasing at every physical vertex. Nevertheless (7,1,2,4,8,6,5,3,0) is an increasing Hamilton path, with edge ranks 3<6<7<10<12<13<25<28.

## Hypotheses
Pure finite edge-ordered K9 example; no minimum-counterexample hypothesis. Order-compatible means distinct selected tails and heads and, at every physical vertex with both a selected incoming and outgoing edge, incoming edge rank is strictly smaller than outgoing edge rank.

## Scope
Exact finite fence against any universal theorem asserting that neutral R980 recombination inside one fixed singleton-seed selected-edge union must eventually yield a two-cover.

## Applicability
Use as a regression test for neutral-recombination escape theorems. Any universal R888 proof based on a fixed chosen singleton seed family must permit introduction of fresh complete-graph edges, replacement of seed representatives, or another operation that enlarges the selected-edge universe.

## Persistence
Retain the exact 36-edge order, all nine seed covers, the 17-edge directed union U, the explicit ordinary size-seven matching, the exhaustive 19448-subset order-compatibility check, and the explicit ambient Hamilton path.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not refute R888; the ambient graph is explicitly Hamiltonian. Does not refute R980 or R986. Does not say every singleton seed family is trapped. Does not rule out escape after changing seed representatives or introducing fresh complete-graph edges.

## Proof
Give the 36 unordered edges zero-based ranks in the displayed order. The consecutive rank triples on the two rails of F0,...,F8 are respectively: F0: 10<12<30 and 9<18<25; F1: 0<25<31 and 10<12<24; F2: 0<25<31 and 10<12<30; F3: 9<18<31 and 10<12<30; F4: 2<17<28 and 9<24<30; F5: 10<17<28 and 9<24<30; F6: 3<15<17 and 7<18<25; F7: 2<17<28 and 7<24<30; F8: 0<25<31 and 7<24<30. Hence all nine displayed objects are increasing 4+4+singleton three-covers. Their directed selected-edge union is exactly U={0->3,1->8,2->5,2->6,3->5,4->2,4->8,5->0,5->3,5->8,6->1,6->2,7->1,7->2,7->3,8->0,8->6}. The seven edges {6->1,1->8,0->3,4->2,5->0,2->6,3->5} have distinct tails and distinct heads, so the ordinary bipartite matching number of U is at least seven. For order compatibility, enumerate all 7-edge subsets S of the 17-edge set U. Reject S if two edges have the same tail or the same head. For every surviving S and every physical vertex v carrying both u->v and v->w, reject S when rank(uv)>=rank(vw). There are exactly binom(17,7)=19448 subsets; none survives both tests. This finite check is deterministic and may be reproduced by the literal loop over combinations(U,7) followed by the two stated tests. Therefore U has no order-compatible matching of size seven. Since order-compatible matchings are hereditary under deleting selected edges, it has none of any larger size either, so its order-compatible rank is exactly six because each seed supplies six. Every R980 componentwise recombination uses only selected edges from its two input covers. Starting from the nine seeds, all neutral recombinations therefore remain inside U. Inductively every cover in their neutral closure has all selected edges in U. A positive recombination from any two closure members would be an order-compatible matching of size seven contained in U, impossible. Thus the fixed selected-edge neutral closure is permanently trapped. Finally the physical Hamilton order (7,1,2,4,8,6,5,3,0) has consecutive edge ranks 3<6<7<10<12<13<25<28, so it is an increasing Hamilton path in the ambient K9. Hence the trap is purely a restriction of the chosen selected-edge universe and not an R888 obstruction.
