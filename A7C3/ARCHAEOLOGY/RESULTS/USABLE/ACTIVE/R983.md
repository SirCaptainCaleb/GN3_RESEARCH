# R983 — Two non-Hamiltonian endpoint extensions of one increasing rail need not Hamiltonize the two-rail union

## Statement
There exists an edge ordering of K_8 with two disjoint increasing paths A=(0,1,2,3) and B=(4,5,6,7) such that both induced five-vertex graphs on A union {4} and A union {7} are non-Hamiltonian in the increasing-path sense, while the full eight-vertex graph is also non-Hamiltonian. One exact edge order is 57<46<47<45<56<37<26<67<34<36<25<35<27<24<01<16<13<02<12<07<17<05<03<23<15<14<06<04. Thus the proposed rail-coupled endpoint lemma 'if both A+b_0 and A+b_m are non-Hamiltonian for the endpoints of a common increasing opposite rail B, then A union B is Hamiltonian' is false even for |A|=|B|=4.

## Hypotheses
Pure finite edge-ordered complete graph; no minimum-counterexample assumption.

## Scope
Exact finite negative result for a tempting R979 maximal-rail endpoint-extension route.

## Applicability
Use as a fence against trying to close R888 solely from a maximal singleton-deletion rail A and non-Hamiltonicity of A plus the two endpoints of the opposite rail. Additional multi-fiber or order-valued information is necessary.

## Persistence
Retain the exact K8 edge order, displayed rails A,B, and exact non-Hamiltonicity checks on A+4, A+7, and all eight vertices.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not refute R888 and does not assert the displayed K8 has path-cover number three. It only refutes the stated rail-coupled Hamiltonization shortcut.

## Proof
Assign ranks 0,...,27 to the displayed edges in order: 57,46,47,45,56,37,26,67,34,36,25,35,27,24,01,16,13,02,12,07,17,05,03,23,15,14,06,04. The rail A=(0,1,2,3) uses ranks 14,18,23 and is increasing. The rail B=(4,5,6,7) uses ranks 3,4,7 and is increasing. For a vertex sequence (v_0,...,v_{r-1}), it is an increasing Hamilton path exactly when rank(v_0v_1)<rank(v_1v_2)<...<rank(v_{r-2}v_{r-1}). A deterministic exhaustive check over all 5!=120 sequences on {0,1,2,3,4} finds none satisfying the inequalities; the same check over all 120 sequences on {0,1,2,3,7} finds none; and the check over all 8!=40320 sequences on {0,...,7} finds none. A reproducing checker is: edges='57 46 47 45 56 37 26 67 34 36 25 35 27 24 01 16 13 02 12 07 17 05 03 23 15 14 06 04'.split(); rank={tuple(sorted(map(int,e))):i for i,e in enumerate(edges)}; def inc(p): return all(rank[tuple(sorted((p[i],p[i+1])))]<rank[tuple(sorted((p[i+1],p[i+2])))] for i in range(len(p)-2)); then assert not any(inc(p) for p in permutations((0,1,2,3,4))); assert not any(inc(p) for p in permutations((0,1,2,3,7))); assert not any(inc(p) for p in permutations(range(8))). Therefore both endpoint extensions A+4 and A+7 are non-Hamiltonian and A union B is non-Hamiltonian, proving the exact negative statement.
