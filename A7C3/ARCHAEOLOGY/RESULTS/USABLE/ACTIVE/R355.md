# R355 — Hamilton-five global density equality has a parity fence

## Statement
Assume equality in the accepted R331 Hamilton-five bad-family ceiling on an r-vertex set W with r>=6. Write r=5q, so every four-set lies in exactly q bad five-sets and every six-set contains either zero or exactly two bad five-sets. Then q must be even. Consequently, combining with the accepted R346 condition q≠2 mod3, one has q≡0 or 4 mod6 and therefore r≡0 or 20 mod30. In particular global density equality is impossible at r=15 and, more generally, at every r divisible by five but not congruent to 0 or 20 modulo 30.

## Hypotheses
Equality holds in R331 on an r-vertex set W with r>=6; B denotes the bad five-set family and q=r/5.

## Scope
Parity realizability fence for the R331 paired four-design normal form.

## Applicability
Use before attempting structural analysis of the global Johnson equality branch. It removes all odd q and combines with R346 to leave only two residue classes modulo 30.

## Persistence
The bad-support family, its four-core occupancies, and six-set occupancies are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Necessary condition only. Does not assert equality is realizable for q≡0 or4 mod6.

## Proof
Fix any five-set F. Let x_S=|{G∈B:S⊂G}| for four-sets S and y_U=|{G∈B:G⊂U}| for six-sets U. Modulo two, Σ_{U⊃F} y_U + Σ_{S⊂F,|S|=4} x_S equals r·1_B(F): a block G=F is counted r-5 times in the first sum and 5 times in the second; a block G sharing exactly four vertices with F is counted once in each; all other G are counted zero times. R331 equality gives y_U∈{0,2} and x_S=q, so the left side is 0+5q≡q. As r=5q, the right side is q·1_B(F). If q is odd, every F is bad, contradicting R195. Thus q is even. R346 excludes q≡2 mod3, leaving q≡0,4 mod6 and r≡0,20 mod30.
