# R621 — Crossed middle gates force four asymmetric opposite-end singleton-deletion weaves

## Statement
Retain the crossed middle-gate phase of R619 with X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, and write L=q_0, u=q_1, R=q_m, s=q_{m-1}. Thus the left M_S gate {a,c} is fully outgoing from L and its complement {b,z} fully incoming to L, while at the right endpoint {b,z} is fully incoming to R and {a,c} fully outgoing from R. R619 gives the deep enlarged covers
H-z=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m],
H-b=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m],
H-c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,b,z,R,s),
H-a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,z,b,R,s).

Then every one of the four roots also has a literal exact opposite-end shallow cover:
H-z=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,a,c,b),
H-b=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,c,a,z),
H-c=(a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[1,m],
H-a=(c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[1,m].

Consequently every singleton deletion H-r in the crossed phase carries two distinct exact two-covers with opposite-end absorption. For the left-enlarged roots z,b the native cover absorbs {L,u} and the shallow opposite cover absorbs {R}; their four selected cross states, comparing the two covers in both directions, are respectively
r=z: L a, s R, u q_2, R a;
r=b: L c, s R, u q_2, R c.
For the right-enlarged roots c,a the shallow opposite cover absorbs {L} and the native cover absorbs {s,R}; their four selected cross states are respectively
r=c: z L, q_{m-2}s, L u, z R;
r=a: b L, q_{m-2}s, L u, b R.
Each displayed state joins the two components of the opposite exact cover, so R176 yields a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair birth from every one of the sixteen root-labelled cross states. Thus the crossed phase is not a no-weave residue: it contains four asymmetric 2-versus-1 endpoint-absorption singleton-deletion weaves. The asymmetry is only that each root retains the deep physical Q cut on its native enlarged side and the outermost Q cut on the opposite side.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the crossed phase of pending R619; Q=(q_0,...,q_m) with m>=4. Use the exact deep covers and the complementary M_S gate polarities supplied by R619/R534, singleton-deletion exactness from R4, and R176 for the cross-state pair births.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Fixed crossed-polarity frame; exact singleton-deletion covers and cross-state weave structure. No pair-deletion staircase, generic payment descendant, or order-ten closure is used.

## Applicability
Use before entering the mixed pair-deletion staircase. In the crossed phase there is already a same-root opposite-end weave for every deletion root. Downstream work should first test consumers of these four asymmetric weaves, especially consumers sensitive to the native deep cut plus opposite shallow cut, rather than assuming that nonlocal coupling first appears only after deleting a mixed root pair.

## Persistence
Retain the four root labels, both M_S gate edges and polarities, all eight exact singleton-deletion covers, the absorbed Q endpoint sets, and the sixteen root-labelled selected cross states. These are simultaneous graph-intrinsic cover certificates in the original H, although R176 descendants are alternative historical births rather than simultaneously paid representatives.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that any R176 pair birth closes H, that the sixteen pair births are simultaneously paid/current, or that the shallow cover can be extended one further Q vertex. Does not by itself discharge the crossed phase. Does not invalidate R620; it shows only that the pair-deletion staircase is not the earliest available nonlocal weave.

## Proof
By R619, in the crossed phase {a,c} is the left fully outgoing M_S edge and {b,z} its fully incoming complement; at the right endpoint {b,z} is fully incoming and {a,c} its fully outgoing complement. The four displayed deep covers are exactly those of R619.

For root z, the deep left P5 contains the tight turn (a,c,b). Since {a,c} is fully outgoing from R, (R,a,c) is tight. Hence (R,a,c,b) is a tight P4. Together with the untouched Q[0,m-1] it covers H-z. Exactness follows from R4: if H-z were Hamiltonian, that Hamilton path plus singleton z would two-cover H. Thus H-z=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,a,c,b). The same argument with the deep H-b P5 turn (c,a,z) and the tight gate turn (R,c,a) gives H-b=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,c,a,z).

Dually, for root c the deep right P5 contains the tight turn (a,b,z). Since {b,z} is fully incoming to L, (b,z,L) is tight. Hence (a,b,z,L) is a tight P4, and with Q[1,m] it gives an exact cover of H-c. For root a, (c,z,b) is a turn of the deep right P5 and (z,b,L) is tight from the same fully incoming left gate, giving the exact cover H-a=(c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[1,m].

Now compare each shallow cover with its deep cover. For H-z, the shallow cover selects L a and s R across the two deep components, while the deep cover selects u q_2 and R a across the two shallow components. For H-b the identical check gives L c, s R, u q_2, R c. For H-c, the shallow cover selects z L and q_{m-2}s across the deep components, while the deep cover selects L u and z R across the shallow components. For H-a the corresponding states are b L, q_{m-2}s, L u, b R. Every comparison is literal from the displayed path orders. Since H-r is proper, R176 applies independently to each selected cross state and gives the stated graph-intrinsic balanced-pair birth. No payment or simultaneous-currentness inference is used.
