# R979 — Every singleton deletion in a minimum R888 counterexample admits a four-by-four exact cover

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888. Then for every vertex z there exists an exact two-path cover G-z=P|Q in which |P|>=4 and |Q|>=4. Proof: minimality gives some exact two-cover of G-z; accepted R959 makes both rails have order at least 3. If both are at least 4, stop. Otherwise one rail A has order 3 and the opposite rail B is Hamilton. Put X=A union {z}. Accepted R972 applies to this trimer fiber, gives |B|>=5, and for every Hamilton-deletable b in B gives Hamiltonicity of X-z+b=A+b. Choose b as an endpoint of any Hamilton order of B; then B-b is Hamiltonian as well. Hence (A+b)|(B-b) is an exact cover of G-z with rail orders 4 and |B|-1>=4.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order counterexample to R888. Uses accepted R959 and R972.

## Scope
Pure edge-ordered minimum-counterexample rail-floor strengthening. It is existential per deletion fiber: some exact cover has both rails at least four.

## Applicability
Use when choosing singleton-deletion cover families for R926/R965: representatives may always be chosen with rail floor four, so no selected trimer port is necessary.

## Persistence
For each z retain an initial exact cover, and if it has trimer A retain X=A+z, a Hamilton order of B, one endpoint b, the R972 Hamilton support A+b, and the repaired cover (A+b)|(B-b).

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say every exact cover of G-z has both rails at least four; accepted R959 only gives the universal floor three. Does not eliminate trimer covers as alternative representatives.

## Proof
Fix z. Minimum-order counterexample minimality gives an exact two-path cover G-z=A|B. By accepted R959 both rails have order at least three. If both have order at least four there is nothing to prove. Otherwise, relabel so |A|=3. Put X=A union {z}. Then this is exactly the accepted R972 trimer-fiber setup. R972 gives |B|>=5 and its critical five-cell duality: for every Hamilton-deletable b in B and every x in X, the support X-x+b is Hamiltonian. Choose any increasing Hamilton order of B and let b be one of its endpoints. Then b is Hamilton-deletable because B-b is the inherited Hamilton suffix or prefix. Apply the critical five-cell conclusion with x=z. The support X-z+b=A+b is Hamiltonian. The two supports A+b and B-b are disjoint and partition V(G)-{z}. Hence they form an exact two-path cover of G-z. Their orders are 4 and |B|-1, respectively, and |B|-1>=4. Thus every deletion fiber has some exact cover with both rails of order at least four.
