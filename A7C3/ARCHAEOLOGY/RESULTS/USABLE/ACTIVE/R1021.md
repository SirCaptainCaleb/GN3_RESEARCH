# R1021 — Every quiet crossed deep gap exports to a current P4-bearing maximum three-forest

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, X={a,b,c,z}, and the R820 deep-gap notation. For every interior deep gap 3<=j<=m-4, at least one of the following holds: (SEAM) U_j or V_j holds; (GATE) G_AC(j) or G_BZ(j) holds; (FOREST) H has a literal minimum spanning three-cover K sqcup P sqcup Qprime in which K is a proper tight P4 crossing the old G_8|N decomposition at the physical gap q_j|q_{j+1}. More precisely, in FOREST either K is one of the four R874 probe P4s on {q_{j-1},q_j,q_{j+1},x} for x in {b,z}, or on {q_j,q_{j+1},q_{j+2},y} for y in {a,c}, or the complete central fan occurs and one may choose any K=(x,q_{j+1},q_j,y) with x in {b,z}, y in {a,c}. In every case accepted R4 gives an exact two-cover of H-V(K), so restoring K gives an actual maximum three-forest of H with the gap labels and X witnesses retained. At the terminal deep gap j=m-3, R820 already forces U_j or G_AC(j), so no terminal FOREST exception is needed.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame; m>=6. Use accepted R820, R874, and R4.

## Scope
R24 long-core export theorem. It does not close the crossed phase. It converts the joint terminal-blocker residue of R820 into current maximum-three-forest dynamics, exactly avoiding the intact-middle limitation R904.

## Applicability
At each crossed deep gap, first consume SEAM/GATE if productive. If both sides avoid those outputs, enter current maximum-three-forest space through the labelled P4 K rather than paying the two terminal packets separately. The P4 support physically uses interior Q vertices and X, so it changes the old G8|N representative.

## Persistence
Retain j, the old Q order, whichever R820 terminal packets fired, the exact R874 probe/FAN certificate, the literal P4 order K, and the exact two-cover of H-V(K) chosen by R4. The old G8|N decomposition remains historical source data, not necessarily current after the new forest is chosen.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the new maximum three-forest has smaller largest rail, that forests from distinct gaps coexist, or that SEAM/GATE branches close. Does not claim the P4 is unique. At j=m-3 the right-terminal R820 window is absent and the theorem uses the stronger existing U_j OR G_AC(j) conclusion instead.

## Proof
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and fix 3<=j<=m-4. Use the exact notation of accepted R820. Its AC-side compression states\n  U_j OR G_AC(j) OR T_AC(j),\nwhere T_AC(j) means both (q_{j+2},q_{j+1},a) and (q_{j+2},q_{j+1},c) are tight. Its BZ-side compression states\n  V_j OR G_BZ(j) OR T_BZ(j),\nwhere T_BZ(j) means both (b,q_j,q_{j-1}) and (z,q_j,q_{j-1}) are tight.\n\nIf U_j or V_j holds, we are in SEAM. If G_AC(j) or G_BZ(j) holds, we are in GATE. Assume neither. Then both T_AC(j) and T_BZ(j) hold simultaneously as graph-intrinsic turn certificates in the one crossed frame.\n\nPut D_j=(q_j,q_{j-1}) and D_{j+2}=(q_{j+2},q_{j+1}). The two turns in T_BZ(j) say exactly that D_j is head-signed by the two distinct witnesses b,z. The two turns in T_AC(j) say exactly that D_{j+2} is tail-signed by the two distinct witnesses a,c. The four witnesses a,b,c,z are distinct and all lie outside Q. Thus the hypotheses of accepted R874 apply with (b_1,b_2)=(b,z) and (a_1,a_2)=(a,c).\n\nR874 gives the following exhaustive physical output. Either one of its four R518 probes already produces a Hamilton P4: a P4 on {q_{j-1},q_j,q_{j+1},x} for some x in {b,z}, or a P4 on {q_j,q_{j+1},q_{j+2},y} for some y in {a,c}; or else its FAN branch holds and all four literal paths\n  (x,q_{j+1},q_j,y),  x in {b,z}, y in {a,c},\nare tight P4s. Choose one such literal P4 and call it K.\n\nBecause m>=6 and H contains X together with all vertices of Q, K is a proper path of H. Accepted smallest-counterexample minimality R4 therefore gives an exact two-cover P sqcup Qprime of H-V(K). Restoring K gives the literal spanning three-cover\n  H = K sqcup P sqcup Qprime.\nSince H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample, pc(H)=3 by R4, so this is a minimum spanning three-cover, equivalently an actual maximum spanning three-forest.\n\nThe new rail K is not contained in either side of the old R810 decomposition H=G_8 sqcup N. Accepted R820 explicitly states that the far-right T_AC window is absent there and its AC-side conclusion sharpens to U_j OR G_AC(j). Hence the terminal deep gap is already SEAM or GATE and no FOREST alternative is needed. This proves the theorem.
