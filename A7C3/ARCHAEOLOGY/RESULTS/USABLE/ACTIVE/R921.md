# R921 — Fully internal crossed recompletion forces dual inward gate anchors and a deep endpoint-capture/Reverse-Ear packet

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten inward-anchor frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, L=q_0, u=q_1, s=q_{m-1}, R=q_m, D={q_2,q_{m-2}}, W=H-D, Y=Q[0,1] union Q[3,m-3] union Q[m-1,m], and physical middle gates G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}. Let T be any exact two-cover of W such that every x in X is internal in T and no selected T-edge has both ends in X; equivalently e_T(X)=0 and delta_T(X)=0.

Then H necessarily has source-labelled GEOM: either a labelled tight Hamilton P4 arising from the crossed/four-cell packet, or an accepted R435 Reverse-Ear output (a reversed old Q-state, tight reverse trimer, or vertex-simple tight cycle) whose Q coordinates are retained.

More precisely, outside an immediate R885/R898 GEOM output, T is the fully fragmented MAX5 face. There exist selected X-Y incidences x_3 y_3 and x_4 y_4 with x_3 in G_3, x_4 in G_4, y_3,y_4 outside {L,R}, such that the associated R898 tested orientations make y_3 a head witness on G_3 and y_4 a tail witness on G_4; at least one of y_3,y_4 lies in the genuine middle Q-cell G_1=Q[3,m-3]. Call such a deep witness y=q_i and its tested gate orientation S.

Apply accepted R682 to S,y in any exact H-V(S) two-cover. In its XET branch the cover contains (R,y,L) or (L,y,R). The first is immediately R435-active against Q. For (L,y,R), probe by q_{i-1} with R518. Either a labelled P4 occurs, or the no-P4 signature forces (y,L,q_{i-1}) tight, whose consecutive Q contacts y=q_i then L=q_0 occur in strict reverse Q-order; R435 gives GEOM.

In the CAP branch R682 supplies a physical endpoint e in {L,R}, an actual selected crossing D_e={e,t} avoiding y, and a lawful R527 capture of e using y. Open the retained R526 actualization certificate. Because the marker is the dimer {e,t} oriented to capture e with witness y, exactly one of the complete reversals (t,e,y) and (y,e,t) is the certified tight terminal actualization turn. If e=L and (y,L,t) is tight, its consecutive Q contacts y then L are already in reverse order, so R435 fires. If instead (t,L,y) is tight, choose z from {q_{i-1},q_{i+1}} distinct from t; such z exists because 3<=i<=m-3. Apply R518 to (t,L,y) with fourth vertex z. Either a labelled P4 occurs, or R518 forces (z,y,L) tight, whose consecutive contacts y then L are reverse-Q, so R435 fires. The e=R case is dual: (t,R,y) is immediately reverse-Q, while from (y,R,t), probing by a neighbor z of y distinct from t yields in the no-P4 branch (R,y,z), whose contacts R then y are reverse-Q. Thus CAP also gives GEOM.

Therefore the fully internal separated/MAX5 face has no protected-contact, payment, or fixed-turn residue: the guaranteed genuinely deep gate witness is consumed locally into a labelled P4 or explicit Reverse-Ear/reversal/cycle geometry. No selected state of T is transported into a gate-deletion cover; only the graph-intrinsic R898 witness turn is reused.

## Proof
Fix the accepted crossed inward-anchor frame and an exact two-cover T of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} satisfying e_T(X)=0 and delta_T(X)=0.

1. MAX5 outside existing geometry.
Since delta_T(X)=0, each x in X has selected degree two. Since e_T(X)=0, all its selected neighbours lie in Y, so q=e_T(X,Y)=8. Neither physical middle-gate pair is selected, hence f(T)=2. Apply accepted R885. Its P4 or Reverse-Ear branch is GEOM. Otherwise T is BOUNDED, and accepted R894 identifies p=0,q=8 as MAX5. Apply accepted R898. Its P4-END branch is GEOM. Otherwise retain FOUR-PACKETS: every selected neighbour y of a certifies (y,a,c), every neighbour of c certifies (y,c,a), every neighbour of b certifies (z,b,y), and every neighbour of z certifies (b,z,y).

2. One inward witness on each physical gate.
If both a and c had only L,R as selected neighbours, degree two would force N_T(a)=N_T(c)={L,R}, giving the selected 4-cycle a-L-c-R-a, impossible in a path forest. Choose x_3 in {a,c} with y_3 in N_T(x_3) outside {L,R}; R898 makes y_3 a head witness on S_3=(a,c) or (c,a) according to x_3. The identical argument on {b,z} gives x_4,y_4 outside {L,R}, with y_4 a tail witness on S_4=(z,b) or (b,z).

3. At least one witness is genuinely deep.
Let B={L,u,s,R}. If all eight selected X-Y incidences ended in B, their degree contribution to B would be eight. Each B-vertex has selected degree at most two, so all four would have degree exactly two, both neighbours in X. The four X vertices already have degree two into B. Hence the selected subgraph on X union B would be 2-regular and contain a cycle, impossible. Thus some selected X-Y incidence ends in Y-B=G_1=Q[3,m-3]. Choose the witness on that physical gate to be this incidence. Write it y=q_i, 3<=i<=m-3, and let S be the corresponding tested orientation of G_3 or G_4.

4. Apply the deep-witness endpoint dichotomy.
Apply accepted R682 to S,y in any exact two-cover T' of H-V(S). No selected state of the original T is used in T'; only the graph-intrinsic witness turn on S is reused.

4A. XET.
Suppose R682 gives XET. Then T' contains a tight cross-end trimer K=(R,y,L) or K=(L,y,R). If K=(R,y,L), the consecutive Q contacts R=q_m then y=q_i occur in reverse Q-order m>i, so accepted R435 gives a reversed old state, tight reverse trimer, or vertex-simple tight cycle.

It remains K=(L,y,R). Apply accepted R518 to K with fourth vertex q_{i-1}, distinct from K because i>=3. If the four-set supports a tight Hamilton P4, GEOM holds. Otherwise the no-P4 branch of R518 forces the reverse contact trimer (y,L,q_{i-1}) tight. Along that trimer the consecutive Q contacts y=q_i then L=q_0 occur in strict reverse Q-order and are nonadjacent. R435 therefore gives GEOM.

4B. CAP.
Suppose R682 gives CAP. Then for some e in {L,R}, T' selects a physical carrier/exterior crossing D_e={e,t} with t distinct from y, and R527 may be oriented to capture e using the exact sign witness y. Retain the R526 actualization certificate inside this capture. Because the marker is the dimer {e,t} oriented so that e is the actualization endpoint, the terminal marker test and its R3 reversal are the complete reversal pair (t,e,y) and (y,e,t); exactly one is tight. Call this certified tight trimer J_e.

First let e=L. If J_e=(y,L,t), its consecutive Q contacts y=q_i then L=q_0 are already in reverse order, so R435 gives GEOM. Otherwise J_e=(t,L,y). The two old-Q neighbours q_{i-1},q_{i+1} both exist because 3<=i<=m-3, and at most one equals t. Choose z in {q_{i-1},q_{i+1}} with z!=t. Apply R518 to the tight trimer (t,L,y) with fourth vertex z. If a Hamilton P4 occurs, GEOM holds. Otherwise the no-P4 signature forces the reverse contact (z,y,L) tight. Its consecutive Q contacts y=q_i then L=q_0 occur in strict reverse Q-order, so R435 gives GEOM.

Now let e=R. If J_e=(t,R,y), its consecutive Q contacts R=q_m then y=q_i are reverse-Q, so R435 gives GEOM. Otherwise J_e=(y,R,t). Choose z in {q_{i-1},q_{i+1}} with z!=t. R518 on (y,R,t) with fourth vertex z gives either a Hamilton P4 or, in the no-P4 branch, the reverse contact (R,y,z) tight. The consecutive Q contacts R=q_m then y=q_i are reverse-Q, so R435 gives GEOM.

Thus both R682 branches are completely consumed into labelled P4 or explicit R435 geometry. Therefore every fully internal separated/MAX5 recompletion lies in GEOM. No protected-contact, payment, or fixed-turn residue remains. This proves the theorem.
