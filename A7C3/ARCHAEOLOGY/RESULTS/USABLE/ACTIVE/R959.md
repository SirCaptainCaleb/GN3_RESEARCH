# R959 — A minimum counterexample to R888 has exact singleton-deletion two-covers with rail order at least three

## Statement
Let G be a finite edge-ordered complete graph that is a counterexample to R888 of minimum vertex order. Then pc_inc(G)=3. For every vertex z, pc_inc(G-z)=2, and in every exact two-path cover A|B of G-z both rails have at least three vertices. Hence any choice of one exact two-cover for each singleton deletion satisfies the hypotheses of the accepted singleton-cover compatibility theorem R926.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order counterexample to the conjecture R888 that every edge-ordered complete graph has increasing path-cover number at most two.

## Scope
Pure edge-ordered minimal-counterexample reduction.

## Applicability
Use as the universal entrance for singleton absorption, deletion-fiber compatibility, or threshold exchange arguments for R888.

## Persistence
Retain minimum-order choice, exact two-cover of each G-z, and the singleton/dimer rail absorption arguments.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prove R888 or any stronger rail floor. Does not assert a chosen deletion cover has unique supports or synchronized orders.

## Proof
Fix z in V(G). By minimum order, G-z satisfies R888, so pc_inc(G-z)<=2. If pc_inc(G-z)=1, let P be a Hamilton increasing path of G-z. Then P together with the singleton {z} is a spanning two-path cover of G, contradicting that G is a counterexample. Hence pc_inc(G-z)=2 for every z. Adding z as a singleton to any exact two-cover of G-z gives a spanning three-path cover of G, so pc_inc(G)<=3. Since G is a counterexample to pc_inc<=2, pc_inc(G)=3. Now let A|B be any exact two-path cover of G-z. If A is a singleton {a}, then B together with the dimer (z,a) is a spanning two-cover of G, contradiction. If A is a dimer on vertices a,b, consider the three ordinary edge labels on {z,a,b}. Let e_min and e_max be the smallest and largest of those three edges. In a triangle any two distinct edges share a common vertex; write e_min=uv and e_max=vw at their common endpoint v. Then (u,v,w) is an increasing Hamilton path of the three-set because lambda(uv)<lambda(vw). Replacing the dimer A and singleton z by this trimer, while retaining B, gives a spanning two-cover of G, contradiction. Thus |A|>=3, and symmetrically |B|>=3. The last statement follows because accepted R926 only requires chosen exact singleton-deletion two-covers with both rails of size at least two.
