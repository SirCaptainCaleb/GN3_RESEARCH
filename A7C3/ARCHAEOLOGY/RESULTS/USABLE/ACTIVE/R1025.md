# R1025 — Every R24 failure is absorbed into current maximum-three-forest dynamics

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and suppose the operative R24 singleton rail-order-four floor fails. Then there is a finite certificate-retaining continuation yielding either a spanning two-cover of H or an actual literal minimum spanning three-cover carrying explicit ancestry from the failed singleton-deletion certificate. More precisely, accepted R881 reduces the failure to the synchronized transitive order-three frame X sqcup Q with m>=6 and the fixed order-eight core G_8 plus Hamilton middle rail N. Accepted R619 gives the exhaustive ALIGNED/CROSSED phase split. In ALIGNED, R1024 exits to closure or current maximum-three-forest dynamics through the R899 common-residue cross-state and paid-floor reentry. In CROSSED, choose the existing deep gap j=3 and apply R1023, which exits to closure or current maximum-three-forest dynamics through a seam trimer, inward-gate witness, or labelled R874 P4. Therefore the unresolved long R24 core is not an independent terminal branch: any putative R24 failure feeds the global current maximum-three-forest program with its physical source ancestry retained.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; the operative R24 floor fails. Use accepted R881,R619 and pending phase-export theorems R1023,R1024 once independently reviewed.

## Scope
Parent routing theorem. It does not prove R24 and does not extinguish maximum-three-forest holonomy/recompletion. Its content is architectural but mathematical: the entire noncircular R24 failure normal form has a finite certificate-preserving map into the current global forest state space.

## Applicability
Use instead of maintaining R24/O6 as a standalone long-core research program. After the clean R881 normalization, dispatch ALIGNED to R1024 and CROSSED to R1023. Downstream work may then consume the resulting literal current forest with retained R24 source coordinates.

## Persistence
Retain the original failed singleton-deletion cover, normalized X,Q labels, G_8|N decomposition, gate phase, and the complete ancestry of the phase-specific export: R899 cross state and paid floor in ALIGNED; chosen deep gap, R820 disjunct, inward witness/P4 or paid floor in CROSSED; and the literal output forest.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert R24 is true, that the output forest has lower largest-rail height, or that its ancestry immediately forces closure. Does not identify forests from different gap choices. R1023 and R1024 are pending and must be accepted before this theorem is usable.

## Proof
Assume the operative R24 singleton rail-order-four floor fails in the hypothetical smallest counterexample H. Thus some singleton deletion H-z has an exact two-cover with a rail of order at most three.\n\nApply accepted R881. It is entirely R24-free as a reduction theorem and gives the unique surviving normalized failure geometry: the short rail has order three; X is the synchronized transitive matching-height four-cell; Q=(q_0,...,q_m) with m>=6; and, writing L=q_0,u=q_1,s=q_{m-1},R=q_m and N=Q[2,m-2], the fixed core G_8=H[X union {L,u,s,R}] has order eight and pc(G_8)=2, while H=G_8 disjoint-union N and N is a nonempty Hamilton path. Retain the original failed singleton-deletion certificate and all normalized physical labels.\n\nAccepted R619 gives the exhaustive middle-gate phase split of this same normalized frame. We distinguish its two cases.\n\nALIGNED. Apply R1024. Its input is exactly the accepted aligned long frame with m>=6. Once R1024 is independently accepted, its finite certificate-retaining continuation yields either a spanning two-cover of H or an actual literal minimum spanning three-cover. The latter retains the R899 common-residue pair-deletion covers, their selected cross state, the paid-floor ancestry, and the output trimer/current forest.\n\nCROSSED. Since m>=6, the deep-gap range 3<=j<=m-3 is nonempty. Choose j=3. Apply R1023 to this physical gap. Once R1023 is independently accepted, its finite continuation yields either a spanning two-cover or an actual literal minimum spanning three-cover. Its ancestry records which R820 species occurred: a seam trimer, an inward gate witness and its R682/payment lineage, or the paired-terminal R874 labelled P4, together with the final current forest.\n\nThe two phases are exhaustive, so every normalized R24 failure has one of these two finite exits. Therefore every operative R24 failure yields a spanning two-cover or enters the actual current maximum-three-forest state space with a complete certificate chain back to the failed singleton-deletion cover.\n\nBecause H is assumed to be a counterexample, the spanning-two-cover outcome is impossible in the standing hypothetical setting; thus a putative R24 failure necessarily supplies the current-forest output. This last observation does not make R24 true: it only shows that the surviving R24 obstruction has been absorbed into the global maximum-three-forest problem rather than remaining a separate long-core branch. No claim of forest-holonomy extinction or strict rank descent is made.
