# R721 — The inward R704 P4 still does not force a Hamilton eight-vertex prefix

## Statement
There exists a Strong Level-(1) orientation F on V=X union {L,u,v,w}, X={a,b,c,z}, satisfying simultaneously: (1) X is the transitive matching-height four-cell M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}; (2) (L,u,v,w) is tight; (3) every endpoint-star turn (u,L,x), x in X, is tight; (4) the full left crossed/high-side gate packet Lbc,Lcb,Lac,Lca,bzL,zbL,czL,zcL is tight; (5) the R658 left rectangle turns Lub,Luz,wvb,wvz are tight; and (6) the explicit R704 labelled Hamilton P4 (b,w,v,z) is tight. Nevertheless F has no Hamilton tight P8. Consequently the accepted R704 inward P4, even together with all of these one-sided crossed/high-side prefix coordinates, does not force X union Q[0,3] to be Hamiltonian. In particular it cannot by itself supply the missing one-rail complementary absorber that would strengthen the natural three-class R567 puncture count from one transition to two. Any such amplification must use a genuinely nonlocal/right-side coordinate or additional cross-frame information.

## Hypotheses
Only Strong Level-(1) complete-reversal antisymmetry. The theorem is witnessed by the explicit eight-vertex orientation certificate in the proof.

## Scope
Finite local R24 strategy fence strengthening the one-sided limitation behind R626 by adding the explicit R704 P4. No smallest-counterexample, deletion-cover, payment, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533, generic R358, or R436.

## Applicability
Do not try to consume the R704 left P4 merely by proving the eight-vertex prefix X union Q[0,3] Hamiltonian from the one-sided crossed/high-side packet. A successful R567 or splice consumer must import opposite-end or other nonlocal data.

## Persistence
Retain the eight labels L,u,v,w,a,b,c,z, all displayed required turns, the complete orientation code table, the explicit P4 (b,w,v,z), and the verified zero Hamilton-P8 count.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not show the full global crossed R24 frame is realizable. Does not refute consumers using right-end data, both R704 P4s, the R621 opposite-end weaves, or additional exact-cover coordinates. It only refutes the stated local implication.
