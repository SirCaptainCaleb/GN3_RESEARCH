## Statement
Retain the G36 source-gate wall packet of SV115482. In either orientation, the source-dimer COLLISION arm already contains a literal source-visible tight P4. Consequently the collision arm is not an independent obstruction and requires no generic R523 payment, replay, fresh witness reservoir, R24, or R5.

More precisely:

- **OUT.** The collision carrier is `K=(A,t,b)`, whose reverse terminal dimer is `D_R=(b,t)`. Choose `z` among the other two source spokes with `z != b`. Testing `(C,b,t)` and `(z,b,t)`, any bad test reverses by R3 and concatenates with `(A,t,b)` to a literal P4. If both tests are tight, `C,z` are two distinct exterior head witnesses on `D_R`, so accepted exact unit SV78488 gives a literal P4.
- **IN.** The collision carrier is `K=(b,t,C)`, whose reverse initial dimer is `D_L=(t,b)`. Choose `z` among the other two source spokes with `z != b`. Testing `(t,b,A)` and `(t,b,z)`, any bad test reverses by R3 and concatenates with `(b,t,C)` to a literal P4. If both are tight, `A,z` are two distinct exterior tail witnesses on `D_L`, so the reverse-initial case of SV78488 gives a literal P4.

Thus every persistent far wall reaching a source gate yields graph-intrinsic source-visible P4 geometry in both the original P4 arm and the collision arm of SV115482.

## Proof
Retain anchors `A,C`, source spoke `t`, the other source spokes `x,y`, and the historical tight turns

`(A,t,C), (A,x,C), (A,y,C)`.

### OUT collision
SV115482 retains `(d,b,t)` and `(A,t,b)` tight. The latter is the carrier `K=(A,t,b)` and its reverse terminal dimer is `(b,t)`. Since `b` is distinct from `A,C`, choose `z in {x,y}` with `z != b`; then `C,z` are distinct vertices outside `K`.

Test `(C,b,t)`. If it is bad, R3 gives `(t,b,C)` tight, and `(A,t,b)` followed by `(t,b,C)` is the literal P4 `(A,t,b,C)`. Otherwise `C` head-signs `(b,t)`.

Now test `(z,b,t)`. If it is bad, R3 gives `(t,b,z)` tight, producing the literal P4 `(A,t,b,z)`. If it is tight, `C,z` are two distinct exterior head witnesses on the reverse terminal dimer `(b,t)` of `K`; accepted exact unit SV78488 therefore gives a literal vertex-simple tight P4.

Hence every OUT source-dimer collision already contains a source-visible P4.

### IN collision
SV115482 retains `(t,b,d)` and `(b,t,C)` tight. Now `K=(b,t,C)` is a tight trimer with reverse initial dimer `(t,b)`. Again choose `z in {x,y}` with `z != b`, so `A,z` are distinct vertices outside `K`.

Test `(t,b,A)`. If it is bad, R3 gives `(A,b,t)` tight, and together with `(b,t,C)` this gives the literal P4 `(A,b,t,C)`. Otherwise `A` tail-signs `(t,b)`.

Test `(t,b,z)`. If it is bad, R3 gives `(z,b,t)` tight, giving the literal P4 `(z,b,t,C)`. If it is tight, `A,z` are two distinct exterior tail witnesses on the reverse initial dimer `(t,b)` of `K`; the reverse-initial case of SV78488 therefore gives a literal P4.

Hence every IN source-dimer collision also contains a source-visible P4.
