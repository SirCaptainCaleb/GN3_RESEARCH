# R369 — Spectral degree restriction for diameter-two girth-five Moore graphs

## Statement
Let G be a finite simple d-regular graph, d>=2, with girth at least five and exact Moore order |V(G)|=d^2+1, equivalently diameter two with every nonadjacent pair having exactly one common neighbor. Then d belongs to {2,3,7,57}.

## Hypotheses
G is d-regular, d>=2, triangle-free, C4-free, and has d^2+1 vertices with exact radius-two Moore geometry.

## Scope
Pure finite graph spectral theorem.

## Applicability
Use whenever equality in a girth-five Moore bound produces a regular diameter-two graph. It gives an immediate arithmetic obstruction on the degree before any graph-specific classification.

## Persistence
Graph-intrinsic adjacency spectrum.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert existence for d=57 and does not classify the graphs for the allowed degrees.

## Proof
Exact Moore geometry gives A^2=(d-1)I-A+J. Besides the trivial eigenvalue d, every eigenvalue satisfies t^2+t-(d-1)=0, so the two roots are (-1±Delta)/2 with Delta=sqrt(4d-3). Their multiplicities are m_±=d^2/2 ± d(d-2)/(2Delta). For d=2 this gives the allowed exceptional degree 2. For d>=3, the multiplicities are rational integers, so Delta must be rational and hence an odd integer q. Then q^2=4d-3 and d=(q^2+3)/4. Integrality of the multiplicities gives q | d(d-2). Modulo q, 4d≡3 and 4(d-2)≡-5, so gcd(q,d) divides 3 and gcd(q,d-2) divides 5. Hence q divides 15. With q odd positive and d>=3, q∈{3,5,15}, giving d∈{3,7,57}. Together with d=2 this yields {2,3,7,57}.
