# R1037 — A v-rooted sharp source cover has a singleton v block and a two-ended three-spoke source block

**Audit state:** PASS
**Proof provenance:** Slack `#a7c3-workspace` root `1789312204.974029`; repaired directly from the sharp source-cover hypotheses rather than the missing historical D17.418/SV provenance.

## Statement
Let `F` be the retained exact two-path source cover of

`G = B disjoint-union X`, `X={v,p,q,r}`,

with `p,q,r` internal vertices of `F` and `tau(F)=3`. Assume the v-rooted residual cell, so the selected F-edge incident with `v` at the first-loss root is an `X|B` transition.

Then

`d_F(v)=1`, `e_XX(F)=2`, `b_X(F)=2`, `b_B(F)=3`.

Moreover the two maximal X-blocks of `F` are exactly:

- the singleton endpoint block `{v}`;
- one internal block containing all three source spokes.

Thus, up to complete reversal, the source block occurs literally as

`B_L -> s_1 -> s_2 -> s_3 -> B_R`,

where `{s_1,s_2,s_3}={p,q,r}`. Its two boundary `X|B` edges are opposite-polarity source gates, and both are X-mated by the internal edges `s_1s_2` and `s_2s_3`. The third and only non-source transition of `F` is the unique `v-B` edge.

## Proof
Because `p,q,r` are internal in the two-path cover, their selected degrees are all two. Let `d=d_F(v)` and let `e_XX` be the number of selected F-edges with both ends in X. Counting selected incidences at X gives

`6+d = 2e_XX + tau(F) = 2e_XX+3`.

The v-rooted hypothesis supplies a selected edge at `v`, so `d` is either one or two. The displayed equation makes `d` odd. Hence

`d_F(v)=1`,

and therefore `e_XX=2`.

Every maximal X-block with `m` vertices contributes exactly `m-1` selected X-X edges, so across the four X-vertices

`e_XX = |X|-b_X = 4-b_X`.

Thus `b_X=2`.

Across two nonempty directed paths, the total number of maximal monochromatic X/B blocks equals the number of X|B transitions plus the number of path components. Hence

`b_X+b_B = tau(F)+2 = 5`,

so `b_B=3`.

Now the unique selected edge incident with `v` is the displayed X|B transition. Therefore `v` cannot be adjacent in F to another X-vertex, and its maximal X-block is exactly `{v}`. Since there are exactly two X-blocks, the other one contains all of `p,q,r`.

All three source spokes are internal in F. Consequently the three-spoke X-block is itself internal on its rail: its two endpoint spokes each have one selected X-X incidence inside the block and one selected X|B incidence outside it. Writing the literal directed order as `s_1,s_2,s_3`, the rail segment is, up to reversal,

`B_L -> s_1 -> s_2 -> s_3 -> B_R`.

The two boundary transitions therefore have opposite path polarity, and each endpoint source gate is X-mated by its adjacent internal X-X edge. These two gates plus the unique v-B edge account for all three transitions because `tau(F)=3`.

No historical D17.418, SV101138, SV109219, R24, or R5 input is required.
