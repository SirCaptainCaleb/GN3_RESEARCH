# R582 — Two-ended middle gates force an endpoint-favorable five-path

## Statement
Let X={a,b,c,z} be a transitive matching-height four-cell with M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, and let L,R be distinct exterior vertices. Suppose at L one M_S edge is fully outgoing and its complementary M_S edge fully incoming, while at R one M_S edge is fully incoming and its complement fully outgoing. After an automorphism of the labelled matching-height cell, assume the L-outgoing M_S edge is {b,z}, so Lbz,Lzb,acL,caL are tight. The R-incoming M_S edge is then either {b,z} (aligned) or {a,c} (crossed). For x in X call a Hamilton tight P5 K_x on {L,R} union (X-{x}) endpoint-favorable if the position of L in K_x is not 1 or 2, or the position of R is not 2 or 3, with positions numbered 0,...,4. Then some x in X has an endpoint-favorable K_x. Equivalently, it is impossible for every Hamilton P5 on every {L,R} union (X-{x}) to place L only in positions 1,2 and R only in positions 2,3.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) exact reversal antisymmetry; X is the displayed transitive matching-height four-cell; the two exterior vertices carry the displayed full M_S gate polarities.

## Scope
Pure six-vertex gate lemma; no smallest-counterexample, deletion-cover, global-longest, R24, or computation hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use with L=q_0,R=q_m in the transitive R24 frame. Combined with R540, endpoint-favorability makes one endpoint-cut spanning proposal have exactly one uncertified turn.

## Persistence
Retain the physical six-set, aligned/crossed gate type, the explicit favorable Hamilton P5, omitted x, and its L/R positions.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say which x is favorable. Does not say the resulting R540 one-hole mate is new information or closes H. Does not assert a Hamilton P6 on the six-set.

## Proof
Relabel within the matching-height cell so the L-outgoing M_S edge is {b,z}. Thus Lbz,Lzb,acL,caL are tight. The matching-height orientation of X supplies, among others, zbc,caz,zca,acb,bac,baz tight. Assume for contradiction that no endpoint-favorable K_x exists. Then every displayed candidate below, each of which uses {L,R} union (X-{x}) for the indicated omitted x and has L or R in a favorable position, must fail at a turn not already certified. Boundary antisymmetry R3 then supplies the complete reverse of that bad turn.

CROSSED CASE. Here the R-incoming M_S edge is {a,c}, so acR,caR,Rbz,Rzb are tight.
1. Candidate (L,R,z,b,c), omitting a, has Rzb and zbc tight. Since L is in position 0 it is favorable; therefore LRz is bad and zRL is tight.
2. Candidate (R,z,c,a,L), omitting b, has zca and caL tight. Since R is in position 0, Rzc is bad and czR is tight.
3. Candidate (c,z,R,L,a), omitting b, now has czR and zRL tight. Since L is in position 3, RLa is bad and aLR is tight.
4. But (z,c,a,L,R), omitting b, has zca, caL, aLR tight. It is endpoint-favorable (L position 3, R position 4), contradiction.

ALIGNED CASE. Here the R-incoming M_S edge is also {b,z}, so bzR,zbR,Rac,Rca are tight.
1. (L,b,z,R,c), omit a: Lbz,bzR tight, so zRc is bad and cRz tight.
2. (c,L,b,z,R), omit a: Lbz,bzR tight, so cLb is bad and bLc tight.
3. (L,R,c,a,z), omit b: Rca,caz tight, so LRc is bad and cRL tight.
4. (z,c,a,L,R), omit b: zca,caL tight, so aLR is bad and RLa tight.
5. (L,R,a,c,b), omit z: Rac,acb tight, so LRa is bad and aRL tight.
6. (b,a,c,L,R), omit z: bac,acL tight, so cLR is bad and RLc tight.
7. (c,R,L,a,z), omit b: cRL,RLa tight, so Laz is bad and zaL tight.
8. (c,R,z,a,L), omit b: cRz,zaL tight, so Rza is bad and azR tight.
9. (L,b,a,z,R), omit c: baz,azR tight, so Lba is bad and abL tight.
10. (R,a,b,L,c), omit z: abL,bLc tight, so Rab is bad and baR tight.
11. Finally (b,a,R,L,c), omitting z, has baR,aRL,RLc all tight. Since L is in position 3, this is an endpoint-favorable Hamilton P5, contradiction.

The two cases exhaust the two M_S edges available for the R-incoming gate. Thus some endpoint-favorable K_x exists. The proof is a finite explicit forcing chain and uses no computational assertion.
