# R953 — Critical equal recurrence yields a common-core saturated two-ear fork

## Statement
Retain every conclusion and hypothesis of accepted R948 in the critical equal-recurrence case with R isolated: the actual source order A=L-z-R, equal petals |B|=|Z|=k<a with Z={z} union R, mixed Hamilton support M=L union B, the two literal exact H-p covers (L union Z)|B and (L union B)|Z, and the saturated non-Hamiltonian deletion-Hamiltonian universe Omega=Z union {p}. The L-isolated case is dual.

ARBITRARY-DELETION UNIQUE-TRANSITION PACKET. Since Omega-z=R union {p} is Hamiltonian, choose an actual Hamilton path K on R+p. Deleting the entire old petal B gives the literal exact two-cover of H-B
  (L followed by z) | K.
With deletion set D=B, absorbable block S=L, complement C=Omega, and absorber Q=M on B union L, this is an actual R508 packet with exactly ONE selected S|C transition, namely the literal old-source boundary from the final vertex of L to z. Thus the critical two-ear fork itself realizes tau=1 for an arbitrary non-singleton deletion, without assuming transition minimization. All accepted R511/R512/R513 consumers may be invoked only with the resulting actual orders and their complete seam windows.

COMMON-CORE DELETION FORCES DIRECT CROSS-PETAL CONTACT. The proper residue H-L has path-cover number exactly two. For EVERY exact two-cover T of H-L, applying R508 to absorber M=L union B forces a B|(Z+p) selected adjacency, while applying R508 to absorber A=L union Z forces a Z|(B+p) selected adjacency in the SAME T. In fact T must contain a direct physical B-Z selected adjacency. If no B-Z state were selected, the two forced crossings would be B-p and Z-p, making p internal with exactly two interclass incidences. The block identity then gives b_B+b_Z=3, so one of B or Z is a single whole maximal block adjacent to p; that would Hamiltonize B+p or Z+p, both non-Hamiltonian. Contradiction.

CROSS-PETAL SPLIT. If B union Z is non-Hamiltonian, then each of B+Z, B+p, Z+p has path-cover number exactly two, every exact H-L two-cover has connected interaction on classes {B,Z,{p}}, and every such cover is either a rainbow hinge or a split-star weave by the R942 block-word argument. In a split-star the singleton {p} cannot be the center, so the center is B or Z. Every exact cover still contains a current B-Z state.

If B union Z is Hamiltonian, retain an actual Hamilton path P_BZ. Then P_BZ|L is an exact H-p source. Since |L|=a-k<a, global minimality makes every L-side vertex quiet; therefore Gamma=L union {p} is non-Hamiltonian and deletion-Hamiltonian and (B union Z)+y is non-Hamiltonian for every y in Gamma. Running the same quiet PORT-CROSS / COMPLETE-EXCHANGE saturation used in R931 yields exactly: a spanning two-cover, the uniform middle-layer residue, or a universal recurrence only on the B union Z side, of size 2k. Hence 2k<a forbids recurrence; 2k=a is the same-size fixed case; 2k>a is a larger recurrence with a strictly smaller saturated opposite rail L of size a-k<k.

THREE-PETAL FIXED POINT. In the same-size subcase 2k=a, if the incoming critical source was already normalized so that B+p is deletion-Hamiltonian, then |L|=|B|=|Z|=k and all three blocks L+p, B+p, Z+p are non-Hamiltonian deletion-Hamiltonian, meeting pairwise exactly in p. Simultaneously all three pair unions L+B, L+Z, B+Z are Hamiltonian. This three-petal saturated fixed point is the only same-size p-isolated recurrence residue asserted here.

## Proof
Retain all accepted R948 data, with R isolated. Thus A=L-z-R is the actual old source order, Z={z} union R, M=L union B is an actual Hamilton support, |B|=|Z|=k<a, and H-p has the two literal exact covers
  (L union Z) | B,
  (L union B) | Z.
Also Omega=Z union {p} is non-Hamiltonian and Omega-y is Hamiltonian for every y in Omega. The old source universe B union {p} is non-Hamiltonian as well: if it were Hamiltonian, it would be disjoint from the old Hamilton rail A=L union Z and those two paths would span H.

1. Arbitrary-deletion tau=1 packet. Since z belongs to Omega and Omega-z=R union {p} is Hamiltonian, choose an actual Hamilton path K on R+p. Delete the whole old petal B. The vertices of H-B are L, z, R, p. The literal old-source prefix L followed by z is tight, and K spans R+p, so
  T_B=(L followed by z) | K
is a literal two-cover of H-B. It is exact: if H-B were Hamiltonian, that Hamilton path together with the old Hamilton path B would two-cover H.

Put D=B and S=L. In H-D the complementary remainder is C=Omega=Z+p. The actual path Q=M spans exactly D union S=B union L. Hence accepted R508 applies. In the displayed cover T_B the L-block is followed by z, giving exactly one selected S|C adjacency, namely the old source boundary ell-z where ell is the final vertex of L. The other rail K lies wholly in C. Thus tau(T_B)=1. This establishes the claimed legal arbitrary-deletion unique-transition packet. Any later R511/R513 use must retain these actual path orders and seam coordinates.

2. Delete the common core. Since L is a nonempty tight path, H-L cannot be Hamiltonian: otherwise L together with a Hamilton path on H-L would two-cover H. Smallest-counterexample minimality gives pc(H-L)<=2, so pc(H-L)=2.

Let T be any exact two-cover of H-L. Apply R508 first with deletion set D=L, absorbable block S=B, and absorber Q=M=L union B. The complementary remainder is Z+p, so T selects a B|(Z+p) adjacency. Apply R508 again to the SAME T with D=L, S=Z, and absorber Q=A=L union Z. The complementary remainder is B+p, so T selects a Z|(B+p) adjacency.

We prove T has a direct B-Z selected adjacency. Suppose not. Then the first forced crossing must be B-p and the second Z-p. Since p is one vertex in a two-path cover and is incident to both required interclass states, it has selected degree two and is internal on one T-rail, with one B-neighbor and one Z-neighbor. There are no other interclass transitions because B-Z is absent and p has no further selected incidence. Let b_B,b_Z be the numbers of maximal B- and Z-blocks. Contracting maximal class blocks in the two T-rails gives a two-component forest with b_B+b_Z+1 vertices and exactly two interclass edges. Hence
  b_B+b_Z+1=2+2,
so b_B+b_Z=3. One of B,Z therefore occurs as exactly one whole maximal block. If Z is whole, its required p-adjacency makes p followed by that actual Z-block, or the reverse, a tight Hamilton path on Z+p=Omega, contradicting non-Hamiltonicity of Omega. If B is whole, its p-adjacency Hamiltonizes B+p, contradicting the old source-universe non-Hamiltonicity proved above. Thus every exact T has a direct physical B-Z selected state.

3. Non-Hamiltonian B+Z branch. Suppose B union Z is non-Hamiltonian. Each pair union B+Z, B+p, Z+p is nevertheless coverable by two tight paths, using its two displayed source classes. Hence each has path-cover number exactly two. If an exact H-L cover had disconnected interaction on the three classes B,Z,{p}, one class would be isolated and the union of the other two classes would be Hamiltonian, exactly as in the elementary isolation argument of R942. This contradicts pairwise non-Hamiltonicity. Thus every exact cover has connected interaction.

The R942 block-word proof is purely combinatorial once three nonempty Hamilton classes and a connected two-rail interaction are supplied. Applying it to B,Z,{p}, every exact H-L cover is a rainbow hinge or split-star weave. In the split-star alternative the center class is split nontrivially between both rails; the singleton {p} cannot be split, so the center is B or Z. The direct B-Z state proved in step 2 remains current in every representative. No absorption is claimed.

4. Hamiltonian B+Z branch. Suppose instead B union Z is Hamiltonian and fix an actual Hamilton path P_BZ. Then
  P_BZ | L
is an exact H-p source. Write l=|L|=a-k<a. A universal crossing on the literal L rail of this source would have offending rail size l<a, contradicting global minimality of a. Hence every y in L is quiet on the L-side.

We verify the resulting saturation rather than transport it. Put Gamma=L union {p}. For y in L choose an exact H-y two-cover with no selected adjacency between L-y and B+Z. The source classes are L and B+Z and p is the only exchanged label. The standard seam-free quiet substitution used in the accepted R931 proof gives L-y+p Hamiltonian unless B+Z+p were Hamiltonian. The latter would be disjoint from the old Hamilton source rail L and would two-cover H, so it is impossible. Hence Gamma-y=L-y+p is Hamiltonian for every y in L; Gamma-p=L is already Hamiltonian. Thus Gamma is deletion-Hamiltonian. Gamma itself is non-Hamiltonian, since Gamma together with the disjoint Hamilton support B+Z would otherwise two-cover H. For every y in Gamma, (B+Z)+y is non-Hamiltonian because its complementary support Gamma-y is Hamiltonian.

We are therefore in exactly the saturated-port situation used in accepted R931/P1003, with small rail size l<a and fixed complementary rail size 2k. For each y in Gamma the source (Gamma-y)|(B+Z) is actual. If for some m in B+Z the replacement (B+Z)-m+y is non-Hamiltonian, that m is a universal crossing on the 2k-side, giving the stated recurrence. If every such replacement is Hamiltonian, the complete-exchange/quiet two-pivot argument of R931 saturates all l|2k support partitions unless a universal crossing occurs. A crossing on the l-side is impossible by l<a; hence the only recurrence size is 2k. If no recurrence occurs, unequal sizes close H by the R931 contiguous-subpath argument, and equal sizes give the uniform middle-layer residue. Thus the exact alternatives are spanning two-cover, uniform middle layer, or a universal recurrence only on the 2k-side.

If 2k<a, the recurrence is forbidden by the definition of a. If 2k=a, it is same-size. If 2k>a, then l=a-k<k, so the recurring source comes with a strictly smaller saturated opposite rail; no global monotonicity beyond this numerical fact is asserted.

5. Three-petal fixed point. Assume 2k=a and the incoming critical source was already normalized so that B+p is deletion-Hamiltonian. Then l=a-k=k. The new Gamma=L+p is deletion-Hamiltonian by step 4 and Omega=Z+p is deletion-Hamiltonian by R948. All three are non-Hamiltonian source universes. Their pair unions are Hamiltonian: L+Z=A is the old source rail, L+B=M is the mixed ear, and B+Z is Hamiltonian by the present branch assumption. Therefore L+p, B+p, Z+p are three equal saturated (k+1)-vertex blocks meeting pairwise exactly in p, with all three pairwise petal unions Hamiltonian. This is the claimed fixed point.

The L-isolated case is obtained by exact left-right duality. This proves R953.
