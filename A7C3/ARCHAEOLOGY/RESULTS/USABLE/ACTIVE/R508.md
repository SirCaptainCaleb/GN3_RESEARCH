# R508 — Absorbable deletion blocks force recompletion crossings

## Statement
Let H be a finite boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let D be a nonempty proper vertex set, put W=V(H)\D, and let S be a nonempty proper subset of W. Suppose Q is a vertex-simple tight path with vertex set exactly D union S. Then every literal spanning two-path cover T=T_1 sqcup T_2 of H-D selects a directed state xy with exactly one endpoint in S and the other in W\S. Equivalently, an exact two-cover of the deletion cannot leave an absorbable remainder block S isolated from its complement. In the anti-aligned bridge setting of R502, take D={a_0,b_q}, S=V(P^o), and Q=P. In the current-trimer setting of R506, delete p and take S={x,z}, Q=J, or delete z and take S={p,x}, Q=J. Thus this single replacement principle gives the crossing core of R502 and both conclusions of R506; R5 is needed in those applications only to supply the deletion two-cover, and R24 is unnecessary.

## Hypotheses
H is finite with a well-defined tight-path cover number and pc(H)>2; D is nonempty and proper; S and W\S are both nonempty; Q is a tight path spanning exactly D union S; T is a literal two-path cover of H-D.

## Scope
Order-free combinatorial parent theorem. Boundary antisymmetry, Strong Level-(1), smallest-counterexample minimality, capture, endpoint roles, and deletion rail-size floors are not used once the two-cover T is supplied.

## Applicability
Use whenever a deletion remainder contains a named block S that can be reabsorbed together with all deleted vertices into one certified tight path Q. Any exact two-cover of the deletion must physically cross from S to the complementary remainder. R502 then uses R176 after this crossing; R506 and retired R333 are direct specializations.

## Persistence
Retain the deletion set D, absorbable block S, certified extension path Q on D union S, the particular deletion two-cover T, and at least one selected directed crossing state xy of T. The direction and location of xy are representative-relative to T; the identities of D,S,Q are fixed input coordinates.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not produce the deletion two-cover; in smallest-counterexample applications that existence comes from R5. Does not prescribe which component or endpoint realizes the crossing, does not make the crossing current in another representative, and does not itself yield a spanning two-cover. It gives no balanced-pair, capture, contact, or clause conclusion without a separate consumer.

## Proof
Assume that T=T_1 sqcup T_2 selects no directed state with one endpoint in S and the other in W\S. A path component containing vertices of both sides would, along its vertex order, have a first adjacent pair at which the side changes; that selected directed state would cross the partition. Hence each T_i is contained wholly in S or wholly in W\S. Both sides are nonempty and T has exactly two nonempty components spanning W, so after relabeling V(T_1)=S and V(T_2)=W\S. By hypothesis Q is a tight path with vertex set D union S. Therefore Q and T_2 are vertex-disjoint tight paths whose vertex sets partition V(H), so Q sqcup T_2 is a literal spanning two-cover of H. This contradicts pc(H)>2. Thus every such T selects the asserted crossing. For R502 choose (D,S,Q)=({a_0,b_q},V(P^o),P). For the two R506 conclusions choose respectively (D,S,Q)=({p},{x,z},J) and ({z},{p,x},J).
