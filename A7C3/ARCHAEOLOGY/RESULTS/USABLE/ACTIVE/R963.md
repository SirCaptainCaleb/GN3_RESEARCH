# R963 — Two P4-free edge-ordered four-sets sharing a triangle force a Hamilton five-path

## Statement
Let G be an edge-ordered complete graph and let T={u0,u1,u2} be a three-vertex set. Let x,y be distinct vertices outside T. If both induced edge orders on T∪{x} and T∪{y} have no increasing Hamilton path, then G[T∪{x,y}] has an increasing Hamilton path.

## Hypotheses
Pure edge-ordered complete graph; two four-vertex induced subgraphs sharing exactly the triangle T are non-Hamiltonian.

## Scope
Pure local edge-order theorem; no minimality or singleton-fiber assumptions.

## Applicability
Use when two critical four-vertex port universes differ by exchanging one completion vertex. It converts simultaneous P4-free constraints under the same numerical edge order into a Hamilton P5.

## Persistence
Retain the shared-triangle normalization, the elementary K4 matching-height classification, and the two candidate Hamilton paths selected by comparing the two middle spoke edges.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert either four-set is unique or that a Hamilton P5 by itself extends to a global two-cover.

## Proof
First note the elementary K4 classification. Let the six edges of an edge-ordered K4 be e1<e2<...<e6. If e1 and e2 shared a vertex, say e1=ab and e2=bc, then with d the fourth vertex the edge cd occurs after e2, so a-b-c-d would be an increasing Hamilton path. Hence e1,e2 are disjoint. Dually e5,e6 are disjoint: if e5=bc and e6=cd, then the remaining edge ab occurs before e5, so a-b-c-d is increasing. Therefore {e1,e2} and {e5,e6} are two opposite perfect matchings, and the remaining {e3,e4} is the third opposite perfect matching. Thus every non-Hamiltonian edge-ordered K4 has its three opposite matchings in strict height blocks. Conversely such a matching-block order is visibly P4-free because the first and third edges of any Hamilton path are opposite and hence lie in the same matching block, so they cannot straddle the middle path edge increasingly.\n\nNow suppose T={u0,u1,u2} and both T+x and T+y are non-Hamiltonian. Relabel T so u1u2<u0u2<u0u1. In the K4 T+r, r∈{x,y}, the opposite matching containing u1u2 is {u1u2,ru0}, the one containing u0u2 is {u0u2,ru1}, and the one containing u0u1 is {u0u1,ru2}. Since the three internal T edges occur in the displayed low/middle/high order, the matching-height classification forces all edges of the first matching below all edges of the second, and all edges of the second below all edges of the third. In particular ru0<ru1<ru2 for r=x,y. Compare xu1 and yu1. If xu1<yu1, then the path u0,x,u1,y,u2 has edge labels u0x<xu1<yu1<yu2 and is increasing. If yu1<xu1, then u0,y,u1,x,u2 is increasing. Thus T∪{x,y} has an increasing Hamilton path.
