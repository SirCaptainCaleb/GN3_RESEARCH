# R804 — The R803 unique pinned deep crossing always forces an old-Q state reversal

## Statement
Retain accepted R803 with L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,M=Q[3,m-3],rho=q_{m-3},d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, and suppose an exact complement two-cover T is in PINNED-ONE. Thus the sole M|C selected state is either (rho,y) or (y,q_3). Then H already contains a tight path containing the reversal of an old consecutive Q-state. Consequently PINNED-ONE is not a genuine quiet residue: every exact cover in the R803 construction satisfies either (MULTI) tau_M(T)>=2 or (EAR+) an explicit old-Q state reversal, tight reverse trimer/contact, or vertex-simple tight cycle. More explicitly, the pinned branch always yields one of the reversed states v u, q_3 v, s d, q_4 q_3, or rho p where p is the old Q-predecessor of rho (p=q_{m-4} when m>=7 and p=v when m=6), embedded in a displayed tight trimer supplied by R579 or R573.

## Hypotheses
Retain accepted R803 and its PINNED-ONE branch for a tight path Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6. Use accepted wrap tetrachotomy R579 and accepted unique-crossing Hamilton-ending lemma R573.

## Scope
Order-free consumer of the only quiet unique-transition cell in accepted R803. No R24 frame, four-cell structure, capture, payment, R436, R508, or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately after accepted R803. Do not split PINNED-ONE by the identity of y: wrap the branch-specific Hamilton absorber. A failed needed wrap seam already gives a reversed old-Q state by R579; a successful wrap moves q_3 or rho to an absorber endpoint, where R573 applied to the unique selected crossing gives a tight trimer containing q_4 q_3 or rho p. Thus only MULTI remains as a non-EAR current-cover residue.

## Persistence
Retain Q, the R803 branch-specific deletion path and absorber, T, the unique selected crossing, the R579 wrap branch, and the exact tight trimer/P4 or R573 reverse-sign trimer exhibiting the old-Q reversal.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim an old-state reversal, reverse trimer, or cycle is itself a contradiction or spanning two-cover. Does not consume the MULTI branch. Does not assert the R573 sign trimer is selected in T; it is graph-intrinsic. When m=6 the deep interval is the singleton {q_3}, and the predecessor/successor formulas specialize as stated.

## Proof
Let the three branch-specific Hamilton absorbers on D union M from accepted R803 be P_L=(u,v,q_3,...,rho,d) in LEFT, P_R=(v,q_3,...,rho,d,s) in RIGHT, and P_F=(u,v,q_3,...,rho,d,s) in FOUR. We treat the two possible PINNED-ONE orientations.

LEFT, crossing (rho,y). For P_L the first wrap seam in R579 is alpha_L=(d,u,v). If alpha_L is bad, R579 is in SW2 or DF and supplies the tight reversed seam (v,u,d), which contains the reversed old Q-state vu. If alpha_L is tight, R579 gives the tight rotation (d,u,v,q_3,...,rho), a Hamilton path on D union M ending at rho. Apply accepted R573 in its CS form to the unique selected state rho y. If p is the penultimate vertex of that rotation, R573 forces (y,rho,p) tight. Here p=q_{m-4} for m>=7, while for m=6 we have p=v; in either case rho p is the reversal of the old consecutive Q-state p rho.

LEFT, crossing (y,q_3). Apply the full R579 tetrachotomy to P_L. In every non-DW branch R579 supplies one of the tight reversed seams (u,d,rho) or (v,u,d), containing respectively the reversed old state d rho or v u. In DW the absorber is a tight Hamilton cycle. Rotate that cycle to start at q_3; its successor is q_4 (equal to d when m=6). R573 in SC form applied to y q_3 then forces (q_4,q_3,y) tight, containing the reversed old state q_4 q_3.

RIGHT, crossing (y,q_3). For P_R the second wrap seam is beta_R=(d,s,v). If beta_R is bad, R579 is in SW1 or DF and supplies (v,s,d) tight, containing the reversed old state s d. If beta_R is tight, R579 gives rot2=(q_3,...,rho,d,s,v), beginning at q_3 with successor q_4; R573 SC gives (q_4,q_3,y) tight.

RIGHT, crossing (rho,y). Apply the full R579 tetrachotomy to P_R. Every non-DW branch supplies either (v,s,d) or (q_3,v,s), containing respectively the reversed old state s d or q_3 v. In DW rotate the tight Hamilton cycle to (d,s,v,q_3,...,rho), which ends at rho. R573 CS gives (y,rho,p) tight with p the old predecessor of rho, hence the reversed old state rho p.

FOUR. Apply the full R579 tetrachotomy to P_F. Every non-DW branch supplies either (u,s,d) or (v,u,s), containing the reversed old state s d or v u. In DW the absorber is a Hamilton cycle. For crossing rho y rotate it to (d,s,u,v,q_3,...,rho) and apply R573 CS, obtaining (y,rho,p) with reversed old state rho p. For crossing y q_3 rotate it to start q_3 and apply R573 SC, obtaining (q_4,q_3,y).

Thus every PINNED-ONE case contains an explicit reversal of an old consecutive Q-state. Combining with the MULTI/EAR/PINNED-ONE trichotomy of accepted R803 eliminates PINNED-ONE as a separate quiet branch and yields the stated MULTI versus EAR+ dichotomy.
