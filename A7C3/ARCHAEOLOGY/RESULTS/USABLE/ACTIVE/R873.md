# R873 — Two-probe reverse-dimer packets propagate as directed witness waves along a tight path

## Statement
Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m) be a vertex-simple tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament and let x,y be distinct vertices outside Q. For 1<=i<=m-1 write D_i=(q_i,q_{i-1}). Let H_i(x,y) mean that D_i is head-signed by both probes, and T_i(x,y) that D_i is tail-signed by both. For each i with q_{i+1} present, apply accepted R518 separately to K_i=(q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}) with fourth vertex x and with fourth vertex y. Either one of the two four-sets K_i union {x}, K_i union {y} supports a Hamilton P4, or simultaneously T_i(x,y) and H_{i+1}(x,y) hold. Consequently a pre-existing H_i packet either meets a probe P4 or becomes bidirectionally saturated while the HEAD packet propagates to D_{i+1}; dually a pre-existing T_{i+1} packet propagates left to D_i. Iterating, a P4-free run propagates the same two probes one dimer at a time and leaves every traversed dimer bidirectionally saturated.

## Hypotheses
Q tight; x,y distinct outside Q; accepted order-free R518. No smallest-counterexample, deletion cover, matching-height frame, payment, or minimality.

## Scope
Pure order-free propagation lemma for same-pair two-probe packets. No R700,R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R542,payment,capture,R436,or computation.

## Applicability
In the crossed frame use accepted R849 for the inward signed seeds and accepted R820 for paired terminal packets. Absent labelled probe P4s, packets move one Q dimer at a time. Retain the wave only as exact sign geometry; R829 forbids treating propagation length itself as closure progress.

## Persistence
Retain Q, index, probe identities, source packet, and at each step either the exact labelled P4 support or the newly propagated packet plus bidirectional saturation of the source dimer. All sign turns coexist along a P4-free run.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a propagation run is P4-free, that a P4 closes H, or that different witness-pair waves interact automatically. Does not spend saturated dimers. Path-length growth is not claimed.

## Proof
Fix i and K_i=(q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}). Apply accepted R518 to K_i with fourth vertex x. If its P4 branch occurs, stop. Otherwise R518 gives the two reverse contacts (q_i,q_{i-1},x) and (x,q_{i+1},q_i). Apply R518 independently with fourth vertex y. Again either a probe P4 occurs or it gives (q_i,q_{i-1},y) and (y,q_{i+1},q_i). Thus in the joint no-P4 branch D_i=(q_i,q_{i-1}) is tail-signed by both x,y and D_{i+1}=(q_{i+1},q_i) is head-signed by both x,y. If H_i was already known, D_i is now bidirectionally saturated and H_{i+1} is new; if T_{i+1} was already known, the dual leftward statement holds. Repeating the same two R518 probes at consecutive trimers proves the iterated wave statement.
