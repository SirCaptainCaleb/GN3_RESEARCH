# R974 — Universal first-inward stars compile to two-ended path geometry and same-frame capture portals

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, be a tight path and let X be a four-vertex set disjoint from Q. Write u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}, w=q_3, and rho=q_{m-3}. Assume the UNIVERSAL FIRST-INWARD STARS
  (v,u,x) tight and (x,s,d) tight for every x in X.
Then:

(1) TWO-ENDED PATH GEOMETRY. Either for some x in X the forward middle turn (u,x,s) is tight, in which case (v,u,x,s) is a tight P4 and, when m>=5, it extends to the vertex-simple tight P5 (v,u,x,s,d); or else every forward middle turn is bad, so (s,x,u) is tight for every x and every unordered pair {x,y} in X supports one of the tight cross-depth P4s (s,x,u,y),(s,y,u,x). Thus the m=4 hinge case is explicitly P4-valued, while the P5 conclusion is restricted to m>=5.

(2) STATIC FOUR-WITNESS SINGLETON PAIR. The first-inward reverse dimers carry opposite polarities with any chosen common witness x: (v,u) is tail-signed by x and (s,d) is head-signed by x. Cutting the intervening Q interval when m>=5, or applying the one-hinge reduction when m=4, gives opposite-sign singleton supports {u} and {s}; the same physical singleton pair retains all four X witness certificates. This is static signed-support geometry, not payment-lineage floor status.

(3) LEFT SAME-FRAME CAPTURE PORTALS. Suppose T_L is any exact two-cover of H-{u,v}. Regard S_L=(u,v) as tail-signed by w via the old Q turn (u,v,w). For each x in X, K_x=(v,u,x) is a tight trimer spanning {u,v,x}. R508 applied to the same T_L forces a selected state C_x={x,y_x} with y_x outside K_x. At most two distinct x can have y_x=w because the selected-state graph of T_L is a union of two paths and deg_{T_L}(w)<=2. Therefore at least two distinct x in X have witness-avoiding selected crossings. For each such x, R527 can orient the crossing marker so that x itself is the captured signed anchor in this same deletion frame.

(4) RIGHT SAME-FRAME CAPTURE PORTALS. Dually, if T_R is any exact two-cover of H-{d,s}, regard S_R=(d,s) as head-signed by rho via (rho,d,s). For every x, K_x=(x,s,d) is a tight trimer. R508 in the same T_R gives four x-labelled crossings; at most two use rho as exterior endpoint, so at least two distinct X vertices are witness-avoiding and may each be captured by R527 in that same right deletion frame.

In a minimum-order counterexample to R888, accepted R967 supplies the displayed universal first-inward stars for every Hamilton order of the fixed opposite rail B in the trimer-fiber setup, and minimum-order induction supplies the required pair-deletion two-covers. Thus this theorem gives an Independent-safe bridge from the D5 trimer shell into the mature first-inward path/capture machinery without assuming the crossed R24 frame.

## Hypotheses
H finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; Q tight with m>=4; X a four-set disjoint from Q; displayed universal first-inward star families. Parts (3),(4) additionally assume literal exact two-covers of the displayed pair deletions.

## Scope
Corrected pure literal-star compiler plus exact-cover capture portal theorem. Generalizes transferable cores of R775/R848/R744 but repairs the m=4 repeated-vertex defect in the old R775 P5 wording. No gate phase, R24, R5, payment lineage, Reverse Ear, or computation is used.

## Applicability
Use whenever two four-witness first-inward stars occur around one tight rail. In the minimum R888 trimer branch they arise directly from R967 endpoint-exchange blocking. At m=4 retain the P4/hinge outputs, not a P5.

## Persistence
Retain Q,u,v,d,s,w,rho,X; all star turns; exact P4/P5 or pair-indexed P4 certificates; singleton signs and four witness certificates; for each portal side the one fixed deletion cover, four R508 crossings, and at least two witness-avoiding captured X anchors.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a P5 at m=4. Does not assert the same two X anchors are captured on both sides, coexistence of multiple actualized descendants, R312 first-contact ancestry, payment-lineage floor status, or closure.

## Proof
Put u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}. For every x in X, (v,u,x) and (x,s,d) are tight.

For the path geometry, apply R3 to {u,x,s}. If (u,x,s) is tight for some x, then the consecutive turns (v,u,x),(u,x,s) make (v,u,x,s) a tight P4. If m>=5 then q_2=v and q_{m-2}=d are distinct, so appending the tight turn (x,s,d) gives the vertex-simple tight P5 (v,u,x,s,d). When m=4, v=d=q_2, so no P5 is claimed; the P4 is the exact forward-middle output. If no x has (u,x,s) tight, then R3 gives (s,x,u) for every x. For distinct x,y, R3 on {x,u,y} gives exactly one of (x,u,y),(y,u,x), yielding respectively the tight P4 (s,x,u,y) or (s,y,u,x). This proves (1) with the m=4 correction.

For (2), (v,u,x) makes (v,u) tail-signed by x and (x,s,d) makes (s,d) head-signed by x. If m>=5, apply R425 to the intervening Q interval exactly as in the standard cut-memory argument: the left surviving singleton u keeps x as tail witness and the right singleton s keeps x as head witness. If m=4, the source dimers meet exactly at v=d=q_2 and R547 hinge reduction leaves opposite-sign singletons u=q_1 and s=q_3. Since x was arbitrary and all star certificates coexist, the same singleton pair carries all four X witness ledgers.

For (3), let T_L cover H-{u,v}. The Q turn (u,v,w), w=q_3, makes S_L=(u,v) tail-signed by w. For each x, K_x=(v,u,x) is tight and spans {u,v,x}. R508 with deletion {u,v}, absorbable singleton {x}, K_x, and the same T_L gives a selected crossing C_x={x,y_x}, y_x outside K_x. Distinct x give distinct selected edges. If y_x=w call x blocked. Since T_L is a two-path forest, w has selected degree at most two, so at most two x are blocked. At least two crossings avoid w; R527 applied in this same frame may orient each marker to designate its X endpoint x as captured. No coexistence of later actualizations is asserted.

For (4), let T_R cover H-{d,s}. The old Q turn (rho,d,s), rho=q_{m-3}, makes S_R=(d,s) head-signed by rho. For each x, K_x=(x,s,d) is tight. R508 gives C_x={x,y_x}; at most two selected crossings can have y_x=rho because deg_{T_R}(rho)<=2. Each other crossing avoids the witness, so R527 captures its X endpoint. Hence at least two distinct right portals exist.

Finally specialize to the minimum R888 trimer setup. Accepted R967 says endpoint deletion/replacement supports are non-Hamiltonian. For the left endpoint q_0, if xq_1<q_1q_2 then (x,q_1,...,q_m) Hamiltonizes (B-q_0)+x, contradiction; hence q_1q_2<q_1x, i.e. (v,u,x). At the right endpoint, if q_{m-2}q_{m-1}<q_{m-1}x then (q_0,...,q_{m-1},x) Hamiltonizes (B-q_m)+x; therefore xq_{m-1}<q_{m-2}q_{m-1}, i.e. (x,s,d). Minimum-order induction gives pc<=2 on each proper pair deletion; if a pair deletion is Hamiltonian, split one Hamilton path into two nonempty tight paths to obtain the exact two-cover needed above. This proves the R888 specialization and R974.
