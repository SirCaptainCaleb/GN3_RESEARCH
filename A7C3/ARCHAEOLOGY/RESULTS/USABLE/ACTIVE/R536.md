# R536 — Middle-gate endpoint exposure and two-witness inward star propagation

## Statement
Let X be a transitive no-P4 four-cell with matching order M_R>M_S>M_L, and let d be a fifth vertex such that X union {d} is P5-free. Let e={a,b} be the fully outgoing M_S-edge supplied by R534, and let e'={c,d'} be the complementary M_S-edge. After naming the two M_L edges as {a,c} and {b,d'}, the paths (d,a,b,d') and (d,b,a,c) are tight. Dually, if f is the fully incoming M_S-edge and f' its complementary M_S-edge, then for each x in f' there is a Hamilton P4 on {d} union (X-{x}) ending at d. For the inward-star specialization, additionally let H have pc(H)>2 and V(H)=X disjoint union V(Q), where Q=(q_0,...,q_m) is tight with m>=3, X+q_0 is P5-free, and q_1 q_0 y is tight for every y in X. Apply the first conclusion with d=q_0 and let L be the complementary M_S-edge to the outgoing q_0 gate. For each x in L the corresponding q_0-starting P4 extends to a tight prefix P5 by prepending q_1; hence H[V(Q[2,m]) union {x}] is non-Hamiltonian, so x q_2 q_3 is bad and q_3 q_2 x is tight. Thus the endpoint four-witness star propagates two positions inward as a two-witness star on L. The exact right-end dual holds under the corresponding right endpoint star.

## Hypotheses
First paragraph: Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry; X is transitive matching-height; X union {d} is P5-free; R534. Inward-star paragraph: additionally H is finite with pc(H)>2, V(H)=X disjoint union V(Q), Q=(q_0,...,q_m) is tight with m>=3, and the displayed endpoint reverse star holds.

## Scope
Pure five-vertex endpoint exposure followed by an explicit two-cover specialization; no undefined historical R24 frame is required.

## Applicability
Use to replace the false assumption that a full four-witness star automatically propagates with a precise guaranteed two-witness propagation on a matching edge.

## Persistence
The exposed P4 orders, omitted M_S-edge, prefix P5s, non-Hamilton suffix augmentations, and resulting q_3 q_2 witness turns are all graph-intrinsic certificates in the named frame.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not propagate all four witnesses. Does not assert that the two-witness star can be iterated without additional same-order seam data. Does not assert favorable endpoint orientation for arbitrary R146 P5s.

## Proof
Let the outgoing M_S edge be e={a,b}, with complementary M_S edge e'={c,d'}. Relabel inside the two M_S edges so that the M_L edges are {a,c} and {b,d'}. Since e is fully outgoing from d, both dab and dba are tight. Because M(ab)=M_S>M(bd')=M_L, the X-only turn abd' is tight. Hence (d,a,b,d') is a tight P4, omitting c. Likewise M(ba)=M_S>M(ac)=M_L, so bac is tight and (d,b,a,c) is a tight P4, omitting d'. This proves the two d-initial exposures. The terminal version is dual: if f={a,b} is fully incoming, then abd and bad are tight; orient the preceding X turn through the appropriate M_R>M_S descent to obtain two P4s ending at d, one for each omitted vertex of the complementary M_S edge.

Now specialize to the explicit second-paragraph frame and d=q_0. Let L be the complementary M_S edge to the outgoing q_0 M_S gate. For x in L, choose the displayed q_0-starting P4 K_x=(q_0,u,v,w) on {q_0} union (X-{x}). Global longestness of Q gives q_1 q_0 y tight for every y in X, in particular q_1 q_0 u. Therefore (q_1,q_0,u,v,w) is a tight P5 spanning {q_0,q_1} union (X-{x}). If the complementary vertex set (q_2,...,q_m) union {x} had a Hamilton path, these two disjoint paths would form a spanning two-cover of H, contradiction. Hence (q_2,...,q_m) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian. Since (q_2,...,q_m) is itself a tight path, the literal prepend candidate (x,q_2,q_3,...,q_m) must fail at its only new turn x q_2 q_3. Thus x q_2 q_3 is bad; boundary antisymmetry gives q_3 q_2 x tight. This holds for both x in L. The right-end statement follows by complete reversal of the roles and using y q_m q_{m-1} tight for every y in X.
