# R568 — Dimer pure-C rail seam refinement in the both-bad SC cell

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

Retain a full R513 both-bad SC packet with s=1. Thus the pure C-rail is the dimer K=(c_0,c_1), the cut satisfies 1<=i<=m-2, the seam four-set is F={a,b,c,d} with a=c_0, b=c_1, c=q_{i+1}, d=q_{i+2}, and both seam holes (a,b,c) and (b,c,d) are bad. Write h=(q_{i-1},q_i,b_0) for the isolated mixed-seam hole of the parent R511 proposal (this turn exists in the genuine three-hole SC range except the already one-hole aligned cells; if i=0 the parent packet is not three-hole). R3 supplies the forced tight reverses (c,b,a) and (d,c,b).

(1) The unique Hamilton P4 on F that retains the oriented Q-boundary dimer (c,d)=(q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) as its suffix is P_R=(b,a,c,d)=(c_1,c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}). It is tight if and only if (c_1,c_0,q_{i+1}) and (c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) are tight.

(2) If P_R is tight, the spanning proposal
U=(q_0,...,q_i,b_0,...,b_r),\nV_R=(c_1,c_0,q_{i+1},...,q_m),
with empty pieces omitted, has complete uncertified-turn set {h} when i>=1, or empty mixed hole together with the two P_R turns when the parent already listed no h. In the genuine three-hole range 1<=i<=m-2 this is a literal one-hole proposal, so h is bad and reverse(h)=(b_0,q_i,q_{i-1}) is tight. The Q-suffix after q_{i+2} is certified by Q.

(3) If (c_1,c_0,q_{i+1}) is bad, then (q_{i+1},c_0,c_1) is tight. If (c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) is bad, then (q_{i+2},q_{i+1},c_0) is tight.

This is the exact SC dual of accepted R550: R550 preserves the Q-prefix dimer in CS, while this result preserves the Q-suffix dimer in SC. It does not rewrite C408.

## Hypotheses

H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2. The full accepted R513 both-bad SC packet is retained with s=1 and 1<=i<=m-2. Tight/bad are complementary by R3.

## Scope

Order-free coordinate-preserving refinement of the accepted R513 SC residue restricted to a dimer pure C-rail. Independent of C429/R550, which treats the CS orientation.

## Applicability

In an R513 both-bad SC cell with s=1, test the two P_R turns (c_1,c_0,q_{i+1}) and (c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}). If both are tight, replace the R513 reverse-four-segment proposal by U sqcup V_R and keep only hole h. If either is bad, retain the corresponding R3 reverse. If s>=2, this theorem does not apply.

## Persistence

Retain the full R513 SC packet, cut index i, dimer pure rail K, both bad natural seam holes, the two P_R turn statuses, the sole-hole proposal when certified, and the displayed reverses when a P_R turn is bad.

## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not replace or narrow R513. Does not treat s>=2 or the CS side (see R550). Does not assert that P_R is always tight. Does not claim an alternative deletion cover or lexicographic decrease.

## Proof

Work in the retained R513 both-bad SC packet with s=1. Then a=c_0, b=c_1, c=q_{i+1}, d=q_{i+2}, F={c_0,c_1,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}}, the turns (c_0,c_1,q_{i+1}) and (c_1,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) are bad, and R3 gives (q_{i+1},c_1,c_0) and (q_{i+2},q_{i+1},c_1) tight. The parent mixed hole h=(q_{i-1},q_i,b_0) is present for 1<=i<=m-2.

(1) Any Hamilton P4 on F with suffix (q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) has the form (x,y,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) with {x,y}={c_0,c_1}. The choice (c_0,c_1,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) begins with the bad seam hole. The remaining choice is P_R=(c_1,c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}), tight exactly when (c_1,c_0,q_{i+1}) and (c_0,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) are tight.

(2) Assume P_R is tight. Form V_R by traversing (c_1,c_0) and then Q from q_{i+1} through q_m. The first two triples of V_R are the P_R turns. Every later triple on V_R is a Q-turn. The rail U is the unchanged R511 mixed rail (Q[0,i] followed by B), whose only mixed attachment in this range is h. There is no omitted C-vertex because s=1. Hence the complete uncertified-turn set is {h}. A one-hole spanning proposal cannot have its unique hole tight, so h is bad and reverse(h) is tight.

(3) Each bad P_R turn has its R3 reverse as displayed. No vertex of T is moved.
