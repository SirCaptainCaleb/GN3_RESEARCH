# R22 — Componentwise exact re-rooting

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

In any displayed minimum three-cover, deleting any one component leaves the other two components as an exact two-cover. In particular, if one component is a prescribed singleton, its complement is already an exact two-cover in the same representative.

## Scope
The singleton specialization imports, without changing representative:

- the one-vertex deletion rail floor;
- the four one-hole endpoint caps;
- the double boundary-collision normal form; and
- the prescribed-singleton disjoint collision package.

Current cap orientations remain distinct from reverse-support orientations, and graph-intrinsic alternatives need not be simultaneously selected.
