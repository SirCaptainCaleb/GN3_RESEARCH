# R530 — Internal captured crossing mirror-orientation normal form

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let A=(...,l,x,r,...) be a graph-intrinsic historical tight carrier path in a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let D={x,z} be a physical crossing of A with z outside A, and suppose x has a valid historical capture certificate through D. Assume x is internal on A, with distinct historical carrier neighbors l,r. Apply R155 separately to the genuine carrier adjacencies {l,x} and {x,r}. The left mirror is exactly one of (l,x,z) or (z,x,l), and the right mirror is exactly one of (r,x,z) or (z,x,r). Together with the inherited carrier turn (l,x,r), exactly one of the following four cells occurs.

(HH) (l,x,z) and (r,x,z) are tight. Then the identical tested oriented dimer (x,z) is head-signed by the distinct witnesses l,r, giving the R523 same-head collision packet on the actual captured support D.

(TT) (z,x,l) and (z,x,r) are tight. Then the identical tested oriented dimer (z,x) is tail-signed by l,r, giving the R523 same-tail collision packet on D.

(RC) (l,x,z) and (z,x,r) are tight. Since the inherited carrier turn (l,x,r) is also tight, the identical tested oriented carrier dimer (x,r) is head-signed by the distinct witnesses l,z, giving an R523 same-head collision packet on the genuine old carrier adjacency {x,r}.

(CY) (z,x,l) and (r,x,z) are tight. Then the three x-middle turns (z,x,l),(l,x,r),(r,x,z) form the cyclic middle-x residue. Apply corrected universal four-cell theorem R518 to the tight trimer (z,x,l) with fourth vertex r. Either {z,x,l,r} supports a tight Hamilton P4, or it is the exact corresponding R516 no-P4 signature; in the latter case the additional reverse contacts (x,z,r) and (r,l,x) are tight. Thus the only internal-capture residue not already an R523 collision is a role-labelled P4 or one explicit corrected no-P4 four-cell on the two carrier neighbors, captured vertex, and crossing vertex.

Every output retains the historical capture event through D and the identities of l,x,r,z. The two R155 mirror trimers may live in separate later representatives; only their graph-intrinsic tight-turn certificates and capture ancestry are combined.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; A is a certified historical tight carrier; D={x,z} crosses A at internal x; x has a valid historical capture through D; l,r are the two distinct genuine carrier neighbors of x. Accepted R155,R523,R518.

## Scope
Exact tested-order normal form for an internal historical capture. It replaces the false interaction-only R311 interface and keeps the cyclic mirror-orientation residue explicit.

## Applicability
Use whenever a captured vertex is internal on a retained historical carrier. Read the two lawful R155 mirror orientations literally. Three orientation cells immediately give a same-tested-order collision; the cyclic fourth cell gives P4 or an exact R516 no-P4 signature.

## Persistence
Retain A,D,x,l,r,z, the historical capture event, both graph-intrinsic R155 mirror-turn certificates, and whichever collision/P4/no-P4-cell output occurs. The two mirror currentizations need not coexist in one representative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not compare reverse-tested certificates on D. Does not claim every internal capture yields an R523 interaction on the captured support. Does not claim the two mirror trimers are simultaneously current. Does not close the P4 or no-P4 residue by itself.

## Proof
Because A is tight and x is internal, the inherited carrier turn (l,x,r) is tight. Apply R155 with E_L={l,x}. The unique tight mirror trimer on {l,x,z} has x as middle, hence is exactly one of (l,x,z) and (z,x,l). Apply R155 with E_R={x,r}; its mirror is exactly one of (r,x,z) and (z,x,r). These choices give four cells. If the mirrors are (l,x,z),(r,x,z), both are head certificates on the identical tested oriented dimer (x,z), witnessed by l,r, so R523 gives HH. If they are (z,x,l),(z,x,r), both are tail certificates on (z,x), so R523 gives TT. If they are (l,x,z),(z,x,r), compare the latter with the inherited carrier turn (l,x,r): both are head certificates on the identical tested oriented dimer (x,r), with distinct witnesses z,l, so R523 gives RC. The only remaining choice is (z,x,l),(r,x,z). Together with (l,x,r), the x-middle orientations are z->l->r->z. Apply R518 to C=(z,x,l) with fourth vertex r. Either the four-set has a Hamilton P4, or the no-P4 branch forces exactly (x,z,r) and (r,l,x), placing the four vertices in the corresponding exact R516 signature. These alternatives are exhaustive. Each mirror certificate was produced by R155 from the same historical capture event, so that ancestry remains attached to the role-labelled output without any simultaneous-currentness claim.
