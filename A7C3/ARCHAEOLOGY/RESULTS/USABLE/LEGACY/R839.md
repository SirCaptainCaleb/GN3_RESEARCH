Thus Q=(L,u,v,w,d,s,R), N=(v,w,d), and the order-eight core has the four exact P4 rails A_1=(u,L,a,c), A_2=(u,L,c,a), B_1=(b,z,R,s), B_2=(z,b,R,s), with every A_i disjoint from every B_k and A_i sqcup B_k an exact two-cover of the core. ## Proof
Write N=(v,w,d). Accepted R817 gives A_1=(u,L,a,c), A_2=(u,L,c,a), B_1=(b,z,R,s), B_2=(z,b,R,s), and for every i,k the pair A_i sqcup B_k is an exact cover of the core, so V(H)=V(N) disjoint-union V(A_i) disjoint-union V(B_k). We now form six spanning two-path proposals.\n\n1. Append A_i after N and retain a disjoint B_k. The candidate is P=(v,w,d,u,L,...) with the remaining A_i vertices following L in their literal order, together with B_k. All turns inside N,A_i,B_k are tight. The only new turns are (w,d,u) and (d,u,L), independent of i. If both were tight the two displayed paths would two-cover H. Hence at least one is bad; R3 gives (u,d,w) OR (L,u,d). This is E_A.\n\n2. Prepend A_1=(u,L,a,c) before N and retain any B_k. The first candidate rail is (u,L,a,c,v,w,d). Its only new turns are (a,c,v) and (c,v,w). Thus R3 gives (v,c,a) OR (w,v,c).\n\n3. Prepend A_2=(u,L,c,a) before N. The only new turns are (c,a,v) and (a,v,w), giving (v,a,c) OR (w,v,a).\n\n4. Append B_1=(b,z,R,s) after N and retain any A_i. The candidate rail is (v,w,d,b,z,R,s). Its only new turns are (w,d,b) and (d,b,z), giving (b,d,w) OR (z,b,d).\n\n5. Append B_2=(z,b,R,s) after N. The only new turns are (w,d,z) and (d,z,b), giving (z,d,w) OR (b,z,d).\n\n6. Prepend B_k before N and retain a disjoint A_i. The candidate rail ends (...,R,s,v,w,d). The only new turns are (R,s,v) and (s,v,w), independent of k. Hence R3 gives (v,s,R) OR (w,v,s), which is E_B.\n\nIn each case supports are disjoint and spanning because N plus the chosen core-cover rails partition V(H), so the hole lists are complete. This proves all six physical clauses.
