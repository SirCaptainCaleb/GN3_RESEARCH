# R342 — Every equality-case trimer bad edge has a balanced five-way routing partition

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the equality-case fixed-trimer setup of provisional R341. Thus r=5q, P={p_1,p_2,p_3} is a tight trimer, and G_P is the q-regular triangle-free bad-extension graph on X=W\\P. Fix any bad edge xy of G_P. Then the remaining vertex set X\\{x,y} partitions canonically into five classes, each of cardinality q-1: N(x)\\{y}; N(y)\\{x}; and, for i=1,2,3, the class A_i of vertices z adjacent to neither x nor y for which the unique paired bad five-set in the occupied six-set P union {x,y,z} is (P\\{p_i}) union {x,y,z}. In particular every bad edge sees exactly q-1 third vertices routed through each of the five possible replacement channels: swap y along an x-neighbor, swap x along a y-neighbor, or delete one of the three trimer vertices.

## Hypotheses
The exact R331 equality case holds and provisional R341 is available; xy is an edge of the fixed-trimer bad graph G_P.

## Scope
Exact local balance refinement of the R331/R341 equality normal form.

## Applicability
Use in attempts to classify or exclude the R331 equality case. It replaces an unstructured bad-edge neighborhood by a canonical five-way equipartition.

## Persistence
The graph G_P, edge xy, and five routing classes are graph-intrinsic combinatorial data determined by the bad-five family B and trimer P.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that these partitions are globally consistent from edge to edge, that G_P is bipartite or multipartite, or that the equality case exists. Does not force a P6 or a two-cover.

## Proof
Fix bad edge xy. Since G_P is q-regular and triangle-free, x and y have no common neighbor; excluding each other leaves two disjoint neighbor classes of size q-1. For p_i in P let S_i=(P\\{p_i}) union {x,y}. R331 says exactly q bad five-sets contain S_i. One is S_i union {p_i}=P union {x,y}; hence there are exactly q-1 other bad blocks S_i union {z}. For each such z, the six-set P union {x,y,z} has P union {x,y} as one bad block and S_i union {z} as the unique partner, so R341 places z in A_i. Conversely every z routed through deletion p_i gives one such bad block. Thus |A_i|=q-1. The routing uniqueness makes A_1,A_2,A_3 disjoint and disjoint from the two graph-neighbor classes. Their sizes sum to 5(q-1)=5q-5=|X\\{x,y}|, so they partition the remainder.
