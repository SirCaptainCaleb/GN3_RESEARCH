# R181 — Capacity-Flat Synchronized Floor Collision

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the synchronized ancestry-bearing floor, suppose the anchored-capacity-flat balanced pair has mass M=2, hence singleton supports (p),(t) with fixed witnesses alpha,beta. Suppose simultaneously that the retained parent envelope A* has at least three vertices and the fixed reserve trimer T_q=(q0,q1,q2) is disjoint from A*. Then the ordinary reservoir of the fixed anchored-capacity representation contains at least two vertices. Consequently the general Anchored-Capacity Reservoir Collision theorem fires in that same representation: before any ordinary-vertex M=4 lift, floor steering, or choice of puncture frame, one has a strict same-polarity extension or a bounded reverse trimer/contact. The one-residual exception of the general capacity theorem is impossible in this synchronized floor.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament. A synchronized ancestry-bearing M=2 floor with singleton balanced supports (p),(t), fixed witnesses alpha,beta, a retained parent envelope A* with |A*|>=3, and a fixed tight reserve trimer T_q=(q0,q1,q2) vertex-disjoint from A*. The anchored capacity for the fixed singleton anchors/witnesses is flat at this floor.

## Scope
Specialized synchronized floor corollary. The extra A*/T_q ancestry is essential to the reservoir cardinality argument. The collision occurs in one fixed capacity representation; separately steered puncture descendants are not combined.

## Applicability
Use to eliminate the one-residual near-spanning exception and force first physical collision at the ancestry-bearing M=2 floor without lifting back to M=4 or choosing left/right puncture frames.

## Persistence
The retained A*, T_q/q1, singleton anchors, fixed witnesses, and preexisting synchronized ancestry remain historical labels. The collision output is graph-intrinsic. The theorem does not discard the stronger common-frame ancestry merely because first progress is forced earlier.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
This is not a theorem about every abstract mass-two balanced pair or every capacity floor. It requires the synchronized package with disjoint A* and T_q and |A*|>=3. It does not close the strict-extension or bounded-contact outputs. It does not combine left/right puncture frames or assert capacity monotonicity.

## Proof
At the synchronized floor the anchored-capacity supports are the singletons (p) and (t), with fixed witnesses alpha,beta. Thus S={p,t,alpha,beta} has at most four vertices. The retained parent envelope satisfies |A*|>=3. The fixed reserve trimer T_q=(q0,q1,q2) has three vertices and V(A*) is disjoint from V(T_q), so |V(A*) union V(T_q)|>=6. At most four of these six or more vertices lie in S. Hence at least two distinct vertices lie outside the two supports and their fixed witnesses, i.e. in the ordinary reservoir X. Apply R180. The X-empty and |X|=1 faces are impossible; the |X|>=2 face gives a same-polarity two-witness collision on a fixed capacity reverse dimer, hence the accepted strict extension or bounded reverse trimer/contact output. All data are taken in one fixed capacity representation.
