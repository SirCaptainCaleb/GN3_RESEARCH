# R557 — Minimum R24 seam cell has dual explicit endpoint-capture pair births

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the unique exactly-two-bad transitive R24 seam cell of R555/R556. Then the two forced collision packets admit explicit same-frame capture/actualization certificates using only the original deletion frame. On the left, the collision support (b,c) is head-signed by q_0 (and also by z). Pair-delete {b,c}. The literal cover H-{b,c}=(z,a) sqcup Q is exact by Pair-Deletion Rigidity, and its selected state {z,a} crosses the singleton short-carrier remainder {a} to the exterior while avoiding q_0. Orient the marker as M_L=(z,a). Since R556 gives q_0az tight, the head-to-tail actualization test zaq_0 is bad, so the actualized opposite support is exactly (q_0,a), tail-signed by z, with captured/signed anchor a. Thus there is a direct balanced pair birth Pi_L=((b,c),(q_0,a)) retaining the left collision, the q_0 witness, physical crossing az, captured endpoint a, and z as the actualization witness. Dually, pair-delete {a,b}; H-{a,b}=(c,z) sqcup Q is exact. The crossing cz avoids q_m. With marker M_R=(c,z), R556 gives zcq_m tight, so the tail-to-head test q_mcz is bad and actualization yields exactly (c,q_m), head-signed by z with captured/signed anchor c. Hence Pi_R=((c,q_m),(a,b)) is a direct balanced pair birth retaining the right collision, q_m witness, physical crossing cz, captured endpoint c, and z as actualization witness. The four nontrivial supports have cross-birth intersections H_L∩H_R={c}, H_L∩T_R={b}, T_L∩T_R={a}, and T_L∩H_R=empty. The two births are simultaneous graph-intrinsic certificate options; no claim is made that two paid descendants are simultaneously current.

## Proof
R555 gives the left same-head collision on tested support (b,c), in particular q_0bc tight, and the right same-tail collision on (a,b), in particular abq_m tight. Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429 says pc(H-{b,c})=pc(H-{a,b})=2. The displayed decompositions (z,a) sqcup Q and (c,z) sqcup Q are literal two-path covers, hence exact.

For {b,c}, the surviving short carrier vertex is a and the selected dimer az is a carrier/exterior crossing. It avoids the admissible collision witness q_0. Apply R527/R526 with support (b,c) head-signed by q_0 and marker orientation M=(z,a), designating a as captured endpoint. The relevant marker test is zaq_0. By R556, az is fully outgoing from q_0, so q_0az is tight; R3 therefore makes zaq_0 bad. The bad-test branch of signed-support actualization gives the exact short tail support (q_0,a), witnessed by z, with tail anchor a. This is disjoint from (b,c), so it is the promised balanced pair birth Pi_L.

For {a,b}, the surviving short carrier vertex is c and cz is the selected carrier/exterior crossing in (c,z) sqcup Q, avoiding q_m. Apply the tail-to-head dual with support (a,b) tail-signed by q_m and marker M=(c,z), capturing c. R556 says cz is fully incoming to q_m, hence zcq_m is tight and the reverse test q_mcz is bad. The bad-test branch gives the short head support (c,q_m), witnessed by z, with head anchor c. Pair it with (a,b) to obtain Pi_R.

All four supports are dimers. Their stated intersections follow directly from the vertex sets and q_0,q_m outside X and distinct in the hard frame.
