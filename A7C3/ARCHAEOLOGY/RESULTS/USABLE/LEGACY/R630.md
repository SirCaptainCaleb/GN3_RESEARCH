# R630 — Crossed endpoint P8 forces a pure-Q two-end collision alternative

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the crossed R24 frame of accepted R621 with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, and write L=q_0, u=q_1, R=q_m, s=q_{m-1}. Then the two-end skip clause

(u,s,R) tight OR (L,u,s) tight

is physically certified by a literal spanning two-cover proposal. Consequently at least one of two explicit same-support same-polarity R38 interactions occurs: either the oriented right terminal dimer (s,R) is head-signed by the two distinct witnesses u and q_{m-2}, or the oriented left terminal dimer (L,u) is tail-signed by the two distinct witnesses s and q_2. Thus the crossed frame cannot keep both ends locally insulated: a single eight-vertex endpoint proposal forces a graph-intrinsic two-witness collision at one physical Q end, with the alternative itself coupling the opposite end.

H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed frame of R621. Use the matching-height orientation on X, the crossed right middle-gate polarity from R621, the endpoint reverse stars from accepted R594, the tightness of Q, boundary antisymmetry R3, and accepted same-support sign interaction R38.

## Proof
Consider the ordered eight-vertex path
P_E=(a,b,z,R,s,u,L,c).
Its consecutive turns are
(a,b,z), (b,z,R), (z,R,s), (R,s,u), (s,u,L), (u,L,c).
The first is tight by the matching-height rule M_R(ab)>M_S(bz). The second is tight because in the crossed phase the M_S edge {b,z} is fully incoming to R. The third and sixth are endpoint reverse-star turns supplied by R594: (z,R,s) and (u,L,c). Hence the only uncertified turns of P_E are h_R=(R,s,u) and h_L=(s,u,L).

The support of P_E is exactly X union {L,u,s,R}. Since m>=4, the complementary interval Q[2,m-2]=(q_2,...,q_{m-2}) is nonempty and tight. Thus if both h_R and h_L were tight, P_E sqcup Q[2,m-2] would be a literal spanning two-cover of H, impossible in the hypothetical counterexample. Therefore at least one of h_R,h_L is bad. Boundary antisymmetry R3 converts this into the physical mate clause
bar(h_R)=(u,s,R) tight OR bar(h_L)=(L,u,s) tight.
The literal proposal P_E sqcup Q[2,m-2] is the complete two-hole certificate.

If (u,s,R) is tight, the oriented physical dimer (s,R) is head-signed by witness u. Because Q itself is tight, its terminal turn (q_{m-2},s,R) is also tight, so the same tested oriented dimer (s,R) is head-signed by q_{m-2}. These witnesses are distinct because u=q_1 and m-2>=2. Accepted R38 therefore gives the same-support same-polarity two-witness interaction on (s,R), retaining both witness identities and tested order.

If instead (L,u,s) is tight, the oriented dimer (L,u) is tail-signed by witness s. The initial Q turn (L,u,q_2) is tight, so (L,u) is also tail-signed by q_2. Since s=q_{m-1} and m-1>=3, s!=q_2. R38 gives the corresponding same-support same-polarity interaction on (L,u).

Thus every crossed frame has one of these two explicit terminal collision packets, and the new witness in each packet is drawn from the opposite end of Q. This proves the statement.
