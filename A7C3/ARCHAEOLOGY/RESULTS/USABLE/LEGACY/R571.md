# R571 — Two-class common-deletion absorber pressure

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

Let H be a finite tight-path system with p=pc(H). Let D be a proper vertex set and write W=V(H)\\D=S disjoint-union C with S and C nonempty. Let T be a literal k-path cover of H-D, and let t be the number of selected adjacent states of T with one endpoint in S and the other in C. Write b_S (resp. b_C) for the number of maximal contiguous S-blocks (resp. C-blocks) of T. Then b_S+b_C=k+t.

If H[D union S] admits an a_S-path cover, then pc(H[C])>=p-a_S. If H[D union C] admits an a_C-path cover, then pc(H[S])>=p-a_C. Since b_S>=pc(H[S]) and b_C>=pc(H[C]), one has t>=2p-a_S-a_C-k whenever both covers exist.

In particular, if both D union S and D union C are Hamiltonian, then t>=2p-2-k. Thus in a smallest two-cover counterexample (p=3) every exact two-cover of such a residue has at least two S|C transitions: there is no tau=1 exact two-cover of H-D when both complementary D-augmented sides are Hamiltonian.

## Hypotheses

H is any finite tight-path system. No Strong Level-(1) hypothesis is used. Path-cover number pc is the ordinary minimum number of vertex-disjoint tight paths covering the vertex set.

## Scope

Order-free two-class special case of absorber/transition pressure. It does not treat r>=3 class partitions and does not manufacture Hamilton orders of an existing absorber.

## Applicability

Given a deletion D and a bipartition of H-D, compute a_S and a_C from whatever path covers of D union S and D union C are already certified. If both sides are Hamiltonian, discard every tau=1 exact two-cover of H-D.

## Persistence

Retain D,S,C,T,k,t and the two absorber covers. The inequality is graph-intrinsic. No currentness is claimed for T beyond the supplied cover.

## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not force either side to be Hamiltonian. Does not produce a second Hamilton order of a single absorber. Does not treat three-or-more class partitions. Does not consume a selected crossing by capture or currentization. Does not depend on pending R566-R570.

## Proof

Let T be a k-path cover of W=S sqcup C. On each rail of T, every selected S|C adjacency ends one monochromatic block and starts another. A rail with t_i such adjacencies therefore contributes t_i+1 monochromatic blocks. Summing over rails gives b_S+b_C=k+t.

Let Q_S be an a_S-path cover of H[D union S] and let R_C be a pc(H[C])-path cover of H[C]. The two families are vertex-disjoint on W and together with D they cover V(H). Hence p=pc(H)<=a_S+pc(H[C]), so pc(H[C])>=p-a_S. The symmetric argument with an a_C-path cover of H[D union C] yields pc(H[S])>=p-a_C.

The S-blocks of T form a path cover of H[S], so b_S>=pc(H[S]). Likewise b_C>=pc(H[C]). Therefore k+t=b_S+b_C>=pc(H[S])+pc(H[C])>=(p-a_C)+(p-a_S), which is the stated inequality.

If both D union S and D union C are Hamiltonian then a_S=a_C=1, so t>=2p-2-k. For p=3 and k=2 this is t>=2. In particular no exact two-cover of H-D can have a unique S|C transition.
