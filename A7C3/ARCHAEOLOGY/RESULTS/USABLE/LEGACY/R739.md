# R739 — The two universal first-inward stars form one common-witness opposite-sign pair

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and accepted R723, with u=q_1,v=q_2,d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1}. Fix any x in X. Then the left first-inward reverse dimer D_L=(v,u) is tail-signed by x and the right first-inward reverse dimer D_R=(s,d) is head-signed by the same witness x. If m>=5 their physical supports are disjoint, so (D_R,D_L) is a direct graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair of total support mass four. In a hypothetical smallest counterexample, accepted R514 therefore gives a finite certificate-retaining continuation to a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor. If m=4, the two supports meet exactly in the hinge q_2=d=v, so accepted R547 instead collapses them directly to the opposite-sign singleton pair {q_1},{q_3}. Thus for every m>=4 the crossed frame already contains a source-localized opposite-sign interaction joining the two first-inward Q boundaries. Moreover the construction is four-witness saturated: the same fixed physical dimer pair has a valid common-witness certificate for every x in X, and all four such source certificates coexist graph-intrinsically.

H is in the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame with m>=4. Smallest-counterexample minimality is needed only for the optional R514 descent when m>=5.

## Proof
R723 states that (v,u,x) is tight and (x,s,d) is tight for every x in X. Hence tested D_L=(v,u) is tail-signed by x and tested D_R=(s,d) is head-signed by x. Their supports are {q_1,q_2} and {q_{m-2},q_{m-1}}. For m>=5 these sets are disjoint, so they form a direct opposite-sign mass-four pair and R514 gives the optional smallest-counterexample descent. For m=4, q_{m-2}=q_2 while q_{m-1}=q_3, so the supports meet in exactly the hinge q_2; R547 deletes the hinge and leaves opposite-sign singletons q_1 and q_3. Since x was arbitrary in X, the identical physical support pair carries four alternative common-witness certificates.
