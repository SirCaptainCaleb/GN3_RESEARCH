# R499 — Long-arm R495 double reversal forces a named-residue cross-state balanced pair

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and retain the DR output of R495 in the R485 notation. Thus K_D=(a_1,a_0,b_q,b_{q-1}) is a tight P4, while the original source three-cover is P sqcup A^+ sqcup B^- with A=(a_0,a_1,...,a_p), B=(b_0,...,b_q), P=(a_0,z_1,...,z_{m-1},b_q), m>=3. Assume p>=2 and q>=2. Delete the four DR vertices E={a_1,a_0,b_q,b_{q-1}} and put W=H-E. Then the three named paths A^{++}=(a_2,...,a_p), B^{--}=(b_0,...,b_{q-2}), and P^o=(z_1,...,z_{m-1}) are nonempty, vertex-disjoint, tight, and form a literal three-component path cover R of W. By accepted four-deletion exactness R5, W has a literal exact two-cover T=U sqcup V. Apply the partition-cover deficit identity R7 to the fixed physical partition Pi={V(A^{++}),V(B^{--}),V(P^o)} and the current cover T. Each class has intrinsic path-cover number one, while T has two components, so the number t_Pi(T) of selected transitions between distinct named classes is 3-2+sigma=1+sigma>=1. Hence T selects some directed state xy whose endpoints lie in two distinct components of the named source cover R. Since W is proper, accepted R176 applies to the literal path covers R and T and yields a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair. Therefore the R495 double ancestral-end reversal is immediately consumable whenever both ancestral arms continue beyond the reversed endpoint pair: it forces a balanced-pair birth whose cross-state provenance retains exactly which two of A^{++},B^{--},P^o were joined by the four-deletion exact cover. The only DR cases not covered are the short-arm cells p=1 or q=1, where one named residual class is empty and the three-to-two transition deficit disappears.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; accepted R495 DR output; p,q>=2; accepted R5,R7,R176.

## Scope
Order-free smallest-counterexample consumer of the R495 DR branch. It converts a simultaneous two-end reversal into a balanced pair without currentizing DR and without losing the named two-cut ancestry.

## Applicability
Use immediately on R495(DR) when both ancestral rails extend at least one vertex beyond a_1 and b_{q-1}. Delete the four DR vertices, keep the three literal residual source pieces as the reference cover, choose any exact four-deletion two-cover, and take its forced cross-class state through R176. Only p=1 or q=1 requires separate analysis.

## Persistence
Retains the DR support, the four-deletion set, all three named residual classes A^{++},B^{--},P^o, the exact two-cover T, and the particular selected cross-class state witnessing the R176 balanced-pair birth. The resulting balanced pair is graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not handle p=1 or q=1. Does not assert which pair of the three named residual classes is crossed. Does not claim the balanced pair itself closes O4; it feeds the accepted pair-payment/descent machinery. Does not require the DR path to be currentized.
