# R368 — No 4-regular Moore graph on seventeen vertices

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
There is no simple graph G on 17 vertices that is 4-regular, triangle-free, C4-free, and has exact radius-two Moore geometry. Equivalently there is no strongly regular graph with parameters (17,4,0,1).

## Hypotheses
G is a finite simple graph on 17 vertices with the stated regularity/girth/Moore assumptions.

## Scope
Pure finite graph obstruction extracted from the R362 equality proof.

## Applicability
Use as the degree-four exceptional-case consumer after the girth-five Moore bound becomes tight.

## Persistence
Graph-intrinsic adjacency structure.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not classify Moore graphs in other degrees.

## Record notes
- **Lifecycle:** retired
- **Lifecycle note:** The (17,4,0,1) Moore obstruction has an elegant independent spectral proof but is a narrow one-consumer proposition; inline that contradiction into the stronger equality/P6 consumer and retire the separate claim.
- **Curation note:** The (17,4,0,1) Moore obstruction has an elegant independent spectral proof but is a narrow one-consumer proposition; inline that contradiction into the stronger equality/P6 consumer and retire the separate claim.
- **Review status:** accepted
- **Review note:** Accepted after independent audit.

## Proof
A 4-regular exact girth-five Moore graph would have degree d=4, but R369 permits only d in {2,3,7,57}. Therefore no such 17-vertex graph exists.
