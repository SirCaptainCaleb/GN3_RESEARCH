# R378 — Johnson paired-design equality has balanced reversible swap routing

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the t=2 equality setting of R375 and write r=kq. For every selected block F in B and every f in F define A_f(F)={x outside F : (F\\{f}) union {x} lies in B}. Then the k classes A_f(F), f in F, partition V\\F and each has cardinality q-1. Moreover the routing is reversible: if x belongs to A_f(F) and G=(F\\{f}) union {x}, then f belongs to A_x(G), where A_x(G) is the replacement class indexed by the element x of G. Thus every equality block carries a canonical balanced k-way routing of all outside vertices into equal swap classes, with every selected Johnson edge paired by the reverse swap.

## Hypotheses
Finite k-uniform family in the t=2 equality case of R375; r=kq.

## Scope
Pure finite-set local routing theorem.

## Applicability
Use to convert abstract equality designs into explicit local replacement coordinates. This generalizes the balanced five-way routing phenomena seen in the k=5 Hamilton-five equality analysis.

## Persistence
The routing classes and reversible Johnson swaps are intrinsic finite-set data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not classify the global compatibility of the local routing partitions and does not assert a group or algebraic structure.

## Proof
R375 gives r=kq, unique swap pairing in every occupied (k+1)-set, and constant (k-1)-core occupancy q. Fix F in B. For every outside x, the occupied union F union {x} contains exactly one selected neighbor of F, necessarily F-f+x for a unique f in F; hence the A_f(F) partition V\\F. For fixed f, the core S=F\\{f} lies in exactly q selected blocks. One is F itself; the other q-1 are precisely S union {x} with x in A_f(F). Thus |A_f(F)|=q-1. If x in A_f(F) and G=F-f+x, then F and G are the two selected members of the union clique F union {x}=G union {f}; uniqueness from G therefore routes f back through the element x, i.e. f in A_x(G).
