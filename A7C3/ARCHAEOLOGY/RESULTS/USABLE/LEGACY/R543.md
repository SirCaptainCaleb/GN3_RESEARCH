# R543 — Order-three rail dichotomy: paid boundary collision or aligned two-ended trap

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample. Let X={a,b,c,z} be a transitive matching-height four-cell with M(ab)=M_R, M(ac)=M_S, M(bc)=M_L. Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=3, be a tight path such that H-z has the literal two-cover on supports {a,b,c} and V(Q), and assume X union {q_0} and X union {q_m} are P5-free with endpoint reverse stars q_1 q_0 x and x q_m q_{m-1} tight for every x in X. The three tight trimer orders on {a,b,c} are (a,b,c),(a,c,b),(b,a,c). For any such order (u,v,w), test the junctions (v,w,q_0) and (q_m,u,v). If either is bad, its R3 reverse together with the forced z-extension reverse contact gives a same-polarity two-witness collision on the corresponding reverse terminal dimer, so R542 forces capture/payment. If all six junctions are tight, then bcq_0, cbq_0, acq_0, q_mab, q_mac, q_mba are tight. Accepted R549 and R534 then force the aligned gate packet: M_L edge az is fully outgoing at q_0; M_S edge bz is fully outgoing at q_0 while ac is fully incoming; M_R edge cz is fully incoming at q_m; M_S edge bz is fully incoming at q_m while ac is fully outgoing. Consequently (q_1,q_0,b,z,q_m,q_{m-1}) is a literal tight P6. Thus the frame lies in the paid-boundary-collision branch or this explicit aligned two-ended trap branch.

## Hypotheses
H hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; X={a,b,c,z} transitive matching-height with the displayed matching labels; Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=3, tight and disjoint from X; H-z has the literal exact two-cover with trimer support {a,b,c} and Q; X+q_0 and X+q_m are P5-free; the displayed endpoint reverse stars hold; R3, R534, R542, and R549.

## Scope
Explicit smallest-counterexample reduction. The unrecovered historical endpoint-trap hypothesis is replaced by accepted R549, and the former hard-frame shorthand is expanded into literal data.

## Applicability
Use before R146 witness selection or pair-deletion fragmentation. The second branch is a fixed aligned gate normal form, not an anonymous hard six-cell.

## Persistence
Retain matching labels, the tested tight trimer order if collision occurs, exact bad seam and two witnesses; or in aligned branch all displayed gate turns and the P6 q_1,q_0,b,z,q_m,q_{m-1}.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Payment may end at a singleton signed-support descendant rather than immediate closure. The aligned P6 alone is not a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
In the transitive matching-height four-cell, the triangle on {a,b,c} has edge heights M(ab)=M_R, M(ac)=M_S, M(bc)=M_L. Hence exactly the three orders abc, acb, bac are tight. Fix any tight order (u,v,w). Since H-z=(u,v,w) sqcup Q is still the same literal exact deletion cover, the tail extension (u,v,w,z) cannot be tight together with Q; therefore (v,w,z) is bad and R3 gives (z,w,v) tight. Similarly (z,u,v,w) cannot be tight together with Q, so (z,u,v) is bad and R3 gives (v,u,z) tight. Now if the P-to-Q side seam (v,w,q_0) is bad, R3 gives (q_0,w,v) tight; together with (z,w,v) this is a same-polarity two-witness collision on tested dimer (w,v), with witnesses q_0,z, so R542 applies. If the Q-to-P side seam (q_m,u,v) is bad, R3 gives (v,u,q_m) tight; together with (v,u,z) this is the dual collision on (v,u), with witnesses q_m,z. This proves the first branch. Assume therefore that both P-side seams are tight for all three tight orders. Reading the three orders gives bcq_0, cbq_0, acq_0 and q_mab, q_mac, q_mba tight. At q_0, bcq_0 and cbq_0 imply by R3 that q_0cb and q_0bc are bad, so the M_L edge bc cannot be the fully outgoing bottom gate supplied by the R549 extreme matching gate existence theorem. Hence the complementary M_L edge az is fully outgoing: q_0az and q_0za are tight. Also acq_0 makes q_0ca bad, so the M_S edge ac cannot be the fully outgoing middle gate. By R534 exactly one M_S edge is fully outgoing and the complementary one fully incoming; therefore bz is fully outgoing at q_0 and ac fully incoming. At q_m, q_mab and q_mba make the M_R edge ab fully outgoing, hence it cannot be the fully incoming top gate; the complementary M_R edge cz is therefore fully incoming. Since q_mac is tight, its reversal caq_m is bad, so ac cannot be the fully incoming M_S edge. R534 therefore makes ac fully outgoing and bz fully incoming at q_m. In particular q_0bz and bzq_m are tight. The standing endpoint reverse stars give q_1q_0b and zq_mq_{m-1} tight. Thus the consecutive turns of (q_1,q_0,b,z,q_m,q_{m-1}) are q_1q_0b, q_0bz, bzq_m, zq_mq_{m-1}, all tight, proving the explicit aligned P6 and the dichotomy.
