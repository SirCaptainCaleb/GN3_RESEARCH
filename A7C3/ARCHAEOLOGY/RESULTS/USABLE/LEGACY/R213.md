# R213 — Six-exterior cross-rail terminal-payment double channel

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the six-exterior slack-one face, fix any accepted R208 singleton root H=K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e}. The two cross-rail diagonal balanced pairs D_K^L with D_Q^R and D_Q^L with D_K^R each satisfy the Historical-Support Terminal-Reversal Payment hypotheses: one signed dimer is a historical signed support, the opposite-polarity partner is a terminal dimer of the entire disjoint opposite rail. Therefore, before any unrelated recompletion, either diagonal may be chosen to launch a lawful payment continuation yielding either a spanning two-cover or strict decrease of its initial mass-four signed-support mass. Thus every R208 root carries two distinct branch-free cross-rail payment channels tied to its four physical rail-end dimers.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the six-exterior face; an R208 singleton root H=K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} is fixed.

## Scope
Order-free payment corollary of the six-exterior common-witness terminal-dimer rectangle. No maximin root, order-ten, or simultaneous-floor synchronization hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use when converting the static R208 cross-rail rectangle into actual signed-pair motion. Each diagonal can be paid immediately, without first steering to a floor or classifying a return corridor.

## Persistence
The chosen diagonal signed supports and the terminal-reversal/extension certificate produced by R162 persist as historical ancestry. The original literal singleton root or opposite rail is not asserted current after payment unless separately retained.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that both payment descendants coexist, that the two strict descents interact, that either descendant reaches mass two in one step, or that a strict payment gives exterior-size descent or closure.

## Proof
Fix the R208 root and its four signed terminal dimers. R208 states that D_K^L and D_Q^R have opposite polarity, as do D_Q^L and D_K^R, and that K and Q_e are vertex-disjoint tight paths. For the first diagonal take the historical signed support S=D_K^L and the disjoint tight path T=Q_e with terminal dimer D_T=D_Q^R. These are exactly the R162 hypotheses, so before unrelated reset the terminal-reversal payment gives either a spanning two-cover or strict decrease from the initial mass four. For the second diagonal take S=D_Q^L and T=K with terminal dimer D_T=D_K^R and apply R162 identically. The two applications are separate lawful choices; no simultaneous descendant is claimed.
