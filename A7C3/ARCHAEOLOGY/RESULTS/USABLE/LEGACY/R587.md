# R587 — Three-vertex tau=1 wrap branches that force an R566 collision

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let D be proper, W=V(H)\D=S disjoint-union C with S,C nonempty, and suppose X=H[D union S] has exactly three vertices and is Hamiltonian. Let T be a literal exact two-cover of H-D with exactly one selected S|C transition, and write x for the unique S-endpoint of that transition. Let Q=(q_0,q_1,q_2) be a tight Hamilton path of X, and classify Q by the wrap tetrachotomy R579.

(CS) Suppose the unique transition is x y with y in C. If x=q_2 and the branch is SW2 or DF, then both Q=(q_0,q_1,q_2) and (q_1,q_0,q_2) are tight Hamilton paths of X ending at x with distinct penultimates q_1,q_0; R566 gives a same-oriented two-tail collision on (y,x). If x=q_1 and the branch is SW1, then rot1=(q_2,q_0,q_1) and the R3 reverse of beta, (q_0,q_2,q_1), are tight Hamilton paths ending at x with distinct penultimates q_0,q_2; R566 again gives a two-tail collision.

Thus the explicitly eliminated CS branch/role pairs are exactly (x=q_2, SW2), (x=q_2, DF), and (x=q_1, SW1). Outside an already-recorded R566/R435 output, R570 then says every surviving CS cell has ending-family size at most one. Equivalently the surviving labelled pairs are: DW with any x; SW1 with x in {q_0,q_2}; SW2 with x in {q_0,q_1}; and DF with x in {q_0,q_1}. No additional elimination is claimed for x=q_0 from this fixed Q.

(SC) The exact head/tail dual holds: whenever the corresponding displayed wrap certificates give two Hamilton starts at x with distinct successors, R566 produces a two-head collision on (x,y).

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); pc(H)>2; |X|=3; X Hamiltonian; T exact two-cover of H-D with tau=1. Uses R579 wrap tetrachotomy, R566, R3.

## Scope
Order-free in |H|. The only size restriction is that the absorber D union S has three vertices. No R24, transitive four-cell, or global-longest hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use when a tau=1 cell has a three-vertex absorber. For a fixed Hamilton order Q, eliminate only the explicit branch/role pairs certified above; do not infer the formerly claimed extra endpoint eliminations at x=q_0 without another Hamilton order.

## Persistence
Retains D,S,C, the 3-vertex absorber, the chosen Q and its R579 branch, the crossing endpoints x,y, and the two explicit Hamilton paths that collide when the listed pairs occur.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not eliminate the surviving labelled branch/role pairs. Does not claim exact Hamilton-path uniqueness, only R570 at-most-one outside explicit interaction outputs. Does not derive any extra x=q_0 elimination by informal symmetry or by reversing Q unless that reversed order is separately certified tight. Does not treat |X|>=4.

## Proof
Since |X|=3 every tight triple on X is a Hamilton path of X. Let Q=(q_0,q_1,q_2). The R579 wrap seams are alpha=(q_2,q_0,q_1) and beta=(q_1,q_2,q_0). R3 fixes the three reverse orders: reverse(Q)=(q_2,q_1,q_0) is bad, reverse(alpha)=(q_1,q_0,q_2) is tight exactly when alpha is bad, and reverse(beta)=(q_0,q_2,q_1) is tight exactly when beta is bad. In CS, if x=q_2 then Q ends at x with penultimate q_1, while reverse(alpha) ends at x with penultimate q_0 exactly in SW2 and DF; these branches therefore give two endings and R566 yields a two-tail collision. If x=q_1, then alpha ends at x when alpha is tight and reverse(beta) ends at x when beta is bad; both occur exactly in SW1, again giving a two-tail collision. If x=q_0, the only displayed order ending at x is beta when beta is tight; reverse(Q) is always bad, so this fixed Q supplies no second ending at q_0 in any branch. Therefore the explicitly eliminated CS pairs are exactly (q_2,SW2),(q_2,DF),(q_1,SW1). The remaining labelled pairs are DW with any x; SW1 with x in {q_0,q_2}; SW2 with x in {q_0,q_1}; DF with x in {q_0,q_1}. Outside an already-recorded R566/R435 output, R570 gives ending-family size at most one in each surviving CS cell. The SC statement is the exact head/tail dual, using starts instead of endings and the head half of R566. No reversal of Q is assumed tight unless separately certified.
