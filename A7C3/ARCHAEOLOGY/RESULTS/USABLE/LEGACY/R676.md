# R676 — Every internal X block carries a same-frame collision-P4 two-sided packet

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten sigma_G(T)=0 five-cell frame and let one X-dimer cell be an internal degree-two block on an oriented exact-cover rail T, with predecessor Q endpoint y^- and successor Q endpoint y^+. If the block is G_3={a,c}, orient it in T as S=(a,c) or S=(c,a). In the first orientation choose the fixed R649 head witness w=b; in the second choose w=z. Then (w,S) is tight. Because the predecessor transition gives (y^-,S) tight, R38 gives a same-tested-order same-head collision on S with witnesses w,y^-. Because the successor transition gives (S,y^+) tight, the four-vertex order (w,S,y^+) is itself a tight Hamilton P4. Thus the same internal G_3 block carries simultaneously a predecessor collision and a successor labelled P4, both retaining one fixed complementary-X witness w. Dually, if the internal block is G_4={b,z}, orient S=(b,z) and take w=a, or S=(z,b) and take w=c. R649 gives the fixed tail sign (S,w). The predecessor transition (y^-,S) concatenates directly to the Hamilton P4 (y^-,S,w), while the successor transition (S,y^+) gives an R38 same-tail collision with witnesses y^+,w. Hence every degree-two X block in the c_X=2 Internal-X shape and in every c_X=3 minimum shape carries a literal two-sided collision/P4 packet in one common exact cover; the P4 uses three X vertices and the Q endpoint on the opposite side of the block, while the collision uses the other adjacent Q endpoint.

## Hypotheses
H is in the accepted crossed R621/R665 sigma=0 minimum frame. One X cell is a degree-two contiguous T-block. Use accepted R649 and R38; P4 tightness is direct concatenation of the fixed source sign with the opposite-side T transition.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Sigma=0 minimum five-cell geometry. No payment, R176, R436, R5, R168, R169, R224, R533, or generic R358.

## Applicability
Use in every Internal-X structural regime from accepted R670. Keep both adjacent Q endpoints and the common complementary-X witness. Do not spend either edge separately before testing splices that use the collision and P4 together.

## Persistence
Retain T and rail orientation, internal X block S, predecessor and successor Q endpoints y^-,y^+, the fixed complementary-X witness w, the R38 collision certificate on one side, and the explicit ordered Hamilton P4 on the other.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the collision or P4 alone closes H. Does not apply to degree-one X blocks. Does not assert descendant payment representatives preserve the same T rail; the theorem is a same-frame graph-intrinsic packet.

## Proof
Let S be the oriented internal X-dimer block and y^- S, S y^+ the two literal adjacent T transitions. First take G_3={a,c}. If S=(a,c), accepted R649 gives the fixed head-sign turn (b,a,c); put w=b. If S=(c,a), R649 gives (z,c,a); put w=z. Thus in either case (w,S) is tight with w in the complementary X-dimer. The predecessor triple (y^-,S) is tight because it is consecutive in T and y^- lies in a Q cell. Since y^- differs from w, R38 gives a same-tested-order same-head collision on S with witnesses w and y^-. The successor triple (S,y^+) is also consecutive in T. Therefore the four-vertex sequence (w,S,y^+) has its two consecutive triples (w,S) and (S,y^+) tight and is a Hamilton P4. For G_4={b,z}, if S=(b,z) take the fixed R649 tail witness w=a, while if S=(z,b) take w=c. Then (S,w) is tight. The predecessor triple (y^-,S) concatenates with (S,w) to the Hamilton P4 (y^-,S,w). The successor triple (S,y^+) is a second tail-sign certificate on the same tested S, distinct from w, so R38 gives the same-tail collision. All certificates occur in the same fixed T and retain both adjacent Q endpoints. This proves R676.
