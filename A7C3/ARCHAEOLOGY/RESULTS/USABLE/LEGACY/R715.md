# R715 — The two first-inward Q vertices Hamilton-couple through every X-root

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted R594 synchronized transitive frame with transitive P4-free four-cell X and Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4. Put u=q_1 and s=q_{m-1}. Then for every x in X the five-set E_x={u,s} union (X\{x}) supports a tight Hamilton P5. Indeed the six-set E=X union {u,s} has six five-subsets. The two five-subsets E\{s}=X union {u} and E\{u}=X union {s} are non-Hamiltonian: a P5 on X union {u} together with the disjoint tight suffix Q[2,m] would two-cover H, and a P5 on X union {s} together with Q[0,m-2] would do the same. Accepted R195 says every six-set has at least four Hamilton five-subsets. Since two of the six are known non-Hamiltonian, all four remaining five-subsets must be Hamiltonian. Those four are exactly E_x for x in X. Thus every X-root admits a graph-intrinsic Hamilton P5 coupling the first inward vertices from the two ends of Q.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted R594; Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4; accepted R195.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 nonlocal bridge theorem. Uses both ends simultaneously and no crossed-middle-gate classification. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,capture,payment,generic R358,or R436.

## Applicability
Use whenever a one-sided route is fenced by R613/R626. The five-sets {q_1,q_{m-1}} union (X-{x}) carry opposite-end Hamilton P5s for every root x. Bare support Hamiltonicity is not closure; preserve an actual Hamilton order and especially endpoint roles of q_1,q_{m-1}. Combine with R582 or source-specific substitution rather than treating this as anonymous path-growth currency.

## Persistence
Retain the six-set X union {q_1,q_{m-1}}, the two certified non-Hamiltonian five-subsets X+q_1 and X+q_{m-1}, and for each x in X at least one Hamilton P5 order on {q_1,q_{m-1}} union (X-{x}). Orders need not coexist in one chosen representation but their support Hamiltonicity is graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe Hamilton orders, endpoint positions of q_1 or q_{m-1}, or simultaneous compatibility of the four P5 orders. Does not itself yield a spanning two-cover or a current exact-cover cut. Does not assert the four P5s are distinct as ordered paths.

## Proof
Put u=q_1 and s=q_{m-1}. If X+u had a Hamilton P5 P, then P and Q[2,m] would be vertex-disjoint tight paths whose supports partition V(H), contradicting pc(H)>2. Thus X+u is non-Hamiltonian. Dually X+s is non-Hamiltonian because a P5 there together with Q[0,m-2] would two-cover H. Now apply accepted R195 to the six-set X union {u,s}. At least four of its six five-subsets are Hamiltonian. Two specific five-subsets, X+u and X+s, are not. Therefore the other four must all be Hamiltonian. They are exactly {u,s} union (X-{x}) for x in X.
