# R768 — Two probes of one trimer force a P4 or a dual-packet Hamilton P5

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let K=(a,b,c) be any vertex-simple tight trimer, and let y,z be two distinct vertices outside V(K). Then at least one of the following holds. (P4) One of the four-sets V(K) union {y} or V(K) union {z} supports a tight Hamilton P4. (P5+DUAL) Neither four-set supports a Hamilton P4; then the five-set V(K) union {y,z} supports a graph-intrinsic tight Hamilton P5, and simultaneously both reverse boundary dimers of K are two-witness short-carrier packets with the same witness pair {y,z}: D_L=(b,a) is tail-signed by both y and z, while D_R=(c,b) is head-signed by both y and z. Hence each boundary packet is independently eligible for the accepted R696 geometric END/FREE/SANDWICH refinement, but the Hamilton P5 should be retained before any such packet is spent. The P5 and both packet ledgers coexist graph-intrinsically; later capture/payment descendants from the two packets are alternative continuations.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K=(a,b,c) is a vertex-simple tight trimer; y,z are distinct and lie outside V(K). Use accepted R700 and accepted R522.

## Scope
Order-free parent compression of two-probe trimer geometry. No crossed frame, five-cell classification, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,generic R358,R436,R312,Reverse Ear,or computation. It strengthens the geometric reading of accepted R700 by exposing the accepted R522 Hamilton P5 in the joint no-P4 branch.

## Applicability
Use whenever a tight trimer is probed by two distinguished outside vertices. Do not treat the R700 DUAL-CP branch as merely two payment packets: before spending either packet, retain the Hamilton P5 on the trimer plus both probes. In the inward-anchor frame, a BAD R176 trimer K lies in H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}, so probes q_2,q_{m-2} give either an inward-anchor P4 or a tight P5 containing K and both inward anchors, with both reverse-boundary packets still available.

## Persistence
Retain K, probes y,z, the two four-cell probe outcomes, and in the joint no-P4 branch the exact R522 Hamilton P5 order/certificate together with both R700 reverse-boundary tested dimers, both witness identities, polarities, carrier K, and complementary singleton identities. Later R696/R527/payment descendants need not coexist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the P5 is spanning or current in a particular path cover. Does not claim the P5 alone closes H or moves a selected cut. Does not assert END/FREE/SANDWICH outcomes for the two boundary packets coexist in one cover, since they use separate support-deletion covers. Does not claim later packet descendants coexist. Does not supersede R700 or R522; it is their source-preserving composition.

## Proof
Fix K=(a,b,c) and distinct outside probes y,z. Apply accepted R700. If one of its two R518 probe tests yields a Hamilton P4 on V(K) union {y} or V(K) union {z}, this is the P4 outcome. Otherwise both tests are in their no-P4 branches. R700 then gives its DUAL-CP conclusion: D_L=(b,a) is tail-signed by both y,z and D_R=(c,b) is head-signed by both y,z, with K as the common trimer carrier. Because neither four-set K union {y} nor K union {z} has a Hamilton P4, accepted R522 applies directly to the same tight trimer K and the two probes y,z and yields a literal graph-intrinsic tight Hamilton P5 on V(K) union {y,z}. The R522 construction does not alter the graph-intrinsic R700 sign certificates, so the P5 and both dual packet ledgers are simultaneously retained. This proves the stated P4-or-(P5+DUAL) alternative.
