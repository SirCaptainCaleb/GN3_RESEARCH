# R345 — All-internal first-contact return admits a same-middle fixed-turn target rotation

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal first-contact return branch and the provisional source-rail endpoint floor of R344. Thus, unless a spanning two-cover has already occurred, one literal residual rail U=(u_1,...,u_r), r>=4, yields an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor with singleton supports {u_1},{u_r}, while the original first-contact turn is J=(p,x,z). Since x is distinct from u_1,u_r, boundary antisymmetry makes exactly one of (u_1,x,u_r) and (u_r,x,u_1) a tight proper turn J_U. Applying accepted R249 to the R344 floor gives a fixed-turn lineage on J_U whose outer target pair is exactly {u_1,u_r} and whose initial clock is Psi_{J_U}=(2,0,0). Hence the all-internal branch admits a role-sensitive target rotation from the original first-contact outer pair {p,z} to the endpoint pair of a literal source residual rail, while retaining the same physical middle x. The old and new fixed-turn lineages are alternative historical continuations, not simultaneous current states.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R338 all-internal branch; provisional R344 endpoint floor; accepted R3 and R249.

## Scope
Order-free fixed-turn recycling consequence of the all-internal R329 return cell.

## Applicability
Use when the original target-return cell does not expose x as an endpoint and one wants a new fixed-turn target tied to literal source geometry rather than an anonymous floor.

## Persistence
The physical middle x and target vertices u_1,u_r are graph-intrinsic. The R344 floor ancestry enters the chosen J_U lineage as in R249. The source rail U and original turn J need not remain current after entry.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not identify the two target pairs, does not assert both fixed-turn lineages coexist, does not preserve the original capture of x as endpoint-capture credit in the new clock, and does not prove a monotone under repeated target rotation.

## Proof
By R344, outside closure there is an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor with singleton supports {u_1},{u_r}, where u_1,u_r are the physical endpoints of a literal source residual rail and x is disjoint from them. Boundary antisymmetry R3 says exactly one complete-reversal orientation of the triple {u_1,x,u_r} is tight; denote the resulting proper turn by J_U. Its unordered outer pair is exactly {u_1,u_r}, the given floor pair. Accepted R249 applies to any ancestry-bearing mass-two floor and any third vertex, so entering the fixed-turn lineage on J_U with no pre-entry endpoint-capture credit gives Psi=(2,0,0). The middle vertex is physically the same x as in the original first-contact turn J=(p,x,z), while the target pair has rotated to the endpoints of U. All representative fences are those of R249/R344.
