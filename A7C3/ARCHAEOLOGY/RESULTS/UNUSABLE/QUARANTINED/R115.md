# R115 — Zero-excess terminal-dimer repetition trichotomy and odd-cycle sign defect

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the R102/R112 zero-excess kernel, let the two terminal physical dimers of each Q_x,Q_y,Q_z be counted by shield incidence. A physical terminal dimer can repeat across two shields only as one of e_xy={alpha,z}, e_yz={x,gamma}, e_zx={y,beta}. The number of such cross-shield repeated dimers is therefore 0, 1, or 3, never exactly 2. In the 3-repeat case the endpoint partitions are Q_x:{e_xy,e_zx}, Q_y:{e_xy,e_yz}, Q_z:{e_yz,e_zx}. Since the two terminal dimers of each Q_i have opposite R112 polarities, the three-repeat triangle cannot assign the same polarity to each repeated physical dimer in both of its shield occurrences. Hence at least one repeated physical dimer carries opposite-polarity certificates across its two shield occurrences; choosing witness delta on one occurrence and epsilon on the other gives distinct witnesses, so R38 yields the opposite-polarity same-support exclusivity/strict two-ended signed-path interaction on that dimer.

## Hypotheses
Standing R102 simultaneous zero-excess kernel, fixed orderings Q_x,Q_y,Q_z as in R112, and accepted same-support interaction theorem R38.

## Scope
Pure endpoint-incidence and sign-parity consequence of the three R102 four-sets. No use of R113 or payment descendants.

## Applicability
Use to split the zero-excess fan analysis by endpoint-support repetition count. The 3-repeat branch has an unavoidable stronger opposite-polarity same-support interaction, while the 0/1-repeat branches are the only places where such a cross-shield sign defect can be avoided.

## Persistence
The four-sets W_x,W_y,W_z are graph-intrinsic in R102. Once Q_i orderings are fixed, their terminal dimers and R112 polarities are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert which of the three shared dimers has the sign defect, does not assert simultaneous currentness of any payment descendants, and does not claim that the strict two-ended signed path alone forces 5+5.

## Proof
Write W_x={y,alpha,z,beta}, W_y={x,alpha,z,gamma}, W_z={x,beta,y,gamma}. Their pairwise intersections are W_x∩W_y={alpha,z}=e_xy, W_y∩W_z={x,gamma}=e_yz, and W_z∩W_x={y,beta}=e_zx. Each Q_i=(q1,q2,q3,q4) has two disjoint terminal physical dimers {q1,q2} and {q3,q4}, whose union is W_i. Therefore any physical dimer appearing terminally in two different Q_i must lie in the corresponding two-set intersection, hence must be the unique e_ij for that shield pair. Suppose e_xy repeats. Then because e_xy is one terminal part of the partition of W_x, the other terminal part of Q_x is W_x\\e_xy=e_zx. Likewise the other terminal part of Q_y is W_y\\e_xy=e_yz. If a second repetition occurs, say e_yz also repeats, then Q_z contains e_yz and therefore its complementary terminal part is W_z\\e_yz=e_zx; since Q_x already contains e_zx, e_zx repeats as well. The same argument is cyclic, so two repetitions imply three. Thus the repetition count is 0,1,or3. In the 3-repeat case, the terminal partitions are exactly Q_x:{e_xy,e_zx}, Q_y:{e_xy,e_yz}, Q_z:{e_yz,e_zx}. By R112 the left and right terminal supports of each Q_i have opposite polarities. Assume for contradiction that every repeated physical dimer has the same polarity in its two shield occurrences. Then each e_ij has a well-defined global polarity, and each of the three displayed shield partitions requires its two incident e-dimers to have opposite global polarities. This would give a two-coloring of the 3-cycle e_xy-e_yz-e_zx-e_xy with adjacent vertices opposite, impossible. Hence some repeated physical dimer e has opposite polarities in its two shield occurrences. R112 supplies both witnesses delta and epsilon on each occurrence. Taking, for example, the delta certificate from one occurrence and the epsilon certificate from the other gives opposite polarities on the same physical support with distinct witnesses. R38 therefore invokes same-support exclusivity and the accepted strict two-ended signed-path interaction on e.
