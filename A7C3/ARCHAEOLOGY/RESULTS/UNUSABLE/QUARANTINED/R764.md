# R764 — Each X vertex in a minimum inward-anchor cover selects a unique P4 block or a unique GOOD/BAD seam

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-order-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame with m>=6 and a minimum exact two-cover T of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}, so sigma_G(T)=0. Write G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}, and let F_T be the contracted five-cell forest of T. Pending R763 applies. Fix an arbitrary vertex r in X={a,b,c,z}, and write S(r) for the unique contiguous T-block among {G_3,G_4} that contains r.

(P4-BLOCK) If S(r) has degree two in F_T, then S(r) is an internal X block. Pending R763(1) therefore supplies an explicit source-labelled tight Hamilton P4 whose middle physical dimer is exactly the oriented block S(r). In particular that P4 uses r.

(SEAM) If S(r) has degree one in F_T, let xi(r) be the unique selected T-state incident with S(r). Accepted R176 applied to xi(r) against the five-cell reference cover splits as follows. GOOD: both seam tests are good, and the output is a single spare-free cross-witness balanced pair whose selected state is xi(r), hence incident with S(r) and therefore with the X-layer of r. BAD: a seam test is bad, and the output is a reverse-trimer support K(r) determined by that blocked seam together with two alternative inward pins q_2 and q_{m-2}. The trimer K(r) uses at least one vertex of S(r), hence uses either r or the unique X-partner of r in S(r).

In the no-P4 source-sink residue of pending R763(2), both X blocks have degree one. Thus every r in X has a well-defined incident seam xi(r). Vertices in the same X block share that seam; vertices in opposite blocks determine the two distinguished endpoint transitions of the residue, namely the unique transition that leaves the oriented G_4 source block and the unique transition that enters the oriented G_3 sink block.

In particular, if a distinguished vertex x in X is supplied by an independent ledger (for example the mixed partner of a first-inward MIXED-SIBLING certificate), that ledger selects either a labelled P4 through x's X-block or a unique GOOD pair / BAD reverse trimer incident with x's layer, without a further shape classification and without an R436 step.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed beyond-order-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame of R665/R667, m>=6, with T an exact two-cover of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} in the minimum case sigma_G(T)=0. Use accepted R176 and pending R763.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 parent consumer of the R763 compression. It assigns to every X vertex a unique labelled P4 or a unique incident GOOD/BAD seam, before R436 or R312. No R5, R168, R169, R224, R533, R350, generic R358, R436, Reverse Ear, shape-specific A-F splice, or computation.

## Applicability
Use on any minimum H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} recompletion after pending R763. To consume a transported mixed-sibling ledger, instantiate r as the mixed partner x. To compare two good anchors, instantiate at both. Prefer a BAD seam at the distinguished layer: one reverse trimer, two inward pins. Do not invoke R436 or R312. Conditional on fresh review of R763.

## Persistence
Retain T, F_T, the block S(r), its degree, the labelled P4 in the P4-BLOCK branch, and in the SEAM branch the unique incident state xi(r) with its GOOD/BAD label, pair or reverse trimer, and both inward pin options. An independently supplied identity of r remains historical ledger data.

## Proof
Work in a minimum exact cover T of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} as in accepted R665/R667, so each of G_3 and G_4 is one contiguous oriented dimer block of T. Pending R763 applies to T.

Fix r in X and let S(r) be its T-block. The contracted degree of S(r) in F_T is either two or one, because R763(2) records that a no-P4 residue has both X degrees equal to one, while R763(1) treats any degree-two X block as internal.

If that degree is two, R763(1) produces a labelled Hamilton P4 whose middle dimer is the oriented block S(r)=(s_0,s_1). Both vertices of S(r) lie on that P4, and r is one of those vertices.

If that degree is one, F_T has a unique edge at S(r). The corresponding selected T-state xi(r) is the unique intercell transition incident with S(r). Apply accepted R176 to the two path covers (five-cell reference, T) at this selected cross state. The persistence clause of R176 has two mechanisms. If both seam tests are good, the pair is the cross-witness pair of xi(r) and does not use an inward spare; this pair is incident with S(r) by construction. If a seam test is bad, R176 produces a reverse-trimer support from that blocked seam and uses a spare vertex outside W as the opposite singleton. The blocked seam of xi(r) is the junction of S(r) with its unique neighboring cell, so the reverse trimer uses a vertex of S(r). The available spares are q_2 and q_{m-2}. This is the BAD branch at r.

The last paragraph of the statement is only instantiation: a ledger that names a vertex of X selects the corresponding P4-block or seam. No property of that ledger is used in the case split.
