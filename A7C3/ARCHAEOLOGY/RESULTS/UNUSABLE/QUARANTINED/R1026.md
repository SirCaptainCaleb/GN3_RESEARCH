# R1026 — The monotone minimum five-cell residue has exactly 43 labelled forests and at most 256 directed rail candidates

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed five-cell frame R862/R865 with D={q_2,q_{m-2}}, W=H-D, and cells G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], G_2=Q[m-1,m], G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}. Let T be an exact two-cover of W with sigma_G(T)=0 and c_X(T) in {2,3}, and work in the monotone branch of accepted R867, outside the explicit R435 reverse-state, reverse-trimer/contact, or vertex-simple tight-cycle outputs. Take the contracted forest F_T with its cell labels; a configuration is its labelled edge set, and a rail reading is displayed monotone in Q whenever that rail contains at least two Q-blocks.

Then F_T is exactly one of 43 labelled configurations: 25 with c_X=2 and 18 with c_X=3, distributed over the six R866 shapes as A:6, B:4, C:12, D:3 and E:12, F:6. The complete list is:

Every Q-Q adjacency in the list is one of G_0G_1, G_1G_2, G_0G_2, realized respectively by the named R867 transitions u q_3, q_{m-3}s, u s.

For the directed rail refinement, the labelled edge set together with the two X-dimer internal orientations does not always determine T. In shapes A, B, and E, every nonsingleton rail contains at least two Q-blocks, so R867 forces its direction; these contribute 6+4+12=22 configurations with one admissible component orientation. In shapes C, D, and F, exactly one nonsingleton rail contains at most one Q-block, so that rail has two admissible directions while the other rail is Q-monotone and forced; these contribute 12+3+6=21 configurations with two admissible component orientations. Since each of the two physical X-dimer blocks has at most two internal orientations, the entire residue has at most 4(22+2*21)=256 explicitly named ordered rail-pair candidates.

## Proof
Let Q={G0,G1,G2} with fixed order G0<G1<G2 and X={G3,G4}. By R866, every contracted configuration in this residue is one of six two-component path-forest shapes A--F, with no X-X edge. By R867, on every oriented rail containing at least two Q-blocks, the Q labels must occur in increasing order. A configuration is the labelled edge set, so reversing a displayed rail does not create a second configuration.

We count each R866 shape directly.

A: X-Q-Q-X plus an isolated Q. Choose the isolated Q in 3 ways. The two labelled X vertices can occupy the two endpoint roles in 2 ways. Hence 3*2=6.

B: Q-Q-X-Q plus an isolated X. Choose which X is isolated in 2 ways. On the long rail the three Q labels are forced in increasing order, and the nonisolated X can occupy either of the two gaps between consecutive Q blocks. Hence 2*2=4.

C: Q-Q-X plus Q-X. Choose the lone Q belonging to the two-vertex component in 3 ways. Choose which labelled X belongs to that component in 2 ways. On the three-vertex component, the remaining X may attach to either endpoint of the Q-Q edge in 2 ways. Hence 3*2*2=12.

D: Q-Q plus X-Q-X. Choose the Q lying between the two X vertices in 3 ways; the other two Q vertices form the Q-Q component. Swapping the two X endpoint roles does not change the labelled edge set because both X-Q edges are present. Hence 3.

E: X-Q-X-Q plus an isolated Q. Choose the isolated Q in 3 ways. For the two remaining ordered Q vertices, choose which X lies between them in 2 ways and choose whether the other X attaches at the low-Q or high-Q outer endpoint in 2 ways. Hence 3*2*2=12.

F: Q-X-Q plus Q-X. Choose the Q on the two-vertex component in 3 ways and choose which X lies on that component in 2 ways. The remaining X is then forced between the other two Q vertices. Hence 3*2=6.

Thus there are exactly 6+4+12+3+12+6=43 labelled edge sets, with 25 in c_X=2 (A--D) and 18 in c_X=3 (E--F). The constructive choices above give exactly the displayed list in the statement, so the list is exhaustive and duplicate-free. R867 also gives the only possible Q-Q transitions: G0G1, G1G2, G0G2, realized by u q3, q_{m-3}s, and u s respectively.

It remains to count directed rail readings correctly. If a nonsingleton rail contains at least two Q-blocks, R867 fixes its direction because the Q labels must increase. Singleton rails have no directional choice. Therefore every A, B, and E configuration has a unique admissible component orientation. Their number is 6+4+12=22.

In each C configuration, the Q-X component contains only one Q-block; in each D configuration, the X-Q-X component contains only one Q-block; and in each F configuration, the Q-X component contains only one Q-block. That component is nonsingleton but R867 imposes no direction because there are not two Q labels to compare. Its reversal gives the second admissible component orientation. The other component has at least two Q-blocks and is forced. Hence every C, D, and F configuration has exactly two admissible component orientations, for 12+3+6=21 such configurations.

Finally each physical X-dimer G3 and G4 has at most two internal orientations. Thus each component-oriented configuration lifts to at most four ordered rail-pair candidates. The total is therefore at most

    4*(22 + 2*21) = 256.

This also pinpoints the error in retired R685: the extra component-direction bit in shapes C, D, and F was omitted. No realizability is asserted, so seam constraints may reduce the 256 candidates further.
