# R399 — Triple-deletion cap-fan versus persistent-middle interaction dichotomy

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten and fix any physical triple T={a,b,c}. Then, unless H has a spanning two-cover, one of the following holds. (A) For some t in T, with opposite pair D=T\\{t}, the vertex t is internal in every exact two-cover of H-D; hence R398 supplies the source-trimer-localized R389 four-birth interaction fan at middle t. (B) No such persistent middle exists. Then for each t in T there is an exact two-cover of H-(T\\{t}) exposing t as an endpoint, and R395 converts the three endpoint returns into a three-cap fan of alternative exact two-covers over the common residue H-T. Thus every physical triple admits a fixed-residue structural normalization: persistent-middle interaction on one opposite-pair fiber, or three labeled restoration caps over the triple deletion.
