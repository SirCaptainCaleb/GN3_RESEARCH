# R333 — First-contact mirror trimer forces dual endpoint-recompletion cut crossings

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the literal first-contact mirror-trimer branch of accepted R329. Thus one minimum three-cover of H has the form H=J sqcup U sqcup V with J a tight trimer on {p,x,z}, where D={x,z} is the actual captured carrier/exterior crossing and E={p,x} is a genuine old-carrier adjacency. Then both endpoint deletions force role-sensitive exact-return crossings. For every exact two-cover of H-p, at least one selected state crosses the physical cut D | (U union V). Dually, for every exact two-cover of H-z, at least one selected state crosses E | (U union V). Hence the rooted mirror contact carries two compulsory recompletion portals over the same current residual rails: one incident with the captured-crossing vertex class and one incident with the old-carrier-edge vertex class.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and the R329 branch-(ii) current mirror-trimer root H=J sqcup U sqcup V is given, with J on {p,x,z}, D={x,z} the captured crossing and E={p,x} the old carrier edge.

## Scope
Order-free exact-cover consequence of the role-sensitive R329 mirror root. No endpoint exposure, capture transport, or General Tight-Path Contact is used.

## Applicability
Use to classify or consume the R329 mirror-trimer branch. Any singleton-deletion recompletion must physically cross out of the surviving role-bearing boundary dimer; neither boundary role can remain an isolated two-vertex rail.

## Persistence
J,D,E and the current residual rails U,V are fixed in the source mirror-root representative. The forced-cut assertion quantifies over every exact two-cover of the corresponding singleton deletion; individual selected crossings are representative-relative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the two endpoint-deletion exact covers are simultaneous or share a selected crossing. Does not preserve D or E as a selected dimer in the recompleted cover. Does not imply a spanning two-cover or endpoint exposure by itself.

## Proof
Write J on {p,x,z} with boundary dimers D={x,z} and E={p,x}. By R5, H-p has exact two-covers; fix any exact two-cover P sqcup Q of H-p. Suppose it selects no state crossing D | (U union V). Then the vertices x,z cannot connect in P sqcup Q to any vertex of U union V. If xz is not selected, x and z lie in two separate components, and since U union V is nonempty there are at least three components, contradiction. If xz is selected, D is an isolated two-vertex component. Because P sqcup Q has exactly two components, D is one rail of the exact two-cover, contradicting R24, which says both rails in any exact one-vertex-deletion two-cover have order at least four. Therefore every exact two-cover of H-p crosses D | (U union V). The argument for H-z and E={p,x} is identical.
