# R293 — Every internal carrier-dimer puncture forces a fixed-trimer nonquiet interaction

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K=(k_1,...,k_m) be any proper tight path of order m>=4. Fix an internal adjacent dimer D={k_i,k_{i+1}} with both neighboring carrier vertices present. Then either H has a spanning two-cover, or the exact D-deletion frame contains a selected crossing {b,z} from the surviving carrier K-D to the exterior E=V(H)\\V(K), and the carrier-side endpoint b has a valid historical capture certificate through that crossing by R37. Independently, the same physical vertex b anchors a capture-free fixed-trimer nonquiet pair family: using five exterior vertices guaranteed by R169, two fresh signed spokes incident with b and one disjoint fixed exterior trimer can be constructed by R3/R41/R8; R39 gives two direct balanced-pair births and R160 makes the second nonquiet. Thus each internal puncture canonically supplies a puncture-indexed historical capture at b together with an independent nonquiet interaction packet rooted at the same physical carrier vertex b.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a proper tight path; D is an internal adjacent dimer with both neighboring carrier vertices present; accepted R5,R21,R37,R169 and capture-free sign/pair machinery R3,R8,R39,R41,R160.

## Scope
Corrected puncture theorem preserving the lawful original-frame capture while separating it from fresh interaction supports.

## Applicability
Use when puncture identity or the captured carrier vertex matters. The interaction packet may be indexed by the same b, but no downstream consumer may treat its spokes as captured without a separate theorem.

## Persistence
D, the exact deletion frame, selected crossing and historical capture at b persist under R5/R21/R37. The separately built spoke signs, fixed trimer and R160 interaction are graph-intrinsic/historical. Their common physical index b persists.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not invoke R155; does not claim capture ancestry on the fresh spokes or fixed trimer; does not make the deletion frame and later pair descendants simultaneous; the nonquiet interaction is not itself closure.

## Proof
R5 gives an exact two-cover of H-D. R21 applied to the punctured retained path K-D supplies a selected carrier/exterior crossing {b,z}. The two neighboring carrier vertices of D are its natural opposite sign witnesses; the crossing meets at most one, so R37 captures the carrier-side endpoint b in this original exact frame. This proves the puncture-specific ancestry. Separately R169 gives at least five vertices outside K. Choose distinct d_1,d_2,y,r,s in E. For each i, R3 gives a d_i-middle tight trimer on {b,d_i,y}, and R41 signs S_i={b,d_i} with witness y. R8 gives a tight trimer T on {y,r,s}; choose for each S_i an opposite-polarity terminal dimer of T and use R39. R160 makes the second birth nonquiet. No capture is transported to S_i.
