# R497 — R495 one-hole fork is native-clause isolated without a second cross-arm cut

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain the head-fork (HF) output of R495. Thus F_H=X_H sqcup A^+ sqcup P^o is the literal spanning three-cover with X_H=(b_0,...,b_q,a_0), A^+=(a_1,...,a_p), P^o=(z_1,...,z_{m-1}), and the two candidate-aware fork mates are H_A=(a_1,a_0,b_q) and H_P=(z_1,a_0,b_q), exact complements of the bad one-hole seams s_A=(b_q,a_0,a_1) and s_P=(b_q,a_0,z_1). Then the bridge-interior prong H_P is intrinsically isolated from any native R173/R453 sibling that leaves X_H intact: in the native X_H-to-P^o R453 clause, H_P is the first mate literal, and accepted R461 says every physical two-hole sibling clause containing its exact complement s_P must break both source endpoint adjacencies b_q->a_0 in X_H and z_1->z_2 in P^o. Since m>=3, both states are defined. In particular the displayed one-hole candidates C_A and C_P of R495 preserve b_q->a_0 and therefore cannot themselves be, nor can any surgery confined to A^+ or P^o while leaving X_H intact produce, a sibling clause complementary to H_P. If p>=2, the same barrier applies to H_A: the native X_H-to-A^+ R453 clause has first mate literal H_A, so any sibling clause containing s_A must break both b_q->a_0 and a_1->a_2. Thus when both fork arms are nontrivial, consuming either head-fork mate through a physical mate cycle requires a genuine two-component cut that abandons the common live source state b_q a_0 and the corresponding arm boundary state. Dually, in the tail-fork (TF), the guaranteed bridge-interior mate T_P=(a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) is the second mate literal of the native P^o-to-X_T splice and any sibling containing its complement t_P=(z_{m-1},b_q,a_0) must break both z_{m-2}->z_{m-1} and b_q->a_0; if q>=2 the ancestral mate T_B likewise requires breaking b_{q-2}->b_{q-1} and b_q->a_0. Therefore the R495 one-hole forks are not themselves clause progress: one-hole candidate identity is valuable, but any R173 consumer of the guaranteed bridge-interior prong must introduce a new cross-arm surgery cutting the common chord-adjacent source state b_q a_0. This strengthens R496 from a currentization incompatibility to a proposal-level two-cut fence.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); R495 head- or tail-fork output; accepted R453 and R461. For the ancestral head arm require p>=2, and for the ancestral tail arm require q>=2. The bridge-interior arms are unconditional because m>=3.

## Scope
Order-free negative-result fence on the candidate-aware R495 fork. It preserves the exact live three-cover, the common source state b_q a_0, both fork witnesses, and the physical R453 clause identities.

## Applicability
Use when attempting to turn R495(HF)/(TF) directly into an R173/R463 mate cycle. Any continuation that leaves the live X_H/X_T chord-adjacent state b_q a_0 intact cannot complement the guaranteed bridge-interior fork mate. Search instead for a surgery that simultaneously cuts b_q a_0 and the relevant P^o endpoint adjacency; when the ancestral arm is nontrivial the same two-cut requirement applies there.

## Persistence
Retain F_H or F_T, the common directed state b_q a_0, P^o endpoint adjacency, the relevant ancestral-arm adjacency when nontrivial, the exact fork mate and its antisymmetric one-hole seam, and the native R453 ordered-pair provenance. The conclusion is a necessary-condition fence on any future physical sibling proposal.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say no cross-arm sibling exists. Does not apply R461 to a singleton ancestral arm. Does not convert a one-hole candidate into a two-hole clause. Does not claim the fork mates are useless; it identifies the additional cut any physical mate-cycle consumer must pay.

## Proof
Head fork. In F_H the ordered X_H-to-P^o splice has source left endpoint states b_q a_0 and right initial state z_1 z_2. By accepted R453 its first mate literal is exactly (z_1,a_0,b_q)=H_P. The exact antisymmetric complement is (b_q,a_0,z_1)=s_P. Accepted R461 applies because X_H and P^o are nontrivial and says any literal spanning two-path proposal whose two-hole mate clause contains s_P must break both b_q a_0 and z_1 z_2. Both displayed R495 one-hole proposals C_A and C_P retain b_q a_0; moreover any surgery confined away from X_H retains it. Hence none can be such a sibling. If p>=2, apply the identical argument to the ordered X_H-to-A^+ splice: its first mate literal is H_A=(a_1,a_0,b_q), complement s_A, and R461 requires breaking b_q a_0 and a_1 a_2. Tail fork. In F_T, consider P^o-to-X_T. The second native mate literal is (a_0,b_q,z_{m-1})=T_P, whose complement is t_P=(z_{m-1},b_q,a_0). R461 requires breaking z_{m-2}z_{m-1} and b_q a_0. If q>=2, the B^--to-X_T splice has second literal T_B=(a_0,b_q,b_{q-1}), so complementing t_B requires breaking b_{q-2}b_{q-1} and b_q a_0. This is exactly the stated proposal-level cross-arm fence.
