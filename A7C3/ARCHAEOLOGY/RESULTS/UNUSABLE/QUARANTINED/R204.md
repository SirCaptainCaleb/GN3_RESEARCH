# R204 — Five-exterior replacement grid has no support-only complementary-swap implication

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
The support-incidence conclusions of R169 and R192 do not by themselves imply any complementary carrier replacement. More precisely, for every integer m>=6 there is an abstract support-incidence model with disjoint sets K,E, |K|=m and |E|=5, in which K is marked as a globally-longest tight support, E is non-Hamiltonian, every carrier vertex k has exactly four successful exterior replacements (E\\{z}) union {k}, yet every complementary carrier swap (K\\{k}) union {z} is declared non-Hamiltonian. All declared proper tight supports satisfy the R169 exterior-size floor. Therefore any proof of complementary replacement synchronization must use information beyond the support-membership conclusions of R169/R192.

## Hypotheses
Only the consumer-level support-incidence conclusions of accepted R169 and R192 are being modeled. This is a logical-independence statement about those conclusions, not an assertion that the abstract model is realizable by a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament or by a smallest counterexample.

## Scope
Contract-level negative result for the arbitrary-order five-exterior equality program.

## Applicability
Use as a proof-design fence: four-of-five replacement saturation alone cannot justify K-k+z tightness. A successful equality-face closure must add orientation, exact-cover currentness, ancestry, signed-pair synchronization, or another invariant coupling the exterior replacement to its carrier complement.

## Persistence
The model is purely combinatorial support-incidence data; no representative-relative state is asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not construct an actual Strong Level-(1) tournament, does not show the equality face is realizable in a smallest counterexample, and does not refute any stronger theorem using additional hypotheses beyond R169/R192.

## Proof
Fix m>=6. Let K={k_1,...,k_m} and E={e_1,...,e_5} be disjoint. Mark K as a tight globally-longest support and mark E non-Hamiltonian. Choose any function h:K->E. For each k in K and z in E with z != h(k), mark the five-set (E\\{z}) union {k} as supporting a tight P5; for z=h(k) leave that replacement non-Hamiltonian. Thus every R192 row has exactly four successful holes. Declare every complementary m-set (K\\{k}) union {z}, k in K, z in E, non-Hamiltonian. No relation in the support-membership conclusion of R192 links these declarations to the exterior-side P5 declarations. Finally, the marked support K has exactly five exterior vertices, while each marked P5 has m exterior vertices, which is at least six; hence every marked proper tight support satisfies the R169 exterior-order-five floor. The resulting incidence model satisfies the stated consumer-level conclusions while containing no complementary carrier replacement at all. Therefore such a replacement cannot follow from those support-level conclusions alone.
