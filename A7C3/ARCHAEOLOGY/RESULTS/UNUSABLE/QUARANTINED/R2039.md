# R2039 — QUARANTINED: consecutive-source anchor insertion packet

**Status:** CLAIMED / re-audit required
**Source:** Slack workspace R2039

## Former statement

For a literal exact two-cover rail containing consecutive internal sources

`(...,x,t,r,y,...)`,

the former result claimed that counterexample status forces at least one of `(x,t,C)` and `(A,r,y)` bad, equivalently one of `(x,C,t)` and `(r,A,y)` tight.

## Trust failure

The former proof is not valid under R3/R887.

1. It replaces `t->r` by `t->C->A->r` and claims the internal turns `(t,C,A)` and `(C,A,r)` follow by cyclic rotation from source turns `(A,t,C)` and `(A,r,C)`. R3 provides only complete-reversal antisymmetry; these rotations are independent comparisons.
2. It claims bad `(x,t,C)` yields tight `(x,C,t)`, but R3 yields the complete reverse `(C,t,x)`. Likewise bad `(A,r,y)` yields `(y,r,A)`, not automatically `(r,A,y)`.

Thus both the detour and the advertised cap conversion are unsupported. No counterexample to the theorem statement has yet been certified here, so the result is quarantined rather than marked invalid.
