Proof of the side-lock elimination: assume no (HT), (REV), or (BP). By R393 choose an exact H-D cover exposing z_1. R489 shows it cannot expose z_1 at a rail tail, so z_1 is a rail head in that cover. Similarly some exact cover exposes z_{m-1}, and R489 forces that exposure to be at a rail tail. Again by R489, z_1 is always a head and z_{m-1} always a tail. Hence z_1 and z_{m-1} lie on different fixed supports. The path P^o joins them, so some selected adjacent state of P^o crosses those two supports. Apply accepted R176 to the fixed exact two-cover of W and the literal source path cover T; this yields (BP), contradiction. Therefore one of (HT),(REV),(BP) must occur.

## Proof
Assume HT, REV and BP all fail. By R393, z_1 is an endpoint in some exact cover F_L of W=H-D. If it were a tail, R489(L) would give HT or REV. Hence it is a head. Likewise z_{m-1} is an endpoint in some F_R and must be a tail by R489(R). Because BP is assumed absent, R408 applied between F_L and any other exact H-D cover shows z_1 can never become internal; similarly z_{m-1} can never become internal. R410 and then R411 place every exact H-D cover in one fixed support partition and supply endpoint-Hamiltonian flexibility on each support. The failure of HT/REV plus R489 means z_1 can never be a tail and z_{m-1} can never be a head, so they are universal oriented endpoint anchors. Suppose they lie in the same fixed support S. Since both are endpoints in every Hamilton path on S appearing in an exact cover, no third vertex of S can be an endpoint. But R411 says every vertex of S is an endpoint of some Hamilton path on S, so S has exactly the two vertices z_1,z_{m-1}. On a two-vertex support both orientations are vertex-simple tight Hamilton paths. Combining (z_{m-1},z_1) with any Hamilton path on the other fixed support, as licensed by the fixed-support factorization, gives an exact H-D cover with z_1 as a tail, contradicting R489 under the assumed failure of HT/REV. Therefore z_1,z_{m-1} occupy different fixed supports. Now T=A^+ sqcup P^o sqcup B^- is a literal path cover of W and P^o begins in one fixed support and ends in the other. Along P^o there is therefore a first adjacent state xy crossing the support partition. Taking R to be any fixed exact H-D two-cover and T as above, accepted R176 gives a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair, which is BP. Contradiction. Finally, R488 says that under failure of HT its two ancestry-bearing four-sets are both Hamiltonian, so the DP4 packet is retained along this reduction.
