# R395 — Endpoint-universal branch yields a triple-deletion three-cap fan

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the endpoint-universal branch of R393 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H. Fix any physical triple T={a,b,c}. Then for each t in T there exists an exact two-cover A_t sqcup B_t of the same triple-deletion residue H-T together with one designated endpoint cap for t: one of A_t,B_t has an endpoint r_t followed by a second vertex s_t such that either (t,r_t,s_t) is tight or dually (s_t,r_t,t) is tight, and reattaching t at that endpoint recovers an exact two-cover of H-(T\\{t}). Thus H-T carries three alternative exact two-cover representatives, one equipped with a lawful endpoint-restoration cap for each deleted vertex a,b,c. The three representatives need not coincide.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the endpoint-universal branch of accepted R393; T is any three-vertex set.

## Scope
Order-free same-residue reformulation of universal pair-deletion endpoint exposure.

## Applicability
Use to replace the global quantified endpoint property by a fixed triple-deletion fiber with three vertex-labeled restoration caps. This creates a common-residue interface for transfer, overlap, component-drop, or assembly arguments.

## Persistence
For each t, the exact H-T cover A_t sqcup B_t and its t-cap are representative-relative; the deleted triple T and the fact that all three cap representatives live over H-T are graph-intrinsic. The three covers are alternative and not simultaneous.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the three cap representatives have the same residual rails, lie in one transfer component, or can be synchronized. Does not itself produce a spanning two-cover, mate cycle, or interaction.

## Proof
Fix t in T. R393 gives an exact two-cover P_t sqcup Q_t of H-(T\\{t}) in which t is a rail endpoint. By R168 the rail containing t has order at least three, so deleting endpoint t leaves a tight path of order at least two. Together with the untouched opposite rail this is a two-path cover of H-T. By R5, H-T has path-cover number exactly two, so this residual cover is exact. The endpoint turn of P_t is tight and records precisely how t reattaches to that residual rail to recover the pair-deletion cover. Repeating independently for t=a,b,c yields the three cap representatives over the common residue H-T.
