# R397 — Endpoint-universal triple residues are return-multiple or terminal-collisional

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the endpoint-universal branch of R393. For every physical triple T, unless H already has a spanning two-cover, at least one of the following holds: (i) H-T has at least two distinct exact two-cover representatives; or (ii) one exact two-cover of H-T carries a same-end terminal dimer with two same-polarity restoration witnesses from T, hence the accepted R38 same-polarity two-extension collision package. Equivalently, if H-T has a unique exact two-cover up to exchange of the two rail labels, then R396 forces the terminal-collision alternative.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the endpoint-universal branch of R393; T is any physical triple.

## Scope
Order-free multiplicity/collision normalization of the R395 three-cap fan.

## Applicability
Use to reduce the endpoint-universal branch to fixed-residue exact-cover multiplicity unless a literal terminal collision is already present.

## Persistence
The multiplicity statement is about exact representatives of the fixed graph H-T. Any collision is localized to one representative and terminal dimer as in R396.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert two distinct exact covers interact, lie in one transfer component, or constitute progress by themselves.

## Proof
For fixed T, R395 supplies three endpoint-capped exact two-covers of H-T, one indexed by each deleted vertex. If at least two of these representatives differ, conclusion (i) holds. Otherwise all three caps live on one exact cover, and R396 gives either a spanning two-cover or the same-polarity terminal collision.
