# A7C3 Spare Parts

This directory contains deliberately rescued reusable mathematics from discarded Engines or genuinely useful results that do not belong to a surviving Engine. Spare-part identifiers use `S<N>` beginning at `S9001`.

Do not promote ordinary incremental research here merely because it passed audit. Prefer coherent Engine development. A part belongs here only when the surrounding Engine is being discarded or no coherent Engine applies, and the part is judged independently useful enough to preserve.

## Index

- `S9001` — [Reverse-Ear Lemma](S9001_REVERSE_EAR_LEMMA.md): a general Strong Level-(1) path-interaction theorem converting reverse-order path contacts into an exact adjacent reversal, a tight reverse trimer, or a vertex-simple proper tight cycle; includes the same-support comparison corollary and the warning that unlocalized Reverse-Ear output is ubiquitous and is not by itself a progress certificate.
- `S9002` — [Punctured Component-Drop Pair Theorem](S9002_PUNCTURED_COMPONENT_DROP.md): a generic compiler from any path-cover component drop on one proper induced residue to a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair; includes the cross-state/spare-vertex proof inline and the endpoint/internal exact-two-cover specialization.
- `S9003` — [Absorbable-Deletion Crossing Lemma](S9003_ABSORBABLE_DELETION_CROSSING.md): if the deleted set together with one remainder block is already absorbed by a tight path, every two-cover of the deletion must select a physical crossing from that block to its complement.
- `S9004` — [Wrap Selection-or-Reversal Lemma](S9004_WRAP_SELECTION_OR_REVERSAL.md): exact four-way classification of the two cyclic wrap seams of a tight path: double wrap gives a Hamilton cycle, single wrap gives one rotated Hamilton order plus one reversed seam, and double failure gives both reversed seams and a reverse P4 when long enough.
- `S9005` — [Boundary-Reversed Hamilton Dimer Absorber](S9005_BOUNDARY_REVERSED_DIMER_ABSORBER.md): opposite orientations of one boundary dimer at the available ends of two Hamilton representatives make the support universally one-vertex Hamilton-extendable.
- `S9006` — [Cycle-Opening Gain Identity](S9006_CYCLE_OPENING_GAIN_IDENTITY.md): compatible exchange gain is reduced by exactly one unit for each physical tight-cycle component that must be opened; actual path-cover improvement is raw gain minus cycle debt.
- `S9007` — [Both-Singleton Sign Degeneracy Guardrail](S9007_BOTH_SINGLETON_DEGENERACY.md): a bare opposite-sign pair of two singleton supports is formally automatic and carries no turn geometry, so productive mass-two use must retain additional ancestry or physical certificates.
- `S9008` — [Three-Cover Six-Clause Splice Fan](S9008_THREE_COVER_SPLICE_FAN.md): every ordered pair of nontrivial rails in a spanning three-cover yields an exact two-hole concatenation ledger and therefore a physical reversed-seam mate clause; all six ordered pairs are available simultaneously.
- `S9009` — [Two-Ended Hamilton Absorber Splice Lemma](S9009_TWO_ENDED_HAMILTON_SPLICE.md): inserting a Hamilton absorber between opposite residue-rail endpoints creates a spanning word with at most two attachment holes; under `pc(H)>2`, both seams must exist and both reversed attachment turns are certified simultaneously.
- `S9010` — [Signed-Path Contact and Historical Anchor Protection](S9010_SIGNED_PATH_CONTACT_ANCHOR_PROTECTION.md): a later path meeting an older signed support either preserves the old signed anchor by strict signed-side shortening or produces explicit growth, Reverse-Ear geometry, or a proper tight cycle; silent later reuse of the historical anchor is excluded.
- `S9011` — [Line-Graph Comparison Representation Theorem](S9011_LINE_GRAPH_COMPARISON_REPRESENTATION.md): represents the tight-turn relation as an orientation of `L(K_V)`, characterizes the edge-ordered subclass by acyclicity, and classifies shortest nonintegrability cycles as star/triangle/simple-cycle holonomy certificates.
- `S9012` — [Two-Ended Endpoint Replacement Lemma](S9012_TWO_ENDED_ENDPOINT_REPLACEMENT.md): one exterior vertex cannot Hamilton-replace both endpoints of a Hamilton path while the full extension remains non-Hamiltonian unless an explicit Reverse-Ear certificate appears.
- `S9013` — [Partition-Cover Deficit Identity](S9013_PARTITION_COVER_DEFICIT_IDENTITY.md): relative to a fixed physical partition, cross-class transition count equals the sum of intrinsic class path-cover numbers minus current component count plus a nonnegative representative-relative excess.
- `S9014` — [Signed-Interval Rebirth and Tight-Path Cut Memory](S9014_SIGNED_INTERVAL_REBIRTH_CUT_MEMORY.md): every maximal surviving interval of a deleted tight path remembers its immediate deleted neighbors as exact cut witnesses; first/last surviving intervals of a signed support automatically inherit or regenerate the corresponding sign.
- `S9018` — [Core-Polarity Signature Compression](S9018_CORE_POLARITY_SIGNATURE_COMPRESSION.md): each core-coordinate match-class is a clique in the Hamilton-P5 exterior-pair extension graph; independent exterior families therefore have pairwise-disjoint signatures of total weight at most three, yielding exactly seven unlabeled three-exterior normal forms.
