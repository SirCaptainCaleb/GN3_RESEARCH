# E9004 — Singleton-star prepayment extraction engine

## Theorem

Let `H` be a smallest Strong Level-(1) boundary-tournament counterexample with `|H|>10`. Fix distinct anchors `A,C`, let `B=(b_0,...,b_m)` be tight, put `X={v,p,q,r}` and

`W:=V(B)∪{v}=V(H)-{A,C,p,q,r}`,

so `G=H[W∪{p,q,r}]=H-{A,C}=X⊔V(B)`. Assume:

- `H[W]` has exact two-cover `(v)|B`;
- for each source `s∈{p,q,r}`, `H[W∪{s}]` has exact two-cover `(v,s)|B`;
- for each pair `{s,t}`, `H[W∪{s,t}]` has exact two-cover consisting of a tight trimer on `{v,s,t}` together with `B`;
- `X` has no tight Hamilton path;
- `(A,s,C)` is tight for every source `s`; and
- the retained top-fiber exact two-cover `F` of `G` has all three sources internal and belongs to the retained phase-one source packet.

Then the packet produces a legal entrance to Engine E9003, *First-source prepayment phase descent*. Moreover, in the structurally continuing bad-low-cut branch the same analysis produces a *canonical defect packet*: `X` is transitive matching-height; both spectator ends carry the universal reverse stars and full three-matching gate packets; `F` satisfies

`τ(F)≥3`, `τ=b_X+b_B-2=2b_X+d_F(v)-2`, `b_B=b_X+d_F(v)`, `d_F(v)∈{1,2}`,

with at least two B-active sources; and there is a B-active source `s` and a literal tight spanning three-forest `J` with `τ(J)=1` whose `s`-component is a singleton or an X-dimer. The packet retains the bad low-cut turn and, after the direct rooted-repair test, either a physical lower-`τ` exact cover or a source-rooted blocker trimer. The canonical defect packet is the structural output consumed by E9005. No `F△J` exchange is performed here.

Every exact two-cover of `G` has two nontrivial rails. Indeed, if one rail were singleton `{u}`, boundary antisymmetry on `{A,u,C}` gives a tight trimer on those vertices; together with the other rail it two-covers `H`, impossible.

## 1. Four-cell dichotomy

Fix one tight turn and encode one representative from each reversal pair in the order

`012,013,021,023,031,032,102,103,123,132,203,213`.

Reject an assignment whenever one of the 24 vertex orders has both consecutive turns tight. Exactly four signatures survive:

`100110010101`, `101100010110`, `110010110001`, `111000110010`.

Three are the transitive matching-height cells; in each, the six ordinary edges split into perfect matchings `M_R>M_S>M_L`, and `xyz` is tight exactly when `M(xy)>M(yz)`. The fourth cell is cyclic.

The cyclic cell has a stronger extension property: for every fifth vertex `d`, there is a Hamilton P5 on the five-set whose two endpoints lie in the original four-cell. In normalized cyclic labels `{a,b,c,z}`, if `(d,a,b)` is tight, successive failure of the candidate Hamilton words forces

`dab, adz, czd, bdz, dba, bdc, dbz, bda, cad, zda, bad`

tight, ending with the reversal of the first turn. If instead `(b,a,d)` is tight, the dual chain

`bad, cda, dcz, cdb, abd, zdb, cbd, adb, daz, adc, dab`

ends with its reversal. Either way antisymmetry is contradicted.

Apply this with `d=b_0`. A Hamilton P5 on `X∪{b_0}` has an endpoint in the source set. Together with the untouched suffix `(b_1,...,b_m)` it gives an exact two-cover `T` of `G` exposing a source endpoint. Since all sources are internal in `F`, `T` is nonidentical with `F`. Restoring `(A,C)` makes `F,T` a source-labelled common-residue portal admitted by E9003. Thus the cyclic branch already gives the asserted E9003 entrance. Henceforth `X` is transitive matching-height.

## 2. Quiet endpoints and complete matching gates

Neither `X∪{b_0}` nor `X∪{b_m}` has a Hamilton P5 unless the E9003 side portal has already appeared. At the left end, a Hamilton P5 with `b_0` as an endpoint would leave a Hamilton P4 on `X`; if `b_0` were internal, both endpoints would lie in `X`, at least one a source, and the P5 together with the untouched spectator suffix would give a source-exposing exact cover of `G`. The right end is dual.

For each `x∈X`, choose a tight trimer on `X-{x}`. If `(x,b_0,b_1)` were tight, the long path `(x,b_0,b_1,...,b_m)` together with that complementary trimer would be an exact two-cover exposing a source endpoint. Hence `(x,b_0,b_1)` is bad, so boundary antisymmetry gives

`(b_1,b_0,x)`

tight for every `x∈X`. Dually,

`(x,b_m,b_{m-1})`

is tight for every `x∈X`. These are the universal reverse endpoint stars.

We need the full gate packet. Let `X` be a transitive matching-height four-cell with `M_R>M_S>M_L`, and let `d` be a fifth vertex for which `X+d` is P5-free. Then some `M_L` edge is fully outgoing from `d`, some `M_R` edge is fully incoming to `d`, and the two `M_S` edges have opposite full polarities.

Normalize

`M_R={{t,r},{l,s}}`, `M_S={{t,s},{l,r}}`, `M_L={{t,l},{r,s}}`.

If neither bottom edge were fully outgoing, choose a reverse witness on each. The four possible witness pairs are eliminated respectively by the paired Hamilton candidates

`(l,s,r,d,t)/(s,l,t,d,r)`,

`(l,r,s,d,t)/(r,l,t,d,s)`,

`(t,s,r,d,l)/(s,t,l,d,r)`,

`(t,r,s,d,l)/(r,t,l,d,s)`.

All turns internal to `X` are fixed by matching height, while each unresolved seam occurs with its complete reversal, so one word in each pair is tight. This contradiction proves the outgoing bottom gate; the incoming top gate is its total-reversal dual.

Choose such bottom and top edges meeting at `t`, relabelling them `{t,l}` and `{t,r}`. Write

`A_0=[dts]`, `B_0=[dst]`, `C_0=[drl]`, `D_0=[dlr]`,

where brackets denote tightness. P5-freeness forces `A_0=B_0` and `C_0=D_0`; the unequal cases are killed by the one-hole words

`(l,d,t,s,r)`, `(r,d,l,s,t)`, `(t,r,l,d,s)`, `(s,l,r,d,t)`.

The two equal values cannot agree. If both are zero, their reversals are tight and the remaining seam forces `(s,l,r,d,t)` Hamiltonian. If both are one, then all four displayed turns are tight and the remaining seam forces `(s,d,r,l,t)` Hamiltonian. Therefore exactly one middle edge is fully outgoing from `d` and the complementary middle edge fully incoming.

Applying this lemma at `b_0` and `b_m` gives complete alternating gate packets at both spectator endpoints, in addition to the reverse stars.

## 3. Source-cover ledger and B-active spokes

Split the two rails of `F` into maximal consecutive `X`-blocks and `B`-blocks. Let their numbers be `b_X,b_B`, and let `τ` count selected `X|B` transition edges. Contracting the blocks leaves a two-component path forest on `b_X+b_B` vertices whose edges are exactly the transitions, hence

`τ=b_X+b_B-2`.  (1)

Since `X` has no Hamilton P4, `b_X≥2`; and `b_B≥1` because `B` is nonempty. We claim `τ(F)≥3`. If `τ=1`, then `(b_X,b_B)=(2,1)` and one X-block is an isolated whole rail. A singleton such rail must be `{v}`, because every source is internal, contradicting the nontriviality proved above; a longer X-only rail has a source endpoint, again impossible.

If `τ=2`, then `(b_X,b_B)` is `(3,1)` or `(2,2)`. In the first case one X-block is again an isolated rail. In the second, after contraction the only shapes are two disjoint `X-B` edges, an `X-B-X` path plus isolated B-block, or a `B-X-B` path plus isolated X-block. The last has an isolated X-rail. In either of the first two shapes, every terminal X-block must have `v` as its outer rail endpoint, since source endpoints are forbidden, but two distinct terminal X-blocks cannot both contain the single vertex `v`. Thus

`τ(F)≥3`.  (2)

Put `d=d_F(v)` and let `e_XX` be the number of selected X-X edges. Since the `b_X` X-blocks partition four vertices,

`e_XX=4-b_X`.

The three sources are internal and have selected degree two, so summing selected degrees on X gives

`6+d=2e_XX+τ`.

Therefore

`τ=2b_X+d-2`.  (3)

Comparing (1) and (3),

`b_B=b_X+d`.  (4)

Because `F` is a two-path forest, `d≤2`. If `d=0`, then `v` is an isolated component and hence a singleton rail, again contradicting nontriviality. Thus

`d_F(v)∈{1,2}`.  (5)

At least two sources are B-active. Otherwise two distinct sources, say `p,q`, have no selected incidence with B. Both are internal in `F`, so both have degree two inside `F[X]`. But `F[X]` is a maximum-degree-two forest on four vertices, and a four-vertex path forest with two distinct degree-two vertices is a spanning P4. Its inherited order is tight, contradicting the retained non-Hamiltonicity of `X`.

We have therefore reached the complete transitive spectator/source frame: universal endpoint reverse stars, full endpoint matching gates, the physical exact source cover `F`, identities (1)-(5), and at least two physically B-active sources.

## 4. Active-source omission or an immediate prepayment birth

Normalize the transitive matching cell as

`M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}`,

and, after an automorphism, take the left outgoing middle gate to be `{b,z}`. Put `L=b_0`, `R=b_m`. Call a Hamilton P5 on `{L,R}∪(X-{x})` *endpoint-favorable* when the positions of `(L,R)` are one of

`(0,1),(4,0),(2,1),(3,2),(3,4)`;

these are precisely the five patterns admitting the one-hole low cuts below.

The gate packet forces such a P5 omitting a B-active source except in one aligned cell. In the aligned case the only exceptional active target pair is the middle edge `{a,c}` complementary to the common gate. If no favorable P5 omits `b`, the candidates

`LRcaz, zcaLR, zcRLa, RczaL, cRLaz`

force the missing seams successively until the final word is tight, contradiction. The dual chain gives a favorable omission of `z`. Hence every active target pair other than `{a,c}` contains a favorable-omittable active source.

In the crossed case, every active target pair meeting both middle edges is eliminated by one of the chains

`RzcaL -> zcaLR -> czRLa -> LRzbc`,

`LRzbc -> bacLR -> RzbcL -> zRLcb`,

`LRbza -> RbzaL -> bRLaz -> zcaLR`,

`RbacL -> bacLR -> abRLc -> LRbza`.

Only the two middle edges themselves could remain exceptional. They do not. For active `{a,c}`, the retained endpoint cells force `zLc,zRc`; absence of an active omission forces `Rcb,Lcb,RzL`, making `(R,z,L,c,b)` endpoint-favorable. For active `{b,z}`, the dual data force `zLa,zRa,czL,czR,RaL`, making `(c,z,R,a,L)` endpoint-favorable. Thus a crossed gate always has a favorable B-active omission.

Consequently the only no-active-omission residue is aligned. There the active sources form one middle edge and the complementary middle edge is `{v,s}`, where `s` is the unique inactive source. The endpoint stars and aligned gates give literal tight paths

`Q^+=(b_1,b_0,v,s,b_m,b_{m-1})`,

`Q^-=(b_1,b_0,s,v,b_m,b_{m-1})`.

Since `|H|>10`, `Q^-` is proper in `H`. It is born directly from the retained phase-one source analysis, so it is already a legal proper-path input to E9003. This is the prepayment exit in the aligned cell; no selected-reversal transport belongs to the downstream exchange Engine.

Outside this aligned exit, fix an endpoint-favorable P5 `K_s` omitting a B-active source `s`, together with an actual selected old `F`-edge from `s` into `B`.

## 5. The five low cuts and the canonical defect forest

The five favorable endpoint patterns give spanning two-word proposals with one uncertified turn.

1. If `K_s=(L,R,x,y,z)`, use

   `(L,b_1,...,b_{m-1}) | (s,R,x,y,z)`.

   The only hole is `(s,R,x)`. If it is bad, cut `sR`.

2. If `K_s=(R,x,y,z,L)`, use

   `(s) | (b_1,...,b_{m-1},R,x,y,z,L)`.

   The only hole is `(b_{m-1},R,x)`. If bad, cut `Rx`; the three components are `(s)`, `(b_1,...,b_{m-1},R)`, and `(x,y,z,L)`.

3. If `K_s=(x,R,L,y,z)`, use the dimer `(x,s)` and

   `(b_1,...,b_{m-1},R,L,y,z)`.

   The unique hole is the all-B turn `(b_{m-1},R,L)`; if bad, cut either edge of that hole.

4. If `K_s=(x,y,R,L,z)`, use the dimer `(s,z)` and

   `(x,y,R,L,b_1,...,b_{m-1})`.

   The unique hole is `(R,L,b_1)`; if bad, cut either edge of that hole.

5. If `K_s=(x,y,z,L,R)`, use

   `(x,y,z,L,s) | (b_1,...,b_{m-1},R)`.

   The only hole is `(z,L,s)`. If bad, cut `Ls`.

If the hole is tight, the proposal is a physical exact two-cover `T` of `G` with `τ(T)≤2<τ(F)`. Hence `T≠F`, and `F,T` with the restored anchor dimer `(A,C)` form the source-labelled common-residue portal admitted by E9003. This branch therefore exits at prepayment.

Suppose instead that the hole is bad. The indicated cut leaves a literal tight spanning three-forest `J` with

`τ(J)=1`.

The component `D_s` containing the omitted B-active source is `(s)` in cases 1,2,5 and an X-dimer in cases 3,4. Moreover the bad hole is itself a packet-born E9003 certificate: by boundary antisymmetry its complete reversal is a tight trimer on the same three physical vertices. Because `|H|>10`, that trimer is proper. Thus the canonical `J` is born together with a legal first-source prepayment entrance.

This is the natural structural seam. Everything used so far is local four-/five-/six-vertex spectator geometry plus a single physical low cut. No symmetric-difference component of `F△J` has yet been examined.

## 6. Complete rooted repair and exact export

Before handing `J` to the exchange mechanism, test the one local repair at its marked B-active source `s`. A repair is declared successful only when the proposed selected graph is a collision-free directed matching and every newly created turn is tight. On any failure we retain the original physical `J` unchanged.

Suppose first that `D_s=(s)`. Both split copies of `s` are free in the matching of `J`. Choose an old `F`-incidence `e` at one split copy of `s`, and let `h` be its other physical endpoint. If the corresponding split copy of `h` is already occupied by a selected J-edge, the attempted insertion has a concrete two-edge collision at `h`; on the three physical vertices involved, boundary antisymmetry supplies the corresponding source-rooted tight reverse trimer, and the repair stops. If that split copy is free, insert `e`. Since `s` was a singleton component, this joins two distinct J-components and cannot create a cycle. The only new consecutive turn can occur at `h`. If that turn is bad, its complete reversal is a tight trimer containing `s`; retain this source-rooted blocker and revert to `J`. If the turn is tight, the result is a physical exact two-cover.

Now suppose `D_s` is an X-dimer. Let `e_0` be the old F-edge at the free split copy of `s`. If `e_0` leaves the dimer, first perform the same far-copy collision test. When the insertion is matching-compatible, it joins two J-components and is acyclic. There are at most two new turns: the turn through `s`, formed by the J-dimer and `e_0`, and the turn at the far endpoint of `e_0`. Test both. If either is bad, boundary antisymmetry gives its complete reversal as a tight trimer containing `s`; retain that blocker and revert to `J`. If both are tight, the selected graph is a physical exact two-cover.

It remains the case in which `e_0` is exactly the reverse of the selected J-dimer `d`. Let `e_1` be the other old F-incidence at `s`; because `F` is a physical path forest, `e_1` leaves the dimer. Replace

`J` by `J-d+e_0+e_1`.

Removing `d` and restoring its reverse does not change the physical dimer component, while `e_1` joins that component to one of the other J-components; thus, if matching-compatible, the selected graph is acyclic with exactly two components. The only possible matching collision is at the far split copy of `e_1`. The turn through `s` is literally the old F-turn determined by `e_0,e_1`, hence is tight. The only remaining new turn is at the far endpoint of `e_1`; if it is bad, its complete reversal is again a source-rooted tight blocker trimer. Thus this repair is physical exactly when the far-copy matching test and that final turn test both pass.

In every successful repair the resulting physical exact two-cover `T` satisfies `τ(T)≤2`: the J-dimer and its reverse lie wholly in X, and only an old F-incidence leaving the marked X-component can add a transition to the single transition already present in J. Since `τ(F)≥3`, `T≠F`; consequently `F,T`, with the restored anchor dimer `(A,C)`, form the E9003 common-residue portal. Every failed repair leaves the original physical `J` in force and retains a literal source-rooted blocker trimer together with the marked source and its old-F incidence ancestry.

Thus the phase theorem has been fully extracted before any `F△J` exchange. The cyclic four-cell gives a source-exposing common-residue portal; the aligned no-active-omission cell gives the literal proper path `Q^-`; a tight low-cut hole gives a lower-transition exact common-residue cover; a bad hole gives a proper reverse trimer; and failed rooted repair gives a source-rooted trimer. Every such birth occurs inside the retained phase-one prepayment analysis, so Engine E9003 applies once to give either a spanning two-cover of `H` or a checkpoint of phased rank strictly below the participating phase-one source checkpoint(s). Nothing here authorizes replay from a later phase-zero parent.

If structural continuation is needed, the only surviving packet after the local repair test is precisely the failed-repair canonical defect packet: transitive matching-height `X`; tight spectator `B`; source turns `(A,s_i,C)`; reverse endpoint stars and full matching gates; physical exact source cover `F` with the complete transition/degree ledger and at least two B-active sources; physical spanning three-forest `J` with `τ(J)=1`; marked B-active source `s` on a singleton or X-dimer; and retained literal blocker/source ancestry. The next mathematical operation is comparison of `F` and `J`, and that operation is deliberately outside this Engine. ∎
