  (x,c,a) is bad,                                         (WR.100)

while Y={a,b,c} is the directed comparison triangle with cyclic tight turns (a,b,c),(b,c,a),(c,a,b).

Before spending any of the four first-bad cells of Section 16, test two cyclic-tail reconstructions on the same enlarged active support V(J) union {a,b}:

  L_a=(j_0,...,d,x,a,b,c),
  L_b=(j_0,...,d,x,b,c,a).                                (WR.101)

The complete new-turn windows are respectively

  A_1=(d,x,a),   A_2=(x,a,b),
  B_1=(d,x,b),   B_2=(x,b,c).                             (WR.102)

Every other turn is inherited from J or is one of the cyclic Y-turns. Hence if either window is fully tight, the corresponding word together with the untouched literal rail B is an exact two-cover of H-{u,v}. This is a genuine codimension-four to codimension-two representative lift.

Assume both cyclic-tail rows fail. Then there is NO Hamilton path on Y union {d,x} beginning with the actual current state (d,x):

  no Hamilton P5 on Y+{d,x} starts (d,x).                 (WR.103)

Indeed, after the forced initial d,x there are only six orders of a,b,c. The suffix a,b,c is exactly L_a and is blocked by failure of its complete window. The suffix b,c,a is exactly L_b and is likewise blocked. The suffix a,c,b contains the bad reverse triangle turn (a,c,b), the suffix b,a,c contains the bad reverse triangle turn (b,a,c), and the suffix c,b,a contains the bad reverse triangle turn (c,b,a). The remaining cyclic suffix c,a,b contains the already established bad lock turn (x,c,a) from (WR.100). These six cases are exhaustive.

Thus simultaneous failure has moved the role obstruction by one PHYSICAL selected J-edge: the previous lock forbade the relevant Hamilton boundary through x->c on Y+{x}; the new lock forbids Hamilton boundary access beginning with d->x on Y+{d,x}. The literal complement B, the full J order, and both blocked two-turn windows remain retained. This is not an anonymous two-witness packet.

The first seams record additional orientation data. If A_1 and B_1 are both bad, R3 gives

  (a,x,d), (b,x,d) tight,                                 (WR.104)

so a,b are two named head witnesses on the reverse tested dimer (x,d). If A_1 and B_1 are both tight, failure of both rows forces A_2,B_2 bad, while A_1,B_1 make a,b two named tail witnesses on the CURRENT tested dimer (d,x). Mixed cells retain one current-direction continuation and one exact reverse witness. None of these refinements is paid through R523/R542 here; (WR.103) is the stronger common conclusion.

The source-lock case is the exact boundary dual, and its words may be written without ambiguity. For

  J=(c,x,d,j_3,...),                                      (WR.105)

retain the source one-sided lock that no Hamilton P4 on Y+{x} ends with (c,x). Test

  L'_a=(c,a,b,x,d,j_3,...),
  L'_b=(b,c,a,x,d,j_3,...).                               (WR.106)

Their only new turns are respectively (a,b,x),(b,x,d) and (c,a,x),(a,x,d). If either row succeeds, it again gives an exact H-{u,v} representative with literal B. If both fail, the same six-order check gives

  no Hamilton P5 on Y+{x,d} ends with (x,d).              (WR.107)

The two cyclic suffixes are the failed rows; the three noncyclic Y orders contain a bad reverse-triangle turn; and the remaining cyclic order a,b,c,x,d is blocked by the inherited source-lock turn (b,c,x).

This is a one-step propagation theorem, not yet an automatically iterable descent. The new obstruction lives on the five-support Y+{d,x}; another inward step requires a new pair of complete reconstruction words. Its value is exactly Director v40's requested strict physical movement of the boundary-role obstruction while the current J edge and literal complement B remain visible.


### 18. The minimum-transition F1G1 bypass collapses to codimension one or three exact block transfers
Specialize Section 16 to the first-first cell F1G1. Write

  A={a,b},
  P={j_0,j_1,m},
  Q={c,x}.                                                  (WR.108)

The literal source orders are the dimer A, the trimer P=(j_0,j_1,m), and the dimer Q. More structure is retained: the original complement rail

  J=(j_0,j_1,m,x,c)

Hamiltonizes P union Q, while the locked triangle path

  L=(a,b,c,x)

Hamiltonizes A union Q with the opposite Q orientation. The two first-bad seams are exactly the two missing A-to-P exterior joins. Thus this cell is a three-block Hamilton triangle A,P,Q with B as the only remaining bypass.

Among all exact two-covers T of H-{u,v}, choose one minimizing the number t of selected transitions between the four coarse cells

  A | P | Q | B.                                           (WR.109)

Section 16 gives t>=2. Suppose first that t=2. Splitting T at the two coarse transitions produces exactly four path fragments, one for each nonempty cell. Hence every coarse cell occurs as ONE contiguous T-block. The induced two-rail cover of the four block labels has exactly two edges.

The hard hypotheses from Section 16 exclude B as a one-block rail, because that is the B-quiet lift (WR.96), and exclude Q as a one-block rail, because that is the c-x dimer improvement (WR.97). Therefore a 3+1 coarse shape can isolate only A or P.

**A isolated.** Then one T-rail is exactly the dimer A. Reverse its orientation freely. Accepted R561 on the two dimer orders says that u (or v) Hamilton-extends A. Keeping the other T-rail therefore gives an exact singleton-deletion two-cover of H-v (respectively H-u). This is strict codimension-two to codimension-one progress.                          (WR.110)

**P isolated.** Then one T-rail is exactly the tight trimer P=(j_0,j_1,m). Test the two four-sets P+u and P+v. If either has a Hamilton P4, replace P by that P4 and keep the other T-rail, obtaining an exact singleton-deletion two-cover. If neither four-set has a P4, accepted R522/P537 applied to the same trimer P with fourth vertices u,v gives a Hamilton P5 on P+{u,v}; together with the untouched other T-rail this would be a spanning two-cover of H. Hence in a counterexample the isolated-trimer branch necessarily makes a codimension-one transition. This is the only step here that uses the |J|=5 singleton-middle fact essentially: P is a trimer.                         (WR.111)

It remains to consider a 2+2 coarse shape. There are exactly three support pairings:

  (B union A) | (P union Q),
  (B union P) | (A union Q),
  (B union Q) | (A union P).                               (WR.112)

Each is already an ACTUAL exact pair-deletion representative because it is the support partition of T. The first two can be normalized without any seam test: in the first pairing replace the P union Q rail order by the retained literal J; in the second replace the A union Q rail order by the retained literal L=(a,b,c,x). Thus they become respectively

  H[B union A] | J,                                        (WR.113)
  H[B union P] | (a,b,c,x),                                (WR.114)

where H[S] denotes the actual Hamilton T-rail on the displayed support S. The first transfers the restored block A into the complement side while preserving the literal current J and x->c boundary. The second transfers P into the complement side and currentizes the reverse c->x boundary on the literal locked path.

In the third pairing, the B union Q rail contains Q={c,x} as one contiguous dimer block and has exactly one coarse attachment to B, while the other rail Hamiltonizes the formerly missing support A union P. Thus this representative moves the entire Q block into the B-side and simultaneously supplies a one-edge current extension of the c-x block into B. Retain the orientation of Q selected by T, the exact B-neighbor at that attachment, and the Hamilton A union P order.                         (WR.115)

No scalar descent is claimed from the three transfers (WR.113)-(WR.115); they are finite exits from the F1G1 paired-blocked state and must be consumed in their new support locations rather than renamed as K improvement.

Therefore the ONLY F1G1 state not already leaving the cell at minimum coarse transition count is

  t>=3.                                                     (WR.116)

Equivalently, after choosing T to minimize t, the split forest has at least five fragments on only four coarse cells, so at least one of A,P,Q,B is physically fragmented in T. This is a strict excess-transition obstruction e=t-2>=1. The next F1G1 consumer should use which small block fragments: fragmentation of A or Q separates a physical dimer; fragmentation of P splits the singleton-middle trimer; fragmentation of B gives multiple literal support-transfer contacts. The present section does not classify those HIGH cells further.
