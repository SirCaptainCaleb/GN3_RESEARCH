**Summary:** The one-middle predecessor-equality residue of SV79142 is genuinely a historical atom, not automatically a current transition. Fix E={a,c}, common predecessor x, and the unique possible rank-flat middle b with tight turns (b,a,x), (x,c,b), (a,b,c). A correct final fully-anchored consumer must use current-frame data at the repeated middle: either the exact H-E cover, the selected predecessor/successor of b, or a source-visible crossing certificate.   H-E=U'|V'                                               (SA.2)

Therefore the old R434 endpoint-anchored dimers are historical descendants, not automatically current selected structures in the new frame.

### 3. What data would make replay lawful
A lawful recurrence or cancellation theorem at the stationary atom must add at least one current-frame bridge, for example:

- the exact current H-E cover together with the selected predecessor and successor of b;
- a proof that every exact H-E cover internalizing b selects one of a finite set of forced incidences;
- a source-visible crossing certificate tying the new selected incidence to an old graph-intrinsic ancestor;
- or a fixed-complement/pair-deletion rigidity theorem which synchronizes the relevant current representatives.

Absent such a bridge, one may use (SA.1) only as historical side geometry.
