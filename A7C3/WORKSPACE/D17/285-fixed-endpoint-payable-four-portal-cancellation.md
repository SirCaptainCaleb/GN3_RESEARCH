# Every phase-zero payable-four portal cancels to strict old-endpoint descent or explicit nonquiet geometry

**Workspace:** D17
**State:** established
**Key:** `fixed-endpoint-payable-four-portal-cancellation`

**Summary:** Fix an old physical endpoint pair E and its completed-endpoint ledger A_E in the fixed-E phase-zero calculus. Call a source-visible output PAYABLE-FOUR when, before any unrelated reset, it contains two physically disjoint opposite-polarity signed supports of total mass four. Accepted R514 pays such a certificate to TWO-COVER or an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor. The paid-exit return map SV57138 steers that floor back to the OLD E and re-enters through R1022 without losing A_E; the completed-macro theorem SV57422 says that a rank-flat return is not a macro endpoint, because the first appended selected-incidence episode either hits a spent endpoint and produces explicit R436 nonquiet geometry or completes a fresh endpoint and strictly lowers epsilon_E. Hence every PAYABLE-FOUR portal yields TWO-COVER, strict decrease of the old fixed-E clock Psi_E, or an explicit nonquiet portal. A proper P4 is automatically PAYABLE-FOUR via its two boundary dimers; therefore so is a P5 or longer proper path, and a universally one-vertex Hamilton-extendable four-set is PAYABLE-FOUR after adjoining any exterior vertex. Applications: the SV50987 transverse Arm-M cap pair now has its previously missing lineage bridge; both branches of SV50536 same-support Arm-M curvature are consumed modulo explicit nonquiet geometry; and every R542 packet, hence every SV77092 hard-run skip-middle P4, is no longer a silent rank-flat transport edge. This does not consume the explicit nonquiet portal itself and therefore does not claim universal strict descent at the old bottom tuple.


### 1. Fixed old endpoint pair and the correct source currency
Fix a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample \(H\) and one physical endpoint pair

  E={a,c}.

Work at a phase-zero checkpoint in the fixed-\(E\) calculus of `fixed-endpoint-completed-phasezero-macro-descent` SV57422. Retain the physical completed-endpoint ledger \(A_E\subseteq E\) and the old checkpoint value

  Psi_E=(2-|A_E|, M-2, delta_E).                         (PF.1)

The active middle, exact \(H-E\) frame, and current pair representative may change later; \(E\) and the historical ledger \(A_E\) are the coordinates to which the macro returns.

Call a graph-intrinsic output born before any unrelated reset **PAYABLE-FOUR** if its source packet contains two physically disjoint signed tight supports \(P,T\) of opposite polarity with

  |P|+|T|=4.                                              (PF.2)

The physical supports, their orders, sign witnesses, and the source packet that produced them are retained as birth ancestry. No old cover or representative is asserted current after payment.

### 2. Parent cancellation theorem
A PAYABLE-FOUR output is exactly an accepted R514 input. Thus, after choosing this birth as the productive phase-zero exit, accepted R514 gives either

  TWO-COVER,

or a certificate-retaining continuation to an ancestry-bearing balanced pair whose two active supports are singletons. In the nonclosing branch apply the paid-exit return map SV57138 with prescribed target pair equal to the **OLD** \(E\), not to any pair naturally suggested by the new geometry. Its proof is deliberately source-agnostic: the exit supports, sign witnesses, and current representative need not use \(E\). The pre-existing \(A_E\) certificates are graph-intrinsic historical facts, survive the excursion as side data, R432 steers the paid floor back to \(E\), and the R1022 entrance construction supplies a new admissible internal middle in an exact \(H-E\) frame.

At that returned checkpoint the old fixed-\(E\) clock is nonincreasing. Now use the stronger completed-macro theorem SV57422. If the return is already strict, stop. If it is rank-flat, do **not** place a macro boundary there: append the first selected-incidence episode at the rebased middle. Then either

1. the anchored endpoint is already in \(A_E\), in which case the historical singleton anchor and the genuinely later endpoint dimer give explicit accepted-R436 nonquiet growth/cycle/reversal/contact geometry; or
2. the endpoint is fresh, in which case the protected continuation either exits explicitly or returns with that endpoint added to \(A_E\), strictly lowering the first coordinate of (PF.1).

Therefore every PAYABLE-FOUR output born at a fixed-\(E\) phase-zero checkpoint has a finite ancestry-retaining continuation with exactly the following parent output alphabet:

  TWO-COVER;
  STRICT Psi_E DESCENT RELATIVE TO THE OLD E;
  EXPLICIT NONQUIET PORTAL.                              (PF.3)

The third alternative is intentionally not renamed “progress”. In particular at the old bottom tuple there is still no fictitious smaller R172 coordinate. The point is that pair payment cannot itself be a silent bottom recurrence: the only rank-flat continuation is forced immediately onto explicit nonquiet geometry.

### 3. Every proper P4 is PAYABLE-FOUR
Let

  K=(x,p,q,y)                                             (PF.4)

be any vertex-simple tight P4 born in the source packet. Its defining turns are

  (x,p,q), (p,q,y) tight.                                (PF.5)

Read the first turn as the tail-signed dimer

  L=(x,p),  tail witness q,

and the second as the head-signed dimer

  R=(q,y),  head witness p.                              (PF.6)

Because the P4 is vertex-simple, \(L\) and \(R\) are physically disjoint. They have opposite polarity and total support mass four. Hence the P4 itself contains a source-visible PAYABLE-FOUR certificate, with exact physical boundary dimers and witnesses retained.

Consequently (PF.3) applies to every phase-zero P4. Any proper P5 or longer proper tight path is covered by taking a consecutive four-vertex subpath. A proper tight cycle of order at least four is covered after choosing a cyclic break and four consecutive vertices.

### 4. Universal one-extension four-cores are PAYABLE-FOUR
Let \(S\) be a four-set such that \(S+z\) is Hamiltonian for every \(z\notin S\). Since the present smallest counterexample has vertices outside \(S\), choose one such physical \(z\). A Hamilton order on \(S+z\) is a proper P5, hence contains a consecutive proper P4. By Section 3 this is PAYABLE-FOUR.

Thus a universal one-extension four-core born at a fixed-\(E\) phase-zero checkpoint has the same output alphabet (PF.3). No separate “small-core payment” theorem is needed.

### 5. Arm-M transverse-cap curvature: the old lineage gap is closed
The transverse-cap theorem SV50987 retains two Arm-M caps \(C,K\) with

  |V(C) cap V(K)| <= 1

and proves that one cross-pair of their reverse boundary wall dimers is physically disjoint and oppositely signed. That is an explicit PAYABLE-FOUR birth in the fixed-cap parent frame.

SV50987 was written before the old-endpoint return theorem and therefore fenced itself with the statement that R514 did not yet compare the paid floor to the pre-curvature R172 checkpoint. That fence is now superseded by the composition above. Indeed SV57138 already used SV50987 as its mandatory mass-four test object: pay the cross-wall pair, steer to the old \(E\), preserve \(A_E\), and re-enter there. SV57422 then completes the equality stratum.

Hence the one-step transverse/parity-dip Arm-M return has outcome (PF.3). It is not an independent phase-zero recurrence species.

### 6. Same-support Arm-M curvature is also consumed
Retain SV50536.

**Directed-triangle branch.** SV50536 upgrades the directed triangle to a universal one-extension Hamilton four-core. Section 4 makes that branch PAYABLE-FOUR, so it has outcome (PF.3).

**Local-reversal branch.** SV50536 retains the exact local reversal

  (x,u,v), (x,v,u) tight

and the fixed-cap wall dimers \(D_L,D_R\). Before any payment choice it exhibits four direct mass-four births

  D_L <-> (u,v),
  D_L <-> (v,u),
  D_R <-> (x,u),
  D_R <-> (x,v).                                         (PF.7)

Choose any one of these births. It is PAYABLE-FOUR, so (PF.3) follows immediately.

This bypasses the old R448 exact-replay obstruction. The two R448 interactions remain true historical structure, but they are no longer needed to prove portal-free cancellation of the local-reversal cell: one may spend a wall pair instead. Exact replay of the source trimer is therefore irrelevant to the PAYABLE-FOUR continuation.

Thus every same-support Arm-M curvature output in SV50536 is now

  TWO-COVER / STRICT OLD-E Psi_E DESCENT / EXPLICIT NONQUIET PORTAL.  (PF.8)

The unresolved object is the explicit nonquiet portal itself, not same-support curvature or pair payment.

### 7. R542 and the alternating hard run cease to be silent transport
SV73681 proves that every R542-ready two-witness boundary packet already contains a literal source-visible P4 **before** its R542 payment branch is chosen. Section 3 therefore makes every such packet PAYABLE-FOUR at phase zero.

In particular SV77092 shows that every interior cell of the alternating hard-run chain contains a skip-middle P4 in the common parent. Each such cell therefore admits the old-\(E\) completed continuation (PF.3). One need not follow the alternative SV73682 middle-pair transport and build a recurrent graph on its new pair floors: the same P4 can instead be paid and steered back to the source \(E\).

Hence there is no portal-free rank-flat R542/P4 transport kernel. If a reconstruction-closed family avoids TWO-COVER and strict old-\(E\) descent, every such P4 episode must expose explicit nonquiet geometry at its completed return.

This is stronger than the earlier finite-capacity analysis of same-pair P4 replay, though those physical seam constraints remain useful for consuming the resulting portal geometry.

### 8. Arm-U stress test and the new conceptual dividing line
Arm U remains stronger at its source because its curvature is born in forest phase. SV50430 currentizes any proper-path portal and returns below the original phase-1 checkpoint by threshold A-growth, with no residual explicit-portal alternative. The present theorem does not replace that argument.

What changes is the conceptual comparison with Arm M. The old slogan

  forest-phase curvature cancels; phase-zero curvature is intrinsically hard

is now too coarse. After SV57138/SV57422, the sharper boundary is:

  source-visible geometry that compiles before reset to PAYABLE-FOUR
  versus geometry that remains only an explicit nonquiet portal.     (PF.9)

Arm-U P4/P5/four-core outputs lie on the PAYABLE-FOUR side but enjoy the stronger forest-phase consumer. Arm-M transverse caps, same-support triangles, same-support reversals, and R542 P4s also lie on the PAYABLE-FOUR side and are now cancellable at phase zero modulo the explicit portal alternative.

Thus the common abstract certificate shared by the two arms is not “curvature” and not a particular P4. It is a **source-visible lawful mass-four pair compiler**. Phase of birth selects the consumer; it no longer decides whether payment can re-enter monotonically.

### 9. Scope fence
This theorem does not consume a bare R436/R435/reverse-trimer/contact portal after (PF.3) emits one. It does not call exact replay of a known trimer progress. It does not claim old representatives remain current through payment. It does not invent a fourth coordinate below the exhausted old bottom tuple.

The theorem removes **silent pair/path transport** from the phase-zero obstruction alphabet. What remains is precisely the family-level absorption of explicit nonquiet geometry.

R24 and R5 are unused.
