# R2015 — Balanced two-dimensional transport returns every neutral/C_* obstruction to a low-transition closure

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789414749.430689`

## Statement

A balanced two-dimensional transport consumes the returned `C_*`-prefix obstruction. Maintain neutral J-state components in total transition weight zero, repair off-prefix blockers by zero-weight or opposite-weight neutral batches, and repair prefix-touching blockers by retreating the zipper frontier past one participating F-edge.

A finite monotone measure forces a physically tight earlier prefix with `tau<=1`. Unless its two zipper free copies coincide physically, the canonical closure to the fixed source endpoint has exact two-cover cardinality and `tau<=2`. In the sole coincidence `a_q=b_m=s`, the two boundary old-F edges at `s` exhaust its F-degree, so either a source gate is exposed or `s` is the middle spoke of the three-source X-block.

## Proof

Use zipper notation

`e_i=a_{i-1}->b_i` (F-only), `f_i=a_i->b_i` (J-only),

with first-positive index `r`. In the OUT-v cell the fixed far free IN copy is `b_m,in=s,in` for a source spoke `s` by R1051, and every standard prefix before `r` has transition count at most one.

For `0<=q<=r-1` and a set `S` of neutral components, let `M(q,S)` use the F-state on the first `q` steps of `C_*`, the J-state on the remaining suffix, the J-state on neutral components in `S`, and the F-state on every other neutral component. Require `S` to contain the R2009 blocker component so the canonical successor at the first-open spectator remains installed, and maintain

`sum_{D in S} w(D)=0`.

R1039 permits this balance. Every nonzero neutral weight belongs to the unique split pair `+1,-1`, so whenever one member must be toggled, toggle its opposite-weight mate in the same batch. Every such batch preserves matching cardinality and preserves the standard prefix transition ledger.

Start at `q=r-1` after the required balanced batch containing the R2009 blocker component. While `M(q,S)` is not physical, choose a bad turn, backtrack, or directed cycle.

If the blocker uses no selected F-prefix edge of `C_*`, the R2011 argument applies at the current frontier. A bad turn whose two incidences are J-like would occur in physical `J`; one whose two incidences are F-like neutral/common would occur in the original physical F-normalized pre-loss state. Hence a bad turn or backtrack avoiding the prefix is mixed and contains an F-state neutral incidence from some `E` outside `S`. Similarly, a directed cycle avoiding the prefix either contains an F-state neutral edge outside `S`, or else consists entirely of J/common edges and would be a cycle in `J`. Toggle such an `E`, together with its opposite-weight mate when necessary. The chosen blocker disappears and `S` strictly grows.

If instead the blocker uses a selected F-prefix edge `e_i`, retreat the frontier to some `q'<i`, for instance `q'=i-1`. Then `e_i` leaves the matching, so the exact chosen blocker disappears. Matching cardinality is unchanged and `q` strictly decreases; neutral balance is untouched.

The integer measure

`q + (# neutral components outside S)`

strictly decreases at every move. Therefore the process terminates. It cannot terminate nonphysically: at `q=0` there is no F-prefix, so any remaining blocker triggers the neutral repair move; and if no neutral component remains outside `S`, the state is literally `J`, which is physical. Hence some terminal `N=M(q,S)` is a tight three-forest.

Because `q<r` and the total toggled neutral weight is zero, `tau(N)` equals the standard pre-first-positive prefix count and is at most one. Its `C_*` endpoint copies `a_q,out` and `s,in` are free and cannot be touched by neutral toggles.

If `a_q!=s`, add the ordinary closure

`g_q=a_q->s`.

It raises matching cardinality by one to exact two-cover cardinality and adds at most one transition, so the result has `tau<=2`. Its remaining physical obligations are exactly the two endpoint seams and the same-rail directed-cycle possibility.

Finally suppose `a_q=s=b_m`. Then

`e_{q+1}=s->b_{q+1}` and `e_m=a_{m-1}->s`

are two distinct old-F incidences at `s`. If `q+1=m`, the edge `e_m` would be the forbidden loop `s->s`, so `m>=q+2`. R1037 gives `d_F(s)=2`, hence these two edges exhaust the old-F incidences of the source spoke. In the three-spoke X-block, an endpoint spoke has one source gate and one X-X mate, whereas the middle spoke has two X-X mates. Therefore the loop residue exposes a source gate unless `s` is exactly the middle source spoke.
