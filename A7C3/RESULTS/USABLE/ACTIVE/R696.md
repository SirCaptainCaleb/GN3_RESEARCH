# R696 — Two-witness short-carrier deletion covers refine to mate geometry, dual-witness capture, or a witness sandwich

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let K=(k_0,k_1,k_2) be a tight trimer, let S be one reverse boundary dimer of K, and let e be the complementary singleton V(K)\V(S). Assume S, in its tested orientation, has two distinct same-polarity witnesses w_1,w_2 outside K. Let T be any exact two-cover of H-V(S). Then e has T-degree one or two, and exactly one of the following geometric branches holds. (END) deg_T(e)=1. Then the R511 unique-transition normal form specialized to the trimer K gives a literal spanning two-path proposal of H with complete uncertified-turn set of size one or two; hence boundary antisymmetry gives either one forced reverse turn or a physically certified two-hole mate clause. The unique selected e-exterior state also avoids at least one of w_1,w_2, so R527 captures e in the same frame. (FREE) deg_T(e)=2 and at least one T-neighbor x of e lies outside {w_1,w_2}. Then {e,x} is a selected carrier/exterior state avoiding both witnesses. The same physical crossing can therefore be fed to R527 twice, once with witness w_1 and once with witness w_2, producing two alternative same-frame capture certificates that both designate e while retaining different exact witnesses. No coexistence of the two descendants is asserted. (SANDWICH) deg_T(e)=2 and the two T-neighbors of e are exactly w_1,w_2. Then one T-rail contains the literal consecutive order (w_1,e,w_2) or (w_2,e,w_1), so that ordered triple is a graph-intrinsic tight trimer. Each of its two selected boundary states still captures e using the other witness. Thus every two-witness short-carrier packet has, before anonymous payment, either endpoint mate geometry, a crossing simultaneously usable with either witness, or a literal tight trimer joining the two witnesses through the complementary singleton. In a hypothetical smallest counterexample, every capture branch may additionally be followed by the accepted aligned-dimer payment continuation.

## Hypotheses
Finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament H with pc(H)>2; K is a tight trimer; S is a reverse boundary dimer of K with two distinct same-polarity witnesses w_1,w_2 outside K; e is the complementary singleton; T is an exact two-cover of H-V(S). Smallest-counterexample minimality is needed only for the optional aligned payment sentence.

## Scope
Order-free geometric refinement of the accepted R542 short-carrier theorem. It uses only the literal exact deletion cover, singleton degree in a path cover, the accepted R508/R511 recompletion normal form, boundary antisymmetry, and same-frame R527 capture. It does not assume any crossed-frame, five-cell, order bound, protected-contact, or generic growth machinery.

## Applicability
Use whenever an R542-ready two-witness trimer packet appears. Inspect the complementary singleton in an exact support-deletion cover before spending the packet. Degree one exposes a one/two-hole proposal; degree two either gives a selected crossing avoiding both witnesses, allowing witness-selectable capture on one physical state, or forces the two witnesses to be the literal rail-neighbors of the singleton and hence gives a witness-sandwich tight trimer.

## Persistence
Durable parent refinement of two-witness short-carrier capture. The witness-sandwich branch is intended to be reusable when the two witnesses are distinguished physical coordinates such as opposite long-rail endpoints.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim the two R527 capture descendants coexist. Does not claim the END mate clause necessarily moves support rather than reminting a witness. Does not claim the witness-sandwich trimer alone closes H.

## Proof
Put D=V(S), W=V(H)-D, and S_0={e}. Since K spans exactly D union S_0, accepted R508 applies to every exact two-cover T of H-D and forces a selected state crossing S_0 | (W-S_0). Because S_0 is the singleton {e}, every selected state incident with e is such a crossing, and the number of such states is deg_T(e). A vertex in a path cover has degree at most two, so deg_T(e) is 1 or 2. If deg_T(e)=1, then tau(T)=1 in the R508 packet (D,S_0,K,T). Accepted R511 applies. Since K has order three, its explicit SC/CS normal forms have at most two uncertified turns: this is the m=2 specialization of the displayed R511 hole sets. Therefore there is a literal spanning two-path proposal with one or two holes. As pc(H)>2, not all holes are tight; R3 yields the reverse of the unique hole, or, in the two-hole case, the certified disjunction of their reversals. The unique selected state {e,x} contains at most one of the distinct witnesses w_1,w_2 and therefore avoids the other, so R527 also captures e in this same cover. Now suppose deg_T(e)=2, and write the two distinct rail-neighbors of e as y,z. If {y,z}={w_1,w_2}, then e is internal on its T-rail and the consecutive rail segment is either (w_1,e,w_2) or (w_2,e,w_1). Every consecutive triple of a T-rail is tight, giving SANDWICH. Moreover {e,w_1} avoids w_2 and {e,w_2} avoids w_1, so either state gives an R527 capture of e using the opposite witness. Finally suppose {y,z}!={w_1,w_2}. Because both sets have size two, at least one neighbor, say x, is not in {w_1,w_2}. Then {e,x} is a selected carrier/exterior crossing and is disjoint from both witnesses. The support S is signed with the same tested polarity by each w_i. Thus the hypotheses of R527 hold separately with the same T, S, and physical selected state {e,x}, first using w_1 and then using w_2. Orient the marker in each application so that e is the designated captured anchor. This produces two alternative valid capture certificates on the same physical crossing, with different retained witnesses. These alternatives need not coexist after actualization. The three branches exhaust deg_T(e)=1,2 and prove the theorem.
