# R578 — Reversed absorber dimer plus singleton mixed C-block spans H

## Statement

Let H be finite with pc(H)>2. Let D be proper, W=V(H)\\D=S disjoint-union C with S,C nonempty, and let X=H[D union S] be Hamiltonian. Let T be a literal exact two-cover of H-D with exactly one selected S|C transition. Write x for the S-endpoint and y for the C-endpoint of that transition.
By R560 the unique-transition normal form holds: one mixed rail carries every vertex of S together with one C-block B_C, and the other rail lies wholly in C. In particular b_C(T)=2.
Suppose in addition that X admits a boundary-reversed Hamilton dimer in the sense of R561, and that B_C={y}. Then R561 Hamilton-extends X by the exterior vertex y, yielding a tight Hamilton path of D union S union {y}. The remaining rail of T is a tight path on C\\{y}. Those two paths cover V(H), contradicting pc(H)>2.
Hence a tau=1 exact two-cover cell with Hamiltonian absorber and singleton mixed C-block cannot carry a boundary-reversed Hamilton dimer on X.


## Hypotheses

Finite tight-path system with pc(H)>2. X Hamiltonian. T exact two-cover of H-D with tau=1. R560 normal form. The R561 branch additionally assumes a boundary-reversed Hamilton dimer on X and B_C={y}.


## Scope

Order-free. Restricted to the singleton mixed C-block face of the R560 unique-transition form. Does not treat longer mixed C-blocks.


## Applicability

After R560, inspect the mixed C-block. If it is the singleton {y}, forbid an R561 reversed dimer on the absorber.


## Persistence

Retain D,S,C,T,x,y,B_C, the absorber, and the R561 dimer when present.


## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not force B_C to be a singleton. Does not treat |B_C|>=2. Does not produce the reversed dimer.


## Proof

R560 applied to a Hamiltonian absorber and a k=2 unique-transition cover yields b_C=2: the mixed rail is an S-block containing all of S, followed or preceded by a C-block B_C, and the second rail R* lies wholly in C. Assume B_C={y} and that X has a boundary-reversed Hamilton dimer (u,v). R561 supplies a tight Hamilton path Q+ of X union {y}. Since B_C={y}, every remaining C-vertex lies on R*. The pair {Q+,R*} is a literal two-cover of V(H), contradicting pc(H)>2.
