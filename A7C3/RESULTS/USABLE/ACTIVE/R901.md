# R901 — The five-cell separator budget is one conservation identity r_Y + p + delta_X = 6

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame R862 with W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}, source cells G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], G_2=Q[m-1,m], G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}, X=G_3 union G_4, Y=G_0 union G_1 union G_2, and let T be any exact two-cover of W. Use the accepted R535/R885 notation p=e_T(X), q=e_T(X,Y), r_Y=c(T[Y]), d_Y(x), c_x=c(T[Y union {x}]), and f=f(T). Define the X endpoint deficiency delta_X(T)=sum_{x in X}(2-d_T(x)), where d_T is selected degree in T.

(1) CONSERVATION. Unconditionally, with no BOUNDED hypothesis, sum_{v in W}(2-d_T(v))=4 exactly, so delta_X is precisely the number of the four rail-end slots of T occupied by X vertices, an isolated vertex occupying two slots, and 0<=delta_X<=4. Moreover
    r_Y + p + delta_X = 6.
This contains the accepted R885 identity r_Y=p+q-2 as the special substitution q=8-delta_X-2p.

(2) BOUNDED SLACK. In the BOUNDED branch of accepted R885 each unfragmented X cell contributes at least one unit to p, namely its own internal selected edge, and at least one unit to delta_X, namely the outer vertex of its forced initial/terminal block. Hence p>=2-f and delta_X>=2-f. Defining the slack s(T)=(p-(2-f))+(delta_X-(2-f))>=0, one has the identity
    r_Y = 2 + 2f - s(T).
Thus the accepted R885 bound r_Y<=2+2f is not an inequality chain but a conservation law with an explicit nonnegative defect, and the defect is physical: it counts extra X-X selected edges and extra X rail-end occupancy beyond what the unfragmented cells already force.

(3) UNIFORM MAX FACE. The MAX face at fragmentation level f is exactly the zero-slack face s(T)=0, equivalently p=2-f and delta_X=2-f. On it every coarse parameter is determined by f alone:
    r_Y=2+2f, q=2+3f;
    every fragmented marker has d_Y=2 and c=1+2f;
    each unfragmented cell has outer vertex with d_Y=0 and c=3+2f, inner vertex with d_Y=1 and c=2+2f.
Specializing: f=0 gives (p,q,r_Y)=(2,2,2) with c multiset {2,2,3,3}; f=1 gives (1,5,4) with d_Y multiset {2,2,1,0} and c multiset {3,3,4,5}, which is exactly the accepted R886 MAX3 parameter point; f=2 gives (0,8,6) with c_x=5 for all x and no selected X-X edge, which is exactly the accepted R894 MAX5 parameter point. Hence the three separately established extremal parameter points are one face read at three values of f.

(4) UNIFORM LOW FACE, PHYSICALLY. The LOW face at level f is exactly s(T)>=1, equivalently: T selects an X-X edge other than the internal edges of the unfragmented cells, OR some X vertex occupies a rail-end slot other than the outer vertices of the unfragmented cells. Combined with the accepted equivalences of R886 and R894, the parameter conditions 'some marker has c<=2' (LOW2, f=1) and 'some x has c_x<=4' (LOW4, f=2) are each equivalent to this single physical excess condition.

(5) LOW4 PHYSICAL NORMAL FORM. When f=2 there is no unfragmented cell, so 2-f=0 and s(T)=p+delta_X with no forced contribution. Therefore LOW4 holds if and only if at least one of the following occurs, and the two are not mutually exclusive:
    (LOW4-EDGE) T selects an X-X edge; since both middle-gate cells are fragmented, neither {a,c} nor {b,z} is a T-edge, so any such edge is necessarily a CROSS-CELL middle-gate edge, one of ab, az, cb, cz joining G_3 to G_4;
    (LOW4-END) some x in X has d_T(x)<=1, that is, some X vertex occupies a rail-end slot of T.
Moreover the LOW4 depth satisfies 5-r_Y = p+delta_X-1, so the coarse parameter c-value deficit is exactly the total physical excess. This replaces the parameter condition c_x<=4 by two named source-labelled physical objects, a G_3-G_4 middle-gate adjacency or an X rail endpoint, both of which retain their crossed-frame cell labels.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R862 inward-anchor five-cell frame and one exact two-cover T of W. Part (1) uses only that T is a spanning two-path forest on W. Parts (2)-(5) additionally use the BOUNDED branch of accepted R885 for the unfragmented-cell endcap rule and the identity r_Y=p+q-2, and accepted R535 for the pointwise formula c_x=r_Y+1-d_Y(x). Parts (4)-(5) cite the accepted low-marker equivalences of R886 and R894. No payment, capture, floor steering, R436, R5, R168, R169, R224, R533, R350, generic R358, finite m-enumeration, or computation.

## Scope
Noncircular crossed R24/O6 parent compression of the numerical layer of the R885 fragmentation family. Order-free and m-independent; a pure conservation identity for the five-cell separator budget together with its zero-slack face and a physical reading of the low faces.

## Applicability
Use as the arithmetic front door to the entire sigma>0 five-cell excess branch, in place of recomputing coarse parameters separately at each fragmentation level. Given f, read the MAX parameter point directly off (3) rather than deriving it; given a configuration, compute the slack s=6-r_Y-... equivalently s=p+delta_X-2(2-f) to place it. For consumers, prefer (4) and (5): the low faces are not a parameter condition but the presence of a physically named excess object, an extra X-X selected edge or an extra X rail-end slot. In particular (5) supplies the previously missing source-labelled normal form for the LOW4 leaf: attack LOW4-EDGE through crossed middle-gate G_3-G_4 adjacency geometry and LOW4-END through X-endpoint role machinery.

## Persistence
Retain T, f, the five physical source cells and all X labels, p, q, r_Y, delta_X, d_Y, c_x, the slack s(T), the identity of which X vertices occupy rail-end slots, and in LOW4-EDGE the exact cross-cell pair among ab, az, cb, cz.

Does not imply the geometric content of R870, R886, or R894: the endcap placement rule, the M0/M1/M2 skeletons, and the N0/N1/N2 skeletons are NOT consequences of this identity, which explains only the numerical layer of that family; accordingly this result is not a ground for retiring or superseding those theorems. ## Proof
SETUP. T is an exact two-cover of W, that is two vertex-disjoint tight paths spanning W. As a graph T is a forest of exactly two paths, hence has |W|-2 edges and maximum degree 2.

PART (1). Partition the edges of T by type: e(T)=p+q+e_T(Y). The induced subgraph T[Y] is a disjoint union of paths on |Y| vertices with r_Y components, so e_T(Y)=|Y|-r_Y. Since X has four vertices, |W|=|Y|+4, and therefore p+q+|Y|-r_Y=|W|-2=|Y|+2, giving p+q=r_Y+2. This is the accepted R885 identity r_Y=p+q-2.
Next count degrees. Each X-X edge contributes 2 and each X-Y edge contributes 1 to sum_{x in X} d_T(x), so sum_{x in X} d_T(x)=2p+q and delta_X=8-(2p+q). Eliminating q via p+q=r_Y+2 gives delta_X=8-p-(p+q)=8-p-(r_Y+2)=6-p-r_Y, that is r_Y+p+delta_X=6.
Finally sum_{v in W}(2-d_T(v))=2|W|-2e(T)=2|W|-2(|W|-2)=4. Every vertex has d_T in {0,1,2}, so this total of 4 is distributed over the rail-end slots: each of the two rails has two ends, and a rail of order one is a single vertex of degree 0 occupying both of its own slots. Hence delta_X is exactly the number of those four slots occupied by X vertices, and 0<=delta_X<=4.

PART (2). Work in the BOUNDED branch of accepted R885. If a cell G_i, i in {3,4}, is unfragmented then b_i(T)=1, so its two vertices lie in one contiguous T-block and are T-adjacent; that edge lies inside X and the edges arising from the two distinct cells are distinct. Hence p>=2-f. By the BOUNDED endcap rule an unfragmented G_4 block is initial on its T-rail and an unfragmented G_3 block is terminal on its T-rail; in either case the outer vertex of the block has d_T<=1 and so contributes at least one unit to delta_X. The two cells are disjoint, so delta_X>=2-f. Substituting both bounds into part (1),
  r_Y = 6 - p - delta_X = 6 - (2-f) - (2-f) - s(T) = 2 + 2f - s(T),
where s(T)=(p-(2-f))+(delta_X-(2-f))>=0. In particular r_Y<=2+2f, which is the accepted R885 bound, now with an explicit defect.

PART (3). Suppose s(T)=0, so p=2-f and delta_X=2-f exactly. Then r_Y=2+2f and q=r_Y+2-p=(2+2f)+2-(2-f)=2+3f.
Since p=2-f is already accounted for by the internal edges of the unfragmented cells, T selects no other X-X edge; in particular every fragmented marker has no X-neighbour. Since delta_X=2-f is already accounted for by the outer vertices of the unfragmented blocks, every other X vertex has d_T=2. Hence every fragmented marker has d_T=2 with both neighbours in Y, so d_Y=2, and by the accepted R535 formula c=r_Y+1-d_Y=(2+2f)+1-2=1+2f.
For an unfragmented cell, its outer vertex has d_T=1 and its unique edge is the internal cell edge, so d_Y=0 and c=r_Y+1=3+2f. Its inner vertex has d_T=2 with one edge inside the cell, hence exactly one Y-neighbour, so d_Y=1 and c=r_Y=2+2f.
Specializing f=0 gives (p,q,r_Y)=(2,2,2) and c multiset {2,2,3,3}. Specializing f=1 gives (p,q,r_Y)=(1,5,4), d_Y multiset {2,2,1,0} with the two 2s on the fragmented cell, and c multiset {3,3,4,5}: this is verbatim the accepted R886 MAX3 parameter point. Specializing f=2 gives (p,q,r_Y)=(0,8,6), no selected X-X edge, d_Y=2 and c_x=5 for every x: this is verbatim the accepted R894 MAX5 parameter point.

PART (4). By part (2), s(T)>=1 is equivalent to r_Y<=1+2f, and by definition s(T)>=1 holds exactly when p>2-f or delta_X>2-f, that is when T selects an X-X edge beyond the internal edges of the unfragmented cells, or some X vertex occupies a rail-end slot beyond the outer vertices of the unfragmented blocks. Accepted R886 states that for f=1 the condition r_Y<=3 is equivalent to some marker having c<=2, and accepted R894 states that for f=2 the condition r_Y<=5 is equivalent to some x having c_x<=4. Combining gives the stated equivalence of the parameter conditions with the physical excess condition.

PART (5). Let f=2. Then 2-f=0, so parts (2) and (4) give s(T)=p+delta_X and LOW4 is equivalent to p+delta_X>=1. If p>=1 then T selects an X-X edge; because both cells are fragmented, b_3(T)=b_4(T)=2, so a and c are not T-adjacent and b and z are not T-adjacent, whence any selected X-X edge joins a vertex of G_3 to a vertex of G_4 and is therefore one of ab, az, cb, cz. If instead p=0 then delta_X>=1, so some x in X has d_T(x)<=1 and occupies a rail-end slot. The two cases may overlap. Finally r_Y=6-p-delta_X gives 5-r_Y=p+delta_X-1 as claimed.
