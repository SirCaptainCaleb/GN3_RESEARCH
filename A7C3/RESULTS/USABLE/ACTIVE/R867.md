# R867 — Reverse-ear normalization makes every minimum five-cell Q block literal and ordered

## Statement
Retain the accepted sigma_G(T)=0 five-cell packet of R865, with original tight rail Q=(q_0,...,q_m), cells G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], G_2=Q[m-1,m], and exact two-cover T of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}. Apply accepted Reverse Ear R435 separately to each T-rail K against the original graph-intrinsic path Q. Then either a T-rail yields an explicit Q-relative reverse selected state, reverse trimer/contact, or vertex-simple tight cycle as in R435, or every Q vertex met along that T-rail occurs in strictly increasing Q-index order. In the latter monotone branch, because sigma_G(T)=0 makes each G_i one complete contiguous T-block containing all vertices of that Q interval, the literal T-order inside every Q-cell block is exactly the original Q orientation: G_0=(L,u), G_1=(q_3,...,q_{m-3}), G_2=(s,R). Consequently every intercell transition endpoint on a Q block is physically named. A Q-X transition leaving G_0,G_1,G_2 uses respectively u,q_{m-3},R; one entering those blocks uses respectively L,q_3,s. Every Q-Q transition is oriented from lower-index cell to higher-index cell and is exactly one of u q_3 (G_0->G_1), q_{m-3}s (G_1->G_2), or u s (G_0->G_2). In particular, in the c_X=2 Internal-X shape of R670, where all three Q cells lie on one T-rail and the degree-two X block separates two Q blocks, monotonicity forces the X block to replace one actual deleted-anchor gap: the rail order is either G_0-X-G_1-G_2 or G_0-G_1-X-G_2. Thus its two Q-X transition endpoints are exactly {u,q_3} in the left-gap case or {q_{m-3},s} in the right-gap case. The alternative in which that X block joins nonadjacent G_0 and G_2 is impossible outside the explicit R435 reverse/cycle output.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R865 minimum sigma=0 five-cell frame and accepted R435 Reverse Ear Lemma. The Internal-X addendum uses the finite c_X=2 forest shape classified in R670, but its path-shape fact may be read directly from the contracted T forest.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Pure path-order normalization of one exact inward-anchor deletion cover. No payment, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533, or generic R358 is used.

## Applicability
Before analyzing c_X=2/3 forest orientations, compare each T rail with Q. Treat any reverse-ear output as an explicit source-localized geometric branch. Otherwise replace every Q-cell block by its literal original Q interval and use only the named boundary vertices for all transition-splice calculations.

## Persistence
Retain Q, T, the five cell identities, literal T rail directions, all Q contacts, and any R435 reverse state/trimer/cycle certificate. In the monotone branch retain the exact original-Q order of every Q block and the named endpoints of every intercell selected state.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close an R435 reverse/cycle branch. Does not constrain the Hamilton order of the X-dimer blocks beyond their two possible orientations. Does not assert a Q-Q skip turn is itself contradictory. The Internal-X conclusion applies only to the c_X=2 degree-two-X shape with all three Q cells on one rail.

## Proof
Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), L=q_0,u=q_1,s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, and let T=T_1 sqcup T_2 be an exact cover in the accepted R865 sigma_G(T)=0 case. Fix one oriented T rail K. Both Q and K are vertex-simple tight paths. List the vertices of Q encountered along K in K-order. Accepted R435 says that if two consecutive K-contacts with Q occur in reverse Q-index order, then K already yields one of the explicit Reverse-Ear outputs: an exact reversed old state, a reverse trimer/contact at a seam, or a vertex-simple tight cycle. Therefore, unless such an output is retained, the Q vertices encountered along K occur in strictly increasing Q-index order. Apply this independently to both T rails.

Assume the monotone branch. By sigma_G(T)=0, each physical cell G_i occurs in exactly one contiguous T-block. For i=0,1,2, every vertex of G_i is a Q vertex, and the block contains all vertices of that interval. Since their K-order is increasing in Q-index, the block order is forced literally: G_0=(q_0,q_1)=(L,u), G_1=(q_3,...,q_{m-3}), and G_2=(q_{m-1},q_m)=(s,R). This also covers m=6, when G_1 is the singleton q_3.

Now read an intercell selected state incident with a Q block. If a rail leaves G_0,G_1,G_2 toward the next block, the Q endpoint is respectively the last displayed vertex u,q_{m-3},R. If it enters those blocks, the endpoint is respectively the first displayed vertex L,q_3,s. If two Q blocks are adjacent on one rail, monotonicity forces the lower-index block first. Thus G_0->G_1 selects exactly u q_3, G_1->G_2 selects q_{m-3}s, and G_0->G_2 selects u s. No reverse Q-Q orientation occurs without the already separated R435 output.

For the final c_X=2 Internal-X claim, suppose the contracted forest has one X vertex of degree two. Since c_X=2, both Q-X edges are incident with that X vertex and the third edge is Q-Q. The other X vertex is isolated. The nonisolated component therefore contains the degree-two X and all three Q vertices and is a four-vertex path. Along its actual T orientation the three Q contacts must appear G_0,G_1,G_2 in this order. The X vertex occupies one of the two gaps between consecutive Q contacts. Hence the only possible rail orders are G_0-X-G_1-G_2 or G_0-G_1-X-G_2. A purported G_0-X-G_2 adjacency would leave G_1 attached by the sole Q-Q edge to either G_0 or G_2; either orientation of the resulting four-vertex path encounters two Q cells out of increasing index order, contrary to the monotone branch. Finally the named Q-X endpoints follow from the block endpoint calculation: {u,q_3} for G_0-X-G_1 and {q_{m-3},s} for G_1-X-G_2. This proves R671.
