# R1005 — A minimum-side exact two-cover has no rail-shortening broad cut

## Statement
Let H be a finite edge-ordered complete graph with pc_inc(H)=2. Among all exact two-path covers of H choose A|B so that |A| is minimum among the cardinalities of all rails occurring in exact two-covers of H; orient A=(a_0,...,a_m) and B=(b_0,...,b_r) increasingly. Then for every selected edge a_i->a_{i+1} of A, neither of the following holds: (SOURCE ROTATION) L_A(a_i)<lambda(a_i b_0)<U_B(b_0); (TERMINAL ROTATION) L_B(b_r)<lambda(b_r a_{i+1})<U_A(a_{i+1}). Indeed either condition would rotate A|B into another exact two-cover having a proper prefix or suffix of A as one rail, contradicting the choice of |A|. If |A|>=2, neither endpoint a_0 nor a_m is insertable into an internal gap of B in the displayed B-order; hence accepted R895 gives canonical barriers beta_B(a_0), beta_B(a_m). More quantitatively, writing k=|A| and l=|B|: if beta_B(a_0)=j>=l-k, then lambda(b_j a_0)>=U_A(a_0); and if beta_B(a_m)=j<=k-2, then lambda(a_m b_{j+1})<=L_A(a_m).

## Hypotheses
Finite edge-ordered complete graph H with increasing path-cover number exactly two; A|B is an exact two-cover whose rail A has globally minimum rail cardinality among all exact two-covers.

## Scope
Pure edge-order representative-extremality normal form. No R888 minimality, R980, threshold matching, or longest-path hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use for a pair-deleted complement or any exact two-cover family when representative changes are allowed. It converts the no-broad-compatible-cut alternative into a canonical minimum-side normal form and supplies honest R895 barriers for the endpoints of the minimum rail.

## Persistence
Retain the exact A|B representative, k,l, all source/terminal broad-cut failures on A, and when |A|>=2 the two endpoint barriers on B with the size-sensitive high/low consequences.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not make the endpoints of B noninsertable into A. Does not assert the two endpoint barriers are distinct or force a two-cover of a larger ambient graph.

## Proof
Write A=(a_0,...,a_m), B=(b_0,...,b_r), k=|A|=m+1, l=|B|=r+1. SOURCE ROTATION ON A. Suppose for some i, 0<=i<=m-1, L_A(a_i)<lambda(a_i b_0)<U_B(b_0). Then C=(a_0,...,a_i,b_0,...,b_r) is increasing: all inherited turns are unchanged and the displayed two inequalities are exactly the new seam conditions. The suffix S=(a_{i+1},...,a_m) is increasing. Thus C|S is an exact two-cover of H and |S|=m-i<k, contradicting the choice of A. TERMINAL ROTATION ON A is dual: if L_B(b_r)<lambda(b_r a_{i+1})<U_A(a_{i+1}), then P=(a_0,...,a_i) and C=(b_0,...,b_r,a_{i+1},...,a_m) form an exact two-cover with |P|=i+1<k, contradiction. ENDPOINT NONINSERTABILITY. Assume k>=2. If a_0 were insertable into an internal gap of B in the displayed order, the resulting Hamilton path on V(B)+{a_0}, together with A-a_0=(a_1,...,a_m), would be an exact two-cover having a rail of size k-1, contradiction. The same argument applies to a_m, using A-a_m=(a_0,...,a_{m-1}). Hence R895 supplies beta_B(a_0), beta_B(a_m). SIZE-SENSITIVE SOURCE BARRIER. Put beta_B(a_0)=j and assume j>=l-k. R895 left-feasibility at the canonical barrier gives L_B(b_j)<lambda(b_j a_0). If also lambda(b_j a_0)<U_A(a_0), then cutting B after b_j and appending A gives the exact cover (b_0,...,b_j) | (b_{j+1},...,b_r,a_0,...,a_m), after choosing the equivalent source-rotation ordering A after the B prefix? More directly use C=(b_0,...,b_j,a_0,...,a_m) and S=(b_{j+1},...,b_r): the displayed inequalities make C increasing and |S|=l-j-1<=k-1, contradiction. Therefore lambda(b_j a_0)>=U_A(a_0). SIZE-SENSITIVE TERMINAL BARRIER. Put beta_B(a_m)=j and assume j<=k-2. R895 right-feasibility gives lambda(a_m b_{j+1})<U_B(b_{j+1}). If also L_A(a_m)<lambda(a_m b_{j+1}), then C=(a_0,...,a_m,b_{j+1},...,b_r) and P=(b_0,...,b_j) form an exact two-cover; |P|=j+1<=k-1, contradiction. Hence lambda(a_m b_{j+1})<=L_A(a_m). This proves all assertions.
