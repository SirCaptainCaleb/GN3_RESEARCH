# R2023 — six cross-rail concatenation candidates and endpoint-cap packet

## Statement

In the sharp cell, let `N` be a literal tight directed three-forest with `tau(N)<=1`, with rails

`P_i=(s_i,...,t_i)`, `i=1,2,3`.

For every ordered pair `i!=j`, the cross-rail edge `g_{ij}=t_i->s_j` gives an acyclic exact-two-cover-cardinality concatenation candidate `N+g_{ij}` with `tau<=2`. Hence there are six such candidates.

If none of the six candidates is physical, then for each ordered pair `i!=j`, R3 forces one of the two reverse endpoint caps associated to the failed seams:

- `(s_j,t_i,t_i^-)` when the left seam `(t_i^-,t_i,s_j)` exists and is bad;
- `(s_j^+,s_j,t_i)` when the right seam `(t_i,s_j,s_j^+)` exists and is bad.

Thus failure of all six candidates yields a six-clause reverse-endpoint-cap packet, one disjunction per ordered rail pair. If one seam is absent because the relevant rail is a singleton, the other cap is forced; if both seams are absent, the concatenation is automatically physical.

## Proof

For distinct rails `P_i,P_j`, the split copies `t_i,out` and `s_j,in` are free and the physical labels are distinct because the rails of a path forest are vertex-disjoint. Adding `g_{ij}=t_i->s_j` therefore joins two distinct directed paths, reducing three rails to exact two-cover cardinality. Since the joined vertices lie on distinct rails, the added edge cannot create a directed cycle.

The base satisfies `tau(N)<=1`. The single new edge contributes at most one transition, so `tau(N+g_{ij})<=2`.

If `N+g_{ij}` is not physical, audited R2021 leaves only failure of one of the two endpoint seams. Writing `t_i^-` for the predecessor of `t_i` when defined and `s_j^+` for the successor of `s_j` when defined, the possible bad seams are exactly `(t_i^-,t_i,s_j)` and `(t_i,s_j,s_j^+)`. R3 converts a bad seam into the corresponding tight reverse trimer `(s_j,t_i,t_i^-)` or `(s_j^+,s_j,t_i)`. This gives one binary cap clause for each ordered pair `i!=j`.

If one seam is absent, only the other seam can fail and its reverse cap is forced. If both are absent, there is no changed turn to violate tightness, so the cross-rail concatenation is physical.
