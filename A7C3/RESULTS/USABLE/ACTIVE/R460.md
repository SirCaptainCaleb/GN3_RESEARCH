# R460 — Native three-cover splice fan is mate-complement-free

## Statement
Let H=A sqcup B sqcup C be a literal spanning three-cover by nontrivial vertex-simple tight paths, and form the six native R453 ordered-pair splice clauses. The twelve turn literals occurring in those clauses are pairwise distinct and belong to twelve distinct boundary-antisymmetry pairs. Hence the six clauses are propositionally separable: no literal or its antisymmetric complement occurs in two different native clauses, no nontrivial resolution step is available between native clauses, and after imposing only boundary antisymmetry R3 plus the six R453 disjunctions there are 2^6 consistent local exact-one valuations obtained by choosing independently one tight literal in each clause. In particular, for every native splice literal L there is such a valuation satisfying all six clauses with L bad, so the native fan alone forces neither a specific native turn nor a contradiction. In the all-internal R359 specialization H=J sqcup U sqcup V, the two splice disjunctions used by R455 are exactly the native U-to-V and V-to-U members of this same separable R453 fan. R455 becomes productive only because R359 supplies four additional non-native shield turns sharing the relevant oriented terminal dimers, allowing whichever seam mate fires to be interpreted by R38 as a same-support collision. Thus R455 does not repair the native R173 mate-linkage obstruction: any Physical Mate-Cycle forcing still requires an additional physically certified proposal introducing an exact complementary literal, or some genuinely non-propositional structural input.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); H=A sqcup B sqcup C is a literal spanning three-cover by nontrivial vertex-simple tight paths; accepted R3 and R173. For the R455 specialization, H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample in the all-internal R359 regime and pending R453/R455 are used provisionally with exact revision IDs.

## Scope
Order-free adversarial fence strengthening the native-splice obstruction. The 2^6 valuations are local truth assignments on the twelve antisymmetry pairs appearing in the fan, not asserted realizable as complete boundary tournaments with pc(H)>2.

## Applicability
Use before any propositional, SAT-like, or R173-resolution attempt on a fixed native three-cover fan. Clause abundance alone has zero cross-clause linkage. For R455, credit the progress specifically to the imported R359 shield geometry and the R38 collision compiler, not to new mate-cycle structure among the native splice clauses.

## Persistence
The separability statement depends only on the fixed literal ordered source paths and their endpoint identities. The R455 interpretation retains the exact source currentization, the two residual-rail splice proposals, and the four R359 shield turns as physical certificate data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not construct a global Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament realizing any of the 2^6 local valuations. Does not claim the six clauses are independent after arbitrary additional graph constraints are imported. Does not invalidate R455 or say its R38 collisions are vacuous. Does not rule out R173 after component surgery, an alternate path representative, endpoint-return machinery, or another physically certified proposal supplies a complementary literal.

## Proof
Fix the literal spanning three-cover H=A sqcup B sqcup C by nontrivial vertex-simple tight paths and the six native ordered-pair splice clauses named in the statement. We first prove the separability assertion directly from the literal endpoint data of those six clauses; no theorem about how they were originally obtained is needed.\n\nTake two distinct source components X=(x_0,...,x_m) and Y=(y_0,...,y_n), with m,n>=1. The X-to-Y native concatenation has seam holes h_1=(x_{m-1},x_m,y_0) and h_2=(x_m,y_0,y_1), so its two mate literals are L^1_{XY}=bar(h_1)=(y_0,x_m,x_{m-1}) and L^2_{XY}=bar(h_2)=(y_1,y_0,x_m). Record only the source-component membership of the three coordinates. These words are YXX and YYX. Because A,B,C are pairwise vertex-disjoint, equality of ordered turn literals forces equality of their component-membership words. As X,Y range over the six ordered pairs of distinct source components, the word YXX uniquely identifies the ordered pair together with the first literal, and YYX uniquely identifies the same ordered pair together with the second literal. Hence the twelve native clause literals are pairwise distinct.\n\nNow compare their R3 reversals. The reversal of L^1_{XY} is (x_{m-1},x_m,y_0), with component word XXY. The only native literal having word XXY is the second literal in the reverse Y-to-X clause, namely (x_1,x_0,y_n). Exact equality would force x_m=x_0, impossible because X is nontrivial and vertex-simple. Likewise bar(L^2_{XY})=(x_m,y_0,y_1) has word XYY. The only native literal with that word is the first literal in the reverse Y-to-X clause, (x_0,y_n,y_{n-1}); equality again forces x_m=x_0. Therefore no native literal is the complete reversal of any other native literal. The twelve literals lie in twelve distinct boundary-antisymmetry pairs.\n\nIntroduce the tight/bad truth value of each native literal as one Boolean variable, with R3 fixing its unlisted reverse to the opposite value. Each of the six already-formed native clauses is a disjunction of two variables that occur in no other native clause, and no complement of either variable occurs in another clause. Thus there is no nontrivial resolution pivot between two distinct native clauses. More concretely, choose exactly one tight literal in each clause and declare its partner bad. Since the twelve R3 pairs are distinct, the six choices are mutually compatible and give 2^6 local exact-one valuations. Given any particular native literal L, choose its clause partner tight and L bad, then choose one tight literal arbitrarily in each other clause. Hence the native fan alone forces neither L nor a propositional contradiction. This is only a local valuation of the clause/antisymmetry data, exactly as fenced in the statement; no global tournament realization is asserted.\n\nNow reconstruct the all-internal specialization without R359, R453, or R455. Assume the specialization setup of the statement, so H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample with proper tight trimer J=(a,x,c) and fixed all-internal source currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V. Apply current accepted R920 to this same named source state. Its direct proof gives |U|,|V|>=3 and the four tight reverse shields. Writing U=(u_1,...,u_r), V=(v_1,...,v_s), the two shields relevant to U-to-V are (x,u_r,u_{r-1}) and (v_2,v_1,x), and the dual pair is available for V-to-U.\n\nBecause pc(H)=3, the literal proposal obtained by concatenating U followed by V while retaining J as the other path cannot be a spanning two-cover. It inherits every old turn of U and V and has exactly two new seam turns h_1=(u_{r-1},u_r,v_1) and h_2=(u_r,v_1,v_2). Therefore at least one hole is bad, and R3 gives exactly the native U-to-V disjunction A OR B, with A=(v_1,u_r,u_{r-1}) and B=(v_2,v_1,u_r). The V-to-U proposal gives the symmetric native clause. Thus the two directional disjunctions historically used by R455 are indeed members of the same six-clause native fan, now reconstructed directly rather than imported through R453.\n\nThe reason these two clauses become productive is the non-native shield geometry supplied by R920. If A fires, then A=(v_1,u_r,u_{r-1}) and the shield (x,u_r,u_{r-1}) are two head certificates on the identical tested oriented dimer (u_r,u_{r-1}) with distinct witnesses v_1,x, so current reconstructible R523 gives the same-support collision packet. If B fires, then B=(v_2,v_1,u_r) and the shield (v_2,v_1,x) are two tail certificates on the identical tested oriented dimer (v_2,v_1) with distinct witnesses u_r,x, so R523 gives the collision. The V-to-U direction is symmetric. Hence the extra information comes from imported non-native shields, not from any complement linkage among native clauses.\n\nConsequently the native fan itself remains mate-complement-free and propositionally separable. Any forcing mechanism that needs complementary mate linkage must add a genuinely new physically certified proposal or other non-propositional structural input. This proves every assertion of R460 using only R3, R523, and R920. No R173, R359, R453, R455, R168, R5, R24, or P22 dependency is used.
