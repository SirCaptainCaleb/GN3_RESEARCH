# R2029 — Canonical triple-exposure lower-residue packet

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace R2029

## Statement

In the retained source frame, suppose a low-transition exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` has literal rails

`(s,v)` and `Q=(t,b_1,...,b_m,r)`,

up to orienting the displayed rails as written, where `{s,t,r}={p,q,r}` and `B=(b_1,...,b_m)` is the retained nontrivial spectator path.

Then the R2025 lift at `s` forces both v-anchor caps `(s,A,v)` and `(v,C,s)` tight. Moreover both source-facing seams `(s,C,t)` and `(r,A,s)` are tight, while R2026 forces both B-facing reverse caps `(b_1,t,C)` and `(A,r,b_m)` tight.

In particular `(r,A,s,C,t)` is a tight five-vertex path through both anchors and all three source spokes, and the untouched complementary residue is exactly the retained lower cover `(v)|B`.

## Proof

Lift at the exposed source endpoint `s`. Deleting `s` from the two-vertex rail leaves the singleton `(v)`, so audited R2025 gives

`N=(A,s,C) | (v) | Q`.

First join the singleton to the anchor rail. The ordered join `v->A` is acyclic and has exact two-cover cardinality; its only possible new seam is `(v,A,s)`. If that seam were tight, `(v,A,s,C)|Q` would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`. Hence it is bad, and R3 gives `(s,A,v)` tight.

Likewise the ordered join `C->v` has only seam `(s,C,v)`. Counterexample status forces this seam bad, so R3 gives `(v,C,s)` tight.

Now apply audited R2026 to `Q=(t,b_1,...,b_m,r)`. Its two source-facing seams are

`L=(s,C,t)` and `R_s=(r,A,s)`.

Suppose `L` were bad. R3 gives `(t,C,s)` tight. Together with the retained source trimer `(A,t,C)` and the cyclic presentation `(C,s,v)` of `(v,C,s)`, this makes

`(A,t,C,s,v)`

a tight path. Removing endpoint `t` from `Q` leaves the nonempty tight path `(b_1,...,b_m,r)`, so these two paths would span `H`, contradiction. Thus `L` is tight.

Suppose `R_s` were bad. R3 gives `(s,A,r)` tight. The cyclic presentation `(v,s,A)` of `(s,A,v)`, followed by `(s,A,r)` and the retained source trimer `(A,r,C)`, makes

`(v,s,A,r,C)`

a tight path. Removing endpoint `r` from `Q` leaves `(t,b_1,...,b_m)`, again giving a spanning exact two-cover. Hence `R_s` is tight.

Therefore R2026 is forced into its third branch. Both rail-facing endpoint seams are bad:

`(C,t,b_1)` and `(b_m,r,A)`.

R3 gives the reverse caps `(b_1,t,C)` and `(A,r,b_m)` tight.

Finally the three consecutive tight trimers `(r,A,s)`, `(A,s,C)`, and `(s,C,t)` certify the tight path

`(r,A,s,C,t)`.

Its complement is exactly `{v} union B`, with retained lower cover `(v)|B`.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: `R2025`, `R2026`, R3, the retained source trimers and spectator path, and the smallest-counterexample assertion that `H` has no spanning exact two-cover.

Provisional dependencies: none.
