# R325 — Dual-clique defect identity and extremal stability

## Statement
Let B be a family of k-subsets of an r-element ground set, r>=k+1, and assume every (k+1)-set contains at most t members of B. Write b=|B|. For each (k-1)-set S let x_S be the number of members of B containing S, let mu=kb/binom(r,k-1), and define the intersection-core variance V:=sum_S (x_S-mu)^2. For each (k+1)-set U let y_U be the number of members of B contained in U, and define the union-clique deficit D:=sum_U [binom(t,2)-binom(y_U,2)]. Then V,D>=0 and the exact identity\n\nV+2D = 2 binom(r,k+1)binom(t,2) + kb - k^2b^2/binom(r,k-1)\n\nholds. Equivalently, for p=b/binom(r,k),\n\nV+2D = k binom(r,k) * [ t(t-1)(r-k)/(k(k+1)) + p - (r-k+1)p^2 ].\n\nThus the density inequality of R322 is exactly the nonnegativity shadow of two structural defects. Equality forces V=D=0; more generally any family whose density is close to the R322 ceiling must simultaneously have nearly uniform (k-1)-core occupancies and nearly maximally filled (k+1)-union cliques in the explicit quantitative sense given by the identity.

## Hypotheses
Integers r>=k+1 and 0<=t<=k+1; B is a k-uniform family; every (k+1)-set contains at most t members of B.

## Scope
Pure finite-set stability theorem refining R322. It retains both physical localization channels instead of collapsing them into a scalar density ceiling.

## Applicability
Use when a support family is close to the dual-clique density ceiling. The identity tells whether residual slack is carried by uneven (k-1)-core concentration or by underfilled (k+1)-local cells, giving two concrete structural branches to attack.

## Persistence
The occupancy vectors x_S,y_U and defects V,D are intrinsic to the set family B.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a universal small-error classification beyond the exact identity. Does not imply realizability or uniqueness of equality designs. Does not by itself produce Strong-specific path growth or two-cover closure.

## Proof
Let e=e(B) be the number of Johnson edges induced by B. Intersection decomposition gives 2e=sum_S x_S(x_S-1)=sum_S x_S^2-kb. Since sum_S x_S=kb and mu=kb/N_- with N_-=binom(r,k-1), sum_S x_S^2=V+N_-mu^2=V+k^2b^2/N_-. Hence 2e=V+k^2b^2/N_- -kb. Union decomposition gives e=sum_U binom(y_U,2)=N_+binom(t,2)-D with N_+=binom(r,k+1). Therefore 2N_+binom(t,2)-2D=V+k^2b^2/N_- -kb, which rearranges to the claimed identity. The normalized form follows from N_-=binom(r,k)k/(r-k+1), N_+=binom(r,k)(r-k)/(k+1), and b=p binom(r,k). Nonnegativity of V and D recovers R322; equality in the density ceiling is equivalent to V=D=0.
