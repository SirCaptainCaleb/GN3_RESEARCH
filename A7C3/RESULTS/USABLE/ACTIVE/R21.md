# R21 — Puncture Crossing Parent

**Status:** USABLE / ACTIVE
**Type:** theorem
**Source:** A7C2_20260829_01_THEOREM_LEDGER.md (M1)

## Statement

If a retained path is punctured and its surviving part is nonempty, every exact two-cover of the punctured graph selects a state crossing from the surviving carrier to the exterior. More strongly, the number of such crossings is at least the intrinsic path-cover number of the surviving carrier. A non-Hamiltonian survivor therefore forces at least two simultaneous crossings of the same cut.

## Scope

For any proper prescribed path removed from a historical cover, every exact two-cover of its complement selects at least the number of surviving historical runs minus two run-crossings. Restoring the prescribed path currentizes the whole packet and all these crossings in one minimum three-cover.
