# R911 — Crossed common pair-deletion concatenations force first-depth gate witness or bidirectional inward-dimer rigidity

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,t=q_3,r=q_{m-3},d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m. Then the two crossed middle-gate pair-deletion residues give two simultaneous accepted-input two-hole systems. LEFT, on H-{b,z}, the exact covers (u,L,a,c) sqcup Q[2,m] and (u,L,c,a) sqcup Q[2,m] imply the graph-intrinsic clauses (v,c,a) OR (t,v,c), and (v,a,c) OR (t,v,a). Consequently either v=q_2 is a head witness on one tested orientation of G_3={a,c}, or the reverse Q dimer D_L=(t,v)=(q_3,q_2) is tail-signed by both a and c. In the latter case accepted R873, applied one step to the left, gives either a labelled probe P4 or also makes D_L head-signed by a,c; hence both P4s (a,t,v,c) and (c,t,v,a) are tight and accepted R843 applies to every exact H-{t,v} two-cover, forcing block count >2 or a source-visible reverse seam. RIGHT, on H-{a,c}, the exact covers Q[0,m-2] sqcup (b,z,R,s) and Q[0,m-2] sqcup (z,b,R,s) imply (b,d,r) OR (z,b,d), and (z,d,r) OR (b,z,d). Consequently either d=q_{m-2} is a tail witness on one tested orientation of G_4={b,z}, or D_R=(d,r)=(q_{m-2},q_{m-3}) is head-signed by both b,z. In the latter case R873 gives either a labelled probe P4 or also tail-signs D_R by b,z, yielding both P4s (b,d,r,z) and (z,d,r,b), so R843 gives fixed-cover block/seam rigidity on every exact H-{d,r} two-cover. Thus at each side the common pair-deletion residue forces a first-depth middle-gate witness or a named bidirectional inward-Q dimer, modulo a labelled P4; no singleton-deletion endpoint rotation or generic payment is needed.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame; m>=6. Use accepted Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429 to exclude Hamiltonicity of H-{b,z} and H-{a,c}, boundary antisymmetry R3, propagation R873, same-support interaction R523, and fixed-cover bidirectional P4 consumer R843.

Preferred first-depth consequence in the crossed phase. Before using pending R905, inspect these two common pair-deletion clauses. In the dimer branch retain the tested reverse Q dimer, witness pair, R873 probe tests, both P4 orders in the no-P4 branch, and the exact R843 cover/block-count or reverse-seam certificate.

## Proof
LEFT. Delete the common crossed complementary pair {b,z}. From accepted R621, deleting b from H-z=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m] gives the literal exact cover (u,L,a,c) sqcup Q[2,m] of H-{b,z}; deleting z from H-b=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m] gives (u,L,c,a) sqcup Q[2,m]. Concatenate the two rails of the first cover in the displayed order P_1=(u,L,a,c,v,t,q_4,...,R). Every turn is certified except alpha=(a,c,v) and beta=(c,v,t). If both were tight, P_1 would be a Hamilton tight path of H-{b,z}, contradicting accepted R429, which says pc(H-{b,z})=2. Hence at least one is bad; R3 gives reverse(alpha)=(v,c,a) or reverse(beta)=(t,v,c) tight. The second exact cover similarly gives P_2=(u,L,c,a,v,t,q_4,...,R), with only holes (c,a,v),(a,v,t), hence (v,a,c) OR (t,v,a). If either first literal occurs, v is a head witness on tested orientation (c,a) or (a,c) of G_3. If neither occurs, both (t,v,c) and (t,v,a) are tight, so D_L=(t,v)=D_3 is tail-signed by probes a,c. Apply accepted R873 in its right-to-left form using K_2=(u,v,t): either one of the two probe four-sets is Hamiltonian, giving the labelled P4 branch, or D_3 also becomes head-signed by both probes. In the latter case R523 gives both P4 orders (a,t,v,c) and (c,t,v,a). Pair-Deletion Rigidity supplies nontrivial exact covers of H-{t,v}, and accepted R843 applies to each such cover, giving b_T(a,c)>2 or a source-visible reverse seam. RIGHT. Delete {a,c}. R621 gives exact covers Q[0,m-2] sqcup (b,z,R,s) and Q[0,m-2] sqcup (z,b,R,s). Concatenating the first gives P_3=(L,u,...,r,d,b,z,R,s), whose only holes are (r,d,b),(d,b,z). R429 excludes Hamiltonicity, so R3 gives (b,d,r) OR (z,b,d). The second gives (z,d,r) OR (b,z,d). If either second literal occurs, d is a tail witness on tested orientation (z,b) or (b,z) of G_4. If neither occurs, D_R=(d,r)=D_{m-2} is head-signed by b,z. Apply R873 at i=m-2: either a probe P4 occurs, or D_R also becomes tail-signed by b,z. Then R523 gives (b,d,r,z) and (z,d,r,b), and R843 gives block-count >2 or a source-visible reverse seam in every exact H-{d,r} cover. This proves both side compressions.
