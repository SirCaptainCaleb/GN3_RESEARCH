# R539 — Two-fragment maximal port deficit is exactly the unique-transition recompletion cell

## Statement
Retain an R508 packet with S of size three and literal deletion two-cover F. Let C be the complement of S in the deletion remainder. If F[C] has exactly two components and F[S] has two selected internal adjacencies, then the three S-vertices occur consecutively as one tight trimer block on a single F-rail, the other F-rail is a pure C-block, and F has exactly one selected S|C transition. Hence the packet is exactly an R511 tau(F)=1 SC/CS cell for the recompletion path Q on D union S. In the R24 notation, if c(F[B_x])=2 and e_F(A_x)=2, then A_x is the unique S-block and the pure-rail obstruction is an R511 cell with Q=K_x.

## Hypotheses
Full R508 packet; |S|=3; F[C] has exactly two components; e_F(S)=2.

## Scope
Order-free path-forest reduction.

## Applicability
Use when a three-vertex absorbable block has maximal internal adjacency and only two complementary fragments. It converts the apparent pure-rail obstruction into the exact R511 unique-transition normal form.

## Persistence
Retain D,S,Q,F, the consecutive S-trimer block, the pure C rail, the mixed C block, and unique crossing orientation.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not resolve the R511 three-hole residue and does not guarantee favorable positions of D inside Q.

## Proof
Because |S|=3 and e_F(S)=2 inside a two-path forest, the three S-vertices form a length-two path and hence occur consecutively as one maximal S-block A on one F-rail. For the induced C-forest, c(F[C])=e_F(S)+delta_F(S)-1. The hypotheses c(F[C])=2 and e_F(S)=2 therefore give delta_F(S)=1. Thus exactly one selected adjacency of F crosses S|C. Since F has two rails and F[C] has exactly two components, one C-component is adjacent to A across that unique transition on the mixed rail, while the other C-component is the entire other rail and is pure C. Hence the rail word is AB|K or BA|K with A the sole S-block and B,K the two C-blocks, and tau(F)=1. This is exactly the R511 SC/CS normal form for the retained R508 recompletion path Q on D union S.
