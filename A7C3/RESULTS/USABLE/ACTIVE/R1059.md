# R1059 — Untouched first-open spectator slot gives an off-augmenter floor-level switch

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789410664.574049`

## Statement

Retain a non-loop OUT-`v` first loss at spectator `b_j` with `j>t+1`, where `t+1` is the first R1033-open spectator position. Suppose that immediately before the loss both spectator incidences at `b_{t+1}` remain in their `J` state. Then

`{b_t->b_{t+1}, v->s} <-> {v->b_{t+1}, b_t->s}`

is a well-defined literal `K_{2,2}` matching switch at exact two-cover cardinality and transition count

`tau(F)=3`,

where `s` is the fixed source-spoke far endpoint from R1051. The new forward seam

`(v,b_{t+1},b_{t+2})`

is tight by the R1033 first-open comparison, and the edge `v-b_{t+1}` lies in neither `F` nor `J`.

The result is a floor-level recompletion candidate only. Remaining endpoint seams, component incidence, and directed-cycle avoidance are separate physical obligations.

## Proof

Let `r` be the canonical first-positive index and put `M=hat M_{r-1}`. By R1051 the corresponding OUT-`v` first-loss rectangle has far endpoint `s∈{p,q,r}`. The two free split copies of `M` are `v,out` and `s,in`, and the old same-side closure

`M+{v->s}`

has transition count one.

Let `t` be the R1033 first-open index. Since `j>t+1` and both spectator incidences at `b_{t+1}` remain in their J-state, the selected edges

`d=b_t->b_{t+1}`, `u=b_{t+1}->b_{t+2}`

are present in `M`.

The detour spoke

`h=v->b_{t+1}`

is not selected by F: R1037 gives `d_F(v)=1`, and R1051 identifies the unique old v-B edge as `v->b_j`, distinct because `j>t+1`. It is not selected by J either, because both split copies of `b_{t+1}` are already occupied by the predecessor and successor spectator incidences `d,u`. Hence `h∉F∪J`, in particular `h∉F△J`.

Compare the old closure

`C=M+{v->s}`

with

`D=M-{d}+{h,b_t->s}`.

On the four distinct split copies `b_t,out`, `b_{t+1},in`, `v,out`, and `s,in`, this is exactly the switch

`{B-B, X-X} <-> {X|B, X|B}`.

Therefore D is a matching with the same cardinality as C, namely exact two-cover matching cardinality. Its transition count rises by exactly two:

`tau(D)=1+2=3=tau(F)`.

Because `u=b_{t+1}->b_{t+2}` survives, the forward new turn is precisely

`(v,b_{t+1},b_{t+2})`.

At the first R1033-open position its comparison is `F_{t+1}->E_{t+1}`, so this seam is tight.

No claim is made that D is automatically a tight acyclic two-cover: the other endpoint turns, component/backtrack incidence, and directed-cycle question remain to be checked.
