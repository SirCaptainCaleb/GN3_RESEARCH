# R42 — Captured-anchor first-contact control

**Status:** USABLE / ACTIVE
**Type:** theorem

## Statement

After choosing one actual capture descendant and one designated quiet continuation, the captured anchor remains protected until it reaches the named target role or the first later event touching it is exposed. If a later operation uses a signed support containing that anchor, the operation cannot be a quiet unrelated epoch: an earlier contact was already exposed or the operation itself is the first contact.

## Scope

Begins only after an actual protected continuation has been chosen. It does not upgrade every historical capture to current protection and does not preserve unrelated current return geometry.

## Preferred proof

Retain the signed support whose signed endpoint is the captured anchor e and designate one continuation. A later contact away from e can only replace the retained support by a shorter signed-side descendant still anchored at e. A later proper path/support containing e is an exposed growth/cycle/reversal contact rather than a quiet reset. Thus e cannot disappear silently before the target role; the first genuine touching event is the first-contact output.
