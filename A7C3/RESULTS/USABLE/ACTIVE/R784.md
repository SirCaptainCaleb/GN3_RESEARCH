# R784 — A raw first-inward R547 hinge does not license Complete Floor Pair Steering

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame and fix x in X. The raw tight turns (q_1,q_2,q_3) and (q_2,q_1,x) make D_Q=(q_1,q_2) tail-signed and D_x=(q_1,x) head-signed, with supports meeting exactly at q_1. Accepted R547 therefore applies at the graph-intrinsic signed-support level and yields disjoint opposite-sign singleton supports (q_2) and (x), together with the two ancestor dimers and hinge-cut witnesses.

This output does NOT by itself satisfy the hypothesis of accepted Complete Floor Pair Steering R432. R547's theorem contract distinguishes the graph-intrinsic balanced pair from the ancestry-bearing floor specialization: the latter is asserted only inside a hypothetical smallest-counterexample signed-payment lineage. R432 in turn requires an active ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor whose historical singleton signs and pair ancestry are available, and its persistence contract explicitly consumes the incoming paid-floor ledger, including original pair birth/refund-contact data and actualizing replacement certificates. The raw D_Q,D_x construction supplies no pair-deletion cover, pair birth, capture, actualization, payment, or earlier refund/contact ledger.

Consequently one may not infer from the raw hinge alone that R432 can preserve q_2 and replace x by q_{m-2}. Any such steering requires a separate theorem establishing that the R547 hinge descendants belong to an active signed-payment lineage with the ledger required by R432, or a new steering theorem whose hypothesis is only a graph-intrinsic balanced pair.

In particular the conclusion of superseded R774 is not established by dependencies R547,R723,R432 alone.

## Hypotheses
Use accepted R547,R432,R723 and the canonical signed-support/floor distinction in their exact contracts. No additional payment-lineage hypothesis is assumed for the raw first-inward dimers.

## Scope
Canonical correction/fence for superseded R774. It separates graph-intrinsic hinge collapse from lineage-qualified paid-floor steering. No new positive route is asserted.

## Applicability
Before invoking R432 on a both-singleton pair obtained from raw signed turns or an R547 hinge, verify an actual signed-payment lineage and its retained pair-birth/actualization/refund ledger. If that ledger is absent, stop at the graph-intrinsic balanced pair; do not steer its coordinates.

## Persistence
Retain D_Q,D_x,their exact witnesses,the hinge q_1,the R547 singleton descendants,and the negative provenance fact that no paid-floor ledger has been established. If later mathematics establishes lineage membership, retain that new certificate separately before invoking R432.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not deny the graph-intrinsic R547 singleton pair, and does not claim no payment lineage can ever be constructed. Does not weaken R432 for genuine ancestry-bearing paid floors. Does not invalidate accepted R723 or R547. It only blocks the unlicensed raw-hinge-to-R432 composition used by R774.

## Proof
The turns (q_1,q_2,q_3) and (q_2,q_1,x) establish the two signed dimers and the one-vertex hinge, so R547 applies exactly as a pure signed-path contact lemma. Its statement first concludes a graph-intrinsic balanced pair of total mass two. Only afterward does it add: in a hypothetical smallest-counterexample signed-payment lineage this is an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor. Thus lineage membership is an additional specialization, not a consequence of the raw hinge hypotheses.

R432 assumes an ancestry-bearing both-singleton balanced-pair floor is active and says the historical singleton signs and pair ancestry are available to the replacement macro. Its persistence clause is stronger still: the continuation retains the full physical ancestry ledger of the incoming paid floor, including original pair birth/refund-contact certificates and actualizing replacement data, and its exclusions forbid bare formal relabeling without those physical certificates.

The raw D_Q,D_x construction used by R774 has only graph-intrinsic tight turns plus R547 hinge-cut ancestry. It explicitly uses no deletion cover, capture, actualization, payment, or refund/contact step. Therefore the hypotheses required by R432 are not established. The claimed coordinate steering is consequently unlicensed unless additional lineage-producing mathematics is supplied. This proves the fence.
