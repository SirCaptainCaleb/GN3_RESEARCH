# R2030 — Deterministic source-endpoint anchor cap

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace R2030

## Statement

Let `T` be any literal spanning exact two-cover of

`G=H-{A,C}`

in the retained smallest-counterexample source frame, and let source spoke `s` be an endpoint of a nontrivial T-rail.

- If that rail begins `(s,u,...)`, then `(s,A,u)` is tight.
- If that rail ends `(...,u,s)`, then `(u,C,s)` is tight.

The statement preserves the literal orientation of the T-rail; no rail reversal is invoked.

## Proof

Suppose first that the exposed rail is

`P=(s,u,u_2,...)`,

with the evident truncation when `P` has only two vertices, and let `Q` be the other T-rail. The retained source trimer `(A,s,C)` is tight, so its cyclic presentation `(C,A,s)` is tight.

If `(A,s,u)` were tight, then

`(C,A,s,u,u_2,...)`

would be a literal tight path: `(C,A,s)` is the retained source turn, `(A,s,u)` is the only new interface turn, and all later turns are inherited from `P`. Together with `Q` this would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting the smallest-counterexample hypothesis. Therefore `(A,s,u)` is bad. Boundary antisymmetry R3 gives its complete reversal `(u,s,A)` tight, equivalently the cyclic presentation `(s,A,u)` tight.

Now suppose instead that

`P=(...,u,s)`.

The cyclic source presentation `(s,C,A)` is tight. If `(u,s,C)` were tight, appending `(C,A)` to `P` would give the literal tight path

`(...,u,s,C,A)`,

which together with `Q` would again be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`. Hence `(u,s,C)` is bad. R3 gives `(C,s,u)` tight, equivalently its cyclic presentation `(u,C,s)`.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: the retained source trimer `(A,s,C)`, boundary antisymmetry R3, and the smallest-counterexample assertion that `H` has no spanning exact two-cover.

Provisional dependencies: none.

No low-transition hypothesis is required. In particular, once R2024 exposes a source endpoint in a physical low-transition exact two-cover, R2030 upgrades that exposure to one deterministic neighbor-marked anchor cap while preserving the actual T-rail orientation.
