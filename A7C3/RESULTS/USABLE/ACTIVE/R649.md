# R649 — Crossed middle-gate saturation supplies twelve simultaneous R542-ready packets over all six distinguished vertices

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R24 frame of R621 with X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, and endpoints L=q_0, R=q_m. Then the crossed gates and matching-height X geometry contain twelve simultaneous graph-intrinsic R542-ready packets, grouped by the four oriented M_S dimers. For S=(a,c): carriers/complementary singleton/witness pair are ((z,c,a),z,{L,R}), ((L,c,a),L,{b,R}), ((R,c,a),R,{b,L}). For S=(c,a): ((b,a,c),b,{L,R}), ((L,a,c),L,{z,R}), ((R,a,c),R,{z,L}). For S=(b,z): ((z,b,c),c,{L,R}), ((z,b,L),L,{a,R}), ((z,b,R),R,{a,L}). For S=(z,b): ((b,z,a),a,{L,R}), ((b,z,L),L,{c,R}), ((b,z,R),R,{c,L}). In every entry K is a tight trimer, S is exactly a reverse boundary dimer of K, and the two listed vertices are distinct same-polarity witnesses outside K on the tested orientation. Hence R542 applies independently to all twelve entries. In particular each distinguished vertex a,b,c,z occurs as a complementary singleton in at least one packet, and each endpoint L,R occurs in four packets. The twelve source packets coexist in the fixed crossed frame; their capture/payment descendants are separate choices.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted crossed R621; accepted R542.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Fixed-frame source-packet theorem. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R24, order bound, or pending result is used.

## Applicability
Use the table whenever a consumer needs a two-witness reverse-boundary payment packet with a prescribed complementary singleton among {a,b,c,z,L,R}. The table supplies source geometry only; preservation of that singleton through later payment requires a separate theorem.

## Persistence
Retain all twelve tested dimers, carriers, complementary singleton labels, witness pairs, tested polarities, and R542 source/payment certificates as graph-intrinsic alternatives.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a complementary singleton is preserved to a later floor, that paid descendants coexist, or that any packet alone closes H.

## Proof
The matching-height frame retained by R621 gives bac and zca tight from M_R>M_S, and bza and zbc tight from M_S>M_L. Its crossed gates give Lac,Lca,Rac,Rca tight and bzL,zbL,bzR,zbR tight. For tested S=(a,c), the tight carriers (z,c,a),(L,c,a),(R,c,a) all end in boundary c,a, so (a,c) is the reverse terminal dimer. The same tested orientation is head-signed by L,R,b through Lac,Rac,bac. Thus use witnesses {L,R} for carrier (z,c,a), {b,R} for (L,c,a), and {b,L} for (R,c,a); each pair lies outside its carrier. R542 applies, with complementary singleton z,L,R respectively. For S=(c,a), carriers (b,a,c),(L,a,c),(R,a,c) end in a,c, so (c,a) is their reverse terminal dimer. The tested orientation is head-signed by z,L,R via zca,Lca,Rca. Choose witnesses {L,R}, {z,R}, {z,L}, yielding complementary singletons b,L,R. For S=(b,z), carriers (z,b,c),(z,b,L),(z,b,R) begin in z,b, so (b,z) is the reverse initial dimer. It is tail-signed by a,L,R via bza,bzL,bzR. Choose witnesses {L,R}, {a,R}, {a,L}, yielding complementary singletons c,L,R. Finally, for S=(z,b), carriers (b,z,a),(b,z,L),(b,z,R) begin in b,z, so (z,b) is the reverse initial dimer. It is tail-signed by c,L,R via zbc,zbL,zbR. Choose witnesses {L,R}, {c,R}, {c,L}, yielding complementary singletons a,L,R. Every witness pair is distinct and outside its carrier, exactly as required by R542. Therefore all twelve R542-ready source packets coexist as graph-intrinsic facts of the fixed crossed frame. R542 descendants are alternative continuations. No endpoint-selective payment or small-deletion theorem is used.
