# R146 — Three-exterior pair extension for a fixed tight trimer

**Status:** USABLE / ACTIVE
**Type:** lemma

## Statement

Let P be a three-vertex support carrying a tight trimer in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament, and let x,y,z be three distinct vertices outside P. Then at least one of P∪{x,y}, P∪{x,z}, P∪{y,z} supports a tight five-vertex path.

## Scope

Pure six-vertex local amplification; no smallest-counterexample, deletion, support-family, sign, payment, or representative assumptions.

## Preferred proof

Relabel P={0,1,2} with (0,1,2) tight and the exterior vertices 3,4,5. Encode the 60 complete-reversal pairs of ordered triples as Boolean variables under R3. Assume all three P-plus-pair five-sets are non-Hamiltonian, adding one clause for each ordered P5 on each support. The resulting 60-variable, 361-clause CNF is exhaustively UNSAT under deterministic DPLL, independently regenerated. Hence at least one exterior pair extends P to a tight P5.
