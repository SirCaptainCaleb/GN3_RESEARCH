# R986 — A singleton-rich family of equal matchings forces a larger matching in their union

## Statement
Let V be a finite set of size n and let L={v_out:v in V}, R={v_in:v in V}. For every z in V let M_z be a bipartite matching on L sqcup R of one common positive size r, with z_out and z_in both unmatched by M_z. Let U be the union graph of all M_z. Then nu(U)>=r+1. More strongly, fix ANY size-r matching M contained in U, not necessarily one of the family matchings. There is an M-augmenting path P in U of one of two forms. (PAIR-GAIN) For some x, P is a connected component of M triangle M_x having one more M_x edge than M edge. (ZERO-TRANSFER-PLUS-EDGE) Otherwise, there are physical labels ell,y and an M-unmatched right copy rho such that P consists of an even zero-weight alternating path Q from an M-unmatched left copy to ell_out, followed by one edge ell_out rho belonging to M_y; Q is either trivial or is the component of M triangle M_ell containing ell_out. Hence every non-M edge of P belongs to at most two singleton-family matchings. In particular, after any zero-weight recombination that remains a size-r matching inside U, the same at-most-three-family augmentation theorem can be applied again from the recombined matching.

## Hypotheses
Finite balanced bipartite copy set; one size-r matching M_z for every physical label z, simultaneously exposing z_out and z_in; r>0; the chosen base M is any size-r matching contained in the union U.

## Scope
Pure family-level bipartite matching theorem with an iterative at-most-three-family augmentation. No edge-order inequalities, R980, R982, or threshold vector is used in the theorem itself.

## Applicability
In a minimum R888 counterexample, the union of one singleton three-cover per vertex contains ordinary cardinality augmentation from every selected-edge-union three-cover, including covers produced by zero-weight R980 recombination. Thus iterative zero-recombine-and-compare work never leaves the domain where an at-most-three-cover ordinary augmentation certificate exists.

## Persistence
Retain the singleton family {M_z}, union U, arbitrary current size-r base M subset U, and either a pair-gain component or a zero-transfer path from M triangle M_ell plus one final edge supplied by M_y.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the ordinary augmenting path is locally increasing. Does not assert that every zero recombination is available without R980. Physical collision/order compatibility is the remaining obstruction.

## Proof
Let M be any size-r matching contained in U, and let X_L,X_R be its unmatched left and right copies. If for some x a component of M triangle M_x contains one more M_x edge than M edge, it is an M-augmenting path and we are in PAIR-GAIN. Assume no such component exists for any x. Since |M_x|=|M|=r, the component weights of M triangle M_x sum to zero, so absence of positive components forces every component to have weight zero. Thus every left copy ell_out is reachable from X_L by an M-alternating zero path using only M and M_ell: if ell_out is unmatched in M take the trivial path; otherwise ell_out is unmatched in M_ell and the balanced component containing it is an even alternating path whose other endpoint is an M-unmatched left copy. Some rho in X_R has a neighbor ell_out in U. If every rho in X_R were isolated, then every family matching M_z would leave all |X_R|=n-r of them unmatched. Because r>0 choose z with z_in outside X_R. The matching M_z additionally leaves z_in unmatched, so has size at most r-1, contradiction. Choose ell_out rho in U and a family matching M_y containing it. Append that edge to the zero path Q from X_L to ell_out. Since rho is M-unmatched, it cannot occur internally on Q, and ell_out rho is not an M-edge. Hence Q+(ell_out rho) is an M-augmenting path. Its non-M edges lie in M_ell except for the last edge, which lies in M_y. This proves the arbitrary-base constructive clause and therefore nu(U)>=r+1. Reapplying the same argument after any size-r recombination inside U is immediate because the hypotheses on the fixed singleton family and union U are unchanged.
