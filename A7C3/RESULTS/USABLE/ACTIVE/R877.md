# R877 — The order-nine R24 frame closes in both gate phases by one-hole cap collisions

## Statement
Retain the m=4 synchronized transitive R594 frame Q=(L,u,v,s,R), X={a,b,c,z}. In the aligned phase, two literal one-hole spanning proposals force both reverse orientations (b,u,v) and (v,u,b) tight, contradicting R3. In the crossed phase, six literal one-hole proposals force Rbu=(R,b,u), aLz=(a,L,z), cRb=(c,R,b), saL=(s,a,L), then both Lzv=(L,z,v) and vzL=(v,z,L) tight, again contradicting R3. Hence no m=4 order-three R24 frame exists in either gate phase.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted R594 with m=4, in either aligned or crossed R619 phase. Use accepted R3,R536,R594,R619 and, in the crossed phase, R723.

## Scope
Finite m=4 closure by explicit one-hole spanning proposals only. No R5,R24,payment,capture,Mate-Fork,or computation.

## Applicability
Use as the complete m=4 finite base once freshly reviewed.

## Persistence
Retain every displayed two-path proposal and its unique uncertified hole. Each forced reverse literal is graph-intrinsic; the final contradiction is a literal reversal pair.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
No residual branch remains in either gate phase.

## Proof
We repeatedly use the following literal one-hole principle. If two vertex-disjoint proposed paths have supports partitioning V(H), and every required turn except one ordered triple tau is already tight, then tau must be bad because otherwise the two paths would be a spanning two-cover of H. Boundary antisymmetry R3 therefore makes rev(tau) tight.

ALIGNED. Let Q=(L,u,v,s,R). Consider
(1) (z,v,u,b) sqcup (L,a,c,R,s). The only uncertified turn is (v,u,b). Indeed (z,v,u) is the aligned right-inward turn from R536 at m=4; (L,a,c),(a,c,R) are the aligned gates and (c,R,s) is an R594 endpoint star. Hence the cap forces the reverse (b,u,v) tight.
(2) (L,c,a,z) sqcup (b,u,v,s,R). The only uncertified turn is (b,u,v): (L,c,a) is the aligned gate, (c,a,z) is a tight X-turn in the R594 matching-height four-cell, and (u,v,s),(v,s,R) are Q turns. Hence (v,u,b) is tight.
The two forced turns (b,u,v) and (v,u,b) are exact complete reversals, contradicting R3.

CROSSED. Retain accepted R723, which at m=4 gives (v,u,x) and (x,s,v) tight for every x in X, and R536, which gives the relevant inward complement turns.
(1) (L,c,a,z) sqcup (v,u,b,R,s) has sole hole (u,b,R): (L,c,a) is a left gate, (c,a,z) an X-turn, (v,u,b) comes from R723, and (b,R,s) is an endpoint star. Thus (R,b,u)=:Rbu is tight.
(2) (u,v,s,R) sqcup (b,z,L,a,c) has sole hole (z,L,a): Q certifies the first rail, while (b,z,L) and (L,a,c) are crossed left gates. Thus (a,L,z)=:aLz is tight.
(3) (L,u,v,s) sqcup (b,R,c,a,z) has sole hole (b,R,c): the first rail is Q, (R,c,a) is a crossed right gate, and (c,a,z) is an X-turn. Thus (c,R,b)=:cRb is tight.
(4) (c,z,b,R) sqcup (u,L,a,s,v) has sole hole (L,a,s): (c,z,b) is an X-turn, (z,b,R) a crossed right gate, (u,L,a) an endpoint star, and (a,s,v) comes from R723. Thus (s,a,L)=:saL is tight.
(5) (c,R,b,u) sqcup (a,s,v,z,L) has sole hole (v,z,L): the first rail uses cRb and Rbu; the second uses (a,s,v) from R723 and (s,v,z) from R536. Thus (L,z,v)=:Lzv is tight.
(6) (c,R,b,u) sqcup (s,a,L,z,v) has sole hole (L,z,v): the first rail again uses cRb,Rbu; the second uses saL,aLz. Thus (v,z,L)=:vzL is tight.
The final forced turns Lzv=(L,z,v) and vzL=(v,z,L) are complete reversals, contradicting R3. Therefore the crossed phase is impossible as well.
