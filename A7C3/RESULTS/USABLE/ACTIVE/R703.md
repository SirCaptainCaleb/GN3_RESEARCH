# R703 — One-step inward reverse-star lift forces a P4 or Reverse-Ear geometry

## Statement
Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m) be any vertex-simple tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament and let x be a vertex outside Q. There are two dual local forms. (L) Suppose 1<=i<=m-1 and the reverse-boundary witness trimer J_L=(q_{i+1},q_i,x) is tight. Then either the four-set {q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1},x} supports a tight Hamilton P4, or explicit Reverse-Ear geometry occurs relative to Q. More precisely, apply R518 to J_L with fourth vertex q_{i-1}. In the no-P4 branch R518 forces the all-Q trimer K_L=(q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i-1}) tight. Along K_L the consecutive Q contacts q_{i+1},q_{i-1} occur in decreasing Q-order and are not adjacent on Q, so R435 applied to Q and K_L yields a tight reverse trimer/reversal cell or a vertex-simple tight cycle. (R) Suppose 0<=i<=m-2 and J_R=(x,q_{i+1},q_i) is tight. Then either {x,q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}} supports a tight Hamilton P4, or explicit Reverse-Ear geometry occurs. Indeed R518 with fourth vertex q_{i+2} forces in its no-P4 branch K_R=(q_{i+2},q_i,q_{i+1}) tight; the consecutive contacts q_{i+2},q_i occur in reverse nonadjacent Q-order, so R435 fires. Thus a new exterior witness on either inward reverse-boundary orientation of an internal Q dimer can never remain only same-support witness reminting: one old neighboring Q vertex lifts it immediately to a labelled P4 or to the Reverse-Ear/cycle queue.

## Hypotheses
Q is a vertex-simple tight path in a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament; x lies outside Q. In form (L), J_L=(q_{i+1},q_i,x) is tight with i>=1 so q_{i-1} exists. In form (R), J_R=(x,q_{i+1},q_i) is tight with i+2<=m so q_{i+2} exists. Use accepted R518 and R435.

## Scope
Pure order-free local path interaction. No smallest-counterexample, deletion exactness, crossed frame, payment, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R358, or R436.

## Applicability
Use immediately whenever a same-polarity packet or failed splice adds a new exterior witness to an inward Q dimer in one of the displayed reverse-boundary orientations. Do not report it as a third witness on an already-payable R542 support. Probe by the next old Q vertex outside the carrier. This directly consumes the shape-F L-post inward packet (q_3,q_2,x) with q_1 and the R-pre right packet (x,q_{m-2},q_{m-3}) with q_{m-1}, as well as the R692 shape-E remint.

## Persistence
Retain Q and its physical index order, x, the source trimer J_L or J_R, the chosen neighboring Q probe, the R518 four-cell test, and either the exact labelled P4 or the all-Q nonmonotone trimer K_L/K_R plus the R435 reverse-trimer/reversal/cycle certificate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not consume the resulting P4 or R435 cycle/reverse cell. Does not apply at a path endpoint where the required outside Q probe does not exist. Does not say an old two-witness packet without a newly exhibited exterior witness has already entered this lemma; the displayed witness trimer must be retained.

## Proof
For (L), set a=q_{i+1}, b=q_i, c=x and y=q_{i-1} in accepted R518. If its P4 branch occurs, the first conclusion holds. Otherwise R518 forces (b,a,y)=(q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i-1}) and (y,c,b)=(q_{i-1},x,q_i) tight. Retain K_L=(q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i-1}). Compare K_L with Q. The consecutive K_L contacts q_{i+1} and q_{i-1} have Q indices i+1>i-1, and the K_L subpath between them is the single state q_{i+1}q_{i-1}. Its interior is empty. Since the two endpoints are distance two on Q, this is not R435's special adjacent old-state reversal case. Therefore R435's nonadjacent branch gives a reverse trimer/reversal cell or a vertex-simple tight cycle. For (R), set a=x, b=q_{i+1}, c=q_i and y=q_{i+2}. Again a P4 settles the first outcome. In the no-P4 branch R518 forces (y,c,b)=(q_{i+2},q_i,q_{i+1}) tight (and also (q_{i+1},x,q_{i+2}) tight). Retain K_R=(q_{i+2},q_i,q_{i+1}). Its consecutive Q contacts q_{i+2},q_i occur in reverse order with gap two, so the identical R435 argument yields explicit Reverse-Ear/cycle geometry. This proves both forms.
