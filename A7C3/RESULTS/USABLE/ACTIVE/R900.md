# R900 — Every fragmented crossed X marker is X-pure or yields an endpoint-augmented two-witness packet modulo a labelled P4

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed inward-anchor frame R862 with W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}, Y=G_0 union G_1 union G_2, and let T be any exact two-cover of W. Let x be one vertex of a fragmented middle-gate X cell, meaning that x is not T-adjacent to its physical mate in the same cell G_3={a,c} or G_4={b,z}. Then at least one of the following holds. (X-PURE) x has no selected T-neighbor in Y. Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429 then implies deg_T(x) is one or two, and every selected neighbor of x lies in the other middle-gate X cell. Hence either x is a rail endpoint adjacent to exactly one vertex of the other X cell, or x is internal and its two T-neighbors are exactly the two vertices of the other X cell, giving a literal three-vertex X-only rail segment through x. (P4-END) For some selected Y-neighbor y of x and some endpoint e in {L,R} distinct from y, H contains one of the source-labelled tight P4s (e,c,a,y), (e,a,c,y), (y,b,z,e), or (y,z,b,e), chosen according as x=a,c,b,z. (TWO-WITNESS) For some selected Y-neighbor y of x and some endpoint e in {L,R} distinct from y, the appropriate tested middle-gate dimer has y and e as two distinct same-polarity witnesses: if x=a, (a,c) is head-signed by y,e; if x=c, (c,a) is head-signed by y,e; if x=b, (z,b) is tail-signed by y,e; if x=z, (b,z) is tail-signed by y,e. Using the fixed all-X carriers from accepted R649, the packet is R542-ready. Therefore every fragmented marker that has even one current Y-neighbor immediately yields P4-END or TWO-WITNESS; the only residue is X-PURE. In addition, in the f=2 LOW4 branch of R894, if x is chosen with c_x<=4 and X-PURE holds, then d_Y(x)=0 and the R535 identity c_x=r_Y+1-d_Y(x) gives r_Y<=3. Thus the useful-output-free LOW4 residue is an X-pure low marker together with at most three maximal Y-runs. FULLY FRAGMENTED COROLLARY. If f(T)=2, so both X cells are fragmented, then X-PURE cannot occur for all four X vertices simultaneously. Hence H necessarily has a P4-END output or at least one TWO-WITNESS packet. Indeed, if every x in X were X-PURE, T would select no X-Y state. Since X and Y are both nonempty and T has exactly two rails, one rail would cover all four vertices of X and the other all of Y, contradicting the accepted fact that the transitive matching-height four-cell X has no tight Hamilton P4. Therefore the entire f=2 branch, including both LOW4 and MAX5, reduces to a labelled endpoint P4 or a physical current-witness two-witness middle-gate packet. Pending R898 is a stronger MAX5 refinement because it gives four simultaneous such packets in its no-P4 branch.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R594/R621/R649 frame and accepted inward-anchor pair-deletion frame R862; T is any exact two-cover of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}; x belongs to a fragmented X cell. Use accepted R3,R429,R535,R542,R594,R649. The r_Y<=3 sentence additionally assumes R894 LOW4. The fully fragmented corollary assumes f(T)=2.

## Scope
Noncircular local consumer for fragmented middle-gate markers, independent of R885 BOUNDED monotonicity and of the LOW/MAX classification until the final LOW4 corollary. No payment execution, capture, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,generic R358,R436, or computation.

## Applicability
Use before detailed fragmented-X casework. If a fragmented marker touches Y at all, immediately obtain a labelled P4 or an R542-ready two-witness packet using one physical endpoint as the second witness. In f=2, not all four markers can be X-pure, so the entire fully fragmented branch already has one of these useful outputs; LOW4 needs no independent X-pure endgame after this parent is accepted. MAX5 may still use pending R898 when four simultaneous packets are materially stronger. Outside f=2, an X-pure marker remains the only local residue. If a LOW4 marker itself is X-pure, then r_Y<=3.

## Proof
Let v=q_2 and d=q_{m-2}. Let x be a fragmented X marker. Suppose first that x has a selected T-neighbor y in Y. Choose e from {L,R} with e!=y; this is always possible because L and R are distinct. Accepted R649 supplies both L and R as same-polarity witnesses on each of the four tested middle-gate orientations used below, together with the fixed all-X short carriers. We compare y with the physical mate of x using R3.\n\nCase x=a. R3 at middle a gives exactly one of (y,a,c) and (c,a,y). If (c,a,y) is tight, R649 gives (e,c,a) tight, so (e,c,a,y) is a literal tight P4. Otherwise (y,a,c) is tight. R649 also gives (e,a,c) tight, so y and e are distinct head witnesses on tested dimer (a,c). The R649 carrier (z,c,a) has reverse terminal dimer (a,c), hence R542 applies.\n\nCase x=c. R3 gives exactly one of (y,c,a) and (a,c,y). If (a,c,y) is tight, R649 gives (e,a,c), so (e,a,c,y) is a P4. Otherwise (y,c,a) is tight, and with fixed head witness e on (c,a) the pair {y,e} is a two-head-witness packet; carrier (b,a,c) makes it R542-ready.\n\nCase x=b. R3 gives exactly one of (y,b,z) and (z,b,y). If (y,b,z) is tight, R649 gives (b,z,e), so (y,b,z,e) is a P4. Otherwise (z,b,y) is tight. R649 gives (z,b,e), so y,e are distinct tail witnesses on tested dimer (z,b); carrier (b,z,a) makes it R542-ready.\n\nCase x=z. R3 gives exactly one of (y,z,b) and (b,z,y). If (y,z,b) is tight, R649 gives (z,b,e), so (y,z,b,e) is a P4. Otherwise (b,z,y) is tight. R649 gives (b,z,e), so y,e are distinct tail witnesses on tested dimer (b,z); carrier (z,b,c) makes it R542-ready.\n\nThus if x has any Y-neighbor, P4-END or TWO-WITNESS holds. Suppose neither holds. Then x has no Y-neighbor. Because T is an exact two-cover of the physical pair deletion H-{v,d}, accepted R429 says both rails are nontrivial, so deg_T(x) is one or two. Fragmentation says x is not adjacent to its same-cell mate. Since x has no Y-neighbor, every selected neighbor lies in the other middle-gate X cell. If deg_T(x)=1, x is a rail endpoint joined to one vertex of that other cell. If deg_T(x)=2, the other X cell has exactly two vertices and these must be the two neighbors, so T contains the literal X-only length-two segment through x. This proves X-PURE.\n\nFinally suppose we are in accepted R894 LOW4 and x is a chosen low marker with c_x<=4. In X-PURE, d_Y(x)=0. Accepted R535 gives c_x=r_Y+1-d_Y(x), hence c_x=r_Y+1. Therefore r_Y<=3. This proves the stated LOW4 corollary.

For the fully fragmented corollary, assume f(T)=2 and suppose for contradiction that neither P4-END nor TWO-WITNESS occurs for any x in X. Both physical X cells are fragmented, so the local conclusion applies to all four vertices and forces X-PURE for every x. Thus no selected T-state joins X to Y. Both X and Y are nonempty in the R862 frame. Since T has exactly two connected rails and no rail can contain both an X vertex and a Y vertex without an X-Y adjacent state, the two rails must consist of one X-only rail and one Y-only rail. The X-only rail contains all four vertices of X and is therefore a tight Hamilton P4 on X. This contradicts the accepted R594 transitive matching-height property that X is P4-free. Hence some x yields P4-END or TWO-WITNESS. This argument does not distinguish LOW4 from MAX5, proving the stated f=2 collapse.
