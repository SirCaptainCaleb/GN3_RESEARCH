# R1039 — Exact off-augmenter transition trichotomy in the v-rooted sharp cell

**Audit state:** PASS_ADJUSTED  
**Proof provenance:** D17.437, repaired against D17.421 and audited R1037/R1038.

## Statement
Assume the v-rooted sharp cell

`tau(F)=3`, `tau(J)=1`, `w(C_*)=2`,

with the remaining components of `F triangle J` neutral in matching cardinality, and retain the audited source-block and forward-gate conclusions R1037/R1038.

Then the total transition weight of the neutral symmetric-difference components is

`W_0 = sum_D w(D) = 0`.

Moreover:

1. If `C_*` carries `(3F,1J)` transition incidences, every neutral component is transition-free.
2. If `C_*` carries `(2F,0J)`, exactly one F-transition and one J-transition remain off `C_*`. The remaining F-transition is the opposite X-mated source gate from R1037. Exactly one of the following occurs:
   - **COMMON:** the F- and J-transition are the same selected edge in `F intersect J`; every neutral component is transition-free.
   - **PAIRED NEUTRAL:** they are distinct edges on one neutral component, which has exactly `(1F,1J)` transition incidences and weight `0`; every other neutral component is transition-free.
   - **SPLIT NEUTRAL:** they are distinct edges on two neutral components; the F-only component has weight `+1`, the J-only component has weight `-1`, and every other neutral component is transition-free.

Thus the first two branches exhaust the `2F/0J` case only if an independent componentwise inequality `w(D)<=0` is available for every neutral component. The aggregate normalization `W_0<=0` recorded in D17.421 does not by itself imply that inequality componentwise.

## Proof
The global transition gap is

`tau(F)-tau(J)=3-1=2`.

The distinguished augmenter contributes `w(C_*)=2`, so additivity of transition weight over the noncommon symmetric-difference edges gives

`W_0 = tau(F)-tau(J)-w(C_*) = 0`.

In the `(3F,1J)` case, `C_*` already contains all three F-transition incidences and the unique J-transition incidence. Hence no transition edge of either matching remains on a neutral component, so every neutral component is transition-free.

In the `(2F,0J)` case, exactly one of the three F-transitions and the unique J-transition remain off `C_*`. By R1037/R1038 the off-augmenter F-transition is the other X-mated boundary source gate. If the two remaining transition incidences are the same selected edge, that edge belongs to `F intersect J` and hence is absent from `F triangle J`, giving COMMON.

Otherwise the two transition edges lie in the symmetric difference. Since there is exactly one remaining F-transition and exactly one remaining J-transition, either they lie on the same neutral component or on two distinct neutral components. In the same-component case that component has local weight `1-1=0`; all other neutral components have no transition incidence. In the distinct-component case the component carrying the F-transition has weight `+1`, the component carrying the J-transition has weight `-1`, and all others have weight `0`. These weights sum to the already established `W_0=0`.

No fourth placement is possible.

## Adjustment to D17.437
D17.437 correctly identified the COMMON correction and the unchanged `(3F,1J)` branch, but it also asserted `w(D)=0` for every neutral component by citing the absent D17.416 componentwise inequality. The surviving durable D17.421 interface records only the aggregate `W_0<=0`; aggregate equality `W_0=0` does not exclude simultaneous `+1` and `-1` neutral components. The exact repair is therefore to retain the COMMON and paired-neutral branches and add the SPLIT NEUTRAL branch above.
