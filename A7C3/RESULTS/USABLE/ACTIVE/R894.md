# R894 — Both fragmented X dimers force a low marker or a three-skeleton fully alternating face

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame and the BOUNDED branch of accepted R885. Suppose both middle-gate X cells are fragmented, f(T)=2. Put Y=G_0 union G_1 union G_2, p=e_T(X), q=e_T(X,Y), r_Y=c(T[Y]), and c_x=c(T[Y union {x}]) as in R535. Then exactly one of the following two regimes holds. (LOW4) Some x in X has c_x<=4; equivalently r_Y<=5. (MAX5) r_Y=6; equivalently (p,q)=(0,8); equivalently c_x=5 for every x in X. In MAX5 every X vertex has d_Y(x)=2, there is no selected X-X edge, and every X vertex is an internal separator between two Y-runs. After contracting each maximal Y-run to Y and retaining the four X vertices individually, the two T-rails have, up to swapping the rails, reversing either rail, and relabelling X vertices, exactly one of three coarse skeletons: (N0) Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y; (N1) Y-X-Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y; (N2) Y-X-Y-X-Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y. Thus the fully fragmented branch has no diffuse all-high region: outside a named c<=4 marker it collapses to one extremal parameter point and three alternating X/Y skeletons.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R885 BOUNDED branch with f(T)=2 in the crossed R594/R862 frame. Use accepted R535 notation/identities and the fact that T is a spanning two-path forest, hence every X vertex has selected degree at most two.

## Scope
Noncircular crossed R24/O6 sharpening of the both-fragmented five-cell excess branch. Pure two-path-forest arithmetic and maximal-run geometry; no payment, capture, R436, R5, R168, R169, R224, R533, R350, generic R358, or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately after R885 when f=2. Split only into LOW4, where a concrete marker x has c(T[Y union {x}])<=4, and MAX5, where p=0,q=8,r_Y=6 and the entire coarse forest is one of N0,N1,N2. In MAX5 there are no X-X selected adjacencies at all, so all four distinguished vertices are pure Y-separators.

## Persistence
Retain T, all four X labels and physical cell memberships, p,q,r_Y,d_Y,c_x, the LOW4 witness when present, and in MAX5 the actual rail assignment and order of the four X labels before quotienting to N0/N1/N2.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close LOW4 or any MAX5 skeleton, assign the six Y-runs to the source Q-cells G_0,G_1,G_2, or assert every coarse MAX5 skeleton is realizable in the Strong frame. The N0/N1/N2 quotient forgets X labels and source-Q-cell labels. Does not consume any labelled P4 or Reverse-Ear output excluded by the R885 BOUNDED branch.

## Proof
Work in the BOUNDED branch of accepted R885 with f(T)=2. Put X={a,b,c,z} and Y=G_0 union G_1 union G_2. Accepted R885 gives r_Y<=2+2f=6 and the exact identity r_Y=p+q-2. Because T is a two-path forest, every vertex of X has selected T-degree at most two. Summing those four degrees gives the elementary budget 2p+q=sum_{x in X}deg_T(x)<=8.

First suppose r_Y=6. Then p+q=8. Combining this with 2p+q<=8 gives p=0 and q=8. Since q=sum_x d_Y(x)=8 and every d_Y(x)<=deg_T(x)<=2, every x in X has d_Y(x)=2. The R535 pointwise identity gives c_x=r_Y+1-d_Y(x)=7-2=5 for all four x. Thus r_Y=6 implies the claimed MAX5 parameter point. Conversely (p,q)=(0,8) gives r_Y=6 immediately, and the same degree sum forces d_Y(x)=2 and hence c_x=5 for every x.

Now suppose instead that no marker is LOW4, so c_x>=5 for every x. Accepted R535 gives sum_x c_x=4p+3q-4. The degree budget implies q<=8-2p, hence sum_x c_x<=4p+3(8-2p)-4=20-2p<=20. But four values each at least five have sum at least twenty. Equality is therefore forced throughout: p=0, q=8, and sum_x c_x=20, so every c_x=5. By the first paragraph this is exactly r_Y=6. Therefore failure of MAX5 is equivalent to r_Y<=5 and forces at least one x with c_x<=4. Conversely a marker with c_x<=4 cannot occur when r_Y=6, because the r_Y=6 face has all c_x=5. This proves the exact LOW4/MAX5 dichotomy and the stated equivalences.

It remains to classify the coarse MAX5 forest. There p=0 and every x in X has d_Y(x)=deg_T(x)=2. Hence no two X vertices are adjacent in T, every X vertex is internal on its rail, and both of its neighbors lie in Y. Contract each maximal Y-run to one symbol Y. Each rail therefore alternates Y-X-Y-X-...-Y and starts and ends in Y. There are four X symbols total and r_Y=6 Y-runs total. If one rail contains t X symbols, it contains t+1 Y symbols, while the other contains 4-t X symbols and 5-t Y symbols. Up to swapping rails we may take t=0,1,2. These give exactly N0: Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y; N1: Y-X-Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y-X-Y; and N2: Y-X-Y-X-Y | Y-X-Y-X-Y. Reversal and X-label permutations create no further coarse species. This classification does not assert realizability after restoring the physical X and Q-cell labels.
