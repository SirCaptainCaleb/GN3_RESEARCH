# R869 — BAD inward-anchor seams avoid both deleted anchors and amplify to an inward P4 or dual two-witness boundary packets

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-order-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame of R862. Put v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, W=H-{v,d}, and let G be the literal five-component reference cover of W from R862. Fix any exact two-cover T of W and any selected intercell transition xi of T relative to G. Suppose the preferred accepted R176 proof for xi lies in its BAD seam branch, so it exposes a tight reverse trimer K=(k_0,k_1,k_2) whose signed nontrivial support is cut from one old G-component at an endpoint of xi. Then V(K) is contained in W. In particular v and d are distinct vertices outside K.

Consequently accepted R700 applies to K with probes v,d. Exactly one of the following useful alternatives is available.

(P4-INWARD) At least one of V(K) union {v} and V(K) union {d} supports a tight Hamilton P4.

(DUAL-CP) Neither probe gives a P4. Then both reverse boundary dimers of K are simultaneously two-witness R542-ready packets with the SAME distinguished witness pair {v,d}: if K=(k_0,k_1,k_2), the reverse initial dimer (k_1,k_0) is tail-signed by both v,d and the reverse terminal dimer (k_2,k_1) is head-signed by both v,d. Thus R542, and if desired its accepted geometric refinement R696, applies independently at both boundaries without generic R436 or protected first contact.

The containment V(K) subset W is literal: in the preferred R176 proof every reverse-trimer vertex is either an endpoint of the selected transition xi or an old neighbor of such an endpoint inside one reference component of G, and all those vertices belong to W. Therefore no BAD reverse trimer produced from an R862 transition can contain either deleted inward anchor. In particular the MEETS-q_{m-2} subbranch stated in pending R765 is empty; every such BAD trimer necessarily avoids both q_2 and q_{m-2}. 

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame, m>=6. Use accepted R862, the preferred full proof P540 of accepted R176, and accepted R700. T is any exact two-cover of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}}, and xi is any R862 intercell transition whose R176 analysis takes a BAD seam branch. 

## Scope
Noncircular accepted-input parent theorem for BAD seams in the simultaneous inward-anchor five-cell frame. No pending mixed-sibling theorem is needed. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R436,R312,Reverse Ear,shape taxonomy,or computation. It also records a correction to the pending R765 branch split. 

## Applicability
Whenever an R862/R761 analysis encounters a BAD R176 seam, first record that its reverse trimer lies wholly in W and hence avoids both deleted inward anchors. Probe that trimer immediately with q_2 and q_{m-2} via R700. Prefer the P4-INWARD output; otherwise retain both reverse boundary dimers as simultaneous two-witness packets with the same inward witness pair. Do not pursue an R765 MEETS-d branch: it is impossible. 

## Persistence
Retain W, the reference cover G, exact cover T, selected transition xi, the exact R176 BAD reverse trimer K and old-neighbor provenance, the two deleted inward anchors v,d as outside probes, both R700 four-cell tests, and in DUAL-CP both tested reverse boundary dimers with the common witness pair {v,d}. Selectedness of xi is T-relative; the trimer, P4s, and sign packets are graph-intrinsic certificate data. 

## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not treat sigma>0 differently from sigma=0: the theorem applies to any supplied R862 intercell BAD transition. Does not canonically reject or repair pending R765; it proves that R765's MEETS-d alternative is mathematically empty. 

## Proof
Let xi be the selected intercell state. In the preferred full proof P540 of R176, a BAD seam is produced as follows. Both endpoints of xi lie in W, in distinct components of the reference cover G. Choose the endpoint k_1 on the reference component being tested and an old G-neighbor k_0 of k_1. Boundary antisymmetry on the other selected endpoint k_2, k_1, k_0 gives the displayed reverse trimer, in one of the two dual orientations. Every one of k_0,k_1,k_2 lies in W. Therefore K is disjoint from {v,d}=V(H)-W at these two distinguished deleted coordinates.

Now v and d are distinct vertices outside the tight trimer K. Apply accepted R700 with y_1=v and y_2=d. If either R518 probe in R700 yields a Hamilton P4, this is P4-INWARD. Otherwise R700's joint no-P4 branch gives both reverse contacts for each probe and therefore makes the reverse initial boundary dimer of K tail-signed by both v,d and the reverse terminal boundary dimer head-signed by both v,d, on the exact tested orientations stated by R700. These are simultaneous two-witness R542-ready packets. This proves the dichotomy. Since d is outside K in every BAD branch, the MEETS-d alternative of pending R765 has no realizations.
