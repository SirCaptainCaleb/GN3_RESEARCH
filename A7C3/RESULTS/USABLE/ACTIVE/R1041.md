# R1041 — Terminal source-mate reduction to a three-path two-seam recompletion

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** D17.439

## Statement

Work in the v-rooted sharp hard cell. Let `t` be the source endpoint of the last old `F`-transition on the unique augmenter, as certified by R1040, and let `x` be the adjacent old `X-X` source-spoke mate supplied by R1037.

Then:

1. `t` and `x` are consecutive internal vertices of one old `F` rail, and `F-{t,x}` is exactly three nonempty literal directed paths on `G-{t,x}`.
2. The retained source turns `(A,t,C)` and `(A,x,C)` force a tight Hamilton `P4` on `{A,C,t,x}`.
3. Consequently, any legal two-seam reconnection that joins the three residual `F` paths into one tight Hamilton path yields a spanning two-path cover of `H`, with the source four-set as the other rail.

This is a conditional recompletion interface. It does **not** assert that the live first-loss `K_{2,2}` cross edges already supply the required residual-component incidence or the two required tight joins.

## Proof

By R1037, the source spokes form one internal three-vertex `X`-block on an old `F` rail, up to complete reversal,

`B_L -> s_1 -> s_2 -> s_3 -> B_R`,

with the two endpoint source spokes incident with the two old `X|B` source gates and each X-mated to the middle spoke. By R1040, the last old `F`-transition on the augmenter is one of these X-mated source gates. Hence its source endpoint `t` is one of `s_1,s_3`, and its adjacent old `X-X` mate `x` is the middle spoke `s_2`.

Thus `t,x` are consecutive internal vertices of one old `F` rail. Removing two consecutive internal vertices from a path leaves exactly two nonempty path intervals on that rail. The other rail of the exact old two-cover remains a nonempty path. Therefore

`F-{t,x} = P_1 | P_2 | P_3`

is exactly a three-path cover of `G-{t,x}`.

The retained source packet gives both `(A,t,C)` and `(A,x,C)` tight. Apply boundary antisymmetry R3 to `(t,A,x)`. If `(t,A,x)` is tight, then

`t -> A -> x -> C`

is a tight Hamilton `P4` on `{A,C,t,x}`. If `(t,A,x)` is bad, R3 gives its complete reversal `(x,A,t)` tight, so

`x -> A -> t -> C`

is a tight Hamilton `P4` using `(A,t,C)`. Call either resulting source path `Q`.

The supports of `Q` and `P_1|P_2|P_3` are disjoint and partition `V(H)`. Therefore, if two legal seams join `P_1,P_2,P_3` into one tight Hamilton path `P` on their union, then `P | Q` is a spanning two-path cover of `H`.

No R24 or R5 input is used.

## Scope

The theorem reduces the v-rooted terminal-source cell to an exact three-path recompletion problem. It certifies the residual path decomposition and the disjoint source `P4`, but not the existence of the two legal seams and not an identification of those seams with the first-loss `K_{2,2}` cross edges.
