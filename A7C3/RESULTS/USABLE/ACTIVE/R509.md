# R509 — Cut-indexed outward-transition recompletion exchange

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2, and retain a fixed R508 packet. Thus D is nonempty and proper, W=V(H)\D, S is a nonempty proper subset of W, Q=(q_0,...,q_m) is a vertex-simple tight path on exactly D union S, and T=T_1 sqcup T_2 is a literal two-cover of H-D; put C=W\S and retain a selected T-state xy crossing S|C.

Decompose the two T-rails into maximal C-blocks.

(1) There are at least two C-blocks.

(2) Suppose there are exactly two C-blocks, B=(b_0,...,b_r) and K=(c_0,...,c_s). Let -1<=i<=m. Assume the outward incidences at the Q-cut after q_i exist in T in the following literal sense: if i>=0, T selects the directed state q_i b_0 immediately before B; if i<m, T selects the directed state c_s q_{i+1} immediately after K. (At i=-1 only the second condition is present, and at i=m only the first is present.) Put L_i=(q_0,...,q_i) when i>=0 and R_i=(q_{i+1},...,q_m) when i<m. Then
U_i = L_i followed by B (or just B if i=-1),
V_i = K followed by R_i (or just K if i=m)
is a literal spanning two-path proposal of H whose complete uncertified-turn set is exactly
E_i = {alpha_i=(q_{i-1},q_i,b_0) if i>=1} union {beta_i=(c_s,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) if i<=m-2}.
Every other turn of U_i sqcup V_i is certified by Q, by the two C-blocks, or by the displayed selected T-incidences.

Consequently E_i cannot be empty. If |E_i|=1, its unique turn is bad and its exact antisymmetric reverse is tight. If |E_i|=2, at least one of alpha_i,beta_i is bad, so R3 gives the physically certified mate clause reverse(alpha_i) OR reverse(beta_i). In particular, if the retained selected crossing xy is S-to-C with S-endpoint q_i, then its Q-position chooses the cut after q_i: at i=m it alone gives the one-hole proposal, while at i<m the exact missing coordinate is a C-to-S transition from the other C-block into q_{i+1}. Dually, a retained C-to-S crossing into q_j chooses the cut before q_j: at j=0 it alone gives the one-hole proposal, while at j>0 the exact missing coordinate is an S-to-C transition from q_{j-1} into the other C-block.

## Hypotheses
H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2. The full fixed R508 data (D,S,Q,T,xy) are retained. For part (2), C=W\S has exactly two maximal blocks in the literal T-rails and the stated cut-indexed outward incidence or incidences occur. Empty Q-side pieces at i=-1,m are omitted.

## Scope
Order-free local/parent recompletion compiler. No smallest-counterexample minimality, capture, sign payment, endpoint universality, or source ancestry is used after the R508 packet is supplied.

## Applicability
Given the selected R508 crossing, inspect its direction and the position of its S-endpoint on Q. If that endpoint is an aligned end of Q and C has exactly two T-blocks, obtain a literal one-hole spanning proposal immediately. If it is internal, search only for the oppositely directed transition at the adjacent Q vertex and on the other C-block; finding it yields the displayed exact two-hole proposal and mate clause.

## Persistence
Retain D,S,Q, the literal two-cover T, its two named maximal C-blocks B,K, the selected crossing xy, the cut index i determined by the S-endpoint's Q-position, every required outward T-incidence, the displayed proposal U_i sqcup V_i, and the complete hole set E_i. Any forced mate turn is graph-intrinsic; the proposal and transition coordinates are representative-relative certificates.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not force a minimal-transition two-cover to have only two C-blocks. Does not force the partner outward transition at the adjacent Q vertex when the selected crossing endpoint is internal. Does not claim that a generic crossing, balanced pair, capture, or collision closes H. With three or more C-blocks, the displayed two-path proposal does not cover all of C and the theorem gives no completion.

## Proof
Let C=W\S. Each maximal C-block is a vertex-simple tight subpath of a T-rail. If there were exactly one such block B, it would contain every vertex of C. Since Q contains exactly D union S, Q and B would be disjoint tight paths partitioning V(H), contrary to pc(H)>2. This proves (1).

Assume now that B and K are the two C-blocks and fix i with the stated incidences. The supports of L_i and R_i partition V(Q)=D union S, while the supports of B and K partition C. Hence U_i and V_i are vertex-disjoint and together span H. They are vertex-simple.

Inspect their consecutive triples. Inside L_i,R_i all turns are certified by Q, and inside B,K all turns are certified by T. At the L_i-to-B splice, when both relevant triples exist, the turn (q_i,b_0,b_1) is certified because q_i b_0 is immediately before B in T; the only other splice turn is alpha_i=(q_{i-1},q_i,b_0), and it exists exactly when i>=1. If B is a singleton, the same inspection simply omits the nonexistent first turn and leaves the same alpha_i when it exists. Dually, at the K-to-R_i splice the turn (c_{s-1},c_s,q_{i+1}) is certified by T whenever it exists, and the only other splice turn is beta_i=(c_s,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}), which exists exactly when i<=m-2. Singleton and empty-side degeneracies merely omit nonexistent triples. Therefore the complete hole set is exactly E_i.

If E_i were empty, every turn of U_i and V_i would be tight, giving a literal spanning two-cover of H, impossible. If E_i has one member, it must be bad; R3 makes its complete reversal tight. If E_i={alpha_i,beta_i}, they cannot both be tight, so at least one is bad and R3 makes at least one of reverse(alpha_i),reverse(beta_i) tight. This is the asserted physical mate clause. Finally, orienting the retained xy identifies whether it supplies the left outward incidence q_i b_0 or the right outward incidence c_s q_{i+1}; its S-endpoint's index on Q identifies the cut and therefore the exact adjacent partner transition stated in the conclusion.
