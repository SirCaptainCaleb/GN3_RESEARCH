# R2022 — Same-rail closure cycles convert to a finite endpoint-cap packet

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789415408.280639`

## Statement

In the R2020 same-rail cycle branch, write the X-only cyclic rail of the physical base `N` as

`R=(s=r_0,...,r_h=a)`

and the other two rails as

`P=(p_0,...,p_k)`, `Q=(q_0,...,q_l)`.

Then each cross-rail edge

`a->p_0`, `p_k->s`, `a->q_0`, `q_l->s`

is an acyclic exact-two-cover-cardinality candidate with transition count at most `2<tau(F)=3`. Hence any physical one gives strict descent.

If none succeeds, R3 forces, for each of the four candidates, at least one reverse endpoint trimer from its two possible seams. Thus the bounded same-rail cycle is convertible into a finite four-clause endpoint-cap packet and is not an independent cycle residue.

## Proof

By R2020, in the same-rail source-closure cycle branch the physical base `N` has `tau(N)=1`, and the rail `R` from source `s` to terminal `a` is X-only. The other two components are disjoint rails `P,Q`.

Every displayed candidate joins the terminal of one rail to the source of a distinct rail. Therefore its matching cardinality rises by one to exact two-cover cardinality, and no directed cycle can be created. Its added edge contributes at most one X|B transition, so every candidate has transition count at most two, strictly below `tau(F)=3`.

Apply R2021 to each cross-rail candidate.

For `a->p_0`, physical failure can only occur at `(r_{h-1},a,p_0)` when `h>=1`, or at `(a,p_0,p_1)` when `k>=1`. R3 therefore forces at least one of the reverse tight trimers

`(p_0,a,r_{h-1})`, `(p_1,p_0,a)`.

For `p_k->s`, failure forces at least one of

`(s,p_k,p_{k-1})`, `(r_1,s,p_k)`

where the corresponding seam exists.

Likewise `a->q_0` forces at least one of

`(q_0,a,r_{h-1})`, `(q_1,q_0,a)`,

and `q_l->s` forces at least one of

`(s,q_l,q_{l-1})`, `(r_1,s,q_l)`.

If an indicated neighbor is absent because a rail is a singleton, that seam simply does not exist, so failure must occur at the other endpoint. If both seams are absent, the candidate is automatically physical. Thus absence of strict descent leaves exactly the finite four-clause endpoint-cap packet above.
