# R785 — Every two-witness short-carrier END branch is a selected-state reversal or a P4

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let K be a tight trimer, let S be one reverse boundary dimer of K in a fixed tested orientation, let e be the complementary singleton, and assume S has two distinct same-polarity witnesses w_1,w_2 outside K on that same tested orientation. Let T be any exact two-cover of H-V(S), and suppose accepted R696 is in its END branch, so deg_T(e)=1. Then the exact R511 trimer-endpoint normal form forces one of two graph-intrinsic outputs. (REV) Some physical state pq selected in T has the reverse state qp occurring inside the forced reverse trimer from the END hole certificate. (P4) The END hole certificate supplies an opposite-polarity sign on the exact tested orientation S; choosing one of w_1,w_2 distinct from the new witness and applying accepted R523 gives a literal tight Hamilton P4. Thus no two-witness R696 END branch needs to terminate as an anonymous one/two-hole mate clause: it always compiles to selected-state reversal geometry or a P4. The reverse-initial/tail-signed case is the exact order dual of the reverse-terminal/head-signed case.

## Hypotheses
H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; K is a tight trimer; S is a reverse boundary dimer of K with a fixed tested orientation and two distinct same-polarity witnesses w_1,w_2 outside K; e is the complementary singleton; T is an exact two-cover of H-V(S); R696 is in END, equivalently deg_T(e)=1.

## Scope
Order-free parent generalization of accepted R720. No crossed frame, smallest-counterexample minimality, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R176,R312,R436,capture,payment,or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately whenever an R696 two-witness short-carrier packet lands in END. Read the exact R511 degree-one trimer specialization before preserving a mate clause. In the cross-depth R781 packet this removes END as anonymous residue and yields an actual current-state reversal or a role-labelled P4.

## Persistence
Retain K,S,e,T,the exact tested orientation of S,the two same-polarity witness certificates,the literal END rail orientation and complete R511 hole set. In REV retain the selected T-state pq and its tight reverse qp. In P4 retain the opposite-polarity certificate on the exact tested S,the chosen old witness distinct from the new witness,and the resulting ordered P4.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say REV or P4 alone closes H. Does not compare certificates on opposite tested orientations of one unordered dimer. Does not consume R696 FREE or SANDWICH. Does not assert a degree-one exact cover can always be chosen. Does not use the optional R696 capture/payment continuation.

## Proof
Write first K=(e,k_1,k_2), with reverse terminal tested dimer S=(k_2,k_1) head-signed by distinct witnesses w_1,w_2. Since R696 is in END, e has degree one in T. If e is the terminal vertex of its mixed T-rail, the aligned endpoint case of the exact R511/R509 trimer specialization has one hole, and the forced reverse trimer contains the reverse of the unique selected state incident with e. This is REV. Otherwise e begins the mixed rail. Write the other pure exterior rail C=(c_0,...,c_s). The exact m=2,i=0 R511 formula gives complete hole set {(c_{s-1},c_s,k_1) if s>=1} union {(c_s,k_1,k_2)}. Since pc(H)>2, not all holes are tight. If the first hole is bad, R3 gives (k_1,c_s,c_{s-1}) tight; its terminal state c_sc_{s-1} reverses the selected T-state c_{s-1}c_s, giving REV. If the second hole is bad, R3 gives (k_2,k_1,c_s) tight. This is a tail certificate on the exact tested dimer S=(k_2,k_1), opposite the fixed head polarity supplied by w_1,w_2. The new witness c_s can equal at most one of the two distinct old witnesses, so choose w_i distinct from c_s. Accepted R523 on that exact tested orientation gives the literal P4 (w_i,k_2,k_1,c_s). These alternatives exhaust END. For the reverse-initial case K=(k_0,k_1,e), where S=(k_1,k_0) is tail-signed by w_1,w_2, reverse all orders. The aligned one-hole branch again yields REV; in the nonaligned branch one bad hole reverses a selected pure-rail state and the other yields a head certificate (c_0,k_1,k_0) on the exact tested S, which combines by R523 with a distinct fixed tail witness to give P4. Hence every two-witness END branch is REV or P4.
