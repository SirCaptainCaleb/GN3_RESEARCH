# R942 — A minimum-side disconnected interaction reduces to fixed-complement-size recurrence

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest boundary-tournament counterexample. Among all literal singleton-deletion source covers C_p=A|B and all universally crossing vertices z on either source rail, choose one with offending rail size |A| minimum, with z in A. Assume A-z is non-Hamiltonian and retain the actual source order A=L-z-R with L,R nonempty. Put W=H-{p,z}. For any exact two-cover F of W define the class-interaction graph J(F) on {L,R,B} by joining two source classes when F selects an adjacency between them.

DISCONNECTED INTERACTION. If some exact W-cover F has disconnected J(F), then the accepted R931 alternatives hold: H has a spanning two-cover, H lies in the odd uniform middle-layer residue, or there is an actual singleton-deletion universal-crossing source whose offending rail size equals the Hamilton support obtained by joining the two nonisolated classes. The recurrence may be equal or larger than the original minimum size and must be retained.

CONNECTED-INTERACTION SUPPORT FORM. Suppose instead that J(F) is connected for EVERY exact W two-cover F. Then
  pc(L union R)=pc(L union B)=pc(R union B)=2.
Conversely these three equalities imply that J(F) is connected for every exact W two-cover. Thus the hard residue is equivalently three nonempty Hamilton atoms L,R,B such that every two-atom union is non-Hamiltonian but exactly two-coverable, while W itself has exact path-cover number two.

PAIR-RESTRICTION LEDGER. For any chosen exact W-cover F, let b_L,b_R,b_B be the numbers of maximal source-class blocks in its two literal rails, let e_LR,e_LB,e_RB count selected transitions of the three pair types, and put theta=e_LR+e_LB+e_RB. For distinct X,Y let c_XY(F) be the number of path components of the restriction F[X union Y]. Then
  c_XY=b_X+b_Y-e_XY,
  c_LR+c_LB+c_RB=4+theta.
In the hard residue c_XY>=2 for every pair. Hence with sigma_XY=c_XY-2,
  sigma_LR+sigma_LB+sigma_RB=theta-2.

RAINBOW-HINGE / SPLIT-STAR DICHOTOMY. Contract each maximal L-,R-,B-block along the two F rails to its class label. Exactly one of the following structural forms occurs.

(RAINBOW HINGE) Some rail contains three consecutive maximal blocks X-K-Y with X,K,Y all distinct. The two literal selected block-boundary states are differently typed transitions sharing the same actual middle F-block K.

(SPLIT-STAR WEAVE) No rail has such a rainbow triple. Then both rails are nontrivial and each alternates between exactly two source classes. Their two class-pairs are distinct and share a unique center K; after naming the leaves X,Y, one rail has support X union K_1 and alternates only X/K, the other has support K_2 union Y and alternates only K/Y, where K=K_1 disjoint-union K_2 with K_1,K_2 nonempty. The retained literal source order on K necessarily contains at least one consecutive source state with one endpoint in K_1 and the other in K_2, hence a graph-intrinsic source adjacency crossing the two F rails.

No identification is made between F-block internal orders and the original source orders. This dichotomy is the canonical arbitrary-fragmentation entrance for the connected branch: the remaining absorption theorem must consume the two current seam types of a rainbow hinge or the split-star weave, or else return to the disconnected/recurrent alternatives above.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample. Universal crossing is the physical selected-adjacency notion. The chosen (p,A|B,z) globally minimizes the offending universal-crossing rail size. A-z is non-Hamiltonian, so z is internal in A=L-z-R with L,R nonempty. W=H-{p,z}. Every cover mentioned is a literal exact two-nonempty-path cover. The connected-interaction clauses apply only after the disconnected R931 alternatives are excluded.

## Scope
Canonical Arm-A parent strengthening accepted R931. It is independent of transition multiplicity and replaces theta-shape taxonomy by a support-only pairwise non-Hamiltonicity characterization plus one exact two-rail block-word dichotomy.

## Applicability
Use before any detailed fragmentation case analysis. If a disconnected J-cover exists, invoke the R931 closure/uniform/recurrence alternatives with recurrence retained. Otherwise choose any exact W-cover and work only with its rainbow hinge or split-star weave. The pair-restriction slack identity is bookkeeping, not a monotone potential.

## Persistence
Retain the literal source order L-z-R and B, the exact W-cover F, all maximal source-class blocks and selected interblock states. In a rainbow hinge retain both boundary states and the entire middle block. In a split-star weave retain the actual partition K=K_1 union K_2, both rail words, and at least one literal source-order K_1|K_2 crossing state. No source-order/current-cover order synchronization is assumed.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not consume the rainbow hinge or split-star weave into a spanning two-cover. Does not make theta or the sigma ledger a global descent. Does not eliminate the equal/larger R931 recurrence. Does not assert F-blocks are intervals of the original source orders. Does not license inserting the omitted label p or z without complete seam verification.

## Proof
The disconnected-interaction alternatives are exactly accepted R931, so assume they have been excluded and prove the new connected-branch structure.

1. W and the pair unions. The common residue W=H-{p,z} is a proper induced subsystem, so smallest-counterexample minimality gives pc(W)<=2. If W were Hamiltonian, a Hamilton path on W together with the vacuous tight dimer (p,z) would be a spanning two-cover of H. Thus pc(W)=2.

Each of L,R,B is a nonempty literal tight source path. Hence each pair union, such as L union B, has path-cover number at most two. Suppose L union B were Hamiltonian. A Hamilton path on L union B together with the literal source path R would be an exact two-cover of W whose class-interaction graph isolates R, contradicting the hard assumption that every exact W-cover has connected J. Thus pc(L union B)=2. The same argument gives pc(R union B)=2. Also pc(L union R)=2; this is already A-z and is non-Hamiltonian by hypothesis. Therefore all three pair unions have path-cover number exactly two.

Conversely suppose all three pair unions are non-Hamiltonian. If some exact W-cover F had disconnected J(F), accepted R931's elementary isolation argument says one source class is a pure F-rail and the union of the other two classes is Hamiltonian in the other F-rail. This contradicts one of the three pairwise non-Hamiltonicity statements. Hence every exact W-cover has connected J. This proves the equivalence.

2. Pair-restriction ledger. Fix any exact W-cover F. Split its two literal rails into maximal L-,R-,B-blocks. Let b_X be the number of X-blocks and e_XY the number of selected F-adjacencies between an X-block and a Y-block. The quotient obtained by contracting blocks is a disjoint union of two paths, so
  b_L+b_R+b_B=2+theta,
where theta=e_LR+e_LB+e_RB.

Now restrict F to X union Y. Its quotient is obtained by deleting all Z-block vertices and incident edges from the two-path block forest. Therefore it remains a forest with b_X+b_Y vertices and exactly e_XY edges. The number of path components of F[X union Y] is consequently
  c_XY=b_X+b_Y-e_XY.
Summing over LR,LB,RB counts every b_X twice and every transition count once:
  c_LR+c_LB+c_RB
   =2(b_L+b_R+b_B)-theta
   =2(2+theta)-theta
   =4+theta.
In the hard residue each pair support has path-cover number two, so every restriction F[X union Y] has at least two components. Put sigma_XY=c_XY-2>=0. Subtracting six from the preceding identity gives
  sigma_LR+sigma_LB+sigma_RB=theta-2.
No monotonicity claim is used.

3. Rainbow-hinge/split-star dichotomy. Replace each maximal source-class block along each F-rail by its label. Adjacent labels in a rail word are distinct. If some rail contains three consecutive labels X,K,Y which are pairwise distinct, retain the corresponding three actual maximal blocks. The two literal selected adjacencies at the block boundaries have types XK and KY, hence are differently typed transitions sharing the same actual middle block K. This is the RAINBOW-HINGE alternative.

Assume no rainbow triple occurs. In any rail word of length at least three, if consecutive labels are a_i,a_{i+1},a_{i+2}, then a_i!=a_{i+1} and a_{i+1}!=a_{i+2}; absence of a three-distinct window forces a_{i+2}=a_i. Inductively every rail word alternates between at most two fixed labels.

Neither rail can contribute no interaction edge. If one rail consisted of a single block, the other rail could realize at most one source-class pair under the preceding two-label conclusion, so J(F) would have at most one edge and be disconnected. Hence both rails have at least two blocks and each realizes exactly one unordered class pair. These two pairs cannot be equal, again because J(F) is connected. On the three labels L,R,B, two distinct pair edges which form a connected graph share a unique center K. Name the other endpoints X and Y. Thus one rail alternates only between X and K and the other only between K and Y.

The leaf X occurs only on the first rail and Y only on the second. The center K occurs on both rails, because each rail realizes its center-leaf interaction. Let K_1 and K_2 be the subsets of K lying on the first and second rails. They are nonempty, disjoint, and cover K, giving
  V(P)=X union K_1,
  V(Q)=K_2 union Y.
This is the SPLIT-STAR WEAVE support form. Finally retain the original literal Hamilton source order on K. Since K_1 and K_2 are both nonempty, scanning that linear order from one end to the other must encounter two consecutive source vertices lying in different parts at the first change of part. That literal source adjacency has one endpoint on each F-rail. Its tightness is inherited from the source path K and is graph-intrinsic; no relation between the internal F-block orders and the source order has been asserted.

The two alternatives are mutually exclusive by construction and exhaustive. Together with accepted R931 they give precisely the claimed canonical arbitrary-fragmentation entrance. This proves R942.
