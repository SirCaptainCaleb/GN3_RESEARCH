# R596 — Synchronized high-side seam roots force explicit inward singleton anchors

## Statement
Retain the common transitive order-three singleton-deletion frame on X and Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=3, in which every re-root X-{x} is an exact trimer-plus-Q deletion cover, both endpoint five-cells are P5-free, and the endpoint reverse stars q_1 q_0 y and y q_m q_{m-1} are tight for every y in X. For a deletion root x, let ell_x and r_x be its numbers of bad left and right P-side seams among the three normalized seams. Then there exists a root x_L with ell_{x_L}=3 and a root x_R with r_{x_R}=3. More precisely, x_L is the unique intersection of a root-pair belonging to a fully-bad M_L edge at q_0 and the root-pair belonging to the fully-outgoing M_S edge at q_0; for its normalized trimer (a,b,c), (q_0,a,c,b) is a tight Hamilton P4 on {q_0} union (X-{x_L}), (q_1,q_0,a,c,b) is a tight P5, and q_3 q_2 x_L is tight. Dually, x_R is the intersection of a root-pair belonging to a fully-bad M_R edge at q_m and the root-pair belonging to the fully-incoming M_S edge at q_m; its normalized trimer has a tight P4 (b,a,c,q_m), a tight P5 (b,a,c,q_m,q_{m-1}), and x_R q_{m-2} q_{m-3} is tight. Thus the four-root seam synchronization always currentizes at least one left and one right high-side root into graph-intrinsic inward singleton anchors two positions inside Q. In the equality case of R576 with badness multiset {2,4,5,5}, these are the two distinct five-bad sibling roots.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; X is a transitive matching-height no-P4 four-cell; Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=3, is the common tight rail; every re-root X-{x} with any tight trimer orientation together with Q is an exact cover of H-x; X+q_0 and X+q_m are P5-free; the common endpoint reverse stars hold. Accepted R3,R4,R534,R543,R546,R549,R576.

## Scope
R24 restoration; order-unbounded and noncircular. Converts the unavoidable side-maximal roots hidden in the R576 counting argument into explicit endpoint P5s and named inward anchor turns. No use of R24, R533, R169, R5, or the order-ten closure.

## Applicability
Use at the first and last internal Q gaps. The turns q_3 q_2 x_L and x_R q_{m-2} q_{m-3} are distinguished witnesses in the R583/R588 obstruction arrays and preserve the physical deleted-root labels.

## Persistence
The roots x_L,x_R, their selected M_L/M_S/M_R physical edges, the endpoint P4/P5 orders, and the inward turns are simultaneous graph-intrinsic certificates in the original common frame.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert x_L=x_R or x_L!=x_R outside the R576 equality-two case. Does not make the middle rail Hamiltonian and does not itself produce a spanning two-cover. A single inward anchor is not yet an R542 two-witness packet; a second witness on the same tested reverse dimer is still required for capture/payment.

## Proof
At the left endpoint put L=q_0. For each physical M_L edge e, the two roots whose trimers contain e use the two turns uvL and vuL as their bottom seams. By R546 at least one of these turns is bad for each M_L edge. By R549 some M_L edge e_L is fully outgoing from L. Boundary antisymmetry R3 then makes both incoming seam orientations on e_L bad. Let F_L be the M_S edge fully outgoing from L, supplied by R534 (with the extreme gates supplied by R549). The root-pair containing e_L and the root-pair containing F_L intersect in exactly one root x_L: the three perfect matchings of K_4 give distinct pair partitions of the four deletion roots, and a pair from two distinct partitions meets in one root. Normalize X-{x_L} as (a,b,c) so bc=e_L is its M_L edge and ac=F_L is its M_S edge. Its two bottom seams bcL,cbL are bad because e_L is fully outgoing, and its middle seam acL is bad because F_L is fully outgoing. Hence ell_{x_L}=3. Since F_L is fully outgoing, both Lac and Lca are tight. The matching-height rule gives acb tight (M(ac)=M_S>M(cb)=M_L), so K_L=(L,a,c,b) is a tight Hamilton P4 on {L} union (X-{x_L}). The standing endpoint reverse star gives q_1La tight, hence A_L=(q_1,L,a,c,b) is a tight P5. Its complementary vertex set is W_L={x_L} union {q_2,...,q_m}. If H[W_L] had a Hamilton path, that path together with A_L would be a spanning two-cover of H, contrary to pc(H)>2. Therefore W_L is non-Hamiltonian. The literal order (x_L,q_2,q_3,...,q_m) differs from the tight Q-suffix only in the first turn x_L q_2 q_3. It cannot be tight, so x_L q_2 q_3 is bad and R3 gives q_3 q_2 x_L tight.

The right endpoint is the exact dual. Put R=q_m. R549 supplies an M_R edge e_R fully incoming to R, so R3 makes both outgoing seam orientations on e_R bad. Let F_R be the M_S edge fully incoming to R. The two associated root-pairs meet in a unique root x_R. Normalize X-{x_R} so ab=e_R and ac=F_R. Then its two top seams Rab,Rba and its middle seam Rac are bad, hence r_{x_R}=3. Full incoming polarity of F_R gives acR tight, and the matching-height turn bac is tight, so K_R=(b,a,c,R) is a tight P4. The endpoint reverse star cRq_{m-1} extends it to A_R=(b,a,c,R,q_{m-1}). If W_R={x_R} union {q_0,...,q_{m-2}} were Hamiltonian, W_R together with A_R would two-cover H. Hence the literal order (q_0,...,q_{m-2},x_R) fails at its only new turn q_{m-3}q_{m-2}x_R; R3 yields x_R q_{m-2} q_{m-3} tight.

Finally consider the equality-two configuration in R576. If r is the unique two-bad root, its selected one-bad M_L pair, common good M_S pair, and selected one-bad M_R pair are the three pairs through r. The root sharing only its M_R pair is the left-side three-bad root x_L, while the root sharing only its M_L pair is the right-side three-bad root x_R. These are two different members of the other three roots and are exactly the two five-bad siblings in the {2,4,5,5} law.
