# R1023 — Every crossed long-phase deep gap exits to closure or current maximum-three-forest dynamics

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R820 frame with Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6. For every deep gap 3<=j<=m-3 there is a finite certificate-retaining continuation yielding either a spanning two-cover of H or an actual literal minimum spanning three-cover whose distinguished proper rail contains one of the physical gap coordinates supplied by R820. For 3<=j<=m-4: a SEAM output U_j or V_j is already a proper tight trimer and currentizes by R4; a GATE output G_AC(j) or G_BZ(j) is an inward witness on one crossed middle-gate dimer, so R682 gives either a cross-end tight trimer, which currentizes by R4, or endpoint capture/payment, which reaches a both-singleton floor by R428 and then closes or re-enters current forest space by R1022; if neither SEAM nor GATE occurs, the two R820 terminal packets coexist and R874 gives a proper labelled P4, which currentizes by R4. At j=m-3, R820 already forces U_j or G_AC(j), so only the first two mechanisms occur.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame with m>=6. Use accepted R4,R428,R682,R820,R874 and pending clean bridge R1022 once reviewed.

## Scope
Parent export theorem for the crossed R24 long phase. It removes deep-gap local taxonomy as a terminal research branch by routing every gap to closure or actual maximum-three-forest representative dynamics. It does not prove extinction of the resulting forest dynamics.

## Applicability
Use anywhere in the crossed long phase after R820. Do not separately spend terminal packets, gate witnesses, or seam literals as terminal outputs; each belongs to one of the explicit forest-entry routes in this theorem.

## Persistence
Retain j, the exact R820 disjunct and its literal turn certificates. SEAM retains its trimer order; GATE retains tested middle-gate dimer, inward witness, R682 capture/XET ancestry, any paid-floor ancestry, and final trimer/forest; terminal-packet residue retains the R874 labelled P4 and R4 complement cover.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that forests obtained at different gaps coexist, that the resulting forest has lower largest-rail height, or that maximum-three-forest dynamics closes. The CAP route depends on R1022, which must be independently accepted before this theorem is usable. Does not claim a unique forest representative.

## Proof
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and the accepted deep-gap compression R820. Fix a deep gap j. We first treat 3<=j<=m-4, so every R820 window exists. Put q=q_{j+1}.\n\nAccepted R820 gives simultaneously\n\n  U_j OR G_AC(j) OR T_AC(j),\n  V_j OR G_BZ(j) OR T_BZ(j).\n\nSEAM. Suppose U_j holds. By definition, at least one of\n\n  (u,q_j,q_{j-1}),   (L,u,q_j)\n\nis tight. Either is a three-vertex tight path. Likewise, if V_j holds then at least one of\n\n  (q,s,R),   (q_{j+2},q,s)\n\nis tight. Since H has order greater than ten by accepted R533, and in any event contains X plus a long Q, each displayed trimer is proper. Accepted R4 exact-covers its complement, and restoring the trimer gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover. Thus every SEAM output already enters current maximum-three-forest space.\n\nGATE. Suppose G_AC(j) holds. Then either (q,c,a) or (q,a,c) is tight. In the first case q is a head witness on the tested crossed middle-gate dimer S=(c,a); in the second it is a head witness on S=(a,c). Because 3<=j<=m-4, the inward vertex q=q_{j+1} lies outside X union {L,R}. Thus accepted R682 applies with inward witness v=q.\n\nIf instead G_BZ(j) holds, then either (z,b,q_j) or (b,z,q_j) is tight. These are respectively tail certificates on S=(z,b) or S=(b,z), with inward witness v=q_j. Again q_j lies outside X union {L,R}, so R682 applies.\n\nIn either GATE case, R682 gives CAP or XET. In XET, one of (L,v,R),(R,v,L) is a graph-intrinsic tight trimer. It is proper, so R4 currentizes it immediately into a minimum spanning three-cover. In CAP, R682 retains an endpoint-capture/payment lineage for the middle-gate dimer S. Its R526 continuation either already gives a spanning two-cover, or reaches an ancestry-bearing balanced opposite-sign pair with a singleton support. If the opposite support is already singleton, retain the resulting both-singleton floor. Otherwise apply accepted R428 with the existing singleton fixed; unless H closes, R428 reaches an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor. Apply R1022 to that genuine paid floor. Once R1022 is independently accepted, its finite continuation gives either a spanning two-cover or a named proper tight trimer J together with an exact R4 complement two-cover, hence a current minimum spanning three-cover. Therefore every GATE output exits to closure or current maximum-three-forest space.\n\nTERMINAL-PACKET RESIDUE. Assume now that none of U_j,V_j,G_AC(j),G_BZ(j) holds. The two R820 trichotomies force both T_AC(j) and T_BZ(j). Thus a,c tail-sign D_{j+2}=(q_{j+2},q_{j+1}) and b,z head-sign D_j=(q_j,q_{j-1}). Accepted R874 applies to these two opposite two-witness waves. It gives either one of its labelled probe Hamilton P4s or the complete central fan (x,q_{j+1},q_j,y), x in {b,z}, y in {a,c}. Choose any resulting literal tight P4 K. It is proper. By R4, H-V(K) has an exact two-cover; restoring K gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover. This is the FOREST output already isolated in R1021, now consumed as one branch of the parent theorem.\n\nFinally let j=m-3. R820 states directly that its AC-side window has no T_AC term and forces\n\n  U_j OR G_AC(j).\n\nThe SEAM and GATE arguments above apply verbatim. For G_AC the witness is q_{m-2}, which is still outside X union {L,R}, exactly within the intended R682 inward-anchor scope. Hence the terminal gap also closes or exports to current maximum-three-forest dynamics.\n\nAll branches are finite and certificate-retaining. The output forest may differ completely from the source G8|N representative; no coexistence of gapwise descendants is claimed. This proves the theorem.
