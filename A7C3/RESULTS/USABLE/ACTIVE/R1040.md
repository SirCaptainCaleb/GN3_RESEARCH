# R1040 — Terminal X-mated source boundary in the v-rooted hard cell

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** D17.438

## Statement

In the v-rooted hard cell, let `C_*` be the unique augmenter and let `e_k` be its last old `F`-transition. Then `e_k` occurs strictly after the v-transition, is one of the two X-mated source gates, and the terminal alternating suffix beginning at `e_k` can be exchanged into `J` to produce a matching of the same cardinality as `J` with transition count at most two. The terminal gate is source-bearing, so its source spoke `t` retains the historical tight source trimer `(A,t,C)` and the adjacent old X-X mate supplied by R1037.

This result does **not** assert, without a separate theorem, that the historical D17.425 wall consumer automatically launches from this matching-level boundary or that the boundary is already a physical tight three-forest.

## Proof

R1037 identifies the three old `F` transitions in the v-rooted cell: the unique v-B transition and the two opposite-polarity X-mated source gates. R1038 shows that at least one of the source gates lies strictly after the v-transition on `C_*`. Hence the last old `F`-transition `e_k` lies strictly after the v-transition and cannot be v-bearing. It is therefore an X-mated source gate.

Write the alternating component in the indexing used for the terminal exchange, and form

`J' = J - {f_{k-1},...,f_{m-1}} + {e_k,...,e_m}`.

The alternating suffix exchange preserves matching cardinality. If `chi` is the X|B-transition indicator, then

`tau(J') = tau(J) - sum_{i=k-1}^{m-1} chi(f_i) + sum_{i=k}^{m} chi(e_i)`.

Since `k` is the last old `F`-transition on the component,

`sum_{i=k}^{m} chi(e_i)=1`.

Using `tau(J)=1` and nonnegativity of the removed-J sum gives

`tau(J') <= 1 + 1 = 2`.

Because `e_k` is source-bearing, its source endpoint `t` still carries the graph-intrinsic tight trimer `(A,t,C)`. R1037 additionally supplies the adjacent selected X-X mate and the ordered three-spoke source block.

## Audit adjustment

The original D17.438 text further said that this boundary automatically satisfies the hypotheses needed to launch D17.425's historical source-oriented wall machinery. That consumer implication was not durably reconstructed during audit and is unnecessary for the terminal-boundary theorem. It is therefore removed from the certified statement rather than treated as mathematical failure.

No R24, R5, or unrecovered D17.420 theorem is used.
