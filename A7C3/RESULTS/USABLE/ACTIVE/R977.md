# R977 — Two first-inward good anchors force a mixed-sibling double capture, a labelled P4, or a no-P4 cell

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888 in the accepted R962/R967 trimer-fiber setup G=X disjoint-union B. Fix an increasing Hamilton order B=(q_0,...,q_m) with m>=4 and write L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,w=q_3. Fix any exact two-cover T_L of G-{u,v}. For every x in X, accepted R967 implies the universal first-inward turn (v,u,x). Apply R508 to the four carriers K_x=(v,u,x) in this same T_L. Choose two distinct GOOD anchors x,y in X whose selected carrier/exterior crossings avoid the old-Q witness w; such a pair exists because deg_{T_L}(w)<=2. Thus R527 captures both x and y separately in the base fiber G-{u,v}.

For this ordered pair x,y the following trigger-complete conclusions hold.

(MIXED-SIBLING.) If (u,x,y) is tight, then K=(v,u,x,y) is a tight P4. The endpoint L is Hamilton-deletable. Accepted R967 makes B-L+r non-Hamiltonian for every r in X, and accepted R963 therefore forces (X-x)+L to be Hamiltonian. Choose any Hamilton P4 P_x^L on (X-x)+L. Then
  G-{u,x}=P_x^L | Q[2,m]
is a literal exact two-cover. Since y lies on P_x^L, choose any selected P_x^L-neighbor z of y. Every such z lies outside K. The mixed dimer S=(u,x) is head-signed by exact witness v via (v,u,x), and {y,z} is a selected carrier/exterior crossing for K in the mixed fiber which avoids v. R527 therefore captures y again in G-{u,x}. Hence y has two distinct source-fiber capture certificates sharing physical pivot u: the base fiber G-{u,v} and mixed sibling G-{u,x}.

(Q-DIMER P4.) Independently, if (y,v,u) is tight, then (y,v,u,x) is a tight P4. This may coexist with MIXED-SIBLING.

(NO-P4 CELL.) If neither (u,x,y) nor (y,v,u) is tight, then for the trimer C=(v,u,x) and fourth vertex y neither opposite-polarity terminal trigger of accepted R518 fires. Therefore {v,u,x,y} lies in one of the exact R516 no-P4 alignment signatures.

The same conclusions hold after interchanging x,y. The exact right-end dual holds with L,u,v,w replaced by R=q_m,s=q_{m-1},d=q_{m-2},rho=q_{m-3}, using mixed sibling fibers G-{x,s} built from Hamilton supports (X-x)+R and Q[0,m-2].

## Hypotheses
G minimum-order counterexample to R888 in accepted R962/R967 trimer setup; B=(q_0,...,q_m) Hamilton with m>=4; T_L exact two-cover of G-{q_1,q_2}; x,y distinct witness-avoiding good anchors supplied by the four-carrier R508 degree argument.

## Scope
Independent-safe multi-fiber transfer of the trigger-complete core of historical R779. Replaces its R723 shallow sibling cover by the critical five-cell Hamilton support forced directly by accepted R963/R967. No R24, gate phase, payment, R312, or computation.

## Applicability
Use after obtaining two good anchors in one first-inward pair-deletion cover. Test the two displayed turns independently. Prefer MIXED-SIBLING because it creates a second capture of one anchor in a distinct exact fiber through the same physical pivot. Joint failure yields a rigid no-P4 cell.

## Persistence
Retain B,L,u,v,w,T_L,x,y and their base selected crossings; in MIXED-SIBLING retain P4 K, Hamilton sibling rail P_x^L, exact cover of G-{u,x}, chosen selected neighbor z, and both capture-fiber certificates at y; in Q-DIMER retain its P4; in failure retain the exact no-P4 signature.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim the two P4 triggers are exclusive, that mixed-sibling must occur, that two capture descendants coexist after actualization, or that the no-P4 cell closes G.

## Proof
Fix B=(q_0,...,q_m), L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,w=q_3. For x in X, endpoint L is Hamilton-deletable and accepted R967 makes (B-L)+x non-Hamiltonian. If xq_1<q_1q_2, then (x,q_1,...,q_m) is a Hamilton path on that support, contradiction. Hence q_1q_2<q_1x, i.e. (v,u,x) is tight for every x.

Choose any exact two-cover T_L of G-{u,v}; it exists because this is a proper induced subgraph of a minimum R888 counterexample, and a Hamilton cover may be split if necessary. The Q turn (u,v,w) makes S_L=(u,v) tail-signed by w. For each x, K_x=(v,u,x) is a tight trimer spanning {u,v,x}. Apply R508 with deletion {u,v}, absorbable singleton {x}, carrier K_x, and the same T_L. It gives a selected edge C_x={x,y_x}, y_x outside K_x. If y_x=w call x blocked. The four C_x have distinct X endpoints. Since T_L is a union of two paths, w has selected degree at most two, so at most two x are blocked. Choose distinct good x,y whose crossings avoid w. R527 then captures x and y separately in this same base deletion frame, orienting each marker at its X endpoint.

Now test A=(u,x,y). If A is tight, the accepted turn (v,u,x) followed by A gives the tight P4 K=(v,u,x,y). We construct the mixed sibling cover of G-{u,x}. First, X+L is non-Hamiltonian: otherwise its Hamilton path together with the inherited Hamilton path B-L=(u,v,...,R) would two-cover G. Suppose (X-x)+L were non-Hamiltonian. Then X and (X-x)+L are two non-Hamiltonian four-sets sharing exactly the triangle X-x. Accepted R963 would Hamiltonize their five-vertex union X+L, contradiction. Hence choose a Hamilton P4 P_x^L on (X-x)+L.

Deleting u and x from G leaves the disjoint union of the support (X-x)+L and B-{L,u}=Q[2,m]. Thus P_x^L | Q[2,m] is an exact two-cover of G-{u,x}. The vertex y belongs to P_x^L and has at least one selected neighbor z there. Every vertex of P_x^L other than y lies in {L} union (X-{x,y}), while K uses {v,u,x,y}; hence z lies outside K. Put S=(u,x). The tight turn (v,u,x) makes S head-signed by exact witness v. Relative to carrier K, the carrier remainder is {v,y}; therefore the selected state {y,z} crosses carrier remainder to exterior and avoids witness v. Apply R527 in this mixed deletion frame and orient the marker to capture y. Since y was already good in T_L, it has one base-fiber capture certificate in G-{u,v} and one mixed-sibling capture certificate in G-{u,x}, sharing physical pivot u. This proves MIXED-SIBLING.

Independently, if Bturn=(y,v,u) is tight, then Bturn followed by (v,u,x) gives the tight P4 (y,v,u,x). The A and Bturn triggers may coexist.

Finally suppose neither A nor Bturn is tight. For carrier trimer C=(v,u,x), the initial dimer (v,u) has its natural tail sign witnessed by x and, because (v,u,y) is also one of the universal stars, the same-polarity tail witness y. The opposite head trigger at this end is exactly (y,v,u), absent by assumption. The terminal dimer (u,x) has natural head witness v from C; its opposite tail trigger is exactly (u,x,y), also absent. Thus neither opposite-polarity terminal trigger in accepted R518 fires, and R518 places {v,u,x,y} in an exact R516 no-P4 alignment signature.

Interchanging x,y is identical. At the right endpoint, R967 gives the universal stars (x,s,d); use the fixed exact cover of G-{d,s}, old-Q witness rho=q_{m-3}, and the Hamilton mixed sibling support (X-x)+R together with Q[0,m-2]. The same proof yields the stated dual. This proves R977.
