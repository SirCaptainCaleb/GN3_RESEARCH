# R463 — Physical Mate-Cycle Forcing

## Statement
Let H satisfy pc(H)>k. Call {A,B} a physically certified mate clause when a displayed literal spanning k-cover proposal has complete hole set exactly {bar(A),bar(B)}, where bar denotes exact boundary-antisymmetric reversal. For any finite family F of such clauses, form the directed physical implication graph G_F on turn literals by inserting, for every certified clause {A,B}, the two labeled arcs bar(A)->B and bar(B)->A, each retaining the underlying proposal and its exact two-hole certificate.

(1) Tightness propagates along every directed path of G_F. Hence if G_F contains a directed path bar(X)->...->X, then X is tight.

(2) Call a literal L collision-decorated when either L=(w,p,q) and a retained tight turn (w',p,q) with w' distinct from w is available, or L=(p,q,w) and a retained tight turn (p,q,w') with w' distinct from w is available. Thus, if L becomes tight, R38 gives a same-polarity two-witness collision on the oriented support (p,q). If one certified clause {A,B} has a directed path from A to a collision-decorated literal L_A and a directed path from B to a collision-decorated literal L_B (paths of length zero allowed), then H carries a named R38 same-support collision, with the realized implication path and every proposal/hole certificate on that path retained.

The R173 Physical Mate-Cycle is the special case in which the clauses give the path bar(X_0)->X_1->...->X_r->X_0. In the all-internal R359 currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V, each directional residual-rail R453 splice clause is already doubly collision-decorated by the two relevant R359 shield turns, so part (2) recovers the partner-only residual-terminal collision conclusion of R455 without requiring a mate cycle.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry R3; pc(H)>k; every implication edge comes from a displayed literal spanning k-cover proposal whose complete hole set is exactly the two reversed endpoint literals. For part (2), the sink decorations are retained tight turns on the identical oriented physical dimer and with distinct witnesses, in the tested order required by R38. The R359/R453 specialization assumes the all-internal proper-trimer currentization and nontrivial residual rails in the scope of those revisions.

## Scope
Order-free, CW-free, certificate-preserving parent compiler for physical two-hole clause systems. It strengthens the cycle-only interface of R173 to arbitrary directed reachability and adds collision-decorated sinks. The abstract graph never replaces the underlying path-cover certificates: every arc is labeled by its literal proposal and exact hole pair.

## Applicability
Use when physical mate clauses are abundant but direct complement cycles are absent. Search for one or more additional certified proposals that create directed reachability between native clause literals, or for structural shield turns that decorate reachable literals. This reframes the R453/R460 bottleneck from 'build a full mate cycle' to 'connect both branches of one live physical clause to productive sinks.'

## Persistence
Forced turn conclusions and R38 collisions are graph-intrinsic once obtained. Every implication step retains the exact proposal and two-hole certificate that justified it; collision sinks retain the oriented physical support and both witness identities. Alternative proposal representatives need not remain simultaneously current.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
An abstract Boolean clause, SAT implication, Hall edge, or unlabeled resolution step is insufficient: every graph arc must retain a literal physical k-cover proposal with its complete two-hole set. The theorem does not create new proposals, does not assert the native R453 fan has nontrivial reachability beyond its six clauses, and does not claim an R38 collision alone closes H. Collision decoration requires identical oriented support, same polarity/tested order, and distinct witnesses; mere support overlap is insufficient.

## Proof
Fix a finite family F of physically certified mate clauses in the sense of R463. For one clause {A,B}, retain its displayed spanning k-cover proposal. By definition the complete unresolved hole set of that proposal is exactly {bar(A),bar(B)}. Because pc(H)>k, those two hole turns cannot both be tight. Hence at least one of bar(A),bar(B) is bad. By boundary antisymmetry R3, at least one of A,B is tight. Thus the physically certified disjunction A OR B holds, with the literal proposal and its complete two-hole certificate retained.\n\nIf bar(A) is tight, then R3 says A is bad. Since A OR B holds, B is tight. Therefore the labelled arc bar(A)->B is a sound tightness implication. Symmetrically bar(B)->A is sound. Repeating this one-clause argument along a directed path in G_F proves tightness propagation while retaining every proposal/hole certificate labelling the path.\n\nFor part (1), suppose G_F contains a directed path bar(X)->...->X. If X were bad, R3 would make bar(X) tight, and path propagation would then make X tight, contradiction. Therefore X is tight. The R173 mate-cycle pattern is exactly the special case whose certified clauses provide the successive labelled arcs bar(X_0)->X_1, X_1->X_2, ..., X_r->X_0, so the same argument recovers that forcing directly; R173 itself is not used as a dependency.\n\nFor part (2), let L be collision-decorated. In the head case L=(w,p,q) and a retained turn S=(w',p,q) is tight with w' distinct from w. Once L is tight, L and S are two head certificates on the identical tested oriented dimer (p,q), with distinct witnesses. Current reconstructible R523 therefore gives exactly the named same-support two-head collision packet. In the tail case L=(p,q,w), S=(p,q,w') the TT clause of R523 gives the corresponding two-tail collision. Now let {A,B} be a certified source clause with directed paths A->...->L_A and B->...->L_B to collision-decorated sinks. The already-proved disjunction A OR B makes at least one source endpoint tight. Propagation along its designated labelled path makes the corresponding sink tight, and R523 then gives the named collision. Every implication proposal, hole pair, tested support, and witness identity on the realized route is retained.\n\nIt remains only to reconstruct the all-internal specialization without the historical R359/R453 route. Assume the specialization hypotheses of R463, so H is the hypothetical smallest counterexample in the all-internal proper-trimer source geometry with J=(a,x,c) and a fixed literal currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V. Write U=(u_1,...,u_r), V=(v_1,...,v_s). Apply current accepted R920 to this same named trimer and source currentization. Its direct all-internal proof gives |U|,|V|>=3 and the four tight reverse shields (u_2,u_1,x), (x,u_r,u_{r-1}), (v_2,v_1,x), (x,v_s,v_{s-1}). No singleton-floor theorem is involved.\n\nTest the literal U-to-V concatenation while keeping J as the second path. Every old turn of U and V is inherited; the complete new hole set is exactly h_1=(u_{r-1},u_r,v_1) and h_2=(u_r,v_1,v_2). If both were tight, (u_1,...,u_r,v_1,...,v_s) together with J would be a spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)=3. Thus one hole is bad. R3 gives the physically certified mate clause A OR B with A=bar(h_1)=(v_1,u_r,u_{r-1}) and B=bar(h_2)=(v_2,v_1,u_r). This is precisely the native directional clause historically supplied by R453, now proved directly.\n\nBoth endpoints are already collision-decorated with zero-length implication paths. For A, the tested oriented dimer is (u_r,u_{r-1}); A is a head certificate with witness v_1, while R920 supplies the tight head shield (x,u_r,u_{r-1}) with distinct witness x. For B, the tested oriented dimer is (v_2,v_1); B is a tail certificate with witness u_r, while R920 supplies the tight tail shield (v_2,v_1,x) with distinct witness x. The witnesses are distinct because J,U,V are vertex-disjoint literal paths. Therefore part (2), using R523 on the exact tested orientations, forces one of the two named residual-terminal collision packets. Exchanging U and V gives the symmetric directional channel. This is exactly the partner-only collision content asserted in the final specialization of R463.\n\nThus every conclusion of R463 follows from R3, R523, and R920. The historical R38 role is supplied by its current fully reconstructible replacement R523, while the historical R359/R453 specialization is reconstructed directly from R920 plus the literal two-hole seam proposal. No R359, R453, R168, R5, R24, or P22 dependency is used.
