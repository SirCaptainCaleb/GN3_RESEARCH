# R998 — Every proper increasing path in a minimum R888 counterexample has an exact two-covered complement

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888, and let K be any nonempty proper vertex-simple increasing path of G. Put H=G-V(K). Then pc_inc(H)=2. Consequently H has an exact two-path cover U|V, and K|U|V is a spanning increasing three-path cover of G containing K literally as one rail. In particular every oriented physical edge, every increasing trimer, and every increasing P4 can be restored literally as one component of a spanning three-cover.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order counterexample to R888; K is a nonempty proper increasing path in G.

## Scope
Pure edge-ordered minimum-counterexample restoration theorem. No boundary-tournament minimality, R980, threshold matching, longest-path hypothesis, or singleton-family structure.

## Applicability
Use whenever completeness or a local edge-order inequality produces a new increasing path K whose old complement ordering should be discarded. It supplies a fresh exact two-cover of the complement while retaining K literally. In particular it is the parent for arbitrary dimer donors and double-block reverse-P4 rebuilds.

## Persistence
Retain G, the literal increasing path K, its vertex complement H, and one exact two-cover U|V of H.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert any relation between U|V and a previous cover of the same vertices. Does not prescribe endpoints or support sizes of U,V beyond nonemptiness forced by pc_inc(H)=2. Does not prove that repeated restorations terminate.

## Proof
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888 and let K be a nonempty proper increasing path. Put H=G-V(K). Since K is proper, H is nonempty and has strictly fewer vertices than G. Being an induced subgraph of an edge-ordered complete graph, H is itself an edge-ordered complete graph. By minimum-order choice of G, H is not a counterexample to R888, so pc_inc(H)<=2. If pc_inc(H)=1, H has an increasing Hamilton path P. Then K|P is a spanning cover of G by at most two increasing paths, contradicting that G is a counterexample. Hence pc_inc(H)=2. Choose an exact two-path cover H=U|V. Since K,U,V are disjoint increasing paths whose vertex sets partition V(G), K|U|V is a spanning increasing three-cover containing K literally. The final dimer, trimer, and P4 specializations are immediate because every such object is a nonempty proper increasing path whenever G itself is a counterexample.
