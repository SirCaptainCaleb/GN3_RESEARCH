# R1048 — A literal v-rooted first-loss pivot meets spectator order in exactly two ways

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** workspace root `1789345175.808309`

## Statement

Retain the sharp v-rooted first-loss cell of R1036, and assume the literal first-loss `K_{2,2}` switch exists, so both adjacent closures at the first-positive pivot are defined. Write

`e_r=a_{r-1}->b_r ∈ F\J`,  `f_r=a_r->b_r ∈ J\F`,

with `e_r` the installed first-positive old-`F` transition. Then exactly one of the following holds:

1. `a_{r-1}=v` and `b_r∈B`. In this case `e_r=v->b_r` is the unique old `v-B` transition, and `f_r` is a genuine consecutive spectator adjacency incident with `b_r`.
2. `b_r=v` and `a_{r-1}∈B`. In this case `f_r=a_r->v` is an `X-X` edge joining `v` to one of the source spokes `a_r∈{p,q,r}`.

Thus every literal v-rooted first-loss cell meets the spectator coordinate at its canonical pivot unless the deleted `J` edge itself is the `v`-to-source-spoke edge.

No such source/v classification is asserted at a numerical first-positive threshold whose adjacent `K_{2,2}` switch is absent because of the unique loop defect.

## Proof

R1036 gives the corrected first-loss dichotomy. Whenever the literal first-loss rectangle exists and is not source-rooted, the physical `X`-endpoint `z` of the installed old transition `e_r` is exactly the unique non-spoke vertex `v`.

R1037 gives the sharp v-rooted source-frame structure: `X={v,p,q,r}`, the three vertices `p,q,r` are the source spokes, `v` has selected old-`F` degree one, and the only non-source transition of `F` is the unique selected `v-B` edge.

D17.411's first-positive-prefix ledger gives

`chi(e_r)=1`,  `chi(f_r)=0`.

Therefore `e_r` crosses `X|B`, while `f_r` is same-side, and the two alternating edges share the physical endpoint `b_r`.

If the `X` endpoint of `e_r` is `a_{r-1}`, then R1036 makes `a_{r-1}=v` and therefore `b_r∈B`. Same-sidedness of `f_r=a_r->b_r` gives `a_r∈B`. R1047 proves that every selected `B-B` edge of the canonical `J` is a consecutive edge of the displayed spectator path. Hence `f_r` is a genuine spectator adjacency incident with `b_r`. R1037 identifies `e_r` as the unique old `v-B` transition.

If instead the `X` endpoint of `e_r` is `b_r`, then `b_r=v` and `a_{r-1}∈B`. Same-sidedness gives `a_r∈X`. Since `f_r` is a selected path edge it is not a physical loop, so `a_r≠v`; R1037 therefore gives `a_r∈{p,q,r}`. Thus `f_r=a_r->v` is exactly a source-spoke-to-`v` `X-X` edge.

The two positions of the `X` endpoint of the crossing edge `e_r` are exhaustive.
