# R1055 — Three-step IN-v corridor to a source gate or ledger reset

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789345603.140719`

## Statement

In the genuine non-loop IN-`v` first-loss rectangle, let `r` be the first-positive zipper index. Within at most the three subsequent zipper indices `r+1,r+2,r+3`, either

1. an installed old-`F` edge is a source gate between `B` and one of `{p,q,r}`, or
2. the unique `J` transition is deleted and the prefix ledger drops from `S=1` to `S=0`.

Hence the non-loop IN-`v` first-loss cliff cannot be followed by an arbitrarily long all-same-side source-free corridor. The isolated closure-loop branch is excluded.

## Proof

At the audited IN-`v` pivot, `a_r=t_0∈{p,q,r}` and `S_r=1`. In the genuine non-loop rectangle, the same-side closure diagonal has its near endpoint in `B`, so its far endpoint `b_m` also lies in `B`.

For `k>r` before either stopping event, suppose `a_{k-1}` is a source spoke. The installed edge is `e_k=a_{k-1}->b_k∈F\J`. If `chi(e_k)=1`, it is already a source gate. Otherwise `e_k` is same-side, so `b_k∈X`. R1037 gives `d_F(v)=1`, already consumed by the first-loss edge `e_r`, so `b_k≠v`; hence `b_k` is another source spoke. If `k=m`, this same-side alternative is impossible because `b_m∈B`, so the terminal edge is necessarily a source gate.

For `k<m`, if `chi(f_k)=1` for `f_k=a_k->b_k∈J\F`, then `f_k` is the unique `J` transition. Since `e_k` is same-side, this step contributes `-1` to the prefix ledger. Earlier continuing post-`r` steps have both alternating edges same-side and contribute zero, so `S_{k-1}=1` and `S_k=0`.

If both `e_k` and `f_k` are same-side, then `a_k∈X`. It cannot equal `v`, since then the next old-`F` edge `e_{k+1}` would give a second selected `F` incidence at `v`, contradicting R1037. Thus `a_k` is again a source spoke.

The augmenter is a simple alternating path in the split matching graph, so its OUT-side vertices are distinct. Each physical vertex has one OUT copy, hence the physical labels `a_0,…,a_{m-1}` are pairwise distinct. Since `a_r` already occupies one of the three source labels, at most two subsequent all-same-side continuations can consume new source labels. Therefore by one of `k=r+1,r+2,r+3`, or earlier termination at `m`, a source gate or the unique `J` transition is forced.

## Scope

The `J`-transition alternative certifies the numerical reset `S=0`, not by itself a physical strict-descent closure. The source-gate alternative still requires its existing seam/blocker consumer. No claim is made for the isolated closure-loop branch.
