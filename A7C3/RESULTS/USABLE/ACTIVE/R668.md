# R668 — Crossed shallow inward punctures force four explicit endpoint-star mate clauses

## Statement
Retain the crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, L=q_0,u=q_1,R=q_m,s=q_{m-1}. Puncturing the four shallow singleton-deletion covers at the adjacent inward Q vertex gives four literal pair-deletion two-covers, exact by R429, and four direct physical two-hole clauses: root z: from H-{z,s}=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (R,a,c,b) and tight (z,R,s), (a,s,R) OR (c,a,s); root b: from H-{b,s}=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (R,c,a,z) and (b,R,s), (c,s,R) OR (a,c,s); root c: from H-{c,u}=(a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[2,m] and (u,L,c), (u,z,b) OR (L,u,z); root a: from H-{a,u}=(c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[2,m] and (u,L,a), (u,b,z) OR (L,u,b). The endpoint-star turns are the accepted R594 turns. Each clause is proved directly by one literal spanning two-cover proposal with exactly the displayed two uncertified turns; if both holes were tight H would be two-covered, so R3 forces the reversal of at least one. No generic R358 or rail-size theorem is used. Every literal contains u or s, so the packet is not decided by the X-only matching-height orientation.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted crossed R621; m>=4. Use accepted R621 shallow covers, accepted R594 endpoint reverse stars, R429 pair-deletion exactness, and R3.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Crossed phase only. Direct two-hole certificates; removes both R5 and the hidden R358->R168 dependency from earlier routes.

## Applicability
Use as the explicit inward endpoint-star clause packet. Search for sibling clauses/reversals or collision decorations involving u or s; do not treat these as X-local tautologies.

## Persistence
Retain the four shallow covers, the four R594 endpoint-star turns, four pair-deletion covers, four explicit spanning proposals, exact two-hole sets, and eight reverse literals.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert any clause closes H or that the clauses form a cycle. Supersedes R662 for reconstructible noncircular use; no R358,R168,R5,R169,R224,R533.

## Proof
Write L=q_0,u=q_1,R=q_m,s=q_{m-1}.

Root z. R621 gives H-z=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,a,c,b). Delete s, leaving Q[0,m-2] sqcup (R,a,c,b), a literal two-cover of H-{z,s}; R429 makes it exact. Accepted R594 gives the endpoint-star turn (z,R,s). Consider P_z=(z,R,s,a,c,b). The turns (z,R,s), (a,c,b), and (R,a,c) are certified; the only uncertified consecutive turns are (R,s,a) and (s,a,c). If both were tight, P_z together with Q[0,m-2] would two-cover H. Hence at least one is bad, so R3 gives (a,s,R) or (c,a,s) tight.

Root b. R621 gives H-b=Q[0,m-1] sqcup (R,c,a,z). Delete s and use R429. R594 gives (b,R,s). The proposal P_b=(b,R,s,c,a,z) has only holes (R,s,c) and (s,c,a), so R3 gives (c,s,R) or (a,c,s).

Root c. R621 gives H-c=(a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[1,m]. Delete u to obtain the exact R429 cover (a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[2,m] of H-{c,u}. R594 gives (u,L,c). The proposal P_c=(a,b,z,u,L,c) inherits (a,b,z) and (u,L,c); its only holes are (b,z,u) and (z,u,L). Thus R3 gives (u,z,b) or (L,u,z).

Root a. From H-a=(c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[1,m], delete u and use R429. R594 gives (u,L,a). The proposal P_a=(c,z,b,u,L,a) has only holes (z,b,u) and (b,u,L), so R3 gives (u,b,z) or (L,u,b).

These are literal physical two-hole certificates. No generic R358 or rail-size theorem is used. Every reverse literal contains u or s, so the clauses are not decided solely by the X matching-height orientation. This proves R668.
