# R2036 — Two-vertex source-rail absorber and B-minus-one residue

**Status:** PASS PENDING (audit complete; chronological frontier only)
**Source:** Slack workspace R2036

## Statement

In the retained source frame, suppose a literal exact two-cover `T` of `G=H-{A,C}` has a two-vertex rail on `{s,u}`, where `s` is a retained source spoke. Then necessarily `u in B`.

Moreover, the six-set `{A,C,p,q,r,u}` has a tight Hamilton `P6`. Consequently `H[(B-{u}) union {v}]` is nonHamiltonian.

If the other `T`-rail contains all of `B-{u}` as one maximal tight `B`-block, then that displayed block has empty `v`-insertion set, and R1033 supplies its local star-triangle or reverse-spoke-hook obstruction.

## Proof

Let `{t,r}` be the other two retained source spokes. By R2032, applied to the two-vertex source rail `{s,u}`, the vertex `u` is not a source spoke and the two anchor caps

`(s,A,u)` and `(u,C,s)`

are tight.

On the triple `{t,A,r}`, R3 gives one of the two opposite orientations. Interchange the names `t,r` if necessary so that `(t,A,r)` is tight. R2033 then applies with its sixth vertex instantiated as `u`, using the three retained source turns `(A,s,C)`, `(A,t,C)`, `(A,r,C)` together with `(s,A,u)`, `(u,C,s)`, and `(t,A,r)`. Hence `{A,C,s,t,r,u}={A,C,p,q,r,u}` has a tight Hamilton `P6`.

If `u=v`, the complementary vertex set is exactly `B`, which has the retained tight spectator Hamilton path. That path together with the displayed `P6` would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting counterexample status. Since R2032 already excludes `u` from `{p,q,r}` and `V(G)=B disjoint-union {v,p,q,r}`, it follows that `u in B`.

Now remove the six-set `{A,C,p,q,r,u}` from `H`. Its complement is exactly

`K=(B-{u}) union {v}`.

If `H[K]` had a tight Hamilton path, that path together with the `P6` would again form a spanning exact two-cover of `H`. Therefore `H[K]` is nonHamiltonian.

Finally assume the other `T`-rail contains every vertex of `B-{u}` in one maximal tight `B`-block `B'=(b_1,...,b_m)`. Then `B'` is itself a tight Hamilton path of `B-{u}`. NonHamiltonicity of `K` implies `m>=2` and forbids every insertion of `v` into the displayed order of `B'`: any successful insertion would be a tight Hamilton path of `K`. Thus `I_{B'}(v)=empty`, and audited R1033 gives the corresponding star-triangle or reverse-spoke-hook certificate on `v` and at most three consecutive vertices of `B'`.
