# R2036 — Two-vertex source-rail ambient normal form

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** Slack workspace R2036 re-audit

## Repaired statement

In the retained source frame, if a literal exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` has a two-vertex rail on `{s,u}` with retained source spoke `s`, then

- `u` is not a retained source spoke, hence `u in B union {v}`;
- `(s,A,u)` is tight;
- `(u,C,s)` is tight;
- `(C,u,A)` is tight.

The former P6 absorber and all consequences derived from it are withdrawn.

## Proof

Corrected R2032 applies verbatim to the two-vertex source rail. It gives the three displayed tight turns and excludes `u` from the other retained source spokes.

Since `V(G)=B disjoint-union {v,p,q,r}` and `s` is one of `p,q,r`, exclusion of the remaining source spokes leaves exactly `u in B union {v}`.

## Removed overclaims

The former proof invoked false R2033 to Hamiltonize `{A,C,p,q,r,u}`. R2033 has an explicit globally edge-ordered counterexample and is unusable. Therefore the deductions `u in B`, nonHamiltonicity of `(B-{u}) union {v}`, and the downstream R1033 insertion obstruction are not certified and are removed.

## Dependencies

Trusted dependencies: corrected R2032 and the retained source-frame vertex partition.

Provisional dependencies: none.
