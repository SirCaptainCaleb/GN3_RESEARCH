# R961 — Role-correct endpoint-return Hall cycles reduce to R435 geometry or a constant-role directed triangle

## Statement
Retain all accepted conclusions of R945. In addition, choose the left-saturating endpoint-core matching and shortest directed cycle as in R945, and assume every adjacent puncture-path comparison is R435-quiet. Then the resulting shortest directed cycle is a triangle and its three matched endpoint roles are all the same: either all three matched endpoints are HEADs of their certified Hamilton puncture paths or all three are TAILs.

Write the triangle labels cyclically as A,B,C and put M=Omega-{A,B,C}. R435-quiet monotonicity gives one common literal order M=(m_1,...,m_r) in all three puncture paths. For each i, y_0=A,y_1=B,y_2=C, the path form is determined by (epsilon_i,epsilon_{i+1}): HH=(y_{i+1},y_{i+2},M), HT=(y_{i+1},M,y_{i+2}), TH=(y_{i+2},M,y_{i+1}), TT=(M,y_{i+2},y_{i+1}). Up to cyclic rotation the mixed patterns are HHT and HTT. In HHT, (B,C,M),(C,M,A),(B,M,A) occur, and the final turn of (C,M,A) appends A to (B,C,M), Hamiltonizing Omega. In HTT, (B,M,C),(M,A,C),(B,M,A) occur, and the final two turns of (M,A,C) insert A before C in (B,M,C), Hamiltonizing Omega. Hence only HHH or TTT survives outside explicit R435 output.

## Hypotheses
Finite boundary tournament Omega, |Omega|>=5, Omega non-Hamiltonian, every one-vertex deletion Hamiltonian. Use the R945 extremal left-saturating endpoint-core matching and assume adjacent comparisons on its shortest cycle are R435-quiet.

## Scope
All-order strengthening of accepted R945 for non-Hamiltonian deletion-Hamiltonian blocks. It removes the mixed-role triangle residual by literal puncture-path seam surgery. This redeposits the mathematics after a procedural review-write error on R960.

## Applicability
Inside a deletion-Hamiltonian non-Hamiltonian block, use R945 and then this theorem: either retain explicit R435 geometry, or work with one directed endpoint-return triangle whose three certified puncture paths have a common HEAD role or a common TAIL role.

## Persistence
Retain the matching, triangle labels A,B,C, actual puncture paths, certified HEAD/TAIL roles, common literal M order, and the exact mixed-role surgery.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not consume the surviving HHH/TTT triangle. Does not reverse any puncture path or synchronize unrelated puncture paths.

## Proof
Apply accepted R945. If an adjacent puncture-path comparison yields an R435 reversal, reverse trimer, or proper tight cycle, retain that output. Otherwise the extremal shortest functional cycle is a directed triangle. Write y_0=A,y_1=B,y_2=C cyclically, and let P_i be the actual Hamilton path on Omega-y_i whose matched endpoint is y_{i+1}; epsilon_i=H or T records whether that endpoint is the certified head or tail. No path is reversed.

Put M=Omega-{A,B,C}. Quiet adjacent comparisons preserve the literal order of their common vertices, so all three puncture paths induce one common order M=(m_1,...,m_r), r>=2. The common vertex y_{i+2} is the matched endpoint of P_{i+1}; if epsilon_{i+1}=H it is the first common contact in both P_{i+1} and P_i, and if epsilon_{i+1}=T it is the last common contact in both. Together with the location of the noncommon matched label y_{i+1} in P_i, this gives exactly:
HH: P_i=(y_{i+1},y_{i+2},M);
HT: P_i=(y_{i+1},M,y_{i+2});
TH: P_i=(y_{i+2},M,y_{i+1});
TT: P_i=(M,y_{i+2},y_{i+1}).

If roles are mixed, cyclic rotation reduces to HHT or HTT.

For HHT, epsilon_0=H,epsilon_1=H,epsilon_2=T, so
P_0=(B,C,M), P_1=(C,M,A), P_2=(B,M,A).
All turns of (B,C,M) are certified by P_0. The sole new turn required to append A is (m_{r-1},m_r,A), which is the final turn of P_1. Thus (B,C,M,A) is Hamiltonian on Omega, contradiction.

For HTT, epsilon_0=H,epsilon_1=T,epsilon_2=T, so
P_0=(B,M,C), P_1=(M,A,C), P_2=(B,M,A).
Insert A immediately before C in P_0. The only new turns are (m_{r-1},m_r,A) and (m_r,A,C), both consecutive turns of P_1. Hence (B,M,A,C) is Hamiltonian on Omega, contradiction.

Thus every quiet triangle has constant role HHH or TTT. This proves R961.
