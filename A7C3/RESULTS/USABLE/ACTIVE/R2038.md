# R2038 — Tau=1 three-vertex isolated-X endpoint packet

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** Slack workspace R2038 re-audit

## Statement

In the retained source frame, suppose a literal exact two-cover `T` of `G=H-{A,C}` has block shape `X-B | X` and its isolated X-rail `P` has exactly three vertices. Then `P` has a retained source endpoint `s`. After deleting `s` and preserving the residual orientation as `(u_0,u_1)`, at least one of

`(s,C,u_0)` and `(u_1,A,s)`

is bad. Equivalently R3 forces at least one of the reverse caps

`(u_0,C,s)` and `(s,A,u_1)`

to be tight.

The former elimination of the entire three-vertex isolated-X subcase is withdrawn.

## Proof

Since `X={v,p,q,r}` contains only one non-source vertex, every three-vertex X-subset contains at least two retained sources. Hence a three-vertex path on such a subset has at least one source endpoint.

Choose a source endpoint `s`. Corrected R2028 applies directly to the isolated three-vertex X-rail and gives the displayed seam/cap disjunction.

## Removed overclaim

The former proof attempted to Hamiltonize the five-set obtained by adjoining `A,C` using cyclic rotations of source turns and R3 caps. Cyclic rotations are not automatic under R3, so the claimed impossibility is not certified.

## Dependencies

Trusted dependencies: corrected R2028, R3, and `X={v,p,q,r}`.

Provisional dependencies: none.
