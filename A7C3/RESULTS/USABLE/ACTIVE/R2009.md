# R2009 — Off-component first-open blockers reduce to physical obstruction

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789413782.426479`

## Statement

If an exceptional first-open blocker `c->z∈F\J` from R2008 lies on an off-`C_*` symmetric-difference component `D`, then either `c` is terminal on the endpoint-truncated rail `J[B]`, or toggling the delta-zero component `D` from its F-state to its J-state preserves matching cardinality and replaces the blocker at `c,out` by the canonical successor `c->b_{t+2}`.

Hence the nonterminal off-component residue is purely a physical seam/cycle obstruction, not a matching-availability obstruction.

## Proof

Let `M=hat M_{r-1}` be the normalized pre-loss matching and let `D` be an off-`C_*` component of `F triangle J` containing the selected exceptional blocker `c->z` at `c,out`. By R2008, `c->z∈F\J`; by normalized-prefix convention, every off-`C_*` component is in its F-state in `M`.

Because `C_*` is the unique nonzero-cardinality symmetric-difference component between the exact-two-cover matching `F` and the three-forest matching `J`, every off-`C_*` component is delta-zero. Thus `D` contains equally many `F\J` and `J\F` edges. Replacing all selected `F\J` edges of `D` by all `J\F` edges of `D` therefore preserves matching cardinality and yields another matching.

Suppose now that `c` is nonterminal on the endpoint-truncated spectator rail `J[B]`. Audited R1049 says that the selected J-successor at `c=b_{t+1}` is exactly the forward spectator edge `c->b_{t+2}`. This edge cannot lie in `F∩J`, because the selected blocker `c->z∈F\J` already occupies the same split copy `c,out`. Hence `c->b_{t+2}∈J\F`, and since it is adjacent at `c,out` to `c->z`, it lies on the same symmetric-difference component `D`.

Toggling `D` therefore removes `c->z` and installs `c->b_{t+2}` at `c,out`. The new successor is precisely the R1033 first-open successor, so the local turn `(v,c,b_{t+2})` is already tight whenever the first-open edge `v->c` is tested.

Thus no matching-level obstruction remains in the nonterminal off-component branch. Any surviving failure after the toggle must come from physical realization of the toggled state, such as a bad inherited seam, a directed cycle/component collision, or another physical obstruction elsewhere on `D`.

If `c` is terminal on `J[B]`, R1049 supplies no canonical successor and this argument stops exactly at the stated endpoint-truncated residue.
