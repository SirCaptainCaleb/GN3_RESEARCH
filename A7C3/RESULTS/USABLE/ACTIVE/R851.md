# R851 — Deleting the first-inward pair yields a singleton-end five-cell triple-transition fan

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with u=q_1 and s=q_{m-1}, and put W=H-{u,s}. Then W has the literal five-component reference path cover G_0=(L), G_1=Q[2,m-2], G_2=(R), G_3=(a,c), G_4=(b,z). All five components are nonempty pairwise disjoint tight paths for every m>=4. For every exact two-cover T of W, the partition-cover deficit identity gives t_G(T)=3+sigma_G(T)>=3 selected transitions joining distinct reference cells, with equality exactly when every G_i occurs as one contiguous T-block. Every such selected intercell state is R176-admissible, and either deleted vertex u or s may be chosen as the arbitrary spare in a separate R176 certificate. This conclusion depends only on the physical deletion pair and five-cell source partition; no signed-floor status of {u,s} is required.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame with m>=4. Use accepted pair-deletion exactness R429, partition-cover deficit identity R7, and cross-state birth R176.

## Scope
Corrected five-cell deletion theorem, detached from the invalid outer-inward floor claim.

## Applicability
Use as the exact H-{q_1,q_{m-1}} transition-count front door. The three or more selected cross states are available regardless of whether the deleted pair belongs to any payment lineage.

## Persistence
Retain the five physical source cells, the exact two-cover T of W, all selected intercell transitions counted by t_G(T), and whichever deleted spare u or s is used in any subsequent R176 certificate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim {q_1,q_{m-1}} is an ancestry-bearing floor, that any R176 birth is automatically progress, or that transitions from different exact covers coexist.

## Proof
Deleting u=q_1 and s=q_{m-1} from X union V(Q) leaves the five displayed paths: singleton L=q_0, the contiguous old-Q interval Q[2,m-2] (nonempty for m>=4), singleton R=q_m, and the two crossed middle-gate dimers (a,c),(b,z), which are tight in the matching-height X cell. They are pairwise disjoint and cover W. Accepted R429 supplies an exact two-cover T of W. Apply accepted R7 to the fixed partition into G_0,...,G_4. Each cell has path-cover number one, so t_G(T)=sum_i pc(G_i)-2+sigma_G(T)=5-2+sigma_G(T)=3+sigma_G(T)>=3, and equality is exactly sigma_G(T)=0, equivalently every source cell occurs as one contiguous T-block. Every counted transition joins distinct cells of the literal five-cover on the proper residue W, so accepted R176 applies to that physical cross state. Its spare vertex is arbitrary outside W and may therefore be chosen as u or, in a separate certificate, as s. No step uses or establishes a balanced-pair floor on {u,s}.
