# R626 — Synchronized two-root left packet still does not force a cover-valued first inward switch

## Statement
There exists a Strong Level-(1) orientation F on V=X union {L,u,v,w}, X={a,b,c,z}, which simultaneously realizes the full local left high-side data of the synchronized two-root packet while avoiding every cover-valued first inward switch visible on this prefix. Specifically: (1) X is the transitive matching-height no-P4 four-cell M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}; (L,u,v,w) is tight; X union {L} is Hamilton-P5-free; (u,L,x) is tight for every x in X; and the full left gate turns Lbc,Lcb,Lac,Lca,bzL,zbL,czL,zcL are tight. (2) F-z has the two literal exact two-covers (u,L,a,c,b) sqcup (v,w) and (L,a,c,b) sqcup (u,v,w), while F-b has the two literal exact two-covers (u,L,c,a,z) sqcup (v,w) and (L,c,a,z) sqcup (u,v,w). Thus the two enlarged roots share the same local suffix and the same physical frame. (3) Every immediate one-turn left reinsertion proposal from those four covers is blocked in the counterexample direction: Luz,vuz,wvz,aLz,Lub,vub,wvb,cLb are tight; the terminal short-rail mates zbc and bza are also tight in X. Hence the packet contains both R614-type seed-concatenation blockers and the full 2-by-2 witness rectangle on (L,u) and (w,v). (4) Nevertheless neither six-set {L,u,v,a,b,c} nor {L,u,v,a,c,z} has a Hamilton P6. Thus neither synchronized root produces the desired advanced local rail on (X-{r}) union {L,u,v}. (5) More strongly, the only tight paths in F ending with the ordered suffix (v,w) are (v,w), (u,v,w), (c,v,w), (L,u,v,w), and (z,c,v,w); in each case the complementary vertices have no Hamilton path. Hence no pair of disjoint tight paths covering F has one rail ending in (v,w), so no prefix configuration visible on this eight-vertex packet can concatenate with an external continuation of Q after w to give a spanning two-cover. Therefore the synchronized two-root left packet, even after closure-saturating all immediate one-hole proposals, does not by itself compile to a spanning two-cover or an advanced H-r cover A_2 sqcup Q[3,m]. Any valid consumer must import information not contained in this local packet, such as a nonlocal/right-root coordinate or a stronger cross-frame invariant.

## Hypotheses
Only Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry is used for the finite countermodel. The displayed packet reproduces the accepted transitive X geometry, endpoint P5-freeness, left gate data, both synchronized enlarged-root truncations, and all immediate left one-turn reinsertion blockers corresponding to the live R598/R614/R616 frame.

## Scope
Durable R24 strategy fence strengthening R613 after the synchronized two-root refinement. It tests the entire left prefix X union Q[0,3], not a standalone one-sided EAR table.

## Applicability
Use to rule out purely local exact-cover exchange/currentization arguments that consume only the two synchronized left roots and their q0..q3 one-hole blockers. A successful next step must retain an additional nonlocal/right-side coordinate or a stronger invariant tying this packet to another frame.

## Persistence
Retain the eight physical labels L,u,v,w,a,b,c,z; roots b,z; the four local deletion covers; the blocker/mate turns; the complete orientation certificate; the two P6-free advanced six-sets; and the complete list of paths ending v,w with non-Hamilton complements.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not show that the full R24 frame is realizable, does not contradict R616, and does not rule out consumers using right-end/root synchronization, global pair-deletion structure, or other nonlocal information. The countermodel is local and is intended only as an implication fence.

## Proof
Fix the vertex order L<u<v<w<a<b<c<z. For every 3-set {x<y<z}, encode the three reversal pairs by bits eps_1 eps_2 eps_3, where eps_1=1 means xyz is tight (else zyx is tight), eps_2=1 means xzy is tight (else yzx is tight), and eps_3=1 means yxz is tight (else zxy is tight). Every omitted 3-set has code 000. Use the following nonzero codes:

Luv 101; Luw 111; Lua 111; Lub 101; Luc 111; Luz 101; Lva 010; Lwa 110; Lwb 001; Lwz 001; Lab 001; Lac 111; Laz 101; Lbc 110; Lbz 001; Lcz 001; uvw 101; uva 101; uvb 001; uvc 001; uvz 001; uwa 001; uaz 010; ubc 011; vwa 111; vwb 111; vwc 100; vwz 001; vac 010; vaz 010; vbc 110; vcz 001; waz 010; abc 111; abz 101; acz 001.

This assigns exactly one tight orientation in every complete-reversal pair, so F is Strong Level-(1). Direct evaluation gives the matching-height rule on X, the tight rail (L,u,v,w), the endpoint star (u,L,x) for all x in X, and the full left gate packet Lbc,Lcb,Lac,Lca,bzL,zbL,czL,zcL. The paths (u,L,a,c,b), (L,a,c,b), (u,v,w), (u,L,c,a,z), and (L,c,a,z) are tight, so the four displayed deletion two-covers are literal. Exhaustive enumeration of all 7! orders shows that F-z and F-b each have zero Hamilton paths. Hence those displayed two-covers are exact.

The same certificate gives Luz,vuz,wvz,aLz,Lub,vub,wvb,cLb,zbc,bza tight. Exhaustive enumeration of the 5! orders on X union {L} gives zero Hamilton P5s. Enumeration of the 6! orders on {L,u,v,a,b,c} and {L,u,v,a,c,z} gives zero Hamilton P6s.

Finally enumerate every vertex-simple tight path ending with ordered dimer (v,w). There are exactly five: (v,w), (u,v,w), (c,v,w), (L,u,v,w), and (z,c,v,w). For the complementary vertex sets of these five paths, exhaustive Hamilton-path counts are respectively 0,0,0,0,0. Therefore no two-path cover of F can have one rail ending (v,w). All conclusions follow from this finite complete-reversal certificate. The certificate was located by a 0-1 feasibility search, but the proof is the explicit orientation table plus exhaustive finite verification; no solver assumption enters the mathematical conclusion.
