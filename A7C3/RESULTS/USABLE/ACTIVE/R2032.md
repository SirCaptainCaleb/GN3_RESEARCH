# R2032 — Two-vertex source-rail normal form

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace R2032

## Statement

If a literal exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` has a two-vertex rail on `{s,u}` with source spoke `s`, then the four-set `{A,C,s,u}` is `P4`-free. Besides the retained source turn `(A,s,C)`, its forced tight orientations are

`(s,A,u)`, `(u,C,s)`, and `(A,C,u)`.

Consequently `u` cannot be another retained source spoke.

## Proof

Let `Q` be the other rail of the exact two-cover. If `{A,C,s,u}` had any tight Hamilton `P4`, that `P4` together with `Q` would be a literal spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting the smallest-counterexample hypothesis. Hence the four-set is `P4`-free.

The retained source turn `(A,s,C)` is tight. Test the other three unordered triples.

First, if `(A,s,u)` were tight, then `(C,A,s,u)` would be a tight `P4`, because `(C,A,s)` is the cyclic presentation of `(A,s,C)`. Therefore `(A,s,u)` is bad. R3 forces the complete reverse orientation, whose cyclic presentation is `(s,A,u)`, tight.

Second, if `(s,C,u)` were tight, then `(A,s,C,u)` would be a tight `P4`. Hence `(s,C,u)` is bad and R3 forces `(u,C,s)` tight.

Third, with `(s,A,u)` already tight, if `(A,u,C)` were tight then `(s,A,u,C)` would be a tight `P4`. Thus `(A,u,C)` is bad, and R3 forces the reverse cyclic orientation `(A,C,u)` tight.

These are exactly the three triple orientations on the four-set other than the retained source triple. If `u` were another retained source spoke, then its source turn `(A,u,C)` would also be tight. But `(A,u,C)` is the orientation opposite to the already forced `(A,C,u)`, a contradiction. Therefore `u` is not another source spoke.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: counterexample status of `H`, the retained source trimer `(A,s,C)`, and R3.

Provisional dependencies: none.

This strengthens the two-vertex special case of R2030 and independently supplies the source-source exclusion needed in that exact geometry.
