# R2032 — Two-vertex source-rail normal form, repaired

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED PENDING
**Source:** Slack workspace R2032 re-audit

## Statement

If a literal exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` has a two-vertex rail on `{s,u}` with retained source spoke `s`, then:

- the four-set `{A,C,s,u}` is `P4`-free;
- `(s,A,u)` is tight;
- `(u,C,s)` is tight;
- `u` cannot be another retained source spoke.

The formerly claimed additional turn `(A,C,u)` is **not** forced and is removed.

## Proof

Let `Q` be the other rail of the exact two-cover. If `{A,C,s,u}` had a tight Hamilton `P4`, that `P4` together with `Q` would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting counterexample status. Hence the four-set is `P4`-free.

The retained source turn `(A,s,C)` is tight.

If `(s,A,u)` were bad, R3 would make its complete reverse `(u,A,s)` tight. Then

`(u,A,s,C)`

would be a tight Hamilton `P4`, contradiction. Thus `(s,A,u)` is tight.

If `(u,C,s)` were bad, R3 would make `(s,C,u)` tight. Then

`(A,s,C,u)`

would be a tight Hamilton `P4`, contradiction. Thus `(u,C,s)` is tight.

Finally suppose `u` were another retained source spoke. Then its source turn `(A,u,C)` would be tight. Together with the already forced `(s,A,u)`, this makes

`(s,A,u,C)`

a tight Hamilton `P4`, again impossible. Hence `u` is not another source spoke.

## Removed overclaim

The original version asserted `(A,C,u)` tight. This does not follow. For example, on the four vertices globally order the ordinary edges by

`As < Cu < AC < su < Au < Cs`.

Under R887's edge-order interpretation, this four-set is `P4`-free and has `(A,s,C)`, `(s,A,u)`, and `(u,C,s)` tight, while `(A,C,u)` is bad because `AC>Cu`.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: counterexample status of `H`, retained source turn `(A,s,C)`, R3, and R887 for the counterexample to the removed overclaim.

Provisional dependencies: none.

The source-source exclusion used downstream remains valid. Any downstream use of the removed turn `(A,C,u)` must be re-audited.
