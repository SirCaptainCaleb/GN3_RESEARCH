# R682 — An inward witness on a crossed middle-gate dimer forces endpoint capture or a cross-end tight trimer

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, endpoints L=q_0 and R=q_m, and let S be any one of the four oriented middle-gate dimers (a,c),(c,a),(b,z),(z,b). Suppose a vertex v outside X union {L,R} is a sign witness on the tested orientation S, with the polarity appropriate to the displayed tight turn. Then every exact two-cover T of H-V(S) has the following nonlocal dichotomy.

(CAP) For at least one endpoint e in {L,R}, T selects a carrier/exterior state on {e,y} with y outside the R649 short carrier K_e=S union {e} and y!=v. Using S with witness v and this same-frame selected crossing, accepted R527 may be oriented to capture e itself. Since S is a dimer, the R526 payment continuation then yields either a spanning two-cover or a certificate-retaining singleton-width descendant, while preserving S, the inward witness v, the physical endpoint e, and the actual selected crossing as source ancestry.

(XET) Otherwise T contains selected states on both physical supports {L,v} and {R,v}. Since v occurs on exactly one T rail and has both L and R as its two selected neighbors, that rail contains either the consecutive order (L,v,R) or (R,v,L); hence one of these is a graph-intrinsic tight trimer. Thus failure of inward-witness capture at both endpoint carriers forces an explicit tight trimer joining the two opposite Q endpoints through v.

The two endpoint crossings needed for the dichotomy are unconditional: R649 supplies for every tested S both a tight trimer carrier K_L with V(K_L)=V(S) union {L} and a tight trimer carrier K_R with V(K_R)=V(S) union {R}. Applying accepted R508 separately to K_L and K_R in the same exact support-deletion cover T forces a selected crossing out of singleton L and a selected crossing out of singleton R. Consequently, for the anchor witnesses v=q_2 or v=q_{m-2} produced by the new five-cell exchange packets, a same-polarity X-dimer collision can no longer be paid anonymously: it either carries that inward anchor into an endpoint-capture lineage or creates the literal cross-end trimer L-v-R / R-v-L.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted crossed R621; S is one oriented middle-gate dimer among (a,c),(c,a),(b,z),(z,b); v is outside X union {L,R} and is an exact sign witness on S. Use accepted R649,R508,R527,R526.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 consumer. Fixed crossed middle-gate X dimers with a new exterior witness. Same exact H-V(S) two-cover is used for both endpoint crossings. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,generic R358, or R436.

## Applicability
Use immediately when an anchor-exchange clause adds q_2 or q_{m-2} as a same-polarity witness on an oriented G_3 or G_4 dimer. Choose any exact H-V(S) two-cover and run both endpoint R508 carriers simultaneously. If either endpoint crossing avoids the inward witness, capture that endpoint using the inward witness itself; only if both endpoint crossings are forced through the inward witness does the exact cover contain a cross-end tight trimer.

## Persistence
Retain S and its tested orientation/polarity, inward witness v, the chosen exact H-V(S) cover T, both R649 endpoint carriers K_L,K_R, one selected L-exterior state and one selected R-exterior state. In CAP retain the avoiding crossing, captured endpoint, R527/R526 actualization and later payment ancestry. In XET retain the literal T-rail order L-v-R or R-v-L.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a singleton-width paid descendant closes H or preserves T as current. Does not assert CAP descendants from L and R coexist; only the source endpoint crossings coexist in T. Does not apply when the additional sign witness lies in X union {L,R}. Does not by itself consume the cross-end trimer.

## Proof
Fix an exact two-cover T of H-V(S). Accepted R649 lists, for each of the four tested orientations S, a tight trimer K_L whose support is V(S) union {L}, and another tight trimer K_R whose support is V(S) union {R}. Concretely, for S=(a,c) these may be (L,c,a) and (R,c,a); for S=(c,a), (L,a,c) and (R,a,c); for S=(b,z), (z,b,L) and (z,b,R); for S=(z,b), (b,z,L) and (b,z,R). Only their supports and tightness matter here.

Apply R508 to the deletion D=V(S), absorbable singleton {L}, recompletion path K_L, and the exact cover T. It forces at least one selected T-state crossing {L} | (V(H)\V(K_L)); write one such physical state as {L,y_L}. Apply R508 again to the same T, now with K_R. It forces a selected state {R,y_R} crossing {R} | (V(H)\V(K_R)). These are simultaneous current facts of the one exact cover T.

If some endpoint crossing avoids v, say y_e!=v for e=L or R, then its physical marker {e,y_e} is disjoint from V(S) union {v}: e is outside S, y_e lies outside K_e and hence outside S and e, and by assumption y_e!=v. Use S as the signed support with exact witness v and apply accepted R527 to the same selected crossing. R527 permits either physical marker endpoint to be designated as the captured signed anchor by orienting the marker accordingly, so designate e. This gives the CAP certificate while retaining S, v, the exact T-state {e,y_e}, marker orientation, and captured endpoint e. Because S has order two, R527 invokes the aligned dimer payment of R526, yielding either a spanning two-cover or a certificate-retaining singleton-width descendant. No later currentness of T is claimed.

Suppose no endpoint crossing avoids v. Then every selected L-exterior crossing is incident with v and every selected R-exterior crossing is incident with v. In particular the two states supplied above have supports {L,v} and {R,v}. The vertex v appears exactly once in the path cover T. Both selected states therefore lie on the same T rail and are the two rail adjacencies incident with v. Since L and R are distinct, v is internal on that rail, with consecutive order either L,v,R or R,v,L. A T rail is a tight path, so its consecutive three-vertex segment is a tight trimer in the corresponding order. This is XET and completes the dichotomy.
