# R513 — Pure-rail seam coupling localizes the unique-transition three-hole residue

## Statement

Retain a full R512 packet and resolve outputs lexicographically by preferring R512(A) or (B) whenever either applies. Then the only unresolved P4 residue has both consecutive pure-C/Q seam holes bad. If the natural seam order is (a,b,c,d), those holes are (a,b,c) and (b,c,d), so R3 makes (d,c,b) and (c,b,a) tight. Hence the single coordinate-preserving P4 needed in the residue is the literal full reverse K*=(d,c,b,a); no enumeration of anonymous Hamilton P4 orders is needed.

CS. Put a=q_{j-2}, b=q_{j-1}, c=c_0, d=c_1. Replace the natural four-segment (a,b,c,d) in the R511 proposal by K*. This gives
U*= (q_0,...,q_{j-3}, c_1,c_0,q_{j-1},q_{j-2}, c_2,...,c_s),
V = (b_0,...,b_r,q_j,...,q_m),
with empty prefix/suffix omitted. Its complete uncertified-turn set is {g=(b_r,q_j,q_{j+1})} union L_CS(j) union R_CS(s), where
L_CS(2)=empty,
L_CS(3)={(q_0,c_1,c_0)},
L_CS(j)={(q_{j-4},q_{j-3},c_1),(q_{j-3},c_1,c_0)} for j>=4,
and
R_CS(1)=empty,
R_CS(2)={(q_{j-1},q_{j-2},c_2)},
R_CS(s)={(q_{j-1},q_{j-2},c_2),(q_{j-2},c_2,c_3)} for s>=3.
Thus (j,s)=(2,1) gives a literal one-hole spanning proposal with sole hole g; (2,2) and (3,1) give literal two-hole spanning proposals with the displayed sole outer hole together with g. Every other CS parameter cell is an exact reverse-seam irreducible cell for this coordinate-preserving substitution, with all missing outer turns displayed above: equivalently j=2,s>=3; j=3,s>=2; or j>=4,s>=1.

SC. Put a=c_{s-1}, b=c_s, c=q_{i+1}, d=q_{i+2}. Replace the natural four-segment in the other R511 rail by K*. This gives
U=(q_0,...,q_i,b_0,...,b_r),
V*=(c_0,...,c_{s-2}, q_{i+2},q_{i+1},c_s,c_{s-1}, q_{i+3},...,q_m),
again omitting empty pieces. Its complete uncertified-turn set is {h=(q_{i-1},q_i,b_0)} union L_SC(s) union R_SC(i), where
L_SC(1)=empty,
L_SC(2)={(c_0,q_{i+2},q_{i+1})},
L_SC(s)={(c_{s-3},c_{s-2},q_{i+2}),(c_{s-2},q_{i+2},q_{i+1})} for s>=3,
and
R_SC(m-2)=empty,
R_SC(m-3)={(c_s,c_{s-1},q_{i+3})},
R_SC(i)={(c_s,c_{s-1},q_{i+3}),(c_{s-1},q_{i+3},q_{i+4})} for i<=m-4.
Thus (s,i)=(1,m-2) gives a literal one-hole spanning proposal with sole hole h; (2,m-2) and (1,m-3) give literal two-hole spanning proposals. Every other SC parameter cell is exact reverse-seam irreducible for this substitution: equivalently s=1,i<=m-4; s=2,i<=m-3; or s>=3,i<=m-2.

The construction is valid whether each Q-side seam vertex lies in D or S, because it changes the spanning R511 proposal rather than the deletion cover T. If either Q-side seam vertex lies in D, K* cannot itself be a path of H-D. If both lie in S, vertex eligibility alone still does not define an alternative exact cover T' of H-D: R511/R512 retain no adjacency or neighbor relation between these two Q-consecutive vertices and their positions in the literal S-block A. Accordingly no lexicographic decrease of T is claimed without additional A-position and outer-turn certificates.


## Hypotheses

H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2, and the full R512(P) tau(T)=1 packet is retained. In CS, s>=1 and 2<=j<=m-1; in SC, s>=1 and 1<=i<=m-2. Outputs (A) and (B) are consumed whenever their seam-hole status permits, leaving both named seam holes bad.


## Scope

Order-free exact consumption of the pure-C/Q seam residual. It uses only boundary antisymmetry and the literal R511/R512 coordinates. The irreducibility conclusion concerns the displayed coordinate-preserving reverse-four-segment substitution, not every possible global rearrangement of T or of the spanning proposal.


## Applicability

In an R512(P) cell, first inspect the two seam holes. If zero or one is bad, use R512(A)/(B). If both are bad, use the forced literal reverse P4 and read the exact CS/SC outer-turn set above. The three listed shallow-boundary cells immediately reduce to one/two holes; all other cells expose the exact additional outer certificates still required.


## Persistence

Retain D,S,Q,T,K,B,A, the unique crossing orientation and Q cut index, the original R511 proposal and isolated mixed-seam hole, both bad seam holes, the forced ordered P4 K*, the D/S membership of both Q-side seam vertices, and every displayed outer turn. P4 tightness is graph-intrinsic; proposal, block, cut, and membership coordinates are representative-relative.


## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not claim that both-bad P4 residue is realizable. Does not claim the forced P4 lies in H-D when a Q-side vertex lies in D. Even when both lie in S, does not infer an exact alternative deletion cover or lexicographic decrease without their literal positions in A and the needed outer turns. Does not rule out a different global splice in the listed irreducible cells. Does not use generic R435, balanced-pair, or anonymous P4 output.


## Proof

Write the natural seam order as (a,b,c,d). In the residual both (a,b,c) and (b,c,d) are bad. By R3 their complete reversals (c,b,a) and (d,c,b) are tight. These are exactly the two consecutive turns of K*=(d,c,b,a), proving K* is a literal tight Hamilton P4 with the required physical labels.

For CS, the original U rail contains the consecutive segment
(...,q_{j-4},q_{j-3},a,b,c,d,c_2,c_3,...),
with nonexistent terms omitted. Deleting (a,b,c,d) and inserting (d,c,b,a) preserves every vertex exactly once. The two internal inserted turns are certified by K*. On the left, there is no junction turn when j=2; one junction turn (q_0,d,c) when j=3; and exactly two, (q_{j-4},q_{j-3},d) and (q_{j-3},d,c), when j>=4. On the right there is none when s=1; one, (b,a,c_2), when s=2; and exactly two, (b,a,c_2) and (a,c_2,c_3), when s>=3. All turns farther from the splice remain turns of Q or K. The other proposal rail is unchanged, so its sole old uncertified turn is g. This proves completeness of the CS ledger and the hole counts.

For SC, the original V rail contains
(...,c_{s-3},c_{s-2},a,b,c,d,q_{i+3},q_{i+4},...).
The identical reverse substitution certifies its two internal turns. Its left junction contributes zero, one, or two turns according as s=1, s=2, or s>=3, exactly as displayed; its right junction contributes zero, one, or two according as i=m-2, i=m-3, or i<=m-4. Every other turn is inherited from K or Q, and U remains unchanged with sole old hole h. This proves the SC ledger.

A complete hole set of cardinality one or two is the asserted literal one-/two-hole proposal. In all other parameter cells the displayed substitution has at least the isolated old hole plus two explicitly named outer holes, so it is irreducible as a one-/two-hole output without certifying at least one of those exact outer turns. Finally, a vertex of D is absent from H-D, while membership of both Q-side vertices in S supplies no information about their locations or neighbors in A; hence the stated deletion-cover fence follows.
