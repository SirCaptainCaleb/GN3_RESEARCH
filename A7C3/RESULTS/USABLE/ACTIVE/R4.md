# R4 — Smallest-counterexample minimality

**Status:** USABLE / ACTIVE
**Type:** reduction
**Source:** A7C2 active-spine migration S2; controlling compressed sources M1/M6/M10

## Statement

In a smallest counterexample H, pc(H)=3 and every nonempty proper induced subsystem has path-cover number at most two. Every proper graph-intrinsic tight path has an exact two-cover of its complement, and restoring it gives a minimum spanning three-cover containing it literally.

## Scope

A smallest counterexample to Strong Level-(1) two-cover.

## Preferred proof

Let H be chosen with minimum vertex order among counterexamples. Every nonempty proper induced subgraph has a spanning cover by at most two tight paths. Choosing any vertex v, a two-cover of H-v plus singleton (v) gives a three-cover of H, while H has no two-cover, so pc(H)=3. For any proper graph-intrinsic tight path K, its complement has a cover by at most two paths. It cannot be Hamiltonian, since K plus that Hamilton path would two-cover H. Thus the complement has path-cover number exactly two, and restoring K literally gives a minimum spanning three-cover containing K.
