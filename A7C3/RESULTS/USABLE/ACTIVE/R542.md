# R542 — Two-witness short-carrier boundary dimer forces capture or singleton-width payment

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample. Let K=(a,b,c) be a tight trimer and let S be either reverse boundary dimer (c,b) or (b,a), viewed as a signed support carrying two distinct same-polarity witnesses w_1,w_2 outside K on the same tested orientation. Then every exact two-cover T of H-V(S) selects a state crossing the singleton carrier remainder V(K)\\V(S) and the exterior V(H)\\V(K). For any such crossing, at least one of w_1,w_2 is not an endpoint of the crossing. Applying same-frame crossing/capture with that witness forces the capture/refund branch. Since S has order two, signed-support payment yields a finite certificate-retaining continuation to either a spanning two-cover or a descendant balanced opposite-sign pair with a singleton support. In particular the concentration branch cannot occur simultaneously for both witnesses.

## Hypotheses
H smallest Strong counterexample; K tight trimer; S one reverse terminal physical dimer of K; two distinct same-polarity signed witnesses on the same tested orientation; pair-deletion exactness for H-V(S).

## Scope
Short-carrier two-witness capture theorem; order-free beyond the trimer geometry.

## Applicability
Use when a same-polarity collision lands on a reverse terminal dimer of a 3-vertex carrier. It turns collision into an unavoidable physical capture across the complementary singleton carrier vertex.

## Persistence
Retain K,S,w_1,w_2, the chosen exact cover T, an actual selected carrier/exterior crossing, the witness avoided by that crossing, and the R527/R526 capture-payment ancestry.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim the singleton signed-support descendant is itself an exact singleton deletion cover or a contradiction.

## Proof
Assume S=(c,b); the head-end case S=(b,a) is dual. Put D=V(S) and W=V(H)\\D. The complementary carrier block is {a}. The original trimer K is a tight path spanning exactly D union {a}. Pair-deletion exactness supplies an exact two-cover T of H-D. Apply R508 with deletion D, absorbable block {a}, and recompletion path K. Therefore T selects at least one state crossing {a} | (V(H)\\V(K)). Such a selected state has physical support {a,y} for one exterior vertex y. Since w_1,w_2 are distinct exterior witnesses, y cannot equal both; choose w among {w_1,w_2} with y != w. The selected crossing {a,y} therefore avoids the exact sign witness w of S. The capture branch of R527 applies in this same deletion cover and actualizes a disjoint marker while retaining S and its witness. Because S has order two, the aligned payment clause built into R527 via R526 gives a finite certificate-retaining continuation to either a spanning two-cover or a descendant balanced opposite-sign pair with a singleton support. Thus the concentration alternative is impossible once both witnesses are retained: whichever crossing R508 supplies avoids at least one of them.
