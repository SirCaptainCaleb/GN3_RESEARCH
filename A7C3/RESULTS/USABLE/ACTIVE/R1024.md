# R1024 — The aligned R24 long phase exits to closure or current maximum-three-forest dynamics

## Statement
Retain the accepted R594/R619 ALIGNED frame in the surviving long range m>=6. Then there is a finite certificate-retaining continuation yielding either a spanning two-cover of H or an actual literal minimum spanning three-cover. Concretely, in the common pair-deletion residue W=H-{b,z}, accepted R899 supplies the exact covers T_L^a=(u,L,a,c) sqcup Q[2,m] and T_R^c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,c,R,s). Accepted R416 therefore yields closure or an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor retaining this same-residue cross-state provenance. In the latter case R1022 closes or re-enters current maximum-three-forest space through a named proper tight trimer.
