# R876 — The aligned order-ten R24 frame has an immediate 6+4 spanning cover

## Statement
Retain the aligned m=5 R594/R619 frame with Q=(L,u,v,d,s,R), X={a,b,c,z}, and aligned middle-gate edge {a,c}. Then H has the literal spanning two-cover P_6 sqcup P_4 where P_6=(u,L,a,c,R,s) and P_4=(b,d,v,z). Hence no aligned m=5 order-three R24 frame exists.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the aligned R619/R594 frame with m=5. Use accepted R594 endpoint stars, aligned gate turns from R619, and aligned inward turns from R536.

## Scope
Finite aligned m=5 closure. Literal 6+4 two-cover; no payment, capture, R5,R24, Mate-Fork, computation, or path-growth theorem.

## Applicability
Use as the complete aligned m=5 finite base once freshly reviewed.

## Persistence
Retain Q,X and the two literal path orders P_6=(u,L,a,c,R,s), P_4=(b,d,v,z).

## Exclusions / nonclaims
No additional phase or residue remains.

## Proof
Write Q=(L,u,v,d,s,R). Accepted R594 gives the universal endpoint-star turns (u,L,x) and (x,R,s) for every x in X, hence in particular (u,L,a) and (c,R,s). In the aligned R619 gate, {a,c} is the same middle-gate edge at both ends, so (L,a,c) and (a,c,R) are tight. Therefore the order P_6=(u,L,a,c,R,s) is a tight path, with consecutive turns (u,L,a),(L,a,c),(a,c,R),(c,R,s).

Accepted R536 in the aligned phase says the complementary middle-gate edge {b,z} gives the right inward turns (b,q_{m-2},q_{m-3}) and (z,q_{m-2},q_{m-3}); at m=5 these are (b,d,v) and (z,d,v). The left inward packet gives (d,v,z) on the same complement. In particular (b,d,v) and (d,v,z) are tight, so P_4=(b,d,v,z) is tight.

The supports of P_6 and P_4 are disjoint and together are {L,u,v,d,s,R} union {a,b,c,z}=V(H). Thus P_6 sqcup P_4 is a spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. Hence the aligned m=5 frame is impossible.
