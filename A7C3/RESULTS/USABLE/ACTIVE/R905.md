# R905 — Crossed singleton-deletion endpoint rotations force deep-coupled two-witness packets at both ends

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m. Put t=q_3, h=q_4, r=q_{m-3}, and ell=q_{m-4}. Then the first deep cut at each end forces a graph-intrinsic short-carrier two-witness packet that genuinely uses a deep rail coordinate. LEFT: at least one of the following four packets exists, where S is the displayed tested reverse boundary dimer of tight trimer K, e is the complementary singleton, and W is a same-polarity witness pair outside K: (L0) K=(d,s,R), S=(R,s), e=d, W={v,z}; (L1) K=(z,c,b), S=(b,c), e=z, W={a,t}; (L2) K=(c,a,z), S=(z,a), e=c, W={b,t}; (L3) K=(v,t,h), S=(h,t), e=v, W={b,z}. RIGHT: at least one of the following four packets exists: (R0) K=(L,u,v), S=(u,L), e=v, W={d,a}; (R1) K=(ell,r,d), S=(r,ell), e=d, W={a,c}; (R2) K=(a,b,z), S=(b,a), e=z, W={c,r}; (R3) K=(c,z,b), S=(z,c), e=b, W={a,r}. Thus the crossed long frame cannot realize the R904-style endpoint-rotation freedom at either side: singleton-deletion non-Hamiltonicity forces a two-witness packet reaching q_3/q_4 or q_{m-4}/q_{m-3}, or a cross-end packet using v or d.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest boundary-tournament counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame; m>=6. Uses only the literal R621 singleton-deletion covers and tight turns, plus boundary antisymmetry R3.

## Scope
Noncircular crossed long phase. Selected-depth endpoint-rotation consumer; no R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,five-cell classification,capture,payment,R436,or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately after R621/R723 when an intact-middle-only consumer stalls. Each forced packet is an exact input type for the accepted two-witness short-carrier consumer R696, but unlike the core-only R649 packet family, every packet here contains a first-deep rail coordinate or a cross-end witness. This supplies a concrete smallest-counterexample constraint violated by the pending R904 witness family.

## Persistence
Retain the two root-coupled three-hole clauses used in the proof at each side and whichever packet is realized. Left clauses: (t,b,c) OR (h,t,b) OR (v,R,s), and (t,z,a) OR (h,t,z) OR (v,R,s). Right clauses: (u,L,d) OR (a,r,ell) OR (b,a,r), and (u,L,d) OR (c,r,ell) OR (z,c,r). These clauses and the resulting packet are graph-intrinsic in the fixed crossed frame.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close the crossed phase, choose which packet occurs, or assert that an R696 descendant closes H. Does not preserve the whole middle rail intact. Does not claim R904 is false; R904 is a pending local-frame witness outside smallest-counterexample minimality. The result is a selected-depth consequence of genuine singleton-deletion non-Hamiltonicity, not a bounded-state normalization of all cover interactions.

## Proof
Let t=q_3 and h=q_4. R621 gives the exact deletion covers H-z=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m] and H-b=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m], where Q[2,m]=(v,t,h,...,s,R). Consider the spanning orders of the two deletion residues obtained by rotating the first vertex v of the long rail to its end:
P_z=(u,L,a,c,b,t,h,...,s,R,v),
P_b=(u,L,c,a,z,t,h,...,s,R,v).
All consecutive triples in P_z are already tight except possibly (c,b,t), (b,t,h), and (s,R,v). All consecutive triples in P_b are already tight except possibly (a,z,t), (z,t,h), and (s,R,v). If all three holes in either order were tight, that order would be a Hamilton path of H-z or H-b; adding the deleted vertex as a singleton would give a spanning two-cover of H, impossible in a smallest counterexample. Hence each order has a bad hole. By boundary antisymmetry R3, the exact reversals give the simultaneous clauses
(1) (t,b,c) OR (h,t,b) OR (v,R,s),
(2) (t,z,a) OR (h,t,z) OR (v,R,s).
If (v,R,s) is tight, then K=(d,s,R) is a tight Q trimer, S=(R,s) is its reverse boundary dimer, and v together with z are same-polarity witnesses because R621 contains the tight turn (z,R,s). This is L0. Suppose instead (v,R,s) is bad. Then the first two literals of each clause remain. If (t,b,c) is tight, K=(z,c,b) is tight from the transitive matching-height orientation of X, S=(b,c) is its reverse boundary dimer, and a,t are same-polarity witnesses because (a,b,c) is tight; this is L1. If (t,z,a) is tight, similarly K=(c,a,z), S=(z,a), and b,t are witnesses because (b,z,a) is tight; this is L2. If neither first literal is tight, clauses (1),(2) force both (h,t,b) and (h,t,z). Since K=(v,t,h) is a tight Q trimer and S=(h,t) is its reverse boundary dimer, b,z are same-polarity witnesses, giving L3. Thus one left packet always exists.

For the right side put r=q_{m-3}, ell=q_{m-4}. R621 gives H-c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,b,z,R,s) and H-a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,z,b,R,s), with Q[0,m-2]=(L,u,...,ell,r,d). Rotate the final vertex d of the long rail to its front and concatenate:
P_c=(d,L,u,...,ell,r,a,b,z,R,s),
P_a=(d,L,u,...,ell,r,c,z,b,R,s).
The only uncertified turns are respectively (d,L,u),(ell,r,a),(r,a,b) and (d,L,u),(ell,r,c),(r,c,z). Non-Hamiltonicity of H-c and H-a plus R3 therefore gives
(3) (u,L,d) OR (a,r,ell) OR (b,a,r),
(4) (u,L,d) OR (c,r,ell) OR (z,c,r).
If (u,L,d) is tight, K=(L,u,v) is tight, S=(u,L) is its reverse boundary dimer, and d,a are witnesses because R621 contains (u,L,a); this is R0. Otherwise, if (b,a,r) is tight then K=(a,b,z) is tight, S=(b,a), and c,r are witnesses because (b,a,c) is tight, giving R2. If (z,c,r) is tight then K=(c,z,b), S=(z,c), and a,r are witnesses because (z,c,a) is tight, giving R3. If neither of those literals is tight, (3),(4) force both (a,r,ell) and (c,r,ell). Since K=(ell,r,d) is a tight Q trimer and S=(r,ell) is its reverse boundary dimer, a,c are witnesses, giving R1. Thus one right packet always exists. The two arguments are simultaneous in the same crossed frame.
