# R844 — Exact-core rails force a full block-insertion obstruction array along a Hamilton complement

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Suppose V(H)=V(G) disjoint-union V(M), where M=(m_0,...,m_t) is a nonempty tight path and G has a literal exact two-cover A sqcup B by nonempty tight paths. Fix A=(a_0,...,a_r). For every insertion slot of A as one contiguous block into the displayed order of M, the complete seam window contains a bad turn. INTERNAL GAP: for 0<=i<t, if r=0, at least one of the present turns (m_{i-1},m_i,a_0) [i>=1], (m_i,a_0,m_{i+1}), (a_0,m_{i+1},m_{i+2}) [i+2<=t] is bad; if r>=1, at least one of the present turns (m_{i-1},m_i,a_0) [i>=1], (m_i,a_0,a_1), (a_{r-1},a_r,m_{i+1}), (a_r,m_{i+1},m_{i+2}) [i+2<=t] is bad. BEFORE M: if r=t=0 then H is already two-coverable; otherwise at least one of the present turns (a_{r-1},a_r,m_0) [r>=1] and (a_r,m_0,m_1) [t>=1] is bad. AFTER M: if r=t=0 then H is already two-coverable; otherwise at least one of the present turns (m_{t-1},m_t,a_0) [t>=1] and (m_t,a_0,a_1) [r>=1] is bad. In every slot, boundary antisymmetry makes the complete reversal of at least one bad displayed turn tight. The same conclusions hold with B in place of A. Thus one exact core cover supplies a synchronized, complete physical seam-window obstruction at every internal and exterior insertion position of the Hamilton complement.

## Hypotheses
Finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament H with pc(H)>2; a vertex partition into a nonempty tight path M and an induced remainder G carrying a literal exact two-cover A sqcup B by nonempty tight paths. Uses boundary antisymmetry R3 only.

## Scope
Order-free corrected parent theorem for block insertion. Repairs accepted R811 by replacing its incorrect exterior one-turn endpoint assertion with the complete two-turn boundary windows. No smallest-counterexample minimality or frame-specific machinery.

## Applicability
Use as the canonical block-insertion compiler once freshly accepted. Internal-gap applications recover the sound body of R811. Exterior applications keep both junction turns when both concatenated paths are nontrivial and directly specialize to finite core-middle concatenation clauses such as R839.

## Persistence
Retain the chosen exact core cover, the Hamilton complement rail, the insertion slot, the complete list of present seam-window turns, and whichever reversed bad turn is certified. Never drop a present junction turn merely because the insertion occurs at an exterior slot.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert which seam-window turn is bad. Does not permit shortening a two-turn exterior window to a single blocker when both concatenated paths are nontrivial. Does not by itself close H.

## Proof
Fix A=(a_0,...,a_r), the untouched rail B, and M=(m_0,...,m_t). At an internal gap insert A between m_i and m_{i+1}. Every consecutive triple wholly inside A or M is already tight. The only new consecutive triples are exactly the turns listed in the statement: three when r=0, subject to boundary omissions, and four when r>=1, again subject to boundary omissions. If all present new turns were tight, the inserted sequence would be a tight Hamilton path on V(A) union V(M); together with B this would be a spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. Hence some present turn is bad, and R3 makes its complete reversal tight. Before M, the candidate sequence is (a_0,...,a_r,m_0,...,m_t). Its only new consecutive triples are (a_{r-1},a_r,m_0) when r>=1 and (a_r,m_0,m_1) when t>=1. If both are absent then r=t=0 and A union M is an automatic tight dimer, so with B it two-covers H. Otherwise if all present boundary turns were tight the same concatenation plus B would two-cover H, so at least one present turn is bad and R3 reverses it. After M the candidate is (m_0,...,m_t,a_0,...,a_r), whose only new triples are (m_{t-1},m_t,a_0) when t>=1 and (m_t,a_0,a_1) when r>=1; the identical argument applies, with r=t=0 again already impossible. Repeating with B proves the complete array.
