# R322 — Dual-clique density principle for locally sparse uniform families

## Statement
Let r>=k+1, let B be a family of k-subsets of an r-element ground set, write b=|B|, M=binom(r,k), and p=b/M. Assume every (k+1)-subset contains at most t members of B. Then (r-k+1)p^2-p <= t(t-1)(r-k)/(k(k+1)), hence p <= [1+sqrt(1+4t(t-1)(r-k)(r-k+1)/(k(k+1)))]/[2(r-k+1)]. For t>=2, equality can occur only if every (k+1)-set contains exactly t members of B and every (k-1)-set lies in the same number kb/binom(r,k-1) of members of B. The case k=5,t=2 is exactly the structural inequality underlying R320.

## Hypotheses
Integers r>=k+1 and 0<=t<=k+1; B is a family of k-subsets of an r-element ground set; every (k+1)-set contains at most t members of B. Equality regularity clause is for t>=2.

## Scope
Pure order-free extremal set-system theorem. Generalizes the dual Johnson-clique mechanism behind R320 from 5-uniform families to arbitrary uniformity k.

## Applicability
Use whenever a support problem supplies a local cap on how many k-supports from a distinguished family may lie inside one (k+1)-set. Converts the local cap to a global density ceiling and exposes design-like equality structure.

## Persistence
The family B and its union- and intersection-indexed occupancy profiles are intrinsic finite-set data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert equality designs exist. Does not imply path growth or Strong-specific closure. No quantitative near-equality stability is claimed.

## Proof
For each (k+1)-set U let y_U=|B intersect binom(U,k)|. Every edge of J(r,k) has a unique union U, so e(B)=sum_U binom(y_U,2)<=binom(r,k+1)binom(t,2). For each (k-1)-set S let x_S=|{F in B:S subset F}|. Every Johnson edge has a unique intersection S, so e(B)=sum_S binom(x_S,2)=(sum_S x_S^2-kb)/2 because sum_S x_S=kb. Cauchy gives sum_S x_S^2>=k^2 b^2/binom(r,k-1), hence e(B)>=[k^2 b^2/binom(r,k-1)-kb]/2. Compare with the upper bound and use binom(r,k-1)=binom(r,k)k/(r-k+1), binom(r,k+1)=binom(r,k)(r-k)/(k+1), b=p binom(r,k), obtaining (r-k+1)p^2-p<=t(t-1)(r-k)/(k(k+1)). The positive quadratic root gives the stated ceiling. If t>=2 and equality holds, every y_U must equal t and Cauchy must be equality, so all x_S are equal.
