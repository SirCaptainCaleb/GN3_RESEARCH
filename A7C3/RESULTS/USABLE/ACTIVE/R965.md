# R965 — Singleton-cover compatibility has a canonical rail-incidence root which is a forest or one spanning odd cycle

## Statement
Retain every conclusion of accepted R926. Let Q be a root vertex whose port universe Omega_Q is SATURATED in the sense that the physical edge labels incident with Q are exactly the vertices of Omega_Q, and suppose |Omega_Q|=deg_R(Q)>=3. Then the connected component of the R926 root containing Q is a depth-two star-of-stars.

More precisely, R is in its forest alternative. For every incident edge x=Q R_x,
  Omega_{R_x}=(V(H)-Omega_Q) union {x}.
Every physical edge p outside Omega_Q that lies in the Q-component has root-edge depth exactly two from Q, so it has the unique form p=R_x S_p for one x in Omega_Q. The far endpoint S_p is a leaf and
  Omega_{S_p}=(Omega_Q-{x}) union {p}.
There are no root edges at depth three or greater in this component.

Consequently, whenever a critical block Omega is realized by the compatible singleton fibers P | (Omega-x) for all x in Omega and these spokes saturate one R926 port Q, every outside physical label that lies in that root component is a literal one-for-one exchanged leaf Omega-x+p. The theorem is support/universe geometry only; it does not assert that all outside labels lie in this component or synchronize Hamilton orders.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R926 setup. A root vertex Q has saturated universe Omega_Q: its incident physical edge labels equal Omega_Q. Degree at least three.

## Scope
Pure strengthening of accepted R926. No boundary antisymmetry or Hamilton-order synchronization is used beyond the R926 rail-incidence root and its one-defect universe colorings.

## Applicability
Use around any saturated critical port universe. It generalizes the root-depth part of R964 and identifies every same-component outside label with one exact one-for-one universe exchange. In the balanced uniform middle layer a deliberate choice of singleton covers can place every complementary label into this depth-two component, recovering the spanning double-star support shell.

## Persistence
Retain Q, Omega_Q, each spoke x=Q R_x, every depth-two physical edge p=R_x S_p, and the exact port universes Omega_{R_x} and Omega_{S_p}. No path-order relation is inherited.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say every physical label outside Omega_Q belongs to the Q-component. Does not prove a critical block is absorbable. Does not assert Hamiltonicity of the exchanged universe Omega_Q-x+p unless supplied separately. Does not synchronize any Hamilton orders.

## Proof
Retain the accepted R926 root R and port universes. Let Q have saturated universe Omega=Omega_Q, so the physical edges incident with Q are exactly the labels in Omega, and deg(Q)=|Omega|>=3.

Because accepted R926 says the root is either a forest or one spanning odd cycle, and every vertex of the spanning-cycle alternative has degree two, Q of degree at least three forces the FOREST alternative.

Fix one incident physical edge x=Q R_x. R926 gives
  Omega_Q union Omega_{R_x}=V(H),
  Omega_Q intersect Omega_{R_x}={x}.
Since Omega_Q=Omega, this determines
  Omega_{R_x}=(V(H)-Omega) union {x}.                       (1)

Now let p be a physical label outside Omega whose root edge belongs to the connected component of Q. Use the accepted R926 one-defect coloring
  epsilon_p(S)=1 iff p in Omega_S.
Since p notin Omega_Q, epsilon_p(Q)=0. For every spoke x=Q R_x, equation (1) gives p in Omega_{R_x}, so epsilon_p(R_x)=1. More generally, every root edge other than the physical edge p is bichromatic under epsilon_p, while the two endpoints of edge p both have color 1.

Define the depth of a root edge e in the Q-component to be one plus the distance from Q to its nearer endpoint. Let the physical edge p have depth d. Along the unique forest path from Q to the nearer endpoint of p there are d-1 root edges, none equal to p, so the one-defect coloring alternates across all of them. Starting from epsilon_p(Q)=0, the nearer endpoint therefore has color congruent to d-1 mod 2. But it is an endpoint of the p-edge, so its color is 1. Hence d is even.                              (2)

By saturation, every depth-one edge is labelled by a vertex of Omega, and conversely no physical label outside Omega can occur at depth one. Thus every root edge at depth at least two has a physical label outside Omega and itself satisfies the even-depth conclusion (2). If some edge had depth at least three, take the first edge at depth three along its Q-path. That edge is outside Omega but has odd depth, contradicting (2). Hence the component has no edge of depth three or greater. Every outside physical edge in the component has depth exactly two.

So each such p has a unique form
  p=R_x S_p
for one spoke x=Q R_x. Since no depth-three edge exists, S_p is a leaf. Apply R926's union/intersection identities to the p-edge. From (1), Omega_{R_x}=(V-Omega) union {x}. Therefore V-Omega_{R_x}=Omega-{x}. The opposite universe Omega_{S_p} must contain every vertex outside Omega_{R_x}, must contain p, and can contain no other vertex of Omega_{R_x} because the intersection across edge p is exactly {p}. Consequently
  Omega_{S_p}=(Omega-{x}) union {p}.                        (3)

Equations (1)-(3) prove the claimed depth-two star-of-stars normal form and exact one-for-one exchange universes. No Hamilton-order synchronization or boundary antisymmetry beyond the already accepted R926 structure was used.
