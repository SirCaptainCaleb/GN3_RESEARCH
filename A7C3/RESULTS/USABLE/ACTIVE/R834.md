# R834 — A signed dimer in a three-cover forces reverse seams at polarity-misaligned rail endpoints

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Suppose H=D sqcup U sqcup V is a literal spanning three-cover by nontrivial tight paths, with D=(p,q) a dimer. (TAIL-START.) If the first vertex y=u_0 of U is a tail witness on the tested orientation D, so (p,q,y) is tight, then the reverse outer seam (u_1,y,q) is tight. (HEAD-END.) If the last vertex x=u_r of U is a head witness on D, so (x,p,q) is tight, then the reverse outer seam (p,x,u_{r-1}) is tight. The same conclusions hold with U and V interchanged. Consequently, if A is any set of tail witnesses on D and B any set of head witnesses on D, then in a source three-cover with no such forced terminal reverse seam, every rail endpoint lying in A can occur only as a rail end, and every rail endpoint lying in B can occur only as a rail start.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); pc(H)>2; H=D sqcup U sqcup V is a literal spanning three-cover by nontrivial tight paths; D=(p,q) has order two. Tail/head witness assertions use the exact displayed tested orientation.

## Scope
Order-free physical-splice lemma. It uses the complete R453 two-hole certificate and boundary antisymmetry, so it is safe under the R812/R816 seam correction. No smallest-counterexample minimality, capture, payment, Reverse Ear, or path-length growth.

## Applicability
Use whenever a signed dimer itself is one component of a literal spanning three-cover and a sign witness appears at an endpoint of one of the other rails. In the central R822 collision fan, D is head-signed by B={b,z} and tail-signed by A={a,c}, while any exact cover T of H-D gives H=D sqcup T_1 sqcup T_2. Therefore every A-start or B-end in T immediately creates a named reverse outer seam; a seam-quiet source cover must orient X-endpoints B-at-start and A-at-end.

## Persistence
Retain the literal three-cover D,U,V, the tested orientation (p,q), the named endpoint witness, its adjacent rail vertex, the exact R453 ordered-pair proposal and hole pair, and the forced reverse outer seam. The reverse seam is graph-intrinsic; endpoint roles are source-cover-relative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that every rail has an endpoint in A union B, that the forced reverse seam closes H, or that a seam-quiet cover exists. Does not permit insertion of D using only one checked seam. Does not infer capture, payment, or a long path.

## Proof
TAIL-START. Write U=(y,u_1,...,u_r). Apply R879 to the literal nontrivial spanning three-cover H=D sqcup U sqcup V and the ordered component pair D=(p,q),U. Its exact physical splice clause is (y,q,p) OR (u_1,y,q), the reversals of the two complete seam holes (p,q,y) and (q,y,u_1). By hypothesis (p,q,y) is tight, so boundary antisymmetry R3 makes its complete reversal (y,q,p) bad. Therefore the R879 clause forces (u_1,y,q) tight. HEAD-END. Write U=(u_0,...,u_{r-1},x). Apply R879 to the ordered pair U,D. Its splice clause is (p,x,u_{r-1}) OR (q,p,x), reversing the complete seam holes (u_{r-1},x,p) and (x,p,q). The head-witness hypothesis makes (x,p,q) tight, so R3 makes (q,p,x) bad; hence (p,x,u_{r-1}) is tight. Interchanging U and V is identical. The seam-quiet endpoint-orientation consequence is the direct contrapositive of these two statements. This proves exactly R834 without the optional smallest-counterexample specialization bundled into retired R453.
