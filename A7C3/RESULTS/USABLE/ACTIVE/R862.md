# R862 — Simultaneous inward-anchor deletion forces an R24-safe five-cell triple-transition fan

## Statement
Retain the crossed beyond-order-ten R621 frame, put D={q_2,q_{m-2}}, m>=6, and W=H-D. Then W has the literal five-component reference cover G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], G_2=Q[m-1,m], G_3=(a,c), G_4=(b,z). All five components are nonempty pairwise vertex-disjoint tight paths. For every exact two-cover T of W, the number t_G(T) of selected T-states joining distinct G_i is t_G(T)=3+sigma(T)>=3, with equality exactly when every G_i occurs in one contiguous T-block. Thus one common exact pair-deletion recompletion contains at least three simultaneous source-labelled cross states. Every such cross state is individually R176-admissible, and because q_2,q_{m-2} are precisely the two vertices outside W, either one may be chosen as the spare singleton coordinate in the explicit R176 construction. The conclusion is exact transition geometry tied to one common deleted inward-anchor pair; no signed-floor or payment-lineage hypothesis is used or asserted.

Retain D={q_2,q_{m-2}}, the five source cells, the exact R429 cover T, every selected intercell state, t_G and sigma, and any separately chosen R176 spare-anchor certificate. No signed-floor ancestry is part of this theorem.

## Exclusions / nonclaims

## Proof
Put D={q_2,q_{m-2}} and W=H-D. No signed-floor input is needed for the cover count below. Since m>=6, the three Q pieces G_0=Q[0,1], G_1=Q[3,m-3], and G_2=Q[m-1,m] are all nonempty contiguous subpaths of the tight rail Q. The two remaining sets G_3={a,c} and G_4={b,z} are two-vertex paths and hence automatically tight. The five vertex sets are pairwise disjoint and their union is exactly W, so R_G=G_0 sqcup G_1 sqcup G_2 sqcup G_3 sqcup G_4 is a literal five-path cover of W. Each induced class H[G_i] has path-cover number one, witnessed by its displayed path. Accepted Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429 gives an exact two-cover T=T_1 sqcup T_2 of W. Apply the accepted Intrinsic Partition-Cover Deficit Identity R7 to the fixed partition Pi={V(G_0),...,V(G_4)} and T. Since the sum of the five intrinsic class path-cover numbers is 5 and T has 2 rails, R7 gives t_Pi(T)=5-2+sigma(T)=3+sigma(T)>=3, where sigma(T)>=0. The equality characterization in R7 says sigma(T)=0 exactly when every class occurs in one contiguous T-block. Every counted transition is a selected adjacent state xy of T whose endpoints lie in two distinct components of the literal reference cover R_G. Since W is proper, accepted R176 applies to each such selected state. In the explicit R176 proof the spare vertex d may be chosen arbitrarily in V(H)-W=D; hence for each selected cross state one may choose d=q_2 or d=q_{m-2}, retaining that inward anchor as the spare singleton coordinate of the resulting balanced-pair certificate. All counted transitions belong to the same exact T and therefore coexist as selected source-labelled states before any separate R176 continuation is chosen. This proves the lemma.
