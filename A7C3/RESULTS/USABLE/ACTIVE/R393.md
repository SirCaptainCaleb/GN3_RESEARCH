# R393 — Universal O4 interaction-or-endpoint-universality reduction

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten. Then, unless H has a spanning two-cover, exactly the following global residual dichotomy is sufficient for the generic program. Either (A) there exists a proper tight trimer J=(a,x,c) whose all-internal return yields the source-trimer-localized R389/R160 historical signed-support/anchor or cross-generation pair interaction at middle x; or (B) for every physical pair {a,c} and every x outside that pair, some exact two-cover of H-{a,c} exposes x as a rail endpoint. In branch (B), orienting the unique x-middle tight trimer on {a,x,c} and applying R358 supplies a literal R173-admissible mate clause for every such triple. Thus, after the universal trimer return is consumed, the generic O4 problem reduces globally to either one localized source-trimer historical interaction or the endpoint-universal pair-deletion regime.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; provisional R390/R392.

## Scope
Top-level structural strengthening of R391. It replaces the local mate-clause alternative by the stronger global exact-cover property that necessarily holds if no source-trimer historical interaction exists anywhere.

## Applicability
Use as the preferred generic frontier if R389-R392 audit successfully. The two remaining targets are qualitatively different: consume one source-trimer-localized historical interaction, or contradict/exploit universal endpoint exposure in every pair-deletion subsystem.

## Persistence
Branch (A) retains its source trimer, middle, residual terminal roles, and historical interaction ancestry. Branch (B) is existential separately for each pair and exposed vertex; exact covers need not coincide. Its mate clauses remain individually certified by literal R358 proposals.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert endpoint universality closes the conjecture, that one exact cover exposes all vertices, or that the mate clauses are linked into a physical mate cycle. Does not assert the historical interaction is already closure.

## Proof

Relabel the outer vertices if needed and write the resulting proper tight trimer as J=(a,x,c). Apply the accepted R24-free universal return theorem R920 to J.\n\nIf every physical pair {a,c} and every x outside it lies in R920 endpoint-return regime, then for every such pair/x there exists an exact two-cover of H-{a,c} exposing x at a rail endpoint. This is exactly R393 branch (B). The endpoint-return compiler built into R920/P992 also supplies the literal one-hole or two-hole R173-admissible mate certificate for each oriented x-middle tight trimer, so the branch-(B) mate assertion follows without R358.\n\nOtherwise fix one tight trimer J=(a,x,c) in R920 all-internal regime. The proof P992 chooses an exact source two-cover U sqcup V of H-J and proves directly that both U and V have order at least three, with no use of R168. Write U=(u0,u1,...,ur) and V=(v0,v1,...,vs), r,s>=2. Since every H-{a,c} return keeps x internal, attaching x directly to the left end of either source rail would create a forbidden endpoint return. Both are nontrivial and are physically disjoint from J.\n\nThe fixed source trimer J=(a,x,c) has head-signed terminal dimer M=(x,c), witnessed by a, with signed anchor x. Consequently D_U and M are disjoint signed supports of opposite polarity, hence a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair birth Pi_U; likewise D_V and M give a second birth Pi_V. Retain Pi_U first and Pi_V later before unrelated reset. Each birth has two nontrivial dimer supports, and each uses the same terminal dimer M of the same fixed trimer J with a partner support disjoint from J.\n\nThese are precisely the hypotheses of R160. For proof-awareness, use its accepted fully reconstructible alternate route P562: P562 applies the corrected cross-generation theorem R441 to the earlier and later nontrivial pair births.
