# R2018 — Ordinary J-neutral prefixes give a physical pre-pivot baseline

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789414942.180039`

## Statement

The stale componentwise neutral-weight step in D17.416 can be bypassed completely before the first positive pivot. In the sharp cell of R1039 the total neutral weight is exactly zero, so the ordinary zipper prefixes `M_q` with every neutral component in its `J`-state have the same transition count as the F-normalized prefixes.

Since `M_0=J` is physical, either every `M_q` through `q=r-1` is a tight three-forest, or at the first physical failure `h<r` the preceding `M_{h-1}` is a tight three-forest with `tau<=1` and every blocker of `M_h` uses the single newly installed edge `e_h`.

Thus, before `r`, a physical low-transition prefix always exists with no neutral-component obstruction debt. Unless its two free physical labels coincide, its canonical zipper closure has exact two-cover cardinality and `tau<=2`, and all remaining physical failure is carried by that closure edge.

## Proof

Use the zipper notation of D17.421. The ordinary prefix matching `M_q` selects `e_1,...,e_q` on the prefix of `C_*`, the J-edges on its remaining suffix, and the J-state on every neutral symmetric-difference component. The F-normalized prefix `hat M_q` is obtained by toggling every neutral component from J to F.

R1039 repairs the stale componentwise claim from D17.416 and proves the exact aggregate identity

`W_0 = sum_D w(D) = 0`.

D17.421 records

`tau(hat M_q)=tau(M_q)+W_0`.

Therefore `tau(hat M_q)=tau(M_q)` for every q. By the first-positive definition used there, `tau(M_q)<=1` for every `q<r`.

Now `M_0=J`, hence is a literal tight acyclic three-forest. If every `M_q` for `0<=q<r` is physical, choose the last available pre-pivot prefix. Otherwise let `h<r` be the least index for which `M_h` is not physical. Then `M_{h-1}` is physical and has `tau(M_{h-1})<=1`.

Consecutive ordinary prefixes differ only by

`M_h = M_{h-1} - {f_h} + {e_h}`.

Any bad selected turn, two-edge backtrack, or directed cycle of `M_h` that avoided `e_h` would use only edges already selected in `M_{h-1}`. Deleting `f_h` cannot create any of those configurations. Hence every blocker at the first physical failure contains the newly installed edge `e_h`. In particular there is no hidden neutral-component obstruction at this frontier.

For either chosen physical prefix `M_q` with `q<r`, its unmatched split copies are the standard zipper defects `a_q,out` and `b_m,in`. If `a_q!=b_m`, adding the canonical closure `g_q=a_q->b_m` raises matching cardinality by one to exact two-cover cardinality and contributes at most one transition. Thus

`tau(M_q+g_q)<=2<tau(F)=3`.

Because `M_q` itself is already tight and acyclic, any bad turn, backtrack, or directed cycle in the closure must contain the sole new edge `g_q`. Hence all remaining physical debt is closure-edge debt.

If `a_q=b_m`, this is the unique zipper loop coincidence already isolated in D17.421 and subsequent results.
