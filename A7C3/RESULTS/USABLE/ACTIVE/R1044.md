# R1044 — Adjacent-slot two-vertex insertion and the diameter-two polarity wall

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** G39 workspace adjacent-gap root; durable development `A7C3/WORKSPACE/two-vertex-insertion-localization.md`

## Statement

Let `B=(b_1,...,b_m)` be tight. Suppose two outside vertices `x,y` are each individually insertible into both adjacent gaps `i` and `i+1` of the displayed B-order. Then `H[B∪{x,y}]` has a tight Hamilton path.

Consequently, under the nonHamiltonian G39 two-vertex-extension hypotheses:

1. If `I_B(v)` has span `1`, every companion insertion set `I_B(s)` has size at most one.
2. If `I_B(v)` has span `2`, say its outer gaps are `a` and `a+2`, then every nonempty companion insertion set is exactly `{a+1}`. For every such companion `s`, the two turns

`(s,b_{a+1},v)` and `(v,b_{a+2},s)`

are tight.

## Proof

Assume `i,i+1∈I_B(x)∩I_B(y)`. Consider the two B-ordered Hamilton candidates obtained by placing `x` in gap `i` and `y` in gap `i+1`, or vice versa. In the first candidate, every consecutive triple is already certified by the two one-vertex insertions except possibly

`(x,b_{i+1},y)`.

In the second, every consecutive triple is certified except possibly

`(y,b_{i+1},x)`.

These two turns are complete reversals. By R3 exactly one of them is tight. Therefore at least one candidate is a tight Hamilton path on `B∪{x,y}`. The same argument includes endpoint gaps.

For span `1`, write the two legal outer gaps of `I_B(v)` as `a,a+1`. R1043 gives `I_B(s)⊆{a,a+1}` for every nonHamiltonian companion. If both gaps belonged to `I_B(s)`, then `v` and `s` would each be insertible into both adjacent gaps, contradicting the first part. Thus `|I_B(s)|≤1`.

For span `2`, write `min I_B(v)=a`, `max I_B(v)=a+2`. R1043 gives `I_B(s)⊆{a+1}`. If nonempty, then `a+1∈I_B(s)`. Insert `v` at gap `a` and `s` at gap `a+1`. Every turn is certified except possibly `(v,b_{a+1},s)`. Since the full subsystem is nonHamiltonian, this candidate fails, so R3 forces its complete reversal `(s,b_{a+1},v)` tight.

Likewise, inserting `s` at gap `a+1` and `v` at gap `a+2` leaves only `(s,b_{a+2},v)` uncertified. Its failure forces the reverse turn `(v,b_{a+2},s)` tight.

## Scope

This is a necessary displayed-order obstruction under two-vertex nonHamiltonicity. It does not itself supply a consumer for the common polarity wall and does not claim interval structure for insertion sets beyond the stated span consequences.
