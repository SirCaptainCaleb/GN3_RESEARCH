# R872 — Opposite-polarity dimer certificates force a two-endpoint recompletion crossing

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let D=(a,b) be a tested oriented physical dimer, and let x,y be distinct vertices outside {a,b} such that (x,a,b) and (a,b,y) are tight. Accepted R523(HT) supplies the vertex-simple tight P4 K=(x,a,b,y). Put Delta={a,b}, S={x,y}, and E=V(H)\(Delta union S), and assume E is nonempty. Let T be any supplied literal exact two-cover of H-Delta. Then accepted R508, applied to deletion Delta, absorbable block S, recompletion path K, and T, forces T to select a directed state with exactly one endpoint in S and the other in E. Equivalently, the two opposite-polarity witnesses on D cannot remain T-isolated from the exterior of K. The same conclusion holds with x,y exchanged under R523(TH), via the P4 (y,a,b,x).

## Hypotheses
Finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament H with pc(H)>2; D=(a,b) tested oriented dimer; x,y are distinct opposite-polarity witnesses on that same tested orientation; E=V(H)\{a,b,x,y} nonempty; T is a supplied exact two-cover of H-{a,b}. Uses accepted R523 and R508 only.

## Scope
Order-free parent compilation of R523(HT/TH) with R508. No smallest-counterexample minimality, R5, crossed frame, first-inward geometry, capture, protected contact, R312, R527, R429, or rail-nontriviality hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use whenever two opposite-polarity certificates share one tested oriented dimer and an exact two-cover of the deleted dimer is independently available. Prefer this parent over manually forming the P4 and then quoting R508. Capture/payment/first-contact are later consumers.

## Persistence
Retain the tested oriented dimer D, witness identities and polarities, R523 P4 K, deletion Delta, block S, supplied cover T, and at least one selected S|E crossing of T. The P4/signs are graph-intrinsic; the crossing is T-relative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not produce the deletion two-cover, does not invoke R5, does not claim both T rails nontrivial, does not determine which witness is the S-endpoint, and does not capture/pay/contact or produce closure. Opposite tested orientations remain distinct data.

## Proof
Write D=(a,b), with (x,a,b) and (a,b,y) tight. By accepted R523(HT), K=(x,a,b,y) is a vertex-simple tight P4. Let Delta={a,b}, S={x,y}, and W=V(H)\Delta. Then V(K)=Delta union S and E=W\S is nonempty by hypothesis. Because pc(H)>2 and T is a supplied exact two-cover of H-Delta, accepted R508 applies to (Delta,S,K,T) and forces a selected T-state with exactly one endpoint in S and the other in W\S=E. The TH orientation is identical after exchanging x and y and using the R523 P4 (y,a,b,x). No existence theorem for T is used.
