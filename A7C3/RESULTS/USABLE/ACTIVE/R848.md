# R848 — The universal first-inward rectangle cuts to a four-witness outer-inward singleton pair

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame. Put u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}. Then the frame contains a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign singleton pair on exactly {u,s}={q_1,q_{m-1}}, and each singleton has all four X vertices available as inherited witnesses of its polarity. For m>=5, for each x in X the R723 dimer (v,u) is tail-signed by x and (s,d) is head-signed by x. Deleting the contiguous interval Q[2,m-2] and applying R425 separately leaves tail-signed singleton (u) and head-signed singleton (s), both still witnessed by x. Since the four source sign certificates coexist, the same physical singleton pair carries all four witness certificates. For m=4, the two source dimers meet exactly at q_2 and R547 hinge reduction leaves the same opposite-sign singleton supports {q_1,q_3}, again with the four witness-indexed hinge certificates. This theorem asserts the signed singleton-pair geometry only. It does not assert that the pair is a payment-lineage floor.

## Hypotheses
H is in the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame with m>=4. Use accepted R425 and R547 only for signed-support rebirth/hinge geometry.

## Scope
Corrected structural form of the former outer-inward floor theorem. No payment-lineage membership, pair descent, capture, actualization, or floor steering is assumed or concluded.

## Applicability
Use as a static signed-support and witness-ledger fact at the first inward Q coordinates. It can support later arguments that need the physical singleton signs or the deletion pair {q_1,q_{m-1}}, but a separate certified pair-lineage theorem is required before invoking any floor-only steering or payment consumer.

## Persistence
Retain the physical singleton supports q_1 and q_{m-1}, their opposite polarities, all four X witness certificates, and the exact R425 cut or R547 hinge certificate used to obtain them.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim an ancestry-bearing floor, signed-payment lineage membership, support descent, cut progress, or permission to invoke R432. Does not claim the four witness-indexed constructions are four different lineages; they are coexisting graph-intrinsic certificates on the same physical singleton pair.

## Proof
Fix x in X. For m>=5, accepted R723 gives (v,u,x) and (x,s,d) tight. Thus D_L=(v,u) is tail-signed by x and D_R=(s,d) is head-signed by x. Delete C=Q[2,m-2]. In D_L the deleted vertex is v and the surviving terminal interval is singleton u, so the tail form of R425 keeps x as a tail witness on u. In D_R the deleted vertex is d and the surviving initial interval is singleton s, so the head form of R425 keeps x as a head witness on s. The supports {u} and {s} are disjoint and have opposite polarity. Because the R723 sign turns hold for every x in X in the same frame, all four witness certificates are graph-intrinsically available on this same physical pair. If m=4, D_L=(q_2,q_1) and D_R=(q_3,q_2) meet exactly at hinge q_2. R547 applied to these opposite-polarity dimers leaves singleton q_1 on the tail side and q_3 on the head side; applying the same hinge calculation to each of the four witness-indexed sign certificates gives the four-witness ledger. No step places these supports into a signed-payment lineage, so the conclusion is exactly the graph-intrinsic balanced singleton pair and no stronger.
