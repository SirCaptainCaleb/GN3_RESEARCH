# R898 — The MAX5 alternating face is a labelled P4 or four simultaneous neighbor-witness middle-gate packets

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R885 BOUNDED branch with f(T)=2 and the accepted R894 MAX5 regime. Thus p=0,q=8, every x in X has exactly two selected T-neighbors, both in Y, and there is no selected X-X edge. For x in X write N_T(x) for its two Y-neighbors. Then at least one of the following holds. (P4-END) H contains a source-labelled tight Hamilton P4 using one physical endpoint L and one selected X-Y incidence of T. (FOUR-PACKETS) All eight selected X-Y incidences have forced middle-gate polarity simultaneously: for every y in N_T(a), (y,a,c) is tight; for every y in N_T(c), (y,c,a) is tight; for every y in N_T(b), (z,b,y) is tight; and for every y in N_T(z), (b,z,y) is tight. Consequently the four tested oriented middle-gate dimers (a,c),(c,a),(z,b),(b,z) are respectively two-head, two-head, two-tail, two-tail witness packets whose witness pairs are exactly N_T(a),N_T(c),N_T(b),N_T(z). Using the fixed all-X carriers already present in accepted R649, all four are simultaneously R542-ready: (a,c) with carrier (z,c,a), (c,a) with carrier (b,a,c), (z,b) with carrier (b,z,a), and (b,z) with carrier (z,b,c). Thus the MAX5 branch no longer needs to split by N0/N1/N2 before its next consumer: outside a labelled endpoint P4 it canonically yields four physical two-witness packets tied to the actual T-neighbor pairs.

## Proof
Work in accepted R894 MAX5. Since p=0 and q=8, every x in X has T-degree two and both of its selected neighbors lie in Y. Because T is a path forest, the two neighbors of each x are distinct. Write N_T(x) for this two-element neighbor set.\n\nWe use the fixed crossed endpoint signs from accepted R649. Its preferred proof records: (a,c) is head-signed by L, so (L,a,c) is tight; (c,a) is head-signed by L, so (L,c,a) is tight; (b,z) is tail-signed by L, so (b,z,L) is tight; and (z,b) is tail-signed by L, so (z,b,L) is tight.\n\nFix y in N_T(a). Boundary antisymmetry R3 at middle a says exactly one of (y,a,c) and (c,a,y) is tight. If (c,a,y) is tight, then tested dimer (c,a) is tail-signed by y while R649 head-signs the same tested orientation by L through (L,c,a). Accepted R523, or direct concatenation, gives the literal tight P4 (L,c,a,y). Hence outside P4-END we must have (y,a,c) tight for every y in N_T(a). Thus (a,c) is head-signed by both members of N_T(a).\n\nFix y in N_T(c). R3 says exactly one of (y,c,a) and (a,c,y) is tight. If (a,c,y) is tight, then (a,c) is tail-signed by y while R649 head-signs (a,c) by L through (L,a,c), giving the literal P4 (L,a,c,y). Hence outside P4-END, (y,c,a) is tight for every y in N_T(c), so (c,a) is head-signed by both members of N_T(c).\n\nFix y in N_T(b). R3 says exactly one of (y,b,z) and (z,b,y) is tight. If (y,b,z) is tight, tested dimer (b,z) is head-signed by y while R649 tail-signs (b,z) by L through (b,z,L), giving the literal P4 (y,b,z,L). Hence outside P4-END, (z,b,y) is tight for every y in N_T(b), so (z,b) is tail-signed by both members of N_T(b).\n\nFinally fix y in N_T(z). R3 says exactly one of (y,z,b) and (b,z,y) is tight.
