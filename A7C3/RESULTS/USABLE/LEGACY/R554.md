# R554 — Transitive order-three rail forces a hinged opposite-polarity double collision

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the full accepted R543 transitive order-three rail frame, normalized by X={a,b,c,z} with M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az} and Q=(q_0,...,q_m). Among the six P-side seams from the three tight orders (a,b,c),(a,c,b),(b,a,c), at least one of bcq_0 and cbq_0 is bad, and at least one of q_mab and q_mba is bad. Hence at least two P-side seams are bad on the distinct physical rail edges {b,c} and {a,b}. By the R543 collision clause, a bad left seam gives a same-head two-witness collision on one tested orientation of {b,c}, with witnesses q_0,z, while a bad right seam gives a same-tail two-witness collision on one tested orientation of {a,b}, with witnesses q_m,z. Thus the frame carries opposite-polarity graph-intrinsic collision packets on dimers overlapping exactly in hinge b and sharing z as a collision witness. Applying R547 at b yields an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor whose physical singleton supports are c on the head side and a on the tail side, retaining both ancestor collision dimers, tested orientations, witnesses, and hinge-cut certificates.

## Proof
Because {b,c} is an M_L edge of the transitive matching-height cell X and X+q_0 is P5-free, R546 says {b,c} cannot be fully incoming to q_0. Full incoming would mean exactly that both bcq_0 and cbq_0 are tight. Therefore at least one of these two seams is bad. Likewise {a,b} is an M_R edge and X+q_m is P5-free. R546 says an M_R edge cannot be fully outgoing from q_m, i.e. q_mab and q_mba cannot both be tight. Hence at least one of those two right seams is bad. The two forced bad seams lie on the distinct physical edges {b,c} and {a,b}, proving the oracle claim without enumeration and without using the aligned all-seams-good branch.

For a bad left seam, the bad-seam clause already proved in R543 applies to the corresponding tight order of the physical rail: R3 supplies the exact reverse turn at q_0 and the failed z-extension supplies the same tested reverse dimer with witness z. Thus one tested orientation of {b,c} carries two head certificates, with witnesses q_0,z. Dually a bad right seam gives one tested orientation of {a,b} carrying two tail certificates, with witnesses q_m,z. These physical dimers overlap exactly in b and have opposite polarities. R547 therefore applies directly: delete the hinge b from the two historical support orders and use Signed-Interval Rebirth. The nonhinge vertex c survives as the head-signed singleton and a survives as the tail-signed singleton. All collision and hinge-cut certificates remain in the ancestry ledger. No simultaneous descendant-currentness is asserted.
