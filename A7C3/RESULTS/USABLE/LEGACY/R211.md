# R211 — Six-exterior carrier-by-exterior central-anchor interaction grid

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K be a tight path with six-vertex exterior E. Then there exists a subset U of E with |U| at least four such that for every internal vertex b of K and every q in U, H carries a graph-intrinsic synchronized central-anchor interaction with anchor pair {b,q} in the sense of R41. More concretely, for each q in U there is a good omission e and a tight P5 order Q_e on E-{e} in which q is an internal vertex; the consecutive trimer window of K centered at b and the consecutive trimer window of Q_e centered at q are disjoint tight trimers, hence R41 supplies two direct mass-four pair births with the same unordered middle-anchor pair {b,q}.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a tight path; E=V(H)\\V(K) has size six. The interaction conclusion is asserted for internal vertices of K.

## Scope
Arbitrary-order six-exterior interaction localization. No order-ten support geometry, maximin singleton-root hypothesis, pair currentization, or payment synchronization is used.

## Applicability
Use in the exterior-slack-one face to replace the anonymous R196 puncture-pair fan by a named bipartite interaction grid between every internal carrier vertex and at least four exterior anchors.

## Persistence
The tight trimer windows and R41 central-anchor interactions are graph-intrinsic/historical. The particular P5 order chosen for each good omission is a proof certificate; no simultaneous current three-cover or paid descendant is asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the interactions close, are simultaneously current, or share one exterior P5 root. Does not identify all six exterior vertices as anchors. Does not give an interaction-capacity contradiction or exterior-size descent.

## Proof
By R196 there are at least four distinct omitted vertices e in E for which E-{e} has a tight P5 Q_e. Fix one tight order for each such Q_e and let I_e be the set of its three internal vertices. Put U=union I_e. If |U| were at most three, then because each I_e has size three we would have one fixed 3-set U=I_e for every good omission e. But I_e is contained in E-{e}, so every good omitted vertex e would lie in E-U. Since |E-U|=3, this is impossible for at least four distinct good omissions. Hence |U|>=4. Now fix an internal vertex b of K and q in U. Choose a good omission e with q in I_e. The consecutive trimer window of K centered at b is tight, and the consecutive trimer window of Q_e centered at q is tight; their supports are disjoint because Q_e is contained in E. R41 applied to these two disjoint trimers supplies the synchronized two-anchor interaction with middle-anchor pair {b,q}. Since b and q were arbitrary, the full stated grid follows.
