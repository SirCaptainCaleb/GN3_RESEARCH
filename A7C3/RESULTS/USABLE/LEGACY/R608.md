# R608 — Minimum R24 cell bidirectionally saturates both terminal edges of the short trimer

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the exact-two-bad transitive R24 cell, let J=(a,b,c), L=q_0, R=q_m. Both tested orientations of physical edge {a,b} are tail-signed with two exterior witnesses, and both tested orientations of {b,c} are head-signed with two exterior witnesses: (a,b) is tail-signed by z and R via abz,abR; (b,a) is tail-signed by z and L via baz,baL; (b,c) is head-signed by z and L via zbc,Lbc; (c,b) is head-signed by z and R via zcb,Rcb. Thus each physical terminal edge is bidirectionally saturated in one polarity and the two orientations share the common witness z. Moreover all four oriented packets are directly R542-payable. For {a,b}, use the tight carriers (b,a,c) and (a,b,c), so the complementary carrier singleton is c; for {b,c}, use (a,c,b) and (a,b,c), so the complementary carrier singleton is a. In each case the displayed two witnesses lie outside the chosen trimer carrier.

## Proof
The minimum seam cbL is bad, hence Lbc is tight; the minimum seam Rba is bad, hence abR is tight. The matching-height cell gives abz,baz,zbc,zcb tight. R607 gives baL and Rcb. Therefore (a,b) has tail witnesses z,R; (b,a) has tail witnesses z,L; (b,c) has head witnesses z,L; and (c,b) has head witnesses z,R, always on the identical tested orientation displayed. For R542, (a,b) is a reverse boundary dimer of the tight carrier (b,a,c), and (b,a) is a reverse boundary dimer of (a,b,c); both carriers have complementary singleton c and the witnesses are outside the carrier. Dually (b,c) is a reverse boundary dimer of (a,c,b) and (c,b) of (a,b,c), both with complementary singleton a and both witnesses outside. Hence R542 applies independently to all four packets. The common witness z also shows that each physical terminal edge is saturated in both tested orientations with the same polarity.
