# R304 — Fresh-triple R38 manufacture is role-blind and does not synchronize historical ancestry

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Suppose x and y are two distinct physical vertices carrying any historical roles whatsoever, for example alternative floor coordinates, captured carrier indices, crossing endpoints, paid descendants, or unrelated labels. If an argument obtains a same-support R38 interaction by choosing fresh vertices u,v and applying only boundary antisymmetry to the triples {u,v,x} and {u,v,y}, then the resulting signed certificates use x and y only as physical witness identities. Their historical roles are not consumed or transported into the R38 certificate. The identical proof works for any two distinct vertices. Therefore such a construction is a role-blind coordinate-labeled interaction, not by itself a synchronization of the histories that happened to name x and y. In particular the fresh-triple mismatch steps in R300 and the first two mismatch steps of R303 are mathematically valid but do not couple paid-floor or puncture-capture ancestry unless a downstream theorem separately consumes the special witness roles.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R3,R38,R41. Historical coordinates x,y may come from arbitrary prior lineages; no same-representative assumption is made.

## Scope
Order-free provenance/synchronization fence. Distinguishes graph-intrinsic witness identity from inheritance of a witness special historical role.

## Applicability
Use when evaluating synchronization arguments that manufacture new signed certificates from historical coordinates by fresh boundary-antisymmetry triples. Such certificates are useful only to the extent a consumer needs those exact physical witness identities; they cannot be cited as evidence that the source histories themselves have merged, interacted, become simultaneous, or transported ancestry.

## Persistence
The newly manufactured signs and R38 interaction are graph-intrinsic. The source historical roles of x,y persist only in their own ancestry ledgers and are not imported into the new signs by this construction.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not invalidate R300 or R303 as literal existence theorems. Does not prevent a later role-sensitive consumer from exploiting the fact that the physical witnesses happen to equal historically important coordinates. Does not apply when the signed certificates themselves arise through ancestry-preserving transport rather than fresh R3 manufacture.

## Proof
Fix distinct physical vertices x,y and choose a physical dimer D={u,v} disjoint from them. By R3, one of (u,v,x),(x,v,u) is tight, giving through R41 a signed certificate on D with witness x. Likewise one of (u,v,y),(y,v,u) is tight, giving a second certificate on D with witness y. R38 applies. No step references any source floor, capture, crossing, payment, selected support, or ancestry property of x or y. Hence the certificate depends only on their physical distinctness. If x and y are renamed to any other distinct vertices, the same proof goes through verbatim. Therefore historical labels attached to x,y are not transported into the newly manufactured R38 packet. R300 and the floor/capture mismatch steps of R303 use exactly this proof pattern, so their interactions are coordinate-labeled but role-blind unless a later consumer explicitly uses the witnesses historical roles.
