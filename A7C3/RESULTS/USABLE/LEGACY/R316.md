# R316 — Order-eleven longest-five residue has exact 5+5 cover after every vertex deletion

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order 11 whose maximum proper tight-path order is five. Then for every vertex v in V(H), every spanning two-cover of H-v supplied by minimality consists of exactly two vertex-disjoint tight P5s. In particular H-v has a literal exact 5+5 cover, and both five-path supports are global-longest supports in H. Thus every vertex v is the unique omitted vertex of at least one pair of disjoint global-longest P5 supports whose union is V(H)\\{v}.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; |H|=11; maximum proper tight-path order is five.

## Scope
Finite order-eleven exact-deletion structure.

## Applicability
Use in the finite residual branch of R315. It converts minimality into eleven literal 5+5 deletion roots.

## Persistence
For each v, existence of the two disjoint global-longest supports is graph-intrinsic; a chosen ordering/cover representative is deletion-root-relative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the eleven deletion roots. Does not say the two supports in different roots intersect or coincide in any prescribed way. Does not by itself close order eleven.

## Proof
Fix v. By smallest-counterexample minimality, H-v has a spanning two-cover by tight paths P,Q. Since every proper tight path of H has order at most five and |H-v|=10, we have 10=|P|+|Q| with |P|,|Q|<=5. Therefore |P|=|Q|=5. Their supports are disjoint and cover V(H)\\{v}. Because the maximum proper path order in H is five, each is global-longest. This holds for every v.
