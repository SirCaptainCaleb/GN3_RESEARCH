# R223 — R171 dimer swallow creates a bidirectional endpoint-refund fork

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In a literal signed-pair frame consumed by R171, suppose the bounded-swallow output completely swallows one historical signed support S of order two. Then the same physical dimer S carries both opposite orientations as signed supports of the same historical polarity, with distinct witnesses: the incoming pair witness signs the original orientation, while the order-three peel-contact marker signs the reverse orientation. Consequently accepted aligned dimer payment R35 applies independently to the two orientations. Unless a spanning two-cover occurs on one route, the two routes produce physically certified singleton descendants supported at the two different physical endpoints of S. Thus an R171 width-two swallow is not a terminal bounded object; it canonically opens an endpoint-separated two-route refund fork.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; H=K sqcup P sqcup T is a literal R171 signed-pair frame; an R171 peel terminates in complete swallow of one pair support S with |S|=2.

## Scope
Order-free refinement of the width-two bounded-swallow output in the accepted literal signed-pair discharge proof. The two paid routes are separate descendants and are not asserted simultaneously current.

## Applicability
Use whenever R171 returns bounded swallow at support order two. It replaces the bare dimer terminal by two endpoint-specific paid continuations, one for each orientation of the swallowed physical dimer.

## Persistence
The physical dimer S, the incoming historical sign, the peel-contact trimer, its marker witness, and the identities of the two physical singleton endpoints are graph-intrinsic/historical. Each R35 paid descendant is lineage-relative; the two singleton descendants need not coexist in one representative and the old literal R171 frame need not survive payment.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not itself contradict the singleton-deletion rail floor because R35 does not keep the old literal third rail current. Does not assert the two endpoint singleton descendants are simultaneously current, form one balanced pair, or close the conjecture.

## Proof
Consider first a swallowed head-signed support S=(s0,s1) with historical witness alpha, so (alpha,s0,s1) is tight. The accepted proof P170 of R171 peels a marker vertex nu from the literal third path. Complete swallow at this width-two support means the support-to-marker seam (s0,s1,nu) is bad. Boundary antisymmetry R3 therefore makes (nu,s1,s0) tight. Hence the reverse ordered support (s1,s0) is itself head-signed, now by witness nu. Moreover nu cannot equal alpha: if nu=alpha, then (alpha,s0,s1) and its complete reversal (alpha,s1,s0) would both be tight, contradicting R3. Thus S carries two genuine signed orientations with distinct witnesses. Apply accepted Signed-support actualization and aligned payment R35 to the historical orientation (s0,s1). Its aligned width-two payment gives either a spanning two-cover or a physically certified singleton descendant on s1. Apply R35 separately to the reverse signed orientation (s1,s0); it gives either a spanning two-cover or a physically certified singleton descendant on s0. These are separate paid lineages, so no simultaneous currentness is asserted. The tail-signed case is obtained by reversing the head/tail roles in the accepted R171 peel and R35 payment; again the two opposite dimer orientations lower to the two opposite physical singleton endpoints. Therefore every width-two R171 swallow canonically carries the asserted endpoint-separated refund fork.
