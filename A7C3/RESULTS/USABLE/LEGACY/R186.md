# R186 — Complement-free intersection-one spectral ceiling on 5-subsets of a 10-set

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let Omega be a 10-set, let F be a family of 5-subsets containing no complementary pair, and let barF={Omega\\A:A in F}. In the Johnson graph J(10,5), where two 5-sets are adjacent when they intersect in four vertices, the number of edges between F and barF is at most 15|F|. Equivalently, the average number of four-overlap neighbors in barF seen from a member of F is at most 15.

## Hypotheses
Pure finite-set statement on 5-subsets of a 10-set; F contains no complementary pair.

## Scope
Order-free finite support combinatorics. Structural replacement for the earlier spectral formulation of the same ceiling.

## Applicability
Use when a longest-support family on ten vertices is complement-free and some local theorem forces many one-vertex replacements whose complements are four-overlap Johnson neighbors.

## Persistence
Pure set-theoretic counting; no representative dependence.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not use eigenvalues or association schemes. Does not bound arbitrary induced degrees in the intersection-one graph directly; the conversion through the complement family must be supplied by the application.

## Proof
For each four-set C subset Omega, let K_C={C union {v}: v in Omega\\C}. This is a K_6 in J(10,5), and every Johnson edge belongs to exactly one such K_C, namely the clique indexed by the four-set intersection of its endpoints. Put r_C=|F intersect K_C| and s_C=|barF intersect K_C|. Since F contains no complementary pair, F and barF are disjoint, hence r_C+s_C<=6. The number of F--barF Johnson edges inside K_C is exactly r_C s_C. For nonnegative r,s with r+s<=6, rs <= (r+s)^2/4 <= (3/2)(r+s). Therefore

  e_J(F,barF)=sum_C r_C s_C <= (3/2) sum_C (r_C+s_C).

Each 5-set contains exactly five four-subsets, so each vertex of F lies in exactly five cliques K_C; likewise each vertex of barF. Hence sum_C r_C=5|F| and sum_C s_C=5|barF|=5|F|. Thus

  e_J(F,barF) <= (3/2)(10|F|)=15|F|.

This proves the ceiling.
