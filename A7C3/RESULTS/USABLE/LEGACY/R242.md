# R242 — Occupied four-core produces a common-substitute global-longest carrier fiber

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the five-exterior equality face and let S be a physical four-set with occupancy x_S=r in the family Ecal of exteriors of global-longest carriers. Let A={a outside S : S union {a} is in Ecal} and U=V(H)\\S, so |A|=r. For every a in A, the complement K_a=U\\{a} supports a global-longest tight path. Moreover U itself is non-Hamiltonian and K_a sqcup {a} is therefore an exact two-cover of H[U]. For any distinct a,b in A one has the literal support identity K_b=(K_a\\{b}) union {a}; hence, relative to the retained carrier K_a, every b in A\\{a} is a genuine common-substitute swap position: replacing b by the single fixed vertex a yields another global-longest carrier support. In addition, R240 gives that in the R192 row of b relative to exterior S union {a}, the failed hole is exactly a and all four holes s in S succeed.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; S is a four-set with occupancy r in the global-longest exterior family; accepted R240.

## Scope
Arbitrary-order structural bridge from four-core occupancy to physical carrier geometry. It converts one occupied Johnson clique into a common-substitute family of actual global-longest carrier supports and exact Hamilton-plus-singleton covers of one fixed subsystem.

## Applicability
Use when an occupancy argument identifies a four-core S with many completions. Fixing one completion a turns all other completion vertices into physical positions of one retained carrier K_a that can each be swapped for the same substitute a while preserving global-longest support. The exact subsystem U=V(H)\\S is common to the whole fiber.

## Persistence
S, A, U, all carrier supports K_a, their global-longest status, and all support identities are graph-intrinsic. A chosen tight ordering of each K_a is representative data; no synchronization of those orderings is asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert compatible orientations of the different global-longest carriers, does not make their path representatives simultaneous, does not force U itself to be Hamiltonian, and does not by itself yield a spanning two-cover or carrier extension. The Hamilton-plus-singleton covers are exact covers of the proper subsystem U, not of H.

## Proof
Put A={a:S union {a} in Ecal} and U=V(H)-S. For each a in A, by definition of Ecal there is a global-longest tight path whose exterior is E_a=S union {a}. Its support is the set-theoretic complement K_a=V(H)-E_a=U-{a}. In the equality face every global-longest path has order |H|-5=|U|-1. If U itself were Hamiltonian, H would contain a tight path of order |U|=|H|-4, contradicting global longestness. Hence the displayed cover U=K_a sqcup {a} has path-cover number exactly two. Now fix distinct a,b in A. Since K_a=U-{a} contains b and K_b=U-{b}, set algebra gives K_b=(K_a-{b}) union {a}. Thus one and the same exterior completion vertex a is a valid one-for-one substitute for every other completion b on K_a, always producing another global-longest carrier support. Finally, R240 applied to E_a and E_b says that relative to E_a the R192 replacement row indexed by carrier vertex b fails exactly at hole a and succeeds at every s in S.
