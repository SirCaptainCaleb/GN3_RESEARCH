# R193 — Every six-set contains a tight five-path

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Every six-vertex subset W of a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament contains a tight path on five of its vertices.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry; W is any six-element vertex set.

## Scope
Order-free local path-density theorem.

## Applicability
Use in higher-order smallest-counterexample arguments whenever a residual or exterior set has at least six vertices. Every chosen six-subset already contains a P5, so exterior slack cannot be treated as path-poor. In particular, a longest-path exterior of size at least six contains a tight P5 wholly inside the exterior.

## Persistence
The resulting P5 support is graph-intrinsic on the chosen six vertices.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a Hamilton P6 on the six-set, does not prescribe the omitted vertex, and does not by itself merge a larger exterior into one path.

## Proof
Partition W into any three-set P and complementary three-set {x,y,z}. By R8, P has a tight trimer orientation. R146 applied to that fixed tight trimer and x,y,z yields a tight five-path on P together with one of the three exterior pairs. Hence W contains a tight P5.
