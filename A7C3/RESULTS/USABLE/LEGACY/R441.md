# R441 — Cross-Generation Two-Pair Collision / Descent

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let Pi_1=(H_1,T_1) and Pi_2=(H_2,T_2) be two historical balanced opposite-sign pair births retained before an unrelated reset, where all four signed supports H_1,T_1,H_2,T_2 are nontrivial tight paths. Then the second pair cannot become an independent quiet historical package. If a support of Pi_2 meets a support of Pi_1, General Tight-Path Contact / Historical Anchor Protection R436 already yields an explicit anchor-contact/growth/cycle/reversal output or a strict signed-side descendant of the older support, hence strict historical support descent. If no cross-generation overlap occurs, then because the two supports inside each balanced pair are disjoint, the four supports are pairwise vertex-disjoint and Four-Support Sign-Splice Checkerboard Descent R440 applies directly. Thus before any unrelated reset one obtains strict historical support descent/contact, fused or strict signed growth, a vertex-simple tight cycle, a bounded reverse/contact or signed-BB core, or bounded-small output. No common birth frame, marker, exact cover, or simultaneous current paid descendants are required.

## Hypotheses
Two historical balanced opposite-sign pair-birth certificates are retained before an unrelated reset. Each of their four signed supports is nontrivial at the time the two births are compared. The two supports within each pair are vertex-disjoint. Across the two births, supports may overlap.

## Scope
Strong Level-(1), arbitrary order. Corrected nontrivial-support cross-generation interaction theorem. This is the scope actually needed by the internal-middle consumer. The broader historical R14 wording that passed through arbitrary overlap normalization is not asserted at exact singleton coincidence.

## Applicability
Use when two retained historical balanced-pair births each still have two nontrivial supports. Any cross-generation overlap is already a paying R436 contact/descent; only the completely disjoint case reaches R440.

## Persistence
The two birth certificates remain historical data. If an overlap occurs, retain the exact old/new supports and the R436 contact or strict signed descendant. If no overlap occurs, retain the four original nontrivial supports and the R440 checkerboard/descent certificate. No simultaneous current paid descendants are asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not cover arbitrary width-one coincidence after supports have been paid to singletons. In particular, two later certificates on the same singleton are not declared productive merely because the certificates are distinct. Same-polarity same-singleton certificates with distinct witnesses may have additional local structure, but no general opposite-polarity singleton collision is claimed here. Does not assert interaction is closure or that pair mass is globally monotone.

## Proof
Write Pi_1=(H_1,T_1) and Pi_2=(H_2,T_2), with all four supports nontrivial. The two supports inside each pair are disjoint by the balanced-pair hypothesis. Suppose first that some support Q of Pi_2 meets some support P of Pi_1. Apply R436 to the older signed support P and the later proper tight path Q. If Q contains the signed anchor of P, R436 gives strict path growth, a vertex-simple tight cycle, or a reverse contact/reversal cell. If Q avoids the signed anchor, R436 gives a nonempty strictly shorter signed-side descendant of P disjoint from Q. This strict historical support descent is already a nonquiet output, so stop; there is no need to continue truncating P toward singleton width. Suppose instead that there is no cross-generation overlap at all. Since H_1 and T_1 are disjoint and H_2 and T_2 are disjoint, all four supports are then pairwise vertex-disjoint. They are nontrivial by hypothesis, so R440 applies directly and its finite checkerboard continuation yields fused/grown path, vertex-simple tight cycle, bounded contact/reversal or signed-BB core, strict signed growth, or bounded-small output. Thus two nontrivial historical balanced pair births cannot form independent quiet epochs before an unrelated reset. The proof deliberately makes no assertion about exact singleton coincidence after later payment; that is the corrected historical fence.
