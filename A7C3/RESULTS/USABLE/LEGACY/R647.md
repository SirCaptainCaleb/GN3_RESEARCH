## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R24 frame of R621 with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, L=q_0 and R=q_m. Both physical middle-gate edges independently carry tested-order-safe two-witness R542 packets, with no small-deletion exactness input. Left: the accepted deep R621 paths contain the tight trimers K_ac^+=(L,a,c) and K_ac^-=(L,c,a), carrying opposite orientations of the physical boundary {a,c}. The three vertices b,z,R lie outside both trimers. By R612, one tested orientation (a,c) or (c,a) has two distinct same-polarity witnesses among {b,z,R} and is the reverse boundary dimer of the opposite carrier; hence R542 applies. The complementary carrier singleton is L. Right: the accepted deep paths contain K_bz^+=(b,z,R) and K_bz^-=(z,b,R), carrying opposite orientations of {b,z}; the three vertices a,c,L lie outside both. R612 again forces a two-witness reverse-boundary packet and its R542 continuation, now with complementary carrier singleton R. Thus the crossed frame contains two coexisting source packets rooted at opposite Q endpoints. Their paid descendants are alternative continuations.

## Proof
From accepted R621, the left-enlarged deep paths include (u,L,a,c,b) and (u,L,c,a,z). Hence their graph-intrinsic subpaths K_1=(L,a,c) and K_2=(L,c,a) are tight trimers. They carry opposite orientations a->c and c->a of the same physical boundary {a,c}. The vertices b,z,R are three distinct vertices outside V(K_1) union V(K_2)={L,a,c}. Apply accepted R612 with A=a, B=c and spare triple b,z,R. If at least two spares certify x_i a c, then tested dimer (a,c) is head-signed by those witnesses and is the reverse boundary dimer of K_2; otherwise at least two certify c a x_i, making (c,a) tail-signed and the reverse boundary dimer of K_1. In either case R612 invokes R542. The chosen carrier is K_2 or K_1, and removing the tested boundary {a,c} leaves the complementary carrier singleton L. Dually, the accepted right-enlarged deep paths include (a,b,z,R,s) and (c,z,b,R,s), so K_3=(b,z,R) and K_4=(z,b,R) are tight trimers carrying opposite orientations of {b,z}. The three distinct vertices a,c,L lie outside their common support {b,z,R}. Apply R612 with A=b, B=z and these spares. Whichever tested orientation wins is the reverse boundary dimer of the opposite carrier and has two same-polarity witnesses outside that carrier, so R542 applies. The complementary carrier singleton is R. Both source packets are graph-intrinsic facts in the accepted crossed frame; only their later paid descendants are alternative. No punctured-cover exactness or R5-type theorem is used.
