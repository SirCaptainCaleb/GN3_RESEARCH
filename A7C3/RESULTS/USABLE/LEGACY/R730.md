# R730 — First-step root advances are same-root state reversals with next-boundary shields

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and accepted R723,R725. Put u=q_1, v=q_2, w=q_3 and d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}, rho=q_{m-3}. Every ADV output of R725 carries more than an anonymous deeper-cut cover.

LEFT. If L-ADV occurs with deleted root r in {a,c}, then the accepted R723 shallow cover of H-r contains Q[1,m] and hence selects the directed state (u,v), while the R725 advanced cover contains the short rail beginning (v,u,...) and hence selects the literal reverse state (v,u). Moreover both root turns (v,u,r) and (u,v,r) are tight, and the next inward reverse-boundary turn (q_4,w,r) is tight. Here (v,u,r) is the universal R723 star. If (r,v,u) were tight, r could be prepended to the advanced short rail, producing a spanning two-cover of H; hence R3 gives (u,v,r). Likewise if (r,w,q_4) were tight, r could be prepended to the advanced long rail Q[3,m], again two-covering H; hence R3 gives (q_4,w,r).

RIGHT. If R-ADV occurs with deleted root r in {b,z}, then the R723 shallow cover selects (d,s) on Q[0,m-1], while the R725 advanced short rail ending (...,s,d) selects its literal reverse (s,d). Moreover both (r,s,d) and (r,d,s) are tight, and the next inward reverse-boundary turn (r,rho,q_{m-4}) is tight. The first is the universal R723 star. If (s,d,r) were tight, r could be appended to the advanced short rail; if (q_{m-4},rho,r) were tight, r could be appended to Q[0,m-3]. Either would two-cover H, so R3 gives the displayed reversals.

Thus every first-step root advance is simultaneously (i) a same-deletion-root reversal of a selected physical Q state and (ii) a source-localized shield on the next deeper Q boundary. The ADV branch may therefore be carried in the same selected-state-reversal currency as accepted R720 while retaining a concrete seed for a second zipper step.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame; m>=4. Use accepted R3, R723 and R725.

## Scope
Noncircular crossed-frame refinement of the accepted R725 ADV branch. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R358,payment,capture,R436,Reverse Ear,or computation.

## Applicability
Whenever R725 returns L-ADV or R-ADV, retain both exact covers of the same singleton deletion and the opposite selected orders of the physical first-inward dimer. Also retain the deleted root as a witness on both state orders and on the newly exposed next-inward reverse boundary. Downstream consumers should treat ADV as a source-labelled reversal plus a second-step zipper seed, not merely as cut movement.

## Persistence
Retain the deleted root r, the R723 shallow cover, the R725 advanced cover, the two oppositely directed selected states, the two root turns on those state orders, and the next-inward reverse-boundary turn with its failed insertion certificate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that a selected-state reversal alone closes H. Does not assert a second advanced cover or universal four-witness star at the next boundary. Does not consume the P4 alternative of pending R727. The left and right ADV outputs are independent alternatives and need not coexist.

## Proof
For L-ADV, R725 gives either H-c=(v,u,a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[3,m] or H-a=(v,u,c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[3,m]. For the same root r, R723 gives H-r=A_r^L sqcup Q[1,m]. Therefore Q[1,m] selects (u,v), while the advanced short rail selects (v,u). R723 already gives (v,u,r). If (r,v,u) were tight, prepend r to the advanced short rail; together with Q[3,m] this is a two-path cover of H, contrary to pc(H)=3. By R3, (u,v,r) is tight. Since m>=4, Q[3,m] begins (w,q_4). If (r,w,q_4) were tight, prepend r to this rail and retain the advanced short rail, again obtaining a two-cover of H. Hence R3 gives (q_4,w,r).

For R-ADV, R725 gives either H-z=(R,a,c,b,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3] or H-b=(R,c,a,z,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3]. For the same r, R723 gives H-r=Q[0,m-1] sqcup A_r^R. Thus the shallow Q rail selects (d,s), while the advanced short rail selects (s,d), and R723 gives (r,s,d). If (s,d,r) were tight, append r to the advanced short rail and retain Q[0,m-3], two-covering H; hence (r,d,s) is tight by R3. Finally Q[0,m-3] ends (q_{m-4},rho). If (q_{m-4},rho,r) were tight, append r and retain the advanced short rail, again two-covering H. R3 gives (r,rho,q_{m-4}). These arguments also cover m=4, where the relevant residual Q rail has order two.
