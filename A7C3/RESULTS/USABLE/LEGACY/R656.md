# R656 — Extreme-edge saturation compiles to a four-fiber short-carrier capture fan

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain branch (B) of accepted R633 in the crossed R621 frame. Then all four extreme collision packets are directly consumable by accepted R542, in four named sibling pair-deletion fibers. Specifically: (i) C_ba makes S=(b,a) a two-witness tail-signed reverse boundary dimer of the tight carrier K=(a,b,z), with witnesses L,c and complementary carrier singleton z; hence every exact two-cover of H-{a,b} contains an R508 z-to-exterior crossing, one of the two witnesses L,c avoids that crossing, and R542 gives capture/payment or a spanning two-cover. (ii) C_bc makes S=(b,c) a two-witness head-signed reverse boundary dimer of K=(z,c,b), with witnesses R,a and complementary singleton z, yielding the analogous packet in H-{b,c}. (iii) C_za makes S=(z,a) a two-witness head-signed reverse boundary dimer of K=(b,a,z), with witnesses R,c and complementary singleton b, yielding the packet in H-{z,a}. (iv) C_zc makes S=(z,c) a two-witness tail-signed reverse boundary dimer of K=(c,z,b), with witnesses L,a and complementary singleton b, yielding the packet in H-{z,c}. Thus the saturated branch is not merely two opposite hinge floors on {a,c}: it contains two z-centered sibling capture fibers across the b-hinge and two b-centered sibling capture fibers across the z-hinge, all with exact physical carrier and witness ancestry.

## Proof
Assume branch (B) of accepted R633, so all four collision packets C_ba,C_bc,C_za,C_zc hold with the stated tested orientations, polarities, and two distinct witnesses. We verify the four R542 instances.

For C_ba, the tested dimer S=(b,a) is tail-signed by witnesses L and c. The matching-height rule on X gives the tight trimer K=(a,b,z), because M(ab)=M_R>M(bz)=M_S. Thus S=(b,a) is the reverse initial boundary dimer of K. Both witnesses L,c lie outside K. Accepted R542 applies. With D={a,b}, its R508 step uses the complementary carrier block {z} and therefore forces, in every exact two-cover of H-D, a selected state crossing {z} to V(H)\V(K). Such a two-vertex crossing cannot contain both distinct witnesses L,c, so one witness avoids it and the R527/R526 capture/payment branch applies unless H already has a spanning two-cover.

For C_bc, S=(b,c) is head-signed by witnesses R and a. The trimer K=(z,c,b) is tight since M(zc)=M_R>M(cb)=M_L. Here S=(b,c) is the reverse terminal boundary dimer of K, and R,a are outside K. R542 therefore applies in D={b,c}, with complementary singleton z and an R508 crossing from {z} to the exterior of K; one of R,a avoids the crossing.

For C_za, S=(z,a) is head-signed by witnesses R,c. The trimer K=(b,a,z) is tight since M(ba)=M_R>M(az)=M_L. S=(z,a) is its reverse terminal boundary dimer, R,c lie outside K, and R542 applies in D={z,a} with complementary singleton b.

For C_zc, S=(z,c) is tail-signed by witnesses L,a. The trimer K=(c,z,b) is tight since M(cz)=M_R>M(zb)=M_S. S=(z,c) is its reverse initial boundary dimer, L,a lie outside K, and R542 applies in D={z,c} with complementary singleton b.

These are four literal R542-ready packets. The first two share the same complementary singleton z while changing the second deleted endpoint across the common hinge b; the last two share complementary singleton b across the common hinge z. This is precisely the claimed four-fiber capture fan.
