# R232 — Five-exterior dense-hole column bound

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the non-Hamiltonian five-exterior equality face of R192, and put m=|V(K)|. Then there exists an exterior vertex z in E such that (E\\{z}) union {k} supports a tight P5 for at least ceil(4m/5) distinct carrier vertices k in V(K). Equivalently, one fixed exterior four-set F=E\\{z} has at least ceil(4m/5) distinct one-vertex carrier completions to tight P5 supports. In the post-R191 O3 equality face m>=6, so one common exterior four-set has at least five distinct carrier completions.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a proper tight path with five-vertex exterior E; E is non-Hamiltonian; accepted R192.

## Scope
Arbitrary-order support-incidence corollary of the R192 replacement grid.

## Applicability
Use when a single fixed exterior four-set is preferable to the row-wise R192 formulation. It gives a large common carrier-witness family over one physical four-set, suitable for sign/dimer pigeonhole or restoration tests.

## Persistence
The exterior hole z, fixed four-set F=E\\{z}, and all successful P5 supports F union {k} are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert which z is dense, does not improve the 4/5 density fraction, does not synchronize P5 orderings, and does not imply any complementary carrier replacement.

## Proof
For k in V(K) and z in E, call (k,z) successful when (E\\{z}) union {k} supports a tight P5. R192 gives at least four successful incidences in every carrier row, so the total number I of successful incidences satisfies I>=4m. There are exactly five exterior-hole columns. Hence some z has at least ceil(I/5)>=ceil(4m/5) successful carrier vertices. Taking F=E\\{z} gives the claimed fixed four-set. After R191, every surviving five-exterior equality face has |H|>10 and m=|H|-5>=6, so ceil(4m/5)>=5.
