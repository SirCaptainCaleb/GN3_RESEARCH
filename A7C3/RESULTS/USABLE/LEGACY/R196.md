# R196 — Six-exterior four-root puncture pair fan

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K be a tight path with six-vertex exterior E. Then for at least four vertices e in E there is a tight P5 Q_e on E-{e}, so H=K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} is a literal spanning three-cover and H-{e}=K sqcup Q_e is a literal exact two-cover. Moreover, for every internal vertex k of K and every such e, the proper subsystem W=(V(K)-{k}) union {e} has a literal three-cover by the two K-k intervals and singleton e, while minimality supplies a cover with at most two components; hence R159 yields a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a tight path; E=V(H)\\V(K) has size six; k is internal when the puncture conclusion is invoked.

## Scope
Arbitrary-order six-exterior root fan and component-drop entry. No order-ten support geometry, sign synchronization, or payment is used.

## Applicability
Use in the exterior-slack-one face. It supplies at least four singleton roots retaining the same K and a balanced-pair birth for every internal carrier puncture in every such root.

## Persistence
The P5 supports Q_e and existence of the balanced pairs are graph-intrinsic. The lower-component recompletion of each punctured subsystem is existential and representative-relative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not identify or synchronize the balanced pairs across e or k; does not assert the balanced pair is selected in the displayed three-cover; does not itself force a spanning two-cover or a five-exterior path.

## Proof
Define M={e∈E:E\{e} supports a tight P5}. For every three-subset T⊂E, orient E\T as a tight trimer by R8. R146 applied to that trimer and the three vertices T shows M∩T≠∅. Hence |E\M|≤2 and |M|≥4. Fix e∈M and choose a tight P5 Q_e on E\{e}. Since E is exactly the exterior of K, the three disjoint tight paths K,Q_e,{e} partition V(H). Because H is a smallest counterexample, pc(H)=3 by R4, so this displayed three-cover is minimum. Componentwise exact re-rooting R22 therefore gives K⊔Q_e as an exact two-cover of H-{e}. Now let k be internal on K. In W=(V(K)\{k})∪{e}, the two nonempty intervals of K-k together with singleton e form a literal 3-cover. W is proper because Q_e and k are omitted. By R4, H[W] has a path cover with at most two components. Applying R159 to the literal 3-cover and a lower-component cover yields a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair. This holds for every internal k and every e∈M.
