# R687 — Crossed middle-gate dimers carry three carriers, so no single exterior witness escapes capture

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, endpoints L=q_0 and R=q_m, and let S be any one of the four oriented middle-gate M_S dimers (a,c),(c,a),(b,z),(z,b). Accepted R649 supplies for each such S not two but three tight trimer carriers whose supports exceed V(S) by a single vertex:

S=(a,c): carriers (z,c,a),(L,c,a),(R,c,a), complementary singletons z,L,R;
S=(c,a): carriers (b,a,c),(L,a,c),(R,a,c), complementary singletons b,L,R;
S=(b,z): carriers (z,b,c),(z,b,L),(z,b,R), complementary singletons c,L,R;
S=(z,b): carriers (b,z,a),(b,z,L),(b,z,R), complementary singletons a,L,R.

Write x_S for the X-vertex complementary singleton, so x_S is z,b,c,a respectively. In every case x_S lies in X and x_S,L,R are three distinct vertices.

Let v be any vertex outside X union {L,R} which is an exact sign witness on the tested orientation of S with the polarity appropriate to the displayed tight turn. Then the carrier-witness saturation theorem R686 applies with k=3 and |W|=1, so capture is unconditional: every exact two-cover T of H-V(S) contains a selected crossing at one of x_S,L,R that avoids v, accepted R527 captures that complementary singleton with v retained as the exact witness, and since S is a dimer accepted R526 yields either a spanning two-cover or a certificate-retaining singleton-width descendant.

The outcome refines into two cases, both of which now carry a capture.

(CAP-E) If some endpoint crossing avoids v, the capture may be taken at the physical endpoint L or R by orienting the marker accordingly. This is exactly the CAP output of pending R682.

(CAP-X) If neither endpoint crossing avoids v, then T contains the selected states {L,v} and {R,v}, so v is internal on its rail with neighbours L and R and T contains the cross-end tight trimer (L,v,R) or (R,v,L). This is the XET output of pending R682. But both rail adjacencies at v are then used, and x_S is distinct from L and R, so the third forced crossing at x_S cannot meet v. It therefore avoids v, and an x_S-anchored capture with the same witness v holds in the same cover T alongside the trimer.

Consequently the R682 dichotomy is not a choice between capture and a capture-free nonlocal alternative. Capture with the inward witness v is unconditional in the crossed frame, and the cross-end trimer, when it occurs, is additional structure that coexists with an X-anchored capture rather than replacing it. The present statement subsumes pending R682, whose two conclusions are CAP-E together with the trimer half of CAP-X, and it does so from the same accepted inputs R621,R649,R508,R527,R526 without depending on the review outcome of R682.

## Proof
Step 1 (three carriers). Accepted R649 lists twelve R542-ready packets grouped by the four oriented M_S dimers, three per dimer, and certifies for each entry that K is a tight trimer and that S is exactly a reverse boundary dimer of K. Reading off supports only: for S=(a,c) the listed carriers are (z,c,a),(L,c,a),(R,c,a) with supports {a,c,z},{a,c,L},{a,c,R}; for S=(c,a) they are (b,a,c),(L,a,c),(R,a,c) with supports {a,c,b},{a,c,L},{a,c,R}; for S=(b,z) they are (z,b,c),(z,b,L),(z,b,R) with supports {b,z,c},{b,z,L},{b,z,R}; for S=(z,b) they are (b,z,a),(b,z,L),(b,z,R) with supports {b,z,a},{b,z,L},{b,z,R}. In every group each support equals V(S) union {one vertex}, and the three extra vertices are x_S,L,R with x_S the X-vertex z,b,c,a respectively. Since x_S lies in X and L,R are the two distinct physical endpoints of Q, the three complementary singletons are pairwise distinct. Hence S carries k=3 retained vertex-simple tight carriers with distinct complementary singletons.

Step 2 (the witness misses every complementary singleton). By hypothesis v lies outside X union {L,R}. Since x_S is in X, v is distinct from x_S, L and R. Also v is not in V(S), which is contained in X. Hence W={v} is disjoint from V(S) union {x_S,L,R}, and every hypothesis of R686 holds with k=3 and |W|=1.

Step 3 (unconditional capture). Clause (ii) of R686 applies: at least one of the three forced crossings avoids v. Applying accepted R527 with that carrier, the signed support S, the exact witness v, the cover T, and the avoiding crossing gives the capture/refund branch, with the corresponding complementary singleton designated as the captured signed anchor. Since S has order two and H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample, R527 invokes the aligned dimer payment of accepted R526, yielding either a spanning two-cover or a certificate-retaining descendant with singleton support.

Step 4 (case CAP-E). Suppose some endpoint crossing {e,y_e} with e in {L,R} has y_e not equal to v. Then Step 3 applies with that carrier and e may be designated as the captured signed anchor by orienting the marker accordingly. This coincides with the CAP output of pending R682.

Step 5 (case CAP-X). Otherwise both endpoint crossings meet v, so their physical supports are {L,v} and {R,v}. These are two distinct selected states of T incident with v. The vertex v lies in H-V(S) and therefore occupies exactly one position on exactly one rail, so these two states are its two rail adjacencies and v is internal on that rail with consecutive order (L,v,R) or (R,v,L). Since a T rail is a tight path, this segment is a graph-intrinsic tight trimer; this is the XET conclusion of pending R682. Now consider the third forced crossing {x_S,y_x} supplied by Step 1 and R508. If y_x were equal to v, then {x_S,v} would be a third rail adjacency at v, contradicting the previous sentence because x_S is distinct from L and R. Hence y_x is not v, the crossing at x_S avoids v, and Step 3 applies with the carrier whose complementary singleton is x_S. This gives a capture anchored at x_S in the same cover T, with v retained as the exact witness, coexisting with the literal trimer order.

Step 6 (conclusion). Steps 4 and 5 exhaust the cases, so capture is unconditional and the cross-end trimer never occurs without an accompanying X-anchored capture in the same frame. The conclusions of pending R682 are Step 4 together with the trimer half of Step 5, so the present statement subsumes R682. All inputs used are accepted: R621 for the frame, R649 for the three carriers, R508 inside R686 for the forced crossings, R527 for capture, R526 for payment, and R686 for the counting.
