# R792 — The R789 shortcut puncture forces a simultaneous two-ended crossing fan in one deletion cover

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the SHORTCUT-CUT branch of accepted R789. Thus D={u,v,d,s}, K=(u,v,d,s) is tight, J#=(L,u,v,d,s,R) is tight, M=Q[3,m-3], and T# is an exact two-cover of H-D. Then the single representative T# simultaneously contains: (L-FAN) a selected state incident with L whose other endpoint lies in {R} union X union M; (R-FAN) a selected state incident with R whose other endpoint lies in {L} union X union M; and (OUTER-FAN) a selected state with one endpoint in {L,R} and the other in X union M. Consequently exactly one of the following coarse geometric alternatives holds. (DEEP) T# has a selected state from L or R into M. (LINK) {L,R} is selected in T#, and T# also has a selected state from one of L,R into X; the two states concatenate in the actual T# rail order to a tight trimer on {L,R,y} with y in X and with L,R adjacent. (SPLIT) {L,R} is not selected, and T# has selected states {L,x_L} and {R,x_R} for some x_L,x_R in X. In SPLIT, if x_L=x_R=x then the actual T# rail contains the literal cross-end tight trimer (L,x,R) or (R,x,L).

## Proof
In the R789 SHORTCUT-CUT branch, D={u,v,d,s} and T# is an exact two-cover of W=H-D. The tight shortcut J#=(L,u,v,d,s,R) has vertex set D union {L,R}. Accepted R508 with S={L,R} therefore gives OUTER-FAN: T# selects a state from {L,R} to W-{L,R}=X union M.

The left truncation P_L=(L,u,v,d,s) is also a tight path, since its consecutive turns are the first three turns of J#, and V(P_L)=D union {L}. Apply R508 to the same deletion D, the same exact cover T#, and S={L}. It forces a selected state incident with L whose other endpoint lies in W-{L}={R} union X union M. This is L-FAN. Dually the right truncation P_R=(u,v,d,s,R) is tight with vertex set D union {R}; R508 with S={R} forces R-FAN.

If any selected endpoint state reaches M, DEEP holds. Assume not. OUTER-FAN then supplies a selected endpoint-X state. If {L,R} is selected, combine that state with the endpoint-X state. They share whichever physical endpoint realizes OUTER-FAN, so in the actual path-cover rail the two selected adjacencies are consecutive through that endpoint; their three vertices form a literal tight trimer, giving LINK. If {L,R} is not selected, L-FAN cannot use R or M and hence has the form {L,x_L} with x_L in X. Similarly R-FAN has the form {R,x_R} with x_R in X, giving SPLIT. Finally if x_L=x_R=x, then x has the two selected rail adjacencies {L,x} and {x,R}; since a path-cover vertex has at most two rail neighbors, these are consecutive and the actual rail contains either (L,x,R) or (R,x,L), a literal tight cross-end trimer. This proves the theorem.
