# R550 — Dimer pure-C rail seam refinement in the both-bad CS cell

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain a full R513 both-bad CS packet with s=1. Thus the pure C-rail is the dimer K=(c_0,c_1), the cut satisfies 2<=j<=m-1, the seam four-set is F={a,b,c,d} with a=q_{j-2}, b=q_{j-1}, c=c_0, d=c_1, and both seam holes (a,b,c) and (b,c,d) are bad. Write g=(b_r,q_j,q_{j+1}) for the isolated mixed-seam hole. Then the unique Hamilton P4 on F retaining the oriented Q-boundary dimer (a,b) as prefix is P_L=(a,b,d,c). If P_L is tight, replacing the seam by P_L yields a spanning proposal with sole uncertified turn g, so g is bad and its reverse is tight. If (a,b,d) is bad, then (d,b,a) is tight and together with the already forced (c,b,a) gives an R523 same-head collision on tested dimer (b,a) with witnesses c,d. If (b,d,c) is bad, then (c,d,b) is tight.

## Hypotheses
H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; the full accepted R513 both-bad CS packet is retained with s=1 and 2<=j<=m-1.

## Scope
Order-free coordinate-preserving refinement of the accepted R513 CS residue restricted to a dimer pure C-rail.

## Applicability
Use inside an R513 both-bad CS cell when s=1. Test the two turns of P_L=(q_{j-2},q_{j-1},c_1,c_0): if both are tight reduce to the sole mixed hole g; if the first is bad retain the R523 collision on the reversed Q-seam dimer; if the second is bad retain its R3 reverse.

## Persistence
Retain the full R513 packet, cut index j, dimer pure rail K, both bad natural seam holes, the two P_L turn statuses, the sole-hole proposal when certified, and the collision packet when the first P_L turn is bad.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not replace or narrow R513. Does not treat s>=2, the SC side, or assert that P_L is always tight. Does not claim an alternative deletion cover or lexicographic decrease.

## Proof
Retain the R513 both-bad CS packet with s=1. Put a=q_{j-2}, b=q_{j-1}, c=c_0, d=c_1. The natural seam turns abc and bcd are bad, so R3 gives cba and dcb tight. The inherited isolated mixed hole is g=(b_r,q_j,q_{j+1}).\n\nAny Hamilton P4 on {a,b,c,d} with prefix (a,b) has form (a,b,x,y) with {x,y}={c,d}. The order (a,b,c,d) fails at abc, so the unique possible prefix-preserving order is P_L=(a,b,d,c), and it is tight exactly when abd and bdc are tight.\n\nIf P_L is tight, set U_L=(q_0,...,q_{j-1},d,c) and keep the inherited mixed rail V=(b_0,...,b_r,q_j,...,q_m). Every turn of U_L is either an old Q-turn or one of abd,bdc; because s=1 there is no further C suffix. Every turn of V is inherited except g. Thus {g} is the complete uncertified-turn set. Since pc(H)>2, g cannot be tight; R3 gives (q_{j+1},q_j,b_r) tight.\n\nIf abd is bad, R3 gives dba tight. Together with inherited cba, this gives two head certificates on the same tested oriented dimer (b,a), with distinct witnesses d and c; R523 identifies the exact same-head collision packet. If bdc is bad, R3 gives cdb tight. These alternatives exhaust the possible failure of P_L. The construction rewrites only the spanning proposal and makes no claim about changing the deletion cover.
