# R62 — Endpoint-exposed seam selection or crossing

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

If a named retained interface state is literally endpoint-exposed in the appropriate current exact cover, its blockers are localized at the exposed endpoint.

## Scope
- No blocker gives an immediate merge and a two-cover.
- One blocker gives a neutral exact cover selecting the retained seam.
- Two blockers give the explicit crossing/reversal configuration and its synchronized historical interaction.

This theorem consumes endpoint exposure; it does not create it. Historical global endpointhood, boundary pinning, capture, and a graph-intrinsic terminal support are all weaker than current endpoint exposure.
