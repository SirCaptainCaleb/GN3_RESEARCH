# R588 — Four-complement gap pressure compiles to local capture/payment or a reverse P4 ear

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=2, be a vertex-simple tight path whose complement X=V(H)\\V(Q) has exactly four vertices. Then at every gap i between q_i and q_{i+1}, the accepted R583 obstruction array compiles to one of two physically typed outputs: (CP) a two-witness reverse-terminal-dimer packet on a local three-vertex subpath of Q, hence the accepted R542 short-carrier theorem forces a certificate-retaining capture/payment continuation; or (EAR) there are distinct x,y in X such that the carrier state q_i q_{i+1} is spanned by a literal reverse P4 ear, namely one of (q_{i+1},x,q_i,y) and (q_{i+1},y,q_i,x) is tight by R584. More explicitly, for an internal gap 1<=i<=m-2, the R583 left packet (x,q_i,q_{i-1}),(y,q_i,q_{i-1}) is exactly a two-witness packet on the reverse terminal dimer (q_i,q_{i-1}) of the tight trimer (q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}); the right packet (q_{i+2},q_{i+1},x),(q_{i+2},q_{i+1},y) is exactly such a packet on (q_{i+2},q_{i+1}) for the tight trimer (q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}). R542 consumes either packet. If neither occurs, R583 supplies two reverse-middle blockers and R584 gives (EAR). At i=0, the only terminal packet is on (q_2,q_1), the reverse terminal dimer of (q_0,q_1,q_2); absent it R583 supplies at least three reverse-middle blockers, any two of which give (EAR). The right endpoint is dual. Consequently an order-three singleton-deletion R24 frame with long rail Q has a gap-by-gap capture-or-ear structure all along Q; generic unspent R38 collision is not a terminal output of this compilation.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; Q is tight with exactly four exterior vertices; |Q|>=3. Accepted R583, R584, R542.

## Scope
R24-relevant but order-unbounded along Q. The underlying four-complement obstruction R583 is order-free; smallest-counterexample structure enters only when terminal packets are consumed through R542.

## Applicability
Apply to a putative R24 counterexample H-z=P sqcup Q with |P|=3, where X=V(P) union {z}. Every gap of Q now has a physical continuation: capture/payment through a named local Q trimer, or a reverse P4 ear using two of the four exterior vertices. This replaces anonymous gap collisions by consumer-ready outputs and leaves the ear orientation as the only unspent local species.

## Persistence
In CP retain the gap index, local Q trimer, exact reverse terminal dimer, two exterior witness identities, and the R542 capture/payment ancestry. In EAR retain the gap index, carrier state, two exterior vertices, and literal P4 order. All input Q and X coordinates remain graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
R542 payment need not close H; it may end at a singleton signed-support descendant. A reverse P4 ear is not itself a spanning two-cover, and its orientation is generally opposite the endpoint role needed by R509/R566. The theorem does not assert simultaneous descendants for different gaps and does not prove R24 by itself.

## Proof
Fix a gap i. For 1<=i<=m-2, R583 gives one of three alternatives. In its left alternative there are distinct x,y in X with (x,q_i,q_{i-1}) and (y,q_i,q_{i-1}) tight. Since (q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}) is a tight trimer inherited from Q, (q_i,q_{i-1}) is exactly its left reverse boundary dimer in the notation of R542. The witnesses x,y lie outside that trimer, so R542 applies and forces its capture/payment continuation. In the right alternative, use the tight trimer (q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}); its right reverse boundary dimer is (q_{i+2},q_{i+1}), carrying the two R583 witnesses, so R542 again applies. In the third R583 alternative, two distinct reverse-middle turns (q_{i+1},x,q_i),(q_{i+1},y,q_i) are tight; accepted R584 gives the displayed P4 ear. At i=0, R583 has only the right terminal packet and the reverse-middle alternative. The right packet is on (q_2,q_1), the reverse terminal dimer of (q_0,q_1,q_2), hence R542-ready; if absent, at least three reverse-middle turns occur and any two invoke R584. The right endpoint is symmetric. These cases exhaust every gap.
