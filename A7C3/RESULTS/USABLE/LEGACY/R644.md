# R644 — Pure-Q two-end branch forces two same-frame cross-end captures in X

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain accepted R630. In the branch (L,u,s) tight, fix any exact two-cover T of H-{L,u}; by R429 both rails are nontrivial. Then at least two distinct vertices x in X are capture-capable in this same T using s as the exact sign witness of S=(L,u). For each x, use the endpoint-star carrier K_x=(u,L,x). Every T-adjacency incident with x is a selected crossing from C={x}=V(K_x)-V(S) to E=V(H)-V(K_x). Call x blocked when every such adjacency contains s. Because x lies on a nontrivial T-rail it has at least one rail neighbor. If x is internal it has two distinct neighbors, so at least one incident crossing avoids the single vertex s; hence a blocked x must be a rail endpoint whose unique neighbor is s. Since s has degree at most two in the two-path forest T, at most two vertices of X are blocked. Thus at least two distinct x are nonblocked, and R527 captures each such x using a selected crossing avoiding s. Dually, in the branch (u,s,R) tight, every exact T of H-{s,R} contains at least two X vertices capture-capable with witness u through S=(s,R) and carriers K_x=(x,R,s). The source crossings coexist in T; capture descendants are alternative historical continuations.

## Proof
Assume first the R630 branch (L,u,s) tight. Then S=(L,u) is tail-signed by exact witness s. Fix any exact two-cover T of H-{L,u}; R429 supplies exactness and says both rails are nontrivial. For x in X, R594 gives K_x=(u,L,x) tight. Relative to signed support S inside K_x, the surviving carrier set in H-V(S) is C={x}; hence every T-adjacency incident with x is a selected C|E crossing in the sense of R527. Because the T-rail containing x is nontrivial, x has at least one rail neighbor. Call x blocked if every incident selected crossing contains s. If x is internal on its rail, it has two distinct neighbors, and at most one can equal s, so one incident crossing avoids s. Thus any blocked x is a rail endpoint and its unique neighbor is s. In the two-path forest T, vertex s has degree at most two, so it can be adjacent to at most two vertices of X. Therefore at most two of the four X vertices are blocked, and at least two distinct x are nonblocked. For each nonblocked x choose an incident selected crossing xy avoiding s. It is disjoint from S and avoids the exact sign witness. Apply R527 with carrier K_x, signed support S, witness s, cover T, and marker xy, orienting the marker to designate x as captured. This gives the claimed same-frame historical capture certificate for x. The source crossings for all such x coexist in T; their actualization descendants are separate choices. In the dual R630 branch (u,s,R) tight, S=(s,R) is head-signed by witness u. R429 fixes any exact non-singleton-rail cover T of H-{s,R}; R594 gives carriers K_x=(x,R,s). The identical blocked-endpoint argument with witness u shows at most two X vertices blocked, so at least two have an incident crossing avoiding u and are captured by R527. No R5 or R168 is used.
