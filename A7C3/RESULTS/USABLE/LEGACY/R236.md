# R236 — Global-longest full carrier insertion-obstruction clauses

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let K=(v_0,...,v_m) be a globally longest tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament and let w be exterior to K. For each gap i between v_i and v_{i+1}, inserting w into that gap cannot yield a tight path of order |K|+1. Hence among the newly required turns A_i=(v_{i-1},v_i,w) when i>=1, B_i=(v_i,w,v_{i+1}), and C_i=(w,v_{i+1},v_{i+2}) when i+2<=m, at least one present turn is bad. Equivalently, by boundary antisymmetry, at least one of the exact reverse turns (w,v_i,v_{i-1}), (v_{i+1},w,v_i), (v_{i+2},v_{i+1},w), with the same boundary omissions, is tight. Thus every exterior vertex carries a complete gap-indexed family of two-way endpoint clauses and three-way internal reverse-turn clauses along the full ordered carrier.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry; K is globally longest; w is outside K.

## Scope
Order-free global-longestness lemma. It extends terminal endpoint shielding from the two ends to every insertion gap of the retained carrier, but records clauses rather than choosing one tight mate in each gap.

## Applicability
Use when a proof needs carrier-position information missing from endpoint-only collision webs. The gap clauses can be combined with fixed exterior replacement supports, exact-cover crossings, or literal mate/candidate certificates to seek a carrier-aware forcing chain.

## Persistence
The ordered carrier K, exterior vertex w, and each finite disjunction of reverse-turn certificates are graph-intrinsic. A particular disjunct is not selected unless separately forced.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert which reverse turn is tight at any gap; does not by itself produce a longer carrier, a replacement path, a path-cover clause admissible to R173, or simultaneous selected turns. Physical Mate-Cycle forcing still requires its own literal candidate-cover hypotheses.

## Proof
Fix i. The proposed inserted ordering is (v_0,...,v_i,w,v_{i+1},...,v_m). Every old consecutive triple of K remains unchanged except those meeting the insertion site. These are exactly A_i when i>=1, B_i always, and C_i when i+2<=m. If all present changed turns were tight, the inserted ordering would be a tight path on |K|+1 vertices, contradicting global longestness. Therefore at least one present changed turn is bad. Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry makes its complete reversal tight, giving the displayed reverse-turn disjunction. The endpoint gaps have two changed turns; internal gaps have three.
