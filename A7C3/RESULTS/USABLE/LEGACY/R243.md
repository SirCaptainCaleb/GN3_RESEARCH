# R243 — Five-exterior longest-carrier Johnson sparsity and spectral ceiling

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order n in the five-exterior equality face, and let Ecal be the family of five-sets occurring as exteriors of global-longest tight paths. For every physical four-set S put x_S=|{E in Ecal:S subset E}|. Then (i) sum_S x_S=5|Ecal|; (ii) the Johnson-edge count inside Ecal is sum_S binom(x_S,2), because every pair of five-sets with intersection four has a unique common four-core; (iii) R192 local sparsity gives sum_S x_S(x_S-1)<= (n-5)|Ecal|, hence sum_S x_S^2<=n|Ecal|. Consequently Cauchy gives |Ecal| <= [n/(5(n-4))] binom(n,5). Equivalently, the same global ceiling previously obtained spectrally follows from the edge-disjoint clique decomposition J(n,5)=union_S K_{n-4}.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; accepted R192 local four-of-five replacement saturation, hence the accepted Johnson local-degree bound for Ecal.

## Scope
Arbitrary-order structural extremal theorem at the five-exterior floor. This is a four-core occupancy reformulation and proof of the R231 ceiling; no Johnson eigenvalues or association-scheme calculation is needed.

## Applicability
Use when global counting must remain physically localized. The variables x_S identify concrete overloaded four-cores and can be combined directly with R240 cross-replacement packets or R239/R241 capture machinery.

## Persistence
Ecal and every occupancy x_S are graph-intrinsic finite-set data. The identities and inequalities are purely combinatorial.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not supply a lower bound on |Ecal| or on any individual x_S, does not assert occupancy uniformity, and does not by itself close the equality face. It strengthens the proof architecture, not the numerical ceiling.

## Proof
Write N=|Ecal|. Each exterior E contains exactly five four-subsets, so summing incidences (S,E) gives sum_S x_S=5N. Two distinct five-sets are adjacent in J(n,5) exactly when they share four vertices, and that common four-set is unique. Therefore the induced Johnson-edge count on Ecal is e(Ecal)=sum_S binom(x_S,2). Accepted R192 local sparsity says every E in Ecal has at most n-5 neighbors in Ecal, so 2e(Ecal)<= (n-5)N. Hence sum_S x_S(x_S-1)<= (n-5)N. Adding sum_S x_S=5N gives sum_S x_S^2<=nN. There are binom(n,4) physical four-sets. By Cauchy, 25N^2=(sum_S x_S)^2<=binom(n,4) sum_S x_S^2<=binom(n,4)nN. Since N>0, N<=n binom(n,4)/25. Using binom(n,5)=binom(n,4)(n-4)/5 yields N<=[n/(5(n-4))]binom(n,5). This is exactly the former spectral ceiling, now with the occupancy profile retained.
