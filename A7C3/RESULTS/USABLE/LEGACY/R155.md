# R155 — Captured crossing / carrier-edge mirror reentry

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let A be a graph-intrinsic historical carrier path, let D={x,y} be a physical crossing support with x in A and y outside A, and retain the historical fact that x was captured using that physical crossing. For any historical carrier adjacency E={p,x} incident with x, there exists a later minimum three-cover in which E and D are the two boundary physical supports of their unique tight mirror trimer through x. The historical capture event at x remains valid event ancestry in that later certificate.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; A is a previously certified graph-intrinsic carrier path; D={x,y} is a previously certified physical crossing of A with x on A and y outside A; x has a valid historical capture certificate using D; E={p,x} is any physical adjacency of x on the historical carrier A.

## Scope
Ancestry-bearing specialization of incident-pair mirror currentization. Its bare two-support coexistence is subsumed by Physical-Star Exact Rank-Two Currentization, but the named historical capture event and carrier-edge identity are retained because later capture/fixed-turn consumers use them.

## Applicability
Use after a capture event when a downstream argument needs a proper current mirror trimer whose middle is the captured physical vertex and whose two boundary supports are the captured crossing and a chosen old carrier adjacency. The terminal-carrier-edge specialization gives a later trimer with the old terminal physical support and captured crossing as boundaries.

## Persistence
Physical support identities D,E and the fact of the historical capture event are graph-intrinsic/historical and remain valid. The later mirror trimer is current in a newly chosen minimum three-cover. The original selected crossing orientation, carrier-edge orientation, old carrier component, residual rails, and any current protection do not persist unless separately proved.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Capture is not upgraded to protection. The theorem does not preserve the original selected crossing orientation, marker orientation, carrier-edge orientation, old carrier as a current component, old residual rails, R33 same-frame concentration, endpoint exposure, or any unrelated selected state. It is not a same-representative transport theorem and does not by itself yield a final seam or two-cover.
