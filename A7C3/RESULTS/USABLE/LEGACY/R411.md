# R411 — Endpoint-universal pair deletion factorizes into endpoint-Hamiltonian rail supports modulo balanced pairs

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the endpoint-universal branch of accepted R393 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H. Fix any physical pair {a,c} and one exact two-cover F=P sqcup Q of W=H-{a,c}. Then either H contains a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair arising from a cross-state comparison with another exact cover of W, or the following fixed-support factorization holds. Every exact two-cover of W has the same unordered rail-support partition {V(P),V(Q)}. Moreover for every x in V(P) there exists a Hamilton tight path P_x on exactly V(P) having x as a physical endpoint, and for every y in V(Q) there exists a Hamilton tight path Q_y on exactly V(Q) having y as a physical endpoint. Consequently for every prescribed pair x in V(P), y in V(Q), the disjoint union P_x sqcup Q_y is an exact two-cover of W exposing x and y simultaneously. Thus the quiet endpoint-universal branch over one pair deletion is not an arbitrary family of covers: it is a product of two endpoint-Hamiltonian path families on fixed disjoint supports.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; accepted R393 and R410; {a,c} is any physical pair; F=P sqcup Q is any exact two-cover of H-{a,c}.

## Scope
Beyond-order-ten structural factorization of endpoint universality. No capture, first-contact, longest-path, or density hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use to replace endpoint-universal cover multiplicity by fixed-support Hamilton-path flexibility unless a balanced pair is already produced. Simultaneous cross-rail endpoint steering is then free by independent choice of the two supportwise Hamilton paths.

## Persistence
The fixed support partition is graph/deletion-relative in the no-balanced-pair branch. Each chosen Hamilton path P_x or Q_y is representative-relative. Different endpoint choices are alternative and may be combined only across the two disjoint fixed supports, where tightness is independent.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe the opposite endpoint or terminal neighbor of P_x or Q_y. Does not assert Hamilton-connectedness, path reversal, or simultaneous realization of two prescribed endpoints on the same support. Does not by itself close the endpoint-universal branch.

## Proof
Let W=H-{a,c} and fix F=P sqcup Q. Assume no balanced pair arises from cross-state comparison with another exact two-cover of W. By R410 every exact two-cover of W then has unordered support partition {V(P),V(Q)}. Fix x in V(P). Endpoint universality R393 gives an exact two-cover F_x of W in which x is a rail endpoint. Since the support partition of F_x equals that of F, the path of F_x containing x is supported on V(P), hence is a Hamilton tight path P_x on H[V(P)] with endpoint x. The same argument gives Q_y for every y in V(Q). Finally P_x and Q_y are tight paths on disjoint fixed supports whose union is W, so for any prescribed x,y their disjoint union is itself a literal exact two-cover of W exposing both endpoints simultaneously. If any exposing cover changes support partition, R410 gives the alternative balanced pair.
