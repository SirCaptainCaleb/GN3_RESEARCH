## Proof
Fix J=(v,u,x,s,d) and T. LEFT. Let {v,h} be a T-selected endpoint-exterior state, h in E. By R3 exactly one of (h,v,u) and (u,v,h) is tight. If (h,v,u) is tight, concatenate it with the three turns of J to obtain the literal tight P6 (h,v,u,x,s,d). Otherwise C=(u,v,h) is tight. Apply accepted R518 to C with fourth vertex d. If R518 returns a Hamilton P4, retain it. Otherwise its no-P4 reverse-contact conclusion (b,a,y) for (a,b,c,y)=(u,v,h,d) is exactly (v,u,d), so K=(v,u,d) is tight. Apply R518 to K with fourth vertex L. If a Hamilton P4 occurs, retain it. Otherwise the forced reverse contact (b,a,y) for (a,b,c,y)=(v,u,d,L) is (u,v,L), so K_Q=(u,v,L) is tight. Along K_Q the consecutive Q contacts v=q_2 then L=q_0 occur in decreasing Q-order and are nonadjacent on Q. Accepted R435 applied to Q and K_Q therefore yields an explicit reverse old state, reverse trimer/contact, or vertex-simple tight cycle. RIGHT. Let {d,h} be a selected endpoint-exterior state. R3 gives either (s,d,h), yielding P6 (v,u,x,s,d,h), or the reverse contact (h,d,s). Apply R518 to (h,d,s) with fourth vertex v. In the no-P4 branch the forced contact (y,c,b) is (v,s,d), giving K=(v,s,d). Apply R518 to K with fourth vertex R. In the no-P4 branch the forced contact (y,c,b) is (R,d,s), whose consecutive Q contacts R=q_m then d=q_{m-2} occur in reverse nonadjacent Q-order; R435 fires. Accepted R776 says that in DUAL-EXTERIOR both a left and right source state occur in the same T, while in ENDPOINT-CHORD at least one occurs and the chord {v,d} is selected. Applying the preceding endpoint compilation proves the theorem.
