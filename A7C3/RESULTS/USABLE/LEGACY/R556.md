# R556 — Unique two-bad seam cell forces the aligned gate packet minus its two bad turns

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the hard transitive R24 order-three frame, assume exactly two of the six P-side seams are bad. By R555 these are cbq_0 and q_mba. Then the endpoint five-cells have the following full gate polarities. At q_0: the M_L edge az is fully outgoing, the M_S edge bz is fully outgoing, and the complementary M_S edge ac is fully incoming. At q_m: the M_R edge cz is fully incoming, the M_S edge bz is fully incoming, and the complementary M_S edge ac is fully outgoing. Thus the exact two-bad cell contains the aligned R543/R544 gate packet except precisely for the two seam turns cbq_0 and q_mba. In particular q_0az,q_0za,z b? are certified as appropriate, and czq_m,zcq_m are certified on the right.

## Hypotheses
Hard transitive R24 frame and exactly two bad P-side seams.

## Scope
The unique minimum two-bad six-seam residue.

## Applicability
Use to actualize the two forced collision packets with the short az and cz crossing markers while retaining exact endpoint roles.

## Persistence
All full-gate turns are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims

## Proof
By R555 the unique two-bad word is bcq_0 good, cbq_0 bad, acq_0 good on the left and q_mab good, q_mac good, q_mba bad on the right.

At d=q_0, the tight turn acq_0 has complete reverse q_0ca bad by R3. Hence the M_S edge ac cannot be fully outgoing from q_0. R534 says the two M_S edges have opposite full polarities, so ac is fully incoming and its complementary M_S edge bz is fully outgoing. Next bcq_0 is tight, so its reverse q_0cb is bad. Thus the M_L edge bc cannot be fully outgoing from q_0. R549 guarantees that some M_L edge is fully outgoing, and the only other M_L edge is az. Hence az is fully outgoing.

At d=q_m, q_mac is tight, so its reverse caq_m is bad. Thus ac cannot be fully incoming to q_m. By R534, ac is fully outgoing and bz is fully incoming. Also q_mab is tight, so its reverse baq_m is bad. Therefore the M_R edge ab cannot be fully incoming to q_m. R549 guarantees a fully incoming M_R edge, hence the complementary top edge cz is fully incoming.

These are exactly the asserted gate polarities.
