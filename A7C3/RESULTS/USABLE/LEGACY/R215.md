# R215 — Global-longest terminal complete exterior-witness collision web

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let K be a proper globally longest tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament, with exterior E of size r. Fix either terminal physical dimer D of K in the reverse orientation supplied by R199. Then every e in E gives a signed certificate on the same oriented physical support D with the same polarity. Consequently every unordered pair of distinct exterior witnesses {e,f} enters the accepted same-polarity two-extension collision package of R38 on D. Thus each terminal dimer carries a complete collision web indexed by the binomial(r,2) witness pairs. In particular, in the six-exterior O3 face each terminal dimer carries fifteen such two-witness collision certificates.

## Hypotheses
K is a proper globally longest tight path; E is its exterior; one terminal physical dimer is fixed.

## Scope
Order-free terminal multi-witness interaction corollary. No smallest-counterexample minimality beyond any downstream use of the collision package, and no exterior-slack specialization is needed for the general statement.

## Applicability
Use whenever terminal adaptive-capture or collision machinery needs two distinct exterior witnesses. Any two may be chosen on either terminal dimer, so witness avoidance constraints can be satisfied without rebuilding signs.

## Persistence
The physical terminal dimer, all exterior witness identities, tested sign orders, and pairwise collision certificates are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert simultaneous current collision descendants, does not say different witness-pair collision packages are independent or compatible, and does not itself force capture, payment, exterior descent, or a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
Fix the left terminal dimer; the right end is identical. R199 states that for every e in E the same reverse-oriented terminal dimer is signed with the same polarity by e. Let e and f be distinct exterior vertices. These are therefore two signed certificates on the same physical support, with distinct witnesses and equal polarity. By the same-polarity branch of R38, they enter the accepted two-extension collision package. Since e,f were arbitrary, this holds for every unordered pair of exterior witnesses, giving a complete collision web of size binomial(r,2).
