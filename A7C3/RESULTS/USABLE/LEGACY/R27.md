**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

Move zero, one, or two short-rail boundary vertices independently at each end. The resulting nine coordinates form a sign-controlled family of exact minimum three-covers whenever their displayed turns have the required signs. Each step is a neutral endpoint transfer with zero cycle debt.

## Scope
The lattice records exactly what each depth purchases:

- one transfer at an end currentizes the corresponding canonical splice;
- the second transfer at that end currentizes the corresponding reverse source-boundary state;
- the middle coordinate can preserve the prescribed singleton as an internal vertex;
- the deepest coordinate trades that internal-singleton state for deeper same-rail endpoint-absorption resources.

Canonical depth-two maximality is only relative to this audited lattice and its cap family. It is not global maximality among all exact covers.
