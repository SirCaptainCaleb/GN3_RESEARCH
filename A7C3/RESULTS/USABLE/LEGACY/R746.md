# R746 — Two-sided first-inward portals share an X anchor or split along one matching layer

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame and the fixed left/right exact pair-deletion covers and source crossings of pending R744. Let A_L be the set of vertices x in X for which the fixed cover T_L of H-{u,v} contains an R744 witness-avoiding carrier/exterior crossing, so x is capture/contact-capable through the genuine carrier edge {u,x}. Let A_R be defined dually in the fixed cover T_R of H-{d,s}, with portal edge {x,s}. Then |A_L|>=2 and |A_R|>=2, and exactly one of the following structural alternatives holds.

(COMMON) A_L intersect A_R is nonempty. Hence some named x in X has both a left protected-contact portal through {u,x} and a right protected-contact portal through {x,s}, with the two R744 source covers, selected crossings, avoiding witnesses q_3 and q_{m-3}, and capture ancestries retained. The later left and right continuations are separate choices.

(MATCHING-SPLIT) A_L intersect A_R is empty. Then necessarily |A_L|=|A_R|=2 and A_R=X\A_L. Consequently the unordered bipartition {A_L,A_R} is exactly one of the three perfect-matching layers of the transitive four-cell: M_R={{a,b},{c,z}}, M_S={{a,c},{b,z}}, or M_L={{a,z},{b,c}}. Which complementary edge is left-capable and which is right-capable remains part of the certificate.

Thus failure of a common two-ended X anchor is not an unstructured residue: all left/right capture portals separate along one named matching layer of X.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621/R723 frame. Use the fixed-cover portal sets supplied by pending R744 and the accepted matching-height labeling of X.

## Scope
Noncircular two-ended compression of the pending R744 direct-capture route. Pure four-element set pigeonhole after R744; no payment,R436,Reverse Ear,five-cell classification,R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R358,or computation.

## Applicability
After constructing both R744 fixed-cover portal sets, test their intersection before following any capture. In COMMON retain the same X anchor and both source ledgers. In MATCHING-SPLIT retain the exact matching layer and left/right assignment; downstream work should use the corresponding crossed/outer gate geometry rather than treating the four portals independently.

## Persistence
Retain T_L,T_R, the full good-anchor sets A_L,A_R, for every retained anchor its selected crossing and avoiding Q witness, and in MATCHING-SPLIT the named matching layer and side assignment.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert left and right capture descendants coexist, that the common anchor closes H, or that a matching-split branch is impossible. The sets are relative to the fixed exact covers and selected R508 crossings used in R744. Does not identify which matching layer occurs.

## Proof
By R744, at most two of the four left carrier singletons can be blocked by q_3 in T_L, so |A_L|>=2; dually |A_R|>=2. If A_L intersect A_R is nonempty, any x in the intersection carries the two displayed source-localized portals, proving COMMON. Otherwise the two subsets are disjoint. Since X has four vertices and both subsets have size at least two, each has size exactly two and their union is X. Every complementary pair partition of {a,b,c,z} is uniquely one of {ab|cz}, {ac|bz}, or {az|bc}, which are exactly the matching layers M_R,M_S,M_L in the accepted frame. This proves MATCHING-SPLIT.
