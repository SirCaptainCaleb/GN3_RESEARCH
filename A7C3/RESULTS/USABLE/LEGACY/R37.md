# R37 — Witness-adaptive capture

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

Suppose one physical support has several admissible signed incarnations with different witnesses. In one exact support-deletion cover, any selected state disjoint from the support is capture-capable whenever at least one admissible witness is outside that selected pair. Choose such a witness, choose either physical marker endpoint to capture, and apply the same-frame theorem. This bypasses concentration for that selected state.

## Scope
For an internal adjacent dimer of a retained path, the two neighboring path vertices are opposite natural sign witnesses. Every selected carrier/exterior crossing meets at most one of them. Hence every such crossing is individually capture-capable in the same deletion frame. If its carrier-side endpoint is neither witness, both polarities give separate capture certificates through the same selected physical crossing.

The different certificates are simultaneous graph-intrinsic/historical facts, not simultaneous payment descendants.

## Proof
Let S be the physical signed support and let xy be the selected state in the exact S-deletion cover. By hypothesis there is at least one admissible signed incarnation of S whose witness w lies outside {x,y}. Choose that incarnation. The selected state xy is disjoint from S because it lies in the S-deletion cover, and it avoids w by choice. Therefore the capture/refund branch of accepted R527 applies in this exact frame. Use the physical dimer {x,y} as the actualizing marker. Since a two-vertex path is tight in either order, choose the marker orientation so that whichever of x or y is prescribed occupies the marker endpoint recorded as the new signed anchor. R527 then gives a valid historical capture certificate for that chosen physical endpoint, while retaining the selected crossing support, chosen witness and actualization orientation as ancestry. This proves witness-adaptive capture for the selected state. If several witnesses are admissible, the choice can be made separately for each selected state; no simultaneous payment descendants are asserted. For an internal adjacent dimer of a retained path, its two natural neighboring witnesses are distinct, and a two-vertex selected carrier/exterior crossing can meet at most one of them, so one is always available and the same argument applies crossing by crossing.
