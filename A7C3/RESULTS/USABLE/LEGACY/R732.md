# R732 — Exterior endpoint test for a tight P4 gives a P5 or a direct terminal-dimer pair

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament, let K=(k_0,k_1,k_2,k_3) be a vertex-simple tight P4, and let y be a vertex outside V(K). Then exactly the following extension test is available. If (y,k_0,k_1) is tight, then (y,k_0,k_1,k_2,k_3) is a tight P5. If (k_2,k_3,y) is tight, then (k_0,k_1,k_2,k_3,y) is a tight P5. If both endpoint attachments are bad, boundary antisymmetry forces (k_1,k_0,y) and (y,k_3,k_2) tight. Hence the reversed initial dimer D_L=(k_1,k_0) is tail-signed by witness y and the reversed terminal dimer D_R=(k_3,k_2) is head-signed by the same witness y. Their physical supports are disjoint, so they form a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair of total support mass four. Thus a tight P4 can fail both one-vertex endpoint extensions only by exposing an explicit opposite-polarity pair on its two terminal physical dimers. In a hypothetical smallest counterexample, accepted R514 may additionally descend this mass-four pair to a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); K is a vertex-simple tight P4; y is outside V(K). Smallest-counterexample minimality is needed only for the optional R514 descent sentence.

## Scope
Order-free P4 consumer. No R24 frame, deletion exactness, capture, payment, longest-path extremality, or cover comparison is used for the local P5-or-pair conclusion.

## Proof
The two positive attachment statements are immediate concatenations with the two certified turns of K. Assume both attachments are bad. By R3, badness of (y,k_0,k_1) gives its exact reversal (k_1,k_0,y) tight, so the tested dimer (k_1,k_0) is tail-signed by witness y. Badness of (k_2,k_3,y) gives its exact reversal (y,k_3,k_2) tight, so the tested dimer (k_3,k_2) is head-signed by y. The physical supports {k_0,k_1} and {k_2,k_3} are disjoint because K is vertex-simple. They therefore form a direct balanced opposite-sign pair; in the smallest-counterexample specialization their total support size is four, so R514 gives the optional descent.
