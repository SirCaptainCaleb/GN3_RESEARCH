# R609 — Minimum R24 cell forces failure of at least one long-rail wrap seam

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the exact-two-bad transitive R24 cell with Q=(L=q_0,q_1,...,q_{m-1},R=q_m), m>=2. Put alpha=(R,L,q_1) and beta=(q_{m-1},R,L), the two wrap seams of Q. Then at least one of alpha,beta is bad. Hence Q is never in the double-wrap branch of R579. More precisely, at least one of the following three Hamilton P5 absorbers is tight: K_a=(c,z,b,R,L) on {L,R} union (X-{a}); K_b=(c,R,L,a,z) on {L,R} union (X-{b}); K_c=(R,L,b,z,a) on {L,R} union (X-{c}). If K_a is tight, R540 forces alpha bad. If K_b or K_c is tight, R540 forces beta bad.

## Hypotheses
Exact-two-bad R24 frame R556; m>=2; X union {L,R} is P6-free; for each x in {a,b,c}, H-(X-{x})=Q sqcup (x) is an exact two-cover (otherwise a Hamilton residual together with a tight trimer on X-{x} two-covers H); R540 and R3.

## Scope
Specialized minimum R24 wrap lemma. No computation, no R5/four-deletion exactness, no R168/R169/R224.

## Applicability
Use before the R579 wrap tetrachotomy. It removes the double-wrap branch and leaves only SW1, SW2, or DF.

## Persistence
Retain whichever explicit K_a,K_b,K_c is tight, its omitted singleton, the associated R540 one-hole spanning proposal, and the exact bad wrap seam/mate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not determine which wrap seam fails and does not rule out either single-wrap branch or the double-fail branch.

## Proof
The matching-height cell gives czb and bza tight, while R556 gives zbR,Lbz,Rca,caL,Laz tight. Thus K_a=(c,z,b,R,L) is tight exactly when bRL is tight, and K_c=(R,L,b,z,a) is tight exactly when RLb is tight. Suppose both K_a and K_c fail. Then bRL and RLb are bad, so R3 gives LRb and bLR tight. The six-set X union {L,R} is P6-free. In the candidate (b,L,R,c,a,z), the turns bLR,Rca,caz are tight; therefore LRc is bad and cRL is tight. In the candidate (z,c,a,L,R,b), the turns zca,caL,LRb are tight; therefore aLR is bad and RLa is tight. Hence K_b=(c,R,L,a,z) is tight, using cRL,RLa,Laz. So at least one of K_a,K_b,K_c is tight. For each omitted x, H-(X-{x})=Q sqcup (x) is an exact two-cover. Apply R540 to the corresponding K. In K_a, L is in terminal position 4 with predecessor R, so the one-hole left endpoint cut has unique hole alpha=(R,L,q_1); alpha is bad. In K_b and K_c, R is in position 1 or 0 with successor L, so the one-hole right endpoint cut has unique hole beta=(q_{m-1},R,L); beta is bad. Therefore at least one wrap seam fails, excluding double wrap.
