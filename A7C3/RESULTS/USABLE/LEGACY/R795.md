# R795 — The R789 shortcut cover forces an outer-to-deep transition in the same four-Q deletion fiber

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-order-ten R789 SHORTCUT-CUT branch with m>=6. Write L=q_0, u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}, R=q_m, D={u,v,d,s}, M=Q[3,m-3], O=X union {L,R}, and let T be the exact two-cover of H-D supplied in R789. R789 forces T to select a state from {L,R} into X union M. Then either (DEEP-END) T selects a state between {L,R} and M, or (TWO-STAGE) T selects both an endpoint-X state between {L,R} and X and an X-middle state between X and M. Equivalently, every R789 SHORTCUT-CUT cover has a selected O|M transition; if that transition is not incident with L or R, it is incident with X, while the original R789 endpoint crossing remains simultaneously selected in the same cover.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R621/R775/R789 frame in the SHORTCUT-CUT branch, m>=6, and pc(H)>2. Use only the exact two-cover T from R789 and the original tight rail Q.

## Scope
Noncircular source-specific strengthening of accepted R789. Same exact four-Q deletion cover throughout. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,R358,R508 beyond its already-built R789 output, capture, payment, floor steering, or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately after R789 SHORTCUT-CUT. If its endpoint crossing already lands in Q[3,m-3], retain DEEP-END. Otherwise retain the endpoint-X state and, in the same exact cover, a second selected state from X into Q[3,m-3]. This replaces the anonymous X-ENDPOINT-CROSSING residue by a same-cover two-stage bridge to the deep Q interval.

## Persistence
Retain D, the exact two-cover T of H-D, the physical partition O=X union {L,R} and M=Q[3,m-3], the original R789 endpoint crossing, and a selected O|M transition. In TWO-STAGE retain both selected states simultaneously in T, with their literal directions and endpoints.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not determine which x in X or which q_i in M realizes the X-middle transition, whether the endpoint-X and X-middle states lie on the same T rail, or whether either state alone closes H. Does not claim an R176/capture/payment descendant. The conclusion is only simultaneous selected-state geometry in the one fixed R789 deletion cover.

## Proof
Let D={u,v,d,s}, M=Q[3,m-3], and O=X union {L,R}. Since m>=6, M is nonempty, and O is nonempty. The exact R789 cover T is a literal two-cover of H-D, whose vertex set is the disjoint union O union M. R789 supplies a selected T-state with one endpoint in {L,R} and the other in X union M. If its second endpoint lies in M, DEEP-END holds. Suppose instead that the R789 state joins {L,R} to X. Assume for contradiction that T selects no state between X and M. By the standing supposition it also selects no state between {L,R} and M. Hence T has no selected state crossing the partition O|M at all. Every T rail is therefore contained wholly in O or wholly in M. Because T has exactly two rails and both O and M are nonempty, one rail must contain all vertices of O and the other all vertices of M. In particular O has a literal Hamilton tight path K_O, namely its T rail. Independently, the original Q subpath Q[1,m-1]=(u,v,q_3,...,q_{m-3},d,s) is a tight path and is vertex-disjoint from O. Its support is exactly D union M. Thus K_O and Q[1,m-1] are two disjoint tight paths whose supports partition V(H), giving a spanning two-cover of H, contrary to pc(H)>2. Therefore T must select an X|M state. The original endpoint-X state and this X-middle state are both selected in the same fixed T, proving TWO-STAGE. The two alternatives are exhaustive.
