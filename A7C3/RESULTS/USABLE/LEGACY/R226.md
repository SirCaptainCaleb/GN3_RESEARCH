# R226 — R171 dimer-swallow 2+3 corner re-enters the five-exterior equality face

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let H=J sqcup S sqcup T be a terminal literal R171 frame in which S is the completely swallowed historical pair support. Suppose the local sizes are one of (|S|,|J|)=(2,3),(3,2),(3,3). Then the untouched opposite support T canonically re-enters the accepted exterior gateway program. In the first two cases T has exactly five exterior vertices E=S union J, hence is globally longest by R23; if E is Hamiltonian there is a spanning two-cover, and otherwise R192 gives four-of-five replacement saturation for every carrier vertex of T. In the (3,3) case T has exactly six exterior vertices E=S union J, and accepted R196 supplies at least four omissions e for which E-{e} is a tight P5 and H=T sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} is a literal singleton-root three-cover, together with the R196 puncture-pair consequences. Thus none of the 2+3, 3+2, or 3+3 bounded-swallow size corners is a new isolated terminal species.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; H=J sqcup S sqcup T is a literal terminal R171 frame after complete swallow of pair support S; the ordered size pair (|S|,|J|) is one of (2,3),(3,2),(3,3).

## Scope
Order-free recycling theorem from the finite R171 small-swallow corner back to accepted five- and six-exterior machinery. No anchored-capacity parent is assumed.

## Applicability
Use whenever bounded swallow has already been compressed to the 2+3 / 3+(2-or-3) corner. The theorem sends every such case back to R192 or R196 rather than opening bespoke small-support cleanup.

## Persistence
The literal terminal supports J,S,T and E=S union J are physical in the R171 frame. Five-exterior global-longestness and R192 replacement supports are graph-intrinsic. In the six-exterior case the R196 singleton roots are separate literal representatives for each good omission.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close the non-Hamiltonian five-exterior equality face or the six-exterior four-root fan. Does not assert complementary carrier swaps, exterior-size descent, or simultaneous currentness across different R196 roots.

## Proof
Because H=J sqcup S sqcup T is a literal vertex-disjoint spanning frame, E:=V(H)\\V(T)=V(J) union V(S). If (|S|,|J|) is (2,3) or (3,2), then |E|=5. The path T is proper and has exactly five exterior vertices, so accepted R23 makes T globally longest. If E supports a tight P5 Q, then T sqcup Q is a spanning two-cover. Otherwise E is non-Hamiltonian and accepted R192 applies to the carrier T and exterior E, giving for every t in V(T) at least four successful five-set replacements (E-{z}) union {t}. If instead (|S|,|J|)=(3,3), then |E|=6. Accepted R196 applies to the tight path T with six-vertex exterior E: at least four e in E have a tight P5 Q_e on E-{e}, giving literal spanning three-covers H=T sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} and the associated puncture balanced-pair fan. These exhaust the listed tiny corners.
