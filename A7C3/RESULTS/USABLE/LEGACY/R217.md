# R217 — Anchored-capacity one-residual branch enters literal signed-pair discharge

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let (alpha;P),(T;beta) be an anchored-capacity realization of a balanced opposite-sign pair in the sense of R179/R180. If the ordinary reservoir X=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T) union {alpha,beta}) has exactly one vertex x, then W=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T)) has order between one and three and supports a tight path J. Hence H=J sqcup P sqcup T is a literal spanning three-cover whose P,T components are the historical opposite-signed pair, so R171 applies. Therefore the one-residual branch yields either a spanning two-cover, strict historical pair-mass descent, or bounded support swallow; it is not an inert capacity exception.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; (alpha;P),(T;beta) is a balanced opposite-sign pair realized at anchored capacity; the ordinary reservoir X of R180 has size one.

## Scope
Order-free composition of anchored-capacity one-residual geometry with literal signed-pair discharge. Witnesses may coincide or lie in the opposite support; no disjoint-witness assumption is needed.

## Applicability
Use in the |X|=1 branch of R180. It removes the former near-spanning dead end by converting the at-most-three vertices outside the two capacity supports into the literal third path required by R171.

## Persistence
The capacity supports and their historical signs are the R180 data. The third path J is a proof/current frame on the physical leftover vertices. R171 outputs retain only the ancestry guaranteed by that theorem.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim the R171 outputs individually close the conjecture. Does not make pair mass globally monotone after unrelated resets. Does not require alpha and beta distinct or outside the opposite support.

## Proof
Let X={x}. Put W=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T)). By the definition X=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T) union {alpha,beta}), every vertex of W belongs to {x,alpha,beta}; moreover x belongs to W, so 1<=|W|<=3. If |W|=1, the singleton is a tight path. If |W|=2, either ordering is a tight two-vertex path because there is no three-turn constraint. If |W|=3, R8 supplies a tight trimer order on W. Thus in all cases W supports a nonempty tight path J. Since P,T are vertex-disjoint tight supports and W is exactly their physical complement, H=J sqcup P sqcup T is a literal spanning three-cover. The historical balanced pair gives opposite signs (alpha;P),(T;beta), so all hypotheses of R171 are met. R171 yields a spanning two-cover, strict historical pair-mass descent, or bounded support swallow. Therefore R180 branch (iii) is not inert once R171 is available.
