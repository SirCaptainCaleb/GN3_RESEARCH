# R375 — Johnson t=2 equality has a universal paired-design normal form

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let B be a family of k-subsets of an r-element ground set, with r>=k+1, such that every (k+1)-set contains at most two members of B. Suppose equality holds in the R326 density ceiling, so |B|/binom(r,k)=r/[k(r-k+1)]. Then k divides r. Every (k-1)-subset S lies in exactly r/k members of B, so B is a (k-1)-(r,k,r/k) design at the core-incidence level. Every (k+1)-set containing at least one member of B contains exactly two. Equivalently, for every F in B and every x outside F there is a unique f in F such that (F\\{f}) union {x} also lies in B. Hence the selected family is locally paired in every occupied union clique and has induced Johnson degree exactly r-k at every selected vertex.

## Hypotheses
Finite k-uniform family; local (k+1)-occupancy at most two; equality in R326.

## Scope
Pure finite-set equality theorem, generalizing the R331 paired four-design structure.

## Applicability
Use when equality or near-equality in a higher-k Johnson local-degree bound is reached.

## Persistence
The design incidences, unique swaps, and Johnson adjacencies are intrinsic finite-set data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert such equality families exist for arbitrary k,r and does not classify them globally.

## Proof
At equality in R326 with t=2, every (k-1)-core occupancy is constant and every selected k-set F has the maximum possible induced Johnson degree r-k, because each of its r-k containing (k+1)-union cliques contributes at most one selected neighbor. The constant core occupancy is mu=k|B|/binom(r,k-1). Substituting |B|=[r/(k(r-k+1))]binom(r,k) and binom(r,k)/binom(r,k-1)=(r-k+1)/k gives mu=r/k, so k divides r and B is a (k-1)-(r,k,r/k) design at the incidence level. For any F in B and x outside F, U=F union {x} contains F. Maximal degree forces the union clique on U to contain one further selected k-set, and the local cap two makes it unique; it must be F-f+x for a unique f in F. Therefore every occupied (k+1)-set has exactly two selected members and every F has induced Johnson degree r-k.
