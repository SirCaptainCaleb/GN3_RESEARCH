# R377 — Johnson paired-design equality has a full lower-incidence divisibility tower

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the t=2 equality setting of R375: B is a k-uniform family on an r-element ground set attaining the R326 ceiling, hence a (k-1)-(r,k,r/k) design. Then for every s with 0<=s<=k-1, every fixed s-subset S lies in the same number lambda_s of members of B, namely lambda_s=(r/k)*binom(r-s,k-1-s)/(k-s). In particular every displayed lambda_s must be an integer. Thus equality imposes a nested arithmetic divisibility tower at all lower incidence levels, not merely k|r. The k=5 arithmetic fences R346/R353/R355 arise as special integrality consequences of this tower together with additional local pairing/parity information.

## Hypotheses
Finite k-uniform family in the equality case of R375.

## Scope
Pure finite-design corollary.

## Applicability
Use to rule out equality arithmetically at higher k or to compute exact link sizes before applying local structural consumers.

## Persistence
All incidence counts are intrinsic finite-set data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the integrality conditions are sufficient for existence and does not incorporate extra parity constraints coming from local pairing beyond the design axioms.

## Proof
R375 makes B a (k-1)-(r,k,r/k) design. Fix an s-set S, 0<=s<=k-1. Count pairs (F,T) where F in B contains S and T is a (k-1)-subset with S subseteq T subseteq F. Each block F containing S contributes binom(k-s,k-1-s)=k-s choices of T. On the other hand there are binom(r-s,k-1-s) choices of T, each lying in exactly r/k blocks. Hence lambda_s(k-s)=(r/k)binom(r-s,k-1-s), giving the formula. Integrality follows because lambda_s counts blocks.
