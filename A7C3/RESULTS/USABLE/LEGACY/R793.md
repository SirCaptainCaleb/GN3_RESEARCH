# R793 — The four-Q shortcut deletion has a canonical three-cell deficit: only an aligned one-sided gate-to-middle weave survives at transition minimum

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain accepted R789 in SHORTCUT-CUT, in the accepted crossed R621 frame with m>=6. Put D={u,v,d,s}, M=Q[3,m-3], W=H-D, and let T# be the retained exact two-cover of W. Normalize the crossed middle gate as M_S={{a,c},{b,z}} with {a,c} fully outgoing from L and {b,z} fully incoming to R. Then W has the literal three-path reference cover G_L=(L,a,c), G_M=M, G_R=(b,z,R). For the partition into their vertex sets, every exact T# satisfies t_G(T#)=1+sigma_G(T#)>=1, with equality iff each G-cell occurs as one contiguous T# block.

Assume T# has no selected state from L or R into M. Apply R508 to the left and right truncations (L,u,v,d,s) and (u,v,d,s,R), and to J# itself, as in the proof below. Then, unless H already has a spanning two-cover, the following sharpened alternative holds. (MULTI) t_G(T#)>=2. (MIN) t_G(T#)=1, and necessarily: {L,R} is not selected; T# has selected internal states {L,x_L} and {R,x_R} with x_L in {a,c} and x_R in {b,z}; the unique intercell transition joins G_M to exactly one of G_L,G_R; the other outer gate cell is an entire isolated T# rail; and the mixed rail consists of one complete outer gate block concatenated with the complete middle block M in one of the two possible block orders. In particular the unique transition is an X-to-M or M-to-X state, never endpoint-to-M.

Equivalently, every LINK branch, every SPLIT branch in which L meets {b,z} or R meets {a,c}, and every outer-to-outer selected transition forces either a spanning two-cover when it is the unique intercell transition or the MULTI regime with at least two simultaneous source-labelled intercell transitions.

## Proof
Because {a,c} is fully outgoing from L in accepted R621, (L,a,c) is tight. Because {b,z} is fully incoming to R, (b,z,R) is tight. Since m>=6, M=Q[3,m-3] is a nonempty tight subpath. Their vertex sets are pairwise disjoint and partition W=H-{u,v,d,s}, so G_L sqcup G_M sqcup G_R is a literal three-path cover of W. Each class has intrinsic path-cover number one. Apply accepted R7 to this fixed three-class partition and the exact two-cover T#: t_G(T#)=3-2+sigma_G(T#)=1+sigma_G(T#)>=1. Equality holds exactly when every class restriction is one contiguous T# block.

The R789 shortcut J#=(L,u,v,d,s,R) is tight. Its left truncation P_L=(L,u,v,d,s) has vertex set D union {L}, so accepted R508 on the same deletion D and same T# forces a selected state incident with L whose other endpoint lies in {R} union X union M. Dually P_R=(u,v,d,s,R) forces such a state at R. R508 on J# with S={L,R} forces a selected state from {L,R} into X union M. Under the present no-endpoint-to-M assumption, the last state is endpoint-to-X; if {L,R} is absent, the singleton conclusions likewise give {L,x_L} and {R,x_R} with x_L,x_R in X.

Suppose t_G(T#)=1. Then sigma=0, so each G-cell is a single contiguous T# block. Contracting the three blocks in two T# rails gives one edge and one isolated block. If the unique intercell edge joins G_L to G_R, one T# rail is the concatenation of a Hamilton path on V(G_L) and a Hamilton path on V(G_R), hence is a tight Hamilton P6 on their union X union {L,R}; the other T# rail is exactly G_M. But independently the original Q supplies the tight interior path Q[1,m-1] on all Q vertices except L,R. Therefore the outer Hamilton P6 together with Q[1,m-1] is a literal spanning two-path cover of H, contradiction. Thus, absent closure, the unique intercell edge is G_L-G_M or G_M-G_R, and the opposite outer gate cell is the isolated T# rail.

In particular {L,R} cannot be selected in this t=1 no-closure branch, because it is itself a G_L-G_R intercell state. Hence the singleton R508 conclusions give selected {L,x_L} and {R,x_R}. If x_L lay in {b,z}, then {L,x_L} would be a G_L-G_R intercell state, again forcing the unique intercell edge to be outer-outer and hence closure. Therefore x_L lies in {a,c}. Similarly x_R lies in {b,z}. These endpoint-X states are internal to their respective contiguous outer gate blocks. Since the unique intercell edge touches M and endpoint-to-M states were excluded, its outer endpoint is an X vertex. The mixed T# rail is therefore one complete outer gate block concatenated with the complete M block, while the other outer gate block is the isolated rail. This is MIN.

If t_G(T#)>=2 we are in MULTI. The final equivalent formulation follows because LINK contains the outer-outer state {L,R}, and any SPLIT with L meeting {b,z} or R meeting {a,c} contains an outer-outer intercell state; such a state can be unique only in the already-closed t=1 case. This proves the theorem.
