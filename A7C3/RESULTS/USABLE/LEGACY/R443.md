# R443 — Linear Internal-Middle Interaction Reservoir

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order n, fix distinct outer vertices x,z, and fix one exact two-cover H-{x,z}=U sqcup V with both rails nontrivial. Suppose an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor is available. Then the fixed frame U sqcup V has exactly n-6 internal rail vertices. For every internal vertex b, boundary antisymmetry uniquely orients the outer pair to a proper turn J_b=(a,b,c) with {a,c}={x,z}. There is an alternative chosen continuation which aligns the floor to {a,c} and then applies Internal Middle Immediate Pair Interaction in the same fixed U sqcup V frame, yielding a spanning two-cover or a zero-quiet historical pair-interaction packet I_b. Thus one fixed pair-deletion frame supports exactly n-6 internal-middle interaction packets indexed by its internal vertices. The turn orientation may vary with b.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong counterexample; x,z are distinct; H-{x,z}=U sqcup V is one fixed exact two-cover with two nontrivial rails; an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor is available. Each b considered is internal on U or V.

## Scope
Strong Level-(1), arbitrary order. Same-frame packet-family corollary. The packets are alternative chosen continuations indexed by internal vertices of one fixed deletion cover; current descendants for different b are not asserted to coexist.

## Applicability
Use when one wants to exploit many internal middles without changing the deleted outer pair or the base rails. In the historical beyond-order-nine setting n>=10, the reservoir has at least four indices.

## Persistence
The unordered deleted pair {x,z}, the fixed rails U,V, each packet index b, its turn orientation, selected predecessor/successor, endpoint-dimer birth certificates, and its R442 interaction output are retained as packet-specific historical data. Different packet continuations need not share later representatives.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert all n-6 paid descendants or pair births are simultaneously current. Does not assert turn orientation is uniform across b. Does not turn a finite family of interaction certificates into a contradiction. Does not prove the historical interaction-saturated frame has a no-replay property; same-frame historical replay remains a separate issue.

## Proof
Because U,V are nontrivial paths, each has exactly two rail endpoints. Among the n-2 vertices of H-{x,z}, exactly four are rail endpoints, so exactly n-6 are internal. Fix one internal b. Boundary antisymmetry uniquely orients x,b,z into a proper turn J_b=(a,b,c) with {a,c}={x,z}. R432 either gives a spanning two-cover or aligns the available ancestry-bearing floor to (a),(c). The displayed U sqcup V remains the same exact cover of H-{x,z}, and b remains internal with its same selected predecessor/successor. Corrected R442 now applies at the nontrivial pair-birth stage and yields its zero-quiet historical interaction output without invoking any same-singleton collision. Repeat independently for each internal b. The n-6 packets are alternative continuations over one common fixed deletion frame; later descendants are not synchronized.
