# R720 — Crossed middle-gate END clauses are selected-state reversals or labelled P4s

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, endpoints L=q_0,R=q_m, and let D be either crossed middle-gate pair {a,c} or {b,z}. Fix one of the two R649 X-complementary packets on D, with tested reverse-boundary dimer S, tight trimer carrier K, complementary singleton e in X\D, and fixed same-polarity endpoint witnesses {L,R}. Let T be any exact two-cover of H-D and suppose accepted R696 is in its END branch at e, so e has T-degree one. Then the END output already has one of two source-localized forms: (REV) T contains a selected directed state pq whose reverse qp is a graph-intrinsic tight state occurring in the forced END reverse trimer; equivalently the END certificate contains a literal reversal of a current T-state. (P4) The END certificate supplies an opposite-polarity sign on the same tested oriented dimer S, and combining it with either fixed R649 endpoint witness distinct from the new witness via accepted R523 gives a labelled tight Hamilton P4. Hence a crossed middle-gate END branch never needs to be retained as an anonymous one/two-hole mate clause: it compiles immediately to selected-state reversal geometry or a labelled P4.

More explicitly, for D={a,c}, the complementary singleton is the initial vertex of K and S is one of (a,c),(c,a), fixed head-signed by L,R. In the aligned one-hole END orientation, the unique forced reverse contains the reverse of the selected e-exterior state, giving REV. In the nonaligned two-hole orientation, the exact R511 specialization has reversals (k_1,c_s,c_{s-1}) when the pure exterior rail has a predecessor and (k_2,k_1,c_s), where S=(k_2,k_1). The first reverses the selected pure-rail state (c_{s-1},c_s), giving REV; the second is a tail certificate on the same tested S, opposite the fixed head polarity, so R523 gives P4 after choosing L or R distinct from c_s.

Dually, for D={b,z}, the complementary singleton is the terminal vertex of K and S is one of (b,z),(z,b), fixed tail-signed by L,R. The aligned one-hole END orientation again forces the reverse of the unique selected e-exterior state. In the nonaligned two-hole orientation, the reverse-dual R511 holes give either the reverse of a selected boundary state of the pure exterior rail or a head certificate (c_0,S) on the same tested S. The latter is opposite the fixed tail polarity and therefore gives a labelled P4 by R523. These cases exhaust END.

## Applicability

The one-hole case and one branch of the two-hole case literally reverse a selected T-state. The other two-hole branch is opposite polarity on the same tested middle-gate dimer and therefore yields a labelled P4 by R523 using the opposite endpoint witness.

In P4 retain the exact opposite-polarity certificate on S, the chosen distinct endpoint witness from {L,R}, and the resulting ordered Hamilton P4 from R523.

## Proof
Fix the packet. First take D={a,c}. By R649 the two X-complementary carriers have the form K=(e,k_1,k_2) with e in {b,z}, and the tested reverse terminal dimer S=(k_2,k_1) is head-signed by both L and R. In END, e has degree one in T. If e is the terminal vertex of its mixed T-rail, the aligned endpoint case of R511/R509 has one hole; the forced reverse trimer contains the reversed physical state e c_s of the selected state c_s e. This is REV. Otherwise e begins the mixed rail. Write the other pure exterior rail C'=(c_0,...,c_s). The exact m=2,i=0 R511 formula gives the complete hole set {(c_{s-1},c_s,k_1) if s>=1} union {(c_s,k_1,k_2)}. At least one hole is bad. If the first is bad, R3 gives (k_1,c_s,c_{s-1}) tight, whose terminal state c_s c_{s-1} reverses the selected T-state c_{s-1} c_s: REV. If the second is bad, R3 gives (k_2,k_1,c_s) tight. Since S=(k_2,k_1), this is a tail certificate on the exact tested S. R649 supplies head certificates (L,k_2,k_1) and (R,k_2,k_1). The witness c_s lies outside K and can equal at most one of L,R, so choose h in {L,R} distinct from c_s. R523 on tested S with head witness h and tail witness c_s gives the literal P4 (h,k_2,k_1,c_s).

Now take D={b,z}. The two X-complementary carriers have the dual form K=(k_0,k_1,e), with tested reverse initial dimer S=(k_1,k_0) tail-signed by both L,R. If e begins its mixed rail, the aligned endpoint case has one hole and its forced reverse contains the reverse of the selected e-exterior state, giving REV. Otherwise e terminates the mixed rail. The exact CS,j=m reverse-dual of R511 has complete holes whose reversals are: one selected-boundary-state reversal on the pure exterior rail, or a turn (c_0,k_1,k_0)=(c_0,S). The first gives REV. The second is a head certificate on the exact tested S, opposite its fixed tail polarity. Choose h in {L,R} distinct from c_0; R523 applied to the head witness c_0 and tail witness h gives the labelled P4 (c_0,k_1,k_0,h). Thus every END case is REV or P4.
