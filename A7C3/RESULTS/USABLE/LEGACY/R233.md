# R233 — Sharp five-exterior grid countermodel survives collision and restoration contact

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
There exists an eleven-vertex Level-(1) boundary-antisymmetric turn system with a tight carrier P6 K on vertices {0,1,2,3,4,5} and exterior E={6,7,8,9,10} such that: (i) E is non-Hamiltonian; (ii) for each carrier k exactly four of the five replacement supports (E\\{z}) union {k} are Hamiltonian, with failed holes f(0)=6, f(1)=7, f(2)=8, f(3)=9, f(4)=10, f(5)=10; hence the five hole-column sizes are 5,5,5,5,4 and the averaging bound ceil(4|K|/5)=5 is attained; (iii) every one of the thirty complementary carrier supports (K\\{k}) union {z} is non-Hamiltonian as a P6; (iv) the repeated exterior dimer (7,8) has same-side tight witness turns (7,8,1) and (7,8,2), arising inside successful replacement P5s, so it realizes the local same-polarity/two-witness pattern behind R207; and (v) applying the tail form of R164 to E0=(7,8) and witnesses 1,2 takes the reverse-contact alternative (2,1,8) tight, while the local restoration P4 (7,8,1,2) is blocked because (8,1,2) is bad. Thus the R192 replacement grid, an R207-type repeated signed exterior dimer, and the R164 restoration/contact dichotomy do not locally force any complementary carrier replacement. Any such global composition must use additional smallest-counterexample, carrier-position, ancestry, or representative information.

## Hypotheses
Only Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry is assumed for the explicit turn model. The claim is a local regression fence, not a smallest-counterexample model and not a refutation of a theorem that uses the full global hypotheses of R192/R207.

## Scope
Actual local countermodel for naive carrier-restoration compositions in the five-exterior program; simultaneously shows local sharpness of the R192 4/5 row density and the R232 column averaging bound at carrier order six.

## Applicability
Use to reject proof steps of the form replacement-grid plus repeated exterior-dimer collision plus bare R164 contact implies K-k+z Hamiltonian. A valid composition must retain extra structure not present in the local contracts, such as the positions of the two carrier witnesses along K, selected exact-cover incidence, ancestry, or a global extremality/minimality argument.

## Persistence
The countermodel is one fixed graph-intrinsic turn table. All positive and negative Hamiltonicity statements can be reconstructed by finite enumeration from the signature below.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not show a genuine smallest counterexample exists. Does not enforce absence of P7s or the universal exterior floor. Does not refute a carrier-restoration theorem using global-longestness plus additional carrier-order information. Does not claim the averaging bound is globally sharp for every carrier order.

## Proof
Let K={0,1,2,3,4,5}, E={6,7,8,9,10}. Encode the Level-(1) turn table as follows. For each unordered triple i<j<k, take the three reversal pairs A:(i,j,k)/(k,j,i), B:(i,k,j)/(j,k,i), C:(j,i,k)/(k,i,j). In lexicographic order of unordered triples and then A,B,C, let a bit 1 mean the displayed canonical order is tight and bit 0 mean its complete reversal is tight. The resulting 495-bit string, interpreted as an unsigned binary integer and left-padded to 124 hexadecimal digits, is 6b4ffffb4ffff3800050000924800bdbe9b6db6d58924e24936db401801f098924e24936db68bc0400a004124902351820924824b01c029040703dbe9c08. This chooses exactly one tight order in every reversal pair, hence satisfies boundary antisymmetry. Direct verification gives K=(0,1,2,3,4,5) tight and finds no tight P5 ordering on E. Exhausting all 120 orders of each replacement five-set gives exactly the successful-hole rows: k=0 succeeds for z=7,8,9,10; k=1 for z=6,8,9,10; k=2 for z=6,7,9,10; k=3 for z=6,7,8,10; k=4 for z=6,7,8,9; k=5 for z=6,7,8,9. Therefore every row has size four and the column sizes for z=6,7,8,9,10 are 5,5,5,5,4. Example certified P5s include (10,7,8,1,9) on (E\\{6}) union {1} and (10,9,7,8,2) on (E\\{6}) union {2}; these contain the tight turns (7,8,1) and (7,8,2), respectively, putting the same oriented exterior dimer (7,8) under two distinct same-side carrier witnesses. In the same table (8,1,2) is bad and its reverse (2,1,8) is tight. Thus R164 applied in tail form to retained dimer path (7,8) and witnesses 1,2 takes the reverse-contact branch, not the P4 restoration branch. Finally, exhaustive enumeration of all 720 orders on each of the thirty six-sets (K\\{k}) union {z}, k in K, z in E, finds no tight P6. Hence the local replacement/collision/restoration data coexist with total complementary carrier-swap failure, as claimed.
