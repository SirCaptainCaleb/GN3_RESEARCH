# R197 — Global-longest terminal-dimer universal adaptive-capture gateway

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K=(k0,...,km), m>=2, be a proper global-longest tight path with exterior E=V(H)\\V(K). For the left terminal physical dimer D_L={k0,k1}, the orientation (k1,k0) is tail-signed by every e in E; for the right terminal dimer D_R={k_{m-1},k_m}, the orientation (k_m,k_{m-1}) is head-signed by every e in E. Fix either terminal dimer D. In every exact two-cover of H-D, the Puncture Crossing Parent supplies a selected crossing from the nonempty surviving carrier K-D to E. Every such selected crossing is witness-adaptively capture-capable: one may choose an exterior sign witness avoiding the crossing and then choose either physical endpoint of the crossing to capture. The same-frame capture carries aligned refund ancestry, so the chosen channel enters the accepted aligned-dimer payment lineage, yielding either a spanning two-cover or a singleton-width paid descendant with the capture/refund ancestry retained.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a proper globally longest tight path of order at least three.

## Scope
Order-free longest-path terminal gateway. No order-ten, five-exterior equality, six-exterior density, payment synchronization, or CW-freeness is used.

## Applicability
Use at either end of any globally longest path before opening specialized exterior-slack casework. It removes the capture/concentration alternative from terminal-dimer puncture frames and provides an aligned paid lineage tied to an actual selected carrier/exterior crossing.

## Persistence
The reverse terminal signs are graph-intrinsic. The crossing is current only in its chosen exact D-deletion cover. Capture, refund, and paid descendants belong to the chosen lineage and retain historical ancestry, not simultaneous currentness across different choices.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe which carrier/exterior crossing occurs, does not make all crossing choices simultaneous, does not prescribe which singleton survives the generic aligned payment, does not assert exterior-size descent, and does not itself close the conjecture.

## Proof
Fix a terminal dimer D of the proper globally longest tight path K=(k_0,...,k_m), m>=2. Accepted R199 gives the required graph-intrinsic reverse sign on D from every exterior vertex e in E=V(H)-V(K): at the left end (k_1,k_0,e) is tight for every e, and at the right end (e,k_m,k_{m-1}) is tight for every e.\n\nFirst note that |E|>=2 without R169. Since K is proper, E is nonempty. If E={e} were a singleton, then K together with the singleton path (e) would be a spanning two-path cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. Thus E contains at least two vertices.\n\nBy accepted Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429, H-V(D) has an exact two-cover T=U sqcup V with both rails nontrivial. Put S=V(K)-V(D). Because m>=2, S is nonempty; because E is nonempty, S is a proper subset of W=V(H)-V(D). The original path K is a tight path spanning exactly V(D) union S. Therefore accepted R508, with deletion V(D), absorbable block S, carrier K, and the fixed cover T, forces a selected T-state xy crossing S | E. Exactly one endpoint of xy lies in E; call it e_xy.\n\nChoose w in E-{e_xy}, possible because |E|>=2. The R199 reverse terminal turn with witness w signs D in the required tested orientation, and the selected carrier/exterior crossing xy avoids w. Apply accepted R527 to the retained carrier K, signed support D, witness w, and the same exact deletion cover T. Its capture/refund branch applies to xy. By choosing the marker orientation, either physical endpoint of xy may be designated as the captured signed anchor, exactly as required in R197. R527 retains the original selected crossing, chosen marker orientation, capture event, and refund ancestry as historical certificate data.\n\nFinally D has order two and H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample, so the dimer payment clause of R527 applies: along the chosen certificate-retaining continuation, either H acquires a spanning two-cover or the lineage reaches singleton width, with the capture/refund ancestry retained. This proves every clause of R197. The only cardinality used is |E|>=2, derived directly from pc(H)>2; the quarantined five-exterior floor R169, and hence R5/R24, never enter.
