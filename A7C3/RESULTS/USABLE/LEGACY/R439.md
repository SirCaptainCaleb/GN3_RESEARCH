# R439 — Historical pair-birth anchors force immediate nonquiet fixed-turn reentry

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order at least seven. Suppose an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor is retained together with the full birth certificate of an earlier balanced opposite-sign pair (alpha;P),(T;beta), and let h,t be the distinct signed anchors of the two disjoint birth supports. Then there is a chosen ancestry-retaining continuation which yields either a spanning two-cover during floor steering or an aligned floor on {h,t} with the following bilateral property. In some exact two-cover H-{h,t}=U sqcup V, one rail has an internal vertex b. Orient the unique tight turn on {h,b,t}; at b both a selected predecessor and a selected successor exist in the same aligned frame. The two R434 endpoint-incidence channels are therefore both available, one anchored at h and the other at t. Each channel separately yields either a spanning two-cover during actualization or a nontrivial signed dimer whose signed endpoint is the corresponding old birth anchor. By R436 that later dimer forces an explicit strict-growth, tight-cycle, or reverse-contact/reversal output. Thus from one aligned frame there are two alternative immediate nonquiet historical-anchor channels, one touching each original pair-birth anchor; neither channel can complete a quiet endpoint-budget episode.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample with |V(H)|>=7; an ancestry-bearing both-singleton floor is active; its ledger retains an earlier balanced-pair birth with disjoint signed supports and signed anchors h,t.

## Scope
Order-free for smallest counterexamples of order at least seven; bilateral ancestry-sensitive strengthening of fixed-turn reentry. In O4 the size hypothesis is automatic.

## Applicability
Use after certificate-retaining pair descent, especially R417. Steer to the two original birth anchors, choose an internal middle of one pair-deletion rail, and obtain two alternative nonquiet channels from one aligned frame.

## Persistence
Retain the original birth supports/anchors, the R432 steering ledger, one exact H-{h,t} cover, the chosen internal middle b and its predecessor/successor, the two endpoint-anchored dimer certificates, and each corresponding R436 output separately. The two actualization descendants are alternative and need not coexist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the two R436 descendants coexist. Does not identify the stronger historical internal-middle simultaneous two-pair collision theorem mentioned in R434. Does not claim any one growth/cycle/reversal output closes the conjecture. Does not require the old birth supports to remain current.

## Proof
Apply R432 to steer the active both-singleton floor to {h,t}, retaining the original birth ledger, unless a spanning two-cover occurs. By R429, H-{h,t} has an exact two-cover by two nontrivial rails. Since |H|-2>=5, one rail has order at least three; choose an internal vertex b on that rail. By R170 exactly one orientation of the turn on {h,b,t} is tight; label its outer endpoints a,c so {a,c}={h,t}. Because b is internal in the displayed rail, it has both a selected predecessor and a selected successor. R434 applied to the predecessor gives either closure or a nontrivial signed dimer anchored at a; applied separately to the successor gives either closure or a nontrivial signed dimer anchored at c. The vertices a and c are exactly the two historical signed anchors h,t of the retained pair-birth supports. Each newly produced dimer is a genuinely later proper tight path containing an old historical anchor and is not the vacuous replay of an old singleton certificate. Therefore R436 forces an explicit at-anchor growth/cycle/reversal/contact output in each nonclosing channel. Hence neither endpoint incidence can be a quiet completed episode, and the aligned frame carries a bilateral alternative fork of nonquiet outputs.
