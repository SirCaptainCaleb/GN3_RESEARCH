# R713 — Canonical high-side P4 punctures force named three-class recompletion crossings

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted R594/R596 high-side frame and accepted R598 enlarged covers, with L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,w=q_3 and r=q_{m-3},d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, m>=4. LEFT. Suppose R518 on K_L=(u,L,x_L) with probe v is in its no-P4 branch. Then (v,x_L,L) and R596's (w,v,x_L) give the canonical tight P4 K_L^*=(w,v,x_L,L). Delete E_L=V(K_L^*) and put W_L=H-E_L. R598 restricts to the literal three-path source cover (u) sqcup (a,c,b) sqcup C_L, where C_L=Q[4,m] is nonempty. Smallest-counterexample minimality R4 gives pc(W_L)<=2. It cannot be 1: a Hamilton path of W_L together with K_L^* would be a spanning two-cover of H. Hence pc(W_L)=2; choose any exact two-cover T_L. Then either (DEEP-L) T_L selects a state with one endpoint in C_L and the other endpoint in {u,a,b,c}, an actual current recompletion cut from the deep Q tail to the first inward singleton or surviving X-trimer; or (LOCAL-L) no selected state crosses C_L | (W_L\C_L), in which case C_L is one entire T_L component and the other component is a tight Hamilton P4 on the fixed four-set {u,a,b,c}={u} union (X-{x_L}). RIGHT is dual. In the no-P4 branch of R518 on K_R=(x_R,R,s) with probe d, K_R^*=(R,x_R,d,r) is tight. Delete its support. R598 restricts to C_R=Q[0,m-4], the surviving X-trimer (b,a,c), and singleton s. R4 gives pc(W_R)<=2, and pc(W_R)=1 would combine with K_R^* to two-cover H, so pc(W_R)=2. Any exact two-cover either selects a state crossing C_R to {s} union the X-trimer (DEEP-R), or else C_R is one whole component and the other component is a Hamilton P4 on {s} union (X-{x_R}) (LOCAL-R). In every DEEP branch R176 may additionally convert the retained selected cross-state, together with the literal three-path source cover, into a provenance-bearing balanced opposite-sign pair. Thus the canonical endpoint-inward P4 branch has a noncircular two-output source-specific normal form: a physical cut at least four Q-indices into the opposite residual side, or a Hamilton local four-cell on the first inward Q vertex plus the surviving X-trimer.

## Proof
LEFT. R518 no-P4 gives (v,x_L,L); R596 gives (w,v,x_L), hence K_L^*=(w,v,x_L,L). R598 gives H-x_L=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup (v,w,q_4,...,q_m). Removing L,v,w and x_L leaves the three tight paths A=(u), B=(a,c,b), C=Q[4,m], all nonempty and spanning W_L. By R4, pc(W_L)<=2. If pc(W_L)=1, let P be a Hamilton tight path of W_L; then P sqcup K_L^* is a spanning two-cover of H, contradiction. Thus pc(W_L)=2. Let T be any exact two-cover. If a selected T-state crosses C | (A union B), take DEEP-L. Otherwise every T-component meeting C is contained wholly in C. Since A union B is nonempty and T has only two components, C cannot be split across two components; hence exactly one T-component has vertex set C, and the other has vertex set A union B, four vertices. The latter is therefore a tight Hamilton P4 on {u,a,b,c}. RIGHT. R518 no-P4 on (x_R,R,s) with d gives (R,x_R,d); with R596 turn (x_R,d,r) this gives K_R^*=(R,x_R,d,r). R598 gives H-x_R=Q[0,d] sqcup (b,a,c,R,s). Removing R,d,r,x_R leaves C=Q[0,m-4], B=(b,a,c), A=(s). R4 gives pc<=2 and a Hamilton residual path would pair with K_R^* to two-cover H, so pc=2. The same component argument gives DEEP-R or a Hamilton P4 on {s} union (X-{x_R}). In a DEEP branch the displayed three-path source cover and selected cross-state satisfy R176.
