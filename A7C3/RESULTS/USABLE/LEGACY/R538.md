# R538 — Complementary prefix absorbers force dual one-witness tail non-Hamiltonicity

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a finite boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let T be a vertex set, let c,d be distinct vertices outside T, and suppose there are tight paths L_c and L_d such that V(L_c)=V(H)\\(T union {d}) and V(L_d)=V(H)\\(T union {c}). Then neither H[T union {c}] nor H[T union {d}] has a Hamilton tight path. In the R24 left-end packet, the two certified prefix P5s q1,q0,a,b,d and q1,q0,b,a,c give this with T=V(Q[2,m]): hence both one-witness tails c+Q[2,m] and d+Q[2,m] are non-Hamiltonian.

## Hypotheses
Finite H with pc(H)>2; disjoint named T,c,d; the two complementary absorber paths exist literally.

## Scope
Order-free replacement lemma; specialized R24 consequence packages the left endpoint gate into two graph-intrinsic non-Hamiltonian tail statements.

## Applicability
Use whenever two alternative path absorbers cover the complement of one of two witnesses. It converts local alternative absorbers into global induced-subgraph Hamiltonicity obstructions.

## Persistence
Persist T,c,d and the two absorber paths. The non-Hamiltonicity conclusions are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert anything about literal insertion positions or orientations of Hamilton paths on larger sets.

## Proof
Assume H[T union {c}] has a Hamilton tight path K_c. By hypothesis L_d is a tight path with vertex set V(H)\\(T union {c}). Thus K_c and L_d are vertex-disjoint tight paths whose supports partition V(H), contradicting pc(H)>2. Therefore H[T union {c}] is non-Hamiltonian. The argument for H[T union {d}] using L_c is identical. In the R24 packet, L_c=(q1,q0,a,b,d) has complement {c} union V(Q[2,m]) and L_d=(q1,q0,b,a,c) has complement {d} union V(Q[2,m]), so both displayed tails are non-Hamiltonian.
