# R28 — Same-rail endpoint absorption

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

For a current rail endpoint and an internal prescribed vertex on the same other rail, boundary antisymmetry chooses one of two antisymmetric endpoint trimers. Replacing the endpoint by that trimer preserves the untouched rail state-for-state, yields a minimum three-cover, and leaves the displayed trimer complement exact.

## Scope
This is not a forbidden direct endpoint attachment. Direct attachment tests one turn and is blocked by the endpoint-shielding theorem; absorption uses the antisymmetric trimer orientation and a different exact exchange.

The theorem replaces older pivot/currentization parents when their only desired output was a current internal prescribed vertex. It does not replace variants retaining a chosen splice, source-maximinity, a captured boundary, or ancestor-core identity.
