# R312 — Protected capture plus lawful old-carrier mirror reentry forces first contact

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let A be a retained historical tight carrier path, D={x,z} a physical crossing of A, and suppose x has a valid historical capture certificate through D. Assume x has at least one genuine historical carrier adjacency E={p,x} on A. Choose one actual capture descendant and one designated quiet continuation to which accepted R42 applies. Then invoking accepted R155 on the same retained packet (A,D,capture) with the old adjacency E is a lawful mirror reentry and cannot be a quiet unrelated epoch: either an earlier event touching the protected anchor x has already been exposed, or this R155 mirror-reentry operation itself is the first contact at x. The later mirror trimer retains the capture event as ancestry. Thus any captured anchor lying on a nontrivial retained historical carrier has an explicit first-contact probe using an actual old carrier edge; no capture transport to a fresh path is required.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; A,D,x form a valid capture packet in the exact scope of R155; x has a historical adjacency E on A; one actual capture descendant and designated quiet continuation are chosen as required by R42; accepted correction R277.

## Scope
Order-free ancestry/contact compiler staying entirely inside the genuine retained historical carrier.

## Applicability
Use after any lawful capture when the captured vertex has at least one old carrier adjacency. It converts the otherwise static capture event into a forced first-contact alternative.

## Persistence
A,D,x,E and the historical capture event are retained. The chosen protected continuation is lineage-relative. The R155 mirror trimer is a later representative and retains capture ancestry as stated by R155.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not apply by substituting a newly invented carrier. Does not say the first contact closes the conjecture. Does not claim capture protection survives after the first contact has been exposed. Does not require two carrier neighbors.

## Proof
Retain the exact R312 packet: a historical tight carrier A, physical crossing D={x,z} with x in A and z outside A, a valid historical capture of x through D, and a genuine carrier adjacency E={p,x} on A. Choose one actual capture descendant and designated quiet continuation in the scope of accepted R42. Since A is a certified historical carrier crossed by D and E is one of its genuine adjacencies, accepted fully reconstructible R155 applies. It gives a later minimum three-cover whose mirror trimer through x has D and E as its two boundary physical supports, while the capture event remains historical ancestry. This later operation uses signed/physical supports containing the protected anchor x. Accepted R42 therefore rules out treating it as a quiet unrelated epoch along the chosen protected continuation: either some earlier operation already touched x, in which case that earlier contact is exposed, or the R155 mirror-reentry operation itself is the first contact at x. This is exactly the R312 dichotomy. No substitution of a fresh carrier is made, and no old selected orientation, old residual rail, or protection after first contact is claimed.
