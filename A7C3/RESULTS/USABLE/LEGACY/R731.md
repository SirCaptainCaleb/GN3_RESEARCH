# R731 — A first-step root advance creates a bidirectional same-root two-seam weave

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain accepted R723 and R725 in the crossed R621 frame. Every ADV output supplies two distinct selected cross states between the same shallow/advanced exact-cover pair of one singleton deletion, one in each comparison direction.

LEFT. Let r=c or a be an L-ADV winner. Write x=a when r=c and x=c when r=a. The shallow cover is T_0=A_r^L sqcup Q[1,m], with x in A_r^L and u=q_1,v=q_2,w=q_3 consecutive on Q[1,m]. The advanced cover is T_1=(v,u,x,...) sqcup Q[3,m]. Then T_1 selects the X-Q state (u,x), whose endpoints lie in the two distinct T_0 components, while T_0 selects the physical Q state (v,w), whose endpoints lie in the two distinct T_1 components. Accepted R176, with spare vertex r, therefore gives two graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair births Pi_L^out from (u,x) and Pi_L^in from (v,w), both retaining the same deleted root and the same ordered pair of exact-cover histories.

RIGHT. Let r=z or b be an R-ADV winner. Write x=b when r=z and x=z when r=b, and rho=q_{m-3}, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}. The shallow cover is T_0=Q[0,m-1] sqcup A_r^R, with x in A_r^R and rho,d,s consecutive on Q[0,m-1]. The advanced cover is T_1=(R,...,x,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3]. Then T_1 selects the X-Q state (x,s), crossing the two T_0 components, while T_0 selects the Q state (rho,d), crossing the two T_1 components. R176 gives two graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair births Pi_R^out and Pi_R^in with the same common source ledger.

Thus ADV is a bidirectional two-seam weave: an outer X-Q recombination state and a deeper Q-Q recombination state are simultaneously certified by opposite comparisons of the same two exact covers. The two births coexist as graph-intrinsic certificates, although their later paid representatives are alternative choices.
