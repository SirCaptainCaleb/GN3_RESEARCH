# R356 — Global Hamilton-five equality is conditionally uniform but never conditionally extremal

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume equality in the global R331 Hamilton-five bad-family ceiling on an r-vertex set W, r>5. Then for every fixed subset S⊆W of size s∈{0,1,2,3,4}, the fraction of five-subsets containing S that are bad is exactly the global bad density r/[5(r-4)], independent of S. In particular, for each positive s∈{1,2,3}, this conditional density is strictly below the accepted R327 conditional ceiling (r-s)/[(5-s)(r-4)] by the explicit gap s(r-5)/[5(5-s)(r-4)]. Thus global Johnson equality cannot simultaneously be equality at any positive level of the R327 conditional hierarchy; the equality design is conditionally uniform rather than conditionally extremal.

## Hypotheses
Equality holds in R331 on W; S is any fixed subset of size at most four.

## Scope
Structural separation of global Johnson equality from the conditional density ceilings.

## Applicability
Use to rule out arguments that stack equality in R331 with equality in R327 at fixed vertices, pairs, or triples. Under global equality all such conditional densities are known exactly and have positive slack.

## Persistence
The conditional bad-support counts are graph-intrinsic incidence data of the R331 four-design.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say the global equality design is realizable. Does not bound conditional densities away from their ceilings outside the global equality branch.

## Proof
Write q=r/5. For |S|=s<=4, the number of bad blocks through S is λ_s=q*binom(r-s,4-s)/binom(5-s,4-s)=q*binom(r-s,4-s)/(5-s). The total number of five-subsets through S is binom(r-s,5-s). Their ratio is q/(r-4)=r/[5(r-4)], independent of s. Subtracting this from the R327 ceiling gives [(r-s)/(5-s)-r/5]/(r-4)=s(r-5)/[5(5-s)(r-4)], positive for s>0 and r>5.
