# R599 — The exact-two R24 cell yields a four-cross-state weave on one pair deletion

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the unique exactly-two-bad root of R555/R556 in the transitive R24 frame, labelled by X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, and Q=(q_0,...,q_m). Then the two side-maximal sibling roots of R596/R598 are x_L=c and x_R=a, and the exact enlarged covers are
H-c = (q_1,q_0,b,z,a) sqcup Q[2,m],
H-a = Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,z,b,q_m,q_{m-1}).
Deleting the opposite endpoint a from the first enlarged P5 and c from the second produces two literal exact covers of the same pair deletion W=H-{a,c}:
T_L=(q_1,q_0,b,z) sqcup Q[2,m],
T_R=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (z,b,q_m,q_{m-1}).
These covers form a four-cross-state weave. T_L selects q_0 b across the two T_R components and selects q_{m-2}q_{m-1} across them; T_R selects b q_m across the two T_L components and selects q_1q_2 across them. Hence R176 applies at four named physical states on one common shadow W, producing four graph-intrinsic balanced-pair birth certificates with the pair-deletion coordinates {a,c}, central X-state bz, and both two-step Q cuts retained.

## Hypotheses
Unique exactly-two-bad transitive R24 root of accepted R555/R556; m>=6 by accepted R574; pending high-side enlargement results R596/R598; Pair-Deletion Rigidity R429; cross-state upgrade R176.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Consumes the left/right enlarged roots in the equality-two branch into two explicit covers of one common pair deletion.

## Applicability
The common-shadow weave is a concrete interaction target for certificate payment or a future component-drop theorem; it is stronger than two unrelated one-sided enlarged roots.

## Persistence
All four selected cross states, the exact two cover orders, deleted physical pair {a,c}, shared vertices b,z, and Q interval cuts coexist in the original graph.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
R176 births are graph-intrinsic and need not be simultaneously current paid descendants. The four cross states do not by themselves yield a spanning two-cover under presently accepted consumers. This theorem does not treat frames with no exactly-two-bad root or the case x_L=x_R.

## Proof
Let r=z be the exactly-two-bad root. By R555 its bad seams are cbq_0 and q_mba, while R556 says that at q_0 the M_L edge az and M_S edge bz are fully outgoing, and at q_m the M_R edge cz and M_S edge bz are fully incoming.

In the construction of R596, x_L is the intersection of the root-pair whose trimer contains the fully-outgoing M_L edge az and the root-pair whose trimer contains the fully-outgoing M_S edge bz. The first pair is {b,c} and the second is {a,c}; their intersection is c. Thus x_L=c. Normalizing X-{c} gives local (a',b',c')=(b,a,z): a'b'=ab is M_R, a'c'=bz is M_S, and b'c'=az is M_L. R598 therefore reads
H-c=(q_1,q_0,b,z,a) sqcup Q[2,m].
Dually, x_R is the intersection of the root-pairs containing the fully-incoming M_R edge cz and fully-incoming M_S edge bz. These pairs are {a,b} and {a,c}, meeting in a. Normalizing X-{a} gives (a',b',c')=(z,c,b), and R598 reads
H-a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,z,b,q_m,q_{m-1}).

In the first cover, a is the terminal vertex of the displayed P5, so deleting a leaves the tight P4 (q_1,q_0,b,z), disjoint from the tight suffix Q[2,m], and these supports partition W=H-{a,c}. In the second cover, c is the initial vertex of the displayed P5, so deleting c leaves (z,b,q_m,q_{m-1}), disjoint from Q[0,m-2], again covering W. By R429 pc(W)=2, hence both displayed two-covers are exact.

Now inspect their selected states. In T_L, q_0b is a selected state of (q_1,q_0,b,z); relative to T_R, q_0 lies in Q[0,m-2] and b lies in (z,b,q_m,q_{m-1}), so it crosses T_R-components. Also q_{m-2}q_{m-1} is selected in Q[2,m]; relative to T_R, q_{m-2} is in its Q-prefix and q_{m-1} in its right endcap, so this is a second crossing. Conversely, bq_m is selected in the right endcap of T_R and crosses the left endcap/suffix components of T_L; q_1q_2 is selected in the Q-prefix of T_R and likewise crosses those two T_L components. R574 gives m>=6 in the surviving exact-two cell, so all four displayed states and component intervals are present without endpoint degeneracy. Applying R176 to W with either cover as reference and each named crossing gives the four asserted balanced-pair birth certificates. The applications share the same proper shadow W and spare vertices a,c, while preserving their individual selected-state provenance.
