# R722 — A cross-end trimer amplifies to a labelled P4 or dual protected endpoint contacts

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted synchronized R594 frame with Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, write L=q_0,R=q_m, and let x be any vertex of X for which the graph-intrinsic cross-end trimer K=(R,x,L) is tight. Choose any two distinct probes y_1,y_2 in X\{x}. Then at least one of the following holds. (P4) For some i in {1,2}, the four-set {R,x,L,y_i} supports a labelled tight Hamilton P4. (DUAL-FC) Both physical endpoints are independently capture/contact-capable through the same cross-end carrier K. More precisely, D_L=(x,R) is tail-signed by both y_1,y_2 and D_R=(L,x) is head-signed by both y_1,y_2. In any exact two-cover of H-{x,R}, accepted R508 applied to K forces a selected crossing from complementary singleton L to the exterior; one of y_1,y_2 avoids that crossing, so accepted R527 may orient the crossing to capture L. Since K retains the genuine old carrier adjacency {L,x}, accepted R312 gives a protected first-contact continuation at L. Dually, in any exact two-cover of H-{L,x}, R508 forces a crossing from complementary singleton R; one probe avoids it, R527 captures R, and R312 through old carrier adjacency {R,x} gives protected first contact at R. The two source signed-dimer packets coexist graph-intrinsically; the later L- and R-contact continuations are separate choices.

Hence a cross-end trimer (R,x,L), including the CROSS output of pending R717, has a direct nonstationary consumer: labelled P4 or dual endpoint first-contact geometry. Generic Reverse-Ear replay is unnecessary.

## Proof
Fix distinct y_1,y_2 in X-{x}. Apply accepted R700 to the tight trimer K=(a,b,c)=(R,x,L) with probes y_1,y_2. If either probe yields the R700 P4 branch, outcome P4 holds. Otherwise the DUAL-CP branch of R700 gives both reverse contacts for i=1,2: (x,R,y_i) and (y_i,L,x). Thus the tested reverse initial dimer D_L=(x,R) is tail-signed by y_1,y_2, while the tested reverse terminal dimer D_R=(L,x) is head-signed by y_1,y_2. These four turns are simultaneous graph-intrinsic facts.

For the left endpoint L, delete D_L={x,R}. Accepted R429 gives an exact two-cover T_L of H-{x,R}. In K the complementary carrier singleton is L, so accepted R508 applied with absorbable singleton {L} forces a selected carrier/exterior state C_L={L,z_L}. This two-vertex crossing contains at most one of the distinct probes y_1,y_2, so choose y_i avoiding C_L. The tested dimer D_L has the exact tail certificate (x,R,y_i). Accepted R527 therefore applies in the same deletion frame; choose the marker orientation designating L as the captured signed anchor. This gives a valid historical capture of L through C_L. The original carrier K is retained and contains the genuine adjacency E_L={x,L}. Hence the exact hypotheses of accepted R312 hold with A=K, crossing C_L, captured anchor L, and old adjacency E_L. Along a chosen protected continuation R312 exposes either earlier contact at L or lawful mirror reentry which is itself first contact at L.

For R, delete D_R={L,x}. R429 gives an exact two-cover T_R. The complementary singleton of the same carrier K is R; R508 forces a selected crossing C_R={R,z_R}. Choose one of y_1,y_2 avoiding C_R. Since D_R=(L,x) is head-signed by both probes, R527 captures R by orienting C_R accordingly. The retained carrier adjacency E_R={R,x} then feeds R312 and yields protected first contact at R. The source packets coexist because they are fixed signed facts of K and the probe tests; only the chosen pair-deletion representatives and later capture continuations are alternative. This proves DUAL-FC.
