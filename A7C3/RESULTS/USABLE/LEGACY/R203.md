# R203 — Five-exterior common-hole synchronization

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the five-exterior non-Hamiltonian equality face of R192. For any t distinct carrier vertices k_1,...,k_t in V(K), with 1 <= t <= 4, there are at least 5-t exterior vertices z in E such that every one of the t five-sets (E\\{z}) union {k_i} supports a tight P5. In particular, any four prescribed carrier vertices have a common exterior omission z for which the same exterior four-set E\\{z} completes to a tight P5 with each of the four carrier vertices. Hence every consecutive four-window of K admits such a common omission.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a proper tight path with exactly five exterior vertices E; E is non-Hamiltonian; accepted R192.

## Scope
Arbitrary order. Pure support-incidence corollary of the R192 four-of-five row saturation.

## Applicability
Use to synchronize several carrier rows before invoking orientation-sensitive or ancestry-sensitive machinery. The four-window specialization gives one fixed exterior four-set with four separate carrier completions.

## Persistence
The successful replacement supports are graph-intrinsic. The common omission z is a physical exterior vertex chosen from the fixed E.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the P5 orderings on the common five-supports, does not assert any complementary carrier replacement K-k_i+z is tight, and does not itself give a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
For a carrier vertex k let F_k be the set of exterior holes z in E for which (E\\{z}) union {k} does not support a tight P5. R192 gives |F_k|<=1. For distinct k_1,...,k_t, a hole z is simultaneously successful for all t rows exactly when z lies in E\\(F_{k_1} union ... union F_{k_t}). The union of the t failure sets has size at most t, so the common success set has size at least |E|-t=5-t. For t=4 this is nonempty. Choosing the four vertices to be any consecutive four-window of K gives the final specialization.
