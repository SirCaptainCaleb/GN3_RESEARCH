# R488 — Reverse-chord bridge cell forces an ancestral-neighbor P4 fork

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the hypotheses and notation of R485 and suppose its residual (P4) branch occurs, so (z_1,a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) is a tight P4. Put l=b_{q-1}, r=a_1, X_B={z_1,a_0,b_q,l}, and X_A={a_0,b_q,z_{m-1},r}. Then at least one of the following occurs: (H) the exact R485 head collision on the oriented bridge chord (a_0,b_q), namely both (z_1,a_0,b_q) and (r,a_0,b_q) are tight; (T) the exact R485 tail collision on the same chord, namely both (a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) and (a_0,b_q,l) are tight; or (DP4) both X_B and X_A support tight Hamilton P4s. In particular, a single ancestry-bearing four-cell failure already upgrades the original reverse-chord P4 to one of R485's source-neighbor collision outputs. Proof: apply R518 to C_B=(z_1,a_0,b_q) with fourth vertex l. If X_B has no P4, R518 forces (l,b_q,a_0) tight. If (b_q,a_0,r) were also tight, then (b_0,...,l,b_q,a_0,r,...,a_p) together with P^o=(z_1,...,z_{m-1}) would be a spanning two-cover, contradicting pc(H)>2. Hence (b_q,a_0,r) is bad, so R3 forces its reverse (r,a_0,b_q) tight, giving (H) with the retained trimer (z_1,a_0,b_q). Dually, if X_A has no P4, R518 forces (b_q,a_0,r) tight; then (l,b_q,a_0) must be bad, so R3 forces (a_0,b_q,l) tight, giving (T) with the retained trimer (a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}). Therefore if neither collision occurs, neither four-cell may be no-P4, and both X_B and X_A support tight Hamilton P4s.

## Hypotheses
All hypotheses of R485, including pc(H)>2, p,q>=1 and m>=3; the R485(P4) branch; accepted R3,R38,R518.

## Scope
Order-free ancestry-preserving amplification of the sole local residual in R485. It keeps both old source neighbors and both bridge-side witnesses and replaces a lone reverse-chord P4 by an exact collision-or-double-P4 trichotomy.

## Applicability
Use immediately in the R485(P4) branch. Test the two exact four-sets obtained by adjoining b_{q-1} to (z_1,a_0,b_q) and a_1 to (a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}). If either cell has no P4, recover the corresponding R485 chord collision; only the double-P4 branch survives without an ancestral-neighbor collision.

## Persistence
Retain A,B,P; a_0=head(A), b_q=tail(B); source neighbors a_1,b_{q-1}; bridge neighbors z_1,z_{m-1}; the original R485 P4; and in DP4 both exact four-set supports X_A,X_B. In H/T retain the exact collision witness pair from R485. Hamilton orders inside X_A,X_B are not fixed.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not specify either Hamilton order in DP4, does not itself produce a complementary R173/R463 sibling clause, does not force any pair-deletion endpoint-side flip, and does not claim the double-P4 residual is contradictory. It does not validate ancestry-free use of R486 or generic R435 outputs.

## Proof
Let l=b_{q-1} and r=a_1. The R485(P4) branch gives C_B=(z_1,a_0,b_q) tight and C_A=(a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) tight. Apply R518 to C_B with l. If X_B has no tight Hamilton P4, its no-P4 reverse contact gives s_B=(l,b_q,a_0) tight. Suppose also s_A=(b_q,a_0,r) were tight. Because B=(b_0,...,l,b_q) and A=(a_0,r,...,a_p) are ancestral tight paths, L=(b_0,...,l,b_q,a_0,r,...,a_p) is then a vertex-simple tight path: its only cross-source turns are s_B and s_A. The source cover P sqcup A^+ sqcup B^- is literal, so the complementary vertices are exactly P^o=(z_1,...,z_{m-1}), a nonempty tight contiguous subpath of P. Hence L sqcup P^o is a spanning two-cover, contradicting pc(H)>2. Therefore s_A is bad. R3 gives its reverse (r,a_0,b_q) tight, which together with retained (z_1,a_0,b_q) is the exact R485(H) witness pair. The dual argument starts from X_A no-P4: R518 gives s_A tight, closure forbids s_B, R3 yields (a_0,b_q,l) tight, and retained (a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) gives R485(T). Consequently, if neither H nor T occurs, both X_B and X_A must support tight Hamilton P4s.
