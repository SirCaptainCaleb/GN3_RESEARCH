# R148 — Three prescribed-middle trimers do not force a core four-path

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Boundary antisymmetry alone does not imply that three tight trimers on {p,b,e}, {p,d,e}, and {b,d,e}, each chosen with e as the middle vertex, force a tight four-vertex path on {p,b,d,e}. There is an explicit Strong Level-(1) local orientation on these four vertices satisfying all three prescribed-middle trimers but admitting no tight P4 on the four-set.

## Hypotheses
Only Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry on four distinct vertices p,b,d,e.

## Scope
Local negative result for the type-(2,2,2) common-exterior spoke motif. It addresses only what follows from the three e-centered short-trimer orientations themselves.

## Applicability
Use to rule out attempts to close type-(2,2,2) from the three common-e short trimers alone. Any successful closure must additionally consume information from the long roots, support minimality, sign interaction, or another cross-root invariant.

## Persistence
The countermodel is a complete assignment of the twelve reversal pairs on the four fixed vertices.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not show the full type-(2,2,2) motif is realizable, and does not rule out a Hamilton path forced after adding the long-root constraints.

## Proof
On the four vertices {p,b,d,e}, declare the following twelve ordered triples tight, one from each complete-reversal pair: dbp, bdp, dpb; ebp, peb, epb; pde, ped, epd; bde, bed, dbe. Their complete reversals are bad. This is therefore a valid local R3 assignment. In particular the three prescribed-middle trimers peb, ped, and bed are all tight, corresponding to the three type-(2,2,2) short supports with common middle e. Now inspect the 24 possible orderings (v1,v2,v3,v4) of the four vertices. A tight P4 would require both v1v2v3 and v2v3v4 to belong to the displayed tight list. Direct enumeration of the 24 orderings gives no such ordering. Hence the three e-centered trimers alone do not force a tight P4 on their union. Since they contain no information involving vertices outside this four-set, they also cannot by themselves force a larger Hamilton complement without additional cross-root input.
