# R353 — Conditional Hamilton-five density equality has divisibility fences

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the setting of accepted R327, fix S⊆W with |S|=s∈{0,1,2,3} and suppose equality holds in the conditional bad-five density ceiling p_bad(W;S)=(r-s)/[(5-s)(r-4)]. Then necessarily (5-s) divides (r-s). More precisely, after quotienting out S as in R327, the bad supersets form a k=(5-s)-uniform family on N=r-s vertices attaining equality in R326 with t=2. The R326 equality condition forces every (k-1)-core to have the same occupancy μ=N/k=(r-s)/(5-s), hence μ is an integer. Thus: s=0 requires r≡0 mod5; s=1 requires r≡1 mod4; s=2 requires r≡2 mod3; s=3 requires r odd. If the R327 ceilings are simultaneously attained for all four levels s=0,1,2,3 at one order r, then necessarily r≡5 mod60. Therefore at all other orders at least one level of the conditional density hierarchy is automatically strict.

## Hypotheses
W has r>=6 vertices in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament; S⊆W has size s∈{0,1,2,3}; equality holds in the R327 conditional bad-density bound.

## Scope
Equality-realizability fence for the Johnson conditional hierarchy.

## Applicability
Use when a density argument approaches or assumes equality in R327. The congruence conditions identify orders where equality is arithmetically impossible before any turn-structure analysis.

## Persistence
The conclusion concerns the bad-support incidence family and is graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
These are necessary conditions only, not existence theorems. Simultaneous equality at r≡5 mod60 is not asserted realizable. Global R331 equality does not imply conditional R327 equality for s>0.

## Proof
With N=r-s and k=5-s, R327 equality has p=N/[k(N-k+1)]. R326 equality forces x_T constant over all (k-1)-cores T, with mean μ=k|B|/binom(N,k-1)=p(N-k+1)=N/k=(r-s)/(5-s). Since each x_T is an integer, k divides N. The four congruences follow. Solving r≡0 mod5, r≡1 mod4, r≡2 mod3, and r≡1 mod2 gives r≡5 mod60.
