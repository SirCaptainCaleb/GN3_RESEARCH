# R237 — Five-exterior dense column contains a consecutive triple

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the non-Hamiltonian five-exterior equality face and let K=(k_1,...,k_m), m>=6, have exterior E. Then there is a hole z in E and three consecutive carrier vertices k_i,k_{i+1},k_{i+2} such that each of the three five-sets (E\\{z}) union {k_i}, (E\\{z}) union {k_{i+1}}, and (E\\{z}) union {k_{i+2}} supports a tight P5.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; E is non-Hamiltonian; accepted dense-hole column bound R232.

## Scope
Pure carrier-order refinement of R232. No sign, payment, currentization, or P5-order synchronization is used.

## Applicability
Use when downstream arguments need the fixed exterior four-core completions to meet actual consecutive positions of the globally-longest carrier, rather than an unordered carrier subset.

## Persistence
The hole z, the three physical carrier vertices, their carrier adjacency/turn, and the three P5 supports are graph-intrinsic.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the three P5 orderings, does not imply a P6 on their union, does not force a complementary carrier replacement, and does not by itself defeat the R233/R234 local countermodels without additional global-longest or exact-cover structure.

## Proof
Let t be the number of failures in the dense column. Then t<=floor(m/5). If no three consecutive positions succeed, every block of three carrier positions contains a failure, so t>=floor(m/3) for the disjoint blocks (1,2,3),(4,5,6),.... For m>=6, floor(m/3)>floor(m/5), contradiction. Therefore some three consecutive positions all succeed, yielding the displayed three P5 supports over the same four-set E-{z}.
