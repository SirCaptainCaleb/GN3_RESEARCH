# R725 — Universal first-inward stars give a first-step root switch or outer-matching saturation

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and pending R723, with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, L=q_0, u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}, R=q_m. Then the two sides admit exact one-hole first-step zipper dichotomies. LEFT: at least one of the following holds: (L-ADV) H-c has the literal exact two-cover (v,u,a,b,z,L) sqcup Q[3,m], or H-a has the literal exact two-cover (v,u,c,z,b,L) sqcup Q[3,m]; (L-SAT) both turns (b,a,u) and (z,c,u) are tight. In L-SAT the two top-matching reverse dimers are new two-witness R542-ready packets: (b,a) is tail-signed by distinct witnesses u,z and is a reverse boundary dimer of K=(a,b,c), while (z,c) is tail-signed by u,b and is a reverse boundary dimer of K=(c,z,a). RIGHT dually: at least one of (R-ADV) H-z=(R,a,c,b,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3] or H-b=(R,c,a,z,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3] is a literal exact two-cover; or (R-SAT) both (s,b,c) and (s,z,a) are tight. In R-SAT the two low-matching dimers are new two-witness R542-ready packets: (b,c) is head-signed by s,z with carrier K=(a,c,b), and (z,a) is head-signed by s,b with carrier K=(c,a,z). Thus failure of a cover-valued first inward root switch on a side forces an explicit new matching family of short-carrier packets rather than witness-only reminting.

## Proof
LEFT. Pending R723 gives (v,u,a) and (v,u,c) tight. The matching-height four-cell gives (a,b,z) and (c,z,b) tight, while the crossed left gate gives (b,z,L) and (z,b,L) tight. Hence P_c=(v,u,a,b,z,L) has every consecutive turn certified except h_c=(u,a,b): its turns are (v,u,a), h_c, (a,b,z), (b,z,L). If h_c is tight, P_c is a tight Hamilton P6 on {v,u,L} union (X-{c}); together with the untouched tight suffix Q[3,m] it is a literal two-cover of H-c, necessarily exact because a Hamilton H-c would two-cover H with singleton c. If h_c is bad, R3 gives (b,a,u) tight. The X turn (b,a,z) is also tight because M(ab)=M_R>M(az)=M_L. Thus tested dimer (b,a) is tail-signed by the distinct outside witnesses u,z. The tight trimer K=(a,b,c) has (b,a) as its reverse initial boundary dimer, and u,z lie outside K, so R542 applies. Similarly P_a=(v,u,c,z,b,L) has sole uncertified seam h_a=(u,c,z), because (v,u,c), (c,z,b), and (z,b,L) are tight. If h_a is tight it gives H-a=P_a sqcup Q[3,m]. If bad, R3 gives (z,c,u). Matching height gives (z,c,b) tight. The tight carrier K=(c,z,a) has reverse initial dimer (z,c), with witnesses u,b outside K, so this is the second L-SAT R542 packet. Therefore if neither L-ADV cover occurs, both L-SAT packets occur. RIGHT. R723 gives (b,s,d) and (z,s,d) tight. Crossed right gates give (R,a,c) and (R,c,a); matching height gives (a,c,b) and (c,a,z). Thus P_z=(R,a,c,b,s,d) has only h_z=(c,b,s) uncertified. If h_z is tight, P_z with Q[0,m-3] is an exact two-cover of H-z. If bad, R3 gives (s,b,c). Matching height gives (z,b,c), so (b,c) is head-signed by s,z; carrier K=(a,c,b) has reverse terminal dimer (b,c), and s,z lie outside K. Likewise P_b=(R,c,a,z,s,d) has sole seam h_b=(a,z,s). Tight h_b gives the advanced H-b cover. If bad, R3 gives (s,z,a); matching height gives (b,z,a), and carrier K=(c,a,z) has reverse terminal dimer (z,a), with outside head witnesses s,b. Hence failure of both right advanced covers gives exactly R-SAT. All displayed paths and witnesses are literal, and the only use of R542 is to identify the resulting two-witness packet type.
