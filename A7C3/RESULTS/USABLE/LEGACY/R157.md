# R157 — Non-Hamilton universal-extension fixed-rail endpoint surgery

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let X be a nonempty proper non-Hamilton vertex set, put Y=V(H)\\X, and assume every r in Y Hamilton-extends X. Fix any exact two-cover Y=U⊔V. For every endpoint r of U, choose any Hamilton tight path P_r on X union {r}. Then r is internal in P_r and P_r⊔(U-r)⊔V is a spanning minimum three-cover of H. Dually the same holds for every endpoint of V. Thus the opposite residual rail survives literally, while every selected state of the touched rail survives except its unique terminal state incident with r, which is exactly the unique blocker paid by the graft.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; X is nonempty, proper, and non-Hamilton; Y=H-X; every r in Y extends X to a Hamilton tight path; Y=U⊔V is one fixed exact two-cover; r is an endpoint of one chosen residual rail.

## Scope
Fixed-representative specialization of the universal internal-vertex graft in the non-Hamilton universal-extension setting. It applies to any chosen exact residual two-cover, not only cyclic-K4 instances. The point is literal residual-rail retention, not a new graft mechanism.

## Applicability
Use when universal-extension arguments need to currentize X union {r} without discarding the residual frame. It gives an endpoint-indexed minimum cover in which one whole old rail survives and the touched rail loses exactly one terminal selected state, enabling same-rail boundary packets, role tracking, and fixed-frame comparisons.

## Persistence
Within each endpoint-indexed output, the untouched residual rail is literally unchanged and all selected states of the touched rail except its terminal state at r survive literally. The Hamilton path P_r is current in that output. Different choices of r or P_r generally give different representatives and are not synchronized. No unrelated older rails or ancestry are inherited.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not expose the paid endpoint as a useful endpoint in the output; does not steer which side of P_r contains r; does not select any prescribed crossing support; does not preserve old carriers, R33/R28/R29 ancestry, capture/protection, or rails from frames other than the fixed U⊔V; does not synchronize different endpoint graft covers; does not imply Two-Seam/One-Hole assembly or a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
Recovered as the accepted Non-Hamilton Universal-Extension Fixed-Rail Surgery / Cyclic-K4 Fixed-Rail Near-End Graft interface from the Aug. 24 Researcher/Critic/Puzzler chain. Fix an exact residual two-cover Y=U⊔V and endpoint r of U. The universal-extension hypothesis gives P_r Hamiltonian on X union {r}. Since X is non-Hamilton, r is internal in P_r; otherwise deleting endpoint r would Hamiltonize X. Universal Internal-Vertex Graft applied to P_r and U⊔V has blocker set equal to the selected incidence at r. At a rail endpoint this is exactly the one terminal state of U, so gain is one and cycle debt zero. The output is literally P_r⊔(U-r)⊔V. Universal-Extension Deletion Rigidity ensures U has at least two vertices, so U-r is nonempty. Hence the output is a spanning three-cover and therefore minimum. The opposite rail V survives intact and every U-state other than the terminal state at r survives. The later maximin handoff explicitly lists Non-Hamilton Universal-Extension Fixed-Rail Surgery among audited inputs, and the Puzzler consolidation carries the fixed-representative rail-retention scope as accepted.
