# R819 — At order ten, every zipper advance re-enters the R594 normal form on a rotated four-cell

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Every R725 ADV output at m=5 is itself a new synchronized transitive R594 frame on a rotated 4+6 partition. LEFT. If L-ADV occurs with deleted root r=c, so H-c=S_c sqcup (q_3,q_4,q_5) with S_c=(q_2,q_1,a,b,z,q_0), then Y_c={c,q_3,q_4,q_5} is a transitive matching-height P4-free four-cell, and for every y in Y_c the same long rail S_c re-roots exactly as in R594; in particular (q_1,q_2,y) and (y,q_0,z) are tight. If L-ADV occurs with r=a, S_a=(q_2,q_1,c,z,b,q_0), then Y_a={a,q_3,q_4,q_5} is transitive matching-height and for every y in Y_a, (q_1,q_2,y) and (y,q_0,b) are tight. RIGHT. If R-ADV occurs with r=z, so H-z=(q_0,q_1,q_2) sqcup T_z with T_z=(q_5,a,c,b,q_4,q_3), then Y_z={z,q_0,q_1,q_2} is a transitive matching-height P4-free four-cell and for every y in Y_z, (a,q_5,y) and (y,q_3,q_4) are tight. If R-ADV occurs with r=b, T_b=(q_5,c,a,z,q_4,q_3), then Y_b={b,q_0,q_1,q_2} is transitive matching-height and for every y in Y_b, (c,q_5,y) and (y,q_3,q_4) are tight. In all four cases the full R594 package is inherited in the new frame: every root of Y gives an exact trimer-plus-new-long-rail singleton-deletion cover, and both new endpoint five-sets are P5-free. Thus an order-ten ADV is not terminal cut movement; it is a self-renormalization of the R24 hard frame.

## Proof
Consider first L-ADV with root c. Accepted R725 gives the literal exact singleton-deletion cover H-c=S_c sqcup P_c, where S_c=(q_2,q_1,a,b,z,q_0) is a tight P6 and P_c=(q_3,q_4,q_5) is a tight trimer. Apply accepted R594 to this exact cover with deleted vertex c, short rail P=P_c, and long rail Q'=S_c. R594 says Y_c=V(P_c) union {c}={c,q_3,q_4,q_5} is a transitive matching-height P4-free four-cell; for every y in Y_c, Q'=S_c together with a tight trimer on Y_c-{y} is an exact cover of H-y; both Y_c plus the first/last endpoint of S_c are P5-free; and the endpoint reverse stars are (s_1,s_0,y) and (y,s_5,s_4), where S_c=(s_0,...,s_5). Here (s_0,s_1,s_4,s_5)=(q_2,q_1,z,q_0), so these stars are exactly (q_1,q_2,y) and (y,q_0,z). The L-ADV root a case is identical with S_a=(q_2,q_1,c,z,b,q_0), giving Y_a={a,q_3,q_4,q_5} and stars (q_1,q_2,y),(y,q_0,b). For R-ADV root z, R725 gives H-z=P_z sqcup T_z with P_z=(q_0,q_1,q_2) and T_z=(q_5,a,c,b,q_4,q_3). Apply R594 with short rail P_z and long rail T_z. Then Y_z={z,q_0,q_1,q_2} is transitive matching-height, and since T_z=(t_0,...,t_5) has (t_0,t_1,t_4,t_5)=(q_5,a,q_4,q_3), the endpoint stars are (a,q_5,y) and (y,q_3,q_4). The root b case with T_b=(q_5,c,a,z,q_4,q_3) gives Y_b={b,q_0,q_1,q_2} and stars (c,q_5,y),(y,q_3,q_4). Every remaining assertion is exactly the corresponding R594 conclusion in the rotated frame. No theorem depending on R24 or R5 is used.
