# R209 — Six-exterior singleton roots carry double endpoint-floor ancestry

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
In the O3 six-exterior slack-one face, fix any R196 singleton root H=K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e}, with K=(k_1,...,k_m) global-longest and Q_e=(q_1,...,q_5) a tight exterior P5. Then the root carries two separate graph-intrinsic ancestry-bearing mass-two balanced-pair certificates: one supported on the carrier endpoint singletons {k_1},{k_m}, supplied by R202, and one supported on the exterior-P5 endpoint singletons {q_1},{q_5}. Thus every such root has a canonical four-physical-endpoint double-floor ancestry on the two rail endpoint pairs.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is proper global-longest with six-vertex exterior E; e is an R196 Hamiltonian omission; Q_e is a chosen tight P5 ordering on E\\{e}; accepted R202 and Multi-Support Adjacent-Color Refund R174.

## Scope
Historical ancestry compression of one literal six-exterior singleton root. The two mass-two floors are separate descendants/certificates, not asserted simultaneously current.

## Applicability
Use to reduce six-exterior root analysis to interactions among the four physical rail endpoints. Any future synchronization theorem need only couple the two endpoint-floor ancestries or couple one of them to the retained singleton witness e.

## Persistence
The four physical rail endpoints and the existence/polarities of their signed singleton descendants are historical graph-intrinsic ancestry data. Witness names may change under rebirth.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the two floor pairs occur in one representative, does not give a literal R171 frame containing both pairs, does not identify reborn witnesses across the two rails, and does not itself force a two-cover or exterior-size descent.

## Proof
The carrier endpoint-floor ancestry on {k_1},{k_m} is exactly R202. For Q_e, first note that E cannot support a tight P6, since K is disjoint from E and K sqcup E would then be a spanning two-cover. Hence in Q_e=(q_1,...,q_5) both direct attachments (e,q_1,q_2) and (q_4,q_5,e) are bad. Boundary antisymmetry gives (q_2,q_1,e) and (e,q_5,q_4) tight, so the disjoint terminal physical dimers {q_1,q_2} and {q_4,q_5} are signed by e with opposite polarities and form a direct balanced pair by R39. Both signed supports lie in the single tight carrier Q_e. The contiguous middle interval C=(q_2,q_3,q_4) meets the left terminal dimer in exactly q_2 and the right terminal dimer in exactly q_4. Applying the adjacent-color refund / Signed-Interval Rebirth mechanism R174 to this interval leaves nonempty signed descendants supported exactly on the untouched outer endpoint singletons {q_1} and {q_5}; their opposite polarities are retained. Thus Q_e has an endpoint-aligned mass-two pair ancestry. Together with the independent R202 ancestry on the endpoints of K, the chosen singleton root carries the stated double endpoint-floor ancestry. The two refund descendants need not be current simultaneously.
