## Proof
Write Q={G0,G1,G2} with the fixed order G0<G1<G2 and X={G3,G4}. By R670, after contracting the five contiguous cells, every configuration in the genuinely new minimum residue is a two-component path forest with three edges, no G3G4 edge, and one of the six typed shapes A--F. By R671, whenever a rail contains at least two Q blocks, their order along the rail is the increasing Q order. Since a configuration is its labelled edge set, reversing a displayed rail does not create a new configuration; for a canonical display, orient a rail so its Q labels increase, and if it contains at most one Q block break the remaining tie lexicographically. We now expand the six typed shapes.

(A) X-Q-Q-X plus an isolated Q. Choose the isolated Q in 3 ways. The other two Q labels then have a forced increasing order, while G3 and G4 may occupy the two path ends in 2 ways. Hence A contributes 3*2=6 configurations, exactly the six A entries displayed in R685.

(B) Q-Q-X-Q plus an isolated X. Choose the isolated X in 2 ways. On the other rail the three Q labels occur as G0,G1,G2 in increasing order, and the remaining X must occupy one of the two internal Q-gaps, giving 2 choices. Hence B contributes 2*2=4 configurations. These are exactly the four B entries, agreeing independently with the four cases already derived in R671.

(C) Q-Q-X plus Q-X. Choose the Q on the two-vertex rail in 3 ways. Choose which X label lies on that two-vertex rail in 2 ways. On the three-vertex rail the other X may be attached to either endpoint of the forced increasing pair of Q labels, giving 2 more choices. Hence C contributes 3*2*2=12 configurations, exactly the displayed C list.

(D) Q-Q plus X-Q-X. Choose the Q that is the centre of the X-Q-X component in 3 ways. The remaining two Q labels form the other edge. There is no extra factor of 2 from exchanging the two X ends: as a labelled edge set, the X-Q-X component has exactly the two edges from its centre Q to G3 and G4, and reversing its display changes no edge. Hence D contributes exactly 3 configurations.

(E) X-Q-X-Q plus an isolated Q. Choose the isolated Q in 3 ways. On the remaining four vertices there are two Q and two X labels and three Q-X edges. Such a path is exactly K_{2,2} with one edge deleted. Equivalently choose the degree-two Q in 2 ways and the degree-two X in 2 ways. Hence E contributes 3*2*2=12 configurations, precisely the twelve displayed E entries.

(F) Q-X-Q plus Q-X. Choose the Q belonging to the two-vertex component in 3 ways. Choose which X is its mate, equivalently which X is the centre of the three-vertex component, in 2 ways. The remaining two Q labels are then the endpoints of that three-vertex component. Hence F contributes 3*2=6 configurations, exactly the six displayed F entries.

Therefore the total number of labelled configurations is

    6+4+12+3+12+6 = 43.

The c_X=2 shapes are A--D, so their total is 6+4+12+3=25. The c_X=3 shapes are E,F, so their total is 12+6=18. This proves both the exhaustiveness and the stated distribution of the displayed list. It is also the same finite specification recorded independently in the project certificate M14: enumerate three-edge labelled path forests on G0,...,G4, forbid G3G4, require exactly two components, require c_X in {2,3}, and require increasing Q order on each rail. That deterministic enumeration returns the same 43 configurations and the same A6/B4/C12/D3/E12/F6 split.

For the transition claim, R671 says that in the monotone branch every Q-Q transition is oriented from the lower-index Q cell to the higher-index Q cell. There are only three possible Q-cell pairs, and R671 identifies them physically as

    G0G1 : u q3,
    G1G2 : q_{m-3} s,
    G0G2 : u s.

Thus every Q-Q adjacency appearing in the 43 configurations is one of exactly those named transitions, as claimed.

R671 also fixes the literal internal order of each Q block to the original Q orientation. Once a labelled contracted configuration is fixed, the canonical rail reading above fixes its block order for enumeration purposes. Each X cell is a physical two-vertex dimer and has at most two internal orientations. Therefore the two X dimers contribute at most 2*2=4 internal choices per labelled configuration, giving at most

    43*4 = 172

explicitly named normalized ordered rail-pair candidates. This is an upper bound only; seam constraints may remove candidates.

Finally, any tightness test wholly inside a Q block is automatic because that block is a contiguous subpath of the original tight path Q. A seam can inspect only the last two vertices of a Q block or the first two vertices of the next Q block, together with vertices of an adjacent X dimer. For G0=Q[0,1], G1=Q[3,m-3], and G2=Q[m-1,m], the only Q vertices that can occur in such seam triples are

    q0,q1,q3,q4,q_{m-4},q_{m-3},q_{m-1},q_m.

Adding the four X vertices a,b,c,z gives exactly the twelve named vertices in the statement. Hence for m>=9 every configuration-distinguishing seam condition is supported on this fixed twelve-vertex set. For m in {6,7,8}, some of those names coincide or the middle interval degenerates, leaving only finitely many exceptional coincidences. This proves the claimed m-uniform finite locality.
