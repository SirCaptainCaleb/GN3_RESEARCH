# R190 — Minimum-overlap-one replacement fan is impossible

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case, the global minimum overlap among distinct global-longest five-supports cannot equal one.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; accepted R4, R8 and R184.

## Proof
Assume for contradiction that the minimum overlap in the global-longest five-support family F is one. Fix A in F and put E=V(H)\\A, so |A|=|E|=5. For a in A and z in E write B(a,z)=(E\\{z}) union {a}. Let x,y be any two distinct vertices of E. By R8 the three-set P=E\\{x,y} supports a tight trimer. Accepted R184 applied to A,P,x,y says that for every a in A at least one of B(a,x),B(a,y) lies in F. Therefore for fixed a the failure set M_a={z in E:B(a,z) notin F} has size at most one. Hence at least four B(a,z) lie in F for each a. Across the five choices of a these give at least twenty distinct supports, each meeting A in exactly {a}.

Let barF={V(H)\\B:B in F}. By R4, F contains no complementary pair: complementary members would be disjoint tight five-path supports and hence a spanning two-cover, contradicting pc(H)=3. Thus F and barF are disjoint. For each replacement B(a,z) in F, its complement C(a,z)=(A\\{a}) union {z} belongs to barF and meets A in four vertices. Therefore every A in F has at least twenty neighbors in barF in J(10,5), so e_J(F,barF)>=20|F|.

For every four-set C, the six five-sets containing C form a K6 in J(10,5), and every Johnson edge belongs to exactly one such clique. Let r_C,s_C count F and barF vertices in that clique. Since F and barF are disjoint, r_C+s_C<=6, hence r_C s_C <= (r_C+s_C)^2/4 <= (3/2)(r_C+s_C). Summing over four-cores gives e_J(F,barF)<= (3/2)sum_C(r_C+s_C). Each five-set contains exactly five four-subsets, so sum_C r_C=5|F| and sum_C s_C=5|barF|=5|F|. Thus e_J(F,barF)<=15|F|, contradicting the lower bound 20|F|. Therefore minimum overlap one is impossible.
