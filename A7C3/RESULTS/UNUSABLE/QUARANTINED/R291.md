# R291 — Five-exterior carrier order at least 34 forces a fixed-trimer nonquiet interaction

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let K be any proper tight path, and fix any carrier vertex b in V(K). Then either H has a spanning two-cover or there exist five distinct exterior vertices d_1,d_2,y,r,s such that the two spokes S_i={b,d_i} are signed with common witness y, the three-set {y,r,s} supports one fixed tight trimer T, each S_i forms a direct mass-four balanced pair with a terminal dimer of T of opposite polarity, and after one pair birth is declared first the second birth is nonquiet by R160, forcing historical signed-support/anchor interaction or cross-generation pair interaction before unrelated reset. Thus the fixed-trimer nonquiet-interaction front door is completely capture-free and is available at every chosen vertex of every proper tight path.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is any proper graph-intrinsic tight path; b is any vertex of K; accepted R169 supplies at least five exterior vertices.

## Scope
Order-free capture-free repair and strengthening of the unusable R261 front door.

## Applicability
Use as the canonical fixed-trimer historical-interaction front door. Capture or puncture data may be carried separately if independently useful, but is not needed to create the pair family.

## Persistence
K,b,the chosen exterior vertices, centered trimer signs, fixed trimer T, pair births, and the R160 interaction are graph-intrinsic/historical under the parent theorems.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not attach capture ancestry to the spokes; does not invoke R155 or R197; the two paid descendants are not simultaneous; the R160 interaction is not itself a contradiction or spanning two-cover.

## Proof
R169 gives at least five vertices outside K. Choose distinct d_1,d_2,y,r,s there. For i=1,2, R3 gives exactly one tight d_i-middle orientation on {b,d_i,y}; R41 reads S_i={b,d_i} as a signed terminal dimer with witness y. R8 gives a tight trimer T on {y,r,s}. R41 gives its two terminal dimers opposite polarities. For each i choose a terminal dimer M_i of T with polarity opposite to S_i. Since b lies on K and d_i is outside T, S_i is disjoint from T and hence from M_i. R39 gives two direct mass-four balanced-pair births. Both births use terminal dimers of the same fixed T and partner dimers disjoint from T, so after one is taken as historical first, R160 makes the second nonquiet before unrelated reset. No capture event or transport is used.
