# R129 — Minimum-overlap-two deletion gateway and disjoint-core fork

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume A,B are globally minimum-overlap supports of global-longest tight five-paths with |A∩B|=2. Put S=A∩B, U=A\\B, V=B\\A, and R=V(H)\\(A∪B), so |S|=|R|=2 and |U|=|V|=3. Then H-S contains a tight five-path with support D in F. Every such D has block profile (|D∩U|,|D∩V|,|D∩R|) equal to one of (2,2,1),(2,3,0),(3,2,0). Consequently at least one of A or B, call it L, has two distinct minimum-overlap partners among {A,B,D} whose two intersection cores inside L are disjoint 2-subsets.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; A,B are a globally minimum-overlap pair in the longest-five support family F with |A∩B|=2.

## Scope
Pure support/deletion geometry for the minimum-overlap-two branch.

## Applicability
Use as the parent normalization if R128 audits and the surviving minimum overlap is two. It replaces arbitrary overlap-two pairs by a longest support carrying two disjoint minimum-overlap cores.

## Persistence
All support blocks and intersections are graph-intrinsic; the constructed existence of D does not depend on retaining a particular deletion cover afterward.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not exclude minimum overlap two. Does not assert D is unique, does not force the (2,2,1) profile, and does not assert simultaneous currentness of A,B,D as paths.

## Proof
Let S=A∩B, U=A\\B, V=B\\A, and R=V(H)\\(A∪B). Since |A|=|B|=5 and |S|=2 in an order-ten graph, |U|=|V|=3 and |R|=2. By R5, H-S has path-cover number exactly two, hence an exact two-cover. By R9 no tight path has more than five vertices, so the two component orders are either 5+3 or 4+4. In the 5+3 case, let D be the support of the five-path. In the 4+4 case, let Q be one four-path and let X be any three vertices of the other four-path. By R8, X admits a tight trimer orientation. Q and that trimer are disjoint, so R126 gives a tight five-path whose support D lies in H-S. In either case D is an order-five tight path, hence global-longest by R9, so D∈F. Because A,B are globally minimum-overlap with intersection two, |D∩A|>=2 and |D∩B|>=2. Since D is disjoint from S, these equal |D∩U| and |D∩V|. Writing u=|D∩U|, v=|D∩V|, r=|D∩R| gives u,v>=2, u,v<=3, r<=2, and u+v+r=5. Therefore (u,v,r) is exactly (2,2,1), (2,3,0), or (3,2,0). At least one of u,v equals two. If u=2, take L=A: L∩B=S and L∩D=D∩U are disjoint 2-subsets of L, and both B,D are minimum-overlap partners of L. If v=2, the symmetric choice L=B works. This proves the fork.
