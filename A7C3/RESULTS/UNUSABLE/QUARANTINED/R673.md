## Proof
Let T be a sigma_G(T)=0 exact cover in the accepted R667 five-cell frame. Then each X cell is a complete contiguous two-vertex T-block. Fix one selected transition between a Q cell and an X cell, and let y be its Q endpoint. For G_3={a,c}, orient the complete T-block as S=(a,c) or S=(c,a). R649 supplies fixed head polarity on either tested orientation, with at least three named head witnesses: L,R,b for (a,c), and L,R,z for (c,a). Choose one fixed witness w distinct from y. If the T transition enters the block, the literal consecutive triple (y,S) is tight, so y is a second head witness on the same tested orientation; R38 gives a same-head two-witness collision. If the transition leaves the block, the consecutive triple (S,y) is tight, giving tail polarity opposite to the fixed head polarity. The fixed source trimer (w,S) and fourth vertex y satisfy R518, so a tight Hamilton P4 exists on {w} union V(S) union {y}. For G_4={b,z}, R649 supplies fixed tail polarity on either tested orientation, with witnesses L,R,a for (b,z) and L,R,c for (z,b). Choose w distinct from y. A transition leaving G_4 gives a second tail witness and hence R38; a transition entering gives opposite head polarity and hence R518 on the fixed source trimer (S,w). This applies independently to every Q-X edge of the sigma=0 forest, proving R673.
