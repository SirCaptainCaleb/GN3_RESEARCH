The identities a_0=head(A) and b_q=tail(B), and therefore the original two-cut ancestry, remain named historical coordinates in this comparison even though the new residual rails U,V need not equal or descend from A^+,B^- or the interior of P.

## Proof
The pair a_0,b_q consists of distinct vertices because they are endpoints of the vertex-simple bridge P in R485. Therefore R153 applies to D={a_0,b_q}. It yields an exact two-cover H-D=U sqcup V and, over those same residual rails, minimum three-cover representatives U sqcup V sqcup (a_0,b_q) and U sqcup V sqcup (b_q,a_0). The latter literally selects the reverse bridge dimer (b_q,a_0). Nothing in this application changes the already certified graph identities a_0=head(A) and b_q=tail(B); those are historical coordinates attached to the same physical vertices. R153 explicitly does not preserve unrelated ancestry in U,V, so no such stronger claim is made. This proves exactly the stated cross-fiber reverse-dimer shadow and no synchronization beyond it.
