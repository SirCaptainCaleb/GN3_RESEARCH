# R103 — Transversal zero-packet paired-trimer reroot symmetry

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the transversal branch and the simultaneous zero-excess alternative of R101. In the x-frame let S_x=T∪{x}, let G_x={gamma,delta,epsilon}, and let Q_x be the zero-excess tight four-path on {y,alpha,z,beta}. Choose any tight trimer orientation on S_x. Then Q_x, G_x, S_x form a spanning minimum three-cover. Consequently Q_x∪G_x is an exact two-cover of H-S_x and, in the same displayed representative, Q_x∪S_x is an exact two-cover of H-G_x. Cyclically the same holds for (S_y,G_y,Q_y) and (S_z,G_z,Q_z), where G_y={beta,delta,epsilon} and G_z={alpha,delta,epsilon}. Thus each zero-excess four-rail is literally preserved under exchanging its deleted common triple with its opposite R-trimer.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R101 simultaneous zero-excess alternative.

## Scope
Exact representative-level reroot symmetry inside the transversal equality kernel.

## Applicability
Provides three paired deletion roots sharing the same four-rails; useful for applying deletion or return theorems from either trimer side without changing the four-path representative.

## Persistence
The preserved four-path and the two exact two-covers are representative-level facts after a trimer orientation of S_i is chosen; their physical supports are fixed.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert compatibility of the chosen S_x,S_y,S_z trimer orientations across the three different displayed covers, and does not create a spanning two-cover by itself.

## Proof
Again use the x-frame; the other two are cyclic. R101 supplies an exact two-cover Q_x∪G_x of H-S_x, where Q_x is a tight four-path and G_x is a tight trimer. By R8, S_x admits a tight trimer orientation. Hence Q_x,G_x,S_x are three pairwise disjoint tight paths covering all ten vertices. Because the standing graph is a counterexample to spanning two-cover, this displayed three-cover is minimum. Apply componentwise exact re-rooting R22. Deleting the S_x component leaves Q_x∪G_x as an exact two-cover of H-S_x, while deleting the G_x component leaves Q_x∪S_x as an exact two-cover of H-G_x, with Q_x literally unchanged in the same displayed representative. Apply the same argument to the y- and z-frame zero packets.
