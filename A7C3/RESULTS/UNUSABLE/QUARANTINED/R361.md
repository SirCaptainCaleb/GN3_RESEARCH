# R361 — First-contact mirror branch reduces to fixed-turn exit, mate clause, or same-middle target-rotation cycle

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the literal first-contact mirror-trimer branch of accepted R329 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten, with J_0=(a_0,x,c_0). Then, unless a spanning two-cover occurs, one of the following finite alternatives holds. (1) The protected fixed-turn lineage on J_0 takes one of the accepted explicit R172/R343 historical contact or pair-interaction exits. (2) At some stage there is a tight turn J_i=(a_i,x,c_i) such that some exact two-cover of H-{a_i,c_i} places x at a rail endpoint; then provisional R358 supplies an explicit R173-admissible two-hole mate clause. (3) There is a cyclic sequence of distinct outer pairs P_0,...,P_{m-1}, m>=2, all disjoint from x, with P_i={a_i,c_i}, such that consecutive pairs are vertex-disjoint cyclically and J_i is the unique x-middle tight turn on P_i union {x}; for every i, an R18 currentization of J_i lies in the all-internal regime of provisional R359, and provisional R360 supplies P_{i+1} as the endpoint pair of one literal source residual rail together with its reverse-shield/refund ancestry and target-aligned fixed-turn floor. Thus the only branch avoiding both an explicit fixed-turn exit and every endpoint mate clause is a finite same-middle target-rotation cycle carrying a literal currentization/shield/refund certificate on every transition.
