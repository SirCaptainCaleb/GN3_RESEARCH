# R125 — Universal four-witness fold cell and anchor clique

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In any transversal common-triple exact-cover frame of R124, let Q=(q1,q2,q3,q4) be the four-path, let K be the three-path, let s be the distinguished deleted singleton for that frame, and put W={s} union supp(K). Define the six-set F={q1,q4} union W. Then for every w in W, the folded trimer J_w=(q4,w,q1) is tight and, for any tight trimer L_w on W\\{w}, J_w and L_w form an exact 3+3 two-cover of the same physical six-set F. Moreover, for every ordered pair of distinct witnesses w,r in W, L_w may be oriented with middle vertex r; hence R41 gives a central-anchor interaction with anchor pair {w,r}. Consequently the four common witnesses of R124 form a complete K4 of graph-intrinsic central-anchor interaction certificates, all rooted in one physical six-vertex fold cell F.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; any one of the R100 transversal common-triple deletion frames and any displayed exact 4+3 cover in that frame; accepted universal fold fan R124.

## Scope
Excess-independent exact-cell compression of the R124 fold fan. It applies to both positive- and zero-excess covers and does not use payment or actualization.

## Applicability
Use in the remaining positive-excess branch before opening payment descendants. In the pure interleaving face, the physical type of the outer pair {q1,q4} (whole wing versus cross-wing) becomes the only extra support-incidence datum on top of this fixed four-witness interaction cell.

## Persistence
The six-set F and the four folded trimers J_w are graph-intrinsic relative to the displayed Q ordering. The exact 3+3 covers and R41 interaction certificates may use different orientations of the complementary witness trimers and are separate representatives.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert simultaneous currentness of the four 3+3 decompositions, a common orientation of the complementary trimers, coupled payment descendants, or an immediate spanning 5+5 cover. The K4 is a family of historical graph-intrinsic interaction certificates, not one selected interaction graph in a single representative.

## Proof
Fix the x-frame; the y- and z-frames are cyclic. Write S_x=T union {x}, let Q=(q1,q2,q3,q4) and K be the displayed exact 4+3 cover of H-S_x, and set W={x} union supp(K). By R124, for every w in W the ordered trimer J_w=(q4,w,q1) is tight. Fix w. The three-set W\\{w} admits a tight trimer orientation by boundary antisymmetry R3: more strongly, if r is any prescribed vertex of W\\{w} and a,b are the other two vertices, exactly one of (a,r,b) and (b,r,a) is tight, so we may choose a tight complementary trimer L_{w,r} with middle r. The two trimers J_w and L_{w,r} are vertex-disjoint and together cover F={q1,q4} union W. The complement of F in H is exactly T union {q2,q3}, a four-set. By R5, deleting that four-set leaves path-cover number exactly two. Hence the displayed two-trimer cover J_w union L_{w,r} is an exact 3+3 two-cover of that same six-set F. Since J_w has middle w and L_{w,r} has middle r, R41 applies to the two disjoint tight trimers and gives the central-anchor interaction through the unordered anchor pair {w,r}. As w and r were arbitrary distinct members of the four-set W, all six unordered witness pairs occur. Thus the R124 four-witness fan is supported on one exact six-vertex fold cell carrying a complete K4 of central-anchor interactions.
