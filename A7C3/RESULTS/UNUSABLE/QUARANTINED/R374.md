# R374 — All-internal trimer return has a terminal-coordinate paid-floor fan

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal regime of R359 for a proper tight trimer J=(a,x,c), with one R18 currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V. Let Z be the set of physical vertices lying in the four terminal dimers D_U^L,D_U^R,D_V^L,D_V^R of the two source residual rails. Then for every prescribed z in Z there is an alternative ancestry-bearing continuation with the following property: either H has a spanning two-cover, or the lineage reaches a mass-two balanced-pair floor in which {z} is literally one singleton support. Consequently R249 launches a pair-bearing fixed-turn lineage whose outer target pair contains z. No same-middle condition is asserted for this new turn. Thus every all-internal trimer return carries a selectable fan of paid-floor coordinates over all terminal vertices of both residual rails, in addition to the special same-middle endpoint-pair rotation of R360.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; J is in the all-internal R359 return regime; z lies on one of the four terminal dimers of the chosen source rails.

## Scope
Order-free payment/fixed-turn branching theorem.

## Applicability
Use to escape a restrictive same-middle rotation by choosing a specific physical terminal coordinate for the next paid-floor/fixed-turn lineage.

## Persistence
The chosen terminal dimer, inherited x-turn sign, prescribed endpoint z, payment orientation, floor coordinate z, and subsequent fixed-turn target role persist in the chosen lineage.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe the second floor singleton, does not guarantee the next fixed-turn middle is x, and does not synchronize choices for two different z.

## Proof
By R373 the four terminal dimers form two cross-rail opposite-sign mass-four diagonals, and every one of the four terminal dimers belongs to one of those diagonals. Choose a terminal dimer D containing the prescribed z and its cross-rail opposite partner. The sign of D is certified by one of the named R359 shield turns with witness x, so R224 applies to the ancestry-bearing balanced-pair lineage and allows the refund orientation selecting endpoint z. Unless a spanning two-cover occurs, the lineage reaches a mass-two floor containing singleton {z}. R249 then enters a fixed-turn lineage whose outer pair is that floor pair, hence contains z.
