# R14 — Low-overlap longest-support pair

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
There exist A,B in the longest-five support family F with |A intersect B| at most three.

## Scope
Order-ten smallest counterexample.

## Proof
Assume every distinct pair in F intersects in at least four. Since supports have size five, choose A=C+{a}, B=C+{b} with |C|=4. Any further D either contains C, or omits c in C and the two intersection inequalities force D=(C-{c})+{a,b}. Thus F lies either in a common 4-star or a common 6-top. The first contradicts empty total intersection; the second contradicts full union on ten vertices.
