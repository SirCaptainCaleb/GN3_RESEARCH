# R392 — No-interaction regime forces universal endpoint exposure after every pair deletion

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten. Then either H contains a source-trimer-localized historical signed-support/anchor interaction or cross-generation pair interaction of the type produced by R390, or the following universal endpoint property holds: for every physical pair {a,c} subset V(H) and every vertex x outside {a,c}, there exists an exact two-cover of H-{a,c} in which x is a rail endpoint. In the universal-endpoint branch, each triple {a,x,c} also supplies an explicit R173-admissible mate clause by R358 after orienting its unique x-middle tight trimer.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; accepted boundary antisymmetry R3 and provisional R390.

## Scope
Order-free global structural corollary of the universal trimer front door. It packages the absence of the historical-interaction output into an endpoint-universality property for every pair-deletion subsystem.

## Applicability
Use when attempting to consume the mate-clause side of R391. If one globally excludes source-trimer historical interactions, every pair deletion becomes endpoint-universal, a much stronger exact-cover condition that may be attacked by endpoint, graft, maximin, or mate-cycle machinery.

## Persistence
Endpoint exposure is existential separately for each triple (a,x,c); the various exact two-covers need not coincide. Each resulting mate clause is graph-intrinsic with its own literal proposal certificate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert one exact two-cover exposes all vertices simultaneously. Does not assert endpoint universality alone closes the conjecture. Does not conjoin the many return representatives. Does not say the mate clauses are automatically linked into an R173 cycle.

## Proof
Fix arbitrary distinct a,c and x outside {a,c}. Boundary antisymmetry on the physical triple {a,x,c} gives exactly one tight x-middle orientation J, either (a,x,c) or (c,x,a). Apply provisional R390 to this J. Its second output is a source-J historical signed-support/anchor or cross-generation pair interaction. If this occurs for at least one choice of a,c,x, the first alternative of the corollary holds. Otherwise it occurs for none. Hence for every choice R390 must be in its endpoint-return branch, which by construction comes from an exact two-cover of H-{a,c} placing x at a rail endpoint. Since a,c,x were arbitrary, every pair-deletion subsystem is endpoint-universal in this existential-per-vertex sense. Accepted R358 applied to each exposed x-middle trimer gives the associated literal two-hole mate clause.
