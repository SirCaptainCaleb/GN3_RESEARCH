# R338 — All-internal outer-pair returns force a four-way reverse endpoint shield

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the literal first-contact mirror-trimer branch of accepted R329 with current minimum three-cover H=J sqcup U sqcup V, J=(p,x,z). Assume further that every exact two-cover of H-{p,z} places x internally on its rail, i.e. the all-internal branch of the R337 return normal form. Write U=(u_1,...,u_r) and V=(v_1,...,v_s) in their current tight orders. Then all four direct endpoint attachments of x to U and V are bad: (x,u_1,u_2), (u_{r-1},u_r,x), (x,v_1,v_2), and (v_{s-1},v_s,x) are bad, with the obvious interpretation when a rail has order two. Hence boundary antisymmetry forces the four reverse turns (u_2,u_1,x), (x,u_r,u_{r-1}), (v_2,v_1,x), and (x,v_s,v_{s-1}) tight. Thus the all-internal contact-return branch carries a graph-intrinsic four-way reverse endpoint shield of the captured anchor x against both literal residual rails of the source mirror root.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; accepted R329 mirror root H=J sqcup U sqcup V; no exact two-cover of H-{p,z} has x as a rail endpoint.

## Scope
Order-free source-root obstruction for the all-internal outer-pair return branch.

## Applicability
Use to consume the all-internal branch of the R329 return trichotomy. It replaces generic component-drop language by four explicit reverse turns tied to the actual source residual rails.

## Persistence
The source rail orders U,V, physical anchor x, and the four forced reverse turns are graph-intrinsic once fixed in the R329 mirror-root representative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert U or V is globally longest. Does not itself currentize the four reverse turns simultaneously with J in another representative, and does not close the conjecture.

## Proof
Because J is a current component of a minimum three-cover, R22 makes U sqcup V an exact two-cover of H-J; R168 therefore gives |U|,|V|>=2. Suppose (x,u_1,u_2) were tight. Then (x,u_1,...,u_r) together with V would be a literal two-cover of H-{p,z}; by R5 this pair-deletion subsystem has path-cover number exactly two, so the displayed two-cover is an exact return with x as a rail endpoint, contrary to the all-internal hypothesis. Hence (x,u_1,u_2) is bad and its exact reverse (u_2,u_1,x) is tight by R3. The same argument at the other end of U and both ends of V gives the remaining three reverse turns.
