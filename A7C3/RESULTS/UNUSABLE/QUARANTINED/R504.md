# R504 — Bridge-cross adaptive capture is fork-witness blind

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain the anti-aligned bridge setup of R502, with D={a_0,b_q}, W=H-D, and a selected bridge-interior cross state xy in an exact two-cover of W. The witness-adaptive capture step used in R503 does not consume any special R495 DR/HF/TF witness packet. Indeed for every vertex w outside D, boundary antisymmetry R3 applied with middle a_0 gives exactly one of (w,a_0,b_q) and (b_q,a_0,w) tight; either turn is a valid signed incarnation of the physical support D with witness w. Since A^+,B^-,P^o are nonempty and |P^o|>=2, W has at least four vertices. For the two-vertex marker {x,y}, choose any w in W-{x,y}; its R3 sign on D avoids the marker, so accepted R37 already makes xy capture-capable and permits capture of either marker endpoint. Thus the bare conclusion of R503 that the P^o endpoint of xy can be captured through D is fork-blind and follows from R3,R37,R502 alone. Any downstream theorem claiming to consume R495 via this capture must use additional fork data after capture, such as the exact one-hole proposal/mate identities or a special witness role; the mere existence of the capture is not such a consumer.

## Hypotheses
Anti-aligned bridge setup with p,q>=1,m>=3; accepted R502 selected cross state xy in an exact H-D two-cover; accepted R3,R37.

## Scope
Order-free role-consumption fence for the R503 capture route. It distinguishes generic sign manufacture on the bridge dimer from genuine use of R495 candidate-aware fork data.

## Applicability
Use when evaluating any R495-to-capture continuation. Do not count adaptive capture of a selected D-deletion state as spending the R495 fork unless a later step explicitly uses the fork one-hole candidates, exact mates, ancestral witness identity, or another non-generic coordinate.

## Persistence
Retain D, the exact D-deletion cover, selected marker xy, and chosen generic witness w. The conclusion is logical non-consumption: the R495 witnesses may remain valid historical facts but are not needed for the capture inference.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not invalidate R503 mathematically and does not say capture is useless. It says only that the capture-existence step is available without R495. It does not rule out a later capture-based theorem that genuinely reuses the fork mate/proposal data.

## Proof
Let xy be the selected cross state from R502. Because p,q>=1 and |P^o|>=2, W=A^+ union B^- union P^o has at least four vertices, so choose w in W-{x,y}. Apply R3 to the three distinct vertices w,a_0,b_q with middle a_0. Exactly one of (w,a_0,b_q) and (b_q,a_0,w) is tight. In the first case D occurs as the terminal physical dimer (a_0,b_q); in the second it occurs as the initial physical dimer (b_q,a_0). Thus in either case D has an admissible signed incarnation with witness w. This witness is outside the selected marker xy by construction. R37 therefore applies in the exact D-deletion cover containing xy and gives a capture certificate for either chosen endpoint of xy. No R495 turn, witness, one-hole proposal, or DR/HF/TF distinction occurs in the argument. Hence adaptive capture existence is fork-witness blind.
