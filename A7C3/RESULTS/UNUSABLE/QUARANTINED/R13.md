# R13 — Longest-five support family geometry

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let F be the family of supports of global-longest tight five-paths in an order-ten smallest counterexample. Then union F is V(H), intersection F is empty, and A intersect B is nonempty for every A,B in F.

## Scope
Order-ten smallest counterexample.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Support membership is graph-intrinsic; it does not make a path current in an unrelated representative.
