**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the beyond-order-ten regime. Then either the earlier-contact branch of accepted R329 already occurs, or R329 supplies a current minimum three-cover H=J sqcup U sqcup V with J=(p,x,z), where D={x,z} is the actual captured crossing and E={p,x} a genuine old carrier edge. In this literal mirror-root branch, choose any exact two-cover P sqcup Q of H-{p,z}. By R168 the rail containing x has order at least three. If some such exact return has x as a rail endpoint, then restoring J at that endpoint gives one of the explicit rooted two-hole candidate-cover clauses: for P=(x,a,b,...) one has (a,z,x) tight OR (b,a,z) tight, while for P=(...,b,a,x) one has (p,a,b) tight OR (x,p,a) tight. Otherwise every exact two-cover of H-{p,z} has x internal. For any one such return, writing P=(...,a,x,b,...), deleting x gives a literal three-cover P_L sqcup P_R sqcup Q of the exact same residue W=H-{p,x,z} that the source mirror root covers by the exact two-cover U sqcup V. Thus the all-internal branch is a same-residue 3-to-2 component-drop cell; in particular R159 supplies a graph-intrinsic balanced pair, while the selected residual star {a,x},{x,b} records the role-sensitive return geometry before x is deleted.

## Proof
Assume the literal R329 mirror root H=J sqcup U sqcup V, J=(p,x,z). By R5 choose an exact two-cover P sqcup Q of H-{p,z}; R168 gives |P|,|Q|>=3. Suppose x lies on P. If x is an endpoint, orient P from x as (x,a,b,...) or toward x as (...,b,a,x). In the first case the spanning proposal (p,x,z,a,b,...) sqcup Q has only the seam turns (x,z,a),(z,a,b) uncertified, so no-two-cover plus R3 gives (a,z,x) or (b,a,z). The terminally reversed case gives the dual clause (p,a,b) or (x,p,a). If no exact return has x as endpoint, then x is internal in every return. For one such P=(...,a,x,b,...), P-x is exactly two nonempty tight path fragments P_L,P_R. Hence P_L sqcup P_R sqcup Q is a literal three-cover of W=H-{p,x,z}. Because J is a current component of a minimum three-cover, R22 says U sqcup V is an exact two-cover of the same W. This is the claimed same-residue component drop; R159 applies since W is proper.
