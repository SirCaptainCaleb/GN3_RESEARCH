# R381 — Every decorated target-rotation state has at least four off-transition paid-floor escapes

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume one transition P_i->P_{i+1} in the decorated same-middle target-rotation cycle of R370, sourced from an all-internal R359 currentization H=J_i sqcup U_i sqcup V_i. Let Z_i be the terminal-coordinate set of that currentization. Then |Z_i|>=6 by R376, while the designated next target pair P_{i+1} consists of exactly two vertices of Z_i, namely the endpoints of the chosen long source residual rail. Hence at least four vertices z in Z_i\\P_{i+1} are available. For each such z, R374 supplies an alternative ancestry-bearing continuation that either closes or reaches a mass-two floor whose target pair contains z; therefore that alternative fixed-turn target pair is not the designated pair P_{i+1}. Thus every state of a decorated same-middle cycle has at least four independent physical coordinate choices that escape the prescribed next transition.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; one decorated all-internal transition in the R370 cycle.

## Scope
Quantitative branching corollary showing the same-middle cycle is not a closed deterministic state graph.

## Applicability
Use when attempting to escape or destabilize a target-rotation cycle by choosing a different paid-floor coordinate at one state.

## Persistence
Each escape lineage retains its chosen terminal coordinate and floor ancestry separately; lineages are alternative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not guarantee the alternative target pair lies outside all other cycle pairs, does not synchronize four escapes, and does not itself force an exit or mate clause.

## Proof
R376 gives at least six distinct selectable terminal vertices in the source all-internal currentization. R360/R370 choose P_{i+1} as the two endpoints of one source residual rail, so P_{i+1} is a two-element subset of this terminal set. At least four terminal coordinates remain outside P_{i+1}. Applying R374 to any such z gives a floor pair containing z; since z is not in P_{i+1}, that floor pair cannot equal the designated next pair.
