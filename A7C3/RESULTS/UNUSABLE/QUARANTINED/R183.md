# R183 — Minimum-overlap-two type (2,2,2) motif is impossible

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case with minimum longest-support overlap two, the type-(2,2,2) disjoint-core triangle of R130/R131 cannot occur. Hence minimum longest-support overlap two is impossible.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; minimum longest-support overlap equals two; accepted type-(2,2,2) normalization R130/R131; accepted three-exterior pair-extension lemma R146; longest-path value R9.

## Scope
Direct support-level closure of the remaining minimum-overlap-two triangle motif. No sign, payment, currentization, return, or ancestry machinery is used.

## Applicability
Use after R147 has removed type (2,2,3), or directly whenever the R130/R131 type-(2,2,2) motif is present. Together with R147 this eliminates minimum overlap two.

## Persistence
The contradiction is graph-intrinsic on one fixed short trimer, two vertices of one overlap core, and one vertex of the opposite longest support.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not address minimum overlap one and does not itself produce a spanning 5+5 cover.

## Proof
Let L,B,D be the type-(2,2,2) triangle from R130/R131, so L=X∪Y∪{p}, B=X∪Z∪{b}, D=Y∪Z∪{d}, where X,Y,Z are pairwise disjoint two-sets and e is the unique exterior vertex. Write X={x1,x2}. By R131, the three-set P={p,b,e} is the short side of an exact 5+3 root after deleting X; by R8 choose a tight trimer orientation on P. Observe that P is disjoint from D, because D=Y∪Z∪{d}. Choose any r∈D. The vertices x1,x2,r are distinct and lie outside P. By R146, at least one of the five-sets P∪{x1,x2}, P∪{x1,r}, P∪{x2,r} supports a tight five-vertex path. By R9 its support is a member of the global-longest support family. Yet each of these three supports meets D in at most one vertex: P∩D is empty, x1 and x2 are outside D, and among the three added exterior vertices only r belongs to D. This contradicts the assumption that the global minimum overlap between distinct longest-five supports is two. Therefore the type-(2,2,2) motif is impossible. Since R147 already eliminates type-(2,2,3), minimum overlap two is impossible.
