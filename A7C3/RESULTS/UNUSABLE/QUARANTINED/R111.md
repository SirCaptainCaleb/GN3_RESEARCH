# R111 — Transversal neighboring-root symmetric-difference shield bridge

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the R106 transversal triangle and the simultaneous zero-excess alternative of R101. Write K_A={z,beta,gamma}, K_B={y,alpha,gamma}, K_C={x,alpha,beta} for the three short trimers of the common-marker roots, and W_x={y,alpha,z,beta}, W_y={x,alpha,z,gamma}, W_z={x,beta,y,gamma} for the three zero-packet four-rail supports. Then K_A∩K_B={gamma} and K_A△K_B=W_x; K_A∩K_C={beta} and K_A△K_C=W_y; K_B∩K_C={alpha} and K_B△K_C=W_z. Moreover the corresponding W_i supports the R101 tight four-path Q_i and is shielded, in the sense of R102, by the shared short-trimer vertex, both common-marker vertices delta,epsilon, and the unique triangle wing vertex indexing that frame. Thus every neighboring pair of common-marker 5+3 roots has its short-trimer symmetric difference realized as exactly one shielded zero-packet four-rail.

## Persistence

## Proof
From R106, K_A={z,beta,gamma}, K_B={y,alpha,gamma}, K_C={x,alpha,beta}. Therefore K_A∩K_B={gamma} and K_A△K_B={z,beta,y,alpha}=W_x. Similarly K_A∩K_C={beta} and K_A△K_C={z,gamma,x,alpha}=W_y, while K_B∩K_C={alpha} and K_B△K_C={y,gamma,x,beta}=W_z. By R101, in the simultaneous zero-excess face these three four-sets support the tight four-paths Q_x,Q_y,Q_z. By R102, W_x is shielded by D_x={x,gamma,delta,epsilon}, W_y by D_y={y,beta,delta,epsilon}, and W_z by D_z={z,alpha,delta,epsilon}. In each case the singleton K-pair intersection is one of the shield witnesses and delta,epsilon are the two common-marker witnesses. This proves all asserted identities and the shield interface.
