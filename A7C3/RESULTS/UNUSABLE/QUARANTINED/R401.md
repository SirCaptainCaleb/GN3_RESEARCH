# R401 — Aligned three-cap dual saturation normal form

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume one exact triple-deletion cover H-T=U sqcup V supports restoration caps for all three deleted vertices T={a,b,c}. Unless H has a spanning two-cover, one of two dual saturation packets occurs. (R) Residual-support saturation: all three caps concentrate at one physical end, so the terminal physical dimer at that end carries three same-polarity signed certificates with the three distinct witnesses a,b,c in one tested orientation. (D) Deleted-support saturation: the cap occupancy is 2+1, and for the two deleted vertices sharing one end, their physical dimer carries both opposite tested orientations as signed supports of one common polarity with the same witness equal to the residual terminal vertex at that end. Thus common-shadow endpoint universality cannot remain diffuse without closure: it concentrates either three deleted witnesses onto one residual dimer or two orientations of one deleted dimer onto one residual witness.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; one exact H-T cover supports all three caps; accepted/provisional R400.

## Scope
Order-free dual saturation reformulation of the aligned three-cap nonclosing residue.

## Applicability
Use as the compact local target after cap synchronization. The two branches exchange the roles of deleted and residual vertices and may admit different signed-support consumers.

## Persistence
All supports, witnesses, tested orientations and cap-end identities are graph-intrinsic/literal in the common-shadow frame.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert either saturation packet closes or can be paid simultaneously. Does not apply when the three caps live on different exact H-T representatives.

## Proof
R400 excludes the distinct-end occupancy by closure. In its three-cap concentrated branch, the three cap turns at the common end are three same-polarity signs on the same terminal dimer with witnesses a,b,c.
