# R119 — Transversal common-core-marker triple 3+3 cell

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the transversal triangle coordinates of R100, let T be the common two-vertex core and M={delta,epsilon} the common exterior marker, and put N=V(H)\\(T∪M)={x,y,z,alpha,beta,gamma}. Then the single four-vertex deletion H-(T∪M) admits three exact 3+3 covers on the same physical six-vertex cell N: (i) {x,y,alpha} ⊔ {z,beta,gamma}; (ii) {x,z,beta} ⊔ {y,alpha,gamma}; (iii) {y,z,gamma} ⊔ {x,alpha,beta}. More strongly, in any one of these three partitions and for any prescribed vertex a of the first triple and b of the second triple, the two triples can be oriented as tight trimers with middle vertices a and b respectively; hence R41 supplies a synchronized two-anchor interaction through {a,b}. Thus each of the three exact 3+3 decompositions carries a 3-by-3 middle-anchor interaction grid, all rooted in the same common deletion cell H-(T∪M).

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; transversal alternative and R100 coordinates.

## Scope
Support-level/common-deletion compression valid throughout the transversal branch, not only the zero-excess face.

## Applicability
Use as a parent exact cell for private-anchor and wing-anchor interactions. In the zero-excess parity branch, choose the parity-forced private anchor and the second anchor from R117 as the two prescribed middles in the corresponding one of these exact 3+3 partitions.

## Persistence
The deletion set T∪M and the three physical 3+3 partitions are graph-intrinsic. Each prescribed-middle trimer orientation and the resulting R41 interaction is graph-intrinsic as a separate certificate; simultaneous currentness of all 27 orientations is not asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
The three exact covers and their different prescribed-middle orientations are separate representatives/certificates. The lemma does not assert a spanning two-cover of H, does not couple paid descendants, and does not make all interaction-grid cells simultaneously current.

## Proof
By R100, after deleting T and M the remaining vertices are exactly N={x,y,z,alpha,beta,gamma}. The three displayed pairs of triples are complementary partitions of N. R8 gives a tight trimer orientation on every three-set, so each displayed partition is a two-path cover of H-(T∪M). Since |T∪M|=4, R5 says the path-cover number of this deletion is exactly two; hence all three displayed covers are exact 3+3 covers. For the strengthened middle-anchor statement, fix one displayed partition X⊔Y and prescribed a∈X, b∈Y. On X, choose the reversal pair of ordered triples having a as middle. By R3 exactly one member of that reversal pair is tight, so X has a tight trimer orientation with middle a. Likewise Y has a tight orientation with middle b. The two trimers are vertex-disjoint, so R41 applies and gives the synchronized two-anchor interaction through {a,b}. There are 3×3 choices per partition. Each is a separate graph-intrinsic orientation/interaction certificate, so no simultaneous-currentness claim is needed.
