# R398 — Pair-deletion endpoint-or-persistent-middle dichotomy

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten. Fix a physical pair D={a,c} and a third vertex x outside D. Orient the unique x-middle tight trimer J on {a,x,c}. Then exactly one of the following return regimes holds. (E) Some exact two-cover of H-D places x at a rail endpoint; in that case R358 gives an explicit candidate-aware two-hole mate clause anchored on J. (I) Every exact two-cover of H-D places x internally; in that case for any chosen exact cover H-D=P sqcup Q containing x internally, the R359 all-internal return structure applies, and after currentizing J over the corresponding triple-deletion residual rails the source turn J carries the accepted R389 bilateral four-birth interaction fan localized at middle x. Thus persistent internality across one fixed pair-deletion fiber is the exact negative-space alternative to endpoint exposure, and the historical interaction output should be regarded as attached to this stronger static fiber property.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; D={a,c}; x is distinct from a,c; accepted R3,R358,R359,R389.

## Scope

## Applicability
Use to retain the fixed-deletion universal-internality predicate instead of collapsing immediately to anonymous historical interaction. The global no-persistent-middle branch is exactly endpoint universality.

## Persistence
The endpoint-existence statement and persistent-internality predicate are properties of the fixed deletion fiber H-D. Any chosen all-internal source cover and its R389 interaction fan are representative/historical data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the historical interaction closes. Does not assert a unique exact cover. Does not synchronize different all-internal source covers.

## Proof
Boundary antisymmetry gives one tight x-middle orientation J on {a,x,c}. Apply R359 to J. By its quantified return classification, either some exact H-{a,c} cover has x as endpoint, in which case R358 supplies the mate clause, or every exact H-{a,c} cover has x internal. In the latter case choose any exact return and use its induced R359 source/currentization packet; accepted R389 then supplies the bilateral four-birth interaction fan at the same source turn J and middle x. The two regimes are mutually exclusive by definition and exhaustive.
