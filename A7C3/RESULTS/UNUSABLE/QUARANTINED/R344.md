# R344 — All-internal first-contact return has a canonical residual-rail endpoint floor

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal outer-pair return branch of accepted R338 for a first-contact mirror root H=J sqcup U sqcup V with J=(p,x,z). Since H is in the beyond-order-ten regime, |U|+|V|=|H|-3>=8, so at least one residual rail, say U=(u_1,...,u_r), has r>=4. The two disjoint terminal physical dimers S_L={u_1,u_2} and S_R={u_{r-1},u_r} carry opposite-polarity signed certificates with the same witness x, namely the R338 turns (u_2,u_1,x) and (x,u_r,u_{r-1}). Hence S_L,S_R form a direct balanced mass-four pair. Moreover the contiguous interval C=(u_2,...,u_{r-1}) meets each support in exactly one vertex. Applying the accepted signed-interval rebirth/refund mechanism of R174 to this chosen interval produces, unless a spanning two-cover occurs, an ancestry-bearing mass-two descendant whose two singleton supports are exactly {u_1} and {u_r}. Thus every all-internal R338 branch canonically manufactures a paid floor on the two physical endpoints of one literal source residual rail, with the captured middle x retained as the common sign witness in the source certificate.

## Proof
Let H=J sqcup U sqcup V be the source mirror-root representative in the all-internal branch, with J=(p,x,z). Since |H|>10, |U|+|V|=|H|-3>=8, so at least one of U,V has order at least four; rename it U=(u_1,...,u_r), r>=4. Accepted R338 gives the tight turns (u_2,u_1,x) and (x,u_r,u_{r-1}). Reading these in the accepted signed-dimer convention, S_L={u_1,u_2} is signed with witness x in one polarity and S_R={u_{r-1},u_r} is signed with the opposite polarity. Because r>=4 the supports are vertex-disjoint, hence they are a direct balanced pair of total mass four. Now take the contiguous tight interval C=(u_2,...,u_{r-1}) of the carrier U. It meets S_L exactly in u_2 and S_R exactly in u_{r-1}. The signed-interval rebirth mechanism used in accepted R174 therefore applies to these two supports along this explicit interval. Each touched support loses exactly its one vertex lying in C and remains nonempty; the resulting signed descendants are precisely the singleton supports {u_1} and {u_r}. Thus, outside the closing alternative of the accepted payment/refund mechanism, the lineage reaches an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor on the two physical endpoints of U. No representative/currentness beyond the stated ancestry is claimed.
