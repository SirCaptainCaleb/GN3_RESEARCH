# R124 — Transversal common-triple universal four-witness fold fan

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the R100 transversal x-frame, let Q=(q1,q2,q3,q4) and K be the 4- and 3-vertex components of any exact two-cover of H-S_x. For every witness w∈{x}∪supp(K), the three turns (q2,q1,w), (q4,w,q1), and (w,q4,q3) are tight, while their direct bridge orientation (q1,w,q4) is bad. Equivalently, each of the four witnesses gives the same folded three-turn pattern joining the two terminal reverse-turn systems of Q through the outer pair {q1,q4}. Cyclically the same holds in the y- and z-frames.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R100 transversal triangle; any exact two-cover of one common-triple deletion frame.

## Scope
Excess-independent orientation package strengthening the universal four-witness shield.

## Applicability
Use as the main orientation interface for the remaining positive-excess branch. In the pure U/V interleaving face, classify the Q label pattern and combine the four folded trimers (q4,w,q1) with the wing geometry. In the non-interleaving face, combine with the larger six-witness endpoint shield.

## Persistence
All asserted turns are graph-intrinsic relative to the displayed Q ordering and witness vertex. No simultaneous exact-cover currentness beyond the original Q,K frame is asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the four folded trimers concatenate with one another, form one current larger path, or can be paid simultaneously.

## Proof
Fix the x-frame. First let w∈K. R105 gives (q2,q1,w) and (w,q4,q3) tight, and its longest-path cap says H-S_x contains no tight five-path, so Q∪{w} is non-Hamiltonian. Now let w=x. If supp(Q)=U_x∪V_x, then Q∪{x} meets the third minimum-overlap support C only in {y,z}; if not, one of the wings U_x,V_x has at most one Q-vertex and Q∪{x} meets the corresponding A or B support in at most two vertices. In either case a tight five-path on Q∪{x} would be global-longest by R9 and contradict minimum overlap three. Thus Q∪{x} is non-Hamiltonian. The two endpoint direct turns by x are therefore bad and R3 gives (q2,q1,x),(x,q4,q3) tight.\n\nHence for every w∈{x}∪K the endpoint reversals (q2,q1,w) and (w,q4,q3) are tight and the support Q∪{w} is non-Hamiltonian. If (q1,w,q4) were tight, then the three consecutive turns of (q2,q1,w,q4,q3) would all be tight, producing a forbidden tight five-path on Q∪{w}. Therefore (q1,w,q4) is bad. Boundary antisymmetry R3 gives its full reversal (q4,w,q1) tight. Rotate the R100 coordinates cyclically for the y- and z-frames.
