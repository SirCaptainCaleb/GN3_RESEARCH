# R289 — Quiet four-core occupancy cap and O(n^4) longest-exterior ceiling

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the five-exterior equality face on n vertices, with longest-exterior family Ecal and four-core occupancies x_S. Then either some four-core has x_S>=14 and the capture-free fixed-trimer nonquiet interaction of provisional R288 occurs, or x_S<=13 for every four-core S. In the latter quiet branch, 5|Ecal|=sum_S x_S<=13*binom(n,4), hence |Ecal|<=(13/5)*binom(n,4). Combining with the unconditional R231 ceiling |Ecal|<=(n/25)*binom(n,4), the quiet branch satisfies |Ecal|<=min(13/5,n/25)*binom(n,4). The two bounds agree at n=65; for n>65 the physical occupancy cap is strictly stronger and keeps the quiet exterior family on O(n^4) scale.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; accepted R231 and provisional corrected C243/R288.

## Scope
Global extremal corollary of the repaired capture-free occupancy threshold.

## Applicability
Use as the structural/global branch complementary to the local fixed-trimer interaction route.

## Persistence
The exterior family and occupancy profile are graph-intrinsic finite-set data. The alternative nonquiet interaction is historical as in R288.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
The quiet branch means avoidance of the specific R160 nonquiet interaction forced by R288; this is not a contradiction by itself. Does not supply a lower bound on |Ecal|.

## Proof
Each E in Ecal contains exactly five four-subsets, so sum_S x_S=5|Ecal|. Avoidance of R288 forces x_S<=13 for all S. Therefore 5|Ecal|<=13 binom(n,4). R231 independently gives |Ecal|<=(n/25)binom(n,4). Equality of coefficients 13/5=n/25 occurs at n=65.
