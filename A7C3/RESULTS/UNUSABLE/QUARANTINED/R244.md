# R244 — Four-core occupancy forces concentrated same-frame carrier capture

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the five-exterior equality face. Let S be a physical four-core of occupancy x_S=r>=2 in the global-longest exterior family, choose one completion a with E_a=S union {a}, and let K_a=V(H)\\E_a be a retained global-longest carrier. Fix any s in S and put F_s=(S\\{s}) union {a}. Then every other completion b of S lies on K_a and F_s union {b} supports a tight P5. For each such b choose a tight P5 ordering and one consecutive turn in which b is an outer witness; let D(b) be the resulting physical dimer in F_s. Among the six possible dimers there is a dimer D for which at least ceil((r-1)/6) completion vertices share D. In the single literal exact D-deletion frame H-D=K_a sqcup (E_a\\D), every internal member of that D-class has a historical same-frame capture certificate at its own carrier position. Consequently one exact deletion frame yields captures at at least max(0,ceil((r-1)/6)-2) distinct carrier vertices, and across the at most six dimer frames at least max(0,r-9) distinct completion vertices are capturable.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; accepted R240 common-four-core cross-replacement saturation, R8 trimer existence, R5 pair-deletion exactness, and R37 witness-adaptive capture.

## Scope
Hybrid four-core occupancy-to-physical-progress theorem. It retains one actual global-longest carrier and converts occupancy of one physical four-core into many capture certificates, including a quantitative concentration inside one fixed exact deletion representative.

## Applicability
Use when the four-core occupancy profile x_S is nonuniform or one S is highly occupied. This is the direct bridge from structural Johnson occupancy to R239-style exact-frame carrier ancestry: occupancy r gives one same-frame capture class of size at least ceil((r-1)/6)-2.

## Persistence
S,a,s,F_s,K_a,D and the exact D-deletion frame are physical/current in the chosen root. The individual capture events are graph-intrinsic historical certificates sourced from that same frame; their paid descendants are alternative and not simultaneously current.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not force the captured positions to be adjacent, does not synchronize later capture descendants, does not itself extend K_a, and does not turn capture density into an R171 frame contrary to R214. Different physical-dimer classes use different deletion frames.

## Proof
Let A={c:S union {c} is a global-longest exterior}; |A|=r. Fix a in A and s in S. For every b in A-{a}, R240 applied to E_a=S union {a} and E_b=S union {b} gives that Q_b=F_s union {b}, where F_s=(S-{s}) union {a}, supports a tight P5. Choose an ordering of Q_b and a consecutive tight turn having b as an outer vertex; the other two vertices form a physical dimer D(b) inside F_s. There are six possible physical dimers. Hence some D-class C has |C|>=ceil((r-1)/6). Now fix any internal b in C and choose b prime in C-{b}. Delete D. Since D is contained in E_a and |E_a|=5, E_a-D has three vertices and supports a tight trimer by R8. Thus K_a sqcup (E_a-D) is a two-cover of H-D, exact by R5. Relative to the retained P5 Q_b containing D, the two selected K_a states incident with b cross from Q_b-D to its exterior because Q_b contains no other K_a vertex. The distinct witness b prime lies on at most one of these two states; choose the other. Q_b prime supplies an admissible signed incarnation of the same physical support D witnessed by b prime, and R37 allows adaptive use of that witness regardless of tested order or polarity. Hence b is historically captured in this same exact D-deletion frame. At most two members of C are endpoints of K_a, so the chosen single frame yields at least max(0,ceil((r-1)/6)-2) captures. Summed across all six D-classes, only vertices in singleton classes and the two carrier endpoints can fail this argument; at most six plus two such vertices are lost from r-1, leaving at least max(0,r-9) capturable completion vertices.
