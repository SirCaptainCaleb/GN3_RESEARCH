If an R495 HF/TF fork packet is additionally retained and one needs genuine fork-role consumption, use the separate spine theorem R505; the bare capture/first-contact conclusion here is intentionally fork-blind.

Accepted R504 proves, using only boundary antisymmetry and witness-adaptive capture, that this selected marker is capture-capable through D and that either endpoint may be captured. Choose x. This yields the required historical capture event on the actual selected physical crossing {x,y}. The retained path P^o has at least two vertices because m>=3, so x has at least one genuine P^o adjacency E. Therefore the hypotheses of accepted fully reconstructible R312 are met with historical carrier A=P^o, crossing {x,y}, captured anchor x and adjacency E. Accepted R530 applies to the same historical capture packet and gives its exhaustive four-case tested-order normal form.
