# R102 — Transversal equality-kernel four-witness shields

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the transversal branch and the simultaneous zero-excess alternative of R101, with A=T∪{x,y,alpha}, B=T∪{x,z,beta}, C=T∪{y,z,gamma}. Let Q_x,Q_y,Q_z be the three zero-excess tight four-path components on W_x={y,alpha,z,beta}, W_y={x,alpha,z,gamma}, W_z={x,beta,y,gamma}. Then for every w in D_x={x,gamma,delta,epsilon}, the five-set W_x∪{w} supports no tight five-path. Cyclically, W_y∪{w} is non-Hamiltonian for every w in D_y={y,beta,delta,epsilon}, and W_z∪{w} is non-Hamiltonian for every w in D_z={z,alpha,delta,epsilon}. In particular, for any chosen ordering Q_i=(q1,q2,q3,q4) of one of the three zero-excess four-paths and every w in D_i, both direct endpoint attachments (w,q1,q2) and (q3,q4,w) are bad, hence the reverse endpoint turns (q2,q1,w) and (w,q4,q3) are tight.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R98 transversal branch; simultaneous zero-excess alternative in R101.

## Scope
Finite equality kernel of the transversal branch.

## Applicability
Use as the labeled orientation package for attacking the simultaneous three-packet equality kernel. The two common witnesses delta,epsilon belong to all three shields.

## Persistence
Non-Hamiltonicity of the five-sets is support-level. The endpoint reverse turns are relative to the chosen zero-excess four-path ordering but are graph-intrinsic once that ordering is fixed.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim the shield alone is inconsistent, nor that reverse endpoint turns automatically concatenate into a five-path.

## Proof
Work in the x-frame; the y- and z-frames are cyclic. R101 supplies a tight four-path Q_x on W_x={y,alpha,z,beta}. Let w∈D_x={x,gamma,delta,epsilon}. Suppose W_x∪{w} supported a tight five-path. By R9 its order five is globally longest, so its support belongs to F. If w=x, then (W_x∪{x})∩C={y,z}; if w=gamma, then (W_x∪{gamma})∩A={y,alpha}; if w is delta or epsilon, then (W_x∪{w})∩C={y,z}. In every case this new support has intersection two with an existing longest support, contradicting that A,B,C are pairwise globally minimum-overlap supports of overlap three by R100. Thus every W_x∪{w} is non-Hamiltonian. Now choose an ordering Q_x=(q1,q2,q3,q4). If (w,q1,q2) were tight, adjoining w at the left would give a tight five-path on W_x∪{w}; similarly, if (q3,q4,w) were tight, adjoining w at the right would do so. Both are therefore bad. By boundary antisymmetry R3, their complete reversals (q2,q1,w) and (w,q4,q3) are tight. The same argument after cyclic permutation proves the y- and z-frame statements.
