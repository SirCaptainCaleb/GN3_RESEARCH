# R376 — All-internal trimer return has at least six selectable paid-floor coordinates

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal regime of R359 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten, with one source currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V. Let Z be the union of the physical vertices lying in the four terminal dimers of U and V. Then |Z|>=6. Moreover for every z in Z, R374 gives an alternative ancestry-bearing continuation that either closes with a spanning two-cover or reaches a mass-two floor containing singleton {z}, hence a fixed-turn lineage whose outer target pair contains z. Thus every all-internal return carries at least six distinct independently selectable paid-floor coordinates.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; all-internal R359 return regime.

## Scope
Quantitative avoidance-reservoir corollary of the terminal-coordinate floor fan.

## Applicability
Use when a downstream floor/fixed-turn consumer needs to avoid a blocker set of size at most five.

## Persistence
The six-or-more physical terminal coordinates are graph-intrinsic relative to the chosen source rails; each selected floor lineage is alternative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the six floors, prescribe their second singleton coordinates, or preserve one common fixed-turn middle.

## Proof
R359/R168 give |U|,|V|>=2 and |U|+|V|=|H|-3>=8. A rail of order 2 contributes 2 distinct terminal-dimer vertices, order 3 contributes 3, and order at least 4 contributes 4. Under two rail orders at least 2 with sum at least 8, the minimum total contribution is attained at (2,6), giving 2+4=6. The rails are vertex-disjoint, so |Z|>=6. Apply R374 separately to every z in Z.
