# R351 — Bilateral protected-contact portals on any proper tight path

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K be any proper tight path of order at least four. Then the left and right terminal physical dimers of K each separately supply a lawful protected-contact lineage. Indeed R169 gives at least five exterior vertices, so either end has two distinct exterior witnesses; provisional R350 applies independently to each terminal dimer. Hence on each end one obtains either an earlier protected contact or a first-contact mirror-trimer root built from the actual terminal-dimer deletion crossing and a genuine surviving old-carrier adjacency. The two lineages are alternative and need not coexist. Thus the bilateral protected-contact portal theorem does not require K to be global-longest and does not require R199 same-polarity endpoint shielding.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is any proper tight path of order at least four.

## Scope
Order-free strengthening of R330 removing global-longestness and terminal same-polarity hypotheses.

## Applicability
Use whenever a retained proper path of order at least four is available. Either endpoint may be chosen as the protected-contact entry side.

## Persistence
Each endpoint lineage retains only its own deletion crossing, capture event, surviving old carrier, and first-contact ancestry. The two endpoint lineages are not simultaneous.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the left and right protected descendants or imply that either first contact closes H.
