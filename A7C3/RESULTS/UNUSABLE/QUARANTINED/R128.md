# R128 — Longest-five support minimum overlap is one or two

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case, if F is the family of supports of global-longest tight five-paths and m=min{|A∩B|: A,B∈F, A≠B}, then m∈{1,2}.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; accepted longest-five support family F.

## Scope
Parent-level frontier compression after elimination of minimum overlap three.

## Applicability
Use to replace the former minimum-overlap-three butterfly frontier. Any surviving smallest counterexample must contain a globally minimum-overlap longest-five pair of overlap one or two.

## Persistence
The conclusion concerns only graph-intrinsic support intersections.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not exclude overlap one or overlap two, and does not by itself produce a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
Let m be the minimum intersection size among distinct members of F. By R13 every two members of F intersect, so m>=1. By R14 there exist A,B in F with |A∩B|<=3, hence m<=3. If m=3, then A,B may be chosen as a globally minimum-overlap pair with intersection three, contradicting R127. Therefore m is neither 0 nor 3 and satisfies 1<=m<=2. Hence m∈{1,2}.
