## Scope
The monotone branch of accepted R671 inside the sigma_G(T)=0 minimum five-cell setting, restricted to the genuinely new residue c_X(T) in {2,3}. Pure enumeration of contracted configurations; no realizability claim.

## Applicability
Supplies an m-uniform finite target for the entire minimum five-cell residue. Any argument that refutes or resolves each of the 43 labelled configurations closes the residue outright, without a separate lemma per R670 shape. Also usable as an audit key: any per-shape lemma should account for exactly the sublist above for its shape.

## Persistence
The count is a property of the contracted forest under the accepted R665 block/transition count, the R667(1) no-X-X rule, and the R671 monotonicity constraint. It survives any change of representative that retains those three results.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not refute or realize any configuration. Does not treat the non-monotone R435 outputs. Does not use X-dimer internal orientations, R621 endpoint gate data, or R658 rectangles to cut the count, so 43 is an upper bound on the surviving labelled residue and the true number may be smaller. Makes no claim that any configuration occurs in an actual smallest counterexample.
