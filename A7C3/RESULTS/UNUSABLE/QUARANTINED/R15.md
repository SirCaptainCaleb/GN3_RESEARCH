# R15 — Radius-One Low-Overlap Localization

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
For every prescribed P in F, either there exists A in F with |A intersect P| at most three, or there are two actual one-vertex replacement supports A=P-{p_i}+{x} and B=P-{p_j}+{y} with i != j and x != y, hence |A intersect B|=3.

## Scope
Order-ten smallest counterexample; F is the actual longest-five support family.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
No generic one-vertex replacement theorem is asserted; only actual members of F are used.

## Proof
Fix P and assume no F-member meets P in at most three. Every F-member differing from P and omitting p_i is then P-{p_i}+{x}. Empty total intersection gives every left p_i positive degree in the replacement graph; full union gives every exterior x positive degree. A bipartite graph with all ten vertices positive degree cannot have matching number one, since a pairwise-intersecting edge family is a star and leaves vertices on one side isolated. Two disjoint edges p_i~x and p_j~y yield actual supports A,B with intersection P-{p_i,p_j}, of size three.
