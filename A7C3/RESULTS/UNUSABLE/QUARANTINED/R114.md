# R114 — Zero-excess fan forces six same-support two-witness collisions

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume R112. For every shield index i in {x,y,z}, the two witness-indexed left supports L_i(delta) and L_i(epsilon) are the same oriented physical dimer and have the same polarity, but carry the distinct witnesses delta and epsilon. Hence R38 places that dimer in the same-polarity two-extension collision package. Likewise R_i(delta) and R_i(epsilon) are the same oriented physical dimer of the opposite polarity with those two distinct witnesses, so R38 gives a second collision site for the same shield. Therefore the R112 zero-excess kernel contains six graph-intrinsic same-support two-witness collision sites, one on each chosen left/right endpoint dimer of Q_x,Q_y,Q_z. In particular, no abstract signed-support realization of the full R112 contract can be collision-free.

## Hypotheses
Standing hypotheses of R112, with fixed orderings Q_i=(q_{i1},q_{i2},q_{i3},q_{i4}) for i in {x,y,z}; accepted same-support interaction theorem R38.

## Scope
Immediate signed-support consequence of R112 and R38. No use of pending R113 or of later payment descendants.

## Applicability
Use before comparing the four R112 common mates. The natural next target is not existence of collisions but synchronization of the six forced collision sites, for example through common ancestry, a shared physical core/anchor, or a rule coupling the delta/epsilon descendants strongly enough to force 5+5.

## Persistence
The six collision sites are graph-intrinsic once the three Q_i orderings are fixed, because their oriented endpoint dimers and both witness certificates are graph-intrinsic under R112.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the six physical endpoint dimers are distinct, that their collision descendants are simultaneously current, that the four R112 mates coincide, or that any single collision forces a spanning two-cover. It only rules out a collision-free abstract model of R112.

## Proof
Fix i in {x,y,z}. By R112, for each w in {delta,epsilon}, L_i(w) is the oriented dimer (q_{i2},q_{i1}); its polarity is the left-endpoint polarity and its witness is w. Therefore L_i(delta) and L_i(epsilon) have identical oriented physical support and identical polarity, while their witnesses are distinct. R38 says that two signed certificates on the same physical support with the same polarity and distinct witnesses enter the accepted two-extension collision package. Hence the left endpoint dimer of Q_i is a collision site. The same argument applies to R_i(w)=(q_{i4},q_{i3}): R_i(delta) and R_i(epsilon) have identical oriented physical support and the common right-endpoint polarity, but distinct witnesses, so R38 gives a collision site there as well. This yields two forced collision sites for each of i=x,y,z, hence six endpoint collision sites in total, counted by shield-side incidence. The conclusion is incidence-level: some of these six sites may coincide physically across different shields, but every listed shield-side carries the stated two-witness collision. Consequently any abstract model satisfying the full R112 signed-support contract and R38 necessarily contains these collisions, so a collision-free realization is impossible.
