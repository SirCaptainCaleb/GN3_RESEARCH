# R303 — Five-path double-puncture coordinate-coincidence normal form

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and K=(v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4,v_5) a proper tight P5. Apply the repaired two-puncture construction to D_L={v_2,v_3} and D_R={v_3,v_4}, retaining lawful original-frame capture crossings {b_L,z_L},{b_R,z_R}, common paid-floor coordinate y, and alternative mass-two floors {y,c_L},{y,c_R}. Then unless H has a spanning two-cover, the coordinate hierarchy is exact. If c_L<>c_R, some dimer {y,t} carries an R38 interaction with the two specified floor witnesses c_L,c_R. If c_L=c_R but b_L<>b_R, some dimer {y,t} carries an R38 interaction with the two specified captured-carrier witnesses b_L,b_R. If c_L=c_R and b_L=b_R=:b but z_L<>z_R, the actual endpoint adjacency of K incident with b carries an R38 interaction with the two specified crossing witnesses z_L,z_R via lawful R155 reentry. Therefore the only branch avoiding all three coordinate-localized R38 interactions has c_L=c_R=:c, b_L=b_R=:b, z_L=z_R=:z, and necessarily b is one of the two physical endpoints v_1,v_5 of K. In particular the R301 captured-anchor/floor-coordinate degeneracy branch is unnecessary.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is a proper tight P5; accepted repaired puncture/floor inputs R294 and R299; accepted R3,R38,R41,R155. H has more than ten vertices in O4, so auxiliary vertices required below exist.

## Scope
Order-free finite normal form for the minimal two-puncture carrier. Every interaction branch is localized by the physical coordinates whose mismatch causes it; no simultaneous floor or deletion-cover currentness is asserted.

## Proof
First suppose c_L<>c_R. Choose a physical vertex t distinct from y,c_L,c_R. By R3, orient the two triples {y,t,c_L} and {y,t,c_R} with middle t. Each gives, via the terminal-dimer sign convention R41, a signed certificate on the same physical dimer {y,t}, with distinct specified witnesses c_L,c_R. R38 gives the floor-indexed interaction. Hence in the no-such-interaction branch c_L=c_R=:c. Next suppose b_L<>b_R. Choose t distinct from y,b_L,b_R. The identical R3/R41 construction on {y,t,b_L} and {y,t,b_R} signs the same dimer {y,t} with the two specified captured-carrier witnesses, so R38 gives the capture-indexed interaction. This works even when c equals one of b_L,b_R, which removes the old R301 degeneracy. Hence without that interaction b_L=b_R=:b. Because b is a surviving carrier vertex in both puncture frames, b lies in {v_1,v_4,v_5} for D_L and in {v_1,v_2,v_5} for D_R, so b belongs their intersection {v_1,v_5}. Finally suppose z_L<>z_R. Let E_b be the genuine endpoint adjacency of the original historical carrier K incident with b: {v_1,v_2} if b=v_1 and {v_4,v_5} if b=v_5. Apply R155 separately to the lawful historical captures through {b,z_L} and {b,z_R}, using the same actual carrier adjacency E_b. Each mirror trimer signs E_b with the corresponding crossing witness z_L or z_R. Since the witnesses are distinct, R38 gives the crossing-indexed interaction. Therefore avoiding all three localized interactions forces z_L=z_R=:z, yielding the stated total coordinate-coincidence residue.
