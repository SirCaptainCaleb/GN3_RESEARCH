# R189 — Minimum-overlap-one twenty-replacement saturation

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case and minimum longest-support overlap one. For every global-longest five-support A with exterior E and every a in A, at least four of the five supports (E\\{z})∪{a}, z∈E, are themselves global-longest. Consequently every A has at least twenty distinct global-longest supports meeting A in exactly one vertex.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; minimum longest-support overlap equals one; accepted R8 and R184.

## Scope
Pure support-level saturation extracted from the two-hole replacement fan. No complement counting or global extremal argument is used.

## Applicability
Use as the local lower-bound half of any global counting, clique, or support-incidence contradiction in the m=1 branch.

## Persistence
All twenty replacement supports are graph-intrinsic members of the global-longest support family.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not identify a common missing exterior vertex for different a. Does not assert all twenty-five replacements. Does not by itself yield a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
Fix A in F, write E=V(H)\\A, and fix a in A. For z in E define B_z=(E\\{z})∪{a}. Suppose two distinct vertices x,y in E both gave B_x,B_y notin F. By R8 the three-set P=E\\{x,y} supports a tight trimer. Applying accepted R184 to A and this P with holes x,y says that for every a in A at least one of B_x and B_y belongs to F, contradiction. Thus at most one of the five B_z is absent, so at least four belong to F. Repeating independently for all five a in A yields at least twenty supports. They are distinct because each such support meets A in the unique adjoined vertex a, and for fixed a distinct z give distinct four-subsets of E. Each therefore meets A in exactly one vertex.
