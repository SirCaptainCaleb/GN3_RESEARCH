# R205 — Six-exterior root fan non-descent and rail-gap fence

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Instantiate the six-exterior O3 face with a global-longest carrier K in an n-vertex hypothetical smallest counterexample. In every R196 singleton root H-{e}=K sqcup Q_e, the rail orders are n-6 and 5, so their order gap is n-11: the rails are equal only for n=11, have gap one only for n=12, gap two only for n=13, and have gap at least three for n>=14. Moreover the support/certificate conclusions of R195/R196 do not themselves imply exterior-size descent: for every m>=5 there is an abstract contract-level model with |K|=m, |E|=6, all six sets E-{e} marked as tight P5 supports, the displayed K+P5+singleton roots and puncture balanced-pair births recorded, but no tight support of order m+1 and hence no carrier with five exterior vertices. Thus an order-free slack descent requires an additional invariant coupling a singleton root or puncture pair back to a genuine carrier extension/restoration; equal-rail or small-gap arguments alone cover only the low-order specializations above.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R169, R195 and R196; for the rail-gap statement K is the global-longest carrier chosen in the O3 six-exterior slack-one face. The abstract model is a logical-independence model of the stated support/certificate conclusions, not a Strong Level-(1) realizability claim.

## Scope
Applicability and non-implication fence for arbitrary-order six-exterior slack reduction.

## Applicability
Use to prevent order-eleven 5+5 symmetry, or the gap-one/gap-two special cases, from being silently promoted to an arbitrary-order induction. A genuine descent theorem must preserve or reconstruct the global-longest carrier hypothesis after any transfer, or otherwise force an order-(|K|+1) path directly.

## Persistence
The rail-size computation is physical and order-invariant. The abstract model records only contract-level support and historical-certificate data.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim an actual smallest counterexample realizes the abstract model. Does not invalidate R196, R197, R198, R201, or R202. Does not say small-gap machinery is useless in orders 11-13; it only fences its order-free use.

## Proof
Let n=|H| and suppose the carrier K has six exterior vertices, so |K|=n-6. R196 supplies roots H-{e}=K sqcup Q_e with |Q_e|=5. Hence the rail-order gap is |K|-5=n-11. The stated equal/gap-one/gap-two classification follows immediately. For the contract-level non-descent model, fix any m>=5, take disjoint K and E with |K|=m and |E|=6, mark K tight, and mark every five-set E-{e} as supporting a tight P5. For every e record both the literal spanning three-cover symbol K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} and the exact two-cover label H-{e}=K sqcup Q_e. For every internal carrier puncture and every e, attach a fresh abstract balanced-pair certificate, independently for that frame, as permitted by the existential/non-synchronizing conclusion of R196. Declare no support of order m+1 tight. Then no path has exactly five exterior vertices relative to the m+6 vertex universe, so no slack descent has occurred, while all support, exact-root, and anonymous-certificate conclusions just listed are present. This shows those conclusions alone do not force descent. Any additional descent must therefore identify/synchronize certificates with carrier structure or force a true extension/restoration. The arithmetic gap calculation simultaneously shows that equal or gap-at-most-two root geometry is confined to n<=13.
