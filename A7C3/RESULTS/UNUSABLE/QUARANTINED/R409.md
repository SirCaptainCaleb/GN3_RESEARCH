# R409 — Endpoint-universal pair deletion forces an internal-vertex component-drop fan

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the endpoint-universal branch of accepted R393 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H of order n>=11. Fix any physical pair {a,c} and any exact two-cover F=U sqcup V of H-{a,c}. By R168 both rails have order at least three, so F has exactly four physical endpoints. For every residual vertex x in V(H)\\({a,c} union End(F)), x is internal on its rail in F. Endpoint universality supplies another exact two-cover F_x of H-{a,c} in which x is a rail endpoint. Deleting x produces two literal covers of the same proper residue H-{a,c,x}: trimming x from F_x gives a cover with at most two components, while deleting internal x from F splits its rail and gives a literal three-cover. Hence R159, equivalently the endpoint-status comparison theorem R408, forces a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair for every such x. The exact component-drop certificate is indexed by the physical puncture x and the fixed source cover F. Since H-{a,c} has n-2 vertices and F has four endpoints, there are exactly n-6 such internal vertices, at least five in the beyond-order-ten regime. Thus every fixed pair deletion in the endpoint-universal branch carries an (n-6)-vertex fan of alternative exact 3-to-2 component-drop certificates over one common source cover.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample with n>=11; accepted R168, R393, R159/R408; {a,c} is any physical pair; F is any exact two-cover of H-{a,c}.

## Scope
Order-free beyond-order-ten consequence of endpoint universality. The fan is indexed over one fixed pair-deletion source cover.

## Applicability
Use to replace vague exact-cover multiplicity in the endpoint-universal branch by at least n-6 explicit puncture-indexed component-drop certificates on triple-deletion residues.

## Persistence
The fixed source cover F and puncture identity x are retained in each component-drop certificate. The balanced pair obtained from R159/R408 is graph-intrinsic but may differ with x and is not asserted current. Different x-descendants are alternative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the balanced pairs for different x are distinct or simultaneous. Does not preserve x as a witness/support of the R159 pair. Does not by itself close endpoint universality.

## Proof
Let W=H-{a,c}. By R168 each rail of F has order at least three, hence End(F) consists of four distinct vertices. Fix x in W\\End(F). In F, x is internal, so F-x is a literal three-cover of W-x. By R393 endpoint universality there exists an exact two-cover F_x of W with x at a rail endpoint; deleting x from that endpoint path leaves a literal one- or two-component cover of W-x. Since W-x is proper in H, R159 applies and yields a graph-intrinsic balanced pair. This is precisely the endpoint-status disagreement mechanism of R408. The construction is available separately for every x in W\\End(F), whose cardinality is |W|-4=n-6.
