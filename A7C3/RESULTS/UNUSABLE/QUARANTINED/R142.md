# R142 — Type-(2,2,3) private-endpoint motif rotation

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the accepted minimum-overlap-two type-(2,2,3) motif L=X∪Y∪{p}, B=X∪Z, D=Y∪Z with |X|=|Y|=2, |Z|=3 and exterior pair {e,f}, so deleting X gives the exact root D ⊔ {p,e,f} and deleting Y gives B ⊔ {p,e,f}. Fix a tight path ordering of D and suppose an endpoint t lies in Y. Then E=(D\\{t})∪{p} is a global-longest five-support. Writing Y'=(Y\\{t})∪{p} and p'=t, the triple L,B,E is again a type-(2,2,3) motif with the same X,Z and exterior pair {e,f}: L=X∪Y'∪{p'}, B=X∪Z, E=Y'∪Z. Moreover deleting X gives the exact 5+3 root E ⊔ {p',e,f}, and deleting Y' gives B ⊔ {p',e,f}; thus the rotated motif has shared short trimer {t,e,f}. The symmetric statement holds when an endpoint t of B lies in X, rotating p with t while preserving D,Z and {e,f}.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; minimum longest-support overlap two; accepted type-(2,2,3) normalization R130/R131 and endpoint replacement rule R140.

## Scope
Support-level and exact-root symmetry of the type-(2,2,3) shared-trimer motif.

## Applicability
Use to convert any private long-rail endpoint into another shared-trimer type-(2,2,3) motif over the same exterior pair. Iterating available private endpoints creates a family of singleton states tied to {e,f} without opening payment descendants.

## Persistence
The rotated support E and all displayed support identities are graph-intrinsic. The exact roots use separate tight trimer orientations on the physical complement three-set.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that every private vertex can be made an endpoint, that different rotations are simultaneously current path representatives, or that the shared exterior dimer {e,f} has one common sign polarity across all rotated trimers.

## Proof
Let t∈Y be an endpoint of the chosen tight ordering of D. By R140, because t is a private Y-endpoint, the replacement witness is forced to be p, so E=(D\\{t})∪{p} is the support of a global-longest tight five-path. Put Y'=(Y\\{t})∪{p} and p'=t. Then E=Y'∪Z and L=X∪Y∪{p}=X∪Y'∪{p'}, while B=X∪Z. The sets X,Y',Z are pairwise disjoint with sizes 2,2,3, so L∩B=X, L∩E=Y', and B∩E=Z. Hence L,B,E have type (2,2,3) with singleton p' and the same two exterior vertices e,f. Now H−X has vertex set E∪{t,e,f}; since E is a tight five-path support and {t,e,f} is a three-set, R8 supplies a tight trimer orientation on {t,e,f}, giving an exact 5+3 cover E⊔{t,e,f}. Likewise H−Y' has vertex set B∪{t,e,f}, so R8 gives the exact root B⊔{t,e,f}. Thus the new motif shares the short trimer support {t,e,f}. The argument with B and X exchanged is identical.
