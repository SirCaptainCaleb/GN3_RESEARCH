# R278 — The bare O4 currentizable two-trimer front door is capture-free

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K be any proper tight path. Fix any carrier vertex b in V(K). Then there exist two vertex-disjoint tight trimers C and T such that C contains b as its middle vertex, C can be currentized as one component of a minimum three-cover, T lies wholly in the exterior of K, and R41 supplies the synchronized central-anchor interaction between C and T. Consequently the bare geometric/currentizable two-trimer conclusion underlying R273 does not require R264 punctures, capture events, crossing synchronization, or capture-reentry spokes. Any additional content of R273 must therefore reside specifically in the claimed puncture/capture ancestry on the terminal supports of C.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is any proper graph-intrinsic tight path; b is any vertex of K. Accepted R3, R41, R154, and R169.

## Scope
Order-free compression of the geometric part of the recent two-puncture front door.

## Applicability
Use when evaluating R272/R273 or designing O4 descendants. Treat currentizable two-trimer geometry plus central-anchor interaction as free once a proper carrier exists; do not spend puncture machinery merely to obtain that geometry. Reserve puncture synchronization claims for ancestry that is genuinely proved.

## Persistence
The two physical trimer supports and the R41 interaction are graph-intrinsic. The minimum-cover representative currentizing C is representative-relative. No puncture or capture ancestry is asserted.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not give capture ancestry, selected-crossing ancestry, endpoint exposure, protection, or any link from the terminal dimers of C to two prescribed punctures. Does not assert C and T are simultaneously selected in one cover. Does not close H.

## Proof
By R169, the exterior E=V(H)-V(K) has at least five vertices. Choose five distinct exterior vertices d_1,d_2,r,y,s. Boundary antisymmetry R3 gives exactly one tight orientation of the three-set {d_1,b,d_2} with middle b; call the resulting tight trimer C. Its two terminal physical supports {d_1,b} and {b,d_2} are distinct supports incident with b, so R154 currentizes their unique compatible mirror packet, equivalently C, as one component of a minimum three-cover. Independently, R3 gives a tight orientation T of {r,y,s} with prescribed middle y. The trimers C and T are vertex-disjoint because b lies on K while all five chosen vertices lie in E and the chosen exterior vertices are distinct. Therefore R41 applies directly: the two terminal dimers of C and the two terminal dimers of T supply the synchronized central-anchor interaction with middle-anchor pair {b,y}. No capture, puncture, crossing, reentry, or ancestry transport is used. Hence the currentizable two-trimer geometry and R41 interaction asserted at the bare structural level by R273 are already forced by R3/R41/R154/R169; only the extra statement that C terminal supports carry ancestry from two particular R264 punctures could constitute additional content.
