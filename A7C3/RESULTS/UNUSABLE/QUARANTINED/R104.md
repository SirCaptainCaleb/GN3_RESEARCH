# R104 — Transversal core-singleton eight-support classification

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the transversal branch with A=T∪{x,y,alpha}, B=T∪{x,z,beta}, C=T∪{y,z,gamma} and T={t,tprime}. Fix one core vertex t and delete it. In every exact two-cover of H-{t}, the five-vertex rail has a support D in F that necessarily contains the other core vertex tprime. Writing E=D-{tprime}, the four-set E is one of exactly eight possibilities: {x,y,z,w} for w∈{alpha,beta,gamma,delta,epsilon}, or {x,y,beta,gamma}, or {x,z,alpha,gamma}, or {y,z,alpha,beta}. In particular every prescribed-core-singleton deletion produces a new global-longest support omitting t, containing tprime, and meeting at least two of A,B,C in exactly three vertices.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R98 transversal branch, in the symmetric coordinates of R100.

## Scope
Pure support classification for singleton deletions at the two-vertex common core of a transversal triangle.

## Applicability
Use to replace arbitrary singleton-root search at t∈T by an eight-case support family. The same list applies symmetrically after exchanging t and tprime.

## Persistence
The classification concerns the physical support of the five-rail; orientations and the complementary four-rail remain representative-dependent.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim every one of the eight supports occurs, nor that the supports obtained from deleting t and tprime are unique.

## Proof
Let T={t,tprime} and delete t. By R46, every exact two-cover of H-{t} has a five-vertex rail whose support D is a global-longest support, hence D∈F. By R100, A,B,C are pairwise globally minimum-overlap supports with minimum overlap three, so |D∩A|,|D∩B|,|D∩C|≥3. Suppose tprime∉D. Since t∉D as well, meeting A=T∪{x,y,alpha} in at least three forces {x,y,alpha}⊆D; meeting B forces {x,z,beta}⊆D; meeting C forces {y,z,gamma}⊆D. Their union has six vertices {x,y,z,alpha,beta,gamma}, impossible because |D|=5. Thus tprime∈D. Put E=D-{tprime}, so |E|=4. The three overlap inequalities become |E∩{x,y,alpha}|≥2, |E∩{x,z,beta}|≥2, and |E∩{y,z,gamma}|≥2. If E contains x,y,z, its fourth vertex may be any one of alpha,beta,gamma,delta,epsilon, giving five possibilities. Otherwise E contains at most two of x,y,z. It cannot contain at most one: summing the three displayed lower bounds would require at least six incidences, while each Greek vertex alpha,beta,gamma contributes to only one inequality and each of x,y,z to two, so four vertices with at most one of x,y,z contribute at most five. Hence E contains exactly two of x,y,z. If they are x,y, the first inequality is already paid but the B and C inequalities each need one more incidence beyond x and y respectively, forcing beta and gamma; thus E={x,y,beta,gamma}. Similarly the pairs x,z and y,z force E={x,z,alpha,gamma} and E={y,z,alpha,beta}. This gives exactly the eight listed possibilities. Direct inspection shows each listed D meets at least two of A,B,C in exactly three vertices. Finally D omits t whereas A,B,C all contain t, so D is a new support distinct from A,B,C.
