# A7C3 Durable Obligations

Obligations are stable mathematical closure contracts, not tasks. They have no owner, assignee, worker lane, or mandatory order. Researchers may attack them directly, strengthen them, bypass them through a stronger theorem, or make them irrelevant by changing the proof architecture.

Only live durable obligations belong in this file. Git history preserves earlier formulations.

## O4 — Arbitrary-order smallest-counterexample closure beyond order ten

**Status:** Open

### Target

Eliminate every surviving hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) boundary-tournament counterexample in arbitrary order beyond the already excluded order-ten case.

### Scope

Work in the smallest-counterexample regime. No particular exterior geometry, occupancy normalization, capture mechanism, payment mechanism, or capacity normalization is built into the obligation. Those are possible strategies, not part of the contract.

### Closed when

A spanning two-cover or contradiction is derived for every surviving hypothetical smallest counterexample, or a stronger accepted order-free parent theorem eliminates all survivors at once.

### Standing fences

- R24 and R5 are not available while quarantined/frozen.
- A route whose relevant support depends circularly on quarantined mathematics is not a closure of O4.
- Tactical machinery belongs in `STRATEGY/CURRENT.md`, not in this obligation.

## O6 — Modern noncircular reconstruction of R24

**Status:** Open

### Target

Give a fully reconstructible R24-independent proof of the singleton order floor and short-complement rigidity conclusion, preferably as an immediate consequence of a stronger general theorem.

Concretely, in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, for every vertex `z` and every exact two-cover of `H-z`, prove that both rails have order at least four.

### Scope

The proof must be independent of the legacy compressed R24 certificate, R5, and any theorem whose only relevant proof route passes through R24/R5.

### Closed when

Either:

1. R24 has a modern fully reconstructible proof from R24-independent usable mathematics; or
2. a stronger accepted theorem is proved from which R24 follows immediately.

### Strategic role

O6 is foundational proof recovery, not archival cleanup. It remains valuable durable background, but current O4 strategy may attack the main theorem directly without waiting for O6.
