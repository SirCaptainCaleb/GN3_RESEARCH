## Provenance convention

When a canonical GN3 theorem or proof passage materially descends from legacy work, record only what is useful for recovery, for example:

- legacy source file or Slack result/Engine identifier;
- exact old audit status when relevant;
- a correction, invalidation, or scope fence that an auditor should know;
- the legacy commit containing a published proof.

Do not reproduce development transcripts here. Do not require ordinary GN3 readers to follow these pointers. The reconstructed GN3 mathematics must stand on its own except where it intentionally cites another canonical theorem.
