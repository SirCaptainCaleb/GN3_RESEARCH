# Ergemlidze–Győri–Methuku rainbow path Turán bound

## Statement

If P_t denotes a path of t edges, then every properly edge-colored n-vertex graph with no rainbow P_t has fewer than ((9(t-1))/7+2)n=((9t+5)/7)n edges.

## Body

Ergemlidze, Győri and Methuku, On the Rainbow Turán number of paths (2018), arXiv:1805.04180. Their Theorem 2 states ex^*(n,P_{k+1}) < (9k/7+2)n, where path length is the number of edges. Substituting k=t-1 gives the displayed form.
