# Győri–Salia (2025): linear 3-graphs without long Berge paths

## Statement

If an n-vertex linear 3-graph contains no Berge path of length k>=4, then it has at most (k-1)n/6 hyperedges; the bound is sharp for infinitely many k,n.

## Body

Paper: Ervin Győri and Nika Salia, 'Linear three-uniform hypergraphs with no Berge path of given length', Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B 171 (2025), 36–48, DOI 10.1016/j.jctb.2024.11.003, arXiv:2211.16184. Their stronger induction theorem says that an n-vertex linear {2,3}-uniform hypergraph with no Berge path of length k>=4 has at most (k-1)n/2 edges in its two-shadow. For admissible k with an STS(k), disjoint STS(k) components attain the 3-uniform bound. This is a Berge-path theorem: it is not directly an upper bound for the weaker condition of forbidding only the acyclic linear path P_k, but its deletion/shadow method is strategically relevant.
