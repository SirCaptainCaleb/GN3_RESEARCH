# Tang–Wu–Zhang (2026): exact P5 and general conjectural scale

## Statement

Every n-vertex linear 3-graph with no P_5 has at most 15n/11 edges, with equality iff it is a disjoint union of the 11-vertex 15-edge graph G_0 (hence equality requires 11|n). They record the general conjecture ex_L(n,P_k) <= (k/3)n + c n for a universal constant c.

## Body

Paper: Chaoliang Tang, Hehui Wu, Junchi Zhang, 'The linear Turán number of the 3-graph P_5', arXiv:2601.19068 (2026). The introduction records the lower benchmark from disjoint maximal partial triple systems MPTS(2k), giving roughly (k-1)n/3 edges while avoiding P_k. Exact MPTS(m) sizes stated there are: m(m-1)/6 for m≡1,3 mod6; (m(m-1)-8)/6 for m≡5; m(m-2)/6 for m≡0,2; and (m(m-2)-2)/6 for m≡4. For k=5, MPTS(10) gives only 13n/10; the exact extremal component G_0 instead has 11 vertices and 15 edges.
