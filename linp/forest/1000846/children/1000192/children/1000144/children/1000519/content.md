# Adak–Verma (2026 v2): uniform hypertrees and P4^r

## Statement

Under the stated design/divisibility assumptions, every k-edge linear r-uniform hypertree has a T_k^r-free construction with n(k-1)/r edges. For P_4^r, the source states the general value (r+1)n/r as a conjecture and proves the exact result for r=4; it also verifies connected cases under additional degree conditions.

## Body

Paper: Rajat Adak and Pragya Verma, 'Linear Turán Numbers of Uniform Hypertrees', arXiv:2607.16854v2, revised 2026-08-09. Results recorded from v2: (i) for the r-uniform linear star S_k^r, ex_r^lin(n,S_k^r) <= n(k-1)/r, with equality exactly for (k-1)-regular linear r-graphs whenever they exist; (ii) for every k-edge linear r-uniform hypertree T_k^r, under the stated divisibility/design-existence assumptions, there are T_k^r-free examples with n(k-1)/r edges, obtained from disjoint S(2,r,(r-1)(k-1)+1) components; (iii) for the four-edge broom B_4^r, ex_r^lin(n,B_4^r) <= (r+1)n/r, with equality exactly for disjoint S(2,r,r^2) components when such systems exist; (iv) for the crown E_4^r, a degree-sensitive estimate implies ex_r^lin(n,E_4^r) <= (2r-1)n/r, with a lower construction leaving a constant-factor gap; (v) for the four-edge path P_4^r, the source presents ex_r^lin(n,P_4^r)=(r+1)n/r as the general conjectured value, proves the exact result for r=4, and establishes additional connected cases under degree hypotheses. The paper also gives counterexamples to a structural claim used in a previously proposed proof of the r=4 path case.
