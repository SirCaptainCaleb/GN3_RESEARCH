# Gyárfás–Ruszinkó–Sárközy (2022): acyclic triple systems

## Statement

For linear triple systems, ex_L(n,P_k) <= 1.5 k n for every k,n. Also ex_L(n,P_2)=floor(n/3), ex_L(n,P_3)<=n with equality iff the system is a disjoint union of Fano planes, and ex_L(n,P_4)<=4n/3 with equality iff it is a disjoint union of affine planes of order 3.

## Body

Paper: András Gyárfás, Miklós Ruszinkó, Gábor N. Sárközy, 'Linear Turán numbers of acyclic triple systems', European Journal of Combinatorics 99 (2022), 103435, DOI 10.1016/j.ejc.2021.103435. Results imported: (i) every fixed k-edge linear triple-system tree T_k satisfies ex_L(n,T_k) <= (2k-3)n; (ii) for n>16(k-1)^2+1, ex_L(n,M_k)=f(n,k), where f(n,k) is the maximum number of triples meeting a fixed (k-1)-set; (iii) ex_L(n,P_k)<=1.5kn for every k,n; (iv) ex_L(n,P_2)=floor(n/3); (v) ex_L(n,P_3)<=n, equality iff the system is a disjoint union of Fano planes; (vi) for F in {B_4,P_4}, ex_L(n,F)<=4n/3, equality iff the system is a disjoint union of affine planes of order 3; (vii) for the fourth four-edge tree E_4, 6 floor((n-3)/4)+epsilon <= ex_L(n,E_4)<=2n with epsilon determined by n-3 mod 4 as in Theorem 1.7. The paper also records the design lower benchmark: when n is divisible by 2k-1 and an STS(2k-1) exists, disjoint design components give n(k-1)/3 edges while avoiding every k-edge tree.
