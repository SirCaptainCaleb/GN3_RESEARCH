# Loss-one case A forms a terminal theta

## Statement

In loss-one sink case (A) of 87a6758ddc73, let P'=(p_1,...,p_{q-2}) be the x-ending path avoiding y,z with opposite last vertex a'. Let f_y and f_z be the two clean edges through a' containing y and z respectively, and let e={x,y,z}. Then P'∪{e,f_y} and P'∪{e,f_z} are linear cycles of length q, while {e,f_y,f_z} is a linear 3-cycle. Thus the union is a theta configuration consisting of the common long a'-to-e arc P' together with the two terminal branches f_y and f_z.

## Body

Because f_y is clean relative to P', it meets V(P') only at the endpoint a'; similarly for f_z. Since P' avoids y,z and ends physically at x, the edge e meets P' only at x. Therefore the cyclic edge sequence p_1,p_2,...,p_{q-2},e,f_y is linear: consecutive intersections are those of P', then x between p_{q-2} and e, y between e and f_y, and a' between f_y and p_1; all other intersections are absent. It has q edges. Replacing f_y by f_z gives the second q-cycle, using z instead of y. Finally f_y and f_z meet exactly at a' by linearity, while e meets them at y and z respectively, so e,f_y,f_z is a linear 3-cycle. Thus the union consists of the long arc P' together with the two terminal branches through y and z.
