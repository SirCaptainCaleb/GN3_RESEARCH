# First repetition in a flat transfer walk lifts to a labeled linear cycle

## Statement

Fix q>=2 and consider a walk in the threshold terminal graph R_q using rank-q ascending nonspecial hyperedges E_i={x_i,v_{i-1},v_i}, where x_i is the entrance color and phi(x_i)=q-1 while phi(v_j)>=q. Suppose we stop at the first repetition among the terminal vertices v_j and entrance colors x_i. Then the corresponding hyperedges contain a linear cycle. If the first repetition is a terminal vertex, the cycle is a rainbow cycle in R_q and every entrance x_i is private on the lifted hypergraph cycle. If the first repetition is an entrance color, the terminal-pair edges form a simple path and the first and last hyperedges close through the repeated entrance; that repeated entrance is the unique low-potential cycle joint and all other cycle joints are terminal vertices.

## Body

The potential separation phi(x_i)=q-1<q<=phi(v_j) implies that no entrance color equals a terminal vertex. If the first repeated object is a terminal vertex, take the subwalk between its two occurrences. By minimality, all internal terminal vertices are distinct and all edge colors on this subwalk are distinct. The corresponding hyperedges meet consecutively in the terminal vertices, the first and last meet in the repeated terminal, and no other nonconsecutive intersections occur: terminal pairs are disjoint off consecutive positions, colors are distinct, and colors cannot equal terminals. Hence they form a linear cycle, and the distinct entrance colors are private vertices of its edges. If instead the first repeated object is an entrance color x, take the subwalk from the first edge of color x to the second. By minimality the terminal vertices on this subwalk are all distinct and the intermediate colors are distinct. Properness of the coloring excludes the two equal-colored edges being adjacent. Thus their terminal pairs are disjoint, while the two corresponding hyperedges meet in x; all consecutive pairs meet in the terminal-path joints and all other nonconsecutive pairs are disjoint. This is again a linear cycle. Its closing joint x has potential q-1, whereas every terminal-path joint has potential at least q, so x is its unique low-potential cycle joint.
