# Shortcut map and amplification program from Astra direct proof

## Statement

After Astra's recovered direct proof, the earliest natural shortcut is immediately after the old low-rank terminal-capacity lemma a570ca900001: strengthen its path-contact count using ascendingness, then sum globally. The later potential-charged/0-1-1 machinery is unnecessary for 11/12. Moreover Astra's splice supports a stronger full conflict graph, already yielding the pending 43/48 leading coefficient caeba14e4f6b. Further improvement requires exploiting ascending labels, multiple rotated path states, or rank-sensitive incidence accounting rather than only optimizing endpoint constants.

## Body


RETROSPECTIVE SHORTCUT MAP AFTER RECOVERY OF ASTRA'S DIRECT PROOF.

The recovered theorem a57011120001 shows that the 11/12 leading coefficient requires only:
(1) unique entrance for nonspecial edges;
(2) one fixed ascending last edge h of rank q;
(3) disjoint path-contact sets for incident edges through a terminal v;
(4) local splice conflicts among singleton contacts;
(5) the global incidence identity 3m-A<=2S-n_+.

The earliest repository object already containing the correct local interface was a570ca900001:
if h is nonspecial rank q and v terminal, then
  J_q(v)={f:v in f, phi(f)<=q}
obeys |J_q(v)|<=2q-3.
Its proof already treats all low-rank incident edges, not merely nonspecial/terminal ones. The certified terminal saturation lemma 672725540541 explicitly asked for a second layer of critical slots/rotations. Astra's recovered argument is precisely such a second layer under the additional ascending hypothesis phi(x)=q-1.

Therefore the historical long route through potential-oriented charging, p=4/p=5 charged-edge classifications, two-rank blocks, 0-1-1 reduction, and source-rail lens networks was not necessary for 11/12. Those tools may remain relevant for stronger bounds, but the direct coefficient proof could have branched immediately after a570ca900001 (or logically even earlier, once unique entrance and ascendingness were defined).

A conceptually cleaner development order is:
  unique entrance
  -> local J_q(v) path-contact capacity
  -> second-order singleton conflict graph
  -> ascending terminal count
  -> global incidence defect.

STRONGER REUSE.
Astra's proof selected only a disjoint matching of local singleton conflicts. The full conflict graph contains, for every skipped path edge g_{i+1}, a complete bipartite exclusion between forward-facing singleton contacts on g_i and backward-facing singleton contacts on g_{i+2}. A four-state transfer argument on this graph gives caeba14e4f6b:
  |J_q(v)| <= (11/8)q+O(1),
hence
  A <= (11/16)S+O(n)
and
  m <= (43/48)S+O(n).
Thus the same local observation is stronger than the recovered 11/12 proof when its overlapping conflicts are retained instead of thinned to a matching.

FURTHER AMPLIFICATION OPPORTUNITIES.

(A) Use that the counted edges f relevant to A are themselves ascending terminal edges.
The full-conflict proof caeba14e4f6b deliberately ignores this and allows arbitrary low-rank incident f. Any additional source/terminal-label exclusion lowers the maximum cycle mean of the four-state contact process below 3/4 and improves 43/48 again.

(B) Re-run the contact conflict after rotations generated by two-contact blockers.
Astra's count treats a double contact as payment for up to two conflict constraints and stops. But double contacts are structured rotation objects. Replacing the host path by a length-preserving rotated path changes the conflict graph while preserving the same terminal family. Intersecting the marked-vertex requirements from two path states could force more than the single-state 5q/4 marked-vertex density.

(C) Keep the actual top ascending rank q(v), rather than relaxing q(v)<=phi(v) immediately.
The global proof bounds t(v) by a function of q(v) and then substitutes phi(v). A rank-sensitive summation may extract extra slack from vertices whose highest ascending terminal rank is substantially below phi(v). Existing examples show this gap can be large, so such a refinement must charge the high-potential tail rather than assume bounded gaps.

(D) Feed the local J_q(v) bound into global incidence accounting, not only into A.
At a vertex whose top relevant ascending rank is close to phi(v), the local theorem controls a large prefix of all incidences counted in 3m-A. A two-threshold incidence decomposition may simultaneously reduce the baseline 2S term and the A term. This is a distinct possible route to a larger coefficient improvement.

(E) Exact contact-conflict optimization.
The local singleton conflict process has a periodic extremal state pattern of mean 3/4 in the coarse model. Boundary optimization improves lower-order terms only. To beat 43/48 by this method one must add genuinely new conflict edges or constraints, most naturally from ascending labels or from a second rotated path state.
