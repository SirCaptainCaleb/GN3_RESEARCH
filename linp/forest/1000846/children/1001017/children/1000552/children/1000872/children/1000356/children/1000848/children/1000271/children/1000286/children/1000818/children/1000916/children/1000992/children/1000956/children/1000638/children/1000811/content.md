# The C4 residue is overlap-rich or a double triple-gate braid

## Statement

Assume the simple source-rail graph is the 4-cycle 01,12,23,30. After relabeling so that the four-overlap diagonal supplied by 905b4dff6b4c is 13, either the other diagonal pair Q_0,Q_2 has at least three distinguished common vertices, or there are two distinct vertices u_2,u_0 such that Q_0∩Q_1=Q_0∩Q_3={u_2} and Q_2∩Q_1=Q_2∩Q_3={u_0}; moreover u_2 is a same-index joint on Q_0,Q_1,Q_3 and u_0 is a same-index joint on Q_2,Q_1,Q_3. Thus the only non-overlap-rich C4 residue is a double triple-gate braid.

## Body

Use the notation e_i={x_i,v,u_i}. By 905b4dff6b4c, after swapping the two diagonals if necessary we may assume the diagonal 13 has source-only foreign contacts in both directions. Hence Q_1 and Q_3 share the four distinguished vertices x_1,x_3,u_0,u_2. For the other diagonal define A by u_2∈V(Q_0) and B by u_0∈V(Q_2). If A=0, complete foreign-edge transversality forces Q_0 to meet e_2 at x_2. Since x_2 is the last vertex of Q_2, the pair Q_0,Q_2 then shares x_2 in addition to the two baseline vertices u_1,u_3 forced by the simple cycle edges 01,12,23,30. Thus Q_0,Q_2 has at least three distinguished common vertices. The same conclusion follows if B=0, using x_0. It remains to consider A=B=1. Then u_2 lies on Q_0 and, because Q_1,Q_3 already share u_2, it lies on each of Q_1,Q_3 as well. But Q_0Q_1 and Q_0Q_3 are simple edges of the C4, so their singleton intersections must both equal u_2. The universal unique-intersection theorem therefore makes u_2 a same-index joint on Q_0 and Q_1 and also on Q_0 and Q_3; hence it occurs at one common joint index on all three paths. Likewise u_0 belongs to Q_2,Q_1,Q_3, and simplicity of Q_2Q_1,Q_2Q_3 forces both singleton intersections to be u_0. Thus u_0 is a same-index joint on Q_2,Q_1,Q_3. The two gates are distinct by linearity of the four offending edges through v. This is exactly the asserted double triple-gate braid. The case in which 02 is the four-overlap diagonal is symmetric.
