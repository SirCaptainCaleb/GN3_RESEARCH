# Terminal-tail blockers recover the sharp central-window packing bound

## Statement

Let v have p=phi(v). For potential-charged ascending nonspecial edges e={x,v,u} with v terminal, phi(u)>=p and phi(e)<=Q, where ceil((p+2)/2)<=Q<=p, let C_Q(v) be their set. Then |C_Q(v)|<=4Q-2p-3. Hence, if their ranks are q_1<=...<=q_k, then q_i>=ceil((2p+i+3)/4).

## Body

Fix a maximum p-edge path P=(g_1,...,g_p) ending at v and an edge e={x,v,u} of rank q in C_Q(v), where x is its unique entrance. Distinct charged edges through v have pairwise disjoint pairs {x,u}. We choose one witness from each pair on P, always choosing x when x lies on P. If x lies privately in g_i, the half-path endpoint-potential bound and phi(x)=q-1 give p-q+2<=i<=q-1. If x is the joint g_i cap g_{i+1}, they give p-q+1<=i<=q-1. Thus whenever x lies on P it already lies in the narrow final q-window. Suppose instead x is absent from P. The terminal-tail blocker lemma applied to the p-edge path ending at v implies that u occurs in one of g_{p-q+2},...,g_{p-1}; the alternative in which e is the last edge cannot occur with x absent, since in that case the penultimate edge meets e at x. If u lies privately in g_i, then p-q+2<=i, and because x is absent the prefix g_1,...,g_i,e is a linear path ending in e, so i+1<=q and i<=q-1. If u is the joint g_i cap g_{i+1}, tail membership gives p-q+1<=i, while the same prefix argument gives i<=q-1. Therefore every chosen private witness for an edge of rank q lies in g_i with p-q+2<=i<=q-1, and every chosen joint witness has p-q+1<=i<=q-1. For q<=Q these lie respectively in positions p-Q+2 through Q-1 and p-Q+1 through Q-1. The first set contains 2Q-p-2 private vertices and the second 2Q-p-1 joints, for a total of 4Q-2p-3 possible witnesses; the endpoint cases obey the same count. Since the witness pairs are disjoint, the chosen witnesses are distinct, proving |C_Q(v)|<=4Q-2p-3. For the i-th smallest charged rank q_i, the first i edges lie in C_{q_i}(v), so i<=4q_i-2p-3, which rearranges to q_i>=ceil((2p+i+3)/4). This recovers the numerical central-packing claim of the failed composition by using the later terminal-tail blocker lemma to repair exactly the missing terminal-witness localization.
