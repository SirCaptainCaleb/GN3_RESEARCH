# Near-dangerous gap-one states admit sixteen-elevenths lens-free overlap

## Statement

In the near-dangerous gap-one regime of a5700a110001, if the local deficit is o(q), then there exist vertices x,y with phi(x)=q-o(q), phi(y)=q-o(q) and maximum endpoint paths Q_x,Q_y such that |V(Q_x) intersect V(Q_y)| >= (16/11-o(1))q, and Q_x,Q_y contain no genuine clean internal lens with distinct sides.

## Body

By a5700a110001, for any choice m=m(q) tending to infinity with m=o(q), among the final m near-top canonical source rails there is a pair R_1,R_2 with |V(R_1) intersect V(R_2)| >= (16/11-o(1))q; both endpoint ranks are q-o(q). Fix R_2. Among all maximum endpoint paths Q_x ending at the endpoint x of R_1, choose one maximizing |V(Q_x) intersect V(R_2)|. Since R_1 is admissible, |V(Q_x) intersect V(R_2)| is at least (16/11-o(1))q. Put Q_y=R_2. The overlap-maximal normalization argument of e42651db0b02 now applies verbatim: if Q_x,Q_y contained a genuine clean internal lens, the clean-lens balance theorem would make its two sides equal in length, and replacing the Q_x-side by the Q_y-side would preserve maximum length while strictly increase vertex overlap with Q_y, a contradiction. Hence Q_x,Q_y are lens-free. The endpoint ranks are unchanged, so both are q-o(q).
