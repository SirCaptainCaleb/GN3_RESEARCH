# Sixteen-elevenths lens-free overlap retains linearly many whole distinguished pairs

## Statement

In the near-equality setup of a5700a110001, let U be the distinguished universe consisting of h=(11/8+o(1))q disjoint source-terminal pairs from the early common-terminal edges. One may choose two near-top maximum endpoint paths Q,R with |V(Q)∩V(R)|>=(16/11-o(1))q and no genuine clean internal lens, such that at least (7/88-o(1))q of the distinguished pairs {x_i,u_i} are wholly contained in V(Q)∩V(R).

## Body

Choose the pair R_1,R_2 of final canonical source rails supplied by the averaged-overlap part of a5700a110001, so |V(R_1)∩V(R_2)|>=(16/11-o(1))q. Write U for the 2h-vertex distinguished universe from that theorem, where h=(11/8+o(1))q. Every final chosen rail has only o(q) vertices outside U; in particular |V(R_2)\U|=o(q). Fix R=R_2. Among all maximum endpoint paths Q ending at the endpoint of R_1, choose one maximizing |V(Q)∩V(R)|. Since R_1 is admissible, |V(Q)∩V(R)|>=(16/11-o(1))q. By the overlap-maximal normalization argument e42651db0b02, Q and R contain no genuine clean internal lens. Because R has only o(q) vertices outside U, |V(Q)∩V(R)∩U|>=(16/11-o(1))q. Let B be the number of distinguished pairs {x_i,u_i}, i<=h, whose two vertices both lie in this common U-set. Among h disjoint pairs, a subset containing B whole pairs has size at most h+B: every non-whole pair contributes at most one vertex. Therefore B>=|V(Q)∩V(R)∩U|-h>=(16/11-11/8-o(1))q=(7/88-o(1))q. Thus the lens-free normalization preserves a linear family of whole labeled competitor pairs, rather than only unlabeled vertex overlap.
