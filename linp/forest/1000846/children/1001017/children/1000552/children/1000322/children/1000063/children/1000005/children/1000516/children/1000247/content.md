# Clean entrance replacements stay at maximum rank

## Statement

Let H be a linear 3-graph, let e be a nonspecial edge of globally maximum rank L with unique entrance x, and let P=(e_1,...,e_{L-1},e) be a globally longest path entering e through x=e_{L-1}∩e. Let f≠e contain x and suppose f meets V(P) only at x. Then φ(f)=L. Moreover, if f is nonspecial, its unique entrance is also x.

## Body

Because f meets V(P) only at x, the sequence e_1,...,e_{L-1},f is a linear path of length L ending in f and entering f through x. Since L is the global maximum edge rank, φ(f)=L. If f is nonspecial, every longest path ending in f has the same entrance label. The displayed L-edge path is longest and enters through x, so that unique entrance must be x.
