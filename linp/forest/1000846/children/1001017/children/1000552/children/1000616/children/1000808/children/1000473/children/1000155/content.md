# Path-relative central-window packing for common-terminal ascending edges

## Statement

Let P=(g_1,...,g_r) be an r-edge linear path ending at a vertex v. Let F_Q be any family of distinct ascending nonspecial edges e={x,v,u} for which v is a terminal vertex and phi(e)<=Q, where ceil((r+2)/2)<=Q<=r. Then |F_Q|<=4Q-2r-3. In particular, if q_1<=...<=q_k are the ranks of such edges, then q_i>=ceil((2r+i+3)/4) whenever P has length r at least q_i. No potential-charging assumption and no maximality of P at v are required.

## Body

For e={x,v,u} in F_Q write q=phi(e), with x its unique entrance. Since all edges in F_Q contain v and the hypergraph is linear, their pairs {x,u} are pairwise disjoint. Choose a witness on P as follows: choose x if x lies on P, and otherwise choose u. If x lies privately in g_i, then phi(x)=q-1 and the half-path endpoint-potential bound gives q-1>=max{i,r-i+1}, hence r-q+2<=i<=q-1. If x is the joint g_i cap g_{i+1}, it gives q-1>=max{i,r-i}, hence r-q+1<=i<=q-1. Now suppose x is absent from P. Apply the terminal-tail blocker lemma to e and P, whose length r is at least q. The alternative in which e is the last edge would put x on the penultimate edge, so it cannot occur. Hence u lies in one of the final q-2 precursor edges g_{r-q+2},...,g_{r-1}. If u is private in g_i, then r-q+2<=i; because x is absent, g_1,...,g_i,e is a linear path ending in e, so i+1<=q and i<=q-1. If u is the joint g_i cap g_{i+1}, tail membership gives r-q+1<=i and the same prefix argument gives i<=q-1. Thus every chosen private witness for an edge of rank q lies in positions r-q+2 through q-1, and every chosen joint witness lies in positions r-q+1 through q-1. For q<=Q all private witnesses therefore lie in positions r-Q+2 through Q-1 and all joint witnesses in positions r-Q+1 through Q-1. These sets contain respectively 2Q-r-2 private vertices and 2Q-r-1 joints, for 4Q-2r-3 possible witnesses. The chosen witnesses are distinct because the pairs {x,u} are disjoint, proving the bound. Ordering the edge ranks and applying the bound with Q=q_i gives i<=4q_i-2r-3 and hence q_i>=ceil((2r+i+3)/4).
