# Near-43/48 obstructions force one globally linear enriched payment currency

## Statement

Assume a 43/48 near-extremal sequence with S=sum_v phi(v), S/n_+ -> infinity. Then, after discarding a set of centers of total endpoint-potential o(S), every remaining relevant center v of sufficiently large p=phi(v) admits a selected strict-gap paid family H_v of size at least (1/8-o(1))p such that every e in H_v is source-clean, terminal-single at both terminals, has edge rank strictly below both terminal vertex ranks, carries its selected common-anchor paid certificate at v, and is a minimum-rank chord of a clean fundamental cycle in the terminal-pair graph. For each such v, at least one of the following holds: (U) at least (1/16-o(1))p members of H_v are terminal-retained on the host path; (T) at least (1/128-o(1))p distinct early doubly occupied certificate cells occur, hence that many switcher triangles; (Z) at least (1/128-o(1))p distinct early paid cells have distinct standard output edges contained in V_{>=p}. Consequently, after partitioning centers by a witnessing case, at least one of U,T,Z has total center-indexed mass Omega(S) (indeed at least (1/384-o(1))S using the weakest coefficient).

## Body

The strict-gap paid extraction and maximum-total-rank forest normalization preserve local mass: outside o(S) total potential, one may choose H_v with |H_v| >= (1/8-epsilon)p for any fixed epsilon>0, while every member simultaneously has the clean two-terminal gap, its selected paid certificate at v, and a minimum-rank fundamental-cycle blocker certificate. Apply the selected-family trichotomy to k=|H_v|. Either |U_v|>=k/2, giving |U_v|>=(1/16-epsilon/2)p; or there are at least k/16-O(log p) early switcher-triangle cells; or at least k/16-O(log p) early superlevel-output cells. Because bounded-p centers carry only o(S) potential in a near-extremal sequence, the logarithmic term is o(p) on all but o(S) potential mass, yielding the stated 1/128 asymptotic coefficients. Assign each good center to one witnessing currency. The sum of its currency contribution is at least (1/128-o(1)) times the good potential mass, hence at least (1/128-o(1))S. Pigeonhole over the three currencies gives one with total center-indexed mass at least (1/384-o(1))S. The fundamental-cycle certificate is retained because the trichotomy is applied to the normalized subfamily H_v itself.
