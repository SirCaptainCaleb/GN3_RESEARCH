# A late Class-III single blocker is pinned to the private vertex of g4

## Statement

In the Class-III rank-five nonspecial setup P=(g_1,g_2,g_3,g_4,e), e={x,y,z}, let o be the unique vertex outside P. If a terminal single blocker h={v,o,w} has no contact in W∩V(g_1∪g_2), then w is the private vertex of g_4, not the joint g_3∩g_4.

## Body

By 0dbbb7d78a48, any terminal edge with no contact in g_1∪g_2 cannot meet g_3, and any such terminal edge must be the unique single blocker {v,o,w} with w∈g_4\e.

The two vertices of g_4\e are the joint j=g_3∩g_4 and the private vertex p of g_4. If w=j, then h meets g_3 at j, contradicting the first conclusion of 0dbbb7d78a48. Hence w=p.

Therefore every genuinely late single-blocker defect is localized to one exact path slot: the private vertex of the penultimate precursor edge g_4.
