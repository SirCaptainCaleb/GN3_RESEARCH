# REFUTED: equal-rank competitor does not automatically block a loss-one wrong-entrance precursor

## Statement

Refuted. In a q-edge path R,h ending in h through the common terminal v, the last precursor edge of R contains v. Therefore another edge f through v is automatically not disjoint from R, and the proposed extension R,h,f has a nonconsecutive repeated v-contact between the last precursor edge and f. The argument cannot force f to have any additional precursor contact.

## Body

The v1 proof incorrectly states that the precursor R avoids v. If R,h enters h through terminal v, then the last edge of R meets h at v, so v belongs to R. Consequently every competing common-v edge f already meets R at v, and R,h,f is not a linear path: the last precursor edge and f are nonconsecutive but both contain v. No blocker conclusion follows. Do not use this lemma in the pure-high odd-boundary branch.
