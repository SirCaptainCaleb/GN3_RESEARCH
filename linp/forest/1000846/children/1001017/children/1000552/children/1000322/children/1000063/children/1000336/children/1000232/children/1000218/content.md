# An external fixed-hole edge through the alternate hole restores full length

## Statement

In the canonical loss-one two-cycle notation, let Q=(g_1,...,g_t), t=q-1, with g_1={a,a',c_1}, let the loss-one blocker be h={a,u,b_{i+2}}, and let b=b_{i+1} be the fixed hole of P_0,P_1. Let f be an edge through b which is disjoint from V(P_1) and suppose f contains a', the second vertex omitted from P_1. Then H contains a (q-1)-edge path ending at x and avoiding y,z, namely g_t,g_{t-1},...,g_{i+2},g_{i+1},f,g_1,g_2,...,g_{i-1}.

## Body

Since f is disjoint from P_1 and a' is outside P_1, write f={b,a',r} with r outside V(P_1). The displayed sequence has (t-i-1)+1+1+(i-1)=t=q-1 edges. Consecutive intersections are inherited from Q on the reversed suffix and forward prefix, together with g_{i+2} cap g_{i+1}={c_{i+1}}, g_{i+1} cap f={b}, and f cap g_1={a'}. The only second Q-contact of g_{i+1} is c_i in g_i, and g_i is omitted. The vertex a' occurs on Q only in g_1, while r lies outside P_1 and hence outside every retained Q-edge. Thus no nonconsecutive intersections occur. The first edge g_t has x as a free last vertex, so orienting the sequence toward g_t gives an x-ending path. Q and all added edges involved in the loss-one construction avoid y,z except the original terminal edge e, which is not used here, so the recovered path avoids y,z.
