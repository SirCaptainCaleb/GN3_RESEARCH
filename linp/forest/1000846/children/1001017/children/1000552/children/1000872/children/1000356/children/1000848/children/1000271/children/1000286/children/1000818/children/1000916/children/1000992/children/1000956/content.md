# The simple source-rail graph is triangle-free

## Statement

In a four-edge source-clean 0-1-1 consecutive-rank block, let G_s join two source rails exactly when they have a unique common vertex. Then G_s is triangle-free. Consequently |E(G_s)|<=4, and if |E(G_s)|=4 then G_s is necessarily a 4-cycle; the triangle-with-pendant extremal case is impossible.

## Body

Suppose three rails Q_i,Q_j,Q_k form a triangle in G_s. By the reciprocal-terminal characterization of simple pairs, Q_i and Q_j both contain u_k, Q_i and Q_k both contain u_j, and Q_j and Q_k both contain u_i. Since each pair has exactly one common vertex, their unique intersections are respectively u_k,u_j,u_i. These three vertices are distinct because the three offending edges are distinct and all pass through v, so linearity makes their non-v vertex pairs disjoint. By the universal unique-intersection theorem, each unique common vertex is a same-index joint on the two paths containing it. Let a,b,c be the corresponding joint indices for u_k,u_j,u_i. They are pairwise distinct: for example Q_i contains u_k at level a and u_j at level b, and two distinct vertices cannot both be the unique joint between the same consecutive pair of path edges. Relabel i,j,k so that a<b<c and so that Q_i contains the intersections at levels a and b, Q_j those at a and c, and Q_k those at b and c. On Q_i, the segment from u_k to u_j has b-a edges. The alternative route from u_k to u_j following Q_j from level a to level c and then Q_k backwards from level c to level b has (c-a)+(c-b) edges, which is strictly larger than b-a. Because the three rail pairs intersect only at the three displayed vertices, the interiors of these two alternative routes are disjoint from Q_i and from each other except at their consecutive joining vertex u_i. Replacing the internal Q_i segment between u_k and u_j by the longer Q_j-Q_k route therefore gives a linear path with the same last vertex as Q_i but strictly more edges, contradicting maximality of Q_i. Thus G_s has no triangle. A triangle-free graph on four vertices has at most four edges, and equality forces C_4.
