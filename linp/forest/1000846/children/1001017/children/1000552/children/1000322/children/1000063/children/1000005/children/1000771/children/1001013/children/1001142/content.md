# Matching deletions of cyclic STS(13) preserve rank six at every surviving block

## Statement

Let S be the cyclic STS(13) generated by the translates of {0,1,4} and {0,2,7}. If M is any matching of blocks, then every block e of S\M has edge rank φ(e)=6.

## Body

Write the two translation orbits of blocks as
A_i={i,i+1,i+4},  B_i={i,i+2,i+7}
for i in Z_13. Let M be any matching of blocks and let H=S\M.

We prove that every surviving block has edge rank 6. Since H has 13 vertices, no linear path has more than six edges, so it is enough to produce a spanning P_6 ending at each surviving block. By translation symmetry it suffices to treat A_0 and B_0.

A-ORBIT.

The all-A spanning path
A_0,A_1,A_2,A_6,A_7,A_8
has endpoints A_0,A_8. The affine automorphism f(x)=3x+1 fixes A_0 and acts on A-indices by i->3i. Using the A_0 endpoint and then the A_8 endpoint translated back to A_0, and applying f,f^2, gives six spanning paths ending at A_0. Their nonterminal block-index sets are

S0={1,2,6,7,8},
S1={3,5,6,8,11},
S2={2,5,7,9,11},
T0={5,6,7,11,12},
T1={2,5,7,8,10},
T2={2,4,6,8,11}.

Two A-blocks are disjoint exactly when their index difference lies in
K={2,5,6,7,8,11}.
Put L={1,3,4,9,10,12}. In the six blocker sets above, the indices in K occur four times each, while the indices in L occur once each. More precisely:

path   omitted K-pair   unique L-index
S0     {5,11}           1
S1     {2,7}            3
S2     {6,8}            9
T0     {2,8}            12
T1     {6,11}           10
T2     {5,7}            4.

The disjointness graph induced by the six A-blocks with indices in K is the 6-cycle
2-7-5-11-6-8-2.
Hence a matching contains at most two K-indexed A-blocks.

If it contains none, at most four L-indexed deleted blocks hit at most four of the six paths, since each L-index occurs in only one blocker set.
If it contains exactly one K-index h, that block hits exactly four paths. The remaining two paths have unique L-blockers, and inspection of the table shows that each of those two L-blocks intersects A_h; hence neither can join the matching.
If it contains two K-index blocks, they must be adjacent in the displayed 6-cycle. They hit five paths, and the unique missed path is exactly the row whose omitted K-pair is those two indices. Its unique L-block intersects both of those A-blocks, so it also cannot lie in the matching.

Thus M cannot hit all six A_0-ending spanning paths. Therefore A_0 has rank 6 in H whenever it survives.

B-ORBIT.

The all-B spanning path
B_0,B_2,B_4,B_12,B_1,B_3
has endpoints B_0,B_3. The affine automorphism h(x)=9x+2 fixes B_0 and acts on B-indices by i->9i. Repeating the same endpoint/stabilizer construction gives six B_0-ending spanning paths whose nonterminal index sets are

S0'={1,2,3,4,12},
S1'={1,4,5,9,10},
S2'={3,6,9,10,12},
T0'={1,9,10,11,12},
T1'={3,4,8,9,12},
T2'={1,3,4,7,10}.

For B-blocks the roles of K and L are reversed: B_i and B_j are disjoint exactly when j-i lies in L. The high-frequency set is now L, and its induced disjointness graph is the 6-cycle
1-4-3-12-9-10-1.
The corresponding table is

path   omitted L-pair   unique K-index
S0'    {9,10}           2
S1'    {3,12}           5
S2'    {1,4}            6
T0'    {3,4}            11
T1'    {1,10}           8
T2'    {9,12}           7.

Exactly the same three-case argument shows that no matching can hit all six B_0-ending spanning paths. Hence B_0 also has rank 6 whenever it survives.

Finally translate an arbitrary surviving A_i or B_i to A_0 or B_0. Translation preserves the cyclic STS and carries matchings to matchings. Therefore every surviving block of S\M has edge rank 6.
