# Star obstruction to unrestricted all-specialness

## Statement

A linear 3-uniform star with at least two edges has no special edges.

## Body

Let all edges share the center c and otherwise be pairwise disjoint. Any two distinct edges form a 2-edge linear path, while no three edges form a linear path because the first and third would also meet at c. Thus for every edge e, phi(e)=2. Every longest path ending in e enters e through c, so the two noncentral vertices of e can be terminal vertices but c cannot. Hence each edge contributes exactly two snake incidences and is nonspecial. Therefore the unrestricted conjecture 'all edges are always special' is false; a minimum-degree/core hypothesis is essential.
