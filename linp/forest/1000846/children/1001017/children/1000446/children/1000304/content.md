# Ascending-edge rainbow-layer formulation

## Statement

Let H be a P_ell^(3)-free linear 3-graph. For each nonspecial edge e of edge rank t with unique entrance x, call e ascending when φ(x)=t-1. Let A be the number of ascending edges. Then 3|E(H)|-A ≤ Σ_v(2φ(v)-1) ≤ (2ell-3)|V(H)|. Moreover, for each k≥1, the ascending edges with entrance level φ(x)=k form a properly edge-colored graph J_k on their terminal pairs, colored by x, and J_k contains no rainbow path of length k+1.

## Body

Proof. First fix a vertex v and q≤φ(v). Take a q-edge linear path ending at v. If an incident edge e with φ(e)≤q, other than the last edge of P, avoided V(P) outside the last edge, then it could be appended at v to obtain a (q+1)-edge path ending in e, contradicting φ(e)≤q. Thus every such e has a witness in V(P) outside the last edge. Distinct incident edges sharing v have distinct witnesses by linearity. Since V(P) outside its last edge has 2(q-1) vertices, at most 2q-1 incident edges have edge rank at most q. Now let e have edge rank t. If e is special, each of its three vertices is a last vertex of a t-edge path, so φ(v)≥t at all three incidences. If e is nonspecial with unique entrance x and terminal vertices y,z, then φ(y),φ(z)≥t, while deleting e from a longest t-edge path shows φ(x)≥t-1. Hence e contributes three incidences to the counts #{e contains v: φ(e)≤φ(v)} unless it is ascending, in which case it contributes only its two terminal incidences. Summing the preceding 2φ(v)-1 bound over v yields 3m-A≤Σ_v(2φ(v)-1)≤(2ell-3)n. For the layer statement, fix k and encode each ascending edge e={x,y,z} with φ(x)=k and φ(e)=k+1 as graph edge yz colored x. Simplicity and properness follow from hypergraph linearity. Every graph vertex occurring in J_k has vertex rank at least k+1, while every color has vertex rank exactly k. Therefore colors are disjoint from graph-path vertices. A rainbow path of k+1 graph edges has distinct colors, so the corresponding triples form a linear (k+1)-edge hypergraph path. The color on an endpoint graph edge is a private vertex of the endpoint triple and may be used as the last vertex of the lifted hypergraph path, forcing its vertex rank to be at least k+1, contradicting that every color has vertex rank k. Thus J_k has no rainbow (k+1)-edge path.
