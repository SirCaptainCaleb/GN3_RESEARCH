# Strict terminal-potential rise forces a narrower upper-half rank band

## Statement

Let e={x,v,u} be an ascending nonspecial edge with rank q=φ(e), and suppose p=φ(v)<φ(u). Then q>=ceil((p+3)/2).

## Body

The certified terminal-potential bound a7b7670e955a gives φ(u)<=2q-2. Strict rise gives φ(u)>=p+1. Hence p+1<=2q-2, so 2q>=p+3 and q>=ceil((p+3)/2).
