# Equal-potential ascending terminal graph has maximum average degree three

## Statement

Let H be a finite linear 3-graph. Form the simple graph T_= whose edges are the terminal pairs {u,v} of ascending nonspecial hyperedges satisfying φ(u)=φ(v). Then every nonempty subgraph J of T_= satisfies 2|E(J)|<=3|V(J)|; equivalently mad(T_=)<=3.

## Body

This is the equal-potential half of the modular reduction 6eab2b03f554. It is strictly weaker than the existing conjecture mad(T_up)<=3 because it discards every terminal pair with unequal endpoint potentials. The three-center one-factorization / K3,3 calibration shows that average degree three is a natural sharp target in the equal-level regime. Combined with any absolute bound on strict-rise outdegree it yields A=O(n), hence the 2/3 leading coefficient.
