# Global compensated ascending-defect bound

## Statement

It is enough for the general 2/3-leading-coefficient route to prove A-H_2=O(n), where A is the number of ascending edges and H_2 is the number of nonspecial nonascending edges e with unique entrance x satisfying φ(x)>2(φ(e)-1).

## Body

The refined accounting gives 3m-A+H_2<=sum_v(2φ(v)-1)<=(2ell-3)n for every P_ell^(3)-free linear 3-graph. Therefore any absolute constant C with A-H_2<=Cn yields m<=(2ell/3+(C-3)/3)n and hence leading coefficient 2/3. This is strictly weaker than both refuted pointwise terminal-degree conjectures and is naturally aligned with the Minty-type potential on the directed graph obtained from nonspecial edges: ascending edges contribute positive potential steps, while strongly nonascending edges are the largest negative compensators. The local counterexample 830b0775567f does not threaten this global statement because its four ascending edges require a long sparse support path.
