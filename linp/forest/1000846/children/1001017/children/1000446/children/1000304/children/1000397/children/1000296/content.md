# Refuted: compensated terminal-degree conjecture

## Statement

Refuted. The inequality a(v)<=3+h(v) is false. In counterexample 830b0775567f, one vertex satisfies a(v)=4 and h(v)=0.

## Body

The proposed compensated local inequality is false. In explicit counterexample 830b0775567f, four ascending nonspecial edges share the same last vertex v, and none of the nonspecial edges counted at v satisfies φ(x)>2(φ(e)-1); hence a(v)=4 while h(v)=0. The refined global accounting inequality 3m-A+H_2<=Σ_v(2φ(v)-1) remains a separate live result, but it cannot be closed by this local compensation rule.
