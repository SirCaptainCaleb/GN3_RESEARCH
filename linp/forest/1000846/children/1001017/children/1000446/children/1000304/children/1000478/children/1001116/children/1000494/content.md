# Five-edge deficit-doubling conjecture

## Statement

Let e_1,...,e_5 be five ascending nonspecial edges having a common last vertex v, ordered so that q_i=φ(e_i) satisfies q_1<=...<=q_5. Then 2q_4>=q_1+q_5+1.

## Body

The stronger four-edge midpoint conjecture is false: there are examples with φ-values (12,16,20,20), and even the version without the +1 can fail, as targeted path-plus-clique examples show. The five-edge statement allows bounded multiplicity at each deficit scale while still forcing geometric growth. It is compatible with the known unbounded construction d5e0ab668a51, whose deficits from the maximum satisfy an exact doubling recurrence, and with the currently known four-edge counterexamples. The intended proof mechanism is that, relative to the canonical precursor of e_5, three intermediate ascending edges should provide enough alternate blockers/rotations to uncross the splice that fails for four edges.
