# Four-edge spacing conjecture at a common last vertex

## Statement

Let e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4 be four ascending nonspecial edges having a common last vertex v, ordered so that q_i=φ(e_i) satisfies q_1<=q_2<=q_3<=q_4. Then 2q_2>=q_1+q_4+1. A stronger form with +3 is suggested by the clean common-path model and by the known extremal constructions.

## Body

This spacing statement is suggested by the unbounded common-last-vertex constructions. In the explicit four-edge counterexample the values are 12,18,21,21 and satisfy 2·18=12+21+3. In the path-plus-clique family the attachment recurrence gives the stronger identity 2q_2=q_1+q_4+3 for consecutive scales. The weaker +1 form is likely more robust under additional blocker contacts and already suffices for the logarithmic bound.
