# Refuted: ascending terminal-degree conjecture

## Statement

Refuted. It is not true that every vertex is a last vertex for at most three ascending nonspecial edges. The explicit counterexample 830b0775567f has four such edges at one vertex.

## Body

The original claim Δ(T^up)<=3 is false. Counterexample 830b0775567f consists of a 21-edge linear path together with three chord edges through the same last vertex; the last path edge and all three chord edges are ascending and nonspecial at that vertex. Retain this object only as a historical fence: local terminal degree cannot be bounded by 3, and the former repeated-blocker proof obligation is not a valid route to the 2/3 coefficient without additional global hypotheses.
