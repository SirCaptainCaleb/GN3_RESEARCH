# Finite falsification search for the potential-oriented local bound

## Statement

Exact induced-path enumeration on 2,250 generated small linear triple systems found no vertex incident as a terminal with four ascending nonspecial edges whose opposite terminals all have endpoint potential at least that vertex's potential. The maximum observed oriented count was three.

## Body

Two independent generated samples were checked by exact enumeration of induced paths in the intersection graph, from which phi(e), longest-path entrance labels, endpoint potentials a(v), nonspeciality, and ascending status were computed. Sample 1 contained 1,000 random linear triple systems on 7<=n<=11 with at most 12 hyperedges. Sample 2 contained 1,250 linear triple systems on 10<=n<=14 with at most 14 hyperedges, each forced to contain four hyperedges through one designated candidate terminal vertex before adding random pair-disjoint triples. In both samples, for every vertex v we counted ascending nonspecial edges e={x,v,u} with v terminal and a(u)>=a(v). No count at least four occurred; the maximum observed count was three. This is finite computational evidence only and is not an exhaustive classification.
