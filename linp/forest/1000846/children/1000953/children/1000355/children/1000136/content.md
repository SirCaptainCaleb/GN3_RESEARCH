# Arbitrary Latin lifts of the G0 five-cycle contain an almost-perfect packing of full laps

## Statement

Fix the rainbow 5-cycle of base edges in G0 and blow up its ten used base vertices by q, using an arbitrary Latin square on each of the five base triples. Label one joint cluster X_0={a_0,...,a_{q-1}} cyclically. For each t, let R_t be the family of five-edge lifted routes that start at a_t, traverse the five base-edge types once in cyclic order, and end at a_{t+1}. Then for q tending to infinity there is a set T of size q-o(q) and choices R_t in R_t for t in T such that the chosen routes are pairwise disjoint outside the prescribed X_0 endpoints. Equivalently, their four intermediate joint vertices and five private-symbol vertices are all distinct across chosen routes.

## Body

Build an auxiliary 10-uniform hypergraph A. Its vertex classes are: one obligation class O={0,...,q-1}; the four intermediate joint-cluster resources X_1,...,X_4; and the five private-symbol resource classes P_0,...,P_4. For each t and each lifted full-lap route from a_t to a_{t+1}, put one auxiliary edge containing t together with the nine resources used by that route. Once the four intermediate joint coordinates are chosen freely, all five private symbols are determined by the Latin squares, so each obligation has degree q^4. Every vertex in an intermediate X_i has degree q^4: for each t, fixing that coordinate leaves three free intermediate coordinates. Every private-symbol vertex also has degree q^4: for each t, fixing one Latin symbol imposes one quasigroup equation and leaves q^3 choices. Thus A is exactly q^4-regular. Any pair of auxiliary vertices lies in at most q^3 edges: an obligation plus a resource leaves at most three free intermediate coordinates; two resource constraints leave at most three degrees of freedom after summing over t, with same-class distinct pairs having codegree zero. Hence Delta_2(A)<=q^3=o(q^4). By the Pippenger near-perfect matching theorem for fixed-uniformity almost-regular hypergraphs with negligible codegrees, A has a matching covering all but o(q) of its 10q vertices. In particular it covers q-o(q) obligation vertices, yielding the claimed route packing. Source theorem: Pippenger-Spencer, JCTA 51 (1989), 24-42; standard almost-perfect matching formulation also appears in Keevash, Designs I, Theorem 2.13.
