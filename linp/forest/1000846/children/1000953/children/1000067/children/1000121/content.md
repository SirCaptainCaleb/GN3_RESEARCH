# AG(3,3) has a spanning P13

## Statement

The affine Steiner triple system AG(3,3) on 27 points contains a spanning 13-edge linear path. Hence the affine-plane P4 obstruction and the joint-sum condition do not extend to a non-Hamiltonian affine family in dimension 3.

## Body

The following affine lines, in order, give the claimed path:
{000,100,200},
{000,001,002},
{001,010,022},
{010,110,210},
{011,110,212},
{012,112,212},
{012,102,222},
{021,120,222},
{120,121,122},
{101,111,121},
{020,111,202},
{202,211,220},
{201,211,221}.
Here xyz denotes (x,y,z) in F_3^3. Consecutive displayed lines meet in exactly one point, every nonconsecutive pair is disjoint, and the union is all 27 points. Thus they form a spanning P_13.
