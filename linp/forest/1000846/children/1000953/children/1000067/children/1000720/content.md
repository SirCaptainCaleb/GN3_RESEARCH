# Human proof that every block of cyclic STS(13) is special of rank six

## Statement

In the cyclic Steiner triple system S on Z_13 generated by translates of B_1={0,1,4} and B_2={0,2,7}, every block has longest-path rank 6 and is special.

## Body

There are two translation orbits of blocks, generated by
B_1={0,1,4} and B_2={0,2,7}. It therefore suffices to treat one representative from each orbit.

For B_1, the six blocks
{0,1,4}, {1,2,5}, {2,3,6}, {6,7,10}, {7,8,11}, {8,9,12}
form a spanning linear P_6. Reversing this path gives a longest path ending in B_1 and entering through vertex 1. No path can have more than six edges because a 7-edge linear 3-uniform path uses 15 vertices whereas S has only 13. Hence phi(B_1)=6.

The affine map f(x)=3x+1 mod 13 is an automorphism of S: it fixes B_1 setwise and cyclically permutes its vertices 0->1->4->0, while it sends B_2={0,2,7} to {1,7,9}=B_2+7. Applying f and f^2 to the reversed spanning path gives longest paths ending in B_1 through entrance labels 4 and 0. Thus B_1 is special.

For B_2, start from the spanning path
{1,2,5}, {5,6,9}, {0,6,8}, {7,8,11}, {3,4,7}, {4,10,12}.
Translate every vertex by 3. The resulting spanning path ends in
{7,0,2}=B_2,
and its penultimate block meets B_2 at 7. Hence phi(B_2)=6 and there is a longest path ending in B_2 through entrance 7.

The affine map h(x)=9x+2 mod 13 is an automorphism of S: it fixes B_2 setwise and cycles its vertices 0->2->7->0, while it sends B_1={0,1,4} to {2,11,12}=B_1+11. Applying h and h^2 to the translated spanning path produces longest paths ending in B_2 through entrance labels 0 and 2. Thus B_2 is special.

Finally, translations x->x+t are automorphisms and act transitively on each of the two 13-block orbits. Therefore every block in both orbits has rank 6 and is special.
