# Leading terms for 3-uniform linear path Turán numbers

## Statement

Improve the leading terms in the general-length bounds for ex_L(n,P_l^(3)), with the supplied source bound ex_L(n,P_l^(3)) <= (l-3/2)n as the current internal upper benchmark; pursue either upper- or lower-bound improvements for general l.

## Body

The supplied linear_paths.tex develops the induced-path/intersection-graph equivalence, the snake digraph, the general r-uniform upper bound ex_L(n,P_l^(r)) <= (l-2+1/(r-1))n, hence (l-3/2)n for r=3, and a now-obsolete detailed P_4^(3) section. The present project focus is the general-length 3-uniform problem. Current literature results should be imported as literature objects and clearly separated from internal/new results.
