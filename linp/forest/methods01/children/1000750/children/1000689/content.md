# Combinatorial-topology and matching toolkit

## Statement

A compact reusable package of certified fixed-point, antipodal-state-space, and hypergraph matching tools used by LINP: the Sperner--Connector--Hex--Pouzet--Brouwer cycle, Tucker/Borsuk--Ulam, the Norine antipodal-coloring toolkit and its local reductions, and the Aharoni--Haxell/Ryser matching package.

## Body

Purpose. This node groups the certified combinatorial-topology and matching results that form a portable toolkit inside LINP and can be copied together to other managed research projects without mixing them with LINP-specific construction experiments. The package contains the Sperner--Connector--Hex--Pouzet--Brouwer equivalence cycle; Tucker's lemma and its Borsuk--Ulam bridge; the Norine antipodal-coloring theorem, rook-labeling reduction, and low-dimensional cube/2-complex forcing; and the Aharoni--Haxell hypergraph Hall toolkit together with the Ryser consequence for 3-partite 3-graphs. The child objects retain their own exact statements, proofs, source metadata, logical dependencies, and independent audit certificates.
