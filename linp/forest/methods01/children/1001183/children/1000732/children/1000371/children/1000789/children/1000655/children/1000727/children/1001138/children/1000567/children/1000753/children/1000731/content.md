# Retaining the strict rank gap gives three-fifths entrance-contact packing

## Statement

Let P=(g_1,...,g_p) be a maximum linear path ending at v in a finite linear 3-graph. Let X be ascending nonspecial edges terminal at v, each of rank at most R<p and each having exactly one off-v contact with P, namely its unique entrance. If X is nonempty, then |X| <= (3/5)(2R-p-1)+9/5. In particular, taking R=p-1 gives |X|<=3p/5. The same bound holds for any subfamily, including the entire entrance-contact family at a misaligned vertex.

## Body

Put z_k=g_k intersect g_{k+1}, and let b_k be the private vertex of an internal edge g_k. The singleton localization lemma puts all contacts into the N=2R-p-1 cells {b_k,z_k}, p-R+1<=k<=R-1; the first eligible private slot is actually unavailable, and may be allowed for an upper bound. Since R<p, all these are internal cells.

Write o_k=1 if a cell is occupied by a contact in X. A private contact in cell k forces o_{k-2}=o_{k-3}=0. A joint contact in cell k forces o_{k-1}=o_{k-2}=0. To see this directly, let an earlier edge e have unique contact first occurring in cell j, and let f be the later edge, with entrance y. For a private y=b_k, k>=j+2, the sequence g_1,...,g_j,e,g_p,g_{p-1},...,g_k is linear, ends at y, and has length p+j-k+2. The omitted interval separates the prefix and reversed suffix; e has no other contact with P. The edge f is not used. Since phi(y)=phi(f)-1<=R-1<=p-2, k-j>=p-phi(f)+3>=4. This forbids differences two and three. For y=z_k and k>=j+1, stop the reversed suffix at g_{k+1}; its length is p+j-k+1, giving k-j>=p-phi(f)+2>=3. This forbids differences one and two. This is the quantitative separation argument with the strict inequality phi(f)<p retained.

Now prepend three empty cells and partition the occupied cells into maximal consecutive runs. No run has length three: the third occupied cell can contain neither a private nor a joint contact, since both forbid occupancy two cells earlier. Distinct runs have at least two empty cells between them, again because both slots forbid occupancy two cells earlier.

Charge each run together with its preceding empty gap. If that gap has exactly two cells, the first cell of the run cannot contain a private contact, because the previous run ends three indices earlier. It therefore has at most one contact. A run of length one then has weight at most one in three cells; a run of length two has weight at most two in four cells, since its second cell cannot contain a joint contact. If the preceding gap has at least three cells, a one-cell run has weight at most two in at least four cells, and a two-cell run has weight at most three in at least five cells. Every ratio is at most 3/5. The charged blocks are disjoint and use at most N+3 cells. Hence |X|<=3(N+3)/5. If X is empty the final bound |X|<=3p/5 is immediate. This proves the theorem without a transfer computation.

The period-five abstract pattern (private+joint), private, empty, empty, empty attains the limiting 3/5 density under these exclusions; no claim of hypergraph realization is made. The previous three-quarters proof used only private exclusion at distance two and joint exclusion at distance one. The strict rank gap supplies the extra exclusions and changes the leading local density.
