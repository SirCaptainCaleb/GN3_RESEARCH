# Ma–Hou–Gao (2020): minimum degree and long linear paths in general 3-graphs

## Statement

Yue Ma, Xinmin Hou and Jun Gao proved asymptotically sharp vertex-degree thresholds in general 3-uniform hypergraphs for forcing linear paths of prescribed length. For k>=0, sufficiently large n, δ_1(H)>=kn+6k^2-3k+3 forces P_{2k+1}, and δ_1(H)>=kn+6k^2+7k+6 forces P_{2k+2}.

## Body

Paper: Yue Ma, Xinmin Hou, Jun Gao, 'Minimum degree of 3-graphs without long linear paths', Discrete Mathematics 343 (2020), 111949; arXiv:1903.04162. Theorem 1.2 gives: for n>=4k+19, minimum vertex degree at least kn+6k^2-3k+3 forces a linear path of length 2k+1; for n>=4k+21, minimum vertex degree at least kn+6k^2+7k+6 forces one of length 2k+2. Their thresholds are tight up to an O(k^2) additive term in the general 3-graph setting. This is conceptually relevant for endpoint-neighborhood and longest-path arguments, but the theorem itself is not directly competitive in the present linear-hypergraph regime: in a linear 3-graph d(v)<=floor((n-1)/2), while the Ma–Hou–Gao thresholds grow like kn for path length about 2k. Thus for k>=1 the general theorem is usually vacuous under linearity. Its proof methodology may nevertheless be useful.
