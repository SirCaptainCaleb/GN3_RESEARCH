# Zhou–Yuan (2025): general r-uniform path upper bound

## Statement

For r>=3 and ell>=4, ex_r^lin(n,P_ell^r) <= ((2r-3)ell/2)n.

## Body

Paper: Junpeng Zhou and Xiying Yuan, 'Turán problems for star-path forests in hypergraphs', Discrete Mathematics 348(11) (2025), 114592, DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2025.114592, arXiv:2403.06637. Main linear-path theorem: for r>=3 and ell>=4, ex_r^lin(n,P_ell^r) <= ((2r-3)ell/2)n. Thus r=3 gives 1.5 ell n, weaker than the supplied Devine–Milans (ell-3/2)n bound. The paper also gives lower constructions for linear paths/star-path forests using combinatorial designs, integer lattices, and Cartesian products. Its linear star-path forest theorem states: for r>=3, ell_0>=4, k>=0, ell_1>=...>=ell_k and L=max{ell_0,ell_1} when k>=1, for sufficiently large n, ex_r^lin(n, P_{ell_0}^r union S_{ell_1}^r union ... union S_{ell_k}^r) <= (k/(r-1)+(2r-3)L/2)(n-k)+k(k-1)/(r(r-1)). The paper further provides two-sided/upper ordinary Turán bounds for unions of paths and stars (Theorems 1.3 and 1.4), which are ancillary to the present linear-path target.
