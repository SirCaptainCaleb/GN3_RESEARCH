# Class-III blocker defects reduce to one or two explicit alternating chains

## Statement

In a 12-vertex 5-regular linear triple system, let P=(g_1,g_2,g_3,g_4,e) be a maximum five-edge path ending in a rank-five nonspecial edge e={x,y,z}. For the terminal blocker matchings on W=V(P)\e, the union has only one or two open alternating chain defects. Their endpoints are exactly the uncovered-mate and single-blocker data. Moreover, whenever a terminal single blocker has no early contact in g_1∪g_2, its path contact is forced to the private vertex of g_4.

## Body

The blocker-count identity gives total double-blocker matching size 6 or 7, hence one or two path components in the alternating union. For a terminal with one single blocker, the two unmatched vertices are precisely its blocker contact and uncovered mate, identifying the open-chain endpoints. Terminal-blocker localization excludes g_3 for a genuinely late defect; among the two vertices of g_4\e, the joint g_3∩g_4 is also a g_3 contact and is therefore impossible. The only late single-blocker contact is the private vertex of g_4.
