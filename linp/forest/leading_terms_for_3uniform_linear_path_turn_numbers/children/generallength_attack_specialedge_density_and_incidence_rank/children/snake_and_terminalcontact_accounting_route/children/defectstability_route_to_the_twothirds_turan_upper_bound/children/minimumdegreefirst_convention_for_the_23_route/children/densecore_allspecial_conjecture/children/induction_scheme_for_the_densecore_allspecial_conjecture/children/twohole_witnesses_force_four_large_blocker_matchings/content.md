# Good-residue two-hole witnesses force four large blocker matchings

## Statement

In the good residue classes, deleting either free first-edge vertex of a spanning top-rank core yields an alternate near-spanning wrong-entrance witness with exactly two holes. Its opposite endpoints have exact alternating blocker defects. For any such witness, the two endpoint blocker systems and the two hole blocker systems give four pairwise edge-disjoint matchings on the common interior set W, of sizes at least delta-3,delta-3,delta-4,delta-4. Hence their union has at least 4delta-14 edges; above the threshold equality cases this forces a vertex incident with blocker pairs from at least three distinct centers.

## Body

Induction supplies the two-hole alternate entrance witness and bounds each endpoint to at most two single blockers, encoding its double blockers as a matching. Each omitted hole similarly yields a large blocker matching after deleting pairs meeting the first path edge. Linearity makes matchings belonging to distinct centers edge-disjoint. Summing their sizes gives 4delta-14 edges on 2ell-6 vertices. In residues ell=0 or 2 mod 3, the excess over |W| is 2m-4, so positive excess forces degree at least three in the union; the small equality cases instead force a 2-factor.
