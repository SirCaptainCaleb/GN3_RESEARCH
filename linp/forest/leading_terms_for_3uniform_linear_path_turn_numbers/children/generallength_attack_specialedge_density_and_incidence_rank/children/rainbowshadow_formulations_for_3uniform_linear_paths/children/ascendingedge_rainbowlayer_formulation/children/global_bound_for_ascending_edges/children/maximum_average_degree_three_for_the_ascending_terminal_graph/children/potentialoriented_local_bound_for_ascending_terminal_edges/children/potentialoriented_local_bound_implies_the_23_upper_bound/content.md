# Potential-oriented local bound implies the 2/3 upper bound

## Statement

Assume 50b6278b9537. Then every finite linear 3-graph has at most 3n ascending nonspecial edges. Consequently every n-vertex P_ell^(3)-free linear 3-graph satisfies |E(H)|<=(2ell/3)n.

## Body

Let T_up be the terminal-pair graph of ascending nonspecial edges. For each ascending edge with terminal pair {u,v}, at least one endpoint has potential no larger than the other. Charge the edge to one such lower-potential endpoint, breaking ties arbitrarily. At a charged vertex v the opposite terminal u satisfies a(u)>=a(v), so 50b6278b9537 permits at most three charges. Therefore A<=3n. By the ascending-edge accounting 419519f0efa5, 3m-A<=(2ell-3)n. Hence 3m<=(2ell-3)n+3n=2ell n, proving m<=(2ell/3)n.
