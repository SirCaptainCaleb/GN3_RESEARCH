# Prefix-excess spacing converts large local switching families into early structural payment

## Statement

For distinct single entrance contacts on one host path, bounded entrance-prefix excess permits only logarithmically many contacts; quantitatively, #{f:sigma_f<=s}<=4s+7+4 floor(log_2(p+2)). In a selected D+Y switching family, contacts with excess sigma_f>s occupy at least half as many distinct early cells, each yielding either a switcher triangle or a paid output edge in V_{>=p}. ## Body

The clean-spacing recurrence makes attachment distances grow geometrically toward the last contact and yields the logarithmic bound when excess is controlled. Allowing prefix excess sigma weakens the recurrence by exactly sigma, giving the logarithmic-plus-excess counting inequality and the quadratic lower bound on total excess. For selected switching certificates, every high-excess entrance lies in an early two-slot cell; linearity limits each cell to two selected contacts. The D+Y semantics then turns every represented cell into either a doubly occupied switcher triangle or a paid cell with standard output in V_{>=p}. Splitting a general selected family into entrance-retained X and terminal-retained U contacts gives the stated three-way linear alternative.
