# A dense equal-potential terminal component forces two incident minimum-rank bicircular chords

## Statement

Let J be a connected subgraph of the equal-potential ascending terminal graph T_= on n vertices, and suppose |E(J)|>3n/2. Give each terminal-pair edge the rank of its corresponding ascending hyperedge. Choose, among spanning connected unicyclic subgraphs B of J, one of maximum total rank. Then E(J)\E(B) contains two edges f,g sharing a vertex v. Moreover each excluded edge h is minimum-rank on its fundamental bicircular circuit in B+h. In particular, for each of f and g, the B-edge adjacent to h at the common endpoint v on the fundamental circuit has rank at least phi(h), so h must block every longest terminal-v witness for that adjacent B-edge.

## Body

Because |E(J)|>3n/2, J contains a cycle and hence has a spanning connected unicyclic subgraph. Every such B has exactly n edges. Therefore |E(J)\E(B)|=|E(J)|-n>n/2. A matching on n vertices has at most floor(n/2) edges, so two excluded edges f,g share a vertex v. Now fix any excluded edge h=ab. The graph B+h has exactly two cycles in its unique connected component, and there is a unique minimal edge set C_h containing h whose removal of any one edge other than h restores a connected unicyclic spanning subgraph; equivalently C_h is the fundamental circuit of h in the bicircular matroid. For every e in C_h\{h}, the graph B-e+h is again a spanning connected unicyclic subgraph. Maximality of the total rank of B therefore gives phi(h)<=phi(e) for every e in C_h\{h}. Thus h is minimum-rank on C_h. At each endpoint of h, the fundamental circuit contains a B-edge incident with that endpoint; in particular let e_v(h) be the one adjacent to h at v. Then phi(e_v(h))>=phi(h). Both hyperedges are nonspecial and share v as a terminal vertex. By terminal adjacency forcing rank rise or a blocker, since phi(h)<=phi(e_v(h)), h must meet every longest path witnessing v as a terminal of e_v(h) in a second vertex. Applying this to f and g gives two excluded minimum-rank chords through the same terminal v, each carrying a canonical blocker obligation against its adjacent basis edge.
