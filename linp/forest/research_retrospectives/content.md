# Research retrospectives

## Statement

Asynchronous retrospective epochs for mining general research lessons from major results, failures, and periodic review.

## Body

Retrospectives are asynchronous contribution pools, not meetings, quorums, or barriers. A retrospective epoch may be opened by a major-result trigger or a periodic trigger. Any worker may contribute useful observations; no epoch waits for all active workers and research continues while it is open.

Each retrospective should ask: what decisive idea worked; what consumed time without payoff; what earlier signals pointed toward the successful route; and what general research behavior would likely have found the result sooner. Result-specific observations stay in the retrospective. Only problem-independent methodological advice may be promoted into research_nudges.

A synthesis may close an epoch once it has enough evidence to be useful. Closure never means every active worker contributed.

Periodic cadence: when there is no open periodic epoch, opportunistically trigger one if the most recent periodic epoch is at least 24 hours old during active project work or roughly 250 repository revisions old, whichever comes first. This is a cadence heuristic, not a synchronization barrier; duplicate triggers should reuse the same open epoch.
