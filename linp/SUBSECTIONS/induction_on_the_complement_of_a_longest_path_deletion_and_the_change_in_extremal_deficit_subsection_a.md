## Composition

The third term \(D_Y\) cannot be treated as a harmless remainder. It records genuine missing edges outside the longest path.

For a real number \(d\), define the \(d\)-deficit of a hypergraph \(G\) by
\[
r_d(G)=d|V(G)|-|E(G)|.
\]
