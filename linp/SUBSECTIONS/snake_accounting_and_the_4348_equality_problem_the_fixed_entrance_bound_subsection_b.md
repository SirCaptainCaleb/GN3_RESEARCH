## Development

For every nonisolated vertex \(v\),
\[
t(v)\le \gamma(\phi(v)). \tag{3}
\]

#### Proof
Choose \(h\in T(v)\) of maximum edge rank \(q\). Every member of \(T(v)\) has edge rank at most \(q\), and \(q\le\phi(v)\). Apply Lemma 1 and monotonicity of \(\gamma\). ∎
