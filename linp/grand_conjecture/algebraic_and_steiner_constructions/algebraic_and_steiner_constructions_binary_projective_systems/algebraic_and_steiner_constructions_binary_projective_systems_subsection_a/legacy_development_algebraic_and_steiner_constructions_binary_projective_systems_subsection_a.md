## Development

Let
\[
V=\mathbb F_2^d\setminus\{0\},
\]
and let \(H_d\) have as edges the triples
\[
\{x,y,x+y\}
\]
with \(x,y\) distinct. This is the projective Steiner triple system on \(2^d-1\) points.

A spanning path in \(H_d\) has a useful parity constraint.
