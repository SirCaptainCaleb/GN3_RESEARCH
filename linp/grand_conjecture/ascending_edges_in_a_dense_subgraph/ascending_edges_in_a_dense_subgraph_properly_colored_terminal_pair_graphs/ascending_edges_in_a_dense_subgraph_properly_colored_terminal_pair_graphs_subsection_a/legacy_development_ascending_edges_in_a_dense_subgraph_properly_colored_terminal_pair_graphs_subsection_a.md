## Development

Fix \(t\ge1\). Form a graph \(R_t\) as follows. For every nonspecial edge
\[
e=\{x,u,v\}
\]
whose unique entrance satisfies \(\phi(x)<t\) and whose two terminal vertices satisfy
\[
\phi(u),\phi(v)\ge t,
\]
put the graph edge \(uv\) in \(R_t\) and color it by \(x\).
