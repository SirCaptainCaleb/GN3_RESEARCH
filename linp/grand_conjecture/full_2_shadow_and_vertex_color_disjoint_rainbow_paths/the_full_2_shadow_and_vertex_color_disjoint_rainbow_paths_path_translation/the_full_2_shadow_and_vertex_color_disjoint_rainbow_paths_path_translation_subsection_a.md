## Composition

Let
\[
x_0x_1\cdots x_k
\]
be a graph path in \(G\), and write
\[
c_i=c(x_{i-1}x_i).
\]
The corresponding hyperedges are
\[
e_i=\{x_{i-1},x_i,c_i\}. \tag{5}
\]
