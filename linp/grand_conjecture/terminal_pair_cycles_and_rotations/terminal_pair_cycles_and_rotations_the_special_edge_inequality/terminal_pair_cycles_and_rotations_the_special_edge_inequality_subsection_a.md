## Composition

Form the snake digraph of \(H\): for every edge \(e\) and every vertex \(v\in e\) with
\[
\phi(e,v)=\phi(e),
\]
include the incidence \((e,v)\). A special edge contributes three such incidences; a nonspecial edge contributes exactly two.
