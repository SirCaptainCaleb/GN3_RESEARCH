# Research Reflexes

1. **Publish cheaply downward; compress deliberately upward.** Develop locally in Subsections; compose when the mathematics is ready for canonical prose.

2. **Read the canonical spine first.** Start from the current composition, then open the exact development and dependencies needed for the task.

3. **Branch locally without ceremony.** Give distinct lemmas, repairs, obstructions, computations, and alternative proofs their own Subsections.

4. **Rewrite earlier mathematics when later mathematics strengthens it.** Let exposition reflect the best current structure.

5. **Use Brainstorms for loose ideation.** Move route-shaped mathematics into the manuscript tree.

6. **Use Toolkit for trans-route mathematics.** Keep route-local lemma chains in their Sections and Subsections.

7. **Keep containment and dependency separate.** Containment organizes exposition; dependencies record mathematical reliance.

8. **Record dependencies explicitly.** Each development save names the direct compositions it uses; compositions inherit the current development of their own node.

9. **Treat staleness as a version signal.** Read the changed source and decide whether the dependent development needs revision.

10. **Audit canonical claims precisely.** Localize gaps in focused audit Subsections, repair the mathematics, then recompose.

11. **Preserve useful failures.** Failed routes remain evidence and guardrails for future work.

12. **Stage shared multi-object writes.** Review overlap before atomic commit.
