# From order fifteen onward a sharp half-order transversal forces local disturbance or reciprocal direct crossings

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample in the sharp half-order shell of order n>=15. If a cyclic deletion-cover transversal exists, then it exposes inherited support crossing or order disagreement by ff36134f3abb. Otherwise choose a forest transversal J whose decreasing component-size sequence is lexicographically maximal, and choose Hamilton orders on its support vertices. Then either some length-two walk of J exposes inherited three-part support crossing or order disagreement, or there exist two selected deletion covers from distinct components of J such that each contains an ordinary edge on the common double-deletion core crossing the other cover's induced support partition.

## Body

If a cyclic transversal exists, the first conclusion follows from ff36134f3abb. Otherwise choose a lexicographically maximal forest transversal J. Suppose no length-two walk of J exposes inherited three-part support crossing or order disagreement, and suppose no two selected deletion covers from distinct components have reciprocal ordinary core edges crossing the other cover's induced support partition on their common double deletion. Then foresttransferbound13 gives |E(J)|<=13. But J has exactly n selected edges, one for each deletion label, whereas n>=15. This contradiction proves that one of the two asserted disturbance alternatives occurs. ∎
