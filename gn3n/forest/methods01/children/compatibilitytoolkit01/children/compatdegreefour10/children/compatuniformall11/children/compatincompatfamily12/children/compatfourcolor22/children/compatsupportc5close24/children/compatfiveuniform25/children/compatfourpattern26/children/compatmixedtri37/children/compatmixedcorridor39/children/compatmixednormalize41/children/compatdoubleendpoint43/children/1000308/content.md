# Every deletion state yields support/order disagreement, a facing-endpoint five-set, or four same-end tight triples

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-b=P|Q be a deletion cover with |P|,|Q|>=3. Choose any endpoint a of P and any endpoint c of Q, and choose arbitrary deletion covers C_a of H-a and C_c of H-c. Let u and v be the neighbors of a and c on P and Q. Then at least one of the following holds: (i) one endpoint comparison exposes an internal-restoration support crossing or an order disagreement on the corresponding common two-deletion subtournament; (ii) if a and c are facing endpoints of the displayed paths, either the appropriate reverse cross triple through b is tight, or W={a,u,b,c,v} is Hamiltonian and H-W is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (iii) if a and c are both initial or both terminal and both endpoint comparisons are same-slot replacements by b, then four endpoint triples are tight. In the terminal-terminal case, (b,a,u),(a,b,u),(b,c,v),(c,b,v) are tight; in the initial-initial case (u,a,b),(u,b,a),(v,c,b),(v,b,c) are tight.

## Body

Apply the endpoint-state trichotomy from deletion01 separately at a and c, using C_a and C_c. If either comparison gives an internal-restoration support crossing or an order disagreement, conclusion (i) holds. Otherwise both comparisons are same-slot replacements by b. If a and c are facing endpoints, apply ba08d1540dd1 to the displayed deletion cover H-b=P|Q. That theorem gives either the appropriate reverse cross triple through b or a Hamiltonian five-set W={a,u,b,c,v} in the displayed order. Since W is proper in a minimum counterexample, H-W has path-cover number at most two; it cannot be Hamiltonian, since then W and H-W would give a spanning two-cover of H. Hence H-W is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, proving (ii). If a and c are both initial or both terminal, apply 8b617062a9ed to the two same-slot replacements. It gives the four tight endpoint triples stated in (iii), with the terminal-terminal and initial-initial orientations related by the stated symmetry. This exhausts the endpoint-position cases. No cyclic rotation of an ordered triple is used.
