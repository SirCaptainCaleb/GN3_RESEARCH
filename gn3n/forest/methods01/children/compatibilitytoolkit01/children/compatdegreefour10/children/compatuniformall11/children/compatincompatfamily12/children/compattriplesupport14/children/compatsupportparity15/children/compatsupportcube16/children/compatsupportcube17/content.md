# The missing antipodal cube states have deficit one or two

## Statement

Retracted: the claimed deficit bound for missing cube corners is unsupported.

## Body

The proof used the false assertion that longest-path deficit changes by at most one when a vertex moves between supports. In a tight 3-uniform path, deleting an internal vertex can destroy the newly adjacent triple, so longest-path order may drop by more than one. The D<=2 conclusion and all descendants depending on it are therefore retracted.
