# Full deletion compatibility leaves only transport, disagreement, or a disturbed balanced odd cycle

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and choose one deletion two-cover F_v of H-v for every vertex v. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) two chosen covers are support-compatible but order-incompatible; (2) for some anchor F_x=P|Q and some endpoint y of P or Q, the chosen cover F_y contains an ordinary path edge joining surviving vertices from the two support classes of F_x; (3) |V(H)|=2k+1 and the chosen supports form the spanning balanced odd-cycle configuration, but this cycle has a positioned two-deletion disturbance: for some consecutive cycle labels x_i,x_{i+1}, a two-cover of H-{x_i,x_{i+1}} crosses the three inherited pieces at an internal exchanged label, or the two inherited endpoint-deleted covers have relative-order disagreement. In case (3), if lambda is the maximum tight-path order then tau*(H)<=2+1/lambda<=2+1/k.

## Body

Assume no support-compatible order disagreement among the chosen deletion covers. By the fixed-path block structure of retain_one_fixed_hamilton_path_except_for_a_spanning_odd_cycle, every ordinary cyclic block of the full-compatibility graph carries one common ordered Hamilton path. Such a block is K4-minor-free by compatibility_graphs_exclude_k4_minors_unconditionally, hence has a degree-two vertex d. Let a,b be its block-neighbors. If ab is absent, then F_a and F_b share the common block support but are not fully compatible, so they are order-incompatible, contradiction. If ab is present, a,b,d form a compatibility triangle. Deleting d preserves 2-connectivity; iterating the degree-two peel either exposes the preceding nonadjacent-neighbor disagreement or reaches a compatibility diamond. The common-gap theorem gapgeom01 rules out a fourth state compatible with two vertices of a cyclic triangle, while a transitive diamond forces pair-order reversal. Thus every ordinary cyclic block with at least four vertices contradicts the no-disagreement assumption. A triangle block is consumed by compatibility_triangle_endpoint_transport01: unless order reversal occurs, it yields a direct endpoint mixed-support edge, giving (2). Therefore, if neither (1) nor (2) occurs, there are no ordinary cyclic blocks unless the whole graph is the exceptional spanning balanced odd cycle. If instead the compatibility graph is a forest, choose a leaf x and write F_x=P|Q. If x has one compatibility neighbor, choose Q to be the anchor side not containing it; if x is isolated choose either side. The two endpoint deletion covers of Q exist because every vertex deletion has been selected, and neither endpoint label is adjacent to x. With order disagreement excluded, both endpoint covers are support-incompatible with F_x. The two-endpoint probe theorem probes_force_a_direct_mixed_edge_or_order_disagreement then yields a direct P-Q path edge in one endpoint cover or order disagreement, giving (2) or (1). Hence failure of (1),(2) forces the spanning balanced odd support cycle on n=2k+1 labels. Because H is a minimum counterexample, every consecutive double deletion has a two-cover. The strengthened odd-cycle theorem of_selected_deletion_supports_force_twodeletion_disturbance therefore applies and supplies the positioned three-part crossing or inherited relative-order disagreement stated in (3). Finally gives_fractional_masstwo_certificates_and_a_dual_obstruction gives a canonical fractional path cover of mass 2+1/|T| for every nonempty tight path T in this odd-cycle configuration. Taking T longest gives tau*(H)<=2+1/lambda, and lambda>=k because the selected supports themselves are Hamiltonian k-sets, so tau*(H)<=2+1/k. No claim lambda>=k+1 is made without an additional consumer of the three-part crossing.
