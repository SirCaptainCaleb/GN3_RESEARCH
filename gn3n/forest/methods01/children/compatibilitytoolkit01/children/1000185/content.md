# Stability at the Mantel barrier

## Statement

For a large family of chosen deletion covers, suppose the full-compatibility graph is close to the certified Mantel upper bound. Conjecture that its near-extremal bipartition can be lifted from an abstract graph partition to a coherent two-class structural partition of the deletion states, with cross-class incompatibilities sharing a common order-disagreement or endpoint-separation pattern. In the exact equality case, seek a rigid complete-bipartite model whose two sides canonically induce the two paths of a global cover.

## Body

This attacks the equality/near-equality regime rather than strengthening the density inequality. The key question is whether extremal graph structure has mathematical content in the underlying deletion states.
