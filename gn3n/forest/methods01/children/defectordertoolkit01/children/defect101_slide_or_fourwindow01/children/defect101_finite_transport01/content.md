# Linear 101 defect transport terminates at a three-side deletion cover or an inherited-complement four-window

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament with path-cover number greater than two and let pi be a spanning ordering with exactly the linear 101 defect pattern. Let x be the middle vertex of its three-center defect window, so by defectspanisdeletion the ordering is the canonical concatenation P,x,Q of a deletion two-cover H-x=P|Q. Then, while keeping the omitted label x fixed, one may repeatedly apply the left transfer of defect101_slide_or_fourwindow01. After finitely many steps, one of two outcomes occurs: (1) H-x has a deletion two-cover P_star|Q_star with |P_star|=3; or (2) a transfer is blocked and H contains the explicit tight four-path W supplied by defect101_slide_or_fourwindow01, with H-W covered by the inherited prefix and suffix paths at that blocked state. The number of successful transfers before termination is at most |P|-3. Symmetrically one may transport toward the other end and obtain a deletion cover with |Q_star|=3 or the symmetric inherited-complement four-window.

## Body

In the 101 ordering, defectspanisdeletion identifies the omitted label x as the vertex between the two defective join centers and gives H-x=P|Q. Inspect the left-swap conclusion of defect101_slide_or_fourwindow01. When the shift succeeds, the same vertex x is still the middle vertex of the shifted 101 window: the swap moves x one position to the left, while the vertex immediately preceding x is transferred from the end of P to the beginning of Q. Thus the new 101 ordering is again the canonical concatenation of a deletion cover of the SAME H-x, now with component orders |P|-1 and |Q|+1. No deletion label changes.\n\nIterate. At each successful left shift, |P| decreases by one, so this process is well-founded. The local slide theorem also says every 101 state has its left defect center at least 3, equivalently the corresponding left deletion-cover component has order at least three. Therefore there can be at most |P|-3 successful left shifts. If the process reaches |P|=3, outcome (1) holds. Otherwise the next attempted shift is blocked; defect101_slide_or_fourwindow01 then supplies its tight four-path W and the explicit inherited two-path cover of H-W, giving outcome (2). The rightward statement is identical, with the roles of P and Q reversed.
