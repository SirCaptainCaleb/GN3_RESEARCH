# The connector-free gap-two peel forces a blocked endpoint-transfer square and two order-incompatible five-paths

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-x=P|Q be a deletion cover attaining the globally minimum smaller-side order mu, with P=(p_0,...,p_r), |P|=mu, Q=(q_0,...,q_s), and |Q|=mu+2. Assume the connector-free branch of fe98a0b8adf0. Then H-x has all four two-covers obtained by independently transferring q_s and q_0 to the corresponding ends of P: P|Q, (q_s,P)|(Q-q_s), (P,q_0)|(Q-q_0), and (q_s,P,q_0)|(Q-{q_0,q_s}). For every one of these four covers, adjoining x to either component gives a non-Hamiltonian induced subtournament. Moreover the two balanced middle covers force (p_r,x,q_0) and (q_0,x,q_1) to be non-tight, so W_1=(q_1,q_0,x,p_r,p_{r-1}) and W_2=(q_2,q_1,x,q_0,p_r) are tight Hamiltonian five-paths. Their common four-set D={q_1,q_0,x,p_r} is Hamiltonian in the two orders (q_1,q_0,x,p_r) and (q_1,x,q_0,p_r), which reverse the order of q_0 and x. The complements of W_1 and W_2 are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.

## Body

The connector-free gap-two conclusion of fe98a0b8adf0 gives the four endpoint-transfer covers: global minimum-side extremality rules out transfers out of P, so the two large-side endpoints q_s,q_0 may be transferred independently, with the mixed states balanced of order mu+1|mu+1. For any corner support A and its complementary Hamiltonian support B, H[A union {x}] cannot be Hamiltonian, since a Hamilton path on A union {x} together with B would two-cover H; the same argument with A,B exchanged blocks all eight x-enlargements. Now use the balanced cover A=(q_s,p_0,...,p_r), B=(q_0,q_1,...,q_{s-1}). In the singleton lift A|{x}|B, the endpoint joins (p_{r-1},p_r,x) and (x,q_0,q_1) and the cyclic seam (q_{s-1},q_s,p_0) are non-tight. If the remaining central join (p_r,x,q_0) were tight, the cyclic defect graph would have run type {1,1,1}, contrary to 8ededbb50716. Hence (q_0,x,p_r) is tight. Endpoint hooks c38e8b5c48ee give (q_1,q_0,x) and (x,p_r,p_{r-1}), yielding W_1. Applying the same argument to the other balanced cover (p_0,...,p_r,q_0)|(q_1,...,q_s) shows (q_1,x,q_0) tight; the endpoint hooks give W_2. Restricting W_1,W_2 gives the two displayed Hamilton orders on D. Since H has order greater than ten, W_1,W_2 are proper, and mincex01 gives non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements.
