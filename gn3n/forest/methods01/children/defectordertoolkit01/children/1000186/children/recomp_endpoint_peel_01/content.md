# Every deletion cover has facing connector-or-transfer forks, and a globally minimum side has a two-end peel

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-x=P|Q be a deletion two-cover with displayed paths P=(p_0,...,p_r), Q=(q_0,...,q_s), both of order at least three. Then the canonical span-three ordering P,x,Q gives at the facing pair p_0,q_s at least one of: (i) the tight four-path (p_1,p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}); (ii) the deletion cover (q_s,p_0,...,p_r)|(q_0,...,q_{s-1}); (iii) the deletion cover (p_1,...,p_r)|(q_0,...,q_s,p_0). The symmetric fork holds at q_0,p_r. Before this fork, one of the two cross-component triples (p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}) and (p_1,p_0,q_s) is tight, together with the endpoint-hook triples (x,q_s,q_{s-1}) and (p_1,p_0,x). If |P|=mu is globally minimum among smaller component orders of deletion covers and |P|<=|Q|, then the alternatives transferring p_0 or p_r out of P are impossible. Consequently either a cross-end tight four-path occurs, or both endpoints q_s,q_0 of Q transfer into P and H-x has the two-cover (q_s,p_0,...,p_r,q_0)|(q_1,...,q_{s-1}). In the latter case |Q|>=mu+2. If |Q|=mu+2 and the next minimum-side state is also connector-free, repeating the same fork returns exactly to the original support/order pair P|Q.

## Body

The minimum-span-three hook theorem gives one of the cross-component triples (p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}) and (p_1,p_0,q_s), while endpoint-hook forcing gives (x,q_s,q_{s-1}) and (p_1,p_0,x). In the first cross-hook case, reversal antisymmetry at {p_1,p_0,q_s} either gives (p_1,p_0,q_s), producing the tight four-path (p_1,p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}), or gives (q_s,p_0,p_1), which extends through P and yields the transfer cover (q_s,P)|(Q-q_s). In the second cross-hook case, reversal antisymmetry at {p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}} either produces the same four-path or gives (q_{s-1},q_s,p_0), yielding the transfer cover (P-p_0)|(Q,p_0). Applying the identical argument to Q,x,P gives the symmetric fork at q_0,p_r. Now assume |P|=mu is the globally minimum smaller deletion-cover side. Any alternative that removes p_0 or p_r from P would create a side of order mu-1, impossible. Hence, unless one of the two cross-end four-paths occurs, q_s transfers into the initial end of P and q_0 into its terminal end. The two transfer triples are simultaneously available, so (q_s,p_0,...,p_r,q_0) is tight and (q_1,...,q_{s-1}) is inherited from Q. Their orders are mu+2 and |Q|-2; global minimality forces |Q|-2>=mu. If equality |Q|=mu+2 holds, the new smaller side is Q^circ=(q_1,...,q_{s-1}) of order mu. Reapplying the same result to this minimum-side cover, absence of a connector transfers the two endpoints q_s,q_0 of the larger path back into Q^circ, reconstructing Q and leaving P. Thus the connector-free gap-two state is a two-state endpoint-peel cycle.
