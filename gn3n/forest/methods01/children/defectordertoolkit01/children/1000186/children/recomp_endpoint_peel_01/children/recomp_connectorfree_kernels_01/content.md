# Connector-free minimum-side peeling gives reverse hooks, endpoint four-set classification, and a Hamiltonian four-set

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample. Let mu be the globally minimum smaller-component order among one-vertex deletion two-covers, and let H-x=P|Q with |P|=mu<=|Q|, where P=(p_0,...,p_r) and Q=(q_0,...,q_s), both of order at least three. Suppose neither facing endpoint pair gives the four-vertex connector alternative of ab8c0e2136f9. Then: (1) T=(q_s,p_0,...,p_r,q_0) is a tight path, Q^circ=(q_1,...,q_{s-1}) is tight, and (q_1,q_0,p_r) and (p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}) are tight; (2) if the facing Hamiltonian five-set alternative is absent at the left endpoint, then X_L={p_1,p_0,x,q_s} is either Hamiltonian or is non-Hamiltonian edge-orderable with matching-block order {p_1x,p_0q_s} < {p_1p_0,xq_s} < {p_1q_s,p_0x}, with the symmetric conclusion for X_R={q_1,q_0,x,p_r}; (3) if X_L is non-Hamiltonian, then either some j gives a tight four-path (p_j,p_0,x,p_{j+1}) or (p_j,x,p_0,p_{j+1}), or both (p_j,p_0,x) and (p_j,x,p_0) are tight for every 1<=j<=r, with the symmetric conclusion on Q; (4) if both facing Hamiltonian five-sets are absent and both X_L,X_R are non-Hamiltonian matching-block four-sets, then H contains a Hamiltonian four-set meeting x and both original deletion-cover components; more precisely, either one of the four-paths in (3) occurs or (q_0,p_r,x,p_0) is a tight Hamiltonian four-path.

## Body

Apply 70435ae42299. It gives the tight path T=(q_s,p_0,...,p_r,q_0), the inherited complementary path Q^circ=(q_1,...,q_{s-1}), and the two tight reverse hooks (q_1,q_0,p_r) and (p_0,q_s,q_{s-1}); no tight-cycle conclusion is used. At the left endpoint apply 69cfeba96c13 to X_L={p_1,p_0,x,q_s}. If the corresponding facing Hamiltonian five-set is absent, then X_L is either Hamiltonian or non-Hamiltonian edge-orderable with matching-block order {p_1x,p_0q_s} < {p_1p_0,xq_s} < {p_1q_s,p_0x}. The symmetric conclusion holds for X_R={q_1,q_0,x,p_r}. In the non-Hamiltonian left case, this matching order gives both (p_1,p_0,x) and (p_1,x,p_0) tight. By 33a06ec35e0f, equivalently by applying e70ecf96df64 along (p_1,...,p_r), either some adjacent pair p_j,p_{j+1} gives a tight four-path (p_j,p_0,x,p_{j+1}) or (p_j,x,p_0,p_{j+1}), or for every j the two triples (p_j,p_0,x) and (p_j,x,p_0) are tight. The symmetric statement holds along Q. Finally assume both endpoint four-sets are non-Hamiltonian matching-block sets and neither facing Hamiltonian five-set occurs. Apply 8797f79d4a78. If propagation opens one of the four-paths just displayed, its support is Hamiltonian. Otherwise propagation reaches p_r, so (p_r,x,p_0) is tight. The right matching-block order gives q_0p_r<p_rx, hence (q_0,p_r,x) is tight. Therefore (q_0,p_r,x,p_0) is a tight Hamiltonian four-path. Thus the connector-free residue always has the stated reverse hooks and endpoint classification, and in the double-matching case contains a Hamiltonian four-set meeting x and both original deletion-cover components.
