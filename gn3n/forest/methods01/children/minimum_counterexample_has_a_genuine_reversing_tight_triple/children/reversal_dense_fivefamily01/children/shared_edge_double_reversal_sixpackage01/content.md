# Two labels reversing one path edge force a common-core six-window transport package

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample. Let P be a tight path containing consecutive vertices u,v in that displayed order, and let x,y be distinct vertices outside P such that (v,u,x) and (v,u,y) are tight. Then there exist distinct a,b outside {u,v,x,y} such that both five-sets K union {x} and K union {y}, where K={u,v,a,b}, are Hamiltonian and have non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements. Consequently, with U=K union {x,y}, either U is a proper Hamiltonian six-set with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, or U is non-Hamiltonian and has at least four Hamiltonian vertex deletions (including x and y), together with the certified one- and two-label path-cover-two transport package of yield_a_hamiltonian_sixset_or_fourgooddeletion_transport.

## Body

Put Z=V(H)-{u,v,x,y}. A minimum counterexample has order greater than ten, so |Z|>=7. For t in {x,y}, the triple T_t=(v,u,t) is a genuine reversal of the displayed edge (u,v). Apply reversal_dense_fivefamily01 to T_t. On Z let B_t be the graph whose edge ab means that T_t union {a,b} is non-Hamiltonian. The bad-extension graph supplied by reversal_dense_fivefamily01 is triangle-free, hence each restriction B_t is triangle-free. Suppose no pair ab is good for both t=x and t=y. Then every edge of the complete graph on Z belongs to B_x union B_y. Color each edge red if it belongs to B_x, and otherwise blue; by the supposition every blue edge belongs to B_y. The red graph is triangle-free as a subgraph of B_x, and the blue graph is triangle-free as a subgraph of B_y. Choose any six vertices of Z. In every red-blue coloring of K_6 there is a monochromatic triangle: from one vertex at least three incident edges have one color, say red to p,q,r; if one of pq,pr,qr is red there is a red triangle, while if none is red then p,q,r form a blue triangle. This contradiction proves that some distinct a,b in Z make both T_x union {a,b} and T_y union {a,b} Hamiltonian. Put K={u,v,a,b}. Thus K union {x} and K union {y} are Hamiltonian five-sets sharing the four-core K. They are proper and their complements have path-cover number two by minimum-counterexample calculus (also explicitly supplied by reversal_dense_fivefamily01). Apply yield_a_hamiltonian_sixset_or_fourgooddeletion_transport to these two five-sets. It gives exactly the asserted dichotomy: U=K union {x,y} is Hamiltonian with a path-cover-two complement, or U is non-Hamiltonian with at least four Hamiltonian deletions including x,y and the associated one/two-label path-cover-two transport graph.
