# The four-label gap network either creates a proper tight cycle or is monotone with the four-path order

## Statement

In outcome (3) of four_side_endpoint_lock_gap_network01, write the Hamiltonian four-side as X=(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3), and order its labels by their distinct second-type obstruction gaps on M. Then either: (1) the gap order differs from x_0<x_1<x_2<x_3, in which case H contains a proper vertex-simple tight cycle whose one arc is a displayed subpath of X and whose opposite arc is the gap-network connector through M; or (2) the gap order is exactly x_0<x_1<x_2<x_3. In either case each x_i also supplies the reverse-through-gap tight triple (b_{g_i+1},x_i,b_{g_i}) at its own distinct displayed edge b_{g_i}b_{g_i+1} of M. Thus the no-cycle residue consists of four distinct displayed-edge reversals occurring along M in the same order as the four vertices of X.

## Body

For each pair of labels u,v with g(u)<g(v), four_side_endpoint_lock_gap_network01 gives a tight u-to-v path whose internal vertices lie in M: if the gaps are adjacent this is the cross triple (u,b_{g(u)+1},v), while if they are separated it is the interval connector (u,b_{g(u)+1},...,b_{g(v)},v). Suppose the gap order differs from the displayed X-order. Then there is an inversion: some x_i precedes x_j on X with i<j, but g(x_j)<g(x_i). The displayed subpath X[i,j]=(x_i,...,x_j) is a tight path from x_i to x_j. The gap network supplies a tight path C from x_j to x_i whose internal vertices lie in M. Since X and M are disjoint, the two paths are internally vertex-disjoint and share exactly x_i,x_j, so their union is a vertex-simple tight cycle. It is proper because it omits at least the two displayed endpoints of P (and also the third cover component Q). Hence outcome (1). If there is no inversion, the two total orders on the four labels coincide, giving (2). Finally, alternative 2 of insert01 at gap g_i gives the reverse-through-gap triple (b_{g_i+1},x_i,b_{g_i}); the gaps are distinct by the gap-network theorem, so these reverse four distinct displayed edges of M.
