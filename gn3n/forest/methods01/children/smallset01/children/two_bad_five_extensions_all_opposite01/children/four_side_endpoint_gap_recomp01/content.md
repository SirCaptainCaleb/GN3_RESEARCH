# A four-side beside a path reduces directly to transport, mixed support, or a complete gap network

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover with |X|=4 and P=(p_1,...,p_m), m>=5. Put M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}). Then at least one of the following holds. (1) There is a legal pairwise repartition of X|P with component orders (5,m-1) and quadratic-potential change 10-2m<=0, with equality only when m=5. (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set U of order four or five meeting both X and V(M), with H-U non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two; U may be chosen as one X-label plus three consecutive vertices of M, two X-labels plus three consecutive vertices of M, or two X-labels plus the two vertices of one displayed gap of M. (3) m>=7 and every x in X has a second-type failed-insertion obstruction at a distinct displayed gap g(x) of M. In this final case, for every distinct x,y in X, if |g(x)-g(y)|=1 then the explicit adjacent-gap cross triple from 36fccff06d48 is tight, while if |g(x)-g(y)|>=2 then x and y are joined by the tight interval path through the displayed subinterval of M between their obstruction gaps. Thus the full endpoint package, bounded-support menu, mixed-support refinement, and gap-network refinement are one argument rather than four sequential nodes.

## Body

Write a=p_1 and b=p_m. First examine the two endpoint extensions X+{a} and X+{b}. If either is Hamiltonian, pair it with the inherited endpoint truncation of P to obtain a legal (4,m)->(5,m-1) repartition, whose potential change is 25+(m-1)^2-(16+m^2)=10-2m.\n\nAssume therefore that both endpoint five-extensions are non-Hamiltonian. By two_bad_five_extensions_all_opposite01, for every t in X the five-set F_t=(X-{t}) union {a,b} is Hamiltonian. If M+{t} is Hamiltonian for some t, then F_t | (M+{t}) is again a legal (5,m-1) repartition of X union P with the same potential change. Thus if (1) does not hold, every M+{t} is non-Hamiltonian, so each t is noninsertable into the displayed path M.\n\nNow apply the failed-insertion classification to these four labels. If some t has a first-type obstruction, 0425e03e2aa3 gives either a Hamiltonian four-window consisting of t and three consecutive vertices of M, or the exceptional cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-window whose extension by any other X-label is Hamiltonian. These are respectively the first two forms in (2). If two labels have second-type obstructions at the same gap, 36fccff06d48 gives a Hamiltonian four-set formed by those two labels and the two vertices of that gap, the third form in (2). Since H is a minimum counterexample, every proper Hamiltonian support appearing here has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.\n\nAssume finally that neither (1) nor (2) occurs. Then every X-label has a second-type obstruction, and the obstruction gaps are pairwise distinct. Hence M contains at least five vertices and m>=7. For any distinct x,y in X, apply 36fccff06d48 to their two obstruction gaps. If the gaps differ by at least two, the theorem gives the stated interval connector. If they are adjacent, its alternative Hamiltonian five-set would be a mixed support of type (2), so the only remaining possibility is the explicit adjacent-gap cross triple. This yields the complete pairwise gap network in (3).\n\nThe overlapping endpoint-rooted Hamiltonian K4s supplied by two_bad_five_extensions_adjacent_four01 in the hard endpoint package remain valid auxiliary structure throughout the hard branch, but they are not needed to split the final trichotomy. The earlier endpoint-package, finite-menu, mixed-menu, and gap-network theorems are therefore successive partial presentations of this single proof and are faithfully absorbed here without weakening any conclusion.
