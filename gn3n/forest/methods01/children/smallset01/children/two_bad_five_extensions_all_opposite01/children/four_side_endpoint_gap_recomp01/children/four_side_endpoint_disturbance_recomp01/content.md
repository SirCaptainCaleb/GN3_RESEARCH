# Four-side transport reduces to support, reversal, or cycle

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and X|P|Q a spanning three-cover with |X|=4 and |P|>=5. Then either X|P has a nonincreasing 5|( |P|-1 ) repartition, or H has a proper mixed Hamiltonian four- or five-set with pc2 complement, or the hard gap branch yields a positioned reversing tight triple, or it yields a proper tight cycle with pc2 complement.

## Body

Apply four_side_endpoint_gap_recomp01. Its transport and mixed-support outcomes give the first two alternatives. In its hard gap-network outcome apply four_side_gap_network_disturbance01, which gives a positioned reversing triple or a proper tight cycle. For a proper cycle, opening it gives a Hamiltonian path on its support; minimum-counterexample calculus gives pc at most two on the complement, and Hamiltonicity of the complement would two-cover H, so the complement is non-Hamiltonian pc2.
