# A componentwise minimal four-side beside a path of order at least six must expose support, reversal, or cycle

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential in its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=4 and |P|=m>=6. Then H contains either a proper mixed Hamiltonian four- or five-set with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, a positioned reversing tight triple from the hard gap branch, or a proper tight cycle whose complement is non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two.

## Body

Apply four_side_endpoint_disturbance_recomp01. Its transport alternative is a repartition of X|P with component orders (5,m-1) and Phi change 10-2m, which is strictly negative for m>=6 and therefore impossible at a componentwise Phi-minimum. The remaining alternatives are exactly the proper mixed Hamiltonian support, positioned reversal, or proper cycle, with the stated complement conclusions in a minimum counterexample.
