# A four-side beside a path of order at least six has a complete endpoint-overlap transport menu

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover, where X is a Hamiltonian four-path and P=(p_1,...,p_m) has m>=6. Put M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}). Then at least one of the following holds. (1) One legal pairwise repartition of X|P strictly decreases quadratic potential. (2) One legal pairwise repartition of X|P preserves the pair orders {4,m} and replaces X by a Hamiltonian four-set contained in an endpoint-rooted five-set F. (3) There exist distinct x,y,z in X, with t the fourth vertex, such that F={p_1,p_m,x,y,z} is Hamiltonian and L=V(M) union {t} is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. (4) There exist such x,y,z,t for which F is non-Hamiltonian and a set D subseteq F of at least four labels such that F-{d} is Hamiltonian and L union {d} is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two for every d in D. In the no-descent branch the underlying endpoint-rooted Hamiltonian four-sets {p_1,p_m,x,y} and {p_1,p_m,x,z} overlap in three vertices.

## Body

If X union {p_1} or X union {p_m} is Hamiltonian, transfer that endpoint into X and retain the inherited truncation of P. The pair sizes change from (4,m) to (5,m-1), so new Phi minus old Phi is 10-2m<0 for m>=6, giving (1). Assume both endpoint five-sets are non-Hamiltonian. Apply two_bad_five_extensions_adjacent_four01 to X and the exterior labels p_1,p_m. It gives distinct x,y,z in X such that W={p_1,p_m,x,y} and W'={p_1,p_m,x,z} are Hamiltonian; let t be the fourth vertex of X, F=W union W'={p_1,p_m,x,y,z}, and L=V(M) union {t}. Then F and L partition V(X) union V(P), with |F|=5 and |L|=m-1. If F is Hamiltonian and L is Hamiltonian, F|L is a legal two-path repartition of X union P with sizes (5,m-1), again giving the strict drop 10-2m<0 and outcome (1). If F is Hamiltonian and L is non-Hamiltonian, minimum-counterexample calculus gives pc(L)=2, yielding (3). Now suppose F is non-Hamiltonian. By the five-vertex small-set theorem, at least four labels d in F satisfy that F-{d} is Hamiltonian; let D be the set of such labels. For d in D, the complementary support in X union P is L union {d}, of order m. If L union {d} is Hamiltonian for some d, then (F-{d}) | (L union {d}) is a legal pairwise repartition of X|P with unchanged orders (4,m), giving (2). If no such d exists, each L union {d} is non-Hamiltonian and proper, so minimum-counterexample calculus gives path-cover number two for every d in D, yielding (4).
