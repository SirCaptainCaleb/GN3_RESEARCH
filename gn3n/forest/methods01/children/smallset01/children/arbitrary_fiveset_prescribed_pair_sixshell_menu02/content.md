# Any five-set and prescribed exterior pair generate a sharpened six-shell transport menu

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let X be any five-vertex set, fix x in X, and let p,q be distinct vertices outside X. Put A=X-{x} and S=A union {p,q}. Then at least two distinct vertices d in A satisfy that S-{d} is Hamiltonian, and each complementary induced subtournament H-(S-{d}) is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Moreover at least one of the following holds: (1) S is Hamiltonian, with H-S non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (2) at least one of A union {p}, A union {q} is Hamiltonian; (3) S is non-Hamiltonian, both A union {p}, A union {q} are non-Hamiltonian, and there are distinct d,e,f in A such that S-{d,e} and S-{d,f} are Hamiltonian four-sets sharing three vertices, while their five-vertex union S-{d} is Hamiltonian; all three complements in H are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (4) S is non-Hamiltonian, both A union {p}, A union {q} are non-Hamiltonian, A itself is a non-Hamiltonian matching-block four-set, and for every d in A both (A-{d}) union {p} and (A-{d}) union {q} are Hamiltonian. Thus the only no-overlap equality residue is a retained non-Hamiltonian four-core with eight Hamiltonian one-label replacements.

## Body

Put A=X-{x}. Apply twofourhamdeletions01 to the disjoint two-set {p,q} and four-set A. It gives at least two d in A for which S-{d}=(A-{d}) union {p,q} is Hamiltonian; minimum-counterexample calculus gives non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements. If S is Hamiltonian, mincex01 gives (1). Assume S is non-Hamiltonian. If S-{p}=A+q or S-{q}=A+p is Hamiltonian, (2) holds. Hence assume both are non-Hamiltonian. By two_bad_five_extensions_all_opposite01, S-{a} is Hamiltonian for every a in A; since the p- and q-deletions are non-Hamiltonian, the Hamiltonian-deletion set D of S is exactly A. Apply sixset_deletion_graph_strengthened01 to S. If its good two-deletion graph J on D=A has adjacent edges de,df, then S-{d,e} and S-{d,f} are Hamiltonian four-sets meeting in three vertices. Their union is S-{d}, which is Hamiltonian because d is in D. Minimum-counterexample calculus gives the complement conclusions, yielding (3). If J has no adjacent edges, the strengthened six-set theorem gives |D|=4, J a perfect matching, D=A a non-Hamiltonian matching-block K4, and, writing S-D={p,q}, all eight four-sets (A-{d})+p and (A-{d})+q Hamiltonian. This is (4). No Hamiltonicity or complement hypothesis on the starting five-set X is used.
