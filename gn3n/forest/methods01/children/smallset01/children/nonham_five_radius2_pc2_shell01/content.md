# A non-Hamiltonian five-set forces a radius-two path-cover-two shell on its complement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let F be a non-Hamiltonian five-vertex set. Put K=H-F and D={d in F : F-{d} is Hamiltonian}. Then |D|>=4. For every d in D, K union {d} is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Moreover, for every two distinct d,e in F, K union {d,e} is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Thus the complement of any non-Hamiltonian five-set carries path-cover-two states on every two-label extension and on at least four of the five one-label extensions.

## Body

By the five-vertex small-set theorem, a non-Hamiltonian five-set has at most one non-Hamiltonian four-subset, so |D|>=4. Fix d in D. Since F-{d} is a proper Hamiltonian four-set, if K+d were Hamiltonian then a Hamilton path on F-{d} together with one on K+d would two-cover H, impossible. Minimum-counterexample calculus therefore gives that K+d is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Now fix distinct d,e in F. The set F-{d,e} has order three, hence is Hamiltonian in every boundary tournament. If K+d+e were Hamiltonian, it together with F-{d,e} would two-cover H. Therefore K+d+e is non-Hamiltonian; being proper, minimum-counterexample calculus gives path-cover number two. No further structure of F is used.
