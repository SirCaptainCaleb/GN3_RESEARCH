# Every proper six-set has an all-order four-good structural package

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and U any proper six-vertex set. At least four one-vertex deletions U-d are Hamiltonian. Consequently exactly one of the following structural branches applies: (i) U is non-Hamiltonian and Hamilton paths on two distinct Hamiltonian five-deletions exhibit order disagreement; (ii) U is Hamiltonian and H-U is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. This dichotomy is order-independent. If |V(H)|>=18, branch (ii) further yields order disagreement or strict quadratic descent by the Hamiltonian-six large-escape theorem, so every six-set has the universal large-order disturbance conclusion. Thus the large-order statement is a consumer corollary of a stronger all-order structural package.

## Body

The certified four-of-six theorem gives at least four Hamiltonian deletions of U. Apply the certified four-good-six interface. If U is non-Hamiltonian, it gives localized order disagreement between Hamilton paths on distinct good deletions. If U is Hamiltonian, minimum-counterexample calculus makes H-U non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number exactly two. No ambient-order lower bound is used.

For n>=18, apply ham6_large_escape01 in the Hamiltonian branch to obtain order disagreement or a lower-Phi pairwise-repartition component. This recovers the universal large-order disturbance theorem while preserving the branch information that special six-set constructions may need.
