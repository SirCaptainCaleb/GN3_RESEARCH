# Above order fourteen every deletion state enters the four-window escape trichotomy

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=15 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover whose two displayed components have order at least three. Then H has a positioned Hamiltonian four-set W meeting both components in a facing endpoint pair and with H-W non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two. Consequently at least one of the following holds: (1) the spanning three-cover W|A|B, for a two-cover A|B of H-W, admits a strict quadratic-potential descent; (2) H contains explicit order disagreement between tight Hamilton paths on overlapping induced supports; (3) H contains a proper Hamiltonian five-set S whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Thus every such deletion state has a Hamiltonian four-set W as above and satisfies one of (1)-(3).

## Body

Apply 3640577112bf to either facing endpoint pair of the deletion cover. It supplies a Hamiltonian four-set W chosen from three explicit local four-sets around that facing pair, and H-W is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Choose any displayed two-cover A|B of H-W. Since n>=15, the hypotheses of 6ea0f4dafd13 apply to the anchored four-window W with complement A|B. That theorem gives exactly one of the three stated outcomes: strict quadratic descent, explicit order disagreement, or a proper Hamiltonian five-set with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.
