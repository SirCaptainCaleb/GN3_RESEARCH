# Fractional path-cover and LP toolkit

## Statement

Reusable fractional path-cover duality, deletion-family averaging, dual slack accounting, laminar rounding, and uncrossing obstructions for boundary tournaments.

## Body

This toolkit collects general fractional-cover mathematics extracted from the Astra weighted and rounding lines. Its contents are organized by mechanism rather than conjecture of origin: LP duality and weighted capture, averaging over deletion covers, dual slack identities, laminar rounding, and sharp set-system fences for naive uncrossing. Astra-specific equality-shell and minimum-counterexample consequences remain in their original reasoning lines and should consume these tools as applications.
