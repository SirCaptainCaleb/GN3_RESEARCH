# Neutral two-crossing bad deletions are encoded by one cut of the longest path

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample in the sharp half-order shell |V(H)|=2lambda+1. Let A=(a_0,...,a_{lambda-1}) be globally longest, U=V(H)-V(A), and let u in U with C=U-{u} non-Hamiltonian. Let T=P|Q be an exact two-cover of H-u having exactly two ordinary crossing edges across A|C. Then either T exposes explicit ordered disturbance against A (some component orders two common A-vertices oppositely, or 8f2c6a91d4e7 produces a tight triple reversing an ordered A-edge), or there is a unique cut k with 1<=k<=lambda-1 such that, after interchanging P,Q, the A-block of P is exactly A[0,k-1] and the A-block of Q is exactly A[k,lambda-1]. Moreover the C-blocks form a two-path cover R|S of C with orders lambda-k and k respectively, and P,Q are obtained by concatenating the prefix with R and the suffix with S in one of the two possible block orders for each component. Thus every completely order-neutral equality-two-crossing bad deletion is represented by a single integer cut k, and its complementary residue C has an exact (lambda-k)|k path cover.

## Body

# Proof\n\nBy the certified equality analysis in 6c4d3f1a8e27, exactly two crossings in the sharp shell force the crosswise four-block form: each of P,Q consists of one nonempty A-block and one nonempty C-block. Since H-u has 2lambda vertices and neither tight path can have order exceeding lambda, both P and Q have order exactly lambda.\n\nAssume no component has relative-order disagreement on its common A-vertices and no reversing A-edge triple is produced. Apply 8f2c6a91d4e7 to P, whose order is s=lambda. If a_i occurs at position p of P, the zero-slack positional band is i<=p<=i, hence p=i. The same holds in Q.\n\nThe A-block of P occupies a consecutive interval of positions in P. Because every a_i in that block occurs at absolute position i, its support is the corresponding consecutive interval of the displayed order A. If the A-block is the first block of P, it occupies positions 0,...,r-1 and therefore is exactly A[0,r-1], a prefix. If it is the second block of P, it occupies the last r positions and therefore is exactly A[lambda-r,lambda-1], a suffix. The same statement holds for the A-block of Q.\n\nThe two A-blocks are disjoint, nonempty, and partition all of A. Hence one is a prefix and the other is the complementary suffix. After interchanging P,Q if needed, there is a unique k in {1,...,lambda-1} for which the P-block is A[0,k-1] and the Q-block is A[k,lambda-1].\n\nBecause |P|=|Q|=lambda, the C-block paired with the k-vertex prefix has order lambda-k, while the C-block paired with the (lambda-k)-vertex suffix has order k. These two C-blocks are tight paths, are disjoint, and partition C, so they give the asserted exact (lambda-k)|k cover of C. The only remaining freedom is whether the C-block comes before or after its A-block inside each of P,Q. ∎
