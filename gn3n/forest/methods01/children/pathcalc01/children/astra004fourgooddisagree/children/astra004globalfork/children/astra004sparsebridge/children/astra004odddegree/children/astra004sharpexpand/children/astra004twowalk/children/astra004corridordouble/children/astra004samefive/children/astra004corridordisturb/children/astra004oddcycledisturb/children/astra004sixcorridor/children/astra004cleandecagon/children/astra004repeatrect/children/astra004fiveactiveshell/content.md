# Every all-clean two-regular support cycle has two fixed long cores and a five-label moving shell

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample in the sharp half-order shell |V(H)|=2lambda+1, and let S_0S_1...S_{m-1}S_0 be a 2-regular connected component of the Hamiltonian-support odd graph. Choose one Hamilton order P_i on each S_i and assume every length-two subwalk S_i-S_{i+1}-S_{i+2} takes the clean branch of astra004twowalk with these chosen orders. Then m is even. There exist two disjoint ordered tight paths C_0,C_1 of order lambda-2 and a five-vertex active set A=V(H)-(V(C_0) union V(C_1)) such that every even support has the form V(C_0) union p_i and every odd support has the form V(C_1) union q_i, where p_i,q_i are 2-subsets of A. Along the cycle, adjacent active pairs are disjoint, and the odd-edge label is the unique fifth element of A outside their union. Equivalently, every all-clean 2-regular component is an even cycle embedded in the bipartite disjointness graph between two copies of binom(A,2), with all ambient order dependence frozen into the two common cores C_0,C_1.

## Body

# Proof\n\nFor every i, cleanliness of the length-two walk S_i-S_{i+1}-S_{i+2} says that the chosen Hamilton order P_{i+2} is obtained from P_i by deleting one endpoint and inserting the new label at that same endpoint, while the entire order on the other lambda-1 vertices is unchanged. Therefore the ordered interior obtained from P_i by deleting its first and last vertices is identical to the ordered interior of P_{i+2}. Denote its support by I_i; then I_{i+2}=I_i and |I_i|=lambda-2.\n\nIf m were odd, repeated addition of 2 modulo m visits every index. Hence all P_i would have the same interior support I. In particular the adjacent supports S_0 and S_1 would both contain I. Since lambda>=3 in the sharp minimum-counterexample shell, I is nonempty, contradicting that adjacent odd-graph supports are disjoint. Thus m is even.\n\nFor even indices the ordered interiors are all identical; call the common ordered tight path C_0. For odd indices call the common ordered interior C_1. Since S_0 and S_1 are adjacent and disjoint, V(C_0) and V(C_1) are disjoint. Their total order is 2lambda-4, so the complement A=V(H)-(V(C_0) union V(C_1)) has order (2lambda+1)-(2lambda-4)=5.\n\nEvery even support S_i contains V(C_0) and has order lambda, hence has exactly two further vertices. It is disjoint from each adjacent odd support, and every odd support contains V(C_1); therefore those two further vertices lie outside V(C_1), hence in A. Thus S_i=V(C_0) union p_i for a 2-subset p_i of A. Symmetrically every odd support is V(C_1) union q_i with q_i in binom(A,2).\n\nFor adjacent supports S_i,S_{i+1}, the cores are disjoint and the supports themselves are disjoint, so their active pairs are disjoint. Conversely, for any two Hamiltonian supports of the displayed opposite-core forms, disjoint active pairs make the supports disjoint and therefore adjacent in the Hamiltonian-support odd graph. Along the given cycle this identifies every edge with a disjoint pair p_i,q_{i+1}. Their union has four of the five active labels, while the two fixed cores account for all other vertices. Hence the edge label is exactly the unique element of A-(p_i union q_{i+1}). ∎
