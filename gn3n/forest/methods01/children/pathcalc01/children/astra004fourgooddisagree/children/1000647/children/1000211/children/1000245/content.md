# Every minimum counterexample has order disagreement on overlapping five- or six-vertex Hamilton paths

## Statement

Every minimum counterexample H contains order disagreement witnessed in one of the following two substantial forms. (1) A displayed Hamilton path W on five vertices and a Hamilton path R on V(W) union {x} order two vertices of V(W) oppositely. (2) Two Hamilton paths R_i,R_{i+1} on six-vertex supports whose intersection has five vertices order two common vertices oppositely. In particular the disagreement witness can always be chosen with both displayed paths of order at least five and with at least five common vertices.

## Body

Assume toward contradiction that no witness of either displayed form exists. By c644ac221801, the absence of order disagreement between Hamilton paths on overlapping induced supports implies that every six-vertex induced subtournament is Hamiltonian and every component of every deletion cover has order at least six. Choose a deletion cover H-x=P|Q and write P=(p_0,...,p_{m-1}), m>=6. The vertex x is noninsertable into the displayed order P, or insertion together with Q would give a spanning two-cover. For 0<=i<=m-5 let W_i=(p_i,...,p_{i+4}). The six-set V(W_i) union {x} is Hamiltonian. If x were noninsertable into W_i, noninsertableorderdisagree01 says every Hamilton path R on V(W_i) union {x} has order disagreement with W_i on common vertices. This is form (1), contrary to hypothesis. Hence x inserts into every W_i.

Positions 2 and 3 in a five-window are impossible, because all new triples checked by such an insertion also occur in the full path P, which would insert x into P. Thus every W_i has a successful insertion on its left side {0,1} or right side {4,5}; choose one and denote the resulting Hamilton six-path by R_i. If adjacent windows W_i,W_{i+1} receive opposite sides, then R_i and R_{i+1} have five common vertices, namely x,p_{i+1},...,p_{i+4}, and order x oppositely relative to p_{i+2} in the R/right then L case or relative to p_{i+3} in the L then R case. This is form (2), contrary to hypothesis. Hence all chosen insertions have one common side. But W_0 cannot use the left side, because such an insertion extends verbatim to P, and W_{m-5} cannot use the right side for the symmetric reason. Contradiction. Thus one of forms (1),(2) occurs. Every path involved has order five or six, and in either form the common support has at least five vertices.
