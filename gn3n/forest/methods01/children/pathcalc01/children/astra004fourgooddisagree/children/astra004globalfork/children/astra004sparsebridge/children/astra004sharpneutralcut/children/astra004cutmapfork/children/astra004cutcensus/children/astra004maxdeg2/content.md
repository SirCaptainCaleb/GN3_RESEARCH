# An extremal longest support has odd degree exactly two unless disturbance already occurs

## Statement

In the sharp half-order shell, choose a globally longest Hamiltonian support A maximizing its degree in the Hamiltonian-support odd graph G, equivalently maximizing the number d(A) of Hamiltonian vertex deletions of its complement. Then at least one of the following holds: (I) some certified mixed-support / crossing / relative-order disturbance already occurs in the Astra-004 deletion analysis; or (II) deg_G(A)=Delta(G)=2. More explicitly, deg_G(A)>=3 triggers astra004threegoodmixed. If deg_G(A)<=1 and no disturbance occurs, then every bad complement deletion admits a neutral equality-two-crossing cut state; astra004cutcensus forces a repeated cut, and astra004samecutfork turns that repeat into a fixed longest support Q with deg_G(Q)>=2, contradicting maximality. Hence the only fully neutral extremal degree is exactly two.

## Body

# Proof\n\nLet d=deg_G(A). The degree dictionary is immediate from the odd-graph definition: if U=V(H)-A, then neighbors of A are exactly the Hamiltonian lambda-sets U-{u} for those u in U with U-u Hamiltonian. Thus d is the number of Hamiltonian vertex deletions of U.\n\nIf d>=3, apply certified astra004threegoodmixed to A and U. This yields a direct mixed-support edge in an endpoint deletion cover or explicit relative-order disagreement. This is outcome (I).\n\nAssume d<=1 and suppose outcome (I) does not occur anywhere in the sparse analysis rooted at A. For every bad u in U, certified 6c4d3f1a8e27 gives at least two crossings across A|(U-u). If every exact cover of some H-u has at least three such crossings, this is already explicit crossing disturbance. Hence for each bad u there is an exact cover attaining exactly two crossings. If such a cover exposes A-order disagreement or a reversing A-edge triple, we again have outcome (I). Therefore the no-disturbance assumption places every bad label in the neutral cut normalization astra004sharpneutralcut.\n\nNow astra004cutcensus applies. Since d<=1, at least one cut repeats. Choose two labels u,v on one repeated cut. By astra004samecutfork, either their common double-deletion comparison already exposes crossing/order disturbance, giving (I), or the pair yields a fixed Hamiltonian lambda-support Q with two distinct odd neighbors. In the latter case deg_G(Q)>=2>d, contradicting the choice of A with maximum odd degree.\n\nThus d cannot be at most one in the no-disturbance branch. Combined with the exclusion of d>=3, we obtain d=Delta(G)=2. ∎
