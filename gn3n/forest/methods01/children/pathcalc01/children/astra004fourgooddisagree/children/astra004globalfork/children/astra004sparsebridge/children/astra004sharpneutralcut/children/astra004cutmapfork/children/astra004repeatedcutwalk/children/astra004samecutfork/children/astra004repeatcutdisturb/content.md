# Every repeated neutral cut forces explicit disturbance

## Statement

In the setup of astra004samecutfork, outcome (II) cannot remain in the clean branch of astra004twowalk. Therefore every pair of distinct bad labels assigned the same neutral cut forces explicit common-double-deletion crossing or relative-order disagreement. Equivalently, the neutral cut map is injective on bad labels whenever all such disturbance is excluded.

## Body

By astra004samecutfork, the only branch not already explicit disturbance is a fixed-support odd two-walk coming from a support-compatible pair u,v. Consider first the form with Q=S A^+ fixed and varying supports P_u=A^- R_u, P_v=A^- R_v, where A^- is a nonempty prefix of A. If astra004twowalk is non-clean, we already have explicit crossing/order disturbance. Suppose it is clean. Then u and v restore to the same end of the common varying-support path after deleting the other label. Because every normalized P_t has the nonempty prefix A^- as its first block, its initial endpoint is a_0 and the exchanged label from U cannot occupy that endpoint. Hence v is the terminal endpoint of P_u and u is the terminal endpoint of P_v. Cleanliness gives the same inherited common path before those terminal labels, so let z be their common path neighbor. Apply the certified endpoint barrier to the exact cover C_u of H-u: terminal endpoint v with omitted label u forces (u,v,z) tight. Apply it to C_v: terminal endpoint u with omitted label v forces (v,u,z) tight. These two ordered triples are opposite orientations of the same three vertices (the reverse of (u,v,z) is cyclically equivalent to (v,u,z)), contradicting boundary antisymmetry. Thus the clean branch is impossible. If instead P is fixed and Q_t=S_t A^+ varies, the exchanged labels lie at the initial endpoint because A^+ is the terminal block. Writing z for the common next vertex, the two endpoint barriers force (z,v,u) and (z,u,v), again opposite orientations. Therefore every support-compatible repeated cut is itself disturbed, and together with outcome (I) of astra004samecutfork every repeated cut forces explicit disturbance.
