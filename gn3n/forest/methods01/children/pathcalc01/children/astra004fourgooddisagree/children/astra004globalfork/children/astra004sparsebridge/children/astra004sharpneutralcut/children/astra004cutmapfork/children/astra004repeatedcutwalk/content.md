# A compatible repeated cut is exactly a fixed-support odd two-walk

## Statement

Use the neutral cut normalization of astra004sharpneutralcut. Let distinct bad labels u,v have the same cut k, with normalized covers H-u=P_u|Q_u and H-v=P_v|Q_v, where P_u=A^- R_u, Q_u=S_u A^+, P_v=A^- R_v, Q_v=S_v A^+, A^-=A[0,k-1], and A^+=A[k,lambda-1]. If the two covers are support-compatible after deleting u,v, then exactly one of the following holds after possibly exchanging the names of the complementary sides: (i) S_u=S_v=S is fixed and R_u=X union {v}, R_v=X union {u}; hence Q_u=Q_v=S A^+ is one fixed Hamiltonian lambda-support and P_u-Q_u-P_v is a length-two walk in the Hamiltonian-support odd graph with edge labels u,v; or (ii) R_u=R_v is fixed and the symmetric statement holds with P fixed and Q_u,Q_v varying by u<->v. Consequently every support-compatible repeated cut feeds directly into astra004twowalk: either that two-walk exposes crossing/order disturbance, or the two deletion states differ by a clean same-end endpoint exchange on the varying lambda-support while the other support is fixed.

## Body

# Proof\n\nThe supports of the normalized u-cover are\nP_u=A^- union R_u,  Q_u=A^+ union S_u,\nand similarly for v. Both A^- and A^+ are nonempty and disjoint. After deleting v from the u-cover and u from the v-cover, support compatibility cannot match the P-side of one cover with the Q-side of the other: the former contains all of A^- and no A^+, while the latter contains all of A^+ and no A^-. Hence the common restrictions match P to P and Q to Q.\n\nThus\nR_u-{v}=R_v-{u}\nand\nS_u-{v}=S_v-{u},\nwhere deleting a label not belonging to the displayed set has no effect. Since |R_u|=|R_v|=lambda-k and |S_u|=|S_v|=k, the membership of the exchanged labels is synchronized: v belongs to R_u iff u belongs to R_v, and v belongs to S_u iff u belongs to S_v. Exactly one of these alternatives occurs because R_u|S_u partitions U-u and R_v|S_v partitions U-v.\n\nIf v in R_u and u in R_v, put X=R_u-{v}=R_v-{u}. Then R_u=X union {v}, R_v=X union {u}, while the equality on the S-side gives S_u=S_v=:S. Therefore Q_u=Q_v=S A^+ is a fixed Hamiltonian lambda-support. The two exact deletion covers give odd-graph edges P_u--Q_u labelled u and Q_u--P_v labelled v, so P_u-Q_u-P_v is the asserted length-two walk.\n\nIf instead the exchanged labels lie on the S-sides, the same argument gives a fixed R and hence a fixed P=A^-R, while Q_u,Q_v differ by exchanging v and u.\n\nApply the certified astra004twowalk theorem to the resulting length-two odd walk. Its alternatives are precisely the asserted explicit disturbance or clean same-end endpoint exchange on the varying support. ∎
