# A compatible triangle at one cut forces a degree-three longest support

## Statement

Keep the completely neutral same-cut normalization of astra004sharpneutralcut for three distinct bad labels u,v,w with common cut k. Assume their three chosen exact deletion covers are pairwise support-compatible after deleting the corresponding label pairs. For a compatible pair color the pair L if the exchanged omitted labels lie in the complementary block attached to the prefix A[0,k-1], and color it R if they lie in the block attached to the suffix A[k,lambda-1]. Then every compatible triangle is monochromatic. Consequently one entire Hamiltonian lambda-support is common to all three deletion covers, while the opposite support takes three distinct one-vertex exchange values. Hence that fixed support has odd-graph degree at least three, its complement has at least three Hamiltonian vertex deletions, and astra004threegoodmixed forces a direct mixed-support edge or explicit relative-order disagreement from endpoint probes of that fixed longest path.

## Body

# Proof\n\nWrite each normalized cover as P_t=A^- R_t and Q_t=S_t A^+ for t in {u,v,w}, where A^-=A[0,k-1], A^+=A[k,lambda-1]. By astra004repeatedcutwalk, compatibility of a pair t,t prime has exactly two possibilities: either R_t and R_tprime differ by exchanging t,t prime while S_t=S_tprime, or S_t and S_tprime differ by exchanging t,t prime while R_t=R_tprime. Call these colors L and R respectively.\n\nSuppose uv and vw have color L. Then S_u=S_v=S_w=:S. Since S_t is a subset of U-{t}, equality of all three S-sets implies none of u,v,w lies in S. Therefore each R_t is exactly (U-{t})-S. In particular R_u and R_w are distinct and differ by exchanging u and w. Thus the pair uw is also color L; it cannot be color R because color R would require R_u=R_w. The same argument with R and S interchanged shows that if two edges of the triangle have color R, then so does the third. Every two-coloring of a triangle has two incident edges of one color, so the whole triangle is monochromatic.\n\nIn the L case Q_u=Q_v=Q_w=S A^+ is one fixed Hamiltonian lambda-support Q. The three opposite supports P_u,P_v,P_w are distinct, and the exact deletion covers yield odd-graph edges Q--P_u, Q--P_v, Q--P_w labelled u,v,w respectively. Hence deg_G(Q)>=3. In the R case the same conclusion holds with the common P-support. By the sharp-shell degree dictionary, the complement of the fixed support has at least three Hamiltonian deletions. Applying certified astra004threegoodmixed to that fixed globally longest support gives the asserted direct mixed-support crossing or explicit order disagreement. ∎
