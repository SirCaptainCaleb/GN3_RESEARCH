# Gap: absence of substantial disagreement does not imply global order-neutrality

## Statement

The proof of 50137573941d has a logical gap. It assumes that neither of its two substantial witness forms exists, but then invokes c644ac221801, whose hypothesis is the absence of all relative-order disagreement. The former does not imply the latter. Therefore universal six-set Hamiltonicity cannot be invoked under the stated contradiction hypothesis, and 50137573941d is not currently proved. Any repair must either show that arbitrary order disagreement can first be upgraded to one of the substantial forms, or rederive the sliding-window argument without assuming global order-neutrality.

## Body

The first sentence of the proof assumes only “no witness of either displayed form exists.” The next invocation of c644ac221801 requires “no two tight Hamiltonian paths on overlapping induced supports exhibit relative-order disagreement.” There may a priori be order disagreement on smaller supports or in another geometry that is not one of the two displayed five/six-window forms. Thus the hypothesis of c644ac221801 is unavailable. This is a logical, not merely expositional, gap.
