# Every minimum counterexample contains local reversal data

## Statement

Every minimum counterexample H contains at least one of the following: (1) two Hamilton tight paths P,Q, each of order at least five and with at least five common vertices, that traverse one common ordinary edge in opposite directions; (2) a tight triple that reverses a displayed ordered edge of a Hamilton tight path of order at least five. Thus the local reversal can be chosen from a substantial Hamilton-path overlap and cannot arise only from vacuous two-vertex paths.

## Body

By 50137573941d, H contains two Hamilton tight paths P,Q of order at least five, with at least five common vertices, that have order disagreement. Apply 1772cf5706fa to P,Q. Either they traverse a common ordinary edge in opposite directions, or there is a tight triple reversing a displayed ordered edge of P or Q. These are exactly conclusions (1) and (2).
