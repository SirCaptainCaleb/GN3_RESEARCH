# A repeated-label corridor has only three two-regular shell completions

## Statement

Assume the five-label shell of astra004fiveactiveshell and normalize a clean repeated-label corridor with edge labels a,b,c,a. Let the active set be {a,b,c,d,e}, where the three same-parity corridor supports have active pairs {e,b},{e,a},{e,c}, and the two intervening opposite-parity supports have pairs {d,c},{d,b}. Rectangle transport supplies the opposite-parity pairs {a,c},{a,b}. Then the connected 2-regular shell component has exactly one of three completions: (I) an 8-cycle, adding the same-parity pair {d,e}; (II) a 10-cycle, adding same-parity pairs {b,d},{c,d} and the opposite-parity pair {a,e}; or (III) a 12-cycle, adding {b,d},{c,d}, opposite-parity pairs {c,e},{b,e}, and the same-parity pair {a,d}. No other completion is compatible with degree two.

## Body

Within the bipartite disjointness graph on two copies of the 2-subsets of A, the known corridor and transport give a path whose endpoint opposite-parity pair types are {a,c} and {a,b}. The pair {a,c} has exactly three disjoint same-parity pair types: {e,b}, already used; {d,e}; and {b,d}. Likewise {a,b} has neighbors {e,c}, already used; {d,e}; and {c,d}. If {d,e} is selected, degree two closes both endpoints at once and its third possible opposite neighbor {b,c} is already forbidden by the degree-two condition at {e,a}; this is the 8-cycle. If {d,e} is not selected, degree two forces {b,d} and {c,d}. The former needs one additional opposite neighbor among {a,e},{c,e}, while the latter needs one among {a,e},{b,e}. If {a,e} is selected it closes both and gives the 10-cycle. Otherwise {c,e} and {b,e} are forced. Their only available common same-parity continuation is {a,d}, because their alternative disjoint pair types {a,b} and {a,c} on that parity were already forbidden by the earlier degree-two constraints. Selecting {a,d} closes both and gives the 12-cycle. At every step the remaining third neighbor of each newly selected pair is forced absent by a vertex whose degree is already two, so no further branch exists.
