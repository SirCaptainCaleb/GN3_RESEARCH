# Every same-cut pair gives double-deletion disturbance or a fixed-support odd walk

## Statement

Keep two distinct bad labels u,v with the same neutral cut k and their normalized equality-two-crossing covers C_u of H-u and C_v of H-v from astra004sharpneutralcut. Then one of the following holds. (I) In the common double deletion H-{u,v}, the two-deletion endpoint trichotomy exposes an ordinary crossing of a three-part inherited path partition, a support-partition crossing between two exact covers, or explicit relative-order disagreement. (II) The pair is support-compatible in the sense of astra004repeatedcutwalk, and hence one entire Hamiltonian lambda-support is fixed while the opposite supports differ by the one-for-one exchange u<->v, producing a length-two walk in the Hamiltonian-support odd graph. Therefore a repeated cut is never an unstructured equality residue: it is either an explicit double-deletion disturbance or a certified longest-support transition.

## Body

# Proof\n\nApply the certified two-deletion endpoint trichotomy in deletion01 to C_u, C_v, and any exact two-cover T of H-{u,v}, which exists by minimum-counterexample calculus.\n\nIf v is internal in its component of C_u, deleting v splits that component into two nonempty inherited tight paths while the other component remains nonempty. The trichotomy forces an ordinary edge of T crossing this three-part partition. This is outcome (I). The same holds symmetrically if u is internal in C_v.\n\nAssume therefore that v is an endpoint in C_u and u is an endpoint in C_v. Deleting them gives exact two-covers T_u=C_u-v and T_v=C_v-u of H-{u,v}. If their unordered support partitions differ, the exact-cover disagreement theorem in deletion01 gives reciprocal ordinary support-partition crossings, hence (I). If their support partitions agree but the corresponding ordered paths differ, the same theorem gives explicit relative-order disagreement and its standard reversed-edge / reversing-triple / tight-cycle witness, again (I).\n\nThe only remaining case is that the common restrictions agree as ordered two-path covers, or more generally agree at the support level without ordered disturbance. Then the original same-cut support algebra applies: the nonempty fixed A-prefix and A-suffix prevent cross-matching of the two support classes, so exactly one complementary block is fixed and the other changes by exchanging u and v. This is precisely the fixed-support odd two-walk of astra004repeatedcutwalk, giving outcome (II). ∎
