# The fixed-endpoint matching residue reduces to two oriented insertion-obstruction pairs

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let C=X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover that minimizes quadratic potential within its connected component of the pairwise-repartition graph, where X is a Hamiltonian four-vertex path and P=(p_1,...,p_m) has m>=7. Put M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}) and U=X union {p_1,p_m}. Assume U is non-Hamiltonian, U-p_1 and U-p_m are non-Hamiltonian, U-x is Hamiltonian for every x in X, and the graph J on X defined by xy in E(J) iff U-{x,y} is Hamiltonian is a perfect matching. Then X has a partition C_+ disjoint-union C_- with |C_+|=|C_-|=2, where C_+={x:(p_1,x,p_m) is tight} and C_-={x:(p_m,x,p_1) is tight}; the two edges of J are exactly C_+ and C_-. Moreover H[V(M) union {x}] is non-Hamiltonian for every x in X, so all four labels are noninsertable into the displayed path M. For each sign separately, writing C_sign={y,z}, the two noninsertability obstructions on M satisfy the following finite alternative: either y or z has a first-type obstruction, yielding a Hamiltonian four-set or the cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-set from 0425e03e2aa3; or both have second-type obstructions, in which case 36fccff06d48 gives a Hamiltonian four-set at a common gap, a direct tight path joining y to z through a nontrivial interval of M, or at adjacent gaps a Hamiltonian five-set or an explicit tight cross triple. Thus the fixed-endpoint matching residue reduces to two oriented two-label insertion-obstruction packages on one common middle path.

## Body

Write a=p_1, b=p_m, and M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}). By fixed_endpoint_matching_orientation01, the perfect-matching hypothesis on J partitions X into the two endpoint-orientation classes C_+={x:(a,x,b) is tight} and C_-={x:(b,x,a) is tight}, each of order two, and these two pairs are precisely the edges of J.\n\nFix x in X. Since U-x is Hamiltonian, if H[V(M) union {x}] were Hamiltonian then the two disjoint Hamiltonian supports U-x and V(M) union {x} would repartition X|P. Their orders would be 5 and m-1 in place of 4 and m. The change in quadratic potential would be\n25+(m-1)^2-(16+m^2)=10-2m<0\nbecause m>=7. This contradicts the assumed minimum of quadratic potential within the connected pairwise-repartition component. Hence H[V(M) union {x}] is non-Hamiltonian for every x in X. Since M itself is the displayed tight path, no x can be inserted into any position of M.\n\nNow fix one orientation class {y,z}. Apply the failed-insertion normal form in insert01 separately to y and z on the common displayed path M. If either obstruction is first-type, 0425e03e2aa3 gives a Hamiltonian four-set or the cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-set. Otherwise both are second-type. The argument applies independently to C_+ and C_-.
