# From order seventeen onward a local quadratic minimum with a four-side has endpoint-six-set order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament of order n>=17 and let C=X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component. If |X|=4, then for one of R in {P,Q}, with displayed endpoints r_1,r_m, the six-set U=V(X) union {r_1,r_m} is non-Hamiltonian and Hamilton paths on the four deletions U-{x}, x in X, exhibit relative-order disagreement.

## Body

Since |P|+|Q|=n-4>=13, one of P,Q has order at least seven; call it R. Apply four_side_m7_arbitrary_escape01 to X|R. Its strict-descent alternative is impossible because C minimizes quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component. Therefore its second alternative holds: the endpoint six-set U formed by V(X) and the two displayed endpoints of R is non-Hamiltonian, and Hamilton paths on the four deletions U-{x}, x in X, contain a pair with relative-order disagreement. No minimum-counterexample or global-minimum hypothesis is used.
