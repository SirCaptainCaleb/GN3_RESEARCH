# Pair-local non-descent already forces the positioned four-side square and its bounded terminal menu

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover with |X|=4 and P=(p_1,...,p_m), m>=7. Assume only that the induced support V(X) union V(P) has no legal two-path repartition whose quadratic contribution is smaller than 4^2+m^2. Then the endpoint six-shell U=X union {p_1,p_m} is saturated exactly as in 1000878: U and the two one-endpoint extensions of X are non-Hamiltonian, every X-label deletion of U is Hamiltonian, and the inherited interior R plus any x in X is non-Hamiltonian. Consequently the hard endpoint graph has adjacent edges and there is a positioned full pc2 square on two original X-labels. Its top state satisfies the same five-way terminal menu as 1000892: derived strict Phi descent; a same-component exposed-endpoint residue of order at most five; universal internality; support-partition disagreement; or order disagreement. If m=7, the same hypothesis also forces the positioned three-leaf common-four-core transport clique of 1000879.

## Body

Every descent contradiction used to prove endpoint-shell saturation is pair-local. If U were Hamiltonian, U|(p_2,...,p_{m-1}) would lower the quadratic contribution of X|P. If X+p_1 or X+p_m were Hamiltonian, moving that endpoint into X would lower the same pair contribution. Four-of-six then forces all four X-label deletions of U to be Hamiltonian. If R+x were Hamiltonian for some x, the Hamiltonian five-set U-x together with R+x would again give a lower-Phi two-path repartition of V(X)∪V(P). Thus the entire saturation conclusion needs only the displayed pair's non-descent hypothesis.

Now apply the certified elimination of the perfect-matching endpoint graph and the adjacent-window square theorem exactly as in the positioned-square route. The square normal form and exposed-endpoint Phi calculations are internal to the derived C|A|B state and do not require the original cover to be componentwise minimal. Hence the same terminal menu follows.

When m=7, R has order five and every R+x is non-Hamiltonian. The common-deletion-core theorem for four bad extensions gives a four-core D⊂R and at least three labels x∈X with D+x Hamiltonian; every pair of those leaves then carries 1000476 transport. Again no stronger global minimality assumption is used.
