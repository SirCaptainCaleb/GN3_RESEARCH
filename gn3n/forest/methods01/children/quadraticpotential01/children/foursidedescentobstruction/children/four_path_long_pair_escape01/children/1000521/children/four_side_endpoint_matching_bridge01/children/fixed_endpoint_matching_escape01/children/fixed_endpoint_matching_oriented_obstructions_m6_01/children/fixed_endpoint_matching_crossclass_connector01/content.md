# Distinct common-middle obstruction gaps force an opposite-class interval path

## Statement

Assume the hypotheses of fixed_endpoint_matching_oriented_obstructions_m6_01, and write X=C_+ disjoint-union C_- for the two fixed-endpoint orientation classes. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) some x in X has a first-type failed-insertion obstruction on M, giving the Hamiltonian/cyclic four-vertex alternative of 0425e03e2aa3; (2) two labels of X have second-type obstructions at the same gap of M, so those two labels together with that gap edge induce a Hamiltonian four-set; (3) there exist y in C_+ and z in C_- whose second-type obstruction gaps i,j satisfy |i-j|>=2, and 36fccff06d48 supplies a tight interval path joining y and z through the displayed subinterval of M. In case (3), fixed_endpoint_matching_crosshooks01 simultaneously supplies the opposite-orientation endpoint hooks (y,p_1,z) and (z,p_m,y). Thus whenever the obstruction gaps are all distinct, the interval-path output can be chosen across the two endpoint-orientation classes.

## Body

All four labels of X are noninsertable into the displayed middle path M by fixed_endpoint_matching_oriented_obstructions_m6_01. Apply insert01 to each label. If any label has a first-type obstruction, outcome (1) follows from 0425e03e2aa3. Assume all four labels therefore have second-type obstruction gaps. If two labels use the same gap, the same-gap case of 36fccff06d48 gives outcome (2). Assume all four gap indices are distinct and order them g_1<g_2<g_3<g_4. Color each g_k by + or - according to whether its label lies in C_+ or C_-. There are exactly two gaps of each color. If g_1 and g_4 have opposite colors, their distance is at least three and they give the required opposite-class separated pair. If g_1 and g_4 have the same color, then g_2 and g_3 have the opposite color; hence g_1,g_3 are opposite-colored with g_3-g_1>=2 (equivalently g_2,g_4 also work). Thus in every all-distinct case there are y in C_+, z in C_- whose obstruction gaps differ by at least two. The separated-gap case of 36fccff06d48 gives the tight interval path joining y and z. Finally fixed_endpoint_matching_crosshooks01 applies to every opposite-class pair and gives (y,p_1,z) and (z,p_m,y).
