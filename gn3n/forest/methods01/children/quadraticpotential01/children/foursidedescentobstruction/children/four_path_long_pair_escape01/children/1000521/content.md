# A four-side next to a path of order at least seven forces strict descent or order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let C=X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover, where X=(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3) is a tight four-path and P=(p_1,...,p_m) has order m>=7. Then either one legal repartition of X|P strictly decreases quadratic potential, or H contains explicit relative-order disagreement between Hamilton tight paths on two five-vertex subsets of the six-set U=V(X) union {p_1,p_m}. More precisely, in the branch where the endpoint-five and crossed-five repartitions of foursidedescentobstruction do not decrease Phi, the two endpoint deletions U-p_1 and U-p_m are non-Hamiltonian while all four deletions U-x_i are Hamiltonian. If U is non-Hamiltonian, arbitrary Hamilton orders on the four sets U-x_i force relative-order disagreement; if U is Hamiltonian, replacing X|P by U|(p_2,...,p_{m-1}) decreases Phi by 4m-24>0.

## Body

Run the proof of foursidedescentobstruction. If X union {p_1} or X union {p_m} is Hamiltonian, the certified endpoint repartition already decreases Phi by 2m-10>0. Hence in the no-descent branch both U-p_m=X union {p_1} and U-p_1=X union {p_m} are non-Hamiltonian. The same proof supplies the crossed Hamiltonian five-sets U-x_3={x_0,x_1,x_2,p_1,p_m} and U-x_0={x_1,x_2,x_3,p_1,p_m}. Now apply the certified four-of-six theorem from smallset01/fivefence01 to the six-set U: at least four of its six vertex deletions are Hamiltonian. Since the two deletions at p_1,p_m are non-Hamiltonian, every deletion U-x_i, i=0,1,2,3, must be Hamiltonian. If U itself is non-Hamiltonian, astra004fourgooddisagree applies to the four good deletion labels x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3 and forces relative-order disagreement among arbitrary chosen Hamilton orders on U-x_i. If U is Hamiltonian, repartition X|P as U | M, where M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}) has order m-2. The old contribution to Phi is 4^2+m^2, the new contribution is 6^2+(m-2)^2, and the drop is 16+m^2-[36+(m-2)^2]=4m-24, which is positive for m>=7. Thus strict descent or explicit order disagreement is unavoidable.
