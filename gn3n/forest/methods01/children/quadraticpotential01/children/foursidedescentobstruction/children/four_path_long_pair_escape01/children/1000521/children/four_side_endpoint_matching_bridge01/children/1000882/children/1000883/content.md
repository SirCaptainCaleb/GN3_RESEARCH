# Every locally minimal four-side beside a path of order at least seven yields a five-side or order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential in its pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=4 and |P|>=7. Then H contains either a proper Hamiltonian five-set with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement or explicit order disagreement between Hamilton paths on overlapping induced supports.

## Body

Its endpoint six-set U=X union {p_1,p_m} is non-Hamiltonian; the two endpoint deletions U-p_1 and U-p_m are non-Hamiltonian; and every X-label deletion U-x is Hamiltonian.
