# A common endpoint-pair core forces an interior reversal on every long side at a local minimum

## Statement

Let W|P|Q minimize quadratic potential in its pairwise-repartition component, where W=D union {w}, |W|=4 and |D|=3. Let R be either P or Q, let E_R be the two displayed endpoints of R, and suppose D union E_R is Hamiltonian. If |R|>=6, then w is noninsertable into the displayed interior path R-E_R. Hence a tight triple through w reverses a displayed edge of that interior path.

## Body

Write r=|R|. If w were insertable into the displayed interior path R0=R-E_R, then R0 union {w} would have a Hamilton path. Together with the Hamiltonian five-set D union E_R this would repartition W|R from component orders (4,r) to (5,r-1), with quadratic-potential change 10-2r<0 for r>=6, contradicting component-minimality. Therefore w is noninsertable in R0. Since R0 has order at least four, acdec36ae3ca applies and gives a tight triple through w reversing a displayed edge of R0.
