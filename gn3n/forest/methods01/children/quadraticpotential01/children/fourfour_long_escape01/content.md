# Two four-sides beside a path of order at least six cannot be locally quadratic-minimal

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament and let X|Y|P be a spanning three-cover with |X|=|Y|=4 and |P|=m>=6. Then the connected pairwise-repartition component containing X|Y|P contains a spanning three-cover of strictly smaller quadratic potential. Equivalently, no componentwise Phi-minimal spanning three-cover has profile 4|4|m with m>=6.

## Body

Assume for contradiction that X|Y|P is Phi-minimal in its connected pairwise-repartition component. First, for every x in X, the five-set Y union {x} is non-Hamiltonian. Indeed, if Y union {x} were Hamiltonian, then X-{x} is a Hamiltonian three-set, so one legal repartition of X|Y replaces the orders (4,4) by (3,5). This raises Phi by exactly 3^2+5^2-2*4^2=2 and yields a reachable state (X-{x}) | (Y union {x}) | P. Apply threesidedescent6 to the three-side X-{x} and the m-path P. If its endpoint-transfer branch occurs, the final profile on these three components is 4|5|(m-1), with total Phi relative to the original 4|4|m equal to [4^2+5^2+(m-1)^2]-[2*4^2+m^2]=10-2m<0. If its two-endpoint branch occurs, the final profile is 5|5|(m-2), with difference [2*5^2+(m-2)^2]-[2*4^2+m^2]=22-4m<0. In either case m>=6 gives a reachable cover of smaller Phi, contradiction. Thus Y union {x} is non-Hamiltonian for every x in X. Symmetrically, X union {y} is non-Hamiltonian for every y in Y. Now fix any Hamilton order on Y and choose any three distinct vertices r_1,r_2,r_3 in X. The hypotheses of c82e8c6d3016 hold: all three extensions Y union {r_j} are non-Hamiltonian. That certified theorem gives a legal 5|3 repartition of X union Y. Starting from X|Y|P, perform such a repartition to reach a 5|3|m state. Its Phi is again exactly two larger than the original. Apply threesidedescent6 to its three-side and P. The same calculation gives final Phi smaller than the original by at least two. This contradiction proves that X|Y|P was not componentwise Phi-minimal.
