# Inner facing four-windows have exact two-move potential formulas

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament and let P=(p_0,...,p_{p-1}), Q=(q_0,...,q_{q-1}) be disjoint displayed tight paths, with x outside them, so P|Q|{x} is a spanning three-cover and p,q>=3. If W_L={p_0,q_{q-1},x,p_1} is Hamiltonian, then two legal pairwise repartitions transform P|Q|{x} into W_L | (p_2,...,p_{p-1}) | (q_0,...,q_{q-2}), with quadratic-potential change 20-4p-2q. If W_R={p_0,q_{q-1},x,q_{q-2}} is Hamiltonian, the symmetric two-move transformation has change 20-2p-4q.

## Body

For W_L, first repartition Q|{x} into the inherited path (q_0,...,q_{q-2}) and the two-vertex path (q_{q-1},x). Then repartition P together with that two-vertex path into the Hamiltonian four-set W_L and the inherited tail (p_2,...,p_{p-1}). These are legal pairwise repartitions and yield component orders 4,p-2,q-1. Relative to p,q,1, the potential change is 16+(p-2)^2+(q-1)^2-(p^2+q^2+1)=20-4p-2q. The W_R case first repartitions P|{x} into (p_1,...,p_{p-1}) and (p_0,x), then repartitions the latter with Q into W_R and (q_0,...,q_{q-3}), yielding orders 4,p-1,q-2 and change 20-2p-4q. No minimum-counterexample or extremality hypothesis is used.
