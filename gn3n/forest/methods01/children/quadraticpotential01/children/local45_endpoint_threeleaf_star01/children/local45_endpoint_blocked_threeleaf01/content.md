# A locally minimal 4|5 plateau has an endpoint-rooted three-leaf path with both adjacent six-unions blocked

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|Y|P be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=4, |Y|=5, and P=(p_1,...,p_m) of order m>=7. Put W=V(X) union V(Y). Then there exist distinct u,v,z_1,z_2,z_3 in W such that, with C={u,v,p_1,p_m}, all three five-sets C union {z_i} are Hamiltonian, while both six-sets C union {z_1,z_2} and C union {z_2,z_3} are non-Hamiltonian. Consequently, for arbitrary Hamilton paths R_i on C union {z_i}, at least one of the following holds: (1) two R_i have relative-order disagreement on the endpoint-containing core C; (2) the only possible Hamiltonian two-root enlargement among the three pairs is the outer union C union {z_1,z_3}; (3) there is a Hamiltonian four-set inside C union {z_1,z_2,z_3}; (4) for some i!=j and c in C, a tight triple on {z_i,c,z_j} reverses a displayed root-core edge of R_i or R_j. Any Hamiltonian four- or six-support arising in (2) or (3) is proper and has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.

## Body

Let F be the family of four-sides of balanced Hamiltonian 4|5 partitions of H[W]. By balanced9_multiplicity36_01, |F|>=36, and every A|B with A in F gives a same-component equal-Phi state A|B|P. Count incidences between pairs {u,v} subset W and four-sides A in F containing that pair. Each A contributes six incidences, so there are at least 216 over the 36 pairs of W. Hence some pair {u,v} is contained in at least six members of F. Build the link graph L on W-{u,v}, joining z,t when {u,v,z,t} belongs to F. Then e(L)>=6. Since L has seven vertices, some vertex z_2 has degree at least two; choose distinct neighbors z_1,z_3. Put A_12={u,v,z_1,z_2} and A_23={u,v,z_2,z_3}; both lie in F. Let C={u,v,p_1,p_m}. Apply local_four_allfour_interior_lock01 to the reachable Phi-minimal states with four-sides A_12 and A_23. Deleting z_2 from A_12 gives C union {z_1} Hamiltonian, deleting z_1 gives C union {z_2} Hamiltonian, and from A_23 we likewise obtain C union {z_3} Hamiltonian. Now C union {z_1,z_2}=A_12 union {p_1,p_m}. If this six-set were Hamiltonian, then together with the inherited middle path M=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}) it would repartition A_12|P from component orders (4,m) to (6,m-2), with quadratic-potential change 36+(m-2)^2-[16+m^2]=24-4m<0 for m>=7, contradicting componentwise minimality. Hence C union {z_1,z_2} is non-Hamiltonian; the same argument with A_23 gives non-Hamiltonicity of C union {z_2,z_3}. Apply three_fourcore_extensions_sync01 to the three Hamiltonian five-extensions C union {z_i}. Its pair-enlargement outcome is already excluded for the adjacent pairs {z_1,z_2} and {z_2,z_3}, so only the outer pair {z_1,z_3} can realize that outcome; the other synchronizer outcomes are exactly (1),(3),(4). Since |V(H)|=m+9>=16, any four- or six-vertex Hamiltonian support is proper, and minimum-counterexample calculus makes its complement non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two.
