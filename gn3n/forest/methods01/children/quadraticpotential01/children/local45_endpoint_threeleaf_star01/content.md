# A locally minimal 4|5 plateau contains a three-leaf five-set star rooted at both long endpoints

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|Y|P be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=4, |Y|=5, and P=(p_1,...,p_m) of order m>=6. Put W=V(X) union V(Y). Then there exist distinct u,v,z_1,z_2,z_3 in W such that, with C={u,v,p_1,p_m}, each five-set C union {z_i} is Hamiltonian. For arbitrary Hamilton paths R_i on C union {z_i}, at least one of the following holds: (1) two R_i have relative-order disagreement on the common four-core C, which contains both displayed endpoints p_1,p_m; (2) C union {z_i,z_j} is Hamiltonian for some i!=j; (3) there is a Hamiltonian four-set inside C union {z_1,z_2,z_3}; (4) for some i!=j and c in C, a tight triple on {z_i,c,z_j} reverses a displayed root-core edge of R_i or R_j. In outcomes (2) and (3), the proper Hamiltonian support has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.

## Body

Let F be the family of four-vertex sides A arising from balanced Hamiltonian 4|5 partitions A|B of H[W]. By balanced9_multiplicity36_01, |F|>=36. For every A in F, the state A|B|P is obtained from X|Y|P by one pairwise repartition of W, preserves the order multiset {4,5,m}, and hence has the same Phi in the same component. It is therefore also componentwise Phi-minimal. Apply local_four_allfour_interior_lock01 to its four-side A and path P. For every w in A, the five-set (A-{w}) union {p_1,p_m} is Hamiltonian. Thus every three-subset of every A in F is good, where a triple T subset W is called good if T union {p_1,p_m} is Hamiltonian. Let S be the three-uniform shadow of F, equivalently the family of good triples obtained this way. Counting incidences (T,A) with T subset A gives at least 4*36=144 incidences, while each triple of W lies in at most six four-subsets. Hence |S|>=24. Equality |S|=24 is impossible: it would force |F|=36 and every T in S to lie in all six possible four-subset supersets T union {x}. Then every Johnson neighbor of T in J(9,3) also lies in S. Since J(9,3) is connected and S is nonempty, this would force S to contain all binom(9,3)=84 triples, contradiction. Therefore |S|>=25. Count pair-triple incidences inside S. Their number is 3|S|>=75 over only binom(9,2)=36 pairs, so some pair {u,v} lies in at least three distinct triples {u,v,z_1},{u,v,z_2},{u,v,z_3} in S. Consequently each C union {z_i}, C={u,v,p_1,p_m}, is Hamiltonian. Apply three_fourcore_extensions_sync01 to these three Hamiltonian one-label extensions of the common four-core C, yielding alternatives (1)-(4). Since m>=6, |V(H)|=m+9>=15, so every four- or six-vertex Hamiltonian support produced in (2) or (3) is proper. In a minimum counterexample its complement has path-cover number at most two and cannot be Hamiltonian, else H would have a spanning two-cover; hence it is non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number exactly two.
