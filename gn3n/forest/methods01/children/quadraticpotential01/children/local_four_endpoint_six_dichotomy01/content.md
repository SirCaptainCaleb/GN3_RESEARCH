# A locally minimal four-side beside a path of order at least six either disagrees or makes the unique neutral endpoint transfer

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, where |X|=4 and P=(p_1,...,p_m) has m>=6. Put E={p_1,p_m}, R=(p_2,...,p_{m-1}), and U=X union E. Then X union {p_1} and X union {p_m} are non-Hamiltonian. Moreover exactly one of the following holds: (1) U is non-Hamiltonian; then every five-set E union (X-{x}), x in X, is Hamiltonian, U has four Hamiltonian deletions, and the six-set U yields explicit order disagreement between Hamilton paths on distinct Hamiltonian five-deletions; (2) m=6 and U is Hamiltonian, in which case replacing X|P by U|R is a legal equal-quadratic-potential pairwise repartition, producing another componentwise-minimal three-cover with four-side R. In particular, if |V(H)|>=17, every componentwise quadratic-minimal spanning three-cover with a four-vertex component forces order disagreement.

## Body

Write Phi for quadratic potential. Moving one displayed endpoint of P into X would change the pair sizes (4,m) to (5,m-1), with potential change 25+(m-1)^2-(16+m^2)=10-2m<0 for m>=6. Hence X union {p_1} and X union {p_m} are non-Hamiltonian. Now consider U=X union {p_1,p_m}. If U is non-Hamiltonian, the six-set four-of-six theorem gives at least four Hamiltonian five-vertex deletions. The two endpoint deletions are precisely X union {p_m} and X union {p_1}, already non-Hamiltonian, so all four deletions U-{x}=E union (X-{x}), x in X, are Hamiltonian. Therefore U is a proper non-Hamiltonian six-set with at least four Hamiltonian deletions, and four_good_six_interface01 yields explicit order disagreement between Hamilton paths on distinct Hamiltonian five-deletions of U. For completeness, each proper Hamiltonian five-set U-{x} has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement by minimum-counterexample calculus. If instead U is Hamiltonian, replacing X|P by U|R is a legal pairwise repartition with pair sizes (6,m-2). Its potential change is 36+(m-2)^2-(16+m^2)=24-4m. Componentwise minimality therefore forces m=6, and then the change is exactly zero. Thus U|R|Q is another minimum-Phi state in the same pairwise-repartition component, with the new four-side R. Finally, if n=|V(H)|>=17 and X|P|Q has |X|=4, then |P|+|Q|=n-4>=13, so one of P,Q has order at least seven. Applying the preceding dichotomy to that path excludes the neutral m=6 case and forces order disagreement.
