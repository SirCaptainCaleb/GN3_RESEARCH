# Endpoint deletion probes reduce an all-equal three-cover to multiple crossings, a generic noninsertion witness, or crossed near-merges

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let A|B|C be a spanning three-cover with |A|=|B|=|C|=r>=2, where A=(a_1,...,a_r). For each d in {a_1,a_r}, choose any two-cover T_d of H-d and count the ordinary edges of T_d whose endpoints lie in different members of the inherited three-part partition (A-{d})|B|C. Then either one of T_{a_1},T_{a_r} has at least two such crossings, or both have exactly one. In the latter case each T_d isolates exactly one of B,C as an entire component and its other component is a Hamilton path on the union of A-{d} with the remaining opposite block; moreover the isolated block enlarged by d is non-Hamiltonian. If the two endpoint probes isolate the same block, then a_1 and a_r are both noninsertable into that displayed block and hence satisfy one of the five universal alternatives of a8c9883902b1. If they isolate different blocks, then after interchanging B,C if needed, H[(A-{a_1}) union C] and H[(A-{a_r}) union B] are Hamiltonian while H[B union {a_1}] and H[C union {a_r}] are non-Hamiltonian. Thus an all-equal quadratic plateau reduces, independently of r, to multiple three-block crossings, the generic paired-noninsertion witness, or a crossed pair of near-merges.

## Body

Fix d in {a_1,a_r} and a two-cover T_d of H-d. Relative to the three nonempty classes A-{d}, B, C, the cover T_d must contain at least one crossing edge: with no crossing, two paths cannot cover three nonempty classes. Suppose it has exactly one. Cutting that edge produces three monochromatic path blocks. Since all three classes are nonempty and covered, there is exactly one block in each class. Hence one class is an entire component of T_d and the other component is a Hamilton path on the union of the other two classes. The isolated class cannot be A-{d}; otherwise B union C is Hamiltonian, and its Hamilton path together with the displayed path A would two-cover H. Therefore the isolated class is B or C. If it is B, then H[(A-{d}) union C] is Hamiltonian. Also H[B union {d}] must be non-Hamiltonian, because otherwise Hamilton paths on B union {d} and (A-{d}) union C would two-cover H. The case with C isolated is symmetric. In particular d is noninsertable into the displayed order of whichever opposite block is isolated. Apply this conclusion to d=a_1 and d=a_r. If either cover has at least two crossings we have the first outcome. If both have one crossing and isolate the same opposite block, both endpoints of A are noninsertable into that same displayed path, so a8c9883902b1 gives its five-way structural alternative. If they isolate different blocks, relabel B,C to obtain the two crossed near-merge Hamiltonian supports and the two corresponding non-Hamiltonian one-vertex enlargements stated above.
