# A Hamiltonian endpoint replacement gives a neutral two-path exchange or a full-path lock

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament, let W be a Hamiltonian four-set, let P=(p_1,...,p_m) be a vertex-disjoint tight path, let e be either displayed endpoint of P, and let w in W. Suppose W'=(W-{w}) union {e} is Hamiltonian. Put R=V(P)-{e}, with its inherited tight path. Then either R union {w} is Hamiltonian, in which case W' | (R union {w}) is a two-path cover of V(W) union V(P) with the same component orders 4,m as W|P, or R union {w} is non-Hamiltonian, in which case w is noninsertable at every position of the inherited path R.

## Body

The supports W' and R union {w} are disjoint and partition V(W) union V(P). If R union {w} is Hamiltonian, choosing Hamilton paths on the two supports gives the stated two-path cover, with the same orders 4,m. Otherwise, if w were insertable anywhere into the inherited displayed path R, that insertion would itself be a Hamilton path on R union {w}, contradicting non-Hamiltonicity. Thus w is noninsertable into R. No spanning-cover, third-component, minimum-counterexample, or potential hypothesis is used.
