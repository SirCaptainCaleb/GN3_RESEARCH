# A size-gap-one pair admits neutral endpoint transport or a universal noninsertion witness

## Statement

Let C=A|B|R be a three-cover of a boundary tournament, with A=(a_1,...,a_{b+1}) and |B|=b. If H[V(B) union {a_1}] is Hamiltonian, then replacing A|B by (a_2,...,a_{b+1}) together with a Hamilton path on V(B) union {a_1} is a legal pairwise repartition preserving Phi exactly; the same holds with a_{b+1}. If neither endpoint enlargement of B is Hamiltonian, then a_1 and a_{b+1} are both noninsertable into the displayed order of B, so the universal paired-noninsertion theorem a8c9883902b1 supplies one of its five structural alternatives. Thus every displayed component pair whose orders differ by one has a scale-independent dichotomy: a Phi-neutral endpoint transfer, or a universal paired-noninsertion witness.

## Body

Suppose first that H[V(B) union {a_1}] is Hamiltonian. Deleting the endpoint a_1 from the displayed tight path A leaves the inherited tight path (a_2,...,a_{b+1}), so these two paths cover V(A) union V(B). The old pair orders are b+1,b and the new pair orders are b,b+1; hence their contribution to Phi is unchanged. The terminal endpoint is symmetric. If neither endpoint enlargement is Hamiltonian, then inserting either endpoint anywhere into the displayed Hamilton order of B would itself give a Hamilton path on that endpoint enlargement, impossible. Hence both endpoints are noninsertable into B, and a8c9883902b1 applies.
