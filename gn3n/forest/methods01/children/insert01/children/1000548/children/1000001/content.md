# Paired noninsertability beside a Hamiltonian four-set reduces locally to a connector, an opposite cross, disagreement, or descent at the generated state

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order at least eighteen. Let S=(u,a,b,v) be a tight four-path and let H-S=R|T be a two-cover with R=(r_0,...,r_m) of order at least six. Put M=(r_1,...,r_{m-1}) and suppose u and v are both noninsertable into the displayed path M. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) H contains explicit order disagreement; (2) one of the Hamiltonian supports of order four, five, or six generated by the paired-noninsertion alternatives has a two-cover complement and its associated three-cover has a strict quadratic-potential descent in its pairwise-repartition component; (3) for one orientation (s,t) of {u,v} there are 1<=i<j<=m-2 with j>=i+2 such that (s,r_{i+1},...,r_j,t) is tight; (4) for some r_k in M the oppositely oriented cross (v,r_k,u) is tight. The descent in (2) is measured from the generated Hamiltonian-support state, not from S|R|T.

## Body

Apply a8c9883902b1 to u and v on M. A Hamiltonian four-set has a non-Hamiltonian two-cover complement by minimum-counterexample calculus. Apply 7bab8dd31d87: it gives descent from the associated four-set state, order disagreement, or a Hamiltonian six-set with two-cover complement. The six-set is consumed by ham6_large_escape01 because |H|>=18, yielding descent at its associated state or order disagreement. If the exceptional cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-set occurs, smallset01 says adjoining any exterior vertex gives a Hamiltonian five-set; its complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, and ham5_large_escape01 gives descent at that five-set state or order disagreement. A Hamiltonian five-set from a8c9883902b1 is treated the same way.

For the cross-triple alternative, if (u,r_k,v) is tight, then S=(u,a,b,v) and (u,r_k,v) are internally disjoint tight corridors with the same ordered endpoints. By 53d257fcf0a8 their five-vertex union is Hamiltonian, so ham5_large_escape01 again yields descent at the associated five-set state or order disagreement. If instead (v,r_k,u) is tight, retain outcome (4); reversing S is not permitted. Finally, the separated-gap alternative of a8c9883902b1 is exactly outcome (3), after translating indices from M to R. This proof uses only the displayed local four-set/two-cover/noninsertability data, not a deletion-state or double-wrap origin.
