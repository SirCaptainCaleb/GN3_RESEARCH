# An unresolved opposite cross forces multiple adjacent Hamiltonian four-windows

## Statement

In the setting of opposite_cross_pivot_five_nonham01, let S={u,s_1,s_2,v} and K=S union {r_k}. Then for at least three distinct vertices x in S, the four-set K-{x} is Hamiltonian. Consequently at least one of K-{u}, K-{v} is Hamiltonian, and at least one of K-{s_1}, K-{s_2} is Hamiltonian. Every such Hamiltonian four-set is proper and has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.

## Body

By opposite_cross_pivot_five_nonham01, K is a non-Hamiltonian five-set, while S=K-{r_k} is Hamiltonian because S is the displayed tight four-path. The five-vertex small-set structure says that a non-Hamiltonian five-set has at most one non-Hamiltonian four-vertex subset. Hence among the four sets K-{x}, x in S, at least three are Hamiltonian. A subset of the four labels {u,s_1,s_2,v} of size at least three cannot omit both endpoints u,v and cannot omit both internal labels s_1,s_2. Therefore at least one endpoint replacement K-{u} or K-{v} is Hamiltonian, and at least one internal-label replacement K-{s_1} or K-{s_2} is Hamiltonian. Since H has order at least eighteen, each such four-set is proper. Minimum-counterexample calculus then gives path-cover number at most two for its complement; the complement cannot be Hamiltonian, or together with the Hamiltonian four-set it would two-cover H. Thus every displayed adjacent four-window has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.
