# An unresolved opposite cross has locked pivot geometry and at least three adjacent Hamiltonian four-windows

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order at least eighteen. Let S=(u,s_1,s_2,v) be a tight four-path, let H-S=R|T with R=(r_0,...,r_m) of order at least six, and put M=(r_1,...,r_{m-1}). Suppose u and v are noninsertable into M and the paired-noninsertion reduction reaches its genuinely unresolved opposite-cross residue: neither explicit order disagreement nor strict quadratic-potential descent at any generated Hamiltonian-support state occurs. Then for some 2<=k<=m-2 the five triples (v,r_k,u), (r_k,v,s_2), (s_1,u,r_k), (r_k,v,r_{k-1}), (r_{k+1},u,r_k) are tight, with the four corresponding second-type prefix/suffix hooks. Moreover K={u,s_1,s_2,v,r_k} is non-Hamiltonian, and for at least three distinct x in S the four-set K-{x} is Hamiltonian. Consequently at least one endpoint replacement K-{u} or K-{v}, and at least one internal-label replacement K-{s_1} or K-{s_2}, is Hamiltonian; every such displayed Hamiltonian four-set has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.
