# Two same-side extensions of an internal path edge force a Hamiltonian five-window or a doubled reverse barrier

## Statement

Let p,x,y,q,u be five distinct vertices of a boundary tournament. Suppose (p,x,y), (x,y,q), and (x,y,u) are tight. Then either H[{p,x,y,q,u}] is Hamiltonian, or both (u,q,y) and (q,u,y) are tight. Symmetrically, if (p,x,y), (x,y,q), and (u,x,y) are tight, then either H[{p,x,y,q,u}] is Hamiltonian, or both (x,p,u) and (x,u,p) are tight.

## Body

For the first assertion, if (y,q,u) is tight then (p,x,y,q,u) is a tight Hamilton path. If (y,u,q) is tight then (p,x,y,u,q) is a tight Hamilton path. If neither is tight, boundary antisymmetry applied separately to the reversal pairs gives (u,q,y) and (q,u,y), the doubled reverse barrier. For the symmetric assertion, if (u,p,x) is tight then (u,p,x,y,q) is Hamiltonian; if (p,u,x) is tight then (p,u,x,y,q) is Hamiltonian. If neither is tight, antisymmetry gives (x,p,u) and (x,u,p).
