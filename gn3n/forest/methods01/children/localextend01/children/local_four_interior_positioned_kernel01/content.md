# A locally minimal complete endpoint family yields a positioned Hamiltonian interior kernel

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |W|=4 and |P|,|Q|>=6. Suppose there are w in W and D=W-{w} such that D union {e} is non-Hamiltonian for every displayed endpoint e of P or Q and D union {e,f} is Hamiltonian for every pair of distinct displayed endpoints. Then H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set Z of order four or five with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, where Z contains w and is supported inside w together with bounded local failed-insertion pivot neighborhoods of the endpoint-stripped interiors of P and Q (and, in the universal cyclic-K4 first-type subcase, possibly one additional label of D).

## Body

Apply four_side_endpointpair_repartition_or_lock01 to each of P,Q. Since both have order at least six and the relevant one-endpoint extensions D+e are non-Hamiltonian, componentwise Phi-minimality excludes the repartition alternative with change 10-2|P| or 10-2|Q|. Thus w is noninsertable into both inherited endpoint-stripped interior paths P° and Q°. Apply insert01 on each interior. If either failed-insertion obstruction is first-type, 0425e03e2aa3 gives a local four-vertex window containing w that is Hamiltonian or the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian K4. In the Hamiltonian case take that window. In the cyclic case add any d in D; 0425e03e2aa3 makes the resulting five-set Hamiltonian. If both obstructions are second-type, apply 4efbe05b945a to the two pivot gaps. In its mixed-four-path branch take that Hamiltonian four-set. In its doubled-cross branch let F={w,p_i,p_{i+1},q_j,q_{j+1}} be the five local pivot vertices. If F is Hamiltonian take F. Otherwise smallset01 says a non-Hamiltonian five-set has at most one non-Hamiltonian four-subset, so at least three of the four subsets F-{v}, v!=w, are Hamiltonian; choose one, which still contains w. In every case Z is proper, and minimum-counterexample calculus implies H-Z is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.
