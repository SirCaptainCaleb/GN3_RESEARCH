# Endpoint pairs either split the four-window or produce a positioned common-core shell

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let W be a four-vertex set, and let H-W=P|Q be a two-cover whose displayed paths have distinct endpoint pairs E_P,E_Q. For R in {P,Q} put A_R={w in W:(W-{w}) union E_R is Hamiltonian}. Then |A_P|,|A_Q|>=2. Either (i) A_P and A_Q are disjoint, hence each has order two and they partition W; or (ii) there is w in A_P intersect A_Q and D=W-{w}. In case (ii), either D union {x} is Hamiltonian for some displayed endpoint x, with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, or all four singleton extensions D union {x} are non-Hamiltonian and every pair extension D union {x,y} is Hamiltonian. In the latter subcase one may choose a Hamilton path P_xy on every D union {x,y} with both endpoints in D, and at least one of the following occurs: two chosen P_xy disagree in relative order on common vertices; some D union T with T a three-subset of E_P union E_Q is Hamiltonian; or E_P union E_Q is Hamiltonian. Every proper Hamiltonian support arising above has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.
