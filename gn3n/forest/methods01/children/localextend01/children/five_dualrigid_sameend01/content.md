# Distinct rigid five-side locks reduce to three synchronized same-end four-core extensions

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a Phi-minimal trapped three-cover with |X|=5 and |P|,|Q|>=7. Assume no equal-Phi endpoint support exchange is legal. For each long path R in {P,Q}, let I_R^- and I_R^+ be the sets of labels c in X for which (X-{c}) together with the initial or terminal endpoint of R is Hamiltonian. Suppose both endpoint-good intersections are singletons with distinct labels a and b: I_P^- intersect I_P^+={a} and I_Q^- intersect I_Q^+={b}, a!=b. Then each of the other three labels c in X-{a,b} is good for exactly one endpoint e_P(c) of P and exactly one endpoint e_Q(c) of Q. For each such c, put D_c=X-{c}. At least one of the following occurs: (1) Hamilton paths on two of the relevant one-endpoint extensions have order disagreement on their common D_c core; (2) D_c union {e_P(c),e_Q(c)} is Hamiltonian for some c; (3) H contains a Hamiltonian four-set supported on e_P(c),e_Q(c) and two vertices of D_c for some c; (4) there is a reverse cross triple through one vertex of D_c and the two endpoints e_P(c),e_Q(c) for some c; (5) for every c in X-{a,b}, D_c has a tight Hamilton order C_c such that both e_P(c) and e_Q(c) extend the same displayed endpoint of C_c. Thus, after excluding the standard local outputs, the distinct-lock residue is exactly three synchronized same-end extension pairs over three Hamiltonian four-subsets of X.

## Body

By 45229a54c95a, singleton endpoint-good intersection for P implies |I_P^-|=|I_P^+|=3 and I_P^- union I_P^+=X; hence every c!=a belongs to exactly one of I_P^-,I_P^+. The same statement for Q gives that every c!=b is good for exactly one Q-endpoint. Therefore every c in X-{a,b} has unique endpoints e_P(c),e_Q(c) such that D_c+e_P(c) and D_c+e_Q(c) are Hamiltonian five-sets. Choose Hamilton paths on those two five-sets. If their restrictions to D_c have different relative order, outcome (1) holds. Otherwise write the common D_c order as C_c and apply d495c62905f0 to the two one-label extensions. If the insertion gaps differ by at least two, D_c+e_P(c)+e_Q(c) is Hamiltonian, giving (2). If they are adjacent, either that six-set is Hamiltonian, again (2), or the reverse cross triple through the common core vertex is tight, giving (4). If the gaps coincide internally, d495c62905f0 gives a Hamiltonian four-set on the old gap edge and the two exterior endpoints, giving (3). The only remaining case is one common endpoint gap. Then C_c itself is a tight Hamilton path on D_c and both exterior endpoints extend its same displayed end, giving (5). Apply this independently to the three labels c in X-{a,b}.
