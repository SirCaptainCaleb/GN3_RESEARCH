# A locally minimal complete endpoint family yields an interior Hamiltonian kernel or doubled cross barrier

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover that minimizes quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |W|=4 and |P|,|Q|>=6. Suppose there are w in W and D=W-{w} such that D union {e} is non-Hamiltonian for every displayed endpoint e of P or Q and D union {e,f} is Hamiltonian for every pair of distinct displayed endpoints. Let P° and Q° be the inherited interior paths obtained by deleting both displayed endpoints from P and Q. Then either (1) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set of order four or five whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, or (2) there exist p in V(P°), q in V(Q°) such that both (q,p,w) and (p,q,w) are tight.

## Body

Apply four_side_endpointpair_repartition_or_lock01 separately to P and Q. For P, the hypotheses D+{p_left} and D+{p_right} non-Hamiltonian force F_P=D+E_P Hamiltonian. Since |P|>=6, the repartition alternative W|P -> F_P | (P°+w) would change the pair contribution to Phi by 10-2|P|<0, contradicting componentwise Phi-minimality. Hence w is noninsertable throughout the inherited interior path P°. The same argument gives noninsertability of w throughout Q°. Now apply the certified failed-insertion normal form insert01 to w against P° and Q°. If either obstruction is first-type, certified 0425e03e2aa3 gives a four-vertex local window that is either Hamiltonian or the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian K4. In the Hamiltonian case it is proper, so minimum-counterexample calculus makes its complement non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. In the cyclic case choose any d in D outside that K4; 0425e03e2aa3 Hamiltonizes the five-set obtained by adding d, and minimum-counterexample calculus again gives a pc2 non-Hamiltonian complement. If both failed-insertion obstructions are second-type, apply certified 4efbe05b945a to their pivot gaps. Either a mixed Hamiltonian four-path occurs, giving outcome (1) by minimum-counterexample calculus, or both cross triples (q,p,w) and (p,q,w) are tight for suitable interior pivot vertices p,q, giving outcome (2).
