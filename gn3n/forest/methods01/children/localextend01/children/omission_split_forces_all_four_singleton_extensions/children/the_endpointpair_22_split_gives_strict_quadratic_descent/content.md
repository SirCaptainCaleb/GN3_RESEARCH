# Above order fourteen the endpoint-pair 2+2 split gives strict quadratic descent

## Statement

Let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover with |W|=4 and distinct displayed endpoints on P,Q. Suppose their same-path endpoint pairs have disjoint two-element Hamiltonian omission sets in W. If |V(H)|>=15, then W|P|Q admits a strict endpoint-transfer quadratic-potential descent.

## Body

The first lemma makes W+e Hamiltonian for every displayed endpoint e. Since |P|+|Q|=n-4>=11, one of P,Q has order p>=6. Moving either endpoint e of that path into W replaces sizes (4,p) by (5,p-1), changing Phi by 10-2p<0.
