# A 2+2 endpoint-pair omission split forces all four singleton extensions

## Statement

Let W be a four-set and E1,E2 disjoint exterior two-sets. For i=1,2 let A_i be the vertices w in W for which (W-{w}) union E_i is Hamiltonian. If A_1 and A_2 are disjoint and each has order two, then W union {e} is Hamiltonian for every e in E1 union E2.

## Body

Fix i and S=W union E_i. Exactly two W-deletions of S are Hamiltonian. By the four-of-six theorem in smallset01, at least four deletions of S are Hamiltonian. Hence both deletions corresponding to labels in E_i are Hamiltonian, namely W union {e} for both e in E_i. Apply this for i=1,2.
