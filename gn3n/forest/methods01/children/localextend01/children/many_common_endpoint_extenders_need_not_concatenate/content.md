# Many common endpoint extenders need not concatenate

## Statement

For every k>=2 and m>=2 there is a boundary tournament with a tight path R=(r_1,...,r_m) and k exterior vertices W such that (w,R) is tight for every w in W, while (x,y,R) is not tight for every ordered pair of distinct x,y in W.

## Body

Choose the reversal-pair orientations independently as follows. Make the consecutive triples of R tight. For every w in W make (w,r_1,r_2) tight. For every distinct x,y in W make (x,y,r_1) non-tight, so its reversal (r_1,y,x) is tight. These prescriptions concern distinct reversal pairs and are therefore consistent. Complete all remaining reversal pairs arbitrarily. Then every w left-extends R, but every sequence (x,y,R) fails at its first triple (x,y,r_1).
