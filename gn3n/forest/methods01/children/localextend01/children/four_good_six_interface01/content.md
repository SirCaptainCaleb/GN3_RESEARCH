# Four Hamiltonian deletions of a proper six-set force Hamiltonicity or localized order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let S be a proper six-vertex set. Suppose there are at least four vertices d in S for which H[S-{d}] is Hamiltonian. Then either H[S] is Hamiltonian, in which case H-S is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, or two Hamilton paths on distinct five-vertex deletions of S exhibit relative-order disagreement on common vertices.

## Body

If H[S] is non-Hamiltonian, apply astra004fourgooddisagree to the induced boundary tournament H[S] and the at least four vertices d whose deletions are Hamiltonian. It gives two Hamilton paths on distinct one-vertex deletions of S with order disagreement. If H[S] is Hamiltonian, then S is a proper Hamiltonian support in a minimum counterexample. Minimum-counterexample calculus gives path-cover number at most two for H-S, while H-S cannot be Hamiltonian because complementary Hamilton paths on S and H-S would form a spanning two-cover of H. Hence H-S is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.
