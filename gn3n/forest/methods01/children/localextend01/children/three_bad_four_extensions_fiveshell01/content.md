# Three bad extensions of a three-path force a complete Hamiltonian five-shell

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament, let D be a three-vertex set equipped with any tight Hamilton path, and let e,f,g be distinct vertices outside D. Suppose D union {e}, D union {f}, and D union {g} are all non-Hamiltonian. Then each of D union {e,f}, D union {e,g}, and D union {f,g} is Hamiltonian. More strongly, each of the three five-sets has a Hamilton tight path whose two endpoints both lie in D.

## Body

Apply the certified 'two bad four-extensions force a controlled five-path' lemma from localextend01 to the tight three-path D and the pair e,f. Since D+e and D+f are non-Hamiltonian, D+e+f has a Hamilton path with both endpoints in D. Repeat with pairs e,g and f,g. This yields all three pair-extensions with the asserted endpoint control.
