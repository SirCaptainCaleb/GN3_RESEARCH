# A tight triple inside a non-Hamiltonian four-set forces a four- or five-set extension

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament, let X be a non-Hamiltonian four-vertex set, let T be a tight three-vertex path contained in X, and let y lie outside X. Then at least one of T union {y} and X union {y} is Hamiltonian.

## Body

Assume for contradiction that both T union {y} and X union {y} are non-Hamiltonian. By smallset01, every non-Hamiltonian five-vertex boundary tournament is edge-orderable, so one edge order represents all tight triples of H[X union {y}]. In this single edge order the two four-sets X and T union {y} are both non-Hamiltonian and meet in the three-set T. The intersecting-four-set lemma in smallset01 then forces their union X union {y} to have an increasing Hamilton path, contradicting its assumed non-Hamiltonicity. Hence at least one of the two extensions is Hamiltonian.
