# Repeated reversed-label internality forces a transverse deletion square or an internality triangle

## Statement

Assume the repeated-internal branch of reversal_pair_three_deletion_normal01. Thus there are distinct stable-shell labels s,t and a fixed reversed label d in {u,v} such that d is internal in every two-cover of H-s and of H-t. Then H-d, H-{d,s}, H-{d,t}, and H-{d,s,t} are all non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Consequently at least one of the following holds for two-covers of H-d: (1) some two-cover has s and t simultaneously as displayed endpoints; (2) two two-covers have different unordered support partitions; (3) two Hamilton paths on one common component support exhibit relative-order disagreement; (4) one of s,t is internal in every two-cover of H-d. In outcome (4), the three deletion states H-s,H-t,H-d exhibit a cyclic universal-internal pattern: d is universally internal in the first two and one of s,t is universally internal in the third.

## Body

By reversal_pair_three_deletion_normal01, H-s and H-t are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two and the fixed reversed label d is internal in every two-cover of both states. The states H-{d,s} and H-{d,t} are lower vertices of the corresponding reversed-pair pc2 squares, hence are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Also H-d is a one-vertex deletion of the minimum counterexample, hence non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Finally H-{d,s,t} is a proper induced subtournament and has path-cover number at most two. It cannot be Hamiltonian: the complementary three-set {d,s,t} is Hamiltonian in every boundary tournament, so Hamiltonicity of H-{d,s,t} would give a spanning two-cover of H. Hence H-{d,s,t} is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.

Therefore H-d with labels s,t forms a full two-label pc2 square. Apply pc2_square_topcover_normal01. Its four alternatives give outcomes (1)-(4) exactly. The final description simply combines outcome (4) with the assumed universal internality of d in H-s and H-t.
