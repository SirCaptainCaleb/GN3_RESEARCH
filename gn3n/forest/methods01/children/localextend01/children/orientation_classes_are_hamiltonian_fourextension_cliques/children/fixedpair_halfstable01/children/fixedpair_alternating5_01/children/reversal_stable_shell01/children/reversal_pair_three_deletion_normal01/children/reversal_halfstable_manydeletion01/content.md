# A genuine reversed pair normalizes across a linear-size deletion family

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample on n vertices. Then there exist a genuine reversed ordered pair u,v and a set C subseteq V(H)-{u,v} with |C|>=ceil((n-2)/2) such that one of (u,t,v),(v,t,u) is tight with the same orientation for every t in C, and for every t in C the four states H-{u,v,t}, H-{u,t}, H-{v,t}, and H-t are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Consequently at least one of the following holds: (1) for some t in C, a two-cover of H-t has u and v simultaneously as displayed endpoints; (2) for some t in C, two two-covers of H-t have different unordered support partitions; (3) for some t in C, two Hamilton paths on one common component support exhibit order disagreement; (4) there are a fixed label d in {u,v} and a set C' subseteq C with |C'|>=ceil((n-2)/4) such that d is internal in every two-cover of H-t for every t in C'. In outcome (4), applying universal_internal_transposition01 gives either endpoint/support/order disturbance among covers of H-d, or a set I subseteq C' with |I|>=|C'|-1 such that d and every t in I are mutually internal across H-t and H-d; for each such t, every two-cover of H-{d,t} has the four-end Hamiltonian-K4-or-doubled-reverse-barrier structure of mutual_internal_endpoint_grid01.

## Body

By counterexample_contains_an_explicit_reversing_tight_triple choose a tight path containing an ordered edge (u,v) and a tight triple reversing that pair. Apply fixedpair_halfstable01 to the prescribed pair {u,v}. Its proof partitions V(H)-{u,v} into the two orientation classes C_+={t:(u,t,v) is tight} and C_-={t:(v,t,u) is tight}; choose the larger class C. Then |C|>=ceil((n-2)/2), and all three-vertex paths on {u,t,v} have the same displayed orientation over t in C.

Fix t in C and let R_t be that tight three-vertex path. Apply path_induces_a_pathcovertwo_endpoint_square_on_its_complement to R_t. Its endpoints are u,v and its complement is H-{u,v,t}, so H-{u,v,t}, H-{u,t}, H-{v,t}, and H-t are all non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.

Apply pc2_square_topcover_normal01 to the top state H-t with labels u,v for every t in C. If any application gives simultaneous endpoint exposure, support-partition disagreement, or order disagreement, we obtain (1), (2), or (3). Otherwise each t chooses at least one d_t in {u,v} that is internal in every two-cover of H-t. By pigeonhole, one fixed d occurs for at least ceil(|C|/2) labels t. Since |C|>=ceil((n-2)/2), this is at least ceil((n-2)/4). Taking those labels as C' gives (4).

A minimum counterexample has order greater than ten, so |C'|>=3. Apply universal_internal_transposition01 to d and C'. It gives either simultaneous endpoint exposure among two labels of C' in a cover of H-d, support-partition disagreement among covers of H-d, order disagreement on a common support in H-d, or a set I subseteq C' with |I|>=|C'|-1 such that every t in I is internal in every two-cover of H-d. In the last case the defining property of C' says d is internal in every two-cover of H-t, so d,t are mutually internal for every t in I. The conclusion of mutual_internal_endpoint_grid01 then applies to every two-cover of H-{d,t}.
