# Above order seventeen a minimum counterexample reaches descent, neutral exchange, a mixed small support, or a second-type reversal

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=18. Then H has a spanning three-cover W|P|Q in which W is a Hamiltonian five-set containing all vertices of a genuine reversing tight triple, H-W=P|Q is a non-Hamiltonian two-cover, and |P|>=7, such that at least one of the following holds: (1) one legal repartition of W|P strictly decreases quadratic potential; (2) one legal repartition preserves the component orders and exchanges one vertex of W with a displayed endpoint of P; (3) H contains a proper Hamiltonian support U of order four or five meeting both W and P, with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement; (4) some vertex x in W is noninsertable into P, participates in a tight triple reversing a displayed consecutive pair of P, and its failed-insertion normal form on P is second-type. Thus the first-type branch of the second positioned reversal is completely converted into a mixed small support.

## Body

Choose W by reversal_five_support_global01: W is a proper Hamiltonian five-set containing a genuine reversing tight triple and H-W has path-cover number two. Write H-W=P|Q with |P|>=|Q|. Since n>=18, |P|+|Q|=n-5>=13, so |P|>=7. Apply five_side_arbitrary_escape01 to W|P|Q. Its strict-descent and equal-profile exchange alternatives give (1) and (2). In its reversal alternative, a vertex x of W is noninsertable into P and a failed-insertion witness reverses a displayed consecutive pair of P. Apply the failed-insertion normal form. If it is second-type, this is (4). If it is first-type, let X0 be its four-vertex window, consisting of x and three consecutive vertices of P. By 0425e03e2aa3, either X0 is Hamiltonian, in which case take U=X0, or X0 is the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-set, in which case adjoining any d in W-{x} gives a Hamiltonian five-set U. In either case U is proper. If H-U were Hamiltonian, U together with its complement would two-cover H; minimum-counterexample calculus therefore gives that H-U is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. This gives (3).
