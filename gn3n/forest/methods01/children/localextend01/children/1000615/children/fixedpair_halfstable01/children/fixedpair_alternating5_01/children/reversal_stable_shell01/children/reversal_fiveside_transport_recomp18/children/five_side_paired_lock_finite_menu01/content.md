# A locally minimal five-side with no neutral exchange reduces paired locks to a bounded menu

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within a trapped pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=5 and P=(p_1,...,p_m), m>=7. Assume no equal-Phi endpoint support exchange between X and P is legal. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) the endpoint-good sets have the rigid 2+1+2 signature of 45229a54c95a; (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian support of order four or five meeting both X and P, with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement; (3) two distinct vertices x,y in X and indices i<j with j>=i+2 form a direct tight connector (x,p_{i+1},...,p_j,y); (4) two distinct vertices x,y in X and some i form an explicit cross triple (x,p_{i+1},y) arising from adjacent second-type obstruction gaps.

## Body

Let I_L,I_R be the endpoint-good deletion sets from 45229a54c95a. Since no equal-Phi endpoint support exchange is legal, every vertex of I_L intersect I_R is globally noninsertable into the displayed path P. By 45229a54c95a, either |I_L intersect I_R|=1 and the rigid 2+1+2 signature holds, giving (1), or there are distinct x,y in I_L intersect I_R. Apply the failed-insertion normal form to x and y. If either obstruction is first-type, 0425e03e2aa3 gives a Hamiltonian four-window or the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-set. In the latter case adjoining the other locked label, which lies outside that four-set, gives a Hamiltonian five-set. Thus (2) holds. Hence assume both x,y are second-type. Apply 36fccff06d48. If their gaps coincide, the corresponding four-set is Hamiltonian, giving (2). If the gaps are separated by at least two, the direct connector (x,p_{i+1},...,p_j,y) is tight, giving (3). If the gaps are adjacent, either the associated five-set is Hamiltonian, giving (2), or the explicit cross triple (x,p_{i+1},y) is tight, giving (4). In every Hamiltonian-support case the support is proper because m>=7, and its complement cannot be Hamiltonian; minimum-counterexample calculus gives non-Hamiltonian path-cover number two.
