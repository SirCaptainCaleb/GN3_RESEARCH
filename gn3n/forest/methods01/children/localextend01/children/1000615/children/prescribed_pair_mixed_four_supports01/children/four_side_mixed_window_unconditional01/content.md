# A four-side has a mixed Hamiltonian four-support in every three-vertex interior window

## Statement

Let X be a vertex set of order at least two, and let (b_1,b_2,b_3) be any ordered triple of distinct vertices outside X in a boundary tournament. For any prescribed pair {x,y} subset X, some Hamiltonian four-support has one of the forms {x,b_1,b_2,b_3}, {y,b_1,b_2,b_3}, {x,y,b_1,b_2}, or {x,y,b_2,b_3}. In particular, for every spanning three-cover X|P|Q with |X|=4 and P of order at least five, every three consecutive vertices of the displayed interior M of P supplies a Hamiltonian four-support meeting X and M in one of these forms. In a minimum counterexample its complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. This conclusion is unconditional: no endpoint repartition, insertion obstruction, interval connector, gap network, or order-thirteen threshold is needed.

## Body

This uses no tightness assumption on the displayed triple and no Hamiltonicity assumption on X.
