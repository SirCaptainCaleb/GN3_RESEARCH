# Two bad extensions of a three-set force a Hamiltonian five-set with exactly three Hamiltonian deletions

## Statement

Let T be a three-vertex set and let e,f be distinct exterior vertices in a boundary tournament. If T union {e} and T union {f} are both non-Hamiltonian, put F=T union {e,f}. Then F is Hamiltonian. Moreover F-e and F-f are non-Hamiltonian, while F-t is Hamiltonian for every t in T. Thus the Hamiltonian deletion labels of F are exactly the three vertices of T.

## Body

The hypotheses say exactly that F-f=T union {e} and F-e=T union {f} are non-Hamiltonian. By two_bad_threecore_opposite_pair_shell01, for every t in T the four-set F-t=(T-{t}) union {e,f} is Hamiltonian. If F itself were non-Hamiltonian, the certified five-vertex structure in smallset01 would allow at most one non-Hamiltonian four-vertex deletion, contradicting that both F-e and F-f are non-Hamiltonian. Hence F is Hamiltonian. The deletion classification then follows: precisely the three labels of T are Hamiltonian deletion labels, while e and f are not.
