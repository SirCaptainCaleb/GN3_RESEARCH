# A two-set has at least two Hamiltonian three-of-four extensions across any four-set

## Statement

Let S and P be disjoint vertex sets in a boundary tournament with |S|=2 and |P|=4. Then for at least two vertices p in P, the five-set S union (P-{p}) is Hamiltonian.

## Body

Apply the certified four-of-six theorem to the six-set U=S union P. At least four of the six one-vertex deletions U-{v} are Hamiltonian. Only two deletion labels lie in S. Therefore at least two Hamiltonian five-vertex deletions must be obtained by deleting vertices p in P. These are exactly the sets S union (P-{p}).

No Hamiltonicity hypothesis on H[P] is used. The result is therefore a line-independent consequence of four-of-six for every disjoint 2+4 split of a six-set.
