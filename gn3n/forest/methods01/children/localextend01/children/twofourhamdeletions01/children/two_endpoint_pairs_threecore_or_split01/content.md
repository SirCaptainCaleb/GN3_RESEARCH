# Two disjoint endpoint pairs force a common three-core or a rigid 2+2 split

## Statement

Let W be a four-vertex set in a boundary tournament, and let E_1,E_2 be two vertex-disjoint two-sets disjoint from W. For i=1,2 define A_i={w in W:(W-{w}) union E_i is Hamiltonian}. Then |A_1|,|A_2|>=2. Hence either A_1 intersect A_2 is nonempty, so one common three-set W-{w} extends Hamiltonianly with both E_1 and E_2, or A_1,A_2 are disjoint and each has order two, giving the rigid partition W=A_1 disjoint-union A_2.

## Body

Apply twofourhamdeletions01 to the disjoint 2+4 split E_1,W to obtain |A_1|>=2, and independently to E_2,W to obtain |A_2|>=2. If the two sets intersect, any w in the intersection gives the common three-core W-{w}. If they are disjoint, two disjoint subsets of a four-set each having order at least two must both have order exactly two and partition W. No minimum-counterexample, path-cover, or extremality hypothesis is used.
