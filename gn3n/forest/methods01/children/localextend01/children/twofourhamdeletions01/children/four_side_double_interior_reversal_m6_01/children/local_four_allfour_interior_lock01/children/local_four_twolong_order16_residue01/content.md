# A locally minimal four-side with two long complements reduces to order sixteen

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament and let X|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component, with |X|=4 and |P|,|Q|>=6. Then either H contains relative-order disagreement among Hamilton paths on overlapping induced supports, or |P|=|Q|=6 and hence |V(H)|=16. In the latter case, writing E_P,E_Q for the displayed endpoint pairs and P°,Q° for the interiors, both six-sets U_P=V(X) union E_P and U_Q=V(X) union E_Q are Hamiltonian, and X|P|Q has Phi-neutral pairwise repartitions U_P|P°|Q and U_Q|Q°|P, each still lying in the same connected pairwise-repartition component.

## Body

Apply four_path_long_pair_escape01 to the pair X|P. Its strict-decrease outcome is impossible, since replacing only X|P by the supplied two-cover is exactly one pairwise repartition inside the connected component in which X|P|Q is Phi-minimal. Therefore either the theorem supplies relative-order disagreement, or its unique neutral outcome holds. In the latter case |P|=6, U_P=V(X) union E_P is Hamiltonian, P° has order four, and U_P|P° is a Phi-neutral repartition of X|P. Apply the same argument independently to X|Q. Unless relative-order disagreement occurs, necessarily |Q|=6 and U_Q|Q° is the corresponding neutral repartition. Thus absence of relative-order disagreement forces profile 4|6|6 and total order sixteen, together with the two displayed neutral migrations. Both migrations are single legal pairwise repartitions and therefore remain in the original connected component.
