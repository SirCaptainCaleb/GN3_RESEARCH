# The cross-swap clause layer alone is always locally satisfiable

## Statement

Fix displayed tight paths A,B and a label c, and take any family of pivot-cell clause pairs of the formal C1/C2 shape from compattogglecross33. Consider only the inherited path triples and the emitted clause literals, without imposing additional native pivot-premise constraints. Then all clauses admit a simultaneous assignment consistent with boundary antisymmetry: declare every c-free side literal tight, declare its reversal non-tight, and orient the remaining central reversal pairs arbitrarily but consistently. Hence no contradiction can arise from propositional propagation among the cross-swap clauses alone, even for arbitrarily many overlapping cells.

## Body

Every C1 clause contains two c-free side alternatives and one central c-containing alternative; every C2 clause likewise contains two c-free side alternatives and one central alternative. By the complete reversal-collision classification 78dfd207e09d, no side literal is the reversal of any other clause literal. A side literal is mixed between A and B, so it is also not the reversal of an inherited consecutive triple lying wholly in A or wholly in B. Therefore we may simultaneously declare every side literal tight and every one of its reversals non-tight while retaining all inherited A- and B-path triples tight. This already satisfies every C1 and C2 clause, independently of all central literals. The only reversal collisions among central literals are the classified diagonal pairs, so choose one orientation in each such pair arbitrarily; unpaired central triples may also be oriented arbitrarily. Complete every remaining reversal pair arbitrarily. The resulting local boundary-tournament data satisfy all emitted clauses. This is deliberately a fence on the abstract clause layer: it does not assert that an arbitrary prescribed family of pivot cells can simultaneously satisfy all extra second-type pivot premises.
