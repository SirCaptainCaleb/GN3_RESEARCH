# A split inherited path gives a reciprocal support crossing or a leave-and-return excursion

## Statement

Let R=(r_1,...,r_m) be a tight path and let T be any path cover of a vertex set containing V(R). Color V(R) as one inherited class and all other vertices as exterior. Suppose the vertices of R occur in at least two maximal R-blocks of T. Then either: (1) V(R) meets at least two components of T, and some ordinary edge r_i r_{i+1} of the displayed path R has endpoints in different T-components; or (2) all of V(R) lies in one T-component, and that component contains a contiguous subpath whose block sequence is R-block | nonempty exterior segment | R-block. In particular T has two distinct ordinary cross-class edges, one leaving R and one re-entering R.

## Body

If V(R) meets two different components of T, assign to each r_i the T-component containing it. Since the finite sequence r_1,...,r_m uses at least two component labels, some consecutive pair r_i,r_{i+1} receives different labels. Thus the ordinary inherited edge r_i r_{i+1} crosses the support partition of T, proving (1). Otherwise all vertices of R lie in one T-component P. Since they form at least two maximal R-blocks in P, choose two consecutive R-blocks along P. By maximality, the segment of P strictly between them is nonempty and contains no R-vertex. Hence P has the displayed block sequence R-block | exterior segment | R-block. The ordinary edge leaving the first R-block and the ordinary edge entering the second are distinct cross-class edges, proving (2).
