# Consecutive edge ranks cannot support a mandatory turn

## Statement

Let H be induced by a strict total order on the edges of a complete graph: (x,y,z) is tight exactly when xy<yz. Assume H has a spanning two-cover. If the incident edges ab and bc are consecutive in that total order, with ab<bc, then H has a spanning two-cover avoiding the consecutive ordered triple (a,b,c).

## Body

Conjecture, unproved. Consecutive ranks remove all intermediate edge values that could obstruct a local exchange. If proved, swapping consecutive incident edges would preserve the existence of a two-cover, giving a possible route through the space of edge orders. First attack: minimize the support carrying (a,b,c) and exploit the absence of an edge rank strictly between ab and bc. This addresses the edge-orderable subclass, not arbitrary comparison orientations.
