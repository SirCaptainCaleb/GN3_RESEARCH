# A cyclic companion prevents a mandatory ordered triple

## Statement

Suppose a boundary tournament H has a spanning two-cover A|B, where A contains the ordered triple T consecutively and the support of B admits a vertex-simple tight cycle. Then H has a spanning two-cover in which T does not occur consecutively in either path.

## Body

Conjecture, unproved. A cyclic companion provides freely movable cuts with certified wrap triples. Perhaps this freedom is enough to break a compulsory triple on the other path. First attack: choose the cut on the companion cycle to maximize the transferable prefix or suffix of A, then compare adjacent cuts. This is a structural restriction on potential mandatory-triple obstructions; it does not assume that every companion is cyclic.
