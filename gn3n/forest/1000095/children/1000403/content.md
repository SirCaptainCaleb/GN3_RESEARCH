# A linear potential allowance suffices to escape any three-cover

## Statement

There is an absolute constant C>0 such that, for every n-vertex boundary tournament H and every spanning three-cover F, a sequence of pairwise repartitions reaches a cover with at most two paths, and every intermediate three-cover F' satisfies Phi(F')<=Phi(F)+Cn. The final cover with at most two paths is exempt from this potential bound.

## Body

Conjecture, unproved. Allow a controlled temporary increase instead of demanding monotone descent. This would close the grand theorem once a spanning three-cover is available. First attack: find an endpoint transport operation whose potential increase is linear in n and whose completion either merges paths or strictly improves the original state. The constant and the number of moves are not prescribed. The obligation is global termination, not merely one excursion away from a local minimum.
