# The minimum-side endpoint cross triple forces quadratic descent or order-eleven disturbance

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-y=R|Q be a globally minimum-side deletion cover, with |R|=mu<=q=|Q| and z an endpoint of R. Write Q=(q_0,...,q_{q-1}) and suppose z is in the endpoint-barrier cross branch, so (q_{q-1},z,q_0) is tight. Then either the pairwise-repartition component of the singleton lift R|Q|{y} contains a spanning three-cover of strictly smaller quadratic potential, or mu=q=5 and |V(H)|=11, in which case the certified order-eleven omission-state theorem supplies a direct mixed-support edge or explicit order disagreement. Thus the endpoint cross triple is not a terminal minimum-side obstruction.

## Body

Every deletion-cover component in a minimum counterexample has order at least three. Since mu<=q and |V(H)|=mu+q+1>10, we have q>=5. Apply 1cde8fd4fb6a to the component Q of H-y. Repartition Q|{y} directly into W_5|M, leaving R fixed. This is one legal pairwise repartition and the resulting component orders are mu,5,q-4. Relative to mu,q,1 the potential change is 5^2+(q-4)^2-(q^2+1)=40-8q<0. If q=5, then mu<=5 and mu+q+1>10 force mu=5 and |V(H)|=11; 26219fd919c2 gives the stated mixed-support or order-disagreement output. Now suppose instead that (q_{q-1},y,q_0) is tight. Together with the assumed (q_{q-1},z,q_0), these are two parallel-middle tight triples with common ordered endpoints q_{q-1},q_0. The certified two-parallel-middle lemma in localextend01 therefore makes W_4={q_0,q_{q-1},y,z} Hamiltonian. Put A=R-z and I=(q_1,...,q_{q-2}). Starting from R|Q|{y}, first repartition R|{y} into A|{y,z}; both new components are tight. Then repartition Q|{y,z} into I|W_4. Hence A|I|W_4 lies in the same pairwise-repartition component. Thus every cross-orientation branch gives strict descent except the order-eleven equality case, which already produces standard disturbance.
