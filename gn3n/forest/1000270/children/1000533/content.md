# Dense five-window gluing over a fixed core

## Statement

Let C be a tight three-vertex path and U a large exterior set such that the graph joining u,v when H[C∪{u,v}] is Hamiltonian has density at least the certified Mantel complement bound. Conjecture that either a bounded collection of Hamilton orders on these five-sets can be synchronized to absorb two or more exterior vertices into one long path through C, or the incompatibility of those orders forces a second tight path on a linear-sized subset of U.

## Body

Mantel already supplies many Hamiltonian five-sets once a three-window repeats. The missing step is to exploit overlapping Hamilton orders, not merely count supports.
