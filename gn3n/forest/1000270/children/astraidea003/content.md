# Pairwise repartition connects three-covers to two-covers

## Statement

For every boundary tournament H and every spanning tight-path cover with at most three components, a finite sequence of legal moves reaches a cover with at most two components, where a move replaces any two components by at most two tight paths on their union.

## Body

Unproved global reconfiguration conjecture. Component orders and internal orders may change without a fixed radius bound. Every intermediate state remains a spanning cover with at most three components. Starting from a deletion two-cover and its omitted singleton would prove the grand theorem. Study a terminal strongly connected class of this finite move graph: if no merge is possible, every allowed pairwise repartition preserves three components. The hoped-for contradiction should use closure under ALL two-component repartitions, not recurrence of a single omitted vertex. A possible extremal choice is to maximize the sorted component-size vector over a reachable class, then exploit all equal-size rearrangements. Risk: separate reconfiguration components may trap some three-covers even if a two-cover exists. Fixed-radius exchange fences do not directly refute this unbounded move set.
