# A quadratic-minimal size gap yields a Hamiltonian kernel, cross triple, or endpoint connector

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let C=P|Q|R be a spanning three-path cover that minimizes Phi in a trapped Astra-003 move component. Write P=(x,...,y), |P|=a, |Q|=b, and assume a>=b+2. Then at least one of the following holds. (1) H has a proper Hamiltonian induced set W of order four or five whose complement has path-cover number exactly two. (2) There is an explicit tight cross triple (x,q,y) or (y,q,x) using both endpoints of P and one vertex q of Q. (3) There is a tight path (x,q_i,q_{i+1},...,q_j,y) or its endpoint-swapped analogue whose internal vertices form a nonempty interval of the displayed path Q. Thus the two endpoint noninsertion obstructions forced by the size gap always compress to a bounded Hamiltonian kernel or a direct endpoint-to-endpoint transport witness.

## Body

By 209aa2be10c0, both endpoints x,y of P are noninsertable into the displayed order of Q, so apply the certified failed-insertion normal form to each. If either endpoint has a first-type obstruction, the certified first-type theorem in transport01 gives either a Hamiltonian four-window W, whose complement has pc=2 by minimality, or the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-kernel X. In the cyclic case adjoining the other endpoint of P, which lies outside X, gives a Hamiltonian five-set W=X union {other endpoint}; again H-W has pc=2 by minimality. It remains that both endpoint obstructions are second-type. Apply the certified spacing trichotomy 36fccff06d48. If the two pivot gaps coincide, the corresponding four-set containing x,y and the two Q gap vertices is Hamiltonian, giving (1). If the gaps are adjacent, either the corresponding five-set is Hamiltonian, again giving (1), or the trichotomy gives an explicit cross triple through the shared Q vertex, giving (2). If the gaps are separated by at least one Q edge, the trichotomy gives a tight path from one exterior endpoint through the intervening displayed Q interval to the other endpoint, giving (3). Every Hamiltonian set obtained here is proper because H has order greater than ten, so its complement has pc exactly two rather than zero or one; otherwise it together with W would two-cover H.
