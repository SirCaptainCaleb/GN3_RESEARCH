# Every quadratic-minimal largest-second size gap forces a Hamiltonian kernel or doubled cross barrier

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let P|Q|R be a spanning three-path cover minimizing Phi in a trapped Astra-003 move component, with |P|=a>=b=|Q|>=c=|R|. If a>=b+2, then at least one of the following holds: (1) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set W of order four or five such that H-W is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number exactly two; or (2) for one endpoint x of P there are q in V(Q) and r in V(R) such that both (r,q,x) and (q,r,x) are tight. Thus every largest-second size gap reaches a bounded Hamiltonian-complement interface or an explicit doubled cross barrier joining the two smaller components through a large-component endpoint.

## Body

Let x be either displayed endpoint of P and let y be the other endpoint. Since a>=b+2 and b>=c, also a>=c+2. The certified size-gap obstruction theorem 209aa2be10c0 applied to P,Q and to P,R says that x is noninsertable into every position of the displayed orders of both Q and R. Apply the failed-insertion normal form to x on each smaller path. If either application has alternative 1, the certified generic first-type theorem 0425e03e2aa3 gives either a Hamiltonian four-set W or the universal cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-kernel X. In the cyclic case y lies outside X, so X union {y} is a Hamiltonian five-set W. Thus alternative (1) holds. It remains that both applications have alternative 2. Apply the repaired and certified two-pivot theorem 0b012e2e3816. Either it gives a mixed Hamiltonian four-set W, again yielding alternative (1), or it gives a doubled cross barrier: for the corresponding pivot-side vertices q in Q and r in R, both (r,q,x) and (q,r,x) are tight, which is alternative (2). In every Hamiltonian-kernel branch W is proper because H is a counterexample and hence non-Hamiltonian. Its complement cannot be Hamiltonian, or Hamilton paths on W and H-W would two-cover H. Minimum-counterexample calculus gives pc(H-W)<=2, hence pc(H-W)=2.
