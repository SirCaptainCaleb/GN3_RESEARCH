# Maximizing migration from the original five-bridge forces endpoint obstructions unless three bridge labels have already escaped

## Statement

Fix the support C_0 of the original canonical five-vertex bridge. Among all reachable minimum-Phi three-covers in the trapped component that have a unique five-vertex component X and the same component-order multiset as the canonical state, choose one maximizing mu=|X-C_0|. Let P be either other component, of order at least seven, and let e be an endpoint of a displayed tight order of P. Then X union {e} is non-Hamiltonian, so at least three vertices x in X satisfy (X-{x}) union {e} Hamiltonian. If mu<=2, at least one such x lies in C_0. For every such original-bridge label x, (P-{e}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian; otherwise exchanging x and e would be an equal-Phi repartition with the same size multiset but would increase mu by one. Consequently x is noninsertable into the inherited order P-e and yields a bounded insertion obstruction. Therefore either the extremal five-side already contains at least three vertices outside the original canonical bridge, or every endpoint of both long components has an original-bridge witness producing a bounded failed-insertion obstruction.

## Body

Because the chosen state is minimum-Phi and |P|>=7>|X|=5, endpoint transfer of e from P to X would replace sizes 5,|P| by 6,|P|-1 and strictly lower Phi. Hence X union {e} is non-Hamiltonian. Apply four-of-six to X union {e}. Deleting e leaves X Hamiltonian, so at least three deletions x in X make (X-{x}) union {e} Hamiltonian. If mu=|X-C_0|<=2, then at most two vertices of X lie outside C_0, so among those at least three good x there is at least one x in X intersect C_0. Fix such an x. If (P-{e}) union {x} were Hamiltonian, then a Hamilton path on (X-{x}) union {e} together with one on (P-{e}) union {x} would repartition X union P with component orders 5 and |P|. This is a legal equal-Phi move preserving the component-order multiset. Its new five-side replaces x in C_0 by e notin C_0, so its migration count is mu+1, contradicting maximality. Thus (P-{e}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian. Any successful insertion of x into the displayed inherited path P-e would itself Hamiltonize that support, so no insertion position succeeds. Insert01 Section 3 therefore gives a bounded local obstruction. Apply the argument independently to each endpoint of each long component. If mu>=3, record instead that at least three of the five bridge positions have already migrated outside C_0.
