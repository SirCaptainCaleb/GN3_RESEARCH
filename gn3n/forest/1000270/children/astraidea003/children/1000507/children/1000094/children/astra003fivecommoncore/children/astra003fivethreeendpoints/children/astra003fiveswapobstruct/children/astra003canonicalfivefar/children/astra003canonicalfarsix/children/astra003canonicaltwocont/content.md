# Two opposite-side continuation labels give controlled transport, disagreement, or obstruction

## Statement

Under astra003canonicalfarsix, after a successful first far-end exchange at a, there are at least two distinct d in D with U-{d} Hamiltonian, where U=D union {a,b}. For each such d, exactly one of the following useful outcomes can be selected: (1) d inserts into the inherited tight order of B-b, giving an explicit equal-Phi exchange that preserves the inherited order of all old B-vertices; (2) (B-b) union {d} is Hamiltonian but d is not insertable into that inherited order, in which case every Hamilton order on this support has relative-order disagreement with B-b and pathcalc01 yields a reversed common edge, a reversing tight triple, or a vertex-simple tight cycle; or (3) (B-b) union {d} is non-Hamiltonian, so insert01 gives a bounded failed-insertion obstruction. Thus a successful first far exchange forces at least two distinct continuation labels on the opposite side, each carrying controlled transport or explicit disturbance.

## Body

By astra003canonicalfarsix, choose distinct d_1,d_2 in D such that U-{d_i} is Hamiltonian. Fix one such d and put R=B-b with its inherited tight-path order. If inserting d into some position of R gives a tight path, then that path is Hamiltonian on V(R) union {d}. Together with a Hamilton path on U-{d}, these two paths partition the pair union of the post-first-exchange five-side D union {a} and B, and their orders are 5 and |B|. Hence they give an equal-Phi exchange whose long-side order visibly retains the old B-order with only d inserted and b removed; this is outcome (1). Suppose no insertion position works. If H[V(R) union {d}] is non-Hamiltonian, insert01 Section 3 gives outcome (3). If it is Hamiltonian, let S be any Hamilton tight path on that support. If the common vertices V(R) occurred in S in the same relative order as in R, then S would be obtained by inserting d into the displayed order of R, contrary to the assumption. Therefore S and R have relative-order disagreement, and pathcalc01 Section 4 gives outcome (2). Apply this independently to the two distinct good labels supplied by astra003canonicalfarsix.
