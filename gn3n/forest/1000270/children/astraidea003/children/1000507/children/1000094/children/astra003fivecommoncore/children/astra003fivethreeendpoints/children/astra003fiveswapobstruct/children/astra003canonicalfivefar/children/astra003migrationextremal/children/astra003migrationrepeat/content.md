# Low migration forces one original bridge label to obstruct at least two outer endpoints

## Statement

In the low-migration branch mu=|X-C_0|<=2 of astra003migrationextremal, one vertex x in X intersect C_0 can be chosen as a failed-insertion obstruction witness for at least two of the four endpoints of the two long components. More quantitatively, if mu=0 some original bridge label witnesses at least three endpoints; if mu=1 or 2, some original bridge label witnesses at least two endpoints. If the two witnessed endpoints belong to the same long path, then x is noninsertable into every position of that full displayed path. Otherwise x carries synchronized bounded failed-insertion obstructions on one endpoint truncation of each of the two long paths.

## Body

Fix one endpoint e of a long component. In astra003migrationextremal the six-set X union {e} is non-Hamiltonian and at least three labels y in X satisfy (X-{y}) union {e} Hamiltonian. At most mu of those labels can lie outside C_0, so at least 3-mu original bridge labels in X intersect C_0 are valid choices. For every such original label y, maximality of mu forbids the reciprocal equal-Phi exchange, hence y is noninsertable into the corresponding inherited endpoint truncation and gives a bounded obstruction. There are four outer endpoints, so the total number of endpoint--original-label incidences is at least 4(3-mu), distributed among exactly 5-mu original labels in X. For mu=0 this is 12 incidences on 5 labels, forcing one label to occur at least three times. For mu=1 it is 8 incidences on 4 labels, forcing multiplicity at least two. For mu=2 it is 4 incidences on 3 labels, again forcing multiplicity at least two. Choose such a repeated label x. If its two incidences are the opposite endpoints e_L,e_R of one displayed path P, then both (P-e_L) union {x} and (P-e_R) union {x} are non-Hamiltonian. Any insertion of x into P survives after deleting at least one of the two endpoints and would Hamiltonize the corresponding truncation, impossible. Thus x is noninsertable everywhere in P. If the repeated incidences lie on different long components, retain the two synchronized bounded obstruction windows on those endpoint truncations.
