# Five-side synchronization yields a full-path insertion obstruction or order disagreement

## Statement

Let X|P|Q be a quadratic-potential-minimal spanning three-path cover in a connected pairwise-repartition component containing no two-cover, with |X|=5 and |P|,|Q|>=7. Choose x in V(X) and at least three of the four endpoints of P,Q as in astra003fivethreeendpoints, so D=V(X)-{x} extends Hamiltonianly with each chosen endpoint. Then either one chosen endpoint gives an equal-potential support exchange as in astra003fiveswapobstruct, or for one of P,Q whose two endpoints are both chosen, x is noninsertable at every position of the displayed path order. In the latter case insert01 gives a bounded local obstruction on that entire long path. Moreover, if the induced tournament on that path support together with x is Hamiltonian, every Hamilton order on it has relative-order disagreement with the displayed path, and pathcalc01 Section 4 yields a reversed common edge, a reversing tight triple, or a vertex-simple tight cycle.

## Body

Let E be the set of at least three synchronized endpoints supplied by astra003fivethreeendpoints, and put D=V(X)-{x}. For each e in E, let R_e be the member of {P,Q} having endpoint e. By astra003fiveswapobstruct, if H[(V(R_e)-{e}) union {x}] is Hamiltonian, then D union {e} together with that Hamilton path gives an exact two-path repartition of X union R_e with the same component orders 5 and |R_e|. Thus an equal-Phi support exchange exists. Assume no such exchange exists. Then for every e in E, H[(V(R_e)-{e}) union {x}] is non-Hamiltonian. Since E contains at least three of the four endpoints of P,Q, one path R=(r_1,...,r_m) has both r_1,r_m in E. We claim x cannot be inserted anywhere into the displayed order of R. Indeed, if x were inserted before r_1, deleting the final vertex r_m from the resulting tight path would leave a Hamilton tight path on (V(R)-{r_m}) union {x}; if x were inserted after r_m, deleting r_1 would leave a Hamilton tight path on (V(R)-{r_1}) union {x}; and if x were inserted at any interior position, deleting either displayed endpoint would give one of these two Hamilton paths. Each possibility contradicts the corresponding non-Hamiltonicity above. Hence every insertion position fails, so insert01 Section 3 supplies a bounded local obstruction involving x and at most four consecutive vertices of R. Finally suppose H[V(R) union {x}] is Hamiltonian and let S be any Hamilton tight path on that support. If the common vertices V(R) occurred in S in the same relative order as in the displayed R, then S would be obtained by inserting x into the displayed order of R, contrary to the preceding paragraph. Therefore S and R disagree in relative order on their common vertices. Pathcalc01 Section 4 gives the stated reversed-edge, reversing-triple, or tight-cycle conclusion.
