# The exact-two-good-core branch also reduces to bounded disturbance or a quadratic-neutral 4-to-5 rotation

## Statement

Continue the post-first-exchange setting of astra003canonicalfarsix, with current pair (D union {a})|B, write b for the far endpoint of B, R=B-b, and |B|=m>=7. Assume exactly two core labels d,e are good deletions, and use the three-pattern normal form astra003sixfourpattern. Then, unless a relative-order disagreement or bounded failed-insertion obstruction is already present, one of the following occurs: (A) all successful insertion positions of d,e into R lie in at most three consecutive gaps; or (B) there is a legal 4|(m+1) repartition of (D union {a})|B. In case (B), foursidedescentobstruction either gives two synchronized bounded insertion obstructions on one common inherited middle path or returns in one move to a 5|m state with quadratic potential exactly equal to the original minimum value.

## Body

Let the two good core labels be d,e. By astra003canonicaltwocont, for each good label, unless relative-order disagreement or a bounded failed-insertion obstruction already occurs, that label inserts into the inherited path R=B-b. We distinguish the three alternatives of astra003sixfourpattern. Case (i): K=U-{d,e} is Hamiltonian. Apply astra003twoinsertlocal to d,e on R. Either their successful insertion positions lie in three consecutive gaps, giving (A), or they insert simultaneously, producing a tight path R^+ on R union {d,e}. Then K|R^+ is a 4|(m+1) repartition, giving (B). Case (ii): D=U-{a,b} is Hamiltonian. If B union {a} is non-Hamiltonian, then a cannot be inserted anywhere into the displayed tight path B, so insert01 gives a bounded obstruction. If B union {a} is Hamiltonian, then D | (B union {a}) is a legal 4|(m+1) repartition, giving (B). Case (iii): after relabelling d,e, K=(D-{d}) union {b}=U-{a,d} is Hamiltonian. In the no-disturbance branch d inserts into R; fix a tight displayed insertion path R_d on R union {d}. If (R union {d,a}) is non-Hamiltonian, then a is noninsertable into the displayed path R_d and insert01 gives a bounded obstruction. If it is Hamiltonian, then K | (R union {d,a}) is a legal 4|(m+1) repartition, giving (B). Finally, in every occurrence of (B), the pairwise quadratic contribution rises from 5^2+m^2 to 4^2+(m+1)^2, an increase 2m-8. Applying foursidedescentobstruction to the four-side and the path of order m+1 either gives its synchronized-obstruction branch or lowers the quadratic potential by 2(m+1)-10=2m-8, returning exactly to the minimum-Phi 5|m plateau.
