# The five-side endpoint-good intersection is either doubled or rigid 2+1+2

## Statement

Let X|P|Q be a quadratic-potential-minimal trapped three-cover with |X|=5 and P=(p_1,...,p_m), m>=7. Define I_L={x in X : (X-{x}) union {p_1} is Hamiltonian} and I_R={x in X : (X-{x}) union {p_m} is Hamiltonian}. Then |I_L|,|I_R|>=3. Assume there is no equal-Phi support exchange between X and P obtained from either endpoint replacement. Every x in I_L intersect I_R is noninsertable into the full displayed path P. Hence either |I_L intersect I_R|>=2, or |I_L intersect I_R|=1 and necessarily |I_L|=|I_R|=3 and I_L union I_R=X, giving a rigid 2+1+2 split.

## Body

By endpoint-transfer minimality both six-sets X union {p_1} and X union {p_m} are non-Hamiltonian. Four-of-six therefore gives |I_L|,|I_R|>=3. Fix x in I_L intersect I_R. If (P-{p_1}) union {x} were Hamiltonian then (X-{x}) union {p_1} together with it would give an equal-Phi 5|m repartition, contrary to hypothesis; similarly (P-{p_m}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian. Any insertion of x into the displayed order of P would Hamiltonize one of these endpoint truncations after deleting the opposite endpoint, so x is noninsertable into all positions of P. Since two subsets of a five-set of size at least three intersect, the intersection is nonempty. If it has size at least two we get two globally locked labels. If its size is one, then |I_L|+|I_R|=|I_L union I_R|+1<=6, while each set has size at least three, so both have size three and their union is X. Thus the two exclusive parts each have size two and are exchanged by complement: X-I_L=I_R-(I_L intersect I_R) and X-I_R=I_L-(I_L intersect I_R).
