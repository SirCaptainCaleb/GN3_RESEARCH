# Low migration yields an exact-two-cover subsystem or one bridge label locked against both long paths

## Statement

Assume the low-migration branch mu=|X-C_0|<=2 of astra003migrationextremal, and let P,Q be the two long components. Let R={r in X:H[X-{r}] is Hamiltonian}. Then |R|>=2. Exactly one of the following useful alternatives holds. (A) For some outer endpoint e of P or Q there is x in R such that (X-{x}) union {e} is Hamiltonian. Then A=X-{x} is a Hamiltonian four-set and K=H-A=P union Q union {x} is non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number exactly two. (B) No endpoint-good label is Hamilton-removable. Then |R|=2, N=X-R has order three, and for every one of the four outer endpoints e the full endpoint-good set is exactly N. Since mu<=2, N contains an original bridge label x in C_0. For this x, secondary maximality forbids the reciprocal equal-Phi exchange at every endpoint, so x is noninsertable into every position of both displayed long paths P and Q. Thus the no-removable-intersection branch produces one original bridge label globally locked against both long components.

## Body

Because X is Hamiltonian on five vertices, deleting either endpoint of any Hamilton order leaves a Hamilton four-path; hence |R|>=2. For each outer endpoint e, endpoint-transfer minimality makes X union {e} non-Hamiltonian, and four-of-six gives an endpoint-good set I_e={x in X:H[(X-{x}) union {e}] Hamiltonian} of size at least three. If I_e meets R for some e, choose x in I_e intersect R. Then A=X-{x} is Hamiltonian by x in R. Its complement in the spanning cover is K=P union Q union {x}. If K were Hamiltonian, Hamilton paths on A and K would two-cover H, impossible; since K is a proper induced subtournament of the minimum counterexample, minimality gives pc(K)<=2. Therefore K is non-Hamiltonian with pc(K)=2, proving (A). Now assume I_e intersect R is empty for all four endpoints. Since |I_e|>=3 and I_e subseteq X-R, we have |X-R|>=3. Together with |R|>=2 and |X|=5 this forces |R|=2, |N|=3, and I_e=N for every endpoint e. Because mu=|X-C_0|<=2, at least one vertex x of the three-set N lies in C_0. Fix it. Then x belongs to I_e for every outer endpoint e. If for an endpoint e of, say, P the support (P-{e}) union {x} were Hamiltonian, it together with the Hamiltonian five-set (X-{x}) union {e} would give an equal-Phi repartition replacing the original-bridge label x by the outer label e on the five-side. Since e is outside C_0 and x lies in C_0, this raises mu by one, contradicting the secondary extremal choice. Hence both endpoint truncations of P enlarged by x are non-Hamiltonian, and likewise for Q. Any insertion of x into the full displayed order of P survives after deleting at least one of its two endpoints and would Hamiltonize the corresponding truncation, contradiction. Thus x is globally noninsertable into P; the same argument gives global noninsertability into Q.
