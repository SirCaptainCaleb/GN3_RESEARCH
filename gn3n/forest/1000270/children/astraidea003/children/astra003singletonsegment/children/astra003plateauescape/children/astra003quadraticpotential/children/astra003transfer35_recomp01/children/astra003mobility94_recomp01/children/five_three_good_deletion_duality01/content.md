# No balanced four-four split forces dual deletion geometry in a mobile three-five pair

## Statement

Let W=T disjoint-union R be an eight-vertex set in a boundary tournament, with |T|=3, |R|=5, and H[R] Hamiltonian. Assume W has no partition into two Hamiltonian four-sets. Put D={r in R:H[R-{r}] is Hamiltonian}. Then for every distinct r,s in D and every u in T, the four-set (R-{r,s}) union {u} is non-Hamiltonian. Suppose in addition that for every u in T there are at least three labels r in R for which (R-{r}) union {u} is Hamiltonian. Then 2<=|D|<=4. If |D|=4 and t is the unique label of R-D, then H[T union {t}] is Hamiltonian, H[R-{t}] is non-Hamiltonian, while H[T union {r}] is non-Hamiltonian and H[R-{r}] is Hamiltonian for every r in D. More precisely, for every u in T, relative to the fixed pair T-{u}, the four labels D lie in one fixed-pair orientation class and u,t lie in the opposite class. If |D|=3, then at least one label t in R-D satisfies that T union {t} is Hamiltonian. Consequently these conclusions apply to every 3|5 pair in a trapped order-thirteen 3|5|5 Astra-003 component, by astra003mobility94_recomp01.

## Body

Write T={u} union A with |A|=2. First fix distinct r,s in D. Since R-r is Hamiltonian and W has no Hamiltonian 4|4 partition, its complementary four-set T+r=A+u+r is non-Hamiltonian. Likewise A+u+s is non-Hamiltonian. Apply the fixed-pair orientation-class theorem bd3c8d17ca06 to the fixed pair A. Non-Hamiltonicity of A+u+r forces u and r into opposite A-orientation classes, and non-Hamiltonicity of A+u+s forces u and s into opposite classes. Hence r and s lie in the same class, so A+r+s is Hamiltonian. Its complementary four-set is {u} union (R-{r,s}); absence of a Hamiltonian 4|4 partition therefore forces that complement to be non-Hamiltonian. This proves the pair obstruction. The lower bound |D|>=2 is the standard five-vertex Hamiltonian deletion bound from smallset01.

Now assume the mobility hypothesis: for every u in T, let G_u={r in R:(R-{r}) union {u} is Hamiltonian}, so |G_u|>=3. If |D|=5, fix u and any r in G_u. The Hamiltonian five-set F=(R-{r}) union {u} has F-u=R-r Hamiltonian, while for every s in D-{r} the pair obstruction makes F-s=(R-{r,s}) union {u} non-Hamiltonian. Thus F has four non-Hamiltonian vertex deletions, contradicting smallset01, which allows at most three for a Hamiltonian five-set. Hence |D|<=4.

Suppose |D|=4 and write R-D={t}. Fix u in T. Since |G_u|>=3, choose r in G_u intersect D. Put F=(R-{r}) union {u}. Then F is Hamiltonian; F-u=R-r is Hamiltonian; and for each of the three labels s in D-{r}, F-s is non-Hamiltonian by the pair obstruction. Therefore the remaining deletion F-t=(R-{r,t}) union {u} must be Hamiltonian, again because a Hamiltonian five-set has at most three non-Hamiltonian four-deletions. Relative to A=T-{u}, the set A+u+r=T+r is non-Hamiltonian, so u and r are in opposite A-orientation classes. If r and t were in the same class, A+r+t would be Hamiltonian; together with the Hamiltonian complementary four-set (R-{r,t})+u this would form a Hamiltonian 4|4 partition of W. Hence r and t are in opposite classes. Thus u and t lie in the same class, and bd3c8d17ca06 gives A+u+t=T+t Hamiltonian. Since t is the unique label outside D, R-t is non-Hamiltonian; while by definition R-r is Hamiltonian for every r in D, and therefore T+r is non-Hamiltonian by the no-4|4 hypothesis. Repeating the class argument for each u gives the asserted 4-versus-2 orientation split relative to every pair T-{u}.

Finally suppose |D|=3. Fix u and choose r in G_u intersect D, which is possible because both sets have size at least three in the five-set R. The two labels of D-{r} give two non-Hamiltonian deletions of F=(R-r)+u by the pair obstruction. If both labels t in R-D also gave non-Hamiltonian deletions F-t, then F would have four non-Hamiltonian four-deletions, impossible. Hence for some t in R-D, F-t=(R-{r,t})+u is Hamiltonian. The same fixed-pair argument as above shows r and t must lie in opposite A-orientation classes, so u and t lie in the same class and T+t is Hamiltonian. In a trapped order-thirteen 3|5|5 state, astra003mobility94_recomp01 supplies |G_u|>=3 for every u, giving the stated specialization.
