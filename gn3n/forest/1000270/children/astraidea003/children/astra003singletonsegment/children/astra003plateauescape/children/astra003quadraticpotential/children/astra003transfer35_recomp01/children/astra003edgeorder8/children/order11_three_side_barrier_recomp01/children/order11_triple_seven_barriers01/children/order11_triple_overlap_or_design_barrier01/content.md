# Order-eleven triples force blocked overlap or a blocked 14-block design

## Statement

In a hypothetical order-eleven minimum counterexample, for every three-set X with U=V(H)-X, either U has two Hamiltonian four-sets meeting in three vertices and both unions with X are non-Hamiltonian, or U has fourteen complement-paired Hamiltonian four-sets covering every triple of U exactly once and every union of X with one of those four-sets is non-Hamiltonian.

## Body

Use the seven balanced 4|4 partitions of U from order11_triple_seven_barriers01. Both sides of every one of those partitions are blocked by X. Apply balanced8_adjacent_or_sqs01 to their fourteen Hamiltonian sides. Its overlap alternative gives two blocked Hamiltonian four-sets meeting in three vertices. Its design alternative gives fourteen complement-paired Hamiltonian four-sets covering every triple of U exactly once. Since every block is one side of a selected balanced partition, every X-plus-block seven-set is non-Hamiltonian.
