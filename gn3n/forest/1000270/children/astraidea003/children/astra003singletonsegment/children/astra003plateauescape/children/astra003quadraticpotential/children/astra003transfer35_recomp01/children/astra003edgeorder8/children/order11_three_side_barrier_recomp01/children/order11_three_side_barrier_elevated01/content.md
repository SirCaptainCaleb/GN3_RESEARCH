# Order-eleven three-side barrier becomes blocked overlap or star

## Statement

If a trapped Phi-minimal three-cover in a minimum counterexample has a three-vertex side, then the counterexample has order eleven and profile 4|4|3. For every three-set X, its eight-vertex complement has either two adjacent Hamiltonian four-sets both blocked by X, or one Hamiltonian four-set adjacent to four distinct Hamiltonian four-sets all blocked by X.

## Body

Use order11_three_side_barrier_recomp01 for the order-eleven 4|4|3 reduction. Then apply order11_triple_blocked_overlap_or_star01 to each three-set X.
