# Order-eleven design multiplicity, swaps, and seven-label normal form

## Statement

Let H be a hypothetical order-eleven minimum counterexample, let X be a three-set, U=V(H)-X, and assume the design alternative of order11_design_boolean_recomp01. Let E be the extendable Hamiltonian four-centers, m=|E|, and A the number of pairs in E meeting in three vertices. Then m>=14 and A>=L(m), where L(14)=7, L(m)=4m-56 for 15<=m<=27, and L(m)=8m-168 for 28<=m<=35. Each adjacent pair yields a common Hamiltonian three-side R, a complementary non-Hamiltonian six-set Y, and a Hamiltonian five-side S whose Hamiltonian deletion labels are exactly R; its good-deletion graph J has minimum degree at least one, and every edge of J yields two balanced 5|5 covers of one deletion differing by a single support swap. The adjacent pairs yield either at least L(m) distinct such three-sides or one three-side carrying a three-label Hamiltonian five-shell. If the universal Astra omission set O has order seven, then in every such Y one has D=Y∩O of order four, where D is the good-deletion set; either J has adjacent edges, giving a reciprocal-swap fork, or J is a perfect matching and H[D] is the non-Hamiltonian matching-block four-set. In that perfect-matching case, writing Y-D={t_1,t_2}, each D∪{t_i} is a non-Hamiltonian edge-orderable five-set whose unique non-Hamiltonian vertex deletion is t_i.
