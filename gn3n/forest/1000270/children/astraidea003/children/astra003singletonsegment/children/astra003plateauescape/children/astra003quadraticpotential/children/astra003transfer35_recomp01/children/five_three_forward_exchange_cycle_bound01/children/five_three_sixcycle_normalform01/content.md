# Every neutral three-five exchange six-cycle has a two-label three-pair normal form

## Statement

In the setting of five_three_forward_exchange_cycle_bound01, suppose the directed exchange graph contains a directed cycle T_0->T_1->...->T_5->T_0 of length six. Then, after cyclic relabelling of the cycle, there are distinct vertices a,d and three pairwise disjoint two-sets A_0,A_1,A_2 partitioning W-{a,d} such that T_0={a} union A_0, T_1={d} union A_1, T_2={a} union A_2, T_3={d} union A_0, T_4={a} union A_1, and T_5={d} union A_2. The six transfer pairs E_i=W-(T_i union T_{i+1}) are A_2,A_0,A_1,A_2,A_0,A_1 in order. Moreover, writing F_i=W-T_i and D(F_i) for its Hamiltonian-deletion labels, one has E_i subseteq D(F_i) subseteq T_{i-1} (indices mod 6). Consequently, in each of the six Hamiltonian five-sets F_i, the current transfer pair E_i consists of good deletion labels while the previous transfer pair E_{i-1} consists of bad deletion labels. Equivalently, for both distinguished labels a and d the same cyclic preference A_0 over A_2, A_2 over A_1, A_1 over A_0 governs which of the two pair-blocks supplies Hamiltonian four-deletions.

## Body

For every exchange T_i->T_{i+1}, put E_i=W-(T_i union T_{i+1}); then |E_i|=2 and W is the disjoint union T_i sqcup E_i sqcup T_{i+1}. The proof of five_three_forward_exchange_cycle_bound01 gives D(F_{i+1}) subseteq T_i, where F_{i+1}=W-T_{i+1}, and the next exchange chooses E_{i+1} subseteq D(F_{i+1}). Hence E_{i+1} is a two-subset of T_i. If a_i is the unique vertex of T_i-E_{i+1}, then T_{i+2}=W-(T_{i+1} union E_{i+1})=E_i union {a_i}. In particular adjacent triples are disjoint, two-step triples meet in one vertex, and three-step triples meet in two vertices.

Choose labels T_0={a,b,c}, T_1={d,e,f}, and E_0={g,h}. Since E_1 is a two-subset of T_0, relabel b,c so E_1={b,c}; then T_2={a,g,h}. Since E_2 is a two-subset of T_1, relabel e,f so E_2={e,f}; then T_3={b,c,d}. Now T_4 is adjacent to T_3, meets T_0 in one vertex (because T_4->T_5->T_0 is a two-step walk), and meets T_1 in two vertices (because T_1->T_2->T_3->T_4 is a three-step walk). The only three-set with those properties is T_4={a,e,f}. Similarly T_5 is adjacent to both T_4 and T_0, and must therefore be {d,g,h}. Put A_0={b,c}, A_1={e,f}, A_2={g,h}; this gives the displayed six triples. Taking complements of successive adjacent triples gives E_0=A_2,E_1=A_0,E_2=A_1,E_3=A_2,E_4=A_0,E_5=A_1.

Finally F_i=W-T_i=T_{i-1} union E_{i-1}. The exchange theorem gives D(F_i) subseteq T_{i-1}, while the outgoing exchange T_i->T_{i+1} chooses E_i subseteq D(F_i). Therefore every label of E_i is a good deletion of F_i, whereas no label of E_{i-1} can be a good deletion because E_{i-1} is disjoint from T_{i-1}. Reading the six cases gives the cyclic pair preference A_0 over A_2, A_2 over A_1, A_1 over A_0, identically for the a-five-sets and the d-five-sets. The remaining distinguished label in each F_i may or may not be a good deletion.
