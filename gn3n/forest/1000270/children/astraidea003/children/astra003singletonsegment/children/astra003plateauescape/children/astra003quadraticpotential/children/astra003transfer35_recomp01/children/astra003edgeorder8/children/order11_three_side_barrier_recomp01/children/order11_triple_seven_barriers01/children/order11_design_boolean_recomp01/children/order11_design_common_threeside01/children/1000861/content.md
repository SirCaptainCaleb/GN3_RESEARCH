# The order-eleven design branch forces a full path-cover-two Boolean cube on the common three-side

## Statement

Let H be a hypothetical order-eleven minimum counterexample and suppose the design branch yields X,T,R,a,b,Y,S as in order11_design_common_threeside01, with Y=X union T and S=R union {a,b}. Then for every subset Q of R, the induced subtournament H[Y union Q] is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Thus the eight states indexed by Q subseteq R form a full three-label path-cover-two Boolean cube. Consequently, for two-covers of the top state Y union R, at least one of the following holds: (1) some two-cover has two labels of R simultaneously as displayed endpoints; (2) two two-covers have different unordered support partitions; (3) two Hamilton paths on one common component support exhibit relative-order disagreement; (4) at least two labels of R are internal in every two-cover.

## Body

From order11_design_common_threeside01, R+a and R+b are both non-Hamiltonian, while R is a three-set. Apply two_bad_threecore_opposite_pair_shell01 with three-core R and exterior labels a,b. For every r in R, the four-set (R-{r}) union {a,b}=S-r is Hamiltonian. We now check every Q subseteq R. If Q is empty, the complement of Y is S, which is Hamiltonian. If |Q|=1, say Q={r}, the complement is S-r, Hamiltonian by the preceding paragraph. If |Q|=2, the complement S-Q has order three and is Hamiltonian. If Q=R, the complement is {a,b}, a two-vertex path. Hence in every case the complement of Y union Q is Hamiltonian. Were Y union Q Hamiltonian as well, the two complementary Hamiltonian supports would form a spanning two-cover of H, impossible. Since Y union Q is always proper, minimum-counterexample calculus gives path-cover number exactly two.

For the final top-state structure, apply pc2_square_topcover_normal01 to the three two-label faces of this Boolean cube. If any face yields simultaneous endpoints, support-partition disagreement, or relative-order disagreement, we obtain (1), (2), or (3). Otherwise each pair of labels in R contains a label internal in every two-cover of the top state. The permanently internal labels therefore form a vertex cover of K_3 and have cardinality at least two, giving (4).
