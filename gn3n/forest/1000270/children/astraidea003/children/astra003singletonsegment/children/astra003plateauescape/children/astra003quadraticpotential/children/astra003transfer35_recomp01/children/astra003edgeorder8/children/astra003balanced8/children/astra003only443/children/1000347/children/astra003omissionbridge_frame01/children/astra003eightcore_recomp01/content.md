# Targeted singleton attempts spread broadly, and an eight-label shell forces a mutual three-by-three bad-extension core

## Statement

Let X|P|Q be a 3|4|4 state and t in X. Targeting t through P yields at least two candidate p in P for a legal 5|2 move. For each candidate, either a second move produces singleton t, or at least three vertices of Q become reachable singletons. Consequently, if exactly eight singleton labels are reachable and t is excluded, there is a reachable state X={t,s,s_prime}|P|Q in which the other excluded labels u,v lie one on each four-side, P+t and Q+t are Hamiltonian, and after relabelling s,s_prime, P+s and Q+s are Hamiltonian while P+s_prime and Q+s_prime are non-Hamiltonian. Writing P={u,a,b,c} and Q={v,d,e,f}, all three Q+a,Q+b,Q+c and all three P+d,P+e,P+f are non-Hamiltonian.

## Body

Four-of-six on (X-t) union P supplies at least two candidate deletions p in P for which (X-t) union (P-p) is Hamiltonian, giving a legal 5|2 repartition with residual pair {t,p}. If Q+p is Hamiltonian, the next repartition makes t a singleton. Otherwise four-of-six on Q union {p,t} gives at least three q in Q for which (Q-q) union {p,t} is Hamiltonian, hence at least three reachable Q-singletons. Now assume exactly eight reachable singleton labels, with excluded set of size three. Choose a reachable 3|4|4 state containing excluded t and two reachable labels on its three-side. Applying the preceding spread on both four-sides forces the other two excluded labels to split one per side. Since those excluded labels cannot become singletons, the same six-set deletion count forces P+t and Q+t Hamiltonian. Applying four-of-six to P union {s,s_prime} and Q union {s,s_prime}, while using exclusion of t to rule out complementary good choices, shows one common label s Hamiltonizes both sides and the other s_prime Hamiltonizes neither; the two six-sets have exact bad-deletion sets {u,s} and {v,s}. Starting from the resulting omission states, every reachable-label deletion on one side can be exposed as a singleton while the opposite five-side remains fixed. If the opposite four-side plus that label were Hamiltonian, one further move would expose t, contradiction. Thus each of a,b,c is a bad extension of Q and each of d,e,f is a bad extension of P.
