# Every pair outside a longest path falls into the universal paired-noninsertion menu

## Statement

Let P be a globally longest tight path in a finite boundary tournament H, and let x,y be distinct vertices outside V(P). Then x and y are both noninsertable into the displayed order of P, so the certified paired-noninsertion theorem a8c9883902b1 applies. Hence every unordered pair {x,y} outside P yields at least one of five scale-independent outcomes: a Hamiltonian four-set, the exceptional cyclic four-kernel with universal one-vertex extension, a Hamiltonian five-set, a tight cross triple through one vertex of P, or a direct tight interval connector from x to y through a subpath of P.

## Body

An insertion of x anywhere in the displayed order of P would create a tight path of order |P|+1, contradicting global maximality; likewise for y. Therefore a8c9883902b1 applies to every pair of outside vertices. The original interval-slot proposal can thus be replaced, at first order, by a complete graph on the outside vertices whose edges carry a finite structural outcome type. This keeps the route genuinely global while avoiding the false local no-ABAB premise.
