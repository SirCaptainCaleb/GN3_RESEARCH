# A common donor endpoint in the unique-large profile forces a standard transport disturbance

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let A|B|C be a Phi-minimal trapped three-cover with |A|=r+1 and |B|=|C|=r, r>=3. Suppose one endpoint x of A has Hamiltonian enlargements H[V(B) union {x}] and H[V(C) union {x}]. Put A0=A-x with its inherited tight order. Then every two-cover T of H-x contains at least two ordinary path edges whose endpoints lie in different members of V(A0)|V(B)|V(C). Consequently T exposes crossing multiplicity, or in the two-crossing equality case an inherited-core crossing or a leave-and-return excursion. Thus the common-donor subcase of the unique-large profile reduces to the standard endpoint-transport / defect-compression inputs, with no assumption on the insertion positions of x in B or C.

## Body

The paths A0,B,C are disjoint tight paths of the same order r. Moreover each of the three supports V(A0),V(B),V(C) becomes Hamiltonian after adjoining x: V(A0) union {x}=V(A) is Hamiltonian by the displayed path A, while the other two Hamiltonian enlargements are hypotheses. Let T be any two-cover of H-x, and let t be the number of ordinary T-edges joining distinct classes of the partition V(A0)|V(B)|V(C). The transition-count identity gives t>=1. Suppose t=1. Cutting the unique crossing edge leaves three monochromatic path blocks, one in each core. Since T has two path components, one of these three core blocks is itself an entire component of T; call its support D. The equality t=1 also forces that block to contain all vertices of its core. But H[D union {x}] is Hamiltonian. Replacing this entire component of T by a Hamilton path on D union {x}, while leaving the other component of T unchanged, gives a spanning two-cover of H, contradiction. Hence t>=2 for every T. If t>=3, crossing multiplicity is explicit. If t=2, cutting the two crossing edges produces four monochromatic blocks, so the three core block counts are (2,1,1). For the split core, either its two blocks lie in different T-components, in which case connectivity of the displayed core path gives an inherited core edge with endpoints in different T-components, or they lie in the same T-component separated by a nonempty block from another core, giving a leave-and-return excursion. These are standard transport-disturbance inputs.
