# The remaining unique-large residue is a crossed pair of one-success one-failure insertion windows

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let A=(a_0,...,a_r)|B|C be a Phi-minimal trapped three-cover with |A|=r+1 and |B|=|C|=r, r>=3. Assume none of the canonical terminal-frontier witnesses occurs and assume the common-donor branch of b5150f71d277 does not occur. Then, after possibly interchanging B,C and reversing A, H[V(B) union {a_0}] and H[V(C) union {a_r}] are Hamiltonian, while H[V(B) union {a_r}] and H[V(C) union {a_0}] are non-Hamiltonian. The opposite labels a_r on B and a_0 on C are noninsertable and each satisfies one of the bounded failed-insertion normal forms of insert01. Moreover H-V(a_1,...,a_{r-1}) has the balanced two-cover (B+a_0)|(C+a_r).
