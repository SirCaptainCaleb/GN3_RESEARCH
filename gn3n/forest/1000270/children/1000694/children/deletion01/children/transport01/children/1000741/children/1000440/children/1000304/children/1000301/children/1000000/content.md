# A binary balance down-step is exactly where strict omission-to-insertion transport is necessary

## Statement

In the deficit-one order-preserving setting of 4d26ca2c1961, partition O=A-C and E=C-A by the ordered gaps of the common core, and let B_t in {0,1} be the cumulative omission-minus-insertion balance after gap t. Then the following are equivalent: (i) every exterior vertex of E can be injectively paired with an omitted A-vertex lying in the same gap; (ii) each gap contains at least as many omissions as exterior vertices; (iii) the binary balance profile B_t is nondecreasing. Since |O|-|E|=1, in this case exactly one gap has one unmatched omission and all other gaps have equal omission/exterior counts, so B is of the form 0...01...1. Conversely, every down-step B_{t-1}=1,B_t=0 occurs at a gap with one more exterior vertex than omission and therefore forces at least one exterior vertex in that gap to be charged to a strictly earlier omission.

## Body

Let o_t,e_t be the omission and exterior counts in gap t. The balance increment is B_t-B_{t-1}=o_t-e_t (with the evident initial convention). Same-gap matching in gap t is possible exactly when o_t>=e_t, and a global same-gap injection exists exactly when this holds for every gap, proving (i) iff (ii). Because every B_t is 0 or 1, condition o_t-e_t>=0 for every t is exactly nondecreasingness of B, proving (ii) iff (iii). The total balance change over all gaps is |O|-|E|=1, so under nondecreasingness the nonnegative integer increments sum to one: exactly one gap has increment one and every other gap has increment zero. Conversely, a down-step has increment -1, so e_t=o_t+1; one exterior vertex in that gap cannot be matched to an omission from the same gap and any left-to-right noncrossing charging must use a strictly earlier omission. Combining this with 844f5e0c94f9, every binary-profile down-step supplies a strict charged surgery candidate that either lowers balance mass, exposes an A-order reversal, or yields a bounded reverse-triple obstruction.
