# A globally minimum-side endpoint probe has no neutral residue

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let mu be the globally minimum smaller-component order among all deletion covers, and let H-y=R|Q be a deletion cover with |R|=mu<=|Q|. Let z be either displayed endpoint of R, and choose any deletion cover T of H-z. Then at least one of the following occurs: (1) the comparison exposes explicit support crossing or order disagreement; (2) an explicit tight triple reverses a displayed endpoint edge or a displayed mixed join edge arising in the comparison; or (3) the singleton-lift pairwise-repartition component contains a spanning three-cover with strictly smaller quadratic potential Phi. In particular, deleting an endpoint of a globally minimum deletion side has no order-neutral, Phi-neutral terminal geometry.
