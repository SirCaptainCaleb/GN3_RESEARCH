# Endpoint-transport / defect-compression bridge

## Statement

From the forced support crossing, order disagreement, or bounded local obstruction supplied by the current route, construct a spanning two-cover directly or transport the obstruction to a reversible component-end edge that endpoint replacement can absorb. Equivalently, produce a spanning ordering of defect span at most two.

## Body

# Unsupported bridge

Everything before this node supplies structure, not closure. What is still missing is a global theorem that consumes that structure.

Starting from an exact deletion state and one of the disagreement/local-obstruction witnesses delivered by the preceding modules, prove one of:

1. a spanning two-cover exists immediately; or
2. the witness can be transported through valid tight-path replacements to an initial or terminal edge whose reversal permits endpoint absorption; or
3. an equivalent rearrangement produces a spanning ordering of defect span at most two.

Any iterative transport must have a proved well-founded potential, a complete no-trapping theorem, or an explicit contradiction from recurrence. Finiteness or repeatedly changing the omitted vertex is not termination.

All newly formed paths must satisfy the canonical path-construction hygiene: every new consecutive triple is checked. A crossing, tight cycle, reversing triple, or bounded insertion window is an input to this bridge, not by itself a proof of the grand theorem.
