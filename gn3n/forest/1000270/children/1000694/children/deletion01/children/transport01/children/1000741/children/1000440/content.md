# Well-founded defect-window transport

## Statement

Seek a canonical choice of span-three ordering minimizing a lexicographic defect potential, for example first the number of vertices strictly between the defect window and the nearer end, then a local orientation code of the three defective joins. Conjecture that every bounded insertion-obstruction witness at such a minimum either gives a two-cover immediately or produces a checked replacement ordering with the same span three but strictly smaller lexicographic potential.

## Body

Why it might matter globally:
The current endpoint-transport bridge fails mainly because repeated local transport has no proved termination. A genuinely well-founded ordering potential would convert the existing bounded obstruction taxonomy into an induction rather than a recurrence argument, closing exactly the present theorem-scale gap.

Plausible first attack:
Fix one canonical ordering P,x,Q from an exact deletion two-cover and choose it lexicographically minimal among all exact deletion states and both concatenation orientations. For each of the four certified bounded obstruction types, attempt one local surgery that shifts the three-center defect window one step toward an endpoint while checking every new triple. Record precisely which obstruction types resist monotone shift; if only one does, isolate that as a smaller general lemma.
