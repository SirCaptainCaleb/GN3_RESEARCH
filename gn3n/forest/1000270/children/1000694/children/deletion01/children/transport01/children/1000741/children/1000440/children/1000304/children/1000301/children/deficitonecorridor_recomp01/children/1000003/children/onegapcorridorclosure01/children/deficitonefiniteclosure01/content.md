# Deficit-one order-preserving transport admits a finite binary-balance descent

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let A be a globally longest tight path, and let C be an A-order-preserving tight path of order |A|-1. Then either an A-edge reversing witness is already present, or there is a finite sequence of canonical charged replacements, each preserving order |A|-1 and A-order compatibility and strictly decreasing the total binary omission-minus-insertion balance mass, after which one obtains at least one of the following standard outputs: (1) a bounded discrepancy kernel of order at most five; (2) a proper Hamiltonian support of order four or five whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (3) explicit order disagreement; or (4) an explicit reversed tight triple supported at a charged-replacement or splice junction. In particular, the deficit-one binary-balance transport process is well-founded: it has no neutral recurrence or unbounded one-gap terminal residue.

## Body

If C already has an A-edge reversing witness, there is nothing to prove. Otherwise apply deficitonecorridor_recomp01. If its same-gap alternative occurs and a reversed mixed-junction triple is exposed, we have (4). If instead it produces a one-gap unit-surplus comparison path, apply onegapcorridorclosure01, which gives (1)-(4). It remains to consider the strict-charge alternative. The charged-replacement theorem 844f5e0c94f9 says that the canonical replacement either exposes an A-edge reversal or a bounded reversed tight triple, giving (3) or (4), or produces another tight A-order-preserving path C_1 of order |A|-1 with no A-edge reversal witness and with strictly smaller total binary-balance mass. In the latter case repeat the argument with C_1. The balance profile takes values in {0,1}, so its total mass is a nonnegative integer. Hence every surviving strict-charge step decreases a well-founded integer potential and only finitely many such steps are possible. At the first step where strict descent is unavailable, deficitonecorridor_recomp01 enters its one-gap alternative, already consumed by onegapcorridorclosure01. Thus the process terminates in one of (1)-(4).
