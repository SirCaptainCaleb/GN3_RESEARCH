# Every minimum-side endpoint probe gives descent or standard disturbance

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let mu be the globally minimum smaller-component order among all deletion two-covers, and let H-y=R|Q be a deletion cover with |R|=mu<=|Q|. Let z be either displayed endpoint of R and let T be any deletion two-cover of H-z. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) the comparison data expose explicit support crossing, a direct mixed-support edge, or relative-order disagreement; (2) the singleton lift R|Q|{y} has a legal pairwise repartition to a spanning three-cover with strictly smaller quadratic potential Phi. Consequently, if R|Q|{y} is Phi-minimal in its pairwise-repartition component, every deletion-cover probe at either displayed endpoint of R necessarily exposes standard support/order disturbance.

## Body

Apply the certified endpoint-deletion classification 89d96023752d to T. Its direct-crossing and order-disagreement branches give conclusion (1). In the support-compatible restoration branch, failure to preserve the inherited order also gives (1), so assume inherited order is preserved. If y restores internally, 694b182c9295 gives either explicit relative-order disagreement or strict Phi descent, yielding (1) or (2). If y restores at an endpoint, b60c4039b25e shows that opposite-end restoration gives explicit order disagreement, while the order-neutral case is the same-end clean-swap form H-y=(A,z)|Q and H-z=(A,y)|Q. Apply 128f71b47605. It gives strict Phi descent or bounded-support order disagreement unless |Q|=mu=5 and |V(H)|=11. In that exceptional order-eleven profile, certified 26219fd919c2 gives a direct mixed-support edge or explicit relative-order disagreement, hence conclusion (1). Finally, if T lies in the rigid two-crossing block branch of 89d96023752d, 84dd27610966 gives an immediate legal pairwise repartition with strict Phi decrease, hence (2). These branches exhaust the endpoint-deletion classification. Therefore only standard support/order disturbance or strict quadratic descent remains. The final Phi-minimal corollary follows because conclusion (2) is then impossible.
