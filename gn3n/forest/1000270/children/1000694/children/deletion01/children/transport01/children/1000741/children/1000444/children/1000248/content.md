# The rigid minimum-side two-crossing block forces a reversed boundary edge

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let mu be the globally minimum smaller-component order among deletion covers, and let H-y=R|Q be a deletion cover with |R|=mu. Let z be an endpoint of R and put A=V(R)-{z}. Suppose a deletion cover T of H-z lies in the rigid two-crossing block branch of 89d96023752d and preserves the inherited orders. Then T exposes an explicit tight triple reversing either the displayed endpoint edge of R incident with z or the displayed join edge incident with y in the mixed component. More precisely, if R=(A,z), then the mixed component cannot have form (Q_2,y,A); hence it has form (A,y,Q_2). If a is the terminal vertex of A and q is the initial vertex of Q_2, at least one of (y,z,a) and (q,y,z) is tight. The initial-end case is symmetric.

## Body

Assume R=(A,z), so z is terminal. Since every deletion-cover component has order at least three, A has order at least two. In the rigid branch of 89d96023752d the Q-vertices split into two nonempty blocks Q_1,Q_2, one of which is an entire component and the other of which occurs with y and the intact A-block in the mixed component. If the mixed component is (Q_2,y,A), append z after A. The only new final consecutive triple is formed by the last two vertices of A and z, and it is inherited from R. Thus (Q_2,y,A,z) is tight, and together with Q_1 it gives a spanning two-cover of H, impossible. Therefore the surviving orientation is (A,y,Q_2). Let a be the terminal vertex of A and q the initial vertex of Q_2. Try to restore z between A and y, forming (A,z,y,Q_2). All consecutive triples are inherited from R or T except (a,z,y) and (z,y,q). If both are tight, the new sequence together with Q_1 is a spanning two-cover of H, contradiction. Hence at least one is non-tight. If (a,z,y) is non-tight, boundary antisymmetry gives (y,z,a) tight, reversing the displayed endpoint edge (a,z) of R. If (z,y,q) is non-tight, boundary antisymmetry gives (q,y,z) tight, reversing the displayed join edge (y,q) of T. The initial-end case is symmetric: the orientation with A initial restores z directly and is impossible; in the surviving orientation inserting z between y and A either succeeds to a two-cover or a failed new triple reverses the corresponding boundary edge.
