# From order twenty-five a Phi-minimal three-cover has minimum side at least eight or explicit local structure

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=25, and let A|B|X be a spanning three-cover minimizing Phi in a connected component of the pairwise-repartition graph containing no cover with at most two paths, with a=|A|>=b=|B|>=c=|X|. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) c>=8; (2) order disagreement occurs; (3) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set of order four or five whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (4) for two displayed endpoints x,y of one component and a vertex q of another component, one of (x,q,y),(y,q,x) is tight; (5) there is a tight path from one displayed endpoint of a component to its other displayed endpoint whose internal vertices form a nonempty interval of another displayed component.

## Body

Apply 4f8d912449ad. If order disagreement occurs, we have outcome (2); otherwise c>=6. Suppose c=6. If b>=8, then both larger components have order at least eight, so b1b9fab88706 gives order disagreement or a proper Hamiltonian four-set with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, yielding (2) or (3). If b<=7, then b is 6 or 7 and n>=25 gives a>=b+2; apply c671dca6534e to A and B, obtaining one of outcomes (3)-(5). Now suppose c=7. If b>=9, let X be the seven-vertex component and let B=(b_0,...,b_{m-1}), m>=9. If X union {b_0} were Hamiltonian, replacing X|B by a Hamilton path on X union {b_0} and the inherited suffix B-b_0 would change the pair orders 7,m to 8,m-1 and change Phi by 16-2m<0, contradicting minimality; likewise X union {b_{m-1}} is non-Hamiltonian. Hence b_0 and b_{m-1} are both noninsertable into a Hamilton order of X. Apply a8c9883902b1. Its Hamiltonian four/five-set alternatives give (3); its cross-triple alternative gives (4); its interval-path alternative gives (5); and its exceptional cyclic four-set becomes a Hamiltonian five-set after adjoining the other endpoint, again giving (3). Finally, if c=7 and b<=8, then b is 7 or 8 and n>=25 gives a>=b+2, so c671dca6534e again gives (3)-(5). Thus if outcomes (2)-(5) do not occur, c>=8.
