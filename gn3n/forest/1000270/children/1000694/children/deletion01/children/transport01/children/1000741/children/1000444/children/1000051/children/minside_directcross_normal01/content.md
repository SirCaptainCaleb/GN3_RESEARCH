# A minimum-side direct edge reduces to inherited-path disturbance, endpoint reversal, descent, or a compatible omission swap

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let H-y=R|Q be a deletion cover with R=(r_0,...,r_m) of globally minimum deletion-side order mu, and let z be either displayed endpoint of R. Put A=V(R)-{z}, and let T be a deletion two-cover of H-z. Suppose T contains a direct ordinary edge joining A to V(Q), and suppose every T-component preserves the relative order of its surviving R-vertices. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) some inherited ordinary edge of R-z has its endpoints in different components of T; (2) one T-component contains two nonempty inherited R-blocks separated by a nonempty exterior segment; (3) a tight triple explicitly reverses the displayed endpoint edge of R incident with z; (4) H has a spanning two-cover; (5) the singleton-lift pairwise-repartition component has a strict quadratic-potential descent; (6) the comparison gives a Phi-neutral one-block endpoint-restoration move, hence by eaf8cbdad3f3 a fully compatible omission swap to another deletion cover. Thus a direct A-to-V(Q) edge at a globally minimum deletion-side endpoint has no further case beyond these alternatives.

## Body

Apply e2ffb6f50728 to the displayed path R, omitted endpoint z, comparison cover T, and exterior class consisting of the vertices outside R-z. The assumed direct A-to-Q edge is a mixed edge of that theorem. If the surviving R-vertices occupy multiple T-blocks, e2ffb6f50728 gives either an inherited edge of R-z joining different T-components, which is outcome (1), or a leave-and-return subpath through a nonempty exterior segment, which is outcome (2). Otherwise all surviving R-vertices form one contiguous inherited-order block and every direct mixed edge is incident with an endpoint of that block. Apply 295ead2879dc to this one-block endpoint attachment. It yields an explicit reversal of the displayed endpoint edge, a spanning two-cover, strict Phi descent, or its sole Phi-neutral singleton-transfer boundary case. In that neutral case eaf8cbdad3f3 identifies the move as a fully compatible omission swap between the old and new deletion covers, giving outcome (6). These alternatives are exhaustive.
