# Global quadratic minima have minimum side four, and a four-side locks both endpoints against both long paths

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament on n>=13 with a spanning three-cover but no two-cover, and choose a spanning three-cover minimizing Phi=sum |C_i|^2. Then every component has order at least four. More generally, if a three-side T has failed facing one-vertex extensions from endpoints u,b of the two outer paths, then T union {u,b} is Hamiltonian and the simultaneous bilateral move decreases Phi by 2n-24 (or gives a two-cover if an outer side is a singleton). In particular, if the global minimum is X|P|Q with X=(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3), |X|=4, and |P|,|Q|>=6, then x_0 and x_3 are noninsertable into every position of both displayed long paths.

## Body

Two failed facing extensions of a tight three-path force the five-set obtained by adjoining both endpoints to be Hamiltonian. Replacing both outer endpoints simultaneously changes the component orders (ell,3,r) to (ell-1,5,r-1), so Phi drops by 2n-24; if either outer side is a singleton the same Hamiltonian five-set already yields a two-cover. Now take a global Phi-minimum on n>=13. Any component of order at most two admits the standard balancing descent. If a component has order three, any partner of order at least six yields the certified one-sided descent; otherwise both other sides have order at most five, forcing the only remaining size possibility at n>=13 to the boundary configuration where the bilateral move gives a strict descent. Hence every component has order at least four. Finally let X have order four and let P,Q have orders at least six. Any endpoint extension X+e is non-Hamiltonian, else moving e into X lowers Phi. For arbitrary endpoints e of P and f of Q, repeated bad extension on X gives Hamiltonian crossed five-sets using x_0 or x_3. If an endpoint truncation of P or Q enlarged by one of x_0,x_3 were Hamiltonian, combining it with the corresponding crossed five-set and the other inherited long-path truncation would lower Phi. Thus all four endpoint truncations fail for each z in {x_0,x_3}; any insertion of z into a displayed long path would survive after deleting a suitable endpoint, contradicting those failures. Therefore both x_0,x_3 are globally noninsertable into both P and Q.
