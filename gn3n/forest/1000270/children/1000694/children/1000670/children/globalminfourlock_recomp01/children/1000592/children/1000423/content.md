# Global four-side minima force a Hamiltonian six-support or explicit order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover minimizing Phi among all spanning three-covers, where W is Hamiltonian of order four and |P|,|Q|>=6. Then either H contains a proper Hamiltonian six-vertex set U such that H-U is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number exactly two, or H contains two tight paths with order disagreement on a common vertex pair.

## Body

Let E_P and E_Q be the two displayed endpoint sets of P and Q. For each w in W let G_w consist of the cross endpoint pairs (e,f) in E_P x E_Q for which (W-{w}) union {e,f} is Hamiltonian. By the certified theorem twofourhamdeletions01, for each of the four cross pairs (e,f), at least two labels w are good. Hence the total number of incidences (w,(e,f)) is at least eight. Suppose first that some G_w contains two cross pairs sharing one endpoint; after symmetry, say both (e,q_0) and (e,q_s) are good. Put D=W-{w} and U=D union {e,q_0,q_s}. Then U-{q_s} and U-{q_0} are Hamiltonian. If U is Hamiltonian, it is proper because |P|,|Q|>=6; minimality gives pc(H-U)<=2, while H-U cannot be Hamiltonian or U together with H-U would two-cover H. Hence pc(H-U)=2. If U is non-Hamiltonian, the certified four-of-six theorem gives at least four Hamiltonian vertex deletions of U, and astra004fourgooddisagree applied to H[U] forces order disagreement between Hamiltonian deletion paths. This gives the stated second outcome. It remains to show that the shared-endpoint case must occur. Suppose no G_w contains two cross pairs sharing an endpoint. Then each G_w is a matching in K_{2,2}, so |G_w|<=2. Since there are four w and at least eight incidences total, equality holds: every |G_w|=2 and every cross pair has exactly two good labels. Thus each G_w is one of the two perfect matchings of K_{2,2}. Fix any cross pair (e,f) and put S=W union {e,f}. Exactly two deletions S-{w}, w in W, are Hamiltonian. Four-of-six says at least four deletions of S are Hamiltonian, so the only two remaining deletions S-{e}=W union {f} and S-{f}=W union {e} must both be Hamiltonian. Varying (e,f) shows W union {e} is Hamiltonian for every displayed endpoint e of P or Q. Since |P|>=6, choose an endpoint e of P. Then (W union {e}) | (P-e) | Q is a spanning three-cover with Phi change [25+(|P|-1)^2]-[16+|P|^2]=10-2|P|<0, contradicting global Phi-minimality. Hence the no-shared-endpoint branch is impossible.
