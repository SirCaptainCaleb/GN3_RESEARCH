# Every singleton-swap cycle closes to a support-compatibility clique

## Statement

Let F_{a_1},...,F_{a_k}, k>=4, be deletion covers with distinct omitted labels and nonempty common surviving core. If their singleton lifts form a cycle in the singleton-swap graph M, then the deletion covers are pairwise support-compatible. Thus every singleton-swap cycle has trivial support monodromy; any failure of full compatibility among its vertices is purely order-theoretic.

## Body
