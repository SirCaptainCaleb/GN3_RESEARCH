# Cyclic defect rotation route

## Statement

For a spanning ordering of minimum defect span three, use the certified reverse cyclic endpoint hook to define a rotation that moves one endpoint across the cyclic cut while preserving span at most three. Conjecture that iterating canonical such rotations either produces defect span at most two or forces a repeated cyclic state whose accumulated reversal data yields a tight cycle/path splice closing the theorem.

## Body

The new ingredient is to use the cyclic closure of a span-three ordering as the state space rather than a fixed linear defect window. The endpoint hook guarantees a local direction to rotate; the challenge is to find a global progress or recurrence certificate.
