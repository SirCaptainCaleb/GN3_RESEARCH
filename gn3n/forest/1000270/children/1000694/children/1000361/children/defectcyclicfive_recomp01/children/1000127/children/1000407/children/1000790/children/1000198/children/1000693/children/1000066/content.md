# The order-fifteen no-descent four-side branch also escapes the canonical cube

## Statement

Assume the order-15 all-equal barrier setting of d3a358471810, with Q_i=(x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3)=(b_i,t_i,t_{i+2},a_{i+1}) and a complementary 6|5 cover P|R, where P=(p_1,...,p_6). Suppose the strict-descent alternative of foursidedescentobstruction does not occur. Then there is nevertheless a legal repartition of Q_i|P into two Hamiltonian five-sets whose resulting spanning 5|5|5 cover lies outside all eight canonical {1,1,1} cube states. Hence at order fifteen the entire four-side barrier branch, descent or no descent, forces genuine escape from the canonical cube.

## Body

In the no-descent branch of foursidedescentobstruction, M=(p_2,p_3,p_4,p_5) satisfies H[M union {x_0}] and H[M union {x_3}] non-Hamiltonian, while the crossed five-set A=F_L={x_0,x_1,x_2,p_1,p_6} is Hamiltonian. Its complementary five-set F=M union {x_3} is non-Hamiltonian. Apply the certified order-ten exchange-matrix theorem in extremal01 to the partition A|F. For a in A and y in F, write X_{a,y}=(A-{a}) union {y} and Y_{a,y}=(F-{y}) union {a}. Every column y has at least three X-good cells and every row a has at least four Y-good cells. Hence there are at least ten double-good cells. We claim some double-good cell has a not in {x_1,x_2}={t_i,t_{i+2}}. Otherwise, in each of the other three rows a not in {x_1,x_2}, at least four cells are Y-good but none is X-good, giving at least 12 X-bad cells. Yet every one of the five columns has at least three X-good cells, so each column has at most two X-bad cells and the whole matrix has at most 10 X-bad cells, contradiction. Choose a double-good cell with a not in {x_1,x_2}. Then X_{a,y} is a Hamiltonian five-set retaining both transferred labels t_i,t_{i+2}, and Y_{a,y} is its Hamiltonian complement inside Q_i union P. Thus X_{a,y}|Y_{a,y}|R is a spanning 5|5|5 cover. By f3965b3ddf4e, the only canonical cube states with profile 5|5|5 are the two antipodes. In either antipode every component consists of one intact four-vertex core M_j together with exactly one transferred label. Since X_{a,y} contains two distinct transferred labels, it cannot be a component of either antipodal cover. Therefore the new 5|5|5 cover lies outside the canonical eight-state cube.
