# From order thirteen every deletion state reaches transport, mixed support, or a certified reversal/cycle disturbance

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=13 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. Then there is a canonical double-wrap spanning three-cover S|R|T with S a Hamiltonian four-path, H-S=R|T a two-cover, and after relabeling |R|>=5. At least one of the following occurs: (1) one legal pairwise repartition of S|R has nonincreasing quadratic potential, neutral only when |R|=5 and strict when |R|>=6; (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian support of order four or five meeting both S and the displayed interior of R, with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement; (3) there is a positioned tight triple supported across S and the interior of R that reverses a displayed edge of the S-subpath or of the gap connector arising from the hard endpoint-lock geometry; (4) H contains a proper vertex-simple tight cycle supported across S and the interior of R. Moreover, when n is 13 or 14, alternatives (3) and (4) are unnecessary: whenever the hard gap-network branch is reached, some legal pairwise repartition involving the third component T strictly decreases quadratic potential.

## Body

By 8ededbb50716 and 687bbaa431c3, every deletion state has a canonical double-wrap Hamiltonian four-side S with H-S of path-cover number two. Choose a displayed two-cover H-S=R|T and relabel so |R|>=|T|. Since n>=13, |R|+|T|=n-4>=9 and therefore |R|>=5. Apply four_side_endpoint_lock_gap_network01 to S|R|T. Its first two alternatives are exactly (1) and (2). In its hard gap-network alternative, apply four_side_gap_network_disturbance01. This gives exactly the positioned reversing triple in (3) or the proper tight cycle in (4). For the final small-order assertion, the hard gap-network alternative itself forces |R|>=7. If n=13 then |R|+|T|=9, so |T|<=2. When |T|=1, repartition S union T, of order five, into arbitrary parts of orders 3 and 2; both are Hamiltonian by smallset01, and the selected-pair contribution to Phi drops from 4^2+1^2=17 to 3^2+2^2=13. When |T|=2, repartition S union T into two triples, dropping 4^2+2^2=20 to 3^2+3^2=18. If n=14 then |R|+|T|=10, so |T|<=3. The same argument gives strict descent for |T|<=2. If |T|=3, then |R|=7 and threesidedescent6 applied to T|R gives a legal strict quadratic-potential descent. Thus at orders 13 and 14 the entire hard gap-network branch is strictly escapable.
