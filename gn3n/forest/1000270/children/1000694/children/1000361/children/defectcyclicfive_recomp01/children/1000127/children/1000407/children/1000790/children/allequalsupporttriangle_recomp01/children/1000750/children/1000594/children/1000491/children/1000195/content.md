# The order-neutral barrier residue is a synchronized six-support family

## Statement

In the endpoint-barrier branch of the all-equal {1,1,1} support triangle, either a relative-order disagreement is already forced by one of the six endpoint tests, or for every i both six-sets L_i={t_i,b_i} union E_i and R_i={a_i,t_{i+1}} union E_i are Hamiltonian. In the latter branch each complement H-L_i and H-R_i is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Thus after excluding standard order disturbance, the all-equal barrier residue reduces to six synchronized Hamiltonian supports, two over the same four-endpoint set E_i at each of the three cyclic positions.

## Body

If any of the resulting six induced six-vertex tournaments is non-Hamiltonian, the corresponding four Hamiltonian endpoint deletions force relative-order disagreement. If no such disagreement occurs, all six induced six-vertex tournaments must therefore be Hamiltonian.
