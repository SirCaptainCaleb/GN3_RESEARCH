# Every interface of a three-isolated-defect cube state is a one-sided slide

## Statement

Let Gamma be a minimum-span cyclic defect certificate of run type {1,1,1}, and let A|B|C be any of its eight cyclic-interval three-covers. At each of the three displayed cyclic interfaces, exactly one of the two join triples is non-tight and the other is tight. Hence every interface is the single-tight slide alternative of the three-block interface trichotomy. In particular, if A|B|C minimizes Phi in its trapped contiguous-block relocation component and two adjacent displayed blocks X,Y satisfy |X|>=|Y|+2, then the interface slide must transfer one endpoint vertex from Y into X; the opposite transfer would strictly decrease Phi.

## Body

Each displayed cut of a cube state is one selected endpoint of one isolated defect edge of Gamma. Since Gamma is a matching, exactly one of the two cyclic defect edges incident with that cut is present: the chosen isolated defect edge; the other incident cyclic edge is nondefective. These two incident cyclic defect tests are precisely the two join triples at the corresponding interface of the cyclic-interval cover. Thus exactly one join triple is non-tight and one is tight, so the local interface trichotomy is always its cut-slide alternative. Now suppose the state is Phi-minimal in its trapped relocation component and |X|>=|Y|+2. Sliding one vertex from X to Y changes Phi by 2(|Y|-|X|)+2<0, impossible. Therefore the actual one-sided slide at that interface must be the reverse direction, moving an endpoint of the smaller block Y into the larger block X.
