# At a trapped quadratic minimum the {1,1,1} cube already yields a canonical closure input

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and suppose a minimum-span cyclic defect certificate of run type {1,1,1} yields a three-cover C in a trapped pairwise-repartition component, with C minimizing the quadratic potential Phi in that component. Then H exposes one of the canonical theorem-closure inputs listed in c195f52d5b31: a proper Hamiltonian four- or five-vertex support with path-cover-two complement, an explicit cross triple or interval connector, order disagreement, an inherited displayed-path edge whose endpoints are separated by a comparison cover, a leave-and-return excursion through another displayed core, crossing multiplicity at least three, a bounded local defect-compression obstruction, or a reversed join. In particular, no {1,1,1} cyclic-defect state can remain as a structureless terminal Phi-minimum.

## Body

Apply c195f52d5b31 directly to C. Its hypotheses are exactly the present ones: H is a minimum counterexample and C is a spanning three-cover minimizing the quadratic potential in a trapped connected component of the pairwise-repartition graph. Therefore H has one of the listed outcomes: a proper Hamiltonian four- or five-vertex support with path-cover-two complement, an explicit cross triple or interval connector, order disagreement, an inherited displayed-path edge separated by a comparison cover, a leave-and-return excursion through another displayed core, crossing multiplicity at least three, a bounded local defect-compression obstruction, or a reversed join.

Thus the {1,1,1} state cannot remain without one of those outcomes. ∎
