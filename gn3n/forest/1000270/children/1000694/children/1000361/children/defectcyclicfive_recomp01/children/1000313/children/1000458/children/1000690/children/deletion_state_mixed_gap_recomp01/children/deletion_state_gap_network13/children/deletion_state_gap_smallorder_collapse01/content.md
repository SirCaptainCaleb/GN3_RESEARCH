## Statement

## Body

If |T|=1, the five-vertex union S union T can be partitioned arbitrarily into sets of orders three and two, both Hamiltonian because every boundary tournament of order at most three is Hamiltonian. But the monotone no-cycle branch of four_side_gap_order_separated01 requires |R|>=8. Therefore at profile 7|4|3 the gap order has an inversion and four_side_gap_order_cycle_or_monotone01 supplies a proper tight cycle. The final summary simply combines this with the non-gap alternatives already present in deletion_state_gap_network13.
