# From order thirteen every deletion state reduces to transport, mixed bounded support, or a proper tight cycle

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=13 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. Then there is a canonical double-wrap spanning three-cover S|R|T with S a Hamiltonian four-path, H-S=R|T a two-cover, and after relabeling |R|>=5, such that at least one of the following holds: (1) one legal pairwise repartition of S|R has nonincreasing quadratic potential, neutral only when |R|=5 and strictly decreasing when |R|>=6; (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian support U of order four or five meeting both S and the displayed interior of R, with H-U non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two; (3) H contains a proper vertex-simple tight cycle whose support meets both S and the displayed interior of R. In outcome (3) the cycle complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two and H contains a tight triple reversing a displayed consecutive pair of the cycle or of a complementary two-cover path. Thus no separated or monotone four-label gap-network residue survives.

## Body

Apply deletion_state_gap_network13. Its first three alternatives are exactly outcomes (1), (2), and the proper-cycle part of (3). Its fourth alternative is the monotone separated four-label gap network. But four_side_gap_network_forces_cycle01 shows that the monotone gap-network residue is impossible: the two interior X-labels force two opposite interface triples that concatenate to a forbidden mixed Hamiltonian four-path. Therefore alternative (4) of deletion_state_gap_network13 never occurs, and the only remaining gap-network case is the proper tight cycle. The complement and reversal conclusions in outcome (3) are supplied by four_side_gap_network_forces_cycle01. The size statement follows from the canonical double-wrap cover: |R|+|T|=n-4>=9, so the larger path has order at least five. The potential change in outcome (1) is 10-2|R|, hence neutral only at |R|=5 and strict from |R|>=6.
