# Minimum-span cyclic certificates generate a connected family of cyclic-interval three-covers

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament with pc(H)>2 and let pi be a spanning ordering of defect span three, with cyclic defect graph Gamma. The minimum vertex covers C of Gamma, all of size three, canonically give spanning three-path covers of H by the three cyclic intervals cut out by C. These covers form a connected subgraph of the pairwise-repartition graph when two states are joined whenever their cut sets differ by exchanging one cut position. For the proper run types {1,1,1}, {2,1,1}, {3,1}, {3,2}, {5}, the numbers of such cyclic-interval three-covers are respectively 8,4,6,3,4; for Gamma=C5 there are 5.

## Body

A cut position interrupts every consecutive triple whose defect edge is incident with that cut. Hence a set C of cyclic cut positions yields tight path arcs precisely when C meets every defect edge of Gamma. Since nu(Gamma)=tau(Gamma)=3 in every proper case, and tau(C5)=3, every minimum vertex cover C of Gamma has size three and cuts the cyclic order into three nonempty tight cyclic intervals, giving a spanning three-cover. Distinct cut sets give distinct cyclic-interval partitions. For a proper Gamma, minimum vertex covers factor over its disjoint path runs. A one-edge run has two minimum covers, its two endpoints; a two-edge run has one, its middle vertex; a three-edge run has three, {0,2},{1,2},{1,3}, whose one-cut exchange graph is a path; and a five-edge run has four, {0,2,4},{1,2,4},{1,3,4},{1,3,5}, again a path under one-cut exchanges. Taking products gives respectively 8,4,6,3,4 states for the five run multisets, and the product exchange graph is connected. For C5 the five minimum three-vertex covers form a 5-cycle under one-cut exchanges. Finally, if two minimum cut sets differ by replacing one cut while fixing the other two, the cyclic interval between the two fixed cuts is unchanged, while the complementary union is repartitioned from two tight paths into two other tight paths. Thus the corresponding spanning three-covers differ by one legal pairwise repartition.
