# From order thirteen every hard deletion-state gap branch has two explicit seam reversals

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=13 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. Then there is a canonical double-wrap spanning three-cover S|R|T with S=(s_0,s_1,s_2,s_3) a Hamiltonian four-path, H-S=R|T a two-cover, and after relabeling |R|>=5. At least one of the following occurs: (1) one legal pairwise repartition of S|R has nonincreasing quadratic potential, neutral only when |R|=5 and strict when |R|>=6; (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian support of order four or five meeting both S and the displayed interior of R, with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement; (3) the hard endpoint-lock geometry has distinct second-type obstruction gaps g(s_k) in the interior M of R, the gap order is not the displayed S-order, and for every inversion i<j with g(s_j)<g(s_i) both seam triples (b_{g(s_j)+1},s_j,s_{j-1}) and (s_{i+1},s_i,b_{g(s_i)}) are tight. Thus every surviving inversion reverses both displayed S-edges at the ends of its inverted subpath. Moreover, for n=13 or 14 the hard branch in (3) always admits a legal strict quadratic-potential descent involving the third component T, so only (1) or (2) is needed after allowing that additional move.
