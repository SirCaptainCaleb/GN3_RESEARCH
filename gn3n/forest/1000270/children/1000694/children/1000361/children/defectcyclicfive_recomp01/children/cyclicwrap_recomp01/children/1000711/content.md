# The single-wrap three-plus-one branch forces a one-vertex transfer between the outer paths

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let pi=(v_1,...,v_n) be a minimum-defect-span-three ordering in the linear 111 branch whose proper cyclic defect graph has run multiset {3,1}. Let the three consecutive linear defect centers be i,i+1,i+2, and write A=(v_1,...,v_{i-2}), C=(v_{i+3},v_{i+2},v_{i+1},v_i,v_{i-1}), B=(v_{i+4},...,v_n), so A|C|B is the canonical three-cover. Exactly one wrap triple is non-tight. If (v_{n-1},v_n,v_1) is the non-tight wrap triple, then B has at least two vertices and (B-v_n)|(v_n,A) is a two-cover of V(A) union V(B). If (v_n,v_1,v_2) is the non-tight wrap triple, then A has at least two vertices and (B,v_1)|(A-v_1) is a two-cover of V(A) union V(B). Thus the single-wrap branch always admits a legal pairwise repartition transferring one endpoint between the two outer paths. If this transfer does not decrease the quadratic potential, then the donor path has order at most one larger than the recipient path.

## Body

By defectspanbridge_recomp01, the linear 111 branch has the displayed tight five-path C and nonempty inherited tight outer paths A and B. By defectcyclicfive_recomp01, a proper cyclic defect graph of type {3,1} in this branch consists of the preserved three-edge linear run plus one isolated cyclic defect edge. Deleting the original cut vertex q_0 must remove that isolated edge and no edge of the three-run, so exactly one of the two wrap triples T_1=(v_{n-1},v_n,v_1) and T_2=(v_n,v_1,v_2) is non-tight. Suppose T_1 is non-tight. Then T_2 is tight. The cyclic concatenation of B followed by A is tight everywhere except at T_1. Cutting instead between v_{n-1} and v_n gives the two displayed paths B-v_n and (v_n,A); the latter is tight because its only new triple is T_2. If |B|=1, then (v_n,A) itself spans V(A) union V(B), and together with C would two-cover H, impossible; hence |B|>=2. The case in which T_2 is non-tight is symmetric in the displayed cyclic concatenation: T_1 is tight, cutting between v_1 and v_2 gives (B,v_1) and A-v_1, and |A|>=2 or else C together with (B,v_1) two-covers H. For a transfer from a donor of order d to a recipient of order r, the change in Phi on these two components is (d-1)^2+(r+1)^2-d^2-r^2=2(r-d+1). Therefore absence of strict decrease implies d<=r+1.
