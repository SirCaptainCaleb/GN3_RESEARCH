# Above order seventeen every deletion state descends or reaches the fixed-endpoint matching frontier

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=18 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. Let S|R|T be the canonical double-wrap spanning three-cover supplied by d26d8171978f, with S a Hamiltonian four-path. Then at least one of the following holds: (1) the pairwise-repartition component containing S|R|T contains a spanning three-cover of strictly smaller quadratic potential than S|R|T; (2) for one complementary path R=(r_1,...,r_m) of order m>=7, the endpoint six-set U=V(S) union {r_1,r_m} yields two Hamiltonian four-sets containing {r_1,r_m} and overlapping in three vertices, so d9a4b66724d2 gives a Hamiltonian five-set on their union or at least four Hamiltonian four-subsets of that union, all with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements; (3) for such an R, V(S)=A disjoint-union B with |A|=|B|=2 and both A union {r_1,r_m} and B union {r_1,r_m} Hamiltonian with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements. Thus the only no-descent residue not already amplified is the perfect matching on the four labels with both exterior endpoints fixed.

## Body

Apply d26d8171978f to the deletion cover and obtain the canonical double-wrap three-cover S|R|T with |S|=4 and H-S=R|T. Since n>=18, |R|+|T|=n-4>=14, so after relabeling one complementary path R=(r_1,...,r_m) has m>=7. If either S union {r_1} or S union {r_m} is Hamiltonian, the corresponding endpoint transfer replaces pair sizes (4,m) by (5,m-1) and strictly decreases quadratic potential, giving (1). Assume both endpoint five-sets are non-Hamiltonian. Put U=V(S) union {r_1,r_m}. By two_bad_five_extensions_all_opposite01, U-{s} is Hamiltonian for every s in V(S). If U itself is Hamiltonian, repartition S|R as U | (r_2,...,r_{m-1}); the pair sizes change from (4,m) to (6,m-2), with quadratic-potential change 24-4m<0 because m>=7, again giving (1). Hence in the no-descent branch U is non-Hamiltonian, its two endpoint deletions are non-Hamiltonian, and all four S-label deletions are Hamiltonian. The theorem four_side_endpoint_matching_bridge01 applies. Its adjacent-edge branch gives exactly outcome (2), while its no-adjacent-edge branch says the graph defined by Hamiltonian two-vertex deletions is a perfect matching on V(S), yielding a partition V(S)=A disjoint-union B into two pairs for which A union {r_1,r_m} and B union {r_1,r_m} are Hamiltonian four-sets with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements. This is outcome (3).
