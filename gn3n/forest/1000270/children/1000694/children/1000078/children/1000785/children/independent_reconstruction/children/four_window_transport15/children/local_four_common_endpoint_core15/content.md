# Above order fourteen a locally minimal four-side has a common endpoint-pair three-core

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=15 and let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover with |W|=4 that minimizes quadratic potential within its connected pairwise-repartition component. Let E_P,E_Q be the two displayed endpoint sets of P,Q. Then there exists w in W such that, with D=W-{w}, both five-sets D union E_P and D union E_Q are Hamiltonian. Moreover, either D union {e} is Hamiltonian for some displayed endpoint e, or every pair of distinct displayed endpoints x,y in E_P union E_Q gives a Hamiltonian five-set D union {x,y}; in the latter case each such proper five-set has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.

## Body

For R in {P,Q}, let A_R={w in W:(W-{w}) union E_R is Hamiltonian}. By certified twofourhamdeletions01, |A_P|,|A_Q|>=2. Suppose A_P and A_Q were disjoint. Then, because |W|=4, each has order exactly two and they partition W. Fix R and put S=W union E_R. Exactly two W-deletions S-w are Hamiltonian, namely those indexed by A_R. By the certified four-of-six theorem in smallset01, at least four one-vertex deletions of S are Hamiltonian. Hence both endpoint deletions S-e=W union (E_R-{e}) are Hamiltonian. Thus W union {e} is Hamiltonian for every displayed endpoint e of P and Q. Since |P|+|Q|=n-4>=11, one of P,Q has order m>=6. Moving either displayed endpoint e of that path into W gives a legal pairwise repartition with size change (4,m)->(5,m-1), whose Phi change is 10-2m<0, contradicting componentwise Phi-minimality. Therefore A_P intersect A_Q is nonempty. Choose w in the intersection and D=W-{w}; then D union E_P and D union E_Q are Hamiltonian. If some D+e is Hamiltonian we have the first refinement. Otherwise D+e is non-Hamiltonian for all four displayed endpoints. Applying certified three_bad_four_extensions_fiveshell01 to D and any three of the four endpoints shows D+{x,y} Hamiltonian for every endpoint pair x,y. Each is proper; if its complement were Hamiltonian it would two-cover H, so minimum-counterexample calculus makes the complement non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two.
