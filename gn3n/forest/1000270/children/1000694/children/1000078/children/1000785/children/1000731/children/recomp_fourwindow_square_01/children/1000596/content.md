# From order sixteen onward a coherent endpoint square forces order disagreement

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=16 satisfying the coherent two-label endpoint-square hypotheses of recomp_square45_01, and let C_a=(D+a)|(F-a) be Phi-minimal in its connected pairwise-repartition component. Then order disagreement occurs.

## Body

Apply recomp_square45_01. If it already gives order disagreement, we are done. Otherwise C_a has component-order multiset {4,5,n-9}. Since n>=16, the third component has order at least seven. Apply 83353015664a to the Hamiltonian five-side and that component. The theorem gives explicit order disagreement at the endpoint six-set. Hence the no-disagreement alternative is impossible.
