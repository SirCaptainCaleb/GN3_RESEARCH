# The weighted half-path principle is exactly the fractional-cover threshold two

## Statement

For every finite boundary tournament H, let tau*(H) be its fractional tight-path cover number. Then tau*(H)=sup_w w(V(H))/max_P w(V(P)), where the supremum is over nonzero nonnegative vertex weights and P ranges over tight paths. Consequently Astra idea 001 holds for H if and only if tau*(H)<=2. More generally, tau*(H)<=T implies that every nonnegative vertex weighting has a tight path of weight at least w(V(H))/T.

## Body

# Fractional-cover duality\n\nLet F be the finite family of supports of tight paths in H. The fractional path-cover linear program is\n\nminimize  sum_{P in F} y_P\nsubject to  sum_{P contains v} y_P >= 1  for every vertex v,\n             y_P >= 0.\n\nIts dual is\n\nmaximize  sum_v w_v\nsubject to  sum_{v in P} w_v <= 1  for every P in F,\n             w_v >= 0.\n\nBy finite-dimensional linear-programming duality, the common optimum is tau*(H). For a nonzero nonnegative weight function w, put W=sum_v w_v and M(w)=max_{P in F} sum_{v in P} w_v. Scaling w by 1/M(w) makes it dual feasible, so W/M(w)<=tau*(H). Conversely every nonzero dual-feasible w has M(w)<=1, and rescaling it by 1/M(w) can only increase its objective to W/M(w). Hence\n\ntau*(H)=sup_{w>=0, w not identically 0} W/M(w).\n\nTherefore tau*(H)<=T exactly when every nonnegative weighting w has M(w)>=W/T. Taking T=2 proves the equivalence with Astra idea 001.\n\n# Consequences for the existing fractional bounds\n\nIf H is a minimum counterexample and A is a tight path of order lambda, the certified bound fractionalpathavg01 gives tau*(H)<=2+1/lambda. Duality therefore gives, for every nonnegative vertex weighting of total weight W, a tight path of weight at least\n\nW/(2+1/lambda)=lambda W/(2lambda+1).\n\nIn the sharp half-order shell n=2lambda+1, fractionalsharpshell01 gives equality tau*(H)=2+1/lambda. Since the dual optimum is attained, there is then a nonnegative weighting for which every tight path has weight at most lambda W/(2lambda+1)<W/2. Thus any hypothetical minimum counterexample in the sharp half-order shell necessarily violates Astra idea 001. Equivalently, proving Astra idea 001 would by itself exclude that entire shell.
