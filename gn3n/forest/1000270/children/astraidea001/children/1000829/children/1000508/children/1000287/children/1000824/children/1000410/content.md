# Sharp half-order shell three-deletion core disagreement is a crossed rectangle or a one/two-vertex transfer

## Statement

Let H be in the sharp half-order shell n=2lambda+1. Let F_a and F_b be lambda|lambda covers of H-a and H-b, and fix a third distinct label c. Put W=V(H)-{a,b,c}. Let A|B and C|D be the unordered support partitions of W induced by F_a and F_b. If these partitions are different, then, after possibly swapping A,B and/or C,D, exactly one of the following holds:
(i) all four intersections A∩C, A∩D, B∩C, B∩D are nonempty;
(ii) there is a set X of order one or two such that C=A union X and D=B-X.
Thus every nontrivial common-core support disagreement is either a genuine four-cell crossing or a support transfer of size at most two.

## Body

Each component support of F_a has order lambda. Passing to W removes the two labels b,c from the union of its two supports. Therefore
|A|,|B| belong to {lambda-2,lambda-1,lambda},
and |A|+|B|=2lambda-2.
The same holds for C,D.

Assume the two unordered partitions are different.

If all four intersections are nonempty, we are in (i). Otherwise some intersection is empty. Relabel the two classes in each partition so that A∩D is empty. Then A subseteq C and D subseteq B. Since the partitions are not equal, X=C-A=B-D is nonempty.

Now
|X|=|C|-|A|.
Both |C| and |A| lie between lambda-2 and lambda, so |X|<=2. Hence |X| is one or two, and
C=A union X, D=B-X,
which is (ii).

The two alternatives are mutually exclusive by construction. ∎
