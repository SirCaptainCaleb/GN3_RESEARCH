# Inseparable pairs force cut barriers and rigid two-crossing deletion geometry

## Statement

Let (H;s,t) be a minimum prescribed-separation failure that is two-coverable, and fix a two-cover A|B with A=(a_1,...,a_m), s=a_i, t=a_j, i<j. Every cut of A between s and t has both B-plus-side unions non-Hamiltonian. If v=a_h with i<h<j and L=A[1,h-1], R=A[h+1,m], then every separating two-cover T of H-v has at least two ordinary edges crossing the partition L|R|B. If exactly two such crossing edges occur, cutting them gives four maximal blocks and exactly one of L,R,B is split. If B splits, the two T-components pair L and R crosswise with the two B-blocks. If L splits, either the components pair crosswise as L_s union B and L_o union R, or the s-containing block L_s is isolated and has order at least three; symmetrically for an R-split.

## Body

A Hamiltonian H would itself split into a separating two-cover by cutting a Hamilton path between s and t, so H is non-Hamiltonian and every two-cover keeps s,t together. Hence for any cut A=L|R between s,t, neither L union B nor R union B can be Hamiltonian, since such a path together with the opposite inherited subpath would separate s,t. Now delete an interior vertex v. Minimality gives a separating two-cover T of H-v. With zero cross-part edges, two path components cannot cover three nonempty classes; with one crossing edge, cutting it leaves exactly the three whole classes as blocks, so T joins either L-R (violating separation), L-B, or R-B (violating the cut barriers). Thus at least two crossings occur. If exactly two occur, cutting them gives four blocks, so exactly one class splits. A split B must pair crosswise with L,R; any three-block alternative would make L union B or R union B Hamiltonian. If L splits into L_s,L_o, the two-two block case forces L_s-B and L_o-R. In the three-block case only L_s can be isolated; the other isolated choices either join s,t or violate a cut barrier. Finally L_s union {v} must be non-Hamiltonian by minimal-counterexample deletion blocking, so |L_s|>=3. The R-case is symmetric.
