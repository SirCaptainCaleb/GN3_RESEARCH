# From order sixteen every deletion state reaches strict descent, one neutral 5|6 transfer, or a synchronized small-side endpoint package

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=16 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. From the singleton lift P|Q|{x}, three legal pairwise repartitions strictly decrease quadratic potential and reach a spanning three-cover with a Hamiltonian side of order four or five and another component of order at least six. Within the same pairwise-repartition component, at least one of the following then occurs: (1) a further legal repartition strictly decreases quadratic potential; (2) a reachable profile contains a five-side beside a six-path and admits the neutral endpoint transfer (5,6)->(6,5); (3) a reachable four-side X beside a path R=(r_1,...,r_m), m>=6, lies in the hard endpoint package: both X+{r_1}, X+{r_m} are non-Hamiltonian, for every t in X the five-set (X-{t}) union {r_1,r_m} is Hamiltonian and the inherited interior V(R)-{r_1,r_m} plus t is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, and in particular two endpoint-rooted Hamiltonian four-sets sharing three vertices have a Hamiltonian five-vertex union; (4) a reachable five-side Y beside a path R of order at least six has a four-set C subset V(Y) such that C union {r_1} and C union {r_m} are Hamiltonian. For n>=18, outcome (2) is unnecessary: the relevant partner in the five-side branch has order at least seven, so the endpoint transfer is strict whenever it exists.

## Body

Write the deletion cover sizes as p=|P|>=q=|Q|. By bc151d5d2a89, two strict legal moves from the singleton lift reach a state P^-|C|Q^+ with component orders p-1,3,q-1. Since n>=16, p>=ceil((n-1)/2)>=8, so |P^-|=p-1>=7. Apply threesidedescent6 to C|P^-. This is a third strict move. If the one-endpoint branch occurs, the new small side has order four and the remainder of P^- has order p-2>=6. Apply four_side_endpoint_package_m5_01 to that four-side and remainder. Any repartition produced there is strict because the partner has order at least six, yielding (1); otherwise its hard endpoint package gives (3), with pc2 residuals by minimum-counterexample calculus. If the two-endpoint branch of threesidedescent6 occurs, the new small side has order five and the other two component orders are p-3 and q-1. Their sum is n-5>=11. More sharply, max(p-3,q-1)>=6 for n>=16: if both were at most five then p<=8 and q<=6, so p+q<=14 and n<=15. Choose a partner R of order at least six and apply five_side_endpoint_core_m6_01. If its endpoint transfer occurs, it is neutral exactly when |R|=6, giving (2), and strict when |R|>=7, giving (1). Otherwise it gives the common four-core in (4). Finally, if n>=18 then max(p-3,q-1)>=7, since both at most six would imply p<=9,q<=7 and n=p+q+1<=17; hence the neutral equality case cannot arise in the five-side branch.
