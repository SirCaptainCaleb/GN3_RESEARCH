# The corrected one-backward Astra-010 neighbor has no singleton-side two-cover

## Statement

In the setting of astra010to005edge, let G be obtained by correcting the unique backward comparison alpha on ordinary edges ab,bc. Then no spanning two-cover of G has a singleton component.

## Body
