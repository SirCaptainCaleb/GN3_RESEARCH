# Order-thirteen degree-three cyclic kernels force dense crossings or explicit order disagreement

## Statement

In the order-thirteen mu=6 shell, either canonical fixed-complement deletion paths already exhibit relative-order disagreement, or the Hamiltonian-support odd graph has maximum degree at most three while the coupled radius-three graph has minimum degree at least four. In the no-disagreement branch, a degree-three/degree-three odd edge with cyclic precedence yields a structured 5|4|4 cover. If both endpoint triangles are cyclic and both common gaps are endpoint gaps, they force either a forbidden tight seven-path or explicit order disagreement. More generally, if one four-extension of the cyclic kernel is non-Hamiltonian, then the associated canonical deletion cover has at least four inherited-class crossings unless explicit order disagreement has already occurred.

## Body

If some Hamiltonian support has at least four good deletion labels, the four canonical covers with fixed complementary support cannot all agree in relative order, else compatibility gluing would two-cover H. Thus absent canonical disagreement the odd graph has maximum degree three. The odd-edge neighborhood bridge bound then gives minimum degree at least four in the coupled radius-three graph. At a degree-three/degree-three odd edge, the canonical deletion triangles are pairwise compatible and have four-vertex common cores. Cyclic precedence at one endpoint makes the associated five-label kernel Hamiltonian and leaves a non-Hamiltonian exact 4|4 complement, giving the structured 5|4|4 cover. If both endpoint triangles are cyclic and both common gaps are endpoint gaps, comparing their gap sides yields either an order-seven tight path when the endpoint gaps lie on the same side or an explicit reversal/order-disagreement witness when they lie on opposite sides. Finally suppose one four-extension of a cyclic triangle is non-Hamiltonian. The exceptional cyclic-K4/fifth-vertex extension gives a Hamiltonian five-kernel with the opposite endpoint label one position from an end. In the canonical deletion cover, deleting that label splits the kernel into 1|3, and the two common-core insertions contribute one or two crossings each; hence the inherited crossing count is 1+beta_P+beta_Q in {3,4,5}. Equality three means both gaps are endpoint gaps and both triangles cyclic, so the preceding endpoint-gap two-triangle argument yields a forbidden seven-path or explicit disagreement. Therefore a surviving canonical deletion cover has at least four inherited-class crossings.
