# Three-side common barriers force consecutive five-windows at both ends

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-x=P|Q be a deletion two-cover with |P|=3 and Q=(q_0,...,q_s), s>=6. Put X=V(P) union {x}. Then there exist distinct z,zprime in X such that each of the four five-sets F_0={z,zprime,q_0,q_1,q_2}, F_1={z,zprime,q_1,q_2,q_3}, F_{s-3}={z,zprime,q_{s-3},q_{s-2},q_{s-1}}, and F_{s-2}={z,zprime,q_{s-2},q_{s-1},q_s} is Hamiltonian. More precisely, for each k in {0,1,s-3,s-2}, one of (q_k,z,q_{k+1},zprime,q_{k+2}) and (q_k,zprime,q_{k+1},z,q_{k+2}) is a tight Hamilton path. Consequently the left pair F_0,F_1 and the right pair F_{s-3},F_{s-2} each consist of Hamiltonian five-sets sharing four vertices. Hence each six-set U_L={z,zprime,q_0,q_1,q_2,q_3} and U_R={z,zprime,q_{s-3},q_{s-2},q_{s-1},q_s} satisfies the dichotomy of 944fd93bda46: it is Hamiltonian with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement, or it is non-Hamiltonian with at least four Hamiltonian deletions and the associated explicit one- and two-label path-cover-two transport package. The same persistent pair {z,zprime} occurs at both ends.
