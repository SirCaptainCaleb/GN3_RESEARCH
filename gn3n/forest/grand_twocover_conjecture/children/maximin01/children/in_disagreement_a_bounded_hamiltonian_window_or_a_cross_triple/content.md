# Global minimum-side deletion normalization ends in disagreement, a bounded Hamiltonian window, or a cross triple

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let mu be the minimum smaller-component order among all one-vertex deletion two-covers. Then at least one of the following holds:
(1) deletion-cover dynamics exposes an explicit support crossing or relative-order disagreement;
(2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set W of order four or five whose complement is non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two;
(3) there is a globally minimum-side deletion state H-y=R|Q, with |R|=mu, an endpoint z of R, and a tight cross triple (q_s,z,q_0) joining the two displayed endpoints of Q through z.
Thus the globally extremal deletion-state search terminates in the standard endpoint-transport bounded-output menu; no raw two-end barrier state remains.
