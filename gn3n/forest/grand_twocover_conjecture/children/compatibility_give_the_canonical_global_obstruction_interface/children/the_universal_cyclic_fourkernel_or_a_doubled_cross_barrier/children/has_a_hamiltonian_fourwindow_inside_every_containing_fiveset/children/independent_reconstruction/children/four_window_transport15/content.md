# Above order fourteen every anchored four-window descends or yields a bounded path-cover transport package

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=15, let W be a Hamiltonian four-set, and let H-W=P|Q be a two-cover. Then either W|P|Q admits an endpoint-transfer three-cover of strictly smaller quadratic potential, or there exist w in W, a three-set D=W-{w}, one displayed endpoint e of one complement path, and both displayed endpoints f,g of the other complement path such that F_f=D union {e,f} and F_g=D union {e,g} are Hamiltonian five-sets. Put U=D union {e,f,g}. If U is Hamiltonian, then H-U is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two and ham6goodsquare01 yields a full two-label path-cover-two square over H-U. If U is non-Hamiltonian, then its Hamiltonian-deletion set has order at least four and contains f,g; moreover d2205c472e75 gives path-cover-two one-label extensions of H-U for every Hamiltonian deletion label and a minimum-degree-one graph whose edges give path-cover-two two-label extensions. Thus the bounded six-window branch can always be represented by explicit path-cover transport data rather than bare order disagreement.
