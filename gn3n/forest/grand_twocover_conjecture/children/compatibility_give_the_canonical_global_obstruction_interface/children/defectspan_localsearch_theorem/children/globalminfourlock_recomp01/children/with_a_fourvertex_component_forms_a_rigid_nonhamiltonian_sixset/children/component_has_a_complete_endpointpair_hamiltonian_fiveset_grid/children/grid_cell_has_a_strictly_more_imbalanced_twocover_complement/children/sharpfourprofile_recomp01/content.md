# Sharp half-order four-side quadratic minima have exact profile and lambda at most ten

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample in the sharp half-order shell |V(H)|=2lambda+1. Suppose X|P|Q is globally quadratic-minimal with |X|=4 and |P|=m>=r=|Q|>=6. Then m=lambda-1 and r=lambda-2. For every Hamiltonian endpoint-pair five-set F=(V(X)-{x}) union {e,f} arising from the displayed endpoints of P,Q, every two-cover of H-F has component orders lambda and lambda-4. Consequently lambda<=10; equivalently this four-side quadratic branch is impossible for lambda>=11.

## Body

For such an endpoint-pair five-set F, global quadratic minimality gives the certified complement-imbalance inequality. Since m+r=2lambda-3, its parity-strengthened form forces every two-cover U|V of H-F to have larger component at least m+1 and smaller component at most r-2. No tight path has order exceeding lambda, so m+1<=lambda. Together with m>=ceil((2lambda-3)/2)=lambda-1, this gives m=lambda-1 and r=lambda-2. The same bounds then force every complement two-cover to have orders lambda and lambda-4. Using one such F and complement cover produces a competing spanning three-cover of profile 5,lambda,lambda-4. Global Phi-minimality therefore gives 4^2+(lambda-1)^2+(lambda-2)^2 <= 5^2+lambda^2+(lambda-4)^2, which simplifies to lambda<=10.
