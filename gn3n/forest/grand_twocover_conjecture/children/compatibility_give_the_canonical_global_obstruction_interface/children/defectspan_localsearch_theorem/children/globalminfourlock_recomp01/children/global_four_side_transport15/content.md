# From order fifteen a global four-side minimum carries a shared-endpoint path-cover transport package

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=15 and let W|P|Q be a spanning three-cover globally minimizing quadratic potential, with |W|=4. Then the strict-descent alternative of four_window_transport15 is impossible. Hence there exist w in W, D=W-{w}, one displayed endpoint e of one of P,Q, and both displayed endpoints f,g of the other such that D union {e,f} and D union {e,g} are Hamiltonian five-sets. The associated six-set U=D union {e,f,g} carries the certified path-cover-two transport package of four_window_transport15. Moreover, by shared_endpoint_fork_compression01, either one of D union {e}, D union {f}, D union {g} is a Hamiltonian four-set, or there exists d in D such that both (D-{d}) union {e,f} and (D-{d}) union {e,g} are Hamiltonian four-sets. Every such proper Hamiltonian four-set has non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement. Thus a global four-side minimum yields positioned shared-endpoint/forked opposite-end transport and need not terminate at bare order disagreement.

## Body

Since W|P|Q is a global Phi-minimum, it is in particular an anchored cover to which four_window_transport15 applies. Its strict endpoint-transfer descent alternative would produce a spanning three-cover with strictly smaller Phi, contradicting global minimality. Therefore the bounded transport alternative holds, giving w,D,e,f,g and the two Hamiltonian five-sets D+e+f and D+e+g, together with the Hamiltonian/non-Hamiltonian six-set pc2 package stated there. Apply certified shared_endpoint_fork_compression01 to these two five-sets. Either D+e, D+f, or D+g is Hamiltonian, or one d in D makes both (D-d)+e+f and (D-d)+e+g Hamiltonian. Any such four-set is proper because n>=15. Its complement cannot be Hamiltonian, since together with the four-set it would two-cover H; minimum-counterexample calculus therefore gives path-cover number two. This gives the positioned transport conclusion.
