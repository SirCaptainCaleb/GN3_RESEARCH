# The all-equal leave-and-return residue localizes to one edge and two wrap triples

## Statement

In the all-equal endpoint-probe setting of 7ae7f3c03f16, if neither relative-order disagreement nor reciprocal support crossing occurs, then some endpoint deletion cover contains a clean exterior detour replacing one inherited displayed edge xy. For that detour, either there is a tight path on the detour support containing the reversed consecutive pair y,x, or one of the two bounded wrap-extension triples (x,y,u_k),(u_1,x,y) is tight.

## Body

With outcomes (i) and (ii) of 7ae7f3c03f16 excluded, outcome (iii) supplies a split inherited path R all of whose vertices lie in one deletion-cover component. Excluding relative-order disagreement means their order in that component is the displayed order of R. The ordered-excursion lemma therefore identifies two consecutive R-blocks whose intervening exterior segment is a tight detour D=(x,u_1,...,u_k,y) replacing one inherited edge xy. Apply detour_wrap. Its tight-cycle branch gives a tight path on the same detour support with y,x consecutive; otherwise one of the two stated wrap extensions is tight.
