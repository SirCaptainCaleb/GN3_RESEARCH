# Endpoint one-gap unit-surplus corridors admit a finite exterior-count descent

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample, let A=(a_0,...,a_{lambda-1}) be a globally longest tight path, and let C be a tight A-order-preserving path of order lambda-1 that agrees with A outside one common-vertex gap. Assume the unique discrepancy gap is an endpoint gap. Then after finitely many repetitions, each strictly decreasing |V(C)-V(A)|, one reaches one of the following: (1) an endpoint discrepancy kernel with at most one exterior vertex, hence support order at most four; (2) a proper Hamiltonian support of order four or five whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two; (3) explicit order disagreement; or (4) an explicit tight triple reversing an A-edge or a splice junction. In particular, the balanced longest-path exchange alternative of onegapbalancedexchange01 cannot persist indefinitely in an endpoint gap.

## Body

Treat the initial endpoint gap; the terminal case is symmetric. Put O=V(A)-V(C) and E=V(C)-V(A), with |O|=|E|+1. If |E|<=1, onegapbalancedexchange01 gives outcome (1). Assume |E|=r>=2 and write the displayed exterior block of C as e_1,...,e_r before the first common anchor. Apply onegapbalancedexchange01 to the consecutive pair e_1,e_2. Its Hamiltonian-support, order-disagreement, and reversed-junction outcomes give (2)-(4), so suppose its balanced outcome occurs. Then for some z in O the path C_z obtained by replacing e_1,e_2 with e_1,z,e_2 is globally longest of order lambda. Delete its initial endpoint e_1. The resulting path C^- is tight of order lambda-1, is A-order-preserving, begins at z, and has omission/exterior sets O-{z} and E-{e_1}. Let z=a_i. At the surviving common vertex z, its position in C^- is zero and there are no exterior vertices before it. By ba4a66971eb6 with deficit one, unless the positional-lag reversed-edge witness already gives outcome (4), the omission-minus-insertion prefix balance at z is i and must lie in {0,1}. Hence i is 0 or 1. If i=1, the A-suffix (a_1,...,a_{lambda-1}) is a tight path of order lambda-1 differing from A only by the endpoint a_0, so outcome (1) holds with no exterior vertex. If i=0, then z is the initial vertex of A; relative to A the path C^- again differs only in the same initial endpoint gap, but now its exterior set is E-{e_1} of order r-1. Repeat. The nonnegative integer r therefore decreases at every surviving balanced step and after at most r-1 such steps one of (1)-(4) occurs. For a terminal discrepancy gap, use the last consecutive exterior pair, delete the final endpoint after the balanced exchange, and apply the symmetric positional-lag bound to conclude that z is a_{lambda-1} or a_{lambda-2}; the latter gives the A-prefix omitting only a_{lambda-1}, while the former decreases r by one.
