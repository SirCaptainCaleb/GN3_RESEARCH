# Compatibility triangles force endpoint transport or order reversal

## Statement

Let H have path-cover number greater than 2, and let D be deletion labels with chosen two-covers. Assume the branch contains no support-compatible but order-incompatible pair. Fix an anchor F_x=P|Q. If two compatible neighbors y,z of x lie on the same anchor side, say P, then x,y,z form a synchronized family F_t=(X-t)|Q with X=P union {x}. Applying the endpoint-synchronization lemma to the two endpoint deletions of Q yields a common t in {x,y,z} such that both endpoint covers are incompatible with F_t and each has at least two cross-class ordinary edges across (X-t)|(Q-q)|{t}. Moreover, unless an order-reversal/disagreement gadget appears, one of these endpoint covers contains a direct ordinary edge joining X-t to Q-q. Consequently, in the residual branch with neither endpoint transport nor order reversal, every anchor has at most one compatible neighbor on each side, hence total compatibility degree at most 2, at least m-3 support-incompatible crossing covers, and at least one incompatible endpoint deletion on each anchor path.

## Body

Fix F_x=P|Q. Suppose y,z are distinct neighbors of x in the full-compatibility graph and both replace x on the P-side. As in compatibility_degree4_prescribed_fan01, the three covers have the common synchronized form
F_x=(X-x)|Q, F_y=(X-y)|Q, F_z=(X-z)|Q
with X=P union {x}; the structural theorem behind this family gives X non-Hamiltonian and Q Hamiltonian.

Apply the endpoint-synchronization result endpoint_crossing_multiplicity_or_order_disagreement to D0={x,y,z}. For the two endpoint labels q0,qs of Q, choose deletion covers G_q. That result supplies one common t in D0 such that both endpoint covers are incompatible with F_t. Each G_q has at least two ordinary edges crossing the three-way partition
A=X-t, B=Q-q, {t}.
If an endpoint cover has at least three cross-class edges, then one of them must join A directly to B, because the singleton t has ordinary path-degree at most two in a two-path cover. Thus the alternative in endpoint_crossing_multiplicity_or_order_disagreement, 'at least three crossings or a direct mixed A-B edge', collapses to the existence of a direct A-B edge. If neither endpoint cover has such a direct A-B edge, the two neutral endpoint-replacement configurations are exactly the case in which endpoint_crossing_multiplicity_or_order_disagreement produces relative-order disagreement, equivalently the associated reversal gadget. Hence a same-side compatibility triangle forces either endpoint transport by a direct A-B edge or an order-reversal/disagreement output.

Now exclude both of those useful exits. Then no anchor can have two compatible neighbors on the same side. Therefore each side contributes at most one compatible neighbor and deg_C(x)<=2 for every x. Hence every anchor has at least m-3 support-incompatible covers, so forces_a_sharp_linear_family_relative_to_one_fixed_support_cut supplies at least m-3 crossing witnesses relative to that prescribed anchor. Also, among the two endpoint labels of P, at least one must be incompatible with x: if both were compatible they would form a same-side triangle with x. The same holds for the two endpoints of Q. Thus the residual obstruction has endpoint-local incompatible deletion covers on both anchor components.
