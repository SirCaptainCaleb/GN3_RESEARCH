# Deletion-state double-wrap transport compresses directly to mixed support or a complete gap network

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=13 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. Then there is a spanning three-cover S|R|T in the canonical double-wrap form, with S a Hamiltonian four-path, H-S=R|T a two-cover, and after relabelling m=|R|>=5. Put M equal to the displayed interior of R. At least one of the following holds. (1) One legal pairwise repartition of S|R has component orders (5,m-1) and quadratic-potential change 10-2m<=0; the move is neutral only for m=5 and strict for m>=6, hence whenever n>=15 it is strict if this alternative occurs. (2) H contains a proper Hamiltonian induced set U of order four or five meeting both S and V(M), with H-U non-Hamiltonian of path-cover number two; U may be chosen as one S-label plus three consecutive vertices of M, two S-labels plus three consecutive vertices of M, or two S-labels plus the two vertices of one displayed gap of M. (3) m>=7 and every s in S has a second-type failed-insertion obstruction at a distinct displayed gap g(s) of M. For every distinct s,t in S, adjacent gaps force the explicit cross triple of 36fccff06d48, while gaps at distance at least two yield the tight interval path joining s and t through the displayed subinterval between them. Thus every deletion state of order at least thirteen reaches, in one canonical argument, nonincreasing same-component transport, a genuinely mixed bounded Hamiltonian support, or the complete four-label gap network.

## Body

Start from H-x=P|Q. By 8ededbb50716 together with 687bbaa431c3, the singleton lift of this deletion cover admits a canonical double-wrap rotation. Hence there is a spanning three-cover S|R|T with |S|=4, S Hamiltonian, H-S=R|T a two-cover, and both wrap failures positioned exactly as in the double-wrap construction. Relabel R,T so m=|R|>=|T|. Since n>=13, m+|T|=n-4>=9, so m>=5. Let M be R with its two displayed endpoints removed.\n\nApply the four-side endpoint package directly to S|R|T. If S together with one displayed endpoint of R is Hamiltonian, or if one of the synchronized residuals M+{s} is Hamiltonian, there is a legal pairwise repartition of S|R with orders (4,m)->(5,m-1). Its potential change is 25+(m-1)^2-(16+m^2)=10-2m. This proves (1), including neutrality only at m=5 and strictness for m>=6. If n>=15, then m+|T|>=11 and m>=|T| force m>=6.\n\nAssume no such move exists. Then the endpoint package gives, for every s in S, a Hamiltonian five-support F_s=(S-{s}) union {r_1,r_m} and a non-Hamiltonian residual M+{s}. Thus all four labels of S are noninsertable into the displayed path M. Apply the complete failed-insertion classification label by label. A first-type obstruction gives, by 0425e03e2aa3, either a Hamiltonian four-window consisting of one S-label and three consecutive vertices of M, or the exceptional cyclic non-Hamiltonian four-window whose extension by another S-label is a Hamiltonian five-set. Either way we obtain outcome (2), and the support meets both S and M. If two labels have second-type obstructions at the same gap, 36fccff06d48 gives a Hamiltonian four-set on those two labels and the two vertices of the gap, again outcome (2). In every such case the support is proper, so minimum-counterexample calculus gives a non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complement.\n\nIt remains that all four labels have second-type obstructions at pairwise distinct gaps. Hence M contains at least five vertices and m>=7. Fix distinct s,t in S. If |g(s)-g(t)|>=2, the separated-gap clause of 36fccff06d48 gives the tight interval path joining s to t through the corresponding displayed subinterval of M. If |g(s)-g(t)|=1, its adjacent-gap clause gives either a Hamiltonian five-set on s,t and three consecutive vertices of M, which would be outcome (2), or the explicit adjacent-gap cross triple. Since we are in the no-(2) branch, the cross triple is forced. This proves the full pairwise network in (3).\n\nThe former order-15, order-14, order-13, and mixed-support nodes are therefore successive partial presentations of this single argument: the n-threshold arithmetic, the mixed-support strengthening, and the complete gap-network refinement are all internal steps of one theorem and no strength is lost by composing them.
