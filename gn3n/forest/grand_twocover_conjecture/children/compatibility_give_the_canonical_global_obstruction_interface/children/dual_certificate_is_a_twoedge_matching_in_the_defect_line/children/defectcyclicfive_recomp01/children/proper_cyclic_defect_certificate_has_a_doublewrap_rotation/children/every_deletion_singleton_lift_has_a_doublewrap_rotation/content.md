# Every deletion singleton lift has a double-wrap rotation

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-x=P|Q be any deletion two-cover. Form the spanning three-cover P|{x}|Q and concatenate its three displayed paths cyclically. Its cyclic defect graph Gamma has vertex-cover number exactly three. Moreover Gamma cannot have run type {1,1,1}. Consequently this cyclic order is a proper nonmatching minimum-span cyclic defect certificate, so by 687bbaa431c3 some rotation is a double-wrap span-three ordering with the canonical tight reversed four-path and two-cover complement supplied there.

## Body

The three path boundaries of P|{x}|Q form a vertex cover of Gamma, so tau(Gamma)<=3. If tau(Gamma)<=2, cutting the cyclic ordering at at most two selected positions gives a spanning cover by at most two tight cyclic intervals, contradicting pc(H)>2. Hence tau(Gamma)=3. In particular, choosing any minimum vertex cover of Gamma and cutting there gives a spanning ordering of defect span three; since a counterexample admits no ordering of defect span at most two, this is a minimum-defect-span-three ordering. Since n>10 in a minimum counterexample and Gamma has a 3-vertex cover, Gamma is proper rather than the full cycle.

Suppose Gamma had run type {1,1,1}. Then by the certified cube theorem 2923b2b2b233 all eight minimum vertex covers obtained by independently choosing one endpoint of each isolated defect edge give spanning three-covers, and by 810c33003128 their component orders satisfy s_i=g_i+x_{i+1}-x_i. Choose the reference endpoints so that the displayed singleton lift is the 000 state. Then one coordinate of g equals 1. Setting x_i=1 and x_{i+1}=0 for that coordinate (with the third bit arbitrary) gives another cube state whose corresponding path order is 0, contradicting that every cube state is a spanning three-cover. Thus Gamma is not {1,1,1}. The certified nonmatching cyclic theorem 687bbaa431c3 therefore supplies a double-wrap rotation and its canonical four-vertex support/complement structure.
