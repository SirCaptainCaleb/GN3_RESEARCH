# Endpoint Hamiltonicity yields either greedy end-hook transport or a forced coarse support crossing

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let X|C|D be a spanning three-cover, where H[X] is Hamiltonian, C=(c_0,c_1,...,c_m) is a nontrivial tight path with m>=1, and D is a nonempty tight path. Suppose H[X union {c_0}] is Hamiltonian. Then at least one of the following holds. (1) Some Hamilton tight path on X union {c_0} has c_0 as an endpoint; in that case the greedy endpoint-transport theorem 883bd2af7e73 applies and, after absorbing a maximal initial segment c_0,...,c_h of C into X, produces an explicit tight triple reversing the terminal or initial edge of the enlarged Hamilton component. (2) The vertex c_0 is internal in every Hamilton tight path on X union {c_0}; in that case every two-cover of H-c_0 contains an ordinary edge crossing the coarse bipartition X | ((V(C)-{c_0}) union V(D)), and by 5ff05be1a5bc every such deletion cover has at least two crossings of the finer four-class partition obtained by deleting c_0 from a Hamilton path on X+c_0. Thus endpoint Hamiltonicity never remains a structureless local fact: it yields either a reversible component-end edge after finite monotone transport along C, or unavoidable support mixing across the original three-cover cut.

## Body

Choose any Hamilton path R on X union {c_0}. If there exists a Hamilton order with c_0 at one end, apply 883bd2af7e73 in the matching orientation. It greedily appends consecutive vertices of C until the first failed append; boundary antisymmetry then gives a tight triple reversing the current component-end edge. This is outcome (1). If no Hamilton order places c_0 at an end, then c_0 is permanently internal. Apply permanent_internal_coarse_crossing01 to obtain the forced crossing of the coarse cut X | ((C-c_0) union D) in every deletion two-cover of H-c_0. Apply 5ff05be1a5bc to the same Hamilton enlargement to obtain the simultaneous lower bound of two crossings across the finer four-piece split. This is outcome (2). The two cases exhaust Hamilton orders on X+c_0.
