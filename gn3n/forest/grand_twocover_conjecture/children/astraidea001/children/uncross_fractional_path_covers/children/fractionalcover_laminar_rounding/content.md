# Fractional-cover laminar rounding

## Statement

Conjecture that every minimum-mass fractional path cover of a boundary tournament with mass at most two can be transformed, without increasing mass, so that its positive-weight path supports form a laminar family; moreover, any laminar fractional cover of total mass at most two can be rounded to a path cover with at most two tight paths.

## Body

This would separate the grand theorem into a continuous packing statement and a structural rounding theorem. The key is not generic LP integrality, but a boundary-tournament exchange rule strong enough to uncross overlapping positive-weight path supports.
