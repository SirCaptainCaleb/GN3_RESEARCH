# Every deletion singleton component reaches a four-window descent or bounded transport state

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. In the pairwise-repartition component containing P|{x}|Q there is a three-cover W|R|S with W a Hamiltonian four-set and H-W=R|S a two-cover.
