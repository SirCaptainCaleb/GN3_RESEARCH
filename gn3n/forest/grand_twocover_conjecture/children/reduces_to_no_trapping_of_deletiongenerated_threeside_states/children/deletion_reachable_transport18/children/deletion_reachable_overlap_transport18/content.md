# Above order seventeen deletion-generated descent reaches endpoint overlap or a five-side common core

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample of order n>=18 and let H-x=P|Q be any one-vertex deletion two-cover. In the pairwise-repartition component containing the singleton lift P|Q|{x}, three strict quadratic-potential-decreasing moves reach a spanning three-cover with a Hamiltonian side of order four or five. Moreover, within the same component at least one of the following occurs: (1) a further legal pairwise repartition strictly decreases quadratic potential; (2) a reachable Hamiltonian four-side W has another component R=(r_1,...,r_m), m>=7, and there are distinct a,b,c in W such that {r_1,r_m,a,b} and {r_1,r_m,a,c} are Hamiltonian four-sets with non-Hamiltonian path-cover-two complements; (3) a reachable Hamiltonian five-side Y has another component R=(r_1,...,r_m), m>=7, and some four-set C subset V(Y) such that both C union {r_1} and C union {r_m} are Hamiltonian. Thus the former generic reachable four-side transport package can be replaced by positioned endpoint-rooted overlap.

## Body

Start from the singleton lift S0=P|Q|{x}. By bc151d5d2a89, two strict legal pairwise repartitions reach a deletion-generated three-side state S2=X|R|T with |X|=3. Since n>=18, the larger inherited side in S2 has order at least eight, so threesidedescent6 gives a third strict legal repartition. If this move absorbs one endpoint of the long side into X, the resulting reachable state has a Hamiltonian four-side W and the remainder of that long side still has order at least seven. Apply four_side_endpoint_overlap_descent_m6_01 to W and that long component. Its strict-descent outcome gives (1), while its other outcome gives two endpoint-rooted Hamiltonian four-sets sharing three vertices; their complements are non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two, giving (2). If instead threesidedescent6 uses the double-endpoint branch, it produces a reachable Hamiltonian five-side Y. The other two components sum to n-5, so one has order at least seven. Apply bc48e8bb931a to Y and such a long component R. Its strict endpoint-transfer outcome gives (1); otherwise it gives a four-set C in Y extending Hamiltonianly with both displayed endpoints of R, giving (3). Every repartition used before outcomes (2) or (3), and every strict descent in (1), occurs inside the original pairwise-repartition component.
