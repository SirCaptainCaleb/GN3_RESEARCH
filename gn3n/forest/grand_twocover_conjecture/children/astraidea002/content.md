# Balanced two-cover conjecture

## Statement

Every finite boundary tournament on n>=2 vertices has a partition into tight-path supports of orders floor(n/2) and ceil(n/2).

## Body

Unproved, deliberately stronger than the grand conjecture. Seek a global balancing argument: among two-component path forests, maximize covered order and then minimize the difference of component orders. The real claim is that a balanced near-spanning obstruction admits coupled rearrangement, rather than independent endpoint insertion. Potential benefit: a symmetric inductive invariant may be easier to preserve than an arbitrary two-cover. Main risk: balanced Hamiltonian partitions may already fail even when an unbalanced two-cover exists; small non-Hamiltonian four- and five-set configurations are the first hostile tests. No test has been performed and no novelty claim is made.
