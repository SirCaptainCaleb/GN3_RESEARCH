# Two prescribed vertices can be separated by a two-cover

## Statement

For every boundary tournament H and distinct vertices s,t, there is a spanning two-cover whose two components contain s and t separately.

## Body

Unproved strengthening designed to supply an inductive invariant. No endpoint or orientation prescription is imposed. If true, it gives freedom to separate vertices responsible for a failed join; this may allow an induction that keeps two attachment sites in different components. A first weaker target is existence of some pair s,t, chosen by an extremal rule, for which separation remains possible in all relevant proper induced subtournaments. Main risk: a boundary tournament may have a two-cover but force certain vertices together in every two-cover. A structural description of such inseparable pairs could still be useful: one might contract equivalence classes only after proving that tight paths lift through the contraction. No contraction principle is assumed.
