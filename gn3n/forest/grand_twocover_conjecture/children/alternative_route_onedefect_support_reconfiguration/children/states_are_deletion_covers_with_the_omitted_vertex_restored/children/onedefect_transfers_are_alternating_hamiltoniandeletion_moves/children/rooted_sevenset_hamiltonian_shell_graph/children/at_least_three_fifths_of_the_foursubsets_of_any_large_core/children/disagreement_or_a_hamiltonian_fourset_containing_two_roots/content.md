# Four exterior roots over a core of order at least six force order disagreement or a Hamiltonian four-set containing two roots

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament, let X be a vertex set of order m>=6, and let E be four distinct vertices outside X. Then either (1) Hamiltonian paths on overlapping induced supports contained in X union E have order disagreement, or (2) H has a Hamiltonian four-vertex induced set containing at least two vertices of E. More precisely, there exist a four-set D subset X and three distinct roots r,s,t in E for which D union {r}, D union {s}, and D union {t} are Hamiltonian; absent order disagreement, the three pair extensions D union {r,s}, D union {r,t}, and D union {s,t} are Hamiltonian and imply outcome (2).

## Body

Let N=binom(m,4). By the rooted density theorem, each e in E is good for at least (3/5)N four-subsets D of X, where good means D union {e} is Hamiltonian. Thus the total root-four-set incidence count is at least (12/5)N>2N. Hence some four-set D is good for at least three roots r,s,t. For a pair, say r,s, put S_rs=D union {r,s}. If S_rs is non-Hamiltonian, smallset01 gives at least four Hamiltonian one-vertex deletions of this six-set; astra004fourgooddisagree applied to Hamilton paths on four good deletions yields order disagreement. Therefore, absent outcome (1), S_rs,S_rt,S_st are all Hamiltonian. Choose Hamilton paths on these three six-sets. If any pair has different relative order on common vertices, outcome (1) holds. Otherwise the three paths are pairwise compatible, so 92efe67cef7b applies with core D and special labels r,s,t. If D union {r,s,t} is non-Hamiltonian, that theorem puts r,s,t in one common insertion gap of a common D-order; any one of the pair paths then contains a four-consecutive Hamiltonian subpath consisting of two roots and two D-vertices. If D union {r,s,t} is Hamiltonian, take a Hamilton order of this seven-set. Among the three root positions, two have distance at most three, so some block of four consecutive vertices contains both; that block is a Hamiltonian four-set. Thus outcome (2) holds.
