# Failure of fixed-defect second-layer synchronization forces a shell-local reversing triple

## Statement

In the fixed-defect lower-shell setup of descent_or_carries_order_disagreement_inside_the_same_43_layer with inherited tail of order at least six, either two lower 4|3 states admit synchronized strict second-layer descent, or there is a reachable lower-layer state K|T|R and a tight triple contained in the same shell five-set that reverses an ordered edge of the Hamiltonian four-path K. Thus the disagreement alternative upgrades to literal positioned reversal inside the small shell support; the pure tight-cycle residue is impossible because the relevant five-set is non-Hamiltonian and therefore edge-orderable.

## Body

Use the dichotomy of descent_or_carries_order_disagreement_inside_the_same_43_layer. If synchronized second-layer descent occurs, there is nothing to prove. Assume instead that its order-disagreement branch occurs.

In the proof of descent_or_carries_order_disagreement_inside_the_same_43_layer this branch is produced inside a non-Hamiltonian five-set F=W-{z,u}, where z is the fixed persistent defect and u is a nonneighbor of z in the degree-four shell case. At least four vertex deletions F-c are Hamiltonian. Choose Hamilton paths P_c on those good four-sets. The theorem astra004fourgooddisagree gives two of these paths with relative-order disagreement.

Among disagreeing pairs drawn from these Hamilton paths, choose P,Q lexicographically minimizing first the size of their union and then the sum of their orders. Apply orderdisagreement_pair_is_local_or_a_pure_twoarc_tight_cycle.

Outcome (3) of orderdisagreement_pair_is_local_or_a_pure_twoarc_tight_cycle, the pure two-arc tight-cycle case, cannot occur. Indeed F is a non-Hamiltonian five-vertex boundary tournament. By smallset01 it admits an edge-order representation, equivalently its comparison digraph is acyclic. A vertex-simple tight cycle would give a directed cycle of consecutive ordinary edges in that comparison digraph, contradiction.

Hence orderdisagreement_pair_is_local_or_a_pure_twoarc_tight_cycle gives either:
(1) P and Q traverse a common ordinary edge in opposite directions; or
(2) there is already a tight triple on V(P) union V(Q) reversing an ordered edge of P or Q.

In case (2) we are done. In case (1), let ab be an ordered edge of P and ba the same edge in Q. Since P has order four, ab belongs to a consecutive tight triple of P. That tight triple contains the ordered edge ab and therefore reverses the ordered edge ba of Q. Thus again there is a tight triple contained in F reversing an edge of one of the chosen Hamiltonian four-paths.

Finally, every chosen good deletion path, say Q on K=F-c, occurs as the four-side of the lower-layer spanning cover
K | T | {z,u,c}
from the construction in descent_or_carries_order_disagreement_inside_the_same_43_layer. That cover lies in the same constant-Phi lower shell layer generated by the original fixed-z corridor.

Therefore failure of synchronized strict second-layer descent produces a tight triple entirely inside the shell five-set F which reverses an ordered edge of a Hamiltonian four-side belonging to a reachable lower 4|3 corridor state. No generic global disagreement or external tight-cycle consumer is required.
