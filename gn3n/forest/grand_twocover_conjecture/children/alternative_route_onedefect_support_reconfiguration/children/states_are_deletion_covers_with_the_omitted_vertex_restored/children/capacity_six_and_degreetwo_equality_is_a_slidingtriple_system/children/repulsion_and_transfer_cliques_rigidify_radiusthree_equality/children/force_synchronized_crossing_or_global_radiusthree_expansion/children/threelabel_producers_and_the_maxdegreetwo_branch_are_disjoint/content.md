# Correction: synchronized three-label producers and the max-degree-two branch are disjoint

## Statement

In the order-thirteen mu=6 shell, if a Hamiltonian six-set Q has a complementary seven-set X with at least three vertices t for which X-{t} is Hamiltonian, then deg_G(Q)>=3 in the Hamiltonian-support odd graph. Consequently this synchronized three-label fixed-complement branch cannot be combined with the alternative hypothesis that every Hamiltonian six-set has odd-graph degree at most two. Any argument consuming the synchronized endpoint-crossing producer must remain in the deg_G>=3 branch; the max-degree-two branch must instead be handled by radius-three expansion.

## Body

The previous version of this object incorrectly combined two mutually exclusive branches of the order-thirteen odd-graph dichotomy. In the exact odd-graph exchange model, for a Hamiltonian six-set Q with complementary seven-set X, deg_G(Q) is exactly the number of vertices t in X such that X-{t} is Hamiltonian. Therefore a fixed-complement family (X-{t})|Q on three distinct labels already gives deg_G(Q)>=3. It is impossible simultaneously to assume that every Hamiltonian six-set has odd-graph degree at most two. The former theorem was therefore vacuous and must not be used. The correct branch separation is: (i) deg_G>=3 produces the synchronized three-label endpoint-crossing datum; (ii) if no such producer exists, all active odd degrees are at most two and one uses the radius-three expansion theorems instead.
