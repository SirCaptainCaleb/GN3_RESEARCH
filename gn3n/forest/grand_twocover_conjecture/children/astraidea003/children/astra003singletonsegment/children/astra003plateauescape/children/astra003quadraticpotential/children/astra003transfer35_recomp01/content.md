# Quadratic endpoint-transfer barriers reduce a three-side to the 3|5 rotation shell

## Statement

Let C=X|P|Q be a Phi-minimal trapped Astra-003 three-cover. Endpoint transfers smaller than a component-size gap are non-Hamiltonian. Hence a three-side has no partner of order at least six; at order thirteen its surviving large-side profile is 3|5|5. For a 3|5 pair X|P with P=(p_1,...,p_5), X union {p_1,p_5} has a Hamilton path R with endpoints in X, and R|(p_2,p_3,p_4) is a legal equal-Phi rotation.

## Body

For components A,B of orders a<=b, transferring endpoint segments of total size t with 0<t<b-a would lower Phi by 2t(b-a-t), so every such enlargement of A is non-Hamiltonian. If |X|=3 and |P|>=6, the t=1 barrier makes both one-end four-extensions non-Hamiltonian; localextend01 then makes the two-end five-set Hamiltonian, while the t=2 barrier makes that same set non-Hamiltonian, a contradiction. Thus |P|<=5. In the surviving |P|=5 case, the two one-end four-extensions are again non-Hamiltonian, so localextend01 gives a Hamilton path R on X with the two endpoints of P, with endpoints in X. The middle triple of P is inherited, giving the legal 5|3 repartition R|(p_2,p_3,p_4), which preserves Phi.
