# A far-end swap from the saturated same-end state creates reciprocal multiple crossing or a bounded obstruction

## Statement

Continue the exceptional mu=5 one-crossing same-end state. Let A be the original outer path, decomposed as A=(X,Y) with |X|=5, |Y|>=2, while the current mixed long path is M=(C_0,Y); the opposite orientation A=(Y,X), M=(Y,C_0) is symmetric. Let y be the far endpoint of M lying at the far end of Y. Then there exists x in X such that both (X-{x}) plus each endpoint of M are Hamiltonian. For the far endpoint y, either (M-{y}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian, in which case x is noninsertable into the inherited path M-y and gives a bounded failed-insertion obstruction, or it is Hamiltonian, giving an equal-Phi exchange whose new five-side X'=(X-{x}) union {y} has at least two ordinary edges of the original path A crossing the support cut X' | (A-X'). Thus every saturated same-end one-crossing state exposes either a bounded far-end obstruction or reciprocal multiple crossing against the original outer path.

## Body

Apply astra003fivecommoncore to the current five-side X and long component M. Choose x in X such that D=X-{x} extends Hamiltonianly with both endpoints of M. In the displayed same-end form A=(X,Y), M=(C_0,Y), let y be the terminal vertex of Y, which is also the terminal endpoint of M; the other orientation is symmetric. If (M-{y}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian, any insertion of x into the inherited displayed path M-y would Hamiltonize that support, so insert01 supplies a bounded obstruction. Suppose instead that (M-{y}) union {x} is Hamiltonian. Together with the Hamiltonian five-set D union {y}, this is a legal equal-Phi repartition of X union M. The new five-side is X'=D union {y}, entirely inside the original outer support A. Along the old path A, the original X occupies the first five consecutive positions and y is the last vertex, while |Y|>=2. Removing any one x from the first five and adding y creates at least two changes between membership in X' and membership in A-X': one change occurs among or immediately after the original first five positions because exactly one original X-vertex is omitted, and another occurs at the final edge entering y from the preceding Y-vertex. Hence at least two ordinary edges of the old displayed path A cross the new support partition X' | (A-X'). These are reciprocal multiple-crossing witnesses against the new three-cover.
