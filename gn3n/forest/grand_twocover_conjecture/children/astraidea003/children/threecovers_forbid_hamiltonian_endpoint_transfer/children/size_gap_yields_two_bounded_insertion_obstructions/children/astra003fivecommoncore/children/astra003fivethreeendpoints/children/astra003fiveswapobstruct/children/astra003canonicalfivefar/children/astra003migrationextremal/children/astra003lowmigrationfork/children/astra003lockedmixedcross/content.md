# A bridge label locked against both long paths forces direct mixed-support crossing or order disagreement

## Statement

In branch (B) of astra003lowmigrationfork, write A=X-{x}. Thus A is a non-Hamiltonian four-set, A union {e} is Hamiltonian for every one of the four outer endpoints e of P,Q, and x is noninsertable into the displayed orders of both P and Q. Then either H[P union {x}] or H[Q union {x}] is Hamiltonian, in which case every Hamilton order on that enlarged support has relative-order disagreement with the displayed old path; or both enlargements are non-Hamiltonian. In the latter case, for every endpoint e of P, every exact two-cover of K_e=(P-{e}) union Q union {x}=H-(A union {e}) contains an ordinary path edge directly joining P-{e} to Q. Symmetrically, for every endpoint f of Q, every exact two-cover of (Q-{f}) union P union {x} contains an ordinary edge directly joining Q-{f} to P. Thus the fully order-neutral locked-label branch forces direct mixed-support crossings from both ends of both long components.

## Body

Because x is noninsertable into the displayed path P, if H[P union {x}] is Hamiltonian then no Hamilton order on that support can induce the same relative order on V(P): such an order would be obtained by inserting x into P. Hence pathcalc01 gives relative-order disagreement. The same holds for Q. Assume therefore that both P union {x} and Q union {x} are non-Hamiltonian. Fix an endpoint e of P. Branch (B) gives A union {e} Hamiltonian. Its complement K_e=(P-{e}) union Q union {x} cannot be Hamiltonian, or it together with A union {e} would two-cover H; by minimality K_e has path-cover number exactly two. Let T be any exact two-cover of K_e. Suppose no ordinary edge of T joins a vertex of P-{e} to a vertex of Q. Since P-{e} and Q are both nonempty, and T has only two path components, the absence of P-Q transitions forces all vertices of P-{e} to lie in one component and all vertices of Q in the other; otherwise one component would contain vertices from both classes and some consecutive path edge would cross the cut. The remaining vertex x belongs to one of those two components. Therefore the two support classes of T are either ((P-{e}) union {x}) | Q or (P-{e}) | (Q union {x}). But the first x-enlargement is non-Hamiltonian by branch (B), because x is blocked at endpoint e, while the second is non-Hamiltonian by the standing no-order-disagreement assumption. Neither can be the support of a path component, contradiction. Thus every exact two-cover of K_e contains a direct ordinary P-Q crossing edge. The argument for endpoints of Q is symmetric.
