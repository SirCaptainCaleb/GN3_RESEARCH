# The rigid no-direct-crossing residue is a central x-bridge plus one two-block long-side split

## Statement

In the no-direct-P-Q branch of astra003lockedbridgecorrect, every exact two-cover T of K_e has one mixed component whose maximal monochromatic blocks occur as B_P,x,B_Q or B_Q,x,B_P, and one residual monochromatic block B_R from exactly one of the two long-side classes. Hence T contains an explicit tight mixed triple (p,x,q) or (q,x,p), where p belongs to P-e and q belongs to Q. If the T-order on any P- or Q-block disagrees with the corresponding displayed old path order on two common vertices, pathcalc01 gives the standard reversed-edge / reversing-triple / tight-cycle witness. Otherwise all monochromatic blocks preserve the inherited relative orders, and the entire residue is reduced to one order-preserving split of exactly one displayed long path into two nonempty blocks together with one unsplit opposite-side block and the central tight x-bridge.

## Body

By astra003lockedbridgecorrect, after cutting the two cross-class edges of T there are exactly four monochromatic blocks: the singleton {x}, one block on the unsplit long-side class, and two blocks on the split long-side class. Both cross edges are incident with x, one to a P-block and one to a Q-block. Since T is a path forest, those two edges lie in one component through x, and the local order of that component is either B_P,x,B_Q or B_Q,x,B_P. Let p,q be the vertices adjacent to x in that path order. The consecutive triple through x is therefore tight and is (p,x,q) or (q,x,p), giving the explicit central bridge. Each monochromatic block is itself a tight path on vertices belonging to one displayed old path. If its relative order differs from the inherited order on that old path, the ordered-path intersection calculus in pathcalc01 supplies the standard disagreement witness. If no such disagreement occurs, every monochromatic block is order-preserving relative to its old displayed path. Since exactly one old long-side class contributes two blocks and the other contributes one, this leaves precisely the claimed one-side two-block split plus the central x-bridge.
