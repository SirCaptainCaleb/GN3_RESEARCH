# A saturated one-crossing five-side swap forces a full-path lock on the untouched outer component

## Statement

Refine the secondary extremal choice as follows: first maximize mu=|X-C_0| among reachable minimum-Phi states with the canonical component-order multiset, and subject to that minimize kappa=max{|X intersect P_0|,|X intersect Q_0|}. Suppose this refined extremum lies in the exceptional mu=5, one-crossing branch of astra003highmigrationsameend. Then X is contained in one original outer support A, so kappa=5, while the other original outer support Z remains an unchanged long component of order at least seven. There exists x in X such that both (X-{x}) union {z_L} and (X-{x}) union {z_R} are Hamiltonian, where z_L,z_R are the two endpoints of Z. For each endpoint z, (Z-{z}) union {x} is non-Hamiltonian: otherwise the reciprocal exchange x<->z preserves Phi and mu=5 but changes the five-side from five vertices of A to four vertices of A plus one of Z, reducing kappa from 5 to 4. Hence x is noninsertable at every position of the full displayed path Z and carries a bounded insertion obstruction there. If H[Z union {x}] is Hamiltonian in some other order, that order necessarily has relative-order disagreement with Z.

## Body

Choose the minimum-Phi state lexicographically by the finite secondary data (-mu,kappa), i.e. maximize mu and then minimize kappa among mu-maximizers. All conclusions using only maximality of mu remain valid. In the exceptional one-crossing high-migration case, astra003highmigrationcross shows mu=5 and the current five-side X is a residual five-vertex block entirely inside one original outer support A; the other old outer support Z is unchanged. Thus kappa=5. Apply astra003fivecommoncore to the current five-side X and the long path Z. It gives x in X such that, writing D=X-{x}, both D union {z_L} and D union {z_R} are Hamiltonian. Fix z in {z_L,z_R}. If (Z-{z}) union {x} were Hamiltonian, these two Hamilton paths would repartition X union Z with component orders 5 and |Z|, hence give an equal-Phi move. The new five-side contains four vertices of A and one vertex of the other original outer class Z; it is still disjoint from C_0, so mu remains 5, but kappa drops from 5 to 4, contradicting the refined extremal choice. Therefore both endpoint-truncated enlargements of Z by x are non-Hamiltonian. Any insertion of x into the displayed order of Z survives after deleting at least one of the two endpoints, contradicting one of those non-Hamiltonian truncations. Thus x is globally noninsertable into Z; insert01 gives a bounded obstruction. Finally, if Z union {x} is Hamiltonian, no Hamilton order can preserve the relative order of all Z-vertices, since that would be an insertion of x into Z, so pathcalc01 supplies the usual order-disagreement witness.
