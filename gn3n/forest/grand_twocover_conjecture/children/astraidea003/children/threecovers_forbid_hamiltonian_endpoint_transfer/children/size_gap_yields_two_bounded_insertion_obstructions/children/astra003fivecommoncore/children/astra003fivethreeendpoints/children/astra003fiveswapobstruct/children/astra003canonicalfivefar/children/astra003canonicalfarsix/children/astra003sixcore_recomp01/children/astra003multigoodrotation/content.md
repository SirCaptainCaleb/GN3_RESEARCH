# The multi-good-core branch reduces to a bounded common window or a quadratic-neutral 4-to-5 rotation

## Statement

Continue the setting of astra003canonicalfarsix after a successful first far-end exchange at a, and let R=B-b be the inherited opposite outer path, with |B|=m>=7. Suppose at least three vertices of D are good deletions. Then there are distinct good core labels d,e such that K=U-{d,e} is Hamiltonian. For each of d,e, unless there is already a relative-order disagreement or bounded failed-insertion obstruction against R, the label is insertable into the displayed order of R. If both are insertable, then either all successful insertion positions of d and e lie in at most three consecutive gaps of R, or d and e can be inserted simultaneously into R. In the simultaneous branch there is a legal repartition of the post-first-exchange pair (D union {a})|B into K | R^+, where R^+ is a tight path on V(R) union {d,e}, with component orders 4 and m+1. Applying foursidedescentobstruction to K|R^+ yields either two synchronized bounded insertion obstructions on one common inherited middle path, or a one-move descent back to component orders 5 and m whose quadratic potential is exactly the original minimum value.

## Body

By astra003sixcorepair, choose distinct d,e in G_D with K=U-{d,e} Hamiltonian. By astra003canonicaltwocont, for each label separately, either its continuation across the opposite side already gives relative-order disagreement, or its enlarged opposite support is non-Hamiltonian and insert01 supplies a bounded obstruction, or the label inserts into the inherited path R=B-b. Thus only the last case for both d and e remains. Apply astra003twoinsertlocal. If its bounded-window conclusion holds, every successful insertion of either label lies among at most three consecutive gaps of R, as claimed. Otherwise choose separated successful insertion positions and insert d,e simultaneously, obtaining a tight path R^+ on V(R) union {d,e}. Since K=U-{d,e}=(D-{d,e}) union {a,b}, the supports K and V(R^+) are disjoint and their union is exactly (D union {a}) union V(B), the union of the post-first-exchange five-side and B. Hence K|R^+ is a legal repartition of that pair with orders 4 and m+1. Its pairwise quadratic contribution exceeds the minimum pair contribution 5^2+m^2 by [4^2+(m+1)^2]-[5^2+m^2]=2m-8. Now apply foursidedescentobstruction to the four-path K and the long path R^+, whose order is m+1>=8. In its descent branch the quadratic potential drops by 2(m+1)-10=2m-8, exactly cancelling the preceding increase, so the resulting 5|m repartition lies again on the minimum-Phi plateau. In its other branch the theorem supplies two bounded failed-insertion obstructions for the endpoints of K against one common inherited middle path of R^+.
