# Any displayed path of order at least five makes unpositioned five-side existence vacuous

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample containing a displayed tight path P of order at least five. Every five consecutive vertices of P form a proper Hamiltonian five-set whose complement is non-Hamiltonian with path-cover number two. Consequently, in any route that already exposes such a displayed path, the bare existence of some Hamiltonian five-side with path-cover-two complement is automatic and can only be a meaningful progress outcome when additional local positioning is prescribed.

## Body

Five consecutive vertices of a displayed tight path inherit a Hamilton tight path. Minimum counterexamples have order greater than ten, so the five-set is proper. Minimality gives path-cover number at most two for its complement; the complement cannot be Hamiltonian, since the two Hamilton paths would give a spanning two-cover. The conclusion is independent of any square, crossing, or endpoint data.
