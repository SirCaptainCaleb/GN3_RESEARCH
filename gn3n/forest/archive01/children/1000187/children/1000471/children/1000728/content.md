# Replace canonical-form preservation by disturbance persistence

## Statement

Proposal: after the first descent from a canonical central 3- or 5-path state, do not require the same canonical form to survive. Instead mark a reachable three-cover whenever it carries either a canonical central state or a bounded order-disagreement/insertion-obstruction witness inherited from the canonical state. Prove a whole-component transition theorem: every marked state in a trapped connected component of the pairwise-repartition graph either yields a two-cover, yields a strict well-founded improvement, or reaches another marked state before the witness disappears.

## Body

The one-step descent fence shows that lowering the quadratic potential at the initial canonical state is logically insufficient. However the certified canonical five-path developments already produce something stronger than bare descent: far-end equal-potential exchanges create synchronized non-Hamiltonian six-sets, and the corresponding Hamilton orders force intrinsic order disagreement; failed exchanges produce bounded insertion obstructions on inherited outer paths. These are precisely the kinds of witnesses consumed by endpoint transport / defect compression. The redesigned route should therefore preserve a disturbance class, not the literal central form. The missing proof obligation is global: show that a reachable pairwise repartition cannot erase all such marked information at a terminal quadratic minimum without either producing a two-cover or regenerating a marked witness that admits further transport. No claim of such persistence is made here.
