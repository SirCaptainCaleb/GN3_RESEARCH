# Asymptotic two-path packing before exact absorption

## Statement

For every epsilon>0 there exists N such that every boundary tournament H on n>=N vertices contains two vertex-disjoint tight paths whose union has at least (1-epsilon)n vertices.

## Body

Unproved asymptotic relaxation of the grand theorem. Deliberately abandon exact deletion induction initially. Try a regularity, reservoir, or maximal-packing argument that exploits all three middle-vertex orientations on each triple. The goal is a macroscopic two-path packing; exact absorption is a separate later problem. Stronger useful intermediate information would keep many possible ordered endpoint pairs while losing only epsilon n vertices. A single almost-spanning packing with rigid ends may be useless for absorption. Main risks: general directed hypergraph tools may impose local consistency not present in boundary tournaments, and density of tight triples alone may not yield two long disjoint paths. Any imported result would require exact hypothesis checking when literature search is permitted. No external theorem is asserted here.
