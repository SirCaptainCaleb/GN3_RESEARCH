# Every deletion singleton carries two bounded failed-splice witnesses

## Statement

Let H be a minimum counterexample and H-x=P|Q a one-vertex deletion two-cover. Then x is noninsertable into every position of the displayed order of P and also every position of Q. Consequently insert01 supplies one bounded failed-insertion obstruction window on P and one on Q, each involving x and at most four consecutive vertices of the corresponding path. Thus the isolated singleton in the success-only exchange graph is canonically incident to two bounded obstruction certificates, one on each nontrivial component.

## Body

If x could be inserted into the displayed order of P, the enlarged tight path on V(P)∪{x}, together with Q, would be a spanning two-cover of H; contradiction. The same holds for Q. Apply insert01 Section 3 separately. This gives a concrete redesign target for the alternating-exchange brainstorm: replace the missing success edges from the isolated singleton by two local obstruction nodes. Any eventual alternating augmentation theorem must explain how these two obstruction witnesses can be consumed into genuine legal repartitions.
