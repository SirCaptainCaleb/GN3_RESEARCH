# Two overlapping paths with a common internal interval admit a two-cover

## Statement

Let P and Q be paths in a boundary tournament whose supports cover V(H). Suppose their intersection is a nonempty contiguous interval in each displayed order, has at least three vertices, occurs in the same order in P and Q, and avoids both endpoints of both paths. Then H has a spanning two-cover.

## Body

Conjecture, unproved. The four exterior arms meet one common ordered interval. Can reversal antisymmetry always route those arms into two paths without duplicating the interval? First attack: compare the two possible assignments of the common interval to one path, using the other path's two arms as the obstruction. The common interval and arms can be arbitrarily long; the desired proof is uniform. This would turn selected overlapping Hamiltonian supports into an actual cover, rather than treating support overlap alone as sufficient.
