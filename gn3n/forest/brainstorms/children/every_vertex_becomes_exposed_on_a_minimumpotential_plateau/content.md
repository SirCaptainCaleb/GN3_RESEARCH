# Every vertex becomes exposed on a minimum-potential plateau

## Statement

Let H be a smallest-order counterexample and let K be a connected component of the graph of spanning three-covers under pairwise repartition. Let M be the minimum of Phi on K. For every vertex v of H, some cover F in K with Phi(F)=M has v as an endpoint of one of its displayed paths.

## Body

Conjecture, unproved. A vertex that remains permanently internal throughout all minimum-potential states may force more rigidity than a boundary tournament can sustain. First attack: minimize the distance of v from a path endpoint over the minimum-potential states, and use deletion at v to seek an equal-potential state with smaller distance. This is an accessibility assertion, not a claim that arbitrary reversal or singleton descent preserves the minimum. If true it makes endpoint constraints available at every label on the same extremal level.
