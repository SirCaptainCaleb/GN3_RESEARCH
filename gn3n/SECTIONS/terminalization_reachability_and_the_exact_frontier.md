# Terminalization, reachability, and the exact frontier

**Summary:** Terminal edge-surgery coherence closes: every rank-two residue is coherent, because the sole maximal endpoint braid cannot contain a terminal sign-flip edge.

## Statement

Every protected terminal sign-flip surgery can be chosen equivariantly and coherently across all rank-two Coxeter residues, yielding an equivariant map from S_r to Y_{r+1} at every witness depth and closing the Article VII relative-index filtration.

## Composition

## Terminalization of the reflected corridor: reduction to two bounded interfaces

Let \(J=C\sqcup\{x,y\}\) be a reflected span-two double, with \(C\) carrying its corridor two-cover. Then
\[
\kappa_2(H[J])\le2.
\]
If \(\kappa_2(H[J])=2\), no repair supported only on \(J\) exists: an internal outward repair is equivalent to a two-cover of \(H[J]\). Small packet connectors are likewise excluded in this genuine two-deletion layer.

For separated protected determining windows, product structure gives a persistence-or-escape dichotomy. Either the carrier already contains a chamber whose selected witness lies farther outward, or one reflected occurrence persists throughout the face. Introducing the corresponding persistent-face separator shows that the only unbounded zero faces without same-face escape are **double-persistent**: both reflected span-two occurrences persist in every chamber. These chambers are symmetric zero-root states.

In the no-farther-witness branch the outward statuses are forced monochromatic guard paths. After the neutral interior is frozen, each endpoint meets the persistent window in at most two slots. A one-slot successful-buffer sector is contractible. A blocked two-slot reservoir is uniformly polarized and carries rooted inward tight paths; moreover a bounded Hamiltonian support of order four or five can cut one or two vertices from the exposed corridor end. Hence the unbounded corridor has been reduced to two bounded rooted endpoint interfaces.

The remaining carrier topology is also finite. A two-slot pair locus is homotopy equivalent to the clique complex of its mutual-pair graph. On at most four labels, each connected component is contractible or a circle. Natural carrier extension therefore reduces to the two-skeleton, and every nontrivial loop reduces to one chordless four-label cycle. Such a cycle can be filled by at most two exterior labels when the explicit six-label compatibility conditions hold.

Protection does not reintroduce unbounded Coxeter complexity. A witness uses at most six consecutive vertices, so every minimal commuting-cube protection failure is supported in at most eight consecutive positions; an eight-position active band has an eighteen-position dependency halo.

Thus Article VII does not need a global finite terminal theorem. Its terminalization problem is reduced to two precise obligations: construct a protected endpoint handoff in the blocked double-persistent two-deletion state, or fill the resulting four-label carrier loop with protected exterior labels.
