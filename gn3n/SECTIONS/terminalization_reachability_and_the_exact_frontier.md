# Terminalization, reachability, and the exact frontier

**Summary:** Terminal edge-surgery coherence closes: every rank-two residue is coherent, because the sole maximal endpoint braid cannot contain a terminal sign-flip edge.

## Statement

Every protected terminal sign-flip surgery can be chosen equivariantly and coherently across all rank-two Coxeter residues, yielding an equivariant map from S_r to Y_{r+1} at every witness depth and closing the Article VII relative-index filtration.

## Cold composition

## Terminalization, rank-two coherence, and closure of the depth filtration

The finite local-witness line is closed by [[local_witness_topology_and_the_finite_terminal_theorem]]: every terminal centered or overlapping determining support has order at most ten and is two-coverable. The remaining global obligation is to turn pointwise outward terminal surgery into a coherent equivariant map on the separator of the witness-depth filtration.

Order the fixed witness-path edges from the center outward,
[
e_1,ldots,e_d,qquad d=m-2,qquad m=n-2.
]
At depth (r), work in the protected region in which every chamber avoids (e_1,ldots,e_{r-1}). The affine zero set of the (e_r)-sign coordinate gives an invariant separator (S_r), and the relative-(mathbb Z_2)-index argument gives
[
gamma(S_r)ge gamma(Y_r)-1.
]
It therefore suffices to construct an equivariant map
[
S_rlongrightarrow Y_{r+1},
]
where (Y_{r+1}) is the existential next-depth face space: a proper permutahedron face belongs as soon as it contains an outward chamber whose selected witness has depth (>r).

### Pointwise outward surgery and minimal separator faces

For a terminal centered or overlapping configuration, [[terminal_support_surgery_gives_a_genuine_outward_escape]] reorders the full terminal positional span by a local two-cover. This removes every selected witness of depth at most (r); any new boundary effect lies strictly farther outward. The replacement chamber need not remain in the source face, and [[a_local_pure_carrier_shows_same_face_escape_is_false]] shows that same-face escape is false.

By [[minimal_separator_faces_and_terminal_edge_surgery_coherence]], the minimal non-outward separator faces are therefore terminal sign-flip Coxeter edges. Higher separator cells impose only compatibility among their outward replacements. Since the type-(A) Coxeter relations are generated by commuting squares and braid hexagons, it remains to verify rank-two coherence.

### Ten-position normalization and one-vertex exchange

By [[ten_position_normalization_of_terminal_surgery]], for (nge11) every terminal repair may be normalized to a contiguous ten-position interval (J) containing the terminal determining span (I). A generator internal to (J) preserves the support and may be collapsed by using the same repaired chamber. A generator disjoint from (J) commutes with the repair. A generator crossing one endpoint changes the ten-set by exactly one vertex.

The endpoint exchange is controlled by [[compatible_two_covers_under_a_one_vertex_terminal_support_exchange]]. If (|T|=9) and (x,y
otin T), there is a partition
[
T=Asqcup B,qquad |A|=4,quad |B|=5,
]
such that (B,A+x,A+y) are Hamiltonian. Thus the adjacent ten-supports (T+x) and (T+y) admit (5|5) covers sharing the entire Hamiltonian side (B), and their replacements lie in a common proper next-depth carrier.

### Commuting squares and nonmaximal braids

The safe-transport/collapse argument of [[rank_two_normalization_squares_close_and_only_the_inside_boundary_braid_remains]] closes every commuting square. For disjoint generators, internal directions are collapsed and safe directions are transported; if both directions are safe, the disjoint swaps commute, so the repaired chambers form a point, edge, or commuting square in the next-depth carrier space. No simultaneous four-support compatibility statement is needed.

The same normalization closes every braid unless one generator is internal to (J), the adjacent generator crosses an endpoint of (J), and the determining span itself already has maximal order ten. If (|I|le9), the ten-window has slack and can be shifted one position toward the braid, making the whole braid triple internal; the residue then collapses. Thus only the maximal order-ten endpoint braid requires attention.

The proposed external-gauge shortcut in [[rank_two_coherence_closes_by_the_external_gauge]] is not used. As recorded in [[audit_the_external_gauge_does_not_by_itself_close_rank_two_coherence]] and [[independent_audit_external_gauge_does_not_close_the_maximal_a2_braid]], the actual labeling uses intrinsic witness orientation whenever exactly one orientation is represented; the external gauge is only a tie-break when both orientations occur (and in the centered self-reverse case).

### The maximal endpoint braid cannot carry a terminal sign flip

The maximal ten-vertex terminal type is necessarily the reflected alternating type. Its two determining windows each use six consecutive vertex positions, and order ten means that their status-word starts differ by exactly four. After translating indices, the corresponding four-bit occurrences occupy positions (1,ldots,4) and (5,ldots,8), each equal to one of
[
0101,qquad 1010.
]

**Lemma.** A protected chamber cannot contain alternating occurrences at both maximally separated reflected starts.

**Proof.**
There are four possible concatenations. In each, a dual-polarity forbidden word occurs strictly between the two reflected starts:
[
egin{array}{c|c}
0101,0101 & 1010	ext{ one step inward},\
1010,1010 & 0101	ext{ one step inward},\
0101,1010 & 011	ext{ two steps inward},\
1010,0101 & 100	ext{ two steps inward}.
end{array}
]
Each displayed inner word belongs to
[
001, 011, 0101, 110, 100, 1010.
]
Its witness-path edge is strictly closer to the center than the selected maximally separated edge, contradicting protection. (square)

Now let (E={pi,pi s_k}) be a protected terminal sign-flip edge for this maximal unsigned witness edge. Suppose (s_k) is disjoint from the left six-vertex determining window. Then the left alternating occurrence has the same truth value at both endpoints. Since the labels on (E) are opposite orientations of the same unsigned witness edge, one endpoint is left-oriented and therefore contains the left occurrence. Hence both endpoints contain the left occurrence. The right-oriented endpoint also contains the right occurrence, so it contains both maximally separated occurrences, contradicting the lemma. The same argument with left and right exchanged gives:

**Endpoint exclusion lemma.** A terminal sign-flip Coxeter edge of maximal ten-vertex reflected-alternating type must have its adjacent transposition meet both six-vertex determining windows. In particular, no generator supported near either endpoint of the ten-position span can be such a sign-flip edge.

This excludes exactly the residue left by the rank-two normalization. Up to reversal, its two generators swap the last two positions of the ten-span and the last position with the first position outside it. Both are disjoint from the far six-vertex determining window. Hence neither can be a terminal sign-flip edge of the maximal type. The purported final (A_2) braid therefore contains no terminal surgery site, and its coherence condition is vacuous.

### Terminal edge-surgery coherence

Every rank-two residue is now coherent:

1. commuting squares close by internal collapse, disjoint-swap transport, and the one-vertex exchange carrier;
2. braid hexagons with determining span at most nine close after shifting the ten-position normalization window, or by safe transport when appropriate;
3. the only unshiftable braid would have maximal ten-vertex support, but the endpoint exclusion lemma shows that its endpoint generators cannot be terminal sign-flip edges.

Choose the local data on one representative of each reversal orbit and reverse the choices on the opposite orbit. Reversal preserves witness depth and terminal type, so the carrier assignment is equivariant. The Coxeter rank-two relations therefore satisfy the hypotheses of the existing acyclic-carrier extension, yielding
[
S_rlongrightarrow Y_{r+1}
]
equivariantly for every depth (r). Consequently
[
gamma(Y_{r+1})gegamma(Y_r)-1.
]

There are (d=m-2) witness depths and (gamma(Y_1)=m+1). Iteration gives
[
gamma(Y_{d+1})ge (m+1)-(m-2)=3,
]
so the final witness-free level is nonempty. A witness-free chamber avoids (001,011,0101), hence satisfies the exact two-cover criterion.

Thus, within the established Article VII filtration and finite-terminal framework, the terminal edge-surgery coherence lemma is proved and the remaining reachability frontier closes.
