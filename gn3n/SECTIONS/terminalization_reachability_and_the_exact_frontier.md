# Terminalization, reachability, and the exact frontier

**Summary:** Terminal edge-surgery coherence closes: every rank-two residue is coherent, because the sole maximal endpoint braid cannot contain a terminal sign-flip edge.

## Statement

Every protected terminal sign-flip surgery can be chosen equivariantly and coherently across all rank-two Coxeter residues, yielding an equivariant map from S_r to Y_{r+1} at every witness depth and closing the Article VII relative-index filtration.

## Composition

## Minimum-counterexample reduction and the exact zero-root frontier

Let \(H\) be a minimum-order counterexample. Minimum-order heredity gives \(\kappa_2(H)=1\).

The deletion-cover side admits a scale-independent structural collapse. Cubical parity promotes partial source stars to full stars; minimum-degree full stars impose uniform exterior polarity and saturate Johnson blocks. If a nonempty antipodally invariant closed deletion residue survives, minimum-counterexample heredity forces the exact odd middle layer:
\[
n=2r+1,
\]
every \(r\)-set is Hamiltonian and every \((r+1)\)-set is non-Hamiltonian. Hamiltonian prefix chains further show that any nonempty closed family of actual deletion edges is the whole middle interface.

In this odd uniform residue, every exterior vertex reverses both exposed end-edges of every maximum \(r\)-path. Actual insertion records yield genuine augmentation whenever two witnesses occur in separated gaps, or in the compatible adjacent-gap configuration. A four-vertex boundary tournament shows that Hamiltonian one-vertex extensions need not arise from insertion into a Hamiltonian order of the core, so support Hamiltonicity alone does not supply the needed compatibility.

The exact-root route is sharper in a minimum counterexample. At \(\kappa_2=1\), augmented role balance reduces every nonzero carrier to a freely permuted four-vertex equal-side central block. Boundary antisymmetry excludes that block. Hence every minimum counterexample contains a zero exact-root chamber \(p=c\); the nonzero exact-root recurrence is eliminated.

Deleting a root vertex from an actual Hamiltonian one-vertex extension leaves at most two adjacent status defects. If both bridge triples fail, boundary reversal exposes a tight reversed four-vertex seam. Independently, two maximum paths with a common endpoint cannot have mutually exterior next vertices in the same endpoint role. More generally, an odd cycle of rooted Hamiltonian supports whose consecutive supports intersect exactly at the common root forces a path one vertex longer. In the odd uniform residue that longer path, together with its Hamiltonian complement, gives a spanning two-cover.

Thus the current frontier is exact: start from a zero exact-root chamber in a minimum counterexample and force either a compatible rooted endpoint cycle / same-role pair, or exploit the adjacent two-defect seam to contradict minimum counterexamplehood.
