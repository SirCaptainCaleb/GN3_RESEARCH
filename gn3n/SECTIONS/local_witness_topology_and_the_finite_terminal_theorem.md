# Local-witness topology and the finite terminal theorem

**Summary:** Local forbidden-pattern topology reduces every terminal witness obstruction to a two-coverable support of order at most ten.

## Statement

The exact two-cover language has three minimal forbidden patterns. Encoding their reflected occurrences by an oriented witness path yields an odd map whose balanced carriers contain opposite copies of the same local obstruction. Face symmetry then compresses every terminal carrier to a finite support, and every such terminal support is two-coverable.

## Cold composition

## Positive local witnesses and the terminal carrier

Use the positive witness family
\[
\mathcal W_+=\{001,011,0101\},
\]
which is exactly the local obstruction to the inversion-window criterion and is closed under reverse-complement. Protection and repair are defined using this one predicate throughout.

The five-position obstruction and ordered-tuple compression bound every centered or overlapping interaction. Reflected alternating doubles are also bounded, and exclusive disjoint alternating carriers do not survive protection. The only unbounded terminal geometry is therefore a reflected span-two double:
\[
\boxed{\text{terminal carrier}
\Longrightarrow
\text{support of order at most ten, or a reflected span-two double corridor}.}
\]

The bounded branch is discharged by the established ten-vertex two-cover theorem. In the unbounded branch the interval between the reflected determining windows is \(\mathcal W_+\)-free, hence has a two-cover by the inversion-window theorem. This leaves one explicit corridor geometry for terminalization.
