## Development

In an interval-saturated no-two-cover state with kappa_2>=2, every seam gives either bounded/transversal disturbance or a pure cross-seam four-support. A pure support either reseeds or descends. Reseeding is support-preserving, hence creates an order-four complementary rail and returns in one seam step to bounded/low-distance structure, or else yields an explicit cross-residual reversal. If both opposite pure seams descend, interval-preserving saturation restarts on shorter contiguous rails; each full round lowers both rail orders by at least four. Therefore pure seam dynamics have no unbounded terminal residue: they terminate in reversal/comparison disturbance, bounded seam structure, bounded reseeding, or deletion distance at most two. The split-hole equality packet is impossible by minimum-hole synchronization.
