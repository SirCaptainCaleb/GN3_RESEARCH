## Development

A 17-label one-hole finite model was built for the long-rail regime. The frame consists of the hole x together with four visible vertices at each of the initial and terminal ends of P and Q. For every one of the 17 possible deleted labels d, the model does not merely assert that the remaining 16 labels admit a two-cover: it explicitly chooses a directed two-path cover, forces exactly two acyclic path components, forces both components to have order at least three, enforces tightness of every consecutive triple, and imposes the one-hole four-end reversal relations for d at the first and last edge of each chosen path. The distinguished deletion cover H-x=P|Q is also required to satisfy the inherited rail triples and the standard x four-end reversals. In addition, all interval-preserving two-cover reroutings of the two long inherited rail interiors by the five movable endpoint/hole labels are forbidden, and every positive opposite-boundary cross entry h(a,x,b)=1 is coupled to the two-sided next-boundary shield disjunctions. This strengthened local minimum-counterexample model remains feasible. One feasible solution has h(q_1,x,p_r)=1, so the rooted-five-path/root-advance branch survives. Separately, forcing all four opposite-boundary cross entries h(a,x,b) to zero, equivalently the complete reverse rectangle R ->_x L in the link tournament T_x, also remains feasible. Therefore local minimum-counterexample two-coverability of every proper deletion, even when fully coupled to its four-end reversal data, does not by itself eliminate either one-hole endpoint branch. Additional global structure such as the fixed-hole Ky Fan dichotomy or maximal-support disturbance conversion is genuinely required.
