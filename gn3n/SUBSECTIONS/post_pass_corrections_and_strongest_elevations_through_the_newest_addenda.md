## Post-pass corrections and strongest elevations through the newest repair addenda

This addendum records the corrections forced by reading the composed Article VII and every repair addendum in chronological order through the current development horizon.

### 1. Correct the root-advance recursion status

[[three_common_reversers_force_a_three_cover_by_finite_root_advance]] is not established as stated. [[root_advance_recursion_drops_three_corridor_vertices_without_a_covering_lift]] correctly observes that its recursive call passes from the original vertex set to a smaller set after removing three corridor vertices, but does not provide a path-count-preserving lift covering those removed vertices. Termination of the local state recursion is therefore not a spanning three-cover proof.

Consequently any later result using that theorem to assert pc <= 3 for the original three-hole core is conditional. In particular [[every_large_minimum_deletion_hole_contains_canonical_deletion_distance_three_three_cover_cores]] correctly proves kappa_2(G_U)=3 for every three-subset U of a minimum hole, but its assertion pc(G_U)=3 is not established by the cited recursive theorem.

The earlier pair-core reduction [[minimum_holes_reduce_to_genuine_two_deletion_states_with_a_five_component]] remains valid: it uses the one-step five-path from [[three_common_initial_reversers_force_a_root_advancing_five_path]] directly, deletes the other k-2 hole vertices, and obtains a genuine kappa_2=2 induced state with an explicit spanning three-cover containing a five-component. No recursion is used.

### 2. Promote small-complement connector exclusion to the earliest deletion-distance toolkit

The generic fact behind Addenda 85 and 87 is stronger and earlier than the packet applications:

If R is any tight path and its complement becomes Hamiltonian after at most t deletions, then kappa_2(H)<=t. Since every set of order at most six becomes Hamiltonian after at most one deletion, kappa_2(H)>=2 implies every tight path leaves at least seven vertices.

Hence whenever a packet of order at most seven lies between two tight tails, no nonempty packet fragment may join those tails in a genuine kappa_2>=2 state. This should be applied before bridge-counting lemmas. In the genuine two-deletion layer, "three bridge candidates suffice" is not the operative restriction: even one connector path is forbidden. Failed short joins should immediately be converted into reverse-junction equations.

### 3. Introduce genuine reflected doubles as minimum-hole Boolean squares

Once the positive corridor classification gives J=C union {x,y} with kappa_2(H[J])=2, the endpoint set {x,y} is a two-element minimum deletion hole. Hereditary exactness gives the square of deletion distances 2,1,1,0, while absolute nonaugmentability gives all four exposed-end reversals and forbids every internal assignment of x,y to the two corridor paths.

Thus the one-deletion states, four-end reversal theorem, and internal nonabsorbability should be derived from minimum-hole theory rather than reproved separately in terminalization.

Together with [[outward_repair_inside_a_double_span_is_equivalent_to_a_two_cover]], this also moves the no-internal-repair statement earlier and removes its unnecessary kappa_2=2 hypothesis: for any reflected-double span J, an outward reorder supported on J exists iff H[J] has a two-cover.

### 4. The persistent-face separator is the correct global elevation

The five-position obstruction should be used first at the separator level. For separated reflected windows, a protected face either contains an outward chamber or has a persistent orientation. The face-level persistent label then shows that the only unbounded zero faces without immediate escape are double-persistent.

Every chamber in this branch is simultaneously a positive reflected double and a symmetric zero-root chamber. This identifies the same interface seen by the exact-root route.

Same-face outward loci cannot be assumed acyclic: they may be disconnected or have arbitrary pair-locus clique-complex topology, including S^1. On a saturated zero-face poset, existence of a natural D(F)=F cap X_{r+1} carried map would force every D(F) contractible. Therefore enlarged protected target carriers are not optional in the obstructed cases.

### 5. Endpoint reservoirs split into solved and rooted branches

After freezing neutral interior factors, a one-slot endpoint reservoir has a contractible successful-buffer sector. If the success set is empty, every reservoir label is uniformly blocked.

A persistent two-slot endpoint reservoir is even more rigid: every ordered pair is uniformly polarized, and triples of reservoir labels give rooted Hamilton four-paths at the fixed inner corridor vertex. No arbitrary ordered-pair topology theorem is needed in the double-persistent two-slot branch.

If there is no farther witness, the outward status rays are monochromatic tight guard paths. A blocked endpoint therefore supports bounded endpoint-cutting Hamiltonian packets while leaving the remaining corridor as a contiguous tight tail. The correct missing combinatorial theorem is directional endpoint transport / repartition, not fixed-root absorption and not unoriented endpoint exposure.

### 6. General locality elevation for protected carrier compatibility

The new eight-position results are instances of a general theorem. If every witness in a filtration is determined by at most L consecutive positions, then a minimal protection failure in a cube of pairwise commuting adjacent swaps has every essential swap support meeting the new witness interval. All essential swaps therefore lie in at most L+2 consecutive positions.

For W_+={001,011,0101}, L=6, so minimal commuting failures are eight-position local. Every witness whose truth may change across that residue lies in the five-position halo on each side, giving an eighteen-position dependency halo. This belongs at the introduction of any bounded-window witness filtration, not at the end of terminalization.

These are localization statements only. Applying an equitable cover to the active eight positions does not automatically preserve protection across the halo.

### 7. Two-skeleton topology is finitized independently of the combinatorial branch

When natural outward carriers factor into one-variable loci and ordered-pair loci on reservoirs of order at most four, all carrier components have no homotopy above dimension one. Thus extension above the two-skeleton is automatic once vertices, edges, and triangular loops are handled.

Every nontrivial loop is detected in one four-label ordered-pair factor whose mutual graph is a chordless C4. One exterior label fills this loop only if it is mutually adjacent to all four old labels; two exterior labels can fill it under the explicit six-triangle disk conditions. Boundary antisymmetry alone does not force either fill.

Hence the repaired Article VII frontier has two finite but distinct obligations:
- directional endpoint transport on the blocked double-persistent minimum-hole interface;
- component/loop compatibility for enlarged protected carriers, with every genuine loop obstruction reduced to one four-label C4 and at most a six-label filling test.

The grand theorem is not closed by this pass. The elevation pass instead removes the obsolete unbounded targets and identifies exactly which later claims are conditional or superseded.
