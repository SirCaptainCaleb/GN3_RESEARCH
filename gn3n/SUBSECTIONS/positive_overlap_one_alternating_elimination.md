## Development

For reflected 0101 windows with status starts 1 and 6, positive inward protection gives: left occurrence implies s3=s5=s7=0 and s4=1,s6=0; by reverse-complement symmetry right occurrence implies s3=s5=s7=1 and the same s4=1,s6=0. Thus the nonconstant side indicator is simultaneously read from s3 and s7. The unique bridging face block B has size at most four by the five-position obstruction and must contain positions 5,6,7. Hence B is [5,7], [4,7], or [5,8]. In these cases the side indicator depends respectively on first/last tuple sizes (1,1), (2,1), or (1,2). Ordered-tuple compression forces |B| at most 2,3,3, contradicting the actual block sizes 3,4,4. Hence overlap-one alternating terminal carriers are impossible using only the positive forbidden words. Together with the disjoint-alternating elimination, every surviving alternating terminal support has order at most ten.
