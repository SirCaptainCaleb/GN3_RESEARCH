## Development

In a saturated kappa_2=2 state S|P|Q, each oriented seam P->Q and Q->P yields a Hamiltonian four-support K by the deletion-critical seam theorem. For every Hamiltonian K, the support-complement lower bound gives Hamiltonian deletion distance at least two for H-K. Hence either pc(H-K)>=3, giving an explicit induced no-two-cover state on n-4 vertices, or pc(H-K)<=2. In the latter case H-K is not Hamiltonian (otherwise K and H-K two-cover H), so pc(H-K)=2 and K is a new admissible seed for seed-preserving maximalization. Therefore each of the two opposite seams independently yields either four-vertex hereditary descent or bounded-seed re-entry into maximal-support theory. This is the same global fork already obtained from surviving carrier loops.
