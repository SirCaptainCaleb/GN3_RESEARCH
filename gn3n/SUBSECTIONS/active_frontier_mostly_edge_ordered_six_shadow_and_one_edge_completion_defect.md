## Development

Let U=C union {a,b}, |C|=4, with H[C], H[C+a], and H[C+b] non-Hamiltonian. Each bad five-set is edge-orderable. The active frontier is to amalgamate the two edge-order representations over C. The target normal form is a common acyclic comparison order on E(K_U)-{ab} realizing every comparison not involving both a and b. In the canonical six-set matching-block exception, the four mixed comparisons ac versus bc are already prescribed by the fixed-pair orientation classes, so the remaining obstruction should be concentrated at insertion of the single shadow edge ab. Closure target: prove that either this almost-order extends to a full edge order, or the first obstruction to inserting ab forces a Hamiltonian four/five-support suitable for the Article VII handoff. This is the current preferred frontier because it replaces two unrelated bad K5 structures by a one-edge defect in an almost globally ordered K6 shadow.
