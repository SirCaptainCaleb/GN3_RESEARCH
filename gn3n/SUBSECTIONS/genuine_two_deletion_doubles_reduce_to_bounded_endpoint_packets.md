## Development

Work in the positive witness filtration. Let J=P sqcup Q sqcup {x,y} be a protected reflected span-two double with kappa_2(H[J])=2. Then P,Q are tight paths of order at least four, and both exterior vertices reverse both exposed end-edges of both P and Q. Hence, at each displayed end edge of P and Q, there are two reverse tight triples through the same edge, one using x and one using y. The local two-reverse-triples lemma from endpoint_transport_and_small_support_gluing therefore applies independently at each end: unless an earlier local two-cover or Hamiltonian four-support already appears, that endpoint produces two Hamiltonian five-sets with a common four-set. Consequently the unbounded corridor has bounded interface complexity: after factoring the untouched interiors of P and Q, every unresolved obstruction is carried by finitely many endpoint packets of order at most five sharing the exterior pair {x,y}. This does not yet prove a two-cover or an outward repair; the old global descent from the common-four-core gadget uses minimum-counterexample hypotheses and is not imported here. The exact remaining local theorem is a rooted packet-gluing statement: these bounded endpoint packets must either absorb x,y into P,Q, create a positive witness farther outward, or force a two-cover of J after using at least one vertex outside J.
