## Development

Retain the six-root common-core state: Y=B union {z}, R=H-Y, C=Y-{b_*}, and E subset R with |E|>=6.

Choose any pair u,v in C and any three distinct roots x,y,w in E. By [[three_exterior_roots_force_a_mixed_four_support_over_every_prescribed_core_pair]], one of {u,v,x,y}, {u,v,x,w}, {u,v,y,w} is Hamiltonian. Call this four-set K.

If pc(H-K)<=2, K enters [[maximal_support_normalization_applies_directly_to_article_vii_bounded_outputs]].

Otherwise pc(H-K)>=3, so deleting the four vertices of K leaves a smaller no-two-cover induced state.

Hence every surviving carrier loop has the exact structural fork:
- maximal-support entry through a Hamiltonian four-support; or
- hereditary descent by four vertices.

The current research restriction treats the second item as a reduction, not standalone closure.

This supersedes the quiet common-core density residue for purposes of bridging the carrier loop to the combinatorial frontier: three exterior roots already force the required bounded four-support analytically.
