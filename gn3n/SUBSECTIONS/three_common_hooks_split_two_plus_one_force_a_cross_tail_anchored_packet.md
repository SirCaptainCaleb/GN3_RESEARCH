## Development

Let (a,w) be an ordered anchor pair and let p_1,p_2,q be three distinct exterior labels with h(a,w,p_1)=h(a,w,p_2)=h(a,w,q)=1. Then there is a Hamiltonian support of order four or five containing {a,w} and containing q together with at least one of p_1,p_2. Suppose not. The ordered-anchor three-hook theorem applied to {p_1,p_2,q} says either the full five-set {a,w,p_1,p_2,q} is Hamiltonian or one of its three anchor-containing four-subsets is Hamiltonian. By hypothesis the full five-set and both mixed four-subsets {a,w,p_1,q}, {a,w,p_2,q} are non-Hamiltonian. Therefore the theorem forces {a,w,p_1,p_2} Hamiltonian. But then the full five-set is non-Hamiltonian and has at least two non-Hamiltonian four-subsets, namely the two mixed anchor four-sets, contradicting the non-Hamiltonian-five-set theorem that at most one four-deletion can be non-Hamiltonian. Hence the mixed Hamiltonian 4/5-packet exists. In maximal-support normalization, take (a,w)=(s_2,s_1), p_1,p_2 to be the two endpoints of P, and q any endpoint of Q. Thus a cross-tail anchored Hamiltonian 4/5-packet is forced already at one end of S. The previously recorded same-tail-only residue is impossible.
