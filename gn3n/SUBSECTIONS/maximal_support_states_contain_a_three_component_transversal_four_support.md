## Development

Let H have no spanning two-cover and let S|P|Q be a maximal-support normalization: S=(s_1,...,s_k) is Hamiltonian of maximum cardinality among supports with two-coverable complement, and P=(p_1,...,p_m), Q=(q_1,...,q_t) form a two-cover of H-S, with k,m,t at least two. Then H contains a Hamiltonian four-support meeting all three displayed components S,P,Q. Indeed P union Q is non-Hamiltonian, since otherwise it together with S would two-cover H. Consider the displayed concatenation (P,Q). At least one of its two junction triples fails. If h(p_{m-1},p_m,q_1)=0, boundary antisymmetry gives h(q_1,p_m,p_{m-1})=1, so q_1 reverses the terminal edge of P. Maximal-support endpoint nonaugmentability also gives h(q_1,s_k,s_{k-1})=1, so the same vertex q_1 reverses two vertex-disjoint terminal edges, one in P and one in S. The common-reverser argument yields a Hamiltonian four-set contained in {q_1,p_m,p_{m-1},s_k,s_{k-1}}, hence meeting Q,P,S. If instead h(p_m,q_1,q_2)=0, then h(q_2,q_1,p_m)=1, so p_m reverses the initial edge of Q. Maximality gives h(s_2,s_1,p_m)=1, so p_m reverses the initial edge of S as well. The initial-initial common-reverser argument again gives a Hamiltonian four-set meeting all three components. Thus every nontrivial maximal-support state contains an anchored four-support transverse to S|P|Q. No minimum-counterexample induction or finite enumeration is used.
