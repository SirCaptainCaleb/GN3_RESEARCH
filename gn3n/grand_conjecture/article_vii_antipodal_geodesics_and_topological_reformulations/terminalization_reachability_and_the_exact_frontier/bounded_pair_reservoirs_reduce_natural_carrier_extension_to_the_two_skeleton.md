# Bounded pair reservoirs reduce natural carrier extension to the two-skeleton

### Reduction to the two-skeleton

Suppose a natural outward carrier factors into neutral factors, one-variable endpoint factors, and pair factors on reservoirs of order at most four. Neutral and one-variable factors are contractible, and every connected pair factor is contractible or a circle. Hence each carrier component is homotopy equivalent to a product of circles and has
\[
\pi_j=0\qquad(j\ge2).
\]
Once component choices, edge paths, and triangular loops are controlled, every higher-dimensional carrier extension follows. The topological obstruction is therefore entirely two-dimensional.
