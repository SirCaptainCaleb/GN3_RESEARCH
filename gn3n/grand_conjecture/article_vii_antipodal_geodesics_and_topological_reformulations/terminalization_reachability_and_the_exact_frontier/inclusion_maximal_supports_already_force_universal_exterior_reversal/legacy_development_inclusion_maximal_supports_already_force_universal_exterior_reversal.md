# Inclusion-maximal supports already force universal exterior reversal — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Let H be a minimum-order counterexample and let S=(s1,...,sk) be an inclusion-maximal proper Hamiltonian support. Then every exterior vertex y is noninsertable at every gap of the displayed order of S. Indeed S union {y} is a proper strict superset of S and hence non-Hamiltonian; the endpoint-replacement/truncation dichotomy therefore puts y in its noninsertable branch. In particular (y,s1,s2) and (s_{k-1},sk,y) are non-tight, so boundary antisymmetry gives (s2,s1,y) and (y,sk,s_{k-1}) tight. Thus universal double-end reversal needs only inclusion-maximality, not maximum cardinality. Consequently any proper Hamiltonian K4/K5 seed in a minimum counterexample may be enlarged while retaining the seed to an inclusion-maximal proper Hamiltonian support, and the resulting saturated support has every exterior vertex reversing both displayed ends. This is a seed-preserving normalization for bounded-support outputs.
