# Terminal ordered-pair loci have the homotopy type of mutual-pair clique complexes

### Pair reservoirs and clique complexes

For a two-slot reservoir \(B\) with ordered-pair admissibility relation \(E\), let \(G_E\) be the graph joining \(u,v\in B\) when both ordered pairs \((u,v)\) and \((v,u)\) are admissible. The terminal pair locus is homotopy equivalent to the clique complex \(K(G_E)\).

When \(|B|\le4\), every connected component of this locus is either contractible or homotopy equivalent to \(S^1\). The unique noncontractible connected type is represented by a chordless four-cycle. Thus all higher homotopy of an individual bounded pair factor vanishes.
