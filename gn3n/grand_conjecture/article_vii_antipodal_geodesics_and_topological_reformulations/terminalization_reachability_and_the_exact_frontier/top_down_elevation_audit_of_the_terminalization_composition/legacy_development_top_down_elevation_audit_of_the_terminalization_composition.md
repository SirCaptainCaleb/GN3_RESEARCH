## Top-down elevation audit of the current terminalization composition

This addendum rereads the current Section composition from its first load-bearing step to its last, importing later Article VII results rather than repairing only the final audited failure.

### Step 1: "the finite local-witness line is closed"

The composition begins from the six-word terminal language and the assertion that every terminal centered or overlapping support is bounded and two-coverable. Later audit showed that this is not the predicate used by the exact two-cover surgery. The correct surgery-compatible language is
\[
\mathcal W_+=\{001,011,0101\},
\]
which is reverse-complement invariant but does not include the opposite-polarity words as forbidden witnesses.

Therefore the old finite-terminal statement cannot be used as a black box for the global filtration. What survives is more precise:

- positive span-two doubles admit the exact corridor classification;
- every reflected-double determining span has \(\kappa_2\le2\);
- the genuine unbounded residue is exactly a mixed \(011/001\) double with \(\kappa_2=2\);
- that residue is simultaneously a symmetric zero exact-root chamber on its determining span.

Thus the elevated first step is not "all terminal cells are finite", but:
\[
\boxed{\text{all unbounded positive terminal geometry lies in the symmetric two-deletion double locus.}}
\]

### Step 2: separator and relative-index filtration

The composition next passes from a sign separator to the existential next-depth space \(Y_{r+1}\). The later audit [[independent_audit_existential_versus_protected_filtration_gap]] invalidates that iteration: a face containing one outward chamber need not be protected, so the local mixed-face theorem cannot be iterated there.

The protected replacement \(X_r\) is the correct filtration. However [[separated_window_outward_loci_are_products_and_can_be_disconnected]] and [[arbitrary_terminal_pair_relations_occur_on_protected_zero_faces]] show that the natural same-face outward locus \(D(F)=F\cap X_{r+1}\) need not be acyclic; it may be disconnected or have \(S^1\)-homotopy.

The later persistent-face label
\[
\sigma_r(F)
\]
is the strongest separator presently available. Its zero faces split exactly into:

1. neither reflected occurrence persistent;
2. both reflected occurrences persistent.

For long separated windows, the first branch contains an outward chamber by the separated-window splice theorem. Hence:
\[
\boxed{\text{the only unbounded separator-zero branch without immediate escape is double-persistent.}}
\]

This is the elevated replacement for the old existential filtration step.

### Step 3: pointwise terminal surgery and minimal separator faces

The composition reduces non-outward separator faces to terminal sign-flip edges and then asks only for compatibility of pointwise repairs. This reduction is too optimistic in the positive language: same-face escape is false, and in the genuine \(\kappa_2=2\) double no surgery supported inside the determining span can be outward.

The later enlargement results give the correct trichotomy. For a genuine mixed double:

- a farther positive witness may already occur;
- an exterior buffer may give an enlarged-window outward repair;
- otherwise an entire outer side is uniformly blocked and becomes a family of common reversers of one exposed corridor edge.

If there is no farther witness, the whole outward status ray is monochromatic. The current endpoint geometry is therefore not arbitrary.

Moreover [[blocked_no_farther_endpoints_admit_bounded_endpoint_cutting_supports]] shows that a two-layer tight guard produces a Hamiltonian \(4/5\)-support cutting off one or two corridor vertices while leaving the remaining corridor tail contiguous and tight.

Thus the elevated local primitive is:
\[
\boxed{\text{endpoint repartition/transport, not fixed-root terminal surgery.}}
\]

### Step 4: ten-position normalization and one-vertex exchange

The old composition normalizes every terminal repair to a ten-position support. This is useful only in the six-word bounded-terminal model and is no longer the natural global coordinate after the positive-language correction.

The newer face analysis is stronger in a different direction. On a double-persistent, no-farther-witness face:

- every relevant face block has order at most four;
- a block crossing an outer determining-window boundary has footprint at most two;
- a persistent window may contain a genuine central three-position free block, so the old stronger footprint-two claim is false;
- one-slot endpoint reservoirs have contractible successful-buffer sectors;
- two-slot persistent endpoint reservoirs are automatically uniformly blocked and supply rooted Hamiltonian four-paths.

Hence the correct normalization is by **bounded source factors and endpoint footprints**, not one fixed ten-position target interval.

### Step 5: commuting squares and nonmaximal braids

The old composition spends substantial effort proving Coxeter rank-two coherence of chosen terminal repairs. Later topology clarifies when this philosophy is actually valid.

For an outward-choice sector whose natural carriers factor into neutral permutahedra, one-variable endpoint loci, and ordered-pair loci with reservoirs of order at most four, [[bounded_pair_reservoirs_reduce_natural_carrier_extension_to_the_two_skeleton]] proves that every carrier component has vanishing homotopy in dimensions at least two.

Consequently, once a carried equivariant map is defined on the two-skeleton, all dimensions at least three extend automatically.

Thus the elevated coherence obligation is only:

1. compatible vertex choices;
2. paths for comparable face pairs inside one carrier component;
3. null-homotopy of triangular chain loops.

Arbitrary rank-two Coxeter normalization is no longer the conceptual endgame; two-skeleton compatibility in the **natural protected carrier** is.

### Step 6: maximal endpoint braid exclusion

The composition's final local lemma excludes a maximal ten-support braid by finding one of the six dual-polarity forbidden words strictly inward. This is not valid for the positive-only filtration: inward words \(110,100,1010\) are not positive witnesses.

Therefore the maximal-braid exclusion is not part of the repaired proof.

The later elevation makes this residue largely irrelevant. Long separated zero faces are handled by persistence unless both occurrences persist. Double-persistent faces are treated through blocked-buffer/rooted-reservoir structure, while outward-choice sectors are controlled by natural carrier topology. The proof should not return to the dual-polarity endpoint-braid classification.

### Step 7: "terminal edge-surgery coherence"

The asserted global edge-surgery coherence is therefore not currently established. But its intended role has split into two smaller and more natural theorems:

- **outward-replacement theorem on the double-persistent branch**, now a bounded endpoint-transport problem after no-farther reduction;
- **two-skeleton compatibility theorem on the outward-choice branch**, after which higher extension is automatic.

This is a much narrower pair of obligations than a universal surgery-coherence theorem.

### Step 8: genus iteration to a witness-free chamber

The final genus computation remains formally useful once a genuine protected one-step map
\[
S_r\longrightarrow X_{r+1}
\]
is obtained. The numerical index loss is not the present difficulty.

The actual frontier is therefore entirely in the one-step theorem. There is no value in further manipulating the final genus arithmetic until the two obligations in Step 7 are closed.

### Cross-route elevation from exact roots

The exact-root results sharpen this picture further. Re-running the role-coordinate surplus argument with the later four-vertex central-block theorem gives
\[
\boxed{\text{every nonzero exact-root recurrent branch has }\kappa_2(H)\le2.}
\]
For \(\kappa_2(H)\ge3\), the augmented exact-root carrier must contain a zero exact-root chamber.

Meanwhile every unbounded positive reflected double is itself a zero-root chamber on its determining span and has deletion distance at most two.

Thus the positive-witness route and exact-root route now meet at exactly the same scale:
\[
\boxed{\text{symmetric zero-root / deletion-distance }1\text{ or }2.}
\]

### Revised strategic frontier

The current composition should be viewed as superseded by the following architecture.

1. Work only with the positive witness language \(\mathcal W_+\).
2. Use the protected filtration \(X_r\), not existential \(Y_r\).
3. Use persistent face labels to isolate the double-persistent zero-root locus.
4. On outward-choice faces, use natural same-face carriers. Under the new bounded factor structure, only their two-skeleton compatibility remains.
5. On double-persistent faces:
   - farther witness closes immediately;
   - successful buffer sectors are topologically automatic;
   - no-farther uniformly blocked sectors have block order at most four and rooted endpoint packets;
   - the remaining local theorem is endpoint repartition/transport across the surviving corridor tail.
6. Use exact-root compression as an independent global reduction showing that no nonzero high-deletion-distance branch remains.

So the strongest current attack is not the old maximal-braid repair. It is the intersection of two finite obligations:
\[
\boxed{\text{endpoint transport on the double-persistent }k=2\text{ locus}}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\text{two-skeleton compatibility on bounded outward-choice carriers}.}
\]

Every higher-dimensional coherence issue and every nonzero unbounded exact-root issue has already been compressed away.
