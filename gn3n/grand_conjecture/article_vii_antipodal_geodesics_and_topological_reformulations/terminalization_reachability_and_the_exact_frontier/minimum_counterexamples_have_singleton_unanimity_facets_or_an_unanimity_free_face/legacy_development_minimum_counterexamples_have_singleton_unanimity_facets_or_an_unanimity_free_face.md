# Superseded: singleton-unanimity facets are automatic — preserved pre-item development

## Development

This development is not a structural restriction and should not be used as a frontier theorem. The later audit [[audit_fixed_hole_singleton_unanimity_facets_are_automatic]] observes that for every counterexample and every vertex x, the extreme permutahedral facet {x}|(V-x) already satisfies A(F_x) union C(F_x)={x}: x is fixed in position 1 and is always canonically left, while every other label can be moved between position 2 and position n and therefore is not unanimous. Thus the singleton-unanimity-facet branch is automatic. The conditional Johnson-layer elimination of the universal fixed-hole branch remains valid when that branch is genuinely entered, but the original sparse-face/universal dichotomy does not force entry into it. A nontrivial replacement must exclude these automatic extreme facets or impose additional carrier geometry.
