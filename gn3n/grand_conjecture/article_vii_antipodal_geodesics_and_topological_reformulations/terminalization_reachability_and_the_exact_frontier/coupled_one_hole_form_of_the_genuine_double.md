## Development

If J=P sqcup Q sqcup {x,y} is a genuine positive reflected double with kappa_2(H[J])=2, then H[J-x] and H[J-y] are both deletion-distance-one instances, with holes y and x respectively, and both reduce after deleting the hole to the same corridor two-cover P|Q. The two holes also reverse all four exposed ends of P and Q. Hence the hard double is a pair of coupled one-hole obstructions with a common corridor frame, not an arbitrary kappa_2=2 tournament. The remaining theorem may be formulated as compatibility of those two one-hole roles: either one absorbs the other and two-covers J, or their one-hole structural alternatives synchronize to a bounded common interface.
