## A common four-core for the maximal braid

Work in the maximal order-eleven braid
[
V=Tsqcup{a,b,c},qquad |T|=8.
]
Fix any Hamiltonian five-set (Bsubset T), and write (C=Tsetminus B), so (|C|=3).

For (xin B) and (yin C), call ((x,y)) **side-good** when (B-x+y) is Hamiltonian. For fixed (y), four-of-six on (Bcup{y}) shows that at least three of the five cells in that column are side-good. Hence at least nine of the fifteen cells are side-good, so some row (xin B) contains at least two side-good cells. Fix such an (x), and put
[
D=Bsetminus{x},qquad R=Ccup{x}.
]

Fix (Pin{{a,b},{a,c},{b,c}}). If (Ccup P) is Hamiltonian, set (y_P=x). Then (D+y_P=B) and ((R-y_P)+P=C+P) are Hamiltonian.

If (Ccup P) is non-Hamiltonian, apply four-of-six to ((Ccup P)cup{x}). Since deleting (x) leaves the bad five-set (Ccup P), at least four of the other five deletions are Hamiltonian. In particular at least two of the three sets
[
(C-y+x)cup P,qquad yin C,
]
are Hamiltonian. The chosen row (x) has at least two side-good vertices (yin C). Two subsets of a three-set, each of size at least two, intersect, so some (y_Pin C) makes both
[
D+y_P=B-x+y_P
]
and
[
(R-y_P)+P=C-y_P+x+P
]
Hamiltonian.

Therefore:

**Common four-core lemma.** There exist complementary four-sets (D,Rsubset T) such that for each moving pair (Pin{ab,ac,bc}) there is (y_Pin R) with
[
D+y_P
quad	ext{and}quad
(R-y_P)+P
]
both Hamiltonian.

Thus all three ten-vertex supports (T+ab,T+ac,T+bc) admit Hamiltonian (5|5) partitions whose first side contains the same four-vertex support core (D). The fifth vertex may vary with the support.

This is weaker than the common Hamiltonian five-core conjecture and is forced by the established four-of-six theorem alone.

### Remaining rooted carrier obligation

The support-level compatibility does not yet automatically give one proper Coxeter face. A sufficient strengthening is to choose the Hamilton orders on the three sets (D+y_P) so that the common four vertices (D) occupy a common consecutive block (for example by making (y_P) an endpoint relative to one common Hamilton order on (D)). Then the three full surgery chambers lie in one proper ordered-partition carrier and the maximal braid closes.

Hamiltonicity of (D+y_P) alone does not supply that prescribed endpoint property, so this rooted-ordering step remains to be proved. Alternatively it would suffice to show directly that the union of proper next-depth faces generated by the three common-four-core surgeries is acyclic; that would avoid prescribing a common path order.
