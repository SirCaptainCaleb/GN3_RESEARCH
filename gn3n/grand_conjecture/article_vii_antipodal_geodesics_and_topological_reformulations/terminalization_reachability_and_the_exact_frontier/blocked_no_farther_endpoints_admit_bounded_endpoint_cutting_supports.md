# Blocked no-farther endpoints admit bounded endpoint-cutting supports

### Bounded endpoint-cutting supports

At a blocked no-farther endpoint, the first two outward guard layers contain a Hamiltonian support of order four or five whose path has the exposed corridor endpoint as an endpoint. Removing this support cuts off one or two initial corridor vertices and leaves the remaining corridor tail contiguous and tight. The valid operation is endpoint repartition or transport; it does not preserve an arbitrarily prescribed old root orientation.
