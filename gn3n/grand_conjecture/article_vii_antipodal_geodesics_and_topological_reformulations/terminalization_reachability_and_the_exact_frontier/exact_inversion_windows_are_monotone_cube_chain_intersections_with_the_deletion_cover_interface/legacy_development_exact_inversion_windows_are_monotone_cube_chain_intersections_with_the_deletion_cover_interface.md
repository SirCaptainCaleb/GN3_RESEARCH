## Development

Let pi=(v_1,...,v_n) be a spanning order and write A_j={v_1,...,v_j}. The vertices A_0,A_1,...,A_n form the monotone maximal chain of the sign cube determined by pi. Suppose d_2(pi)=1. Then q=p+2, the canonical partial cover is P=(v_1,...,v_{p+1}) on support A_{p+1} and Q=(v_n,...,v_{p+3}) on support V-A_{p+2}, and the unique uncovered label is x=v_{p+2}. Hence the canonical deletion cover of H-x is exactly the cube edge A_{p+1}--A_{p+2}, whose coordinate label is x. Reversal of pi reverses/complements the monotone cube chain and exchanges the two support sides. Thus exact deficiency one is precisely an intersection of a permutation maximal chain with the deletion-cover edge interface in the sign cube. More generally positive deficiency q-p-1 is the number of labels strictly between the last prefix support A_{p+1} certified tight by the displayed order and the first complementary suffix support V-A_q certified tight after reversal; the exact inversion coordinates record the two ends of this uncertified interval along the same monotone cube chain. In a minimum counterexample kappa_2=1, the cubical deletion-cover separator is therefore the order-free global object whose chainwise positional shadow is the exact inversion-window theory. This explains why the early antipodal root and the late family of one-hole deletion covers repeatedly produce the same balanced/odd geometry: they are two coordinatizations of the same cube interface.
