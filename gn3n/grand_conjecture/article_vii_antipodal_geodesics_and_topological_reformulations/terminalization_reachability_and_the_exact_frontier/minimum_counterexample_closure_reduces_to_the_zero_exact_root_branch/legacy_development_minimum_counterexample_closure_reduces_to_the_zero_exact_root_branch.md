## Minimum-counterexample closure reduces to the zero exact-root branch

Let H be a minimum-order counterexample to the spanning two-cover conjecture.

By minimum-order minimality, H-x has a spanning two-cover for every vertex x. Hence
kappa_2(H)=1.

Apply the augmented exact-root role-balance argument of [[kappa_one_role_balance_and_ky_fan_subsection_a]] for any prescribed triple of vertices. Borsuk-Ulam gives a positive carrier with exact-root balance and zero average in the three prescribed role coordinates.

If that carrier contained no zero exact-root chamber, the kappa-one central-block reduction forces the unique nonzero possibility to be a freely permuted four-vertex equal-side central block. The theorem [[the_kappa_one_nonzero_four_block_is_impossible]] excludes that possibility by boundary antisymmetry.

Therefore the carrier must contain a chamber pi with
p(pi)=c(pi),
i.e. a zero exact root.

Thus every minimum-order counterexample lies in the zero-root branch of Article VII. The nonzero recurrent-face branch is completely eliminated at deletion distance one.

### Important audit boundary

This conclusion should NOT be combined with the earlier fixed-hole Ky Fan statement by claiming that the alternative x-sparse unanimity face is restrictive. The later audit [[audit_fixed_hole_singleton_unanimity_facets_are_automatic]] shows that such a face is automatic: the facet with x fixed first already has unanimity set contained in {x}. Hence the fixed-hole Ky Fan dichotomy, in that formulation, does not force the universal balanced-deletion branch.

The genuine closure frontier is therefore:

> Convert a zero exact-root chamber in a kappa_2=1 minimum counterexample into a spanning two-cover, or derive an additional structure from the zero-root chamber that contradicts minimum counterexamplehood.

This reduction is scale-independent and uses no small-order cutoff.
