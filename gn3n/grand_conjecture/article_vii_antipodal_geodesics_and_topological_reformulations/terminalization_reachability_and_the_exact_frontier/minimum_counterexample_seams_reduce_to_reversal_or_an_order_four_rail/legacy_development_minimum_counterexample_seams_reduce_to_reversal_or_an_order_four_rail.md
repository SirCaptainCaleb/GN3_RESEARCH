# Minimum-counterexample seams reduce to reversal or an order-four rail — preserved pre-item development

Let H be a minimum-order counterexample and let S|P|Q be a saturated maximal-support state.

Every proper Hamiltonian support K has pc(H-K)=2: minimality gives pc(H-K)<=2, while a Hamiltonian H-K would two-cover H together with K.

Apply the audited seam four-support theorem.

If the seam output is transversal, its construction already supplies a positioned external reversal: one exposed complementary endpoint reverses a same-type end edge of S and a second-layer edge of the opposite rail.

If the seam output is the pure rail four-support K, minimality gives pc(H-K)=2. Deleting K leaves S together with two inherited residual rail intervals. By [[every_seam_reseed_is_support_preserving_or_exposes_a_cross_residual_reversal]], either those residual rails concatenate, giving H-K=S|R and hence H-S=K|R with an order-four complementary rail, or an endpoint of one residual rail reverses an exposed end edge of the other.

Therefore every saturated seam in a minimum counterexample yields either a positioned external end-edge reversal or a complementary two-cover of S with one component of order four. The hereditary-descent and independent reseed-recurrence branches disappear under minimum-counterexample minimality.
