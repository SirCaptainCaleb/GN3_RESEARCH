# Persistent face labels isolate the double-persistent zero-root locus

### The persistent-face separator

Label a protected face by the persistence of its reflected positive occurrences: left-persistent, right-persistent, both, or neither. The label is monotone under passage to subfaces and is exchanged by reversal. For a long separated zero face, the neither-persistent case contains an outward chamber by the persistence-or-escape theorem.

Consequently the only unbounded zero faces that cannot be removed by this separator are the **double-persistent** faces, on which both reflected positive occurrences persist in every chamber. Every such chamber is a symmetric zero-root chamber on its reflected determining span. Thus the positive-witness route and the exact-root route meet on the same zero-root geometry.
