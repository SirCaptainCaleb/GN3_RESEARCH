# A local pure carrier shows same-face escape is false — preserved pre-item development

## The same-face strengthening is false locally

The terminal coherence problem cannot be removed by asserting that every protected mixed face itself contains an outward chamber. There is already a six-vertex local model in which a proper face is a pure balanced centered-witness carrier with no third chamber available.

Let the vertex set be
[
{0,1,2,3,4,5},
]
and consider the one-dimensional permutahedron face
[
F={0}mid{1}mid{2,3}mid{4}mid{5}.
]
Its two chambers are
[
pi=012345,
qquad
pi'=013245.
]
Choose the boundary-tournament orientations on the six triples used by these two status words so that, in increasing order,
[
h(012)=0,quad h(123)=1,quad h(234)=0,quad h(345)=1,
]
and
[
h(013)=1,quad h(245)=0.
]
(The remaining triples may be oriented arbitrarily.) Boundary antisymmetry then gives
[
epsilon(pi)=0101,
qquad
epsilon(pi')=1010.
]
Thus both chambers have the centered alternating forbidden witness. If the external antipodal gauge is the relative order of vertices (2) and (3), the two chambers receive opposite orientations of the centered pendant witness edge. Consequently (F) is a pure balanced one-edge face, but it has only those two chambers and hence contains no outward chamber.

On six vertices the whole tournament is of course two-coverable by the established finite theorem, so this is not a counterexample to the grand conjecture. Its role is structural: it disproves the proposed **local same-face escape** as a theorem derivable solely from protected mixed-cell geometry.

Therefore the terminal branch of the relative-index proof must genuinely leave the original face. The viable target is the weaker, topologically sufficient statement already identified in the audit:
[
S_rlongrightarrow Y_{r+1}
]
equivariantly, or an equivalent acyclic-carrier construction. The bounded terminal surgery must be made coherent across chains; literal inclusion of the terminal separator in the next-depth face poset cannot be expected.
