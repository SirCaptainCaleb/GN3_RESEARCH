## Development

Audit-safe local repair: the square and braid constructions can be chosen as actual Coxeter cells all of whose chambers are outward. Internal directions collapse. Safe square directions give an outward edge or square. Safe braid directions give an outward hexagon. Mixed internal/boundary braids with slack are renormalized to internal directions, while the maximal no-slack endpoint braid is excluded. Hence every rank-two terminal carrier lies wholly in X_{r+1}, not merely in existential Y_{r+1}. This does not yet prove the higher-face carrier theorem: the remaining obligation is to assemble these protected rank-two carriers over every higher-dimensional protected zero face into an equivariant acyclic carrier monotone under subfaces.
