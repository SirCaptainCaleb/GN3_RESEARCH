# Balanced ten-position repairs have explicit protected endpoint orbits — preserved pre-item development

## Development

## Full endpoint-transport orbits of a balanced ten-position repair

This proves the orbit condition left explicit in [[frozen_window_carriers_and_separator_relabeling_require_precise_invariants]], using only positive witnesses.

Let \(J\) have ten consecutive vertex positions. Choose a \(5|5\) tight cover of its support and order it as \((P,Q^{\rm rev})\). Its internal status word is
\[
111ab000,\qquad a,b\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Freeze the eight interior vertex positions of \(J\). Allow arbitrary permutations of the positions to the left together with the first position of \(J\), and independently of the positions to the right together with the last position of \(J\). These are precisely the full parabolic orbits generated by exterior adjacent swaps and the two endpoint-crossing swaps, with every internal generator of \(J\) excluded.

**Lemma.** Every chamber in this orbit has internal status word
\[
x11ab00y,\qquad x,y\in\{0,1\}.
\]
It has no \(0101\) occurrence wholly inside \(J\), and its only possible positive span-two occurrences wholly inside \(J\) are \(011\) at relative start 1 and \(001\) at relative start 6.

**Proof.** Only the first and last triple statuses can depend on the two variable endpoint occupants. Statuses 2 through 7 use only the frozen eight vertices, giving the displayed word.

The six three-bit windows are
\[
x11,\ 11a,\ 1ab,\ ab0,\ b00,\ 00y.
\]
Only the first and last can be \(001\) or \(011\). None of the five four-bit windows can be \(0101\): each either starts with one, has two consecutive ones, ends in two zeros, or has its second bit zero. \(\square\)

**Protected-orbit criterion.** Suppose every determining window of depth at most \(r\) is contained in \(J\), and the two outer span-two starts just identified are strictly farther outward than \(r\). Then the entire parabolic target face lies in the positive protected complex \(X_{r+1}\).

This is protection of every orbit chamber, not an induction from one-step safety. The target face is an explicit product of a left and a right permutahedron with eight fixed singleton positions. It is proper and contractible.

For a centered/overlapping span-two terminal interval of order at most eight, choose \(J\) with at least one slack vertex at both ends. Its outer span-two starts are then farther outward. For an alternating interval of order at most ten, the outer span-two windows of a containing ten-window are likewise farther outward in the interlaced reflection ordering: they extend past at least one endpoint of the alternating determining interval. Thus the familiar balanced normalization does have a wholly protected endpoint-transport orbit for these bounded types, when the positive depth rule is used.

**Exception: the surviving disjoint span-two interval of order ten.** In this case \(J=I\), and relative starts 1 and 6 are the current reflected span-two pair itself. An endpoint exchange can recreate the current witness. The blanket safe-transport assertion cannot be used on this newly retained branch. Its endpoints must remain frozen, or another repair with extra slack must be proved.

The orbit lemma does not give a globally nested assignment when repair orders or normalization windows vary. An inherited generator mask gives nesting only within a family sharing the same base repair and normalization, as in [[frozen_window_carriers_and_separator_relabeling_require_precise_invariants]]. It also does not handle the unbounded positive span-two reflected-double branch.

Accordingly the local square/hexagon machinery can be retained for the bounded alternating and overlapping span-two types with a consistent positive polarity. The remaining local issues are precisely the ten-position exclusive disjoint span-two endpoint exchange and the unbounded span-two double branch, in addition to global compatibility of carrier choices.
