# Two exterior labels fill the terminal four-cycle by a six-triangle disk

### A six-label fill for the four-cycle

Let a terminal pair factor on four boundary labels be a chordless four-cycle. Two exterior labels \(g,h\) satisfying the stated mutual pair-admissibility relations with the cycle vertices and with each other enlarge the clique complex by six triangles whose union is a disk with boundary equal to the original four-cycle. Thus a nontrivial pair-factor loop can be killed using at most two protected exterior labels, provided the explicit six-label compatibility tests hold.
