## Development

Assume n=2r+1 and x,y are two universal fixed holes. Put W=V(H)-{x,y}. For every R subset W with |R|=r-1, the universal pair-critical state gives R+x and R+y Hamiltonian while R+x+y is non-Hamiltonian. Choose Hamilton orders on R+x and R+y. If their induced orders on R disagree, this is an order-disturbance. If the insertion gaps of x and y are adjacent, non-Hamiltonicity of R+x+y forces the positioned-reversal alternative of the compatible-extension theorem. If they coincide at an internal gap, the same theorem gives a Hamiltonian four-support on that core edge with x,y. Therefore, if these disturbance outputs are all excluded, x and y occupy the same endpoint gap of one common Hamilton order on R. Deleting x or y from the corresponding extension leaves that common order, so R itself is Hamiltonian. Hence a quiet odd two-universal state forces every (r-1)-subset of W to be Hamiltonian. Together with the fixed-hole theorem, every r-subset of W is also Hamiltonian. Thus the quiet residue has two consecutive complete Hamiltonian layers in W. This is scale-independent.
