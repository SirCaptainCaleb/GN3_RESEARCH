# Exact-root compression and bounded central structure

## Compression of the exact-root carrier

Let \(F\) be a positively balanced exact-root carrier whose chambers all have positive deficiency. If \(F\) contains no zero root, every varying root coordinate is controlled by one face block of order at most four, and at most ten actual vertices determine all varying root labels. Thus nonzero exact-root recurrence has no unbounded permutahedral residue.

More strongly,
\[
\boxed{\kappa_2(H)\ge2
\Longrightarrow
F\text{ contains a chamber with }p=c.}
\]
The nonzero configurations of corridor length two have deficiency at most one. The sole four-vertex residual configuration is also impossible under the global deficiency lower bound unless \(p=c\).

Accordingly, for deletion distance at least two, exact-root topology reaches a symmetric canonical partial cover
\[
P\mid X\mid Q,\qquad |P|=|Q|.
\]
The remaining task is not further root compression; it is to exploit the actual hole vertices and the local positive witnesses around such a zero-root state.
