# Positive balance on every chamber and the switch-separation bound

### The exact inversion-root map

Let \(m=n-2\), let
\[
c(\pi)=m+1-q(\pi),
\qquad
\psi(\pi)=e_{p(\pi)}-e_{c(\pi)}.
\]
Reversal exchanges \(p\) and \(c\), hence \(\psi(\pi^{\rm rev})=-\psi(\pi)\). Apply the cellular construction of the preceding Section to obtain an odd map on the permutahedron boundary.

If \(\kappa_2(H)=k>0\), then every chamber has
\[
m-p(\pi)-c(\pi)=d_2(\pi)\ge k.
\]
Hence the exact-root image lies in the sum-zero subspace on at most \(m-k-1\) coordinates, of dimension at most \(n-k-4\). Bourgin--Yang therefore gives
\[
\dim \Psi^{-1}(0)\ge k+2.
\]

In particular there is a carrier face \(F\) with strictly positive chamber weights
\[
\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal V(F)}\lambda_\pi\psi(\pi)=0,
\qquad \lambda_\pi>0.
\]
Every root occurring on \(F\) lies on a directed return cycle in the root digraph.

For a chamber of positive deficiency, the canonical partial cover is
\[
P_\pi\mid X_\pi\mid Q_\pi,
\]
where \(|X_\pi|=d_2(\pi)\), \(|P_\pi|=p(\pi)+1\), and \(|Q_\pi|=c(\pi)+1\). Thus the root records the imbalance of the two canonical path orders, while the anti-diagonal deficit is the number of uncovered vertices. This exact-root construction is the topological input for the compression theorem below.
