## Development

Retain the maximal-support state S|P|Q with all three paths nontrivial. Applying the transversal-four-support construction to the displayed concatenation (P,Q) gives a Hamiltonian four-support meeting S,P,Q whose distinguished exterior carrier is either q_1 or p_m. Applying the same argument to the reverse concatenation (Q,P) gives a second Hamiltonian four-support meeting S,P,Q whose distinguished carrier is either p_1 or q_t. Since P and Q are nontrivial, the two carrier choices come from disjoint endpoint pairs {q_1,p_m} and {p_1,q_t}; in particular the two certificates are rooted at opposite sides of the complementary two-cover. Thus every maximal-support normalization carries a two-sided pair of transversal Hamiltonian four-support certificates. The individual certificate need not have a two-coverable complement: removing it can leave three inherited path fragments, so no direct closure is claimed. The correct residual problem is to use the two opposite transversal certificates together, rather than treating a single bounded support as sufficient.
