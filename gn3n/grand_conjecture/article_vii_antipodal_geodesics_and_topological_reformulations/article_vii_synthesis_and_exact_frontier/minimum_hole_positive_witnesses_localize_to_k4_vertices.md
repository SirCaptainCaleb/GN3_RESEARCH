## Development

Let X be a minimum two-cover deletion set of order k, and fix H-X=P|Q with displayed orders P=(p_1,...,p_r) and Q=(q_1,...,q_s). For any ordering x_1,...,x_k of X, put pi=(P,x_1,...,x_k,Q^rev). By minimum-hole exactness, p(pi)=r-1 and q(pi)=r+k. Hence every status strictly before r-1 is 1 and every status strictly after r+k is 0. Therefore every positive witness from W_+={001,011,0101} is contained in the central status interval [r-1,r+k], of order k+2. Such an interval is determined by k+4 consecutive vertices, namely {p_{r-1},p_r} union X union {q_s,q_{s-1}}. Thus after a minimum deletion set is identified, positive-witness geometry is independent of the lengths of the complementary paths except through their two terminal anchor pairs. For k=2 this recovers the six-vertex localization of a genuine two-deletion state. The proof uses only the exact inversion-window identity and the positive witness language.
