## Correction: previous second-layer propagation claim withdrawn

Development version 1 of this subsection is invalid. It used the false implication
[
h(s_2,s_1,u)=1Rightarrow h(s_1,u,s_2)=1.
]
The project's boundary-tournament axiom relates only
[
h(x,y,z)quad	ext{and}quad h(z,y,x);
]
cyclic rotations belong to different reversal orbits and are not determined.

Hence the same-tail hook relations and individual noninsertability do not by themselves produce the rooted triple (h(s_1,u,s_2)=1), and the claimed second-layer reversal (h(s_3,s_2,u)=1) is not established.

The following conditional statement remains valid: if (h(s_1,u,s_2)=1) is proved independently and (u) is noninsertable into the displayed order of (S), then insertion at the first internal gap forces
[
h(u,s_2,s_3)=0,
]
and boundary antisymmetry gives
[
h(s_3,s_2,u)=1.
]

No unconditional propagation theorem is claimed here. See [[audit_cyclic_rotation_invalidates_the_new_descent_and_second_layer_claims]].
