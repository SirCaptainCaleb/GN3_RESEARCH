## Development

Retain a maximal-support normalization S=(s_1,...,s_k) with complementary two-cover P=(p_1,...,p_m)|Q=(q_1,...,q_t), all paths nontrivial. At the initial support edge, maximality gives h(s_2,s_1,z)=1 for all four exposed complementary endpoints z. By the four-common-hook theorem, either there is a Hamiltonian support of order four or five containing {s_2,s_1} and at least one endpoint from each of P,Q, or both same-tail four-cores {s_2,s_1,p_1,p_m} and {s_2,s_1,q_1,q_t} are Hamiltonian. Apply the same statement in the reversed boundary tournament at the terminal support edge: the relations h(z,s_k,s_{k-1})=1 become common ordered-anchor hooks through (s_{k-1},s_k) after reversal, and Hamiltonicity is preserved under reversing a Hamilton order. Thus either there is a cross-tail Hamiltonian 4/5-packet anchored at the terminal end, or both {s_{k-1},s_k,p_1,p_m} and {s_{k-1},s_k,q_1,q_t} are Hamiltonian. Consequently every maximal-support state has the following two-ended dichotomy: either some support-end anchor participates in a Hamiltonian 4/5-packet using endpoints from both complementary paths, or all four same-tail anchor cores, two for P and two for Q, are Hamiltonian. This is the current bounded attachment frontier.
