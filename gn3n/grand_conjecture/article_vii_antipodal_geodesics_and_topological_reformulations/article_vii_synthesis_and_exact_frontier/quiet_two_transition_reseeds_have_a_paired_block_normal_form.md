# Quiet two-transition reseeds have a paired-block normal form

## Development

In a genuine support-splitting reseed H-K=A|B with inherited three-cover S|P'|Q', suppose there are exactly two interclass transitions relative to the partition S,P',Q'. Then cutting them yields exactly four monochromatic blocks. Since both A and B meet S, S contributes two blocks, while nonempty P',Q' contribute one each. Thus each rail is a single comparison block. If there is no order disagreement on P' or Q' and no leave-and-return through S, the two rail blocks cannot lie in the same comparison path: separated by S would be leave-and-return, while adjacent would concatenate P' and Q' into a Hamiltonian path on their union, contradicting pc(P' union Q')=2. Therefore each comparison path consists of exactly one S-block and one whole rail block. Up to swapping paths and orientations, A=S_A P' or P' S_A and B=S_B Q' or Q' S_B, with S_A,S_B partitioning S. Hence the quiet t=2 branch reduces to two cross-junction triples.
