## Development

A finite binary model on the eight labels (u,a,b,x,y,c,d,v) was checked. It imposed the inherited tail relations, the minimum-hole terminal reversals, positive obstruction for all 24 permutations of the four middle labels {b,x,y,c}, failure of every compatible two-tail routing of those four labels, and failure of every compatible routing after deleting any one middle label. The model is feasible. Therefore these central-band constraints alone do not force the spanning two-cover routing. Additional maximal-support or carrier-exit structure is genuinely needed.
