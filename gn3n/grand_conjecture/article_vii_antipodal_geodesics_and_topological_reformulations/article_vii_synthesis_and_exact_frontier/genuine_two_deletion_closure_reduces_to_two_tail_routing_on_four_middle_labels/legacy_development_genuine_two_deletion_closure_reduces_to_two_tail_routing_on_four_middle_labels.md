## Development

Let H satisfy kappa_2(H)=2, let {x,y} be a minimum deletion pair, and fix H-{x,y}=P|Q with P=(p_1,...,p_r), Q=(q_1,...,q_s), r,s>=2. Put a=p_{r-1}, b=p_r, d=q_{s-1}, c=q_s and M={b,c,x,y}. Let P^-=(p_1,...,a) and Q^-=(q_1,...,d). Suppose M is partitioned as L sqcup R and ordered as L=(l_1,...,l_i), R=(r_1,...,r_j), allowing either side to be empty, such that P^- followed by L and Q^- followed by R are tight paths. Then these two paths partition V(H), so H has a spanning two-cover. Thus closure at deletion distance two requires only a rooted two-path routing of the four middle labels between the two inherited tails. The stronger anchored six-path condition, which routes all four labels to one tail, is sufficient but not necessary. Consequently the finite terminal theorem should be formulated with the two inherited anchor edges (p_{r-2},a) and (q_{s-2},d) and four free middle labels, not as one-sided Hamiltonicity of the six-vertex packet. This formulation also avoids any reversal of a tight path: both inherited tails retain their displayed orientations.
