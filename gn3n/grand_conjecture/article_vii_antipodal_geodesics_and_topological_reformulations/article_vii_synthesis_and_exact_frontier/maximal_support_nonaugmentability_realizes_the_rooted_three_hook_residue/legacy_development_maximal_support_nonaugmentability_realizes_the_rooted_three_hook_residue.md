## Development

Let S=(s_1,...,s_k) be a maximal Hamiltonian support with two-coverable complement P|Q, where both complementary paths are nontrivial. Maximal-support endpoint nonaugmentability gives h(s_2,s_1,z)=1 for every exposed endpoint z in {p_1,p_m,q_1,q_t}. Hence any three exposed endpoints form three common hooks through the same ordered anchor pair (s_2,s_1). This is exactly the local predicate isolated in the rooted-corridor absorption analysis as the bounded three-hook residue: there the obstruction is a set of distinct labels t_i satisfying h(y,x,t_i)=1 through one ordered anchor pair (y,x). Therefore the maximal-support branch lands directly in the same local three-hook geometry, with one extra hook available. In particular the local extension toolkit immediately gives a Hamiltonian support of order four or five containing the anchor pair (s_2,s_1) and drawn from the anchor plus three complementary endpoints. The remaining issue is no longer identification of a common bounded obstruction on the combinatorial and carrier sides; that identification is exact. The remaining bridge is to exploit the ambient roles of the hooks—here exposed endpoints of a two-cover, there predecessor candidates at a protected corridor interface—to obtain a spanning two-cover or protected outward enlargement.
