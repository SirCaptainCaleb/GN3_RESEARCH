## Development

Let G be a boundary tournament with pc(G)>2 and choose a maximum-cardinality Hamiltonian support S=(s_1,...,s_k) whose complement has a two-cover P|Q. Every displayed endpoint z of P or Q is noninsertable in the inherited order of S, hence h(s_2,s_1,z)=1 and h(z,s_k,s_{k-1})=1. Any cross pair z in E(P), w in E(Q) also satisfies that S union {z,w} is non-Hamiltonian, by maximality. Assume both complementary paths have order at least two and let A={p_1,p_m}, B={q_1,q_t}. Define Gamma on A union B by uv an edge iff {s_2,s_1,u,v} is Hamiltonian. The prescribed-pair Hamiltonicity theorem gives alpha(Gamma)<=2. Therefore either some cross pair u in A,v in B is an edge, giving a cross-path Hamiltonian four-core through {s_2,s_1}, or there are no cross edges and Gamma is exactly the perfect matching {A,B}. In the latter case the fixed-pair orientation classes for {a,b}={s_2,s_1} are exactly A and B up to exchange. Thus every cross u in A,v in B satisfies h(a,u,b)=1, h(b,v,a)=1, and non-Hamiltonicity of {a,b,u,v} forces h(u,a,v)=1 and h(v,b,u)=1. Hence the maximal-support interface reduces to a cross four-core or an explicit six-label oriented matching-block rectangle. This argument uses maximality plus local boundary-tournament extension facts, not minimum-counterexample calculus.
