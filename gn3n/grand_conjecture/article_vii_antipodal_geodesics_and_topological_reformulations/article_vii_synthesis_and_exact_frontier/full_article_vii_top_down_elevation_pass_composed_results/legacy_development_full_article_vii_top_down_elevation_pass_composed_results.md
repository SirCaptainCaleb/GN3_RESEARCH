## Full top-down elevation pass over the current Article VII composition

This pass applies the project elevation reflex to the current Article VII composition from its first exact order formulation through terminalization. The question at each step is: can later mathematics generalize it, strengthen it, or move it earlier so that downstream obligations disappear?

### 1. Status words and defect intervals

Retain the status-word language. The defect-interval formulation is exact, but later exact inversion coordinates show that the full defect family is more information than closure needs. The first zero (p) and last one (q) are the theorem-relevant extremal statistics.

**Elevation:** introduce (p,q) immediately after the status word. The defect-line Helly theorem becomes an exact geometric interpretation rather than the main state variable.

### 2. Defect Helly and separated defect pairs

**Elevation:** strengthen the order-level failure certificate immediately to
[

iff
	ext{the word contains }001,011,	ext{ or }0101.
]
The minimal positive witness language should occur here, not near the end of the article.

### 3. Reversal

Retain reversal-complement exactly.

**Elevation:** the positive language
[

[

No six-word dual-polarity enlargement is required for antipodality. This removes the later polarity mismatch at its source.

### 4. Extreme switch coordinates

The first/last-switch coordinates are legitimate exploratory compression but are strictly weaker than (p,q).

**Elevation:** demote the extreme-switch root to historical/topological motivation. It should not be a load-bearing closure invariant once the exact root is available.

### 5. Exact inversion window

Retain and promote. This is the first real pivot of Article VII.

**Elevation:** combine it immediately with the later auxiliary-center fiber theorem. For one fixed original order, insertion of the auxiliary vertex at position (s) gives a directed one-change order exactly when
[
qle sle p+1.
]
Thus auxiliary exactification is fiberwise the same inversion-window theorem, not a separate obstruction theory.

**Elevation:** treat minimum deletion holes as a primary structural object immediately. Their constant-root faces, hereditary exactness, and absolute nonaugmentability should be introduced before the topological sections.

### 7. Canonical role transitions

The composition splits this into the (kge2) no-direct-side-change lemma and a later (k=1) classification.

**Elevation:** combine them into one theorem: a direct canonical (Pleftrightarrow Q) change across one adjacent transposition is possible only when (kappa_2=1); then both endpoint chambers have deficiency one and the swapped partner is the same unique hole in both. Otherwise roles move through the hole.

This is the uniform local input wanted by every later Tucker/Ky Fan role-label argument.

### 8. Minimum-hole faces and Boolean cubes

Retain and apply earlier.

**Elevation:** the hereditary theorem is much stronger than a statement about one order. If (X) is minimum, every (Ysubseteq X) satisfies

Together with absolute nonaugmentability, this is a reusable local engine.

In particular, the genuine reflected-double residue with endpoints (x,y) is a two-element minimum hole. This immediately yields the deletion-distance square (2,1,1,0), all four exposed end reversals, and internal nonabsorbability; see [[elevation_genuine_reflected_doubles_are_minimum_hole_boolean_squares]].
