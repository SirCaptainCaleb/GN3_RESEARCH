# A leaf of the minimum-pair graph forces universal balanced two-covers — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Let H satisfy kappa_2(H)=2 and let xy be a minimum deletion pair. Define the minimum-pair graph M on V(H) by uv in E(M) iff H-{u,v} has a two-cover. Suppose x has the unique neighbor y in M. Put J=H-x. Then kappa_2(J)=1, and y is the unique vertex whose deletion two-covers J. Apply the deletion-distance-one Ky Fan dichotomy of kappa_one_role_balance_and_ky_fan. Either J has a proper face with no vertex unanimous on either canonical path side, or for every prescribed total ordering of V(J) there is a one-hole deletion cover whose two support roles alternate after removing the hole. In the second branch the hole must always be y, by uniqueness. Let K=H-{x,y}. Given any balanced bipartition V(K)=A sqcup B, prescribe an order of J with y first and the remaining vertices interleaved alternately from A and B. The alternating-cover conclusion then has hole y and support sets exactly A and B up to exchange. Hence H[A] and H[B] are Hamiltonian. Therefore every balanced bipartition of K is a Hamiltonian two-cover. Moreover none of A union {x}, B union {x}, A union {y}, B union {y} is Hamiltonian: any such extension together with the untouched complementary side would two-cover H after deleting only the other hole, contradicting kappa_2(H)=2. Consequently every leaf edge xy of the minimum-pair graph has the dichotomy: a unanimity-free distance-one carrier in H-x, or a complement K with universal balanced Hamiltonicity and universal failure of all four one-hole extensions across every balanced split. In particular, if the unanimity-free branch is excluded and no such universally blocked complement can occur, the minimum-pair graph has minimum degree at least two and therefore contains a cycle.
