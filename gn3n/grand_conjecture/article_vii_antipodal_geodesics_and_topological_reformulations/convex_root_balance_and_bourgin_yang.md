# Convex root balance and Bourgin–Yang multiplicity

## Positive exact-root balance

A convex combination of type-\(A\) roots \(e_i-e_j\) is zero exactly when the corresponding directed arcs contain a nonzero circulation, equivalently a directed cycle. Thus every cellular root zero has a finite recurrence interpretation inside one carrier face.

For the exact inversion coordinates put
\[
c(\pi)=m+1-q(\pi),\qquad
\psi(\pi)=e_{p(\pi)}-e_{c(\pi)}.
\]
Reversal negates \(\psi\). If \(k=\kappa_2(H)>0\), then
\[
m-p(\pi)-c(\pi)=d_2(\pi)\ge k
\]
for every chamber. Hence the image of the odd exact-root map lies in a subspace of dimension at most \(n-k-4\). Bourgin--Yang yields a zero set of dimension at least \(k+2\), and in particular a carrier face \(F\) with strictly positive weights
\[
\sum_{\pi\in\mathcal V(F)}\lambda_\pi\psi(\pi)=0.
\]

For a chamber of positive deficiency, \(\psi\) records the two path orders in the canonical partial cover \(P_\pi\mid X_\pi\mid Q_\pi\), while
\[
|X_\pi|=m-p(\pi)-c(\pi).
\]
The topology therefore acts directly on partial two-cover size profiles, not merely on auxiliary switch data.
