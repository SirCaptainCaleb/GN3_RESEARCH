## Development

Fix x with kappa_2(H)=1. For each proper face G let A(G),C(G) be the unanimously left/right canonical-role sets. Fix any total order of V(H)-{x}. If every proper face has some unanimous vertex other than x, label its barycentric vertex by the signed least-ranked element of (A(G)-{x}) union -(C(G)-{x}). Reversal negates labels and comparable faces cannot receive complementary labels. Ky Fan on S^{n-2}, with exactly n-1 available absolute labels, gives an alternating maximal simplex using every label in V(H)-{x}. In its bottom chamber every vertex other than x is unanimously on a canonical side, so the unique canonical hole is x; the left/right roles alternate in the prescribed order. Therefore for each fixed x: either some proper face satisfies (A(G) union C(G)) subseteq {x}, or every prescribed ordering of V(H)-{x} yields an alternating deletion cover with hole exactly x. Counting these orders is sharp. If m=n-1=2r, one unordered r|r state certifies at most 2(r!)^2 orders, so at least m!/(2(r!)^2)=1/2 binom(m,r) states occur, exactly the total number of balanced bipartitions. If m=2r+1, one r|(r+1) state certifies at most r!(r+1)! orders, so at least binom(m,r), again all balanced bipartitions. Hence failure of the x-sparse-face alternative forces every balanced bipartition of H-x to have both sides Hamiltonian. In a counterexample, adjoining x to either side of every such partition is non-Hamiltonian.
