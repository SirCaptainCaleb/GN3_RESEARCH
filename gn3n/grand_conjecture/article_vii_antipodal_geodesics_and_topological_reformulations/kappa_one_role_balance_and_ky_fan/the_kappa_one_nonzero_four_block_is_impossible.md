# The kappa-one nonzero four-block is impossible

## Composition

Assume kappa_2(H)=1. In the augmented exact-root role-balance argument, any nonzero carrier reduces to one freely permuted central four-block. Every order of that block would have central status word in {0010,0110,1001,1011}. Five permutations suffice to contradict boundary reversal: P0=(b,d,c,a), P1=(c,d,b,a), P2=(d,b,a,c), P3=(d,c,a,b), P4=(d,c,b,a). Shared-triple comparisons imply: P0!=0110 forces P1=0110 while P0=0110 forces P1!=0110; P0!=1001 forces P3=0110; P1!=1001 forces P2=0110; P2!=1001 forces P3=1001; and P1,P3,P4 have the same first status bit. Checking P0 in the four allowed words yields a contradiction in every case. Therefore the nonzero kappa-one four-block branch is empty, so the augmented exact-root topology must produce a zero exact root.
