# Deletion distance one: role-balanced four-blocks and Ky Fan alternation

## Deletion distance one: elimination of the nonzero exact-root branch

Assume \(\kappa_2(H)=1\). The exact-root image leaves three surplus antipodal dimensions, which may be spent on three prescribed actual-vertex role coordinates. Borsuk--Ulam therefore supplies a positively balanced carrier with zero weighted role average for each prescribed vertex.

If the carrier contains no zero exact root, exact-root compression forces a unique geometry: one freely permuted four-vertex central block with equal left and right sides. The three prescribed zero role averages, together with exact-root balance, force all four central roles to balance.

The four-block is impossible. Boundary antisymmetry on five selected permutations forces incompatible central status words among \(0010,0110,1001,1011\). Therefore every positively balanced \(\kappa_2=1\) carrier contains a zero exact root.

Consequently, for minimum-counterexample work, the nonzero exact-root branch is closed and the active problem begins at \(p=c\).
