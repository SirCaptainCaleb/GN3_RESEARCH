# Research Reflexes


These are recurring self-checks, not separate tasks.

**Theorem reflex.** Keep the actual theorem target separate from the current sufficient condition. Prefer work that can change the theorem, expose structure, or unlock a route; do not spend substantial effort polishing a local refinement that cannot matter downstream. When a route stalls, return to the earliest step where the needed strength was lost and ask what information was discarded there. Do not replace a proof by an unbounded hand-case ladder: case analysis is appropriate only when the complete configuration space is proved uniformly finite over the theorem class.

**Elevation reflex.** For every worthwhile result, identify the mechanism that really proves it and seek its strongest natural formulation. Test which hypotheses can be weakened or removed, useful generalizations, and stronger consequences. If the result belongs to a predecessor chain, try to apply it as early as possible without importing all predecessor steps; identify the minimum additional conditions needed there and ask whether they can themselves be proved or forced. If a result materially changes a Main Line and the new synthesis is already clear, include that Main Line update in the same publication batch. This is synthesis, not a substitute for keeping sustained route-local development in a Research Line hot chunk.

**Crystallization reflex.** Treat the hot chunk as a subsection being written, not an append-only log. Rewrite it as understanding improves. Crystallize only at a natural subsection boundary, after the local mathematical story is stable enough to stand on its own. A mature Research Line should be roughly section-sized before its content is rewritten and compressed into an existing or new Main Line.

**Correction reflex.** When a durable claim or Main Line argument is found to be wrong or unsupported, determine the narrowest actual failure before propagating distrust. An audit failure does not by itself mean the claim is false. Repair the canonical object in place when the statement or proof can be corrected. If an upstream research result is genuinely false, mark or replace that result and repair the direct consumers that materially relied on it. Keep audit failure, refutation, and supersession distinct. Use version-guarded staged publication with the actual dependencies; do not create a duplicate repair manuscript. A corrected current version should return to independent audit rather than being self-certified by its repairing worker. For an older crystallized Research Line subsection use repair_line_chunk(); for ordinary ongoing development use the hot chunk; direct Main Line editing is appropriate when the Main Line synthesis itself is what requires correction.

**Stewardship reflex.** Put mathematics in its canonical home rather than creating parallel versions. Durable non-self-contained claims must record the premises actually used; self-contained claims should explicitly declare none. Integrate stronger results, supersede genuinely obsolete formulations, close or promote Brainstorms when their status is clear, and compress accumulated work when synthesis can replace clutter. Preserve genuinely different approaches when neither supersedes the other. Leave the shared research representation cleaner and more useful to the next researcher.
