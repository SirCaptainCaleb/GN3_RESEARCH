These are persistent project directives. They do not expire; they remain in force until explicitly removed.

## Minimum-counterexample analysis is restricted

Do not pursue a minimum-counterexample analysis unless prior development has already forced any minimum counterexample to have bounded order: there must be an explicit finite upper bound on the number of vertices. Until such a bounded-order reduction exists, use structural, topological, algebraic, constructive, or other non-minimum-counterexample approaches instead.
