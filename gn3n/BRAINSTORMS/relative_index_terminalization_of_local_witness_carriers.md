# Relative-index terminalization of local-witness carriers

All terminal finite local-witness configurations are now classified and reduce to an audited ten-vertex local two-cover. The sole missing implication is global: convert an escape chamber/subface with the innermost witness removed into a new balanced carrier supported on farther witness edges, or directly into a spanning two-cover.

Let the fixed-path local-witness labels be ordered from the center outward. A Borsuk--Ulam/Bourgin--Yang zero gives a proper face C whose chamber labels positively balance edgewise. Let e be the innermost occurring witness edge. Every chamber of C is protected from earlier edges and both orientations of e occur. If the two determining windows can be separated through the product structure of C, one can fix the corresponding local block orders to obtain a nonempty refinement/subface on which e is absent; in the terminal overlap case the audited finite classification reduces the residue to a ten-vertex local two-cover. The unresolved theorem is to promote this local removal to topology: either (i) construct a new positive balanced carrier all of whose labels lie strictly outward of e, or (ii) use the local two-cover to splice the surrounding structure into a spanning two-cover. A promising route is relative Z2-index. The +e and -e chamber regions are separated by the e-free locus. If the e-free locus can be realized as a closed invariant separator carrying the outer-label map, cohomological-index subadditivity/Bourgin--Yang should lower the index by at most one and preserve enough index to force a zero of the outer map. Iterating along the finite witness path would terminalize. The technical issue is that a carrier face is not itself antipodally invariant and convex cancellation between +e and -e chambers can occur without an actual e-free chamber in the same barycentric simplex. The audited finite local classification should be used to resolve precisely these mixed cells. This terminalization mechanism is likely part of the proof-transfer core relevant to the generalized Norine meta-conjecture.
