## Simplified statement

Within any permutahedron face, the status bits on pairwise disjoint three-position windows contained in blocks can be prescribed independently: every 0/1 pattern occurs equally often among the compatible spanning orders.

## Statement

Let F=B_1|...|B_k be a permutahedron face of a boundary 3-tournament, and let I be a set of status positions such that the three-position windows {i,i+1,i+2}, i in I, are pairwise disjoint and each lies wholly in one block of F. Then the projection of the chamber status vectors of F to coordinates I is the full cube {0,1}^I, with every pattern occurring equally often.

## Body

# Disjoint internal status windows form Boolean cubes

Let `H` be a boundary 3-tournament and let

`F=B_1|...|B_k`

be a face of the permutahedron. Let `I` be a set of status positions such that the windows

`W_i={i,i+1,i+2}`, `i in I`,

are pairwise disjoint and each window lies wholly inside one block of `F`.

For each `i in I`, let `tau_i` be the involution on chamber permutations of `F` that swaps the vertices in positions `i` and `i+2`. Because `W_i` lies inside one block, `tau_i` preserves the chamber set of `F`. Boundary antisymmetry flips the status at coordinate `i`:

`epsilon_i(tau_i pi)=1-epsilon_i(pi)`.

Because the windows are pairwise disjoint, the involutions `tau_i` commute. Moreover `tau_i` leaves `epsilon_j` unchanged for every other selected coordinate `j in I`, since the three positions of `W_j` are disjoint from the swapped positions.

Thus the group generated by the `tau_i`, isomorphic to `(Z/2Z)^I`, acts freely on every orbit of chamber permutations and the status projection on an orbit runs through every vector in `{0,1}^I` exactly once. Consequently every binary pattern on `I` occurs among the chamber vertices of `F`, and all patterns occur equally often.

This strengthens the face-average cancellation lemma: internal status coordinates on disjoint windows are not merely marginally balanced but jointly independent under the uniform distribution on the chamber set.
