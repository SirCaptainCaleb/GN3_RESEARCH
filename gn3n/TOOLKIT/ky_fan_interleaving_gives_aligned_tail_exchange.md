# Ky Fan interleaving gives an aligned tail exchange

**Summary:** For an equal-side one-hole cover, prescribing p1,q1,...,pr,qr,x turns any alternating-role Ky Fan output into an explicit aligned tail exchange of the two old supports.

## Statement

Let H-x=P|Q with P={p_1,...,p_r}, Q={q_1,...,q_r}, and suppose an alternating-role deletion-cover conclusion applies. For the prescribed order p_1,q_1,...,p_r,q_r,x, deleting p_j or q_j yields the corresponding parity-flip tail exchange of P and Q; deleting x leaves the original support pair.

## Body

Let
[
H-x=Pmid Q,qquad
P={p_1,ldots,p_r},quad
Q={q_1,ldots,q_r},
]
and suppose an alternating-role deletion-cover conclusion applies.

Prescribe the order
[
p_1,q_1,p_2,q_2,ldots,p_r,q_r,x.
]
After deleting one label, the two support roles are the two parity classes of the remaining order. Before the deleted position they agree with the original (P/Q) parity classes; after deletion the parity flips.

If the hole is (p_j), then, up to swapping the two new supports,
[
A={p_i:i<j}cup{q_i:ige j},
]
[
B={q_i:i<j}cup{p_i:i>j}cup{x}.
]

If the hole is (q_j), then
[
A={p_i:ile j}cup{q_i:i>j},
]
[
B={q_i:i<j}cup{p_i:i>j}cup{x}.
]

If the hole is the final label (x), the parity classes are exactly (P) and (Q).

Thus Ky Fan alternation on the interleaved prescribed order becomes an explicit aligned tail exchange of the old supports.
