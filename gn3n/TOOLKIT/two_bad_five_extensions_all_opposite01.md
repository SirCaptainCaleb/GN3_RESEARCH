# Two non-Hamiltonian five-extensions force all four opposite five-deletions Hamiltonian

**Summary:** Two non-Hamiltonian five-extensions force all four opposite five-deletions Hamiltonian.

## Statement

Let H be a boundary tournament, let X be a four-vertex set, and let a,b be distinct vertices outside X. If H[X union {a}] and H[X union {b}] are both non-Hamiltonian, then for every x in X the five-set (X-{x}) union {a,b} is Hamiltonian.

## Body

Put S=X union {a,b}, a six-vertex set. By the four-of-six theorem in smallset01, at least four of the six one-vertex deletions of S are Hamiltonian. The deletions S-{a}=X union {b} and S-{b}=X union {a} are non-Hamiltonian by hypothesis. Therefore the remaining four deletions must all be Hamiltonian. These are exactly S-{x}=(X-{x}) union {a,b} for x in X.
