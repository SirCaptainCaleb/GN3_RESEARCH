# Alon's Combinatorial Nullstellensatz

**Summary:** A nonzero top-degree coefficient forces a nonzero evaluation on every sufficiently large Cartesian grid.

## Statement

Let F be a field and f∈F[x_1,...,x_n] have total degree Σt_i. If the coefficient of ∏x_i^{t_i} is nonzero, then for arbitrary finite S_i⊂F with |S_i|>t_i there exists s∈∏S_i with f(s)≠0.
