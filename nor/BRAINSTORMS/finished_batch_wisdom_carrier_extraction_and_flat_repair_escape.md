### What combines effectively

The strongest general framework combines three different jobs:
1. The two-phase ordered-tail automaton and rankwise prefix/suffix reachability (110 and the flag-prism Toolkit) specify what an extracted witness must retain.
2. The switch-cut permutohedral prism (106,111,116) supplies a reversal-compatible domain of the right dimension.
3. Alternating three-window certificates (97,101,108) and signed physical-middle labels (115,116) supply labels tied to actual bad orders rather than arbitrary support choices.

Keep these jobs distinct. A phase bit says whether a switch has occurred; the cut parameter specifies where it is proposed to occur. They are related through a completed path but are not interchangeable coordinates on every partial state. Nor is a separator symbol already a bijection between coordinate gaps and window cuts.

The next general-uniformity goal should be one carrier-to-path extraction statement, with the complete ordered tail and support in its hypotheses. Prove either that a connected separating carrier can be converted into compatible prefix and suffix witnesses, or that a minimal root-zero carrier can be reduced to a controlled local residue and repaired there. Do not call an arbitrary complementary-label edge a NOR witness: equal physical middles can belong to different windows and different orders.

### The localization boundary

Subsection 106 proves only that a root-zero carrier contains a tied block of size at least r. It does not prove that a minimal zero has a block of size exactly r. In particular, the two caps P_V x {0,m} are part of the prism boundary and contain the entire permutohedron, with the tied block V. A forced zero can remain in a large cap face. For r=3, subsections 109 and 113 classify what happens IF a carrier has just one three-coordinate block; they do not establish that such a carrier exists.

Therefore A_2 circulation classification is ready as a conditional local tool, while a large-carrier reduction or cap-removal argument is the missing global theorem. Such a theorem must preserve the condition that each selected window really violates its cut target. Subdivision labels should be convex combinations of labels in the same minimal product face; averaging over a larger incident star would destroy the stated localization control.

The tangent construction (104) has a genuine odd, nonzero tangent vector at each bad order, unlike the earlier affine reflection. Its present obstacles are compatible extension over all faces and the even-n restriction of the hairy-ball obstruction. Put it behind the switch-prism route until it supplies a gluing rule unavailable there.

### The most concrete ternary subproblem

Treat the coboundary-flat alternating sector as a separate constructive campaign:
- 99 replaces the false fixed-tournament target by a switching-class target.
- 102 gives both directions of flat endpoint repair.
- 95 retains the fourth affected variation and shows transport by two or three positions, or annihilation.
- The audit in 117 repairs 103: two identical blockers cancel when the switch tetrahedron is flat. In a curved orientation they need not cancel; an eight-coordinate prescribed-carrier example defeats every consecutive pair insertion.

These pieces now share the same sector and can be used together. The useful next lemma is escape from a closed component of the full-support repair graph, ideally by reaching a singleton run or a locally flat paired insertion. A singleton run already permits insertion of any exterior vertex in the pure alternating sector. What remains is to force that favorable configuration, not merely to give it another equivalent description. Two-or-three-position transport alone gives no decreasing potential and can jump another switch.

For the pure three-element front circuit, retain 100's matching front/rear pair cycles and the audited one-step propagation. The tube escapes in 91 and 94 remain conditional because their pair-cycle persistence used a dropped coordinate. The viable task is to control a first departure and return of the pair cycle using full-support orders; repeated tube propagation is not established.

### What to stop using as closure steps

- Endpoint attainability on each support does not synchronize ordered tails or switch phases (51).
- Accessible support families need not be union-closed; the separate top-missing-square Brainstorm identifies witness incompatibility, not a generic Frankl implication.
- Fixed representatives, unchecked full-curvature tube propagation, and universal two-blocker cancellation each have a recorded obstruction.
- More odd labels or reachability reformulations are useful only if they supply a new adjacency, carrier-reduction, or witness-synchronization lemma.

Recommended allocation: one general campaign on carrier extraction in the switch prism; one ternary campaign on escape in the coboundary-flat repair graph. Use the signed-middle and Radon labels as extraction diagnostics, and use curvature to mark precisely where flat cancellation ceases to apply. This focuses the batch on two explicit missing lemmas while preserving the broader formulations for later use. The grand conjecture remains open in the present work.
