# Article I — Ordered-tuple Norine geodesics

The NOR index (k) is the arity of the colored tuple of consecutive cube vertices. Thus (N_k) colors
[
(X_0,ldots,X_{k-1})
]
and seeks an antipodal geodesic whose sliding color word changes at most once.

The anchor levels are indexed literally by tuple size: (N_1) colors vertices, (N_2) colors edges, (N_3) colors three-vertex windows, and (N_4) colors four-vertex windows. GN3 belongs to (N_4).

For translation-invariant colorings, an (N_k) window has (r=k-1) flipped coordinate directions and can be encoded by (h(v_1,ldots,v_r)). The coordinate arity (r) is auxiliary and must not be used as the NOR index. Earlier results written in that convention have been reindexed as statements about (N_{r+1}).

This article develops the one-change conjecture under that convention and keeps basepoint-dependent window models separate as comparison classes and guardrails.
