# Article I — Ordered-tuple Norine geodesics

The main NOR program studies directed ordered-tuple colorings
\[
h(v_1,\ldots,v_k)
\]
with reversal antisymmetry
\[
h(v_k,\ldots,v_1)=1-h(v_1,\ldots,v_k),
\]
and asks for a permutation whose sliding color word changes at most once.

Equivalently, this is the translation-invariant ordered-window problem on antipodal cube geodesics. Absolute Boolean-rank or starting-vertex information is not part of the directed tuple coloring.

A previously recorded rank-parity construction refutes only a broader basepoint-dependent cube-window model. It does **not** refute the directed NOR Grand Conjecture. The distinction is now explicit throughout this Article.

A second, orientation-blind higher-uniformity family is also mathematically natural and may independently be true. It contains ordinary undirected Norine at uniformity one, but it does not contain the directed GN3 \(k=3\) example, so it is tracked as a neighboring conjectural family rather than as the common grand formulation.

For directed tuples, \(N_2\) follows from the Hamilton-path theorem for tournaments, while \(N_3\) contains GN3 as a canonical subclass.
