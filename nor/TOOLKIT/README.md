# Toolkit

Toolkit is for genuinely reusable mathematics whose natural formulation transcends its originating route. There is no intermediate Toolkit state: an object either is Toolkit or it is not. Route-local lemma graphs belong in Sections/Subsections.

- [Disjoint internal status windows form Boolean cubes](disjoint_status_windows_form_boolean_cubes.md) — lemma — Pairwise disjoint internal triple windows inside permutahedron blocks can have their status bits prescribed independently.
- [Oriented-tree balance pairs every used edge](oriented_tree_balance_pairs_every_used_edge.md) — lemma — Positive balance of oriented tree-edge labels forces both orientations of every used tree edge.
- [Relative-order gauges force common face blocks](relative_order_gauges_force_common_face_blocks.md) — lemma — Balanced relative-order gauges force their endpoint labels into common permutahedron face blocks.
- [Status averages localize at permutahedron face boundaries](permutahedron_face_status_localization.md) — lemma — For any ordered-partition face, internal triple-window statuses average to zero; only coordinates adjacent to block boundaries can survive.
- [Superincreasing compression preserves zero chambers](superincreasing_compression_preserves_zero_chambers.md) — lemma — A bounded odd defect vector can be compressed to one odd scalar with the same zero chambers using superincreasing weights.
- [Ternary reversal-odd labels are endpoint selectors; changes are inner/outer defects](ternary_reversal_odd_labels_are_endpoint_selectors.md) — lemma — For h(a,b,c), let color 0 select a and color 1 select c. Reversal keeps the same selected endpoint. Then 01 on a,b,c,d selects a,d (outer defect) and 10 selects c,b (inner defect). NOR asks for an order with no inner defects or no outer defects.
- [Two-cover status words avoid three local patterns](two_cover_words_avoid_three_local_patterns.md) — lemma — A binary status word satisfies q≤p+1 iff it avoids 001, 011, and 0101.
- [Abstract ternary insertion theorem](ternary01.md) — other — In any ternary path system with cyclic coverage and reversal symmetry, every exterior vertex has at least two insertion positions into a tight path, and every n-vertex system has at least 2^(n-1) Hamilton tight paths.
- [Alon's Combinatorial Nullstellensatz](literature_combinatorial_nullstellensatz.md) — other — A nonzero top-degree coefficient forces a nonzero evaluation on every sufficiently large Cartesian grid.
- [Discrete ham sandwich theorem](literature_discrete_ham_sandwich_theorem.md) — other — Any d finite point sets in R^d admit one affine hyperplane simultaneously bisecting all d sets.
- [Entropy compression: finite reconstruction and combinatorial algorithms](entropy_compression_finite_reconstruction_and_combinatorial_algorithms.md) — other — Failures can reveal enough erased data to reconstruct the random input from a shorter history. Prove the decoder and count all histories; then compare against the number of random inputs. This can establish a finite successful construction without a monotone defect count.
- [Every nonblock four-set in a 3-(8,4,1) design has a canonical complementary matching frame](in_a_3841_design_has_a_canonical_complementary_matching_frame.md) — other — A 3-(8,4,1) design canonically matches every nonblock four-set to its complement through four complementary block pairs.
- [Finite block-design toolkit](finite_blockdesign_toolkit.md) — other — Reusable finite block-design incidence and complement structure.
- [Fractional path-cover and LP toolkit](fractionaltoolkit01.md) — other — Fractional path-cover duality, deletion averaging, dual slack accounting, laminar rounding, and uncrossing form a reusable toolkit for boundary tournaments.
- [Grossman–Häggkvist–Yeo alternating-cycle theorem](literature_grossman_haggkvist_yeo_alternating_cycle_theorem.md) — other — If every vertex sees at least two colors and there is no color-separating cut vertex, then the edge-colored graph contains an alternating cycle.
- [Minty's walk-potential proof: paths emerge by extremality](literature_minty_walk_potential_extremization.md) — other — Optimize over walks, not paths. If every closed excursion has nonpositive potential, loop erasure never hurts, so an extremizer is automatically simple. For NOR, seek a defect potential whose extremizers also cannot profitably repeat cube dimensions.
- [Quadratic component-size potential under path-cover moves](quadraticpotential01.md) — other — Quadratic component-size potential under path-cover moves
- [Two-deletion reconstruction for partitions into linearly ordered blocks](pairdeletionreconstruct01.md) — other — Two-deletion reconstruction for partitions into linearly ordered blocks
- [Wu--Yang chain-level Borsuk--Ulam proof of Norine's conjecture](literature_wu_yang_norine_chain_level_borsuk_ulam.md) — other — Norine's original non-geodesic antipodal-coloring conjecture is proved by reducing to rook labels and excluding them with a Freudenthal/chain-level Borsuk--Ulam obstruction.
- [Freudenthal cube-to-simplex coning construction](freudenthal_cube_to_simplex_coning_construction.md) — theorem — Freudenthal-triangulate Q_n, cone each positive facet to a new coordinate vertex, and the result is exactly a triangulated n-simplex. Each permutation sector is cut in two at the midpoint of the edge from the distinguished vertex 0 to its first coordinate vertex.
- [NOR reversal is antipodality on the simplex-boundary Coxeter sphere](nor_reversal_is_antipodality_on_the_simplex_boundary_coxeter_sphere.md) — theorem — The completed cube places all coordinate orders on a standard antipodal sphere: permutations are chambers of sd(boundary simplex), and reversing a permutation is exactly subset complementation on that sphere.
- [One-change NOR is reachability in a two-layer flag prism](one_change_nor_is_reachability_in_a_two_layer_flag_prism.md) — theorem — Keep the last r-1 coordinates as a flag state and add one bit recording whether the unique color switch has already happened. Then NOR is exactly source-to-sink reachability in a two-layer prism over the barycentric simplex.
- [Pedestrian dimension-sweep lemma for cube subgraphs](pedestrian_dimension_sweep_lemma_for_cube_subgraphs.md) — theorem — Put one pedestrian at every vertex and activate edge-permutations one at a time. In an undirected subgraph of Q_n, coordinate-matching swaps give a geodesic of length at least the average degree. In a directed graph with an arc decomposition into directed cycles, cycle rotations give a directed walk of length at least the average outdegree, though the walk need not be geodesic.
- [Polytopal Sperner packet theorem for face-respecting labels](polytopal_sperner_packet_theorem_for_face_respecting_labels.md) — theorem — A face-respecting vertex labeling of any triangulated polytope forces one simplex whose geometric simplex meets the convex hull of its labels. This is the Brouwer/Sperner form suited to cut-valued labels rather than coordinate-valued labels.
- [Projected Brouwer normal-cone alternative](projected_brouwer_normal_cone_alternative.md) — theorem — Project x-epsilon V(x) back to a convex polytope and apply Brouwer. A fixed point is either a zero of V or a boundary point where V is normal to a supporting face. This gives a parity-free fixed-point alternative useful for carrier-support descent.
- [Sperner choice-rule theorem on the completed-cube simplex boundary](sperner_choice_rule_on_the_completed_cube_boundary.md) — theorem — Any rule choosing one element from each nonempty subset has an ordering pi for which every prefix chooses its newest element. This is exactly Sperner lemma on the barycentric simplex boundary produced by the cube-to-simplex completion.
- [Strict containment in deletion-partition trees forces a unique smaller support](strict_containment_in_deletion_partition_trees.md) — theorem — A support in a deletion-partition tree properly contains at most one other support; containment forces a leaf and a precise path-with-pendant-edges structure.
- [Ternary NOR decomposes into a simplex-face orientation plus a defect vertex](ternary_nor_decomposes_into_triangle_orientation_and_defect_vertex.md) — theorem — A ternary NOR coloring is an honest orientation of every triangle of the coordinate simplex, plus at most one marked defect vertex per triangle. The color of a-b-c is the triangle orientation, flipped exactly when b is the marked vertex.
- [The ternary memory lift is the flag-adjacency graph of the simplex triangulation](ternary_memory_lift_is_the_flag_adjacency_graph_of_the_simplex.md) — theorem — The memory-lift graph is already inside the simplex: its vertices are oriented 2-faces (three consecutive prefix sets), and its edges connect overlapping 2-faces inside a 3-face. So Connector/Sperner topology can be applied to the actual memory states.
