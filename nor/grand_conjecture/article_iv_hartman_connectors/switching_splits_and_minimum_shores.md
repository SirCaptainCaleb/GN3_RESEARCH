# Switching splits and minimum-shore structure

Failure of a protected shortcut produces a universal switching split. In the normalized tournament representation, the flat residue has B dominating A with B→z→A→x. Minimum unresolved shores are protectively complete; a canonical common-A3 protected triangle is available, and each minimum-shore edge is either refinable or cyclic.

These minimum-shore statements supply local structure, not spanning closure. The later connector bases and homogeneous-cut composition theorem need neither minimum-shore status nor a triangle hypothesis. Invoke minimum shores only when their protective refinement structure is needed to force growth or organize an obstruction.
