# Full deletion connector orders glue from four coherent witnesses or two nonadjacent-gap witnesses

Put U=A∪{x,z}, and for each d in a chosen subset D⊆A let C_d be a compatible zero connector on U\{d}. Coherence means agreement of all relative comparisons on intersections, including the special coordinates. If |D|≥4 and the C_d are pairwise coherent, their comparisons determine a unique total order on U: every triple lies in at least one deletion domain, hence transitivity and every internal zero condition are inherited; deletion witnesses also certify the two endpoint ports. The resulting order is a spanning compatible connector. For two coherent deletion connectors C_d,C_e, write their shared order on U\{d,e} as O. If the insertion gaps for d and e are separated by at least two gaps, insertion is compatible. If the same gap is used, the tournament cocycle identity allows an orientation making the two new triple colors simultaneously zero. Only adjacent distinct gaps can expose one genuinely unverified triple (d,w,e); the color-one case is an exact obstruction to that gluing move, not a proof of nonexistence of another spanning connector. Thus the residual global problem is coherent selection or a new exchange eliminating the adjacent-gap defect.
