# Three coherent full deletion connectors localize to one omitted triple

Study three pairwise common-order-compatible full deletion connectors C_a,C_b,C_c. Their pair comparisons define a total order on U except possibly a directed comparison 3-cycle on a,b,c. In the acyclic case they determine a spanning order whose ternary zero windows and endpoint ports are inherited except possibly the consecutive omitted triple. This is a closure-relevant strengthening of the four-witness result, with explicit unresolved cyclic and color-one exceptions.
