## Development

Keep the path-normalized square-path gauge of the previous subsection. Let sigma_i be the switching bit on c_i, and for a missing shore vertex a let r_i=1 when c_i dominates a before choosing the switch bit of a. Interior insertion is possible exactly at 0011 or 1100. For prepending, after possibly switching a, one needs a to dominate both c_1 and c_2; this is possible when r_1=r_2, and the new first ordered pair is forward in the original fixed representative exactly when the chosen switch bit of a equals sigma_1. Equivalently compatible prepending occurs exactly when r_1=r_2=sigma_1. Dually, compatible appending occurs exactly when r_{m-1}=r_m=1-sigma_m. Therefore a compatible connector is support-maximal iff every missing shore vertex has an incidence word r which avoids both 0011 and 1100 and also fails these two endpoint equalities. This blocker description is invariant under the global complement ambiguity of the path-normalizing switching bits.
