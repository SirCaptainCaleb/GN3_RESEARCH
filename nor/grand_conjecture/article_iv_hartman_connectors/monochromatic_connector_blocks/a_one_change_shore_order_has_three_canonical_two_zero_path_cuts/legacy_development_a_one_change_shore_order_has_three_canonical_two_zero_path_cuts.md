## Development

Let O=(a_1,...,a_k) be a shore order with ternary word 0^p1^q, p,q>=1. For any cut index j in {p,p+1,p+2}, let P_j=(a_1,...,a_j) and let Q_j=(a_k,a_{k-1},...,a_{j+1}). These two orders partition the shore. Every internal window of P_j has original rank at most p and hence color zero. Every internal window of Q_j is the reversal of an original window of rank at least p+1 and hence also has color zero. Thus the one-change order canonically supplies three neighboring two-zero-path covers. If t_i records the fixed tournament direction of a_i,a_{i+1}, the near-cut endpoint ports are t_{j-1} for P_j and 1-t_{j+1} for Q_j. Hence failure of all three cuts to have both near-cut ports forward forces t_{p-1}<=t_{p+1}<=t_{p+3} and t_p<=t_{p+2}. The three-cut family provides a finite local interface on which x and z can move independently.
