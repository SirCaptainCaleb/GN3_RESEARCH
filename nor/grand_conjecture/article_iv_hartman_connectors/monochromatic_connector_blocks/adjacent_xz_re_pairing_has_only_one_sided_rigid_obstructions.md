# Adjacent xz re-pairing has only one-sided rigid obstructions

## Composition

In a zero connector segment H,p,u,x,z,v,w,S, the two local shore pairs p,u and v,w are forward. Label each local vertex by its dominance relation to H and to S. Any perfect matching of the four shore vertices can be placed around the adjacent pair x,z after orienting each matched pair forward; it glues exactly when the left matched pair has equal H-labels and the right matched pair has equal S-labels. If no alternative matching glues, the obstruction is necessarily one-sided: either both right vertices have the H-label opposite to the left pair while S distinguishes the left pair, or both left vertices have the S-label opposite to the right pair while H distinguishes the right pair. These two cases are mutually exclusive. Hence a rigid adjacent-xz quartet exports failure to exactly one exterior collar.
