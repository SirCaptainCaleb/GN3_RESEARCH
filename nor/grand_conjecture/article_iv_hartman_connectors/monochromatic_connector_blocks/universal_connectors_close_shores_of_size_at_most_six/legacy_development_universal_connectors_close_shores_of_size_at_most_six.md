## Development

For four shore vertices, split them into two pairs and orient each pair forward. Then the order first-pair, x, z, second-pair is a compatible zero connector. For five shore vertices, choose a transitive triple and orient the remaining pair forward; placing the pair before x,z and the transitive triple after x,z again gives a compatible zero connector. For six shore vertices, use the existing complementary-transitive-triples theorem together with the two-transitive-block connector construction. Therefore every switching split with shore size at most six has a spanning compatible connector, with no minimum-shore hypothesis. Any minimal obstruction to collective connector construction has shore size at least seven.
