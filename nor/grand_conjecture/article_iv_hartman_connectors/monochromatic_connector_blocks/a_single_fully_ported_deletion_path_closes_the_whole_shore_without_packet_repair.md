# A single fully ported deletion path closes the whole shore without packet repair

Let A be a shore of size at least two in the normalized split B→z→A→x, and let a∈A. Assume A\{a} admits one fully ported zero Hamiltonian path P. Then C=(P,x,z,a) is a compatible zero connector spanning A∪{x,z}. Indeed, triples wholly within P are zero; the terminal forward edge of P gives zero on its crossing triple into x; and the triples ending (x,z,a) are zero by the special-coordinate identities. The initial endpoint pair comes from P and the final pair z→a is forward. When P is a singleton, the same conclusion follows directly from the two special-coordinate triples. By the size-independent connector insertion theorem, C closes the global NOR instance. Thus a genuine minimal connector obstruction cannot possess a one-path fully ported deletion witness, irrespective of any separately studied fixed-boundary cyclic packet.
