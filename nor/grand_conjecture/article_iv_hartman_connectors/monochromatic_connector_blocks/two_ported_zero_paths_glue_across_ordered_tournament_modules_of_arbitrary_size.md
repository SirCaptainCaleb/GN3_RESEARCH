# Two ported zero paths glue across ordered tournament modules of arbitrary size

When a shore tournament is an ordered domination join H→K, any two fully ported monochromatic-zero path covers of H and K concatenate railwise to give a ported two-path cover of H∪K: all crossing triples are forward transitive. Iteration along strongly connected tournament components yields a spanning compatible connector and hence full NOR whenever every strong component has a ported two-path cover. Unconditionally this includes every shore with all strong components of order at most six, regardless of total size, by the universal two-transitive-class theorem. An inclusion-minimal connector obstruction with multiple strong components must contain at least two components without a ported zero Hamiltonian path, by the extremal-module absorption theorem.
