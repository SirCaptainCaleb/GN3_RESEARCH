# A one-change shore order has three canonical two-zero-path cuts

The cuts p,p+1,p+2 of a good 0^p1^q shore order give three two-zero-path covers. Their near-cut ports are t_(j-1) and 1-t_(j+1). Failure of all three favorable near-cut pairs forces two-step edge monotonicity. The remote endpoint ports still require repair.
