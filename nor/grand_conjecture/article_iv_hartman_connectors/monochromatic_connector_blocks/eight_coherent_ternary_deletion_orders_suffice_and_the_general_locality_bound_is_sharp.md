# Eight coherent ternary deletion orders suffice, and the general locality bound is sharp

If at least 2r+2 coordinate deletions admit pairwise order-coherent one-change r-window orders, their uniquely reconstructed full order is one-change. A first pair of adjacent opposite-color windows plus the first subsequent return window involves at most 2r+1 labels, so survives a deletion outside them. The bound is sharp even for reversal-odd r-tuple labels: on [2r+1], prescribe the full consecutive-window word 0,1^r,0, assign all skipped-middle deletion windows zero and all other newly introduced deletion windows one, and extend by reversal oddness. For ternary NOR the coherence threshold improves from ten to eight; seven coherent deletion witnesses alone do not prove a selected order good.
