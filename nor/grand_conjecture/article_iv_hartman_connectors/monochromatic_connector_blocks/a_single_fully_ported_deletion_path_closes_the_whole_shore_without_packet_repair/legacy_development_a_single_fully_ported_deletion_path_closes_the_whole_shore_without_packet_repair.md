# A single fully ported deletion path closes the whole shore without packet repair — preserved pre-item development

## One-deletion Hamiltonian port implies unconditional spanning closure

Fix the switching-normalized flat split B→z→A→x. Suppose a∈A and P is a fully ported monochromatic-zero Hamiltonian path on A\{a}: every consecutive ternary window of P has color zero and its first and last ordered pairs point forward in the fixed tournament (when defined).

THEOREM. For |A|>=2, the order C=(P,x,z,a) is a spanning compatible monochromatic-zero connector of A∪{x,z}. Thus the complete NOR instance closes by the homogeneous-cut insertion theorem, with no coherence, exterior collar hypothesis, cyclic-packet repair, or restriction on the placement of a relative to the existing shore path.

PROOF. For |P|>=2, internal triples of P are zero, the new triple (p_{s−1},p_s,x) has color 1−t(p_{s−1},p_s)=0 by the terminal forward port, and the last two new triples (p_s,x,z),(x,z,a) have color zero by the special-pair signatures. The initial ordered pair of C is the initial forward port of P, and the final pair z→a is forward. If |P|=1, C=(p,x,z,a); the two triples are (p,x,z),(x,z,a), both zero, and the exposed pairs p→x,z→a are forward. For |A|=1, instead use (z,a,x) directly. Apply the size-independent connector insertion theorem over B. QED.

DUAL FORM. The order (a,x,z,P) is also a spanning compatible zero connector whenever P has a forward initial port; its new windows (a,x,z),(x,z,p_1),(z,p_1,p_2) are zero and its first pair a→x and final pair of P are forward.

COROLLARY 1. A minimal whole-shore compatible-connector obstruction of shore size at least two has no one-vertex deletion admitting a fully ported zero Hamiltonian path. Hence every fully ported two-path deletion-cover witness in an obstruction has TWO paths, EACH OF SIZE AT LEAST TWO. Indeed, if one deletion path is a singleton (q), orient the pair {a,q} as a forward 2-vertex zero path and keep the other deletion path unchanged. These two fully ported zero paths cover A and close the instance unconditionally. The omitted a can always be paired with q in the forward direction in a tournament.

COROLLARY 2 (retroactive frontier simplification). The cyclic three-deletion coherent-packet results (§§44,46,48,49) study a genuine fixed-collar local repair geometry, but their 'single-path deletion' specialization never produces a global obstruction: each deletion's fully ported one-path witness, independently of the other two, already closes via P,x,z,a. In particular, the double-backward seven-coordinate no-surgery theorem remains a valid fixed-boundary statement and has no bearing on this global single-deletion closure, because P,x,z,a releases that boundary and uses the omitted shore coordinate.

The remaining Article IV closure frontier is to obtain one fully ported shore-deletion Hamiltonian zero path, or a coherent TWO-nonempty-path deletion family, or a direct collective compatible connector; minimum-shore deletion-critical connectors alone do not provide these certificates.
