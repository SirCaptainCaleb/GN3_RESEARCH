C_0=(u,x,z,v,w).

Its three ternary windows are
α(u,x,z)=α(x,z,u)=0,
α(x,z,v)=0,
α(z,v,w)=0.
Hence C_0 has word 000. Its reversal
C_1=(w,v,z,x,u)
has word 111.

This is stronger than an abstract root dependence: every minimum unresolved split contains an actual five-coordinate monochromatic block containing both connector vertices x,z.
