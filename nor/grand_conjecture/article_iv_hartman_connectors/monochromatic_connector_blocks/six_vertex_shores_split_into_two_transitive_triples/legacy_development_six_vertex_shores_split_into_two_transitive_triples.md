# Six-vertex shores split into two transitive triples — preserved pre-item development

## Development

In a six-vertex tournament, count transitive triples by their unique top vertex. The count is the sum of choose(d,2) over the six tournament outdegrees. Their sum is 15, so convexity gives at least 12 transitive triples. The 20 triples are grouped into 10 complementary pairs. If no complementary pair had both triples transitive, there would be at most 10 transitive triples. Hence some complementary pair consists of two transitive triples. Together with the two-block connector theorem, every six-vertex shore has a spanning compatible connector.
