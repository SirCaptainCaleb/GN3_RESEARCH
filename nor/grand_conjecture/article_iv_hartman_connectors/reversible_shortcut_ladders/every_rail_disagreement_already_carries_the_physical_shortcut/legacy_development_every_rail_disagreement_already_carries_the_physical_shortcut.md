# Every rail disagreement already carries the physical shortcut — preserved pre-item development

## Development

At any index i with X_i≠Z_i, the four-coordinate order
(x,w_i,w_{i+1},z)
has consecutive statuses X_i,Z_i and therefore is a genuine transition carrier with physical root x→z.

Its two off-faces are 1-R_i and 1-R_{i+1}. Since a rail disagreement forces R_{i+1}=1-R_i, the carrier is fully curved exactly when X_i=R_i and flat exactly when X_i≠R_i.

So the ladder does more than encode a connector problem: every disagreement explicitly realizes the desired physical shortcut. Curvature records whether that shortcut is already protected.
