# Opposite insertion scans form an exact binary ladder

For distinct exterior coordinates x,z and a middle order W=(w_1,…,w_m), define X_i=α(x,w_i,w_(i+1)), Z_i=α(z,w_i,w_(i+1)), and R_i=α(x,z,w_i). The ternary tournament parity identity on {x,z,w_i,w_(i+1)} gives R_(i+1)⊕R_i=X_i⊕Z_i. Hence the two insertion scans determine a binary ladder whose rung toggles exactly at rail disagreement. This identity is unconditional and local; the existence of a legal global shortcut requires the associated carrier to satisfy all additional boundary requirements.
