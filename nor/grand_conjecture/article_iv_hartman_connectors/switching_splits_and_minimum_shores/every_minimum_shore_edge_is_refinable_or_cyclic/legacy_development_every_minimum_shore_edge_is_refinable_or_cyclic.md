# Every minimum-shore edge is refinable or cyclic — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Fix an oriented edge u->v in the minimum signature shore A. Protected completeness implies that the pair signature alpha(u,v,.) takes the value t(u,v)=1 at some w in A minus {u,v}. The tournament parity identity then gives t(v,w)=t(w,u). Therefore exactly one of two cases holds: either u->w->v, so w is a transitive intermediate refining the edge u->v; or v->w->u, so u->v->w->u is a directed triangle. Thus every shore edge is either refinable by a third shore vertex or belongs to a directed triangle. This is the corrected structural replacement for the false claim that every edge lies in a directed triangle.
