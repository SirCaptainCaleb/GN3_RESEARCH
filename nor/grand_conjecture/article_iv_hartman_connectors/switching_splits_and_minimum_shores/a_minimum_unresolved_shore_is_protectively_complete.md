# A minimum unresolved shore is protectively complete

## Composition

Minimal nonempty signature shore A forces protected pairs inside A and from A to both connector vertices. A union {x,z} is a protected almost-clique with xz the possible missing edge. This strengthens root structure; the universal connector bases need no minimality hypothesis.

## Development

Choose a shortcut-free pair x,z whose smaller nonempty signature shore A has minimum size.

For every u∈A, minimality forces protected shortcut cells x↔u and z↔u: otherwise the signature of x,u or z,u would have a smaller nonempty shore contained in A.

For distinct u,v∈A, their pair signature is constant on every vertex outside A. If u,v were shortcut-free and nonclonal, the opposite signature shore would again be a nonempty subset of A\{u,v}, contradicting minimality.

Therefore A∪{x,z} is a protected almost-clique: every pair is protectively adjacent except possibly xz itself. In particular |A|≥3.
