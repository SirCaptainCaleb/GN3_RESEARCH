# Same-gap absorptions have a rank-two filler — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Let C be path-normalized. Suppose two missing vertices a and b are each insertable in the same interior gap. Choose their switching states so both have local collar 1100 around that gap. Then the two connector vertices on the left dominate both a and b, while both a and b dominate the two connector vertices on the right. Order a,b according to their mutual tournament edge and insert them together in the gap. All new distance-one and distance-two edges point forward. Hence the resulting order is again a compatible monochromatic connector containing both vertices. Thus two absorption directions at one gap always fill a rank-two cell.
