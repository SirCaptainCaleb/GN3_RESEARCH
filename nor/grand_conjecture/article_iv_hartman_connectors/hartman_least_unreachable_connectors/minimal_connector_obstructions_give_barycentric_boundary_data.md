# Minimal connector obstructions give barycentric boundary data

## Composition

An inclusion-minimal shore support without a compatible connector supplies connectors on every proper complement. Missing sets index the barycentric boundary. The empty shore complement is represented by (z,x) in the general size-two state model. Chosen witnesses still require coherent legal transitions; proper-support existence alone supplies no such transitions.

## Development

Assume whole-shore connector construction fails and choose an inclusion-minimal shore subset S with no spanning compatible connector on S together with x,z. Every proper subset T of S has such a connector. Therefore every nonempty missing set U subset S has at least one connector on the complement S minus U together with x,z. These nonempty missing sets are the vertices of the barycentric subdivision of the simplex on S. The absent empty missing set is exactly the desired full connector. A codimension-one inclusion corresponds to absorbing one missing coordinate. Thus the Hartman problem becomes coherence of these proper-support connector states across the subset lattice: rank-one extension is absorption, and rank-two coherence is a local repair-square problem.
