## Development

For a path-normalized connector, an interior absorption only uses the four-vertex collar around its gap. If two missing vertices are absorbed at gaps with disjoint collars, performing either insertion leaves the other collar and all of its required incidences unchanged. Hence both insertion orders are legal and lead to the same enlarged connector up to the obvious commuting order. Thus separated rank-one absorption directions form an actual square in the repair complex.
