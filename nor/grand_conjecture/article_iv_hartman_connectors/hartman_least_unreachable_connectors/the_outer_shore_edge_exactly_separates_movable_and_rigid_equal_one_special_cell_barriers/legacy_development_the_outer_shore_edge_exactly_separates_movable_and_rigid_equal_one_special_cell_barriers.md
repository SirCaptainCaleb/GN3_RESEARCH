# The outer shore edge exactly separates movable and rigid equal-one special-cell barriers — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Combine the audited special-vertex rotation law with one-sided packet surgery.

Let (p,a,x,b,c) be a monochromatic-zero five-coordinate packet with p,a,b,c in the shore. Zero labels give p→a, b→a, b→c. Assume both central rotations are blocked, so u=t(p,b)=t(a,c)=v.

The equal-zero branch u=v=0 permits deletion of x with both ordered exterior pairs retained, as previously proved. Its one-sided expulsion requires the appropriate exterior release.

The equal-one branch has p→b and a→c. Put w=t(p,c). If w=1, the four shore vertices form the transitive order p,b,a,c. Two alternative zero orders on the same five-coordinate support are
(a,x,p,b,c), preserving the old right pair (b,c), and
(p,a,c,x,b), preserving the old left pair (p,a).
For the first, the three zero windows follow from p→a, p→b, and the transitive triple p,b,c. For the second, they follow from the transitive triple p,a,c, a→c, and b→c. These are exact one-sided rotations; their other exterior side remains an explicit two-window collar obligation.

If w=0, the old order is the only zero order of these five coordinates with initial ordered pair (p,a), and also the only zero order with final pair (b,c). Proof for the initial pair: a third vertex b or c gives color one, so the third coordinate must be x; choosing c next gives α(a,x,c)=1, hence the remaining order is forced. Proof for the final pair: a preceding p or a gives color one, so the preceding coordinate is x; the alternative remaining prefix (a,p) gives α(a,p,x)=1. Thus even releasing just one exterior collar cannot move x by a permutation of this five-coordinate support while retaining the other collar. The shore orientations form the directed four-cycle p→b→a→c→p, with chords p→a and b→c.

This classifies the unresolved equal-one branch rather than transporting it blindly. A transitive outer shore edge gives exact one-sided passage; the cyclic outer shore edge requires more support, both-side collar changes, or access to the second special vertex. Global port compatibility and any support-growth claim require their separate verification.
