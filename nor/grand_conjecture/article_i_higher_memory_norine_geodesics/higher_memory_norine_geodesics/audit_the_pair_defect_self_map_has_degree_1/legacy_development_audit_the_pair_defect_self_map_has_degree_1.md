Thus the remaining route in Subsection 28 cannot be to prove degree (0) for the canonical pair-defect self-map. The odd self-map produced by a minimum counterexample is topologically compatible with the identity carrier.

### Consequence for strategy

The useful content of the pair-defect construction is therefore its face-localization and root recurrence, not a continuous degree contradiction. To obtain a topological obstruction one needs additional local data of the Wu--Yang kind—such as a genuine rook-local label system giving a chain map into a target of lower chain dimension—or a combinatorial surgery that exploits the localized defect cores directly.

This also explains why the one-dimension gap persisted after incidence compression: after normalization the pair-defect construction naturally fills that dimension as an identity-carried self-map rather than contradicting it.
