# All-arity minimal counterexamples admit exactly-two-change full-support witnesses

Every minimal reversal-odd coordinate counterexample in arity r≥2 admits a full-support order with exactly two changes, by appending one coordinate to a good deletion order. All good deletion orders are bichromatic. The argument belongs to N_{r+1} and is independent of ternary flatness or root-carrier geometry; repair moves need not preserve this class.
