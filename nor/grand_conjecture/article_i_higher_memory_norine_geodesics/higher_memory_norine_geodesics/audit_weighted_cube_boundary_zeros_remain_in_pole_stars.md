# Audit: weighted cube-boundary zeros remain in pole stars

The weighted odd cube-boundary map localizes zeros to pole stars, not pole vertices. The normalized zero-free bottom-link map has absolute degree one, so its extension over the closed star necessarily has a zero; positive weight variation does not remove the obstruction. A NOR proof must extract a one-change order from such a zero or replace the carrier. This preserves the topology while withdrawing the claimed closure.
