# Spanning construction from paired deletion orders and common-tail restrictions

For a reversal-antisymmetric coordinate label of arity r, let M have length r-2. Suppose deletion orders (M,b,T) and (M^rev,x,T) have at most one change and the same initial color sigma. Then one of (x,M,b,T) and (b,M^rev,x,T) is spanning and one-change: their new first windows are reverses, so one has color sigma and agrees with its verified deletion suffix.

A common nonconstant one-change tail T forces the initial colors to agree. At r=3, two feasible prefixes (a,b) and (a,x) of such a tail therefore close the instance. In a counterexample the feasible predecessor graph on its three residual vertices has outdegree at most one. At general r the involution (x;M,b)<->(b;M^rev,x) permits at most one feasible certificate per pair.

The construction retains every vertex. Forcing the paired certificates remains unresolved: minimality does not synchronize tails. Section 39 gives an arbitrarily long family in which all residual deletions are supported by a common tail, yet their feasible prefixes form a directed cycle and no full prefix works. The grand conjecture remains open.
