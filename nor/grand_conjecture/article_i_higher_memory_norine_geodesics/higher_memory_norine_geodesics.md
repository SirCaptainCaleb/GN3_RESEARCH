# Ordered-tuple Norine geodesics

The project studies reversal-odd ordered coordinate windows in the translation-invariant sector of N_k. Here k is the number of cube vertices in a colored window and r=k−1 is coordinate arity; ternary coordinate NOR is N_4. The edge-order subclass encodes increasing versus decreasing paths and a spanning one-change order gives a two-path monotone cover. General center-dependent labels need not arise from a global edge order. Mirrored-pair factors are already soluble in monochromatic form by residue-class tournament paths.

The all-arity canonical target is a spanning converging tight fork. Its two branches share a reversed (r−1)-coordinate center and together retain every coordinate. The newly elevated endpoint argument shows that a minimal coordinate counterexample has minimum full-order variation exactly two, independently of arity, and every good deletion order is bichromatic. This sharply bounds the witness class without asserting invariance under subsequent repair moves.

Support accessibility and antimatroid structure do not synchronize tight witness orders. Even identical feasible support families can have disjoint maximal-support witnesses. Likewise deletion-tail truncation does not inherit counterexamplehood; the singleton-run exclusion is valid by an independent full-support construction. The recent two-window final-run theorem supplies exact rear-cap polarization under its common-tail hypotheses, but the spanning splice remains open. A near-spanning monochromatic path, if available, forces a rigid two-hole cage and a four-change cyclic word; its existence is not universal.

Full-support cyclic interval reversal is a reusable repair calculus: internal variation is preserved and only at most six distinct reconnection transitions matter. Its extremal inequalities are not yet contradictory. The topological alternative also retains a precise obstacle: weighted cube-boundary zeros lie in pole stars, and the bottom link has absolute degree one. A zero must be converted into a witness rather than identified with a pole vertex. The live frontier is an order-sensitive, full-support augmentation or carrier-to-path extraction theorem.
