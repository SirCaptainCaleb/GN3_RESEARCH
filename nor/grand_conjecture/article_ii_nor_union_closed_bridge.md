# Article II — Directed NOR and union-closed sets

Reversing that full-support packet preserves 10 and reverses its physical root; its exposed collar consists of r−1 crossing windows on either side. Article I's interval calculus therefore turns root geometry into an explicit packet move. The same root cannot persist after refinement making its endpoints adjacent.
