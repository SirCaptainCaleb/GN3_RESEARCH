## Minimum full-order variation is exactly two in every coordinate arity

Let h be a reversal-odd binary label on ordered r-tuples of distinct coordinates, r>=2. A good coordinate order is one whose consecutive r-window word has at most one change. Let V be an inclusion-minimal counterexample.

For any x in V, minimum-counterexamplehood gives a good order O of V minus {x}. Appending x adds exactly one r-window, so the resulting full order has at most two changes. Counterexamplehood excludes words with at most one change. Therefore the minimum variation over full coordinate orders is exactly two.

Furthermore every good single-coordinate deletion order is bichromatic. A monochromatic deletion order would extend at an endpoint with at most one change, independently of the new label. Thus the appended full witness has a single endpoint threshold defect.

The proof uses locality of ordered coordinate windows and minimum-counterexamplehood. It does not use r=3, alternation, tetrahedral flatness, or a chosen root carrier. Hence Article III root §282 elevates to every translation-invariant coordinate arity and belongs at the start of protected deletion descent.

The result supplies a two-change witness; it does not show that arbitrary repair moves retain the two-change class. Basepoint-dependent cube colorings have a separate locality issue and are outside this statement.
