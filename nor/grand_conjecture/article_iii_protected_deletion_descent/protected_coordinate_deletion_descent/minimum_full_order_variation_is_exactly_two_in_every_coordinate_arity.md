# Minimum full-order variation is exactly two in every coordinate arity

In every coordinate arity r≥2, a minimum reversal-odd NOR counterexample has minimum full-order variation exactly two. Append the omitted coordinate to a good deletion order: precisely one new window appears, so variation is at most two; counterexamplehood excludes zero and one. Monochromatic deletion orders are likewise excluded. This elevates the former ternary lemma to the initial general reduction, without asserting move-class invariance.
