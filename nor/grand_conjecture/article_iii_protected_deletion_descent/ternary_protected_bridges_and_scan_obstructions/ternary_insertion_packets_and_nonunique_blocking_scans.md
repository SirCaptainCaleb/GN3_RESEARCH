# Ternary insertion packets and nonunique blocking scans

In an alternating ternary carrier, insertion changes the packet to (s_{i-1},1-s_i,s_{i+1}). Blocking all insertions forces p,q>=2, but not the special scan 1^(p+1)0^q. The different scan 11,0^(p+q-1) blocks every insertion for arbitrary p,q>=2 and is globally flat tournament-realizable. Thus the scan premise of the claimed flat closure is unproved.
