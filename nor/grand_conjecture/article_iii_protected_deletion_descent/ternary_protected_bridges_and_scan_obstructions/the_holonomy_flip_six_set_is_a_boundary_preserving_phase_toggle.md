# The holonomy-flip six-set is a boundary-preserving phase toggle

Two full-support orders at the special holonomy flip, (x,a,b,d,c,e) and (x,a,d,b,c,e), have internal words 1111 and 0000 and share exactly the same ordered attachment pairs (x,a) and (c,e). Hence replacing one by the other changes only the four internal statuses and leaves every exterior-crossing ternary window unchanged.

This is a genuine boundary-preserving phase toggle relative to the new attachment pairs. It does not say those pairs coincide with the original carrier pairs; the old-boundary preservation claim is separately false.
