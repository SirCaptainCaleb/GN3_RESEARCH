# Arbitrary flat blocking scans admit a replacement on one side of the switch

For arbitrary insertion-blocking scans in the flat alternating ternary sector with p,q>=3, failure of two switch-adjacent insertions forces a nonincreasing five-bit core. If s_{p+1}=1, replacing v_p gives (s_{p-2},1,1) and shortens the zero phase by two or three. Otherwise replacing v_{p+3} gives (0,0,s_{p+4}) and shortens the one phase by two or three. This proves local good-replacement existence without scan uniqueness. Short phases and recurrent replacement cycles remain unresolved.
