# The replacement bridge identity and conditional flat descent

The ternary protected replacement packet is (s_{p-2},1 xor s_{p-1} xor s_p xor kappa,s_{p+1}). In the flat sector kappa=0. The special scan makes this 111 and gives the valid descent p->p-3, but insertion failure does not force that scan. The flat closure and curvature corridor remain conditional on a scan-selection or equivalent bridge theorem.
