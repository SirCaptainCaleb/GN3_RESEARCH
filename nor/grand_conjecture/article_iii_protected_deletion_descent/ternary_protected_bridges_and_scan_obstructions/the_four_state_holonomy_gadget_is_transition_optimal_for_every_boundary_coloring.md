# The four-state holonomy gadget is transition-optimal for every boundary coloring

The four holonomy-flip states with common attachment pairs (x,a),(c,e) have local words 0000, 1111, 001, and 100. If the immediate exterior status bits are l and r, their local-plus-boundary transition counts show that one state always attains the absolute minimum compatible with l,r: zero transitions when l=r and one transition when l!=r.

Hence the special holonomy six-set itself never forces excess variation. Any obstruction surviving all four states is entirely in the common exterior reconnection data. In particular, whenever the exterior between the two attachment sites requires at most one phase change, one of the four states realizes a global one-change continuation.
