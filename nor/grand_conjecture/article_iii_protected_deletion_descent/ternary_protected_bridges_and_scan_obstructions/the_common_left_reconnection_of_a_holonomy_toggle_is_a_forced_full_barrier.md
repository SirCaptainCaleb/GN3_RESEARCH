# The common left reconnection of a holonomy toggle is a forced full barrier

Assume the special perfect-blocker holonomy flip occurs at an interior index j>=3, so a=v_j has two old predecessors t=v_{j-2},u=v_{j-1}. Both phase-toggle chambers begin with the new pair (x,a). The old zero phase gives alpha(t,u,a)=0, while the special blocker scan gives alpha(x,t,u)=alpha(x,u,a)=1. Therefore the common left reconnection has statuses alpha(t,u,x)=1 and alpha(u,x,a)=0, preceded by an old 0-window: a fixed 010 oscillation.

Flat four-face parity forces alpha(t,x,a)=1. Thus neither endpoint repair of the central 10 transition is available; its tetrahedron {t,u,x,a} is fully curved. The transition emits the actual window-slide root e_t-e_a=e_{v_{j-2}}-e_{v_j}.

So for interior flips the exact handoff is: transition-optimal/toggleable six-set interior, followed on the left by a forced full barrier root. The endpoint cases j=1,2 are not covered by this statement and require their clipped left-boundary analysis separately.
