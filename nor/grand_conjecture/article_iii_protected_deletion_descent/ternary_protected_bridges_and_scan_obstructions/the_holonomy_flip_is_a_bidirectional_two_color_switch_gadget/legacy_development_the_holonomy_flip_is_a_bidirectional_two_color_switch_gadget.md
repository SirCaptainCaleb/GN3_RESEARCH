## The holonomy flip is a bidirectional two-color switch gadget

Continue with the special perfect-blocker holonomy-flip interface on the six physical coordinates {x,a,b,c,d,e}.

Subsection 46 proves two full-support color-0 Hamilton orders:

W_0^L=(x,a,d,b,c,e),

W_0^R=(a,d,b,c,e,x).

Thus the blocker coordinate x may be placed at either endpoint of a completely 0-monochromatic local order.

Because ternary reversal complements the window word, reversing these two orders gives two full-support color-1 Hamilton orders:

W_1^R=(e,c,b,d,a,x)=(W_0^L)^rev,

W_1^L=(x,e,c,b,d,a)=(W_0^R)^rev.

Hence for each color epsilon in {0,1} there is an epsilon-monochromatic six-coordinate order with x at the left endpoint and another with x at the right endpoint.

In addition, subsection 45 exhibits the distinct color-1 order
(x,a,b,d,c,e),
so the color-1 side has further internal flexibility, but the four orders above already give the symmetric statement.

### Switch-gadget theorem

A 1-to-0 residual-holonomy interface in the special perfect-blocker tube is internally universal for the two pieces of data relevant to protected reconnection:

1. desired monochromatic color epsilon;
2. desired endpoint side of the blocker x.

The internal six-coordinate packet itself therefore cannot be the obstruction. Any surviving obstruction is entirely in the two crossing windows at the chosen exterior boundary pair(s).

### Strategic consequence

When the interface lies inside a target-color phase, choose the monochromatic exit of that phase color and place x on the side facing outward from the protected threshold band. This confines all possible damage to the outward reconnection packet. If that packet is flat, threshold-band combing enlarges B. If it is fully curved, it emits a protected physical root.

Thus the special perfect-blocker holonomy-flip branch plugs directly into the current Article III dichotomy:
monochromatic interior -> outward boundary only -> B increase or root certificate.

What remains to verify for an actual closure lemma is the exact unchanged boundary pair on the inward side for the chosen embedding into the full carrier; the switch-gadget theorem itself is local and full-support.
