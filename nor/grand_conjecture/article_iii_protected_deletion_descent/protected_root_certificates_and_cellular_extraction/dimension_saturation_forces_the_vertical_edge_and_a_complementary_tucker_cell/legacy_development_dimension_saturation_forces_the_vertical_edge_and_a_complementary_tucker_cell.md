# Dimension saturation forces the vertical edge and a complementary Tucker cell — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Let a support-minimal honest lifted zero have connected physical support spanning all n coordinates and be genuinely multi-cycle. By the cycle-rank theorem, the support graph is connected bicyclic, hence has n+1 edges, while the lifted columns have rank n in W direct-sum R. Thus the minimal zero is a full linear circuit of n+1 labels, so any domain simplex carrying it is an n-simplex.

Block-rank neutrality forces every support edge to lie inside one tied block of the carrying permutahedral face. Connectedness and full support make that block all of V, so the horizontal factor is the top permutahedron P_V, dimension n-1.

The vertical factor cannot be a single cut vertex: P_V times {k} has dimension n-1 and cannot contain the required n-simplex. Hence the product carrier necessarily contains an actual vertical cut edge [k,k+1].

Apply the vertical-edge complementary-middle lemma to that same edge. The ternary window whose rank is crossed by the edge, together with its reversed orientation at the opposite endpoint, gives genuine violating states in the same product cell with labels (rho,s) and (-rho,-s), sharing the same physical middle coordinate b.

This uses state availability in the cell, not which violation was chosen by the single-valued degree selector. Therefore no selector tie rule is needed.

The arbitrary complementary-cell theorem now applies and yields a controlled Article III event: strict threshold-defect improvement, a controlled equality transport, or a phase-compatible switch-crossing repair.

Consequently every connected spanning multi-cycle honest lifted zero automatically hands off to complementary-middle Tucker repair. Together with the existing removal theorems for one-cycle, disconnected two-cycle, and proper-support multi-cycle zeros, the topology-to-local-repair interface is complete. The remaining closure problem is downstream: termination/extraction from the fully-curved protected barriers produced by the repair/threshold-band handoff.
