# Canonical switch roots lie in a strict protected cut cone

At the canonical switch insertion of an omitted coordinate x into a one-change deletion witness 0^p1^q, every internal 10 descent gives a physical root e_a-e_c with a strictly left of x and c strictly right of x. Hence all canonical roots for one witness lie in one strict protected cut cone; reversal negates the cone. Positive root cancellation must therefore combine genuinely different protected cuts.
