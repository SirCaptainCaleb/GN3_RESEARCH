## Development

Audit of the full-permutahedron odd descent-root map. The construction of R(pi) and the identity R(pi^rev)=-R(pi) are valid, as is nonvanishing of every permutation-vertex label. However the claimed forced zero on the whole centered permutahedron is tautological and does not by itself supply a nontrivial protected-root carrier. Central inversion on the full permutahedron has a fixed point: its center. Any continuous odd map on the whole ball satisfies F(0)=0. For the specific barycentric extension, the barycenter of the top-dimensional face is labeled by the average of R(pi) over all permutation vertices, and this average is exactly zero because vertices pair as pi,pi^rev with opposite labels. Expanding that center zero gives a positive root dependence that decomposes term-by-term into antipodal reversal pairs rho+(-rho)=0. Thus no cut-changing circuit, proper-face zero, or compatible replacement bridge follows. The boundary degree argument proves only that an odd extension of a zero-free boundary map must hit zero in the interior, which is already forced at the fixed center. Consequently subsection 14 should not be cited as constructing the missing free antipodal protected-root carrier. To obtain genuine extraction progress one must additionally force a zero on the free boundary or in a proper permutohedral face not explained by reversal pairing, or modify the domain/quotient so the antipodal action is free. The common-cut and cut-projection lemmas remain applicable once such a nontrivial positive carrier is obtained.
