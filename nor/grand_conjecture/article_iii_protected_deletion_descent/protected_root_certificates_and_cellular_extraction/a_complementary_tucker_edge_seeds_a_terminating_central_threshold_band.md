## A complementary Tucker edge seeds a terminating central threshold band

Work in the coboundary-flat alternating ternary sector with a switch target of color eta on the pre side and 1-eta on the post side.

Suppose a genuine horizontal adjacent-transposition edge carries complementary signed-middle labels +b and -b.

By the complementary-edge theorem, before the swap the local order is

...,a,b,c,d,...

with the b-centered window (a,b,c) at the last pre-switch rank k and violating the pre target:

alpha(a,b,c)=1-eta.

After swapping b,c,

...,a,c,b,d,...

the b-centered window (c,b,d) at rank k+1 is a post-switch violation, so

alpha(c,b,d)=eta.

Alternation gives

alpha(a,c,b)=eta.

Therefore after the horizontal swap the two central statuses are

eta, eta.

Now move the proposed cut vertically from k to k+1. Rank k+1 changes target from 1-eta to eta, so BOTH central ranks k,k+1 are matched.

### Central-band seed

Let B be the maximal target-compatible interval containing the new switch between ranks k+1 and k+2 (equivalently containing the just-repaired central pair in the switched state).

The complementary Tucker move guarantees that B contains at least the two central repaired ranks.

The horizontal adjacent swap can affect only the two additional outer window ranks. Thus every new mismatch created by the move lies immediately outside or farther outward from this matched central seed; no mismatch remains inside the seed.

### Outward combing

Apply the audited threshold-band theorem independently at the nearest unresolved boundary of B.

Whenever that boundary transition is flat in the outward repair direction:
- the repair preserves every rank already in B;
- the former boundary mismatch becomes matched;
- B strictly increases.

Hence flat repairs cannot cycle.

If all mismatches are absorbed, the resulting full order agrees everywhere with the one-change target and NOR closes.

If combing stops, every unresolved nearest boundary is fully curved and emits its actual protected physical root, with the entire matched central band and the opposite already-certified side preserved.

### Theorem

Every complementary signed-middle Tucker edge in the flat alternating ternary sector has a terminating local-to-global handoff:

1. perform the horizontal switch-crossing swap;
2. move the cut one level with the tracked middle coordinate;
3. outward-comb the resulting matched central band.

The outcome is either:
- a spanning one-change full order; or
- one or two fully-curved terminal barriers bounding a nonempty target-compatible central band.

No separate global potential for cut-changing equality is needed for THIS event.

### Consequence for the cellular Tucker program

Combine with:
- endpoint-avoiding path choice for complementary cells;
- the fixed-cut lexicographic potential (E,-Q) for same-side and neighbor-replacement equality transports.

Then every complementary Tucker cell exposes either:
- a strict defect improvement;
- a terminating fixed-cut equality transport;
- or a complementary switch-crossing edge which enters the threshold-band dichotomy above.

Thus the remaining gap in turning cellular Tucker into a full closure theorem is no longer termination of the complementary-cell path itself. It is extraction from the fully-curved barrier state produced at the end of the handoff.

This aligns the Tucker route with the existing protected-root / graded-cut frontier rather than requiring a separate cut-changing potential.
