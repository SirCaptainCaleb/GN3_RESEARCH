## Every lexicographically extremal width-two band closes NOR or exits through a protected root

Continue from the uniform strong-weave theorem §272.

Every lexicographically extremal global width-two full/full state, independent of its two six-set bits, admits a full-order modification with the following structure:
- the old right exterior is preserved exactly;
- a consecutive target-compatible seed has status word
  0,1,1,1,1;
- every newly uncontrolled window lies strictly to the left of that seed;
- more precisely the immediate exported kernel is
  ell_0,1,0,1,1,1,1,
  with only ell_0 free when the full two-window left collar exists.

The resulting order may have more than two changes. We do not attempt to return immediately to the two-change comparison class.

### Start a fresh threshold target at the seed

Fix the one-change target whose switch is the displayed
0 -> 1
inside the seed. Let I be the maximal contiguous interval of window ranks containing the entire seed and agreeing with that target.

The seed contains at least one matched zero and four matched ones, so I is nonempty on both target sides.

All mismatch created by the strong weave and singleton resolution lies outside I. The old right zero suffix may supply a mismatch on the right; the exported kernel may supply a mismatch on the left.

### Outward dynamics

At either endpoint of I:

- if the nearest mismatching transition tetrahedron is flat in the outward direction, apply the audited threshold-band endpoint repair;
- by §151 this preserves every window already in I and strictly enlarges I;
- if the endpoint transition is fully curved, it supplies an actual protected physical root with the whole current matched interval retained on its protected side.

Because the ambient word has finitely many windows, flat outward repair cannot continue indefinitely.

### Terminal outcomes

A maximal sequence has exactly two possible outcomes.

1. I reaches both global endpoints. Then the full status word agrees everywhere with one one-change threshold target. This is a spanning NOR-good order.

2. Before reaching both endpoints, one endpoint of I is fully curved. Then the process exits through a certified protected root.

No recurrence to the width-two packet is possible because |I| strictly increases at every flat repair.

### Theorem

Every lexicographically extremal width-two full/full band satisfies the finite dichotomy
width two -> spanning NOR order OR protected fully-curved root.

This conclusion applies uniformly to all four six-set types of §264. It uses the common strong weave of §272 rather than type-specific right reconnection analysis.

Consequently width two is closed as an independent Article III branch. Its only surviving output belongs to the general protected-root extraction/gluing problem.

### Scope

This theorem does not claim that the split-band output is an improvement in the original two-change potential. The progress measure after §272 is the maximal threshold-compatible interval I in the enlarged full-order state class. The handoff is therefore valid even when the one-sided singleton resolution shifts the target switch or creates an additional band outside I.
