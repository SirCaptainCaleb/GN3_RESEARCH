## Development

For a protected crossing root rho=e_a-e_c with a in a p-cut C and c outside C, the centered cut vector z_C has endpoint values z_C(a)=1-p/n and z_C(c)=-p/n. Since rho vanishes on every other coordinate, z_C odot rho=(1-p/n)e_a+(p/n)e_c. After projection to the root space this depends only on p and rho, not on the remaining membership data of C. Therefore the cut-moment perturbation Psi=rho+epsilon s M(C,rho) genuinely encodes the side sign s without increasing target dimension, and its side-cycle exclusion formulas remain valid, but it does not distinguish two different p-cuts carrying the same oriented root. The actual protected cut must remain explicit carrier/domain provenance. Any topological zero obtained only from the perturbed vectors can still mix incompatible cuts unless the domain construction separately enforces cut compatibility. Thus the faithful state is still root plus cut plus side; the perturbation faithfully vectorizes the side coordinate, not the full cut coordinate.
