# A minimum shortcut-free signature shore has size at least three

## Development

Continue from §341. Let x,z be a shortcut-free pair with minimum nonempty signature shore A, and choose distinct u,v in A. By §341, u,v has a protected shortcut, so h(w)=alpha(u,v,w) is nonconstant. Every vertex outside A has the same h-value. Therefore the opposite h-value occurs at some w in A\{u,v}. Hence A\{u,v} is nonempty and |A|>=3. In particular the |A|=2 branch is impossible. If |A|=3, the unique third vertex distinguishes every pair; cyclic invariance forces the induced switched tournament on A to be a directed triangle.
