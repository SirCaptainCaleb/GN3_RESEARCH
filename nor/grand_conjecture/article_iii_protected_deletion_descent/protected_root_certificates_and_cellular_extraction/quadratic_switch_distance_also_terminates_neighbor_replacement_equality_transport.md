# Quadratic switch distance also terminates neighbor-replacement equality transport

At a fixed switch cut, the quadratic distance Q_k of threshold defects strictly increases not only for ordinary same-side bubble equality but also for neighbor-replacement equality: the surviving defect moves exactly one rank farther from the switch. Hence (E,-Q_k) is a strict lexicographic potential for both interior non-strict repair classes. Cut-changing and global-endpoint events remain outside this fixed-cut potential.
