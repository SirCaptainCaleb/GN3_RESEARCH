## Audit correction: connected full-support lifted circuits reduce to fixed-middle vertical transport

This replaces the overstrong first version of this subsection.

Let a support-minimal positive honest lifted zero have connected physical support spanning every physical coordinate.

### What survives unchanged

Let the underlying permutahedral carrier face be the ordered partition
B_1 | ... | B_t.
Every support root is weakly forward for the block-rank functional. Since the positive physical dependence sums to zero, the total nonnegative block increment is zero. Hence every support root is block-neutral.

Connectedness then puts every support coordinate in one tied block; full support makes that block all of V. Thus the PERMUTAHEDRAL factor of the carrier is the top face.

Choose a support label carried by a violating ternary window (u,b,v) at some window rank i, with side sign s.

Alternation gives
alpha(v,b,u)=1-alpha(u,b,v).
If the reversed triple is placed on the opposite threshold side, its target is also complemented, so it is again violating and has opposite signed-middle label -s b.

Thus top-cell freedom guarantees a complementary b-state somewhere over the same physical top face.

### The missing point: vertical locality

It does NOT follow that the two signed-middle states lie in the same elementary product cell.

The switch coordinate is subdivided into adjacent cut levels. If the original violating window is distance d>1 from the active cut, moving that rank from one threshold side to the other requires d vertical cut moves. Root §206 audits precisely this point.

Therefore the earlier attempted immediate invocation of the arbitrary complementary-cell theorem (§81) is valid only in the cut-adjacent case.

### Correct reduction

A connected spanning lifted circuit has only two possibilities.

1. The chosen violating middle b is cut-adjacent.
   Then reversal can be realized across the active vertical edge inside one product cell. We obtain +b and -b in that cell, and §81 gives a controlled legal repair event.

2. The chosen violating middle b is not cut-adjacent.
   Then the obstruction canonically produces a FIXED-MIDDLE VERTICAL TRANSPORT PROBLEM: retain the physical middle b and its reversed triple while moving the cut through the intervening threshold ranks until the tracked rank becomes cut-adjacent.

At each elementary cut move before the tracked rank is crossed, the b-centered window itself remains unchanged and remains on the same threshold side; only the crossed rank changes target. When the cut finally crosses the b-window rank, its target flips. Hence the only way the long vertical transport can avoid an immediate strict threshold-defect improvement is through the intervening rank-by-rank defect profile. This is exactly the threshold-band phenomenon already isolated elsewhere in Article III, not a new figure-eight/theta obstruction.

### Consequence

The honest lifted topology still gives a major structural reduction:

- one-cycle zeros are removed by parity transversality (§192);
- disconnected full-support two-cycle zeros admit transverse cross-shore removal (§194);
- proper-support multi-cycle zeros admit transverse removal (§195);
- connected full-support multi-cycle zeros force the top permutahedral carrier and reduce to cut-adjacent complementary-middle extraction OR fixed-middle vertical threshold-band transport.

Thus figure-eight/theta classification is not the final conceptual bottleneck. The remaining genuine closure task is to prove termination of the fixed-middle vertical/equality transports while preserving the tracked middle and provenance.

No closure is claimed here. The previous same-elementary-cell assertion is withdrawn.
