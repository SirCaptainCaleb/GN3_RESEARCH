# The seven-coordinate weave loses two leading zeros: corrected weighted descent

The seven-coordinate all-one weave changes a clean width-two profile from (A,2) to (A-2,B'), B'>=5. It loses two leading zeros. Consequently 7A+4B need not increase, correcting §270. Both lexicographic (A+B,A) and scalar 4A+3B strictly improve on this move and on the other proved clean surgeries. The weave applies whenever the exact six-set bit s=bcf is one, independently of r=abf. A split-band export still requires a realized class-restoring surgery.
