## Audit correction: top-cell reversal alone does not give same-product-cell complementarity

The previous development overstated the consequence of the top permutahedron localization.

For a connected full-support lifted circuit, block-rank neutrality still correctly forces the PERMUTAHEDRAL carrier face to be the top face.

However a product carrier cell also has a VERTICAL switch-cut factor. If a violating window (u,b,v) lies several ranks away from the active cut, reversing it to (v,b,u) and placing that reversed window on the opposite threshold side generally requires moving the cut across several ranks. One adjacent vertical product cell contains only neighboring cut levels.

Therefore the opposite signed-middle state supplied by reversal need not lie in the same product cell as the original support label.

The claimed contradiction with sign-separated middle fibers is consequently not established.

What remains valid:
- connected full-support lifted circuits live over the top permutahedron face;
- reversing a violating triple produces an opposite signed-middle violation somewhere in the full switch prism;
- if the tracked b-window is already cut-adjacent, the opposite state lies across the same vertical edge and the complementary-cell extraction applies;
- more generally, any proof that transports the fixed b-centered violation to cut adjacency while preserving a nonincreasing global repair potential would complete this handoff.

Thus the live gap is exactly a FIXED-MIDDLE CUT-ADJACENCY transport theorem, not further physical root-circuit classification.
