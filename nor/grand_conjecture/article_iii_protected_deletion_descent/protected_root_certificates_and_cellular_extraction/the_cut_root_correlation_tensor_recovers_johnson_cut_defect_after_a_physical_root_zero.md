# The cut root correlation tensor recovers Johnson cut defect after a physical root zero

For a legitimate cut-root state define T=(1_C-1/2·1) rho^T. T is reversal-even and tr T=1. On a support-minimal simple physical root cycle rho_i=e_{x_{i+1}}-e_{x_i} with equal weights, H=average T satisfies k H e_{x_i}=1_{C_{i-1}}-1_{C_i}; hence the exact cut-defect vector is D_i=kH e_{x_i}+rho_i. Thus odd topology may force the physical root zero while the even tensor on its zero set records the entire cut-defect circulation without dimension-preserving perturbations.
