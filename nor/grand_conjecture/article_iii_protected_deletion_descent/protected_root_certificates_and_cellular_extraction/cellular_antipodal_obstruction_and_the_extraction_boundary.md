# Cellular antipodal obstruction and the extraction boundary

Cellular antipodality forces a complementary switch-prism cell by the crosspolytope degree obstruction. It does not force a complementary edge or a same-deleted-order fiber. The b-only bubble calculation needs its fixed-other-order premise; its same-side two-defect potential is unavailable from E=1 states. Protected root certificates still require a symmetric domain, gluing, and witness extraction.
