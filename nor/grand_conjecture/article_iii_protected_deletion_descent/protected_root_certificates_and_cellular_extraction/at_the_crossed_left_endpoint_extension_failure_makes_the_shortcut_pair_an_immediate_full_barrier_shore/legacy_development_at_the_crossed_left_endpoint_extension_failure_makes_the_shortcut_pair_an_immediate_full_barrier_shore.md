# At the crossed left endpoint, extension failure makes the shortcut pair an immediate full-barrier shore — preserved pre-item development

## Development

## At the crossed left endpoint, either a pair orientation extends or the shortcut pair is an immediate full-barrier shore

Work in the crossed normalization
\[
X_1=0,\qquad Z_1=1,\qquad R_1=0,\qquad R_2=1,
\]
with
\[
O=(w_1,\ldots,w_m),\qquad w(O)=0^p1^q,\qquad p\ge3.
\]
Hence the old window
\[
\alpha(w_2,w_3,w_4)=0
\]
whenever the displayed indices exist in the interior zero phase.

Consider the two pair orientations inserted after \(w_1\).

### Orientation \(x,z\)

The order
\[
\Pi=(w_1,x,z,w_2,w_3,\ldots)
\]
begins with
\[
0,\ 1,\ Z_2,\ 0,\ldots
\]

For the increasing threshold target \(0|1\), the third window is compatible exactly when
\[
Z_2=1.
\]

### Orientation \(z,x\)

The swapped order
\[
\Pi'=(w_1,z,x,w_2,w_3,\ldots)
\]
begins with
\[
1,\ 0,\ X_2,\ 0,\ldots
\]
because alternation gives the first two bits \(1,0\).

For the decreasing threshold target \(1|0\), the third window is compatible exactly when
\[
X_2=0.
\]

### Only one local assignment blocks both extensions

Therefore at least one pair orientation extends its threshold seed one additional window unless
\[
\boxed{X_2=1,\qquad Z_2=0.}
\]

Assume this exceptional assignment. Then in the \(x,z\) orientation the packet
\[
(x,z,w_2,w_3)
\]
has transition
\[
\alpha(x,z,w_2)=1,\qquad
\alpha(z,w_2,w_3)=Z_2=0.
\]

Its off-faces are
\[
\alpha(x,w_2,w_3)=X_2=1
\]
and
\[
\alpha(x,z,w_3)=R_3.
\]
The rung identity gives
\[
R_3\oplus R_2=X_2\oplus Z_2=1,
\]
so, since \(R_2=1\),
\[
R_3=0.
\]

Thus the off-face pair is exactly
\[
(0,1)
\]
in the normalized \(10\)-transition convention, and the tetrahedron is fully curved.

Hence it realizes the complete protected square
\[
\boxed{
\{x,z\}\longrightarrow\{w_2,w_3\}.
}
\]

### Dichotomy

At the first crossed endpoint:
\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{one of the two pair orientations extends its threshold seed}\\
\text{or}\\
\{x,z\}\text{ is itself the source shore of an immediate fully-curved }K_{2,2}\text{ barrier.}
\end{array}}
\]

So failure of both one-step threshold continuations is maximally rigid: it upgrades the desired shortcut pair from frozen provenance to the actual source shore of the protected barrier.
