## Closed width-two reflection orbits carry an exact lifted gallery circulation

Work in the coboundary-flat alternating ternary sector and use the exact width-two full/full reflection gadget of root §219.

A reflection state consists of a full coordinate order pi together with a target cut k. One reflection step is supported on six consecutive coordinates with local word
A,B,B,A,
both end transitions fully curved. The step swaps the two central coordinates and moves the target cut by two window ranks toward the former boundary. Its inverse is the reflected gadget
B,A,A,B
with the opposite cut move.

For a reflection step t, let x_t,y_t be the two central physical coordinates in their order immediately BEFORE the adjacent swap, and put
rho_t = e_{x_t}-e_{y_t}.
Also put
s_t=+1
if the target cut moves two ranks to the right and
s_t=-1
if it moves two ranks to the left.

### Closed permutation chronology

Suppose a finite sequence of reflection steps returns to the same full coordinate order.

Each adjacent swap moves x_t one position to the right and y_t one position to the left. Therefore, for each physical coordinate v, the coefficient of e_v in
sum_t rho_t
is exactly
(number of rightward unit moves of v) - (number of leftward unit moves of v),
which equals the net change in the position of v along the gallery.

Since the final coordinate order equals the initial order, every coordinate has zero net displacement. Hence
sum_t rho_t=0.

This is an exact positive physical-root dependence with unit coefficients, retaining the actual chronological order of the reflection moves.

### Closed cut chronology

If the reflection sequence also returns to the same target cut, then
k_final-k_initial = 2 sum_t s_t =0,
so
sum_t s_t=0.

Consequently the reflection-edge labels
(rho_t,s_t) in W direct-sum R
satisfy the exact lifted dependence
sum_t (rho_t,s_t)=0.

### Coxeter localization

The underlying coordinate-order path is a closed adjacent-transposition gallery in the permutahedron. As a gallery relation it is generated by commuting squares and A2 braid hexagons. Thus any CLOSED recurrent width-two reflection dynamics has no hidden long-range permutation topology: its gallery chronology reduces to the standard rank-two Coxeter relations.

### Scope and limitation

The roots rho_t here are the ADJACENT-SWAP roots of the reflection edges. They are not automatically the honest violating-window roots used by the switch-prism lifted-zero theorems, nor the terminal first-minus-last barrier roots. Therefore the existing A2/A3 extraction theorems cannot simply be invoked on the displayed dependence without a label-conversion lemma.

The useful reduction is exact:

- an infinite deterministic width-two reflection process in the finite state space either leaves the width-two reflection class / reaches an endpoint or improved state;
- or it eventually contains a closed reflection orbit;
- every such closed orbit carries a chronology-preserving lifted gallery circulation as above.

Hence a closure proof for recurrent reflections may focus on converting one commuting-square or braid-hexagon gallery circulation into a genuine Article III repair event, rather than controlling arbitrary long reflection recurrence.
