## Development

In the distance-two same-profile ternary recurrence [z,y;x] -> [y,x;z] -> [x,z;y], the three deletion carriers have the same word 0^p1^q and differ only at positions p+2,p+3. Hence their first p physical coordinates are identical. For any canonical switch-insertion 10-descent on any of the three carriers, the protected-root theorem gives rho=e_a-e_c with a among the first p coordinates and c strictly to the right of the cut. Let L be the common first-p set and put every other coordinate, including x,y,z, in R. The cut functional phi=+1 on L and -1 on R satisfies phi(rho)=2 for every such physical protected root from all three carriers. Therefore the formal exchange identity (e_x-e_z)+(e_z-e_y)+(e_y-e_x)=0 is not a physical protected-root cancellation: all actual canonical descent roots from the A2 recurrence lie in one strict open halfspace. No positive dependence can be supported only on those three recurrent carriers. Any genuine protected-root Radon zero meeting this A2 recurrence must also use a state with a genuinely different protected cut. Consequently an A2 exchange cell whose labels are canonical root certificates from its three recurrent states admits a convex local fill avoiding zero, because phi is positive on every label and on every convex combination. This is the same local mechanism as the cooriented residual antipodal exits, without identifying exchange roots with window-slide roots.
