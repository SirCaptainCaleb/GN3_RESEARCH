# Four-window neighbor replacement gives a sharp outward descent criterion

An interior neighbor replacement changes four windows, not two. If its tracked b-centered defect disappears, and the four old defect bits are A,B,C,1 with new exterior bit N, then E'-E=N-A+1-2(B+C). In the pre-switch outward direction, B+C>=1 implies strict lexicographic improvement of (E,-Q_k); equality raises Q by 8d+4 or 4d+2. If B=C=0, E may rise by two; equality instead lowers Q by four. Explicit coboundary-flat alternating pair-bit labels realize both failures, even with nearest initial violation selection. Thus roots §§81,92 do not justify unrestricted neighbor descent or termination of arbitrary complementary-cell paths. A global extraction must enforce the intervening-defect condition or resolve its satisfied-window alternative.
