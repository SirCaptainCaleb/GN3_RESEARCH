# Odd order cube carriers force a radial terminal root packet

For a terminal central cut C, u_C=1_C-1/2·1 has norm squared n/4 and <u_C,rho>=1, so tau=rho-(4/n)u_C is a nonzero antipodally odd tangent label. If a faithful continuous odd terminal-root carrier exists on the centered-cube boundary, then for odd n the sphere S^{n-1} is even-dimensional and hairy ball forces a radial packet G(x)=lambda x. This is the parity complement of the even-n permutahedral theorem. The unproved step is construction of the faithful carrier from realized terminal states, not the fixed-point implication.
