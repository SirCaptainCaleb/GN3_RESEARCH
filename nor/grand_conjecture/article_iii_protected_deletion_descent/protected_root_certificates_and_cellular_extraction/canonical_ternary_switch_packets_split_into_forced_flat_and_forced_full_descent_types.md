# Canonical ternary switch packets split into forced flat and forced full descent types

For a canonical ternary switch insertion, the unique bad packet is one of 010,100,101,110. In the coboundary-flat sector, AB-descent packets 100 and 101 are forced fully-curved barriers at the normalized cut, while BC-descent packets 010 and 110 are forced flat switches entering endpoint-repair/threshold-band transport. Hence canonical switch states intrinsically split into transverse-full and mobile-flat types; only the mobile branch needs transport to recover terminal provenance.
