## Local packets of size at most four are hemisphere-safe for the consecutive-change carrier

Let
\[
\pi=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)
\]
be a bad ternary order, with change positions
\[
p_1<\cdots<p_k,\qquad k\ge 2.
\]
Recall
\[
C(\pi)=\sum_{j=1}^{k-1}\bigl(e_{v_{p_j}}-e_{v_{p_{j+1}+3}}\bigr).
\]
Every summand has endpoint-position gap
\[
(p_{j+1}+3)-p_j\ge 4.
\]

This gap controls a larger class of local permutation cells than the Coxeter rank-two relations alone.

### Packet-collapse functional

Partition the ambient positions into consecutive packets, each of size at most four. Give all coordinates in one packet the same real weight, and choose packet weights strictly increasing from left to right. Let \(w\) be the resulting coordinate functional.

Consider any order \(\sigma\) obtained by arbitrarily permuting coordinates inside the packets.

For every summand
\[
e_x-e_y
\]
of \(C(\sigma)\), the coordinate \(x\) occurs at least four positions before \(y\). Two positions inside one packet of size at most four differ by at most three. Hence \(x\) and \(y\) lie in distinct packets. Their packet order is fixed across all packet permutations, so
\[
w(x)<w(y)
\]
and therefore
\[
\langle w,e_x-e_y\rangle<0.
\]
Summing,
\[
\boxed{\langle w,C(\sigma)\rangle<0}
\]
for every bad packet-permutation state \(\sigma\).

Thus the convex hull of all corresponding defect labels lies in one strict open halfspace.

### Coxeter cells through A3

This immediately gives:

1. **Adjacent-swap edges.** Collapse the swapped pair. Both endpoint labels lie in one open halfspace.
2. **Commuting squares.** Collapse each independently swapped pair. All four labels lie in one open halfspace.
3. **A2 braid hexagons.** Collapse the three braid positions. All six labels lie in one open halfspace.
4. **A3 local permutahedra.** Collapse any four consecutive positions. Every bad order obtained by permuting those four coordinates has its label in one common open halfspace.

If any state in one of these cells is NOR-good, closure has already occurred. Otherwise every affine filling of the cell by the displayed vertex labels is zero-free.

### First possible local support of a carrier zero

A local permutation packet can contain both endpoints of a consecutive-change macro root only when it contains two positions whose difference is at least four. Therefore a packet of size five is the first possible packet on which the common packet-collapse separation can fail.

Hence:

\[
\boxed{\text{Every local carrier zero for }C\text{ requires support on at least five consecutive positions.}}
\]

This is a carrier statement: arbitrary physical transition-root circuits may already occur on four coordinates, while the symmetric consecutive-change defect has a larger built-in separation radius.

### Consequence for the block-merge gallery

Root §199 expands every canonical Sperner block merge into adjacent-swap galleries whose ambiguity is generated by commuting squares and A2 braid moves. All of those coherence cells are zero-free for \(C\). The same conclusion holds for any A3 refinement introduced while reorganizing four neighboring coordinates.

Thus gallery choice is topologically harmless through the entire rank-three Coxeter layer. A forced zero in the witnessed-degree program must occur where at least five consecutive physical positions interact, at the top merge, or across genuinely different witness sheets whose common support cannot be compressed into four positions.

This supplies the label-side homotopy requested after §199 and aligns the next local frontier with the existing five-coordinate curvature and double-full/transport machinery.
