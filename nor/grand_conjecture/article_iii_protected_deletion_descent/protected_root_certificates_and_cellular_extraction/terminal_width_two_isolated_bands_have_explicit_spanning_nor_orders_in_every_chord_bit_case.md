# Terminal width-two isolated bands have explicit spanning NOR orders in every chord-bit case

Every flat alternating global word 0^A1^2 0 has an explicit spanning NOR order. Complete chord-bit classification gives a six-coordinate weave or the seven-coordinate all-one weave (p,a,d,c,f,b,e), each with a verified frozen prefix. Reversal covers 0 1^2 0^A. Remaining flat alternating good deletion witnesses have both phases at least three.
