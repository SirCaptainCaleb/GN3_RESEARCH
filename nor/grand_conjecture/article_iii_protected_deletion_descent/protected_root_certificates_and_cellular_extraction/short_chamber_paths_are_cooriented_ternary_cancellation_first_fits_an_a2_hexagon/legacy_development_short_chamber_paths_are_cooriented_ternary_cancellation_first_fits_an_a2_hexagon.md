# Short chamber paths are cooriented; ternary cancellation first fits an A2 hexagon — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Short chamber paths are strictly cooriented. Let sigma_0,...,sigma_m be a path in the permutohedral chamber graph, each step an adjacent transposition, and let r be the ordered-window arity. For each t let p_t(v) be the rank of coordinate v in sigma_t, and define the averaged-rank functional Phi(e_v)=-(1/(m+1)) sum_t p_t(v). Consider any actual window-slide root rho=e_a-e_c from chamber sigma_s, so p_s(c)-p_s(a)=r. Along a path of d adjacent swaps starting at sigma_s, the rank difference delta=p(c)-p(a) can decrease by at most one per step until a and c become adjacent; the crossing swap then changes +1 to -1, and subsequent steps decrease it by at most one. Hence at path distance d the universal lower bound is f_r(d)=r-d for d<r and f_r(d)=-(d-r+1) for d>=r. Therefore Phi(rho) is bounded below by the average of f_r(|t-s|). In particular, for a one-sided path segment of length m<=2r-2 the endpoint case is worst and the sum lower bound is sum_{d=0}^{r-1}(r-d)-sum_{j=1}^{m-r+1}j, which is at least r when m=2r-2 and larger for shorter m. Interior source chambers have two shorter distance tails and a larger bound. Thus every actual window-slide root carried by any chamber of a path of at most 2r-2 walls is strictly positive under the same averaged-rank functional. No positive root dependence can be supported on such a path. For ternary arity r=3, every chamber path with at most four walls, hence at most five chambers, is strictly cooriented. The first path length at which this certificate can cease to be strict is five walls/six chambers. This matches the chamber count of the smallest two-dimensional A2 Coxeter face (a six-chamber permutohedral hexagon), identifying that hexagon rather than the three-state omitted-coordinate A2 recurrence as the first plausible local physical-root cancellation cell.
