## Development

Audit of the dimension-saturation handoff. The switch-prism construction in root 88 allows a subdivision-vertex label to be a convex average of several incident genuine state labels. A zero of the affine PL map on a domain simplex therefore expands to a positive dependence among genuine state labels that need not correspond one-for-one to the vertices of that domain simplex.

Consequently, even if a support-minimal expanded genuine-label dependence is a connected spanning bicyclic circuit with n+1 labels and rank n in W direct-sum R, one cannot infer that the domain simplex carrying the zero has n+1 vertices or dimension n. Several of those genuine labels may occur inside the convex average attached to a single subdivision vertex.

Thus the argument in the preceding dimension-saturation subsection that rules out a vertical cut vertex by domain dimension is not valid for the averaged carrier as currently defined.

What remains valid is purely algebraic: a connected spanning genuinely multi-cycle support-minimal dependence has n+1 genuine labels and full lifted rank n, and its permutahedral provenance is top-face. What is missing is a carrier-realization theorem saying that these n+1 circuit labels occur as distinct vertices of one carrier simplex, or at least that their supporting product cell has a nontrivial vertical edge.

There are two clean repair routes. First, refine the carrier so every relevant subdivision vertex carries one genuine state label while preserving continuity/odd boundary degree. Second, work directly with the convex support of each averaged vertex and prove that a full-rank positive circuit extracted from those supports cannot be contained in top permutahedron times one vertical cut vertex.

Until one of these is proved, the selector-free vertical-edge conclusion is not established. The canonical vertical-edge complementary-middle lemma itself remains valid.
