# Alternation supplies the singleton endpoint-swap law; general reversal-odd extraction remains separate

## Composition

The endpoint-pair swaps of §246 use alternating transposition laws. Seven explicit general reversal-odd triple assignments give an original 010 packet and both swapped packets also equal to 010. The local resolution therefore has an alternating-sector hypothesis. The outermost-change boundary-degree construction remains valid for general reversal-odd labels and requires its separate witness-exchange extraction.

## Development

## Alternation is the hypothesis for the singleton endpoint-swap law

The endpoint-swap calculation in §246 uses
alpha(b,a,c)=1-alpha(a,b,c)
and
alpha(c,e,d)=1-alpha(c,d,e).
These are transposition identities supplied by alternation.

For a general ternary label h satisfying only h(w,v,u)=1-h(u,v,w), distinct permutations of a triangle occur in three independent reversal pairs.

An explicit permissible assignment on five distinct coordinates is
h(a,b,c)=0, h(b,c,d)=1, h(c,d,e)=0,
h(b,a,c)=0, h(a,c,d)=1,
h(b,c,e)=1, h(c,e,d)=0.
Assign the reversed triple of each displayed value its complement, and extend the remaining reversal pairs freely.

The original packet (a,b,c,d,e) has word 010.
The first-pair swap (b,a,c,d,e) also has word 010.
The last-pair swap (a,b,c,e,d) also has word 010.

Thus §246's two local one-change resolutions apply to alternating ternary orientations. Its general reversal-odd version requires a separate surgery theorem.

The current branch closures in §§250,253,257,259 state their flat alternating hypotheses explicitly. The outermost-change degree construction in §243 remains valid for general reversal-odd labels. General-sector extraction from that carrier is a separate live obligation.
