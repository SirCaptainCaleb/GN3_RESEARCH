# Every outermost root reduces to two six-coordinate collars joined by one good bridge

Using minimum-counterexample induction on the deep core between the frozen four-coordinate extreme collars, every outermost root has a representative consisting of a left six-coordinate collar, one NOR-good proper-subinstance bridge, and a right six-coordinate collar. The exact physical root, outside order, and extreme transition certificates are preserved. Thus all unbounded-length behavior is already one-change; remaining realization difficulty is confined to the two bounded bridge attachments.
