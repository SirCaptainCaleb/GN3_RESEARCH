# Lexicographic extremality forces full boundaries on every wide two-change band

For a lexicographically maximal two-change word 0^A1^B0^C in the flat alternating sector, flat endpoint swaps give strict improvement: if B>=3 a flat left boundary increases A, and if C>=2 a flat right boundary preserves A and increases B. Together with the singleton descent, every nonterminal wide extremal band is forced into the full-full regime. The residual geometries are width two, terminal short tails, and full-full boundary crossing.
