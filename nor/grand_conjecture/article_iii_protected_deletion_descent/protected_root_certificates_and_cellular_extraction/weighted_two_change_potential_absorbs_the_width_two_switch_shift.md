## Audit correction: the scalar 7A+4B undercounts the seven-coordinate weave

The original development proposed Psi=7A+4B for a two-change word 0^A1^B0^C. Subsection §273 audits the seven-coordinate all-one weave and shows that this scalar is not monotone for every clean width-two surgery: the exact trade can be
(A,2) -> (A-2,B') with B'>=5,
whose minimum 7A+4B change is -2.

Accordingly the claimed universal scalar descent is withdrawn.

The valid replacement is the lexicographic potential
(A+B,A)
proved in §268. It handles:
- flat-left wide-band moves, preserving A+B while increasing A;
- flat-right moves, increasing A+B;
- singleton descents;
- the clean width-two switch shift;
- the seven-coordinate trade, which increases A+B by at least one.

Subsection §273 also notes that 4A+3B is a valid scalar for the currently listed clean moves, but the lexicographic potential is the established comparison theorem.

The substantive conclusion retained from the original note is only that a clean width-two return to the two-change class is a strict improvement under the corrected potential. Split-band exported kernels still require an actual surgery or threshold-band/protected-root handoff.
