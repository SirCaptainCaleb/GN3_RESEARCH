# One-sided flat transport is a quotient path with disjoint block defects

An audited one-sided flat transport has strictly nested central cuts. Their difference blocks turn its physical roots into the edges of a directed path after quotienting within-block differences. Distance-three steps contribute defects in pairwise disjoint blocks. The quotient path roots are linearly independent, so a single transport flag supports no positive root dependence. A forced topological zero must involve interactions between distinct realized trajectories.
