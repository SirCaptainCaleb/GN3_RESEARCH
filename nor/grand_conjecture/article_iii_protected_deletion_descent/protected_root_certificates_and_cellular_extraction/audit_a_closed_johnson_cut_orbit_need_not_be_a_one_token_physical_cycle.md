# Audit: a closed Johnson-cut orbit need not be a one-token physical cycle

A simple closed Johnson shore orbit need not be a simple physical root cycle or a one-token orbit. The square K+ab -> K+cb -> K+cd -> K+ad -> K+ab is successor-compatible but its roots split into two physical two-cycles, each acquiring positive cut defect when extracted. Root §66's finite-orbit conclusion remains valid; its one-token consequence requires the additional simple-physical-cycle hypothesis of §54. This diagnoses a cut-geometry implication, not NOR.
