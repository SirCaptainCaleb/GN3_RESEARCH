## Deep-core flat combing preserves the outermost root and terminates at full barriers or endpoint collars

Let \(\pi\) be a bad ternary order with first and last change positions
\[
p<q
\]
and outermost root
\[
D(\pi)=e_{v_p}-e_{v_{q+3}}.
\]

Fix a one-change threshold target and a matched band as in the threshold-band potential theorem §151. Consider only outward flat-boundary repairs for which the actual adjacent transposition used by the repair is at positions
\[
r,r+1
\]
with
\[
p+4\le r\le q-2.
\]

### Root preservation

By §252, every such transposition leaves
\[
c_p,c_{p+1},c_q,c_{q+1}
\]
unchanged, creates no change before \(p\) or after \(q\), and leaves the physical coordinates at positions \(p\) and \(q+3\) fixed. Hence
\[
D(\pi s_r)=D(\pi).
\]

Thus every permitted deep-core flat repair preserves the exact physical root used by the root-valued degree carrier.

### Strict termination

By §151, every outward flat-boundary repair strictly increases the matched-band length
\[
B(\pi,k).
\]
Therefore a sequence of permitted deep-core repairs cannot cycle.

A maximal root-preserving deep-core combing sequence terminates in one of three ways:

1. the threshold target is matched globally, giving a spanning NOR-good order;
2. an unresolved boundary of the matched band is supported by a fully-curved tetrahedron;
3. the next flat repair would require a transposition outside
   \[
   p+4\le r\le q-2,
   \]
   so the unresolved boundary has entered one of the bounded extreme collars.

In a counterexample, outcome 1 is impossible.

### Normal form for a carrier edge

Hence every outermost-root witness may be normalized, without changing its root, until every unresolved defect away from the two extreme collars is a fully-curved barrier.

Combining this with the nine-change compression of §251 gives a finite-barrier representative for every edge of the flag-compatible cycle:

- the same physical directed root;
- both extreme-change collars retained;
- at most nine total changes initially;
- no interior flat boundary remaining after combing;
- every surviving interior obstruction fully curved.

### Consequence for the cycle splice

The algebraic root cycle of §§244–249 can therefore be held fixed while its individual witness orders are simplified by actual full-order surgeries. Long flat corridors are not part of the remaining compatibility problem.

The remaining realization obstruction for each cycle edge is confined to:

- bounded endpoint collars, where the three-coordinate collar law §238 and exported-reconnection results apply; and
- finitely many fully-curved barriers, each carrying the realized \(K_{2,2}\) exchange square of §230.

Thus the root-valued degree route reduces to gluing endpoint collars through a monotone sequence of canonical full-barrier cells, with the physical root cycle unchanged throughout the normalization.
