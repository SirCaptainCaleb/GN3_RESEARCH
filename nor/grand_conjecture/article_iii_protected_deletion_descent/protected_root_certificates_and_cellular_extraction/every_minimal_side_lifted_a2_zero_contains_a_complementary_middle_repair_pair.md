# Every minimal side-lifted A2 zero contains a complementary middle repair pair

## Composition

A support-minimal honest side-lifted zero confined to a ternary A2 block is necessarily a two-term pair (rho,+1),(-rho,-1). Three-term root triangles cannot balance the side coordinate, and four-term supports reduce to reversal pairs whose same-side simultaneous violation is impossible. The pair has one physical middle coordinate on opposite sides of the switch, so the cellular Tucker repair machinery extracts a controlled local repair. Thus an unextracted lifted zero must lie in an A3 or larger Coxeter block.

## Development

## Every minimal side-lifted A2 zero contains a complementary middle repair pair

Work in ternary arity with the honest switch-prism lifted labels

Lambda=(rho,s) in W direct-sum R,

where rho is the first-minus-last root of a selected violating ternary window and s is its pre/post-switch side sign.

Suppose a positive lifted zero is carried in one exact A2 Coxeter block B={a,b,c}. The block occupies three fixed consecutive coordinate ranks in every refining chamber. Any ternary window whose root is internal to B is therefore exactly a permutation of B.

Choose a support-minimal positive lifted dependence.

### Support size two

Two positive vectors cancel only when they are exact negatives:

(rho,s), (-rho,-s).

For an exact A2 block, opposite roots rho=e_a-e_c and -rho=e_c-e_a come from reversed orders of the same physical triple {a,b,c}, so both have the same middle coordinate b.

Thus a two-term lifted zero is exactly a complementary signed-middle pair +b,-b in one product cell. The cellular Tucker repair theorem applies.

### Support size three is impossible

The only nontrivial positive physical dependence on three non-collinear A2 roots is a directed triangle

rho_1+rho_2+rho_3=0,

with equal positive coefficients.

The lifted scalar equation would require

s_1+s_2+s_3=0,

impossible for three signs in {+1,-1}.

A collinear three-root support reduces to an opposite pair and is not support-minimal.

Hence no support-minimal lifted zero has size three.

### Support size four

Any positive circulation on three physical vertices decomposes into directed 2-cycles and directed 3-cycles. A four-edge circulation cannot contain a 3-cycle plus another nonzero cycle, since the latter needs at least two edges. Therefore a four-edge support consists of two physical reversal pairs.

If either reversal pair has opposite side signs, that pair already gives a two-term lifted zero, contradicting support minimality. Hence each physical reversal pair must have the SAME side sign, with the two pairs carrying opposite common signs so that the total scalar coordinate can cancel.

But such a same-side reversal pair cannot occur among violations in one A2 product cell.

Indeed the two opposite physical roots arise from reversed orders of the same ternary window occupying one fixed window rank i. Reversal-oddness makes their actual colors opposite. If both states put that window on the pre-switch side, the prescribed threshold color at rank i is the same in both states, so exactly one reversed window can violate it. The same argument holds if both are post-switch. Thus two reversed A2 windows cannot both be violating states with the same side sign.

Therefore the four-term possibility is impossible.

### Theorem

Every support-minimal positive lifted zero confined to one ternary A2 Coxeter block is a two-term antipodal lifted pair

(rho,+1), (-rho,-1)

up to global sign.

Its two violating windows have the same physical middle coordinate and lie on opposite sides of the switch. Hence the containing product cell has complementary signed-middle labels and supplies a controlled local repair event; in the direct adjacent case it is exactly the switch-crossing endpoint repair.

### Consequence

The honest side-lifted switch-prism degree construction has a complete extraction theorem on its first possible ternary carrier. Any terminal lifted obstruction must either:
- live in a Coxeter block of size at least four; or
- fail to be terminal because the A2 zero yields a threshold repair/improvement.

This connects the fixed-point topology directly to the existing A3/protected-root frontier.
