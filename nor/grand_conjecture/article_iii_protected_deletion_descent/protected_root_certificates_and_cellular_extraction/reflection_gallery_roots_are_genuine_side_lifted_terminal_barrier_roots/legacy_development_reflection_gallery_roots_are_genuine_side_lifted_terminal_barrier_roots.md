# Reflection gallery roots are genuine side-lifted terminal barrier roots — preserved pre-item development

## Label conversion for width-two reflection dynamics

Use the rigid width-two full-full corridor normalized as
(0,1,2,3,4,5)
with local word 0,1,1,0.
The exact reflection step swaps the two central coordinates 2,3 and moves the target cut two ranks to the RIGHT. Its gallery label in the notation of the closed-reflection theorem is
(e_2-e_3,+1).

The gallery root is in fact a genuine Article III barrier root.

### Companion rotation

Rotate the right barrier coordinates by moving 3 across 4 and 5:
(0,1,2,3,4,5) -> (0,1,2,4,5,3).

For the rigid residue this companion state again has local word
0,1,1,0
with the same target cut and a fully-curved RIGHT boundary.

That right boundary is carried by the consecutive windows
(2,4,5) -> (4,5,3).
Its physical slide root is
e_2-e_3,
because the slide drops the first coordinate 2 and enters the last coordinate 3.

Since it is the right boundary of the protected band, its side bit is +1. Hence its side-lifted protected root is exactly
(e_2-e_3,+1),
the reflection-gallery label.

### Left-moving reflection

Apply global complement-reversal. A left-moving reflection with central swap root e_x-e_y has a mirrored companion state whose fully-curved LEFT boundary carries physical root e_x-e_y and side bit -1.

Therefore every reflection step t has a same-six-set companion terminal barrier whose honest protected label is exactly
(rho_t,s_t),
where rho_t is the adjacent-swap/gallery root and s_t records the direction of cut translation.

### Consequence for closed reflection orbits

The closed-reflection theorem proves that any reflection orbit returning to the same coordinate order and cut satisfies
sum_t (rho_t,s_t)=0
with unit positive coefficients.

By the present conversion, each summand may be replaced by a genuine fully-curved terminal-barrier realization with the SAME lifted label. Thus every closed recurrent width-two reflection orbit carries an exact side-balanced positive dependence of actual protected barrier roots, preserving the chronological gallery order.

This closes the root-species gap in the reflection-orbit theorem. What remains is extraction from that protected lifted circulation. Because the underlying gallery relation is generated by commuting squares and A2 braid hexagons, that extraction can now be sought on genuine protected barrier labels in those rank-two cells rather than on abstract adjacent-swap roots.
