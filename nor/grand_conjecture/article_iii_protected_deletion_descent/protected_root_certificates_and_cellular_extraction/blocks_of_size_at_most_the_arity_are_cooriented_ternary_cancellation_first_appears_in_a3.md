# Blocks of size at most the arity are cooriented; ternary cancellation first appears in A3

If one Coxeter block B of size b is freely permuted while exterior coordinates remain fixed, and b<=r for ordered-window arity r, averaging ranks over the whole face gives one functional strictly positive on every actual length-r window-slide root from every chamber of that face. Hence no positive physical-root dependence can occur in a block of size at most r.

For b=r+1, opposite slide roots already become possible. Therefore in ternary arity r=3 every A2 three-coordinate block is still strictly cooriented; the first possible physical-root cancellation block is A3 on four tied coordinates. This supersedes the earlier suggestion that an A2 hexagon is the first possible cancellation cell.
