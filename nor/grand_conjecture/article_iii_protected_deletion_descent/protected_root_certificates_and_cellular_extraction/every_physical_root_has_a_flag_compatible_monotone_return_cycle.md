## Development

Let D be the zero-free nonzero-degree root-valued boundary carrier from section 243. Fix any nonzero type-A root rho=e_s-e_t, including a protected root emitted by a terminal barrier.

Cone the boundary triangulation to an interior vertex labeled rho and extend affinely. A zero-free extension would radially extend the nonzero-degree boundary map across the ball, impossible. Hence some cone simplex gives a positive dependence containing rho and boundary labels D(pi_F) from one nested proper-face flag. Boundary zero-freeness forces the apex coefficient to be positive.

A support-minimal positive dependence of type-A roots is one directed simple cycle. Thus rho=s->t has a return path t=x_0->x_1->...->x_m=s whose edges are canonical proper-face outermost roots from one flag.

Refine the flag to a chamber order tau. Every boundary root points strictly forward in tau by the proper-face crossing theorem. Therefore t=x_0 <_tau x_1 <_tau ... <_tau x_m=s, and rho is the unique backward edge.

Consequently every protected-root handoff from the local width-two or threshold-band machinery feeds directly into the same monotone flag-cycle architecture. The remaining uniform task is to resolve one backward protected edge plus its canonical monotone return chain by a spanning NOR order or strict admissible descent.
