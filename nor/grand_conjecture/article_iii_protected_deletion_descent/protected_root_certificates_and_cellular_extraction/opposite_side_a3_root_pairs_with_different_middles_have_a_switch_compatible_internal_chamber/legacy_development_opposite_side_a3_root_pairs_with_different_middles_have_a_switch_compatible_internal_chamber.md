## Opposite-side A3 root pairs with different middles have a switch-compatible internal chamber

Work in the coboundary-flat alternating ternary sector on one exact A3 Coxeter block {a,b,c,d}. Suppose two violating ternary windows carry opposite physical roots and opposite switch-side signs, but have different middle coordinates. Up to relabeling they are

(a,b,c) on the pre-switch side,
(c,d,a) on the post-switch side,

with roots e_a-e_c and e_c-e_a.

Let the target color on the pre-switch side be eta. Since the first window violates there,

alpha(a,b,c)=1-eta.

The post-switch target is 1-eta, so the second violating window has

alpha(c,d,a)=eta.

By cyclic invariance of an alternating ternary orientation,

alpha(c,d,a)=alpha(a,c,d)=eta.

Write

X=alpha(a,b,d).

Coboundary flatness on the tetrahedron {a,b,c,d} gives

alpha(a,b,c) xor alpha(a,b,d) xor alpha(a,c,d) xor alpha(b,c,d)=0.

Substituting the known values yields

alpha(b,c,d)=1-X.

There are two cases.

### Case 1: X=eta

The order

(d,a,b,c)

has consecutive internal window colors

alpha(d,a,b)=alpha(a,b,d)=eta,
alpha(a,b,c)=1-eta.

Thus its two internal windows exactly realize the one-change switch target.

### Case 2: X=1-eta

The order

(a,c,b,d)

has consecutive internal window colors

alpha(a,c,b)=1-alpha(a,b,c)=eta,
alpha(c,b,d)=1-alpha(b,c,d)=1-eta.

Again the two internal windows exactly realize the one-change switch target.

### Theorem

Every opposite-side A3 physical reversal pair with different middle coordinates contains a chamber of the same four-coordinate block whose two internal ternary windows are exactly

eta, 1-eta.

Hence the lifted A3 two-cycle is not a purely algebraic cancellation: it determines a switch-compatible internal ordering of the whole block.

### Scope

This is an INTERNAL A3 resolution only. To turn it into a full-support NOR order one must still verify the two boundary-crossing windows between this reordered block and the fixed exterior coordinates. The existing boundary-safe splice theorems may apply when their ordered-pair hypotheses are met, but that compatibility is not automatic from the present calculation.
