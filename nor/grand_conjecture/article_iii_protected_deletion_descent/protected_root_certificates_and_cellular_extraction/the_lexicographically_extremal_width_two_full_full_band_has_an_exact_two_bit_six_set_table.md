# The lexicographically extremal width-two full-full band has an exact two-bit six-set table

A lexicographically extremal width-two full/full band has a complete six-set table with only two free bits r=alpha(a,b,f) and s=alpha(b,c,f). All other increasing triples follow from the forced chord bits of §257, full curvature, and tetrahedral parity. Hence there are exactly four local six-set types in the lexicographic comparison class, without importing the stronger maximal-threshold-band rotations of §221.
