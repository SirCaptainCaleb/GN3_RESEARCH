## Elevation from the currently composed Article III

The current Article III can be reorganized around one abstract state space rather than a sequential pipeline.

A protected deletion state has two qualitatively different kinds of motion.

1. **Fiber motion.** Holding the global deletion witness/protected threshold data fixed, ternary flat-boundary repairs enlarge the threshold-compatible band. The band length is a well-founded local potential. A maximal fiber state has no admissible flat boundary repair; every unresolved band boundary is fully curved.

2. **Base motion.** A canonical protected switch root crossing a legitimate physical cut is an exchange vector
[

Thus it prescribes a unique adjacent cut state. The root is not merely a certificate of obstruction; it is the forced transverse escape direction from the current cut state.

The deletion theory supplies the protected states, the ternary theory gives terminating motion inside a fiber, and the root theory describes transverse cut exchange.

### Elevated closure schema

The Article III handoff should therefore be formulated as a **fiber/base escape theorem**:

> Choose an extremal protected state. If an admissible move remains inside its cut fiber, the local band potential improves. If the fiber is locally maximal, every unresolved boundary emits a transverse physical root. Closure follows once each such terminal root can either be realized as a legal base exchange, combined with other terminal roots into a positive circulation, or converted into a state with strictly better global extremal data.

This formulation subsumes the earlier language of singleton transport, A2 scans, and individual A3 circuits. Those are local models for fiber motion or transverse exchange, not separate closure mechanisms.

### Why this elevation matters

It exposes the exact interface theorem the composed Article presently lacks. The local threshold-band root and the canonical minimum-phase switch root are not automatically the same protected species. Therefore one must not identify the terminal fiber obstruction with an edge of the original fixed Johnson layer without additional provenance.

The correct global proof should attach a legitimate cut state to every terminal barrier, or transport the barrier back to a canonical switch state. Once that bridge is available, the whole proof can be expressed as termination of a fibered exchange system rather than a collection of local gadgets.

This is an architectural strengthening, not a closure theorem. It is the natural elevated form of the mathematics already present in the composed Article.
