Work in ternary arity with the honest nearest-violation switch-prism labels

### Opposite-sign reuse of one middle is already extractable

Suppose one physical coordinate b occurs as the middle coordinate of two support labels with opposite signs.

Then the product carrier cell containing the lifted zero contains a state with a +b violating window and a state with a -b violating window.

Root §81, with the endpoint-path repair of root §143, applies to an arbitrary such complementary cell: one may choose a product-cell path avoiding global endpoint loss outside the already local A2 exception, and the first disappearance/side-crossing event for the b-centered violation is one of:

- vertical cut improvement;
- same-side bubble, strict or equality transport;
- neighbor replacement, strict or equality transport;

The equality same-side and neighbor-replacement classes are controlled by the common fixed-cut quadratic-distance potential, while a genuine switch-crossing event seeds the terminating central threshold-band process of root §149.

Thus opposite-sign middle reuse is not an unexplained lifted-circuit phenomenon; it hands the topology back to the established Article III repair/transport frontier.

### Residual middle normal form

Therefore, in a genuinely UNEXTRACTED full-dimensional lifted circuit, each physical coordinate b may occur as a middle only with one side sign.

Equivalently there is a partial sign assignment

epsilon: V -> {+,-}

such that every support label (u,m,v,s) satisfies

s=epsilon(m)

whenever m occurs as a middle.

Call this the sign-separated middle-fiber condition.

Because the circuit has n+1 labels but only n physical coordinates available as middles, pigeonhole gives at least one physical coordinate that occurs as a middle on at least two support edges. Under sign separation those repeated occurrences necessarily have the SAME side sign.

### Consequence

The final full-dimensional switch-prism residue is now simultaneously:

1. a connected bridgeless bicyclic physical root graph (figure-eight or theta);
2. a full circuit of n+1 lifted labels in n dimensions;
3. sign-separated by physical middle coordinate;
4. with at least one same-sign middle fiber of multiplicity at least two.

Any closure of the honest lifted topology may therefore focus on the interaction of repeated same-side edges in one link tournament G_m, rather than arbitrary bicyclic root labels.
