# Inert exchanges and descent root certificates

At an admissible minimum-first-phase witness, every noninert replacement packet contains 10 and has a nonzero descent-root sum because dropped and entering coordinates are disjoint. An inert exchange gives mutual blockers on a codimension-two core; each pair order has a pair-window one followed by an inherited zero, producing a second-level descent certificate.
