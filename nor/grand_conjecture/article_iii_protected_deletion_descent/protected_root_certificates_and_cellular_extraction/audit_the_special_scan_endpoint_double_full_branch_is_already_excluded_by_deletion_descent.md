## Audit: the special-scan endpoint double-full branch is already excluded by deletion descent

Root §102 studies a canonical endpoint carrier with deletion word

0^p 1^q,

p,q>=3,

and the SPECIAL perfect-blocker scan

s=1^(p+1)0^q.

It then swaps the omitted blocker past the final deletion coordinate and reaches a double-full 101 gadget.

In a minimum coboundary-flat alternating ternary counterexample, however, these same hypotheses already fall under the earlier audited protected deletion-carrier descent.

### Existing special-scan closure

The audited endpoint-run theorem for the special perfect-blocker scan excludes p=3 and, by reversal, q=3. Hence under §102's hypotheses one actually has

p,q>=4.

The audited protected deletion-carrier descent then constructs

H=(v_p,v_1,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},...,v_m)

with status word

A, 0^(p-3), 1^(q+3).

If A=0 this is a spanning one-change order. In a counterexample A=1, and deleting v_p leaves a genuine one-change deletion carrier

0^(p-3)1^(q+3)

omitting v_p.

Counterexamplehood makes v_p a blocker for the new carrier. The descent is self-renewing: reapply the minimum-counterexample special-scan/run-length theorem to the new carrier. Iteration forces

p, p-3, p-6, ...

all to be at least four, impossible.

Therefore no minimum counterexample satisfying the special perfect-blocker scan exists.

### Consequence for root §102

The double-full endpoint handoff of §102 is locally correct, but it is not an active minimum-counterexample closure frontier under its stated special-scan hypothesis. That branch is already eliminated before the gadget is needed.

The genuine endpoint frontier is the arbitrary-scan endpoint geometry that survives the audit 'insertion blocking does not force the special blocker scan'. In particular the rank-two endpoint protected-root cycle of root §84 and its Johnson-square partner defects remain relevant because they do not assume the special scan.

### Strategic correction

Do not spend closure effort trying to terminate the non-returning resolution of §102 unless the special-scan hypothesis is first weakened substantially.

The live endpoint target should instead be:

- prove the endpoint square-lift lemma for arbitrary endpoint witnesses; or
- derive a strict witness improvement from a failed square lift;

while retaining the exact rank-two cuts and unit partner defects of root §§84,93.

This is not a claim that the arbitrary-scan flat sector is closed. It removes only the special-scan endpoint subbranch.
