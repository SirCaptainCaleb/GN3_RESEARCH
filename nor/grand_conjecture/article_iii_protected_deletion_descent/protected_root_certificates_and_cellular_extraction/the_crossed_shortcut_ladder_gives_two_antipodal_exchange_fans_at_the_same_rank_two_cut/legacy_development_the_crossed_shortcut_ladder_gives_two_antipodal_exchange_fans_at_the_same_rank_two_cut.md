## The crossed shortcut ladder gives two antipodal exchange fans at the same rank-two cut

Continue with the two-ended barrier ladder of §305:
[
{x,z}	o{w_1,w_2}
quad|quad
O=(w_1,ldots,w_m)
quad|quad
{w_{m-1},w_m}	o{x,z}.
]

### Left central cut

The left fully-curved packet occurs at the global beginning:
[
(x,z,w_1,w_2)
]
up to swapping (x,z).

Its canonical central prefix cut is therefore exactly
[
C_L={x,z}.
]

By the complete barrier-square theorem §230, this one packet realizes all four same-rank exchanges from (C_L):
[
x	o w_1,quad x	o w_2,quad
z	o w_1,quad z	o w_2.
]

Equivalently, at the Johnson vertex ({x,z}) it realizes the four neighboring cuts obtained by replacing either member of ({x,z}) with either of (w_1,w_2).

### Right central cut

The right fully-curved packet occurs at the global end:
[
(w_{m-1},w_m,x,z)
]
up to the same orientation normalization.

Its central prefix cut is
[
C_R=Vsetminus{x,z}=C_L^c.
]

It realizes the roots
[
w_{m-1}	o x,quad w_{m-1}	o z,quad
w_m	o x,quad w_m	o z.
]

Now complement the cut certificate. Complement sends
[
C_Rmapsto C_L
]
and negates every oriented exchange root. Therefore the right barrier, viewed antipodally at the rank-two cut (C_L), realizes
[
x	o w_{m-1},quad x	o w_m,quad
z	o w_{m-1},quad z	o w_m.
]

### Consequence

The crossed residue is not merely a pair of distant endpoint barriers. In the graded-cut geometry it is one rank-two state ({x,z}) carrying two certified exchange fans into opposite ends of the same good middle order.

The remaining task is to propagate this common rank-two fan from an endpoint pair of (O) to the unique switch of (O). Any such propagation from either end would meet the internal switch structure and create a same-cut shortcut/splice cell.
