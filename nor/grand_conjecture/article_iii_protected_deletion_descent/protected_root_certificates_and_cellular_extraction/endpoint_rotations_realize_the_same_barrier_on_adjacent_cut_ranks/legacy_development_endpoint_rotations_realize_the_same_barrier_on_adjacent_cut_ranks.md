## Endpoint rotations realize the same barrier on adjacent cut ranks

Continue with the synchronized trajectory of §§308–309. At every stage there is a common middle order M and a distinguished coordinate x such that xM and Mx are both good deletion witnesses omitting the same coordinate.

All replacement moves occur in the switch collar of M. Since both phases remain at least three, x stays outside every local packet.

Suppose the trajectory ends at a fully-curved four-coordinate barrier inside M, with physical exchange rho=e_s-e_t.

The middle coordinates have exactly the same relative order in xM and Mx. Therefore the same four-coordinate barrier, and the same exchange rho, occurs in both witnesses.

Let C be its central prefix cut in Mx. Since x is last, x is not in C. In xM the same barrier is shifted one position right and the prefix contains x. Its central cut is therefore C union {x}.

So the same physical exchange rho is realized at the two adjacent cut states

(C,rho) and (C union {x},rho).

Equivalently, the four cut vertices

C,
C-s+t,
C union {x},
(C-s+t) union {x}

form a realized rectangle in the graded cut cube. The horizontal edges carry the same exchange rho; the vertical edges add or remove x.

Thus a failed shortcut followed through the synchronized deletion dynamics exits through a rank-lifted barrier rectangle, rather than through an isolated exchange. The endpoint coordinate x supplies the vertical cubical direction.

This gives an actual reversible local cell linking rank-changing cut motion with a protected same-rank exchange, which is the local structure needed by the graded-cut connector program.
