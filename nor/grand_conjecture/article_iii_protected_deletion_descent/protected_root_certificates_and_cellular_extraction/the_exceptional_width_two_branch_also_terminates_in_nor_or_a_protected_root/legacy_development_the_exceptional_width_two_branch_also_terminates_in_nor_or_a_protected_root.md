# The exceptional width-two branch also terminates in NOR or a protected root — preserved pre-item development

## The exceptional width-two branch has a finite threshold-band handoff

Continue from §266 in the exceptional (r,s)=(1,1) width-two type. The stronger rigid weave forces its unique left reconnection bit to be one and creates the double-full singleton
\[
(q,a,d,b,c)
\]
with word
\[
1,0,1.
\]

Use the right-preserving double-full resolution from §260:
\[
(q,a,d,b,c)\longmapsto(a,q,d,b,c).
\]
Inside the full ambient order this produces a certified consecutive status block
\[
0,1,1,1,1.
\]
The ordered right data of the double-full packet and the right side of the rigid six-set are preserved exactly. The only newly uncontrolled data lie to the left of this seed; the old trailing side begins beyond the displayed 1-band.

### Maximal compatible interval

Fix the one-change threshold target whose switch is the displayed
\[
0\to1
\]
inside the seed. Let I be the maximal contiguous interval of window ranks containing the entire displayed block and agreeing with this target.

The seed has positive length on both target colors, so I is nonempty and contains the original rigid obstruction strictly in its interior on the resolved side.

At either endpoint of I, if the adjacent boundary tetrahedron is flat in the outward direction, apply the audited threshold-band endpoint repair. By the threshold-band potential theorem, this preserves the already matched interval and moves that endpoint strictly outward.

Therefore repeated flat repairs terminate.

### Terminal outcomes

A maximal sequence has only the following outcomes.

1. The compatible interval reaches both global endpoints. Then the full order agrees with one threshold word and is a spanning NOR-good order.

2. Before global closure, an endpoint of the compatible interval is supported by a fully-curved tetrahedron. That tetrahedron supplies an actual protected physical root with the entire current compatible interval retained on its protected side.

No flat boundary can be terminal, and the strict interval-length potential forbids recurrence to the original width-two packet.

### Theorem

The exceptional lexicographic width-two type (r,s)=(1,1) is not a terminal local obstruction. Its rigid weave and double-full resolution give a finite boundary-safe handoff:
\[
\boxed{\text{width two}\longrightarrow\text{NOR closure or protected full-curvature root}.}
\]

Combined with §267 for the three nonexceptional types, **every** lexicographically extremal width-two full/full band has the same final dichotomy: a spanning one-change order or an actual protected root after a strict local improvement.

Thus width two is discharged as an independent branch. The remaining Article III obligation lies in the protected-root extraction/gluing problem, not in further six-set classification.
