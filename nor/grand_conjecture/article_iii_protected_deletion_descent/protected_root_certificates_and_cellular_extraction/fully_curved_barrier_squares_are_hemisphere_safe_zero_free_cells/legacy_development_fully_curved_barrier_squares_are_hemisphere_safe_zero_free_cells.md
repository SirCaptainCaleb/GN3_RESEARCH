### Topological consequence

This repairs one local coherence issue in the compatible-carrier program.

When two protected root choices differ only by the independent left/right endpoint swap of the same fully-curved tetrahedron, they need not be treated as unrelated labels or joined by an arbitrary interpolation. The four realized roots already span a canonical square, and the affine extension across that square cannot create a spurious zero because the whole cell lies in one strict hemisphere.

Thus fully-curved rank-two commuting cells are **safe fillers** for an equivariant root carrier:
- the local cell is canonically determined by the physical tetrahedron;
- its antipode is obtained by reversal;
- the odd root map extends affinely over the cell;
- no zero occurs in the filled cell.

The remaining global topology is therefore not obstructed by commuting ambiguity *inside one full barrier*. Any forced zero in a complex obtained by gluing these cells must occur where different central-cut/provenance sheets meet, not in the interior of an individual barrier square.

This identifies a sharper closure target: construct the gluing complex of protected states using these hemisphere-safe (K_{2,2}) cells and analyze only the intersections where the central cut changes.
