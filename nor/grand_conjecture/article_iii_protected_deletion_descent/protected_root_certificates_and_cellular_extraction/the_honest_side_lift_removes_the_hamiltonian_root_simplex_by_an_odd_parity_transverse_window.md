## The honest side lift removes the Hamiltonian root simplex by an odd-parity transverse window

Work in ternary arity with the honest switch-prism lifted labels

widehat rho=(rho,s) in W direct-sum R,

where rho is the first-minus-last root of a violating ternary window and s in {+1,-1} records its side of the proposed switch.

Suppose a support-minimal lifted zero in one product carrier cell has physical projection equal to a Hamiltonian directed cycle

rho_i=e_{x_i}-e_{x_{i+1}},
i in Z/nZ,

using every physical coordinate exactly once.

Because a simple physical cycle has a unique positive dependence up to scale, the lifted zero has equal coefficients after normalization. Hence

sum_i rho_i=0,
sum_i s_i=0.

In particular n is even.

### The lifted Hamiltonian span is a graph over W

The physical Hamiltonian roots span the type-A space W and have one-dimensional kernel generated by (1,...,1).

Let H be the linear span in W direct-sum R of the lifted vectors

widehat rho_i=(rho_i,s_i).

Because sum_i s_i=0, the same unique physical relation is also a lifted relation. Thus dim H=n-1=dim W.

Projection H->W is therefore an isomorphism. Consequently H is the graph of a unique linear functional

f:W->R

satisfying

f(rho_i)=s_i.

For every i,

f(e_{x_i}-e_{x_{i+2}})
=
f(rho_i+rho_{i+1})
=
s_i+s_{i+1}
in {-2,0,2}.

### The Hamiltonian coordinate order supplies a transverse honest label

Consider the full coordinate order

pi_H=(x_0,x_1,...,x_{n-1}).

Choose any cut vertex in the vertical interval cell of the product carrier containing the lifted zero. Since the ambient instance is a counterexample, this switch state is bad and the honest switch-prism labeling selects some violating ternary window.

Every ternary window of pi_H has the form

(x_i,x_{i+1},x_{i+2})

for some linear index i, so its physical window root is

sigma_i=e_{x_i}-e_{x_{i+2}}.

Its honest lifted label is

widehat sigma_i=(sigma_i,t)

with t in {+1,-1}.

But

f(sigma_i)=s_i+s_{i+1}

is even, while t is odd. Therefore

t != f(sigma_i),

so

widehat sigma_i notin H.

Thus EVERY honest violating-window label available on the Hamiltonian coordinate order is transverse to the lifted Hamiltonian span. No choice of threshold polarity or selected violating window is needed.

### Local blow-up

The n lifted Hamiltonian labels widehat rho_i are affinely independent because their physical projections rho_i are affinely independent. Their convex hull is an (n-1)-simplex lying in the hyperplane H, with zero in its relative interior.

Subdivide that zero simplex by adding an interior vertex labeled by the genuine transverse state label widehat sigma_i above, and cone its boundary to the new vertex.

Every new cone simplex is zero-free:
- if the coefficient of widehat sigma_i is positive, projection to (W direct-sum R)/H is nonzero;
- if that coefficient is zero, the point lies on a proper face of the original support-minimal zero simplex, which is zero-free.

Hence the Hamiltonian lifted zero is locally removable relative to its boundary.

### Why this does not contradict the bare-root degree audit

In bare W the Hamiltonian circuit simplex is full-dimensional and has local degree plus or minus one, so root §184 correctly proves that it cannot be removed relative to its boundary.

In the honest lifted target W direct-sum R, the same Hamiltonian labels span only the codimension-one hyperplane H. The extra side coordinate supplies a genuine transverse direction, and the parity calculation above produces an ACTUAL state label in that direction.

This is exactly the role the switch-prism dimension was meant to play.

### Scope

The theorem removes a support-minimal honest lifted zero whose physical projection is ONE Hamiltonian simple cycle.

It does not yet classify a support-minimal lifted zero whose physical positive circulation decomposes into several physical cycles with nonzero side imbalance on the individual cycles but total side balance across the union. That multi-cycle lifted case is the remaining root-topology obstruction.
