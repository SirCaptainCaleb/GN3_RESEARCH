# Bourgin-Yang turns target compression into a large zero locus


For a continuous odd map \(f:S^d\to W\) with \(\dim W=q\le d\), Bourgin--Yang gives
\[
\dim f^{-1}(0)\ge d-q.
\]
Thus a low-rank reversal-odd compression of NOR defects can produce a positive-dimensional family of balanced states, not merely one zero. A separate combinatorial argument is still required to show that motion in this zero locus changes poles, chambers, or defect supports.
