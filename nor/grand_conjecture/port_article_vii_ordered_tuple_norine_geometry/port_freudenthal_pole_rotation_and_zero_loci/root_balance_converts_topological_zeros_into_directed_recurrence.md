# Root balance converts topological zeros into directed recurrence

For a finite directed graph D on [n], associate the type-A root e_i−e_j to each arc i→j. Then 0 lies in the convex hull of the arc roots if and only if D has a directed cycle. Indeed, a directed cycle gives a zero sum of its roots with equal positive weights. Conversely a convex zero yields nonnegative arc weights, not all zero, whose net flow is zero at every vertex. Following a positive-weight outgoing arc repeatedly in the finite support gives a directed cycle. Thus when chamber obstruction labels are genuine directed roots, a zero of the interpolated odd map forces a combinatorial recurrence among labels carried by a face. Showing that this recurrence yields a NOR-good geodesic is an independent problem.
