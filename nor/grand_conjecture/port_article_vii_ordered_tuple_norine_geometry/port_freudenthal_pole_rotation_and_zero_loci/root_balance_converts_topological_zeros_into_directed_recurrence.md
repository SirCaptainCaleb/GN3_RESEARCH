# Root balance converts topological zeros into directed recurrence


Type-\(A\) roots turn a balanced topological carrier into recurrence. For a finite directed graph,
\[
0\in\operatorname{conv}\{e_i-e_j:i\to j\}
\]
iff the graph contains a directed cycle. Hence if a NOR obstruction can be encoded by reversal-odd roots, a cellular zero yields a directed recurrence among obstruction coordinates.
