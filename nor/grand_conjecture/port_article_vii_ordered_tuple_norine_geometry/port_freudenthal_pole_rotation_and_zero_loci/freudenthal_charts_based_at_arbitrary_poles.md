# Freudenthal charts based at arbitrary poles


Each cube vertex \(S\) defines a Boolean-order chart by \(A\preceq_S B\) iff \(A\triangle S\subseteq B\triangle S\). In this chart, \(S\) and \(\bar S\) are the two poles and every antipodal geodesic between them is monotone. This is a change of ambient coordinates only: NOR tuple colors are not renormalized. Hence the Freudenthal chamber picture exists around every antipodal pole pair, giving a family of overlapping pole charts.
