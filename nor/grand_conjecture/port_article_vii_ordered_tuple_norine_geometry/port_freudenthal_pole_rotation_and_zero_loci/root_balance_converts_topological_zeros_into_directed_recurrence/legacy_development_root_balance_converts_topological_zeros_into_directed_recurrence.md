## Development


A particularly useful algebraic compression uses type-\(A\) roots.

Associate to a directed relation \(i\to j\) the vector
\[
e_i-e_j.
\]
For any finite directed graph \(D\),
\[
0\in\operatorname{conv}\{e_i-e_j:(i,j)\in E(D)\}
\]
if and only if \(D\) contains a directed cycle.

Indeed, a directed cycle sums to zero. Conversely, nonnegative coefficients giving a convex zero define a nonzero circulation, and every finite nonzero circulation contains a directed cycle.

Thus whenever a NOR obstruction can be encoded as a reversal-odd root \(e_i-e_j\), the cellular topology can return a balanced face whose root labels contain a directed recurrence among obstruction coordinates.

This was the conceptual bridge in Article VII between a topological zero and finite combinatorial recurrence. It is independent of deletion covers and may apply to first/last transition fronts or other ordered-window defects in NOR.
