## Development


Article VII found a reusable fence against graph-only Tucker reasoning.

A chamber labeling may be antipodal and may change in a tightly controlled way under each adjacent transposition without forcing any desired complementary edge on the chamber graph. For example, the label
\[
Q(v_1,\ldots,v_n)=(v_1,v_n)
\]
is reversed by permutation reversal, while an adjacent transposition changes at most one coordinate of \(Q\). This rook-like local behavior still does not force the kind of complementary chamber pair one would want from a naive Tucker argument.

The lesson for NOR is structural: local control on adjacent permutations is weaker than coherent control over the higher-dimensional Coxeter cells. Any topological proof should either:
1. work on a genuine antipodal triangulation/cell complex with a globally defined extension, or
2. verify compatibility across the commuting-square and braid-hexagon relations.

The barycentric face-average construction avoids this gap because it is defined directly on the full face poset.
