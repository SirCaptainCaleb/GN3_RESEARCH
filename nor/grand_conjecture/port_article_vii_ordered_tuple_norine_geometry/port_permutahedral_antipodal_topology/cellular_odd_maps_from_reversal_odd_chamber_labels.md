# Cellular odd maps from reversal-odd chamber labels

Let K be the type-A Coxeter complex whose chambers are permutations π of V and whose antipodal chamber involution reverses each permutation. Suppose L assigns a vector L(π) in a real vector space W to each chamber and obeys L(π^rev)=−L(π). For each face F, assign to its barycenter the arithmetic mean of L over all chambers containing F, and interpolate affinely over the barycentric subdivision along chains of nested faces. Reversal carries the chamber star of F bijectively onto the star of its antipode and negates its labels; hence the interpolation defines a continuous odd map Φ on |K|. If Φ vanishes at a point of a barycentric simplex, zero belongs to the convex hull of chamber labels associated with its supporting face flag. This is a balance certificate, not automatically a monochromatic geodesic: an additional extraction theorem must convert the balanced labels into a legal NOR order.
