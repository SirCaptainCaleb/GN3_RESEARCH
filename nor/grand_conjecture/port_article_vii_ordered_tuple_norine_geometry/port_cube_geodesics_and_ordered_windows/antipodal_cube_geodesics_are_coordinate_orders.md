# Antipodal cube geodesics are coordinate orders


An antipodal geodesic in \(Q_V\) has length \(|V|\), hence flips every coordinate once. Its flip sequence is therefore a permutation of \(V\). Conversely, any start vertex together with any permutation determines an antipodal geodesic. Thus arbitrary antipodal geodesics are naturally parameterized by a start sign pattern and a coordinate order.
