# Port from Article VII — Permutahedral antipodal topology


## Permutahedral antipodal topology and chain-level rook architecture

Reversal-odd chamber statistics on coordinate orders admit canonical cellular extensions by averaging over permutahedral faces. Minimal zero carriers yield positive balanced chamber relations, while graph-only adjacent-swap control is generally insufficient without higher-dimensional coherence.

Wu and Yang's proof of Norine's original conjecture supplies a stronger chain-level template. A counterexample gives a rook labeling; rook pairs become type-\(A\) roots; Freudenthal subdivision turns cubical faces into coherent monotone galleries; gallery roots define subdivision-invariant spherical polyhedral chains; and a purely algebraic equivariant chain obstruction rules out the construction. For NOR, this suggests that a successful topological proof may only need coherent chain-valued data from ordered-window defects rather than a pointwise continuous map.
