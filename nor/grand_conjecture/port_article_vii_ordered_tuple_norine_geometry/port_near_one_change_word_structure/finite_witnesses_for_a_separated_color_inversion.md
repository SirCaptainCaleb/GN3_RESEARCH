# Finite witnesses for a separated color inversion


For a binary word with first \(0\) at \(p\) and last \(1\) at \(q\),
\[
q\le p+1
\]
holds iff the word avoids \(001\), \(011\), and \(0101\) as consecutive subwords. Hence every failure of the width-one inversion relaxation is witnessed on at most four positions. Reverse-complement exchanges \(001\) and \(011\) and fixes \(0101\).
