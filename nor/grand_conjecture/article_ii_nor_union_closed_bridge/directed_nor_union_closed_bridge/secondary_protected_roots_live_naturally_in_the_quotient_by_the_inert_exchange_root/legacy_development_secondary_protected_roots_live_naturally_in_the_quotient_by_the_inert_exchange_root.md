## Development

Codimension-matched quotient target for the two-level protected root certificate. At an inert protected exchange replacing y by x, let n_xy=e_x-e_y in the type-A root space W_V. The primary protected-descent certificate vanishes exactly on this inert locus. Subsection 195 then supplies a mutual-blocker pair and subsection 197 supplies a nonzero secondary descent root rho=e_a-e_c from one of the pair-insertion packets. Pass to the quotient W_V/<n_xy>. The projected secondary root is still nonzero. Indeed a type-A physical root e_a-e_c can vanish in this one-dimensional quotient only if it equals plus or minus e_x-e_y, so {a,c}={x,y}. But adjacent pair-insertion windows never replace x directly by y: through the first r-2 packet transitions both blockers remain present and only background coordinates are dropped/entered; at the final transition one blocker may leave but the entering coordinate is a background coordinate. Hence rho is not plus or minus n_xy. Reversal negates both physical descent roots and the normal root line, so the projected secondary certificate retains the required odd equivariance. Thus the algebraic two-level carrier has the correct dimension drop: primary roots live in W_V, while the obstruction on an inert codimension-one exchange naturally lives in W_V/<e_x-e_y>, of dimension one less. The remaining topological obligation is to identify the inert exchange stratum/link with an antipodal carrier of matching dimension and apply Tucker/Borsuk-Ulam there. This avoids the problematic infinitesimal sum R1+epsilon R2 and suggests an inductive obstruction-theory proof across protected codimension strata.
