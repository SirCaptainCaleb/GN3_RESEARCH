# In the flat switching model, curvature is the three-step chord direction — preserved pre-item development

## Development


Work in the coboundary-flat ternary sector. Choose a tournament representative and switch it so a coordinate order v_1,...,v_n is a directed Hamilton path. Then alpha(v_i,v_{i+1},v_{i+2}) is exactly the tournament bit of the distance-two chord v_i v_{i+2}.

For four consecutive path vertices a->b->c->d carrying a status change x=alpha(a,b,c) != y=alpha(b,c,d), a direct substitution into the tournament formula shows:
- if the long chord a->d points forward along the Hamilton order, the tetrahedron is fully curved;
- if d->a points backward, the tetrahedron is flat.

Thus, along a switched Hamilton path,

fully curved transition  <=>  the three-step chord points forward,
flat transition          <=>  the three-step chord points backward.

This gives a simple tournament interpretation of pinned versus mobile switches in the coboundary-flat sector.
