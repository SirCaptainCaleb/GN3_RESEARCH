## Development

Protected descent with arbitrary tetrahedral coboundary in the pure alternating ternary sector. Let O=(v_1,...,v_m) have word 0^p1^q and omitted perfect blocker x with scan 1^(p+1)0^q; no global coboundary-flatness is assumed. Consider H=(v_p,v_1,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},...,v_m). As in the flat proof, the two outer replacement statuses are forced to 1: alpha(v_{p-2},v_{p-1},x)=s_{p-2}=1 and alpha(x,v_{p+1},v_{p+2})=s_{p+1}=1. The middle replacement status is alpha(v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1})=1-alpha(x,v_{p-1},v_{p+1}). Let kappa be the tetrahedral coboundary on Q={x,v_{p-1},v_p,v_{p+1}}. The four-face identity gives alpha(x,v_{p-1},v_{p+1}) = s_{p-1} xor w_{p-1} xor s_p xor kappa = kappa, since (s_{p-1},w_{p-1},s_p)=(1,0,1). Hence the protected replacement packet is exactly (1,1-kappa,1). If kappa=0, all three bridge windows are 1 and the audited deletion-carrier descent p->p-3 applies. Therefore any minimum pure-orientation ternary counterexample must have kappa=1 at this protected crossing for every one-change deletion carrier. Reversing the deletion carrier gives the symmetric nonzero-coboundary tetrahedron at the other side of the switch. Thus the flat-sector proof extends to a localization theorem: the only obstruction to protected descent is a specific nonzero tetrahedral curvature at the replacement packet. The next target is to show these forced curvature tetrahedra cannot persist under the self-renewing change of omitted coordinate, or to feed them into the switch-prism/Radon carrier as the required local obstruction certificate.
