## Development

For a successful right endpoint repair swapping c,d with fixed far ordered pair (q,r), subsection 95 says the transport distance is 2 when alpha(c,q,r)=alpha(d,q,r) and 3 otherwise. Define g_{q,r}(v)=alpha(v,q,r) and lambda(E)=0 for distance 2, 1 for distance 3. Then lambda(E)=g_{q,r}(c) xor g_{q,r}(d). Hence, for fixed far tail, the selector is an exact 1-coboundary on the adjacent-transposition graph. Explicitly, define H_{q,r}(pi) as the xor of g_{q,r}(v_i) over odd positions of pi. Swapping adjacent c,d toggles H by g(c) xor g(d)=lambda(E). Therefore every closed repair loop preserving (q,r) has even parity of distance-3 transports. Nontrivial repair holonomy can only be generated when the far tail changes. This localizes the closed-component problem to codimension-two tail-change residues, the same rank-two geometry highlighted by the switch-prism/Tucker program.
