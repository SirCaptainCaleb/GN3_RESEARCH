## Development

For ternary reversal-odd NOR, write epsilon=alpha times the middle-vertex defect flip, where alpha is an alternating orientation of every coordinate triangle and d(T) is the optional defect vertex. The defect-field insertion theorem gives at least one Hamilton order on which no consecutive triple is read with its marked defect vertex in the middle; along such an order epsilon equals alpha on every window. Therefore any ternary NOR counterexample has the following exact incompatibility: every defect-free Hamilton order has at least two changes in the alpha-sign word. Conversely, if an alpha-one-change Hamilton order is also defect-free then it is immediately a NOR witness. Neither side alone can be the obstruction: arbitrary defect fields always admit a defect-free Hamilton order, while endpoint-only/coherent special orientation classes have independent spanning witnesses. The remaining closure problem can be stated as an intersection theorem: for every pair (alpha,d) arising from the triangle decomposition, prove that the reversal-invariant family of defect-free Hamilton orders intersects the family of alpha-orders with at most one sign change. This is a sharper target for the simplex/Coxeter-sphere machinery than generic root balance, because recurrence alone is already available from deletion-root cycles.
