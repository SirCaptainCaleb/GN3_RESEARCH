## Development

Idea: blow up the inert protected-exchange locus instead of trying to glue a root map through zero. In the first-run-minimal protected bridge complex, a noninert replacement bridge B carries the nonzero descent-root certificate R_1(B). The only zero states are inert bridges B=0^r. Each such state canonically exposes a mutual blocker pair x,y over a fixed codimension-two core, and the two pair orders (x,y) and (y,x) each carry a nonzero pair-insertion descent-root certificate R_2 by subsection 197. Modify the protected exchange complex by replacing every inert state with a small interval whose two endpoints are these two pair orders. Attach the interval to the neighboring noninert bridge cells according to which blocker order is approached. Reversal swaps the pair orders and reverses every physical root, so the blow-up inherits a free antipodal involution and an odd root labeling. The key point is geometric: the level-1 zero is removed from the state space rather than filled analytically. Every new vertex now carries an actual protected nonzero root coming from either a single replacement descent or a pair-insertion descent. The desired topological statement becomes: after this real-oriented/stellar blow-up, the protected root labeling extends over cells without creating a Radon zero except when a physical coordinate cycle of certified exchanges occurs. This is a natural place to apply a polytopal Tucker/Ky-Fan or oriented-matroid theorem. A minimal Radon zero would then be a physical root cycle; the base cases are exactly the antipodal two-cycle and directed A2 triangle already appearing in the corrected flat replacement dynamics. This construction may therefore unify the general carrier-extraction problem with the two remaining flat rank-two obstructions. It is presently a proposed modified triangulation, not a proved topological reduction; the attachment rule and cell structure around overlapping inert states still need to be checked.
