## Development

Work in the coboundary-flat pure-orientation sector, so every tetrahedron is flat or fully curved. Let C be a minimum cyclic four-change carrier with canonical run lengths 1,p,q,2. Let T be the transition entering the final length-two run from the preceding q-run. The singleton-trap theorem shows that T cannot admit the rightward endpoint repair: the two far transition bits hit by that repair are exactly the switch exiting the length-two run and the switch exiting the following singleton run, so the repair would lower cyclic variation from 4 to 2. In the flat sector, however, every flat transition tetrahedron admits both endpoint repairs. Therefore T cannot be flat and must be fully curved. Thus every canonical minimum four-change carrier in the coboundary-flat sector has a universal switch gadget pinned at the corner entering the length-two run. The other transitions are either additional fully curved barriers or bidirectionally mobile flat switches, and no mobile flat switch can be transported into the pinned singleton trap without closing NOR.
