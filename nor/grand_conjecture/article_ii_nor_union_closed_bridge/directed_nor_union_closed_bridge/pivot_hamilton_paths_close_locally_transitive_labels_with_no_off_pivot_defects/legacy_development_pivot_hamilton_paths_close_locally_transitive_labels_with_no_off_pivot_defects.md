# Pivot Hamilton paths close locally transitive labels with no off-pivot defects — preserved pre-item development

Work in ternary coordinate arity, with reversal-odd h. For a pivot o define a directed graph G_o on V minus {o}: put a->b precisely when h(o,a,b)=h(a,b,o)=0. Reversal shows that both directions cannot occur. In the triangle-orientation/defect decomposition, a pair {a,b} has a directed edge in G_o if and only if the triangle {o,a,b} is coherent or its defect vertex is o. If the defect is a or b, the pair is absent.

Pivot-path lemma. Suppose G_o has a directed Hamilton path (u1,...,um), and for each consecutive triple ui,ui+1,ui+2 the center tournament at ui+1 has no directed triangle on {ui,o,ui+2}. Then (o,u1,...,um) is a monochromatic spanning order of color 0. Indeed, the path edges give h(ui,ui+1,o)=0 and h(o,ui+1,ui+2)=0. These are ui->o->ui+2 at center ui+1. The absence of the closing directed triangle forces h(ui,ui+1,ui+2)=0. The first window has color 0 by the first path edge. Orders on fewer than three vertices are vacuous.

Corollary. If all center tournaments are transitive and some pivot o is never an unmarked vertex of a defect triangle (every triangle containing o is coherent or marked at o), then NOR has a monochromatic spanning witness. Under that defect condition G_o is a tournament. A directed Hamilton path exists by elementary insertion: insert a new vertex before the first existing vertex it beats, or append it if it beats none; its predecessor, when present, beats it. The pivot-path lemma then applies. In particular, coherent reversal-odd labels with transitive center tournaments always admit a monochromatic spanning order, in every finite size.

Counterexample consequence. Within the locally transitive sector, any counterexample must have, for every vertex o, a triangle containing o whose defect is marked at another vertex. Equivalently, the unmarked vertices of defect triangles cover V. More generally, even when this coverage holds, a directed Hamilton path in any G_o still closes the conjecture under local transitivity. Thus defect coverage is necessary but not sufficient for failure: the remaining task is Hamilton-path existence in the pivot graphs, or an argument handling their missing pairs.

Scope and dependencies. This uses the toolkit decomposition ternary_nor_decomposes_into_triangle_orientation_and_defect_vertex and the center-tournament definition. It neither assumes support union closure nor invokes Frankl's conjecture. The unrestricted directed conjecture remains open here; centered directed triangles are a separate obstruction, and defect coverage alone is not a contradiction.
