Audit of the protected deletion-carrier descent. The descent step is self-contained under the already audited flat-sector facts. Let O have word 0^p1^q and omitted perfect blocker x. The constructed full order H=(v_p,v_1,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},...,v_m) has first status A=alpha(v_p,v_1,v_2), then exactly p-3 old zero statuses, then three forced one-statuses, then the untouched q-status one-run. The middle forced values use only s_{p-2}=1, the full tube identity alpha(x,v_{p-1},v_{p+1})=0, and s_{p+1}=1. Therefore A=0 would be a spanning one-change order, so A=1. Deleting v_p leaves a deletion order O' with exactly p+q statuses and word 0^(p-3)1^(q+3); the counts sum correctly. Because the ambient full instance is a counterexample, no insertion of the omitted vertex v_p into O' can be good, so v_p is automatically a perfect blocker for this new carrier. No inheritance of the old blocker scan is assumed. Hence every minimum-counterexample theorem for an arbitrary deletion carrier applies afresh to O', including the endpoint-run lower bound. If p-3<4, this immediately contradicts that bound; if p-3>=4, the same descent can be reapplied. Iteration therefore gives p-3k>=4 for all k, impossible. No cyclic extremality, canonical-wrap flatness, or unproved propagation is used. The closure of the coboundary-flat alternating ternary sector is therefore stable under this audit.
