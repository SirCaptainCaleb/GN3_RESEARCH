Protected long-range blocker forced by endpoint surgery. Let O=(v1,...,vm) have word 0^p1^q in a minimum coboundary-flat ternary counterexample, with p,q>=4 and omitted perfect blocker x of scan 1^(p+1)0^q. Consider the full order H=(v_p,v_1,v_2,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},v_{p+2},...,v_m). Its first status is A=alpha(v_p,v_1,v_2). The subsequent untouched statuses w_1,...,w_{p-3} are all 0. Next alpha(v_{p-2},v_{p-1},x)=s_{p-2}=1 by cyclic invariance. The next status alpha(v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1}) equals 1-alpha(x,v_{p-1},v_{p+1})=1, because the full blocker-tube tetrahedron Q_{p-1} forces the off-diagonal face alpha(x,v_{p-1},v_{p+1})=w_{p-1}=0. Then alpha(x,v_{p+1},v_{p+2})=s_{p+1}=1, and every later untouched status lies in the old 1-run. Hence if A=0, H has word 0^*1^* and closes NOR. Therefore every counterexample forces alpha(v_p,v_1,v_2)=1. Reversing the deletion carrier yields the symmetric forced blocker alpha(v_{p+3},v_m,v_{m-1})=1. These are protected long-range constraints: each arises from a full-support reorder that leaves the opposite suffix/prefix exactly in the original deletion order. For p=3 the first forced value contradicts cyclic invariance alpha(v_3,v_1,v_2)=alpha(v_1,v_2,v_3)=0, recovering the endpoint-run lower bound. The next target is to use coboundary flatness to propagate these two long blockers toward the unique central flat tetrahedron.
