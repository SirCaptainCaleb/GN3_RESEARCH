# Minimum flat-sector endpoint runs have length at least three — preserved pre-item development

## Development


Let O=(v_1,...,v_m) be a one-change deletion carrier with word 0^p1^q in a minimum coboundary-flat ternary counterexample, and let x be the omitted perfect blocker. Its scan is 1^(p+1)0^q.

If p=2, the endpoint tetrahedron {x,v_1,v_2,v_3} is fully curved. Hence alpha(x,v_1,v_3)=0. The full order

(v_2,v_1,x,v_3,v_4,...,v_m)

has first two statuses 0,1. Its third status is alpha(x,v_3,v_4)=1 because the blocker scan begins 111, and all later statuses are the untouched tail of O, which is entirely 1 when p=2. Thus the full word is 0 followed by only 1s, a one-change order.

So p is not 2. Reversing the deletion carrier exchanges p and q, and the same argument excludes q=2.

Since earlier insertion calculus already gives p,q>=2, every minimum flat-sector counterexample must satisfy p,q>=3.
