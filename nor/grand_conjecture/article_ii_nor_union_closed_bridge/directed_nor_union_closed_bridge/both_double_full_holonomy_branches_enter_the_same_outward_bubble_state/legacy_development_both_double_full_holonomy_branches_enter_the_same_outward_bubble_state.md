# Both double-full holonomy branches enter the same outward bubble state — preserved pre-item development


Assume the minimum flat-sector endpoint normal form with p,q>=3. Perform the canonical flat wrap repair, producing a singleton 0-run bracketed by two fully-curved switches.

Use the local notation

a=v_{m-3}, b=v_{m-2}, c=v_{m-1}, d=x, e=v_m.

The singleton packet has status pattern 1,0,1 on (a,b,c,d,e). Its residual five-set holonomy bit may take either value.

The double-full singleton theorem supplies, in either holonomy branch, a 0-monochromatic resolution compatible on the right-hand side and ending with the ordered pair

(e,d)=(v_m,x).

Thus after the five-set resolution the cyclic order has a suffix segment

...,v_m,x,v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4,...

with local statuses

alpha(v_m,x,v_1)=0,
alpha(x,v_1,v_2)=1,
alpha(v_1,v_2,v_3)=0.

The first transition tetrahedron {v_m,x,v_1,v_2} is the canonical flat wrap tetrahedron. The second {x,v_1,v_2,v_3} is the fully-curved perfect-blocker endpoint tetrahedron.

Apply the mixed flat/full surgery swapping v_2 and v_3:

...,v_m,x,v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4,...
->
...,v_m,x,v_1,v_3,v_2,v_4,...

The affected statuses begin

alpha(v_m,x,v_1)=0,
alpha(x,v_1,v_3)=0,
alpha(v_1,v_3,v_2)=1,
alpha(v_3,v_2,v_4)=1.

Here alpha(x,v_1,v_3)=0 follows from full curvature of the endpoint blocker, while the last two equalities follow by alternation from the old 0-statuses alpha(v_1,v_2,v_3)=alpha(v_2,v_3,v_4)=0.

Hence the local pattern 0,1,0 is replaced by 0,0,1,1.

Only the next outer window, beginning with v_2,v_4, can carry a newly exported defect. Therefore both values of the double-full holonomy bit enter the same outward defect-transport regime inside the p-run.

The residual five-set bit controls the choice of local singleton resolution but does not create distinct global terminal branches. After that choice, the remaining obstruction is the same-side bubble transport already governed by the lexicographic threshold-energy potential.
