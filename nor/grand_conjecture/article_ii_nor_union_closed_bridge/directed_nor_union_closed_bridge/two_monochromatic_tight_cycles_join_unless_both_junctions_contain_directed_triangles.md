# Two monochromatic tight cycles join unless both junctions contain directed triangles

Given two vertex-disjoint monochromatic ternary tight cycles, orient both with color 0 and prescribe junction vertices c,d. Cut and concatenate in the two opposite-orientation choices C+ D- and C- D+. Their words have at most one change unless their two crossing colors are respectively (1,0) and (0,1). Simultaneous failure is equivalent to directed triangles p->s->d->p at center c and c->q->t->c at center d, where p,s and q,t are the respective cycle neighbors. If either relevant centered triple is transitive, one join succeeds. Hence a locally transitive instance covered by two such cycles has a spanning one-change order. Proper support and repeated merging of more cycles remain unresolved.
