# Exact simultaneous insertion calculus for triangle orientation and defects

For a defect-free order and a new vertex x, let s_i=alpha(x,v_i,v_{i+1}). At a defect-safe interior insertion gap i, the three new orientation statuses are exactly (s_{i-1},1-s_i,s_{i+1}). Defect safety is controlled independently by which vertices are marked in the adjacent x-triangles. This isolates the simultaneous insertion problem into a sign sequence plus a local forbidden-gap pattern.
