# Corrected Hamilton chord identities for status curvature and five-set holonomy

With alpha(a,b,c)=1 xor t(a,b) xor t(b,c) xor t(c,a) and all adjacent linear-order edges forward, alpha(v_i,v_{i+1},v_{i+2})=x_i=t(v_i,v_{i+2}). For z_i=t(v_i,v_{i+3}), alpha(v_i,v_{i+1},v_{i+3})=1 xor x_{i+1} xor z_i. At a transition, full curvature is equivalent to z_i=1 and flatness to z_i=0, agreeing with §147 and correcting §139. In a double-full 010 five-set the residual bit h=alpha(0,1,4) equals t(0,4), correcting §165.
