# Maximal feasible supports plus one blocker already give four-change carriers — preserved pre-item development

## Development

Work in ternary arity. Let P be an inclusion-maximal sigma-tight path with front pair F and rear pair R, let X be its omitted set, and put tau=1-sigma. Let M be any tau-feasible support at F with a tau-tight witness Q ending at F. For any x in X minus M, concatenate x,Q with the remainder of P. Its linear word is a,tau^m,sigma^p, where a is the single new first status and may be either color. Maximality of the reversed core gives h(R,x)=tau. Closing cyclically gives variation at most four regardless of a and regardless of the second wrap status. Thus every feasible support M can be enlarged by one arbitrary omitted blocker at the level of a four-change-or-better cyclic carrier. In particular a maximum-cardinality feasible support yields a carrier containing P, all of M, and one further omitted vertex. This shifts the absorption frontier from minimal circuits toward maximal feasible layers and interfaces directly with alternating feasible-support peeling.
