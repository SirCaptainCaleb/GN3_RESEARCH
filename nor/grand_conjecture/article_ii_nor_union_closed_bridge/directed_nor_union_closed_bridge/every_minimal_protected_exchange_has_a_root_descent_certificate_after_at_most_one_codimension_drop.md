# Every minimal protected exchange has a root descent certificate after at most one codimension drop

For a first-run-minimal deletion witness in general ordered-window arity r, let B be the r-window protected replacement bridge. Associate to every adjacent 10 descent the physical root e_dropped-e_entered and sum them to R(B). The dropped coordinates lie in the left block and entering coordinates in the disjoint right block, so R(B)=0 iff B has no 10 descent. First-run minimality excludes every nontrivial monotone bridge, hence R(B)=0 iff B=0^r, the inert locus. Reversal sends B to complement(reverse(B)) and each descent root to its negative, so R is odd. If B=0^r, the exchange produces a mutual-blocker pair on a common codimension-two core; inserting both blockers in either order yields a local packet ending in 0 but containing a 1, hence a 10 descent and a nonzero codimension-two root certificate. Thus every minimal protected exchange has a nonzero root obstruction after at most one codimension drop. The remaining general task is to glue the level-1 and level-2 certificates equivariantly on the protected deletion-fiber complex.
