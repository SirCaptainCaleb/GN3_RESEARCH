## Development

General protected-descent schema suggested by the flat-sector closure. Let h be a reversal-odd binary label on ordered r-tuples, and suppose a minimum coordinate counterexample has a one-change deletion order O=(v_1,...,v_m) with word 0^p1^q and omitted coordinate x. Assume p>=r. Form the replacement deletion order O'=(v_1,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},...,v_m), which now omits v_p. Relative to O, all windows of O' are unchanged except the r windows whose starts are p-r+1,...,p; these are exactly the windows crossing the replacement coordinate x. Call them the protected bridge windows. If all r bridge windows have color 1, then O' has word exactly 0^(p-r)1^(q+r). Now form the full order H=(v_p,v_1,...,v_{p-1},x,v_{p+1},...,v_m). Apart from its first new r-window A=h(v_p,v_1,...,v_{r-1}), its status word is the word of O'. Hence A=0 would give a spanning one-change order; in a counterexample A is forced to 1. Deleting v_p then leaves the new one-change carrier O' with first-run length p-r. If the bridge-window hypothesis is available again for every deletion carrier, this yields an impossible finite descent p,p-r,p-2r,... . Thus the grand coordinate conjecture would follow from a single protected bridge lemma: in every minimum counterexample and every suitable one-change deletion carrier, the r replacement windows obtained by replacing v_p with the omitted coordinate x are all in the second phase. For r=3 in the coboundary-flat alternating sector these three bridge windows are exactly: one blocker-scan window, one full-curvature off-diagonal window, and one blocker-scan window, which is why the descent closes there. This identifies the correct target for the general switch-prism/ordered-tail/Radon program: extract phase compatibility for one r-window replacement packet while preserving the deletion order of all other coordinates. Arbitrary carrier connectivity is more than needed, and support-only compatibility is less than needed.
