## Development

Strengthen subsection 143 to a genuine product cell C=F times [k,k+1]. Suppose a bottom state (pi,k) has +b in its set-valued violation label and a top state (sigma,k+1) has -b. Consider the bottom horizontal slice and bubble the physical coordinate b from pi to sigma through vertices of F while keeping cut k fixed. At the start the b-centered window is a pre-switch violation. Examine the bottom state (sigma,k) directly below the top endpoint. If the b-centered window is also violating there, then the bottom horizontal bubble path starts and ends with a b-violation. The subsection-143 dichotomy applies: either it stays violating through the cut crossing and gives a switch-crossing repair, or it toggles on a same-side edge and gives the two-window repair packet. If instead the b-centered window is satisfied at (sigma,k), then along the bottom bubble path the predicate changed from violating to satisfied, so a same-side violation-to-satisfaction toggle edge already occurs. Thus the only remaining possibility is handled before the vertical move. The vertical edge itself never needs to be interpreted as a repair. Therefore every complementary cellular-Tucker product carrier, horizontal or prismatic, contains a genuine horizontal adjacent-transposition edge with the local repair structure of subsections 143-144. This closes the cut-level caveat in the carrier-to-legal-edge extraction.
