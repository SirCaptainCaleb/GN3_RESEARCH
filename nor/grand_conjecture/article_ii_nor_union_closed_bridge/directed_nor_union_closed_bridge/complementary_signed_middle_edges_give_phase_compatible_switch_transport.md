## A complementary signed-middle edge gives a phase-compatible switch transport in the prism

Work in ternary arity. Suppose a genuine horizontal adjacent-transposition edge at cut k carries the complementary signed-middle situation of subsection 118.

Before the swap the local order is
...,a,b,c,d,...
and the window
(a,b,c)
is the pre-switch b-centered violation at rank k. Let the pre-switch target color be eta. Hence
alpha(a,b,c)=1-eta.

After swapping b,c, the local order is
...,a,c,b,d,...
and the window
(c,b,d)
at rank k+1 is the post-switch b-centered violation. Its post-switch target is 1-eta, so
alpha(c,b,d)=eta.

Alternation gives
alpha(a,c,b)=eta.
Therefore, after the horizontal swap but before moving the cut, the two central statuses are
eta, eta:
the rank-k window is matched and the rank-(k+1) window is the unique one of the pair mismatched only because it still lies on the post-switch side.

Now take the genuine vertical prism edge moving the cut from k to k+1. This changes the target of rank k+1 from 1-eta to eta. Consequently both central windows become matched.

Thus every horizontal complementary signed-middle edge canonically extends to a two-edge path in the switch prism:
horizontal adjacent swap, then one vertical cut move,
which transports the proposed switch one rank through the physical coordinate b while leaving the two central windows compatible with the threshold phase.

Reversal gives the corresponding backward transport.

### Significance
This is stronger than interpreting the complementary edge merely as an endpoint repair. It preserves the switch phase explicitly. The cellular Tucker carrier therefore extracts an actual local move in the two-layer/switch-prism automaton:
- a physical coordinate crosses the proposed switch;
- the cut follows it by one rank;
- the central ordered-tail data are compatible after the move.

Only the two outer windows changed by the horizontal swap remain uncontrolled. They are exactly the boundary defects addressed by flat outward combing and the Radon equality certificate.

This supplies a concrete bridge from the topological carrier to ordered-tail reachability: complementary labels generate phase-compatible switch transport, while any failure to extend the move globally is pushed to the two outer boundary windows.
