## The fixed deletion fiber contains three consecutive interior one-defect states

Audit and strengthen the fixed-fiber claim. Work in a minimum coboundary-flat ternary counterexample with deletion order
[
O=(v_1,ldots,v_m),
qquad
w=0^p1^q,
qquad p,qge4,
]
and omitted perfect blocker (x) with scan
[
s=1^{p+1}0^q.
]

Insert (x) between (v_j) and (v_{j+1}), keeping the relative order of every (v_i) fixed. The three new statuses are
[
L=s_{j-1},qquad M=1-s_j,qquad R=s_{j+1},
]
with the untouched prefix (w_1,ldots,w_{j-2}) and untouched suffix (w_{j+1},ldots).

There are at least three consecutive interior gaps giving global threshold defect one.

### Gap (j=p)

The word is
[
0^{p-2},1,0,1^{q+1}.
]
Flipping the displayed singleton (0) gives a one-change threshold word. Thus (E=1).

The singleton packet is
[
1,0,1
]
on
[
(v_{p-1},v_p,x,v_{p+1},v_{p+2}),
]
bounded by (Q_{p-1}) and (Q_p), both fully curved.

### Gap (j=p+1)

The word is
[
0^{p-1},1,0,0,1^{q-1}.
]
Flipping the displayed singleton (1) gives a one-change threshold word. Thus (E=1).

More importantly, the singleton packet
[
0,1,0
]
lies on
[
(v_{p-1},v_p,v_{p+1},x,v_{p+2}).
]
Its two bounding transition tetrahedra are again
[
Q_{p-1},Q_p,
]
hence fully curved.

It is also genuinely two-sided inside the zero phase:
[
alpha(v_{p-2},v_{p-1},v_p)=w_{p-2}=0,
]
and
[
alpha(x,v_{p+2},v_{p+3})=s_{p+2}=0.
]
Therefore this is a same-deleted-order double-full singleton trap with matching zero windows on both immediate exterior sides.

### Gap (j=p+2)

The word is
[
0^p,1,1,0,1^{q-2},
]
so the displayed (0) is again a unique threshold defect. Its bounding tetrahedra (Q_{p+2},Q_{p+3}) are fully curved.

### Consequence

The fixed deletion fiber does not have a unique interior (E=1) state; subsection 185's uniqueness assertion is false.

For protected surgery the middle state (j=p+1) is preferable: it is a genuine two-sided double-full singleton trap, lies entirely in the same-deleted-order fiber, and has both immediate reconnection colors already fixed to the surrounding zero phase.

The residual five-set bit of this middle packet is
[
t=alpha(v_{p-1},v_p,v_{p+2}).
]
Thus the next protected closure target is sharply local: resolve this canonical packet while accounting for the two NEXT outer windows, not merely the immediate reconnections.
