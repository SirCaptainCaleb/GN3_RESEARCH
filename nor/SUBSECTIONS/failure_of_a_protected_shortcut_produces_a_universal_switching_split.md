## Composition

A nonclone pair without a protected shortcut induces the switching split B->z->A->x, with B dominating A. This is the structural input for connector construction. The pair-signature and ordered crossing windows remain explicit; arbitrary shore phase concatenation is not implied.

## Development

Assume a pair x,z has no fully-curved carrier of x→z. Its pair signature
d(u)=α(x,z,u)
is nonconstant in a minimum counterexample; otherwise x,z are clone-contractible.

Set
A=d^{-1}(0), B=d^{-1}(1).
The flatness identities force every cross-shore pair to behave uniformly. In a tournament representative switched so that x is a sink, the whole tournament has the strict block dominance pattern
B→z→A→x,
and every B-vertex dominates every A-vertex.

Thus absence of a protected shortcut is a global structural decomposition, not merely local flatness. The connector pair x,z contributes a universal 0→1 central junction between the two shores.
