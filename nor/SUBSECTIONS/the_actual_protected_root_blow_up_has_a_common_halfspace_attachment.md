## Development

Local attachment theorem for blowing up the actual protected-root zero locus. Work at an inert protected replacement bridge B=0^r between omitted/replaced coordinates x,y over a common codimension-two core. Let L be the r-1 core coordinates immediately to the left of the protected gap and R the r-1 core coordinates immediately to the right. The level-1 descent-root vector on any neighboring noninert bridge is a positive sum of roots e_l-e_r with l in L and r in R. Define the cut functional phi by phi=+1 on L, phi=-1 on R, and phi=0 on x,y and all other coordinates. Then phi is strictly positive on every nonzero level-1 root in this local star. Now blow up the inert state into the two consecutive pair orders (x,y) and (y,x). For order (x,y), define the secondary vector R2_xy as the sum of all descent roots e_a-e_c over 10 descents in its pair-insertion packet. Counterexamplehood guarantees at least one such descent. For every internal packet slide, a lies in L and c lies in R; for the final slide, the dropped coordinate may be x while the entering coordinate still lies in R. Hence every summand has phi>0, so R2_xy is nonzero. The identical argument gives phi(R2_yx)>0, with y replacing x at the possible final slide. Consequently R2_xy and R2_yx cannot be negative scalar multiples, and the straight segment between them avoids the origin. More strongly, every level-1 boundary vector and both secondary endpoint vectors in the blown-up local star lie in the common open halfspace phi>0. Therefore any affine triangulation of this local blown-up star whose vertices carry those actual protected root vectors extends without a zero. Reversal sends the entire construction to the distinct antipodal star and negates all roots; by the free-cell pairing lemma the modification can be mirrored there. This resolves the local attachment problem around a single inert protected state for the true §197 window-slide root map. The remaining global issue is only whether neighborhoods of distinct inert states can be chosen/disentangled so that these local blow-ups patch to one regular equivariant complex; no local Radon zero is forced at an inert state.
