## Composition

The general coordinate reduction is still endpoint-defect plus protected replacement: minimum counterexamples admit one-change deletion witnesses, and minimum first phase forces admissible replacement packets to be inert or contain a 10 descent, with short phases still requiring arity-specific handling.

That six-set is an exact one-Coxeter-wall phase toggle with fixed new attachment pairs and two opposite one-change deletion shadows, but it does not preserve the old carrier boundary pairs automatically.

On the physical-root side, same-cut families, adjacent chambers, and ternary Coxeter blocks of size at most three are cooriented. Independently, for even n the gap-weighted 10/01 root fields force a genuine free-antipodal Coxeter-wall degeneracy.

The closure target is therefore no longer an A2 recurrence. It is extraction at the first noncooriented A3/cut-changing carrier, together with reconnection of the special holonomy-toggle boundary gadget where that provenance is available.
