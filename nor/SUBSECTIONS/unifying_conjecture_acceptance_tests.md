## Unifying-conjecture acceptance tests

A future common conjecture should use one canonical class of cubical objects carrying both support structure and ordered traces. It should recover Frankl from coordinate-frequency conclusions and directed NOR from one-change trace conclusions.

Do not promote such a conjecture until five checks hold:

1. every union-closed family canonically gives an instance;
2. every directed NOR coloring canonically gives an instance;
3. a hypothetical obstruction to Frankl violates the common conclusion;
4. a hypothetical obstruction to directed NOR violates the common conclusion;
5. standard nuisance examples outside the intended models do not immediately violate it.

The leading structural candidate is square confluence. In a union-closed family, membership of the lower three vertices of a Boolean square forces the top. In NOR feasible-support systems, a missing top can occur only because the two side witnesses require incompatible orders.

A useful common axiom should encode this as follows: two legal coordinate extensions either commute to a common upper support or produce a controlled signed defect.

The research task is to determine whether antipodality plus controlled square defects forces a conclusion with two simultaneous shadows:
- a coordinate-frequency imbalance strong enough to yield Frankl;
- a low-variation antipodal trace strong enough to yield directed NOR.

This is a research target, not yet a promoted conjecture.
