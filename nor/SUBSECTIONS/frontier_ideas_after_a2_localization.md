## Frontier ideas after A2 localization

These are research directions, not established theorems.

1. Mixed braid-switch cell. Since an antipodal A2 pair can occur only across the cut wall of the same physical ternary window, analyze the local A2 x I prism cell explicitly. Reversal of the window and crossing the switch layer both complement the relevant sign. There may be a direct local deformation showing the pair cancellation is removable unless a directed-root triangle is also present.

2. Triangle branch identification. A support-minimal A2 zero with three roots is a directed circulation on the tied coordinates. Try to identify the three selected violating windows with one of the already known centered directed-triangle / three-element front-circuit packets. If the identification is exact, invoke endpoint repair or pair insertion rather than new topology.

3. Flat-sector mobile-switch picture. In the coboundary-flat pure-orientation sector, every non-full transition tetrahedron is flat and admits both endpoint repairs. Minimum-variation four-change carriers therefore consist of pinned fully-curved switches and bidirectionally repairable flat switches. The canonical (1,p,q,2) carrier has at least one pinned fully-curved switch at the entrance to the length-two run. Use five-set sparsity of fully-curved tetrahedra to seek a neighboring flat switch that can be transported into the singleton trap, forcing variation two.

4. Corrected size-three rotation. For a whole omitted pure three-circuit, only one-step singleton propagation is currently proved. If the cyclic rear wrap has path color sigma, an edge flip at the next pivot is impossible, so the whole pair cycle shifts one step and the core can be cyclically rotated without losing coordinates. Iterate until the first opposite-color wrap. The front and rear pair cycles are independently pinned and equal, so the stopping point should be attacked as an interpolation/collision problem. Do not use the invalid full-tube propagation.

5. Codimension-two pair extension. Two omitted vertices cannot both be perfect blockers of the same one-change carrier; consecutive pair insertion cancels two identical blocker scans. In a minimum counterexample, any codimension-two carrier therefore has an asymmetric blocker pair. Classify that asymmetric residue using the cross-bit transport identity c_i xor c_{i+1}=s_i^x xor s_i^y. This may yield a two-vertex induction that bypasses one-vertex insertion failures.

6. Switch-prism minimal-zero program. Borsuk-Ulam localizes any obstruction to a Coxeter block of size at least three. On a minimal A2 carrier, root cancellation is only an antipodal pair or directed triangle. The current best topological closure program is therefore finite/local: eliminate the mixed antipodal prism cell, then match the directed triangle branch to known surgery obstructions.
