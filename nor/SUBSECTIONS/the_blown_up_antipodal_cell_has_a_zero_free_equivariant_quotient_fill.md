## Development

Local zero-free fill of the blown-up antipodal cell. Let an inert exact-backtrack cell have normal root n=e_x-e_y. Replace its zero state by the proposed pair-order interval and pass to the quotient Q=W/<n>. In the residual caged case the two actual protected boundary exits carry nonzero quotient roots u=[e_q-e_a] and v=[e_d-e_f]. By the coorientation theorem there is a linear functional phi on Q with phi(u)>0 and phi(v)>0. Therefore every convex combination (1-t)u+t v, 0<=t<=1, also satisfies phi>0 and is nonzero. Hence the quotient root map extends affinely across the inserted interval without creating a zero. Under reversal, n is sent to -n, the quotient is canonically identified, and both exit roots are negated; use the negated affine segment on the antipodal interval. The two fills are equivariant. Thus the antipodal protected-root two-cycle is not a local obstruction after codimension-one blow-up: its normal zero can be replaced by a zero-free quotient interval whose endpoints are the two genuine boundary transports. This proves the base local extension step of the blow-up strategy. It does not yet prove that blow-ups for overlapping inert exchanges attach to a globally well-defined cell complex; that compatibility remains the topological frontier.
