## Composition

At extremal total cut defect Delta=n, write each reduced cut B_i as the p-1 coordinates whose membership intervals cover state i. Constant row size forces the interval-start multiplicities s_i to satisfy s_i=s_{i-(p-1)}, and Delta_i=s_i. If every step has positive defect, then sum s_i=n forces s_i=1 for all i. The secondary defect exchange is then conjugate to the cyclic shift by p-1 and decomposes into gcd(n,p-1) directed cycles. Hence a support-irreducible equality case must have gcd(n,p-1)=1 and a second Hamiltonian defect cycle; otherwise there is a zero-defect step or a proper smaller defect circulation.

## Development

Assume the Hamiltonian regular-cut obstruction of the two-moment perturbation and the extremal total defect Delta=n. Write C_i=B_i union {v_{i+1}} as in the defect-variation theorem. Then every coordinate x belongs to B_i for one cyclic interval I_x of exactly L=p-1 consecutive indices.

Let s_i be the number of intervals I_x whose first index is i. Because every B_i has cardinality L, the coverage count c_i=#{x:i in I_x} is constantly L. Comparing consecutive coverage counts gives
c_i-c_{i-1}=s_i-s_{i-L}=0,
so
s_i=s_{i-L}
for every i.

Hence the start multiplicity sequence is constant on the residue cycles generated by adding L modulo n.

Moreover Delta_i=d_J(B_{i-1},B_i)=s_i: at step i exactly the intervals ending at i-1 leave and the intervals starting at i enter, and the two coordinate sets are disjoint because 0<L<n.

Therefore, if every physical step has positive cut defect, then every s_i>=1. Since sum_i s_i=n, all s_i=1. In that case there is exactly one entering and one leaving coordinate at every step. Label by y_i the unique coordinate whose interval starts at i. The secondary defect exchange at step i sends y_{i-L} to y_i. Consequently the secondary defect cycle cover is conjugate to the permutation i -> i+L on Z/nZ, and has exactly gcd(n,L)=gcd(n,p-1) directed cycles.

Thus an extremal Delta=n obstruction has one of three immediate reductions:
1. some s_i=0, equivalently a zero-defect physical step;
2. gcd(n,p-1)>1, in which case the secondary defect circulation has a proper directed cycle on strictly fewer than n coordinates;
3. every step has defect one, gcd(n,p-1)=1, and the secondary defect circulation is itself Hamiltonian.

Hence any support-irreducible minimum-defect Hamiltonian regular design must satisfy gcd(n,p-1)=1 and consists of two Hamiltonian cycles: the physical protected-root cycle and a secondary defect cycle conjugate to the shift by p-1 in the cyclic order of interval starts.

This is a structural reduction, not yet a surgery theorem: a zero-defect step still needs conversion to a legal protected transition, and a proper secondary defect cycle still needs provenance before it can be fed back into the protected carrier induction.
