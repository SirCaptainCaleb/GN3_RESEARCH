## Composition

A directed shore triangle gives the compatible zero seed (u,x,z,v,w) and its complementary reversal. Elevation removes the triangle requirement: any three shore vertices support this seed after orienting its last pair, and smaller general bases are available. Twin A3 information remains a separate structural benefit.

## Development

For the directed triangle u→v→w→u in A, consider
C_0=(u,x,z,v,w).

Its three ternary windows are
α(u,x,z)=α(x,z,u)=0,
α(x,z,v)=0,
α(z,v,w)=0.
Hence C_0 has word 000. Its reversal
C_1=(w,v,z,x,u)
has word 111.

This is stronger than an abstract root dependence: every minimum unresolved split contains an actual five-coordinate monochromatic block containing both connector vertices x,z.

The Hartman state can therefore be based on the repair component of this concrete block, with boundary targets recording which exterior shore states can be absorbed while retaining monochromaticity.
