## Development

The proposed sufficient terminal theorem for the coboundary-flat sector is false: not every tournament has a directed Hamilton path whose distance-two shortcut directions change at most once. Take two vertex-disjoint directed 3-cycles A and B and orient every cross edge from A to B. In the tournament condensation A precedes B, so every directed Hamilton path must list all three vertices of A before all three vertices of B. The unique shortcut type inside each 3-cycle is backward, while both distance-two shortcuts crossing from A into B are forward. Thus every directed Hamilton path has shortcut word B,F,F,B, with two changes. This does not refute NOR in the coboundary-flat sector, because a NOR-good coordinate order need not be a directed Hamilton path of the representing tournament. It refutes only the proposed directed-Hamilton-path terminal route and reinforces the need to use the full formula alpha_i=e_i xor e_{i+1} xor r_i for general orders.
