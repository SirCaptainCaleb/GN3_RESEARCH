## Composition

Minimum-shore internal in/out-neighbors supply a directed triangle and twin A3 protected-root carriers with x and z. They are useful root data. Monochromatic seed existence elevates to every small shore support without the triangle assumption.

## Development

Every vertex of the minimum shore has both an in-neighbor and an out-neighbor in the induced switched tournament, so A contains a directed triangle
u→v→w→u.

Together with the connector vertex x, the four-set {x,u,v,w} carries a positive protected-root 3-cycle in one A3 permutahedral cell:
u→v, v→w, w→u.
The same ternary face table is obtained with z in place of x, because α(x,z,a)=0 for every a∈A implies
α(x,a,b)=α(z,a,b)
for all a,b∈A.

Thus the minimum-shore obstruction contains two twin A3 carrier faces over the same directed shore triangle.
