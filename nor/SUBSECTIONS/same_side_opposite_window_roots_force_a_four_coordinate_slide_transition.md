## Same-side opposite window roots force a four-coordinate slide transition

Work in the coboundary-flat alternating ternary sector.

Suppose two honest violating-window labels on the same switch side have opposite physical roots:
(u,m,v,s) and (v,m',u,s).

Both windows violate the same target color, so their actual colors are equal. Write
alpha(u,m,v)=c,
alpha(v,m',u)=c.

The middles are distinct: if m=m', reversal of the ordered triple would complement its color.

By cyclic invariance,
alpha(u,v,m')=c.
By alternation,
alpha(u,v,m)=1-c.

Flat four-face parity on {u,v,m,m'} gives
alpha(u,m,m') xor alpha(v,m,m')=1.

Now consider the order
(u,m,m',v).
Its consecutive ternary statuses are
x=alpha(u,m,m'),
y=alpha(m,m',v)=alpha(v,m,m')
by cyclic invariance.
Hence x!=y, so this order carries an actual adjacent-window transition.

Its slide root is exactly
e_u-e_v,
the same oriented physical direction as the first honest window root.

The two off-faces of this transition are
alpha(u,m,v)=c
and
alpha(u,m',v)=1-c.

Thus there are exactly two cases:
- if (x,y)=(c,1-c), the off-faces match the flat transition pattern, so both endpoint repairs are available;
- if (x,y)=(1-c,c), the off-faces are reversed, so the tetrahedron is fully curved and emits the protected slide root e_u-e_v.

Therefore every same-side opposite pair of honest ternary WINDOW roots exposes a concrete Article III SLIDE transition with the same physical direction, either flat-repairable or fully curved.

In particular, any nonzero-side directed 2-cycle lobe in the final lifted figure-eight residue cannot remain algebraically inert: it hands off immediately to the audited flat-transport/full-barrier architecture.
