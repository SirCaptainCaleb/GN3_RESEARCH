## Development

For a ternary deletion carrier on n full coordinates, the deletion order has n-3 ternary windows, so its normalized one-change profile satisfies p+q=n-3 and p<=q. Hence p< n/2. The cut-moment perturbation theorem shows that a primitive side-balanced protected A3 two-cycle or four-cycle can satisfy the perturbed zero equations only when p=n/2. That exceptional value is therefore impossible for every normalized ternary deletion state, in particular for globally minimum first-phase witnesses. Consequently the dimension-preserving cut-moment perturbation removes all primitive side-balanced A3 two- and four-cycles in the actual Article III state space. The only support-minimal side-lifted A3 zero type not excluded by this arithmetic corollary is the union of two oppositely side-imbalanced physical cycles.
