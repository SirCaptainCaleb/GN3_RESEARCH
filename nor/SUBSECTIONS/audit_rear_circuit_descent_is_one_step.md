## Development

The rear-circuit theorem proves existence of a smaller rear circuit or a bipolar circuit, but not automatic iteration. If the rear circuit D is a proper subset of the omitted set X, it is not yet proved that D is the entire omitted set of another maximal tight path. Thus repeated descent requires an additional recentering lemma. The rear blocking and rear circuit existence remain valid.
