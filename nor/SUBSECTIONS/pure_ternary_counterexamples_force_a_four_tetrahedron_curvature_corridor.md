## Development

For a one-change deletion carrier O with word 0^p1^q and omitted perfect blocker x, let kappa_i be the tetrahedral coboundary on {x,v_i,v_{i+1},v_{i+2}}. The protected left descent forces kappa_{p-1}=1, since kappa_{p-1}=0 would give the strict deletion-carrier descent. Reversing O gives another deletion carrier with word 0^q1^p, so the same argument forces the symmetric curvature bit, which translates back to kappa_{p+2}=1. Hence every surviving pure-orientation ternary counterexample has a four-tetrahedron corridor Q_{p-1},Q_p,Q_{p+1},Q_{p+2} with the two outer curvature bits pinned to 1. The next local task is to analyze the middle pair and the shared five-set coboundary relations.
