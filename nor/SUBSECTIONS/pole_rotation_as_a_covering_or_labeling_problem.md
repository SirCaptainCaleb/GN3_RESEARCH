## Composition


Directed NOR has no distinguished-pole obstruction. Global symmetric-difference translation by a fixed \(S\) is a cube automorphism commuting with complementation, so it carries antipodal geodesics and ordered tuple colorings to translated copies while preserving the antipodal-reversal axiom. XOR is therefore legitimate as a global change of coordinates when the whole coloring is transported.

The earlier obstruction concerned window-dependent local corrections while holding one fixed coloring; those can alter a geodesic's color word. That issue does not arise in the directed NOR formulation.

Freudenthal charts based at different poles are symmetry-related coordinate charts. Tucker, Ky Fan, KKM, or related arguments may still exploit the family of pole-indexed charts and their incidences, but not as a repair for a pole problem.


## Development

Directed NOR has no distinguished-pole obstruction. Global symmetric-difference translation by a fixed S is a cube automorphism, commutes with cube complementation, carries antipodal geodesics to antipodal geodesics, and transports ordered tuple colorings to ordered tuple colorings while preserving the antipodal-reversal axiom. Thus XOR is legitimate as a global change of coordinates when the whole coloring is transported. The earlier obstruction concerned a different operation: applying different local XOR corrections to different windows while keeping one fixed coloring, which can change the color word along a geodesic. Freudenthal charts based at different poles should therefore be viewed as symmetry-related coordinate charts. Tucker, Ky Fan, KKM, or related arguments may exploit the family of pole-indexed charts or their incidences, but not as a repair for a pole problem.
