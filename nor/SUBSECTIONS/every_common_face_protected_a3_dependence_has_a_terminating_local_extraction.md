## Development

Let a positive protected-root dependence be localized to one common ternary A3 Coxeter face with fixed outside order and normalized first-phase rank. Its physical roots form a positive circulation, so the support contains a directed simple cycle of length two, three, or four. The cut trace size in the four-block is fixed across the face. If it is one, directed cycles of length three or four are impossible by the face-bit incompatibility theorem, while the length-two reversal pair has a boundary-safe full-support splice. If it is two, a length-four cycle is impossible, a length-three cycle forces an exterior double-full 010 packet and hence resolves by one-sided monotone surgery to closure or protected-root handoff, and a length-two cycle either closes, shortens the deletion first phase, or emits a protected insertion root with an endpoint outside the A3 block. Therefore no positive protected-root dependence confined to a common A3 face is terminal. It yields a spanning one-change order, a strictly improved deletion witness, or a new protected root leaving the four-coordinate block. This completes local extraction on A3; any remaining global obstruction must propagate to a larger coordinate block or fail common-face/protected provenance.
