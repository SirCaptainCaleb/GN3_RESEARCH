## Development

The horizontal boundary step of section 215 is unproved. Concatenating one-change orders on proper face blocks does not force an actual 10 transition to cross blocks: different blocks may each contain their allowed internal 10 change while block boundaries match. This is the same orientation gap already audited for earlier actual-root face carriers. The witnessed-state provenance idea survives, but strict horizontal face separation needs the orientation-symmetric consecutive-change or pair-defect carrier, or a new actual-window crossing theorem.
