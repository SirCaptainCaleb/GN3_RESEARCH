## Composition

# Reassessment and guidance through revision 2878

NOR remains open. Article III has accumulated strong local surgery and several useful topological constructions. The main closure obligation is a relative witness-exchange theorem: connect two certified orders while retaining their outside windows, target data, and the class used for extremality.

## Assessment

The recent work improves three interfaces.

First, the proper-face degree construction now has orientation-symmetric labels. For change ranks p_1<...<p_k in pi=(v_1,...,v_n), use
C(pi)=sum_{j=1}^{k-1}(e_{v_{p_j}}-e_{v_{p_{j+1}+3}}).
Every term witnesses one isolated monochromatic band. The order-rank functional proves C(pi)=0 exactly when pi has at most one change. This definition works for general reversal-odd ternary labels.

Choose a NOR-good order g(S) for each proper nonempty subset. A proper ordered-partition face F=B_1|...|B_s has the concrete witness pi_F=g(B_1)...g(B_s). Every consecutive-change term crosses F-blocks: two changes whose entire supporting interval lies in one block would contradict that block's good order. On a face flag, the largest-face block-rank functional weakly separates every label and strictly separates its own label. This gives the zero-free boundary carrier and nonzero face-normal degree of §§154-156,170. The fixed selector supports ordinary degree. The face-normal proof applies to independently fixed choices.

Second, canonical face witnesses differ inside one merged proper block with the outside order fixed (§163). Abel summation decomposes a flag-zero equation into label increments (§165). This is an algebraic decomposition; each increment still needs an actual legal replacement. Disjoint merge chains agree as coordinate orders, and three-block merge paths agree at their endpoint (§167). These equalities supply coherence data. Monotone repair along the paths and relative boundary safety require additional theorems. A merge ending in the whole coordinate set is the top-face extraction obligation and must be handled explicitly.

Third, the local endpoint and barrier routes have much sharper reconnection data. Whole-phase reversal confines an arbitrary reversal-odd endpoint defect to two right crossing windows (§213); the minimum-phase version is §174. In the rigid flat alternating width-two corridor, §226 supplies the stronger weave (0,3,1,2,4,5), preserving the first coordinate and the last ordered pair. It leaves ONE left reconnection bit. Its bad value produces a double-full singleton. This improves the earlier two-window weave of §223 and the common-kernel assessment §235. The next result must control the exported reconnection and its terminal fully-curved stop.

The residual A2 scan analyses in ternary §§65-66 cover both rises and no-rise scans by explicit close-or-root exits. Use these local exits with their exact packet and boundary hypotheses. They deliver a root handoff when closure fails; global extraction from that handoff remains an obligation.

## Primary route: witnessed degree plus relative block splicing

Use the fixed-selector consecutive-change carrier as the principal degree construction. Its labels retain an explicit band witness, and its proper-face orientation proof permits either direction of a good block word.

Choose a first unresolved block replacement along the actual supporting flag. Compare
P g(A) g(B) Q
with
P g(A union B) Q
for a proper merged block. Record the two crossing windows on each side, the full ordered collars, target polarity, and selected band. Apply the existing local repair theorems only where their stated hypotheses hold.

Seek a relative splice theorem returning one of:
1. a spanning NOR-good order;
2. a strict improvement within the declared protected-witness class;
3. an actual compatible replacement with a proved well-founded potential.

If the selected proper-block order changes, account for every affected canonical face witness in the potential. If the merged block is the full set, extract directly from the top-face witness and its closing relation.

The central creative task is to use proper-subinstance good orders as boundary-conditioned alternatives. Establish the boundary-conditioned theorem required by the supporting flag. This is stronger than existence of a good order inside the block and directly connects the induction to the degree obstruction.

## Immediate local task: finish the exported reconnection

Start from the exact §226 one-bit weave, or the §213 two-window endpoint reversal, with its original extremal witness attached.

Classify each crossing-window value together with the parity, blocker, and outside-order relations inherited from that source. For a bad reconnection, follow the proved one-sided repair to its first full barrier or its first interaction with another certified path. Prove a spanning order or a strict admissible witness improvement there.

A useful common interface theorem should state the incoming and preserved ordered boundary data, all changed ternary windows, and its progress measure. Carry the flat alternating hypotheses explicitly when using tetrahedral parity or cyclic invariance. §213's compression itself uses reversal-oddness alone; its repair requires a theorem in the same stated sector.

This task supersedes further classification of internal width-two reflections. The stronger weave already reduces their unresolved content to the exported boundary.

## Compatible central-cut route

Roots §237 prove a strong and elementary localization: if certified central cuts form an inclusion chain, an increasing difference-block potential strictly separates every root. Thus any positive dependence among those protected roots uses incomparable cuts.

A fully-curved packet supplies four actual roots from its independent endpoint-pair swaps, forming the same-cut K_2,2 parallelogram (§230). Its convex hull has cut-normal pairing one and is zero-free (§234). Flat repairs supply the boundary-separated square of §§109-110.

Use these cells to investigate the FIRST compatible passage between incomparable cut states. Prove that the relevant side roots and replacement orders remain admissible in the chosen carrier. The existence of a common-cut square supplies local interpolation; attainment of its successor cuts requires a witness theorem.

A bounded square/braid description of the first incomparable-cut event remains a conjectural reduction. Prove its localization with full boundary data before applying low-rank extraction.

## Focused alternative: endpoint corner lifting

For actual endpoint cuts {x_i,a_i} and {x_{i+1},a_{i+1}}, target a witness for the SAME root x_i->x_{i+1} with cut {x_i,a_{i+1}}, or a spanning order or strict witness improvement.

This operation copies a_{i+1} backward. Global alignment requires a finite potential and a treatment of pinned junctions. Root §128 supplies a genuine endpoint three-cycle when its second splice bit is zero and closes the phase-two first stop; at a phase-three pinned stop it gives a shorter cycle or first-phase descent. Longer stopped branches now also have the whole-phase reversal interface.

When shortening cycles, retain the exact family over which minimum length was chosen (§§129,232). A minimum among all admissible endpoint cycles supports an endpoint shortcut. A minimum among Hamiltonian cycles has a different comparison class. Every emitted replacement must satisfy the declared class conditions.

## Topological obligations

Use the switch prism as a secondary extraction framework until its current gaps are repaired.

The centered odd prism map has an automatic center zero (§138). A useful theorem must extract that zero or force an event on a domain whose obstruction excludes concentration at the center.

The Hamiltonian physical-root simplex has nonzero local degree (§184). A chord substitution can transfer the zero; relative-boundary removal requires accounting for the preserved degree.

Averaged subdivision labels may contain several genuine state labels (§212). The size and rank of an expanded circuit determine its label geometry. Domain-cell dimension and vertical-edge incidence require a separate realization theorem. The vertical-edge complementary-middle pair of §209 is a valid cell-level statement; a selected-label carrier must retain the specified pair and prove its selection and degree properties.

Declare the domain, target dimension, boundary condition, and actual state support before using a fixed-point theorem. State the extraction that converts its forced event into a legal move.

## Allocation and completion standard

Allocate the main effort to the relative block-splice theorem and exported reconnection. Use central-cut squares as constructive tools in those states. Keep endpoint corner lifting as a focused alternative. Reserve further lifted-circuit classification for a proved carrier-realization or extraction step.

The special perfect-blocker descent belongs to its stated scan class (§§106,118,233). Reapplication requires verification that the new witness remains in that class. Use arbitrary scans for the general flat-sector frontier.

The general ternary target and the flat alternating sector have separate closure obligations. Track every extra symmetry hypothesis in the theorem statement.

Persist an explicit order or a proved terminating improvement as the main progress result. Preserve exact repair scope, full window verification, the comparison class, and selected composition dependencies. Current article summaries should carry the latest orientation, center, averaging, and cycle-class audits.

The next desired output is a relative witness-exchange theorem connecting a forced degree relation to a spanning NOR-good order or a well-founded admissible descent.
