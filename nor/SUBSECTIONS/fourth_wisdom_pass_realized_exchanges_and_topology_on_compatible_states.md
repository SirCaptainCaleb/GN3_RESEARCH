# Fourth wisdom pass — updated after the transport-flag and switch-prism developments

Assessment through live revision 2718: Article III composition 12, ternary composition 9, root composition 15, and the relevant new developments through root §105. This supersedes the earlier allocation of work in this subsection. NOR remains open. Keep the general reversal-odd coordinate problem separate from the alternating coboundary-flat ternary sector; coordinate arity three corresponds to translation-invariant N4.

## What changed

One-sided audited flat transport now has more than a finite rank bound. Its central cuts form a strict nested chain, and its roots project to the consecutive edges of a path on ordered-partition blocks (root §§82,104). The path roots are independent, so the original roots are independent. Distance-three transports contribute defects in pairwise disjoint blocks.

Thus a positive dependence of these physical slide roots cannot live within one such trajectory. Any relevant dependence must combine distinct trajectories, branching/rejoining states, or additional root species. This shifts the main constructive question toward the places where transport paths meet. Independence does not itself provide compatible fillings of those places.

The endpoint route has a new exact handoff (root §102). A canonical endpoint carrier with p,q>=3 AND its displayed perfect-blocker scan reaches a 101 double-full singleton by one adjacent cyclic swap. Both bounding tetrahedra are fully curved without an assumption on the old corner. This removes that local hypothesis; it does not show that every arbitrary endpoint witness has the displayed scan. One resolution returns toward the old state, while the other exports risk to one side. Closing or improving the exported state remains the target.

The honest switch-prism construction (root §88) uses the interval dimension for the genuine side coordinate in W plus R. Its degree argument supplies a side-balanced dependence of violating-WINDOW first-minus-last roots, conditional on the described compatible continuous extension. This is an existing topological route to develop and extract, rather than a reason to begin another raw-root zero construction. These labels differ from the dropped-entering slide roots of 10 transitions.

A2 extraction supplies complementary-middle events. The A3 calculations provide internal chambers and conditional cycle restrictions, but the assertion that every A3 zero is extracted remains blocked by root §103: changing the middle coordinate can leave the ORIGINAL middle defective in an exterior window. Root §101 produces an internal switch-compatible chamber in its balanced-four-cycle case; the full boundary packet still needs verification.

## Revised priority: a legal improvement where paths meet

Choose actual endpoint or canonical-switch witnesses. Follow only the proved one-sided transports, recording the full coordinate order, target cut and polarity, current blocker constraints, central barrier cut, both ordered boundary pairs, and all affected windows. At a meeting, braid, commuting square, or fully-curved stop, seek one of:
- a full-support one-change order;
- a genuine improved protected witness in the same declared extremal class;
- a new compatible state with a strict common potential.

The most useful next theorem is a witness-preserving move in one explicit class. A root edge or an internal chamber without its boundary splice is still an intermediate result.

Two concrete entry points deserve priority.

First, use the non-returning resolution of the canonical endpoint double-full gadget. Determine exactly which data survive its exported risk, then prove a boundary-safe escape at its first fully-curved stop or at a meeting with another actual transport path. A theorem here can exploit the displayed blocker scan, rather than the four unconstrained reconnection bits.

Second, use the rank-two endpoint cycle of root §84. Its cuts C_i={x_i,a_i}, roots rho_i=e_(x_i)-e_(x_(i+1)), and defects D_i=e_(a_(i+1))-e_(a_i) give a small, concrete witness-realization problem. Root §93 identifies a missing corner of a Johnson square. Prove that corner is realized by a suitable endpoint witness, or obtain an actual strict improvement instead.

A square-lift lemma alone does not yet prove global partner alignment. The partner walk can be a nontrivial directed cycle with no pair of inverse consecutive jumps; telescoping sum D_i=0 is weaker than cancellation by inverse pairs. Give a finite potential or a legal triangular/square reduction before concluding constant partners. Likewise root §87's support reduction of a closed defect interval need not preserve a closed physical cycle: its physical sum is e_(x_i)-e_(x_j). Root §89's small-cycle result is conditional on an already synchronized injective partner cycle, not a repair of that gap.

## Make topology extract an event in the usable state class

The degree obstruction of the honest prism is useful because it balances a genuine side coordinate. The next missing step is simplex/state compatibility and extraction into a move usable by the global extremal argument. Do not equate a convex combination of labels from a product cell with common deletion-witness provenance unless that relation is proved.

For Tucker, track the SAME middle coordinate along the entire path. At a block boundary control its exterior centered window explicitly. One promising repair to the A3 audit is a boundary handoff lemma that either satisfies that fixed middle, moves it to a global endpoint, or returns a certified external defect to the existing transport class. An alternate satisfied middle alone does not license this handoff.

The fixed-cut quadratic potential (E,-Q_k) is useful for the stated outward equality-bubble class. The proposed neighbor extension in root §92 needs its full adjacent-transposition packet and side orientation checked: a generic event may move a defect toward the cut or change additional exterior windows. Root §§81,94 give a local event description; a global decrease of the FULL energy requires all changed windows. Endpoint and cut-changing events remain distinct. A path leaving an extremal class cannot contradict extremality within that class.

If topology is restricted to an energy/band subcomplex, prove that the subcomplex retains the required equivariant obstruction. Contractible individual transport flags and their embeddings do not by themselves provide it. The creative topological question is whether compatible gluing of the zero-free flags forces a witness-preserving repair or a nontrivial obstruction on the remaining state complex.

## Do not overread the signed-distance lift

Root §100's all-descent labels have physical component independent of the switch k. Under the natural product-affine extension they take the form
F(x,k)=(G(x),a(x)-k),
where G is the all-descent root average and a is the average transition rank.

Lemma. If a(x) lies in the allowed switch interval I, projection to x identifies the zeros of F with the zeros of G, by the graph k=a(x).

Proof. F(x,k)=0 is equivalent to G(x)=0 and k=a(x). Average transition ranks lie in the convex hull of the allowed ranks, so the required k belongs to I for the standard interval containing all those ranks.

Consequently the distance equation may only choose the switch parameter; it need not add a restriction to the physical root zero. To obtain stronger extraction, tie k to independent witness or extremal data and prove the resulting domain still carries the obstruction, or prove a useful relation at the chosen k. The at-least-six-block localization comes from the all-refinements physical carrier's existing face exclusion, not a new closure mechanism. Do not turn it into another sequence of larger-block classification targets without an improvement rule.

The earlier cube observation remains available: an odd faithful map from the full n-cube boundary to W, dim W=n-1, has a zero in every parity by Borsuk–Ulam. Its unproved step is faithful coverage by realized states. The honest prism and the cube serve different labels and domains; neither zero-forcing statement alone supplies witness extraction.

## Safeguards that still matter

Central cuts identify actual geometric exchange directions but do not prove successor attainment. At a Johnson vertex, convex tangent feasibility of that edge is already equivalent to discrete attainment. Raw separation over a full reversal-closed family is vacuous because rho and -rho occur together. An odd lift also cancels a genuine reversal pair; its value is the additional relation it imposes inside a compatible support.

The reversal-even tensor records cut defects after a physical zero, but its universal trace-one identity does not force their removal. Physical simple-cycle extraction does not preserve temporal cut chronology automatically. Small perturbations in W cannot remove Hamiltonian physical circuits, and irrational parameters do not separate arbitrary real zero weights.

Do not reopen the universal four-bit reconnection classification: ternary §39 shows those local hypotheses permit all sixteen patterns. Do not reuse the refuted monotone-scan p=2 shortcut. State the exact scan hypotheses of a canonical endpoint theorem. Keep normalized minimum-FIRST cuts, transported central cuts, window roots, slide roots, and partner-defect vectors distinct.

The current article has absorbed several earlier qualifications, especially the A3 middle audit. Its fixed-cut interior termination language should still be tied to the verified full-packet repair classes. Future compositions should cite the selected lower compositions explicitly; frontier coverage and “not stale” do not certify mathematical validity.

## What counts as the next decisive result

Prioritize a boundary-safe escape from the endpoint gadget, a witness-supported Johnson square or triangle with proved finite reduction, or a fixed-middle carrier extraction that stays inside a usable extremal class. Each should return an actual one-change order or a strict improvement. Use the topology and cut language to obtain that output. Further localization, raw cancellation, or another equivalent formulation should be reported with its remaining realization obligation.
