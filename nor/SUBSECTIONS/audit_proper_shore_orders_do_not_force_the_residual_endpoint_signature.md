## Composition

Endpoint insertion constructions give good orders on the proper subinstance U=A∪{x,z}. They do not contradict full counterexamplehood when B is nonempty. Universal ambient endpoint signature (0,1), derived 00/11 collars, and rail exclusions require a separate extension theorem. Conditional endpoint-bit lemmas remain valid.

## Development

In the full switching split V=B union {z} union A union {x}, an order on U=A union {x,z} is a proper-subinstance order whenever B is nonempty. Therefore producing a NOR-good order on U does not contradict full-instance counterexamplehood. The endpoint constructions that prepend or append x,z to a good A-order are valid local U-witnesses, but they do not force e_1=0 or e_{k-1}=1 in the global problem without a separate extension theorem across B. Consequently the claimed universal endpoint signature (0,1), and the derived forced two-edge collars e_1=e_2=0 and e_{k-2}=e_{k-1}=1, must not be used on the full switching-split frontier. Conditional lemmas assuming particular endpoint bits remain valid.
