## Freudenthal charts, translation symmetry, and zero loci

Directed NOR has no preferred pole. Every antipodal pole pair gives a Freudenthal chart, and global symmetric-difference translation carries one chart and its coloring to another while preserving the NOR symmetry. These charts are therefore equivalent coordinate views, not repairs for a defect.

Their collective symmetry may still be useful: Tucker, Ky Fan, KKM, or related arguments could exploit incidences among pole-indexed chamber families. Separately, low-rank reversal-odd defect statistics admit cellular extensions; Bourgin--Yang then supplies positive-dimensional zero loci, while type-\(A\) root balance converts balanced carriers into directed recurrence. The remaining challenge is to convert this symmetry or recurrence into a genuine one-change geodesic.
