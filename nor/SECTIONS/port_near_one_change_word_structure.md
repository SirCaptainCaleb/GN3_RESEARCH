## Near-one-change word structure and odd defect maps

NOR asks for a genuine one-change word. Article VII supplies a weaker width-one inversion relaxation with bounded witnesses \(001,011,0101\), useful as a local test but not equivalent to the NOR target.

A second recovered construction is more directly topological: when violations can be paired across a chosen center, the coordinates
\[
F_d=x_d-y_d+g\,x_dy_d
\]
form an odd defect vector whose chamber zeros are precisely the centered one-change chambers. This gives a model for constructing odd maps whose chamber-level zero set matches the desired word condition.
