# Ordered-tuple Norine geodesics

## Composition


## Ordered-tuple NOR

Throughout NOR, (k) means the **arity of the colored tuple of consecutive cube vertices**. The conjecture (N_k) colors ordered (k)-tuples
[
(X_0,ldots,X_{k-1})
]
and asks for an antipodal geodesic whose sliding (k)-tuple color word changes at most once.

Accordingly:
[
N_1=	ext{vertex level},quad
N_2=	ext{edge level},quad
N_3=	ext{three-vertex/two-step level},quad
N_4=	ext{four-vertex/three-step level}.
]
The GN3 coordinate-triple problem sits at (N_4).

A translation-invariant (N_k) coloring can be represented by a coordinate label on (r=k-1) successive flipped coordinates,
[
h(v_1,ldots,v_r).
]
All results formerly written with (k) for this coordinate arity are now written with (r) and belong to (N_{r+1}).

The broader basepoint-dependent window model is retained only as a guardrail; its rank-parity obstruction does not refute the translation-invariant directed sector.
