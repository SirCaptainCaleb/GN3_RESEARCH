# Ordered-tuple Norine geodesics

## Composition


## Directed and orientation-blind NOR families

The primary NOR Grand Conjecture \(N_k\) is the **directed ordered-tuple** problem. A coloring is a function
\[
h(v_1,\ldots,v_k)\in\{0,1\}
\]
on ordered \(k\)-tuples of distinct coordinates satisfying
\[
h(v_k,\ldots,v_1)=1-h(v_1,\ldots,v_k).
\]
The target is a permutation whose sliding \(k\)-tuple word changes at most once.

In cube language this is exactly the translation-invariant ordered-window model: colors depend only on the ordered flipped coordinates, not on absolute rank or starting vertex. The rank-parity construction therefore does not refute \(N_k\); it refutes only a larger basepoint-dependent window model.

There is also a neighboring **orientation-blind** higher-uniformity family. It contains ordinary undirected Norine at \(k=1\) and may independently satisfy a one-change theorem. It is not used as the main grand formulation because it does not contain the genuinely directed GN3 \(k=3\) subclass.

The broader basepoint-dependent window models remain useful as boundary cases: rank parity is the unique start-only obstruction of its type, and the first nontrivial support-orientation-blind dimension already satisfies a one-change theorem.
