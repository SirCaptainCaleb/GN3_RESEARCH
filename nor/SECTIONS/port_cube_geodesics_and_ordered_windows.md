## Cube geodesics, ordered windows, and the change process

Every antipodal cube geodesic determines a coordinate permutation by its flip order, and ordered local windows therefore apply to arbitrary antipodal geodesics without a distinguished pole. The ordinary edge problem and higher-window NOR differ mainly in the degree of the local datum: color changes always compare consecutive overlapping local windows. In the GN3 \(k=3\) subclass the window coloring has the extra rigidity of translation invariance in the Boolean base set.
