
BRAINSTORM MODE

There is one permanent Brainstorms collection. Publish brainstorm ideas directly under that root; do not split the collection into round-specific containers.

Your assignment is primarily maintenance of that collection, with idea generation only when the collection has become small.

Review every direct brainstorm route and judge first whether THAT ROUTE ITSELF has received a substantial mathematical research attempt.

A substantial attempt means sustained work aimed at the route's own central conjecture, mechanism, or proposed maneuver: for example, a descendant reasoning chain that develops the idea through multiple inferential steps, a serious direct attack that materially tests or refines the mechanism, or a comparably deep body of work explicitly organized around resolving the route.

Do NOT count any of the following by themselves as a substantial attempt:
- nearby lemmas that would merely be useful ammunition for the route;
- incoming informs edges or consumers mentioning the route;
- results developed primarily for another route;
- one isolated lemma, bound, toy calculation, or preliminary reduction;
- a large surrounding subtree that is not actually descended from or organized around the brainstorm mechanism;
- work that develops only a relaxation, proxy, or prerequisite while leaving the brainstorm's distinctive conjectural step essentially untouched.

When uncertain, classify the route as underexplored and leave it in Brainstorms. Promotion requires positive evidence that the brainstorm idea has become an actual sustained line of reasoning; absence of such evidence is not cured by relevance or promise.

Then maintain placement:
- If a brainstorm route itself has matured into a real active line of reasoning with a sustained route-specific development, move it to its natural reasoning-tree parent.
- If it has been shown false in the needed form, infeasible, or definitively unhelpful for grand-theorem closure, move it to the grand-theorem Archive.
- If it remains playable or underexplored, leave it in Brainstorms.
- Preserve mathematical identity and provenance when moving nodes; reparent rather than clone when possible.
- Related supporting work may remain elsewhere and point to an underexplored brainstorm route through informs edges; such support is not a reason to promote the brainstorm node.

After maintenance, count the direct brainstorm nodes that remain. If five or fewer remain, generate roughly three new mathematically distinct playable conjectures or maneuvers. Fewer is fine when you do not have that many genuinely good ideas. Prefer mechanisms and representations that escape the current local research basin. Add the new ideas directly under the single Brainstorms root.

A new idea should have a short descriptive title, a concrete conjecture or maneuver, why it could matter globally, and a plausible first attack. Ideas may be unlikely and need not be proved.

Do not spend the brainstorm-maintenance assignment proving one idea deeply. Ordinary research assignments choose their own mathematical routes autonomously; brainstorm nodes are available as optional research directions, not scheduler mandates.
