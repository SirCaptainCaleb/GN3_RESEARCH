# R227 — Universal anchored-capacity reduction to contact or five/six-exterior recycle

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Every hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H satisfies the following order-free alternative. Either a spanning two-cover occurs; or an anchored-capacity balanced-pair realization has ordinary reservoir of size at least two and yields the capacity-specific R180 strict same-polarity two-witness extension/collision output or bounded reverse trimer/contact; or, after a one-residual anchored-capacity lineage and finite literal R171 discharge, the untouched opposite pair support T re-enters one of the accepted exterior gateways: a five-exterior non-Hamiltonian R192 replacement-saturation frame, or a six-exterior R196 four-root singleton fan. Thus outside immediate closure the arbitrary-order problem reduces to the capacity-specific |X|>=2 contact/extension branch or the already-developed five/six-exterior gateway programs.

H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample.

## Proof
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample.

CLEAN UNIVERSAL ENTRANCE. Fix any two distinct vertices. Accepted R654 yields either a spanning two-cover of H or an ancestry-bearing mass-two balanced opposite-sign floor. In the latter case apply accepted R179 and choose an anchored-capacity realization (alpha;P),(T;beta). Let X be its ordinary reservoir and apply accepted R180.

If X is empty, H is two-covered. If |X|>=2, R180 gives exactly the first nonclosing output species of R227: the fixed capacity reverse dimer has two same-polarity reservoir witnesses and therefore yields the capacity-specific strict two-witness extension/collision output or bounded reverse trimer/contact. Hence only |X|=1 remains.

ONE-RESIDUAL LITERAL LINEAGE. Accepted R217 converts |X|=1 into a literal R171 frame

  H = J_0 sqcup P_0 sqcup T_0,

where 1<=|J_0|<=3 and P_0,T_0 are opposite-signed historical supports. Follow the fully reconstructible literal peel of R171/P597. Every strict-descent output stays in the same no-recompletion lineage, leaves the third rail as a surviving interval, keeps both historical supports nonempty, and strictly lowers their integer total mass. Therefore strict descent cannot continue indefinitely. A first non-descent output is either a spanning two-cover or bounded support swallow.

Assume swallow and write the terminal frame

  H = J sqcup S sqcup T,

with S the swallowed historical signed support and T the untouched opposite support. Literal interval persistence gives |J|<=3, while R171 gives |S|<=3. Both are nonempty. If |S|+|J|<=3, the whole exterior E:=V(S) union V(J) of T is Hamiltonian (singleton/dimer, or an R3 trimer), so E together with T is a spanning two-cover. Thus |E|>=4.

We exclude |E|=4. The untouched support T retains a historical sign witness beta, and beta lies in E because beta is outside T and the terminal frame partitions H. Construct an exact singleton-deletion cover H-z=Q sqcup T with |Q|=3 and V(Q) union {z}=E as follows. For profile (1,3), delete the singleton S and take Q=J. For profile (2,2), delete one vertex z of S and orient the other S vertex together with the dimer J as a tight trimer Q by R3. For profile (3,1), delete one vertex z of S and orient the remaining two S vertices together with the singleton J as a tight trimer Q by R3. The displayed cover is exact because Hamiltonicity of H-z together with singleton z would two-cover H.

Apply accepted R594 to Q sqcup T, keeping the literal terminal order T=(t_0,...,t_m) as its long rail. In the surviving counterexample normalization it gives, for every e in E,

  (t_1,t_0,e) tight,     (e,t_m,t_{m-1}) tight.

Taking e=beta contradicts the retained sign of T by R3: for a head sign it reverses (beta,t_0,t_1), and for a tail sign it reverses (t_{m-1},t_m,beta). Therefore |E| cannot be four.

Thus every nonclosing terminal swallow has |S|,|J|<=3 and |E|>=5, so exactly

  (|S|,|J|)=(2,3),(3,2),(3,3).                             (1)

FIVE/SIX-EXTERIOR REENTRY. In the first two profiles of (1), E has exactly five vertices. If E supports a Hamilton tight P5, that P5 together with T is a spanning two-cover. Otherwise T is a proper tight path with exactly five exterior vertices E and E is non-Hamiltonian, so accepted R192 applies directly and supplies the five-exterior four-of-five replacement-saturation frame required by R227.

In the final profile (3,3), E has exactly six vertices. Accepted R196 applies to the tight path T with six-vertex exterior E and supplies at least four singleton roots H=T sqcup Q_e sqcup {e}, together with its puncture balanced-pair fan. This is exactly the six-exterior gateway stated in R227.

Hence every smallest counterexample satisfies the R227 alternative: closure, the R180 reservoir>=2 capacity contact/extension branch, the R192 five-exterior replacement gateway, or the R196 six-exterior four-root gateway. No R24, R5, R168, R169, R198, or R214 is used.
