# R618 — Right-root coincidence with the synchronized left pair yields a four-cross-state singleton-deletion weave

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the synchronized transitive R24 frame, the pending synchronized left-root pair {b,z} of R616, and the accepted right high-side root x_R of R596/R598. Suppose x_R is one of {b,z}; write r=x_R. Then the same singleton deletion W=H-r carries two literal exact two-covers with opposite-end absorption: T_L=A_L sqcup Q[2,m], where A_L=(q_1,q_0,ell_1,ell_2,ell_3) is the R616 left enlarged P5 on {q_0,q_1} union (X-{r}), and T_R=Q[0,m-2] sqcup A_R, where A_R=(rho_1,rho_2,rho_3,q_m,q_{m-1}) is the R598 right enlarged P5 on (X-{r}) union {q_{m-1},q_m}. These two covers form a literal four-cross-state weave on W. Relative to T_R, T_L selects q_0 ell_1 and q_{m-2}q_{m-1} across the two T_R components. Relative to T_L, T_R selects q_1q_2 and rho_3 q_m across the two T_L components. Since W is proper, R176 applies independently to all four selected cross states, producing four graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair birth certificates while retaining the same deleted root r and both physical Q cuts. Consequently the nonlocal side-coupling dichotomy is sharp: either x_R lies in the left synchronized pair {b,z}, in which case this singleton-deletion four-weave exists, or x_R lies in the complementary pair {a,c}.

## Proof
Because r is one of the two synchronized left roots, R616 gives the exact left cover T_L=A_L sqcup Q[2,m] of W=H-r, with A_L containing q_1,q_0 followed by all three vertices of X-{r}. Because r=x_R, R598 gives the exact right cover T_R=Q[0,m-2] sqcup A_R of the same W, with A_R containing all three vertices of X-{r} followed by q_m,q_{m-1}. In T_R, q_0 belongs to the Q-prefix component while ell_1 belongs to A_R, so the selected T_L state q_0 ell_1 is a cross state. Also q_{m-2} lies in the Q-prefix component and q_{m-1} lies in A_R, so the selected T_L state q_{m-2}q_{m-1} is a second cross state. Dually, in T_L, q_1 lies in A_L while q_2 lies in Q[2,m], so the selected T_R state q_1q_2 crosses T_L; and rho_3 lies in A_L while q_m lies in Q[2,m], so rho_3 q_m crosses T_L. W is proper with spare vertex r, hence R176 applies to each cross state. The final dichotomy is just X={a,b,c,z} partitioned as {b,z} union {a,c}.
