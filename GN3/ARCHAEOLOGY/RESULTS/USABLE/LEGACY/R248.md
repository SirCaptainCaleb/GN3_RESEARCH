# R248 — Every longest-carrier exterior vertex is selectable at the paid floor

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let K=(k_1,...,k_m), m>=2, be a proper globally longest tight path with exterior E. Fix any prescribed exterior vertex a in E. Then either H already has a spanning two-cover, or there is an ancestry-bearing mass-two balanced-pair floor in which singleton (a) is literally one floor coordinate. The construction is endpoint-local: R199 gives the tight reverse endpoint turn (k_2,k_1,a), whose terminal dimer (k_1,a) is head-signed by k_2. Choose any three vertices of E\\{a} and orient them as a tight trimer by R8; its tail-signed terminal dimer is disjoint from {k_1,a}. R39 gives a direct mass-four balanced pair, and R224 applied to the named inherited turn selects the endpoint a through payment to the mass-two floor. The right endpoint gives the dual construction.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is proper and globally longest of order at least two; a lies outside K; accepted R199, R8, R39, and R224.

## Scope
Order-free prescribed-exterior-vertex floor-selection theorem. No five-exterior equality, four-core occupancy, order-ten geometry, or simultaneous-currentness assumption is used.

## Applicability
Use whenever downstream fixed-turn, capture, or ancestry machinery needs a chosen physical exterior vertex to survive all the way to the mass-two floor. In a five-exterior equality frame, every one of the five exterior vertices is separately selectable.

## Persistence
The prescribed physical vertex a, the endpoint reverse turn, the selected endpoint-refund channel, and the literal floor coordinate (a) persist in the chosen lineage. The opposite floor singleton and the source carrier representative need not persist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Different choices of a produce separate alternative lineages and are not simultaneous. Does not prescribe the second floor singleton, does not currentize the floor into R171 contrary to R214, and does not by itself close the conjecture.

## Proof
Fix a in E. R199 gives (k_2,k_1,a) tight at the left end of K. The terminal physical dimer D=(k_1,a) is therefore head-signed by the named witness k_2. Since every proper tight path in a smallest counterexample leaves at least five exterior vertices, E-{a} has at least four vertices; choose any three of them and orient that triple T=(t_0,t_1,t_2) as a tight trimer by R8. Its left terminal dimer (t_0,t_1) is tail-signed by t_2 and is vertex-disjoint from D. Thus D and (t_0,t_1) are disjoint opposite-polarity signed supports of total mass four, so R39 gives a direct balanced-pair lineage unless a spanning two-cover has already occurred. The sign on D comes from the named inherited turn (k_2,k_1,a). Apply R224 and choose the endpoint-selective refund channel whose surviving endpoint is a. R224 yields either a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor in which (a) is literally one singleton coordinate. No claim is made about the identity of the other singleton or about simultaneous floors for different exterior vertices.
