# R600 — Minimum R24 cell has dual short-carrier-payable spoke collisions

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the unique exactly-two-bad transitive R24 cell of R556, with X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, L=q_0 and R=q_m. Then the two hinge spokes {a,z} and {z,c} already carry tested-order-safe two-witness packets that are directly consumable by R542, without using the later source-replay contacts. On the left, R556 gives Laz tight and the matching-height cell gives caz tight. Thus the tested dimer (a,z) is head-signed by the two distinct witnesses L and c. The tight trimer K_L=(b,z,a) has (a,z) as its reverse terminal dimer, and both witnesses L,c lie outside K_L. Hence R542 applies to (a,z), with complementary carrier singleton b. Dually, R556 gives zcR tight and X gives zca tight, so the tested dimer (z,c) is tail-signed by the distinct witnesses R and a. The tight trimer K_R=(c,z,b) has (z,c) as a reverse boundary dimer, both witnesses R,a lie outside K_R, and R542 applies again, with the same complementary carrier singleton b. The two payable spoke dimers have opposite polarity and intersect exactly in hinge z, so R547 also cuts them to the ancestry-bearing floor on physical singletons (a),(c). Thus the minimum cell contains a dual R542 payment packet whose two short carriers share the same complementary singleton b, the middle of the original turn (a,b,c).

The accepted exact-two-bad R24 frame R556; smallest-counterexample hypotheses needed by R542; accepted R547.

## Proof
Put L=q_0 and R=q_m. Since az is fully outgoing at L by R556, Laz is tight. In the matching-height four-cell, caz is tight because M(ca)=M_S>M(az)=M_L. Hence Laz and caz are two head certificates on the same tested dimer (a,z), with witnesses L and c. Also (b,z,a) is tight because M(bz)=M_S>M(za)=M_L. Its reverse terminal dimer is exactly (a,z), and L,c are both outside {b,z,a}. Therefore all hypotheses of R542 hold and the az packet forces capture/payment with complementary carrier remainder {b}. On the right, R556 gives zcR tight because cz is fully incoming at R. The intrinsic turn zca is tight since M(zc)=M_R>M(ca)=M_S. Thus zcR and zca are two tail certificates on the same tested dimer (z,c), with witnesses R and a. The carrier (c,z,b) is tight because M(cz)=M_R>M(zb)=M_S; (z,c) is one of its reverse boundary dimers, and R,a are exterior to that carrier. R542 applies again, with complementary remainder {b}. The az packet is head-signed and the zc packet tail-signed, and their supports meet only at z. R547 therefore yields the ancestry-bearing opposite-sign singleton floor on a and c. No source-replay turn baz or zcb is used.
