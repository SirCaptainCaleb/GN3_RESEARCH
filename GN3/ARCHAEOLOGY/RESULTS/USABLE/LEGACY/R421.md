# R421 — Common-shadow cross-state pair has a certificate-paid both-singleton checkpoint

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order n>=11, let W be a proper vertex subset, and let R,T be literal path covers of W such that T selects a state crossing two components of R. Apply R176 and then the certificate-retaining descent R416. Unless H already has a spanning two-cover, this yields an ancestry-bearing mass-two balanced-pair floor F0 with singleton supports (p),(t), named sign witnesses alpha,beta, and the full R176 common-shadow birth provenance retained. Put B0={p,t,alpha,beta}. Then for every prescribed z in V(H)\B0 there is a separate chosen continuation which either closes by a spanning two-cover or reaches another ancestry-bearing mass-two floor F_z in which (z) is literally one singleton support, while the entire common-shadow/R176 ancestry of F0 remains historical data. Hence every nonclosing common-shadow cross-state branch has at least n-|B0|>=n-4>=7 independently selectable fresh paid-floor coordinates. Each such continuation is supplied by R420: choose one auxiliary u outside B0 union {z}, actualize a signed dimer containing z from one singleton support, and use inherited-turn endpoint-selective payment to retain z at the descendant floor. The bad-seam branches retain the positive support-mass payment already guaranteed by R416; in the good/good branch no positive net payment from the original R176 birth is asserted, but its previously opaque support orders no longer obstruct coordinate-rich floor continuation.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample with n>=11; W is proper; R,T are literal covers of W with a selected T-state crossing R-components; accepted R176,R416; provisional R420.

## Scope
Order-free common-shadow ancestry theorem in the beyond-order-ten regime. It strengthens R416 by adding a large prescribed fresh-coordinate floor fan after the first certificate-retaining mass-two checkpoint.

## Applicability
Use on the Type II/common-shadow branch of the top-level O4 normalization. It removes good/good support-order opacity as a reason to split the frontier: after the first R176-ancestry floor, at least seven fresh physical coordinates are separately selectable. Downstream work should therefore consume cross-state ancestry or compare role-bearing coordinates, not merely ask for another floor or another arbitrary selectable vertex.

## Persistence
The common residue W, compared covers, selected cross state, exact R176 good/good cross-witness or bad-seam reverse-trimer/spare-vertex birth data, the first floor ancestry, chosen fresh coordinate z, auxiliary marker, actualization turn, endpoint-selective refund, and literal descendant singleton (z) are retained as historical ledger data within each chosen lineage. Different z-lineages are alternative and need not coexist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe the second singleton of F_z. Does not claim floors for different z coexist. Does not assert that z is an original cross-state endpoint or witness. Does not assert positive net support-mass descent in the original good/good R176 birth. Does not make arbitrary coordinate selectability itself progress or close the conjecture.

## Proof
Apply accepted R416. If it closes, stop. Otherwise obtain a mass-two floor F0 whose singleton supports are (p),(t), with named sign witnesses alpha,beta, and whose historical ledger contains the complete R176 common-shadow provenance. Let B0={p,t,alpha,beta}; |B0|<=4. Fix z outside B0. Since n>=11 there exists u outside B0 union {z}. The hypotheses of provisional R420 hold for F0 and z, so R420 yields either a spanning two-cover or a descendant ancestry-bearing mass-two floor F_z containing literal singleton (z). By the persistence clause of R420 the complete prior floor ancestry is retained, hence so is the R176 common-shadow birth certificate retained by R416. There are at least n-|B0|>=n-4>=7 possible choices of z. These are alternative lineages. In R176 bad-seam branches, R416 already guarantees at least one unit of support-mass descent from the dimer-plus-singleton birth. In the good/good branch the direct birth support orders remain unspecified, but this is irrelevant to the coordinate-fan conclusion because R420 begins from the R416 mass-two checkpoint.
