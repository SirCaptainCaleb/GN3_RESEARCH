# R733 — Every R727 P4 residue births an inward-coordinate signed-dimer pair before payment

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and accepted R723,R727. Then every P4 branch of R727 has a source-localized balanced-pair consumer that retains the first inward Q coordinate on a nontrivial signed dimer. LEFT: write u=q_1,v=q_2. If R727 returns a tight Hamilton P4 K on {a,b,c,u}, put r=z; if it returns one on {c,z,a,u}, put r=b. Then H contains a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign pair one of whose signed supports is a dimer containing u. More precisely, apply R732 to K with exterior vertex L=q_0. If neither endpoint attachment of L to K is tight, R732 gives the direct pair on the two reversed terminal dimers of K; exactly one of those two dimers contains u. If an endpoint attachment is tight, it produces a tight P5 J on {L,u} union (X-{r}), so J sqcup Q[2,m] is a literal exact two-cover of H-r. Compare this with the accepted R723 shallow cover A_r^L sqcup Q[1,m]. In J, the vertex u has a selected adjacency to a vertex of A_r^L, hence that state crosses the two components of the shallow cover. Apply the reconstructed R176 proof with the shallow cover as R, the P5 cover as T, spare vertex r, and crossed endpoint x=u. Since u is the endpoint of the old long rail Q[1,m] with old neighbor v, the resulting pair may be chosen with one signed support exactly a tested orientation of the physical dimer {u,v}, while the opposite signed support is the singleton (r). RIGHT is exact dual. Write d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1}. If the R727 P4 is on {a,c,b,s}, put r=z; if on {c,a,z,s}, put r=b. Testing exterior vertex R=q_m by R732 either gives a direct terminal-dimer pair with one dimer containing s, or gives a tight P5 J on {R,s} union (X-{r}). In the latter case Q[0,m-2] sqcup J is an exact cover of H-r; comparing it with the R723 right shallow cover Q[0,m-1] sqcup A_r^R and applying the endpoint-symmetric reconstruction of R176 at crossed endpoint s gives a balanced pair with one signed support a tested orientation of {d,s} and opposite singleton (r). Thus a labelled P4 in R727 is not terminal and need not be converted into an advanced P6: before any generic payment it already yields an inward-coordinate dimer pair tied either to the literal old first-inward Q edge or to one terminal dimer of the actual P4.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame; accepted R723 and R727 hold; use accepted R732 and the fully reconstructed R176 cross-state proof.

## Proof
Consider the left side; the right side is the exact reversal. Let K be the R727 P4 and let r be the unique X vertex omitted from its support, so r=z for support {a,b,c,u} and r=b for {c,z,a,u}. Test L against the two ends of an actual tight ordering K=(k_0,k_1,k_2,k_3). If both attachments fail, R732 gives the pair ((k_1,k_0),(k_3,k_2)) with common witness L and opposite polarities. The two terminal dimers partition V(K), so exactly one contains u. Otherwise one attachment succeeds and gives a tight P5 J on {L,u} union (X-{r}). The untouched Q[2,m] is disjoint and tight, hence J sqcup Q[2,m] is a literal exact two-cover T of H-r. R723 supplies the shallow exact cover R=A_r^L sqcup Q[1,m], where A_r^L has support {L} union (X-{r}). Since J contains the unique vertex u outside A_r^L and all other J vertices lie in A_r^L, at least one selected J-state uy or yu is incident with u and crosses the two components of R. Apply the reconstructed R176 proof to this physical cross state using spare d=r and choose x=u as the crossed endpoint lying in the R-component Q[1,m]. That component is nontrivial and u has old neighbor v=q_2. Boundary antisymmetry on {v,u,y} yields one signed tested orientation of the physical dimer {u,v} with witness y; the automatic singleton sign at r with the same witness gives the opposite polarity. Hence a balanced pair is born with nontrivial support {u,v} and opposite singleton {r}. On the right, a successful R-attachment gives J sqcup Q[0,m-2] and the old shallow cover Q[0,m-1] sqcup A_r^R. A selected J-state incident with s crosses the old components; use x=s and its old Q-neighbor d=q_{m-2} in the same R176 proof to obtain a signed dimer on {d,s} and opposite singleton r. This exhausts the P4 branch.
