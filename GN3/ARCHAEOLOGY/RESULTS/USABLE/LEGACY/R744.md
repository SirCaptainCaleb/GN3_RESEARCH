For every x in X, accepted R723 gives the tight trimer K_x=(v,u,x), whose reverse initial boundary dimer is exactly S_L and whose complementary carrier singleton is x. Applying R508 to each K_x in the same T_L forces a selected state C_x={x,y_x} with y_x outside K_x. Call x blocked if the chosen crossing has y_x=w. Hence at least two distinct vertices x in X have witness-avoiding selected carrier/exterior crossings C_x. For each such x, R527 with S_L,w,K_x,C_x may orient the marker so that x itself is captured. Thus one fixed exact H-{u,v} cover contains source crossings making at least two distinct X anchors individually capture/contact-capable through literal first-inward X-Q carrier edges. ## Proof
LEFT. By R429 choose an exact two-cover T_L of H-{u,v}. Since Q is tight, (u,v,w) is tight, so S_L=(u,v) is tail-signed by witness w. For each x in X, accepted R723 gives (v,u,x) tight. Hence K_x=(v,u,x) is a tight trimer with reverse initial boundary dimer S_L and complementary singleton x. Apply accepted R508 to D={u,v}, absorbable singleton {x}, recompletion carrier K_x, and the same T_L. Obtain a selected T_L-state C_x={x,y_x} with y_x outside V(K_x). Do this for all four x. If y_x is not w, then witness w avoids C_x, so accepted R527 applies and, by its endpoint-choice clause, the marker may be oriented to designate x itself as the captured anchor. If y_x=w, call x blocked. Distinct blocked x give distinct selected edges xw incident with w. Since the selected-state graph of T_L is a union of two paths, deg_T_L(w)<=2. Therefore at most two x are blocked, leaving at least two distinct capture-capable X vertices. For each such x, K_x contains the genuine historical adjacency {u,x}; accepted R312 applied to the capture through C_x and this carrier edge gives either an earlier protected event touching x or lawful mirror reentry whose first contact is at x. RIGHT. Choose exact T_R of H-{d,s}. The old Q turn (rho,d,s) makes S_R=(d,s) head-signed by rho. For each x, R723 gives K_x=(x,s,d), whose reverse terminal boundary dimer is S_R and complementary singleton x. R508 in the common T_R gives C_x={x,y_x}; at most two have y_x=rho because deg_T_R(rho)<=2. Every other crossing avoids the witness, so R527 captures x and R312 uses the genuine K_x edge {x,s}. This proves the dual claim.
