# R633 — Crossed endpoint collision box forces dual terminal payment or full extreme-edge saturation

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the crossed R24 frame of accepted R621 with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, L=q_0, u=q_1, R=q_m, s=q_{m-1}. Define C_L to mean a tested-order same-tail R38 collision on the physical dimer (L,u), with one witness q_2 and another witness in X. Define C_R dually as a same-head R38 collision on (s,R), with one witness q_{m-2} and another witness in X. Define four extreme-X collision packets:
C_ba: (b,a) tail-signed by distinct witnesses L,c;
C_bc: (b,c) head-signed by R,a;
C_za: (z,a) head-signed by R,c;
C_zc: (z,c) tail-signed by L,a.

Then at least one of the following two structural outputs holds:
(A) both terminal packets C_L and C_R occur. In this case the disjoint oppositely signed terminal dimers (L,u) and (s,R) form a direct mass-four balanced pair, so accepted R39 yields either a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor retaining the two terminal collision ancestries;
(B) all four extreme-X packets C_ba,C_bc,C_za,C_zc occur. In this case R547 applied at hinge b to the opposite-polarity dimers (b,a),(b,c) gives an ancestry-bearing floor on physical pair {a,c}, while R547 applied at hinge z to (z,a),(z,c) gives a second ancestry-bearing floor on the same physical pair {a,c} with the singleton polarities reversed. The two floor ancestries are distinct and both are retained; by R444 their bare opposite singleton labels are not themselves declared a contradiction.

The dichotomy is forced by eight literal two-hole spanning proposals on the fixed endpoint set X union {L,u,s,R}. Thus the crossed frame cannot avoid both a genuinely two-ended terminal balanced-pair channel and complete collision saturation of the four extreme matching edges of X.

## Proof
All proposals below use the same untouched complementary tight interval Q[2,m-2], which is nonempty because m>=4. Hence an eight-vertex endpoint path on E=X union {L,u,s,R} with exactly two uncertified turns gives a literal spanning two-cover proposal with exactly those two holes.

First build four right-terminal clauses.
(1) P=(u,L,a,b,z,R,s,c). Its only holes are (L,a,b),(R,s,c), so R3 gives (b,a,L) OR (c,s,R).
(2) P=(u,L,a,c,b,R,s,z). Its holes are (c,b,R),(R,s,z), giving (R,b,c) OR (z,s,R).
(3) P=(u,L,c,a,z,R,s,b). Its holes are (a,z,R),(R,s,b), giving (R,z,a) OR (b,s,R).
(4) P=(u,L,c,z,b,R,s,a). Its holes are (L,c,z),(R,s,a), giving (z,c,L) OR (a,s,R).
Every other turn in these paths is one of: an endpoint star uLx or xRs, a crossed gate turn Lac/Lca/bzR/zbR, or a matching-height X turn abz/acb/caz/czb, respectively, so the displayed hole sets are complete.

Now build four left-terminal clauses.
(5) P=(a,u,L,c,z,b,R,s). Holes (a,u,L),(L,c,z), giving (L,u,a) OR (z,c,L).
(6) P=(b,u,L,c,a,z,R,s). Holes (b,u,L),(a,z,R), giving (L,u,b) OR (R,z,a).
(7) P=(c,u,L,a,b,z,R,s). Holes (c,u,L),(L,a,b), giving (L,u,c) OR (b,a,L).
(8) P=(z,u,L,a,c,b,R,s). Holes (z,u,L),(c,b,R), giving (L,u,z) OR (R,b,c).
Again all remaining turns are accepted endpoint-star, gate, or matching-height turns.

Decorate every literal by R38. The Q turns (L,u,q_2) and (q_{m-2},s,R) are tight. Thus any tight literal (L,u,x), x in X, makes (L,u) tail-signed by distinct witnesses x,q_2 and gives C_L; any tight (x,s,R) gives C_R using witnesses x,q_{m-2}. For the X literals, matching-height gives (b,a,c),(a,b,c),(c,z,a),(z,c,a) tight. Therefore (b,a,L) gives C_ba with witnesses L,c; (R,b,c) gives C_bc with R,a; (R,z,a) gives C_za with R,c; and (z,c,L) gives C_zc with L,a.

Consequently clauses (1)-(4) imply, respectively,
C_ba OR C_R,
C_bc OR C_R,
C_za OR C_R,
C_zc OR C_R,
while clauses (5)-(8) imply
C_L OR C_zc,
C_L OR C_za,
C_L OR C_ba,
C_L OR C_bc.
If both C_L and C_R hold, branch (A) follows. The tested dimer (L,u) is tail-signed and (s,R) is head-signed; their supports are disjoint for m>=4. By accepted R39 they form a direct mass-four balanced pair, with the stated closure-or-floor continuation.

If branch (A) fails, at least one of C_L,C_R is absent. If C_R is absent, the first four implications force all C_ba,C_bc,C_za,C_zc. If C_L is absent, the last four force the same four packets. Hence branch (B) holds.

In branch (B), C_bc is head polarity on (b,c) and C_ba tail polarity on (b,a); the two supports meet exactly at b. R547 cuts the hinge and produces opposite-signed singletons on c and a, hence an ancestry-bearing floor on {a,c}. Likewise C_za is head polarity on (z,a) and C_zc tail polarity on (z,c); cutting hinge z by R547 gives the same physical singleton pair {a,c} with the two polarities reversed. The original collision witnesses and hinge cuts remain ancestry. R444 forbids treating the bare reversed singleton labels themselves as a contradiction, so the durable output is the pair of distinct ancestry-bearing floors. This proves the theorem.
