# R988 — A zero-transfer augmentation either exposes its donor gain or collision-transports the opposite defect

## Statement
Let G be an edge-ordered graph and let {M_z:z in V} be a singleton-rich family of equal-size increasing-cover matchings as in accepted R986, with union U. Let M subset U be any increasing matching of the same size r. Assume the R986 augmentation from M is in its ZERO-TRANSFER-PLUS-EDGE branch: there are labels ell,y, an M-unmatched right copy rho_in, a zero-weight alternating component Q of M triangle M_ell, and a donor edge e=ell_out rho_in in M_y, where Q has ell_out as the endpoint that is unmatched in M_ell. Assume also that the pair (M,M_ell) has no M_ell-heavy component; equivalently, since |M|=|M_ell|, every component of M triangle M_ell has weight zero.

Apply the accepted R980 collision preorder to (M,M_ell), and let W be the upper closure of Q. Then W has total weight zero, so flipping W gives another increasing size-r cover matching H. The copy ell_out is unmatched in H.

Let D_rho be the symmetric-difference component containing rho_in, if M and M_ell differ at rho_in. Exactly one of the following occurs.

(DONOR-EXPOSED) D_rho is absent or D_rho is not in W. Then rho_in remains unmatched in H. Consequently the component of H triangle M_y containing e is exactly the singleton edge {e}, with weight +1. Thus the ordinary final donor edge of R986 has been converted into a literal R980 gain component.

(FORCED-COUPLING) D_rho belongs to W. Then Q<=D_rho in the collision preorder, so there is an explicit directed chain of physical collision constraints from the terminal-defect transfer Q to the right-side component D_rho. Since rho_in is unmatched in M but changed on D_rho, D_rho is a zero alternating path whose flip matches rho_in and exposes one other right copy sigma_in. Thus the neutral currentization that exposes ell_out necessarily transports the source defect rho_in to sigma_in, and the complete collision chain witnessing that coupling is retained.

The dual statement holds with left/right and incoming/outgoing interchanged. The lemma is conditional on accepted R980 and R986 and is not a termination theorem.

## Hypotheses
Accepted R986 zero-transfer setup from an increasing size-r base matching M inside the singleton-family union; accepted R980 collision preorder for M versus M_ell; no pair-gain component in M triangle M_ell, so all its components have weight zero.

## Scope
Family-level neutral-recombination consumer. Converts the R986 zero-transfer certificate into either a literal singleton +1 donor component or an explicit forced collision chain that transports the opposite-side unmatched defect.

## Applicability
Use inside the neutral closure demanded by the Independent guidance. Iterating the dichotomy yields either a positive upper-set opportunity or a directed dependency network coupling terminal and source defect transfers. Any claimed saturation theorem should consume that network rather than only pairwise min-cut flows.

## Persistence
Retain M,M_ell,M_y,Q,e,rho_in, the R980 upper closure W, H, and either the singleton gain {e} or D_rho together with the full directed collision chain Q<=...<=D_rho and the new exposed right copy sigma_in.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say the singleton gain itself is an upper set. Does not force a positive R980 upper set. Does not prove that repeated defect transport terminates or that collision chains from different comparisons are coherent.

## Proof
Let Q be the R986 zero-weight component of M triangle M_ell with endpoint ell_out unmatched in M_ell. By the no-pair-gain hypothesis, no component of M triangle M_ell has positive weight. Since the total weight over all components is |M_ell|-|M|=0, no component has negative weight either. Hence every component has weight zero.

Let W be the upper closure of Q in the R980 collision preorder. Because every component has weight zero, W has total weight zero. Accepted R980 therefore says that flipping exactly W gives an increasing matching H of the same size r.

The bipartite copy ell_out belongs only to Q among the symmetric-difference components. On Q, H uses the M_ell state. Since ell_out is unmatched in M_ell, ell_out is unmatched in H. Components added to the upper closure are bipartite-copy disjoint from Q and cannot change this conclusion.

Now inspect rho_in. It is unmatched in M by the R986 certificate. If M and M_ell agree at rho_in, then it is also unmatched in M_ell and no symmetric-difference component contains it; therefore rho_in remains unmatched in H. If they differ there, let D_rho be the unique symmetric-difference component containing rho_in.

If D_rho is not in W, H retains the M state at rho_in, hence rho_in is unmatched in H. Thus both ell_out and rho_in are unmatched in H. The donor matching M_y contains e=ell_out rho_in. Because M_y is a matching, e is the only M_y edge incident with either of those two bipartite copies. Because H has no edge incident with either copy, the connected component of H triangle M_y containing e consists of e alone. Relative to H it therefore has one M_y edge and no H edge, so its weight is +1. This is DONOR-EXPOSED.

Suppose instead D_rho belongs to W. By definition W is the upper closure of Q, so Q preceq D_rho. Hence the transitive closure contains a directed path from Q to D_rho, each arc witnessed by one physical R980 collision inequality; retain those witnessing vertices and inequalities.

Because rho_in is unmatched in M but M and M_ell differ at rho_in, M_ell matches rho_in. The component D_rho has weight zero. A zero-weight alternating component containing an endpoint unmatched in exactly one of the two matchings cannot be a cycle. It is an even alternating path. Since rho_in lies on the right bipartition and is unmatched in M but matched in M_ell, its other endpoint also lies on the right bipartition and is unmatched in M_ell but matched in M. Call that endpoint sigma_in. Flipping D_rho from the M state to the M_ell state therefore matches rho_in and exposes sigma_in. Since D_rho is included in W, H realizes exactly that transfer. This is FORCED-COUPLING.

The two cases are exhaustive. Interchanging out/in, left/right, and reversing the roles of source and terminal copies gives the dual statement.
