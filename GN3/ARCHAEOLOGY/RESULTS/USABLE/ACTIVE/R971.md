# R971 — Every edge-ordered K7 has a spanning cover by at most two increasing paths

## Statement
Let K_7 have any strict total order on its 21 ordinary edges. Then its seven vertices can be partitioned into at most two vertex-disjoint increasing paths. Equivalently, no edge ordering of K_7 has increasing path-cover number at least three.

## Hypotheses
Pure edge-ordered complete graph K7 with a strict total order on all 21 edges.

## Scope
Exact finite edge-order theorem. Computational certificate is complete and self-contained at the CNF level; no boundary-tournament shared strategy or minimum-counterexample hypothesis is used.

## Applicability
Finite base for R888. In particular, a minimum R888 counterexample has order at least eight; hence in the R962/R969 trimer-fiber setup the fixed opposite rail B cannot also have order three, and the R969 long-rail reduction applies to every trimer deletion rail once this finite theorem is canonically accepted.

## Persistence
Retain the 21 lexicographically indexed ordinary edges, 210 pairwise edge-order variables, 2660 transitivity clauses, 12600 deduplicated two-path-cover-forbidding clauses, canonical CNF hash, and the independent DPLL/MILP infeasibility checks.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prove R888 for order eight or larger. Does not classify extremal K7 edge orders or produce a canonical two-cover algorithm. Pending canonical review must remain distinct from the checked computational evidence.

## Proof
Index the 21 ordinary edges of K_7 lexicographically as e_0,...,e_20. For each 0<=i<j<=20 introduce one Boolean variable X_{ij}, interpreted as “e_i precedes e_j in the edge order”. Since exactly one of e_i<e_j and e_j<e_i holds, one bit per unordered edge-pair is enough; there are C(21,2)=210 variables.

TOTAL-ORDER CLAUSES. For every i<j<k add
  (not X_{ij}) OR (not X_{jk}) OR X_{ik},
  X_{ij} OR X_{jk} OR (not X_{ik}).
These are exactly the two clauses excluding the two directed 3-cycles on {e_i,e_j,e_k}. There are 2*C(21,3)=2660 such clauses. A tournament on the 21 edge-objects with no directed triangle is transitive, so these clauses are equivalent to the existence of one strict total edge order realizing all X_{ij}.

TWO-PATH-COVER CLAUSES. Take every ordered vertex permutation pi=(v_0,...,v_6) and every cut c in {1,...,6}. This represents the two oriented vertex paths
  P=(v_0,...,v_{c-1}),
  Q=(v_c,...,v_6).
For a path W=(w_0,...,w_s), write f_t={w_t,w_{t+1}}. The path is increasing exactly when every consecutive comparison f_t<f_{t+1} holds, t=0,...,s-2; a singleton or dimer contributes no comparison. For each (pi,c), collect all comparison literals required simultaneously by P and Q and add the single clause which is the disjunction of their negations. Thus the clause says that this particular ordered two-path cover is not increasing on both rails.

Every spanning cover by two increasing paths occurs among these permutation/cut representations after orienting each rail increasingly and ordering the two rails. A Hamilton increasing path also yields one of these two-path covers by splitting off its first vertex as a singleton, so forbidding all represented two-path covers forbids covers by at most two paths. Conversely, a satisfying assignment of all cover-forbidding clauses represents a strict edge order with no spanning increasing two-path cover.

After exact deduplication, the cover family contributes 12,600 distinct clauses: 7,560 clauses of length three and 5,040 clauses of length four. Together with the 2,660 transitivity clauses, the final CNF has
  210 variables,
  15,260 clauses,
  10,220 clauses of length three,
  5,040 clauses of length four.
Sorting literals in each clause by variable and sorting clauses lexicographically, the DIMACS-style text “lit ... lit 0” joined by newlines has SHA-256
  625270abc73035479584b613d96049c50cf10f22eb8e4bf565928f96e246c9eb.

EXHAUSTIVE CHECK 1: DPLL. A deterministic watched-literal DPLL verifier performs unit propagation to closure, then branches on the unassigned variable of highest total literal occurrence (ties by lowest variable index); it tries the majority-occurrence polarity first, with positive chosen on a tie, then the opposite polarity. On the full unsymmetrized CNF above it terminates UNSAT after 355 recursive search nodes, maximum branch depth 24, with no satisfying leaf. The watched-literal implementation was separately compared against brute-force truth-table satisfiability on 300 randomly generated CNFs with at most eight variables and agreed in every case. No vertex or edge-order symmetry assumption is used in this 355-node run.

EXHAUSTIVE CHECK 2: INDEPENDENT BINARY MILP. Independently encode the same 210 comparison bits as binary variables. Each of the two transitivity clauses per edge triple is written as its equivalent linear 0-1 inequality, and each forbidden two-path conjunction is written as “sum of its required comparison indicators <= number of requirements minus 1”. This gives 15,260 linear constraints before solver presolve. SciPy 1.17 scipy.optimize.milp with bundled HiGHS reports the model infeasible. This model was generated independently of the watched-literal propagation code.

Therefore the CNF has no satisfying assignment. Hence no strict edge ordering of K_7 can avoid every spanning cover by at most two increasing paths. Every edge-ordered K_7 has increasing path-cover number at most two, proving R971.
