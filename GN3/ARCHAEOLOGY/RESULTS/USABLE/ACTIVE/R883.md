# R883 — Minimum inward-anchor five-cell recompletions introduce no new quiet species

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-ten inward-anchor five-cell setting and let T be an exact two-cover of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} in the minimum case sigma_G(T)=0. Then every such T compiles into one of the following already-named interfaces. (UT) If c_X(T)=1, T is exactly the accepted R865 specialization of the R511 unique-transition cell, with its dimer pure-C hard residue localized by the R550/R568 seam refinements. (P4/CLOSE) If c_X(T) is 2 or 3 and some X block is internal, accepted R868 gives a source-labelled Hamilton P4. In the remaining endpoint-X regimes, accepted R855 consumes shape A by literal closure or, only at m=6, a labelled inward P4. Accepted R856 consumes split-X shape C by literal closure, a labelled P4, or a physical one/two-hole reverse-X or facing-anchor clause whose realized branch is P4- or R542-ready. Pending-current-interface shape-D theorem R882 consumes the final same-rail endpoint-X shape D by literal closure for adjacent Q pairs or a physical two-end mate clause whose realized branch is uniformly P4-type or R542-ready. Consequently, once R882 is accepted, the sigma_G(T)=0 five-cell layer has no unnamed quiet combinatorial residue: every minimum recompletion is either an established unique-transition packet, already closes H, produces a labelled P4, or lands in an explicit source-decorated R542/mate-clause interface.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R862 frame with m>=6; T is an exact two-cover of W in the minimum case sigma_G(T)=0. Use accepted R865,R866,R868,R855,R856 and the current-interface shape-D rebase R882.

## Scope
Noncircular structural parent for the complete minimum five-cell layer. It is a classification/compilation theorem, not a new closure theorem. It packages existing accepted consumers and the current-interface shape-D rebase without adding payment, protected-contact, or path-growth claims.

## Applicability
Use immediately after detecting sigma_G(T)=0. Do not enumerate five-cell forests again. First apply R865. If c_X=1, enter the existing unique-transition machinery. Otherwise apply R868; only endpoint-X residues remain, and shapes A,C,D are consumed by R855,R856,R882 respectively.

## Persistence
Retain the exact deletion pair, T, the five source cells, sigma_G(T)=0, c_X(T), the R866 shape/regime label, and whichever named downstream packet is produced.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that every minimum recompletion immediately two-covers H. Does not consume the resulting P4, R542 packet, or mate clause. Does not treat sigma_G(T)>0 or fragmented-X excess covers.

## Proof
Let T be a minimum inward-anchor five-cell recompletion, so sigma_G(T)=0. By accepted R865, c_X(T) is 1,2,or3. If c_X(T)=1, R865 itself proves that the contracted forest is P4 disjoint union P1 relative to the five source cells and that, relative to the full-Q R508 partition, T is exactly an R511 unique-transition packet with two physical X-dimer C-blocks. After R512/R513, the surviving both-bad dimer pure-C residues lie in the accepted R550/R568 refinements. This is the UT output and introduces no new five-cell species. Now suppose c_X(T) is 2 or3. Accepted R866 classifies the contracted two-rail forest into shapes A-F and the three regimes Internal-X, Same-rail endpoint-X, and Split-X. Accepted R868 proves that every Internal-X configuration, namely every c_X=3 shape and c_X=2 shape B, yields a source-labelled Hamilton P4. Outside that P4 branch, both X blocks are degree-one source/sink endcaps, so only shape A or D in the same-rail regime and shape C in the split-X regime remain. Accepted R855 consumes shape A: for m>=7 literal restoration gives a spanning two-cover, while at m=6 the only failed restoration seam reverses against the inward signed dimer to a labelled P4. Accepted R856 consumes shape C: every monotone configuration either closes, yields a labelled P4, or produces an explicit one/two-hole facing-anchor or reverse-X literal whose realized branch is P4- or R542-ready. Finally R882 consumes shape D: adjacent Q pairs restore to a spanning two-cover, and the unique G_0-G_2 skip gives a physical two-end mate clause whose realized literal is uniformly P4-type or uniformly R542-ready according to the left/right X identity. These cases exhaust c_X=1,2,3 and all six R866 shapes. Therefore no unnamed quiet minimum five-cell residue remains.
