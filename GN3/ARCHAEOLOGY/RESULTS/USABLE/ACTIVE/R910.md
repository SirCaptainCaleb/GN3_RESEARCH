# R910 — Every crossed middle-gate pair-deletion cover is Reverse-Ear active

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), L=q_0, R=q_m, and crossed middle-gate pairs G_3={a,c}, G_4={b,z}. Fix either tested orientation S=(s_0,s_1) of G_3 and let T=T_1 sqcup T_2 be any exact two-cover of H-V(S). Then T together with the fixed crossed frame always yields an accepted R435 Reverse-Ear output: a reversed selected/old state, a tight reverse trimer/contact, or a vertex-simple tight cycle. Dually the same holds for either tested orientation of G_4. More precisely, for G_3, accepted R649 makes R a head witness on S. Let K be the T-rail containing R. If R is terminal on K, accepted R834 gives a tight reverse outer seam (s_0,R,y), where y is the T-predecessor of R; this trimer contains the reversed selected state R y of the T-state y R, so R435 applies relative to K. If R is not terminal, let y be its T-successor. Since V(H)-V(S)=V(Q) union G_4, either y=q_i is a Q vertex or y is one vertex x of G_4. In the Q case K encounters the consecutive Q-contacts R=q_m then q_i with i<m, so R435 applies relative to Q. In the G_4 case, writing x' for the other G_4 vertex, the crossed right gate gives the tight trimer (x',x,R); it contains the reversed state x R of the selected T-state R x, so R435 applies relative to K. These cases exhaust T. For G_4, accepted R649 makes L a tail witness on S. On the T-rail containing L, if L is initial, R834 gives a reverse outer seam containing the reverse of its selected successor state; if L has a predecessor q_i in Q then q_i,L are decreasing Q-contacts; if its predecessor is x in G_3 then the crossed left gate trimer (L,x,x') contains the reverse of the selected state x L. Hence R435 applies in every case. Therefore no exact crossed middle-gate pair-deletion cover is Reverse-Ear quiet.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame. S is either orientation of one crossed middle-gate pair G_3 or G_4. T is any exact two-cover of H-V(S). Use accepted R649 for endpoint polarity, R429 for nontriviality of both T rails, R834 for the endpoint case, the literal crossed gate trimers from R621, and R435 Reverse Ear.

## Scope
Accepted-input crossed-frame parent theorem. No R905/R906, selected-depth witness, capture, payment, five-cell taxonomy, generic growth, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533, or computation.

## Applicability
Whenever an argument reaches an exact deletion cover of either crossed middle-gate pair, do not branch into CAP/XET/payment merely to exploit endpoint carriers. Apply this theorem first: the source cover already carries explicit R435 reversal/reverse-trimer/cycle geometry. In particular, once reviewed, it consumes the entire middle-gate alternative of pending R906, including both R682 CAP and XET.

## Persistence
Retain the tested middle-gate orientation S, exact cover T, the distinguished endpoint R for G_3 or L for G_4, its actual rail role/neighbor, and the exact R435 trigger: R834 reverse seam, decreasing Q-contact pair, or crossed-gate reverse selected state. Retain the resulting R435 geometric output.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim an R435 output alone closes H or that every exact cover has the same output type. Does not transport selected states between covers. Does not claim middle-gate deletion itself is impossible. The theorem only says every such exact cover is Reverse-Ear active.

## Proof
Fix S=(s_0,s_1) an orientation of G_3={a,c}. By accepted R649/P728, the tested orientation S is head-signed by both physical endpoints L,R; in particular (R,s_0,s_1) is tight. Let T=T_1 sqcup T_2 be any exact cover of H-S. R429 makes both rails nontrivial. Let K be the oriented T-rail containing R. If R is the terminal vertex of K, write y for its predecessor. In the literal spanning three-cover H=S sqcup T_1 sqcup T_2, R is a head witness on S, so accepted R834 HEAD-END gives the tight reverse outer seam (s_0,R,y). Compare this trimer with K: its consecutive K-contacts are R then y, while K selects y then R. Thus accepted R435 is in its adjacent-state reversal output. Suppose instead R is not terminal and let y be its successor on K. The deleted vertices are exactly G_3, so y lies in Q or in G_4. If y=q_i is in Q, then K has consecutive Q-contacts R=q_m then q_i with i<m, strictly decreasing in Q-order; accepted R435 applies to K relative to Q. If y=x lies in G_4, let x' be its mate. The accepted crossed right gate in R621 gives (x',x,R) tight: explicitly (b,z,R) and (z,b,R) are both tight, so whichever x occurs, choose the other as x'. This tight trimer contains the state x R, the reverse of the selected T-state R x. Comparing it with K, R435 again gives the adjacent-state reversal output. These cases exhaust the G_3 deletion cover. For G_4 the proof is the exact dual. R649/P728 says either tested orientation of G_4 is tail-signed by L,R, in particular by L. Let K be the T-rail containing L. If L is initial, with successor y, R834 TAIL-START gives a tight reverse outer seam whose first state reverses the selected L y state, so R435 applies. If L is not initial, let y be its predecessor. If y=q_i lies in Q, K encounters q_i then L=q_0 in decreasing Q-order, so R435 applies relative to Q. If y=x lies in G_3, let x' be its mate; the crossed left gate gives both (L,a,c) and (L,c,a) tight, hence (L,x,x') is tight and contains the reverse state L x of selected x L. R435 applies relative to K. Therefore every exact deletion cover of either crossed middle-gate pair is Reverse-Ear active.
