# R598 — High-side R24 roots absorb two rail vertices into exact enlarged deletion covers

## Statement
In the synchronized transitive R24 frame of R596, the unavoidable side-maximal roots x_L and x_R give literal exact two-covers
H-x_L = (q_1,q_0,a,c,b) sqcup (q_2,...,q_m)
and
H-x_R = (q_0,...,q_{m-2}) sqcup (b,a,c,q_m,q_{m-1}),
where (a,b,c) denotes the corresponding normalized trimer in each root. Thus a three-seam side-maximal root is not merely a collision or inward-star certificate: it re-roots the deleted singleton and absorbs exactly two named physical vertices from that end of Q into a tight rail of order five, while preserving the remaining Q interval literally. Both enlarged roots coexist as graph-intrinsic cover certificates in the original H.

## Hypotheses
Accepted common transitive R24 hard frame and pending R596 with m>=3.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration corollary. Exact literal cover and shortened-Q coordinates; no maximin normalization is asserted.

## Applicability
Provides the two one-sided enlarged roots that must be compared or iterated in the remaining long-middle branch.

## Persistence
The deleted labels x_L,x_R, absorbed endpoint pairs {q_0,q_1},{q_{m-1},q_m}, complete P5 orders, and untouched Q suffix/prefix remain literal.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert x_L=x_R. Does not say the order-five rail is globally longest or maximin. The two exact covers generally delete different vertices and therefore are not automatically simultaneous current covers of one deletion.

## Proof
R596 gives the tight path A_L=(q_1,q_0,a,c,b) on {q_0,q_1} union (X-{x_L}). The untouched suffix B_L=(q_2,...,q_m) is tight because it is an interval of Q. Their supports are disjoint and their union is V(H)-{x_L}; hence A_L sqcup B_L is a literal two-cover of H-x_L. It is exact: if H-x_L had a Hamilton tight path, that path together with the singleton (x_L) would be a spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. This proves the left formula and records that precisely q_0,q_1 were absorbed from Q.

Dually, R596 gives A_R=(b,a,c,q_m,q_{m-1}) and the untouched prefix B_R=(q_0,...,q_{m-2}). They partition H-x_R and are tight. A Hamilton path of H-x_R would again pair with singleton x_R to two-cover H, so this displayed cover is exact. Both are literal facts of the same H, although their deleted labels may differ.
