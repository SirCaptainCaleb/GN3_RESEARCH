# R1001 — Disjoint global extreme edges force crossed endpoint shields on one shared complement cover

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888. Let m0m1 be the globally minimum edge and M0M1 the globally maximum edge, and assume these two physical edges are vertex-disjoint. Put W=V(G)-{m0,m1,M0,M1}. By R998, pc_inc(G[W])=2; fix any exact two-cover U|V of W, with sources s_U,s_V and terminals t_U,t_V. For h,k in {0,1}, the path K_{h,k}=(m_{1-h},m_h,M_k,M_{1-k}) is increasing, so K_{h,k}|U|V is a spanning three-cover. Define terminal activity T_R to mean that for some k, L_R(t_R)<lambda(t_R M_k), and source activity S_R to mean that for some h, lambda(m_h s_R)<U_R(s_R). If T_U and S_V both hold, then for suitable independent choices h,k accepted R956 applied to the middle edge m_h->M_k of K_{h,k} gives a spanning two-cover of G. Likewise T_V and S_U cannot both hold. Hence every such U|V satisfies the crossed shield clauses (not T_U or not S_V) and (not T_V or not S_U). Equivalently: either both edges from t_U to {M0,M1} lie below L_R(t_U), or both edges from {m0,m1} to s_V lie above U_R(s_V); and symmetrically with U,V exchanged.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order R888 counterexample; its unique global minimum and maximum edges are vertex-disjoint; U|V is any exact two-cover of their four-vertex complement.

## Scope
Completeness-specific synchronized extreme-edge carrier lemma. Uses one common complement representative for four Hamilton P4 orientations and accepted R956 cooperative splicing.

## Applicability
Use to convert failure of all four global-extreme carrier splices into explicit pairwise endpoint shields on a fixed complement two-cover. The two endpoint choices are independent and no selected-state transport between complement representatives is used.

## Persistence
Retain the global extreme edges, W, one exact U|V, its four endpoints, the four carrier paths K_{h,k}, and which literal shield clause holds in each crossed direction.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prove the shield clauses are contradictory. Does not prescribe which disjunct holds. Does not cover the case where the global minimum and maximum edges share a vertex.

## Proof
Let mu=lambda(m0m1) be the global minimum edge label and Omega=lambda(M0M1) the global maximum. For every h,k in {0,1}, K_{h,k}=(m_{1-h},m_h,M_k,M_{1-k}) has consecutive labels mu, lambda(m_h M_k), Omega, hence is increasing. Its vertex support X={m0,m1,M0,M1} is independent of h,k. By R998, W=V(G)-X has exact path-cover number two; fix one exact two-cover U|V once and for all. Then K_{h,k}|U|V is a spanning increasing three-cover for every h,k. Write t_U for the terminal of U and s_V for the source of V. Suppose terminal activity T_U holds and source activity S_V holds. Choose k with L_U(t_U)<lambda(t_U M_k) and choose h with lambda(m_h s_V)<U_V(s_V). Apply accepted R956 to the three components A=U, C=K_{h,k}, B=V and to the selected middle carrier edge p->v=m_h->M_k. The first R956 seam condition is L_U(t_U)<lambda(t_U M_k)<U_K(M_k). Its left inequality is the chosen terminal activity, while U_K(M_k)=Omega because M_k->M_{1-k} is the global maximum edge, so the right inequality is automatic. The second seam condition is L_K(m_h)<lambda(m_h s_V)<U_V(s_V). Its right inequality is the chosen source activity, while L_K(m_h)=mu because m_{1-h}->m_h is the global minimum edge, so the left inequality is automatic. Hence R956 gives a spanning increasing two-cover of G, contradiction. Thus not(T_U and S_V), which is the first crossed shield clause. Interchanging U and V gives not(T_V and S_U). Finally, because all edge labels are distinct, failure of T_U means both lambda(t_U M_0),lambda(t_U M_1) are strictly below L_U(t_U); failure of S_V means both lambda(m_0 s_V),lambda(m_1 s_V) are strictly above U_V(s_V), and similarly after swapping U,V. This proves the equivalent shield formulation.
