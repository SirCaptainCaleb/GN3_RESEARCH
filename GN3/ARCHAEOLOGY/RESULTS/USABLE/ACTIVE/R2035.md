# R2035 — Tau=1 two-vertex isolated-X normal form

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** Slack workspace R2035 re-audit

## Repaired statement

In the retained source frame, suppose a literal exact two-cover `T` of `G=H-{A,C}` has block shape `X-B | X` and its isolated X-rail has exactly two vertices. Then the isolated rail is `{s,v}` for a retained source spoke `s`; both anchor caps

`(s,A,v)` and `(v,C,s)`

are tight. The other two retained source spokes lie in the unique X-block of the mixed rail, so that mixed rail has a source at its X-side endpoint.

The former claim that this entire tau=1 subcase is impossible is withdrawn.

## Proof

The isolated two-vertex X-rail contains at least one source spoke because `X={v,p,q,r}` contains only one non-source vertex `v`. Choose such a source `s` and call the other vertex `u`.

Corrected R2032 applies to this two-vertex source rail. It gives `u` non-source, hence within X necessarily `u=v`, and forces `(s,A,v)` and `(v,C,s)` tight.

The mixed rail contains every remaining X-vertex, namely the two source spokes other than `s`. Because its block pattern is `X-B` or `B-X`, its unique X-block lies at one rail endpoint. Therefore one of those two sources is the X-side endpoint of the mixed rail.

No cyclic rotation of any turn is used.

## Removed overclaim

The previous elimination proof tried to turn the two anchor caps and source turns into Hamilton P5/P6 paths using cyclic rotations of tight turns. R3 gives only complete reversal, so those recompletions are not certified. The exact normal form above is what survives.

## Dependencies

Trusted dependencies: corrected R2032 and the `X={v,p,q,r}` block shape.

Provisional dependencies: none.
