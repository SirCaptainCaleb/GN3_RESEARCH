# R850 — A common long-rail cut exposes the simultaneous depth-two four-witness singleton pair

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame in the beyond-order-ten range m>=6. Put u=q_1, v=q_2, d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}. Then one fixed contiguous cut of the physical long rail Q exposes a graph-intrinsic balanced opposite-sign singleton pair exactly on {q_2,q_{m-2}} with a simultaneous four-witness ledger: q_2 is tail-signed by both b,z and q_{m-2} is head-signed by both a,c. More precisely, the crossed frame forces S_L=(q_3,q_2) tail-signed by b,z and S_R=(q_{m-2},q_{m-3}) head-signed by a,c. Cutting C=Q[3,m-3] and applying R425 separately to S_L and S_R leaves the two named singleton supports with those inherited witnesses. The two supports are distinct for m>=6 and have opposite polarity, so they form a graph-intrinsic mass-two balanced pair. The retained static ledger contains the four sign turns and the one common Q-cut interval. No ancestry-bearing floor or payment-lineage membership is asserted.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621 frame, m>=6. Use accepted R3,R536,R425 and the crossed gate/endpoint-star data of R621.

## Scope
Corrected coordinate theorem. It preserves the simultaneous two-ended signed singleton geometry while retracting the unsupported floor/payment-lineage upgrade.

## Applicability
Use when an argument needs the exact physical pair {q_2,q_{m-2}}, its opposite singleton signs, or the common-cut four-witness ledger. Before invoking R432 or any other floor-only consumer, separately establish a certified signed-payment lineage for this pair.

## Persistence
Retain q_2 tail-signed by b,z; q_{m-2} head-signed by a,c; the common cut Q[3,m-3]; and the source rectangle certificates producing those signs.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim an ancestry-bearing floor, pair descent, strict payment progress, or R432 eligibility. Does not claim the four witness turns are contradictory or that the original long rail remains current in later continuations.

## Proof
Accepted R536 and the crossed endpoint/gate data give the left and right inward sign rectangles exactly as follows. R536 gives q_3q_2b and q_3q_2z. If buL were tight, then (b,u,L,c,a,z) together with Q[2,m] would two-cover H, so R3 gives Lub; similarly Luz. Thus S_L=(q_3,q_2) is tail-signed by b,z. Dually the crossed right gate and endpoint stars imply that Rsa and Rsc are bad by the analogous spanning-cover proposals, so R3 gives asR and csR; the right-end R536 clause gives a q_{m-2} q_{m-3} and c q_{m-2} q_{m-3}. Thus S_R=(q_{m-2},q_{m-3}) is head-signed by a,c. Now delete C=Q[3,m-3]. In S_L the deleted q_3 leaves the terminal singleton q_2, and R425 preserves each old tail witness b,z. In S_R the deleted q_{m-3} leaves the initial singleton q_{m-2}, and R425 preserves each head witness a,c. Since m>=6, q_2 and q_{m-2} are distinct. Their singleton supports are physically disjoint and oppositely signed, hence form a graph-intrinsic balanced pair of total mass two. Because the two R425 applications are separate signed-support rebirths rather than a certified pair descent, the proof concludes only this static pair and its witness/cut ledger, not floor status.
