# R846 — A 2x2 signed-dimer rectangle forces reverse seam, high block count, closure, or a pure head-to-tail exterior weave

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2. Let D=(p,q) be an oriented dimer and let A={a,c}, B={b,z} be four distinct vertices disjoint from D. Suppose D is head-signed by every vertex of A and tail-signed by every vertex of B: (a,p,q),(c,p,q),(p,q,b),(p,q,z) are tight. Let T=T_1 sqcup T_2 be a literal exact two-cover of H-V(D) with both rails nontrivial. Then at least one of the following holds. (HIGH) For some h in A,t in B, the R833 block count b_T(h,t)>2. (SEAM) T forces an R833/R834 source-visible reverse seam at one of a,c,b,z. (CLOSE) H has a spanning two-cover. (WEAVE) The two T-rail starts are exactly a,c, the two T-rail ends are exactly b,z, T selects no X-X state on X=A union B, and consequently each of a,c,b,z has its unique endpoint adjacency to a vertex of V(H)\\(V(D) union X). Thus in WEAVE the two rails each have the form A-start -> nonempty exterior block -> B-end, with the four X-exterior endpoint states simultaneous in one fixed cover.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1), pc(H)>2; D=(p,q) is disjoint from X={a,b,c,z}; A={a,c} are head witnesses and B={b,z} tail witnesses on the identical tested orientation D; T is one exact two-cover of H-D with both rails nontrivial.

## Scope
Order-free fixed-cover strengthening of the 2x2 opposite-polarity dimer rectangle. No smallest-counterexample minimality once T is supplied. No R508,R825,capture,payment,protected contact,Reverse Ear,path growth,or computation.

## Applicability
Preferred consumer of a full 2x2 same-dimer sign rectangle. In crossed m=6, an LT-AC R583 packet on D=(w,v) combines with accepted R658 tail witnesses b,z and enters this theorem immediately. The quiet residue is a rigid four-endpoint exterior weave rather than an anonymous P4 fan or R825 shape.

## Persistence
Retain D and tested orientation, witness bipartition A|B, the fixed exact cover T, all four cross-pair P4s h-D-t, every relevant R833 block count, and in WEAVE the exact start/end assignment plus the four selected X-exterior endpoint states.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim HIGH or SEAM alone closes H. Does not prescribe which B-end pairs with which A-start in WEAVE, nor the exterior block orders. Does not transport endpoint roles to another exact cover.

## Proof
For every h in A={a,c} and t in B={b,z}, the certified turns (h,p,q) and (p,q,t) concatenate to a tight P4 K_{h,t}=(h,p,q,t). Fix the exact cover T. If b_T(h,t)>2 for some cross pair, retain HIGH. Hence assume b_T(h,t)=2 for all four cross pairs. If any application below yields a reverse seam, retain SEAM. Fix h,t and apply accepted R833 to K_{h,t}. At block count two, outside a reverse seam the only alternatives are REVERSE-CHORD, in which T selects the directed state t h, or ANTI-ENDS, in which h and t are nonadjacent degree-one endpoints with h first and t last. We show REVERSE-CHORD cannot occur without a reverse seam. R833 gives b_T(h,t)=deg_T(h)+deg_T(t)-1=2 in the chord case, so {deg_T(h),deg_T(t)}={1,2}. If deg_T(t)=1, then because the selected chord is directed t h, the tail witness t is the first vertex of its T rail. But H=D sqcup T_1 sqcup T_2 is a literal spanning three-cover and t is a tail witness on D; accepted R834 TAIL-START forces a reverse outer seam, contrary to the seam-quiet supposition. If deg_T(h)=1, then the directed chord t h makes the head witness h the last vertex of its rail; accepted R834 HEAD-END again forces a reverse seam. Thus in the absence of SEAM every cross-pair R833 application is ANTI-ENDS. Therefore for each h in A and each t in B, h is a T-rail start and t is a T-rail end. Since T has exactly two rails and a,c are distinct, its two starts are exactly a,c. Likewise its two ends are exactly b,z. Now suppose T selects an X-X state. A state ac would join the two distinct rail starts and is impossible; similarly bz cannot join the two distinct rail ends. Every other X-X edge is a cross pair h t with h in A,t in B. Because h is a rail start and t a rail end, if they are adjacent on one T rail then that rail contains exactly the two vertices h,t. Its orientation must be h t: the reverse t h would make t a start and h an end, contradicting the established endpoint roles. But K_{h,t}=(h,p,q,t) is tight. Replacing the two-vertex T rail (h,t) by K_{h,t} and leaving the other T rail unchanged inserts exactly the deleted dimer D and gives two vertex-disjoint tight paths spanning H. This is CLOSE. Hence outside CLOSE no X-X state is selected. Finally each of a,c,b,z is a degree-one endpoint of a nontrivial T rail. Since it has no selected X-X neighbor, its unique selected neighbor lies in V(H)\\(V(D) union X). Thus the rails begin at a,c, end at b,z, and have nonempty exterior blocks between their X endpoints. This is WEAVE and proves the theorem.
