# R820 — Crossed 4+4 core orientations compress every deep gap to endpoint seam, middle-gate witness, or paired terminal blockers

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, L=q_0,u=q_1,s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, and N=Q[2,m-2]. The crossed gates give the four graph-intrinsic core P4s A_1=(u,L,a,c), A_2=(u,L,c,a), B_1=(b,z,R,s), B_2=(z,b,R,s), with every A_i disjoint from every B_k and together spanning the order-eight core. Fix a deep gap 3<=j<=m-3 and put q=q_{j+1}. Define U_j to mean at least one of (u,q_j,q_{j-1}) and (L,u,q_j) is tight. Define G_AC(j) to mean at least one of (q,c,a),(q,a,c) is tight, equivalently q is a head witness on one tested orientation of the crossed middle-gate edge {a,c}. When j<=m-4 define T_AC(j) to mean both (q_{j+2},q,a) and (q_{j+2},q,c) are tight. Then U_j OR G_AC(j) OR T_AC(j) holds; at the terminal deep gap j=m-3 the T_AC term is absent, so U_j OR G_AC(j) holds. Dually define V_j to mean (q,s,R) is tight or, when j<=m-4, (q_{j+2},q,s) is tight. Define G_BZ(j) to mean at least one of (z,b,q_j),(b,z,q_j) is tight, equivalently q_j is a tail witness on one tested orientation of {b,z}. Define T_BZ(j) to mean both (b,q_j,q_{j-1}) and (z,q_j,q_{j-1}) are tight. Then V_j OR G_BZ(j) OR T_BZ(j) holds. Thus every crossed deep gap has two simultaneous three-way compressions: on the {a,c} side, endpoint/deep-Q seam or middle-gate witness or a paired right-terminal R583 packet; on the {b,z} side, the exact dual with a paired left-terminal R583 packet.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted crossed R621; m>=6. Uses accepted R811 block-insertion obstruction and only the four crossed gate/endpoint-star P4s already graph-intrinsic in R621.

## Scope
Crossed-phase O6/R24 finite-state compression. Accepted-input theorem; does not depend on pending R817,R818,R812,R816. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R542,capture,payment,R436,or Reverse Ear.

## Applicability
Use at each deep gap before consuming individual R583 packets. G_AC or G_BZ is immediately admissible to accepted R682 because q or q_j lies outside X union {L,R}. T_AC and T_BZ are exact two-witness terminal-blocker packets of the accepted R583 type and may be retained simultaneously rather than paid. The quiet residue therefore consists only of the two endpoint/deep-Q seam states U_j and V_j plus explicit paired terminal packets.

## Persistence
Retain j,q,the four fixed P4 rails A_1,A_2,B_1,B_2,the exact R811 seam-window alternatives for each orientation,and whichever U/G/T disjuncts occur. GATE witnesses and paired terminal blockers are graph-intrinsic in the common crossed frame.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the same trichotomy branch occurs at adjacent gaps, that a GATE/R682 descendant closes H, or that paired terminal blockers are contradictions. U_j and V_j are disjunctions of two exact turns and must not be collapsed to one representative. At j=m-3 the absent q_{j+2} windows are not to be invented.

## Proof
The crossed R621 turns give A_1=(u,L,a,c), A_2=(u,L,c,a), B_1=(b,z,R,s), B_2=(z,b,R,s) as tight P4s, and any A_i together with any B_k is an exact two-cover of the fixed order-eight core. Fix a deep gap 3<=j<=m-3 and put q=q_{j+1}. Insert A_1 between q_j and q in N=Q[2,m-2]. Every old turn of A_1 and N remains tight; the only new consecutive triples are (q_{j-1},q_j,u), (q_j,u,L), (a,c,q), and, when j<=m-4, (c,q,q_{j+2}). If all present new turns were tight, the inserted path together with any disjoint B_k would be a spanning two-cover of H, impossible. Therefore at least one new turn is bad, and R3 yields one of the reversals (u,q_j,q_{j-1}), (L,u,q_j), (q,c,a), and, when present, (q_{j+2},q,c). The same insertion of A_2 yields one of (u,q_j,q_{j-1}), (L,u,q_j), (q,a,c), and, when present, (q_{j+2},q,a). If neither U_j nor G_AC(j) holds, the shared first two alternatives and the respective middle-gate alternatives are false, so both far-right reversals are tight, giving T_AC(j). At j=m-3 those far-right triples are absent, so U_j OR G_AC(j). Dually insert B_1 and B_2 at the same gap. Their new triples are respectively the two left boundary triples meeting q_j, the middle-gate triples through b,z, and the two right boundary triples meeting q; reversing at least one bad new turn gives the clauses A_j(b) OR (z,b,q_j) OR V_j and A_j(z) OR (b,z,q_j) OR V_j, with the far-side term omitted exactly at the terminal gap. If neither V_j nor G_BZ(j) holds, both A_j(b) and A_j(z) are forced, giving T_BZ(j). Thus the two stated trichotomies hold using only the graph-intrinsic crossed core paths, the old Q order, pc(H)>2 from the R621 frame, and R3.
