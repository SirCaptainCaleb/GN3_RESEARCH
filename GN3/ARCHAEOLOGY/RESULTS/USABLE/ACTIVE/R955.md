# R955 — Minimum rigid endpoint-Hall circuits force nonrigid chords; at k=5 the K4-e exception is impossible

## Statement
Retain accepted R944 in the k=5 uniform middle-layer residue on eleven vertices and choose F to be a minimum-cardinality Hall-deficient left family in the COMPLETE all-endpoint incidence graph. Then F necessarily contains a PORT-SWITCH. The clean PAIR-SWITCH alternative of R944 is impossible.

Indeed, suppose all endpoint cores of F have unique ports and S in F is a clean PAIR-SWITCH support. Write S={a,b,c,d,e} with its two disjoint realized endpoint pairs {a,b} and {c,d}. Root the factor-critical Hall family at S and choose one perfect matching M from F-{S} onto N(F). Let R_a=S-a, R_b=S-b, R_c=S-c, R_d=S-d, and let T_a,T_b,T_c,T_d be their distinct M-matched supports. Write x_a=T_a-R_a, ..., x_d=T_d-R_d. Unique ports force actual Hamilton endpoint pairs {x_a,b},{x_b,a},{x_c,d},{x_d,c} on the four matched supports. Moreover the endpoint-pair graph of every displayed support is a matching: two realized endpoint pairs sharing exactly one vertex would create a PORT-SWITCH at their common endpoint-deletion core.

For every possible equality pattern among x_a,x_b,x_c,x_d, these five support conditions are incompatible with the absence of a tight P6. Up to relabeling the exterior completion labels there are exactly the 15 restricted-growth patterns
0000,0001,0010,0011,0012,0100,0101,0102,0110,0111,0112,0120,0121,0122,0123.
Each corresponding exact boundary-SAT instance is UNSAT. Therefore clean PAIR-SWITCH cannot occur in F, leaving PORT-SWITCH as the only R944 alternative.

## Hypotheses
Uniform middle-layer residue with k=5, |V(H)|=11: every five-set is Hamiltonian and no six-set supports a tight P6. Complete all-endpoint incidence graph. F is a minimum-cardinality Hall-deficient left family. Use accepted R944 for the in-circuit PORT/clean-PAIR dichotomy and accepted R946 for common-base rooted currentization.

## Scope
Order-eleven strengthening of R944. It eliminates the clean PAIR-SWITCH alternative by the full four-way rooted matching fan. This is not a general-k theorem.

## Applicability
Use as the preferred k=5 Arm-B entrance. Start directly with a minimum-cardinality Hall obstruction containing a physical PORT-SWITCH. Clean PAIR-SWITCH is no longer a live order-eleven branch, though R946 remains a valid historical/simultaneous matching theorem. The next closure theorem must consume the in-circuit PORT-SWITCH into P6, R561, or a fully accounted Hall improvement.

## Persistence
Retain F and its complete endpoint neighborhood, the physical PORT-SWITCH core and its competing actual endpoint realizations. For the eliminated pair-switch branch retain S, R_a,R_b,R_c,R_d, one common root matching M, T_a,T_b,T_c,T_d, completion labels x_a,...,x_d, all 15 equality patterns, exact CNF encodings, and independent UNSAT certificates.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not consume PORT-SWITCH. Does not prove Uniform Middle-Layer Growth for k>5. Does not synchronize Hamilton orders across different supports. Does not say the four-way fan theorem holds without the no-PORT cleanliness condition. Does not alter the historical validity of R943, R944, or R946.

## Proof
Retain the k=5 order-eleven uniform residue and choose F to be a minimum-cardinality Hall-deficient left family in the complete all-endpoint incidence graph. Accepted R944 says that F contains either a PORT-SWITCH or, if every endpoint core has a unique port, a clean PAIR-SWITCH support. Suppose for contradiction that there is no PORT-SWITCH and that S in F is a clean PAIR-SWITCH.

Relabel the five vertices of S as {a,b,c,d,e} so that two actual Hamilton paths on S have disjoint endpoint pairs {a,b} and {c,d}. Under unique ports the endpoint-pair graph of any support is a matching: if two realized endpoint pairs {u,v} and {u,w} shared exactly one endpoint u, then the common endpoint-deletion core S-u would realize two distinct ports v,w. Thus S has no endpoint pair sharing one endpoint with either displayed pair; on five vertices there is no third pair disjoint from both, so these are exactly its two endpoint pairs.

Root the factor-critical Hall family at S. By accepted R946 choose one perfect matching M from F-{S} onto N(F). Put
  R_a=S-a, R_b=S-b, R_c=S-c, R_d=S-d.
These are four distinct endpoint neighbors of S. Let T_a,T_b,T_c,T_d be their distinct M-matched supports and write
  x_a=T_a-R_a, x_b=T_b-R_b, x_c=T_c-R_c, x_d=T_d-R_d.
Each x_u lies outside S. The incidence T_a-R_a is realized by an actual Hamilton path on T_a exposing completion x_a. Since R_a has the unique port b, that path has endpoint pair {x_a,b}. Similarly the four matched supports have required realized endpoint pairs
  T_a: {x_a,b},
  T_b: {x_b,a},
  T_c: {x_c,d},
  T_d: {x_d,c}.
Again, because there is no PORT-SWITCH, the complete endpoint-pair graph of each T_u is a matching.

It remains to show that these five support conditions force a P6 regardless of coincidences among x_a,x_b,x_c,x_d. Up to relabeling the exterior completion labels, their equality type is one of the Bell-number B_4=15 restricted-growth strings
  0000, 0001, 0010, 0011, 0012,
  0100, 0101, 0102, 0110, 0111,
  0112, 0120, 0121, 0122, 0123.
For a pattern with r distinct exterior labels use n=5+r vertices, with a,b,c,d,e=0,1,2,3,4 and the r exterior labels 5,...,4+r. The five supports are
  S={a,b,c,d,e},
  T_a={b,c,d,e,x_a},
  T_b={a,c,d,e,x_b},
  T_c={a,b,d,e,x_c},
  T_d={a,b,c,e,x_d}.
They remain pairwise distinct for every equality pattern.

Exact CNF encoding. Introduce one Boolean turn variable for every complete-reversal pair of ordered triples, with the lexicographically smaller orientation positive. For every ordered six-tuple forbid simultaneous tightness of its four consecutive turns. For each of the five displayed supports introduce one endpoint-pair indicator E_Q for each unordered pair Q of its vertices. For every one of the 120 orders O of that support, with endpoint pair Q and three consecutive turn literals t_1,t_2,t_3, add
  not t_1 OR not t_2 OR not t_3 OR E_Q.
Thus every actual Hamilton order activates its physical endpoint-pair indicator. For every two endpoint pairs Q,Q' sharing exactly one vertex add
  not E_Q OR not E_Q',
which is exactly the no-PORT matching condition on the endpoint-pair graph. Finally require actual existence of each named endpoint pair, not merely its indicator: for each required pair use the 12 vertex orders with those endpoints, introduce one witness variable per order, make each witness imply its three turn literals, and require at least one of the 12 witnesses. There are six such existence requirements: two on S and one on each T_u. No Hamiltonicity or endpoint restriction is imposed on any unrelated five-set.

The resulting instances have, according to r, 182 to 374 variables and 1,692 to 61,452 clauses. The Integrator independently generated all fifteen CNFs from the preceding definition and solved them with a standalone watched-literal DPLL implementation, separate from the DR17.45 solver and proof trees. The solver uses exact unit propagation, chooses an unassigned variable of maximum static sum of 2^{-|C|} over its incident clauses, breaks ties by variable index, and branches false before true. It was cross-checked against exhaustive truth-table search on 100 random CNFs with up to seven variables, with no disagreement. Every one of the fifteen pair-switch instances is UNSAT. The independent search-node counts, in the pattern order above, are
  21387,14915,15115,14641,13447,
  16947,13385,13379,13385,18107,
  13373,13377,13371,14645,13981,
with maximum decision depth at most 27.

Therefore the assumed clean PAIR-SWITCH packet cannot exist in any P6-free boundary tournament. R944 leaves only PORT-SWITCH. Hence every minimum-cardinality Hall obstruction in the k=5 uniform residue contains a physical PORT-SWITCH, proving R955.
