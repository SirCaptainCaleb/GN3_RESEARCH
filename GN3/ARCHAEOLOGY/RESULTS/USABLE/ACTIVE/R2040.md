# R2040 — Local six-vertex absorber insufficiency

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace R2040

## Statement

The repaired two-vertex source-rail packet does not locally force the former R2036 six-vertex absorber. There is a globally edge-ordered Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament on `{A,C,s,t,r,u}` in which

- `(A,s,C)`, `(A,t,C)`, `(A,r,C)` are tight;
- `{A,C,s,u}` is P4-free;
- corrected R2032 turns `(s,A,u)`, `(u,C,s)`, `(C,u,A)` are tight;
- the six-set has no tight Hamilton P6.

Hence any elimination of the surviving short-rail branch must use additional anchored, rail, or global structure.

## Counterexample

Use the R887 edge-order model and order the 15 ordinary edges increasingly as

`ur < At < rs < us < Ct < rt < st < AC < Cu < ut < Ar < As < Cr < Cs < Au`.

A turn `(x,m,y)` is tight exactly when edge `xm` precedes `my`.

The required turns follow from

`As<Cs`, `At<Ct`, `Ar<Cr`, `As<Au`, `Cu<Cs`, `Cu<Au`.

The four-set `{A,C,s,u}` has no tight Hamilton P4. Exhaustive increasing-edge enumeration on the six-set gives no Hamilton P6 and exactly the following 12 Hamilton P5 orders:

`A t r C s`; `C t s A u`; `C t r A s`; `C t r A u`; `s r t u A`; `t A r C s`; `t r A s C`; `r s t u A`; `r t s A u`; `u r A s C`; `u r t s A`; `u r t s C`.

Each fails extension by its omitted vertex at both ends. Since every Hamilton P6 would yield a Hamilton P5 after deleting an endpoint, no Hamilton P6 exists.

An independent audit re-enumerated the edge-order model and confirmed exactly 0 P4s on `{A,C,s,u}`, exactly these 12 P5s on the six-set, and 0 P6s.
