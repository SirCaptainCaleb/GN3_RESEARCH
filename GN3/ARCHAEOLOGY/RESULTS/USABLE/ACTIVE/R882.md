# R882 — c_X=2 shape D closes adjacent placements or forces a typed two-end mate clause

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R862 sigma_G(T)=0 inward-anchor five-cell frame with m>=6 and suppose the accepted R866 forest is c_X=2 shape D: one T rail consists of two Q blocks G_i-G_j with i<j, while the other has type X_L-G_k-X_R on the remaining Q block. Apply accepted R867. Outside its explicit Reverse-Ear outputs, adjacent Q-pair placements {i,j}={0,1} or {1,2} restore q_2,q_{m-2} to a literal spanning two-cover and are impossible in a counterexample. Hence the sole monotone residue is the nonadjacent skip G_0-G_2 together with X_L-G_1-X_R. Writing X_L=(x_1,x_2), X_R=(y_1,y_2) in their actual T orders, the untouched G_0-G_2 rail together with K=(q_2,x_1,x_2,G_1,y_1,y_2,q_{m-2}) is a literal spanning two-path proposal whose complete hole set is exactly {(q_2,x_1,x_2),(y_1,y_2,q_{m-2})}. Therefore the reverse literals A=(x_2,x_1,q_2) or B=(q_{m-2},y_2,y_1) form a physical mate clause. If X_L=G_3={a,c} and X_R=G_4={b,z}, accepted R649 and R523 make either realized branch a labelled Hamilton P4. If X_L=G_4 and X_R=G_3, accepted R649 and R542 make either realized branch an explicit two-witness R542-ready packet. Thus shape D has no quiet monotone residue: adjacent Q pairs close, while the unique skip placement forces a two-end clause whose realized branch is uniformly P4-type or uniformly R542-type according to the left/right X identity.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R862 sigma=0 frame; m>=6; accepted R866 shape D; accepted R867,R649,R3,R523,R542.

## Scope
Current-interface rebase of the accepted historical shape-D theorem R680. Noncircular crossed R24 restoration; pure literal restoration plus one physical two-hole clause. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,generic R358, payment, capture, or R436.

## Applicability
After R866 identifies shape D, discard adjacent Q-pair placements by literal Q restoration. In the unique G_0-G_2 skip placement, preserve the actual X_L-G_1-X_R order and use the two deleted inward anchors to obtain the displayed physical mate clause. The identity of X_L determines whether both branches are P4-type or R542-ready.

## Persistence
Retain the exact T rails, the physical Q-cell identities, actual orientations of X_L and X_R, the untouched G_0-G_2 rail, the two deleted anchors q_2,q_{m-2}, and the complete two-hole proposal K with both reverse clause literals.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that shape D itself closes in the nonadjacent skip placement. Does not choose which mate-clause literal is tight. Does not infer payment, protected contact, long-path growth, or any consequence beyond the stated P4/R542 typing.

## Proof
Let v=q_2 and d=q_{m-2}. By accepted R867, outside its explicit Reverse-Ear outputs the Q-Q rail occurs in physical increasing order G_i-G_j. If (i,j)=(0,1), replace its selected G_0-G_1 transition by the old Q segment G_0,v,G_1 and then append d. The resulting path is exactly Q[0,m-2], hence tight for every m>=6. The other T rail X_L-G_2-X_R is untouched and tight. These two paths span H, contradiction. If (i,j)=(1,2), prepend v and restore d between G_1,G_2; the resulting path is Q[2,m], while X_L-G_0-X_R is untouched. Again H is two-covered. Thus only (i,j)=(0,2) can remain. Write the surviving source rails as G_0-G_2 and X_L-G_1-X_R, with X_L=(x_1,x_2) and X_R=(y_1,y_2) in their actual T orders. Consider K=(v,x_1,x_2,G_1,y_1,y_2,d). Inside X_L-G_1-X_R every turn is inherited from T. Prepending v creates exactly the endpoint turn h_L=(v,x_1,x_2), and appending d creates exactly h_R=(y_1,y_2,d); there are no other new turns, even when m=6 and G_1 is the singleton q_3, because the entire source subrail X_L-G_1-X_R remains untouched internally. The support of K together with the untouched G_0-G_2 rail is all of V(H). If h_L and h_R were both tight, these two paths would two-cover H. Hence by R3 at least one reverse A=(x_2,x_1,v) or B=(d,y_2,y_1) is tight. The tested dimers in A and B are exactly the reverse orientations of the two X blocks. R649 supplies an R542-ready fixed head polarity on either orientation of G_3={a,c} and fixed tail polarity on either orientation of G_4={b,z}. Suppose X_L=G_3 and X_R=G_4. Then A is a tail certificate on a reverse G_3 dimer, opposite its fixed head certificate, so R523 gives a labelled tight P4. Likewise B is a head certificate on a reverse G_4 dimer, opposite its fixed tail certificate, and R523 gives a labelled P4. Thus either clause branch is P4-type. If instead X_L=G_4 and X_R=G_3, then A has the same tail polarity as the fixed G_4 packet, with new witness v outside the R649 short carrier; it therefore supplies a two-witness R542 packet. Similarly B is same-head on the reverse G_3 dimer with new witness d outside its R649 carrier, so R542 applies. Thus either clause branch is R542-type. This exhausts shape D.
