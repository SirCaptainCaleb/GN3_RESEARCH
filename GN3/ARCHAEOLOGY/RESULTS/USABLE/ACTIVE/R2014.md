# R2014 — The distinguished D_v path exposes a canonical v-to-endpoint closure

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789414626.763839`

## Statement

If the distinguished neutral path `D_v` of R2012 exists, its opposite F-only endpoint is an IN-copy `w,in` with `w!=v`. After toggling `D_v` to its J-state, `v,out` and `w,in` are free, so `v->w` is a canonical nonloop exact-two-cover-cardinality closure candidate.

Whenever the toggled state is a literal tight three-forest, the only new physical obligations of this closure are the endpoint seams at `v,w` and the possibility that `v,w` lie on the same rail.

## Proof

R2012 gives `D_v` as a delta-zero alternating path in the split bipartite graph `OUT union IN`, with one endpoint `v,in` incident only to a J-only edge and the opposite endpoint incident only to an F-only edge.

Delta zero means that `D_v` has equally many F-only and J-only edges, hence even length. An even path in the OUT/IN bipartite graph has both endpoints on the same shore. Since one endpoint is `v,in`, the opposite endpoint is therefore some `w,in`. The two endpoints of the path are distinct split vertices, so `w!=v`.

Start from the F-normalized pre-loss matching `M`. R1060 gives `v,out` free. Toggling `D_v` from its F-state to its J-state preserves matching cardinality, matches `v,in`, frees `w,in`, and does not touch `v,out`. Thus in the toggled matching `M'`, both `v,out` and `w,in` are free.

Adding the ordinary nonloop edge `v->w` raises the matching cardinality by exactly one, from three-forest cardinality to exact two-cover cardinality.

Assume now that `M'` is a literal tight three-path forest. Since `v,out` is free, `v` is terminal on its rail; since `w,in` is free, `w` is the source of its rail. Adding `v->w` creates new turns only at the two endpoints: the predecessor seam at `v`, and the successor seam at `w` when a successor is selected. If those seams are tight and the endpoint rails are distinct, the new edge joins two path components and yields a literal tight two-path forest. If the endpoints lie on the same rail, it closes exactly that rail into a directed cycle.
