# R807 — Each crossed seven-vertex puncture core exposes its exceptional outer endpoint in an exact two-cover

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, endpoints L=q_0,R=q_m, first inward vertices u=q_1,s=q_{m-1}, and crossed middle gate M_S={{a,c},{b,z}}. Put C_L=X union {L,s,R} and C_R=X union {L,u,R}. Then C_L has a literal exact two-cover by two nonempty tight paths in which R is an endpoint of one rail, and C_R has a literal exact two-cover in which L is an endpoint of one rail. More precisely, LEFT: apply R518 to the tight trimer (L,c,a) with fourth vertex s. If {L,c,a,s} supports a Hamilton P4 P, then P sqcup (b,z,R) is an exact two-cover of C_L exposing R. Otherwise R518 forces the reverse contact (c,L,s) tight, and (c,L,s) sqcup (a,b,z,R) is an exact two-cover of C_L exposing R. RIGHT: apply R518 to the tight trimer (b,z,R) with fourth vertex u. If {b,z,R,u} supports a Hamilton P4 P, then (L,a,c) sqcup P is an exact two-cover of C_R exposing L. Otherwise R518 forces the reverse contact (u,R,z) tight, and (L,a,c,b) sqcup (u,R,z) is an exact two-cover of C_R exposing L.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R621 frame; accepted universal four-cell theorem R518.

## Scope
Fixed seven-vertex local endpoint-exposure lemma. No minimality, R803/R806, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,capture,payment,R436,or computation.

## Applicability
Use in the R803/R806 LEFT and RIGHT puncture cores whenever a two-rail bracketing argument needs the exceptional physical endpoint R (left core) or L (right core) to be available as a rail endpoint. Together with the previously explicit core covers, this removes the sole endpoint exception in the contiguous-middle minimum-MULTI bracketing analysis.

## Persistence
Retain the physical core C_L or C_R, the R518 branch, and the displayed exact endpoint-exposing two-cover with its literal rail orders.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert a unique endpoint-exposing cover, Hamiltonicity of the seven-vertex core, or closure of H. Does not use or imply R572 by itself.

## Proof
LEFT. Accepted R621 gives the tight trimer (L,c,a), the tight trimer (b,z,R), and the tight P4 (a,b,z,R) as a prefix of (a,b,z,R,s). Apply accepted R518 to C=(L,c,a) with fourth vertex s. If the four-set {L,c,a,s} is Hamilton-P4, choose any Hamilton tight path P on it. Its support is disjoint from {b,z,R}, so P and (b,z,R) are two disjoint nonempty tight paths covering C_L, with R an endpoint of the second rail. If the four-set is P4-free, R518 forces the reverse contact (c,L,s) tight. The two tight paths (c,L,s) and (a,b,z,R) are disjoint, nonempty, and their supports partition C_L; R is an endpoint of the second rail. Thus C_L has the required endpoint-exposing exact two-cover. RIGHT. Accepted R621 gives the tight trimer (b,z,R), the tight trimer (L,a,c), and the tight P4 (L,a,c,b). Apply R518 to C=(b,z,R) with fourth vertex u. In the Hamilton-P4 branch, a Hamilton path P on {b,z,R,u} together with (L,a,c) exactly two-covers C_R and exposes L. In the no-P4 branch, R518 forces the reverse contact (u,R,z) tight. Then (L,a,c,b) and (u,R,z) are disjoint nonempty tight paths covering C_R, with L an endpoint of the first rail. This proves both assertions.
