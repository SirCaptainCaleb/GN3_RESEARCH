# R2026 — Cap-guided one-cut/two-join surgery from a source-anchor lift

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace R2026

## Statement

In the audited R2025 source-anchor lift

`N=(A,s,C) | R | Q`,

let the nonanchor rail be `R=(u_0,...,u_k)` with `k>=1`. Then counterexample status forces at least one of the following:

1. the source-facing seam `(s,C,u_0)` is bad;
2. the source-facing seam `(u_k,A,s)` is bad;
3. `k>=2` and both rail-facing endpoint seams `(C,u_0,u_1)` and `(u_{k-1},u_k,A)` are bad.

By R3 these alternatives yield, respectively, the tight reverse cap `(u_0,C,s)`, the tight reverse cap `(s,A,u_k)`, or both tight reverse rail-facing caps `(u_1,u_0,C)` and `(A,u_k,u_{k-1})`.

## Proof

The audited R2025 lift is a literal tight spanning three-forest of `H` whose anchor rail is the retained source trimer `(A,s,C)`. Fix an edge `u_i->u_{i+1}` of `R`, delete it, and add the two cross joins

`u_k->A` and `C->u_0`.

The resulting non-`Q` component has the literal directed order

`u_{i+1},...,u_k,A,s,C,u_0,...,u_i`.

Thus the operation is acyclic. Together with the untouched rail `Q`, it has exact two-cover cardinality and spans `H` whenever every newly created seam is tight.

Write the two source-facing seams as

`L=(s,C,u_0)` and `R_s=(u_k,A,s)`.

If `k=1`, cut the sole edge `u_0->u_1`. Both rail-facing seams disappear: `u_1` begins the left fragment and `u_0` ends the right fragment. The only new seams are `R_s=(u_1,A,s)` and `L=(s,C,u_0)`. If both were tight, the displayed recombination together with `Q` would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting counterexample status. Hence at least one of `L,R_s` is bad.

Now suppose `k>=2` and, toward the third alternative, that both source-facing seams are tight. Cut the first edge `u_0->u_1`. Then the `C->u_0` side has no rail-facing seam because `u_0` is terminal, while the `u_k->A` side retains exactly the additional seam `(u_{k-1},u_k,A)`. If that seam were tight too, the surgery would give a spanning exact two-cover, impossible. Hence `(u_{k-1},u_k,A)` is bad.

Dually, cut the last edge `u_{k-1}->u_k`. Then `u_k` begins the left fragment, so the `u_k->A` side has no rail-facing seam, while the `C->u_0` side retains `(C,u_0,u_1)`. Counterexample status forces `(C,u_0,u_1)` bad.

This proves the trichotomy. In each bad seam all three physical vertices are distinct by the three-forest decomposition, so boundary antisymmetry R3 gives the stated complete-reversal tight cap.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: `A7C3/RESULTS/USABLE/ACTIVE/R2025.md`, R3, and the smallest-counterexample hypothesis that `H` has no spanning exact two-cover.

Provisional dependencies: none.

This is a genuine cap-guided physical surgery. It applies to either nonanchor rail of an R2025 lift whenever that rail contains an edge. It does not by itself close the remaining branch when the third alternative occurs; there the two reverse rail-facing caps become structured input for a subsequent exchange or recompletion.
