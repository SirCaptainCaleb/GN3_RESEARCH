# R428 — Fixed-Singleton Opposite-Support Descent to the Both-Singleton Floor

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let (alpha;(p)),(T;beta) be a balanced opposite-sign pair with one singleton support p and a nontrivial opposite support T. There is a finite chosen certificate-retaining continuation that preserves the singleton support p and yields either a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing balanced pair with both supports singleton. At each nonclosing stage, take an at-most-two-path cover of the outside reservoir. If it contains a nontrivial path, the One-Rail Unsigned-End Refund R175 strictly shortens T while avoiding p; if the reservoir consists of two singleton components, combine them as a tight dimer and apply the same refund; if the reservoir is a single vertex, the two seam tests of the literal candidate (p,u,T) give either a spanning two-cover or a reverse-trimer certificate disjoint from p that strictly shortens T. Hence every nonclosing repeat preserves p and strictly decreases |T|, so the process terminates at the both-singleton floor. The head/tail dual is exact.

## Hypotheses
H is a smallest Strong counterexample; a balanced opposite-sign pair is present with one support the singleton (p) and the other support T nontrivial. The chosen descent retains the singleton side and uses exact outside-reservoir covers.

## Scope
Strong Level-(1), arbitrary order. This is the historical singleton/nontrivial direct-descent package used by Complete Floor Pair Steering.

## Applicability
Use after any operation has installed a desired singleton coordinate in an ancestry-bearing balanced pair. It pays the opposite support to singleton width while keeping that coordinate fixed, unless a spanning two-cover occurs.

## Persistence
The singleton support p is unchanged throughout the chosen nonclosing continuation. Retain nested descendants of T, the graph-intrinsic sign witnesses supplied by Signed-Interval Rebirth, and the ordered trimer/outside-boundary refund ledger. The witness on T may shift when certified rebirth requires it.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not preserve an unrelated source cover or all original witnesses; does not make pair mass globally monotone across arbitrary resets; does not close the final both-singleton floor; does not claim every historical refund marker remains selected.

## Proof
Let O be the outside reservoir. Minimality gives an at-most-two-path cover. Any nontrivial outside component triggers R175. Two singleton components are combined into a dimer. The sole remaining one-vertex reservoir is handled by the exact two seam tests of (p,u,T); every bad case is either an immediate two-cover or a physical marker disjoint from p that clips two unsigned T vertices. Iterate.
