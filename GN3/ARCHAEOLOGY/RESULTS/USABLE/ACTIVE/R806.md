# R806 — The crossed arbitrary-order frame renormalizes to one fixed order-ten exact-two-cover core plus the middle rail

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame in the beyond-order-ten range m>=6, with X={a,b,c,z}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), L=q_0, u=q_1, v=q_2, M=Q[3,m-3], d=q_{m-2}, s=q_{m-1}, and R=q_m. Put G=H[X union {L,u,v,d,s,R}]. Then |V(G)|=10, V(H)=V(G) disjoint-union V(M), M is a tight Hamilton path on its support, and pc(G)=2. Consequently every exact two-cover G=A sqcup B gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover H=A sqcup B sqcup M. Moreover the three bounded deletion supports appearing in accepted R803, D_L={d,v,u}, D_R={s,d,v}, and D_F={u,v,d,s}, all lie in this same fixed core G. Thus every R803 exact complement cover is a recompletion of one bounded puncture of G coupled to the unchanged middle rail M; in particular the R803/R804 MULTI branch is intrinsically a same-cover coupling between M and G-D, not an arbitrary-order outer residue.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R621 frame with m>=6 and smallest-counterexample minimality R4. The final interpretation of the three named bounded punctures uses accepted R803.

## Scope
Elevator renormalization of the crossed beyond-ten R24 frame. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,capture,payment,R436,or computation.

## Applicability
Use before classifying MULTI crossings. Replace the variable-size outer world by the fixed induced ten-vertex core G. Choose and retain an exact two-cover A sqcup B of G; then M sqcup A sqcup B is a canonical minimum three-cover of H. Analyze R803 MULTI by comparing its exact cover of H-D against the punctured geometry of this fixed core, rather than by treating its M-crossings independently.

## Persistence
Retain the physical ten-set X union {L,u,v,d,s,R}, the middle interval M, the exact equality pc(G)=2, one chosen exact two-cover A sqcup B of G, and the fact that D_L,D_R,D_F are all subsets of G while M is untouched.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert G is a smallest counterexample or permit use of the order-ten counterexample theorem R191 on G. Does not classify the exact two-covers of G, force endpoint universality in G, or by itself consume MULTI. Does not claim different R803 deletion-cover representatives coexist. The conclusion is a representation/renormalization theorem: fixed order-ten pc=2 core plus one long tight rail.

## Proof
Because m>=6, the six Q-coordinates L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2,d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1},R=q_m are pairwise distinct. The four-set X is disjoint from V(Q), so G has exactly ten vertices. The remaining Q vertices are exactly M=Q[3,m-3]; hence V(H)=V(G) disjoint-union V(M). Since Q is tight, its interval M is a tight Hamilton path on V(M), including the singleton case m=6. In particular M is nonempty, so G is a proper induced subsystem of H. Accepted smallest-counterexample minimality R4 therefore gives pc(G)<=2. Suppose pc(G)=1. Then G has a Hamilton tight path P_G. The two disjoint tight paths P_G and M cover all vertices of H, giving pc(H)<=2, contradicting the R4 conclusion pc(H)=3. Therefore pc(G)=2. Choose any exact two-cover G=A sqcup B. Then A,B,M are three disjoint tight paths covering H. Since pc(H)=3, this is a literal minimum three-cover. Finally, accepted R803 uses only D_L={d,v,u}, D_R={s,d,v}, or D_F={u,v,d,s}; each is visibly contained in V(G), while M is disjoint from all three. Hence every R803 recompletion deletes a bounded subset of the same fixed ten-core and leaves the same middle rail untouched. This proves the stated renormalization and the interpretation of MULTI as M-to-(G-D) coupling.
