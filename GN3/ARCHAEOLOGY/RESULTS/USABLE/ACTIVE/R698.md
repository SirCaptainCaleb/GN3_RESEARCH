# R698 — Paired crossed middle-gate orientations cannot both collapse to cross-end witness sandwiches

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with endpoints L=q_0 and R=q_m. Fix any exact two-cover T of H-{a,c}. Apply R696 simultaneously to the two R649 X-complementary packets S=(a,c), K=(z,c,a), e=z, witnesses {L,R}, and S=(c,a), K=(b,a,c), e=b, witnesses {L,R}. The SANDWICH branch cannot occur for both packets in the same T. Indeed it would require T to contain both L-z-R (or its reverse) and L-b-R (or its reverse) as consecutive tight rail trimers, equivalently both distinct vertices z and b would have T-neighbor set {L,R}; no disjoint union of paths can contain that configuration. Consequently, in every exact H-{a,c} two-cover, at least one of z,b lies in the R696 END or FREE branch: either that complementary X singleton is a rail endpoint and supplies a literal one/two-hole spanning proposal plus capture, or it has a selected exterior crossing that avoids both L and R and hence can be used to capture the singleton with either endpoint as the retained sign witness. Dually, in every exact two-cover of H-{b,z}, applying R696 to S=(b,z), e=c and S=(z,b), e=a shows that at least one of c,a lies in END or FREE. Thus each crossed middle-gate deletion pair has an unavoidable non-sandwich X-complementary packet: a physical mate geometry or a selected X-exterior crossing simultaneously avoiding both physical Q endpoints.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame. T is any exact two-cover of H-{a,c} or H-{b,z}. Use the two X-complementary R649 packets on the two orientations of the deleted middle-gate dimer and pending R696.

## Scope
Crossed-frame simultaneous-cover consequence of the order-free R696 trichotomy. It uses only the two opposite tested orientations that delete the same physical X pair and the fact that a two-path cover is a path forest. No five-cell, order bound, reverse-ear, protected-contact, or generic growth machinery is used.

## Applicability
When working in an exact deletion cover of one crossed middle-gate pair, inspect both oriented R649 packets together rather than spending one orientation in isolation. At least one complementary X vertex gives either endpoint mate geometry or a crossing avoiding both L and R. The two cross-end-sandwich residues cannot coexist.

## Persistence
Durable simultaneous-orientation refinement of R649. It converts the shared witness pair {L,R} into a non-sandwich guarantee on at least one of the two complementary X coordinates for each middle-gate deletion.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert which complementary X vertex is non-sandwich. Does not assert END is impossible. Does not assert the resulting mate clause or dual-endpoint-free crossing alone closes H.

## Proof
Consider T, an exact two-cover of H-{a,c}. Accepted R649 gives two X-complementary two-witness short-carrier packets on this same deletion: for tested S=(a,c), the tight carrier (z,c,a) has complementary singleton z and witnesses L,R; for tested S=(c,a), the tight carrier (b,a,c) has complementary singleton b and witnesses L,R. Apply R696 to both packets using the same T. Suppose for contradiction that both are in SANDWICH. Then z has exactly the two T-neighbors L,R, and b also has exactly the two T-neighbors L,R. Hence the selected-state graph of T contains the four distinct edges Lz,zR,Rb,bL. These form a cycle on L,z,R,b. But the selected-state graph of a two-path cover is a disjoint union of paths and is therefore acyclic. Contradiction. Thus at least one of the two packets is not SANDWICH. By the exhaustive R696 trichotomy it is END or FREE, with exactly the stated mate/capture or dual-witness-free crossing conclusion. The proof for H-{b,z} is identical using the R649 packets (b,z) with carrier (z,b,c), complementary c, witnesses L,R, and (z,b) with carrier (b,z,a), complementary a, witnesses L,R.
