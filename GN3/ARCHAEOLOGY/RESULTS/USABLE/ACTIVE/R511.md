# R511 — Unique-transition recompletion has a sharp three-hole obstruction

## Statement

Retain a fixed R508 packet (D,S,Q,T,xy), write C=(V(H)\D)\S, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), and let tau(T) be the number of selected T-states crossing S|C. Suppose tau(T)=1.

(1) Since R508 forces tau(T')>=1 for every exact two-cover T' of H-D, T is transition-minimal. Its component word has exactly three maximal monochromatic blocks. R509(1) forces exactly two of them to be C-blocks, so after relabelling the rails one rail is a pure C-block K=(c_0,...,c_s), and the other is A followed by B or B followed by A, where A is the unique S-block and B=(b_0,...,b_r) is the other C-block.

(2) In the SC case A B, let q_i be the last vertex of A. If i=m, R509 gives the aligned one-hole proposal. If i<m, the proposal
U=(q_0,...,q_i,b_0,...,b_r),  V=(c_0,...,c_s,q_{i+1},...,q_m)
has complete uncertified-turn set
E_SC = { (q_{i-1},q_i,b_0) if i>=1 }
       union { (c_{s-1},c_s,q_{i+1}) if s>=1 }
       union { (c_s,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) if i<=m-2 }.

(3) In the CS case B A, let q_j be the first vertex of A. If j=0, R509 gives the aligned one-hole proposal. If j>0, the proposal
U=(q_0,...,q_{j-1},c_0,...,c_s),  V=(b_0,...,b_r,q_j,...,q_m)
has complete uncertified-turn set
E_CS = { (q_{j-2},q_{j-1},c_0) if j>=2 }
       union { (q_{j-1},c_0,c_1) if s>=1 }
       union { (b_r,q_j,q_{j+1}) if j<=m-1 }.

Each displayed set is nonempty and not all of its members are tight; therefore R3 makes the complete reversal of at least one displayed member tight. The nonaligned SC proposal has three holes exactly when s>=1 and 1<=i<=m-2. The nonaligned CS proposal has three holes exactly when s>=1 and 2<=j<=m-1. In particular j=m is an upper-end degeneracy with at most two holes, not a generic three-hole cell. The only formally zero-hole nonaligned parameter cells are m=1,i=0,s=0 in SC and m=1,j=1,s=0 in CS, and pc(H)>2 excludes their occurrence.

(4) This is a conditional normal form, not a realized countermodel. It does not assert that any Strong Level-(1), pc(H)>2 R508 packet with minimum transition count one exists. Consequently it does not by itself refute a theorem that might derive the missing R509 partner incidence for every transition-minimal cover from additional boundary-tournament structure. What it proves sharply is: if the minimum-one case occurs, the minimizing representative contains no second selected crossing, so its transition count and component word alone cannot supply the partner outward incidence. Any argument eliminating that case or forcing the partner must use additional graph-intrinsic information beyond transition minimality.


## Hypotheses

H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; (D,S,Q,T,xy) is the full fixed R508 packet; tau(T)=1. Blocks are maximal in the literal T-rail orders. Empty or singleton pieces omit nonexistent triples.


## Scope

Order-free conditional obstruction and logical fence for the transition-minimization strategy. No existence of a minimum-one packet is claimed.


## Applicability

When a chosen R508 cover has tau=1, use the exact SC/CS proposal and corrected hole-count cells. Treat the result as a conditional obstruction only; a genuine refutation of a full transition-minimality implication still requires a realizable packet or a theorem showing the minimum-one branch occurs.


## Persistence

Retain D,S,Q,T, the unique crossing with its orientation and exact Q-index, the pure C-rail K, mixed C-block B, the displayed proposal, and its complete hole set. Tight reversed holes are graph-intrinsic; word, block, and proposal roles are representative-relative.


## Exclusions / nonclaims

Does not construct a boundary tournament or R508 packet with tau=1. Does not show that every transition-minimal cover has tau=1. Does not rule out an additional Strong Level-(1) theorem forcing the R509 partner incidence or excluding the minimum-one branch. Does not turn a three-hole proposal into a two-literal clause.


## Proof

R508 applies to every exact two-cover of the fixed deletion H-D and forces at least one S|C crossing, so tau=1 is the global minimum. Across two nonempty rails, the number of maximal S/C blocks is tau+2=3. R509(1) supplies at least two C-blocks. Since S and C are nonempty, the types are exactly C,C,S, giving a pure C rail K and a mixed rail AB or BA.

In SC, supports of the two displayed paths partition V(H). All internal Q-, B-, and K-turns are tight. At Q-left|B, the T-order q_i,b_0,b_1 certifies the second seam turn when it exists, leaving only (q_{i-1},q_i,b_0). At K|Q-right there is no K-to-S selected state, leaving exactly the two displayed K|Q seam turns when their vertices exist. This proves the E_SC list. The CS inspection is dual: the retained T-order b_{r-1},b_r,q_j certifies that seam turn when it exists, while the Q-left|K seam contributes its two displayed turns and B|Q-right contributes (b_r,q_j,q_{j+1}). Thus E_CS is complete.

If a displayed set were empty, or if all its members were tight, the proposal would be a literal spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. R3 therefore reverses at least one bad displayed turn. Direct counting gives three SC holes iff s>=1, i>=1, and i<=m-2; it gives three CS holes iff s>=1, j>=2, and j<=m-1. This corrects the false claim that j<=m-1 follows merely from j>0. The two stated zero-hole parameter cells follow by simultaneously negating all three membership conditions and are impossible because the proposal would already be a spanning two-cover.

Finally, tau=1 says only that a hypothetical minimizing cover has a single crossing. No construction or prior dependency proves that such a packet is realizable in the full Strong Level-(1), pc(H)>2 class. Hence absence of a partner crossing inside this conditional representative is a valid data/word-level fence, but not a counterexample to any implication allowed to use additional graph structure.
