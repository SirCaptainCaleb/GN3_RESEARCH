# R808 — Every four-exterior long rail has a branch-independent ten-vertex pc=2 core

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, be a vertex-simple tight path with exactly four exterior vertices X=V(H)-V(Q). Put M=Q[3,m-3] and G=H[X union {q_0,q_1,q_2,q_{m-2},q_{m-1},q_m}]. Then |V(G)|=10, V(H)=V(G) disjoint-union V(M), M is a tight Hamilton path on its support, and pc(G)=2. Consequently every exact two-cover G=A sqcup B gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover H=A sqcup B sqcup M. This conclusion is independent of every endpoint-gate, matching-height, aligned/crossed, seam-badness, capture, payment, and reverse-ear choice.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; Q is a proper vertex-simple tight path with exactly four exterior vertices; m>=6. Uses accepted smallest-counterexample minimality R4 only.

## Scope
Order-free branch-independent core renormalization for the four-exterior long-rail regime. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358, gate classification, capture, payment, R436, Reverse Ear, or computation.

## Applicability
Apply upstream of the aligned/crossed R541 split. Replace the variable-length four-exterior frame by one fixed ten-vertex pc=2 core G plus the untouched Hamilton middle interval M. Downstream branch-specific arguments should be phrased as attempts to splice M onto an exact two-cover rail of G, or to prove that such a splice is impossible and exploit the resulting bad seam.

## Persistence
Retain X,Q,M,G, the exact equality pc(G)=2, and one chosen exact two-cover A sqcup B of G. No claim is made that a particular core cover is canonical.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close H. Does not say G is a smallest counterexample or permit R191 on G. Does not expose any prescribed endpoint in a core two-cover and does not certify a seam between M and G. It is a representation theorem only.

## Proof
Because Q has exactly four exterior vertices and m>=6, the six named Q vertices q_0,q_1,q_2,q_{m-2},q_{m-1},q_m are pairwise distinct and disjoint from X, so G has order ten. The remaining vertices of Q are exactly M=Q[3,m-3], hence V(H)=V(G) disjoint-union V(M). Since Q is tight, M is a tight Hamilton path on its support; when m=6 it is a singleton path. Thus M is nonempty and G is a proper induced subsystem of H. Accepted R4 gives pc(G)<=2. If pc(G)=1, let P_G be a Hamilton tight path of G. Then P_G and M are two vertex-disjoint tight paths whose supports partition V(H), yielding pc(H)<=2, contradicting the R4 conclusion pc(H)=3. Therefore pc(G)=2. Any exact two-cover G=A sqcup B then gives the literal minimum three-cover A sqcup B sqcup M of H.
