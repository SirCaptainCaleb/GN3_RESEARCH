# R946 — A minimal endpoint-Hall obstruction is factor-critical and splits into port-switch, pair-switch, or a rigid common-core bicycle

## Statement
Retain every accepted conclusion of R939: inclusion-minimal endpoint-Hall structure, the exact arbitrary rooted replacement ledger, the proper rigid fresh-core compression bound, and the switch-free common-core component normal form.

ROOTED ENDPOINT-FAN CURRENTIZATION. Let F be an inclusion-minimal Hall-deficient left family with N=N(F), and let S be any support in F. Let R_1,...,R_q be distinct endpoint-deletion cores of S arising from actual Hamilton endpoint realizations. Choose one perfect matching M from F-{S} onto N, whose existence is guaranteed by Hall minimality. For each i let T_i be the unique support matched by M to R_i. Then the T_i are distinct. Define
  M_i = M - {T_i R_i} + {S R_i}.
Then M_i is a matching of F-{T_i} onto all of N: every right core remains saturated, S becomes matched, and T_i is the unique unmatched left support. Thus every actual endpoint incidence S-R_i can be currentized into a near-perfect matching using one COMMON base matching M.

For i!=j, the symmetric difference M_i triangle M_j is exactly the four-edge alternating path
  T_i - R_i - S - R_j - T_j.
All other matching edges are identical. Therefore q endpoint realizations on one support give a q-way common-base exchange fan, not q unrelated Hall representations.

In particular, in the k=5 clean PAIR-SWITCH branch of accepted R944, the switched support has exactly four distinct endpoint-deletion cores coming from its two disjoint endpoint pairs. These four incidences admit four simultaneous rooted near-perfect matchings with the common-base length-four exchange geometry above.

## Hypotheses
Complete all-endpoint incidence graph. F is inclusion-minimal Hall-deficient. S belongs to F. R_i are distinct actual endpoint-deletion neighbors of S. The final four-way specialization assumes k=5, no PORT-SWITCH, and a clean PAIR-SWITCH support as in accepted R944.

## Scope
Strengthening of the canonical Hall parent by the exact simultaneous matching interface needed for in-circuit switch consumption. Pure matching theory once the physical endpoint incidences are supplied.

## Applicability
For a clean in-circuit pair-switch, root the Hall circuit at the switched support S, choose one common perfect matching of F-{S}, and retain all four currentized matchings M_i simultaneously. Any proposed path-order consumer may compare these representations, but their Hamilton path orders are not synchronized merely because the matching skeleton is. Use the full R939 neighborhood ledger for any support replacement.

## Persistence
Retain F,N,S, all actual endpoint-deletion cores R_i, one common root matching M, the distinct matched supports T_i, every M_i, and the exact alternating path T_i-R_i-S-R_j-T_j for each pair i,j. Do not replace the complete endpoint neighborhood by a selected endpoint pair.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not produce P_{k+1}, R561, or a Hall improvement by itself. Does not synchronize Hamilton orders attached to different incidences. Does not eliminate PORT-SWITCH. Does not say abstract matching currentization licenses arbitrary switched-edge path surgery.

## Proof
All pre-existing structural and replacement-ledger conclusions are exactly accepted R939, so only the endpoint-fan assertion needs proof.

Let F be inclusion-minimal Hall-deficient and N=N(F). Accepted R939 includes the factor-critical property: for every chosen root support S in F, the balanced bipartite graph between F-{S} and N has a perfect matching M.

Let R_1,...,R_q be distinct actual endpoint-deletion neighbors of S. Because M saturates every right core in N, each R_i is matched by M to a unique support T_i in F-{S}. Distinct R_i have distinct T_i, since M is a matching and one left support cannot be used on two matching edges.

For each i remove the matching edge T_i-R_i and add the actual incidence S-R_i. The result
  M_i=M-{T_iR_i}+{SR_i}
uses each right core exactly once: R_i remains matched, now to S, and all other right cores keep their M-edge. On the left, every support of F except T_i is used exactly once. Therefore M_i is a perfect matching from F-{T_i} onto N, equivalently a near-perfect matching of F which leaves exactly T_i unmatched. This proves simultaneous currentization of every endpoint incidence S-R_i from one common base matching M.

For i!=j, M_i and M_j agree on every base edge of M except T_iR_i and T_jR_j, and respectively contain SR_i and SR_j. Thus their symmetric difference is exactly
  {T_iR_i, R_iS, SR_j, R_jT_j},
which is the alternating path
  T_i-R_i-S-R_j-T_j.
No other edge differs. Hence all currentizations form a q-way common-base exchange fan.

Finally specialize to k=5 and a clean PAIR-SWITCH support S under no PORT-SWITCH. Accepted R944 places such S inside the minimum Hall obstruction. Under unique ports, distinct endpoint pairs on one support cannot share an endpoint: otherwise the common endpoint-deletion core would realize two different ports. On five vertices there can therefore be at most two distinct endpoint pairs, and PAIR-SWITCH means exactly two disjoint pairs. Their four endpoint deletions are distinct, so q=4 and the preceding construction yields the claimed four rooted near-perfect matchings. This proves R946.
