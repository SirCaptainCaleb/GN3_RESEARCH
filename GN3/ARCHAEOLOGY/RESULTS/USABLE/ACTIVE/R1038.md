# R1038 — Every v-rooted sharp first-loss augmenter has a forward X-mated source gate

**Audit state:** PASS
**Proof provenance:** Slack `#a7c3-workspace` root `1789312289.841599`; repaired after the direct reconstruction in R1037.

## Statement
Retain the v-rooted sharp first-loss cell with

`tau(F)=3`, `tau(J)=1`, `w(C_*)=2`,

and let `r` be the first zipper index with positive prefix ledger `S_r>0`. Assume the installed F-transition at `r` is the unique v-B transition from R1037.

Let `n_F` and `n_J` be the numbers of F-selected and J-selected X|B transition edges on the unique augmenter `C_*`. Then

`(n_F,n_J)` is either `(2,0)` or `(3,1)`.

In either case at least one of the two X-mated source gates from R1037 occurs strictly after the v-transition along `C_*`. If a source gate occurs before `v`, then it is numerically neutralized by the unique J-transition before the first-positive v-pivot.

## Proof
Since `w(C_*)=2`,

`n_F-n_J=2`.

Globally `F` has exactly three transitions and `J` exactly one, so

`0<=n_F<=3`, `0<=n_J<=1`.

Therefore the only possibilities are

`(n_F,n_J)=(2,0)` or `(3,1)`.

By R1037 the three F-transitions are exactly the unique v-B transition and the two endpoint source gates of the three-spoke source block, and both source gates are X-mated.

### Case 1: `(2F,0J)`
There is no negative J contribution anywhere on `C_*`. Because `r` is the first index with positive prefix ledger and the v-transition is an F-transition at `r`, no F-transition can occur earlier. Hence the other F-transition on `C_*` lies strictly after `r`. It cannot be the v-transition again, so it is one of the two X-mated source gates.

### Case 2: `(3F,1J)`
All three F-transitions lie on `C_*`, together with the unique J-transition. Suppose both source-gate F-transitions occurred before the v-transition at `r`. Their total positive contribution would be `+2`, while the entire component contains only one J-transition contributing `-1`. After both source gates and that possible negative contribution have been encountered, some proper prefix before `r` would therefore have ledger at least `+1`, contradicting the definition of `r` as the first positive prefix.

Thus at least one source gate lies strictly after `r`.

The same prefix argument shows that any source gate occurring before `r` must be neutralized by the unique J-transition: it cannot appear alone and leave positive ledger before the designated first-positive pivot.

## Scope
The result is a numerical-ordering consequence of the sharp augmenter together with the directly reconstructed source-block structure R1037. It does not assert that transport to the forward gate is physically successful or that the gate itself closes the global two-cover problem.
