# R878 — The crossed order-ten R24 frame closes by a six-Mate-Fork companion certificate

## Statement
Retain the crossed m=5 frame Q=(L,u,v,d,s,R), X={a,b,c,z}. Ten explicit one-hole spanning caps force the graph-intrinsic turns Rbv=(R,b,v), Rzv=(R,z,v), aLb=(a,L,b), aLz=(a,L,z), aRb=(a,R,b), cLz=(c,L,z), cRb=(c,R,b), cRz=(c,R,z), daL=(d,a,L), dcL=(d,c,L). Six applications of the accepted physical Mate-Fork rule R173 then force, in order, Rbu=(R,b,u), saL=(s,a,L), Ras=(R,a,s), asu=(a,s,u), sub=(s,u,b), and ubL=(u,b,L). Consequently P_6=(R,a,s,u,b,L) and P_4=(c,d,v,z) are disjoint tight paths spanning H. Hence no crossed m=5 order-three R24 frame exists.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted crossed R621/R594 with m=5. Use accepted R3,R173,R536,R594,R621,R723 and the matching-height X-turn table encoded in R594.

## Scope
Finite crossed m=5 closure by a literal ten-cap plus six-Mate-Fork companion certificate. No R5,R24,R824,R350,payment,capture,Hamilton-growth theorem,or computation.

## Applicability
Use as the complete crossed m=5 finite base once freshly reviewed. Preserve the literal proposals and hole pairs; do not compress this proof to anonymous Mate-Fork existence.

## Persistence
Retain all ten one-hole proposals, their unique holes and forced reversals; then retain each of the twelve two-hole Mate-Fork proposals, the mate pair and shared hole, and each forced shared-hole reversal. The final P6 and P4 are literal graph-intrinsic paths.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not use the intermediate R824 half-crossed gate theorem, any paid-floor lineage, or generic path growth. The certificate is finite and source-turn exact.

## Proof
Write Q=(L,u,v,d,s,R). We use the same one-hole principle as usual: a spanning two-path proposal with one uncertified turn tau forces rev(tau) by pc(H)>2 and R3. Every non-hole turn below is one of: a Q turn; an R594 endpoint star; a crossed R621 gate turn; an R723 first-inward star; an R536 inward turn; an R594 matching-height X turn; or a literal previously forced in the proof.

TEN ONE-HOLE CAPS.
1. (u,L,c,a,z) sqcup (d,v,b,R,s), hole (v,b,R), forces Rbv=(R,b,v). The certified turns are uLc,Lca,caz and dvb,bRs.
2. (u,L,a,c,b) sqcup (d,v,z,R,s), hole (v,z,R), forces Rzv=(R,z,v). Certified: uLa,Lac,acb and dvz,zRs.
3. (u,v,d,s,R) sqcup (c,z,b,L,a), hole (b,L,a), forces aLb=(a,L,b). Certified: Q and czb,zbL.
4. (u,v,d,s,R) sqcup (b,z,L,a,c), hole (z,L,a), forces aLz=(a,L,z). Certified: Q and bzL,Lac.
5. (L,u,v,d,s) sqcup (c,z,b,R,a), hole (b,R,a), forces aRb=(a,R,b). Certified: Q and czb,zbR.
6. (u,v,d,s,R) sqcup (a,b,z,L,c), hole (z,L,c), forces cLz=(c,L,z). Certified: Q and abz,bzL.
7. (L,u,v,d,s) sqcup (b,R,c,a,z), hole (b,R,c), forces cRb=(c,R,b). Certified: Q and Rca,caz.
8. (L,u,v,d,s) sqcup (a,b,z,R,c), hole (z,R,c), forces cRz=(c,R,z). Certified: Q and abz,bzR.
9. (u,L,a,d,v) sqcup (c,z,b,R,s), hole (L,a,d), forces daL=(d,a,L). Certified: uLa,adv and czb,zbR,bRs.
10. (u,L,c,d,v) sqcup (a,b,z,R,s), hole (L,c,d), forces dcL=(d,c,L). Certified: uLc,cdv and abz,bzR,zRs.
Here adv,cdv,dvb,dvz are the accepted m=5 R536 inward turns.

We now apply accepted physical Mate-Fork rule R173 six times. In each pair below all unlisted turns are certified, and the two hole sets are {h,t} and {h,rev(t)}; therefore R173 forces rev(h).

F1. Proposals
(a,d,c,L,z) sqcup (v,u,b,R,s), holes {adc, ubR},
(c,d,a,L,z) sqcup (v,u,b,R,s), holes {cda, ubR}.
The mate holes adc/cda are reversals. Nonholes use dcL,cLz in the first rails and vub,bRs in the second. Hence R173 forces Rbu=(R,b,u).

F2. Proposals
(u,L,a,s,d) sqcup (c,R,b,v,z), holes {Las, bvz},
(u,L,a,s,d) sqcup (c,R,z,v,b), holes {Las, zvb}.
The mate holes bvz/zvb are reversals. Nonholes use uLa,asd and cRb,Rbv in the first proposal, cRz,Rzv in the second. Hence R173 forces saL=(s,a,L).

F3. Proposals
(c,d,v,z,L) sqcup (s,a,R,b,u), holes {vzL, saR},
(d,c,L,z,v) sqcup (s,a,R,b,u), holes {Lzv, saR}.
The mate holes vzL/Lzv are reversals. Nonholes use cdv,dvz and dcL,cLz, while the common second rail uses aRb,Rbu. Hence R173 forces Ras=(R,a,s).

F4. Proposals
(u,s,a,L,b) sqcup (R,c,d,v,z), holes {usa, Rcd},
(u,s,a,L,b) sqcup (d,c,R,z,v), holes {usa, dcR}.
The mate holes Rcd/dcR are reversals. The first rail uses saL,aLb; the second rails use cdv,dvz or cRz,Rzv. Hence R173 forces asu=(a,s,u).

F5. Proposals
(a,d,v,z,L) sqcup (c,R,b,u,s), holes {vzL, bus},
(d,a,L,z,v) sqcup (c,R,b,u,s), holes {Lzv, bus}.
The mate holes vzL/Lzv are reversals. Nonholes use adv,dvz or daL,aLz, while the second rail uses cRb,Rbu. Hence R173 forces sub=(s,u,b).

F6. Proposals
(s,a,L,b,u) sqcup (d,c,R,z,v), holes {Lbu, dcR},
(s,a,L,b,u) sqcup (R,c,d,v,z), holes {Lbu, Rcd}.
The mate holes dcR/Rcd are reversals. The first rail uses saL,aLb; the second rails use cRz,Rzv or cdv,dvz. Hence R173 forces ubL=(u,b,L).

FINAL COVER. The six forced turns concatenate as
P_6=(R,a,s,u,b,L),
whose consecutive turns are Ras, asu, sub, ubL. Accepted R536 gives cdv and dvz, so
P_4=(c,d,v,z)
is tight. Their supports are disjoint and together equal V(H)=X union {L,u,v,d,s,R}. Thus P_6 sqcup P_4 is a spanning two-cover, contradiction.
