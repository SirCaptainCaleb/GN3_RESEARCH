# R778 — P5 internal-trimer recompletion forces a P6 or same-cover nonreplay endpoint packets

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2, let J=(j_0,j_1,j_2,j_3,j_4) be a proper vertex-simple tight P5, put D={j_1,j_2,j_3}, E=V(H)\V(J), and let T be any literal exact two-cover of H-D. Then either H contains a tight P6 obtained by adjoining to one endpoint of J a T-selected vertex of E, or, in the no-P6 branch, the R776 endpoint-chord dichotomy upgrades as follows. (CHORD-NONREPLAY) If {j_0,j_4} is selected in T, then at least one endpoint has a T-selected exterior neighbor h in E; for that endpoint the only boundary-antisymmetric orientation compatible with no P6 is the reverse terminal-contact orientation, so accepted R773 yields either an adjacent local P4 or its one-step-inward R703 Reverse-Ear/reversal/cycle geometry. Retain the selected endpoint chord simultaneously in T. (DUAL-NONREPLAY) If {j_0,j_4} is not selected in T, then T has selected exterior states {j_0,a} and {j_4,b} with a,b in E. In the no-P6 branch boundary antisymmetry forces both reverse terminal contacts (j_1,j_0,a) and (b,j_4,j_3) tight. Accepted R773 therefore yields, independently at both ends, an adjacent local P4 or a one-step-inward R703 Reverse-Ear/reversal/cycle packet. The two source exterior states coexist in the same exact cover T. Thus internal-trimer recompletion of a proper P5 cannot leave a bare endpoint-crossing residue: absent immediate path extension, it produces a chorded nonreplay packet or simultaneous two-ended nonreplay geometry.

## Hypotheses
H is finite Strong Level-(1) with pc(H)>2; J is a proper vertex-simple tight P5; T is a literal exact two-cover of H-{j_1,j_2,j_3}. Use accepted R776, boundary antisymmetry R3, and accepted R773.

## Scope
Order-free generic consumer of the accepted R776 P5 recompletion theorem. No crossed frame, smallest-counterexample minimality beyond supplied T, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R176,R312,R436,capture,payment,or computation.

## Applicability
Use immediately after R776. First test the forced T-selected endpoint-exterior states against the terminal turns of J. Any forward orientation is a literal P6 extension. If no such extension exists, use R773 on the forced reverse terminal contacts. In the DUAL-EXTERIOR branch this yields two source-labelled nonreplay packets tied to one common exact recompletion; in the ENDPOINT-CHORD branch retain the selected endpoint chord while consuming the forced exterior state.

## Persistence
Retain J,D,T,the endpoint-chord selected/not-selected bit, every R776 source endpoint-exterior selected state used, the exact R3 orientation at each endpoint, and either the resulting literal P6 or the full R773 certificate (adjacent P4 or shifted R703 Reverse-Ear/reversal/cycle geometry). In DUAL-NONREPLAY retain that both endpoint source states coexist in the same T; later R773 outputs are graph-intrinsic certificates and are not asserted to coexist as selected states.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not consume the resulting P6, local P4, Reverse-Ear, reversal, or cycle. Does not assert the two exterior neighbors a,b are distinct, lie on different rails, or survive as selected states after any later continuation. In CHORD-NONREPLAY does not prescribe which endpoint has the exterior neighbor. Does not say the endpoint chord alone carries sign information or forms a cycle. Does not close H by itself.

## Proof
Apply accepted R776 to the fixed exact cover T of H-D. First consider any T-selected exterior state {j_0,a} at the left endpoint, with a in E. Boundary antisymmetry R3 on the three distinct vertices {a,j_0,j_1} gives exactly one of (a,j_0,j_1) and (j_1,j_0,a) tight. In the first case, the old P5 turns (j_0,j_1,j_2),(j_1,j_2,j_3),(j_2,j_3,j_4) concatenate with (a,j_0,j_1), so (a,j_0,j_1,j_2,j_3,j_4) is a vertex-simple tight P6. In the second case, (j_1,j_0,a) is exactly the reverse head-contact hypothesis of accepted R773 on K=J with h=a. Hence R773 yields either the adjacent local P4 or the shifted one-step-inward R703 Reverse-Ear/reversal/cycle geometry. Dually, for any T-selected exterior state {j_4,b}, R3 on {j_3,j_4,b} gives either (j_3,j_4,b) tight, yielding the P6 (j_0,j_1,j_2,j_3,j_4,b), or (b,j_4,j_3) tight, exactly the reverse tail-contact hypothesis of R773. Now suppose no such P6 exists. If R776 is in ENDPOINT-CHORD, the chord {j_0,j_4} is selected and R776 supplies at least one endpoint-exterior state; by the preceding classification its forward orientation is impossible, so it yields an R773 nonreplay packet while the chord remains selected in the same T. If R776 is in DUAL-EXTERIOR, it supplies both {j_0,a} and {j_4,b} in the same T. No-P6 excludes both forward orientations, so both reverse terminal contacts are forced and R773 applies at both ends. This proves the stated alternatives.
