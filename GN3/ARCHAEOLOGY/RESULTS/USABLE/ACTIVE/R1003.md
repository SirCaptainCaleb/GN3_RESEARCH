# R1003 — A doubly extreme-shielded Hamilton rail need not absorb the global extreme four-set

## Statement
There exists an edge ordering of K8 with globally minimum edge 01 and globally maximum edge 23, disjoint, and with U=(4,5,6,7) an increasing Hamilton path on the other four vertices, such that both edges from {0,1} to the source 4 lie strictly above the first U-edge 45 and both edges from the terminal 7 to {2,3} lie strictly below the last U-edge 67, yet the full K8 has no increasing Hamilton path. One exact order is 01<03<13<46<26<45<35<27<57<17<04<37<47<36<12<02<25<14<16<05<34<56<06<24<07<67<15<23. Here rank(45)=5<rank(04)=10,rank(14)=17 and rank(27)=7,rank(37)=11<rank(67)=25. Exhaustive checking of all 8! vertex orders finds no Hamilton increasing path. Nevertheless {0}|(6,4,5,7,1,2,3) is a spanning two-cover with long-path edge ranks 3<5<8<9<14<27.

## Hypotheses
Pure finite edge-ordered K8 example.

## Scope
Exact route fence for consuming the mixed doubly-shielded branch of R1001 by Hamiltonizing the shielded rail together with the four global-extreme endpoints alone.

## Applicability
Use to reject any proposed theorem that disjoint global extreme edges plus one Hamilton rail shielded at both ends must have Hamilton union. Any R1001 consumer must use the second complement rail or additional representative exchange.

## Persistence
Retain the full 28-edge order, U=(4,5,6,7), the four shield inequalities, exhaustive 8! Hamilton check, and explicit two-cover.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not refute R888 or R1001. Does not say the full three-cover extreme-carrier configuration is trapped; the graph is explicitly two-coverable.

## Proof
Number the displayed edges from rank 0 through 27. The global minimum is 01 at rank 0 and the global maximum is 23 at rank 27. On U=(4,5,6,7), the selected ranks are rank(45)=5<rank(56)=21<rank(67)=25, so U is increasing. Its source is 4 and terminal is 7. The two minimum-pair-to-source edges have ranks rank(04)=10 and rank(14)=17, both strictly above rank(45)=5. The two terminal-to-maximum-pair edges have ranks rank(27)=7 and rank(37)=11, both strictly below rank(67)=25. Thus U has both R1001-style extreme shields. To certify non-Hamiltonicity, enumerate all 8!=40320 ordered vertex lists (v0,...,v7); for each compute the seven ranks of consecutive ordinary edges and accept only if they are strictly increasing. None is accepted. Finally the path (6,4,5,7,1,2,3) has edge ranks 3,5,8,9,14,27, strictly increasing. Together with singleton {0} it is a spanning two-cover.
