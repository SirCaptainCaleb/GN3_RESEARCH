# R1041 — Three parallel source turns do not force endpoint flexibility

## Statement
There exists a five-vertex Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament on

    {A,C,p,q,r}

such that `(A,s,C)` is tight for every `s in {p,q,r}`, but the only tight Hamilton paths of order five are

    A,r,p,C,q
    q,p,A,r,C
    q,p,r,C,A.

Consequently every Hamilton source P5 has exactly one source-spoke endpoint, that endpoint is always `q`, no Hamilton source P5 has two source-spoke endpoints, and the endpoint source spoke cannot in general be prescribed from the three parallel source turns alone.

## Construction
For each middle vertex `m`, write `u ->_m w` iff `(u,m,w)` is tight. By boundary antisymmetry R3, it suffices to specify one orientation for every pair of endpoints in each local tournament.

Use:

`m=A`:

    C->p, q->C, C->r, q->p, p->r, r->q.

`m=C`:

    A->p, A->q, r->A, p->q, r->p, q->r.

`m=p`:

    A->C, q->A, r->A, q->C, r->C, q->r.

`m=q`:

    A->C, p->A, A->r, p->C, r->C, p->r.

`m=r`:

    A->C, A->p, q->A, p->C, C->q, p->q.

Each displayed list orients all six endpoint pairs for its middle vertex, hence defines a local tournament and therefore a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament.

The arcs `A->_p C`, `A->_q C`, and `A->_r C` give the required tight turns `(A,p,C)`, `(A,q,C)`, `(A,r,C)`.

## Exhaustive verification
For an order `w=(w0,w1,w2,w3,w4)`, tight Hamiltonicity is exactly the conjunction of

    (w0,w1,w2), (w1,w2,w3), (w2,w3,w4)

being tight. Exhaustion of all `5! = 120` vertex orders gives exactly

    A,r,p,C,q
    q,p,A,r,C
    q,p,r,C,A

and no others.

Each of these has exactly one source-spoke endpoint, namely `q`. Therefore the three parallel source turns alone force existence of a source Hamilton P5 by R1031, but do not force endpoint choice, a prescribed source endpoint, or two source-spoke endpoints.

## Scope
This construction uses only R3 and the three source turns. It does not use minimal-counterexample structure, R24, R5, the spectator path, old-F data, or D17 ancestry.

## Live consequence
Any minimality-splice argument that requires freedom to prescribe which source spoke is an endpoint of the source P5, or requires two source-spoke endpoints, needs additional global or smallest-counterexample structure. That flexibility is false at the level of the source five-set alone.

## Audit note
Audited directly from the finite certificate. Each local orientation is a tournament on the four vertices distinct from its middle vertex, the three source turns are present, and exhaustive checking of all 120 orders yields exactly the three stated Hamilton P5s. The `[INTERESTING]` nomination is endorsed because this counterexample permanently fences a natural strengthening of the live minimality-splice route.
