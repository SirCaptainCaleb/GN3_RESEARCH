# R498 — R495 fork prongs cannot form their own physical sibling clause

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain either candidate-aware fork of R495. In the head fork, the two bad one-hole seams are s_A=(b_q,a_0,a_1) and s_P=(b_q,a_0,z_1), with exact mates H_A=(a_1,a_0,b_q) and H_P=(z_1,a_0,b_q) both tight. There is no literal spanning two-path proposal whose complete two-hole set is {H_A,H_P}; equivalently there is no physical two-hole mate clause having exactly the two fork complements {s_A,s_P} as its literals. Indeed if such a proposal existed, every turn outside its two displayed holes would be certified tight by definition, while both displayed holes H_A,H_P are already graph-intrinsically tight, so the proposal itself would be a spanning two-cover of H, contradicting pc(H)>2. Dually, in the tail fork there is no two-hole proposal with hole set {T_B,T_P}, hence no physical clause whose two literals are exactly {t_B,t_P}. Consequently the two one-hole prongs of R495 cannot close into a direct physical sibling edge with one another. Any R173/R463 use of a fork complement must pair it with a genuinely third clause literal. Combined with R497, the guaranteed bridge-interior prong can reach such a third literal only through a proposal that also cuts the common chord-adjacent source state b_q a_0 and the relevant P^o endpoint adjacency.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1); pc(H)>2; the R495 head- or tail-fork output; literal complete-hole/mate-clause semantics. R497 is retained for the combined third-literal cut fence.

## Scope
Order-free physical-clause fence on the two-prong candidate-aware R495 fork.

## Applicability
Use when attempting to connect the two R495 fork complements directly into a mate cycle. They cannot be the two literals of one physical clause. Any implication route must leave the fork through a third literal; combine with R497 to obtain the required cross-arm cut budget on the bridge-interior exit.

## Persistence
Retains both one-hole candidate identities, both exact tight mates, both bad seams, and the live fork labels. The impossibility is graph/candidate intrinsic and independent of later currentization.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not forbid a physical clause containing one fork complement together with a third literal. Does not itself construct or rule out longer mate cycles. Does not require the two one-hole candidates to be simultaneously selected; only the graph-intrinsic tightness of their exact mates is used.

## Proof
In HF, suppose a literal spanning two-path proposal Q had complete uncertified-turn set {H_A,H_P}. R495 certifies H_A and H_P tight. Every other turn of Q is certified tight by the definition of its complete hole set. Hence every turn of Q is tight, so Q is a literal spanning two-cover, contradicting pc(H)>2. The associated mate clause would have literals bar(H_A)=s_A and bar(H_P)=s_P, so no such physical clause exists. The TF proof is identical with T_B,T_P and t_B,t_P. Therefore a fork cannot directly pair its two complements; any clause route must use a third literal. R497 then supplies the extra cut requirement for the bridge-interior prong.
