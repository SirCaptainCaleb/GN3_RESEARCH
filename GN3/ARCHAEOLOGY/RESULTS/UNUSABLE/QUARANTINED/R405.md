## Statement

Assume neither of the two role-bearing obstruction packets used in the current endpoint program occurs: (P) no fixed pair deletion H-{a,c} has a vertex x that is internal in every exact two-cover, hence no attached R398/R389 persistent-middle source-trimer packet; and (C) no triple-deletion common-shadow three-cap frame produces the R404 role-linked cap interaction. If these three cap certificates lived on one residual cover, R404 would produce the excluded packet (C). Therefore H-T is nonunique.
