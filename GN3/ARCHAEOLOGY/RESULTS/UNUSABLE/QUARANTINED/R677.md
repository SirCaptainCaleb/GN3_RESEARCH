## Proof
Let G_0=(q_0,q_1), G_1=(q_3,...,q_{m-3}), G_2=(q_{m-1},q_m), and let X_i be the internal degree-two X block in shape E, with X_e the other X block. By R671, unless an explicit Reverse-Ear output is already present, the Q contacts on the four-block rail occur in increasing Q-index order. Hence its literal quotient order is X_e-G_i-X_i-G_j with i<j.

Suppose first that i=0. Delete the contiguous block X_i from this rail. Keep the inherited initial splice X_e-G_0, then after G_0 restore q_2, traverse G_1 in its original Q order, restore q_{m-2}, and traverse G_2. If j=1 or j=2, the same construction works: every turn after the inherited X_e-G_0 splice is a consecutive turn of the original Q. Thus one obtains a tight path on V(H)\V(X_i), while X_i itself is a tight dimer. These two paths span H, contradiction. Therefore i is not 0. Since i<j among {0,1,2}, necessarily i=1,j=2 and G_0 is the isolated Q cell.

Write the actual contiguous T-order of X_e as (x_1,x_2). The nontrivial T rail is then X_e-G_1-X_i-G_2. Assume m>=7 and consider
K=(q_0,q_1,q_2,x_1,x_2,q_3,...,q_{m-3},q_{m-2},q_{m-1},q_m).
Together with the untouched dimer X_i, its support partitions V(H). Inspect the turns of K. The turn q_0q_1q_2 and all turns internal to Q after q_3 are inherited from Q. The turn (x_1,x_2,q_3), and when present the next turn (x_2,q_3,q_4), are inherited from the contiguous T blocks X_e-G_1. Because m>=7, restoring q_{m-2} after the complete G_1 block creates only consecutive old Q turns. Consequently the only uncertified turns are h_1=(q_1,q_2,x_1) and h_2=(q_2,x_1,x_2). If both were tight, K together with X_i would be a spanning two-cover, impossible. By R3 at least one complete reversal is tight, giving the physical mate clause A=(x_1,q_2,q_1) OR B=(x_2,x_1,q_2).

We now retain the source roles of both literals. R616 supplies the exact enlarged covers H-z=(q_1,q_0,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m] and H-b=(q_1,q_0,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m]. From the first, if (z,q_1,q_2) were tight then (z,q_1,q_2,...,q_m) together with (q_0,a,c,b) would two-cover H. Hence (z,q_1,q_2) is bad and R3 gives (q_2,q_1,z) tight. The second enlarged root gives identically (q_2,q_1,b) tight. Thus the tested dimer D=(q_2,q_1) has tail witnesses b,z. If A=(x_1,q_2,q_1) is tight, it is a head certificate on D. Choose r in {b,z} with r!=x_1. R523 then concatenates the opposite-polarity certificates to the tight labelled P4 (x_1,q_2,q_1,r).

For B, regard D_X=(x_2,x_1), the reverse of the actual endpoint-X block orientation. If X_e=G_3={a,c}, accepted R649 gives a fixed head witness w in the complementary X block with (w,x_2,x_1) tight. Then B is a second tail certificate with the distinct witness q_2, and R523 gives the named same-tail collision on D_X.

It remains to check m=6. Then G_1 is the singleton q_3 and G_2=(q_5,q_6). The same proposed K=(q_0,q_1,q_2,x_1,x_2,q_3,q_4,q_5,q_6) has exactly the two holes h_1,h_2 above plus one additional potentially uncertified seam h_3=(x_2,q_3,q_4).
