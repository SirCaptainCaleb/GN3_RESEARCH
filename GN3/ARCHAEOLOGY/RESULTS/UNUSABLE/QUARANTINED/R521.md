# R521 — Universal carrier/exterior spoke fan and four-cell normal form

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, K any proper tight path, E=V(H)\V(K) with |E|=r>=5, and b any chosen carrier vertex. Then every physical spoke {b,d}, d in E, carries signed certificates with every other exterior vertex as witness; by R519 every spoke therefore has a graph-intrinsic R38 two-witness interaction. Moreover for every three-set {x,y,z} subset E, choose the unique b-middle tight trimer on any two of x,y,z and apply the corrected universal four-cell theorem R518 to the remaining exterior vertex. Hence the four-set {b,x,y,z} either supports a tight Hamilton P4 or is one of the four exact R516 no-P4 signatures. Its opposite-edge matching-block quotient is classified by R517: three signatures are vertex-uniform transitive up to block relabeling and the doubly misaligned 00 cell is vertex-uniform cyclic. Thus the carrier/exterior fan admits a complete support-level interaction family and a corrected complete four-cell normal form, with no universal lower bound on strict spokes or P4 cells.

## Proof
By R169, every proper tight path K in the smallest-counterexample setting has at least five exterior vertices, so E has size at least five. Fix b in V(K). Apply R519 with common anchor b and outer set E. For each d in E, the spoke {b,d} carries signed certificates with every witness in E\{d}, and therefore an R38 two-witness interaction. Now fix distinct x,y,z in E. Boundary antisymmetry determines a tight b-middle trimer on {b,x,y}; call its tight orientation C. The remaining vertex z is distinct from C, so corrected universal theorem R518 applies to C and z. Either {b,x,y,z} supports a Hamilton tight P4 or it is one of the exact no-P4 signatures R516 retained inside R518. In the latter case R517 gives the matching-block quotient: 11,10,01 are vertex-uniform transitive up to the stated block permutations, and 00 is vertex-uniform cyclic. Since the exterior triple was arbitrary, this gives the complete corrected local normal form over the whole carrier/exterior fan. No counting claim beyond existence of each spoke interaction and the exact four-cell alternative is used.
