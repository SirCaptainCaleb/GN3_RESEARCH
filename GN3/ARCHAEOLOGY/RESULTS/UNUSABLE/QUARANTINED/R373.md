# R373 — Universal all-internal trimer return has a double cross-rail payment channel

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal regime of R359 for a proper tight trimer J=(a,x,c), with one R18 currentization H=J sqcup U sqcup V and source rail orders at least two. Let D_U^L,D_U^R and D_V^L,D_V^R be the left/right terminal physical dimers of U,V in the reverse orientations supplied by the R359 four-way endpoint shield. Then D_U^L and D_V^L have the same polarity with common witness x, while D_U^R and D_V^R have the opposite polarity. Hence the two diagonal cross-rail pairs (D_U^L,D_V^R) and (D_V^L,D_U^R) are disjoint direct mass-four balanced pairs. Each diagonal satisfies the Historical-Support Terminal-Reversal Payment hypotheses R162, taking one dimer as historical support and the opposite dimer as a terminal support of the entire disjoint opposite rail. Therefore either diagonal may independently launch a lawful payment continuation yielding either a spanning two-cover or a strict paid descendant of mass less than four. The two payment lineages are alternative and need not coexist.

## Hypotheses
Smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; J is a proper tight trimer in the all-internal R359 return regime.

## Scope
Order-free generic payment consequence of the universal trimer return shield.

## Applicability
Use as a rail-length-independent continuation from an all-internal trimer return. It generalizes the six-exterior R213 double channel and runs in parallel with the R360 same-middle target rotation.

## Persistence
The four terminal dimers and their x-signs are graph-intrinsic relative to the chosen R18 currentization; each paid lineage retains its own terminal-reversal ancestry.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not synchronize the two paid descendants, does not guarantee mass two in one step, and does not turn the low-mass descendant into an R171 literal frame contrary to R214.

## Proof
Write U=(u_1,...,u_r), V=(v_1,...,v_s). R359 gives (u_2,u_1,x),(x,u_r,u_{r-1}),(v_2,v_1,x),(x,v_s,v_{s-1}) tight. Thus the reverse left terminal dimers of U,V share one polarity with witness x and the reverse right terminal dimers share the opposite polarity. Cross-diagonal pairs are physically disjoint and opposite-signed, so R39 gives direct mass-four balanced pairs. For (D_U^L,D_V^R), take S=D_U^L and T=V; D_V^R is a terminal dimer of the entire tight path V and has opposite polarity, so R162 applies. The other diagonal is dual.
