D_Q=(q_0,b_q,a_0) OR H_Q=(q_1,q_0,b_q).

Thus Left REV compiles to two collision-decorated physical clauses; absent immediate collision, it produces two named shield winners together with two exact endpoint-absorbing P4s, not merely unspecified four-set Hamiltonicity.

## Proof
Left REV: write a=a_0,b=b_q,l=b_{q-1}. Absence of R485(T) makes (a,b,l) bad because C_R is retained tight, hence L_B=(l,b,a) is tight by R3. Let R=(r_0,...,w,z_2,z_1) and S=(s_0,...,s_N). R168 gives |R|,|S|>=3, so R^-=R-z_1 is nontrivial; C_L=(z_1,a,b) is tight by R485(P4), hence R^- sqcup S sqcup C_L is a literal spanning three-cover.

R453 on S-to-C_L gives mate clause (z_1,s_N,s_{N-1}) OR (a,z_1,s_N). Since z_2 lies in R^- and S is disjoint, s_N!=z_2, so the second literal is collision-decorated by O_L=(a,z_1,z_2). R453 on C_L-to-Q gives (q_0,b,a) OR (q_1,q_0,b). The two heads of R^- and S are distinct, so one Q_* has q_0!=l; then (q_0,b,a) is collision-decorated by L_B. If either decorated literal is tight, R38 gives the asserted collision. Otherwise both are bad, so their clauses force T_S=(z_1,s_N,s_{N-1}) and H_{Q_*}=(q_1,q_0,b) tight. Boundary antisymmetry now gives the exact reverses (s_N,z_1,a) and (a,b,q_0) tight. Concatenating the former with C_L gives K_T=(s_N,z_1,a,b); concatenating C_L with the latter gives K_H=(z_1,a,b,q_0). These are explicit tight Hamilton P4s. R488 retains X_A,X_B independently because H,T were excluded.

Right REV is obtained by reversing head/tail roles. Absence of R485(H) gives L_A=(b,a,a_1) tight. Shift z_{m-1} from the beginning of the REV rail into C_R=(a,b,z_{m-1}). The C_R-to-S clause has decorated literal (s_0,z_{m-1},b), whose reverse (b,z_{m-1},s_0) concatenates after C_R when the decoration is bad, giving (a,b,z_{m-1},s_0). For Q with tail q_N!=a_1, the Q-to-C_R decorated literal is (b,a,q_N); if bad its reverse (q_N,a,b) concatenates before C_R, giving (q_N,a,b,z_{m-1}). The opposite literals of the two physical clauses are forced tight. This proves the strengthened claim.
