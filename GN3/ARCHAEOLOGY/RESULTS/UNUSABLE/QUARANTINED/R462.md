# R462 — Deleted-neighbor restoration is fresh-witness-blind

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let E=(e_0,...,e_m), m>=1, be a graph-intrinsic tight path and let k,z be distinct vertices outside E. In the head orientation, once (k,E) is tight, Boundary Antisymmetry R3 already forces the R164 restoration/contact dichotomy for z without using the additional hypothesis that (z,E) is tight: either (z,k,E) is tight or (e_0,k,z) is tight. Dually, once (E,k) is tight, R3 already forces either (E,k,z) tight or (z,k,e_m) tight, without using that (E,z) is tight. Thus the fresh-replacement extension in the hypotheses of R164 is logically unused by the forcing step of its dichotomy; it supplies provenance/interpretation, not extra combinatorial pressure. Consequently, applying R164 by itself to an R455 same-polarity residual-terminal collision does not consume the second R455 signed certificate: fixing the x-signed extension as the old extension, the same restoration/contact alternative is available for any distinct opposite-rail vertex z outside the retained terminal dimer, even if z did not provide the second sign. Therefore R164 alone cannot convert the two-witnessness of an R455 collision into additional physical linkage or closure. Any productive continuation must use both signed extension facts through some further consumer, or add independent ancestry/structural input.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry R3. For the generic head/tail statement, E is a nontrivial graph-intrinsic tight path and k,z are distinct vertices outside E. The R455 consequence is in the all-internal R359 state certified by R455.

## Scope
Order-free negative/consumer-discipline lemma. It identifies the exact logical strength of the R164 routing step and fences a tempting but role-blind use of R455. It does not invalidate R164 as a role-preserving compiler.

## Applicability
Use whenever a same-support two-witness collision is proposed to be spent solely by R164. Before claiming progress, require a downstream step that genuinely consumes both signed extensions or additional retained ancestry.

## Persistence
The logical non-consumption statement is graph-independent. In the R455 specialization, the physical terminal dimer and both named witnesses remain valid historical certificates; the result only states that R164's own dichotomy does not logically use the second certificate.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say R164 is useless: its restored extension/contact packaging may be valuable when a later theorem consumes the preserved old-neighbor role. Does not rule out closing an R455 collision by another theorem, by combining both witness facts elsewhere, or by adding a genuinely complementary proposal. Does not weaken R455 itself.

## Proof
Head form. Assume only that (k,E) is tight, with E=(e_0,...,e_m), m>=1, and that z is distinct from k and outside E. Test the ordered triple (z,k,e_0). If it is tight, then (z,k,e_0,e_1,...,e_m) is a tight path: its first turn is the tested turn, while every remaining turn is already part of the tight path (k,E). Hence (z,k,E) is tight. If (z,k,e_0) is bad, Boundary Antisymmetry R3 makes its complete reversal (e_0,k,z) tight. This is exactly the head conclusion of R164. At no point was (z,E) used.

Tail form. Assume only that (E,k) is tight. Test (e_m,k,z). If it is tight, then (e_0,...,e_m,k,z) is tight because all earlier turns are already contained in (E,k). If it is bad, R3 makes the complete reversal (z,k,e_m) tight. This is exactly the tail conclusion of R164, again without using (E,z).

Therefore the extra fresh-extension hypothesis in R164 does not strengthen the logical disjunction it concludes. It records that z is itself a certified endpoint witness, which can matter to later ancestry-sensitive consumers, but R164's own forcing step is fresh-witness-blind.

Now specialize to either same-polarity residual-terminal collision supplied by R455. For example, in a head-signed collision let E be the reverse terminal dimer and let k=x be the retained R359 middle witness. R455 also supplies a second witness w from the opposite residual rail, so (w,E) is tight; however the generic argument above shows that the R164 restoration/contact alternative with z=w already follows from R3 and (x,E) alone. The same conclusion would hold for any distinct z outside E, whether or not z signed E. The tail-signed case is identical by the dual argument. Thus an R455-to-R164 step by itself does not consume the second R455 certificate and cannot be credited as additional linkage produced by two-witnessness. A genuine consumer must use both signed facts elsewhere or introduce additional structure.
