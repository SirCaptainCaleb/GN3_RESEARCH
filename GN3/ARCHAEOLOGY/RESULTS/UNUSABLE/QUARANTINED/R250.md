# R250 — Repeated four-core dimer class gives bilateral same-frame carrier capture

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the R244 four-core occupancy setup. Thus one fixed exact dimer-deletion frame H-D=K_a sqcup (E_a\D) retains the global-longest carrier K_a, and let B be one physical-D class of completion vertices whose successful cross-replacement P5 certificates use D with the completion vertex as an admissible outer witness. If |B|>=4, then every member b of B that is internal on K_a has two graph-intrinsic historical capture certificates sourced from this same exact D-deletion frame: one using the left K_a-edge incident with b and one using the right K_a-edge incident with b. Hence the frame yields bilateral capture at at least |B|-2 distinct carrier vertices. In particular, using the largest D-class supplied by R244, any four-core occupancy r with ceil((r-1)/6)>=4 (equivalently r>=20) yields one exact deletion frame with bilateral captures at at least ceil((r-1)/6)-2 carrier positions.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; accepted R244 concentrated same-frame capture setup; accepted R37 witness-adaptive capture. The bilateral conclusion is invoked only for a fixed physical-D class B of size at least four.

## Scope
Order-free strengthening of R244 inside one occupied-four-core exact deletion frame.

## Applicability
Use when high four-core occupancy has produced a repeated physical dimer class. Bilateral capture exposes both carrier incidences at each internal class position and is designed to feed carrier-order consumers such as mirror reentry, insertion-obstruction, or first-loss arguments without changing the deletion frame.

## Persistence
The fixed dimer D, exact D-deletion frame, carrier K_a, class B, carrier positions, and both capture events at each internal b are retained as graph-intrinsic/historical certificates in the sense of R37/R244.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that capture descendants for different edges or vertices are simultaneously current or paid. Does not preserve a later recompletion, does not itself create a carrier extension, and does not license direct R171 entry. Bilateral capture means two historical capture certificates sourced from the same fixed exact frame.

## Proof
Fix b internal in B. Choose b' in B\{b,k_{i-1},k_{i+1}}; this is possible because |B|>=4. R244 inherits the R239 fact that, in H-D=K_a sqcup (E_a\D), each of the two K_a-edges incident with b is a selected crossing of the retained cross-replacement support Q_b relative to D. Since b' lies on neither edge, both selected crossings avoid the admissible witness b'. Apply R37 to the left edge and choose physical endpoint b to capture; apply R37 separately to the right edge and again choose b. The two resulting capture statements are simultaneous graph-intrinsic/historical facts sourced from one unchanged exact frame, though any downstream paid descendants remain alternative. Repeat for every internal b in B. Only the two K_a endpoints can be excluded, giving at least |B|-2 bilateral positions. The occupancy corollary follows from the largest-class estimate |B|>=ceil((r-1)/6) in R244.
