# R118 — Paired-anchor trimer lift and third-witness dichotomy

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the paired-anchor polarity rectangle R117, with opposite-polarity stars {p,u},{p,v} at anchor p and {r,a},{r,b} at anchor r, all four dimers carrying the two common witnesses delta and epsilon. Boundary antisymmetry permits tight trimer orientations J_p on {u,p,v} with middle p and J_r on {a,r,b} with middle r. These trimers are vertex-disjoint and their four terminal physical dimers are exactly the four rectangle dimers. Hence R41 gives a synchronized two-anchor trimer interaction through {p,r}. Moreover, at each anchor separately there is an alignment dichotomy between the pre-existing R117 polarities and the trimer-induced terminal polarities: either both terminal dimers align, in which case each gains a third same-polarity witness (the opposite outer vertex of that trimer) in addition to delta and epsilon; or both anti-align, in which case R38 gives an opposite-polarity same-support exclusivity / strict two-ended signed-path interaction on each of the two terminal dimers. Thus each anchor produces either two three-witness same-polarity signed dimers or two strict opposite-polarity same-support interactions, while the two anchors are simultaneously linked by the R41 interaction certificate.

## Proof
Write the p-star as D_1={p,u}, D_2={p,v}, with R117 polarities s and -s, and the r-star as F_1={r,a}, F_2={r,b}, again of opposite polarity. By R3, for the ordered reversal pair (u,p,v),(v,p,u), exactly one orientation is tight; choose that tight orientation as J_p. Thus J_p is a tight trimer with middle p and terminal physical dimers D_1,D_2. Likewise one of (a,r,b),(b,r,a) is tight, giving J_r with middle r and terminal physical dimers F_1,F_2. The R117 rectangle lists six distinct vertices u,p,v,a,r,b in these two stars, so J_p and J_r are vertex-disjoint. R41 therefore supplies the synchronized two-anchor interaction through p and r. R41 also assigns opposite trimer-induced polarities t,-t to D_1,D_2. Since the old polarities are s,-s, either t=s, in which case both trimer signs align, or t=-s, in which case both anti-align. In the aligned case the trimer witness for D_1 is v and for D_2 is u; these are distinct from delta,epsilon and from the relevant support, so each support now has three distinct witnesses delta,epsilon and its trimer outer witness, all in polarity s or -s respectively. In the anti-aligned case each D_i has one trimer certificate of polarity opposite to its delta/epsilon certificate, with distinct witnesses, so R38 gives the strict two-ended same-support interaction on both D_1 and D_2. The same argument applies at r. No payment or representative transport is used.
