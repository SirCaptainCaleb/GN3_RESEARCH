### 1. Full root kernel and a connected rim skeleton
Retain a nonclosing reconstruction-closed G23 family generated by the Hamilton P5 K and common hub x of SV58568/SV58856. By SV61245 no proper subset of K can support a closed all-choice capture sink. Hence the normalized root kernel contains all five physical vertices of K.

Use the all-choice capture graph on K and choose any connected spanning undirected skeleton G_R from arcs inside its full five-root strongly connected kernel. For every skeleton edge {d,e} retain one exact pair-deletion cover of H-{d,e}; for every root d retain one exact spoke cover of H-{x,d}. Exact covers exist by R429.

Apply the Johnson-triangle compiler of SV61535 to each triangle {x,d,e} with {d,e} in G_R. If any triangle exposes the current crossing/component-drop branch, the wheel is already nonquiet and we stop. Thus consider the quiet SUPPORT-COMMUTING branch.

The gluing proof of SV61535 uses only connectivity of the rim adjacency graph, not cyclic degree two: along every skeleton edge the two spoke partitions agree on the common triple-deletion shadow, so all five spoke partitions glue to one bipartition P=A|B of V(H)-{x}. Thus extend P to one global bipartition

  P*=A|B of V(H).                                          (CH.1)

Every spoke cover and every chosen rim-skeleton cover has support partition exactly equal to the restriction of P* to its deleted-pair residue.

### 2. One global block is automatically Hamiltonian
Name the classes so that

  x in A.                                                   (CH.2)

Suppose first that some P5 root d lies in A. The spoke cover on H-{x,d} has support partition

  (A-{x,d}) | B.

Both rails are literal tight Hamilton paths on their supports, so the intact block B is Hamiltonian.

Suppose instead that no P5 root lies in A. Then

  V(K) subseteq B.                                         (CH.3)

The connected rim skeleton G_R has at least one edge {d,e}. Its exact pair-deletion cover has support partition

  A | (B-{d,e}),

so the intact block A is Hamiltonian.

Therefore every quiet coherent full five-root wheel satisfies

  A Hamiltonian OR B Hamiltonian.                           (CH.4)

If both are Hamiltonian, those two disjoint paths span H, contradicting counterexamplehood. Hence the coherent nonclosing survivor has EXACTLY ONE non-Hamiltonian global block. This is already a fixed-complement critical configuration, not a diffuse wheel.

### 3. Residual when the non-Hamiltonian block is opposite the hub
Assume B is non-Hamiltonian, so A is Hamiltonian. Then no root d in K may lie in A: if d in A, the spoke deletion {x,d} would leave B intact as a Hamilton rail, contradiction. Thus necessarily

  K subseteq B.                                             (CH.5)

For every d in K, the spoke cover H-{x,d} has rails

  A-x   and   B-d.

Hence

  A-x is Hamiltonian,
  B-d is Hamiltonian for EVERY d in K.                      (CH.6)

Since K itself is a Hamilton P5 and B is non-Hamiltonian, B properly contains K. Thus the full-wheel residue in this orientation is a non-Hamiltonian block B with five distinguished Hamilton-deletable vertices forming the physical Hamilton P5 K, paired with a Hamilton complement A whose x-puncture is also Hamiltonian.

### 4. Residual when the non-Hamiltonian block contains the hub
Assume A is non-Hamiltonian, so B is Hamiltonian. Let

  K_A=K cap A,   K_B=K cap B.

K_A is nonempty: if K_A were empty, every rim-skeleton edge would delete two B-roots and leave A intact as a Hamilton rail, contradiction.

No rim-skeleton edge may have both endpoints in K_B, because deleting such a B-B edge again leaves A intact Hamiltonian. Therefore

  K_B is an independent set of the connected rim skeleton G_R.  (CH.7)

The coherent covers retain exact Hamilton puncture information:

- for d in K_A, the spoke {x,d} gives A-{x,d} Hamiltonian while B stays Hamiltonian;
- for d in K_B, the spoke gives A-x Hamiltonian and B-d Hamiltonian;
- for a rim edge a-b with a in K_A,b in K_B, its cover gives A-a Hamiltonian and B-b Hamiltonian;
- an A-A rim edge gives A-{a,a'} Hamiltonian with B intact.

Thus the only quiet survivor is again one non-Hamiltonian block carrying a highly synchronized deletion-Hamiltonian shell, now with the B-colored roots forced independent on the rim skeleton.
