# Two maximum forests generate a weighted collision preorder with explicit holonomy debt

**Workspace:** D17
**State:** established
**Key:** `compatible-forest-collision-preorder`

**Summary:** Encode two oriented compatible spanning k-path forests F0,F1 as bipartite matchings and flip whole symmetric-difference components. Unsafe physical mixed turns are exactly Boolean implications, so locally compatible recombinations are upper sets of a collision preorder; an upper-set recombination with gain delta(U) and c(U) physical directed-cycle components opens to a spanning compatible forest with k-delta(U)+c(U) paths, and delta(U)>c(U) is a genuine augmentation. Double-unsafe collisions retain explicit comparison holonomy. In the R887 acyclic branch c(U)=0. For any desired cross-component terminal-to-source edge e=t->s, R4 supplies at least one maximum forest containing e, initially as a literal dimer. Choose instead a closest maximum forest among ALL maximum forests containing the directed edge e. Since t_out and s_in are unmatched in the base forest, e remains an isolated +1 symmetric-difference component even when it is not a separate rail. For every proper collision-closed region U containing this seed, delta(U)<c(U): equality would allow one added nonseed edge to be removed from each directed cycle, preserving e and producing a closer maximum edge-containing representative. The remaining full-O4 target is to show that these corrected gain/cycle obstructions cannot trap all six ordered terminal-source merge choices of one maximum three-cover.

### 1. Matching exchange coordinates for arbitrary boundary path forests
Let F_0,F_1 be two spanning compatible path forests on the same finite boundary tournament H, with the same number k of components. Orient every nontrivial component in its tight direction. Encode F_i by the bipartite matching M_i in V_out disjoint_union V_in, selecting u_out v_in exactly when u->v is a consecutive directed edge of F_i. Then

  |M_0|=|M_1|=n-k.

Let C be the connected components of M_0 triangle M_1. Each is an alternating path or even cycle in the bipartite graph. Put

  delta(C)=|M_1 cap C|-|M_0 cap C| in {-1,0,1},

and sum_C delta(C)=0. For a set U of alternating components, let M_U use the M_1 edges on U, the M_0 edges off U, and all common edges. It is automatically a bipartite matching, with

  |M_U|=|M_0|+delta(U),
  delta(U)=sum_{C in U} delta(C).                       (CP.1)

The only new issue is physical turn compatibility and physical cycles.

### 2. Every physical mixed collision is a Boolean implication
Fix a physical vertex v. If its incoming selected edge is unchanged between F_0,F_1, or its outgoing selected edge is unchanged, every recombinant local state is inherited from one of the two tight source turns whenever both sides exist. Likewise, if the changed incoming copy v_in and changed outgoing copy v_out lie in the SAME alternating component, one flip bit chooses both sides from F_0 or both from F_1, again safe.

Thus only the case of two distinct alternating components matters. Write C_in(v),C_out(v) for them. Let p_i be the F_i predecessor of v when it exists and q_i the F_i successor when it exists. A mixed assignment chooses either

  incoming from F_1, outgoing from F_0:  p_1 -> v -> q_0,

or

  incoming from F_0, outgoing from F_1:  p_0 -> v -> q_1. (CP.2)

If one chosen side is absent, the state is a source/terminal and is safe. If both sides exist and the predecessor equals the successor, the mixed state is a physical backtrack u->v->u and is forbidden. If the three physical vertices are distinct, the mixed state is safe exactly when the corresponding ordered triple is tight.

For every unsafe mixed state impose the corresponding implication on flip bits. With bit 1 meaning 'use F_1 on this alternating component':

  unsafe (1,0)  gives  C_in <= C_out,
  unsafe (0,1)  gives  C_out <= C_in.                   (CP.3)

Let <= be the preorder generated by all such physical implications. Then a subset U of alternating components avoids every local mixed collision exactly when U is an upper set of this preorder. This is the boundary-turn analogue of the R980 collision preorder, with bad triples/backtracks replacing failed numerical inequalities.

### 3. Double-unsafe collisions are explicit local holonomy
Suppose both mixed states in (CP.2) are unsafe. They cannot both be backtracks: p_0=q_1 and p_1=q_0 would make the two source turns complete reversals, contradicting R3 because both F_0,F_1 are tight at v.

First suppose neither mixed state is a backtrack. Then p_0,q_0,p_1,q_1 are four distinct neighbors. The source turns

  (p_0,v,q_0), (p_1,v,q_1)

are tight, while the two mixed turns are bad. R3 reverses the mixed failures to

  (q_0,v,p_1), (q_1,v,p_0) tight.

Therefore the local comparison tournament at v contains

  vp_0 -> vq_0 -> vp_1 -> vq_1 -> vp_0.                (CP.4)

The induced five-set {v,p_0,q_0,p_1,q_1} is nonintegrable; accepted R902 gives an actual Hamilton tight P5 on those five vertices. This is a five-vertex holonomy blossom, with no endpoint prescription.

Now suppose one mixed state is the backtrack, say p_0=q_1=u, and the other mixed state (p_1,v,q_0) is bad. R3 gives (q_0,v,p_1). Together with the two source turns, the three incident edges vu,vq_0,vp_1 form a directed star triangle in the local comparison tournament at v. Thus the double-unsafe degenerate collision still contains an explicit shortest R887 holonomy certificate, though no Hamilton P4/P5 conclusion is asserted from this four-vertex star cell.

Hence a bidirectional implication/equality is never anonymous: it comes with a local comparison-holonomy blossom.

### 4. Upper sets are locally tight directed pseudoforests
Let U be an upper set. By construction every physical vertex avoids every unsafe mixed state. Therefore M_U has physical indegree and outdegree at most one, contains no selected two-edge backtrack, and every selected predecessor-successor triple on distinct vertices is tight. Its physical components are consequently directed tight paths, singleton vertices, or vertex-simple directed tight cycles. Conversely any componentwise flip whose physical realization has these properties avoids every unsafe state and hence is an upper-set assignment.

The cardinality formula remains (CP.1). If M_U has c(U) directed cycle components, delete one selected edge from each cycle. Every cycle opens to a tight Hamilton path on its own support; deleting edges creates no new turns. The resulting object is a genuine compatible spanning linear forest with

  k - delta(U) + c(U)                                  (CP.5)

path components. Therefore

  delta(U) > c(U)                                       (CP.6)

is a genuine component-reducing augmentation criterion. In particular, starting from k=3, (CP.6) gives a spanning two-cover.

This identifies the exact extra obstruction absent from R980: positive matching gain may be spent on physical directed-cycle debt.

### 5. The acyclic comparison branch has zero cycle debt
If Gamma(H) is acyclic, a physical directed tight cycle in M_U would itself give a directed comparison cycle, impossible. Thus c(U)=0 for every locally compatible upper set. Likewise a double-unsafe collision from Section 3 would already give a local directed comparison cycle, impossible. Therefore in the acyclic R887 branch every collision forbids at most one mixed state and every upper-set recombination is automatically a genuine path forest. The boundary formulation then specializes exactly to the weighted collision-poset mechanism of accepted R980.

This supplies the required acyclic-side lifting mechanism without pretending that comparison chains alone are path forests in the cyclic branch.

### 6. Every terminal-source merge has a canonical isolated +1 seed
Now specialize to a hypothetical smallest counterexample and a maximum spanning three-cover F_0. Let t be the terminal of one F_0 rail and s the source of a different rail, with the displayed orientation t->s for the desired merge edge e. The dimer (t,s) is a proper tight path. By accepted R4 its complement has an exact two-cover, so there exists a maximum spanning three-cover F_1 containing (t,s) literally as one dimer rail.

In M_0, t_out and s_in are both unmatched because t is an F_0 terminal and s is an F_0 source. In M_1 they are joined by the new edge e=t_out s_in. Hence e by itself is a connected component E of M_0 triangle M_1 and

  delta(E)=+1.                                           (CP.7)

Thus every attempted physical component merge starts from a canonical unit-gain alternating component. All obstruction to taking that gain alone is carried by collision implications emanating from E.

### 7. Closest edge-containing representatives force surplus cycle debt on every proper closed gain region
Among ALL maximum spanning three-forests F_1 whose directed matching contains the physical edge

  e=t_out s_in,

choose one minimizing |M_0 triangle M_1|. This minimizing class is nonempty by Section 6, because R4 supplies a representative in which (t,s) is literally a dimer rail. The minimizing condition itself does NOT require e to remain a separate rail.

Because t_out and s_in are unmatched in M_0 and are joined to each other in M_1, the single edge e is still an isolated connected component E of M_0 triangle M_1, regardless of what M_1 does at the distinct copies t_in and s_out. Hence

  delta(E)=+1.                                           (CP.7')

Retain the collision preorder and this isolated gain seed E.

Claim: for every PROPER upper set U containing E,

  delta(U) < c(U).                                       (CP.8)

Indeed, if delta(U)>c(U), Section 4 gives a spanning compatible forest with fewer than three components, contradicting counterexamplehood. Suppose delta(U)=c(U).

If c(U)=0, M_U itself is a maximum spanning three-forest containing e. Since U is a proper subset of the full symmetric-difference component set, M_U differs from M_0 on strictly fewer symmetric-difference edges than F_1, contradicting the minimizing choice of F_1.

Assume c(U)>=1. Every directed physical cycle of M_U contains at least one added edge from M_U-M_0, because M_0 is a forest. If such a cycle contains the distinguished edge e=t->s, it contains another added edge besides e. Otherwise deleting e from that cycle would leave an M_0 path from s back to t, impossible because s and t lie in different M_0 components. Therefore choose, on every directed cycle, one added edge different from e and delete those c(U) edges.

Deleting one edge from each directed tight cycle creates no new turns and opens every cycle into a tight path. The resulting object is a compatible spanning linear forest, still contains e, and has

  |M_0| + delta(U) - c(U)=|M_0|

selected edges, hence exactly three components. It is therefore another maximum three-forest in the corrected minimizing class. All of its changes relative to M_0 lie inside the proper component set U, with some added edges removed when the cycles were opened. Since U omits at least one nonempty component of M_0 triangle M_1, its symmetric difference from M_0 is strictly smaller than that of F_1. Contradiction. This proves (CP.8).

Thus the closest-representative obstruction survives intact after enlarging the minimizing class: every proper collision-closed region containing the isolated +1 seed either has net negative alternating weight or carries strictly more directed-cycle debt than its gain.

### 8. Moonshot interpretation and remaining consumer
The compatible-forest augmentation theorem is now reduced to destroying this corrected gain-transport obstruction for at least one of the six ordered terminal-to-source pairs of one maximum three-cover. In the acyclic branch cycle debt is identically zero, so a blocked isolated gain is transported by physical collision implications into loss components. In the cyclic branch, gain may instead be trapped in explicit tight-cycle blossoms or local double-unsafe holonomy cells.

The next target is a SIMULTANEOUS PHYSICAL MERGE ESCAPE theorem: the six corrected edge-containing comparisons rooted at one maximum three-cover cannot all realize nonaugmenting charged-preorder / cycle-debt obstructions. Any representative reselection is legal provided the directed seed edge and all physical ancestry are tracked. No claim of that final step is made here.
