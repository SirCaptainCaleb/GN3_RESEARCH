# Architecture

## Authority and surfaces

**This Canvas is the sole active architecture authority.** Slack Engines are the active coherent proof surface; workspace `R<N>` items are notebook movements whose audits certify local validity, not automatic theorems or publications; GitHub `main` is the durable surface for completed `[PASS]` Engines, deliberate Spare Parts, history, and archaeology. There is no hidden registry. **Outside an active audit, the research default is optimistic:** every Slack-posted mathematical result is treated as true and available for use immediately unless it has been explicitly failed, invalidated, quarantined, or superseded. Audit status records certification debt; it does not create a hard mathematical frontier for research. Read the entire relevant Engine thread before reasoning from it, and understand its actual witnesses, physical vertices, literal orders, selected states, cuts, ancestry, equality cases, and exceptions rather than treating the interface as a lossless black box.

## Workspace roots: headline only

**Every workspace root is one concise, mathematically precise headline statement. This is non-negotiable.** Use `R<N> E<M> <headline>`; use `E?` only when the destination is genuinely unknown. The root contains only the mathematical statement plus indispensable hypotheses needed to make it well posed. **Proofs, sketches, derivations, case analysis, caveats, provenance, dependency lists, computation, audit reasoning, discussion, plans, bookkeeping, status narration, and progress reports belong in replies or the worker conversation, never in the root.** Reply 1 normally begins the proof or derivation. Prefer movements that advance, repair, test, or sharply fence a coherent Engine rather than miscellaneous nameable mechanisms. Inspect the current R maximum before allocating a number and repair concurrent collisions immediately. Root count is not a productivity metric. When deleting a Slack thread, delete replies first and the root last.

## Engine lifecycle

<#C0C2NHL61PS> is the Engine room. An Engine root is a concise vision statement and bounded completion contract, approximately **input state(s) -> bounded output/interface**, optionally with one short mechanism sketch. Anyone may propose the next free E-number; inspect the live frontier and repair collisions. Engine numbers are identifiers rather than proof-topology order. Before working on an Engine, read its entire current thread.

Engines are scoped mechanisms, not containers for the whole theorem. During every Engine assessment the Vice Director must restate the contract in one sentence, classify the frontier as **contract-completion** or **downstream-consumption**, and ask whether a stable interface already exists. Before substantial successor work, also test whether a completed Engine or Core Spare Part already consumes the parent interface, or does so after one elementary transformation of its raw witnesses. If substantial remaining work consumes a stable output rather than establishing it, freeze or narrow the old contract and move the consumer to a successor Engine. Such narrowing must preserve the Engine's mathematical identity and may not disguise a genuine failure. A valid bypass, contraction, or Engine deletion is a first-class research success. Repeated contract expansion requires explicit justification.

After an Engine-associated R-result passes audit, propagate its surviving statement and actual proof mechanism into the Engine thread with enough hypotheses/caveats to understand it; workspace links are provenance, not substitutes. Corrections propagate to every live consumer. **Backward applicability is also mandatory, and it is resultwise rather than occasional:** for every new or materially relevant result on the recent frontier of the active Engine, the Integrator or Vice Director first performs the elevation test described in the Director cycle below, then tests both the original result and every proved elevated parent statement against the earliest plausible retained interfaces of the active proof spine. Start from the earliest retained states and use their actual witnesses, allowing one cheap elementary transformation when natural. Do not stop at the Engine in which the result was discovered. If the mechanism works earlier, recompute the shortest route and explicitly test whether an Engine, case split, state coordinate, or auxiliary machine is now bypassed, contractible, or obsolete. Because every researcher has read the full active proof spine, ordinary Researchers are also expected to notice and pursue cross-Engine connections when they arise naturally during local work; the Integrator/Vice Director owns the systematic sweep, not exclusive awareness of the chain.

Engine threads are curated. `[UNHELPFUL]` means low utility, not falsehood. A second researcher who agrees may remove that reply; disagreement becomes `[UNHELPFUL CONTESTED]` and is surfaced in changelog for Director/Vice Director arbitration.

`[RETIRED]` means a mathematically valid boundary was superseded by a better decomposition, not failure. `E8999` and `E9000` are retired provenance. `[FAIL] E<N>` is terminal only when the stated mechanism is mathematically impossible or internally incoherent under its scope after a reasonable repair attempt. One researcher gives the obstruction and repair attempt; a different researcher independently confirms the obstruction **and that no repair preserving the same Engine remains**. Hard, stalled, unfashionable, or superseded is not FAIL. After confirmation, mark the Engine root `[FAIL]`, surface it in changelog, and stop routing active work there. A materially different rescue receives a new E-number; specifically reusable surviving mathematics may become a Spare Part.

## Audit protocol

A fresh unaudited R-root has no audit tag. **Outside the Auditor role, workers optimistically assume Slack-posted unaudited results are true and may reason from them immediately.** Researchers, Integrators, Vice Directors, and Astra should not throttle mathematics merely because a useful dependency is awaiting audit. A downstream result built on unaudited inputs still carries certification debt for publication and later audit bookkeeping, but that debt is not a reason to hedge every research step or wait. Researchers must not self-certify their own new results as `[PASS]` or `[PASS_ADJUSTED]`. If a later audit repairs, weakens, or fails a dependency, propagate that correction promptly through affected live consumers.

**Audit is the exception to the optimistic-truth rule.** It is a separate skeptical lane, normally owned by a dedicated **Auditor** rather than by ordinary Researchers or the Vice Director. While auditing a claimed result, verify it from its actual proof and dependencies rather than assuming the target true merely because other workers are using it. Active audit ownership is `[CLAIMED X]` with a fresh random 4-6 character nonce. Dependency-settled verdicts inside the certified chronological prefix are `[PASS]`, `[PASS_ADJUSTED]`, or `[FAIL]`; beyond an earlier unresolved dependency use `X PENDING`; dependency-blocked passing claims use `X CONTINGENT` and name the contingency. Do not use bare `[CLAIMED]`, `TARGETED`, bare `[PENDING]`, or active `[INTERESTING]`.

To audit, the Auditor writes `[CLAIMED X]` and **immediately rereads the root**. Proceed only if that exact nonce remains. Before a final passing verdict/status edit, reread again and verify the same nonce still owns the claim. If ownership changed, do not overwrite or issue the final verdict; material defect evidence may still be posted in-thread for the current owner. Audit useful and load-bearing movements first, not necessarily the chronologically oldest. **Research work does not pause while this happens.**

Repair before failure: try a direct proof, nearby trusted machinery, bounded provenance reconstruction, and a useful `[PASS_ADJUSTED]` statement. Missing provenance alone is proof debt, not falsity. Contain known contradictions promptly.

Passing an R-audit does **not** cause GitHub promotion. For a live Engine, the auditor propagates the surviving theorem and proof mechanism into its Engine thread and assesses Engine completeness. For `E?`, identify a coherent destination or leave it unpromoted.

The dedicated Auditor continuously cleans the relevant unresolved certification debt, prioritizing load-bearing dependencies and recent results feeding the active Engine. `PENDING`/`CONTINGENT` are used honestly when blocked, but they **do not define a research cutoff**. Outside audit, later workers continue to treat those posted mathematical statements optimistically as true unless contrary evidence appears. Ordinary Researchers are **not expected to spend research cycles auditing** unless they explicitly take the Auditor role or are asked for an independent check. Root/audit counts are not productivity metrics. Start audits from the root, its thread, named dependencies, and relevant Engine context; use archaeology only as needed.

## Director cycle and Astra escalation

The default worker is an autonomous Researcher; Explorer, Elevator, Integrator, and Moonshotting are temporary cognitive mantles. Anyone may propose an Engine. The Integrator mantle is explicitly global and synthesis-seeking: ask why the current state is needed, try later machinery earlier, match reusable theorems to upstream raw states, and look for contractions, bypasses, or retired machinery rather than merely extending the frontier. Vice Director handles routine continuous direction and wave integration; Astra is scarce capacity for genuine strategic forks, load-bearing integration, elevation, and consequential moonshots.

**The Director cycle is non-negotiable:** **synchronize with the full live mathematical state, including newly posted concurrent results, while separately noting the Auditor's current certification state -> assess the real Engine state and perform the Engine scope-drift check -> integrate proof-aware mathematics into the strongest working mechanism, including skeptical `[UNHELPFUL]` curation -> perform resultwise elevation on the recent active-Engine frontier -> perform the corresponding earliest-interface backward-applicability/compression sweep -> moonshot toward closure -> issue a small number of valuable focuses -> record the Astra escalation disposition.** There is no hard certified-frontier cutoff for direction: outside audit, recent unaudited Slack results are working mathematical facts and may be used in Vice-Director guidance.

**Resultwise elevation is mandatory.** For every new or materially relevant mathematical result in the most recent active Engine thread (at minimum, every result added since the previous Vice-Director cycle; on a cold start, the whole recent frontier), including useful results posted concurrently while the cycle is in progress, ask what broader mechanism it is exhibiting: a parent theorem, invariant reformulation, reusable local principle, stronger hypothesis-free statement, or common explanation of several neighboring lemmas. Outside audit, perform this analysis under the optimistic assumption that posted unaudited results are true. Test the strongest natural generalization against the actual proof, hypotheses, equality cases, and plausible counterexamples. Promote only what is actually supported by that proof; preserve the solid local fact when the generalization fails, and record the failure as a scope fence rather than replacing fact by conjecture.

**Then, for each result and each proved elevation, ask how far back the technique applies.** Test it against the actual witnesses at every plausible earlier interface of the active proof spine, not merely the current Engine input. If it applies earlier, follow the consequence aggressively: shorten the route, absorb casework, contract interfaces, bypass or retire machinery, or delete an Engine from the shortest proof topology when justified. This per-result elevation-then-backreach operation is required even when the current architecture does not look especially complicated; added complexity remains an extra trigger, not the only trigger.

The Vice Director does not stop a research wave for routine audit cleanup. Certification debt is surfaced by the Auditor but does not gate research or guidance. **Concurrent mathematics is part of the live state:** Researchers and Directors should periodically notice newly posted relevant results during their work and incorporate them when they materially change the mechanism, target, or shortest route rather than finishing against a stale snapshot. This is a should, not a demand for constant channel polling; synchronize at natural mathematical boundaries. A moonshot should be genuinely unlikely; theorem closure is a hit, while an important fence or reduction from a failed attempt is still valuable.

**Wave guidance is team guidance by default, not per-researcher assignment.** Publish the mathematical focuses and operational constraints on the control/changelog channel, then let autonomous Researchers self-select and coordinate useful attacks within that envelope. Individual researcher directions are exceptional and should be used only when a genuinely isolated ownership need, audit conflict, or other concrete operational reason makes them worthwhile; do not routinely partition the mathematics into named worker lanes. Team-composition guidance may specify an overall concurrency target or role mix without prescribing each Researcher's mathematical task.

Escalate to Astra when scarce high-level judgment is likely to change direction: a genuine strategic fork, collapse of the governing architecture, a load-bearing audit failure, exhaustion of the current narrow target, or a consequential team-wide refocus. Do not escalate merely because a local obligation is hard. Astra should not do clerical work, routine audit administration, or solver/computation work. An escalation handoff gives the governing target, strongest trusted frontier, provisional dependencies, exact fork/failure, fences, and requested judgment. **Every Vice Director cycle records its escalation disposition; if not escalating, state briefly why the state remains local/routine and which trigger is absent.**

## Terminal review and publication

**Engine terminal review is two-mind, two-sided, standalone, Vice-Director-composed, and workspace-audited.** When an Engine appears complete under its frozen contract, the Vice Director writes a fresh composition `R<N>` root whose root remains headline-only; reply 1 begins the complete standalone proof. If needed, continue through consecutive composition replies, clearly ordered and with no unrelated interleaving. Those replies are one proof document; later defects, repairs, and audit reasoning come only after the composition sequence. Ordinary Researchers may prepare assembly maps, branch checks, local lemma packages, or editorial suggestions, but these are inputs to the Vice Director composition, not substitutes for it.

The terminal proof must read as mathematics, not research history: no workspace R-number dependencies, audit-status language, Slack/changelog/architecture references, or proof-by-transcript. Every Engine-internal ingredient needed for verification is stated and proved inline; previously completed published Engines or genuine external references may be used only through clearly stated exact interfaces.

An independent **Auditor** claims and audits **the whole composition document** for mathematics, explicit hypotheses, branch coverage, completion-contract fidelity, continuity across message boundaries, and standalone form. The Auditor must be independent of the composition author. Material audit repairs must be incorporated into one recomposed theorem-and-proof document and trigger a new whole-document audit. Before promotion, every load-bearing hypothesis and specialized definition must be explicit, the exact exported conclusion/interface must be stated, and any final interface change triggers a downstream-consumer check.

The GitHub artifact must be journal-like: no audit transcript, repair log, patch list, or development commentary. Prefer a small number of meaningful local lemmas over micro-atomization. **Only after the final recomposed document passes independent whole-document audit may the Engine root become `[PASS]` and `A7C3/ENGINES/E<N>.md` be created or replaced.** A draft containing R-number proof dependencies is only an assembly map, not terminal-review eligible. Failed Engines are not published under `ENGINES/`; only specifically reusable rescued mathematics may become Spare Parts.
