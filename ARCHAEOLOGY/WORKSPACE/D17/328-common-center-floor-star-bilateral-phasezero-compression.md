### 2. Every root has one literal internal-middle frame with two endpoint channels

  J=(e_L,b,e_R),   {e_L,e_R}={a,d}.                       (BZ.6)

  TWO-COVER or explicit nonquiet geometry.                 (BZ.8)

### 4. R445 protected return disappears completely
Use the predecessor channel of J as the first birth and apply usable historical R445/P459 to the pair of same-frame predecessor/successor births. R445 has only three output types:

1. TWO-COVER;
2. explicit non-descent local geometry: anchor/witness contact, strict growth/fusion or +2 extension, reverse-contact/reversal trimer/cell, signed-BB reverse P4, or vertex-simple tight cycle;

Output 3 is impossible in the epsilon_*-minimal stratum. If e_L was fresh, that completed R434 episode would append e_L to A_* and lower epsilon_*. Therefore every root floor E_d satisfies the sharpened same-frame alternative

  TWO-COVER,

No protected-return residue remains.
