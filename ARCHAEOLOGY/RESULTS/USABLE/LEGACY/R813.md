# R813 — Every four-complement insertion gap already forces a local Hamilton P4; no payment branch remains

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a finite Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament with pc(H)>2, let Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=2, be a vertex-simple tight path, and suppose its complement X=V(H)\\V(Q) has exactly four vertices. Then every gap q_i|q_{i+1}, 0<=i<m, supports a tight Hamilton P4 on a four-set containing vertices from both sides of that gap and at least one vertex of X. More precisely, apply accepted R583 at the gap. If the reverse-middle branch occurs, accepted R584 gives one of (q_{i+1},x,q_i,y) or (q_{i+1},y,q_i,x) for distinct x,y in X. If a left two-witness packet occurs, choose distinct x,y in X with (x,q_i,q_{i-1}) and (y,q_i,q_{i-1}) tight (so i>=1), and apply accepted R518 to the tight Q-trimer K=(q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}) with probe x. Either K union {x} has a Hamilton P4, or the no-P4 branch forces (q_i,q_{i-1},x) tight. In the latter case (y,q_i,q_{i-1}) is a head certificate and (q_i,q_{i-1},x) a tail certificate on the same tested dimer (q_i,q_{i-1}); accepted R523 yields the literal P4 (y,q_i,q_{i-1},x). The right packet is exact dual: from distinct x,y with (q_{i+2},q_{i+1},x),(q_{i+2},q_{i+1},y) tight, probe K=(q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) by x; if no P4, R518 forces (x,q_{i+2},q_{i+1}), which with the old tail certificate of y yields the literal P4 (x,q_{i+2},q_{i+1},y) by R523. The endpoint gaps are covered by the R583 endpoint versions: only the available terminal packet or reverse-middle branch occurs. Hence the CP/capture-payment branch of accepted R588 can be strengthened at the graph-intrinsic level to P4; no R542 payment is needed to consume any R583 gap.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1), pc(H)>2, Q tight of order at least three, exactly four vertices outside Q. Accepted R583,R584,R518,R523.

## Scope
Order-free strengthening of the R583/R588 four-complement gap compiler. No smallest-counterexample minimality, R24,R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R542,payment,capture,R436,Reverse Ear,or computation.

## Applicability
In the R24 order-three hard frame supplied by R594, every gap of the long rail Q therefore has a graph-intrinsic local P4 output before any signed-payment spend. Use the simultaneous family of gap-local P4s as the object to compare across gaps; do not route R583 terminal packets into R542 merely to obtain progress.

## Persistence
Retain the gap index i, the relevant R583 witness identities, the physical adjacent Q coordinates, and the exact P4 order produced in the non-P4 R518/R523 or R584 branch. Direct R518 P4 outputs retain their labelled support even if their exact Hamilton order is not unique.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the P4 at different gaps can be chosen simultaneously with compatible orders, that the same witness pair recurs, that a gap P4 contains the carrier state q_i q_{i+1} consecutively, or that any one local P4 closes H. It does not prove R24 by itself.

## Proof
Fix a gap i. Accepted R583 gives its reverse-middle alternative or one of the available terminal two-witness alternatives. In the reverse-middle alternative there are distinct x,y with (q_{i+1},x,q_i) and (q_{i+1},y,q_i) tight; accepted R584 gives one of the literal P4s (q_{i+1},x,q_i,y),(q_{i+1},y,q_i,x). Suppose instead the left terminal packet occurs. Then i>=1 and there are distinct x,y with (x,q_i,q_{i-1}),(y,q_i,q_{i-1}) tight. The old Q-trimer K=(q_{i-1},q_i,q_{i+1}) is tight. Apply accepted R518 to K with fourth vertex x. If K union {x} has a Hamilton P4, we are done. Otherwise the exact no-P4 conclusion of R518 includes (q_i,q_{i-1},x) tight. On the tested dimer D=(q_i,q_{i-1}), y is therefore a head witness via (y,q_i,q_{i-1}) and x is a tail witness via (q_i,q_{i-1},x). Since x!=y, accepted R523 gives the literal tight P4 (y,q_i,q_{i-1},x). The right terminal packet is dual. There i+2<=m and distinct x,y satisfy (q_{i+2},q_{i+1},x),(q_{i+2},q_{i+1},y) tight. Probe K=(q_i,q_{i+1},q_{i+2}) by x. If no Hamilton P4 occurs, R518 gives (x,q_{i+2},q_{i+1}) tight. On D=(q_{i+2},q_{i+1}), x is head and y is tail, so R523 gives (x,q_{i+2},q_{i+1},y). At i=0 and i=m-1, R583 omits the unavailable terminal side and the same arguments handle the remaining branches. Thus every gap yields a graph-intrinsic P4.
