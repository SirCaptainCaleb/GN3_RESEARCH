# R32 — Root basins and extremal transfer components

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

A cover is isolated in the fixed-root transfer graph exactly when every endpoint is completely shielded. The whole graph is edgeless exactly when every exact deletion cover is completely shielded. If an edge exists, that edge enters the accepted good-splice/root-mate basin without importing selected states from its endpoints.

## Scope
Within one transfer component, maximize the rail-size gap. No transfer can move an endpoint from the shorter rail to the longer rail there, so the longer rail is triply shielded at both ends; at least one transfer from longer to shorter remains. The minimum-gap dual is valid only when the minimum gap is at least two. Extremality belongs to the chosen component and representative, not to the entire transfer universe.
