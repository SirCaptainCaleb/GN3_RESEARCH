# R208 — Six-exterior singleton-root common-witness terminal-dimer rectangle

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample, let K=(k_1,...,k_m), m>=5, be a proper global-longest tight path with six-vertex exterior E, and let e be any R196 omission for which Q_e=(q_1,...,q_5) is a tight P5 on E\\{e}. Then the four reverse terminal dimers D_K^L=(k_2,k_1), D_K^R=(k_m,k_{m-1}), D_Q^L=(q_2,q_1), D_Q^R=(q_5,q_4) are all signed by the same physical witness e. The two left dimers have one common polarity and the two right dimers the opposite polarity. Hence every left dimer forms a direct mass-four balanced pair with every right dimer, giving a 2-by-2 common-witness rectangle of pair births that includes both cross-rail pairs D_K^L with D_Q^R and D_Q^L with D_K^R. R196 supplies at least four distinct singleton roots carrying such a rectangle.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample; K is proper and global-longest with |E|=6; e is one of the at least four omissions supplied by accepted R196; Q_e is a chosen tight ordering of E\\{e}. Boundary antisymmetry R3, signed terminal-dimer convention R41, and direct balanced-pair theorem R39 are available.

## Scope
Order-free, root-specific coupling in the six-exterior slack-one face. No maximin singleton-root normalization, payment synchronization, or order-ten geometry is used.

## Applicability
Use instead of anonymous puncture-pair births when a literal R196 root is available. It ties graph-intrinsic signed supports simultaneously to the terminal geometry of the retained global-longest carrier and the exterior P5, providing cross-rail pair choices for capture/refund/fixed-turn descendants.

## Persistence
The four physical terminal dimers, common witness e, tested reverse turns, and polarity rectangle are graph-intrinsic. The literal root K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e} is a separate current representative for the chosen e.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say any of the four dimer supports are separate current cover components, does not put a cross pair directly into the literal three-component form required by R171, does not synchronize rectangles for different e in one representative, and does not itself force exterior-size descent or a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
Fix an R196 root H=K sqcup Q_e sqcup {e}. Since K is global-longest, neither (e,k_1,k_2) nor (k_{m-1},k_m,e) can be tight, because either would extend K to a longer tight path. R3 therefore gives (k_2,k_1,e) and (e,k_m,k_{m-1}) tight. Thus D_K^L and D_K^R are signed by witness e with opposite terminal polarities. Next E itself cannot support a tight P6: if it did, that P6 together with the disjoint tight path K would be a spanning two-cover of H. Therefore, in the chosen ordering Q_e=(q_1,...,q_5), neither (e,q_1,q_2) nor (q_4,q_5,e) can be tight, since either would extend Q_e to a tight P6 on all of E. R3 gives (q_2,q_1,e) and (e,q_5,q_4) tight. Hence D_Q^L and D_Q^R are likewise signed by e with opposite terminal polarities. The left reverse turns have the same witness placement pattern, as do the right reverse turns, so D_K^L and D_Q^L have the same polarity, while D_K^R and D_Q^R have the opposite polarity. All carrier dimers are disjoint from all exterior dimers; m>=5 makes D_K^L,D_K^R disjoint, and the order-five Q_e makes D_Q^L,D_Q^R disjoint. By R39 every left/right choice is therefore a direct balanced pair of total support size four. R196 provides at least four choices of e with Q_e, yielding at least four such root-specific rectangles.
