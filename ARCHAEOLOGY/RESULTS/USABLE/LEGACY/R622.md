Otherwise it reaches an ancestry-bearing both-singleton balanced-pair floor while retaining the original Pi birth and the full refund ledger. Apply accepted R432 with prescribed target pair {h,t}. Choose any vertex b distinct from h,t; such a vertex exists in the R621 frame since |X|=4 and m>=4. By R3 exactly one of (h,b,t) and (t,b,h) is a proper tight turn J; retain that orientation.
