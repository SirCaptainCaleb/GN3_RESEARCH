# R285 — Bilateral four-core capture yields an anchored carrier-spoke interaction fan

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let K=(v_0,...,v_m) be a tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament, let d be a vertex outside K, and let B be any set of internal vertices of K. For each b=v_i in B with carrier neighbors l_b=v_{i-1} and r_b=v_{i+1}, the physical spoke {b,d} carries two signed certificates with the distinct witnesses l_b and r_b. Consequently R38 forces on every such spoke either the same-polarity two-extension collision package or the opposite-polarity strict two-ended signed-path interaction. Thus one fixed exterior anchor d supports an R38-productive spoke at every chosen internal carrier position. In the historical R250 repeated-dimer setting this applies to every internal member of the class B, hence at at least |B|-2 positions.

## Hypotheses
Strong Level-(1) boundary antisymmetry; K is a graph-intrinsic tight path; d is outside K; B consists of internal vertices of K.

## Scope
Order-free capture-free replacement for the invalid R251 capture-to-spoke proof.

## Applicability
Use wherever the old R251 interaction fan was consumed only for its signed-spoke/R38 conclusion. The R250 class-size count may still be imported, but no capture ancestry is attached to the fresh spokes.

## Persistence
K, d, the carrier neighbors, centered trimer certificates, spoke supports, witnesses, and R38 interaction outputs are graph-intrinsic. Any unrelated historical captures at the same carrier vertices remain separate ancestry facts.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not claim that any historical capture reenters {b,d}; does not invoke R155; does not make different R38 descendants simultaneous; does not currentize a whole fan.

## Proof
Fix b=v_i. Boundary antisymmetry gives exactly one tight b-middle orientation of {l_b,b,d}; whichever orientation is tight, its terminal dimer {b,d} has l_b as the opposite outer witness under R41. Likewise the unique b-middle tight trimer on {r_b,b,d} signs the same physical spoke with witness r_b. Since l_b and r_b are distinct, R38 applies to these two tested certificates. This is independent of any capture event or representative. Repeating independently over B gives the fixed-anchor fan. In the R250 setting only the at most two endpoints of K fail internality, so at least |B|-2 class vertices contribute.
