# R335 — Unit Hamilton-five density does not force a tight P6

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
There exists a six-vertex Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament on V={0,1,2,3,4,5} in which every one of the six physical five-subsets supports a tight P5, but V itself supports no tight P6. Thus even Hamilton-five support density 1 does not force path growth from order five to order six. Consequently any generalization of the order-ten proof that needs P5 density to force longer-path existence must use additional structure beyond the density statistic itself, even if that density were strengthened all the way to 100%.

## Hypotheses
Explicit six-vertex Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament. For each middle b, notation a>c means (a,b,c) is tight. The local tournaments are: T0: 1>2,1>3,1>4,5>1,3>2,4>2,5>2,3>4,5>3,5>4. T1: 0>2,3>0,0>4,5>0,3>2,4>2,5>2,3>4,5>3,5>4. T2: 1>0,3>0,4>0,5>0,3>1,4>1,5>1,4>3,5>3,4>5. T3: 1>0,0>2,4>0,0>5,1>2,4>1,5>1,4>2,5>2,4>5. T4: 0>1,2>0,3>0,5>0,2>1,3>1,1>5,3>2,2>5,3>5. T5: 1>0,0>2,0>3,0>4,1>2,1>3,1>4,3>2,2>4,3>4.

## Scope
Explicit local regression fence for density-only path-growth arguments. No smallest-counterexample hypothesis.

## Applicability
Use to reject any inference of the form sufficiently high Hamilton-five density alone forces a tight P6. The obstruction survives even at density one.

## Persistence
The explicit turn table and Hamiltonicity/non-Hamiltonicity statements are graph-intrinsic and finitely checkable.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not refute a theorem using smallest-counterexample exact-cover structure, role-bearing ancestry, or other information in addition to P5 density. Does not assert the example is a smallest counterexample.

## Proof
Boundary antisymmetry is built into the displayed local-tournament encoding: each unordered outer pair at each middle has exactly one orientation. P5 witnesses, indexed by their five-set, are: {0,1,2,3,4}: (3,0,4,1,2); {0,1,2,3,5}: (0,3,5,2,1); {0,1,2,4,5}: (1,5,4,0,2); {0,1,3,4,5}: (0,5,3,1,4); {0,2,3,4,5}: (3,5,4,0,2); {1,2,3,4,5}: (4,3,5,2,1). Direct finite enumeration of all 6!=720 vertex orderings against the displayed turn table finds zero tight P6 orderings. Hence all six five-subsets are Hamiltonian while the six-set is not.
