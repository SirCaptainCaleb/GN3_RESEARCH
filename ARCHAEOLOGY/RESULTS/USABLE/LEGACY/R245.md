# R245 — Occupied four-core Hamilton-five support cascade

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Assume the five-exterior equality face and let S be a four-core with completion set A={a:S union {a} is a global-longest exterior}, |A|=r. Then: (1) for every distinct a,b in A, the six-set S union {a,b} has exactly four Hamiltonian five-subsets, namely (S\\{s}) union {a,b} for s in S; the two remaining five-subsets S union {a} and S union {b} are non-Hamiltonian. Equivalently, for every s and every tight trimer orientation of S\\{s}, R146 applied with exterior triple {a,b,s} is forced to exit through the unique cross-support (S\\{s}) union {a,b}. (2) For every three distinct completions a,b,c in A there are at least two distinct physical pairs R subset S such that R union {a,b,c} supports a tight P5. Consequently S union A contains at least 4*binom(r,2) distinct Hamilton five-supports of type 3-core+2-completion and at least 2*binom(r,3) distinct Hamilton five-supports of type 2-core+3-completion.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the five-exterior equality face; accepted R240 cross-replacement saturation, R195 four-of-six Hamilton-five density, R146 disjoint-trimer three-extension forcing, and R8 trimer existence.

## Scope
Pure graph-intrinsic support cascade localized on one occupied physical four-core. No carrier-order synchronization, exact-cover currentization, sign payment, or spectral argument is used.

## Applicability
Use when a high-occupancy four-core must be converted into a richer local Hamilton-support packet. The second layer supplies supports containing three completion vertices, which may be used for carrier-carrier signed-dimer or insertion-obstruction arguments unavailable from pairwise R240 cells alone.

## Persistence
S, A and every listed five-support are graph-intrinsic. The P5 orderings witnessing Hamiltonicity need not be synchronized.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not force a P6, does not make any listed support a longest-carrier exterior, does not synchronize P5 orderings, and does not imply type 1-core+4-completion supports are Hamiltonian.

## Proof
Fix a!=b in A. R240 says Q_s=(S-{s}) union {a,b} is Hamiltonian for every s in S. The other two five-subsets of W=S union {a,b} are E_a=S union {a} and E_b=S union {b}; each is the exterior of a global-longest carrier, hence cannot be Hamiltonian, since otherwise that exterior path together with its complementary carrier would be a spanning two-cover. Thus W has exactly the four cross Hamiltonian five-subsets. Now fix s. By R8 orient S-{s} as a tight trimer and apply R146 with the three exterior vertices a,b,s. Its three candidate P5 supports are E_a,E_b,Q_s. Since the first two are non-Hamiltonian, the R146 conclusion is forced to Q_s. For part (2), fix distinct a,b,c in A and a three-subset T of S. In W_T=T union {a,b,c}, the three five-subsets T union {b,c}, T union {a,c}, T union {a,b} are Hamiltonian by part (1), because T=S-{s} for the unique s outside T. Accepted R195 says W_T has at least four Hamiltonian five-subsets. Therefore at least one of the other three five-subsets, obtained by omitting a vertex t of T, is Hamiltonian; it has the form (T-{t}) union {a,b,c}=R union {a,b,c} for a pair R subset T. This holds for each of the four triples T subset S. One fixed pair R subset S is contained in exactly two of those four triples, so a single successful R cannot serve all four T; at least two distinct successful core pairs are required for the fixed completion triple. Counting over completion pairs/triples gives the stated distinct-support totals.
