# R779 — Two left first-inward good anchors force a sibling mixed-fiber capture or a no-P4 four-cell through q_1

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement

Fix an exact left first-inward cover T_L of H-{u,v} as in accepted R744, and let x,y be distinct left good anchors supplied by that theorem. Write C=(v,u,x) for the R723 first-inward trimer at x. Then the following trigger-complete conclusions hold.

(MIXED-SIBLING TRIGGER.) If (u,x,y) is tight, then (v,u,x,y) is a literal tight Hamilton P4. Accepted R723 supplies the shallow root-x cover H-x=A_x^L sqcup Q[1,m], which restricts after deleting u to the exact mixed sibling cover T_x=A_x^L sqcup Q[2,m] of H-{u,x}. Since y lies on the tight P4 A_x^L, choose any selected A_x^L-neighbor z of y. Then z lies outside {v,u,x,y}, and the selected state {y,z} is a carrier/exterior crossing for the retained carrier (v,u,x,y) relative to the signed mixed dimer S=(u,x), whose exact head-sign witness is v via (v,u,x). The crossing avoids v. Accepted R527 therefore captures y in this exact mixed fiber, with marker oriented at y. Together with the R744 base-fiber capture of y in H-{u,v} with witness w=q_3, this gives two distinct source-fiber capture certificates at y sharing the physical pivot u=q_1.

(Q-DIMER P4 TRIGGER.) If (y,v,u) is tight, then (y,v,u,x) is a literal tight Hamilton P4, using the already-named first-inward dimer {u,v}. This branch may coexist with MIXED-SIBLING if both trigger turns are tight.

(NO-P4 CELL.) If neither (u,x,y) nor (y,v,u) is tight, then neither terminal opposite-polarity trigger in accepted R518 fires for the trimer C and fourth vertex y. Hence {v,u,x,y} is one of the exact R516 no-P4 alignment signatures.

Thus the two explicit P4 triggers are nonexclusive, and only their joint failure forces the no-P4 cell. The same conclusions hold with x and y interchanged, and the exact right-end dual holds with u,v,w replaced by s=q_{m-1}, d=q_{m-2}, rho=q_{m-3} and mixed fibers H-{x,s}, H-{y,s}.

## Proof
Accepted R723 gives C=(v,u,x) and C'=(v,u,y) tight. First test A=(u,x,y). If A is tight, concatenating the consecutive tight turns (v,u,x) and (u,x,y) gives the literal tight P4 K=(v,u,x,y). Accepted R723 gives H-x=A_x^L sqcup Q[1,m]. Deleting u from the long rail gives the exact cover T_x=A_x^L sqcup Q[2,m] of H-{u,x}. The rail A_x^L is a tight P4 on {L} union (X-{x}) and contains y, so y has a selected neighbor z on A_x^L. Since z lies in {L} union (X-{x,y}), it lies outside V(K). Put S=(u,x). The turn (v,u,x) makes S head-signed with exact witness v. Relative to carrier K, C_K=V(K)-V(S)={v,y}; the selected state {y,z} crosses C_K to the exterior and avoids v. Accepted R527 therefore applies in T_x and captures y, with marker orientation chosen at y. R744 already supplies the distinct base-fiber capture of y in H-{u,v} with witness w=q_3. This proves the MIXED-SIBLING trigger conclusion.

Independently test B=(y,v,u). If B is tight, then B followed by the accepted turn (v,u,x) gives the literal tight P4 (y,v,u,x). Nothing in the argument forbids A and B from both being tight, so both P4 certificates may coexist as graph-intrinsic facts.

Finally suppose neither A nor B is tight. For the trimer C=(v,u,x) and fourth vertex y, the initial physical dimer (v,u) has natural tail sign witnessed by x; accepted R723 gives the same-polarity tail sign witnessed by y, while the opposite head trigger is exactly B and is absent. The terminal physical dimer (u,x) has natural head sign witnessed by v; its opposite tail trigger is exactly A and is absent. Hence neither opposite-polarity terminal trigger of accepted R518 fires. R518 therefore places {v,u,x,y} in one of the exact R516 no-P4 alignment signatures. These cases establish the trigger-complete formulation. Interchanging x,y and dualizing at the right end are literal copies.
