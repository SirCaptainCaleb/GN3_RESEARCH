# R533 — Every hypothetical smallest counterexample is beyond order ten

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Every hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H has |V(H)|>10.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample.

## Scope
Global order gate for the smallest-counterexample program. It converts every theorem stated in the beyond-order-ten regime into a theorem covering every surviving hypothetical smallest counterexample.

## Applicability
Use before the modern O4 spine whenever a theorem is phrased for counterexamples beyond order ten.

## Persistence
Pure order statement; no representative-relative coordinates are involved.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not itself produce a two-cover or specify any O4 provenance.

## Proof
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample of order n. We prove n>10 without using the exterior-five floor R169.\n\nFirst suppose n<=8. By R4, pc(H)=3 and every nonempty proper induced subsystem has path-cover number at most two. Fix any vertex z. The subsystem H-z cannot be Hamiltonian: a Hamilton tight path on H-z together with the singleton path (z) would be a spanning two-path cover of H, contradicting pc(H)=3. Hence H-z has an exact two-cover A sqcup B. Since |A|+|B|=n-1<=7, one rail has order at most three. Therefore H satisfies the failure hypothesis of accepted R881: some exact singleton-deletion two-cover has a rail of order at most three. The conclusion of R881 places every such surviving failure in a synchronized order-three frame H=X sqcup Q with |X|=4 and Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6. Thus n=4+(m+1)=m+5>=11, contradiction. Hence n>=9.\n\nNow suppose n=9. Choose any six-vertex subset W of H. Accepted R193 supplies a tight path K on five vertices of W. Its exterior E=V(H)-V(K) has exactly four vertices. Choose any z in E. The remaining three-set E-{z} supports a tight trimer T by R3: for any ordering of its three distinct vertices, exactly one of that ordering and its complete reversal is tight. Thus K sqcup T is a literal spanning two-cover of H-z. Again H-z cannot be Hamiltonian, since a Hamilton path of H-z together with singleton z would two-cover H. Hence K sqcup T is an exact singleton-deletion two-cover with the rail T of order three. Accepted R881 therefore forces n>=11, contradicting n=9. Consequently n>=10.\n\nFinally n=10 is impossible by R191, the order-ten closure theorem. Therefore every hypothetical smallest counterexample has n>10, exactly the conclusion of R533.\n\nThe route uses R881 only through its clean noncircular implication from an actual short-rail singleton-deletion exact cover to the surviving m>=6 frame. It does not assume R24. The historical dependency R169 is completely absent. At present R191 has the clean pending alternate proof P1096; thus this repair isolates the only remaining review dependency of the order-ten endpoint in R191 rather than the quarantined R24/R169 artery.
