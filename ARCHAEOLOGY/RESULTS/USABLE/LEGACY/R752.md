# R752 — One-witness carrier fans expose all but two complementary singletons to protected contact

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample. Let S be a fixed tested signed physical dimer with exact sign witness w, and let T be one exact two-cover of H-V(S). Let K_1,...,K_k, k>=3, be retained tight trimers such that S is the same reverse boundary dimer of every K_i and V(K_i)=V(S) union {c_i}, where c_1,...,c_k are pairwise distinct and w lies outside V(S) union {c_1,...,c_k}. Then one fixed cover T makes at least k-2 distinct complementary singletons c_i individually capture/contact-capable with the same witness w. More precisely, accepted R508 applied to each carrier K_i in the same T forces a selected state C_i={c_i,y_i} with y_i outside V(K_i). At most two indices can have y_i=w, because those blocked states are distinct selected edges incident with w and the selected-state graph of T is a union of two paths, so deg_T(w)<=2. Hence for at least k-2 indices, C_i avoids w. For each such i, accepted R527 may use that exact state and witness w and orient the marker so that c_i itself is the captured anchor. Since K_i is a trimer with S as a reverse boundary dimer, c_i has a genuine historical carrier adjacency to one endpoint of S inside K_i. Accepted R312 therefore converts the capture into an earlier-contact-or-first-contact protected lineage at c_i using that genuine old-carrier edge. All forced source crossings C_i coexist in the single exact cover T; the later capture/contact descendants are alternative historical continuations. In particular k=3 guarantees one protected-contact-capable complementary singleton and k=4 guarantees at least two.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample. S is a fixed tested signed dimer with exact witness w. T is an exact two-cover of H-V(S). K_i are retained tight trimers sharing S as the same reverse boundary dimer, with pairwise distinct complementary singletons c_i, and w is disjoint from S and every c_i. Uses accepted R508, R527, and R312.

## Scope
Order-free parent theorem for same-frame carrier counting and protected contact. No crossed frame, endpoint normalization, R5, R168, R169, R224, R533, generic R358, payment, R436, Reverse Ear, or computation.

## Applicability
Use whenever several one-vertex trimer carriers share one tested signed dimer inside one exact support-deletion cover. Count the carrier singletons before designing per-carrier arguments: a single witness can block at most two of them. For four carriers, retain at least two simultaneous witness-avoiding source crossings and feed each alternative capture through its genuine trimer edge to R312. This is the parent counting mechanism behind first-inward four-carrier portals and applies equally to terminal-dimer witness packets.

## Persistence
Retain S with tested orientation and witness w, the one fixed exact cover T, all carrier trimers K_i and complementary singletons c_i, all R508-selected source crossings C_i, the set of blocked indices y_i=w, and at least k-2 witness-avoiding indices. For any chosen unblocked index retain marker orientation, capture of c_i, its genuine K_i carrier adjacency, and the R312 protected-contact lineage. Source crossings coexist in T; descendants are alternative.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that different capture or protected-contact descendants coexist after actualization. Does not assert the forced exterior endpoints y_i are distinct, that the witness-avoiding crossing for a given c_i is unique, or that protected first contact closes H. Does not assert the selected state remains current after capture. Gives no positive count when k<3. Does not itself produce the exact deletion cover or the signed witness.

## Proof
For each i, put D=V(S) and S_i={c_i}. Because K_i is a tight trimer spanning exactly D union S_i and T is an exact two-cover of H-D, accepted R508 applies with absorbable block S_i and recompletion path K_i. It yields a selected T-state C_i={c_i,y_i} with y_i outside V(K_i). Call i blocked when y_i=w. Since the c_i are pairwise distinct, distinct blocked indices give distinct selected edges {c_i,w} incident with w. The selected-state graph of an exact two-path cover is a disjoint union of two paths, so every vertex has selected degree at most two. Therefore at most two indices are blocked. Hence at least k-2 indices satisfy y_i!=w. For such an index, c_i!=w by hypothesis and y_i!=w, so the exact witness w avoids C_i. Accepted R527 applies to the retained signed support S, witness w, carrier K_i, exact cover T, and selected carrier/exterior crossing C_i. By its marker-orientation clause we designate c_i itself as the captured signed anchor. Because S is a reverse boundary dimer of the trimer K_i, the complementary singleton c_i is an endpoint of K_i and has a genuine carrier adjacency E_i to one endpoint of S. The physical crossing C_i crosses the retained historical carrier K_i at c_i, so accepted R312 applies with historical carrier A=K_i, crossing C_i, captured anchor c_i, and old adjacency E_i. It gives a protected continuation on which either an earlier event touching c_i is exposed or the lawful old-carrier mirror reentry is itself first contact at c_i. The R508 crossings for all i were selected in the same original T, so those source states coexist. R527/R312 descendants are chosen separately and no coexistence after actualization is claimed. This proves the count and the k=3,4 specializations.
