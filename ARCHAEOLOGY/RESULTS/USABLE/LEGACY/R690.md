# R690 — The XET cross-end rail self-generates a two-witness endpoint packet

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, physical endpoints L=q_0 and R=q_m, and let S be one oriented crossed middle-gate dimer among (a,c),(c,a),(b,z),(z,b). Let v lie outside X union {L,R}, and suppose the R684 XET normal form holds in an exact two-cover of H-V(S): one entire rail is K=(L,v,R) or K=(R,v,L), and the other rail P is Hamiltonian on the complementary vertex set. Then K itself is an R542-ready short carrier at one physical endpoint. More precisely: if V(S)={a,c} and K=(L,v,R), the reverse terminal dimer (R,v) is head-signed by both a and c; if K=(R,v,L), the reverse terminal dimer (L,v) is head-signed by both a and c. If V(S)={b,z} and K=(L,v,R), the reverse initial dimer (v,L) is tail-signed by both b and z; if K=(R,v,L), the reverse initial dimer (v,R) is tail-signed by both b and z. Hence accepted R542 applies to K in every XET case, with complementary singleton equal to the opposite physical endpoint. It yields an actual endpoint capture/refund packet. Since that endpoint has the genuine old carrier adjacency {e,v} inside K, accepted R312 gives a protected endpoint-contact continuation: either an earlier event touching e is already exposed, or the lawful old-carrier mirror reentry using {e,v} is itself first contact at e. In the inward-anchor applications v in {q_2,q_{m-2}} with m>=6, the retained carrier adjacency {e,v} is a strict Q-skipping chord rather than an original consecutive Q edge.

## Proof
Let the R684 XET cover be T=K sqcup P, where P is Hamiltonian on V(H)\(V(S) union {L,v,R}). Because H is a hypothetical counterexample, there is no tight Hamilton P5 on the five-set V(S) union {L,v,R}: such a P5 together with P would be a spanning two-cover. We now use this single observation in four literal cases.

Case 1: V(S)={a,c} and K=(L,v,R). Accepted R649 contains the crossed-gate turns (R,a,c) and (R,c,a). If (a,R,v) were bad, R3 would make (v,R,a) tight, and then (L,v,R,a,c) would be a tight P5, using the turns (L,v,R), (v,R,a), (R,a,c), contradiction. Thus (a,R,v) is tight. Likewise, if (c,R,v) were bad, then (v,R,c) would be tight and (L,v,R,c,a) would be a tight P5 because (R,c,a) is tight. Hence both a and c head-sign the tested dimer (R,v). Since (R,v) is the reverse terminal boundary dimer of the tight trimer K=(L,v,R), K is an R542 short carrier with complementary singleton L.

Case 2: V(S)={a,c} and K=(R,v,L). Use the crossed-gate turns (L,a,c) and (L,c,a). Badness of (a,L,v) would force (v,L,a), making (R,v,L,a,c) a tight P5; badness of (c,L,v) would force (v,L,c), making (R,v,L,c,a) a tight P5. Therefore (L,v) is head-signed by both a,c. It is the reverse terminal boundary dimer of K, so R542 applies with complementary singleton R.

Case 3: V(S)={b,z} and K=(L,v,R). Accepted R649 contains (z,b,L) and (b,z,L). If (v,L,b) were bad, R3 would make (b,L,v) tight, and (z,b,L,v,R) would be a tight P5 using (z,b,L), (b,L,v), (L,v,R). Thus (v,L,b) is tight. If (v,L,z) were bad, R3 would make (z,L,v) tight, and (b,z,L,v,R) would be a tight P5 using (b,z,L). Hence the tested dimer (v,L) is tail-signed by both b,z. It is the reverse initial boundary dimer of K, so R542 applies with complementary singleton R.

Case 4: V(S)={b,z} and K=(R,v,L). Use the crossed-gate turns (z,b,R) and (b,z,R). Badness of (v,R,b) would force (b,R,v), giving the tight P5 (z,b,R,v,L); badness of (v,R,z) would force (z,R,v), giving the tight P5 (b,z,R,v,L). Thus (v,R) is tail-signed by both b,z, and R542 applies to K with complementary singleton L.

In every case R542 therefore yields, from an exact cover after deleting the displayed boundary dimer of K, an actual capture/refund packet at the complementary physical endpoint e. The tight trimer K is the retained historical carrier and e has genuine carrier adjacency {e,v}. Accepted R312 applies to this exact historical capture packet and old adjacency: along a chosen protected continuation, either an earlier event touching e has already appeared or the lawful mirror reentry through {e,v} is itself first contact at e. Finally, if v=q_2 or q_{m-2} and m>=6, then e is q_0 or q_m and {e,v} is not a consecutive edge of the original path Q. This records the desired strict Q-skipping carrier coordinate in the first-contact branch.
