## Proof
Write L=q_0 and Y={a,c,b}=X-{x_L}. The R598 seed supplies the tight P4 K=(L,a,c,b).

(1) Consider U=(x_L,q_1,q_2,...,q_m) sqcup K. These supports partition V(H). Every turn of K is tight and every later turn of U is an interval of Q, so the only uncertified turn is (x_L,q_1,q_2). If it were tight this would be a spanning two-cover, contrary to R4. Thus that turn is bad and R3 gives (q_2,q_1,x_L).

(2) Consider U2=(x_L,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[1,m]. These supports again partition V(H), and the only uncertified turn is (x_L,L,a). Tightness would two-cover H, so the turn is bad and R3 gives (a,L,x_L).

R613 takes x_L=z with L=q_0,u=q_1,v=q_2 and lists both zuv=(z,q_1,q_2) and Lac as tight. Clause (1) forbids that pair inside any smallest-counterexample frame with m>=3. Hence R613 is not an induced prefix of the live R24 frame.
