# R734 — Every first-step root advance self-mints a reversed-state R696 packet

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621 frame and accepted R723,R725. Every ADV output of R725 canonically creates a two-witness short-carrier packet on the reverse of the first-inward Q state, so accepted R696 applies immediately without generic payment.

LEFT. Suppose L-ADV occurs with deleted root r in {a,c}. Write x=a when r=c and x=c when r=a, and u=q_1,v=q_2,w=q_3. The advanced short rail begins with the tight trimer K_L=(v,u,x). Its reverse initial boundary dimer is S_L=(u,v). The original Q turn (u,v,w) makes w a tail witness on S_L. If (r,v,u) were tight, r could be prepended to the advanced short rail and, together with Q[3,m], would two-cover H. Hence boundary antisymmetry gives (u,v,r), so r is a second tail witness on the same tested orientation S_L. The witnesses r,w are distinct and lie outside K_L. Therefore S_L,K_L,{r,w} satisfy accepted R696.

RIGHT. Suppose R-ADV occurs with deleted root r in {b,z}. Write x=b when r=z and x=z when r=b, and rho=q_{m-3},d=q_{m-2},s=q_{m-1}. The advanced short rail ends with the tight trimer K_R=(x,s,d). Its reverse terminal boundary dimer is S_R=(d,s). The original Q turn (rho,d,s) makes rho a head witness on S_R. If (s,d,r) were tight, r could be appended to the advanced short rail and, together with Q[0,m-3], would two-cover H. Thus R3 gives (r,d,s), making r a second head witness on S_R. Again the witnesses are distinct and outside K_R, so R696 applies.

Consequently, in either ADV branch and in every exact two-cover of H-V(S_L) or H-V(S_R), the complementary singleton x has exactly one of the accepted R696 forms: (END) a literal one/two-hole spanning proposal plus same-frame capture; (FREE) one selected crossing usable with either named witness; or (SANDWICH) the literal witness trimer (r,x,w) or its reverse on the left, and (r,x,rho) or its reverse on the right. Hence a first-step root advance is not a terminal cover currency: it compiles directly to source-localized mate/capture/trimer geometry on the physically reversed rail state.

## Proof
In L-ADV, the advanced exact cover is (v,u,x,...) sqcup Q[3,m], where x=a for deleted root c and x=c for deleted root a. Hence K_L=(v,u,x) is tight and S_L=(u,v) is its reverse initial boundary dimer. Since Q is tight, (u,v,w) is tight. If (r,v,u) were tight, (r,v,u,x,...) together with Q[3,m] would be a spanning two-cover of H, impossible. R3 therefore gives (u,v,r). Thus w and r are distinct tail witnesses on S_L outside K_L. Apply R696.

In R-ADV, the cover is (R,...,x,s,d) sqcup Q[0,m-3], where x=b for root z and x=z for root b. Thus K_R=(x,s,d) is tight and S_R=(d,s) is its reverse terminal boundary dimer. The Q turn (rho,d,s) is tight. If (s,d,r) were tight, appending r to the short rail would yield a spanning two-cover together with Q[0,m-3]; hence R3 gives (r,d,s). Therefore rho and r are distinct head witnesses on S_R outside K_R. Apply R696. Substituting the named witnesses into R696 gives the stated END/FREE/SANDWICH alternatives.
