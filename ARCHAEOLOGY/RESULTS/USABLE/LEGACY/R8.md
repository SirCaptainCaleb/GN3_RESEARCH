# R8 — Every three-set admits a tight trimer orientation

**Record status:** `USABLE/LEGACY`

## Statement
Every three-element vertex set admits an ordering that is a tight three-vertex path.

## Scope
Strong Level-(1) boundary tournaments.

## Proof
For any three distinct vertices a,b,c, exactly one of abc and cba is tight, so one of those two orders is a tight trimer.
