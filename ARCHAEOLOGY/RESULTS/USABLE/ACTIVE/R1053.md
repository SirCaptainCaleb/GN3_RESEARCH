# R1053 — The IN-v closure-loop exception is source-pinned

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789345739.325309`

## Statement

Retain the IN-`v` literal first-loss closure-loop branch. Thus the pivot has `b_r=v`, `a_r=t` for a source spoke `t`, and the loop equality is

`a_r=b_m=t`.

Then the two boundary old-`F` edges of the remaining augmenter suffix,

`e_{r+1}=t->b_{r+1}`

and

`e_m=a_{m-1}->t`,

are exactly the two selected old-`F` incidences of `t`.

Consequently either at least one of them is an old source gate across `X|B`, or both are `X-X`. In the latter case R1037 forces `t` to be the middle spoke of the ordered three-source block

`B_L -> s_1 -> s_2 -> s_3 -> B_R`,

and `e_{r+1},e_m` are exactly the two source-block edges incident with `s_2=t`.

Thus the closure-loop exception is not source-anonymous: it is either boundary-source-gated or pinned to the middle source spoke with both `X-X` mates exposed.

## Proof

R1048 gives the IN-`v` orientation of the v-rooted first-loss pivot: `b_r=v` and the deleted same-side edge is `f_r=t->v` for a source spoke `t=a_r`. In the closure-loop subcase the fixed far endpoint satisfies `b_m=a_r=t`.

The zipper coordinates give

`e_{r+1}=a_r->b_{r+1}=t->b_{r+1}`

and

`e_m=a_{m-1}->b_m=a_{m-1}->t`.

Both are selected old-`F` edges. They use the OUT and IN matching copies of the same physical source spoke `t`. They are genuine distinct incidences. If `m=r+1`, the two formulas collapse to the forbidden physical loop `t->t`, so the closure-loop branch has `m>=r+2`.

Every source spoke is internal in the old exact source cover `F`, hence has selected `F`-degree two. Therefore these two suffix-boundary edges exhaust the selected old incidences of `t`.

If either edge crosses `X|B`, it is an old source-spoke-to-spectator gate. Suppose neither crosses. Then both other endpoints lie in `X`. R1037 gives `d_F(v)=1`, and that unique old incidence of `v` is already the first-loss transition `e_r`; hence neither boundary edge can have `v` as its other endpoint. Their other endpoints are therefore source spokes.

R1037 also gives the complete source block, up to reversal,

`B_L -> s_1 -> s_2 -> s_3 -> B_R`.

The endpoint spokes `s_1,s_3` each have one source gate and one `X-X` mate, while only the middle spoke `s_2` has two selected `X-X` incidences. Thus a source spoke whose two selected incidences are both `X-X` must be `s_2`. Therefore `t=s_2`, and the two suffix-boundary edges are exactly its two source-block mates.
