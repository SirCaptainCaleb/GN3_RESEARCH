# R840 — Safe crossed X endcaps can touch a monotone Q rail only at the physical endpoints

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed R621/R649 frame with Q=(q_0,...,q_m), L=q_0, R=q_m, and let K be any vertex-simple tight path. Suppose G_4={b,z} occurs as one contiguous initial block of K in either order S_4, and G_3={a,c} occurs as one contiguous terminal block of K in either order S_3 whenever that endcap is present. Then for each present endcap one of the following holds. (NONLOCAL) Its adjacent Q vertex is interior, i.e. lies in V(Q)-{L,R}; that vertex is a sign witness on the tested X dimer, so accepted R682 yields either an endpoint-capture lineage or a graph-intrinsic tight cross-end trimer L-v-R or R-v-L. (ENDPOINT) Its adjacent Q vertex is L or R. Consequently, if neither R682 nonlocal output is retained and K is also outside the Reverse-Ear outputs of R435 relative to Q, every Q-contacting crossed X endcap is attached directly to a physical endpoint. If both endcaps lie on the same K rail, writing E for the Q vertex immediately after G_4 and F for the Q vertex immediately before G_3, the only endpoint pairs compatible with increasing Q-order are (E,F)=(L,L),(L,R),(R,R); the reversed pair (R,L) forces an R435 output. Thus an intact no-P4/no-RE/no-R682 crossed rail has X attachment geometry drawn from a bounded three-state endpoint list, independent of m.

## Hypotheses
Accepted crossed R621/R649 frame; accepted R682 and R435. The path K contains whichever X dimers are under discussion as contiguous endcaps, with its remaining contacts drawn from Q.

## Scope
Crossed-frame endpoint normalization. No deletion-cover choice, sigma assumption, five-cell quotient classification, capture payment, or order bound beyond the named Q endpoints is used.

## Applicability
After R837 pins intact G_4 to a rail start and intact G_3 to a rail end, inspect their immediate Q neighbors. Any interior neighbor is already an R682 nonlocal event. Otherwise replace arbitrary long-rail attachment positions by endpoint labels L/R; on a common rail only LL, LR, RR survive outside Reverse Ear.

## Persistence
Retain the tested X-dimer orientation, its actual adjacent Q vertex, the R682 inward-witness packet when interior, or the endpoint label when safe; for a common rail retain the ordered endpoint pair.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not say endpoint attachments themselves close H. Does not exclude G_4-R-G_3 or G_4-L-G_3 short rails. Does not treat fragmented X dimers. Does not claim CAP and XET outputs coexist.

## Proof
Let S_4=(s_0,s_1) be the actual orientation of a contiguous initial G_4 block and let v be the next K vertex, assumed to lie in Q. Tightness gives (s_0,s_1,v), so v is a sign witness on the tested orientation S_4. If v is interior to Q then v lies outside X union {L,R}, and accepted R682 applies directly, yielding CAP or XET. Therefore outside that nonlocal output v is one of L,R. Dually, let S_3=(t_0,t_1) be the actual orientation of a contiguous terminal G_3 block and let v be the preceding Q vertex. Tightness gives (v,t_0,t_1), again a sign witness on the tested orientation S_3; if v is interior, R682 applies, so outside its output v lies in {L,R}. Finally suppose both endcaps occur on one rail and neither R682 output occurs. Let E follow G_4 and F precede G_3. Both are in {L,R}. Outside R435 outputs, Q contacts along K occur in increasing Q-index order. Since L=q_0 precedes R=q_m, the ordered endpoint pair cannot be (R,L). The remaining possibilities are (L,L),(L,R),(R,R), where equality means the same sole endpoint contact lies between the two endcaps. This proves the bounded endpoint normalization.
