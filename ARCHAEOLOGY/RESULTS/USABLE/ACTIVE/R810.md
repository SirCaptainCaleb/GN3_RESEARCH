# R810 — The synchronized transitive frame renormalizes before the gate split to one order-eight exact-two-cover core plus the middle rail

## Statement
Retain the accepted synchronized transitive frame R594 in the beyond-order-ten range, with X the transitive matching-height four-cell and Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6. Write L=q_0, u=q_1, s=q_{m-1}, R=q_m, put N=Q[2,m-2], and let G_8=H[X union {L,u,s,R}]. Then |V(G_8)|=8, V(H)=V(G_8) disjoint-union V(N), N is a tight Hamilton path on its support, and pc(G_8)=2. Consequently every exact two-cover G_8=A sqcup B gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover H=A sqcup B sqcup N. Moreover the complete aligned/crossed middle-gate split of accepted R541 is internal to this same eight-vertex core: the endpoint stars (u,L,x) and (x,R,s) for x in X and all L/R-to-M_S gate orientations involve only vertices of G_8. Thus ALIGNED and CROSSED are two internal orientations of one fixed bounded core coupled to the identical long middle rail N; the arbitrary-order part does not participate in the gate split.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted synchronized transitive R594 frame; beyond order ten gives m>=6. Use accepted R4 for complement exactness and accepted R541 for the aligned/crossed gate split.

## Scope
Global O4-v2 Elevator renormalization upstream of ALIGNED/CROSSED. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,capture,payment,R436,Reverse Ear,or computation.

## Applicability
Use before branching into aligned or crossed analyses. Any global closure theorem may work with the fixed order-eight core G_8 and the middle rail N, treating ALIGNED/CROSSED only as different internal gate orientations of G_8. This is the preferred common representation; crossed R806 remains a deeper branch-specific refinement when q_2 and q_{m-2} must be moved into the bounded core.

## Persistence
Retain the physical decomposition H=G_8 disjoint-union N, the exact pc(G_8)=2 fact, the labels X,L,u,s,R, the endpoint reverse stars from R594, and the R541 gate classification as data internal to G_8. Exact two-cover representatives of G_8 are not canonical.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close either ALIGNED or CROSSED. Does not assert G_8 is Hamiltonian, prescribe endpoints in an exact two-cover of G_8, or imply an R572 splice. Does not identify the exact support partition of an arbitrary G_8 two-cover. Does not replace branch-specific deeper geometry such as R574 or R806 when that geometry is needed.

## Proof
Put N=Q[2,m-2]=(q_2,...,q_{m-2}). Since m>=6, N is nonempty, and as a contiguous subpath of Q it is tight. Its complement is exactly V(G_8)=X union {L,u,s,R}, so |V(G_8)|=4+4=8 and V(H)=V(G_8) disjoint-union V(N). Because G_8 is a proper induced subsystem of the hypothetical smallest counterexample H, accepted R4 gives pc(G_8)<=2. If pc(G_8)=1, let P be a Hamilton tight path of G_8. Then P and the tight path N are disjoint and together span H, giving pc(H)<=2, contradiction. Therefore pc(G_8)=2. Any exact two-cover A sqcup B of G_8 consequently yields the literal minimum spanning three-cover A sqcup B sqcup N of H. Finally, the synchronized endpoint stars retained by R594 are (u,L,x) and (x,R,s) for x in X, all supported inside G_8. The accepted R541 ALIGNED/CROSSED split compares the left and right M_S gate edges at L and R, hence likewise depends only on turns inside X union {L,R}, a subset of G_8. Thus the entire gate split is an internal orientation distinction of one fixed eight-vertex pc=2 core, while the arbitrary-length middle rail N is identical in both branches.
