# R619 — Middle-gate polarity partitions all four R24 roots into synchronized enlarged-cover pairs

## Statement
Retain the synchronized transitive order-three R24 frame with X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=4, and write L=q_0, u=q_1, R=q_m, s=q_{m-1}. Normalize the left fully outgoing M_S gate to e_L={a,c}; hence its complementary M_S edge is f_L={b,z}. Then both roots in f_L, not only the distinguished high-side root, have literal exact left-enlarged singleton-deletion covers: H-z=(u,L,a,c,b) sqcup Q[2,m] and H-b=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup Q[2,m]. Let e_R be the fully incoming M_S edge at R and f_R its complement. Both roots in f_R have literal exact right-enlarged covers obtained by the right dual of R536. Consequently the two-ended geometry has exactly two phases. (A) Aligned phase e_R=e_L={a,c}: f_R=f_L={b,z}, and both H-z and H-b carry simultaneous opposite-end enlarged covers. Explicitly, H-z also equals Q[0,m-2] sqcup (b,a,c,R,s), while H-b also equals Q[0,m-2] sqcup (z,c,a,R,s). For r=z the left/right pair of covers has cross states L a, q_{m-2}s, u q_2, cR; for r=b it has cross states Lc, q_{m-2}s, u q_2, aR. Each selected state crosses the two components of the opposite cover, so R176 gives a balanced opposite-sign pair birth from each state, with the same deleted root and both physical Q cuts retained. (B) Crossed phase e_R={b,z}: f_R={a,c}. Then the right enlarged covers are H-c=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (a,b,z,R,s) and H-a=Q[0,m-2] sqcup (c,z,b,R,s). Thus the four roots split cleanly into the left-enlarged pair {b,z} and the right-enlarged pair {a,c}. In particular the residual branch previously phrased as x_R in {a,c} is exactly the crossed middle-gate phase, not a one-root exception.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the accepted synchronized transitive R24 frame of R594; X={a,b,c,z} has the displayed matching-height orientation; Q=(q_0,...,q_m) with m>=4 is the common tight rail; both endpoint five-cells are P5-free with the common endpoint reverse stars. Normalize the left outgoing M_S gate to {a,c}. Use accepted R536 and R541; exactness of each singleton-deletion two-cover uses R4. The aligned cross-state upgrades use accepted R176.

## Scope
Noncircular R24 restoration. Two-ended fixed-frame synchronization of all enlarged singleton-deletion roots. No payment descendant, role state, maximin normalization, or order-ten closure is used.

## Applicability
Use immediately after fixing the left synchronized pair {b,z}. It strengthens the one-root coincidence dichotomy: in the aligned phase both b and z support left/right four-cross-state weaves; the only geometric residue is the crossed phase, where {b,z} are precisely the left-enlarged roots and {a,c} precisely the right-enlarged roots. The next consumer should therefore work on the common pair-deletion geometry joining one root from each pair rather than re-testing a distinguished x_R.

## Persistence
Retain the physical matching partition {a,c}|{b,z}, the left/right gate polarity, all four exact enlarged covers in the relevant phase, the deleted-root labels, and the physical Q cuts q_1|q_2 and q_{m-2}|q_{m-1}. In the aligned phase retain the eight root-labelled cross-state certificates listed above.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the R176 pair births are distinct, simultaneously current, or individually closing. Does not close the crossed phase. Does not assert a Hamilton middle or a spanning two-cover in the crossed phase. Does not require or imply that one distinguished high-side root carries all useful information.

## Proof
Apply accepted R536 at L. With e_L={a,c} fully outgoing and complementary edge {b,z}, choose the M_L pairing az and cb. R536 gives the two tight endpoint-starting P4s (L,a,c,b), omitting z, and (L,c,a,z), omitting b. Prepending u by the common endpoint reverse star gives the tight P5s (u,L,a,c,b) and (u,L,c,a,z). Each together with the untouched suffix Q[2,m] partitions H minus the omitted root. If either deletion graph were Hamiltonian, its Hamilton path together with the deleted singleton would two-cover H, contradicting R4; hence both displayed left covers are exact. Apply the exact right dual of R536 at R. If the fully incoming M_S gate is {a,c}, then the two R-ending P4s are (b,a,c,R), omitting z, and (z,c,a,R), omitting b, because ba c and z c a are the M_R>M_S descents. Appending s by the right endpoint reverse star gives the two exact right covers displayed in (A). If instead the incoming gate is {b,z}, the corresponding P4s are (a,b,z,R), omitting c, and (c,z,b,R), omitting a, giving the two exact right covers in (B). R541 says these aligned and crossed choices exhaust the two M_S edges. In the aligned phase compare the two exact covers of H-z. Relative to the right cover, the left cover selects La and q_{m-2}s across its two components; relative to the left cover, the right cover selects uq_2 and cR across its two components. Since H-z is proper, R176 applies independently to all four. The H-b comparison is identical with La replaced by Lc and cR replaced by aR. This proves the phase description and the aligned four-weave strengthening.
