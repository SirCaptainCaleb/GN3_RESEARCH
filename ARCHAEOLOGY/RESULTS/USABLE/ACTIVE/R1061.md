# R1061 — An untouched first-open slot gives a literal tight three-forest recompletion

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789410776.990429`

## Statement

Retain the later non-loop OUT-`v` first-loss setting with `j>t+1`. Suppose the normalized pre-loss prefix `M=hat M_{r-1}` is a literal tight three-path forest and both spectator incidences at the first R1033-open slot remain selected:

`b_t->b_{t+1}`, `b_{t+1}->b_{t+2}`.

Then

`M' = M-{b_t->b_{t+1}}+{v->b_{t+1}}`

is again a literal tight three-path forest. Its free copies are `b_t,out` and the same far source copy `s,in`. Hence the entire remaining first-open interception obstruction is the single source-gated closure

`b_t->s`,

with exactly the usual predecessor seam at `b_t`, successor seam at `s` when defined, and same-rail directed-cycle alternative.

## Proof

By R1060, `v` is an isolated singleton component of the normalized pre-loss selected graph. Deleting the selected path edge

`d=b_t->b_{t+1}`

cuts one path component into two directed path pieces, one ending at `b_t` and one beginning at `b_{t+1}`. The selected graph now has four path components, including the singleton `v`.

Add

`h=v->b_{t+1}`.

Its endpoints lie on two distinct components, so this edge cannot create a directed cycle. There is no turn at `v` because both split copies of `v` were free. The successor edge `b_{t+1}->b_{t+2}` remains selected, and at the R1033 first-open position the turn

`(v,b_{t+1},b_{t+2})`

is tight. No other selected incidence changes. Therefore `M'` is literally a tight three-path forest.

The newly free OUT copy is `b_t,out`; the fixed far free IN copy remains `s,in`. Their unique direct closure is `b_t->s`. Because `b_t∈B` and `s∈X`, this edge is not a loop. Adding it has exactly four physical possibilities: a bad predecessor seam at `b_t`; a bad successor seam at `s` when a successor exists; both seams tight with endpoints on distinct rails, yielding a tight two-cover; or both seams tight with endpoints on the same rail, yielding the single directed cycle closed by the added edge.

Thus, conditional on a physical pre-loss forest and untouched first-open spectator incidences, first-open interception is reduced completely to one source-gated seam/cycle closure.
