# R2006 — Occupied first-open successor blockers collapse to two exact no-P4 signatures

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** workspace root `1789412894.171249`

## Statement

Retain the first-open blocker geometry of R2005. Put

`b=b_t`, `c=b_{t+1}`,

and suppose the occupied replacement successor `c->z` produces the reverse tight trimer

`(z,c,v)`.

Then a tight Hamilton P4 is forced in every R1033 star-triangle or reverse-spoke-hook case except exactly two local no-P4 residues:

1. **Star-triangle residue.** Here `t>=2`, `z=b_{t-1}`, and the four-set `{b_{t-1},b_t,b_{t+1},v}` is exactly R516 signature `00`.
2. **Reverse-spoke-hook residue.** Here `t=1`, `z=b_1`, and the four-set `{b_1,b_2,b_3,v}` is exactly R516 signature `11`.

Both R516 rows are genuine no-P4 cells, so the list is exact at the local four-vertex level.

## Proof

Write `u=b_{t-1}` in the star-triangle branch. R1033 gives the tight turns

`(v,b,u)`, `(u,b,c)`, `(c,b,v)`.

If `z=b`, the blocker `(b,c,v)` concatenates with `(u,b,c)` to give the tight P4 `u,b,c,v`.

Assume `z` is distinct from `u,b`. Apply R3 to the triple `{z,c,b}`. Either `(z,c,b)` is tight, giving the P4 `z,c,b,v`, or `(b,c,z)` is tight, giving `u,b,c,z`.

Thus only `z=u` can remain. If `(u,c,b)` is tight, then `u,c,b,v` is a P4. Otherwise R3 gives `(b,c,u)`. Suppose there is still no P4. For the tight base trimer `(u,b,c)`, no-P4 forces the same-polarity terminal contacts needed by R516: `(b,u,v)` must be tight, since otherwise R3 gives `(v,u,b)` and hence `v,u,b,c`; and `(v,c,b)` must be tight, since otherwise R3 gives `(b,c,v)` and hence `u,b,c,v`. The star turns `(v,b,u)` and `(c,b,v)` force the R516 alignment bits

`A=0`, `B=0`.

Therefore R516 gives exactly signature `00` on `{u,b,c,v}`. Conversely the R516 `00` row contains all these required turns and has no tight Hamilton P4.

Now take the reverse-spoke-hook branch. R1033 gives

`(c,v,b)` and `(c,b,v)`.

If `z!=b`, the blocker `(z,c,v)` concatenates with `(c,v,b)` to give the tight P4 `z,c,v,b`. Hence only `z=b` remains, so the blocker is `(b,c,v)`.

If `t>1`, the spectator turn `(b_{t-1},b,c)` gives the P4 `b_{t-1},b,c,v`. Therefore a no-P4 residue requires `t=1`. Since the live later-loss setting has `b_3`, put `d=b_3`. Tightness of the spectator path gives `(b,c,d)`, while the R1033 hook right flank gives `(v,c,d)`. Relative to the R516 base trimer `(b,c,d)` with fourth vertex v, the blocker `(b,c,v)` and the hook flank `(v,c,d)` give alignment bits

`A=1`, `B=1`.

The same-polarity terminal hypotheses are therefore satisfied, and R516 gives exactly signature `11` on `{b_1,b_2,b_3,v}`. Conversely that row contains the required blocker/hook turns and has no tight Hamilton P4.

No other occupied-successor no-P4 case survives.
