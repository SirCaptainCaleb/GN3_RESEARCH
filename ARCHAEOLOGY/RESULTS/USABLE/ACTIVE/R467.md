# R467 — All-internal source-trimer four-birth interaction fan

## Statement
Retain every conclusion of R458. In addition, fix one directional residual seam in the all-internal R359 source state H=J sqcup U sqcup V, where J=(a,x,c), U=(u_1,...,u_r), V=(v_1,...,v_s), r,s>=2. For the native U-to-V R453 clause write A=(v_1,u_r,u_{r-1}) and B=(v_2,v_1,u_r). Then the seam has the following exhaustive source-coupled amplification.

(I) If A is tight and B is bad, then bar(B)=(u_r,v_1,v_2) is tight, so H=J sqcup U^- sqcup V^+ is a second literal minimum three-cover with U^-=(u_1,...,u_{r-1}) and V^+=(u_r,v_1,...,v_s). R168 forces both shifted residual rails nontrivial. Reapplying the all-internal R359 shield conclusion to this shifted currentization gives (v_1,u_r,x) tight. Hence the winning trimer A has same-polarity R38 collisions on both of its physical dimers: on (u_r,u_{r-1}) with head witnesses x,v_1, and on (v_1,u_r) with tail witnesses x,u_{r-1}. The second collision is itself the R455 collision of the shifted U^- to V^+ seam, with exact shifted-splice provenance.

(II) If A is bad and B is tight, then bar(A)=(u_{r-1},u_r,v_1) is tight, so H=J sqcup U^+ sqcup V^- is a second literal minimum three-cover with U^+=(u_1,...,u_r,v_1) and V^-=(v_2,...,v_s). Again R168 keeps both shifted rails nontrivial, and R359 gives the shifted shield (x,v_1,u_r) tight. Hence the winning trimer B has same-polarity R38 collisions on both physical dimers: on (v_2,v_1) with tail witnesses x,u_r, and on (v_1,u_r) with head witnesses x,v_2. The second collision is the R455 collision of the shifted U^+ to V^- seam.

(III) If both A and B are tight, then K=(v_2,v_1,u_r,u_{r-1}) is a tight four-path. The original R359 shields (v_2,v_1,x) and (x,u_r,u_{r-1}) show that both terminal dimers of K carry same-polarity R38 collisions: (v_2,v_1) with tail witnesses x,u_r, and (u_r,u_{r-1}) with head witnesses x,v_1.

Thus every directional native residual splice yields two distinct role-linked same-polarity collision supports whose x-signed polarities are opposite. In the unique-winner cases the two supports are the old residual terminal dimer and a new cross-seam terminal dimer obtained only after the exact one-vertex seam transfer; in the double-winner case they are the disjoint terminal dimers of a tight P4. The symmetric V-to-U statement also holds. In every case each collision channel may be reminted separately by R458. Unlike exact-support replay, the unique-winner amplification genuinely consumes the named opposite-rail endpoint together with its source neighbor and the exact R453 splice certificate: replacing that endpoint by an arbitrary exterior witness no longer supplies the shifted literal three-cover.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; J=(a,x,c) is a proper tight trimer in the all-internal R359 regime; H=J sqcup U sqcup V is a fixed literal currentization with U,V nontrivial. Accepted R3,R38,R168,R359,R453,R458 are available.

## Scope
Order-free strengthening of the all-internal interaction fan. It converts each single R453 seam outcome into a two-support, polarity-controlled collision packet while preserving the exact source seam and shifted-cut provenance.

## Applicability
Use after an R455 directional splice is selected. In a unique-winner branch, shift the literal U|V cut across the unique good seam turn, reapply R359 to the same fixed trimer J, and harvest the cross-seam collision. In a double-winner branch, use the tight reversed-seam P4 and its two terminal collisions. This is a role-consuming replacement for fresh-witness-blind exact-support replay.

## Persistence
J, x, the original U,V paths, the exact R453 proposal and seam holes, the winner status, the one-vertex shifted three-cover when present, the shifted R359 shield, all named collision supports/witnesses/tested polarities, and the tight P4 in the double-winner case are graph-intrinsic or retained literal historical certificates. Original and shifted currentizations are alternative representatives; their graph-intrinsic tight turns and collision certificates persist, while later payment descendants need not coexist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the two collision supports alone close H, that alternative currentizations are simultaneously current, or that generic R38/common-anchor interaction preserves these source roles. In unique-winner cases the two supports overlap and therefore do not themselves form an R39 direct balanced pair. In the double-winner case exact-support replay of later pair births still does not by itself consume fresh witnesses. No monotone iteration of seam transfers is claimed.
