## Statement

Let I be the unique unfragmented middle-gate X cell, and orient the actual T-rail containing I in its forced R885 direction: if I=G_4={b,z}, then I is the initial block of that rail; if I=G_3={a,c}, then I is the terminal block. (NONLOCAL) That adjacent Q vertex is interior to Q, and R840 yields the accepted R682 nonlocal output: an endpoint-capture lineage or a graph-intrinsic tight cross-end trimer through that interior witness. (ENDPOINT) The adjacent Q vertex is one of the physical endpoints L=q_0 or R=q_m. Thus outside the named R840/R682 nonlocal event the anonymous MAX3 skeletons acquire a physical endpoint label, and M1/M2 have a unique natural end attachment.

## Proof
Work in accepted R886 MAX3 inside the accepted R885 BOUNDED branch. Let I denote the unique intact X cell. By R885, if I=G_4={b,z}, the whole two-vertex block I occurs initially on its T-rail; if I=G_3={a,c}, it occurs terminally. Also by R885, outside its already-separated Reverse-Ear branch, Q vertices encountered along either T-rail occur in strictly increasing Q-index order.\n\nApply accepted R840 to the Q vertex adjacent to I. If that Q vertex is interior to Q, R840 gives its NONLOCAL alternative, namely the retained R682 output. This is exactly the stated NONLOCAL branch. Hence suppose not. Then R840 says the adjacent Q vertex is L=q_0 or R=q_m.\n\nNow suppose the MAX3 skeleton is M1 or M2. By the R886 classification, the rail containing I also contains at least one fragmented marker. Consequently, besides the Y-run immediately adjacent to I, that rail contains at least one additional Y-run separated from it by a fragmented X marker.\n\nIf I=G_4, orient the rail in its actual R885 direction, starting with I. If the adjacent Q vertex were R=q_m, then every later Q vertex on that same rail would have to have Q-index strictly larger than m, impossible. But M1/M2 requires a later Y-run containing a Q vertex. Therefore the adjacent endpoint is L.\n\nDually, if I=G_3, orient the rail in its actual R885 direction ending with I. If the adjacent Q vertex were L=q_0, then every earlier Q vertex on that rail would have to have Q-index strictly smaller than 0, impossible. But M1/M2 requires an earlier Y-run. Therefore the adjacent endpoint is R.\n\nFor M0, R886 says the intact-endcap rail contains no fragmented marker and hence no second Y-run. The above monotonicity contradiction is unavailable, so R840 leaves exactly the two endpoint possibilities L or R. This proves every asserted branch and no stronger endpoint claim is made.
