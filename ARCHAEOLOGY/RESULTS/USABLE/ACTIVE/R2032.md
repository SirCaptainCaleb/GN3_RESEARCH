# R2032 — Two-vertex source-rail normal form, repaired

**Status:** PASS_ADJUSTED
**Source:** Slack workspace R2032 re-audit

## Statement

If a literal exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` has a two-vertex rail on `{s,u}` with retained source spoke `s`, then:

- the four-set `{A,C,s,u}` is `P4`-free;
- `(s,A,u)` is tight;
- `(u,C,s)` is tight;
- `(C,u,A)` is tight;
- `u` cannot be another retained source spoke.

The formerly claimed turn `(A,C,u)` is **not** forced; the correct complete-reversal consequence is `(C,u,A)`.

## Proof

Let `Q` be the other rail of the exact two-cover. If `{A,C,s,u}` had a tight Hamilton `P4`, that `P4` together with `Q` would be a spanning exact two-cover of `H`, contradicting counterexample status. Hence the four-set is `P4`-free.

The retained source turn `(A,s,C)` is tight.

If `(s,A,u)` were bad, R3 would make its complete reverse `(u,A,s)` tight. Then `(u,A,s,C)` would be a tight Hamilton `P4`, contradiction. Thus `(s,A,u)` is tight.

If `(u,C,s)` were bad, R3 would make `(s,C,u)` tight. Then `(A,s,C,u)` would be a tight Hamilton `P4`, contradiction. Thus `(u,C,s)` is tight.

If `(A,u,C)` were tight, then the already forced `(s,A,u)` followed by `(A,u,C)` would make `(s,A,u,C)` a tight Hamilton `P4`, contradiction. Hence `(A,u,C)` is bad, and complete-reversal R3 gives `(C,u,A)` tight.

Finally, if `u` were another retained source spoke, its retained source turn `(A,u,C)` would be tight, contradicting the preceding badness. Therefore `u` is not another source spoke.

## Removed overclaim

The original version asserted `(A,C,u)` tight by treating a complete reversal as though it could also be cyclically rotated. That is not valid. The correct forced orientation on the unordered triple `{A,C,u}` is `(C,u,A)`.

For example, a globally edge-ordered four-set can satisfy the other hypotheses while `(A,C,u)` is bad, so the distinction is real rather than notational.

## Dependencies and scope

Trusted dependencies: counterexample status of `H`, retained source turn `(A,s,C)`, and R3.

Provisional dependencies: none.

The source-source exclusion used downstream remains valid. The repaired theorem also retains the exact forced orientation `(C,u,A)`, which may be useful in later short-rail recompletions.
