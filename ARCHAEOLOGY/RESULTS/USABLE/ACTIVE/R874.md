# R874 — Oppositely directed two-witness waves at distance two collide into a four-P4 central fan

## Statement
Let Q=(q_0,...,q_m) be a vertex-simple tight path in a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament. Fix 1<=j<=m-2 and four distinct vertices a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2 outside Q. Suppose D_j=(q_j,q_{j-1}) is head-signed by both b_1,b_2 and D_{j+2}=(q_{j+2},q_{j+1}) is tail-signed by both a_1,a_2. Apply accepted R518 separately to K_j=(q_{j-1},q_j,q_{j+1}) with probes b_1,b_2 and to K_{j+1}=(q_j,q_{j+1},q_{j+2}) with probes a_1,a_2. Then either one of those four probe four-sets supports a Hamilton P4, or the central reverse dimer D_{j+1}=(q_{j+1},q_j) is head-signed by both b probes and tail-signed by both a probes. In the latter FAN branch, accepted R523 gives all four literal tight P4s (b_r,q_{j+1},q_j,a_s), r,s in {1,2}. Thus opposite two-witness waves separated by one Q dimer cannot pass quietly: absent a probe P4 they meet in a complete 2-by-2 central P4 fan.

## Hypotheses
Q tight; a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2 four distinct vertices outside Q; D_j head-signed by b_1,b_2; D_{j+2} tail-signed by a_1,a_2. Uses accepted R518 and R523 only.

## Scope
Pure order-free two-wave collision lemma. No R700, smallest-counterexample, R24 frame, deletion cover, payment, capture, R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R436,Reverse Ear,or computation.

## Applicability
In the crossed R24 frame take {b_1,b_2}={b,z} and {a_1,a_2}={a,c}. Accepted R820 paired terminal packets can provide the source hypotheses. Retain the FAN as one simultaneous graph-intrinsic object; use exact-cover seam consumers such as R833/R834 rather than generic path-growth optimism.

## Persistence
Retain Q,j,the ordered witness pairs,the two source packets, all four R518 probe tests, and in FAN all four central sign certificates and all four mixed P4 orders.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert any P4 closes H, that FAN extends to a long path, or that source packets arose in one cover. Does not spend or actualize the central dimer.

## Proof
Apply R518 to K_j=(q_{j-1},q_j,q_{j+1}) with b_1 and b_2 separately. If either P4 branch occurs, P4-LEFT holds. Otherwise the two no-P4 outputs give (b_r,q_{j+1},q_j) tight for r=1,2, so D_{j+1} is head-signed by both b probes. Independently apply R518 to K_{j+1}=(q_j,q_{j+1},q_{j+2}) with a_1,a_2. Outside a probe P4, its no-P4 outputs give (q_{j+1},q_j,a_s) tight for s=1,2, so the same tested D_{j+1} is tail-signed by both a probes. In the joint no-P4 branch, each b_r head certificate and a_s tail certificate share the identical tested orientation and have distinct witnesses. Accepted R523(HT) therefore gives the tight P4 (b_r,q_{j+1},q_j,a_s). All four choices coexist graph-intrinsically.
