# R881 — Every R24 failure reduces to one fixed order-eight pc=2 core coupled to a nonempty Hamilton middle rail

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and suppose R24 fails: for some vertex z an exact two-cover H-z=P sqcup Q has a rail P of order at most three. Then, after the noncircular normalization of R594 and the finite-base exclusion R880, the unique surviving case has an order-three short rail, a transitive matching-height four-cell X, and Q=(q_0,...,q_m) with m>=6. Writing L=q_0, u=q_1, s=q_{m-1}, R=q_m, N=Q[2,m-2], and G_8=H[X union {L,u,s,R}], one has V(H)=V(G_8) disjoint-union V(N), N is a nonempty tight Hamilton path on its support, |V(G_8)|=8, and pc(G_8)=2. Every exact two-cover G_8=A sqcup B therefore gives a literal minimum spanning three-cover H=A sqcup B sqcup N. Moreover the aligned/crossed middle-gate split is entirely internal to G_8. Thus the whole unresolved content of R24 is a bounded eight-vertex exact-two-cover core coupled to one Hamilton middle rail of arbitrary positive order.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and R24 fails through some singleton-deletion exact two-cover with a rail of order at most three. Use accepted R594, pending finite-base parent R880 once reviewed, and accepted R810.

## Scope
Noncircular O6 long-case entrance theorem. It packages the reconstructed front half, finite-base closure, and common bounded-core renormalization. No R24,R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,or generic R358 is used.

## Applicability
Preferred startup theorem for the remaining O6 long case after fresh review. Work directly with the fixed order-eight pc=2 core G_8 and Hamilton middle rail N; ALIGNED/CROSSED is only an internal orientation choice in G_8. Use deeper R806 only when q_2,q_{m-2} must be moved into a ten-vertex bounded core.

## Persistence
Retain the original failed singleton-deletion certificate, normalized X,Q labels, m>=6, the physical decomposition H=G_8 disjoint-union N, pc(G_8)=2, the endpoint reverse stars, and the internal aligned/crossed gate classification. Exact G_8 two-cover representatives are not canonical.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close the m>=6 case, assert G_8 Hamiltonian, prescribe endpoints of an exact G_8 two-cover, or make the middle rail N interact with the core automatically. Does not replace R594/R810 when their detailed certificates are needed.

## Proof
Apply R594 to the assumed R24 failure. The order-one and order-two short rails close immediately, so the surviving short rail has order three and yields the synchronized transitive matching-height frame X,Q with m>=4. Apply R880 to exclude m=4 and m=5; hence m>=6. Now apply R810 to this same R594 frame. With L=q_0,u=q_1,s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, N=Q[2,m-2], and G_8=H[X union {L,u,s,R}], R810 gives |G_8|=8, V(H)=V(G_8) disjoint-union V(N), N a nonempty tight Hamilton path, and pc(G_8)=2. It also records that every exact G_8 two-cover extends with N to a literal spanning three-cover and that the aligned/crossed gate split uses only vertices of G_8. These are exactly the claimed conclusions.
