# R2034 — Low-transition export normal form

**Status:** PASS
**Source:** Slack workspace root `1789422532.306899`

## Statement

Let `T` be any literal exact two-cover of `G=H-{A,C}` with `tau_{X|B}(T)<=2` in the retained source frame `X={v,p,q,r}`.

Then either some source spoke `s` is an endpoint of a `T`-rail of order at least three, so the R2025 lift at `s` has both nonanchor rails edge-bearing and R2026 applies to each; or `tau(T)=2` and `T` has one of two rigid exceptional forms:

- **(E_X)** a two-vertex X-rail on `{s,v}` and a `B-X-B` rail whose X-block is the other two source spokes `{t,r}`;
- **(E_B)** a two-vertex mixed rail on `{s,b}`, `b in B`, and an `X-B` or `B-X` rail whose X-block is `{v,t,r}`, with `v` the X-side rail endpoint.

In either exception `t,r` are consecutive internal vertices of the long rail. Deleting them leaves exactly three nonempty tight residual paths, while `{A,C,t,r}` has a tight Hamilton `P4`. Hence the exceptional branch reduces exactly to a two-seam recompletion of those three residual paths.

## Proof

For the two rails of `T`, let `b_X,b_B` be the total numbers of maximal X- and B-blocks. Audited R2024 gives `b_X>=2`, `b_B>=1`, and

`tau(T)=b_X+b_B-2<=2`.

Thus only

`(tau;b_X,b_B)=(1;2,1),(2;3,1),(2;2,2)`

occur, with the R2024 rail shapes:

- `(2,1)`: an `X-B` or `B-X` rail plus an isolated X rail;
- `(3,1)`: an `X-B-X` rail plus an isolated X rail;
- `(2,2)`: either two mixed rails, an `X-B-X` rail plus an isolated B rail, or a `B-X-B` rail plus an isolated X rail.

Every `T`-rail is nontrivial.

If a source endpoint lies on a rail of order at least three, delete it and insert the retained source anchor `(A,s,C)` as in R2025. The shortened rail still contains an edge and the other `T`-rail is nontrivial, so both nonanchor rails in the lift are edge-bearing and R2026 applies to each.

Assume now that no source endpoint lies on a rail of order at least three.

The `(2,1)` shape cannot survive. Its isolated X rail either has order at least three, yielding a long source endpoint, or has order two. In the latter case R2032 forces it to be `{s,v}` rather than a source-source pair. The remaining two sources lie in the mixed rail's unique X-block, forcing a source at its X-side endpoint on a long rail, contradiction.

The `(3,1)` shape cannot survive because the long `X-B-X` rail has two X endpoints and at most one is `v`, so one is a source endpoint. The same argument excludes the `(2,2)` shape `X-B-X` plus isolated B.

In the two-mixed-rails case, at least one X-side endpoint is a source `s`. Its rail must therefore have order exactly two, hence is `{s,b}` for some `b in B`. All remaining X-vertices `{v,t,r}` lie in the other rail's unique X-block. Since that rail is long, its X-side endpoint cannot be `t` or `r`, else there is a long source endpoint. Therefore its X-side endpoint is `v`, giving `(E_B)`.

In the `B-X-B` plus isolated-X case, the isolated X rail cannot have order at least three, so it has order two. R2032 excludes a source-source pair, hence the rail is exactly `{s,v}`. The other two sources form the complete internal X-block of the `B-X-B` rail, giving `(E_X)`.

Both exceptions have `tau(T)=2`.

Let `{t,r}` be the two sources not on the short rail. In `(E_X)`, the long `B-X-B` rail has nonempty B-blocks on both sides of the X-block `{t,r}`, so `t,r` are consecutive internal vertices and deleting them splits that rail into two nonempty B-paths; the short `{s,v}` rail survives. In `(E_B)`, the long rail has X-block `{v,t,r}` with `v` at the X-side endpoint, so `t,r` are consecutive internal vertices; deleting them leaves singleton `(v)`, the nonempty B-block, and the short mixed rail `{s,b}`. Thus in either case exactly three nonempty residual paths remain.

Finally, retained source turns `(A,t,C)` and `(A,r,C)` force a Hamilton `P4` on `{A,C,t,r}`. If `(t,A,r)` is tight, use `(t,A,r,C)`; otherwise R3 gives `(r,A,t)` tight and use `(r,A,t,C)`. Consequently any legal two-seam reconnection of the three residual paths into one tight Hamilton path, together with this source `P4`, is a spanning exact two-cover of `H`.

## Dependencies and scope

Dependencies: audited R2024, R2025, R2026, R2032, retained source turns, and R3. No R24 or R5 is used.

This is an export normal form. It does not assert that the two required recompletion seams exist.
