# R868 — Internal X blocks force a labelled P4; the minimum five-cell P4-free residue is source-sink oriented

## Statement
Retain the accepted crossed beyond-order-ten inward-anchor five-cell frame of R862/R865 with m>=6, and let T be an exact two-cover of W=H-{q_2,q_{m-2}} in the minimum case sigma_G(T)=0. Thus each X cell G_3={a,c} and G_4={b,z} occurs as one complete contiguous oriented dimer block of T. Then the following hold.

(1) INTERNAL-X P4. If either X-dimer block has degree two in the contracted five-cell forest F_T, then H contains an explicit source-labelled tight Hamilton P4. More precisely, write the degree-two block in its literal T-order as S=(s_0,s_1), with Q vertices y_- immediately before S and y_+ immediately after S on that T rail. If S=G_3, accepted R649 supplies fixed head polarity on S with at least three named source witnesses. The outgoing triple (s_0,s_1,y_+) is a tail sign on S, opposite to that fixed head polarity; choosing a fixed head witness w distinct from y_+, accepted R518 forces a Hamilton P4 on {w,s_0,s_1,y_+}. If S=G_4, accepted R649 supplies fixed tail polarity on S; the incoming triple (y_-,s_0,s_1) is an opposite head sign, and choosing a fixed tail witness w distinct from y_- makes R518 force a Hamilton P4 on {y_-,s_0,s_1,w}.

Consequently every R866 Internal-X minimum configuration has a labelled P4: this includes all c_X(T)=3 shapes E and F and the c_X(T)=2 internal-X shape B.

(2) SOURCE-SINK ORIENTATION OF THE NO-P4 RESIDUE. Suppose no Q-X transition of T yields the opposite-polarity R518 P4 described above. Then neither X block has degree two, so both have degree one. Moreover the unique transition incident with G_3 must ENTER the oriented G_3 block, while the unique transition incident with G_4 must LEAVE the oriented G_4 block. Hence G_4 is an initial block of its T rail and G_3 is a terminal block of its T rail. Therefore the only surviving R866 structural regimes are Same-rail endpoint-X and Split-X. In the same-rail regime the common oriented rail runs G_4 -> ... -> G_3. In the split-X regime the rail containing G_4 begins at G_4 and the rail containing G_3 ends at G_3.

Thus the six-shape minimum taxonomy collapses, before any R176 payment or generic contact, to a labelled-P4 branch or a canonically source-sink-oriented endpoint-X residue.

## Proof
Let T be a minimum sigma_G(T)=0 exact two-cover in the accepted R862/R865 frame. Every physical cell G_i is one contiguous T-block. In particular G_3={a,c} and G_4={b,z} each appear as a literal oriented two-vertex block.

First suppose an X block has degree two in the contracted forest. It lies internally on one oriented T rail, hence has one selected Q-X state entering it and one selected Q-X state leaving it.

Take S=G_3 in its literal T-order S=(s_0,s_1). Accepted R649 gives fixed head polarity on either possible orientation of this physical dimer. Concretely, in orientation (a,c) it gives the distinct head witnesses L,R,b, and in orientation (c,a) it gives L,R,z. Let y_+ be the Q vertex immediately after S on the T rail. Tightness of T makes (s_0,s_1,y_+) tight, so y_+ is a tail witness on the same oriented dimer S, opposite to the fixed R649 head polarity. Choose one fixed R649 head witness w distinct from y_+; this is always possible because at least three are available. The fixed source trimer (w,s_0,s_1) is tight, while (s_0,s_1,y_+) is an opposite-polarity sign on its terminal physical dimer. Accepted R518 therefore forces a tight Hamilton P4 on {w,s_0,s_1,y_+}.

Now take S=G_4 in its literal T-order. Accepted R649 gives fixed tail polarity on either possible orientation, with at least three named source witnesses. Let y_- be the Q vertex immediately before S. Tightness of T makes (y_-,s_0,s_1) tight, so y_- is a head witness on S, opposite to the fixed tail polarity. Choose a fixed R649 tail witness w distinct from y_-. The fixed source trimer (s_0,s_1,w) is tight and the y_- sign has the opposite polarity on its initial physical dimer, so R518 forces a tight Hamilton P4 on {y_-,s_0,s_1,w}. This proves the Internal-X P4 assertion.

Accepted R866 says every c_X=3 minimum forest and c_X=2 shape B contains an internal degree-two X block, so all of those configurations lie in the P4 branch.

For the second assertion, suppose no Q-X incidence produces the opposite-polarity P4 above. Then no X block can have degree two, hence both X blocks have degree one. Let the unique incidence of G_3 be considered in its literal T direction. If it left G_3, it would be a tail sign opposite to R649's fixed head polarity and the preceding argument would force a P4. Therefore it must enter G_3, so G_3 is the terminal block of its rail. Dually, if the unique incidence of G_4 entered G_4, it would be a head sign opposite to R649's fixed tail polarity and would force a P4. Therefore it must leave G_4, so G_4 is the initial block of its rail.

R866 now leaves only Same-rail endpoint-X and Split-X. If the two X blocks lie on one rail, its orientation necessarily begins at G_4 and ends at G_3. If they lie on different rails, the G_4 rail begins at G_4 and the G_3 rail ends at G_3. No R176 birth, payment descent, or shape-specific splice is used. This proves the theorem.
