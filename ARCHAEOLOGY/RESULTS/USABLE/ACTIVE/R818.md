# R818 — Corrected deep-cut split-splice clauses couple both R583 terminal blockers to two core seams

## Statement
Retain accepted R594 and R619 with X={a,b,c,z}, M_R={ab,cz}>M_S={ac,bz}>M_L={bc,az}, Q=(q_0,...,q_m), m>=6, L=q_0,u=q_1,s=q_{m-1},R=q_m, and normalize the left outgoing M_S gate to {a,c}. For a deep cut 3<=j<=m-3 put q=q_{j+1}, define A_j(x)=(x,q_j,q_{j-1}), and when j<=m-4 define C_j(x)=(q_{j+2},q,x). Then literal split-splice proposals from the synchronized enlarged-root covers give the following complete physical hole clauses. LEFT, in both phases: for root z, A_j(z) OR (R,z,q_j) OR (q,b,c) OR C_j(b); for root b, A_j(b) OR (R,b,q_j) OR (q,z,a) OR C_j(z). CROSSED right pair: A_j(a) OR (b,a,q_j) OR (q,c,L) OR C_j(c); and A_j(c) OR (z,c,q_j) OR (q,a,L) OR C_j(a). ALIGNED right pair: A_j(b) OR (a,b,q_j) OR (q,z,L) OR C_j(z); and A_j(z) OR (c,z,q_j) OR (q,b,L) OR C_j(b). At the terminal deep cut j=m-3 the C_j literal is absent from each clause because the Q suffix has only the singleton q_{m-2}; the remaining three literals are exhaustive. Each displayed disjunction means at least one listed turn is tight. These are the corrected full seam-window replacements for the invalid R816 two-hole clauses.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in accepted R594; accepted R619 supplies the phase-labelled enlarged singleton-deletion covers; R594 supplies the endpoint-star trimers used to restore the deleted root inside G8; m>=6. Boundary antisymmetry R3 converts a bad seam turn to the displayed reverse literal.

## Scope
R24/O6 exact array-intersection theorem. Corrects and supersedes pending R816; does not depend on pending R812. No R5,R168,R169,R224,R533,R350,generic R358,R542,capture,payment,R436,Reverse Ear,or computation.

## Applicability
Use as the exact deep-cut interface between accepted R583 and the synchronized enlarged-root covers. Unlike R816, every physical junction window is retained. The outer literals A_j and C_j are precisely the two R583 terminal blocker families; the two middle literals are matching-labelled core/cross-end turns. Compare these complete clauses across roots and adjacent cuts before invoking any payment machinery.

## Persistence
Retain j, phase, root label, the exact two candidate spanning rails, every present junction turn before reversal, and the corresponding displayed reverse literals. At j=m-3 retain the three-hole boundary specialization.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
R812 and R816 are not licensed by this theorem and should remain fenced. No clause may be shortened by dropping a companion junction turn without a separate proof that the dropped turn is tight. The theorem does not choose a disjunct, close H, or imply a binary 2-SAT system. It does not assert any selected-state currentness.

## Proof
Fix 3<=j<=m-3 and q=q_{j+1}. LEFT ROOT z. Accepted R619 plus the R594 star gives the G8 cover (u,L,a,c,b) sqcup (z,R,s). Consider P=(q_2,...,q_j,z,R,s) and S=(u,L,a,c,b,q,q_{j+2},...,q_{m-2}), omitting the tail after q when j=m-3. The only new turns of P are h1=(q_{j-1},q_j,z) and h2=(q_j,z,R). The only new turns of S are h3=(c,b,q) and, when j<=m-4, h4=(b,q,q_{j+2}). If all present h_k were tight, P sqcup S would be a spanning two-cover of H. Thus some h_k is bad. Their complete reversals are respectively A_j(z)=(z,q_j,q_{j-1}), (R,z,q_j), (q,b,c), and C_j(b)=(q_{j+2},q,b), proving the first clause. LEFT ROOT b uses G8=(u,L,c,a,z) sqcup (b,R,s). The four hole turns are (q_{j-1},q_j,b),(q_j,b,R),(a,z,q),(z,q,q_{j+2}); reversing yields A_j(b),(R,b,q_j),(q,z,a),C_j(z). CROSSED RIGHT ROOT c uses G8=(u,L,c) sqcup (a,b,z,R,s). The prefix candidate (q_2,...,q_j,a,b,z,R,s) has new turns (q_{j-1},q_j,a),(q_j,a,b); the suffix (u,L,c,q,q_{j+2},...) has new turns (L,c,q) and, when present, (c,q,q_{j+2}). Reversing a bad hole gives A_j(a),(b,a,q_j),(q,c,L),C_j(c). Crossed root a similarly uses (u,L,a) sqcup (c,z,b,R,s) and yields A_j(c),(z,c,q_j),(q,a,L),C_j(a). In the aligned phase the right G8 covers are (u,L,z) sqcup (b,a,c,R,s) and (u,L,b) sqcup (z,c,a,R,s). Their four hole reversals are respectively A_j(b),(a,b,q_j),(q,z,L),C_j(z), and A_j(z),(c,z,q_j),(q,b,L),C_j(b). At j=m-3 the second Q-side turn after q does not exist, so h4 is absent and the corresponding C_j literal is simply omitted. This proves every displayed clause with the complete seam window retained.
