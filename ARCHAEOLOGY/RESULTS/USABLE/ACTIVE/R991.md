# R991 — A global minimum edge forces mutual source exclusion and canonical fresh-edge three-covers

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888, with distinct edge labels, and let uv be the globally minimum ordinary edge. Then pc_inc(G-u)=pc_inc(G-v)=2. In every exact two-path cover of G-u, the vertex v is not the source of either rail; symmetrically, in every exact two-path cover of G-v, u is not a rail source. Indeed, any source exposure would let the omitted endpoint be prepended through uv and would give a spanning two-cover of G.

More precisely, let G-u=A|B be any exact increasing two-cover, orient both rails increasingly, and suppose v lies on A=(a_0,...,a_i=v,...,a_r). Then i>=1. Writing p=a_{i-1}, delete the selected edge p->v and add the fresh edge u->v. The resulting selected-edge set is the spanning increasing three-cover
  (a_0,...,p) | (u,v,a_{i+1},...,a_r) | B,
with the obvious dimer interpretation if i=r. The construction is always legal because uv is globally minimum, hence lambda(uv)<lambda(v a_{i+1}) whenever the latter edge exists. It preserves matching cardinality n-3 while introducing the complete-graph edge uv. The symmetric construction holds from every exact two-cover of G-v, producing a three-cover whose corresponding rail begins v,u.

Consequently, any general theorem asserting that every prescribed vertex of every edge-ordered complete graph with increasing path-cover number at most two can be exposed as a rail source would imply R888 immediately.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order counterexample to R888; uv is its globally minimum edge. Uses accepted singleton-deletion exactness/minimality R959.

## Scope
Pure edge-ordered minimum-counterexample reduction and completeness-specific fresh-edge construction.

## Applicability
Use the global minimum edge as a fresh edge outside a restricted neutral selected-edge family. Every deletion-fiber cover yields a canonical three-cover using uv. Source exposure of the opposite endpoint closes R888 immediately.

## Persistence
Retain uv, the chosen exact cover of G-u or G-v, the position of the opposite endpoint on its rail, the deleted predecessor edge, and the canonical fresh-edge three-cover.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prove prescribed-source exposure. Does not prove R888. Does not assert the fresh-edge three-cover has a positive R980 recombination with any particular seed. Does not reopen the longest-path-complement route.

## Proof
Accepted R959 gives pc_inc(G)=3 and, for every vertex x, pc_inc(G-x)=2 with every exact two-cover having two nonempty rails. Fix the globally minimum edge uv.

Take any exact cover G-u=A|B and orient both rails increasingly. If v were the source of its rail, write that rail as (v,a_1,...,a_r). If r=0, then adjoining u gives the dimer (u,v), which together with the other rail two-covers G. If r>=1, global minimality of uv gives lambda(uv)<lambda(v a_1), so (u,v,a_1,...,a_r) is increasing. Again the untouched other rail gives a spanning two-cover of G. Both contradict that G is a counterexample. Hence v is not a source in any exact cover of G-u. Interchanging u and v proves the symmetric assertion for G-v.

Now retain G-u=A|B and suppose v lies on A=(a_0,...,a_i=v,...,a_r). We have i>=1. Let p=a_{i-1}. Delete the selected adjacency p->v. This splits A into the nonempty prefix (a_0,...,p) and the suffix (v,a_{i+1},...,a_r), with the suffix possibly the singleton {v}. Add the fresh directed edge u->v. If i<r, then uv is globally smaller than v a_{i+1}, so (u,v,a_{i+1},...,a_r) is increasing; if i=r, (u,v) is a dimer and is vacuously increasing. Every other selected adjacency is inherited from A or B. Therefore
  (a_0,...,p) | (u,v,a_{i+1},...,a_r) | B
is a spanning increasing three-cover of G. The selected matching lost one edge and gained one edge relative to the size-(n-3) matching of G-u, so it still has n-3 edges, exactly as a three-cover should. The construction from G-v is identical after swapping u and v.

Finally, if a general prescribed-source theorem held for every edge-ordered complete graph of path-cover number at most two, apply it to G-u with prescribed vertex v. The first paragraph would then produce a spanning two-cover of G. Thus that theorem implies R888.
