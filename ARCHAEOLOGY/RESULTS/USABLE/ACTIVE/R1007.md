# R1007 — A trimer rail in a pair-deletion complement forces a fixed-complement four-rail singleton fiber

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888 and let x,y be distinct vertices. Suppose G-{x,y}=T|B is an exact two-cover with |T|=3. Then at least one of the four-sets V(T) union {x} and V(T) union {y} is Hamiltonian. Consequently, for at least one z in {x,y}, the singleton deletion G-z has an exact two-cover P|B in which P is a Hamilton path on V(T) union ({x,y}-{z}) and therefore has order four; the opposite rail is literally the same B as in the pair-deletion cover. If both four-sets are Hamiltonian, both singleton fibers may be chosen with the same complement rail B.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order R888 counterexample; a prescribed pair deletion has an exact two-cover with a trimer rail T and opposite rail B.

## Scope
Pure R888 pair-deletion trimer bridge using the accepted local five-vertex theorem R963.

## Applicability
Use after a pair-deletion or minimum-side representative reaches a trimer rail. It upgrades to one or two singleton-deletion four-rail covers while preserving the opposite rail exactly.

## Persistence
Retain x,y, the literal trimer support V(T), the same opposite rail B, and whichever Hamilton four-set extension is obtained.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert both deleted vertices extend T. Does not classify the resulting four-vertex rail or close the singleton fiber.

## Proof
Let S=V(T), |S|=3. Suppose for contradiction that neither S union {x} nor S union {y} is Hamiltonian. Accepted R963 applies to the common triangle S and the two exterior vertices x,y, and yields an increasing Hamilton path P on S union {x,y}. Since B is disjoint from S union {x,y} and is already an increasing Hamilton path on its support, P|B is a spanning two-path cover of G, contradicting that G is a counterexample to R888. Hence at least one of S+x,S+y is Hamiltonian. If S+y is Hamiltonian, choose a Hamilton path P_y on it. Then G-x=P_y|B is an exact two-cover with a four-vertex rail and the literal unchanged complement rail B. The case S+x is symmetric. If both four-sets are Hamiltonian, both exact singleton-deletion covers exist simultaneously with the same B.
