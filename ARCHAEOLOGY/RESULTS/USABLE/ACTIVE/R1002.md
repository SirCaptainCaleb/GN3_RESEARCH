# R1002 — Pair deletion in a minimum R888 counterexample has exact two-cover number and no singleton rail

## Statement
Let G be a minimum-order counterexample to R888 and let x,y be distinct vertices. Then pc_inc(G-{x,y})=2. Moreover every exact two-path cover G-{x,y}=U|V has |U|,|V|>=2.

## Hypotheses
G is a minimum-order counterexample to R888; x,y are distinct physical vertices.

## Scope
Pure edge-ordered pair-deletion rigidity. R888-subclass analogue of the full-boundary pair-deletion theorem, proved independently from edge-order minimality.

## Applicability
Use whenever a prescribed physical pair is deleted and a nontrivial exact two-cover is required, including reverse-P4 middle-dimer recompletion.

## Persistence
Retain the deleted pair and the chosen exact pair-deletion cover; nontriviality is universal over every exact cover of that deletion.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prescribe endpoint locations, rail supports, or a canonical representative. Does not synchronize covers of different pair deletions.

## Proof
Fix distinct x,y and put H=G-{x,y}. Since H is a smaller edge-ordered complete graph than the minimum counterexample G, pc_inc(H)<=2. If pc_inc(H)=1, let P be an increasing Hamilton path of H. The dimer (x,y) is vacuously increasing, so P|(x,y) is a spanning two-cover of G, contradiction. Hence pc_inc(H)=2. Now let U|V be any exact two-cover of H. Suppose U is a singleton {z}. Then V is an increasing Hamilton path on V(G)-{x,y,z}. The three physical vertices x,y,z induce a triangle whose three edge labels are distinct. Let e_1<e_2<e_3 be those labels. The edges carrying e_1 and e_2 share one vertex, because every two edges of a triangle do. Orient the three vertices so that the e_1 edge is traversed first and the e_2 edge second. This is an increasing Hamilton trimer Q on {x,y,z}. Then Q|V is a spanning two-cover of G, contradiction. Thus U is nontrivial; the same argument applies to V. Therefore every exact pair-deletion two-cover has both rails of order at least two.
