# R130 — Minimum-overlap-two disjoint-core triangle normalization

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case and minimum longest-support overlap two. Then there exist three global-longest five-supports L,B,D such that X=L∩B and Y=L∩D are disjoint 2-subsets of L, while Z=B∩D is disjoint from L and has size 2 or 3. Hence X,Y,Z are pairwise disjoint and L,B,D have one of exactly two intersection types: (2,2,2), in which case |L∪B∪D|=9 and there is one exterior vertex; or (2,2,3), in which case |L∪B∪D|=8 and there are two exterior vertices.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; the minimum intersection among distinct global-longest five-supports equals 2; accepted R129.

## Scope
Pure support-incidence normalization of the minimum-overlap-two branch.

## Applicability
Use immediately after R129. It replaces the overlap-two branch by two finite support-triangle motifs with pairwise-disjoint intersection cores.

## Persistence
All three supports and their pairwise intersections are graph-intrinsic; the classification is purely set-theoretic once R129 supplies L,B,D.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not rule out either triangle type, does not assert simultaneous currentness of path representatives, and does not by itself force a spanning two-cover.

## Proof
By R129 choose global-longest five-supports L,B,D such that X=L∩B and Y=L∩D are disjoint and |X|=|Y|=2. Because the global minimum overlap among distinct members of F equals 2, |B∩D|≥2. Put Z=B∩D. If z∈Z∩L, then z∈B∩L=X and z∈D∩L=Y, contradicting X∩Y=∅. Hence Z∩L=∅. Since B has five vertices and |B∩L|=2, B\\L has size 3; similarly D\\L has size 3. Therefore Z⊆(B\\L)∩(D\\L), so |Z|≤3. Consequently |Z|∈{2,3}. Also X,Y⊆L while Z∩L=∅, so X,Y,Z are pairwise disjoint and L∩B∩D=∅. Inclusion-exclusion now yields |L∪B∪D|=|L|+|B|+|D|-|X|-|Y|-|Z|=15-2-2-|Z|=11-|Z|. Thus |Z|=2 gives union size 9 and one exterior vertex, while |Z|=3 gives union size 8 and two exterior vertices. This proves the normalization.
