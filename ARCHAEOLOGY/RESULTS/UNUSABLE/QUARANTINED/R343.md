# R343 — Protected first-contact mirror root reduces to fixed-turn exit or exact target-return cell

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the beyond-order-ten regime. Then either the earlier-contact branch of accepted R329 already occurs, or R329 gives the literal current first-contact mirror root J=(p,x,z) with captured middle x. In the latter branch enter the protected captured-middle fixed-turn lineage of provisional R339. Along the chosen continuation, either a spanning two-cover or one of the accepted explicit fixed-turn historical contact/pair exits occurs before the target pair is reached, or the R172 lexicographic descent necessarily reaches the target-aligned mass-two floor with singleton supports {p},{z}. At that first target-floor occurrence, provisional R340 applies to the same physical outer pair: either some exact return of H-{p,z} exposes x as a rail endpoint and supplies the rooted two-hole mate clause of R336, or every exact return keeps x internal and the branch supplies the same-residue 3-to-2 component-drop cell, R159 balanced pair, and R338 four-way reverse endpoint shield. Thus outside earlier/existing interaction exits, the entire role-sensitive R329 mirror-contact branch reduces to one finite exact-return dichotomy on its own fixed-turn target pair.

H is a hypothetical smallest counterexample beyond order ten. Accepted R329 and R172 are available; provisional R339 and R340 are used in their stated scopes.

If R329 takes its earlier-contact alternative there is nothing to prove. Otherwise the mirror reentry gives the current turn J=(p,x,z), and R339 lawfully injects it into the fixed-turn lineage. Follow the designated continuation. Whenever M>2, R172 decreases M unless an explicit exit/closure is taken. Once M=2, if the floor is not {p,z}, R172 decreases delta_J. Hence absent an explicit exit/closure, after finitely many quiet macro steps the first target-aligned floor {p},{z} is reached. At that physical pair invoke R340. Its exact-return classification yields either an endpoint return with the R336 two-hole mate clause, or the all-internal R337/R338 component-drop, balanced-pair, and reverse-shield packet.
