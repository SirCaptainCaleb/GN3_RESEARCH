# R287 — Repeated-dimer class admits a fixed-anchor centered-trimer capture-reentry fan

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the accepted R244 repeated-dimer setup with retained global-longest carrier K_a, exterior E_a, repeated physical exterior dimer D, and completion class B. Fix either endpoint d in D and any exterior vertex y in E_a\\D. Then every b in B gives a graph-intrinsic signed certificate on the spoke {b,d} with the same witness y: orient the unique d-middle tight trimer on {b,d,y} and read the terminal spoke by R41. Thus d supports a common-witness-y signed spoke fan on all of B. Independently, whenever the R250 hypotheses hold and b is internal on K_a, b has the bilateral historical capture certificates on its two original K_a incidences supplied by R250. These two facts are co-indexed by the same physical vertex b but no capture-to-spoke transport is asserted.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample in the R244 repeated-dimer setup; d is an endpoint of D; y is in E_a\\D; accepted R3,R41,R244, and R250 for the optional bilateral-capture clause.

## Scope
Corrected separation of the fresh-spoke geometry from the original exact-frame capture ancestry.

## Applicability
Use when later arguments need the same carrier indices to carry both original-frame capture history and a fresh fixed-anchor common-witness sign family. Any consumer must keep those resources separate unless a new transport theorem is proved.

## Persistence
D,E_a,K_a,B,d,y and the centered trimer/spoke signs are graph-intrinsic. R250 captures remain historical events on the original carrier incidences. Co-indexing at b persists, but the fresh spoke has no capture ancestry.

## Proof
Because b lies on K_a while d,y lie in E_a, the three vertices are distinct. Boundary antisymmetry gives exactly one tight orientation (b,d,y) or (y,d,b). In either case {b,d} is a terminal physical dimer and its opposite outer vertex is y, so R41 supplies the signed certificate with witness y. This construction is independent for every b in B. Separately, R250 already certifies bilateral historical captures at internal b in the original D-deletion frame. No step identifies those captures with the fresh spoke support.
