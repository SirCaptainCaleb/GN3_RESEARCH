# R113 — Transversal zero-excess canonical-core fan actualization

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the simultaneous zero-excess transversal kernel in the R100 coordinates with common core T={t,tprime}, common marker M={delta,epsilon}, and the four three-support common-mate families of R112. For every witness w in M and each endpoint side s in {L,R}, the three same-polarity supports D_x(s,w), D_y(s,w), D_z(s,w) may be actualized jointly by R40 using the same physical core dimer T as the nontrivial marker path. Thus there exists a graph-intrinsic opposite-polarity support E_T(s,w), born from a single marker test on T, that forms a balanced pair with all three members of that family. Consequently all four R112 fan births can be rooted at the same physical core dimer T; only the chosen marker orientation/test and the resulting opposite support may vary with s and w.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R102 simultaneous zero-excess transversal kernel; R112 common-mate families.

## Scope
Canonical localization of the pre-payment actualization step in the transversal equality kernel.

## Applicability
Use before any pair payment. It puts the delta/epsilon and left/right fan families through one fixed physical core marker, making cross-family collision, orientation, or central-anchor comparisons meaningful without introducing arbitrary spare vertices.

## Persistence
The physical core T and all family supports are graph-intrinsic in the R100/R102 coordinates. The common mate produced by each actualization is graph-intrinsic as in R40.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the four mates E_T(s,w) coincide, that the same orientation of T is used in all four tests, that later paid descendants are coupled, or that a spanning two-cover follows immediately.

## Proof
Fix w in M and one endpoint side s. By R102, the three zero-packet four-rail supports are W_x={y,alpha,z,beta}, W_y={x,alpha,z,gamma}, and W_z={x,beta,y,gamma}, while the common core is T={t,tprime} and M={delta,epsilon}. Hence T is disjoint from W_x union W_y union W_z and from w. By R112, D_x(s,w), D_y(s,w), and D_z(s,w) are the corresponding terminal dimer supports on Q_x,Q_y,Q_z, so each physical support is contained in its W_i. Thus the physical dimer T is disjoint from all three supports and from their common witness w. Choose a tight orientation of T as a nontrivial marker path. R112 gives that the three D_i(s,w) have one common polarity and witness w. Applying R40 once to this three-support family with marker T therefore produces one graph-intrinsic support E_T(s,w) of the opposite polarity, balanced with every D_i(s,w). Repeating for s=L,R and w=delta,epsilon gives the four claimed fan births, all using the same physical core dimer T. R40 does not couple later payments, and nothing here identifies the resulting mates or marker orientations across the four applications.
