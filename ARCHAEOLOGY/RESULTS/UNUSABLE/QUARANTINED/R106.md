# R106 — Transversal triangle common-marker triple root

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the transversal branch coordinates A=T∪{x,y,alpha}, B=T∪{x,z,beta}, C=T∪{y,z,gamma}, with exterior pair M={delta,epsilon}, deleting M admits three exact 5+3 two-covers with long rails A,B,C respectively. Namely the complementary three-sets are K_A={z,beta,gamma}, K_B={y,alpha,gamma}, and K_C={x,alpha,beta}; each admits a tight trimer orientation, so A∪K_A, B∪K_B, and C∪K_C are exact two-covers of H-M. Thus the same physical exterior marker supports three distinct globally longest 5+3 roots forming the symmetric minimum-overlap triangle.

## Hypotheses
Standing order-ten smallest-counterexample case; R98 transversal branch in the R100 coordinates.

## Scope
Pure support-level common-marker normalization.

## Applicability
Use to apply fixed-exterior-pair or gap-two pivot theorems to A,B,C over the same physical marker without changing the deleted pair.

## Persistence
The three exact covers need not share one representative or compatible trimer orientations; only the physical marker and long-rail supports are common.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert simultaneous currentness of the three roots or any relation between their endpoint-pivot descendants.

## Proof
By R100, A=T∪{x,y,alpha}, B=T∪{x,z,beta}, C=T∪{y,z,gamma}, and the only vertices outside A∪B∪C are delta,epsilon. Hence M={delta,epsilon} is disjoint from A,B,C. In H-M, the complement of A is exactly {z,beta,gamma}; the complement of B is {y,alpha,gamma}; and the complement of C is {x,alpha,beta}. By R8 each of these three-sets admits a tight trimer orientation. Since A,B,C are supports of tight five-paths, pairing the corresponding five-path with the chosen complementary trimer gives an exact 5+3 two-cover of H-M in each case.
