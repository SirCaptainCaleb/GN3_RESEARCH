# R300 — Common-coordinate puncture floors either coincide or force same-support sign interaction

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume two repaired puncture lineages have alternative mass-two floors {q,c_1} and {q,c_2} sharing the same prescribed physical coordinate q, as supplied by provisional R299. Then either c_1=c_2, in which case the two histories share the same physical floor pair and may choose the same graph-intrinsic fixed turn through R249, or c_1<>c_2, in which case for any physical vertex t distinct from q,c_1,c_2 the physical dimer {q,t} carries two signed certificates with the distinct witnesses c_1,c_2. Hence R38 forces either the same-polarity two-extension collision package or the opposite-polarity strict two-ended signed path; in the latter case R263 gives an explicit P4. Thus alternative floors sharing q cannot remain physically unrelated.

## Hypotheses
Hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; provisional corrected common-coordinate floor theorem R299; accepted R3,R38,R41,R249,R263.

## Scope

## Applicability

## Persistence

## Exclusions / nonclaims

## Proof
If c_1=c_2, the physical floor pair is the same and R249 can use the same chosen proper turn. Otherwise choose t outside {q,c_1,c_2}. R3 gives a tight t-middle orientation on each triple {q,t,c_i}. R41 reads {q,t} as a signed terminal support with witness c_i. The witnesses differ, so R38 applies. If the polarities are opposite, R263 concatenates them to a literal P4. No historical capture is used.
