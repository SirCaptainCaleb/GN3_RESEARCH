# R220 — Anchored-capacity one-residual branch terminates in cover or bounded swallow

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and let (alpha;P),(T;beta) be an anchored-capacity realization of a balanced opposite-sign pair with ordinary reservoir of size one. Then repeated use of the literal signed-pair discharge in the one-residual frame cannot continue by strict pair-mass descent indefinitely. After finitely many literal-frame peel steps, either H has a spanning two-cover or a bounded support swallow occurs. More precisely, if the initial literal pair mass is M0, there are at most M0-5 consecutive strict-descent outputs before a non-descent R171 output is forced.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; an anchored-capacity balanced pair has ordinary reservoir of size one.

## Scope
Order-free finite termination of the R180 one-residual branch after recovery of R171 and the universal literal-frame mass floor R214.

## Applicability
Use in the |X|=1 capacity branch to remove strict pair-mass descent as a terminal unresolved outcome. The only surviving nonclosing output is bounded support swallow.

## Persistence
The iteration is only along the literal R171 peel lineage whose successful descent steps remain in the displayed literal frame. No claim is made across unrelated recompletions or remints.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not close bounded support swallow. Does not assert global pair-mass monotonicity outside this literal peel lineage. Does not apply if a descent step abandons the R171 literal-frame continuation.

## Proof
Let X={x}. Put W=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T)). As in the direct one-residual construction, 1<=|W|<=3; choose a tight path J on W, using R8 if |W|=3. Then H=J sqcup P sqcup T is a literal R171 signed-pair frame. Let its pair mass be M0=|P|+|T|. R171 gives either a spanning two-cover, bounded support swallow, or a strict historical pair-mass descent. In the strict-descent branch, the R171 interval-peel persistence clause keeps the continuation in the displayed literal frame, so the resulting pair again satisfies the literal-frame hypotheses. By R214 every such literal frame has pair mass at least five. Hence along consecutive strict-descent outputs the integer mass forms a strictly decreasing sequence M0>M1>...>=5, of length at most M0-5. Therefore eventually a non-descent R171 output occurs, namely a spanning two-cover or bounded support swallow.
