# R218 — Universal balanced-pair anchored-capacity front door

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In every hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H, either a spanning two-cover already occurs or one may choose an ancestry-bearing balanced pair from R198, realize it at anchored capacity, and obtain one of the following explicit productive outputs: (i) a spanning two-cover; (ii) a strict graph-intrinsic same-polarity two-witness extension/collision output or bounded reverse trimer/contact from a reservoir of size at least two; (iii) in the one-residual branch, a literal signed-pair frame and hence by R171 either a spanning two-cover, strict historical pair-mass descent, or bounded support swallow. Thus after anchored-capacity maximization no quiet anonymous residual branch remains.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample.

## Scope
Order-free universal front door. It composes the accepted adaptive mass-two pair floor with anchored-capacity maximization and the recovered literal signed-pair discharge. No exterior-slack or order-ten assumption.

## Applicability
Use as an alternative parent to O3 exterior-slack casework. It reduces every surviving smallest counterexample to explicit physical extension/contact or R171 descent/swallow outputs after one anchored-capacity maximization.

## Persistence
The initial floor and later capacity realization are lineage/representative data as in their parent theorems. Collision/contact, pair descent ancestry, and bounded swallow outputs persist only with the guarantees of R180/R171.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that the nonclosing outputs are themselves contradictions. Does not make anchored capacity globally monotone after reset. Does not close arbitrary-order Strong Level-(1) by itself.

## Proof
Assume no spanning two-cover initially. By R198 choose an ancestry-bearing balanced opposite-sign pair, with fixed signed anchors and witnesses. Apply the anchored-capacity construction R179 to obtain a maximizing disjoint realization (alpha;P),(T;beta), and let X be its ordinary reservoir. R180 gives three cases. If X is empty, its branch (i) already gives a spanning two-cover. If |X|>=2, branch (ii) gives a strict graph-intrinsic same-polarity two-witness extension/collision output or bounded reverse trimer/contact. It remains to treat |X|=1. Write X={x} and W=V(H)\\(V(P) union V(T)). By definition of X every vertex of W lies among x,alpha,beta, and x is in W, so 1<=|W|<=3. A one- or two-vertex set supports a tight path; if |W|=3, R8 supplies a tight trimer orientation. Hence W supports a nonempty tight path J and H=J sqcup P sqcup T is a literal spanning three-cover with P,T the historical opposite-signed pair. R171 applies, giving a spanning two-cover, strict historical pair-mass descent, or bounded support swallow. Thus every anchored-capacity branch has one of the stated productive outputs.
