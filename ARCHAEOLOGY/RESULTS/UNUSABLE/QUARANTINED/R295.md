# R295 — Two punctures force one currentizable capture-spoke trimer and a central-anchor interaction

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Let H be a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and K a proper tight path with two distinct internal adjacent dimers. Use the corrected puncture theorem to retain their two historical carrier capture indices b,b' (possibly equal), without transporting those captures to any fresh support. Then either H has a spanning two-cover or there exist two vertex-disjoint tight trimers C and T with the following properties. If b<>b', choose one exterior vertex d and let the two incident physical supports {b,d},{b',d}; R154 currentizes their unique compatible tight trimer C on {b,d,b'}. If b=b', choose distinct exterior d,d' and R154 currentizes the unique tight trimer C on {d,b,d'}. In either case choose three exterior vertices disjoint from C and orient them as a tight trimer T. Then R41 gives the synchronized central-anchor interaction between C and T. Thus the two puncture histories canonically determine physical carrier indices that can be coupled into a currentizable trimer, but the terminal supports of C carry no capture ancestry unless separately proved.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; K is proper with two distinct internal adjacent dimers; corrected puncture captures as in provisional R293; accepted R8,R154,R169,R41.

## Proof
Apply R293 to both punctures unless closure occurs, retaining physical carrier indices b,b'. R169 gives at least five exterior vertices. If b<>b', choose exterior d. The two distinct supports {b,d},{b',d} meet only at d, so R154 supplies their unique compatible tight trimer C and a minimum-three-cover currentization of C. Since C uses only one exterior vertex, choose any three other exterior vertices and orient them as T; T is disjoint from C. If b=b', choose distinct exterior d,d' and apply R154 to {b,d},{b,d'}, obtaining C on {d,b,d'}; at least three other exterior vertices remain and form T. In both cases R41 applies to the disjoint tight trimers C,T. The old capture events are retained only as historical labels on b,b', not on the fresh supports.
