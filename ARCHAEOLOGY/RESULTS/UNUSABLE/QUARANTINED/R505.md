# R505 — R495 fork mates synchronize to the forced bridge cross or expose a witness-witness state

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Retain a non-DR candidate-aware fork of R495 and the forced bridge-interior cross state supplied by R502 in an exact D-deletion cover T, D={a_0,b_q}. In the head fork HF, let M_A=H_A=(a_1,a_0,b_q) and M_P=H_P=(z_1,a_0,b_q), the exact mates of the unique bad seams of C_A and C_P. Let xy be a selected T-state with exactly one endpoint x in P^o and the other in A^+ or B^-. Then either (HH) {x,y}={a_1,z_1}, so T literally selects a directed state between the two named head-fork witnesses, the heads of the two non-X_H source components; or (MC) at least one witness w in {a_1,z_1} avoids {x,y}. Choose its corresponding exact fork mate M_w as the signed incarnation of D in R37. Then R37 captures the P^o endpoint x through the actual selected cross {x,y}, and the capture certificate is synchronized to the same graph-intrinsic turn M_w that is the exact mate of its displayed one-hole spanning proposal C_w. Choosing any genuine old P^o adjacency at x, R312 therefore yields either an earlier protected contact at x or a lawful first-contact R155 mirror reentry while the exact fork candidate C_w, its unique bad seam, its mate M_w, D, T, xy, P^o, and the capture event remain retained as historical/certificate data. In the tail fork TF the exact dual holds: with fork mates M_B=T_B=(a_0,b_q,b_{q-1}) and M_P=T_P=(a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}), either (TT) T selects the literal state on {b_{q-1},z_{m-1}}, the two named tail-fork witnesses, or one exact fork mate avoids xy and supplies the synchronized capture/first-contact route. Thus every non-DR R495 fork either exposes a direct witness-witness endpoint cross in the common D-deletion fiber or feeds one of its actual one-hole mates into the capture/first-contact lineage as the sign used for the capture.

H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; accepted R495 HF or TF; accepted R502 selected P^o cross in an exact H-D two-cover; accepted R37,R312.

## Proof
Consider HF. R495 gives the exact one-hole candidates C_A,C_P with mates M_A=(a_1,a_0,b_q) and M_P=(z_1,a_0,b_q), so M_A and M_P are admissible signed incarnations of the physical bridge dimer D with distinct witnesses a_1,z_1. R502 gives an exact D-deletion cover T selecting a state xy with exactly one endpoint x in P^o. If {x,y}={a_1,z_1}, record HH. Otherwise at least one w in {a_1,z_1} is outside {x,y}. Use the corresponding mate M_w as the R37 signed incarnation of D. Because its witness w avoids the selected marker xy, R37 permits capture of either marker endpoint; choose x, the endpoint in P^o. The original P^o is a nontrivial graph-intrinsic historical carrier and y lies outside it. Choose any genuine P^o adjacency E incident with x (one exists because |P^o|>=2). R312 applied to the retained packet (P^o,{x,y},x,E,capture) gives either earlier protected contact at x or makes R155 mirror reentry the first contact. Independently, the displayed one-hole proposal C_w, its unique bad seam bar(M_w), and the graph-intrinsic mate M_w remain valid historical/certificate facts; hence the capture sign is literally synchronized to the candidate mate. For TF, R495 supplies exact mates M_B=(a_0,b_q,b_{q-1}) and M_P=(a_0,b_q,z_{m-1}) with witnesses b_{q-1},z_{m-1}. The identical two-vertex argument gives either TT on that witness pair or an avoiding exact mate which is used in R37, followed by R312 on the P^o endpoint. This proves the theorem.
