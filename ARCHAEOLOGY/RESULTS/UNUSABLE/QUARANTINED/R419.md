# R419 — Every all-internal source-trimer vertex is selectable at the paid floor

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal regime of R359 for a proper tight source trimer J=(a,x,c) in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample H. Then for every prescribed source vertex z in {a,x,c}, there is a chosen ancestry-bearing balanced-pair continuation that either produces a spanning two-cover or reaches a mass-two balanced-pair floor in which (z) is literally one singleton signed support. The chosen lineage retains J, the physical middle x, the selected J-terminal mate containing z, the R389 pair-birth certificate through that mate, the endpoint-selective refund orientation, and the literal floor coordinate z. The three choices z=a,x,c are alternative lineages and need not coexist.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample; J=(a,x,c) is a proper tight trimer in the accepted R359 all-internal regime; accepted R389 and R224.

## Scope
Order-free source-trimer coordinate-selection theorem inside the all-internal R359 branch. It consumes the inherited-turn provenance of the R389 four-birth fan rather than merely descending an anonymous balanced pair.

## Applicability
Use when downstream work needs a mass-two floor carrying a prescribed physical coordinate of the original source trimer. In particular, it upgrades Type I ancestry from R417 to a concrete selectable floor coordinate tied to J.

## Persistence
For the chosen z-lineage retain J, x, the selected terminal signed dimer M_L or M_R of J containing z, one R389 direct pair-birth certificate using that mate, the R224 aligned refund orientation, the prescribed physical endpoint z, and the final singleton floor coordinate (z). Other source representatives and the second floor singleton need not persist.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert the three z-selective floors coexist or can be synchronized. Does not prescribe the second floor singleton. Does not claim the selected floor is target-aligned to any independently chosen vertex or closes the conjecture. Does not apply to the common-shadow Type II branch without an inherited-turn signed dimer satisfying R224.

## Proof
Write the two terminal signed dimers of the proper tight source trimer J=(a,x,c) as M_L and M_R, so V(M_L)={a,x} and V(M_R)={x,c}. By R389, in the all-internal R359 state there are four direct mass-four balanced-pair birth certificates, with two births using M_L as the unique J-terminal mate of opposite polarity to a role-bearing residual terminal dimer and two births using M_R. Fix z in {a,x,c}. If z=a choose M_L; if z=c choose M_R; if z=x choose either terminal mate. Choose any one of the R389 direct balanced-pair births that uses the selected mate M. This produces an ancestry-bearing balanced-pair lineage containing the signed physical dimer M, and the sign of M is certified by the named inherited tight turn J itself. Therefore all hypotheses of R224 hold for M. Choose in R224 the endpoint-selective refund channel whose prescribed endpoint is z. R224 then gives either a spanning two-cover or an ancestry-bearing mass-two floor in which (z) is literally one singleton floor coordinate. Its persistence clause retains the inherited turn J, chosen refund orientation, physical endpoint z, refund certificate, and literal floor coordinate; adjoining the R389 birth certificate and source middle x to the historical ledger yields exactly the stated persistence. Since the choices of birth, terminal mate, and refund channel are made separately for each z, the three conclusions are alternative lineages and no simultaneity is claimed.
