# R360 — Universal all-internal trimer return admits a disjoint same-middle target rotation

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the all-internal regime of provisional R359 in a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten. Thus one R18 currentization has H=J sqcup U sqcup V with J=(a,x,c), every exact two-cover of H-{a,c} keeps x internal, and the four reverse endpoint-shield turns of R359 hold on U and V. Since |H|>=11, |U|+|V|=|H|-3>=8, so at least one source residual rail, say U=(u_1,...,u_r), has r>=4. Its disjoint terminal dimers S_L={u_1,u_2} and S_R={u_{r-1},u_r} carry opposite-polarity signed certificates with the same witness x, namely (u_2,u_1,x) and (x,u_r,u_{r-1}). Hence they form a direct balanced mass-four pair. The contiguous interval (u_2,...,u_{r-1}) meets each terminal support in exactly one vertex, so the R174 signed-interval refund leaves the ancestry-bearing singleton descendants {u_1} and {u_r}; unless a spanning two-cover occurs this is a mass-two floor on the two physical endpoints of U. Since x is outside U, R3 makes exactly one of (u_1,x,u_r),(u_r,x,u_1) a tight proper turn J_U. Applying R249 to the endpoint floor enters a fixed-turn lineage on J_U with initial clock Psi=(2,0,0) and outer target pair {u_1,u_r}. Thus every all-internal outer-pair return either closes or rotates the fixed-turn target from {a,c} to a new pair {u_1,u_r} disjoint from {a,x,c}, while preserving the same physical middle x.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample beyond order ten; the all-internal branch of provisional R359 holds for a proper tight trimer J=(a,x,c).

## Scope
Order-free/beyond-order-ten recursion obtained by combining the generic return shield with the accepted endpoint-refund and fixed-turn front door.

## Applicability
Use after R359 when no endpoint return exists. It restarts the fixed-turn machinery on a new target pair tied to literal residual-rail geometry and keeps the same physical middle.

## Persistence
The source currentization and refunded floor are alternative lineage states. The source rail endpoints and middle x are graph-intrinsic identities retained in ancestry; no old capture credit is imported into the new clock.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not prove a global monotone under repeated rotations. Does not assert old and new target turns are current simultaneously. A later rotation may revisit an earlier physical target pair; such recurrence is not by itself a contradiction.

## Proof
In the R359 all-internal branch, the source complement U sqcup V has total order at least eight, so one rail U has order at least four. The four-way shield gives the two displayed reverse endpoint turns on U. Read their terminal dimers as opposite-polarity signed supports with common witness x, exactly as in the accepted R344 argument. They are disjoint, hence R39 gives a balanced mass-four pair. The middle interval of U meets each once; R174 rebirth leaves the two outer endpoint singletons with their ancestry. Apply R3 and then R249 using x as the chosen middle. Disjointness of the new target pair from the old turn is immediate because U lies in H-J.
