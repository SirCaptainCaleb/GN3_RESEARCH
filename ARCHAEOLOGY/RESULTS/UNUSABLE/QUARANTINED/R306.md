# R306 — Corrected two-puncture chain is index-coupled but not capture-ancestry-coupled

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
In the repaired R293-R296 two-puncture chain, the puncture/capture theorems contribute physical carrier indices b,bprime and retain separate historical capture events in their source frames. After those indices are obtained, the fixed-trimer packet, common-witness spokes, currentizable coupling trimer C, disjoint trimer T, and their central-anchor interaction are constructed using only the physical identities of b,bprime together with fresh exterior vertices and capture-free R3/R8/R39/R41/R154/R160/R169 machinery. The proofs are unchanged if b,bprime are replaced by arbitrary carrier vertices satisfying the same distinct/equal case split. Therefore the repaired chain currently establishes index-level co-labeling of generic geometry with two puncture histories, not a mathematical interaction or synchronization of the two capture ancestries. Any downstream claim that the capture histories themselves have synchronized must additionally invoke a theorem whose hypotheses consume the capture events or protected descendants, such as an applicable R42/R155-style ancestry transport/contact theorem.

## Proof
R293 obtains a lawful puncture-frame capture at b, but explicitly builds its later spoke/fixed-trimer packet independently by capture-free R3/R8/R39/R41/R160/R169. R294 takes two such indices b,bprime and constructs fresh spokes with a common witness y and a fixed trimer T; no capture theorem appears among the construction dependencies after the indices are named. R295 constructs C from incident physical supports through R154, using only whether b=bprime, then chooses a disjoint exterior trimer T and applies R41. R296 merely chooses the two punctures and retains the resulting indices while invoking R295. Thus, holding the same ambient vertex sets fixed, replacing the historical predicates captured(b), captured(bprime) by no predicates at all leaves every fresh geometric construction valid. The captures remain true historical facts, but they are not consumed by the derivation. By the persistence fences in R42/R155, historical capture cannot be upgraded to protection, same-representative currentness, or ancestry on a fresh support without an explicit theorem. Hence the chain is index-coupled but not capture-ancestry-coupled.
