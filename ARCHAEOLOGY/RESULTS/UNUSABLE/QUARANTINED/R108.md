# R108 — Singleton-star hub kernel has an abstract support model

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
The pure five-set incidence constraints used in R13 and the hub alternative of R98 do not by themselves contradict the singleton-star outcome of R107. Concretely, on disjoint blocks S={s1,s2,s3}, U={w,u}, V={v1,v2}, R={r1,r2,r3}, the family F0={A=S∪U, B=S∪V}∪{H_i=S∪{w,r_i}:1≤i≤3}∪{D_j=(S\\{s_j})∪{w,v1,v2}:1≤j≤3} consists of eight 5-sets, has union S∪U∪V∪R, has empty total intersection, and every two members intersect in at least three vertices. Its minimum pairwise intersection is three, every R-touching member is a singleton-wing hub, and every S-omitting member is exactly the forced singleton-star support from R107.

## Hypotheses
Abstract set-system statement using the same block cardinalities as the standing overlap-three butterfly.

## Scope
Negative-space sanity check for the R98/R107 support-incidence kernel; no boundary-tournament realizability is asserted.

## Applicability
Use to rule out attempts to close the exceptional singleton-star kernel using only support sizes, pairwise intersection lower bounds, union coverage, or empty total intersection. Additional tight-path/deletion/orientation information is necessary.

## Persistence
Pure finite set-system construction.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not assert that F0 is realizable as the actual global-longest support family of a Strong Level-(1) boundary tournament.

## Proof
Let F0 consist of A,B,H_1,H_2,H_3,D_1,D_2,D_3 as stated. Every member has size five. The union is all of S∪U∪V∪R: A covers S,U; B covers V; and the three hubs cover R. The total intersection is empty because A∩B=S while D_j omits s_j for j=1,2,3.

It remains to check pairwise intersections. We have |A∩B|=3. For every i,j,
|A∩H_i|=|S∪{w}|=4,
|A∩D_j|=|(S\\{s_j})∪{w}|=3,
|B∩H_i|=|S|=3,
|B∩D_j|=|(S\\{s_j})∪V|=4.
For i≠k, H_i∩H_k=S∪{w}, of size four. For every i,j, H_i∩D_j=(S\\{s_j})∪{w}, of size three. For j≠k, D_j∩D_k=(S\\{s_j,s_k})∪{w,v1,v2}, of size four. Thus every pair meets in at least three vertices, and several pairs meet in exactly three, so the minimum pairwise intersection is exactly three.

Finally, only the H_i meet R, and each is S∪{w,r_i}; hence the hub-wing set is W={w}. A member omits s_j∈S exactly when it is D_j, and D_j=(S\\{s_j})∪{w}∪V, exactly the singleton-star pattern of R107. Therefore the incidence axioms alone admit the exceptional kernel. The construction makes no claim of realizability by tight-path supports in an actual boundary tournament.
