# R334 — First-contact mirror root has outer-pair deletion endpoint-or-star-replacement normal form

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the literal first-contact mirror-trimer branch of accepted R329, with a current minimum three-cover H=J sqcup U sqcup V, J a tight trimer on {p,x,z}, D={x,z} the actual captured crossing, and E={p,x} a genuine old carrier edge. Delete the two outer contact vertices {p,z}. Then every exact two-cover of H-{p,z} places the retained captured anchor x on a rail of order at least three. Consequently exactly one of two local role forms holds in that exact return: (i) x is an endpoint of its rail, giving literal endpoint exposure of the captured physical anchor in the pair-deletion subsystem; or (ii) x is internal on its rail and its two selected rail incidences form a new residual physical star {a,x},{x,b} with a,b in U union V, equivalently a tight trimer (a,x,b). Thus the R329 root cannot return after deleting its outer pair with x quiet or isolated: it must either expose x as a rail endpoint or replace the old current mirror-star {p,x},{x,z} by a selected two-edge residual star at x.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical smallest Strong Level-(1) counterexample and the accepted R329 branch-(ii) root H=J sqcup U sqcup V is given, with J on {p,x,z}.

## Scope
Order-free exact pair-deletion normal form for the rooted protected-contact mirror trimer. No General Tight-Path Contact and no capture-to-fresh-support ancestry transport.

## Applicability
Use to classify the R329 rooted mirror contact. The outer-pair deletion forces the captured anchor into either a genuine endpoint role or a new selected residual star, giving a concrete role-sensitive object for subsequent exact-cover comparison.

## Persistence
The old mirror supports D,E and capture event remain historical/source-root data. The endpoint role or new residual star is current only in the chosen exact two-cover of H-{p,z}.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not restore p,z while preserving the return rail. Does not assert endpoint exposure in a spanning cover of H. Does not synchronize the old and new stars in one representative.

## Proof
By R5 choose any exact two-cover P sqcup Q of H-{p,z}. By R168, both P and Q have order at least three. The vertex x belongs to exactly one rail, say P, so |P|>=3. If x is an endpoint of P, branch (i) holds. Otherwise x is internal in the tight path ordering of P, with distinct immediate predecessor a and successor b. Since p,z are deleted, a,b lie in U union V. The consecutive turn (a,x,b) is tight and the selected supports {a,x},{x,b} form the new residual star at x. These are exhaustive because a path vertex has selected degree one at an endpoint and two internally.
