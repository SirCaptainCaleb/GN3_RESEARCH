# R317 — Order-eleven 5+5+1 singleton roots have canonical 4+4+3 companion covers

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the order-eleven longest-five residue of R315/R316. Let K=(k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4,k_5) be a global-longest P5 with six-vertex exterior E, and let e be any omission supplied by R196 for which Q=(q_1,q_2,q_3,q_4,q_5) is a tight P5 on E\\{e}. Then H=K sqcup Q sqcup {e} is a literal 5+5+1 spanning three-cover. Since both K and Q have equal global-longest order five, R44 gives both endpoint reverse-four paths. With the natural endpoint labeling these are the left cross-rail P4 on {k_1,k_2,q_1,q_2} and the right cross-rail P4 on {k_4,k_5,q_4,q_5} (with the tight orders supplied by R44). They are vertex-disjoint. The remaining vertices are exactly {k_3,q_3,e}, which support a tight trimer by R8. Hence every such singleton root has a literal companion spanning 4+4+3 cover. R196 supplies at least four distinct omissions e carrying this companion normal form.

## Hypotheses
H is a hypothetical order-eleven smallest counterexample with maximum proper path order five; K is global-longest P5; e,Q form an R196 singleton root; accepted R44 and R8.

## Scope
Finite order-eleven equal-rail normal form.

## Applicability
Use in the unique finite residue of R315 to replace a 5+5+1 root by two cross-rail reverse P4s plus the middle trimer.

## Persistence
The two reverse P4 supports and leftover trimer support are graph-intrinsic once K,Q and their endpoint orderings are fixed. The displayed 4+4+3 cover is literal.

## Exclusions / nonclaims
Does not sign the two P4s as an opposite historical pair. Does not by itself invoke R171 or close order eleven. Does not synchronize the companion covers for different omissions e.

## Proof
Fix one R196 root H=K sqcup Q sqcup {e}. Because the order-eleven residue has global longest-path order five, both K and Q are globally longest and have equal order. The equal-order clause of R44 therefore gives both end reverse-four paths. At one end the reverse P4 uses exactly the first two vertices of K and first two of Q; at the opposite end it uses exactly the last two vertices of K and last two of Q. For P5 rails these support sets are disjoint. Removing their eight vertices from H leaves k_3,q_3,e. Every three-set has a tight orientation by boundary antisymmetry, equivalently R8, so those three vertices form a tight trimer. The three tight paths are pairwise disjoint and cover all eleven vertices.
