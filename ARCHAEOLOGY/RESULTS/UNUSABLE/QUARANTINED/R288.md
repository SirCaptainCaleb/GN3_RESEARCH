# R288 — Four-core capture fan forces a fixed-trimer common-mate nonquiet pair family

**Record status:** `UNUSABLE/QUARANTINED`

## Statement
Assume the R244 four-core setup with repeated physical-dimer class B, |B|>=q=ceil((r-1)/6). Fix one endpoint d of the repeated exterior dimer D and choose y in E_a\\D. For every b in B, the unique d-middle tight trimer on {b,d,y} gives by R41 a signed certificate on the spoke {b,d} with the same witness y. Hence one polarity class P has size h>=ceil(|B|/2). Choose any three-set T contained in E_a\\{d}; by R8 it supports a tight trimer and is disjoint from every spoke {b,d}, b in P. Its two terminal signed dimers have opposite polarities by R41, so one fixed terminal dimer M has polarity opposite to the common polarity of P. Therefore every spoke in P forms with M a direct mass-four balanced pair by R39. If h>=2, after declaring one birth first, every later birth through the same fixed trimer T is nonquiet by R160 and forces historical signed-support/anchor interaction or cross-generation pair interaction before unrelated reset. Thus |B|>=3 suffices, and the R244 bound implies four-core occupancy r>=14 suffices.

## Proof
Fix d and y. For each b in B, R3 gives one tight d-middle orientation of {b,d,y}; R41 signs {b,d} with witness y. Two polarities partition B, so one class P has h>=ceil(|B|/2). Take any T subset E_a\\{d} of size three; R8 makes T a tight trimer. Since b lies on K_a and d is excluded from T, each spoke {b,d} is disjoint from T. R41 gives opposite polarities on T terminal dimers, so choose the one M opposite to P. R39 gives a direct pair birth (spoke,M) for every b in P. If |B|>=3 then h>=2. R160 makes the second such birth nonquiet. Finally ceil((r-1)/6)>=3 exactly for r>=14.
